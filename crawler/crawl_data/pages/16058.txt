Demonkind and Humankind have co-existed in peace. Suddenly, the Demon Lord declared war and began a full assault on humanity. All anyone could do was fight to survive. The question still remains: Why did the Demon Lord attack? Notice Me Leena-senpai! focuses on the lives of Leena and her friends studying at the All-Girls School of Magic, where one of the Demon Lords minions has begun to attack. Why is the magic school a target? Will Leena and her friends be able to defend the school, her city, and her beloved food? Gameplay Notice Me Leena is a grid based tower defense game with visual novel elements. Fight your way through countless waves of enemies with 28 types of towers to push back the forces of the Demon Lord! Maybe you'll find out why he's attacking along the way. Unlock new tower types to aid you in battles! Be careful however, as the Demon Lord has many minions, and he just might have noticed you. Features The game features ten worlds in a story filled campaign mode. With twenty-eight unique towers to build your defenses with, the possibilities are endless! There are also eight unique consumable items you can purchase to aid you in combat. The game also has two survival modes, and if you want an even greater challenge, you're in luck as there's five difficulty levels to choose from! Tons of endless fun! Supported Control Modes Keyboard, Mouse, and Controller. Press or click a button, with the game in focus, on the respective devices to swap!
