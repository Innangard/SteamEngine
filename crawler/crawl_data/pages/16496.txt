Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Roll the Bones is a solo-developed game - a vision of a single person, developed by the very same person. It was playtested by a small group of friends and Steam volunteers, and it's ready to be played and enjoyed, but there's so much more planned for the game. Roll the Bones is taking the Early Access route to fine-tune each aspect of the game and build up on the in-game content in the direction largely based on community feedback.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “At this point the most cautious estimate would be: no longer than one year.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The plan for the course of Early Access is to improve the difficulty curve so the game is challenging but not overly frustrating, introduce quality-of-life improvements as per EA players' suggestions, and to add some extra content, to make the game world even richer for the full release.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game is entirely playable and all of the features mentioned in the "About This Game" section of the storepage are already in the game.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The game's Early Access price is somewhat reduced as a token of appreciation towards the players who get the game early and support its further development. About This Game Let's Roll The Bones Welcome to a fast-paced roguelite game, where your survival depends on quick reflexes and magic dice . Explore the deep dungeons , collect new powers , and destroy your enemies . Master the Rolling Mechanic Your power comes from rolling the dice . Every die has six unique sides, and each side holds a different magical effect. Survival requires precise aiming and good timing , not just pure luck. However, the dice can still surprise you. Landing a Critical roll completely changes your attack and creates a massive chain reaction . Endless Build Synergies No two runs are the same . You can choose from a massive variety of spell-dice, upgrades, and artifacts. During your adventure, you will find rare modifiers that completely change how your spells work. For example, a simple fireball can become a bouncing attack that clears the entire room. You can create a build focused on raw explosive damage, freezing defense, or pure chaos . Combine your choices to find powerful synergies . Conquer Epic Bosses Deadly bosses wait at the end of your path. These epic fights will test your skills to the limit. You must dodge complex enemy attacks , manage your spell cooldowns, and find the perfect moment to strike back. Only the most skilled players will survive. Survive the Hordes and Challenges Dungeons are full of different enemies. Each enemy has unique attacks, so you must constantly adapt your strategy . You will also find special challenge rooms . Test your skills in these dangerous rooms to earn rare and powerful loot . Wishlist Now!
