The house is enormous from Silky's perspective, and every room is packed with secrets to uncover and plenty of opportunities to make a beautiful mess. BECOME A WEB DESIGNER  🕸️ Stretch your webs from wall to wall, lift objects, build bridges, and create structures nobody asked for. Your webs, your rules. Whether you build a carefully engineered web to transport objects across the room or fling your webs at everything in sight, the physics system responds to every decision you make. Connect objects, suspend them from the ceiling, and use your webs to swing yourself to new heights. CHORES HAVE NEVER BEEN THIS FUN  🧼 The humans are out fighting the mysterious mortgage. Now it's time for Silky and the Web Scrubbers to pick up the slack, tackle chores, solve mysteries, and make the house their own. One web at a time. Each room is home to a cast of new spiders with their own personalities, tasks, and problems that need solving. Cook breakfast, fix the aquarium, chase down runaway balloons, or help Vera crack the case of the mysterious Prof. Quack. No two quests are the same, and most of them can be solved in more than one way.
