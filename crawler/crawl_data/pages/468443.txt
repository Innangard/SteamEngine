The Knight clashes against endless waves of undead, but for what? The memories, the reasons, The Order they swore themselves to, all faded to dust long ago. Now they simply fight, die, and rise. Again, and again, and again. Yet, the fleeting flames of Remembrance have begun to spark. Unravel the mystery of the Knight's immortality curse, piece together a tattered past, and perhaps grant them the chance to break the Cycle of Undeath once and for all. A endless battle against the undead in the corner of your screen Infi-Knight lives in a resizable, movable window beside your other tasks. Look over to tithe your Gold, swap a Weapon, ignite a surge of Zeal , or unlock what the Knight has remembered in the Memory codex. Death rejects you The Knight has one hit point and has been curse-bound to never truly die. Sooner or later, the horde will reach them. The Knight will fall, and rise, and fight again. However , nothing is lost when the Knight dies: every coin and every XP orb gained from the undead contributes to the Knight's growing power. Immortality is your punishment and penance . A worthy arsenal Ten Weapons , each with their own way of unmaking the undead. A lane-clearing Halberd, a Holy Mace blessed by God, a full-field Arbalest, a burning Lance that sweeps across the horde, a massive Greatsword to cleave the graves, and more. Every kill contributes to that weapon's Mastery , a system that rewards sticking with a specific weapon to unlock passive upgrades with powerful Tier 5 and 10 Capstone Perks that change the way each weapon plays . Steadily grow your power As The Knight stands against the undead, they collect XP orbs and gain up to 99 Levels to fuel their baseline power and unlock new gameplay features. Swear your Oath and gather lost Relics Swear one of six Oaths , each a bargain to be weighed. Equip Relics that pair with your Weapon and Oath to create your very own build with which to stand against the endless undead. Rekindle Memories to uncover your past Reach milestones to reawaken your history as a Knight of The Order. With rekindled memories come new Weapons , Relics , Lore and more. Gather Embers from Bosses Pry valuable Embers from the bones of Bosses . Then, spend them by performing Last Rites to gain permanent upgrades back at your Camp . Restore your Camp Your hard-won Embers are consumed at Camp, which provides you with permanent meta-progression that unlocks new gameplay elements and steadily improves your power. A long incremental game with a Story Mode ending, and an unlockable Endless Mode. Dozens of Memories across four acts, rekindled by your milestones and accomplishments. Piece together who the Knight was, the nature of their Immortality Curse, what their Order did, and why the dead will not remain buried. A true desktop companion incremental idler. Resizable, borderless, option for always-on-top.
