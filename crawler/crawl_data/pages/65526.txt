Explore a handcrafted pixel-art fantasy world alone or with up to seven other players in seamless drop-in/drop-out co-op. The Shadow Over Mirkea Corruption spreads across Mirkea. Sylbos, Last of the Ogre Magi, seeks to claim Dimraeth and the power upon which it stands. By a twist of fate, you are granted an ancient power you do not understand. The venerable wizard Faustas recruits you to stand with Mirkea’s free peoples against Sylbos and the dark gods. Create Your Archetype Combine any of eight races with any of six classes to create 48 distinct Archetypes. Each pairing has its own skill tree and playstyle. Every Attack Is a Commitment, Manage your stamina, read enemy telegraphs, and position yourself before choosing to strike, dodge, block, or parry. Equip five spells at a time and build tactical rotations and combinations around your loadout. Your race and class offer their own spells, while crafted spellbooks grant access to universal magic, provided you meet each spell’s attribute requirements. More than 300 spells are available in total. Test your build against demanding enemies and bosses. Adventure, Then Return Home Explore Mirkea, completing quests and recruiting allies as you fight, claim loot, and gather resources. Return home to craft equipment, brew potions, and cook hearty meals that increase your vitality. Build farms, quarries, and other structures for a steady supply of crops and raw materials. Deeper relationships unlock new conversations, personal quests, and potential romances. Invest materials in your allies’ facilities to expand shop inventories, add new crafting services, and open more farming plots. Every journey leaves your hero stronger and your home more complete. Together or Alone Play alone or in seamless drop-in/drop-out co-op for up to eight players. Your hero’s level, equipment, inventory, spells, and personal progression travel between worlds, while story progress, recruited allies, and buildings remain tied to each world. Dimraeth is in active development toward Early Access Release on September 15th 2026.
