View Community Hub About This Game No Body, No Problem is an asymmetrical, physics-based social stealth game for 4 players, packed with laughter as you and your friends attempt to pull off the perfect crime. A murder has taken place. There is a body that needs to be hidden. The call has been made, and the police are on the way. Three players control the suspects, while one player takes the role of the police officer responding to the report. The suspects must hide the body, remove the evidence, and leave the scene without raising suspicion. The officer must search every corner, track suspicious behaviour, and find the body that proves the murder. Time is your greatest enemy. The police could knock on the door at any moment, and one small mistake can bring the entire plan crashing down in seconds. There Is No Such Thing as a Perfect Crime Every match unfolds through complete improvisation. A friend may panic while carrying the body, the wardrobe you chose may not be as safe as you expected, or the tiniest clue left behind may catch the officer’s attention. Plans often fall apart within the first few minutes, forcing you to act entirely under pressure. That is exactly where the fun begins. The Cop Is Trying to See Everything The cop is not only looking for the body. An open door, a broken object, a freshly cleaned floor, or even strange behaviour from the players can create suspicion. Examine the crime scene carefully, search every room, and turn the suspects’ smallest mistake into an advantage. A new clue could appear at any second. Every Location Is a Different Puzzle No two matches play out the same way. The crime scene may be a small apartment, a crowded shop, a multi-storey car park, or somewhere completely different. Every map offers its own hiding places, interactive objects, and unexpected opportunities. A method that works on one map may expose you on another. Physics-Based Chaos Almost every object in the game interacts through physics. While carrying the body, you may knock over furniture, get stuck in narrow corridors, accidentally attract attention, or turn the entire place upside down without meaning to. Even the best plan can become a hilarious disaster because of a single mistake. How Much Do You Trust Your Friends? The suspects must work together. Good coordination can remove every piece of evidence before the police arrive. But as the pressure rises, panic begins. A friend may make the wrong decision, expose you, or blame everything on you to save themselves. Every Match Tells a New Story No two games are ever the same. Different maps, hiding methods, physics-based interactions, and player decisions ensure that every match creates its own funny, chaotic, and unforgettable story. One match may end with the perfect crime, while another may fall apart seconds after the police open the door. Because sometimes it is not the police who ruin the perfect plan... It is your friends. No body, no problem.
