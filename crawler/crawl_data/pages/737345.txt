Buy Baldur's Gate II: Reflections Of Myth & Valor $4.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Customize your character’s look and feel with sixteen brand new portraits created by Mike Sass! Yes, THE Mike Sass, the original artist who designed all of the portraits of our beloved Baldur’s Gate companions: Minsc, Dynaheir, Jaheira, Khalid, Edwin, Viconia, the list goes on. These 16 portraits represent a huge variety of classes, subclasses, races, and alignments that the Baldur’s Gate games are known for: from a half-orc Swashbuckler, to an elven Priest of Tyr, to a Dwarven Defender. With the impeccable style of Mike Sass, these new portraits are the beginning of your next role-playing adventure. “I think people like the BG portraits because the dialogue and voices combine with a living, realistic picture of the character. Taking photos of specific poses and expressions allowed me to break from generic stereotypes and portray these characters in a manner where the portrait communicates a character's motivation or background.” ~ Mike Sass Now, let’s gather a party of fresh faces and venture forth onto Faerûn!
