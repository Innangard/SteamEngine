Instead of avoiding mines, you'll be discovering cute cats hiding in boxes. But finding them is just the beginning – these hungry kitties need treats to stick around! Key Features: Search for hidden cats but remember to keep biscuits ready! Each discovered cat must be fed before you continue, or they'll scamper away. Score & Progress: Feed cats with varying amounts of biscuits to earn points. Reach the target score to advance and unlock new challenges! Collect coins and get useful items: Biscuit Jars to replenish your treats Cat Teasers to safely peek inside boxes Capture Nets to catch escaping cats And more Collect & Progress: 100 unique cats to discover and add to your collection Each cat has its own personality and appearance New cats appear as you play more, encouraging exploration 5 Exciting Difficulty Modes: Easy & Normal: Perfect for casual play (5-10 minutes) Hard, Challenge & Extreme: Face more complex maps and special cats. Extended sessions (15-30 minutes) with tricky surprises. Watch out for mischievous dogs in higher difficulties! With its charming art style and casual gameplay loop, Unbox The Cats is the purr-fect choice for puzzle enthusiasts and cat lovers alike. Each session brings new cats to discover, making every game a delightful adventure of tactical decisions and cute rewards. Whether you're looking for a quick coffee break distraction or a longer strategic challenge, there's a difficulty level that's right for you. Can you collect all the cats and become the ultimate feline friend?
