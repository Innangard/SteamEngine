Composed and produced by Tatreal (Slava Korystov) and Giacomo Giunchedi from their studios in Canada and Italy, the soundtrack features 40 original tracks across four albums, each with its own distinct sound: folk, shoegaze, ambient electronica, and spiritual ambient. From acoustic guitars and dreamy, reverb-drenched textures to electronic soundscapes and moments of quiet contemplation, the music shifts and evolves like the journey itself. Drawing on their experience as multi-instrumentalists and electronic producers, Tatreal and Giunchedi combine acoustic instrumentation with analog synths to create a soundtrack that follows the landscapes, atmosphere, and challenges of over the hill.
