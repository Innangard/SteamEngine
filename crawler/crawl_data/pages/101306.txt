Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game ## About This Game Micro Party throws you into 150+ micro-games. Five to fifteen seconds each. No tutorial, no explanation, no warning. Rock climbing, fishing, dodging traffic, a black hole, jousting, a thing with bubbles. You figure it out while you're playing it. Local couch multiplayer is the intended way to play it. 2 to 4 people, sharing keyboards and gamepads. Online multiplayer also exists. It's direct P2P, no servers, no accounts. It works. It does not sync particularly well. I consider this a feature. ### What's actually in the box - 150+ micro-games across various categories: Race, Fight, Climb, Sports, Survival, Music, etc. - 2-4 local players, keyboards and gamepads - Online multiplayer (P2P, just type an IP, like 1998) - Solo play, all games unlocked, no progression bullshit - Random mode, Party mode, Chaos mode and custom micro game selection - Procedural sound, vector graphics, no microtransactions, no battle pass, no season pass, no premium currency, no daily login bonus, and in the paid steam version also no crypto miner - Available in most languages AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Most of this game is AI-generated. In-game text (UI, game names, descriptions), graphics (player icons, vector elements), code and the majority of the micro-game implementations — all AI, then reviewed and curated by the developer. Localization to all non-English languages is AI-translated. No AI runs at runtime; everything is pre-generated and shipped statically.
