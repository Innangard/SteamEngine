View Community Hub About This Game The city is dead... but you're not. Fight your way through the infected streets of a Brazilian slum , a single-player zombie survival game where every bullet matters and every decision can mean the difference between life and death. Face relentless hordes of the undead,  collect powerful weapons, and survive long enough to get the best gun of the game SURVIVE AGAINST THE HORDE The infected never stop coming. Manage your ammunition, choose your fights wisely, and master a growing arsenal of realistic firearms, from revolvers and pistols to assault rifles, shotguns, and heavy weapons. SURVIVE Zombies can drop  the mysterious Cursed Blood . The longer you survive and the more infected you eliminate, the greater your chances of getting it. Death is not the end. Return stronger, unlocking new weapons, upgrades, and equipment for your next attempt. BUILD YOUR ARSENAL Get and equip various of weapons, each with its own characteristics, handling, and stopping power. Adapt your loadout to your preferred playstyle and prepare for increasingly dangerous encounters. FEATURES • Fast-paced zombie survival gameplay • Large selection of realistic firearms • Weapon progression and equipment upgrades • Persistent progression between runs • Shop system after each death • Atmospheric environments inspired by a fallen city Will you survive long enough to escape... or become another corpse feeding the streets of Slum Z ?
