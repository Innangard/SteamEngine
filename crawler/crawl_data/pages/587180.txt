We also believe that feedback from players will help the game reach the level of quality we aim for.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan for the Early Access period to last approximately 3 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Throughout development, we will continue to add new content, balance adjustments, visual and audio improvements, as well as general gameplay improvements. More enemies, towers, and levels are also among our plans.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version offers the core gameplay loop in a fully playable state. Players can place defensive towers made from breakfast foods, combine different towers, and defend the stomach against waves of bacteria.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We do not plan to change the price after Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We plan to involve the community throughout Early Access by actively collecting feedback through the Steam Community Hub, Steam Discussions, reviews, and player bug reports. We will regularly read player feedback and use it to help prioritize updates and improvements. Community feedback will have a direct impact on areas such as game balance, tower and enemy balancing, difficulty progression, level design, user interface, quality-of-life improvements, bug fixes, and the overall gameplay experience. We will also consider suggestions for new content and features when they fit the direction of the game. During Early Access, we plan to share updates and development progress through Steam announcements and discussions so players can see what is changing and provide further feedback.
