Multiple parallel universes are being consumed by a mysterious blight known only as "The Swarm." These ravenous creatures devour everything in their path, causing the laws of spacetime to break down and worlds to catastrophically merge. To combat this threat, the interdimensional "Agent Alliance" has been formed. Now, the Alliance needs you—an elite "Paw Agent"! Alongside the bravest, furriest companions in the cosmos, you will be deployed into worlds on the brink of collapse. Your mission: execute clean-up operations, push back the tide of chaos, and trace the Swarm back to its terrifying source. Paw Agents is a 2.5D top-down roguelite that blends high-quality hand-drawn cartoon aesthetics with intense, satisfying combat. Prepare for exhilarating action, limitless build-crafting, and a sci-fi adventure that hides a deep, unfolding mystery beneath its charming and humorous surface.
