View Community Hub About This Game Protect your brain from viruses in Increbraintal, an incremental auto battler. Build your team and unlock upgrades in the Skill Tree to eliminate the contamination taking over the body. The resources you collect can be used in the Skilltree to unlock permanent upgrades. When it comes to dominating viruses, you can never be too careful! Count on your companions, which can be found in the Deck . Build an effective layout where everyone can complement each other in some way. Need long-range support as well? Let your minions handle it. Even though they’re tiny little cuties, they can take care of the dirty work.
