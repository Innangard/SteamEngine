View Community Hub About This Game The clock is ticking. The Earth is breaking. Will you survive the Terraclysm? Verticality is Your Best Weapon Forget the flat planes of traditional survivors. In Terraclysm, the environment is as dangerous as the enemies. Navigate treacherous, crumbling stages where platforming meets bullet-hell. Master the art of the jump to dodge screen-filling boss attacks and rain down elemental fury from above. In this world, if you stand still, you’re already dead. The Ultimate Doomsday Build Every run is a fresh chance to become a god of the wasteland. Scavenge ancient tech and eldritch shards to create game-breaking synergies. Do you want to be a gravity-defying rogue or a walking mountain of tanky destruction? With hundreds of item combinations, your "ultimate build" isn't just a dream, it’s a necessity to survive the escalating Terraclysm. 4-Player Cooperative Chaos Why save the world alone when you can bring the whole squad? Terraclysm Survivors features massive 4-player online co-op. Coordinate your builds to create a literal wall of firepower. When the screen is 90% projectiles and 10% monster, you'll need every friend you can get to find the path to victory. Epic Boss Encounters Face colossal, world-ending bosses that push your skills, build, and squad to the absolute limit. Study their devastating patterns, forge the perfect strategy, and unleash every ounce of firepower you've got. These aren't just fights, they're the ultimate test of everything you've built to survive the Terraclysm.
