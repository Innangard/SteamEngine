View Community Hub About This Game Elven Huntress: Sunveil is an atmospheric third-person 3D action RPG with exploration, loot, crafting, and character customization. Play as an elven huntress, explore fantasy locations through magical portals, fight orcs, humans, and the undead, find rare equipment, and improve your character. Explore a fantasy world Travel through the portal to a variety of locations: Snow-covered mountain ruins. Coniferous forests. Ancient fortresses. Dungeons. Dark lands of the undead. Fight enemies, gather resources, unlock new equipment, and improve your character. Create your own elven huntress Use the character creator, customize your appearance, and collect unique sets of equipment. Sunveil Fortress Sunveil is your main hub and progression center. Between raids, upgrade your fortress and unlock new features: Weapon and armor crafting. Alchemy and potion making. Equipment transmogrification. Item enhancement with runes. Shop. Character progression system. Elven Huntress: Sunveil is an atmospheric single-player adventure with a beautiful protagonist, enjoyable progression, and a world you'll want to return to.
