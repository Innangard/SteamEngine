Normalt $9.99 – nedsatt till $7.99 Köp Visa gemenskapscentral Om detta spel Voodoo Fishin’ is a 1–6 player online co-op creepy-cozy fishing game featuring proximity voice chat and swamp-dwelling surprises. Venture into the bayou with friends in search of rare and legendary fish. The deeper you go, the stranger things get. Fish together. Laugh together. Try not to lose your haul. If you die, the swamp keeps your catch. (And any Tackle you have equipped) Craft your puppet angler and hit the dock! Then dress them up with Puppet Parts you can only earn out in the swamp. Meet the Locals Lil’ Lucile, Jonah Swol, Kenny the Mechanic, and Sketchy Steve all need a hand, and they don’t always want to explain why. Take on 37 missions, from tea parties to “legally ambiguous” deliveries, and earn exclusive Tackle and Puppet Parts you can’t get anywhere else. The locals move around the swamp every day with a new mission of the day, so keep your eyes open. It’s chill… until it isn’t. This swamp hunts back. This swamp hunts back, and some of what lives here is bigger than the fish. If you make it back alive, the Fish Monger pays by the pound. Fill the Quota, Together Every fish your crew sells fills a shared Dock Quota. Hit it and the whole dock unlocks upgrades like bigger coolers, Sprint Boost, and Jump Boost. Two Fish Mongers each offer their own Bounties and Quota rewards, so pick your prize as a crew and start hauling. Need backup? Bonk a buddy’s fish with the Fish Club, or toss chum from the Chum Bucket to get a fished-out spot bubbling again. Sell, Upgrade, Repeat If you make it back alive, the Fish Monger pays by the pound. Sell your junk at Rusty Remy’s, hunt for deals at the Pawn Shop, and upgrade your Bait, Reels, and Rods to chase bigger and rarer catches. Then pay Auntie Brigitte a visit for voodoo-enchanted bait that lures in the swamp’s strangest species. Play Forever The swamp doesn’t run on corporate servers. Voodoo Fishin’ is self-hosted , so once you’re in, you can always jump back in with friends. No server shutdowns. No “service discontinued.” The swamp stays open as long as you can stay alive. The demo provides access to the full core gameplay systems, but limits world exploration and higher-tier Tackle.
