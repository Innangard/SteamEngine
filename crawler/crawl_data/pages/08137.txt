Break the furniture, crash the truck or somehow keep everything under control. Let's see how your first deliveries turn out. MOVE TOGETHER Some items can't be moved alone. Stretch out your arms, sync up with your teammate and lift together. Watch out for tight corridors, slippery stairs and, of course, your troll friends. If your friend is behind the wheel, there's only one question: Do you really trust your driver? YOUR FIRST DELIVERIES Every delivery can bring a different challenge. Carry items through tight spaces, overcome obstacles and try to get everything to its destination in one piece.
