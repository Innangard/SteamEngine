View Community Hub About This Game Gildoria - Organize a Dragon's Hoard For a few hundred years, a dragon collected and never once tidied up. Now the gold has swallowed the shelves, the furniture and most of the floor and everything the dragon actually treasures is buried somewhere inside it. You have a bag. You have time. Get sorting. Sweep up the hoard Aim your bag and pull coins straight off the pile one at a time at first, then in fat, greedy handfuls. Pour them into the chests around the lair; when one fills up, it slams shut, and that gold is yours to spend. Get scarily good at it A deeper bag. Faster hands. More coins per grab. Nothing changes the job everything changes how it feels. By the end, you'll clear in seconds what used to take minutes. Find what the gold buried Books, relics, keepsakes, oddities the hoard has been swallowing them for centuries. Spot something glinting through the coins, dig it free, carry it to the shelves, and set it down exactly where it belongs. Complete a collection, and the lair opens up a little more. Pet the cats The cats were here long before you, and they'll be here long after. Every single one can be petted. The game keeps count. Take your time No timers, no fail states, nobody looking over your shoulder. The pile isn't going anywhere, and your progress saves itself.
