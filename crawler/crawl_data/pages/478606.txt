About This Demo This is the “Shrink Rooms” demo. Players can try out typical early - game level puzzles and embark on an intense puzzle - solving escape journey, fully engaging their brainpower! Unique Demo Version The demo version will open more than 40 levels of game content. In the demo, players can experience the main puzzles of the first chapter of the game and the representative levels of subsequent chapters. New Level Mechanism New level mechanisms such as "Polymer" and "Portal Wall" will appear. Advanced Puzzles Rack Your Brains Are you still not satisfied with the basic puzzles? Let's increase the difficulty level for you! The basic gameplay of Shrink Rooms is to solve puzzles by pushing boxes. Different from other puzzle games, the walls of the level's room will contract inward at the end of each round. You must accurately plan the number of steps for pushing boxes to escape from the secret room, so as to avoid being crushed into a puddle of flesh by the walls. There are various interesting mechanisms in the shrink rooms. The characteristics of different mechanisms are combined to generate many puzzles with rich variations and high difficulty. Welcome to challenge! You can move freely in the selection scene. You can choose easier puzzles to continue the game, or try to solve all difficult puzzles. At the same time, in addition to solving puzzles, you can also explore and collect virtual exhibits and find hidden levels in the scene to explore different fun.
