Lives on Your Desktop A semi-transparent window runs right alongside everything else on your screen Drag it anywhere, zoom in and out, pin it always-on-top, or click straight through it Glance over between tasks, click a dropped chest, and collect your rewards Five Heroes, One Party Knight, Warrior, Assassin, Archer, and Mage — five classes, five distinct roles Level up each class with stat points and a kit of active & passive skills — equip a loadout of up to four actives Dress every class in unlockable costumes — wearing is purely cosmetic, so mix and match freely Unlock new classes and expand your skill slots as you grow Endless Loot, Deep Gear Crafting Over 400 pieces of gear across 8 grades and 12 slots Layered enhancement with gems, engravings, and enchantments Forge, fuse, and craft at the Blacksmith; engrave, enchant, socket, and extract at the Mage Tower Stockpile the random chests that drop in battle and open them whenever you like Four Regions, 200 Battles 4 acts × 10 stages × 5 difficulties (Normal, Hard, Nightmare, Hell, Inferno) Stage bosses and powerful act bosses that hit back with their own signature skills Auto-challenge farms your current stage on repeat — and you keep earning gold & XP even while the game is closed (offline catch-up, up to 16 hours) Progression That Never Stops Orb Circuit — a sprawling directional growth tree with 200+ nodes Constellations — a second growth axis with powerful keystone bonuses Collections & Factions — completing costume gear collections grants permanent, party-wide bonuses, and owning more costumes from the same faction makes every hero stronger Pets — unlock them by slaying monsters and keep their bonuses just by owning them Automated Combat With Real Strategy Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, and Chaos elements, each with their own status effects A layered mitigation pipeline of dodge, block, damage reduction, and armor Downed heroes and revives, plus a live damage meter to track each hero's
