Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Desktop Companion is not the kind of vibe-coded AI slop thrown together in a weekend. It's a real application, hand-built over the past year and a half. I'm a full-stack developer with 10+ years of experience, and the app is already feature-rich and stable today, including its full agentic toolset. I'm releasing it in Early Access because the best ideas for a desktop AI companion come from the people who live with one all day. What I still want to refine and grow (more app integrations, more languages and voices, and continuous polish) is exactly the kind of work that benefits most from real user feedback. Early Access lets me ship what already works and prioritize what to build next based on how people actually use it.” Approximately how long will this software be in Early Access? “I currently expect Desktop Companion to remain in Early Access for roughly 3 to 6 months. This estimate may shift as the roadmap evolves and depending on the scope of community feedback, but my goal is to reach a feature-complete 1.0 within that window.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version will build on the Early Access app rather than replace it. I plan to expand the agentic integrations with more connected apps and services over time. Other planned additions include more interface and voice languages, additional and higher-quality voices, broader on-device model support, and ongoing work on performance, UI scaling, and stability. Characters, memory, and customization will keep growing based on community requests.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Desktop Companion is already fully usable today: an always-on-top animated avatar with screen vision, voice and text chat, 9 hand-crafted characters plus full custom-character creation (Live2D/VRM), long-term memory, and 8 interface languages, with a bundled local AI model and no subscription. The agentic toolset is already built in: web search, site browsing, live trend tracking, file creation, and app launching. It runs safely out of the box and works fully offline.” Will this software be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. Desktop Companion is offered at a lower price during Early Access to thank early supporters, and I plan to increase the price as the app becomes more complete and gains new features toward its full release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback drives the roadmap. I read and respond to suggestions, and many features have come directly from user requests. Users can share ideas, report issues, and help prioritize what comes next through the Steam Community Hub and my Discord, and I post regular updates on what's changing and what's coming.” Read more Buy Desktop Companion INTRODUCTORY OFFER! Before you buy: hardware requirements Desktop Companion runs entirely locally on your own PC (or on another machine on your local network). It requires a recent dedicated graphics card with at least 10 GB of VRAM ; 12 GB or more is recommended. Integrated graphics, found in most thin laptops, are not supported. Results may vary depending on your hardware and the model you use. It lives on top of everything Your companion floats above every window (games, apps, browsers), always one glance away. A real desktop presence, not a tab you alt-tab to. It doesn't just answer, it acts Ask for something and it goes and does it. It searches the live web and reads real pages, so it answers with today's information instead of stale training data, with nothing to sign up for and nothing to configure. It tracks what's blowing up on Reddit and X, checks the weather, and opens your apps and your folders. And when there's something worth keeping, it writes the file itself: a quick note, a Word document, a full spreadsheet, saved and ready to open. One request, and the work is done. It sees your screen Show it anything on screen and it reads it straight from a screenshot: a game, a chart, a line of code, a menu in another language. Point it at a chess position and it'll walk you through your next move. Screen vision runs on the built-in model, on your own graphics card, so what's on your screen stays on your machine. Your Twitch co-host Connect your Twitch account in one click and your companion joins your stream as a live AI co-host: it reads your chat and replies to your viewers in character, out loud, with moderation controls you set. Bring your own VTuber model and never stream to dead air again. It remembers you Real long-term memory. It keeps what matters from your conversations and brings it back days later. Close it, come back, and it picks up exactly where you left off. It runs entirely on your machine Desktop Companion comes with its own AI model, built in and ready the moment it's installed. Nothing to download, no account to create, no key to paste, no credits to buy, no monthly bill. Every reply is generated on your own graphics card, and your conversations stay on your machine. Unplug the network cable and it keeps working exactly the same. One purchase, and it's yours. It speaks, and you can clone a voice Three text-to-speech engines are included and run side by side, all of them local and free: Pocket-TTS, Kokoro and OmniVoice. Clone a voice on your own machine and give every character its own: in high quality with OmniVoice, or on the CPU alone with Pocket-TTS. Nothing is sent anywhere. Live captions show every spoken line in the language you choose, so it can speak one language while you read along in another. Switch on Anime Mode and it speaks real Japanese with subtitles on screen. Nine characters, or build your own Start in seconds with nine hand-written personalities, each a fully scripted persona localized into all 8 languages: Anna the Playful Sibling, Alice the Witty Historian, Michael the Gaming Buddy, Amelia the Supportive Friend, Mei the Yandere Girlfriend, Rei the Calm Mentor, Iris the Tsundere Companion, Lyra the Creative Muse, and Marc the Stoic Coach. Or build your own from four pieces you choose: a premade Live2D (2D) or custom VRM (3D) avatar, a persona, a voice engine, and a cloned voice. Import any VRM model from VRoid Hub, booth.pm, or your own creations. It's less an app than an AI companion platform, and a VTuber maker on the side. It speaks your language Interface, voice recognition, text-to-speech and every character are fully localized, not half-translated, in 8 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, 日本語, Русский.
