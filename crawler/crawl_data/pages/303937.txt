View Community Hub FOLLOW US About This Game 🍗This might just be the most delicious, wackiest, and most addictive tower defense game ever made!🍗 ​​The story begins at a mysterious BBQ stall You have been chosen as the new Grillmaster! Your foes are not scary monsters, but a cheeky horde of mice drawn by the smell of your skewers. Their goal is to nibble everything in sight. Your mission is to protect the grill and keep your skewers safe! Your Skewers Are Your Towers Drag, drop, and watch your tasty skewers jump into battle. Shoot, bounce, and splash. You can even boop enemies right off the field. Survive 20 minutes of fast-paced roguelite runs where your only goal is to defend your grill at all costs. Choose Your Perfect Build 20+ Grillmasters, each with unique talents Every Grillmaster can become the core of a different playstyle, letting you craft your own exclusive skewer squad! 40+ tasty and powerful foods Whether they split, bounce, or knock enemies back, combine these effects to master your signature playstyle! 100+ powerful seasonings and items spawn randomly, adding constant depth to your playstyle and making every run a brand new experience! Blazing Fast, Thrilling Fun and Fresh Every Run! A minute to learn, ten minutes to get hooked, twenty minutes to never stop! Every match is unique, and every skewer brings surprises!
