The lanterns are lit, the slots are warm, and your lucky cat is ready to work. Maneki Mania is a kawaii fortune shrine bursting with gold and good luck. Pet the neko to earn koban and fill the fortune meter, then spin the charm slots for the jackpot. Pet, spin, repeat. Pet for koban Pet the cat with the mouse or a controller, whichever you like. Every pet pays out and charges the fortune meter a little more. Spin the charm slots A full meter lights the lever: pull it yourself until a cat paw starts pulling it for you. Pairs, triples and the triple-koban jackpot each pay, with casino synth that swells the bigger you hit. Collect ten lucky charms Each charm arrives at a milestone of upgrades bought and sits on the shrine shelf, with its own power and a stacking income bonus. The tanuki even pays interest on your haul since the last milestone. Grow the shrine Climb an 85-node upgrade tree, spin the Wheel of Fortune for talents, and raise shrine ranks that multiply everything you earn. Mouse or controller. 31 in-game languages. All the luck you can grab.
