View Community Hub About This Game PLOT There is no more land left to bury the dead. Meanwhile, the planet is drowning in trash, car exhaust, and oil. Witness the strange happenings on the beach of a once-top resort. You are a bot, a beach cleaner and trash sorter named S.A.N.D.Y. - Sweeper & Neutralizer of Debris (Yours). For your happiness, it's enough that human eyeglasses stay on your camera. And year after year, you work diligently, no matter what. Thank you. GAMEPLAY Clean the marked beach area → Sort the collected trash for disposal → Rescue the animals → A new area  is unlocked → Repeat. As you progress through the game, you will develop the story and upgrade your character.
