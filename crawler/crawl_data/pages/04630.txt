We plan to share updates as the timeline becomes clearer.” Hur är det tänkt att den fullständiga versionen ska skilja sig från versionen i Early Access? “We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. The scope and priorities may change during development.” Hur är det nuvarande läget i versionen med Early Access? “The game supports single-player and online co-op. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required.” Kommer spelet att prissättas annorlunda under och efter Early Access? “We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release.
