All it wants to do is quietly go fishing. “Right in the corner of your desktop!” Collect fish to fill up the cat’s encyclopedia, earn money, and dress it up in adorable outfits! Desktop Fishing Cat is a calm and charming idle desktop game . Watch this little black cat fish peacefully on your screen and enjoy a soothing moment. Please help this cat... “Right in the corner of your desktop!” -------------- This game was inspired by Cornerpond’s systems and the artwork of Illu Finch.
