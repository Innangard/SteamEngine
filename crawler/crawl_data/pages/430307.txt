A dark story in which love turns into obsession, and memories into uncanny echoes. Every line of dialogue is fully voiced, so you can experience the characters' obsessions, screams, and madness up close. The decisions you make, the questions you ask, and the moments you choose to stay silent will change the course of the story. The paths you open across three acts can lead you to eight different endings. Be careful what you choose, and what you wish for.
