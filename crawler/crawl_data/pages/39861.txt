View Community Hub About This Game Stuffed into a drum of glowing goo and left for dead by an abandoned rest stop, you emerge a mutated monster with an insatiable new appetite... and a bloodthirsty desire for revenge... Stalk! Harvest! Feed! You are the terror that lurks amongst the shadows in My Cannibal Family, a gloriously grotesque monster horror sim with co-op multiplayer for up to 4 players. Build a twisted theme park beside a remote highway and use it to lure visitors to a gruesome death - harvesting their bodies to feed your growing family of loveable mutants. Gather resources and construct attractions during the day - then when night arrives, use traps and stealth attacks to eliminate guests within a deadly sandbox of your own creation! Use creative building tools to develop a truly unique brand of theme park filled with attractions, decorations and deadly traps. Watch your park grow as you play, from a simple wooden shack to a labyrinth of entertainment and gory horror! Your hideous face will terrify visitors, so use attractions, signage and devious building to lead your guests into the shadows. Work behind the curtain, wait for the right moment, then make your move... Eliminate guests with a range of tools, weapons and traps, then bag up that tasty meat for later. If someone spots you, they’ll attack or run - don’t let them escape! Your family hungers! Use any bodies you’ve bagged to satisfy your appetite and feed your growing brood of mutants. Bodies can also be used to unlock new traps, tools and weapons… or even gestate new family members. MORE FUN STUFF! Play solo or with friends in online co-op multiplayer for up to 4 players, or via split-screen (2 players only) Your mutant family are there to help you! Give them names like ‘Gregg’ and assign them tasks, like gathering resources, reviving players...or burlesque dancing. Use your growing park business to attract members of the mob crew who left you for dead, then enact terrible revenge upon them! Progress through a campaign that tells a story of love, loss and of course, family. The area surrounding your park is filled with enemies, bonus resources and hidden secrets to find. 6 different unlockable park themes, including funhouse, carnival and saloon. 15+ guns, melee weapons, and throwables, featuring slasher favourites like the machete, drill and harpoon 12+ traps, including spike walls, saw pits and The Crusher! 20+ attractions and distractions to entertain your guests with, including ‘The Mesmeriser’, ‘Vortex Tunnel’ and ‘Whack a’ Jelly’ Dozens of additional tools and equipment.
