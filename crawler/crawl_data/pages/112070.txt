Dive into a handcrafted open world where your choices and actions shape the environment. A society has discovered the power of a dead empire: to capture the spirits that govern nature and rule over them instead. However, the anger of the remaining spirits divided them. Where will you stand? Content of the Demo The Demo features the intro and the beginning of the game. You can fully explore the woodcutter camp and get known to the mechanics of Drova. You can test out how the quest system works, get a first impression of the combat and some magic spells. Savegames can be taken over into the full game as well. The Demo has been updated (June 2026) to be on par with the full game. SHEEP OR SHEPHERD, FIND YOUR GROUND Forge your own path around dangerous landscapes, fulfill tasks, trade, gather and craft equipment. You start with nothing and as a nobody. Study your environment, and use the surrounding clues to reveal mysteries and grow stronger. Only your flow of battle stands between you and certain death. MASTER THE FLOW OF BATTLE Join one of two factions, each with their own values and in pursuit of their own goals. Your choice will be relevant for the rest of the game and impact the entire story. Everything comes with a price. Meet teachers and learn numerous abilities, but expect foes and betrayals too. UNRAVEL THE POWER OF NATURE Explore nature and seal the power of spirits that rule it. Learn how to use them in your favor, but be prepared to battle their channeled anger in the world surrounding you.
