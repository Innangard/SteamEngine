Taking place in an authentic Southern Italian setting, the game puts you in the shoes of one man - Tugg DiMamma - embarking on a quest for revenge. ALL MASHED OUT! Float like a butterfly and sting like a Thug across SEVEN stages of intense mash-out mayhem, experiencing a slice of Southern-Italian flavored urban decay. Employ a mix of vicious body-flinging kicks , gravity defying acrobatics and truckloads of blunt force trauma to beat (literally!) the overwhelming odds set before you . Introduce a wide array of Thugs to your mean side - enemies will sport tactics such as Improvised body armor , Jerry Rigged Shields and even... JETPACKS ?!? Collect melee implements the likes of Lead pipes , Shovels , Rusty Chains, Bricks and Reinforcing Bars to discover new facets of Thug-Style combat, portrayed with best-in-class animations inspired by over-the-top street fighting and advanced Martial Arts ! When all else fails, bring a gun to a fist fight and re-balance the playing field with Revolvers , Shotgun blasts and contraband Machine guns ! COMBO OR DIE BRAWLING! Evade incoming attacks and chain together your moves to build up your HYPE. Keep the HYPE going to reach the highest scores, regenerate health in combat and TURN UP THE MUSIC . The dynamic OST ( inspired by the dustiest boom-bap records of yesteryear ) introduces fresh layers and more instrumentation as the HYPE stays flowing. Master the battlefield, set new score records and fight on to unlock BOSS DIFFICULTY - an extreme challenge with unique enemies only for the worthiest of thugs... It's up to YOU to brawl your way through hordes of Thugs to come face to face with the Don - and avenge the slight that broke the camel's back .
