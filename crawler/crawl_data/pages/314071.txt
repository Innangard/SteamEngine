Through unique, self-developed systems, Psych offers a new and innovative gaming experience that will not be forgotten. The world invites you to explore, if you dare. Every corner is filled with stories, puzzles or secrets to uncover. However, the biggest mystery is what happened on the day of the accident. While the main story has a few ways to unravel the mysteries, the side stories also provide plenty of clues and information. Psych is divided into 5 chapters that advance the story immersively, without the player noticing. The customized and countless sound effects will give you goosebumps after goosebumps. With Psych we want to create real horror, a game that will shake up the horror genre. Here in detail the main features of Psych: An emotional, thrilling and unforgettable main story divided into 5 chapters. Fully dubbed story with experienced voice actors Lots of side stories and mysteries, as well as puzzles to solve An atmosphere you won't forget A dynamic and interactable world Multiple locations to explore Unique mechanics Varied gameplay with different tasks to accomplish A customized system for experiencing horror A dozen of goosebump-inducing sound effects Stunning graphics with optimized performance Average playtime: 3 hours You are a lost lunatic looking for answers. You don't know what happened and the more time passes, the more questions come to your mind. In search of the truth you encounter some surprises. Experience the story of Alexander Green and find out what happened on the day of the accident. Follow the development of Alex, solve puzzles around the event and try not to fall into madness. Nothing is as it seems. Something or someone is following you. You must always be on your guard and be prepared for possible dangers. Especially at night, it can be quite dangerous when you see things you'd rather not see. Unstoppable tension. Not a minute will pass when you can feel safe. New clues will keep coming up and will tempt you to take another step. No step is like the other and leads to ever new exits. Scary horror atmosphere at every step of the game will make you fear. Whether you are prepared for it or not. There is no one who will not be afraid here. Self-developed systems that control the horror and your state of mind bring a new, intuitive and exciting way to play this psychological horror thriller. A small pinch of coincidence to the emotional and harrowing story of Psych brings each player a personalized gaming experience that can differ from time to time. Dozen side stories, puzzles and secrets to discover in the open world of Psych. Each with its own unique story that will captivate you. Goosebump sound effects will send a cold shiver down your spine. If you think that you have already seen and, above all, heard everything, you will be mistaken. There are so many tailor-made sound effects, perfectly tuned to the atmosphere, that will take your breath away again and again. Fascinating graphics through modern technology and a little bit of dexterity, additionally provides the right thrill while playing and takes you on an unforgettable journey through the world and story of Alexander Green. Varied places that invite you to explore. Whether it's a spooky park, a dark underground or a disturbing sewer system.
