Sell drugs on the side. Try not to get caught. CASH ONLY is a co-op crime simulator where you and your friends run a small café that doubles as a front for a much less legal business. By day, you're baristas. Customers line up, place orders, and expect their coffee hot and their panini not burned. Grind beans, pull espresso shots, prepare food, set your prices, and grow the reputation of your café. But coffee isn't where the real profit is. Grow different strains , prepare product and figure out who wants what. Every customer has their own preferences, tolerance and level of trust. Negotiate the price, prepare the order and choose a dead drop somewhere around town — a bench , a bush , or anywhere else that doesn't look suspicious. Better with friends CASH ONLY can be played solo or in co-op. One player can handle the café while another grows product, someone else makes deliveries, and another keeps an eye on the police. Or everyone can completely ignore the plan and make everything worse. Features: Run and upgrade your own coffee shop Prepare coffee, food and customer orders Grow and sell illegal products Build trust and tolerance with recurring customers Negotiate deals through an in-game phone Create and manage dead drops around town Avoid police patrols, searches and unwanted attention Explore a living town with NPC schedules and homes Shared co-op objectives Persistent world progression Fully playable solo Coffee by day.
