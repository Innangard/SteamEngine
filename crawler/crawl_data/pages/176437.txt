Guide your cube through intricate mazes, avoid enemy cubes, spikes, lasers, and flamethrowers, and discover how to overcome each level. Use moving platforms, elevators, cranes, and companion cubes to activate switches, solve puzzles, and reach your destination. Every level brings new challenges that test your timing, precision, and puzzle-solving skills. Features 36 Challenging Levels – Navigate increasingly complex puzzles and obstacles. 25 Hidden Relics – Explore levels to discover collectibles and unlock special rewards. 6 Cube Skins – Customize your cube with different colors and unique unlockable designs. Bonus Level – Collect all Relics to unlock an extra challenge featuring rotating bridges and magnetic walls. Creative Puzzle Mechanics – Control companion cubes, move crates, activate switches, and use cranes and elevators. Dangerous Obstacles – Avoid spikes, flamethrowers, lasers, and enemy cubes. Easy to Learn, Challenging to Master – A mix of puzzle-solving, precise movement, and timing. Quality-of-Life Improvements – Updated settings, improved controller navigation, and support for 31 interface languages. Can you complete every level, find all 25 Relics, and unlock every cube skin?
