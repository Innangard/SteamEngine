Raise these frogs and sell for them for a profit or keep them around to use in mini-games, breeding and to passively generate money online and offline! Open packs to get rare frog combinations! Be on the lookout for ultra-rare golden frogs! Raise your frogs by feeding them, and decide to keep them or sell them! Discover hundreds of attributes and millions of combinations of frogs! Breed your favourite frogs together to get new frogs! A big thank you for supporting my first Steam title! As a solo developer I truly appreciate everyone who has supported the game.
