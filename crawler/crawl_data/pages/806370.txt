Buy Pulse: FINAL FANTASY XIV Remix Album $18.06 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content A remix album featuring popular tracks from FINAL FANTASY XIV! This release includes 14 carefully selected tracks from A REALM REBORN, Heavensward, STORMBLOOD, and SHADOWBRINGERS, reimagined across genres such as EDM, Future Bass, House, Techno Pop, and Electro Swing. The arrangements are led by Takafumi Imamura and Daiki Ishikawa of the FFXIV Sound Team, who also worked on the SHADOWBRINGERS: FINAL FANTASY XIV Original Soundtrack, with participation from FFXIV Sound Director Masayoshi Soken.
