Simulator 3. SBGR delivers a high-intensity ATC experience with a dense mix of domestic, international, and cargo traffic . Manage continuous arrivals, coordinate frequent departures, and maintain efficient ground operations across a large and complex airport layout. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). With its parallel runway operations, heavy airline presence, and fast-paced traffic flow, SBGR offers a rewarding challenge for experienced controllers while still being accessible to those looking to step up their skills. Precision, timing, and strong planning are key to keeping operations running smoothly. Take the tower at São Paulo and handle one of South America’s most demanding and dynamic airports.
