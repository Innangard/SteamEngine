In the first DLC for Mini Airways , we’re taking you to the Outer Hebrides of Scotland to master the world's most unique airport—Barra Airport (EGPR). Here, the runways aren't built on concrete—they are ruled by the tide. [Key Features] Beach Runways: Manage takeoffs and landings on Traigh Mhor beach. There are no conventional runways, only three marked lines on the sand waiting for your guidance. "High Tide" Mechanic: Following real-world tidal patterns, the map periodically enters "High Tide Mode." When it does, all runways will be submerged, and your airport will come to a complete standstill. Space-Time Management: During high tide, you must coordinate all landing flights to circle and wait. Facing dwindling fuel and crowded airspace, you’ll need to restore traffic with precision and speed the moment the tide recedes.
