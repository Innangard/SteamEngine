Help the gnomes build their colony in a magical forest. This is a relaxing game where you can build at your own pace. Looking for a challenge? A hard mode is there for you. How does the gameplay work? You start with cards . Play a card to grow a mushroom that gives you stars . Stars are the main resource you use to get new cards. You can also merge mushrooms together to create better buildings that give you more stars. Your main goal is to build the Golden Gnome Statue! Key Features: Cozy city-building puzzle Mushroom merge and synergy mechanics Cards and simple deckbuilding Hard mode for a challenge Warm forest atmosphere What the game does not have: 4X and battles Multiplayer Fairies or leprechauns (sorry, but it's a world of gnomes!)
