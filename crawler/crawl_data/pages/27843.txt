Vaultbreakers delivers top-down action combat that rewards smart decisions over reckless aggression. Positioning, preparation, and timing matter just as much as fast reflexes. Explore and tame a dynamic world where no two runs feel the same. Not every fight should be taken. Some enemies are better avoided, at least until you’re ready. Study enemy behavior, exploit moments of vulnerability to deal massive damage , and turn the tide through careful planning. Each victory makes you stronger, but every mistake can cost you everything. Equip your Vaultbreaker with legendary weapons and armor , unlock game-changing perks, and experiment with powerful synergies to shape your own playstyle. In building your Haven, you shape your permanent boons to suit you. Craft and find many useful devices to master, and evolve into a relentless force capable of taking on the world’s deadliest foes. DEMO CONTENT Core gameplay systems, with 2 playable Vaultbreakers, upgradable haven, perks, etc. Part of the first map, Wolfswood. Onboarding quests and main quests up until your first Vaultbreak. Co-op with up to 2 friends. Legacy Points that carry over after launch, unlocking great rewards. DEMO LIMITATION The demo only features PvE Progress (except Legacy Points) will not carry over from the demo to game release. Demo progress might be reset before full game launch. Check the main store page or the calendar link in the demo to know when our next PvPvE test is! Opplysning om AI-generert innhold Utviklerne beskriver hvordan spillet bruker AI-generert innhold slik: During development, we use AI-assisted tools to support development and help our team make full use of its capabilities. Specifically, we use these tools for: Prototyping simple assets Assisting game design: Handling large-scale data entry Speeding up research into mechanics and reference games Suggesting first iterations for item names and stats Generating and reviewing code Generating a few select environment textures and various icons Cleaning up text By using these tools, our small team is able to deliver a richer experience for players. Every asset, system, and creative decision included in the final game is reviewed by our team and either adjusted or approved.
