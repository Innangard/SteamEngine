Choose from over 10 different worlds to mine in, 33 different weapons to mine with, and 20 new ores to discover. Process your ores into bars in furnaces, which can be upgraded. Use your bars to craft different materials in the crafting section. Use bars and crafted materials in an upgrades tree with 400+ unique upgrades, to upgrade your artillery and economy. Maxing out a world and destroying it completely will grant you a prestige cube which can be assigned to auto miners for idle collection and boosting ore dropped.
