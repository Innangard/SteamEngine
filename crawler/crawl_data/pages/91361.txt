Ketahui lebih lanjut Kata-kata daripada pembangun: Kenapa Akses Awal? “We believe the best way to build a compelling post-apocalyptic survival game is with the people who play it. Early Access allows us to open a direct line of communication with our players. Your feedback will be essential in helping us refine the core experience and guide the development of Nomad Drive into the game we all want to play.” Berapa lama permainan ini dijangka akan berada dalam Akses Awal? “We are targeting a full release within 6 to 12 months. This timeline may shift based on the feedback we receive and the new ideas suggested by our community, as our priority is to deliver a stable and polished experience.” Apakah perbezaan yang dirancang antara versi penuh dan versi Akses Awal? “The 1.0 version of Nomad Drive will be a more expansive and polished experience. Our roadmap includes new, distinct biomes to explore, a much wider array of RV customization parts, and deeper, more engaging survival and crafting systems. Throughout Early Access, we will be heavily focused on performance optimization, bug fixing, and adding content based on player feedback to ensure the full release is as feature-rich and stable as possible.” Bagaimanakah keadaan semasa versi Akses Awal? “The Early Access build provides a solid foundation of Nomad Drive's core gameplay loop. Players can already drive, maintain, and repair their RV, explore a procedurally generated world, gather resources, and manage their survival needs. Online co-op multiplayer is also implemented. While the core experience is in place, players should expect to encounter bugs, and system-heavy updates may require save file wipes as we improve the game.” Adakah harga permainan ini berbeza semasa dan selepas Akses Awal? “Yes, the price will be lower during Early Access to thank our early supporters. We plan to increase the price as we add significant new content and features, with a final price increase upon the full 1.0 launch.” Bagaimana anda merancang untuk melibatkan Komuniti dalam proses pembangunan anda? “Community involvement is the primary reason for this Early Access launch. We will be actively monitoring our Steam Discussion forums and Discord server for feedback, bug reports, and new ideas. Each new game offers a different starting point and experience, allowing you to collect resources and survive in abandoned locations and roads. Explore a continuously shifting world of distinct biomes. Each unique region offers its own specific climate and ecosystem. Begin your journey with a rundown RV, repairing and upgrading each part. Personalize everything from stoves and refrigerators to toilets and seating areas, creating your own living space. Stay alive with activities such as cooking, fishing, repairing, and making coffee. Use your RV's kitchen to cook the food you find, making more nutritious meals. Discover Nomad Drive, where every part is fully interactive. Touch, customize, and explore everything from furniture to appliances. Make the RV truly yours! Plant the fruit and vegetable seeds you discover in the world of Nomad Drive in pots and grow them by taking care of them. They will reward you with delicious fruits and vegetables.
