The possibilities are endless with a variety of skin tones, hairstyles, body types, and costumes to choose from. Maybe, you'll find that special someone in your life as you chat with and befriend various people from around the world! Go around various fishing spots to catch new species of fish and show them off to others! Catch over 600 species of fish in places such as a pond, the sea, and even a swimming pool. It's never a dull moment as new fish to catch are constantly added to the game. Every fishing spot has fish not found in other spots, so visit them all to complete the collections listed in the Illustrated Book and show people what you've caught! Catch insects and lizards with your friends in various locations or go excavate rare ores and fossils. Over 300 species of insects are thriving throughout the in-game world! Also, look forward to a unique and fun experience digging up dinosaur fossils and rare diamonds. Add a spoonful of happiness to your life in Play Together with one of many adorable and ever-present pets that follow you around, such as puppies, kittens, ponies, and more.
