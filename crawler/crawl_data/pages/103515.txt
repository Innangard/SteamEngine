View Community Hub About This Game About Our Game Necro Factory is an exhilarating, stress-relieving idle game focused on mass troop spawning. On this perilous continent, you will gradually build your very own skeleton army. Lead your heroic spirits and legions to conquer land after land. You may also compete against other players to become the mightiest ruler in the game! Name the server after yourself, claim supreme glory, and make other players bow before your throne! Core Gameplay Summon Heroic Spirits, Your Mightiest Guardians Enjoy an ultra-high drop rate when summoning powerful heroic spirits. Each spirit comes with unique skill mechanics, allowing you to craft exclusive lineups. Awaken spirits to unlock new forms and brand-new skills waiting for you! Build Altars, Strengthen Mercenaries Skeletons march forth. Empower them via unique altars to grant varied abilities and turn them into new mercenaries, including warriors, archers, bomb troopers, mages and more troop types. Diverse Game Modes Full of Challenges Carefully crafted stages and varied maps stretch from forests to glaciers, brimming with mysteries and dangers. Brand-new content such as Realm Creation Mountain and Illusion Trials delivers an unprecedented thrilling combat experience. Conquer the Continent for Supreme Glory Fight alongside your guildmates. Wage wars across the continent against rival players, seize territories and cities with your allies. Defend your throne and force others to submit!
