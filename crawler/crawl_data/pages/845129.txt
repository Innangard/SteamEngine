Located just north of Dublin's city center, Dublin Airport serves as the country's primary international gateway, handling over 30 million passengers annually and connecting Ireland with Europe, North America, the Middle East, and beyond. EIDW offers an exciting mix of short-haul, long-haul, cargo, and business aviation traffic. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). Manage three parallel runways, coordinate busy arrival and departure waves, and keep aircraft flowing efficiently through an extensive taxiway network. From rapid Ryanair turnarounds to transatlantic heavy departures, every session presents new operational challenges requiring precision and quick decision-making.
