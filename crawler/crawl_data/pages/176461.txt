View Community Hub About This Game Push The Box 3D is a relaxing, 3D Sokoban-inspired puzzle game featuring 30 levels filled with clever challenges and creative mechanics. Push crates to clear your path, cross water, activate switches, and reach higher platforms using lifts and other interactive elements. Rotate the camera to find the best angle and plan your next move! Take your time and think ahead! With no time limits and the ability to undo moves, you can experiment with different solutions at your own pace. Complete levels using as few moves as possible and compare your performance with other players on the global leaderboards. Explore More, Collect Relics! Discover 18 hidden Relics across 14 levels ! Revisit puzzles, explore every corner, and track your collection with the new Relic Progress overview. Can you find them all? Use lifts to reach higher platforms! Push different kinds of blocks! Activate switches and solve clever puzzles! Key Features 30 Puzzle Levels – Solve a variety of increasingly intricate 3D puzzles. 18 Hidden Relics – Discover collectibles and track your progress across levels. New Character Skins – Give your character a fresh look with new cosmetic appearances. Creative Puzzle Mechanics – Use crates, switches, lifts, and water crossings to overcome obstacles. Rotatable 3D Camera – Change your perspective to plan your moves. Undo Your Moves – Experiment with solutions without time pressure. Global Leaderboards – Challenge yourself to complete levels in fewer moves. Quality-of-Life Improvements – Enjoy updated settings, improved menu navigation, and support for 31 interface languages. Whether you're looking for a peaceful puzzle experience or trying to perfect every solution, Push The Box 3D offers plenty of opportunities to put your puzzle-solving skills to the test. Can you solve every puzzle and discover every hidden Relic?
