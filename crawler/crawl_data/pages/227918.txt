The villains are nice and campy giving me the impression the actors had fun with this giving it their all.” Treat Your Geek About This Game Ziggy's Cosmic Adventures is a VR cockpit game combining ship management with action-arcade combat. You are a bounty hunter with possibly questionable morals, currently in possession of a powerful little alien that you may or may not have stolen/liberated from the evil Empire. They are in hot pursuit, and as the Empire closes in, you'll need to find a way to balance your ship's management and deal with their onslaught of attacks. Do you have what it takes to survive? Immerse yourself in a cockpit packed full from floor to ceiling with distinct, physics-based interactive management systems. Though make sure to keep your eye on them all to improve your chances of survival! Engage in thrilling dogfights as squads of enemies pursue you to re-capture the small green treasure aboard your ship - shoot bullets, launch bombs, and raise your shields. It's going to get bumpy! Control the ship with a radical yet incredibly easy-to-use virtual flight stick design, dodge your way through many hazardous space environments, and don't forget to do a barrel roll! Who could forget your cosmic friend, Ziggy?! This mysterious creature is a perfect fuel source. You can also play with them, use them as a resource, feed them, and most importantly pet them! Throughout your journey, you will meet a wide cast of characters, both friend and foe alike. You will encounter new allies and friends in your quest to escape from the Empire that will help you to survive. But The Empress has sent her elite squad to stop you before you can realize your goal. Fight your way through 13 unique levels, each with unique mechanics and obstacles to avoid. Unlock different path through the system each time by beating challenges and unlocking harder worlds as you progress throughout the adventure.
