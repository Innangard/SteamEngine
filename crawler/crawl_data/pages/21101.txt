Many have been playing the SULFUR demo for hundreds of hours already, so we hope to add to the replayability and variaty!” Eltérően lesz árazva a játék a korai hozzáférés alatt és azt követően? „Yes, we will likely offer SULFUR at a lower price during Early Access as a thank you to the community for participating and providing feedback.
