Right, the Gacha Fighters ! In this bullet heaven survivor, pull your character from your gacha machine, and destroy your enemies with cuteness ♡ Keychains ? No ! They are weapons ! Collect and upgrade them to see what they do. Choose your weapons carefully, from the machine gun, to the magic wand and not the Buble-Canon Choose your items wisely to maximize your statistics. Collecter les Bubble Teas ! Mochis ! Fuse items to have more powerful ones ! When you're not surviving, you're decorating your sticker book with all the success you won during missions ! In this genre of vampire survivor, we want to give the player the possibility to choose their path, their environment as well as the boss to face. A simple crafting system to slightly complicate the genre. So, ready to take on Gacha Fever ? Just be careful of the area ! Survive waves of increasingly dangerous enemies with Hard Mode. More enemies and more elite enemies ! CONTROLS Nothing’s easier ! Armed with your computer keyboard and mouse, all you’d have to do is using the directional pad to move from one way to another during the stage and pick the items you like by clicking on it after each fight ! - Lots of kawaii heroes to obtain who will give you different runs ! - Fast runs (under 25 minutes) - Auto-firing weapons - Lots and lots of weapons with different effects ! - A Craft system linked to item loot ! - Between each wave of enemies you can buy weapons, loot and craft !
