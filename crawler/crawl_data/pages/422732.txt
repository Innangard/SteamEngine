It captures a child's wonder in its adventure, meeting danger with positivity and joy alongside determination.” Indie Games Weblog “It's neat playing through a game where characters were dreamed up by an eight year-old, because eight year-olds are crazy” Kotaku “Super Chibi Knight is one of the most colourful, charming and fun RPGs you’ll ever come across.” Indie Game Magazine About This Game The Kingdom of Oukoku is threatened by the spicy influence of General Tso. Citizens have placed their hope in a small knight who fits the requirements of ancient legends. Which path will the Chibi Knight choose... the path to the mysterious Island of Mahou to learn spells from its sorcerers or the path to the Beastmaster mountains to summon and ride an array of ferocious animals? Your choice will determine Oukoku's fate! Super Chibi Knight is an action adventure platformer with RPG elements. It was made by 33 year-old Dad and 8-year-old Daughter team PestoForce. The cute and colorful art and animation brings to life a rich world with a deep history and many secrets. Your in-game choices will determine your specialty, Sorcerer or Beastmaster, each affecting the course of the game. Do you have what it takes to reach the dastardly General Tso and stop him from taking over the world?
