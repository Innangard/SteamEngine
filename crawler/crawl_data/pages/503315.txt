So is everything else. The mission is straightforward: head out across the ice, visit a series of decommissioned research sites, and recover old survey data on the region's habitability. The previous team already did the hard work. You're just picking up what they left. Except the instruments show things that don't add up. The stars aren't quite right. And there is no one left to ask why. Whatever happened here, the snow hasn't buried all of it. ARPC reminds you not to leave the mission area. The Machine ARPC has issued you a retrofitted Antarctic off-road vehicle. Outfitted with a mechanical arm and vital instrumentation, it's the only thing standing between you and the cold. Balance fuel , heat , and power usage to keep both you and your vehicle operational. Features: A vast open Antarctic snowscape with stations to explore Vehicle-driven survival where rationing fuel and managing temperature is essential.
