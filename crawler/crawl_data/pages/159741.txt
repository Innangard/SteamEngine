This pack contains two skins: one Blue Spotted Frog skin for the Frog and one Anteater skin for the Elephant Calf. The animals you can play in Meadow aren’t poisonous or venomous (or otherwise dangerous for that matter) but in the natural world of frogs, poisonous is exactly what the most beautiful specimens oftentimes are. Did you know that the blue poison dart frog gets their poisonous skin from their diet? Particularly through the ants they love to chow down on. Speaking of eating ants, anteaters cometh! Although, despite the name, they do eat more than ants. They love termites, and have been known to eat fruit, bird eggs, worms, bees… the list goes on.
