Run Your Farm Grow, harvest, and process a variety of crops Manage your warehouse, seed market, and production facilities Fulfill orders from the war zone and assign characters to keep the farm running The Night Is Not Safe When night falls, monsters may attack the farm. Changing weather, events, and war zones ensure that no two days on the farm are exactly alike. Move Forward with Your Companions Rock, Mia, Lin Lan, and more characters will gradually join your farm. Learn their stories through dialogue, cooperation, and missions, and put their unique talents to work around the farm. A Farm That Keeps Changing The main story, random events, orders, and facility upgrades will continue to drive the farm's growth.
