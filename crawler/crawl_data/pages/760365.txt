Break into human homes, steal everything you can fit in your belly, and get back to the boot before the Gobblin King loses patience. The more loot you eat, the less stamina you have to climb and jump. Fill your belly to the limit, and getting out becomes the hard part. FIND A WAY IN A locked front door is a problem for human burglars. Cling to walls, stretch your gobblin arms, scramble up pipes, pull your friends onto rooftops, and hunt for another way inside. Every home has loot worth taking and usually more than one dangerous way to reach it. GRAB LOOT. AVOID DEATH. REPEAT. The richer the target, the more dangerous the heist. Dodge fireballs, angry humans, dangerous falls, and cats with very strong opinions about gobblins. When everything goes wrong, save your friends, grab the boot, and try not to leave with fewer limbs than you arrived with. MAKE THE KING HAPPY In Classic mode, your crew must bring enough loot back to meet the Gobblin King’s quota. Random events keep each run unpredictable, so the same neighborhood can turn into a very different heist. Keep the King happy, earn access to richer targets, and see how far your gobblin career can go. GET PAID IN GOBBLOONS Deliver enough loot and the Gobblin King rewards your crew with Gobbloons. Spend them at the slot machine for random cosmetics and power-ups. You might get a useful boost. You might get a hat. Either way, you are richer than you were in the Trailer Park. DRESS LIKE A PROFESSIONAL CRIMINAL Mix hats and outfits to build the perfect gobblin look. Sneaky, fashionable, or deeply embarrassing. TRUST NO GOBBLIN Not every gobblin is trying to help. In the social-deduction mode, players can have secret roles and hidden win conditions. Work together to complete the heist. Keep an eye on the gobblin helping a little too enthusiastically, the one hoarding all the loot, or the one who keeps suggesting somebody should go to jail. FEATURES Online co-op for up to 8 players Physics-driven climbing, grabbing, stretching, and stealing Stash loot in your belly. The more you take, the less stamina you have.
