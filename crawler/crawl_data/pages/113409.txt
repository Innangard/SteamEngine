Victory isn’t about depleting your opponent’s health, but making them fall from their pillar. Outmaneuver their attacks and knock them off with your projectiles to be the last one standing. There is no blocking in Joltt. Read your opponent’s mind, dodge, evade and counter everything to be victorious! Online Ranked & Casual Match Local VS Challenge Mode (do whatever you can to survive a huge wave of incoming projectiles) Story Mode Training Mode Tutorial Mode Learn and master 6 different wizards, each having a vastly different gameplay! Ell uses powerful feints and casts multiple projectiles. Okhpum has to summon spirits to gain access to his most powerful moves. Xioko uses the power of the wind and can fly freely. Myra has access to different modes by igniting her sword with colored flames. Braboz summons unpredictable portals and can teleport around the battlefield. Alsnad has great control over the speed of his projectiles. Be the first one to fill up your super bar and unleash your devastating super move. No complicated inputs.  All powers are cast with a simple button press, sometimes associated with a direction. You’ll go straight into the mind games without passing hours in the training room.
