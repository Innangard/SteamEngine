View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to the Middle of “Nowhere” You’ve arrived where the maps end and the wild, untamed frontier begins. Your mission is to build a thriving town from the ground up — a place that will attract settlers, traders, and fortune‑seekers. This land may look worthless… but give it time. Beneath the surface lie riches that could change your life forever. Dig, Mine, Prosper Explore deeper layers of the earth and gather the resources that will become the foundation of your growing empire: coal, iron, copper, gold, and diamonds crude oil hidden in deep underground deposits metal smelting furnaces and oil extraction machines Every resource brings you closer to a bigger town and greater opportunities. Build the Town of Your Dreams You start with a few planks and a tent, but soon you’ll be able to: construct houses and attract new residents expand your infrastructure decorate and customize your own living space however you like Your settlement can become a true oasis in the middle of the wasteland. Upgrade Your Gear and Optimize Your Work Progress requires investment. Buy better tools and explosives from traveling merchants. Improve your logistics: crates and wheelbarrows for transporting materials horses and wagons for hauling large loads Hire workers so your town keeps growing even when you’re busy elsewhere. You’re Not Alone Out Here The Wild West isn’t just gold and oil — it’s danger too: attacks from wild animals bandits roaming the area pistols and shotguns available for purchase to defend yourself and your townsfolk Your settlement must be safe if it’s going to survive. A Moment of Rest After a long day of hard work, you can: visit the saloon play darts shoot to the bottle Even a sheriff needs a break. From Dusty Outpost to Bustling Metropolis Will you manage to transform this forgotten “Nowhere” into a thriving metropolis and rise from a small‑town sheriff to a wealthy governor?
