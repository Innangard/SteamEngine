Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game WELCOME TO THE ARCHIVE, DETECTIVE The year is 1995. Behind the heavy reinforced doors of the central precinct lies decades of unsolved crimes, forgotten evidence, and chaotic stacks of confidential files. As the precinct's new archival investigator, your duty is to restore absolute order to its records—and uncover the secrets hidden between the lines. KEY FEATURES Process 3,360 Unique Criminal Dossiers: Inspect detailed police files, identify each crime category, assess its severity, determine its public notoriety level, and place every case in its designated location. Experience a 1990s Police Archive: Explore a fully interactive 3D precinct filled with CRT terminals, fluorescent-lit archive aisles, metal filing cabinets, and satisfying retro soundscapes. Crack Safes & Discover Hidden Compartments: Decode combination locks, find forgotten keys, and unlock classified evidence compartments tucked deep within the archive. Uncover Crime Syndicates & Group Crimes: Piece together cases involving organized groups, match gang members to their ringleaders using visual markers and color-coded clues, and discover hidden details in special dossiers. Play at Your Own Pace: Enjoy a satisfying organizing experience without stressful time limits, combat, or failure states. BRING ORDER TO THE CHAOS Grab your keys, power on the archive terminal, and begin sorting through the precinct's forgotten cases. Every file has a place. Some files have a story.
