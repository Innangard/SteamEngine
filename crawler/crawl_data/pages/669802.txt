View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game ACRODRIFT is an FPV drone simulator with flight physics modeled on real flight controllers. Whether you're learning your first flips before risking a real quad or you're a seasoned pilot keeping your thumbs sharp, this is a place to fly hard, crash - free, and get better every session. Fly your way Six handcrafted maps, from wide open spaces to tight technical courses, and three ways to fly them. Cruise and experiment in Free Flight, chase the clock through checkpoint gates in Race mode, or hunt down targets in Collection. Over 20 challenges of varying difficulty keep your skills moving forward, and 5 flight modes take you from beginner-friendly Angle and PosHold all the way to full Acro and 3D. Build and tune your drone The workshop works like the real thing. Pick from 11 frames from 1.6 to 5 inches, 13 motors, props and batteries, and watch every component change how your drone flies. Tune your PID controller, set up custom rates profiles for racing, freestyle or cinematic lines, or grab a preconfigured drone and just fly. Fly with your own gear Plug in a gamepad and take off, or connect a real FPV radio controller for the authentic stick feel. ACRODRIFT supports a wide range of radio transmitters with USB connectivity, and every axis is fully remappable, with automatic stick detection to make setup painless. Key Features Realistic FPV flight physics based on real flight controllers, with full PID tuning 6 handcrafted maps and 20+ challenges 3 game modes: Free Flight, Race, and Collection 5 flight modes, from Angle and PosHold to Acro and 3D Drone workshop with 11 frames, 13 motors, and full component customization Custom rates profiles for racing, freestyle, and smooth cinematic flying Gamepad and radio controller support with full axis remapping System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 64-bit Processor: Intel® Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 1500X Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: Geforce RTX 2060 / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT (4GB VRAM or more) DirectX: Version 12 Storage: 20 GB available space Sound Card: DirectX compatible Additional Notes: For the best flight experience, we recommend using an RC transmitter.
