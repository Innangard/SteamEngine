Simulator 3 - EHEH Airport $12.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content EHEH - Eindhoven Airport Eindhoven Airport (IATA: EIN, ICAO: EHEH) is a smart and efficient gateway to Europe, located approximately 7.6 km west of Eindhoven city centre in the Netherlands. As the second-busiest airport in the country, Eindhoven Airport serves over 6 million passengers each year and connects travelers to more than 80 European destinations. Operations are centered around a single 3,000-meter asphalt runway (03/21), which is well suited for medium-sized passenger and cargo aircraft, making the airport a dependable regional hub for both leisure and business travel. The airport is best known for its strong low-cost and hybrid carrier presence, including airlines such as Ryanair and Wizz Air. Its compact, purpose-built terminal prioritizes efficiency, offering fast check-ins, short walking distances, and smooth passenger flow from curb to gate. Passengers benefit from a well-balanced range of shops, dining options, lounges, and essential services, complemented by convenient parking and excellent road and public transport connections to Eindhoven and the surrounding region. Originally established as a military airbase, Eindhoven Airport still shares its facilities with the Royal Netherlands Air Force, giving it a distinctive dual-use character and a strong operational heritage. Whether departing for a weekend getaway, a business trip, or a longer holiday, Eindhoven Airport delivers a modern, streamlined travel experience—without the congestion and complexity of a major international hub.
