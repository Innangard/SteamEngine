Simulator 3. Located just outside Vienna, LOWW handles a diverse mix of European short-haul operations, long-haul international services, cargo traffic, and regional connections , creating a dynamic and engaging air traffic control experience throughout the day. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). Manage coordinated departures and arrivals across a busy airport environment, maintain smooth ground operations, and adapt to changing traffic patterns as aircraft move between gates, taxiways, and runways. Whether you enjoy efficient sequencing, complex ground movement, or handling busy departure waves, Vienna delivers a rewarding experience for controllers of all skill levels. Step into the tower and keep one of Europe’s key transport gateways moving safely and efficiently.
