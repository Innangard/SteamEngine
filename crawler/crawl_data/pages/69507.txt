Your supermarket empire, Wizamart, has been waiting all this time for the perfect flagship store location, and this castle is second to none! Thus begins your short journey to claim the castle as your own (or for your boss)... even if danger lurks beneath the generous offer. Experience a Power Trip in a fully 3D action game! Grind power with various dropped weapons that you can dual-wield, and collect power-ups to increase your stats permanently! Jump, dodge and stomp across multiple areas of the celestial skies, filled with floating islands, treasure chests, and monsters alike... and with good play, tear through the strongest foes with your newly acquired upgrades! Featuring over 60+ weapons , derived from 8 different weapon types (Swords, Bows, Staves, Spears, Miniguns, Boots, Shields and Pistols), grab and experiment with simple to use but varied weapons, be it a spear that leaves after-cuts, a pair of boots that make you zip wildly, or a sword that creates a super deformed doppelganger! Fast, Cute and Bouncy, with arcade-like inspirations! Play as an adorably gifted storeclerk angel, or an equally gifted store owner demon through 3 different courses with fast and loose action! Each course is split into two acts: grinding in the skies before the end of a time limit, and then challenging the course's dungeon with unlimited time. Play at your best in the grinding phase to extend the timer and become even stronger: then, utilize all your acquired strength and loot to overwhelm the course's dungeon and boss! It's no rogue-like! It's inspired by arcade games! Well... with some random elements, such as room selection, loot drops, and special room events! You can play through each course individually in Quickplay , or, if you're in for a challenge, go through all three courses in a single, continuous run in Quest , where foes become increasingly strong with each room! But the more daring you are, the more points you'll get! Features: Arcade-like Inspiration: It's not a rogue-like! You can complete the game on your first run, in either a single course mode (Quickplay), or a continuous, three courses in a row mode (Quest), with no mandatory unlocks from previous runs required to win! Cute and Bouncy Characters : Play as a cute angel storeclerk, Faune, or an equally cute demon CEO of a massive shopping corporation, Hime-Alice, and dash, stomp, smash, and push foes away into the void! Dual-Wield 60+ Weapon Variations: Collect and dual-wield from 8 broad weapon types, and utilize their diverse, and sometimes un-optimal effects: shoot beams from a sword, charge a laser beam from a minigun, summon chicken from a staff, and more! Some Randomized Elements: The room selection, weapon drops, special room events, and more, keep each playthrough varied! Unlock Secrets with Achievements: Complete achievements to unlock bonuses such as secret characters, outfits, mini-games , and more! (You can also unlock the rewards without having to complete the task, using currency earned throughout the runs, but you'll still need to accomplish the task for the online achievement.) Online & Local Leaderboards: Challenge yourself and the world with leaderboards for each course and Quest mode!
