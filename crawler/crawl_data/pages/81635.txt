Engeor, a first-year student at the magical university, becomes one of many victims of the Moharn dictator Wahargem, who is considered a living legend. More than ten years ago, he stood on the highest floor of the Skeldal Tower and, along with his comrades, won the victorious battle at the gate to the forbidden dimension of Zohar. But even a legend does not have the right to destroy someone's life, so Engeor decides to confront not only him but also his own fate. Genre Combination The point-and-click adventure, a genre so popular in the nineties, has been supplemented with RPG character development, where the main hero learns spells that are used not only in hexagonal turn-based battles but also for solving adventure tasks.
