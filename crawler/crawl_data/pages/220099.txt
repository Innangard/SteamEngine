Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Content Reflections of the Chronicler is one part artbook, one part story! Taking place shortly after the events of Upwards, Rain! The Post Office of Farewells , a courier by the name of Sulien makes land in the village of Vitae to chronicle the events surrounding Rain and her adventures through Mortem Post. Read journal entries and solve cipher-like puzzles in the artbook, then follow her adventure via new in-game scenes with point-and-click minigames to learn more about the world of Upwards, Rain! Reflections of the Chronicler consists of an artbook and extra in-game scenario! The artbook includes: Developer commentary and notes! Interviews from the Upwards, Rain! voice actors! Exhaustive concept art! Multiple puzzles to help decipher things like the in-universe fantasy language! Several in-character journal entries from Sulien that ties into the narrative of the Reflections of the Chronicler scenario! The in-game scenario includes: A tale following two brand-new characters: the mysterious chronicler Sulien and her unusual acquaintance Techy! Several new scenes that tie directly into the artbook's journals! New point-and-click segments where you gather lore and worldbuilding notes! New music tracks from the original game's composer, Sarah Mancuso! System Requirements * Starting February 15, 2024, the Steam Client will no longer support 32-bit games or macOS 10.14 or lower.
