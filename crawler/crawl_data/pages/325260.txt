Custom Corner is developed by an indie couple dev team. Our goal is to help you create that peaceful, personal space to accompany you in the background while you work, study, or just unwind. Sit back and enjoy this cozy corner! Recreate your own isometric room from scratch with over 325 unique pixel items. From wallpapers and floorings to plants and PC cases, arrange every detail to your liking. Also, thanks to our in-game clock system, the lighting adapts to the time of day; watch your room settle into a soft, cozy glow while you work in the evenings. Design freely and build the corner of your dreams. Not just a game, but your personal assistant on your desk. Manage your time with the Focus Timer, organize your To-Do list, and take important notes. Keep your desk colorful and organized at all times by customizing your widgets with different color themes. Sit back and dive into the atmosphere. Maximize your focus with carefully selected Lo-Fi music and background sounds. Express your own style by decorating your music player with adorable stickers and charms. We will continue to grow our game together with you! We invite you to join our Discord community to directly contribute to the game's development and share your ideas with us.
