What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We want to allow players to help develop the game and give them the opportunity to influence its direction. Therefore, we present our vision for the gameplay and we are looking for feedback and new ideas from the players.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “As long as it takes to make the game as enjoyable and bug-free as possible. We are looking to leave Early Access in Q4 2022.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The current version is our vision for the game, which we will develop together with the players until we have a complete version that appeals to most people. We would like to add, among many other features, electronics repair service and the ability to buy used, broken devices and repair it.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game is fully playable and provides hours of gameplay. All mechanics that are available in the game work.
