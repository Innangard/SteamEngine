About This Game Lyca is a small, relaxing, incremental game about a magical wolf restoring a barren wasteland into a lush meadow. Run freely through the grass, gather resources, and explore a vast upgrade tree. Unlock incredibly powerful upgrades, helpful abilities and valuable resources. Bring life and rain back into a desolate wasteland. Enjoy: Incremental progression through resource gathering A vast upgrade tree to explore Unlockable "idle" and "endless" modes Approximately 3-4 hours of gameplay to complete the campaign Simple and relaxing experience Accessible controls: Keyboard, Mouse-Only, and Controller support Lofi royalty-free beats to chill to by Purrple Cat System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 7 or higher Processor: 2 GHz Memory: 1 GB RAM Storage: 100 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
