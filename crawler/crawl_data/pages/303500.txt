Learn more Leaving Early Access: Dec 2, 2026 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Our goal is to release a game that is funny, silly and makes you want to play just one more run! To reach that goal we think that involving players in an early stage of making the game will be great.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We are hoping for a full release sometime in 2026. Initially we were aiming for full release in July 2024, 6 months after EA release, but we needed more time.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We hope and plan for the full version of our game to have more of everything, more frogs, more bugs, more upgrades, more environments, more achievements, more tongues, more hats, more friends! We are planning to add a branching system for choosing upgrades when you level up, power ups, dynamic music and more. We are also working on a new system for map generation.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “In this version of the game you get to decide what frog to play as, what tongues you have, what upgrades you get and what story to tell. Find your favourite combinations of tongues, items and chapters to eat all the flies! Use your hard earned froins in the shop to become even more powerful. And then, go again!” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We might increase the price a bit if we feel like the full release of our game is worth that.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We will gladly read all your thoughts about the game, what you love and what you don't like about the game at its current state. We want to hear all your silliest ideas and we think that together we can make this game great!
