Play the front yard and Elvira's entire backyard, solo or online with up to 5 friends (the map editor and the Steam Workshop come with the full game). Your save carries over to the full game on November 12. It's Saturday, 5:42 PM. The mower won't start, the beer is warm, and nobody has cut this grass in weeks. An old man, an old mower You're not here to relax. You're here to get it done, your way. Hold the key until the engine finally coughs to life, light a cigarette, crack a can and reach over to switch on the radio strapped to the hood. One hand on the wheel, the other one busy. The most satisfying cut Tall, thick, wind-blown grass that falls blade by blade under the deck. A sharp edge on every pass, clippings flying out of the chute, and stripes that follow the way you drive. When you're done, watch the whole job again as a timelapse. Get off and finish the job The mower can't reach everything. Climb down, grab the string trimmer and clean up along the fence, and around that one garden gnome. Out of gas? Walk to the gas can. Nobody's bringing it to you. One more lawn Start with your own front yard. Then the neighbors start calling. Elvira pays well, and she's picky: every blade cut, five centimeters, not one more, and nothing broken. Especially not her gnome. When you're done, she writes you a receipt. With comments. Then, the golf course Rubén wants the old Los Teros club back in shape: greens, fairways and tees, each at its own height. Between passes, play a couple of holes, fish the lost balls out of the pond, rake the bunkers, and pull the handbrake on the wet parking lot to see if a riding mower can drift. It can. And then, an island Marcela's niece is getting married on Saturday. 120 barefoot guests, no second takes. Load the mower onto an old ferry, cross the lake and mow the whole island. Drive into the water and the engine drowns (you can push it out). Also: a boat, a fishing rod, a hammock, coconuts and a beach bar. Bring friends. Or don't. Online co-op for up to 6 players, each on their own mower, all on the same lawn. Join with a Steam invite or a 6-digit code. Proximity voice chat, everyone's radio playing from their own hood, and plenty of ways to get nothing done: wave, point, dance, throw fruit at each other's heads, or punch a friend in the face. No damage. Just dizziness. Take a break. Or don't. Fire up the barbecue in Elvira's yard and keep an eye on the smoke while you mow. Toss your empties into the bin from the mower seat. Juggle the fruit from the table. Have a few too many and watch the stripes get creative. Make your own lawns A built-in map editor: paint grass, sand, dirt and paths, dig ponds, raise hills, plant trees, put up fences and place as many gnomes as you dare. Hit Test Drive and mow it. Share your maps on the Steam Workshop and play the ones other people make. Features A first-person riding mower with a steering wheel you can see turn and a mechanical dashboard: hold-to-start key, blade switch, deck-height lever, lights, fuel gauge and a radio. Dense, living grass that reacts to the wind, the wheels and the blades. Real stripes, clean edges, flying clippings. Four jobs: your front yard, Elvira's backyard, Rubén's golf course and Marcela's island. Each client judges your work, and each one has opinions. Get off the mower: string trimmer, gas can, rake, golf clubs, fishing rod, boat. You can even swim. Online co-op for up to 6 players with proximity voice chat. Gestures and dances, fruit throwing and punches between friends. Map editor with Steam Workshop support. Bring your own music: load audio files from your PC into the tractor's radio. Optionally share the sound live with friends in your session (only music you have the rights to share). Files are never uploaded or sent. Streamer Mode turns it off. Steering wheel and pedals with force feedback, gamepad, or mouse and keyboard. 22 languages. He smokes, drinks, coughs and complains. He doesn't care. He's still the best at this. Streamers: the built-in radio music is free to use in streams and videos, including monetized ones. Music loaded by players (yours or your friends') is not: turn on Streamer Mode to block it. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Some sound effects were generated with AI tools (ElevenLabs) and the in-game radio songs were generated with AI (Suno), under commercial licenses, then selected, edited and mixed by the developer. All visuals (3D models, textures, grass, logo, store art), gameplay and writing are made by the developer. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Comedy game. The player character, a grumpy old man, smokes cigarettes and drinks beer on screen, including while driving the riding mower (drinking blurs the screen and makes steering harder). Slapstick, bloodless violence: you can kick and break a ceramic garden gnome, and in online co-op players can punch or throw fruit at each other (it only makes them dizzy). The in-game radio plays comedy songs with strong language (including the f-word) and crude, toilet and sexual humor; the radio starts off and can be turned off at any time. Online co-op includes unmoderated proximity voice chat and optional "Shared Music": players can let others in their private session hear music they loaded from their own computer (off by default; the host and each listener must accept). Voice and shared music come from other players and are not reviewed by the developer; any player can be muted or blocked, and Streamer Mode turns shared music off.
