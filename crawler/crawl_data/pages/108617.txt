Multi-instrumentalist Eduardo Antonello has rerecorded every note from Four Last Things , The Procession to Calvary , and Death of the Reprobate , breathing life into authentic medieval and classical instruments for his sweeping compositions. These masterpieces from across centuries of western musical history form an auditory tapestry of unparalleled elegance, sublime in its curation, the utmost pinnacle of the West's climb to aesthetic heavenly perfection... ...Which makes it the perfect soundtrack for slapping bishops, stealing wheelchairs, and drawing your sword whenever the point-and-click puzzle is too complicated. Conspiracies with inept magicians, the convoluted bureaucracy of sinning, and every unspeakable act the game demands of you will sound like they belong in the Louvre thanks to Eduardo's diligence and refined taste. Includes the expanded, unified musical scores of Four Last Things , The Procession to Calvary , and Death of the Reprobate . Please note that these are original recordings of many classic compositions, along with music inspired by the game.
