It replaced Tempelhof, Tegel and the old Schönefeld airports, becoming the single commercial airport for a region of around six million people and ranking as Germany’s third busiest. The airport opened in October 2020 after years of delays caused by severe construction and management issues. Its initial capacity was 46 million passengers per year across Terminals 1, 2 and 5, with planned expansion aiming for about 58 million by 2035. It mainly handles European routes with some intercontinental services.
