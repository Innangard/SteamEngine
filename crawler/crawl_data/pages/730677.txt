This all-new story sees our buddy duo plunged into a brand new secret tome world in a race against the evil Capital B to reach the legendary "Kracklestone," an ancient artifact fabled to grant its holder invincibility. To reach the treasure, they’ll run, swim, trip, tumble, and fly through nine distinct, character-filled landscapes.
