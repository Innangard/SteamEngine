A history-rich civilization floating in the sky is in danger of crashing down to earth, and it is up to the Dungeon Gals to save the day! Slice, smash, and blast your way through dangerous dungeons and rustic ruins forgotten by time. -ABOUT THIS GAME- Fight monsters and save the day! The Sky Isles have been infested with monsters who act like they own the place. If they aren't stopped, their evil powers will cause untold destruction! Venture deep into the frigid Sky Monastery, the mysterious Great Library, and the boiling Shrine of Souls, and free these holy sites from the evil which grips them tight. Use your abilities to solve brain-busting puzzles and navigate deadly traps! In Dungeon Gals, you play as three traveling mercenaries who share a special bond; Wendy the satyress, Izzy the cow, and Rosa the moth. Each girl has their own respective abilities, all of which must be mastered to save the citizens and history of the Sky Isles from certain doom Unravel ancient mysteries and seek the truth! The Sky Isles have long, forgotten histories intertwined by fate. Piece by piece, you will discover ancient secrets, forbidden truths, and that perhaps not everything is as it seems... And last, but certainly not least... Get to know our three intrepid heroes! Wendy, Izzy, and Rosa are three unique girls who were brought together by fate many years ago, and have since formed inseparable bonds. Each girl has their own stories to tell, problems to overcome, and reasons to love that you can learn over the course of this journey! Venture forth and save the day!
