Along the way, you’ll encounter the mischievous rival crew known as the Fung Gang , who claim they’re after the same goal... sort of. A sense of adventure! Want to put your platforming to the test? A side quest unlocks 40 optional parkour gauntlets hidden behind special portals scattered around the world! They’re tough, rewarding, and totally skippable, perfect for players who love a good challenge.
