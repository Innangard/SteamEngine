Gals are tough on cults,.., Imperial year 248. The empire is in the midst of deteriorating security, hopeless diplomatic relations, and endless war. The oppressed people of the devastated society believe in the apocalypse, and soon a huge religious organization rises up. People were kidnapped by the organization and disappeared one by one. The empire sent its own troops to the organization, but they were all destroyed. The imperial army, unable to devote its strength to the country due to the worsening war situation with neighboring countries, They transferred one gal to a religious organization, Galwoya. ・・・・ CULT VS GAL is an ultra old-school, yet high-tempo, user-friendly FPS. Take control of the "Hell Smasher Saki" and destroy the cult that threatens the empire! This is a 4-episode, 16+ map, old school FPS. The simplified maps do not interfere with your fighting instincts and allow you to just focus on fighting the cult. Right click to manufacture raw heads! When you cut off a head, Saki's tension rises and she becomes invincible for a moment. Close combat is dangerous, but daring to charge in and hunt for the head is one of the strategies. You can unleash a melee attack that can pop a cult's head off in an instant. Mince the cult with your shotgun, tactically attack with your assault rifle, and slash the cult's head off with your machete!
