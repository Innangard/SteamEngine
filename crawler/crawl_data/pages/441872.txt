This indie-developed builder game allows you to immerse yourself in farm management and oversee your crops, workers, equipment, animals and production through the changing weather of the seasons. Grow, refine and sell your goods to expand your farm and turn it into a thriving business. Enjoy playing the game through the sandbox, the scenarios or through the campaign. Play at your own pace and enjoy the farming experience and the beautiful scenery. You decide your goals, whether you wish to build a large successful estate or simply unwind and escape from everyday life. Acquire and place buildings, hire and train workers, maintain and oversee your crops, animals and equipment through the seasons. Harvest, refine, produce and sell your goods. Set up your logistics and production chains with efficiency, buy and sell at the market, and refine your farm's produce into commercial goods to turn a bigger profit. With 48 different crops, fruits and vegetables, 15 various animals, 35 machines and over 60 different placeable buildings such as production, warehouses, specialist buildings and decorations, you will have your hands full. Reap what you sow and make more hay. Sustain and cultivate your farm and watch it grow by increasing the variety of crops and expand your industries with new production lines. Process and refine your products while balancing your economy. Buy more land and use materials from abandoned buildings to expand. Transform your farm from two fields and a pen of chickens into a thriving estate.
