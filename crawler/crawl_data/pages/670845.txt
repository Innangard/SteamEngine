You are in the process of weakening your ego-structure The dissolution of the ego-structure may be accompanied by unpleasant experiences, the powers of the heavens will be shaken, the heavens will be set on fire and destroyed, the stars will crumble in fire, and the earth and its works will be found. This process will be painful, but will bring the blissful fruit of release Help Alice cross this path, destroy the ego-structure, get out of the abyss, find the key. Alice Mesmerizing Episodes of Neurosis (AMEN) is a hidden object game. The player's task is to find things hidden on the map. The story is based on the motifs of Alice in Wonderland, but it presents the struggle with neuroticism in a graphic and symbolic way. In the game we will meet characters known from the works of Lewis Carroll, such as the Red Queen, the Mad Hatter, the White Rabbit, the Caterpillar, Chester the cat and the Jabberwocky. Alice finds herself beyond the event horizon, from which there seems to be no return. But is there nothing that can be done? Can we help her get out of the abyss? If there is even a spark of hope, it is worth trying Characteristic hand-drawn graphics, with a strong symbolic style, complement the overall impression and build the mood of the game world.
