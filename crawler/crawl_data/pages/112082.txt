This is where you make a surprising realization: You are... Death? Why you of all people? And what even happened in the first place? A TRUSTY COMPANION A talking sword? A wise mentor? Think again! Only a Pig stands by your side! 🐷 You set off together - but soon a horrible conundrum arises: No one is able to die anymore?! How long will a world without death be able to last? DUNGEONS To find answers, you make your way through perilous dungeons. But not every problem can be solved with a scythe! Only those who pay close attention will find a path to the deepest parts of the dungeon and closer to evil's root. BOSSES Mysterious creatures lurk in the depths, scheming to obstruct your path. Do they keep guard over the lost souls? Or are they indeed the reason that nobody may ever die again? FEATURES Don the Grim Reaper's cape and find out what it truly means to be Death. You can pet the Pig ... and play as it! Explore a beautiful world filled with secrets and discover its truly unique landscapes! Delve into five challenging dungeons and face their riddles, traps and secrets! From tiny creatures to giant bosses - every battle will challenge you in new ways! Every character you meet will develop and change throughout the story. Experience a gripping tale about death and the many ways we choose to deal with it.
