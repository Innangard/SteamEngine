View Community Hub About This Game Who knew saving the world could sound this good? Join Cello and her friends as they explore their ruined world, fight murderous puppets, and uncover the mysteries of the powerful force known as music. What shattered this world? What is music? What is Alpha Nomos? The answers are waiting — if Cello can fight her way to them. Time your attacks to the beat and vanquish your foes! Rhythm meets action: you're rewarded for striking on the beat and crafting combos to the music — and for mixing it up. Don't just hit the downbeats; weave quick eighth notes and rests between strikes to unleash punishing combos. Gather musical powerups that change not just how you fight, but how you sound! Combine them differently each run to grow deadlier — and to cook up funky fresh tunes along the way. Every upgrade is inspired by a real-world musical effect, making each run a unique sonic experience! Explore a devastated world that reacts to the music you make! Breathe life back into your surroundings as you journey through new environments, uncover mysteries, and beat back the forces that broke your world - and took your friends!
