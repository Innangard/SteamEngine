Featuring dynamic gameplay and energetic music, it puts both your logic and reflexes to the test. Are you ready for the challenge? Fast, challenging, and addictive gameplay. Careful planning is essential alongside quick, reflexive thinking. Progress through 100 handcrafted levels that each introduce fresh and original puzzles. New layouts, obstacles, and traps with unique mechanics ensure that no two challenges feel the same. Battle against a ruthless hivemind in solo mode. Or invite friends and family to PvP splitscreen challenges for up four players to try lay claim to the title of the ultimate hacker Craving more of a challenge? Try hacking the system in Arcade Mode, where the pace gets faster, and lives are limited. The challenge is brutal, but the most skilled players can earn a spot in the global Top 20 hacker ranking leaderboards. If you’re missing the classic retro era, Cyber Protocol has got you covered.
