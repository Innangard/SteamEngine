She wants nothing more than to help the town’s inhabitants save their most beloved possessions, but when it’s their personal lives that are starting to fracture, she’ll need to find a way to hold them together, one spare screw at a time. Figure out the inner workings of the objects brought to Maria and find a way to fix them. Meet the townsfolk of Bellariva. Come to love their quirks and help them reconnect.
