In a constant battle of "Man vs. Machine", react quickly to time-critical system failures and malfunctions to give yourself a chance to complete your journey to Venus and the base of Omphalos-1. DASHBOARD MULTITASKING Managing the ship's interfaces means you don't get the luxury of dealing with one problem at a time—something always blows up in your face. Learn to juggle competing tasks and system repairs while malfunctions and meteors interrupt your plans. TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE Every task takes time, and another one always awaits. All compete for your attention as systems continue to deteriorate. You won't find time to rest when every repair is a chance to keep breathing for just a few more seconds. ALONE TO VENUS Venus has been your destination this entire time, so you keep drifting toward your one hope in your metal coffin. Through a cold computer screen, the Captain's voice occasionally breaks through, guiding you as you push onward. Not quite company, but better than complete silence. How close can you get before the pressure and isolation break you? In Signal Lost, you will… Immerse yourself in the role of a doomed astronaut. Carry the sole responsibility of your survival through the vastness of space. Repair systems and complete tasks in a constant battle against time. Experience the tenacity of the human spirit in an inhospitable universe. Discover the cost of fighting for your hope. A short experience intended to be finished in around an hour. So, will you manage to reach the relative safety of Venus and Omphalos-1… or will you die trying?
