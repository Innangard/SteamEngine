Hire unique players, manage your benchwarmers, discover synergies between them, choose new abilities, bribe the referees, visit the Blademaster for upgrades and a lot more. CRAFT YOUR TEAM Take control of Angus McShaggy and lead your squad across a world filled with rival teams, strange characters and increasingly ridiculous challenges. Hit the Training Center to boost your attributes, visit the No. 1 Fan to unlock a new player talent, or stop by the Infirmary to sacrifice a player... for the good of the team. But this isn't a regular hockey season. Lose and your run is over. Start again, take a different route, build a different team using your new unlocks and discover new combinations along the way. FACE TEAMS THAT DON'T PLAY BY THE RULES Your opponents aren't interested in playing ordinary hockey. Take on cheating referees, farting princesses and the most sinister opponents of all: the Golfers. Face teams with their own players, abilities, strategies and occasionally absurd interpretations of the rules. Learn how they play, adapt your strategy and figure out how to counter their strengths before they end your run. MASTER EVERY SQUAD Each Squad introduces its own twist on the game and can completely change how you approach a run. Build your roster by hiring Free Agents with the GM Squad, embrace the physical side of hockey with the Violence Squad, go back to old-school hockey physics with the Retro Squad, or forget the wrist shot entirely and blast away with the Slapshot Squad. DEEP AND REWARDING GAMEPLAY Tape to Tape combines fast-paced arcade hockey with responsive controls, big hits, one-timers, saucer passes, desperate saves and plenty of opportunities to send your opponents flying. Easy to pick up but with plenty to master, the game rewards good positioning, passing, timing and learning how to take advantage of your team's strengths. KEY FEATURES A hockey roguelite campaign where every run brings a new path, opponents, events and opportunities to build your team All the replayability and progression of a roguelite, built around fast-paced arcade hockey Face a colorful cast of unique rival teams, each with their own players, abilities and ridiculous ways of playing hockey Experience the entire campaign solo or in local couch co-op Fast, responsive arcade hockey featuring big hits, one-timers, saucer passes, dekes, rebounds and desperate saves Keep coming back with persistent unlocks, including new players, abilities, Relics and Squads Challenge your friends with up to 10 local players in Play Now, Tournament, Shootout and other game modes LOCAL/STEAM REMOTE PLAY MULTIPLAYER Note: Tape to Tape does not feature native online multiplayer. However, you can still play with friends remotely using Steam Remote Play Together or third-party services such as Parsec . Experience the Roguelite Campaign at its best in co-op . Team up with friends to conquer the campaign, debate every power-up choice, and embrace the glorious chaos that makes each run unforgettable. You can also jump into Play Now mode, where up to 10 players can face off locally. With a wide variety of teams, deep gameplay, and countless power-ups, every match feels chaotic, competitive, and endlessly replayable.
