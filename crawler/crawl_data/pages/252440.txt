HOW TO PLAY GOAL: Capture and destroy all evil mages. 1) You roll the dice to select a character 2) If you get 1, 2 or 3 a warrior is selected; if you get 4, 5 or 6 a mage is selected 3) When a warrior is selected you can choose roll the dice again to reposition it trying to capture a mage or take a card to apply a special power 4) You must capture all mages before the time counter reachs the end position This is a challeging game to you that like strategic combats.
