Ekorella is a colorful top-down RPG where plants and animals can be sentient. You and your new friends will explore a fantastic and mysterious world in a surprising journey. Solve puzzles, fight monsters in turn-based battles and interact with unique and lively NPCs! SYNOPSIS Green is a tomato who moved with his brother to Larandella, a serene city where the oranges live. There, Green meets Palms, Laranje and a bipedal dog called Crimson. However, in one night everything goes wrong, and Green is forced to go on a dangerous and fascinating journey across Ekorella. GAMEPLAY Ekorella is a turn-based RPG, where you fight strange creatures to gain more power, skills and loot. Grinding is purely optional, and some enemies will require different strategies to defeat. Every party member is important to the team. Ekorella is full of puzzles and secrets to discover. Exploration will be an important part of your journey.
