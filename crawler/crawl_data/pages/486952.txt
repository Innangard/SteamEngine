Catch and train more than 120 Coromon , master strategic battles, and explore a handcrafted world packed with memorable places and details to discover. Coromon Remastered optimizes the entire journey for PC, with a presentation designed for larger screens. Clearer menus and battle details make everything easier to understand at a glance, while mouse-hover information and quick battle actions make every interaction faster and more natural. SIX BIOMES, COUNTLESS SECRETS. Your first day as a Battle Researcher for Lux Solis should've been straightforward, receive your first Coromon and begin investigating Velua’s six Titans. But when outsiders wielding an element unknown to science attack a  laboratory, your assignment becomes something far more dangerous. Travel through six distinct biomes, each with its own challenges and mysteries. Begin among the fields around Hayville, then journey to a thunderous city, through a haunted swamp, into a guarded desert palace, beneath a volcano, and across frozen mountains toward ancient ruins. Between your main objectives, meet the people who call Velua home, help solve their problems, and leave the path for side quests, secrets, and minigames . Upgrade your Gauntlet to open blocked routes and reveal hidden items, then take to the water as even more of the world opens around you. WHAT DOES IT TAKE TO WIN A COROMON BATTLE? More than raw power! Build your Squad around Skills, Traits, and held items. Then adapt to entry hazards, weather, and status effects to outsmart any opponent you meet, whether it's a wild Coromon, rival Battle Researchers, or a mighty Titan. Master a stamina-based battle system where every Skill costs energy. Spend too much and your Coromon will need to rest, giving its opponent an opening. Hunt for rare Potent and Perfect Coromon . Their distinctive colors make them stand out, and they can earn additional bonus stat points which you can distribute yourself. Evolve a Coromon for higher base stats, or keep it unevolved and enhance its Trait with additional or stronger effects. UNRAVEL A MYSTERY BEYOND SCIENCE As you track down each Titan, the scope of your mission grows. Every region brings a new crisis, an unexpected discovery, and another clue to the outsiders’ plans. Cross paths with new allies, survive dangerous expeditions, and venture into places no Battle Researcher was ever meant to reach. As the pieces connect, you uncover a power capable of changing Velua’s natural order. And by the end of your journey, only one question matters: who, or what, is really in control? YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR RULES Coromon lets you shape the adventure around the way you want to play. Looking for a relaxed playthrough? Make shops and recovery more forgiving, or reduce the pressure of battle and focus on exploring Velua, building your squad and following the story. Looking for higher stakes? Combine built-in Randomize r settings with configurable difficulty rules to change encounters, trainers, Skills, Traits, items, evolutions and even the order of the Titans. Create the Battle Researcher you want to be with a wide selection of outfits, hairstyles, facial hair, headgear and colors. Give your Coromon new looks with unlockable Skins earned through collecting, battles, side quests, minigames and other gameplay challenges. THE FINAL TITAN ISN’T THE END Continue beyond the story in the endless Battle Dome , where Coromon Rush, Trainer Rush and Titan Rush challenge you to see how far your squad can go. Test your strategies against other players in ranked, casual or custom 3v3 and 6v6 battles. A WORD FROM THE DEVELOPERS We’re an independent studio that started with a big dream: to create the ultimate retro monster-taming adventure we always wanted to play, with the care and quality-of-life features we value in modern games. We believe in handcrafted worlds, memorable places and thoughtful details. That philosophy shaped Coromon, brought Coromon Remastered to life, and continues to guide us as we build Coromon: Rogue Planet . We’re incredibly proud of how far that original dream has taken us, and grateful to everyone joining us on the journey.
