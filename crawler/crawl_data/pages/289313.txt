Your only chance is to transform into snake form, gather as much treasure as you can, and retreat to the top of your tower where you'll be safe. The Gameplay SnekromancY is a roguelite dungeon crawler that combines classic snake-style movement with bullet heaven combat and progression. Instead of upgrading weapons, you summon undead minions who ride on your back and attack in either automated or manual modes. You slither through retro arcade-inspired dungeons, survive massive enemy hordes, fight powerful bosses, and create increasingly ridiculous builds as you unlock more and more game-breaking upgrades and relics. Master the art of snecromancy and defeat your enemies! The Demo This is a full game experience, but ends at the completion of act 1 (the full game has 3 acts). Starter content is identical to the full version, but unlocks are limited and some features such as leaderboards are disabled. When you purchase the full game, save game files (steam cloud enabled) and unlock progress will transfer over.
