View Community Hub About This Game Yet Another Climbing Game is a punishing ascent, a twisted tribute to the agony of determination. You guide your climber with a single, clumsy mechanic, grappling upward with imprecise precision. That’s all there is. With persistence, you might find yourself soaring, stumbling, and sometimes hanging by a thread. Great mysteries and perhaps some reward await those daring (or foolish) enough to reach the top. To quote the game's inspiration: "Only a person who loves pain will reach the peak, because there is no other way." -A random from the internet You’ll drag yourself up with a simple but precise control system that seems purpose-built to test your patience. Each grasp, each swing, each slip has the power to make you question your choices. With enough resilience, you might just find your way up. Lose your progress, over and over again. For the few who reach the top, something curious awaits.
