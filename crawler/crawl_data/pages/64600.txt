Experience every part of running a ramen shop, from preparing ingredients and cooking to serving food, looking after customers, and cleaning up. Create a menu you can be proud of, design a shop to your taste, and run it your way. Switch between a first-person view that lets you immerse yourself in cooking and a third-person view that lets you see your character and surroundings. If you're prone to motion sickness when playing in first person, you can choose a view based on your preferences and comfort. Prepare, Cook, and Serve Prepare the soup, cook the noodles, and arrange the toppings to make each bowl just as your customers ordered. Show your skills through the texture of the noodles, the temperature of the soup, the aroma and Umami of your ingredients, and the time it takes to serve each order. Different soup ingredients and physics-based plating give every bowl its own finish. Recreate your favorite ramen or try making a bowl unlike anything anyone has seen before. Let your imagination guide you as you create your signature dish. More Than Ramen Offer gyoza, rice bowls, beer, and other menu items to enjoy alongside your ramen. Watch the browning on your gyoza and pour beer with just the right balance of liquid and foam. Serve the ingredients you prepared for ramen over rice to make dishes such as Char Siu Don. Expect the Unexpected A demanding ramen critic may visit your shop. Fine-tune the aroma, Umami, and toppings to serve a bowl that earns their approval. During business hours, thieves may target the money in your cash register, and fires can break out without warning. Use shuriken, fire extinguishers, and other items to get your shop out of trouble. Or perhaps you could try your luck at the gambling hall and win a fortune... Arrange Your Space and Create a Shop of Your Own Place cooking equipment, furniture, storage, and decorations to arrange your kitchen and dining area just the way you like. Build an efficient shop that handles orders smoothly or a distinctive restaurant worth taking pictures of. Use My Designs to apply your own drawings to posters, noren curtains, ramen bowls, and more. Visit a friend's shop and leave your autograph. You'll also find unusual interactive fixtures and equipment, including toilets and gas cylinders. Customize your outfit, body shape, and emotes, as well as your noodle-draining animations. Apply your own designs to clothing and create matching uniforms with your friends. Unlock new ingredients and equipment as your Shop Level rises. Use the money you earn to expand your shop, add to your menu, and improve your dining area. Online Multiplayer for Up to Six Players Divide ingredient preparation, cooking, serving, customer service, and cleaning among your friends, and work together to handle the orders as they pour in. In-game spatial voice chat is supported. Distance and walls affect how voices sound, making it feel as though you're working together in the same shop. You can also hire part-time NPC staff and take your time running the shop solo. Your Perfect Bowl, Served in Your Perfect Shop Pursue the ultimate flavor. Perfect your efficiency. Build a bustling restaurant with friends. Create a one-of-a-kind ramen shop filled with your own ideas and passion.
