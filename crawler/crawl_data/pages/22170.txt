Become a God. Soulstone Survivors is a fast-paced action roguelite where you obliterate hordes of enemies, battle colossal bosses, and forge your path to godlike power. As a Void Hunter, harness devastating skills, craft unique weapons, and unlock game-breaking synergies in your quest for power. Master the Void 350+ Skills, Endless Builds – Customize your playstyle with hundreds of active and passive abilities. Face the Titans – Overcome massive bosses that test your strategy and reflexes. Deep Progression – Unlock characters, upgrade a vast skill tree, and shape your perfect build. Craft & Conquer – Forge over 100 unique weapons to push your power even further. Ever-Changing Challenges – Take on curses, explore diverse maps, and master multiple game modes. Become a Void Hunter – Learn about the order of the Void Hunters and face the Void King himself. Join over a million players in the ultimate roguelite experience and carve your legend in the void!
