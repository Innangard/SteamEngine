ONE GOAL: DOMINATE. Jump into a fast, chaotic multiplayer shooter built around four unique ways to play. Every mode rewards skill, strategy, and quick thinking. 🔥 Free-for-All — 5 rounds of pure chaos. Every round ends when only one player remains. Win a round for 20 points, and rack up 10 points per kill. Most points at the end takes the crown. ⚔️ 2v2v2 — 5 rounds, three teams, no allies for long. A round ends when only one team — or a single duo — remains standing. Winning teams score 20 points, and every kill adds 10 points to your squad's total. Highest team score wins it all. 🛡️ Team vs Team — Classic best-of-5 showdown. First team to win 3 rounds takes the match. 🚪 Trivia Gates — Think fast, move faster! Race through two doors, but only one leads to survival — the one behind the correct answer. Wrong guess, and you're out. Simple to learn, endlessly replayable. Whether you're a lone wolf or squad player, Brainrot Fire has a mode built for your playstyle.
