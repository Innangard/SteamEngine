Sort through mementos and solve light puzzles on every page to discover the real-life-inspired story of Connie and Beth’s friendship. Enjoy 1 to 2 hours of carefully crafted playtime full of emotional highs and lows - and plenty of cats! Meet Connie , a 30-something looking back on a difficult time in her life: at 10 years old, her family was uprooted to the city, leaving her feeling lost and alon e. Just as she was at her lowest, a new friend came into the picture - bold, confident Beth ! The were inseparable all through their school years. Now, it's been years since they spoke. Connie is using her scrapbook to sort through her memories, and her feelings, about this friendship from the past, penning an emotional letter to her former best friend as she goes. Connie needs to decide: does she want to reach out, or is the past best left behind? Enjoy a variety of scrapbooking activities and light puzzles - every page is unique! You'll find yourself doing all sorts of activities in the book's pages, whether its sorting mementos , reassembling torn up photos, or solving gentle logic and word puzzles - the scrapbook is full of surprises! As you uncover the story, you'll also step into Connie's shoes as she relives the past through unique emotional moments. Of course, it wouldn't be scrapbooking without decoration - unlock new stickers in every chapter and  use them to customise pages! What Pieced Together offers: 1-2 hours of light puzzles A carefully crafted, poignant story about life, friendship and getting older Beautiful illustrations and hand-drawn animations Stickers to decorate with An original acoustic soundtrack High quality voice acting It doesn’t offer: Hardcore brain-teasers Branching narrative Free-form creative mode Glowfrog Games is a tiny company of two people, Kate and Ellie, who left the world of free-to-play to make more meaningful games together. Pieced Together is the studio’s first game, built with the help of a talented freelance team. We poured a lot of our personal experiences into the story and its details, and we hope you enjoy playing it as much as we enjoyed making it!
