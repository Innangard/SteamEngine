Fight hordes of enemies and gain experience. Strengthen your character and skills. Defeat cultists and the Mithos they summon. Collect and upgrade items, obtain unique items, and use them to fight in dark arenas and procedurally generated dungeons. Learn the behaviors of your opponents. Unlock new heroes and try out new builds. What can you expect in Cult of Shadows?
