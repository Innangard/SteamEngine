Navigate the open seas and explore each distant structure, uncovering every hidden nook and cranny with a diverse, expressive move-set. Beat challenges or find hidden chests to collect talismans along the way that modify your abilities and allow you to express yourself through your schmovement. You can even switch up your drip with a plethora of discoverable outfits! And never forget, you're not alone in this journey! Compete with other players for speedrunning times by challenging their ghosts, or tag a wall and share your message with the entire world! Are you ready to conquer this kingdom and expose all of its mysterious secrets? Or will Beebz be crushed by what awaits her? Only one way to find out! FEATURES A deeply expressive platforming move-set. Express yourself through your schmovement and get lost in the flow! A seamless, open ocean to explore at blazing speeds! Chill out with your crew and buy new items of interest at your mobile hub! Discover hidden secrets and unravel the world’s mysteries! Place your own checkpoints wherever you want and return to them any time you meet your untimely demise! Collect unique talismans and modify your move-set with paragliders, hookshots, bubbles and more! Change up your appearance with head gear, outfits and hair dyes; there’s no shortage of drip! Race other players and compete for the best completion times, may the best runner win! Create and share graffiti that will appear in other player’s worlds! Extra game modes like NG+ and Randomizer where rewards, enemies and even the weather are randomized! FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS Do you need to play Demon Turf before playing Demon Tides? You can hop right into Demon Tides without ever having played Demon Turf before! There’s plenty of easter eggs for existing fans, but we’ve designed the narrative for new players to jump right in! What does “Expressive” platforming mean, exactly? Simply put, it’s a platformer where every solution is the right one! We want you to express yourself through the game’s movement, and to do that we’ve created a highly customisable move-set with nearly unlimited combinational depth. The further you get into the game, and the more comfortable you get with the movement, the more unique your gameplay will become! What’s Fabraz done before Demon Tides? We’re most well known for the 3D Platformer Demon Turf and 2D Platformer Slime-san! But we’re also behind lesser known titles such as Cannon Crasha, Planet Diver and even published Skellboy and SpiritSphere DX! Wait, are you also behind Bubsy 4D? We sure are, wild huh? We've teamed up with Atari to finally give that bobcat the love he always deserved!
