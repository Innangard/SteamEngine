This  demo features two game modes: 🎃 Fangtopia Mode 🎃 Shape a wonderfully cozy world with stacks of hex tiles 🏝️ Island Mode 🏝️ Build a vast and vibrant network of Ghost Train railways to link multiple islands Each mode has a 1,000-point limit in the demo. And don't miss ASMR Mode in the Audio settings for a more soothing and ethereal soundscape! Who said ruling over a creepy kingdom couldn't be chill? Coming in the full game on 26th October... Build magical landscapes, raise spooktacular landmarks, and craft a monsterrific para dise for friendly beasties. Design a carefree, scare-free world of spooky delights. Build the most beautiful worlds you can or try to construct the highest-scoring kingdoms. It’s entirely up to you! A MODE FOR EVERY MOOD 🎃 Fangtopia Mode 🎃 Jump right in and craft delightfully spooky worlds in short, restful sessions 🧛 Ghoulish Mode 🧛 Settle in and build bigger, bolder worlds with a wider spread of tile types 🏝️ Island Mode 🏝️ Connect an ocean of archipelagos by laying down railway track tiles for the 🚂Ghost Train🚂 ALL TREATS, NO TRICKS 👻 100% scare-free: Fangtopia’s fiends are friendly, not frightening! ⏱️ No time pressure: Relax and build your world at your own pace 🧩 Collect them all: Unlock over 100 unique titles for your kingdom 👑 Global & Friends leaderboards: Compete for the crown of Fangtopia 🦇 Interactive sticker book: Fulfil special goals to grow your cozy collection 🎧 ASMR Audio Mode: Relax your senses and switch on ASMR settings for added comfort HOW TO PLAY MATCH TILES Place land tiles from a stack, connecting terrain of the same type to score points and expand your kingdom. Watch as your world grows into a hauntingly beautiful landscape filled with misty mountains, dark woods, ghostly railways, snaking rivers, charming towns, and more! BUILD LANDMARKS Meet score goals to acquire a wide array of weird and wonderful landmarks, then complete their special objectives to unlock more tiles and expand your world. What new and devious discoveries will you make as your kingdom expands? DISCOVER MONSTERS Complete portal objectives to draw friendly monsters into your world, then place their habitats! Where does the werewolf's forest belong? Which mountain cluster is just right for the yeti? All manner of quirky creatures are out there, just waiting to find their perfect home!
