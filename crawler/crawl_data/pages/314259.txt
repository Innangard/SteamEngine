Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Doll Inc is still in early development, and we believe Early Access is the best way to grow the game alongside our community. Our goal is to regularly update the game with new content, features, and improvements—many of which will expand upon the universe and ideas introduced in Doll Impostor, the game that inspired this world. Early Access allows us to gather player feedback, test new mechanics in real conditions, and ensure that every addition aligns with the experience players expect from a title like Doll Inc. We're fully committed to seeing this project through and delivering something truly engaging, eerie, and unique.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect Doll Inc to remain in Early Access for approximately one year.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version of Doll Inc is expected to include new dolls, boxes, and a wider variety of products to inspect and classify. Additional features such as leaderboards and other gameplay systems are also being considered to expand the overall experience.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Doll Inc includes the complete main story and an endless mode. The core gameplay systems are fully functional, allowing players to experience the game’s atmosphere, decision-making mechanics, and key features from start to finish.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, the price is expected to increase when the game leaves Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Currently, Doll Impostor and Doll Inc share the same community and player base. If you're looking for action, quick thrills or casual gameplay... this might not be for you. But if you enjoy attention to detail, routine, and challenge — welcome to the job. Warning: This game contains jumpscares. Warning: This game is based on the universe of another game by the same creator, Doll Impostor. Dolls, boxes, robots, and other elements share the same models, names, and assets. When Doll Impostor is updated, this game will also receive updates, such as new dolls, boxes, and more. Story Mode: Your 30-Day Trial Begins You've just been hired by Doll INC, the mysterious factory behind the production of Doll Impostor dolls. You have 30 trial days to prove you're fit for the job. Each day brings new tasks, tools, and choices that will test your obedience. Will you follow orders… or dare to disobey? Job Simulator: Inspect, Scan, Survive Experience a unique job simulator: inspect products, scan for defects, operate tools, and detect hidden anomalies. You’ll work with dolls, boxes, and robots—nothing and no one is free from flaws. Learn fast. Act with precision. Your future depends on your performance. Extra Modes: Beyond the Trial ✔️ Worker Mode: endless routine. ✔️ Time Attack: speed up, but don’t mess up. ✔️ Accumulator: produce non-stop, rise in rank. ✔️ Endless Mode: what if you could never leave?
