Content For This Game Browse all (1) Truck Mechanic: Dangerous Paths Soundtrack $2.99 $2.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Truck Mechanic: Dangerous Paths is a mechanic and truck driver simulator, with a singular focus on the mechanical function of your vehicle. Every job is a journey and a true test of your resilience and skill. Drive through realistic South American routes that are as picturesque as they are treacherous. Complete unique cargo transport missions. Can you overcome the challenges the road throws at you and keep your truck running under extreme conditions? Perform detailed and realistic repairs en route: from engine tune-ups to changing tires. You have a wide variety of tools and parts for diagnostics and repairs at your disposal. Think strategically and keep your truck in tip-top shape. Brave the dangerous but mesmerizing roads of South America: realistic views of wild nature, a variety of landscapes, day/night cycles, changing weather conditions. Everything comes together to provide an absolutely breathtaking experience. But don’t forget that conquering dirt roads, rocky paths, and dusty outbacks will take a toll on your truck, so keep a close eye, and don’t wear it out! Jobs don’t grow on trees: check trading spots and gas stations for lucrative offers. Earn the trust of the locals to get access to better contracts. Each completed mission will pay out in funds you can invest in your truck to upgrade it or change its style. There are many paths to your destination: how you get there is completely up to you. Find your own way while roaming the beautiful wilderness. But be prepared for anything: a truck driver braving the unknown must be adept at repairs and navigation. Look for safe passages: exploring the area can be very rewarding, and you won’t have to waste precious time on fixing your truck afterward. Your truck doesn’t have to be dull: you can customize its appearance in your garage. Change body kits and decorate your cabin space. Upgrade your truck to get better performance out on the rough roads. Check out an immersive and engaging campaign that will add depth to your road mechanic adventure. Every job takes you for a ride with unique driving and repair challenges. Can you conquer them all? Test your mettle! Get ready for: Repair and maintenance of the many components of your truck during your runs. Over 1000 parts to take apart and reassemble during repairs. Countless tools, from wrenches, to machetes, to winches. Extremely high levels of detail for your engines and other mechanisms. A range of cargo to move across the land. Gas stations for refueling. Five vast, fully open locations for you to explore on foot or behind the wheel. Atmospheric and enchanting South American vistas. Dozens of routes of various difficulty levels that will take you across rough terrain, forest trails, and hardcore off-roading territory. An engaging story that will land you in unexpected situations. Locations to explore that yield new parts, hidden spots, and bonus objectives. Your own cozy garage: your base of operations and point of departure for your missions. Truck customization, including cabin space. CB radio communications: call for help or get in touch with your assistant. Player character improvements. Player character reputation system and reactive NPCs. Dynamic weather and day/night cycles.
