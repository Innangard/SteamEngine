Design unique track layouts with elevations, banked curves, crossings, junctions, and any combination you can imagine. Place buildings for loading, unloading, stations, and other railway operations, then shape the world around them with decorations, nature, environments, structures, signs, and details that make every map feel alive. Simple to Drive, Hard to Master Driving the train is easy to understand, but mastering it is another story. As you move across the map, you will need to make quick decisions: switch tracks, control your speed, reverse when needed, and use the map view to plan the fastest route ahead. Speed matters, but so does control. Your locomotive type, wagon count, cargo weight, and current speed all affect how safely you can take each turn. Push too hard and you may derail, and nobody wants that. Take on Delivery Missions Each run begins with delivery missions made up of different tasks. You will be told what cargo you need, how many wagons are required, and where everything needs to go. Some missions may ask for several wagon types in different quantities, and as you progress, routes become more complex, deliveries spread across multiple locations, and planning becomes just as important as speed. Cozy Multiplayer Everything is better together. Team up with 2–4 players and work together to complete missions across miniature railway worlds. Decide how you want to play: split the wagons between players, coordinate different trains, or create special combinations to tackle each delivery your own way. There’s no competition — just teamwork, coordination, and a little railway chaos along the way.
