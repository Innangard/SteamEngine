With every step you take, Liminal Shift pushes you deeper into an eerie, unsettling experience like no other. Key Features In Liminal Shift it's all about exploring. There is no such monsters or jumpscares in game. It's just you and the environment. Listen, feel and find a way through. You can interact with objects around you, allowing you to truly feel immersed in the game. The game delivers an uninterrupted, realistic experience with dynamic transitions; it includes no black screens or loading screens, focusing solely on gameplay and cutscenes. Every sound is almost real; with Meta Sound Technology, listen and explore every detail. The Levels The demo of Liminal Shift begins in white corridors, allowing you to transition through Level 507 and Level 141 to reach Level 37, the poolrooms. You can feel and experience the unique atmosphere of the poolrooms. Note : Liminal Shift features unsettling enclosed spaces and themes of getting lost in liminal environments, which may cause distress for players with claustrophobia or a fear of disorientation.
