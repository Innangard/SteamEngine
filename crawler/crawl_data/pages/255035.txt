Take control of the situation by pushing boxes, stacking them, and even jumping between platforms, acquiring the best strategy to help the adorable kittens reach one of the available beds for a well-deserved rest. In Mimi the Cat – Meow Together , you have two ways to play. Cooperative Mode: In this mode, you and your friend will take control of different kittens. Teamwork is essential to solve the puzzles, and cooperation is the key here. Single Player Mode: In single player mode, the player will need to switch between the available cats, solving the puzzles alone. Strategy and cooperation are crucial for success, but some toys in the environment can help solve the puzzle. Throughout the journey, you'll come across items. Collecting them provides possibilities; customize the kittens, making them even cuter. There is no time limit or resources to manage, so you can calmly focus on solving the challenges. Features: 50 handcrafted levels. Local cooperative or single player mode 18 Uniques outfits. 9 cute kittens. Stack boxes. Jump between platforms, like a good cat would. Minimalist interface and gameplay. Restart level. A tip on how many boxes are needed. Catchy soundtrack. Fun and sweet sokoban with relaxing Mimi, Branquinho, Nevezinho, Melzinha, Thomas, Nhoque, Pipoca, Cinzendo, Negan, are here to warm your heart ♥.
