With a focus on the beauty of the levels, an inspiring original soundtrack, and a short story that involves the player, Sky Journey - Jigsaw Landscapes, takes you on a relaxing and surprising experience, with : Gorgeus scenes of Jigsaw Puzzles. A beautiful Story about recovering a friendship. A Unique New mechanic that takes you into the scene after each puzzle. For Jigsaw Puzzle lovers, this game has the best of its genre : Complete 6 Jigsaw Puzzle scenes at 5 levels of dificulty. Enjoy a minimalist interface that helps you focus on the puzzle. Use great gameplay functions that make the experience even better. Relax with an original Soundtrack for each level! For any players who enjoy a beautiful experience, the game has an Unique innovation of its genre: With each puzzle assembled, the player is taken to a live scene in 2.5D, where it is possible to discover more about the story behind the game, in addition to finding more achievements from Steam. At the end of the game, you will have followed a little boy's little journey flying through beautiful landscapes in search of his lost robotic friend! Story : In the year 2086, the planet earth is no longer the same. Our little boy, Akil, lives alone on a mountain that makes a laboratory, where, together with his robotic friend Buddy, they build flying suits to fly over the landscapes. But accidentally, his robotic friend is lost in a very distant place, and the only thing Akil knows, is his direction, thanks to the laser beacon they created. Follow Akil's journey flying across landscapes until he finds his friend and brings him back! Can you put them back together?
