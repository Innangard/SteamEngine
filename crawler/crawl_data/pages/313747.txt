If you manage to complete these tasks, your reputation will increase and you will be trusted, earning you valuable rewards in the process. However, the tasks will not be without danger. It is rumored that especially at midnight strange figures move through the office buildings. The real challenge isn't breaking in, it's surviving the night There are rumors of illegal experiments that have attracted the attention of mysterious and terrifying entities. For some years now, these entities have been terrorizing the city, but something isn't right about it, and you must find out what. Enhance your character to take on even tougher heists UPGRADE YOUR GEAR by earning Experience Points to unlock and purchase deadlier, high-tech equipment. LEVEL ORGANIZATIONS by completing their missions to gain access to permanent bonuses. MASTER PERKS by equipping and leveling up to three at a time, forging your unique playstyle. CRAFT MODULES with your stolen cash. Each randomized Module can supercharge your skills or grant entirely new abilities. Infinite Replayability —  Every heist is unique 8 maps in different sizes, combined with randomly generated tasks, offer huge variety. Each heist receives random main and side objectives, as well as completely random loot and special items. Featuring 6 unique entities, each differing in both looks and skills, no two rounds ever feel the same. Want to customize the challenge? Take on Black Market Missions to modify your heists even further. The Black Market is alive and influences your heists On the Black Market, you can sell highly valuable loot, whose price changes every day, and access Black Market Missions. These special missions allow you to dramatically manipulate your heists to maximize your loot in exchange for harder challenges. Discover the story of Midnight City There are two major Operation campaigns featuring a total of  60+ missions that you can complete right alongside your heists. Each mission offers rewards like experience points, money, or special skins you can't get anywhere else. Every mission is packed with exciting story content, pulling you right into the middle of the action. And that's not all...
