View Community Hub About This Game "Desk Garden" is a soothing desktop idle game that quietly grows in the corner of your screen while you work or study. Freely arrange your plants anywhere on screen - they'll never interrupt your productivity. Meet your quirky plant babies! From shy little sprouts to chatty humanoid blossoms, your Tiny Island blooms with personality. Each adorable plant grows with its own unique charm! Every mystery plant box brings a new surprise! Discover a world of surprises with our decorative mystery boxes! Plus, enjoy soothing background music to accompany your work and study sessions.
