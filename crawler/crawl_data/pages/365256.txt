Graveyard Cabin is a psychological FMV anomaly horror game where observation is your only weapon. Work the night shift inside an isolated graveyard security cabin and monitor a network of black-and-white surveillance cameras. Detect anomalies, report supernatural activity, and survive until morning. Spot the Anomalies Memorize every corner of the graveyard and carefully monitor the live camera feeds. If a gravestone moves, a figure appears, or something changes unexpectedly, report it before it’s too late. But be careful... Too many false reports will anger the entities hunting you. You only have 3 chances. If You Hear Crying, Hide Not every anomaly should be observed. If you hear the crying of a little girl, immediately shut down the cameras and stay silent in the dark until the sound disappears. Looking too long may attract something you cannot escape. The Cabin Is Not Safe The danger is not limited to the cameras. While monitoring the graveyard, supernatural entities can appear inside the cabin itself. Check the internal cabin camera frequently and stay alert to movement beside you… or directly behind you. The observer can quickly become the victim. Defend Yourself If an entity breaches the cabin, you must react instantly. Use the Cross against hostile creatures. Use the Amulet to banish the Dead Woman. Choose the wrong item or hesitate for even a second and your shift will end permanently.
