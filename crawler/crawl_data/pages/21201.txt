Geliştirici ürün hakkında ne diyor: Neden Erken Erişim? "We believe Farm to Table will become a much better game by growing together with the community. Early Access gives us the opportunity to gather real player feedback on the core gameplay loop of farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Balancing progression, economy, and content pacing is especially important for a management game like this. We want to make sure the player’s journey feels rewarding, that new systems unlock at the right time, and that the overall gameplay flow feels smooth and enjoyable rather than overwhelming or repetitive. Player feedback helps us understand what feels fun, what needs improvement, and how we can create the best possible experience. We also want to shape the game alongside our players, improving systems and expanding the experience based on community feedback rather than building everything in isolation." Bu oyun ne kadar süre için Erken Erişim'de olacak? "We currently expect Farm to Table to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 to 12 months. However, this timeline may remain flexible depending on community feedback and the overall development process. Our goal is to take the time needed to improve the game properly, polish the experience, and make sure the full version reaches the quality we aim for rather than rushing the process." Erken Erişim versiyonunun tam versiyonundan ne kadar farklı olması planlanıyor? "We plan for the full version of Farm to Table to significantly expand both the depth and the overall scope of the experience. This includes more recipes, crops, animals, deeper restaurant management systems, expanded staff management, automation systems, more customization options, seasonal events, and stronger progression systems. We also plan to expand island exploration, allowing players to discover more ingredients in the wild, go fishing, and unlock new ways to grow and produce food. In the full version, players will also be able to sell their home-grown farm produce directly to customers through their own Farmers’ Market, adding another layer to the farming and business side of the game. Many players have also asked about co-op. We’d love that, but it also depends on player support. The first version of the game will launch as a singleplayer experience, and the support and interest we see around release will help us better evaluate the possibility of adding co-op in the future." Erken Erişim sürümünün şu anki durumu nedir? "The Early Access version of Farm to Table already offers the full core gameplay experience, centered around farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Players can grow crops, cook and serve dishes in their restaurant, go fishing, explore the island for wild ingredients, hire staff, manage their restaurant, and customize both their farm and restaurant to match their own style. The game also includes production systems using machines to create more advanced ingredients, as well as the Farmers’ Market where players can sell their home-grown produce directly to customers. Our goal is to provide around 30–40 hours of gameplay in Early Access, with a strong foundation of systems and progression already in place, while continuing to expand and improve the experience based on player feedback throughout development." Erken Erişim sırasında ve sonrasında oyunun fiyatı değişecek mi? "We plan to offer Farm to Table at a lower price during Early Access and gradually increase the price as we add more content, features, and improvements throughout development. As the game grows in scope and depth, the price will better reflect the overall amount of content and the full experience. Players who join us early during Early Access will be able to support the game from the beginning and benefit from the lower entry price." Topluluğu, geliştirme sürecine nasıl dahil etmeyi düşünüyorsunuz? "Community feedback is one of the most important parts of our development process. Our Discord server is our most active space, where we stay in constant communication with players, gather feedback, discuss ideas, and listen to suggestions directly from the community. We are also active on Steam Discussions, making sure players can easily share their thoughts, report issues, and help us improve the game. We plan to share regular development updates, roadmap progress, and balance changes based on player feedback. Community voting and ongoing discussions on Discord will also help us better understand what players care about most and which features should be prioritized as development continues. We want Farm to Table to grow together with its players, and community involvement will remain a core part of that process throughout Early Access." Farm to Table , kendi restoranını sıfırdan inşa edebildiğin bir çiftçilik ve restoran işletme simülasyonu. Rengarenk bir adayı keşfe çık! Pişirecek malzemeler yetiştirebilir, doğadan yabani sebze meyveler toplayabilir, taptaze ürünler için hayvan yetiştirebilir, balığa çıkabilir, makinelerle daha iyi ürünler üretebilir ve aç misafirlerin için lezetli mi lezzetli tarifler pişirebilirsin. Hayallerindeki mekanı  istediğin gibi inşa et, kendi tarzında dekore et, çalışanlar işe al ve arka bahçende kurduğun organik işini 5 yıldızlı bir restorana dönüştür!! Sıfırdan İnşa Et Renkli bir adaya bakan sahil tepesinde restoranını sıfırdan kur! Mutfağının ve çiftliğinin her köşesini içerde ya da dışarda özelleştir. Duvar, zemin, pencere, dekorasyon ve mobilya stillerinden dilediğini seç. Ekin Ek, Yetiştir & Hasat Et Kapının önünde taze ürünler yetiştir, hayvanlar besle! Taze mahsul topla ve evcil dostlar sahiplen. Her hasatta hayallerine bir adım daha yaklaş! Pişir & Servis Et Pişirip servis et ya da çiftlik marketinde sat. Adada malzemeler keşfederek yeni tarifler öğren! Farklı menüler oluştur, pişir, fırınla, ızgara yap ve lezzetli tabaklar servis ederek itibarını artır; daha çok müşteri çek! Topla & Balık Tut Doğada yabani mantarlar, meyve sebzeler ve daha birçok özel lezzeti keşfet yada açık denizde nadir balıklar yakala! Üret Hasadını daha gelişmiş malzemelere dönüştüren faydalı makineler edin ve yeni tariflerin kilidini aç. Yönet Kaynaklarını yönet, aşçı, garson ve çiftçi işe al, mekânını geliştir ve itibarını büyüt. İster çiftlikte sakin sakin ilerle, ister mutfakta tempoyu yükselt; mütevazı, sıcacık mekanını 5 yıldızlı bir restorana dönüştür!
