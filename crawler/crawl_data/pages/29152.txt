Slotbound is a solo-developed roguelite defense game that fuses explosive 3x3 slot machine jackpots with deep autobattler strategy. Say Goodbye to Ordinary Defense Games! Tired of the same old tower defense? We've injected pure dopamine into the very core of combat preparation. Exploit system loopholes, bend RNG to your will, and become the master architect of your own victory.
