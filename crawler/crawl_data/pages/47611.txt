Lihat Hab Komuniti Laporkan pepijat dan tinggalkan maklum balas untuk permainan ini di papan perbincangan Lihat semua perbincangan Ulasan “This game is SO fun” Markiplier “Blood is fuel, for my COCK” Civvie “Humans were not supposed to be doing this!” Jerma Tentang Permainan Ini ULTRAKILL is a fast-paced ultraviolent old school FPS that fuses together classic shooters like Quake , modern shooters like Doom (2016) and character action games like Devil May Cry . Mankind has gone extinct and the only beings left on earth are machines fueled by blood . But now that blood is starting to run out on the surface... Machines are racing to the depths of Hell in search of more. Use your many movement abilities to stay mobile and avoid the relentless attacks of the dead, demons and other machines. Destroy them with an arsenal of incredibly powerful weapons, each with multiple available variations. Soak yourself in their blood to regain health and keep fighting. Kill fast and with SSStyle to rack up combos and gain points that can be used on weapon variations between missions. Master the many levels to achieve high ranks and take on unique optional challenges. Explore the varied and unique campaign environments inspired by Dante's Inferno to find plentiful hidden secrets Penerangan Kandungan Dewasa Pembangun menerangkan kandungan ini seperti berikut: This game contains scenes of explicit violence and gore.
