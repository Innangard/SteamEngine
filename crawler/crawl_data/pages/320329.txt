View Community Hub About This Game This is a first-person organization simulation game where you shelve the products, unlock new skills, and use your unique methods to tidy up the mall. Game Modes Solo, together, or competition... Enjoy the different game modes: Single player, online co-op, and online PvP. You can also try "Sandbox Mode", where all your skills are unlocked and always ready to use, or "No-Skill Mode" to challenge yourself. Stores Your task is to shelve 3,682 products into the correct stores in the mall. Supermarket, bags store, bookstore, cosmetics store, video games store, gift shop, hardware store, hat store, toy store, sporting goods shop, tobacco shop, and sunglasses store. Skills Sorting : Sorts the products in your hand Highlighting Shelf : Highlights the correct shelf for the topmost product in your hand Highlighting Products : Highlights the same products as the topmost product in your hand Auto-Placement : Automatically places your products on the shelf Auto-Collect : Automatically collects the same products as the topmost product in your hand System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 7 64-bit | Windows 8 64-bit | Windows 10 64-bit Processor: Intel Core i5-11400F | AMD Ryzen 5 5600x Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 | AMD Radeon R9-290X Network: Broadband Internet connection Storage: 1800 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 7 64-bit | Windows 8 64-bit | Windows 10 64-bit Processor: Intel Core i5-13400F | AMD Ryzen 5 7600x Memory: 16 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 | AMD Radeon RX-6600 Network: Broadband Internet connection Storage: 1800 MB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
