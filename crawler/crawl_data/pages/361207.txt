See you in Castor Yard! S4U: CITYPUNK 2011 AND LOVE PUNCH is a text-driven adventure game powered by keyboard interactions. The story is set in Castor Yard, a fictional modern city in 2011. You play as Miki, who works part-time chatting for clients, meeting a variety of people and cyberlife online. Through countless small and big decisions made via the keyboard, you’ll create your own memories in Castor Yard. Welcome to Castor Yard, 2011. Let your fingertips dance on the keyboard as you play as Miki, a daytime architect and part time online mouthpiece. Make a living, fall in love, and do your best to earn your place in Castor Yard! It's more than just typing; it's unique gameplay is full of surprises, just as life! Your fate's intertwined with more than 20 unique characters in Castor Yard; love comes in all shapes and sizes. Muster up the courage to go for the one your heart desires and take on the ultimate romantic roller coaster ride. Don't just stare into your computer screen, go downstairs, take a walk, chat with somebody, play with the cats. What's the password for this photo album... Well, forget it, just go downstairs and get one last smoke! No, no, no...Those memories in Castor Yard, I still care for them… How the story ends and which memories you’ll want to hold onto are all yours to decide! About BOOOMJAM The BOOOMJAM is a game development event held twice a year, where participants are given three weeks to develop a game based on a specific theme. After the development phase, we host an online game festival where all players can try out the games, and these games are also showcased at the G-FUSION offline video game carnival.
