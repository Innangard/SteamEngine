View Community Hub About This Game Resultare is a Pachinko Roguelike inspired by Peggle’s physics and Balatro’s quirky combinations. In a game full of decisions, precision, and chaos, can you find the right synergies to crush the quota? Carefully plan each shot as you unlock powerful tokens, build unique combinations, and chase ever-higher scores! One more run won’t be enough. Every shot in Resultare is an opportunity. Take aim and watch the effects of your tokens snowball, all while trying to reach the score quota. With over 100 Items, no two runs will be the same. Discover new and powerful synergies that shape how you’ll play! Features 120+ Tokens to mix and match for endless strategic builds. 50+ Tickets offering tactical advantages and gameplay twists. 30+ Handcrafted stages to conquer. 25+ Bosses that will do their best to slow you down. 5 Different game modes to unlock that change the way you play. 7 Difficulties for those who love a challenge. And more to come in future updates! Unlock countless items, difficulties, and game modes when playing. You’ll always have a goal in Resultare... and if that isn’t enough, you can also pet the cat!
