Unlock powerful abilities, grab legendary gear, and fight your way to the top. A true legend in Flash gaming, Swords & Souls remains the highest-rated game of all time on Armor Games and Kongregate. Yes, it’s that good. Rogue Soul Take on life as a rogue in this fast-paced runner where the streets are yours to conquer. Leap across rooftops, dodge traps, and take down enemies as you collect loot and build your reputation. With endless exploration, 50 treasures to discover, and a town full of challenges, this game lets you carve your name into history as the most wanted rogue in town. Rogue Soul 2 The runner continues with even more adventure. Rogue Soul 2 expands the world with new enemies, detailed environments, massive upgrades, and tough-as-nails boss fights. Whether you’re perfecting your skills, finding rare treasures, or taking on challenges, this sequel is packed with so many reasons to keep coming back for more. The Soul Driver Buckle up and hit the road in this chaotic chase game! As a wanted outlaw, it’s you versus the police force in a mad dash to the border. Customize and upgrade your car, smash through roadblocks, and dodge everything from spike strips to helicopters. With 42 miles of mayhem, 6 unique challenges, and 24 achievements, the cops won’t know what hit them. The Gentleman Put on your top hat, grab your umbrella, and explore the life of a true gentleman. Navigate a world of clever puzzles and platforming challenges as you defy gravity and master the art of finesse. Equal parts stylish and tricky, this game is perfect for players who enjoy an elegant twist on the puzzle solving genre.
