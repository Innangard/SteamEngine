The world waits for no one; as you are exploring and collecting, crafting and building, planning, the forces of darkness are amassing power and executing their own designs. Left unchecked, they will unleash devastating consequences! Will you find strength in numbers, building a massive town with an army of dozens or even hundreds of NPCs, gathering enough resources and resilience to survive whatever crisis is thrown your way? Or will you strike out alone against the evil, relying on your own skills and strength to prevail? FEATURES Truly vast character customization system, with dozens of classes, each with their own skill-trees, strengths and weaknesses. Craft and equip the best gear with different stats and perks for your perfect warrior! Multiplayer mode lets your friends join in the fun; survive, thrive, and push back the forces of the evil gods together! Mighty and varied enemy forces, always scheming and striking and escalating. Monsters can plan ambushes and even instigate uprisings! If you do not work to stem the tide, you will be overwhelmed. Every choice has consequences. Who will you stop to save? Will you focus on curing the deadly plague or will you gather resources instead? Every moment has a hundred different possibilities! PLAY WITH A MIND TOWARDS MINING Stone and clay; trees; walls and mountains and the ground beneath your feet; if you can see it, you can take it! Gather resources wherever you go, both in the foreground and the background, allowing you to craft all sorts of powerful items, potions, buildings, and more! But be judicious: all the time you spend gathering stones is time the enemy spends gathering strength! A WORLD AT WAR A massive variety of enemies– hundreds of different monster-types to be found in different biomes, with different abilities and behaviors, coordinated by powerful bosses with a will of their own! Monsters do more than just wait around for you to come kill them, and they don’t simply seek you out, either. The forces of darkness will concoct different strategies as you play, trying to lure you into deadly ambushes, pilfer your resources, spread plagues and curses throughout your town, or keep you busy long enough to build up power and unleash a devastating cataclysm!
