View Community Hub About This Game Master the art of auctions and score big against others for treasures in Storage Hunter Simulator! Stay ahead of the competition in the high risk, great rewards world of storage auctions. Showcase your skills, win auctions in intense bidding wars and sell your hard-earned valuables to your customers. But make sure you turn a profit by getting the best deal! Maximizing your earnings is key to staying in business and building a solid reputation. Take risks and get in the fight for the best abandoned storages. Enjoy relaxing road trips in your cars as you drive to storage auctions. Spot hidden treasures that no one else sees and get the best bid. Make yourself a name and reputation that's well known and feared among the competition. Are you ready for the next storage hunt? From trash to treasures: each abandoned storage locker up for auction can contain thousands of unique items for you to spot and auction. Some items may look like garbage! Only with your knowledge and expertise will you be able to recognize their true value and squeeze every dollar from them into your earnings. Not sure about a certain item? Ask the experts, broaden your horizons, or check the in-game glossary. All items come with conditions, certain categories, and there are also collectible sets. Got the highest bid? Now it's time to take what's yours and try to sell it in your pawnshop or maybe to a quirky collector. Collectors offer unique rewards like cosmetics, cars, or reputation boosts when you help them complete their sometimes bizarre and weird collections. Use your available cash and run a hands-on business with your own pawnshop. Sell your valuables and roll up to the next auction in style. An item doesn't sell? Use marketing to boost your item's exposure to make a buck quicker! Build your business from the ground up, expand it, and build a network of pawnshops. Handle shelf placement, NPC customers, haggling, upgrading employees, capacities, and specializing in item categories. But keep track of loans and daily costs and financials to avoid going broke. That's the loser's path! And losers don't get the buy their own property. If you act smart, you'll eventually be able to leave your rusty trailer home behind and expand into residences with garages, larger mansions, and luxury and dedicated warehouses for your most valuable items. Decorate and furnish properties in Architect mode. Don't want to go on your next storage hunt alone? Invite up to three friends to a wild and chaotic drop-in, drop-out coop ride. Work as a team and live your dream of finding valuable treasures together. Customize your look and hit the road. But make sure you work together on a plan before the auctions start! Because when it's time to pay your bills, your company shares a bank account and only the most capable bidder teams will succeed. Take part in auctions together or share the work by visiting multiple auctions at the same time to maximize your profits. You can also enjoy a fun experience together at the amusement park and invest your hard-earned cash and unlock some unique items while playing fun mini-games.
