View Community Hub About This Game STREETOIR is a first-person / open-world / graffiti-centric / sandbox game, where you paint everywhere you want and whatever you want. Roam a small city or take you time painting and hiding from workers on train-yard, or even try becoming subway bomber star. Try to complete side-quests or just have fun interactions with other characters and items. You also can try to master your painting skills, because game offers a huge palette and choice of tools and spray paint caps. FEATURES Huge palette of colors Different painting tools, starting from graffiti spray-paint ending with fire extinguisher Several maps, so you can paint graffiti not only in city, but also in metro A lot of interactions on the map A lot of side-quests so you can interact with NPC Freedom of action, do whatever you want and break the rules! Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Drug and alcohol use, and mild cartoon violence.
