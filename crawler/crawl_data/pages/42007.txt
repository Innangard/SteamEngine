You will face waves of enemies, challenge the elite, and stand against strong Bosses. As a rebel, you will use marble pinball as your weapon. Associate with new companions, unlock new marbles, then further enhance your marbles with your loot. All your foes shall perish! In Lonkata, you will challenge the endless army of Plutocracy alone, but fear not for the powerful marbles in your hands will treat them! Marble Pinball will bounce between objects and enemies on the battlefield. You will charge your energy bar after kills. Once full, you can summon the helper Diting to re-bounce all your marbles. You are not alone in your journey. Different companions will bring you new marbles after their special events. Build your own combo-play with different marbles! You can also collect resources from fights to have mega-evolution to your marbles——from a mere fireball to extreme giga explosion! There are over 20 marbles and 100 upgrades in the game , providing infinite possibilities for your adventure! Every region in Lonkata has its warden Boss. They have their identical skills, which requires adaptable use of your marbles. Ice for slow and Fire for ignite, don't forget the Regen marble that can heal your HP. Exploit your surroundings with flexible positioning. Terminate all threats! The story happens in a Ultra-Capitalism city called Lonkata, where the legend of "Six Lights Befall, Sukhavati Appraoch" has been going around. You will play as Jory who seems to have serendipity with the legend. After encountering the mythical creature Ting, Jory decides to help it find clues "She" had left, and discovers the hidden truth about Lokanta.
