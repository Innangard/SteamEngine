Every run demands your full attention and sharp reflexes as you spin the inner ring and strategically tweak the pachinko to rack up even bigger scores. After a catastrophe that has loomed over a village for generations, it’s up to you to help rebuild it. Using the power of the pachinko, you’ll generate money, unlock upgrades, and slowly restore the world around you. This demo showcases a small portion of the full game, featuring the first level along with a selection of starter balls, pins, and upgrades to experiment with. Expect around 40–60 minutes of gameplay. Core Gameplay Rotate the barrier to guide balls toward the void and rack up points. After each round, invest your earnings to buy new balls, special pins, workers, and upgrades to boost your chances to earn more points during the next run. Features Build your own pachinko! Place pins strategically on the board to create powerful scoring setups. Unlock a vast skill tree to strengthen your runs and discover new synergies. Different levels with unique mechanics to keep the challenge fresh. Rebuild the village and watch the environment slowly recover from disaster as your progress unfolds.
