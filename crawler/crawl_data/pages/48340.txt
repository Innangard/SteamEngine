You and other AI artists are collaborating for a new exhibition… But one of you is an impostor that doesn't know the exhibition theme. Find out who the impostor is. Or if it's you, fake it 'til you make it! Simple Rules - A theme is chosen at random. - The theme is shown to the real artists, but not to the impostor! - Each player uses the AI to make drawings and shows them to the other players. - Finally, players vote on who is the impostor. - If the impostor is caught, the artists win! - BUT if the impostor can guess the theme, then the impostor wins!! Groundbreaking Gameplay Have you ever tried a game where you can play with AI-generated art? Now is your chance to try it out! No Need For Artistic Talent All you need to do is to give the AI a few words or a short sentence related to the theme. Then the AI will generate completely unique art for you. You don't need to have aesthetic taste or an artistic background. Sometimes AI-generated arts look weird and strange, but that's a form of art, too. Cross-Play with Other Players You can play this game with strangers around the world or with your friends via Room ID. Your friends don't have a computer? No problem. You can play this game on a smartphone too. Short Game Time It only takes around 5 minutes to play one match. Play anytime! You can always sneak in one more round. Low System Requirement This game doesn't require an expensive high-spec computer to play. You may be able to play this game on an old computer stored in a closet. Customizability Choose avatars and styles to stand out in the game! ※There are two types of items that can be purchased with the in-game currency and the ones purchasable with the In-App Purchase currency. ※Currently, the Steam edition doesn't support In-App Purchase and we'll add this feature in a future update.
