You know something is wrong. The risk of a nuclear war is in the air and you need to get ready for what's coming. Struggle for your freedom and deal with the authoritarian government in this survival crafting game. - Build an underground shelter ; - Craft machines and facilities that can keep you alive: farms, greenhouses, tools, vehicles, etc.; - Trade with neighbors, give them what they need, get what is useful to you; - Explore atmospheric locations, scavenge materials and items you could use; - Make a plan – something radical needs to be done; - Meet other rebellious characters, complete quests for them and help each other in the opposition to the regime; - Outsmart the secret police – they don't want you to be prepared, they want you to stay controlled. Mr. Prepper is a guy living in a country which once had been the land of the free, but its new government has changed everything. Obey, stay in your lane, and love the President – otherwise, the secretive and all-powerful Agency will take interest in you. Mr. Prepper has decided to defy this and get out of this delusion of life. The only way for him to hide his plans and doings is to build an underground shelter and construct a machine that will help him get out. But for that, he will need materials, time, and secrecy. Help him succeed and break the status quo.
