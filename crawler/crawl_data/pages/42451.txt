View Community Hub About This Game The Game Croakoloco is an idle game where you collect frogs! Buy frog packs in the shop to discover all the different types of frogs from common to epic. Sit back and watch your frogs jump around the world or take control of your favorite croaker to hunt down special flies for more coins and xp! Key Features Collect Frogs - There are all kinds of frogs to discover. Save up your froggy money to buy all the packs for a chance to see them all. Froggy Mode - In Froggy Mode you will take control of a frog in the world. This will let you croak, jump, and eat golden flies that only appear in this mode. Feed the Frogs - Spawn flies for the frogs to eat making them grow faster. Upgrades & Progression - When a frog becomes an adult they will receive random bonuses. These bonuses can help shape the play style you are looking for. Want to play more active? Gather frogs that make golden flies worth more money. Want to sit back and relax? Having a few frogs with a high chance to burp flies will automate the fly spawning for you! Frogs purchased from more expensive packs will give better bonuses. Shiny Hunting - Want to be the ultimate collector? Don't just find all the frogs, find all the frogs with an epic quality rating. Breeding - Once breeding is unlocked, frogs can be selected to mate. After some time a new frog will be born into the tank! The baby frog will receive the highest rarity from it's parent, as well as one of the bonuses from their parent, with a chance to be more powerful! Prestige - Once you have found all the frogs, you will have the option to prestige. In Croakoloco, with each prestige you will earn money faster and find higher quality frogs.
