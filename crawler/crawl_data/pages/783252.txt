More content awaits you in the full game including 100+ plants, orders and shop expansion. Step into the role of a plant shop owner. Grow plants, serve your customers, and create a warm, inviting space filled with greenery and charm. Watch your plants grow over time, unfolding leaf by leaf as you care for them. What Leafy Corner does NOT offer: Hardcore management (deep economy, hiring staff). Advanced plant care. Storyline. We are working hard and pouring our hearts into making Leafy Corner.
