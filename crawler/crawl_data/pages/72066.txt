Content For This Game Browse all (2) New Rogue Point - Supporter DLC $3.99 Rogue Point Soundtrack $3.99 $7.98 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game From the creators of Black Mesa: When the richest CEO on Earth dies, cutthroat conglomerates seek to carve up his kingdom. Using the MERX app, rival tycoons can order private mercenary armies as easily as takeout, making them perfect soldiers for the corporate war! Only Rogue Point, an elite vigilante squad, dares to fight back. Dive into an adrenaline-fueled campaign where explosive co-op firefights meet strategic planning. Team up, gather intel, complete objectives, and battle through dynamic missions. Earn skills and upgrade weapons to confront the 5 Star Mercenaries, lethal contractors who will test the limits of your team. Fight and maneuver your way through missions spread across 5 diverse locations: The Airport The Mall The Office The Oilrig The Clinic Each sprawling map is reshaped by the game's Parametric Design System, offering new strategies and challenges with every play. The constantly evolving gameplay ensures every mission feels as thrilling and unique as your first, keeping the excitement alive in every campaign. Each MERX class has their own AI playstyle that packs a punch in their own unique way. Soldier Not to be underestimated, the soldier is the workhorse of the MERX squads. Soldiers will utilize their environment and work with other squad units to challenge Rogue Point head-on. Berserker Juiced up on substances and with no regard for their own life, the Berserker's singular goal is to close the distance and slice your whole team with a machete.  They are agents of chaos and will wreck your carefully crafted game plan. Sniper Heavily fortified in the far distance, snipers will pin your team down and deny you entry into important areas.  Their only tell is the laser beam from their gun, and your teammate’s head exploding next to you. Lookout Quick-footed and fast-talking, the Lookout disrupts your squad with flashbangs and relentless hit-and-run tactics. Heavy Heavies are big, slow brutes that don’t take cover and eat bullets.  You can dump all your ammo into them with no effect.  Run the other way, or find their one weakness. 5-Star Mercenaries These legendary MERX have received the top-tier 5-star rating in the app. They should be approached with caution and treated as extremely dangerous to you and your team. Intel on these MERX is sketchy, but we know that these endgame encounters will require your team’s full strength to have a chance to take them down. Rogue Point is easy to learn and hard to master, perfect for both fresh recruits and seasoned veterans. Crave fast-paced action? How about slow, methodical, skill-driven gameplay? Rogue Point kicks open the door to both those worlds and invites players to choose their own playstyle. Earn your keep on the frontlines to purchase new hardware for your arsenal. Start with nothing more than a pistol and an armored vest, then earn an elite arsenal through successful missions and strategic teamplay. Upgrade your loadout with an assortment of attachments and gear to get out alive. Own the battlefield on your terms. Work together to battle against the MERX threat. Rogue Point is purpose-built to reinforce teamplay and pull players into combat. Plan, Adapt, and Overcome the odds by building your unique Rogue. Earn money to buy weapons and gear.
