About This Game Immerse yourself in the Victorian Era! Develop quaint settlements into bustling cities and build industrial complexes surrounded by smoke and smog. Shape your cities by balancing technological progress with the needs of your population. Build attractive cities rich in leisure and culture, ranging from local pubs to exotic zoos. Balance social peace and economic efficiency to ensure the well-being of your residents. Optimize supply chains and urban planning, and take on the challenge of using electricity and steam engines to your advantage. Every choice shapes your legacy. Will you prioritize factories' output or workers' happiness? Will your cities drive progress or draw crowds? Navigate these tensions as you collaborate with the press and launch daring expeditions. Build a trading network across distant lands to sustain your ever-growing population.
