Dusty is... that crate over there. Probably. They're both cats. Every match, up to 8 players split into two teams however they like: the Mangos orange cats, armed, angry, and on the clock and the Dustys , grey cats who can look at any object on the map, press a button, and become it . A crate, a hay bale, a candy cane, a barrel. If you can see it, you can be it. BE THE SCENERY Playing Dusty? Pick your spot carefully. A crate in a warehouse corner? Invisible. A crate floating in mid-air because you panicked? That's on you. Nudge into position, settle in, and don't move a pixel. One small problem: you're still a cat under there, and the crate keeps your eyes . They blink on a timer you can watch counting down and cannot stop. When it hits zero, you make a sound. A small one. Just loud enough for a Mango standing nearby. SNIFF OUT THE FAKES Playing Mango? Everything is a suspect. Was that barrel there before? Why are there three chairs and one of them is slightly crooked? Trust nothing, listen for a blink you weren't supposed to hear, and clear the map before the timer runs out. GRAB THE FISH The Dustys can't just hide forever. Blue fish are scattered across the map, and collecting them all wins the round. There's always one more fish than there are Dustys: three hiders, four fish. Do the math. Somebody has to leave cover twice. Every fish run means abandoning your perfect hiding spot and waddling across open ground as a very suspicious piece of furniture. PLAY TOGETHER Host a room for up to 8 players, invite your friends, and split the teams however you want — one Mango against seven crates, an even 4v4, or anything in between. Swap sides every match, and friends joining mid-game hop in as spectators and jump into the next round automatically.
