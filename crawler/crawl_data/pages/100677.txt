to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Ghost Hunters is a co-op horror game. In which, you play as one of 4 ghostbusters. You choose the contract/house you want to go to with your group, or alone.The company you work for gives you a list of tasks, for each task you will receive money and experience as a ghost hunter.For this money you can buy equipment.But, all the equipment is not immediately available to you, in order to gain access to a larger list of things, you must successfully complete tasks on contracts. Play together with friends, or with random dudes (Up to 4 people). Complete tasks together, earn money and exp together. Lots of different maps. Various tasks. Lots of equipment.
