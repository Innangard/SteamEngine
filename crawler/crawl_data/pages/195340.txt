Listen to The Caribou Trail Soundtrack Free Play Soundtrack View Community Hub About This Content The Caribou Trail Original Soundtrack invites you back to the Gallipoli campaign through a collection of atmospheric and emotional compositions from the game. Blending orchestral arrangements, intimate piano pieces, and haunting ambient soundscapes, the soundtrack accompanies Fisher and his fellow Newfoundlanders on their journey through friendship, survival, and remembrance. Drawing inspiration from Newfoundland's rich folk music traditions, its warm sense of community, and the harsh realities of war, each track captures the emotional heart of The Caribou Trail . Whether you've already experienced the story or simply enjoy cinematic game music, this soundtrack offers a chance to revisit the world of The Caribou Trail long after the journey ends.
