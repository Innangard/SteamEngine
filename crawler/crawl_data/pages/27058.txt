It provides a comprehensive set of common tools, so that you can focus on making games without having to reinvent the wheel. Games can be exported with one click to a number of platforms, including the major desktop platforms (Linux, macOS, Windows), mobile platforms (Android, iOS), as well as Web-based platforms and consoles. Free, open source and community-driven Godot is completely free and open source under the very permissive MIT license. No strings attached, no royalties, nothing.
