Seclusa is a blend of casual photography simulator and cozy idle game where you can play as actively or passively as you want. Each area has been handcrafted to be highly ambient and immersive, whether you're photographing the local wildlife or relaxing by the fire as the sun sets. Snap Photos Get your camera ready for a variety of East African wildlife as you take on photography jobs for conservation groups. As you level up, you'll unlock more animals and locations, as well as camera upgrades that can completely change the look and feel of your photos. Gain Experience & Level Up Fulfill daily tasks, complete objectives, or turn on the radio and watch the day go by as nature lives on around you. Leveling up earns you credits which can be used to improve your camera, purchase amenities at the lodge, build up your card collection, and even unlock the power to change the weather and time of day on a whim. Photography Sandbox With fully unlocked abilities, you'll have the freedom to change the time of day and weather, tweak the processing to fit the mood, and call on a specific animal species to be your subject. Relax & Unwind Seclusa is designed to be a stress-free, predominantly point & click experience, complete with immersive African soundscapes and an original soundtrack. Snap photos, work on your card collection, and get cozy with a jigsaw puzzle. You're on safari, enjoy it!
