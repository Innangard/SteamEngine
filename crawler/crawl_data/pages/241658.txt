About This Game Fujii is a meditative and magical journey through dreamlike landscapes filled with light and wonder. An adorable gnome companion welcomes you home. It’s the beautiful garden of your dreams, ready to come to life through the mystical power that lies at your fingertips. Rejuvenate your soul as you affectionately nurture bountiful seeds, solve gentle puzzles, and explore otherworldly biomes eager to surprise and delight you. Take a breath, allow yourself to forget the troubles of the real world, and bask in this serene and uplifting experience about caring and cultivating. 🌿 FEATURES 🌿 Explore three organic biomes filled with magic, enchanted flowers, and beautiful plants Nurture your own unique garden by collecting seeds to harvest colorful landscapes Introduce gentle critters to your garden and solve puzzles with your adorable gnome companion by your side Embark on a peaceful and whimsical adventure set to a meditative soundscape composed by French artist and composer Norman Bambi System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 64-bit Processor: Intel Core i5-4590 (AMD FX 8350) or better Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: Nvidia GTX 970 4GB or higher DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 400 MB available space VR Support: SteamVR.
