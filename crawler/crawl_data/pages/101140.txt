A cluster of cute ball characters spins in the middle of your screen, and every one of them is watching your shot. Aim, bounce your ball off the edges of your desktop and hit the cluster. Heavy ones hit harder and spin the cluster faster. Chain pops into combos, with juicy effects and sounds that feel great. Picture levels: every few levels the cluster becomes a picture made of colors, like a rainbow, a flower or a tiny planet. Reward levels: beat your own record and a surprise level appears. Pop it all with a single shot. Keep the pops coming: 11 shots in a row without a pop ends your game. Launch from the bottom, the right or the top center, and switch any time.
