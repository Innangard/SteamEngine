Set out on a path of a ghost-hunting renovator. Your task - restore haunted houses to a usable state! This is not an easy job, applying a new wallpaper won't be enough to render a house safe. You also need to deal with whatever is haunting it. Accept requests to renovate haunted houses from various eccentric owners. Scout abandoned mansions and look for signs of any paranormal activity. Make sure you're ready for the job by preparing a set of tools to aid your work. Chase away ghosts by any means possible. Sometimes your job will be as easy as catching an entity with a net and sometimes you will need to draw elaborate magic circles and perform sinister rituals. And if nothing works, you can always look for an alternative way to solve your issue - regular hauntings have two ways of getting rid of them, nice and mean. Choose your playstyle and earn different rewards based on your choices. You can even adopt certain hauntings! Looks are important, especially when convincing your customers that a house is safe. Change wallpapers, paint the walls, and replace furniture. Find a passion for interior decorations as you do what you can to keep a house from looking haunted. Features First-person paranormal renovator simulator with magical tools and emphasis on banishing hauntings Spooky, not scary - it's all about the atmosphere rather than actual fear Different haunted houses and flats, each with its own hauntings and a story to tell 4 different endings based on the choices you make Help for inexperienced players - press the middle mouse button to automatically equip the right tool or use the Camera to photograph hauntings and learn how to deal with them A large selection of interior decorations Investigation element when dealing with Wraiths - major hauntings Two ways of solving every minor haunting - a nice way and a mean way Partner hauntings that can join you and help you with new contracts Expansive upgrade system for your tools and abilities Realistic, moody graphics Singleplayer campaign mode with story elements System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10, 11 - 64bit Processor: Intel Core i5-10300H Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: GeForce 1650 4 GB VRAM DirectX: Version 12 Storage: 8 GB available space Additional Notes: The game also runs on some older GPUs like GeForce 1050 4 GB vram with an average 30fps on low settings Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10, 11 - 64bit Processor: Intel Core i5-9600KF Memory: 32 GB RAM Graphics: RTX 2060 SUPER View all What Curators Say 22 Curators have reviewed this product.
