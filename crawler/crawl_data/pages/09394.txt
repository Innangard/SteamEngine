ROLL DUNG, SCORE GOALS What do you get when you combine up to 12 players at once, flashy beetle abilities, and a ton of dung balls? The greatest dung beetle soccer game ever made. Probably. TEAM UP Teamplay and creativity matters just as much as mechanics. Experiment with beetle class team comps to climb ranks, or just mess around with friends in custom lobbies. USE CRAZY ABILITIES Teleport from ball to ball with the Tiger Beetle, create mushroom launchpads as the Fungus Beetle, shoot ladybugs out of the air with the Cyborg Beetle's laser sniper, and much more! Cyborg beetles are real, ok? COMPETE FOR DUNG Merge many balls into one GOLDEN DUNGBALL to score. Multiple balls in play adds a new layer of team strategy beyond standard one ball soccer. PLAY IN ACTUAL MAPS Glide from dandelions to donuts, roll dungballs up cakes (gross), and headbutt enemies off of sandcastles. Beetleball's unique map design offers much more variety than standard FLAT and BORING soccer fields. CLIMB THE RANKS Outsmart enemy beetles with class synergies, map tricks, and momentum plays to become the biggest and best beetle baller to ever roll dung. BECOME BEETLE-FUL Have you ever seen a beetle with a hat on? Now you have. Isn't it cute? Level up, get beetle bucks, and blow them all on beetle eggs for a chance at mythical cosmetics. FEATURES Beetle soccer Up to 6v6 Easy to get good, hard to master Momentum-based movement Multiple balls at once Proximity voice chat 14+ unique classes 8+ unique maps Ranked + unranked matchmaking Alternate gamemodes Custom lobbies Beetle hats!
