Buy War of Dots Plus $14.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content About War of Dots Plus War of Dots Plus is, at its heart, a donation. If you've enjoyed the game and want to see it keep growing, this is a direct way to make that happen. The perks are my way of saying thank you. War of Dots is an indie game built out of personal passion. It's not a studio, not a business. It's a hobby project that I pour my spare time, energy, and personal funds into. The game is completely free and always will be. No ads, no paywalls, no monetization tricks. If you want to support it, this pack is how. What's Included Once you own the pack, all features are accessible through the heart button in the game's menu. Here's what you'll find inside: High-Quality Textures A complete visual upgrade with high-resolution textures, giving the battlefield a crisper and more detailed look than the base game. Historic Maps: Operation Barbarossa I recreated some of the most significant battles of Operation Barbarossa as War of Dots maps. You can replay the key engagements of the Eastern Front and see how history might have unfolded differently under your command. Experimental Shaders and Visual Effects A growing collection of creative visual modes to experience War of Dots in entirely new ways, including Pixel Mode, a retro-pixelated take on the battlefield; the Dizzy Effect, a disorienting trippy visual twist; and more experimental effects to discover. Some features included in this supporter pack may eventually be moved to the free version of War of Dots after receiving significant rework and polish. That said, I am committed to continuously adding new content to this pack to make sure supporters always have something to show for their generosity. Thank you for believing in this project.
