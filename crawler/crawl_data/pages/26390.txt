T-44 comes into its own when mobility and initiative matter most. This agile medium tank is well suited for flanking attacks, quickly securing advantageous positions, and supporting an advance. The M53/59 provides the team with protection against enemy aircraft. This mobile SPAAG can accompany the main force, quickly relocate along with it, and meet incoming aircraft with a barrage of automatic cannon fire. This version features a distinctive appearance, with camouflage netting, chains on the wheels, and a spare wheel mounted on the hull. Three vehicles offer three different approaches to battle.
