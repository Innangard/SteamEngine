Including the Tupolev Tu-114, the world’s fastest and most powerful commercial prop aircraft, the Airport CEO Beast of the East DLC contains a total of four different aircraft models significant to the eastern aviation history. The following eastern aircraft models are included in the DLC: Tupolev Tu-104 Tupolev Tu-114 Antonov An-148 Tupolev Tu-154 The eastern aircraft are brought to life in Airport CEO complete with stunning, authentic and accurate graphics and are accompanied by two new eastern airlines. Downloading this DLC will add the eastern aircraft to the new eastern airlines Yuri Air and Сибирские авиалинии (Siberian Airways). The eastern aircraft can, just like any other aircraft in Airport CEO, be modded with custom liveries using the official MDK. In order to traffic the eastern aircraft you will need to sign contracts with the eastern airlines Yuri Air and Сибирские авиалинии (Siberian Airways).
