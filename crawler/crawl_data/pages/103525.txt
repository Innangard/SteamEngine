View Community Hub About This Game Ragnar Lothbrok, once a legendary Viking hero, has been summoned back to life by Odin the All-Father. In his previous life, Ragnar fought valiantly, yet his soul was tainted by misdeeds, and his spirit was forced to wander in the cold mists of Hel’s domain, deprived of Valhalla's glory. But Odin saw something of value in Ragnar’s unyielding spirit. And now, the All-Father has granted him a second chance - not only to redeem himself but to serve the gods in a quest that spans realms few have ever seen. Our hero embarks on a mysterious journey to recover powerful artifacts scattered by ancient forces. Ragnar's quest leads him through enchanted lands, filled with magic, illusions, and strange creatures, his path lit only by glimpses of fate and the weight of his own redemption. The hero begins his quest at the edge of the Enchanted Forest, where the trees loom tall and whisper with ancient magic. The forest is filled with hidden treasures - powerful and mysterious artifacts that are either buried deep within the forest or guarded by magical creatures. On his quest Ragnar encounters powerful foes - evil trolls and fearsome beasts that lurk in the shadows, cunning sorcerers, ancient guardians and even mighty dragons. Every step brings our hero closer to redeeming his soul and finally claiming his rightful place among the honored warriors in Valhalla. For Ragnar, this journey is more than a quest; it is a rite of passage, a final chance to rewrite his fate and reclaim the honor he lost.
