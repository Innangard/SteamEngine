"Invokyr is a cooperative horror game built around unpredictable board games, changing rules, monsters, events, and chaotic dice. With so many systems interacting with each other, player feedback is incredibly valuable to us." Por quanto tempo aproximadamente este jogo estará em acesso antecipado? "We currently expect Invokyr to remain in Early Access for approximately one year. Throughout Early Access, we plan to release regular updates around new themes, with new monsters, events, items and features. This timeframe may change depending on the development process and what we learn from players." Como a versão completa será diferente da versão de acesso antecipado? "Our goal for the 1.0 version is to significantly expand the variety and depth of Invokyr. We plan to add new boards and themes, monsters, events, dice, cursed rules, items, upgrades. We also plan to expand the narrative mysteries surrounding Invokyr and the Game Master, and continue improving replayability based on what we learn during Early Access." Qual é o estado atual da versão de acesso antecipado? "At Early Access launch, Invokyr will already offer a complete multiplayer gameplay loop for 1 to 6 players. Players will be able to explore several themed boards, survive more than 15 monsters and 20 events, discover and use more than a dozen different dice and cursed idols, purchase upgrades and equipment, and face increasingly dangerous combinations of rules as they progress toward the ultimate Invokyr Board. The game also includes progression between boards, a shop, environmental secrets, and a rule system designed to make each game unfold differently." O preço do jogo será alterado após o fim do acesso antecipado? "We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features." Como vocês planejam envolver a comunidade durante o processo de desenvolvimento? "Community feedback has already played an important role in the development of Invokyr through our demos and playtests, and we want that relationship to continue throughout Early Access. We’ll actively follow player feedback through Steam discussions, Discord, playtests, surveys, reviews, and direct community discussions. Feedback will help us improve balancing, difficulty, onboarding, replayability, and existing systems, but also identify which monsters, events, rules, dice, and unexpected multiplayer situations players enjoy the most. The Game Master may control the board, but the community will help shape what Invokyr becomes."
