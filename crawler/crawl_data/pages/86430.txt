Content For This Game Browse all (1) Recommended BLACKWOOD: Supporter Edition $4.99 $4.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub Supporter Edition Available About This Game Step into the world of BLACKWOOD , a gritty third-person action thriller that blends smooth, responsive combat with cinematic storytelling. By day, you are a seemingly ordinary store owner, managing your small DVD shop in an authentic recreation of New York, 2012. When night falls, you descend into the city’s underworld as a contract killer, caught between survival, morality, and a past that refuses to stay buried. One Epic Story Told In Three Parts. Anton's story takes place over the course of three Acts. Each Act is a complete part of the overall story, with new missions added in between the major acts. Current Act Roadmap: Act 1: September 16, 2026 Act 2: March 2027 Act 3: September 2027 When you purchase BLACKWOOD, you are purchasing the full game. All future acts will be provided as free updates to existing owners. If you buy BLACKWOOD at launch, you will not need to purchase Act 2 or Act 3 separately. Act One Coming September 16, 2026. FIGHT YOUR WAY THROUGH THE CITY Gunplay is fast, stylish, and unforgiving. Keep the Action relentless with fluid movement options like dives, rolls, and slides. Every encounter feels cinematic yet responsive, giving you the freedom to create your own moments of chaos. MASTER BRUTAL TAKEDOWNS BLACKWOOD’s signature mechanic: countless contextual takedowns. From one-on-one hand-to-hand executions to using the environment around you, every fight is an opportunity to end your enemies with precision and style. Grounded, weighty, and satisfying to master. A DOUBLE LIFE TO BALANCE Your store is more than just a front. It's where you live, decorate, and roleplay a “normal” existence. Buy and sell DVDs, change outfits, interact with your apartment, and explore RPG elements that make your dual identity feel tangible. The contrast between day and night defines the world and the choices you make. CINEMATIC STORYTELLING BLACKWOOD introduces a cast of flawed, human characters navigating a violent city full of secrets. Every ad, tattoo, and poster tells a story. Every mission pulls you deeper into a web of conspiracies and consequences. STORE MANAGEMENT Take control of your DVD shop and decide how hands-on you want to be. Restock shelves, organize inventory, set prices, and jump in at the counter to keep profits high. Let it run on autopilot, and you will get by.
