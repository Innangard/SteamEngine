View Community Hub Reviews “It isn’t often that a game makes you want to grab a book to spend more time with the characters but this game succeeded” 10 – TechTudo “This is a classic adventure game from the Brazilian studio Mother Gaia” 8 – Gamehall “Mother Gaia created something that only someone truly passionate about what they do could have made” 80 – Adrenaline About This Game Master the Time Loop: You have one day to solve the crime. Learn the schedules of 14 unique characters, spot their contradictions, and manipulate their routines. Keep your knowledge when the day resets and stay one step ahead of the killer. No hand-holding. Use your "Thinking Board" to link evidence, reconstruct the timeline, and accuse the culprit. Only pure logic will free you from the loop. A Living Literary World: Experience a satirical, witty, and dark adaptation of Brazil’s legendary author, Machado de Assis. Your investigation takes you through a hand-drawn 1937 Rio de Janeiro that drips with style, sarcasm, and atmosphere. There is no single correct answer: every accusation has its logic, and the truth is yours to define. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: The Posthumous Investigation features discussions about murder, death, mental and physical illness and incorrect forms of treatment. Some characters drink and smoke. It's not graphic, but not recommended to children nonetheless.
