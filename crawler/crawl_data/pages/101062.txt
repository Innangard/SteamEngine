Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Survive the Tide of Infected Endless waves of infected surge forward. The disaster struck without warning and gave no one time to prepare. Polandball: The Sixth Day - Destroy is a single-player, top-down endless survival shooter. Choose a character, collect weapons, strengthen your abilities, and recruit AI-controlled mercenaries. Adapt to a changing battlefield, build firepower for yourself and your squad, and face increasingly dangerous infected and bosses. The environment tells the story of a disaster growing worse: society clings to order, then finally collapses. Will the protagonist escape through the same old plot armor, or become just another passerby making a last stand? The answer may lie in every build and every choice you make. From One Fighter to a Full Squad You may begin with one character and limited equipment. As the battle continues, recruit mercenaries, arm them, and combine different ranges, rates of fire, and damage types. They acquire targets, fire, and reload beside you, turning an isolated fight into a defensive line of gunfire and explosions. Find Weapons and Shape Your Build Blades, pistols, rifles, shotguns, machine guns, sniper rifles, RPGs, and special equipment can all become part of your build. Defeat enemies, earn experience, choose upgrades, and resupply between fights. The battlefield changes by the moment; turning limited resources into effective firepower depends on your judgment. Survival, Collection, and Lasting Progress Alongside roguelite upgrades and equipment builds, the game includes survival and light extraction elements. You must consider not only the fight in front of you, but also how to use what you find, making rare equipment and collectibles part of progress beyond a single run. Fight from Daylight into the Night Time does not stop for battle. Daylight fades into dusk, city lights come alive, and muzzle flashes and explosions briefly illuminate the battlefield. Fast infected, mutants, spawning creatures, and enormous bosses constantly change the pressure. The early waves are only the beginning; the real challenge has no end. Create Your Own Fictional Ball Alongside the existing cast, you can create your own fictional ball, customize its appearance and signature weapon, and send it into battle. The game also provides a local mod interface for visual, content, and script extensions. What it ultimately becomes is for creators and players to write together. A Story Woven into the Day-Night Cycle The game is adapted from an original animation by the developer. Its story emerges through the changing day-night cycle and the battles themselves. You do not need to know the animation to play; you may focus on combat or slowly uncover the characters and the world. Frequently Asked Questions Q: How is this different from a traditional roguelite? A: Alongside upgrades and equipment builds, it adds survival and light extraction elements. You must weigh immediate survival against long-term collection. A completed build can feel exhilarating, while a rare find can bring an entirely different kind of excitement. Q: Does the game have a story? A: Yes. It is adapted from an animation I created, but the story never overwhelms the main play. It quietly unfolds with the day-night cycle. Follow its traces, or focus entirely on fighting the horde. Q: What special systems does the game have? A: A storage system lets equipment collection continue beyond one battle. Eligible items can be stored as a lasting collection. Decide whether to use resources now or save them for adventures ahead. Q: What makes the game replayable? A: Characters, weapons, upgrades, and mercenaries can be combined in many ways. You can collect rare equipment and seek unconventional tactics. A local mod interface also supports visual, content, and script extensions. Q: Can the translations feel unnatural or difficult to understand? A: They can. Most localized text currently relies on machine translation, so some wording may be awkward or inaccurate. A solo-development budget cannot cover professional translation for every language, but reports are welcome and the text will continue to be revised. Q: If AI assisted development, why is the game still paid? A: AI was one tool used during development; the planning and artwork were still made independently by me. Turning an idea into a finished game still requires substantial time, effort, and cost, including the cost of the AI tools themselves. Sales help support that work and future updates. Whether it is worth buying should ultimately be answered by the experience it gives you. Q: Where can I watch the original animation? Do I need to see it first? A: The original animation is available on Bilibili. No prior knowledge is required. It offers another path into the setting, not homework you must finish before playing. Q: What if parts of the game still feel unfinished to me? A: This is a work I completed and delivered to the best of my ability as an independent developer. I will not pretend it is beyond criticism. Honest reports are welcome; I will weigh their impact, fit with the game, and what I can realistically improve.
