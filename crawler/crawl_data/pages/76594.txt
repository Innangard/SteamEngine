The available fashion packs at this moment are 1560s, 1680s, 1780s, 1820s, 1880s and 1920s. With more to come! Every garment is researched and based on museum collections and contemporary art, offering an authentic experience of each era's style. You can also read more about historical fashion in our info menu. Customize the appearance of your characters. Experiment with facial features, hairstyles, accessories, and more to bring your character to life. Compose your own scenes using characters and backgrounds from across all fashion packs. Add decorative stickers to tell your own stories. Save screenshots of your favorite characters and outfits. Share your creations with friends or use them as inspiration for your next design. Bring your digital creations to life by printing out your characters as charming paper dolls. Print in color, or color the dolls yourself! Fill your wardrobe with all your favorite outfits and save them for later. This game is designed for those seeking a relaxing experience while also learning a thing or two about historical fashion. Or for all the fashion enthusiasts out there wanting to dive into the details of historical fashion. Unleash your inner fashion designer and relive the grace and style of past fashions. Whether you're a history enthusiast or a fashion lover, this is where you can create, explore, and appreciate fashion throughout history!
