It's Chopping Time!” Wallacetaa About This Demo This is a 100% hand-drawn game by Alexandre Mailliu! It's Chopping Time! Demo is a short incremental game about chopping down trees on a secluded island by using your mouse and the lightning strikes! In this demo you'll only have access to the lightning strikes, which are overpowered on purpose to give you a taste of the mighty endgame unlimited power! But this is just one of the 5 forces of nature that will be featured in the final game! This demo is about 20-30minutes and is meant to give you a taste of the full power of the final game! Please don't hesitate to give me your feedback with a Steam review as it's the best way for me to enhance my work and learn from my mistakes, and to make the best final full game version in the end! I hope you'll enjoy my Demo! CHOP DOWN THOSE TREES! There's no need to click, simply hover your cursor over the trees to chop them automatically. ZEUS WOULD BE JEALOUS! Use the lightning strikes by hovering a spark when it appears on top of a tree. BUY UPGRADES IN THE SKILL TREE! Spend your money and colored wood logs to unlock permanent upgrades in the skill tree.
