Firepower. The FV201 (A45) has a DPM of 2,093 HP/min and a quick reload time, which lets you make great use of any mistakes your opponents make on the battlefield. You will be especially effective when facing more cumbersome and slow-shooting enemies, managing to send a couple of shells their way before they can respond or fall back. As obvious as it may sound, make sure to aim for the weakest spots in their armor because your gun doesn’t boast outstanding penetration values. Remember that you have quite a lot of shells in your ammo rack, so doing blind shots is also a viable option. Protection. The FV201 (A45) is among the top three Tier VII heavies with the biggest HP pools, but don’t let that make you too careless in the heat of battle. Your turret armor is good, but you need to hide your hull or else your 1,500 HP will run out quite quickly. Keep in mind that your alpha damage and armor is not the best for trading shots, especially if you’re facing a hard-hitting opponent. The FV201 (A45) was not designed for close-quarters combat or sitting under heavy fire. However, its good view range and fast-shooting gun lets you reliably put pressure on your enemies. Utilize the terrain to cover your hull and bounce opponents’ shots off your turret, all while constantly raining your own shells down on the enemy team.
