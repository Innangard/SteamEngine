About This Game Blast Head is a meta progression-focused bullet heaven roguelite where the screen quickly transforms into a colorful kaleidoscope of bullets, explosions, and increasingly bizarre weapons. Select your loadout, progress each weapon on its own skill tree, unlock passive and active skills and build mighty synergies on the fly. Every run is an experiment! Over 10 unique, absurd weapons – each with its own upgrade tree featuring 10–15 skills to unlock. This isn’t just about pumping stats – it’s about shaping your playstyle. Turn a simple slingshot into a weapon of mass destruction, or build a setup that drags enemies into a spiral of chaos. Weapon customization - Weapons can be mixed, upgraded, and experimented with to discover surprising synergies that can clear the screen in an instant. A slingshot, a flip-flop, an acid tank, chaos orbs, or even a bow of love - go crazy! Mechanics-modifying runes - Want the game to play itself? Turn on auto-fire and focus on dodging. Craving risk? Increase enemy density for bigger rewards. Want to force special events? Runes let you bend the core rules of the game – ensuring each run feels fresh and different. Fast, Intense gameplay sessions - You don’t need to commit an entire evening – one run lasts 10–30 minutes, but the tension never lets up for a second. Pure action, as the screen turns into a kaleidoscope of bullets, effects, and enemies. Menacing enemies, huge bosses - At first, it’s just small critters you smash to pieces. But every passing minute makes things worse: bigger swarms, nastier enemies, and bosses that demand creativity – and sometimes desperation. Every mistake costs your life, and every victory tastes twice as sweet. Love bullet heavens with a good twist? You want a game where you'll need to think and plan ahead, not just reap enemies by the hundred? Start BLASTING!
