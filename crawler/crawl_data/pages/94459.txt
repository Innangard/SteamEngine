View Community Hub About This Game BAKUDO: The Ultimate Ball-Combat Sport Beyond Imagination Experience the unique combat system of BAKUDO—an all-new ball game packed with excitement and tension. On a battlefield with no rules or limits, your determination is your greatest weapon. Every competitor stakes it all, pulling out every trick to claim ultimate supremacy. In the BAKUDO arena, fiery determination and unshakeable spirit collide! Main Setting Strength is everything at this elite academy founded specifically for BAKUDO. Here, students fight relentlessly to reach the pinnacle. You'll step into the shoes of Yuria as she enrolls at the academy in search of her missing father, and forge deep bonds with a colorful cast of characters along the journey. However, the academy holds more secrets than it appears, and what awaits our hero is far from simple... When Ball-Sport Combat Meets Action Engage in real-time battles against formidable bosses with a unique, fast-paced combat system centered around dynamic ball-throwing techniques. Observe your opponents, learn their movements, and exploit their weaknesses to seize victory. Are you ready to enter this battlefield fueled by fiery determination? The Eight Stars of Academy Face off against the academy's reigning elites. Known as the Eight Stars, each boasts a unique and formidable combat style. Only by reading their movements and countering at just the right moment can you hope to defeat them.
