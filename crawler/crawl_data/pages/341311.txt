View Community Hub About This Game Unleash brutal justice as Night Jackal , a masked vigilante driven to the edge after a violent assault on his manor leaves his world shattered. All signs point to his arch-nemesis, the elusive Burt the Barbarous—but is there a deeper conspiracy afoot? Built in Easy FPS Editor, this bite-sized boomer shooter throws you into a retro-fueled rampage of precision firepower, relentless enemies, and a protagonist hell-bent on settling his blood debt ! Brains Meet Bullets: Balance raw gunplay with stealth, puzzles, underwater mechanics, and more. Story-Driven Mayhem: Experience this original story of a super hero coming unhinged. Original Artwork: Featuring visuals pulled from the Night Jackal comic, including work by industry veteran Derec Donovan ( Spider-Man, Superman, Wonder Woman ).
