In Sea, Sun & Salt, you build a city on a small mediterranean island. Define the tenets of your culture, deal with your subjects' needs and withstand the forces of the elements. This is the demo version of Sea, Sun & Salt , where you can begin building your civilization and experience several of the early stages of development. It’s the perfect chance to get a taste of the city-building, decision-making, and survival mechanics before diving into the full game. Build Progress through the game and build a magnificent city. The further you go, the more complex your city's needs will get. Survive If you fail your subjects, your city will not last long. Water recedes when times are good but when things go wrong, beware of the rising tide. Decide As your city's population grows, your subjects will look up to you for answers. How was the world created? What do they worship? When's the best time to bathe? Make these choices, and more, to direct your people.
