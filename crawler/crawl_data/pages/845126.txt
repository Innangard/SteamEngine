Simulator 3. Calgary offers a dynamic mix of domestic, transborder, international, and cargo traffic , creating a varied and engaging ATC experience. Manage parallel runway operations, coordinate efficient ground movements, and handle fluctuating traffic levels throughout the day in a modern and spacious airport layout. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). With its wide runway configuration and diverse airline activity, CYYC provides both accessibility for newer players and a rewarding challenge for experienced controllers. From coordinating departures during peak hours to sequencing arrivals in changing conditions, every session requires precision and situational awareness. Take the tower at Calgary and keep traffic flowing smoothly in one of Canada’s busiest and most important airports.
