Slip into the metal shoes of a young, smart robot named Telly. One day, as you are walking to your grandpa’s house, you witness his kidnapping. His garage is left thrashed, his inventions are broken, and all you have is a radio station connecting you to Grandpa. Who did this? What do they want? You must unveil this mystery full of brain-scratching riddles, powerful enemies, and unusual worlds. Connect to different machines and hack their mechanisms by playing classic mini-games wrapped in robot clothes. Complete hundreds of levels in our Arcade room and earn exciting rewards. All bad guys know that a well-placed killer mega bot here and there greatly enhances their chances of executing their plan for world domination. They don’t know that this just makes your journey more challenging and fun! Collect hidden fragments and combine them into artefacts on a cosy table in your very own garage. A neatly crafted artefact is a must when dealing with boss bots! If you must go out there and outwit your enemies, at least do it in style. Customize your robot with hundreds of different combinations! A shark head connected to a jet engine instead of legs makes your journey much more personal.
