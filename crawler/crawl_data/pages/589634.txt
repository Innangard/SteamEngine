Story You are a small, unassuming cleaning bot that desires nothing more than a neat and tidy home. But ever since the planet was dragged closer to the sun by a monomaniacal tech nerd, dust and death swirl across the surface, making for a very messy situation. If your house will ever be clean again, you need to fix the world. A clean world means a clean house. So, fix the world. Simple. All you have to do is descend to the planet’s core, overcome the monomaniacal tech nerd’s wily schemes, and reorder the planet, all the while battling through the unimaginable horrors of the deep. Oh, and one more thing: all you start with is a modified vacuum sweeper. Happy cleaning!
