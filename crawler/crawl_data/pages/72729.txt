View Community Hub About This Game Tame magical creatures called Decklings and lead them into challenging battles in this survival deck-building card game. Explore biomes, gather resources, cook nourishing meals, and craft to build your base as you shape your path to become the Deckling Champion. Discover and tame over 100 unique Decklings , each with their own abilities, stats, and specialties Craft specialized items and lures that can help you attract new Decklings Find and nurture mysterious Eggs until they hatch Cook delicious meals to keep your Decklings well fed, or they’ll run away Hunt for rare ✨ Holo Decklings ✨ Stack cards to perform all functions at your base! Your Decklings work with you to gather resources, plant crops, cook food, and operate your base Set up production lines that let your Decklings automate jobs like farming crops, chopping lumber, cooking meals and much more Unlock new recipes and craft equipment and items to improve your base and give your Decklings powerful bonuses in battle Battle other Tamers in challenging 3v3 card duels , managing Deckling positioning and elemental type match-ups Support your team with powerful buffs, while mitigating debuffs, casting your own back at the opponent Experiment with combinations of Decklings, crafted items, and abilities to find your perfect synergy Level up your Decklings to learn new moves or upgrade existing ones, enhancing their decks Defeat Team Leaders to unlock new islands as you rise through the ranks Every region is packed with cards to discover, resources to gather, recipes to learn, and Decklings to collect Use newly unlocked resources and recipes to expand your production lines, craft specialized items, and prepare for tougher challenges Fulfill the requirements, bring your Deckling to the Altar of Ascension , and witness its evolution Push yourself to the limit in the hardcore “ Survival Mode ”, which deletes your save file if all your creatures run away System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: 2Ghz Memory: 1 GB RAM Graphics: DirectX9 compatible with at least 1GB of memory Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system View all What Curators Say 5 Curators have reviewed this product.
