View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions DEVELOPMENT HIATUS - APRIL 2026 WE'LL BE BACK!! We've recently made the difficult decision to pause development of Die For The Lich. It's painful to see new reviews claiming the game is "abandoned" - but we knew there would be backlash to this announcement, and understand that it feeds into many negative perceptions around Early Access. We have no desire to hide from our fans or ghost anyone who has supported us, and feel it is best to communicate honestly. Early sales came up short of our projections (likely due to the initial Mixed reception) which forced us to reassess our Early Access runway. The options were (1) push out something half-baked and move on, or (2) put things on a hiatus until a new plan can be made. We chose the latter, which is in our mind the much more customer-friendly version. The delays is frustrating, I know. But I am certain that would be preferred over having paid for something that never gets finished. However, the financials were not the only consideration, otherwise this could be resolved by further publisher support. Die For The Lich is made by two brother who don't live in the same city, don't get to see each other in person very often, and felt that their relationship had become only game development after 1.5 years of grinding.  I empathize with their need to recharge, and feel that these two deserve room to be brothers-first for a little while before returning to this project. Whatever the next game is under the Monovoid name, it will likely be done either solo or with a new collaborator. This gives the boys a chance to spend quality time together without drowning in development decisions. They can explore their individual creativity and expand their skills, then come back together stronger for Die For The Lich! We apologize for this detour, and the ways in which it lets our fans down. This is our promise that the game WILL be finished as soon as we can. Thank you for your patience and understanding. -Graeme (2 Left Thumbs) About This Game You want to endlessly roll dice and battle folklore monsters? We got you covered. Roll magical dice, collect rare loot, nudge your fate in the right direction, slay folklore creatures and finally face the King of Undead, The Lich! With Die for the Lich, we set out to recreate the magic of playing a unique deck-builder for the first time. We replaced cards with dice that you need to pair with your gear and the game took on a life of its own, a curious blend of strategy, chaos, and pushing your luck! Will you strike the perfect balance between dice, weapons, abilities and trinkets? Do you wish to always roll what you need, then find a Lucky Die. Do you need custom made dice, then visit the Dice Fusion table. Do you want to re-roll 1s, then grab the Eye Patch ability. Do you think you are strong enough for a tougher battle, then seek the Blood Moon. The options are endless and there is nothing more exciting than having your own unique journey in a chaotic world.
