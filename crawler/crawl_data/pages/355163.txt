View Community Hub About This Game If you die on the battlefield it’s not my fault. You think boot camp’s hard? Try surviving my camp. In Full Metal Sergeant 2 you’re an hellish instructor. Your mission is to transform raw recruits into specialized war machines. Mold their minds and bodies, instill discipline, and hone their combat skills. TRAIN HARDER, OR DIE TRYING: Assign recruits to 9 elite special forces roles. Push them past their limits: mental breakdowns, injuries, personal traits ,and desertion are your problem. BUILD A CAMP THAT BREAKS BONES: Construct training areas designed to crush every weakness. Shooting ranges, obstacle courses, anything to forge the perfect warriors. 12 WEEKS TO PERFECTION: Every training decision ripples into battle. If they're not ready they won't last much on the battlefield TURN-BASED COMBAT, PERMADEATH CONSEQUENCES: Lose focus for one turn? Say goodbye to your best soldier. Turn-based combat system The main highlight for this sequel is a completely new turn based battle system. The key is to strategically utilize the unique abilities of each soldier's specialization to gain a tactical advantage. 12 Week gameplay loop The game is divided into 12 weeks. Each week you’ll be given some training points. Use them to train your recruits with a variety of different trainings taken from real military camp. Get rid of those negative traits! 9 special forces unit Discover nine unique special units, including snipers, mortarmen, assault riflemen, and machine gunners. Transform your recruits into these specialized classes, each with their own distinct perks and quirks. What will be the perfect platoon composition? Uncharted Territories: Map, Missions, and Movement system I will put my boot up your ass and go on a march! Renewed, focused skill tree Our Commander has refocused your training!
