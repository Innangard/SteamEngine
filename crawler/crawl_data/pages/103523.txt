View Community Hub About This Game Space Cleaners is an online co-op horror game where you and up to 6 friends are A.I. units building a space cleaning business. Accept contracts from space corporations, scrub the goo off filthy stations, and survive hostility long enough to finish the job. The dangers inside and outside the stations will try to stop you. But you can't back off; the business isn't going to grow itself. Buy equipment, save your crew, and do everything possible to keep your reputation intact. Clean the goo. Dodge the threats. Stay in business. Endure hostilities until the last goo is cleaned. Do not let the business die. Every job funds the next. Every loss sets you back.
