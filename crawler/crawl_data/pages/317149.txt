View Community Hub About This Game Doomies (Damikira) is a frenetic arcade shooter filled with guns, violence, dark humor, and even a grappling hook! It’s built to help you waste time (in the best way possible) and throw you into a flow state where your only goal is to obliterate freaky enemies and climb the online leaderboard, proving just how much you enjoy staying indoors. It might look simple, but this beast hides layers of chaos under the hood. Beneath the blood and bullets lie secrets, tricks, and twisted mechanics for those brave (or bored) enough to dig deep.
