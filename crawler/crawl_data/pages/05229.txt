Players can already drive, maintain, and repair their RV, explore a procedurally generated world, gather resources, and manage their survival needs. Online co-op multiplayer is also implemented. While the core experience is in place, players should expect to encounter bugs, and system-heavy updates may require save file wipes as we improve the game.” Jocul va avea prețuri diferite pe durata accesului timpuriu și după părăsirea acestuia? „Yes, the price will be lower during Early Access to thank our early supporters. Each new game offers a different starting point and experience, allowing you to collect resources and survive in abandoned locations and roads. Explore a continuously shifting world of distinct biomes. Each unique region offers its own specific climate and ecosystem. Begin your journey with a rundown RV, repairing and upgrading each part. Personalize everything from stoves and refrigerators to toilets and seating areas, creating your own living space. Stay alive with activities such as cooking, fishing, repairing, and making coffee. Use your RV's kitchen to cook the food you find, making more nutritious meals. Discover Nomad Drive, where every part is fully interactive. Touch, customize, and explore everything from furniture to appliances. Make the RV truly yours! Plant the fruit and vegetable seeds you discover in the world of Nomad Drive in pots and grow them by taking care of them. They will reward you with delicious fruits and vegetables.
