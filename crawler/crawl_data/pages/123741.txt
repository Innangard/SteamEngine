Start from a small warehouse, buy properties, dig deep for resources, process what you extract, and turn it into profit. Expand step by step and turn a simple setup into a fully functioning factory. Play solo or with up to 4 players, running the entire operation yourself or working together to build it faster! Procedural Mining at Its Core Every property features procedurally generated underground resources with no fixed paths, allowing you to dig freely and shape your own tunnels. Start with a shovel, move on to pickaxes for tougher layers and clear large sections with dynamite. As you go deeper, resources become more valuable, making each decision more important. From Warehouse to Industrial Production Your factory starts with simple machines and basic resources, then expands as you unlock new machines and recipes through progression and licenses. Handle logistics with pallets and forklifts, connect your machines with conveyor belts to create more efficient production flows, gradually turning your warehouse into a larger and more capable industrial system. Buy Properties, Dig for Profit Purchase different types of properties, each featuring randomized resource distribution and profit potential. Dig, extract, process, and turn every property into profit as you grow into larger and more valuable operations. Contracts, Sales, and Growth Take on contracts from companies requesting specific products and quantities, negotiate deals, prepare deliveries, and load trucks to fulfill orders for higher rewards. Manage your stock, organize shipments, and balance your growth with fast stock sales. Tools, Upgrades, and Equipment Begin with simple tools and unlock and upgrade more powerful mining equipment as you progress. Customize your character with different outfits, faces, gloves, and helmets. Build Solo or Scale Together Run everything yourself or work together as a team. Take full control and progress at your own pace, or split the work, optimize production, and grow faster together.
