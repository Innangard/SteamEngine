View Community Hub About This Game The hit Armor Games physics destruction game is back! Crush the Castle Legacy Collection lets you revisit the chaos and mayhem of the original series with upscaled graphics and new achievements. Load up your trebuchet with special projectiles to topple your foes and score big to carry yourself to victory. This collection includes all levels from the original Crush the Castle, Crush the Castle: Players Pack, Crush the Castle 2, Crush the Castle 2: Players Pack, and Crush the Castle: Adventures. Features Play through 300+ levels from across the Crush the Castle series! Enjoy these classic games with upscaled graphics and revised achievements. Fling rocks, bombs, and more at monumental towers and castles to bring them to their knees! Equip power-ups to increase your destructive potential.
