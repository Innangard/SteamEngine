View Community Hub About This Game Bit Breaker is a short brick-breaking incremental bullet hell . Collect resources to upgrade your balls and fight back against the nodes taking over humanity. DESTROY NODES Break the nodes down one by one by throwing balls in their direction. There are over 10 unique enemies , each with their own method of attacking you. BOSS FIGHTS At the end of every level is a unique boss , bringing unique attacks and giving a taste of the nodes to come later. These give precious rewards upon defeat. ENHANCE YOUR BALLS The skill tree offers a wide variety of upgrades to both your balls and you ship, from basic stat increases to entirely new ways of attacking. Buy upgrades to get stronger to collect more resources to buy upgrades to get stronger to collect more resources to... IMPROVE YOUR OWN SKILLS While upgrades can make even the toughest bosses trivial, it's imperative that you learn to dodge through enemy attacks if you want to take home more resources. Early levels might be easy enough, but don't become complacent or else the bullet hell will consume you . GAMEPLAY FEATURES 2-4 hours of satisfying incremental progression. 15 levels of increasing difficulty. Unlock over 120 new skills by collecting resources from destroyed nodes.
