Inspired by traditional Chinese music, Mark draws upon instruments like the xiao and erhu recorded live to create sentimental, nostalgic themes with a tender and human touch. Fellow AiliA developer Jingyu Sui (WakaWhale) and Mark teamed up to compose the final song, “See You in the Mirror”, which seamlessly joins Jingyu’s vocals with the game's main theme.
