Earning medals isn’t required to finish the game… And if you prefer, you can hide the timer in the accessibility options… but GOLD medals are the only way to become THE DUCK LEGEND! Show mother nature who is the real apex predator! Just like in the story of David and Goliath , be cunning like a... Duck! The Duck Amikaze, just as the mighty Phoenix rises from the ashes, will always Strikes Back! Travel through 5 different regions , and hunt to ashes all the hunters nearby your duck nest to avenge your family, eaten by those... humans. But beware the wildlife of those regions… savage animals might try to eat some of your crispy duck tenders as you charge headlong into each hunter’s hut. Try to collect the 4 vegetables in each level of the game, sometimes well hidden behind secret walls . But beware of the traps set by the hunter! Accessibility: Select Easy Mode in the option menu to have infinite hearts and infinite jumps if you struggle. You can adjust the deadzone value of your controller's joystick to prevent joystick drift/drifting on older/rusty controllers. The value can be set from 20% min to 80% max (with 0% meaning permanent joystick drifting, and 100% meaning the joystick does not register any input at all). If you prefer you can hide the three User Interface element separately : hearts, collectible, timer, from the option menu. Once you beat the game in either easy or normal mode, you'll unlock Hardcore Mode , where just one hit turns you into... crispy duck tenders. Prove yourself worthy by earning all the golden duck medals, to become THE Duck Legend!
