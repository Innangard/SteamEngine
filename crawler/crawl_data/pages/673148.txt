About this DLC Step outside the kitchen and into a brand-new outdoor cooking simulator experience . The BBQ DLC adds depth, replayability, and smoky chaos to your favourite cooking game expansion . Grab your tongs, light the coals, and prove you’re the BBQ master of Cooking Simulator.
