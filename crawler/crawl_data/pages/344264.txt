About This Game Get ready to race across an apocalyptic Japan in your very own KEI TRUCK! You're being chased by ALIENS! Dodge their attacks...OR ELSE! You can even drive on walls! Evade their onslaught of attacks so you don't get eaten! *This manner of operating a vehicle is extremely dangerous and should not be imitated under any circumstances. Destroy everything in your path with NITRO BOOSTS! We're not even sure how you recover Nitro though… Launch a counterattack against your enemies! Fight epic battles against outerworldly beings! The progression system of this game is driven by crossing the finish line stage after stage. You'll not only fight some teeny-tiny enemies, but you'll also throw your steering wheel against HUGE BOSSES too! Some stages don't have any enemies at all. You can test your driving skills against other online players through an in-game leaderboard system. Your scores and clear times are tracked after every stage, so you can see how you stack up against other Kei drivers.
