Buy We Were Here Together: Supporter Edition $4.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content We Were Here Together is a first-person cooperative puzzle adventure set in and around a mysterious castle in Antarctica. The game is the third entry in the We Were Here series. It’s all about cooperative discovery, puzzle solving, and immersion. Working together with a partner, you must solve puzzles via careful observation and smart communication. When you find yourself trapped in different parts of the sinister Castle Rock, you will only have your walkie-talkies and your wits to figure out how to escape… The incredible support from our fans has been central in our journey from student team with We Were Here to the release of We Were Here Too , and We Were Here Together . This special Supporter Edition DLC is a way for you to show your support for our personal brand of cooperative adventure games!
