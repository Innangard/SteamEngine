Hired 2 Die is a cooperative survival horror game up to 4 players where otherworldly distortions roam the workplace as dangerous creatures and strange anomalies. The horror begins when the shift starts. You were hired by the shady Intersection. Clean the garbage, mop the floor and organize the environment silently in order to not attract the distortions. Running away is not enough, you need to capture the distortions. It’s up to you to risk your life using CCTVs, remote doors, motion sensors and other equipment handled by Intersection to guarantee an effective capture. Interior design is not just for looks! Use your hard earned money to buy anomalous furniture that grants you perks for all future jobs. Single and Coop play: The game is designed differently for single-player or teams that want to die together. Progressive Gameplay: Even the most ordinary task is permanent and will help you in future contracts. Proximity Voice Chat: Use the security systems and walkie-talkies to plan and communicate with your co-workers, but be careful, they are listening. Keep talking and nobody dies: Coordinate with your friends to fix complex systems using the instruction manual.
