Dive into another new story. You have to go to Earth to take a little walk and find out what has changed. The distant future... There are almost no people left on planet Earth, everyone has moved to Mars. The age of prosperity, the age of robots, androids, technology. And then one day, the robot Zero1 decided to take a walk around planet Earth. What awaits him? A desert? Vast forests? Who will he meet on the way? All this is to be found out in the walking simulator. Immerse yourself in a walk through mysterious places with Zero1! A game for one evening, the passage time is 2-3 hours maximum. The game is made on the Unreal 5.4 engine. A simple sketch project. Developer, publisher - Sveta Sky. Backgrounds, characters, assets - Unreal 5.4. Detailed information about free publicly available assets can be found in the description in the game itself, as well as in the game folder. Music - Andreas Rönnberg, Bxmmusic "Brandon". You can catch a bug or scream (whichever is more convenient) when pressing the spacebar (it is better not to press it). The controls are easy - your mouse, WSAD - to go forward, backward, left, right. E - switch the view from the first and third person. P - read a book. T - exit to the main menu.
