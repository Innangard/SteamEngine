Seventh Mansion: The First Escape is a first-person psychological horror escape room where every death changes the experience. Explore an eerie, atmospheric mansion filled with secrets, solve puzzles, and survive encounters with the lurking Wicked Nuns. You could fight your way with a camera, or you could break the static radio. This will be your choice or else Death is inevitable. But death is not the end. Each time you fall, the mansion changes—puzzles evolve, and new dangers emerge. No two attempts are ever the same. Features: 📸 Camera-based survival mechanic inspired by tension and timing 🏚️ A haunting mansion with multiple variations 🧩 Puzzle-driven escape room gameplay 💀 Permadeath system that resets your run 🔁 Procedural elements that change each playthrough 🌫️ Immersive atmospheric horror experience Can you escape… or will the mansion keep you forever? Warning: This game relies heavily on proximity-based audio cues. For the intended experience, headphones are required.
