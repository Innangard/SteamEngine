From the instant the first spark of Soul Power stirs within you, your destiny is set: to stand at the heart of the storm and become the legend itself. Now, it is time to carve your name into your own divine epic.Redefine the Soul Land Universe!Beyond Boundaries: Explore the Breathtaking Realm of Soul Land From the humble fields of Saint Village to the eternal frost of the Icebound Forest; from the untamed wilds of Aster Forest to the crashing tides of Isle of Poseidon—the world is yours to command. Every corner holds a secret. Scout for hidden treasures, delve into ancient caves to hunt Spirimon, or challenge the Spirimasters at Spirit Temple. In this boundless world, your journey has no limits!Shatter the Chains: Master the Art of Limitless Combat Experience a faithful recreation of the original Soul Land combat system, featuring non-targeted mechanics, precise dodges, aerial maneuvers, and fluid skill execution. Seamlessly switch between Spirits, customize your tactical loadouts, and exploit attribute advantages to suppress your foes. Transform every encounter into a masterclass of skill and strategy. Experience the true rush of high-octane battle and the unique charm of Spirimaster duels!Hunt Unbound: Carve Your Legend as the Strongest Spirimaster Warning! Spirimon spotted ahead! Ascend to godhood with ease!Supreme Quality: Immerse Yourself in a Flawless Anime Recreation Experience the definitive Soul Land journey, authentically adapted from the original novels and animation.
