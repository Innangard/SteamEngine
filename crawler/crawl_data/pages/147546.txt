Play as Maya, a child stolen from her family on the whim of a lonely, forsaken deity, and make your way through over 100 screens filled with spikes, lasers, switches, and traps as you unravel the horrifying secret of your new prison. Gorgeous hand-drawn pixel art graphics bring the god’s realm to life alongside meticulously handcrafted animations boasting many thousands of frames A lush, atmospheric score, equal parts unsettling and beautiful Responsive, snappy controls provide challenges with a sense of mastery and acrobatic accomplishment A psychological horror narrative contorts and unfolds to reveal choices beyond comprehension Will you find your way home, or wander these halls carved of memory forever?
