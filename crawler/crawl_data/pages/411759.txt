Player are again  young man whose mission is collect all blue music notes at different levels. Then when all notes collected player can use Piano and open portal and finish levels.  Game contain 4 different themed levels with different obstacles, like rotating objects, disappearing clouds, moving platforms etc Levels contain flying enemy with missiles to avoid. Player can find notes at different place at levels and some notes are hidden at boxes and barrels which can open by hitting them. Also player need collect coins. Coins can be used to unlock different jumping and moving platforms. Coins are hidden at levels and different barrels. If player dies, Level restart from starting point. So can you find all hidden notes and play Piano FEATURES: - 4 different themed maps - collect all music notes to finish level - flying enemies with rockets - Collect  enough coins to unlock different platforms - Jumping,moving platforms. - Disappearing clouds - Rotating objects - Find blue Crystal every level to get steam achievements - Collect hearts to increase health - Box and barrels that contains coins and notes - Piano and portal which can be used after all notes collected System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: windows 8 Processor: i5 Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: nvidia 1000 series DirectX: Version 9.0 Storage: 2 GB available space Sound Card: Direct x9 Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 8.1 Processor: i5 Memory: 6 GB RAM Graphics: nvidia 2000 series DirectX: Version 9.0 Storage: 2 GB available space Sound Card: Direct x9 * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
