View Community Hub About This Game Physics-based party platformer in the disco. Your goal: get to the dance floor – faster than everyone else. Shortcuts, risky jumps, clean timing. 1–4 players locally (supports Remote Play Together). Editor & Community Levels provide a constant stream of new courses with online leaderboards. Play locally with up to 4 friends . Help each other... or push each other out at the last second. The main thing is that the room is roaring. Now also via Remote Play Together ! With the intuitive editor , you can create your own disco courses in minutes and share them immediately. Browse through community levels . New challenges every day. Every course is a race . Use physics to your advantage. Discover shortcuts , hunt for the best online time , and climb the leaderboard. Build chains of chaos with precise lasers, catapulting cannons, and bouncy disco balls. Combine the objects to create nasty timings or fun party traps. Perfect for trolling, tinkering, and celebrating. Colorful scenery, glittering balls, and cute cube characters. The perfect setting for chaotic party platforming fun.
