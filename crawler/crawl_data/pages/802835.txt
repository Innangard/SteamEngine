This variant is fitted with a camouflage net, and beneath it are secured canisters and sandbags. This field-installed kit provided extra protection against enemy fire. Its primary armament is the 76 mm M1 gun. M18 Hellcat A highly mobile tank destroyer armed with the 76 mm M1 gun. This version is equipped with a lightweight camouflage net, external brackets carrying the crew’s personal gear, and an additional 7.62 mm machine gun mounted on the turret. The look is based on authentic photographs of late-production vehicles sent to Europe — the turret even bears an information plate similar to those used when rolling off the factories en route to the front. Skink A rare Canadian-designed anti-aircraft vehicle armed with four 20 mm automatic cannons. Extra track links are mounted on the hull, and wooden side panels echo those used on Sherman-series tanks. Its fully enclosed turret offers a high degree of crew protection, making the Skink an effective air-defense platform capable of repelling ground-attack aircraft and fighters even at close range.
