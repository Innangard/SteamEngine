Play as Ethan, a police officer in Ashwood Creek, a town hiding secrets beneath its tranquil surface. A Snapshot of Mystery Investigate a disappearance, uncover cryptic radio signals, and navigate the haunting Ashwood Creek National Park. Your radio is more than a tool—it’s a lifeline, guiding you through a story where every signal could reveal a clue or lead you into danger. This demo showcases the game’s atmospheric exploration, supernatural intrigue, and stylized 1980s aesthetic. Will you uncover the truth, or will the shadows claim you? Key Features An Edge-of-Your-Seat Mystery : Unravel the beginnings of a story filled with suspense, supernatural elements, and dark secrets that blur the line between reality and the unknown. Captivating Characters : Dive into a gripping narrative as you uncover the past and present lives of those in Ashwood Creek, including your own. Stylized Timeless Graphics : Step back into the 1980s with beautifully stylized visuals that capture the nostalgic vibe of the era and the beauty of the nature that engulfs you. A Beautiful National Park to Explore : Wander through the serene yet unsettling landscapes of Ashwood Creek National Park, where every corner hides a clue... or a threat. Can You Uncover the Secret? Armed with only your radio and a walkie-talkie, you’ll need to think on your feet, listen carefully, and trust no one.
