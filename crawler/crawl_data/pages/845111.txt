This airport is the second busiest in CA after Los Angeles Airport [LAX] and among top 10 busiest airports in the U.S. Beautifully modelled and very unique tower building and surroundings, terminals. It has four asphalt runways, arranged in two intersecting sets of parallel runways. - Runway 01L/19R - Runway 01R/19L - Runway 10L/28R - Runway 10R/28L The airport has four terminals (1, 2, 3, and International) and seven concourses.  It is the largest international terminal in North America. Terminal 1 (Boarding Area B), Terminal 2 (Boarding Areas C and D), and Terminal 3 (Boarding Areas E and F) handle domestic flights. The International Terminal (Boarding Areas A and G) handles international flights and some domestic flights too.
