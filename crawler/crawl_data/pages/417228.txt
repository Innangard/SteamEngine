How does it play? Pick a character, jump into a dangerous map, and battle massive hordes of enemies while completing missions, collecting loot, leveling up, and upgrading your build. Weapons fire automatically, but you can manually aim your primary weapon for extra control. Between the chaos, you can interact with buildings, trigger events, hire party members, and use powerful consumable items by pausing the action at the right moment. Every run pushes you to adapt, survive, and become strong enough to face the boss waiting at the end.
