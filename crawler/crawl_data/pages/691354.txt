DEMO FEATURES Fight as Three Outcast Heroes Challenge the minions of Sun King Azra with Galandra's fencing prowess and necromantic might, Karl's sturdy knuckles and explosive firepower, or Cider's acrobatic combos and mechanical gadgets. Play solo or have a friend join the rebellion in local or online co-op! Brave the Dangers of Grandery The first area of the full game, Grandery, is an expansive region where Azra's rise to power shattered the peace between goblins, dwarves, and humans. Carve your path through their enclaves and discover the stunning, hand-crafted environments of a world that changes as your adventure progresses. Reach New Heights of Power Have a taste of the "Rogue 'em Up" style of Absolum in this Demo: explore multiple paths, face unpredictable encounters, and invoke mighty rituals to empower your attacks. Liberate enshackled magic, train (and trade) with the rebel followers of Mother Uchawi, and gain new powers and abilities after each run. Experience the Beat 'em Up Apex Dominate Azra's legion with a vast arsenal of moves and combos. Dodge, Deflect, and Clash with your enemies' attacks; and make clever use of items, mounts, and powerful Arcana moves to dispatch the lackeys of the Crimson Order. Rediscover the original feeling of the classic arcade Beat'em Ups, refined by the best artisans of the genre.
