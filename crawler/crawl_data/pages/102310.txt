Añadir al carro Ver centro de la comunidad Acerca de este juego HippoHop is a psychedelic jazz-club flyer inspired by the music and art of the 50s. You tap to flap through handcrafted levels of colorful musical instruments rendered in a flat, bold-color, screen-printing aesthetic. A variety of drink-themed pickups grant you extra points or temporary abilities, enabling you to traverse a range of unexpected situations. As you progress from track to track, not only does the game require tighter flying and the combined abilities of multiple pickups to succeed, but intrusive thoughts of unpaid bills emerge to block your path more and more often. One button to fly, one button to blow away your worries — flap through obstacles, then puff away the unpaid bills that drift into your path 8 pickups that progressively unlock throughout the game and combine in new and interesting ways 14 levels , each with its own track, colorful light show, and its own flavor of chaos 36 achievements to chase, from your first cocktail to clearing the whole bar cart A local leaderboard to face off with friends and family Localized into 15 languages Escape the grind and fly away to smooth riffs and psychedelic grooves as you chase the high score!
