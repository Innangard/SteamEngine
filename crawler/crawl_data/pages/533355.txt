View Community Hub About This Game Pack My Order is a meditative online shopping simulator created for you to relax and enjoy the moment. There is no rush, no timers, and no stress. Just you, a shopping list, and the aesthetics of a perfectly filled cart. Study customer wishes Each level is a new task from a customer. In it, a list of specific products and special wishes awaits you: for example, buy milk and bread, but don't forget to add "something for tea for guests." Carefully study the preferences and budget to collect exactly what is needed. Choose products in the catalog Choose products in the catalog and switch between categories, just like in a real online supermarket: from fresh vegetables and pastries to sweet and savory treats. Pick what fits the customer's request and add it to the cart, adjusting the required quantity immediately. Control the contents of the cart Go to the cart where you can control everything: adjust the quantity of items, delete unnecessary ones, or return to the catalog if you want to add something else. Monitor the total receipt in real-time — it’s important not to exceed the budget allocated by the customer. Bring the order to perfection Press the check button to submit the order. The system will immediately tell you if you did everything correctly: if it lights up red, it means the budget has been exceeded or some item from the customer's list was overlooked. Simply fix the error at your own pace and bring the order to perfection! Move on to new meditative levels Fulfilled all the conditions? Congratulations, the level is passed! Feel that pleasant joy of a completed task and move on to a new interesting list. Game Features: Relax Mode: Want to just collect beautiful products without restrictions? Turn on relax mode with a huge budget and free orders. Assemble your ideal cart just for pleasure! Cozy in the details: Cute animations, soft colors, and ASMR interface sounds will create an atmosphere of complete tranquility for you. Pleasant mindfulness: Order lists will make you linger in the catalog a little longer to find that very "crunchy movie snack" or the perfect breakfast. It's not difficult, but very addictive! Anti-stress gameplay: The perfect game to turn on your favorite podcast or music in the background and relax after a workday.
