Cole starts a night shift at an isolated gas station. The station's owner offers him a job and a room to stay in. For Cole, it seems like the opportunity he needs to get his life back on track. Work the register, fuel cars, restock the shelves, and keep the station clean. Talk to the people who stop by and get to know the station and its surroundings. When the Routine Breaks As the nights go on, this routine becomes increasingly unsettling. A strange conversation can turn into an argument. Small details you notice around the station begin to make you question what you know about the place. As strange sounds and unexplained events pile up, you start to wonder whether the building where you work and sleep is safe. Over Several Nights Try to make sense of what is happening at the station while carrying on with your work. Each shift leaves you with a new question about the station's past and the people around you. Discover how the events at the station unfold over several nights. Features A first-person psychological horror experience. Everyday gas station tasks, from working the register and fueling cars to restocking shelves and cleaning. Conversations with customers and unsettling encounters. An atmospheric horror story unfolding over several nights. Multiple endings.
