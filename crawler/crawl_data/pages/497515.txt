Content For This Game Browse all (1) Once Alive Soundtrack $1.99 $1.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Part I: THE LAST SURVIVOR Sixteen years have passed since humanity's extinction due to a global virus outbreak. You play as James, who, along with his brother, believes they are the last survivors. However, this changes when he discovers an invitation to a survivors' base, HaustVille . Part II: WHATEVER IT TAKES Determined to find others, the brothers leave everything behind. After a few days, they reach HaustVille , but something feels wrong about this village. Part III: THEY ARE GONE " Where is everyone? What happened here? " The player must explore HaustVille to uncover the mystery behind the villagers' disappearance and learn more about the virus. KEY FEATURES Developed using Unreal Engine 5 , utilizing cutting-edge technology for a fully immersive experience. The entire village is waiting to be explored. And every place has a story to tell. Discover the past through environmental storytelling, speech echoes, and objects left behind by other survivors. A hostile world awaits, where the virus has not only decimated the population but made animals more aggressive. Caution is essential in the wilderness. Experience cinematic cutscenes that intensify the narrative.
