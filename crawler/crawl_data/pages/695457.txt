View Community Hub About This Content Pack yer rods and cross the big blue, 'cause Cast n Chill is driftin’ into uncharted waters with Eastern Winds - a serene and spellbinding new DLC that brings a breath o' fresh air to your fishing adventures. Explore a string o' tranquil fishing spots inspired by storied lands where cherry blossoms drift on the breeze, bamboo groves rustle by quiet shores, and ancient shrines overlook misty coves teemin’ with mysterious fish you’ve never seen before. Whether you're castin’ from a lily-covered riverbank or driftin’ through foggy mountain lakes, there's always somethin' rare on the line. What’s in the Tacklebox: 🎣 New Fishing Spots – Journey through 8 new handcrafted waterscapes filled with vibrant flora, unique ambient sounds, and fishin' holes that’ll test your patience and your line. 🐟 Exotic New Fish – Fill yer logbook with a couple dozen new species - some real lookers, others downright slippery tricksters. 🐕 Companions – Bring along new boat companions - each with their own tail waggin' charm. 🧰 New Gear – From lacquered rods to handcrafted lures, there’s a whole new spread of equipment to collect, customize, and show off at the local dock. Let the Eastern Winds carry you to calm waters and curious catches. Just mind the weather, and keep yer net ready - there’s more than koi lurkin’ in these lakes.
