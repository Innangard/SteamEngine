Puni the Florist is a cozy simulation game where you run a magical flower shop with the help (and occasional chaos) of a clueless fairy. Run a flower shop, arrange unique bouquets, and build connections with the quirky townsfolk who wander through your doors. Express Your Creativity Fulfill townsfolk’s requests with your own floral designs using unique combinations of flowers and decorations. Wholesome Story Engage in a silly, light-hearted, and relatable tale of self-discovery of Puni. Learn Floriography Complete flower collections and uncover the language of flowers through your scrapbook. Decorate Your Shop Make your shop your own with cute, customizable decor and personal touches. View Your Designs Admire the bouquets you’ve created in the gallery Befriend the Townsfolks Meet a quirky cast of characters, uncover their stories, and form lasting friendships.
