This multi-threaded anthology of interactive stories is a gritty, literary journey through the darkest corners of the human psyche. Nine intricately woven tales will plunge you into a whirlwind of extreme emotions, where nihilism blends with a desperate struggle for survival, and the line between good and evil blurs under the weight of tragic accidents. At the same time, all of this unfolds within a familiar reality of everyday life. You won't find easy answers here — every decision carries irreversible consequences, leading the protagonist toward a greater or lesser downfall. MAIN FEATURES Nine atmospheric, independent stories: Discover diverse scenarios exploring themes of urban realism, thriller, horror, and dystopia. Each story is a completely unique experience with its own color palette and distinct thematic weight. Decisions that truly shape the plot: Your choices are not an illusion. Every path branches out, leading to many different events and giving you real control over how the characters behave. A web of unique endings: Each story offers at least five different endings. Discovering them all can take anywhere from a few to a dozen or more hours, depending on your reading pace. A unique, literary style: The game's text was written with careful attention to deep, vivid, and evocative language, staying true to the literary source material by Polish author Tymoteusz Peta. The suffocating atmosphere of the provinces, bone-chilling frost, and brutal realism make it impossible to tear yourself away from the stories. Simultaneously, they serve as a perfect pretext for psychological and sociological reflection.
