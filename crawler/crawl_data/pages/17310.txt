View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Chess: Three Kingdom is a strategy game set in the chaotic Three Kingdoms era, seamlessly blending the timeless rules of Chinese Chess with tactical turn-based strategy. Featuring the pieces you familiar but tactics you’ve never imagined. This is an all-out war centered on formations, skills, unit combo, and frontline advancement! In Chess: Three Kingdom, you’ll step into the turbulent era of the Three Kingdoms. Every unit is built upon classic Xiangqi rules. No longer just static symbols, they are specialized legions of the Three Kingdoms, each with its own role. Across the Chu River and Han Border, the board comes alive in a struggle of strategy, sacrifice, and shifting tides. Every move is a march; every match is a conquest. In these turbulent times, a powerful army is forged, not born. Accumulate Military Merit by crushing enemy forces to recruit and expand your ranks through randomized drafts. Every unit features distinct traits and branching progression paths—your choices will define the soul of your formation. Will you overhaul the enemy with a hail of arrows or shatter their lines with a heavy cavalry charge? The mandate is yours. The fate of the realm has never rested in one person’s hands. Enter Multiplayer and pit your wits against other players! On this compact board, every move is a test of nerve and deception—and every piece could decide who lives and who falls. Victory is never guaranteed. The tide of battle can turn in an instant. One decision is all that stands between victory and defeat.
