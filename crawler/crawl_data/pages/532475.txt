View Community Hub About This Game Tidy Farm is a cozy organizing simulator where you transform a chaotic farm into your dream homestead. Pick everything up, figure out where it belongs and sort 4,544 Items. Was That A Tornado? The farm is a complete disaster. Vegetables and fruits are scattered across the fields, tools are lying everywhere, flowers are piled up, eggs are rolling around, and even the chickens are running freely... Your job is simple: Pick it up. Find where it belongs. Put it back. Start with complete chaos and work your way towards a beautifully organized farm, one item at a time. Unlock Helpful Upgrades As you tidy up the farm, you'll earn money that can be spent on upgrades designed to make every trip more efficient. Carry more items at once Move around the farm faster Get your dog to lend a paw Use a wheelbarrow to haul much more at once And more... Become faster, more efficient, and better equipped to take on the farm's mess. A Cozy, Satisfying Experience There are no timers, no deadlines, and no pressure . Take your time, pick up a few items or tackle an enormous pile, it's completely up to you. Enjoy the simple satisfaction of cleaning, sorting, organizing, and watching your farm slowly become the cozy homestead you've always wanted. Features Organize 4,544 items Sort vegetables, fruits, flowers, tools, eggs, chickens and more Recruit your dog to help around the farm Use a wheelbarrow to move large amounts of items Earn money by cleaning up and spend it on helpful upgrades Upgrade your carrying capacity and movement speed and more Tackle huge piles of items and turn chaos into order Watch your entire farm transform as you clean Enjoy a relaxing, cozy organizing experience No time limits, no pressure, tidy at your own pace No Generative AI used System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Core 7 Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: GeForce GTX 1060 Storage: 2 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system View all What Curators Say 7 Curators have reviewed this product.
