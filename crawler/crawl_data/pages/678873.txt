Then you are given a random building that you can rotate and place where you choose.  After placing it, you will be given another building, to build a nice little pretty island. Objects that are placable include: Trees Rocks (as of v1.03) Buildings Other Structures The placable objects that are randomly given, are dependent on the style of the island which is randomly picked between the current styles. Current Styles include: - Medieval (castle-type stuff) - Plain (modern-day normal-looking buildings) - Adobe (pueblo architectural style) - Guarda (a style based on the village of Guarda, Switzerland) The Game is solo developed, hopefully, you enjoy it, it's a very simple concept. And this is my first game in actual 3D where I had to use Blender to model the buildings.
