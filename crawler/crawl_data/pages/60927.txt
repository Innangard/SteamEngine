About the Professional Baseball Spirits Series eBaseball™: PRO SPIRIT is a title in the Professional Baseball Spirits series. The Professional Baseball Spirits series is a professional baseball game franchise dedicated to realism. The first home console title, Professional Baseball Spirits 2004, was released on March 25, 2004. With Professional Baseball Spirits 2015, the series introduced 3D scanning technology, capturing real professional baseball players from every angle to recreate them in three dimensions. Laser scanning technology was also adopted to precisely measure and reproduce stadium layouts, delivering an exceptionally realistic baseball experience. The mobile game Professional Baseball Spirits A, released in 2015, has celebrated its 10th anniversary and surpassed 65 million total downloads as of February 2026.
