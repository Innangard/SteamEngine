One witch's manor desperately in need of a little tidying up. Welcome to an old Victorian manor filled with magic, mysteries, and an unbelievable amount of clutter! Potions, pumpkins, skulls, candles, mysterious artifacts, and all kinds of witchy trinkets have taken over every corner. Your task is simple: find a place for every item and turn this charming chaos into a perfectly organized collection. But don't be too quick to leave. It seems the lady of the manor has other plans for you... A Place for Everything, Your Way Pick up objects, explore the manor, and organize your discoveries across themed racks. You decide how to arrange the shelves! Choose the order of categories, rearrange items, correct mistakes, and gradually watch an overwhelming mess transform into a cozy, organized space. It's not just about finishing the job. It's about enjoying every little step along the way. A Little Magic Goes a Long Way Sorting thousands of objects is no small task. Luckily, you have four magical abilities to lend a hand: Item Finder: Reveal matching items, even through obstacles. Rack Finder: Discover the correct rack for your chosen category. Attraction: Magically pull matching items into your inventory. Magic Sort: Send items straight to their proper shelves with a spell. Earn upgrade points, strengthen your abilities, and expand your inventory. A mysterious cat resting on the central table might even help you unlock a few extra magical talents. A Manor Full of Atmosphere Explore two floors of a cozy witch's home. Rain falls outside the windows, candles illuminate antique furniture, spiders lurk among the decorations, and an occasional thunderstorm briefly plunges the manor into darkness. There are no jump scares or monsters chasing you. Just gentle supernatural charm, autumn vibes, and the satisfying feeling of bringing order to a wonderfully chaotic place. Every Manor Has Its Secrets Not every mystery in this house is hiding in plain sight. Explore unusual corners, discover hidden keys, unlock mysterious locks, and pay attention to the little details. Some of the manor's secrets might be much more amusing than you expect. A Cozy Adventure at Your Own Pace Halloween Tidy Up combines relaxing organization, exploration, physics-based object interactions, and a touch of playful witchcraft. Take your time, save your progress, and return whenever you're ready to tidy up a little more. And when the very last item finds its place... who says you'll want to leave?
