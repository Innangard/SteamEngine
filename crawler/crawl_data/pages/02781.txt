Lihat Hab Komuniti Tentang Kandungan Ini The Frozen Trail Collector Set is the ultimate way to jump-start your journey into the mysterious northern regions of the Rust Belt. Brave the icy slopes of Pendola Pass in style with this unrivaled Frozen Trail Collector Set, containing exclusive Raider cosmetics and new weapon and Raider Outpost customizations first introduced with the Frozen Trail (2.0) update. This bundle contains exclusive Raider cosmetics including the Renzo outfit, Dragon's Breath Weapon Stencil, Bob Hair Style, as well as 2,400 Raider Tokens. Scale to even greater heights with immediate access to the Premium Track of the Frozen Trail Reward Pass. About The Frozen Trail Reward Pass The Frozen Trail Reward Pass consists of 60 levels of in-game rewards, and two tracks: the Free Track, available to all players, and the Premium Track included in this bundle (can also be obtained separately). The Premium Track allows access to up to 50 additional rewards within the Frozen Trail Reward Pass, including exclusive Raider cosmetics and up to 1,150 total Raider Tokens. The Frozen Trail Collector Set includes the following content: - Access to the Frozen Trail Reward Pass - Premium Track - Outfit - Renzo (Gold, Green, Black Silver, Ice Blue) - Scrappy Cosmetic - Renzo Helm - Hair Style - Bob - Weapon Stencil - Dragon’s Breath - Raider Tool - Scasso - Backpack Attachment - Renegade Censer - Face Style - Kabuki Face Paint - Raider Outpost Furniture Recipe - Retro Sofa - Musical Instrument Blueprint - Banjo - 2,400 Raider Tokens Raider Tokens can be used to get reward passes, bundles, or other cosmetic items, including outfits, backpacks, charms, emotes, Scrappy outfits, etc. ARC Raiders game license (purchased separately), Internet connection, Embark ID user account (+13), third-party platform account, and acceptance of the Embark Terms of Service and End User License Agreement are required to play, see our user terms: .
