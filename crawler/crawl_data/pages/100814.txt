to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Tiny Wizard Tavern is a cozy fantasy management game about running a magical roadside tavern in a medieval world. Buy food, ale, and herbs in the morning, prepare for the evening rush, and welcome a colorful mix of travelers: mages, merchants, mercenaries, bards, and pilgrims. Each guest has their own needs, mood, and traits, so every evening becomes a small story. Keep your tavern supplied, protect your reputation, and make difficult choices when resources run low. Offer a discount to calm an impatient guest, invest in upgrades, and decide what kind of tavern you want to build: a warm refuge for travelers, a lively gathering place, or a strange magical stop on the road. The world outside your door is never quiet. Unexpected events can change the flow of the game: wealthy caravans, moonlit markets, tavern brawls, guild attention, hungry rats, and even a dragon drawn by the scent of gold. Features: - Simple and readable tavern management loop - Fantasy guests with different traits and behavior - Magical events that affect each playthrough - Tavern upgrades and strategic choices - Cozy illustrated style with a warm medieval fantasy atmosphere Build your name, survive the season, and turn a small tavern into a place travelers will never forget.
