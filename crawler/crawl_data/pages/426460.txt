Allies now have new visual models that correctly reflect their class and experience level. Their equipment is now fully-customizable with player being able to manually give them any desired item — including poisoning their weapons and supplying them with various medicines. On top of that, the squadmates' receive an expanded control menu in battle, allowing the player to choose their target of attack, fighting style and — if things start to go south — order the character to retreat from battle, saving their life at the cost of experience.
