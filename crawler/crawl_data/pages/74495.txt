Now imagine that, but on a heavy dose of magic mushrooms.” User Review, Oz About This Game Mow-down at the hoedown! Pack your bags and move to the idyllic countryside. Pay no heed to the nuclear fallout – it adds a lovely glow to your complexion! Upsides – everyone wants a piece of you! Downsides – everyone wants a piece of you... Grab your blunderbuss and get ready for twin-stick, bullet-hell, roguelite, corn-chuckin' action game that will last until the cows come home! As any self-respecting farmer would: Farm. Marry. Kill. Sow and harvest as many fast-growing crops as you can before the day is done to earn a huge profit. Purchase weapons and items in town at the end of each day. Celebrate the end of each season with special shops and rewards. Permanently Upgrade your farming and fighting abilities. Earn a payout each run that can be used on permanent improvements and unlocks. Progress through the years with ramping difficulty and even greater rewards! Befriend an entourage of pig pals, chicken chums, and cow colleagues to automate farming tasks. Woo and marry a kindred spirit from the local town to fight and farm alongside you. Unlock new characters, spouses, difficulties, and permanent upgrades. Most importantly, you can unlock many cats to permanently roam your homestead (yes, you can pet them). Defend your crops from unforgiving nightly attackers looking to rampage your hard-earned harvest. Fight challenging bosses each season until you reach the final season - the Nuclear Winter. Explore the far reaches of the biomes to find useful relics of farms long past. Powerful loot increases your farming abilities and helps you fight enemies.
