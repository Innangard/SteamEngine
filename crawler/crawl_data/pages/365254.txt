View Community Hub About This Game Some nights you just want to ride. Motorcycle Night Ride is a ride with nowhere you need to be. Dark highways, city lights and the sound of your engine. Every road has its own time of day and its own mood, from deep night to early morning. Ride your way Free Ride is the relaxing side of the game. There is no timer, no score and no finish line. Choose a road and go. Close Pass is for the times you want more. Pass the traffic as closely as you can at speed, keep your combo alive and beat your best score. Touch a car and the pass is lost. Five motorcycles, your colours You start on the RONIN SHADOW 250. Keep riding and the STRAY 500, the SBR 600, the SB2 1000 and the ATLAS B1000X join your garage one by one. Each motorcycle has its own sound, speed and feel. Make each one yours with matte, metallic, candy and chrome paint, headlight colours and underglow. Every motorcycle keeps its own look. Ten roads, straight or curved City streets, long tunnels, snow, the coast and the open highway. Some roads are deep in the night and some are bright. Every map comes with a Classic road and a Curved road, and each one rides differently. Your Journey Every kilometre moves you forward. New motorcycles, new roads and new paint open up as you ride. The Journey screen shows what comes next and how far away it is.
