If the traps don’t get you, the locals will. These forgotten temples are the perfect breeding ground for anything that slithers, stings, or bites. It belongs in a museum... but it’ll look way better in your bank account. Deep within these ruins lie priceless artifacts and solid gold idols just waiting to be claimed.
