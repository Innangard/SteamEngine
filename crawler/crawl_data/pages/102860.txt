Its magical Heartstones, gems that turn into brick, timber, glass and gold at an Architect's touch, fell silent, and the city crumbled to dust. Now a new Architect has arrived. That's you. Swap neighbouring stones to line up three or more of a kind, wake them up as building materials, and raise Gemhaven one building at a time. Every level you complete builds something new on the living city map: cottages and a windmill, a bustling market, a harbor with its own lighthouse, and finally the Crown Castle on the hill. A match-3 with a goal Every move counts. Each level has a move limit and its own building requirements: collect 24 bricks, forge three Blast Stones, reach a target score... Powerful specials. Match four to create a Blast Stone that explodes in a 3×3 blast. Match five for a Prism that pulls every stone of one colour from the board in a burst of lightning. Combine them. Swap a Prism with a Blast Stone for a chain reaction, or two Prisms together to wipe the whole board. Earn three stars. Finish with moves to spare. Leftover moves turn into bonus points.
