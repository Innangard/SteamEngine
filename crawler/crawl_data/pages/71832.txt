During the demo release, your feedback was incredibly valuable, and we want to keep that going. Our goal is for players to have fun, compete on the leaderboard, and help shape what comes next for SCHROTT.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We’re aiming for a full release early 2026. That timeline might shift a bit depending on development and community feedback, but that’s the target.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to add include an extra map, a new driver, and more pre-built levels for world generation - plus plenty of balance and polish improvements.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game is fully playable and includes progression. Everything listed in the "About This Game" section is already in. From here until full release, we’ll be adding more content for replayability, tuning balance, and polishing the experience based on your feedback.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “No - the price will stay the same during Early Access and after full release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We have a public Discord server where players can report bugs, share feedback, and discuss ideas.
