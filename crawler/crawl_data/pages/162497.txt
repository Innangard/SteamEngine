Clicker Clicker Clicker is a clicker game where you will be doing a lot of clicking. As you click on the main cursor, golden falling cursor will begin to spawn. Their spawn rate is based on the progress you make by clicking. Clicking on these falling cursors will reward you with click gold that is more than the click gold received by active clicking. Clickscension! Ascend and restart the game to receive clickscension coins that can be used to purchase permanent upgrades. These clickscension upgrades have no end, which means you can keep playing and make progress for as long as you'd like!
