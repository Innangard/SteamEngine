Roots of Pacha offers a relaxed and fresh take on the classic farming sim genre. Set in an ancient era, you’ll gather, farm, and explore the enchanting world of Pacha. Befriend animals, craft tools, and customize your character, home, and farm. Expand your village while forging deep community bonds. Thrive and evolve together with your clan through the seasons, and shape your own story at a calm and peaceful pace. Meet your Clan, delve into the biomes, and discover charming surprises wherever you go. From new flora and fauna to mini-games and cave mysteries. Unearth a long-forgotten world. ‎‎‎‏‏‎ Manage crops, gather resources, fish, and discover ways to process and preserve food. Domesticate and breed animals either for goods or as cute pets. Develop tools, items, and buildings to shape your society. ‎‎‎‏‏ You are not alone in Roots of Pacha, strength comes through cooperation. Contribute to your Clan to progress as new ideas arise and see your village grow. Play single-player or co-op with friends. ‎‎‎‏‏‎ Attend festivals and develop friendships, tame your dream pet, and find that special one to start a family with. Customize your character, home and farm to tell your unique story. ‎‎‎‏‏‎‏‎ Make your way at your own pace; the world of Roots of Pacha is a relaxing haven to explore and play in peace. There is no need to rush, as all ideas and prophecies can be completed in any season.
