As a highly trained unit stranded in enemy territory, it’s imperative to push over your limit and get out of there ALIVE. Through your journey, you will find out what led to these unforeseen events. Bodycam-Infused Horror The bodycam is still recording, capturing every moment of your assignment. From the mundane... to the supernatural. A.A.U. Black Site combines realistic visuals, found-footage aesthetics designed to deliver an immersive journey through multiple levels. Finish the Mission They think you are weak. Prove them wrong. Check every corner and stay alert in this intense first-person  gunplay where you are the prime target. Remember, there are not many left in these lands who can watch your six. 20+ Weapons Explore an array of over 20 weapons, including but not limited to assault rifles, sub-machine guns and pistols.
