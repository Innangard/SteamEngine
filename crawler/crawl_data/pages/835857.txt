You're just misplaced. Infinite hallways? Yes. Something lurking in the dark? Probably. A bathroom designed for two people to use at the same time? Unfortunately, yes. Welcome to FailRooms, a strange world of liminal spaces inspired by the Backrooms, where fear of the unknown meets terrible architecture. Expect stairs that lead into ceilings, bone-dry pools, fake doors, and rooms that make absolutely no sense. Explore a place where nothing feels right Wander through endless spaces built in ways they never should have been. The deeper you go, the harder it becomes to tell whether something is dangerous... or just another terrible design choice. Laugh at the absurd... while you still can One moment, you're alone in a silent hallway. The next, you're staring at something so badly designed that you forget you're supposed to be scared. FailRooms turns liminal horror into something stranger, funnier, and much less predictable. Don't let your guard down Feeling comfortable is usually a bad sign. Empty hallways aren't always empty, harmless-looking things may not stay harmless, and some mistakes are more dangerous than others. Keep moving, stay alert, and whatever you do, stay away from the rubber duck. Will you find a way out, or will FailRooms keep you lost inside?
