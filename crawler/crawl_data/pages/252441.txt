In this game, you'll need to think tactically to execute precise player movements in order to score a goal. Don't waste your moves and organize your plays intelligently to successfully score a goal. The match takes place in turns and follows the sequence below: 1) You turn over a card from the deck 2) You choose one of your players 3) According to the pattern drawn on the chosen card, you decide to move your player or kick the ball 4) You must place one of your players in the on-goal kick region 5) And finally must kick to score the goal before time runs out Every move made with one of your players consumes game time and if the time runs out the game is over. Kicking the ball doesn't take time, but if it touches an opponent it will kick it away. Have fun with this game that is a real strategy challenge for those who love football.
