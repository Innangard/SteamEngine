A PS1-style horror game set at a gas station. At midnight, a car suddenly runs out of fuel and stalls. Players must find a way home within the eerie gas station. The game features multiple endings and various interactive objects, allowing you to freely decide how to complete it—whether to learn from experience and outsmart the situation, flee forcefully, or even choose not to go to the gas station in the first place. The gameplay duration is approximately between 30 minutes to one and a half hours. It includes one monster, two holdable items, and multiple interactive objects. You can progress by interacting with different objects in various states or reaching designated locations. The game has a total of five endings, with Ending 5 being a hidden ending. You can freely explore the game and discover all endings through logical deduction. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Below are some in-game items and interactive objects: 1.
