WE’RE ALL IN! pits you against your friends in high-stakes bets on who will emerge victorious in a wide variety of minigames, including 1v1s, team modes, and free-for-alls. BET How many chips are you willing to lay down on your buddy’s reflexes? Are they infamously shit at rock-paper-scissors? WE’RE ALL IN! will shine truth on your friendships. PLAY Bean your friends in the face with javelins. Dodge lasers while pushing your rivals over. Bet on who can obby the fastest. All this and much, much more! Plus, lobbies are highly customizable – turn off the minigames you hate with a passion or settle your disputes via sniper rifle duels. You dream it, WE’RE ALL IN! probably has it. WIN Chips get converted into stars at a gradually increasing cost. Whoever has the most stars at the end wins. Watch out: bonus stars are awarded based on performance and just plain luck. When you’re done winning, spend your stars on unlockable cosmetics to mog your friends with. WE'RE ALL IN! Are you?
