View Community Hub About This Game Hannah is a surreal 3D platformer about childhood trauma, 80s-inspired horror, and broken memories. Explore, feel, and choose your path across a VHS-styled dreamworld with multiple endings. A journey through childhood nightmares Hannah has lost her favorite doll and now has to face a surreal and enigmatic world shaped by her own mind and her memories of the 1980s, a world where every shadow hides a secret. In this atmospheric 3D puzzle-platformer, you must explore mysterious settings, solve intriguing challenges, and confront your worst childhood fears in an immersive and unforgettable experience where every decision determines the ending of the story. Explore the dark side of your mind From narrow paths to vast surreal landscapes, each environment tells a visually stunning story. An ever-changing environment The game adapts to player's choice, altering the environment and influencing the ending. Solve challenging puzzles Use logic and creativity to overcome obstacles and unravel the path ahead bringing you closer to understanding yourself. A deep and interpretive narrative Find objects that tell your story, clues that reveal fragments of your past. A story of self-discovery that unfolds as you explore. Soundtrack composed by a Grammy winner The music and sound effects created especially for the game completely envelop you, creating an extraordinary, immersive, and emotional experience.
