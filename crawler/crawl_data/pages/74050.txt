Chronicon: Survivors is a fast paced horde survival game based on the ARPG Chronicon . Fight a mix of familiar and new dangerous foes in fast paced 20 minute rounds, featuring random events, boss fights, and more! Obtain gold to permanently unlock skills, perks, masteries and even items in a passive skill tree with over 100 nodes. Offer gold and perform feats of strength to increase your standing with deities and restore their shrines, letting their Avatars spawn in combat and grant you powerful Boons. Boons let you specialize your build into one or several synergetic elements, with special effects, triggers, and stat modifiers. Advance through 6 different and increasingly challenging maps to unlock more items, weapons, and a total of 10 playable characters - each with an unique weapon and passive skill. Each map features 2 bosses with unique attack patterns to keep you on your toes. Every weapon offers a choice of upgrades, letting you pick the upgrade path you want, such as increased range, attack speed, number of projectiles, etc. Create the build and style you want to play, banish the items you don't want, and pray at Shrines to increase your chances of getting the items you desire.
