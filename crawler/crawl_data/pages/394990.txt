Demo features include: An introduction to the tragic story of Signy and her dog, Mino Explore the first chapter of the game, complete with secret paths and hidden collectibles Receive a primer on building powerful, turn-based combos Experience a harrowing and fiery boss fight A cliffhanger cutscene to leave you wanting the full game (we warned you!) 30-40 minutes of total playtime Build over-the-top combos one move at a time. Position is everything, and most moves chain into the next if your timing is right. See how far you can carry the chain! Line up your Big Swing so the impact carries to enemies behind your target Launch a target skyward, juggle them in midair, then slam them back into the ground Trade your own HP to push the combo into the next target when the current one is incapacitated. Finish with a full-screen attack that shreds every enemy caught in the chain Mino is more than a cute face. He's your combo partner, and the chain lives or dies on when you tag him in. Call Mino in for a Nose Launch to send your target rocketing skyward Use Dig to claw a downed enemy back up so the combo can keep running Mix in Fetch and Fling, where Mino snatches an enemy out of midair and throws them wherever you point Earn skill points as you level up and spend them across the skill tree to learn new moves or sharpen the ones you already have. Spend you skill points on Signy's moves, Mino's tricks, or your defenses Unlock Combo Extend to chain more skills into a single combo Befriend a washed-up warrior who'll teach you tricks no one else can A volcanic explosion masks a much more sinister problem: Augur, the short-tempered god of rock and magma, has been reborn with an axe to grind. Take on the burden of being the only one who can face him.
