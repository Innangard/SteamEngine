Experience a collection of three short stories about ghosts that haunt everyday locations. Jump scares and creepy moments lurk around every corner. Each story has multiple endings to unlock. The total playthrough time is 45-60 minutes. Story 1: Mirror, Mirror You’ve just purchased your first house, and you can’t wait to settle in. Unfortunately, an unwelcome presence is turning your dream home into a nightmare. Story 2: Laundry Night It’s late at night and you’re washing your clothes in a laundromat alone. Or at least, you think you’re alone, until you suddenly become aware of a ghost who is asking for your help. Story 3: Unfinished Business You're working the night shift as a janitor in a seemingly empty sales office. As the night continues, it becomes clear that multiple ghostly presences have joined you for the evening. Multiple Endings Each of the three stories has multiple endings to explore and unlock.
