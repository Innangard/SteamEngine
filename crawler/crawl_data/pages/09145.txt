Dude, Trust Me Three friends walk into a wonderland with 4 levels and 6 puzzles, each level harder than the last, and nobody holds more than a third of any answer. One of you goes blind, one goes deaf, and one loses their voice. So you describe it wrong. You guess. You try to explain a color to someone who cannot see one, and your hands are on something while your friend across the room is reading the very symbol you need. Pick your role BLIND, the Hands. No color; you only see white outlines in the dark. You hear everything and you can speak, so whatever you find has to leave your mouth. DEAF, the Eyes. Total silence, not a word from your friends. In return, you have your magic goggles; use them to see the room as it really is. You can still talk, so talk. MUTE, the Brain. You are the only one who can read, and the magic book is yours. You hear everything and say nothing: your only answers are emotes and hand gestures. Every room deals the jobs out again Each map brings its own puzzles, its own unique mechanics in different difficulties, and its own answer to who is allowed to do what. Blind Passage Can you get your friends through the passage without feeding them to the sharks? The Offering Fire Good luck trying to summon Sefik, in the dark forest full of mysteries. Sunken Dome Try to figure out what lies in the deep lands of Atlantis. Carousel Playground The carousel hasn’t moved in years. Don’t you wonder why?.. What's in the box Exactly three players, online. 4 levels in their unique environments containing 6 puzzles with different difficulties. Find new friends to argue through the lobby browser. Trouble communicating? Try punching! 👊😀 Trouble punching? THEN TRY DANCING!!! 🕺 Voice chat built straight into the game and shaped by the role you picked. Every run is unique and replayable. Swap roles and experience other perspectives to prove you are better than your ex-friend. An end of run summary, so all three of you have something to argue about. Grab two friends and test your friendship by finding out how well you actually understand each other.
