A union of industrialists has chartered a ship and financed an expedition to a frozen coast. The goal is to find new deposits, set up mining and ship the minerals back to the mainland. You start with nothing but a port, a single boiler house and the last coal in the warehouse... The Board Is Watching Send geologists into the unknown, zone by zone, and find the deposits the mainland is waiting for. The Board expects steady progress and will remind you when exploration stalls. Each contract comes with its own terms, and the Board keeps count. Keep the Fires Burning Every town that grows on this coast gets its own boiler house, and every boiler needs coal before time runs out. If the fire goes out, the cold takes the town and factories with warehouses freeze and are lost for good. Your Expedition, Your Terms Set the expedition up the way you like: Boiler houses coal supply terms: how long a new boiler house can wait for coal Natural resource reserves: from scarce to unlimited Deadline terms: how much time the Board gives you Task reward level: how generously the sponsors pay For Railroad Corporation Veterans Although Winter Expedition can be played in a relaxed mode, it hasn’t forgotten the spirit of the first Railroad Corporation’s Challenges, such as Yellow Fever or All or Nothing. Set all four starting settings to their toughest, and it plays much like a classic Railroad Corporation Challenge. Expect a tight schedule, a lean budget and every ton of ore counted. One missed deadline can cost you a town, or the whole expedition. Win on those terms, and all four achievements are yours in a single run.
