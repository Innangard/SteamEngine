View Community Hub About This Game Idle Critter Adventure will let you go on a full Critter capturing adventure whether you're around to play or not! Choose an area and Alfa will begin running through battling trainers and capturing Critters automatically. If you're not AFK, mash the Run button to run faster and Cheer On your Critters in battle to have them attack faster. As you spend longer in each area your chances of finding Uncommon and Rare Critters will begin to increase. Construct a cohort with your captured collection of Critters to take on the Clubs which, unlike regular routes, are filled to the rafters with tough trainers. Keep defeating these underlings and the Club Leader will appear, besting them in battle will grant you a Club Pin and a step closer to the Champion's Plateau. Critters who aren't used in your team can be sent on Expeditions to passively gain Exp and money with a reward bonus for matching the participants type to the Expedition. Or put a Critter in the training room to quickly gain entire levels using Punching Bags gained from capturing Critters you already own. Upgrade the Capture Chance of your Capture Chip, the efficiency of the Healing Centre and the rewards gained from Expeditions through the Ferret Sisters Supplies website. Once powered up you can claim the Champion title and Prestige, gaining upgrades to exp and money gain, uncommon and rare encounter chance and the chance of finding Shiny Critters! Game Features Over 80 Critters to find and used in your battle team Fully Autonomous gameplay where your Critters will battle and be healed by themselves Want to get involved?
