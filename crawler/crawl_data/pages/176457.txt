View Community Hub About This Game Path Maker invites you into a charming world of 2D puzzle-platforming , where creativity and problem-solving skills reign supreme! In this unique game, each level presents you with an unfinished path , and it’s up to you to use an array of in-game editor tools to complete it and reach the coveted shining star. Features: Innovative Gameplay : Engage in a refreshing blend of puzzle-solving and platforming . Each level challenges you to creatively use blocks to build bridges, ramps, and paths while navigating through dynamic obstacles. Diverse Blocks : Start your journey with simple platform blocks, and as you progress, discover powerful items like trampolines for jump boosts , TNT for explosive shortcuts , and unique size-altering blocks that help you fit through tight spaces. Each level introduces new challenges and mechanics, keeping the gameplay fresh and exciting. Increasing Complexity : The levels grow larger and more complex as you advance, demanding strategic thinking and ingenuity. Prepare to tackle the ultimate test of your skills as you unlock and master a variety of blocks to construct your path to victory! Charming Aesthetic : Enjoy a delightful visual style that captures the essence of retro 2D platformers, complemented by a whimsical soundtrack that enhances the immersive experience. Leaderboards & Achievements : Compete with friends and fellow players globally! Challenge yourself to beat your best times on each level and unlock achievements that showcase your progress and creativity. Why You’ll Love Path Maker: Whether you’re a seasoned platformer enthusiast or a newcomer to the genre, Path Maker offers a cozy and engaging experience that invites players of all ages to think outside the box! With its simple controls and intuitive mechanics, you'll be lost in a world where every mistake is an opportunity to learn and improve. Plus, the satisfaction of successfully creating your path and reaching that shining star is unbeatable! Join the community of players who have already started building their own adventures in Path Maker ! Get ready to create, explore, and conquer - the stars are waiting for you!
