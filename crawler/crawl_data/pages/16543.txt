Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The community has already helped us a lot in shaping the current version of the game. We want to continue involving players in the development of Wish Me Well by gathering feedback on balance, discovering unimaginable builds, exploring different strategies and progression in the game.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect to remain in Early Access for 6–12 months, but this might change depending on player feedback and overall satisfaction with the product.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Early Access already includes all the core mechanics we aimed for but we still have much more planned in terms of content. This includes new levels, new bosses, new enemies, much more items and spells, additional progression options and hopefully much more.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “For the Early Access launch, the game contains core mechanics such as crafting a wand by combining spells, traversing procedurally generated dungeons, meta-progression and more. In terms of content it includes approximately 100 items (spells, passive abilities, utility items), 3 bosses, 2 NPCs, 20 enemies, 8 meta upgrades and over 10 additional upgrades.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We almost certainly will increase the price of Wish Me Well as it exits Early Access. Our goal is to ensure the price always feels fair based on the quality, variety and length of the gameplay experience.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We've been actively listening to player feedback on our Discord server and will continue to do so. However, we understand that not everyone wants to be on hundreds of servers which is why we'll also be closely monitoring the Steam forums. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Jump into the Magic Well to enter the world of books. Traverse procedurally generated arcane dungeons , fight unique ink monsters and become a powerful wizard who gathered all the wisdom. Combine magical scrolls and spells found in the books to craft a customizable magic gun as you descend deeper into the well. Create a single, devastating projectile unleashing immense destruction in a single strike or trigger a chain of projectiles one after another. Experiment with builds to reach mastery. With each run, unlock new items , spells and dungeon rooms that permanently expand future playthroughs. Level up your wizard and adapt your builds to face new challenges. Play. Improve. Repeat. Every run is a unique experience. Unlock new scrolls, discover powerful spell combinations and synergies and create builds that best suit your playstyle. Main features Create a custom magic gun using powerful spell combinations Procedurally generated levels ensure no two runs are the same Fight ink monsters and challenging bosses Unlock dozens of scrolls and magical items Easy-to-learn combat system About the Starrow Castles Team Hi! Thank you for giving us and our game a try and have fun playing it!
