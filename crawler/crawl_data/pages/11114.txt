Those moments inspired Cloudscrapers , our minimalist strategy game about building towers and scraping the clouds. It’s perfect for anyone who enjoys thoughtful gameplay without overly complex systems. You start with simple tools and build the tallest tower you can. With every climb, you will learn something new, improve, and unlock upgrades to help you build even higher next time. As your tower rises, you’ll find ancient relics and uncover mysteries buried deep within the clouds – though some of them might be better left untouched. What to expect: ▪ Bite-sized strategy gameplay that’s perfect for a relaxing session after work. ▪ Easy to pick up , with just the right amount of depth. ▪ No complicated menus or systems — just intuitive building mechanics. ▪ Meaningful progression that feels rewarding. ▪ A hand-drawn, atmospheric world with a mystery that unfolds as you reach new heights.
