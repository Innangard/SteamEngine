Handle nightly routines, keep the store in order, and assist a late-night regular in finding just what he needs — be it a warm meal, a treat for his pet, or a familiar drink that stirs old memories. Through highly intuitive interactive conversations, you'll have a chance to offer a kind word, a bit of advice, or simply listen — sometimes, even the smallest exchange can brighten someone's night! The quiet challenges of working alone can feel daunting at first, but Aunt Hina is just a phone call away with helpful guidance whenever you need it. Demo Features Enjoy a highly replayable, narrative-focused gameplay; Experience an introduction to Makoto’s observational journey; Soak in the nostalgic atmosphere of a small-town konbini in early 1990s Japan; Interact with regulars and help them find exactly what they need through intuitive, heartfelt conversations; Explore the store for subtle narrative clues hidden in plain sight; Call Aunt Hina for friendly advice or guidance whenever you need a hand.
