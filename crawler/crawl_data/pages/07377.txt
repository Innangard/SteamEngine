Turn the odds in your favor with various prizes and charms that trigger big big combos, snowballing into a dazzling lucky run. A sinister tale of addiction and escape! P.S. IS THIS GAMBLING? Hell no! CloverPit is a rogue-lite horror game, and not a slot machine simulator. Our slot machine is designed to be broken and ultimately overcome, and will never ask you for real money!
