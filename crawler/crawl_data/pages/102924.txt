Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo The cats have taken over the factory, and it’s your job to find them. Cat Over Here is a relaxing 3D hidden-object game set in a stylish black-and-white world. Explore a massive industrial landscape at your own pace. Peek behind machinery and search every corner to spot the clever hiding places of 100 cats. A Cozy Hunt Through a Factory Full of Cats Slow down, explore, and enjoy a cozy game of hide-and-seek. 100 cats are hiding somewhere inside a massive black-and-white factory. Wander through its machinery, pipes, and platforms at your own pace and see if you can find them all. No timers. No penalties. No pressure. Just you, the factory, and a lot of hidden cats. Explore Every Corner Walk freely through the factory and look carefully. Cats can be hiding behind machines, between pipes, under platforms — or right in front of you. Find Them All Some cats are easy to spot. Others might take a closer look. Explore every area and find all 100. Take Your Time There’s no time limit and no need to rush. Explore at your own pace and enjoy the distinctive black-and-white world. Think You're Done? Finding every cat isn't the end. Complete the hunt to unlock a secret bonus mission. Features 100 hidden cats to find Fully explorable 3D factory Cozy, stress-free gameplay Distinctive black-and-white art style No timers or penalties Secret post-game content Monochrome Art Style: A clean, striking black-and-white visual puzzle.
