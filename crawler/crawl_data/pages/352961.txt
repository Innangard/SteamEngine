View Community Hub About This Game An incremental monster-farming game where you catch and hatch unique creatures, unlock powerful upgrades, expand your skill tree, harvest resources, and battle in the arena to become the ultimate monster hatcher. Catch Monsters & Earn Gold Unleash powerful elemental abilities to speed up your monster catching. Master the elemental match-ups to counter wild creatures, catch them faster, and maximize your gold earnings. Buy Powerful Upgrades Unlock powerful abilities and take your own path on the skill tree. Customize your strategy and choose exactly what to upgrade: focus on boosting your Water monsters first, or max your Fire element if that is more your type. Expand your Lab & Hatch Hundreds of Monster Eggs Expand your lab and go from hatching just a few eggs to hatching hundreds of them at once. As you progress, you will be able to completely automate the whole harvesting process. Claim Victory in Arena Battles! Assemble your team and take your strongest monsters into arena battles. Actively heal and boost your monsters during battle to secure victory and earn great rewards.
