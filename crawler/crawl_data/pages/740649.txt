Shards of the Realm is a complex old-school Strategy/Tactical RPG , inspired by the classic X-Com series. It features a living open world and a wide range of deeply interconnected mechanics across both tactical and strategic layers, with over 50 hours of gameplay. Develop your stronghold, manage workers, trade, build — everything demands smart strategic planning. On the battlefield, a flexible class system, multiclassing, skill synergies, and destructible environments allow for a wide variety of tactical approaches. A deep research and crafting system lets you prepare your squad for any challenge the world throws at you. Tactical combat — physics, positioning, destruction No dice rolls or abstractions — real projectiles and physical collisions drive the combat. Positioning, cover, and interaction between units are crucial. No grid and no fixed number of actions — action points are your only limit. Spend them however you want. Anything can be used as cover : terrain, trees, even allies or enemies. Directional armor zones (front, flanks, rear) reward clever flanking and ambushes. The environment is destructible — blow up cover or open new paths. Friendly fire is enabled — even melee attacks can harm allies if you're reckless. Build your team — no templates, just your tactics Squad of up to 9 fully controllable characters 4 base classes and 8 unique subclasses with distinct playstyle , roles and abilities Multiclassing support and a skill tree with 60+ active and passive abilities Deep status effects system with differenct auras, buffs, debuffs, synergies No "correct" builds — design your heroes your way: classic tank/DPS/support setups or hybrid roles Strategic depth — your stronghold, your rules There’s no loot rain — nearly all gear is hand-crafted via research and production. Your fortress is the core of your economy, science, recruitment, and crafting Over 200 v arious items such as weapons, armors, tools, rings, trading goods to develop and produce People are your main resource — who goes to the lab, who to the forge, who to the battlefield? Six NPC factions with their own goals and rivalries. Your choices shape the political map — trade, ally, or wipe them out Day-night cycle affects visibility on both global and tactical levels Hidden dungeons with unique rewards and challenges Demonic Sieges — a global threat and a tactical opportunity. They can destroy your stronghold or help you eliminate a rival Shards of the Realm is a game where one mistake can cost you everything. But if you enjoy meaningful decisions, deep mechanics, and a world that reacts to your choices — this is the game for you.
