Move the mouse and the sword follows. When it touches an enemy, it attacks automatically—no need to mash an attack button. Choose one of the Awakening effects offered during combat. The effect you choose changes how your sword attacks and how it develops. Use rewards earned in combat to upgrade your sword. Keep upgrading it to take on stronger enemies and bosses. Once your sword is strong enough, enter the Raid. Defeat the boss and move on to the next battle.
