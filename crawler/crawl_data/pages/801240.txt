View Community Hub About This Content The official soundtrack to Annihilated — a story-rich dungeon-crawling RPG where the Hero's party has been annihilated, and you can only save ONE. Annihilated Original Soundtrack features 27 tracks composed by NINOMIYA Yuji, infused with reverence for the golden age of 90s JRPG music that first inspired him. Echoing the game's evocative yet darker fantasy world, delicate piano lines and instrumentation that shift floor by floor through the dungeon bring its world to life. The soundtrack also adopts the "suite" form — one of NINOMIYA's signature approaches. Played in order, the album becomes a single continuous work, tracing the journey from the dungeon's deepest depths back up to the surface. This is music that could only exist because of Annihilated — made for this game, and this game alone.
