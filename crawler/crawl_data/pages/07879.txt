Buy House Flipper 2 - Digital Deluxe Edition DLC $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content For those who seek even more House Flipper 2 in their lives - we prepared an exclusive Supporter Pack. It adds unique items to the game to help you finish off your freshly built houses. You will find there things like colorful stickers, a framed House Flipper game box, a figurine of the classic selling tool you know from the original game, and more! With the Supporter Pack, you can feel the flipper vibes even when you're not in the game! Enjoy your new wallpaper from the unique House Flipper 2 art set, or admire the collection of exclusive concept art. Take your time and relax while listening to the official soundtrack by Richard Williams, Weifan Chang, and Leszek Karczewski, and have a sneak peek of the creation process of the House Flipper 2 music. Learn more about Pinnacove and what inspired the dev team while making the game with the House Flipper 2 digital artbook. For full details, see below: Digital Soundtrack - Enjoy the music composed exclusively for House Flipper 2 by renowned artists: Richard Williams, Weifan Chang, and Leszek Karczewski. Relax while listening to the tunes recorded with a professional orchestra. Digital Artbook - Album with House Flipper 2 graphic assets created throughout the development, enriched with additional notes and descriptions of the game world. Concept Art set - A collection of artworks that inspired the developers who worked on creating the world of House Flipper 2. House Flipper 2 wallpapers - A selection of high-resolution loading screen images. Special Supporter Pack icon - An exclusive icon, which will appear next to the player's in-game profile, as well as on the House Flipper official Discord server. House Flipper 2 Soundtrack making of - An unpublished video showing the recording process of the official House Flipper 2 Soundtrack with the Polish Sinfonia Iuventus Orchestra. Extra in-game items related to the first House Flipper game (but not only): Snow globe - The first office from the original House Flipper, turned into a winter-themed decoration. Sticker set - a collection of colorful stickers inspired by the game. Portrait of the Flipper - A wall decoration portraying the player character from the original House Flipper game... in 3D! Selling Tool - The tool used in original House Flipper to get rid of unwanted items, in the form of an elegant figurine. Flipper's Glove - A figurine proudly presenting one of the gloves worn by the main character of House Flipper 2. House Flipper game box - The box edition of the original House Flipper game in an elegant frame. Even though these extras are not essential to having a wonderful time playing House Flipper 2, they can make decorating your houses even more enjoyable!
