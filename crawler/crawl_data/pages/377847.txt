In this tiny cozy building game, stack and arrange uniquely shaped wooden blocks to craft charming dioramas. Design cozy bakeries, elegant living rooms or quirky studios, create beautiful spaces filled with warmth and personality. Whether you’re building a serene bedroom or a vibrant cafe, Block Block Block offers a relaxing, creative space. Experiment with colors, shapes and layouts to make personalized designs that reflect your imagination. Share your dioramas with friends or simply enjoy the soothing satisfaction of bringing your ideas to life. With simple drag-and-drop tools and an intuitive interface, Block Block Block makes designing fun and effortless. Whether you’re a casual player or a design enthusiast, enjoy the relaxing satisfaction of creating and sharing your beautiful interiors with friends. Dynamic Scenes Multiple customizable scenes. Seamless Stacking Stack, layer, and arrange items effortlessly to craft intricate designs. Transform with Ease Move, rotate, and scale your items with intuitive controls to achieve the perfect placement. Shape your World Deform select items to reshape them, giving you even more creative flexibility. Expressive Customization Personalize your items by changing wood types, painting them, and adjusting reflectivity for a polished or rustic look. Interactive Realism Certain items react just like they would in real life—touch, tilt, or interact to add authentic details to your creations. Quick Duplication & Randomization Duplicate items with ease and apply randomized variations to populate your scenes quickly and natural. Studio Mode Set up cameras to frame, preview, and capture your builds with precision. Lighting System Add ambient, directional, and item-based lights to bring your creations to life.
