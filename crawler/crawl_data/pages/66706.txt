Every note brings someone closer to death. Step behind the velvet curtain of a grand opera house, where art and deception share the same stage. Summoned from the newsroom by Barbara Clark, you arrive to cover the highly anticipated production of Adrian Morro. A closed rehearsal, a prestigious assignment - nothing out of the ordinary. Until... In the middle of the most intense scene, actor Garrett Vale fires what should have been a blank shot. Instead, a real bullet tears through the air, by chance missing the diva, Evelyn Shaw. Panic spreads. This wasn't an accident! Constable Melinda Watson confirms your suspicion: the shot was planned. Precise. Timed to the music. Someone turned the performance into an execution. As you dig deeper, the theater reveals its darker side - whispers of a "cursed opera," rivalries buried in the past, and a forgotten composer's name tied to stolen music and broken lives. From dressing rooms to hidden tunnels beneath the stage, every clue pulls you closer to a truth someone is willing to kill for. And as opening night approaches, one thing becomes clear: the final act has already been written. The only question is - who survives it. FEATURES: ★ Investigate a cinematic mystery set inside a living, breathing opera house ★ Explore diverse locations: backstage, rehearsal halls, hotel rooms, and secret underground passages ★ Collect and connect clues: annotated scores, letters, photos, and hidden archive records ★ Uncover a conspiracy driven by ambition, jealousy, and revenge! Take your seat. The curtain is rising - and the final act may be your own.
