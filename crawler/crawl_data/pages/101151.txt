The Littles cannot be directly commanded or forced to build. Instead, they will gather resources and expand the village on their own. Your role is to watch over them and protect the village, helping it survive for as long as possible. You aren't all-powerful. Manage your limited energy to shield the Littles from slimes, demons, and even Giant Cat. Every prayer becomes your power. Convert your village’s faith into a custom ability tree that fits your playstyle Discover ancient artifacts and craft them into powerful charms. No two runs are the same as you combine ancient artifacts to help the Littles survive unexpected dangers.
