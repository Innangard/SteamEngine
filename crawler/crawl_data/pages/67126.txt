SPRAY, FILL, FINISH Paint layered sneaker zones with a satisfying spray. Work fast, land clean fills, and watch each finished pair drop into a box. BUILD THE DROP Complete shifts, earn cash, and invest it in better cans, faster production, and new ways to turn a messy floor into a money-making studio. CHASE GRAILS Unlock wild sneakers with animated effects and special reveal moments. Build a collection wall of increasingly ridiculous footwear.
