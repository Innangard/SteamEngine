View Community Hub About This Game The lab is a mess. Time to fix it! Equipment is buried under piles of junk, test tubes are unlabeled, and the lab animals have escaped and are running loose. Quarantine Lab Sort is a first-person game about sorting items and restoring order. Clear out huge piles of clutter, find the right place for every item, and watch as the messy laboratory gradually transforms. From Chaos to Order Microscopes, medicine, books, Petri dishes, sample jars — around 250 types of items are waiting to be put in their proper place. You won't find 500 identical objects here: as you clear the clutter, you'll discover all kinds of laboratory equipment, samples, and other items. Arrange equipment on shelves, return samples to the appropriate storage containers, and throw away the trash. What's Inside the Test Tube? The lab contains 24 types of unknown samples. Place a test tube in the centrifuge and start an analysis to discover what's inside. You only need to examine one sample of each type — after that, you'll be able to identify every test tube of that kind. Catch Them First! The escaped animals won't patiently wait for you to carry them back to their cages. Chase them through the laboratory clutter or use an ability to stop the runaways for a few seconds. Catch every animal and return it to its cage — they won't escape from there again. Warning: Radiation! Radioactive waste requires special handling. While you're holding it, your radiation meter gradually fills up. If you don't reach the disposal container in time, you'll have to drop your entire radioactive load. Plan your route, take breaks while your radiation level decreases, and upgrade your skills to carry hazardous waste for longer and recover faster. More Order, More Possibilities Earn points for every item you place correctly and spend them on upgrades. Carry more items, move faster, jump higher, and use abilities to find items and suitable shelves. With every pile you clear, your work becomes easier and the laboratory gets tidier.
