Download Victoria 3: Trains - Bonus Pack Free Download View Community Hub About This Content The power and potential of steam transportation is brought to new life for Victoria 3 in Trains Bonus Pack . This new free pack for Paradox Interactive’s grand strategy society simulation is free for all players. In this bonus pack, free for download for all Victoria 3 owners, players will see new models of classic train engines from the dawn of rail transport to the years of the iron horse, when the expansion of railways across continents signalled a new age and a smaller world. This free pack includes a total of ten new 3D models of train engines travelling across the game map: five experimental engines from the early years of steam power (such as the American John Bull and German Saxonia) and five engines from mid-century (including the classic profiles of the GNR Stirling 4-2-2 and the Jupiter.)
