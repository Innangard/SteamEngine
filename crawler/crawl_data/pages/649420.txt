Among them are both instrumental parts and atmospheric ambients. All music was written by composer Vasily Kashnikov, author of the soundtracks for the games Mor 1-2, Turgor, Knock-knock-knock. Mother's lullaby was performed by Natalia Radina. Composer assistant — Nick Balinov. The game's soundtrack is inspired by music from the films of Hayao Miyazaki, French New Wave cinema and experimental electronics. MAIN 1. When music was playing at home 2. Calm forest 3.
