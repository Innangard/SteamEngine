View Community Hub Updates & Announcements About This Game PeeVPee is a chaotic multiplayer party game where your bladder is your ammo. Drink whatever you can find, fill up, and get ready to cause problems. PEE ON EVERYTHING Pee on objects around the map to claim them and score points. Just remember, your friends are trying to do the same. PEE ON YOUR FRIENDS Why fight normally when you can pee on them? Damage players, knock them around, steal their points and make sure you're the one on top. DIFFERENT DRINKS. DIFFERENT PROBLEMS. What you drink can change your pee. Set things on fire, push players around, change colors and discover other ridiculous effects. Drink. Pee. Fight. Win.
