Dive into old cache files, chat logs, and nostalgic camcorder footage as you try to uncover what caused the shutdown of the only home you've ever known. Why did the network shut down? How many years have passed? Anon and his friends must solve the mystery of their past before the files are destroyed, or their friends will be lost, detached from the internet forever.
