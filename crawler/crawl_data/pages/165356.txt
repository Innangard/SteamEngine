Arcade mode: It consists of 8 stages. The game is cleared when you break through all the stages. You can choose from three difficulty levels. Caravan mode: 3 minute score attack. *Game scores are recorded both online and locally. You can compete with other users for scores online. *Two-player cooperative play is possible. HOW TO PLAY: Use the directional stick and three buttons: forward shot, wide shot, and speed change. You can change the button assignments as you like. Main weapon can switch between forward shot and wide shot at any time. Sub weapon can switch to 5 unique patterns by getting items.
