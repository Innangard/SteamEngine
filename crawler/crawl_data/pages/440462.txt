View Community Hub About This Game Vacations at grandmas are going well, but tonight... she's not quite herself. Here you are alone in a creaky, dimly lit house, and your only company is your beloved grandma - possessed by a sinister demon. With every step, you can feel her presence, lurking and waiting to strike. Your task is simple but deadly: search the house to find torn pages of an ancient book, put them together, and carefully place sacred objects in the right spots to complete the ritual. Each step brings you closer to freeing your grandma from the demon’s clutches. But be careful—she’s watching. Move too close, make a sound, or hesitate, and she’ll catch you. One wrong move, and it’s game over. Will you conquer the evil within and save your grandma, or fall victim to the horrors lurking within her? Dare to play? Your fate awaits.
