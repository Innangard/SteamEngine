View Community Hub About This Game This is an eerie clicker idle game with light puzzles and idle combat. Alice wakes in darkness, trapped in a strange mansion, seeking escape. The Cat God's altar drops gold, Rabbitfolk gather carrots, and dungeon monsters block the way... Can you accompany Alice to the final truth? Game Story You open your eyes in the darkness. Your mind is a blur—even your name has been erased. By the faint glow of the fireplace, you vaguely make out a girl in the mirror with golden hair and a blue dress: “Alice.” The name is reclaimed. The stale air mingles with the dust of bygone days. The shadows around you are bottomless, and the writhing unknown in the darkness seems capable of devouring even a sliver of light. The dead silence is broken by the intrusion of a cat. It lounges lazily on the altar, purring in expectation of your touch. With the crisp clink of a falling gold coin, a sealed will begins to stir... Your keen eyes discover a hidden door. From the staircase leading into the depths, a faint shriek echoes from afar—is it the wind? Or something else? You know there is no escape. The closer you get to danger, the closer you come to the truth! You brush the dust off the tea table, revealing several crystal balls. Each represents an independent world, holding both fortune and challenge… Solve the puzzles within the mansion, battle the enemies in the dungeon, hoard resources, and amass wealth. In the end, will you escape this nightmarish realm—or become a part of it? Game Features Idle gameplay + Horror atmosphere — Experience the deepest chills in the laziest way. Retro pixel art + Dark fairy tale — A uniquely styled perspective. Collect diverse materials, achieve automation , and watch your wealth grow as you sit back. Unique idle mechanic — Industries only operate when you activate the "Gaze" upon them. Repair various furniture to unlock new rooms and industries. Descend hundreds of dungeon floors and defeat terrifying monsters. Upgrade equipment to enhance combat prowess. Explore rooms, obtain items, solve puzzles , and uncover the secrets hidden deep within the mansion.
