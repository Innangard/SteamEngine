Piece Count \ Series Below are some of the details of the scene the player will build in this diorama: TYRANNOSAURUS REX (T-REX) One of the museum’s main attractions is the enormous Tyrannosaurus Rex skeleton , located right at the entrance. It is known as one of the largest carnivorous dinosaurs ever discovered. APOLLO 11 CAPSULE The command capsule is one of the three parts of the Apollo spacecraft and was responsible for housing the three astronauts during most of the Apollo 11 mission. Among its highlights is the section where the parachutes and deployment system are located. PANORAMIC ELEVATOR The panoramic elevator allows visitors to move between the museum's three floors more quickly and comfortably than by stairs, while also offering a privileged view of the exhibits during the ride. MOAI The Moai, also known as the Easter Island Heads, are more than 887 giant stone statues scattered across Easter Island , in Chile. They were carved between the years 1250 and 1500 by the Rapanui people . Just like in all other Diorama Builder dioramas: The MUSIC IS ORIGINAL, composed exclusively for this diorama. Upon completing the diorama, special animations will be automatically triggered, and the INTERACTION MODE will be unlocked, allowing you to click on various parts of the scene to see fun animations. In INTERACTION MODE, you can activate NIGHT MODE , turning on all the lights at once to enjoy a very special view of the museum. You'll have new ACHIEVEMENTS to unlock!
