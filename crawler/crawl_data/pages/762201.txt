About This Game In Little Corners , we’ve tried to capture the magic and satisfaction of sticker books. You’ll be able to build endless tiny worlds from the ground up using just your imagination, and thousands of stickers. We hope you enjoy peeling, placing and discovering all our little game has to offer. Begin with a blank space and let your creativity bloom as you bring these corners to life. Chill out to lofi beats as you satisfyingly place furniture, trinkets and fluffy animals around the room. Discover 8 little corners of fantastical worlds to get lost in, including a boisterous adventurer’s tavern, a mystical alchemist’s tower and the quarters of a ruthless sea captain. Each room features hidden stickers for you to discover, try some different combinations, who knows what you might find? We’ve included a range of customisation options including cursor size and transparency, audio settings and you can even customise your workspace with a range of backgrounds and mats. Once you’re happy with your design, use the in-game screenshot feature to share your creations with the community and get inspired for your next creation.
