What would happen if a rock concert  was invaded by a horde of zombies? Raise the bet; what if the members of the band were three witches with supernatural powers and an attitude? The answer is Rock Zombie, a non-stopping button-mashing experience that will bring you back to the golden era of the arcades, with modern 3D graphics, animations and camera angles. Rock Zombie is a game inspired by classic movies of the genre, from George A. Romero’s dead trilogy to those exploitations that populated video stores in the eighties. A perfect mix between horror, gore and a sense of humor. Enjoy different gameplay styles across 20 levels, set in iconic locations such as the graveyard, the streets of a small city or the sewers, and feel the thrill of riding a bike or driving a muscle car through zombie-infested streets. Help Zoe, Shasha and Crystal, the three leather-clad members of the band, in their fight against the forces of evil. Each one of the three witches has unique abilities and stats, including special attacks to unleash their true power. Unlock new costumes, collectibles and weapons for each of the three characters and get to know their unique personalities and fighting styles. Their tale is told through more than 300 comic strips. Rock Zombie features a hardcore soundtrack, full of rock and roll, nu metal and alt rock themes that match the dark setting of the game. Originally released in 2014, this hidden gem is the debut of awarded developer Miguel G. Corchero, whose works as creative director include One Military Camp and Citadelum.
