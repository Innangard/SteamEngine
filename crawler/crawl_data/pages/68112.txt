It never takes a step. Monsters march down the lanes toward you, your spells fire on their own, and every run comes down to what you build before the line breaks. Build it wave by wave Level up to pick new spells and upgrades, then spend your gold in the shop between waves. Turn on Monsters' Ascension and every wave the monsters get a new item too.
