View Community Hub About This Game STORY: Player controls woman character in this third person low poly shooter. Player character is called Lara and She is only who can defeat zombie armies in place called Valkeala. Valkeala is separated 4 different small open world maps, which are full of different zombies. 4 different zombie types. Player Lara can use melee knife to beat zombies or then 2 different weapons: Pistol and Rifle. Player can also collect more ammos and pineapples to get more health FEATURES: 4 different locations Different zombies: - Normal Zombie – just idle and walk slowly - Pitchfork Zombie – More aggressive, equipped with a pitchfork. - Beehive Zombie – Leaves behind a lingering damage area even after death. - Axe Zombie – Moves faster, dealing higher damage. 2 different weapons: Pistol, Rifle Melee knife attack Steam achievements.
