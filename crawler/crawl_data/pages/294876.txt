ADAPT. SURVIVE. Welcome to Memory Lost, a thrilling fast-paced cyberpunk-style top-dawn shooter where you are not just fighting enemies — you become them. A CYBERPUNK NIGHTMARE In the sprawling, dystopian city of Detraxis , ruthless corporations rule, conducting inhumane experiments on the helpless. You are an AI, created by RedSky Corporation , now fighting to overthrow your own creators. But in this brutal world, survival demands more than just firepower—it requires intelligence, strategy, and the ability to seize control of your enemies . BODY-SHIFTING COMBAT Forget traditional combat — your body is your greatest weapon . As a digital entity, you can possess enemies , instantly adapting their skills, weapons, and abilities. Jump from host to host in rapid succession, combining 77 unique weapons and devastating melee attacks to wipe out your foes. Control and switch between enemies in real time 60 enemy types & 10 powerful bosses , each with unique abilities Five upgrade trees to customize your playstyle A WORLD OF SECRETS & CHOICES Memory Lost offers more than just action — it delivers a deep, choice-driven narrative with three different endings . Your decisions will shape the fate of Detraxis. Explore a rich cyberpunk world , divided into diverse hubs and levels Engage with unique NPCs and uncover hidden stories — some tragic, some darkly humorous Hack, infiltrate, and outsmart your foes to uncover the truth behind RedSky’s experiments Hand-drawn animated cutscenes bring the world to life Control the minds of your enemies, and rewrite your destiny. The future of Detraxis is in your hands. Will you break free, or will you be just another lost memory?
