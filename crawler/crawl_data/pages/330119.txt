View Community Hub About This Game Cleaning? Did you say boring? No way! Cleaning Simulator invites you to grab your mop, vacuum cleaner, and sponge for a delightful cleaning adventure. Dirty floors, mountains of dishes, buzzing flies, dusty carpets, and chaotic rooms… They’re all waiting for you. But don’t worry—if you’ve got the right tools in hand, this mess will be sparkling clean in no time. Whether you play just to unwind or aim to complete every task and earn the title of Cleaning Master , the game lets you play your own way. Mop the floors, scrub surfaces with the sponge, load the dishwasher, take out the trash, spray the flies, vacuum the carpets, organize scattered items, and blast away stubborn grime with a pressure washer! Watching the environment transform as you clean is truly satisfying. And with HDR and ray tracing enabled, the way light gleams off those spotless surfaces… It actually makes you want to clean. 🧼 What Can You Do? 🧹 Mop the Floors : Use the mop to clean up stains and spills. 🧽 Scrub with the Sponge : Scrub walls, tables, and countertops thoroughly. 🍽️ Use the Dishwasher : Load dirty dishes, then put them away once they’re clean. 🦟 Spray the Flies : Eliminate flies and keep things hygienic. 🗑️ Collect Trash : Pick up garbage and throw it away. 📦 Organize and Arrange Items : Tidy up scattered boxes and furniture. 💦 Use the Pressure Washer : Blast away stubborn dirt with high-pressure water. 🧹 Use the Vacuum Cleaner : Vacuum the carpets and freshen up the floors. ✨ Why Should You Play? Because every cleaning job ends with that satisfying “nailed it” feeling. Because with Ray Tracing and HDR, a freshly cleaned room looks stunning. Because cleaning can be more relaxing than you'd expect. Because sometimes, just ticking tasks off the list feels really good. Whether it’s a messy student apartment, a cluttered warehouse, or a fly-infested kitchen… Every space will transform with your touch. If you’re ready, grab your sponge. The vacuum’s running—let the cleaning begin!
