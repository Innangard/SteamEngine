No time to rest. Ever. Gather materials, process them, craft items, and serve customers―― A hyper-fast shopkeeping game where every task is performed with your aim! Let a customer get away... and it’s game over. Orders keep piling up, and customers keep flooding in. Keep your shop running with efficient task prioritization, quick decisions, and precise flick aiming. Every task is controlled with your aim! First, grab passersby and bring them into your shop. ------------------ Once a customer places an order, it’s time to gather materials. Fling your mouse around to collect materials from the field at lightning speed! --------------------- Check the recipe, then get to processing. Select the materials you need quickly and process them precisely. ------------------ Finally, crafting. Combine materials with tracking aim to complete the customer’s order. And of course, deliver it as soon as it’s ready. Extreme pressure: Let them leave, and it’s over! Even when your shop starts filling up with orders, taking your hand off the mouse is not an option. Customers get angry as time passes and eventually leave. As mentioned earlier, if a customer leaves, it’s game over. The moment you lose your cool, your shop falls apart. -------------- Chaos that changes with every stage The further you progress, the more complicated the orders become. Required materials, processing steps, and priorities. Everything speeds up, putting your decision-making skills to the test. The shop is overflowing with customers, and everything’s about to fall apart! ...That’s when you should use your skills . Spend the mana you’ve collected to deal with customers. Choose the skills that suit your playstyle and save your shop from disaster. Some customers come with special orders or bring trouble of their own. Your goal is to reach the sales target set for each stage. Can you keep this chaotic tool shop running? -------------- Online Co-op Multiplayer! Work together... or get in each other’s way. Online Versus Mode! Whenever you process a customer, a new customer is sent to your opponent’s screen. Keep going until your rival’s shop falls apart! -------------- A Roguelike Game Mode, Too! The final stage and versus mode use a roguelike format. Experiment with a variety of builds. The high-difficulty mode supports leaderboards, letting you check scores and builds from players around the world.
