View Community Hub About This Content Lights, Camera, Scandal! After a series of baseless attacks on television, your party’s good name has been dragged through the mud! So, what’s a serious politician at the helm of a nation of nearly 40 million supposed to do in a situation like this? If your answer was to become a media circus monkey, then you must be out of your minds! And you'd be absolutely right... It's time to tame the unruly media and restore their rightful independence. Of course, as long as that independence remains dependent on our party... Step into the spotlight and rub shoulders with the stars! Climb the celebrity ladder and reach new levels of fame. Learn the ruthless rules of the television industry, build your influence, and do whatever it takes to get your name back on everyone's lips across the country!
