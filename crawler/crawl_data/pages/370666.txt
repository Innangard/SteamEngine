What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We chose the Early Access model because the core appeal of An Amazing Wizard lies in its " infinitely combinable spell system " and " dynamically evolving adventure ." Throughout development, we believe that real-time adjustments based on player feedback are crucial to refining and perfecting the game. To deliver a fresh and engaging experience, we decided to launch in Early Access. By incorporating player feedback during actual gameplay, we can quickly identify and address issues, continuously optimizing game mechanics and content to ensure An Amazing Wizard lives up to expectations. As such, community participation and support are invaluable to us. We sincerely hope you'll join us on this journey—your voice matters. Let’s work together to create the best possible version of An Amazing Wizard!” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We estimate that An Amazing Wizard will remain in Early Access for at least one year before the full release, allowing us to continuously gather community feedback and expand content. However, the exact duration will be flexible and may be adjusted based on development progress and player input.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to greatly expand the game's content over time. The full version is intended to feature more spells and additional enemies, aiming to enhance replayability and variety. We're also working on adding new items, NPCs, rooms, and making improvements to the user interface. On top of that, we're actively refining game balance to offer the best possible experience. While development priorities may evolve, we're committed to continuing to improve and enrich the game based on player feedback.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version offers a stable and engaging core gameplay loop, allowing players to fully experience the essence of An Amazing Wizard. It includes: 200+ permanent upgrades, 120+ spells, 200+ artifacts, 7 elemental wizards and 5 levels. However, based on ongoing player feedback, we will continuously iterate and improve the game, adding more content throughout the Early Access phase.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price may increase as we approach the full release or once the game is fully released.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We actively collect player feedback through Steam discussions, Discord, X, and other platforms. We closely monitor community discussions and personally manage our Discord groups, ensuring that bug reports, balance suggestions, and content requests are heard and addressed. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- What do you get when you combine a roguelite with metroidvania? A RogueVania ! After every death you will respawn in one of the four nature-inspired magical worlds. There are no checkpoints... A The prophesied child is born once in a millennium among the wizards : Although born in the Wizard Village, he is the only one who cannot cast any magic. Instead, a mysterious Magic Hands casts spells at will on his behalf. Someone has stolen your memories and forced you into this ever-changing world. Reclaim your memories, learn the whole truth about your power, and free yourself from this world! Myriad of builds to have! Choose from 7 unique elements, mix 100+ spells with 200+ enchantments! 200+ Artifacts and Perks will add unexpected twists to your run. Be a SUMMONER, MELEE BRAWLER, or THROW FIREBALLS with a boomerang effect. Each biome has distinctive mobs and elites . To get to the next level, you need to defeat the boss of the world . Beware, with each next iteration dimension upgrades itself and enemies learn new tricks, become faster and stronger. What? You say the boss muttered something strange before dying? Oh, don’t mind that at all, it’s probably nothing. Explore 5 worlds , with each next one being distorted by Chaos more and more. Look for hidden rooms, complete challenges, fall into trap caves, and unlock more elements to help you on your journey. Psst. Don't bully the Shopkeeper. PROCEDURALLY GENERATED WORLD - Explore a unique world every time you play. ACTION ROGUEVANIA - This means permanent deaths where you lose everything you have found, but there are lots of permanent upgrades available. 200+ PERMANENT UPGRADES - Upgrade your starting items or statistics, add more elements to the world, increase your chances of finding better items and many more! 7 PLAYABLE ELEMENT WIZARD – Wheel the power of seven unique elements, each offering distinct abilities and combinations to match your playstyle. ENCHANT YOUR SPELLS - Search for Astral Forges to add enchantments to all your Spells and Magic Weapons to add some special effects to them! 200+ UNIQUE ARTIFACTS - Every artifact has it's own special use. A lot of them change your Basic Spell behavior or add special abilities. Literally countless combinations. 120+ POWERFUL SPELLS - Cast Fire Balls or Tornados, Summon Creatures, Slow Time, Create Gravity Zones and many, many more... DOZENS OF MAGIC WEAPONS - Use one of many Magic Weapons to help you survive! 50+ CHALLENGES - Test your skills by completing the trials of the Goddess of Death! 5 NONLINEAR LEVELS - Only 5 levels? Yes, but each time you die you will respawn in one of the four nature-inspired worlds and face other enemies based on the current biome. Levels order is never the same! FEW DIFFICULTY LEVELS - Every time you kill the end boss, you will gain the possibility to change the difficulty level and face stronger foes. MANY BOSSES - After every level you will have to face a merciless boss. LEVEL UP YOUR CHARACTER - Gain Experience Points for killing enemies. Select one of dozens of perks after leveling up to make you even stronger! A LOT OF SECRETS AND MYSTERIES TO DISCOVER - No spoilers! HOURS OF FUN - This one depends on your skill. Something about 50 - 80 hours or even more!
