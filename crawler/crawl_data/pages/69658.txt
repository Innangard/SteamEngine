In this free 4 player multiplayer mode, work as a team to save clients from fire and defuse bombs planted by rival corporate spies. But keep an eye on those teammates... not all of them can be trusted. One of you, the secret Hosr agent, will work against the brave Embr Respondrs. Their new goal? Make you fail your mission. Careful though, sometimes your team’s suspicious actions may just be an innocent mistake. Intentional detonation, or failed defusal? Client ran into the fire, or into the Hosr agent’s axe?
