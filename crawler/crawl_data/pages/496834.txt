Estimated playtime is between 40 minutes and 1 hour. 🕳️ About the Game Locked In My Darkness is a first-person psychological horror game set within the claustrophobic walls of a haunting apartment complex. You awaken in an unfamiliar space, surrounded by silence, dread, and the unsettling feeling that something is terribly wrong. As you explore your surroundings, reality begins to shift—walls bleed into a twisted red dimension , and the line between hallucination and truth blurs. With each step, you're drawn deeper into the darkness, piecing together clues and solving puzzles to uncover a way out... if there is one. “After playing through the Locked In My Darkness Demo, I am excited about this upcoming indie horror game.” — Lawod 🧠 Key Features Psychological Terror: A slow-burning, story-rich horror experience designed to keep you on edge. Expect disturbing imagery, a chilling atmosphere, and unsettling audio design. Atmospheric World: Explore an apartment complex that transforms around you—shifting between bleak realism and surreal horror as the story progresses. Puzzle-Driven Exploration: Search for hidden clues and solve light puzzles to progress through your journey. Single-Sitting Experience: The game is meant to be completed in one go, offering a compact but intense psychological horror experience. No Combat, Just Dread: This is not a fast-paced horror shooter. The fear comes from what you see, what you hear—and what you imagine might be waiting around the corner. 🔴 Can you escape before the red consumes everything? Face your fear. Discover the truth. But don’t look back...
