View Community Hub About This Game As a stickman, you find yourself in a mysterious magical world. Though you stand out among other creatures, this world is already accustomed to all sorts of strange beings. Choose your own way of life, explore towns, villages, and the vast wilderness, and experience the richness of this world! "Stickman Strikes: Conquer Fantasy World" draws inspiration from the combat systems of "Meteor Butterfly And a Blade" and "Sekiro," but with simpler controls.You can unleash smooth and stylish combat moves or recruit companions for team battles. Whether fighting solo or in a group, the battles are always thrilling and challenging! You can accept various quests in towns, from delivering messages to teaching drunks a lesson, to escorting goods. Each quest takes you on a new journey, exploring multiple towns and villages, experiencing rich stories and diverse adventures. Watch out for bandits on your travels and use your combat skills to take them down! As the leader of your team, you must not only lead them in battles but also manage their needs. Buy food and water, cook in the wild, bathe in town bathhouses, handle hygiene in the wild, and restore energy and morale through sleep. Make your game experience more realistic and immersive! In the tavern, you can recruit new team members and equip them with necessary weapons and gear. These items can be bought from shops or crafted by gathering resources. Collect food, fruits, vegetables, minerals, and more in the wild. Each town has different resource distributions, adding more strategic choices to your adventure! Future Updates! Future versions of "Stickman Strikes: Conquer Fantasy World" will include more features: Magic System: Implementing magic skills in combat for more battle variations. Dungeon System: Explore tombs, caves, and ruins on the map to uncover hidden stories and treasures. Faction System: Conquer towns, establish your own faction. Trade System: Buy caravans to automatically trade between towns. Agriculture System: Buy land, grow crops, and interact with the trade system. Industry System: Build factories in towns to manufacture goods. Guild System: Join or create mercenary guilds, thief guilds, etc., and enjoy guild benefits and quests. Get ready for this epic adventure and become a legendary stickman! Currently, the game is developed solely by me, filled with creativity and ideas that I hope to realize step by step. If you’re interested in game design or providing feedback on testing, join our Discord to discuss. Thank you all for your support!
