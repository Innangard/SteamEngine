Help Chloe outsmart the toy villain Joe and rescue her friends across 72 handmade puzzles. Every level looks simple and playful with one goal: get Chloe and her partner to the red targets. But each puzzle has its own twist, testing your strategy, Sokoban skills and timing as you figure out how the mechanical toys work. Stuck? Every level comes with a hint. WHAT'S IN THE BOX? All miniatures! Chloe and Bunny, Arvi, Dummy, the Guards, Kitty, Teddy and the playful toys. Explosive bombs, tunnels, cannons, sacks, flying balloons, a claw machine, gates and much more. Each new mechanic is introduced with its own card the first time you meet it. PLAY TOGETHER Play solo and switch between Chloe and her partner, or invite a friend: in Act 2 a second player just presses any button on a controller or keyboard to drop in. Perfect for the couch or Steam Remote Play Together. TOY WORLD Explore the hub, collect gold coins and buy colourful shirts from the Genie. Change your look any time from the pause menu.
