Or take control of the monsters in PvP game and prevent the other team from stealing all the gold from the castle. Lured by rumors of untold treasures in an abandoned castle, you stumble upon a secret tunnel some prisoners used to escape from its dungeon. Your quest is to find the fabled Golden Duck, a magic artifact of unlimited power. Single player campaign with 5 playable heroes 2-4 player co-op campaign PvP game: 1v1, 2v1, 2v2 modes for 2-4 players 100 handcrafted levels Run around the maze and collect all the gold while being chased by spooky monsters. Complete the levels before the time runs out to get extra lives. Lure monsters into traps and away from treasure. Hide and sneak behind their backs. Power Gem allows you to chase down and banish the enemies Ice Bomb freezes all the enemies on the screen Clover Leaf makes you invisible Pick up keys to unlock doors Use secret passages when enemies are closing in In versus games, players side with Team Heroes or Team Monsters. Heroes have to collect all the gold, while monsters have to chase them down before the time runs out. Players can use powerups to escape tough situations.
