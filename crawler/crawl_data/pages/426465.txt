Rise of the White Company Prove yourself a capable soldier and join the company of John Hawkwood — an aspiring captain in the English king's service who is so skilled at waging war that European nobles line up before him, offering chests full of gold for his services. Friends Forever Why part ways with friends when you can stay together? Join Bartholomew and Jeanne as they escape from Cambrai to settle down in Paris. Navigate the medieval metropolis and hustle hard to find ways to make coin, earn reputation and connect with influential people. Balkan Crusade Enter the service of Amadeus VI, Count of Savoy — a man of extravagant character, exquisite taste, and extraordinary talents, as well as a blood relative of the Byzantine Emperor, who, when asked by his cousin for help, never leaves a plea unanswered. Prove your worth to the count, then join him on his crusade to the Balkans against the Ottoman hordes in a desperate attempt to save the once-great but now crumbling Byzantium. Ungraceful and Inglorious Continue the journey of Grace and Glory, who — after miserably failing the most important contract of their lives and subsequently turning from hunters into prey themselves — now attempt to seize back the initiative.
