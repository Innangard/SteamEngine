Martin Stig Andersen weaves together the work of Gildaa, The Toxic Avenger, and his own compositions to accompany Aava on her ascent of Mount Kami. The ambient, ethereal score evokes the accomplishments of the ascent and the vivid memories forged along the way. The original music from the game is a collaboration between Martin Stig Andersen, Cairn audio director well known for creating the immersive soundtracks of LIMBO, INSIDE and Control, Gildaa, a rising talent on the French music scene with a debut album releasing later this year, and The Toxic Avenger — a key figure of the French electronic music scene you will recognize from Furi's soundtrack. 11 tracks, 41 minutes.
