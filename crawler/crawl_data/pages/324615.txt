About This Game BuGarden is a tiny garden in the corner of your screen, where lovely flowers sway happily in the sunlight you bring them! Plant seeds and watch them slowly bloom as you work, study, or just hang out with your little snail companion. Use water and fertilizer to nourish your seeds. Feeling lazy? No worries – the little snail will help water them for you too! As your garden grows you’ll unlock new seeds and tools, and even decorations for your desktop. Rest assured – since this is a desktop companion, you can hide all the annoying stuff with a single click, leaving only your favorite flowers quietly keeping you company.
