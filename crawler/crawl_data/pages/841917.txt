Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game has been in development for several years, and its core gameplay loop is already solid. Some players have already spent hundreds of hours playing the demo. The demo has been updated almost daily since its release, and I plan to continue working closely with the community throughout Early Access.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I expect the game to remain in Early Access for approximately 3 to 9 months. The game is already close to the experience I want to deliver, but I will use Early Access to gather feedback and make improvements based on what the community wants and expects. As a player, I dislike Early Access games that remain unfinished for years or make unrealistic promises. My goal is to avoid that and keep the development process transparent and focused.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The game already contains a good amount of content, but I still have several areas that I would like to expand. Current plans include completing the Dungeon Complex, expanding the asynchronous multiplayer features, adding more playable classes and continuing to increase the number of abilities and spells.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “- Arena combat with two main playstyles: control your captain directly or autobattle. - 30 handcrafted rival teams. - 50+ mercenary classes. - About 100 champions. - More than 700 different abilities and spells. - Fully simulated markets, leagues, and an infinite seasonal gameplay loop. - Scout rivals, find Shiny fighters and track your roster's growth with the Codex. - PvE modes such as Defend the Iron Gate and Hall of Legends challenges. - Different cups and tournaments: Chaos Cup free-for-alls and Allstar events, plus Draft Mode. - Asynchronous multiplayer through the Challenge Tower, with both Ranked and Unranked game modes. - Local co-op and Steam Remote Play support.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “There are currently no confirmed plans to change the price, but it may increase as more content and features are added during Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “As playtesters and demo players can confirm, I am highly receptive to feedback and greatly value the community’s ideas and opinions. The game would not be nearly as good as it is today without all the thoughtful suggestions and feedback I have received.
