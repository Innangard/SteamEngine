View Community Hub About This Content Operation: Rolling Stone DLC Prepare for intense jungle warfare with the Operation: Rolling Stone DLC for Operation: Harsh Doorstop . Set against the backdrop of the Vietnam War , this expansion drops players into the heart of Tan Binh , where dense vegetation, hidden threats, and asymmetrical combat define every firefight. Whether you're launching coordinated assaults or defending isolated outposts, Operation: Rolling Stone delivers brutal, close-quarters combat across an unforgiving battlefield where the terrain is as much an enemy as the opposing force. Key Features: 🌴 Vietnam War Jungle Combat: Battle through the thick jungles, rice paddies, and rural villages of Tan Binh , a meticulously designed environment that captures the unique challenges of Vietnam-era warfare—where ambushes are constant and visibility is limited. 🪖 Authentic Weapons and Factions: Fight with era-accurate weapons and equipment, from M16s and AK-47s to rocket and grenade launchers , while playing as either U.S. forces or local guerrilla fighters. 🎯 Exclusive Dynamic Campaign Mode: Take part in a persistent conflict across single-player , co-op , and multiplayer modes where control of the battlefield shifts based on your team's success. Secure objectives, disrupt supply lines, and hold your ground as the frontlines evolve with every match. Welcome to the Jungle. The Operation: Rolling Stone DLC transforms Operation: Harsh Doorstop into a high-stakes, tactical Vietnam War experience. With immersive environments, authentic weaponry, and intense asymmetrical combat, this is more than just a fight—it's survival.
