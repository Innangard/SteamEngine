They pad along the tops of your open windows, curl up and sleep soundly in some corner, and perk up—"what's that?"—when your mouse moves. At 2 in the morning they'll suddenly tear across the screen at full speed. Yes, they really do get the zoomies. If you've ever had a cat, you'll probably just get it. What these cats are like The whole desktop is their walking course — they stroll leisurely all over your desktop Jumping onto windows — they explore your workspace as they please Curious about the cursor — when the mouse moves, they might quietly sidle up… or might not Click to play with them — one click to catch their eye, two for a reaction, three and they just might actually play with you Their mood shifts with the time of day — liveliest at dawn and dusk, and utterly lazy at midday (this is what's called being "crepuscular") Late-night zoomies — a sudden sprint around 1–4 a.m.; count yourself lucky to catch it Fine with a big screen — no matter how many monitors you have, every one becomes part of the cats' walking course They usually stay out of your way, but every now and then they'll bat at your cursor. Please cut them some slack there. They're cats, after all. If you find a cat you love, give it a name and make it your very own. Adopt two cats for free Sill Cats is free to start. The first two you can adopt are a sleek Abyssinian and a dashing Tuxedo. Once you're smitten, you can grow the family through breed packs. Even mulling over which one to welcome first is part of the fun. By default, two cats are shown on screen. If you'd like just one, click the cat signboard, or right-click the icon in the Windows system tray to open the settings menu, and change it under "Number of Cats." About additional cats Purchasing DLC adds more cat looks and breeds you can choose from. DLC adds the cat looks and breeds you can choose from. It does not raise the maximum number of cats that can appear on screen at once. Sill Cats also offers a variety of cat toy DLCs to liven up your screen. There's a free "Basic Toy Pack" as well, so please start there. How to set things up Changing the cat breed, quitting the app, and adjusting the various settings are all done from the settings menu. Click the cat signboard, or right-click the Sill Cats icon in the system tray, to open the settings menu. If you can't find the icon, check the hidden icons via the "^" button at the bottom-right of the screen. Up to two cats can be shown at once. ※ If you spot a bug, it would be a great help if you reported it on the discussion boards in the "Community Hub."
