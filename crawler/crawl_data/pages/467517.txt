Somewhat rewarding to beat.” 7/10 – https://www.united-critics.com/post/gold-for-retirement About This Game Gold for Retirement - is a hardcore platformer that will challenge you and push your skills to the limit! Embark on an exciting adventure where every gold coin brings you one step closer to a carefree retirement. Explore dozens of levels filled with tough challenges and dangerous traps.Enjoy stylish pixel art that immerses you in the atmosphere of classic platformers, and lose yourself in the game’s world thanks to its incredible soundtrack. But the main highlight - is the unique flying mechanic! Your knight is equipped with a special device built into their helmet, allowing them to soar, glide, and overcome even the most difficult obstacles. You are a knight, albeit not so young as you once were, but your heart is still full of nobility and your sword is still sharpened to a fine point. Every time you put on your armour, you experience a familiar thrill - your past is full of heroic deeds and adventures, and new quests await you in the future. You have rescued beauties out of the paws of terrible dragons, fought mighty beasts, and even faced small but cunning monsters. But behind these exploits lies one big truth: gold. Yes, gold! It gleams brightly in the sun, and every coin sound delights your ear. Who else would think of a carefree old age if not you? Which banner or medal would help you rest easy in the decline of your years? Your old legends? Or that humble fee the kingdom ordered to pay you for your hazardous adventures? Alas, neither can provide you with a peaceful old age. Meanwhile, your knighthood privileges are beginning to crack like the armour that has served you for more than a decade. Your purpose is clear: it's time to hit the road, to take enough gold with you to end your days in luxury and comfort. This year will be a year of good fortune - you are about to set out on an exciting journey, where the sword will be drawn only for defence, and the search for wealth will become your primary mission in life.
