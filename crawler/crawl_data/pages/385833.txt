Explore downtown Boston and the surrounding areas six years after the apocalypse. Point and Click your way through multiple areas including - The city, abandoned buildings, parks, the cemetery, pitch black sewers, the library, the medical research facility and more. Stay alive or you're dead for good. Eaten Alive uses a perma-death system, there's no loading or saving. You have one life. Decide your own fate, make choices and try to stay alive. Find and help other survivors, loot them, kill them or just insult them, it's your choice! Find and collect records, use them at the jukebox and enjoy original music.
