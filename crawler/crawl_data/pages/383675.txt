When their final experiment went cataclysmic, the Engine shattered into 33,333 fragments scattered across reality. You are the explorer hunting these fragments. Every level is a new piece of the puzzle. Every clear brings you closer to rebuilding creation itself. Pentad - Eons of Time is not just another Tetris clone. It’s a fusion of retro puzzle mastery and cosmic ambition — “ AI-Retro ” at its finest. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: While developing the game we used various AI software to generate background images for our levels. Overall, we created more than 20,000 image out of which we selected the ones to be used in this first game release. We then further edited these / combined them in a photo editor to create the final background renderings seen in the game. Some of these images are also visible on the Steam Store page. However, the game itself does not use on-the-fly generative AI while the game is running. All AI images were pre-rendered during the development phase. We also used AI to generate background music for all levels. We create over two hundred hours of such music, and then edited these to better suite our needs. The game itself does not contain any real time generative AI for music.
