Chop Chains is a relaxing incremental game where everything revolves around trees! Hover over them with your cursor to deal damage automatically. Every tree that touches your axe takes damage — simple, satisfying, and addictive! 🌳 Gather Wood! Chopped trees drop wood. The more you chop, the more you collect! Discover different types of wood, each with its own value and rarity. 🌲 The Skill Tree Spend your wood to unlock permanent upgrades in the Skill Tree . Increase your power, axe size, luck, and much more to become the ultimate lumberjack! 🪄 Unlock Powers! Powers give you new abilities and boost your wood collection! Experiment with different combinations to find the most powerful upgrades and supercharge your plains adventures. ♾️ Unlock the Void Skill Tree! Once unlocked, the Void Skill Tree offers a new way to unlock skills in the Normal Skill Tree. 🪓 Craft New Axes! Combine different types of wood to craft new axes , each offering: Better stats And sometimes… a unique power! Keep progressing to unlock legendary axes and slice through forests at lightning speed! 🌿 The Forest Once unlocked, The Forest automatically generates wood. It’s the idle mechanic of Chop Chains ! 🌱 Leaderboards Show everyone who's the best Chopper! ⏱️ Time Attack Mode Race against the clock and prove you're the fastest Chopper!
