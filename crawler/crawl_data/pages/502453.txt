Torchance 6 returns to the virtues of classic football managers with an eye on the essentials. Back to the roots: Torchance 6 offers everything a good football manager needs. Be it advertising, merchandising, transfer market, statistics, stadium expansion, expansion of the stadium environment, youth development, training camps, training, tactics, etc. Torchance 6 offers numerous playing options: Play in international leagues, master challenges, watch the game, etc. in text mode and negotiate, train and manage in detail.
