Learn more Leaving Early Access: Q4 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Cornucopia brings together the things I love about farming games with ideas inspired by my years homesteading in rural Canada. Early Access lets me improve it with people who are actually playing: growing crops, raising animals, getting to know the town, and finding things I can make better. The core experience is playable today. Development continues on improvements, additional content, and fixes, with player feedback helping me decide what needs attention most.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I’m currently targeting late 2027 for version 1.0. This is an estimate, and it may change as development progresses. I’ll share progress and any changes to that target through Steam announcements. Please base your purchase on the game available today, rather than on a release date or a planned feature.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “For version 1.0, I plan to refine the existing experience and expand it with additional content and improvements to the world, progression, and everyday play. The roadmap outlines the current direction, but its scope and priorities may change. Some larger additions, including the Tropical Island, are planned for after version 1.0. The roadmap is not a list of features that must all be completed before leaving Early Access.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “You can build and expand your farm, grow crops, raise and breed animals, form friendships, date, marry, and raise a family. You can also fish, cook, decorate, take part in seasonal festivals, and explore dungeons with enemies, bosses, and treasure. The game has no forced bedtime and includes adjustable game speed. It supports keyboard and mouse and controllers, and is Steam Deck Verified. Cornucopia is currently single-player. Multiplayer co-op is in development and is not included in the current release. The game is still being developed, so you may encounter bugs, rough edges, and changes to existing systems. The About This Game section describes the current features, and Steam announcements document released updates.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price may increase as the game develops, but I haven’t made a final decision about launch pricing. I’ll announce any planned increase ahead of time so players know what to expect.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I read feedback on the Steam discussion boards and in the Cornucopia Discord, and use community polls to help understand what players want most. Bug reports, suggestions, and descriptions of your experience help me identify confusing mechanics, prioritize improvements, and decide where to focus development. I can’t include every request, but your feedback helps shape the game. About This Game ✨ Grow the farm. Win their hearts. Raid the depths below. ✨ A cozy 2.5D farm-life RPG with 34+ romances and 50+ characters, magical animals you can breed, living soil that rewards a green thumb, and a century-old mystery waiting right under your feet. No forced bedtime. No stress. Your dream life, your pace. Farm, build, romance, raise magical animals, and fight through the dungeons below, all in one warm, handmade world. Who you marry and how you mix your soil both change how your story goes. The mystery: Thawed after a century, you wake among curious villagers. A camera-loving conspiracy theorist shadows your every step. Deep below, lost relics and gemstone fruits are waiting. Romance 34+ characters: date, marry, and start a family. Living soil: feed your land and grow legendary crops. Easy to plant, deep to master. Magical animals: hatch, breed, and pass rare traits down the generations. Dungeons and bosses: farm by day, then grab a weapon and dive the caves for loot. Your farm, your layout: upgrade, expand, decorate, or move into a new home entirely. No bedtime: play all night, set your own speed, never pass out. Play At Your Own Speed Cornucopia is built to be low-pressure. You decide how it plays: No bedtime pressure: stay up as long as you want. No forced sleep, no pass-outs. Your camera, your call: adjust tilt, zoom, and FOV. Flatten it into a paper storybook, or angle it like a classic top-down farm. Overflow inventory: extra items slip straight into storage. No more full-bag frustration. Tools level by use: your watering can and axe get stronger just from using them. Adjustable game speed: slow it down to soak it in, or speed it up to get more done. 97 achievements and 5 badges: earn them at your own pace, or ignore them and just wander. Frequent updates and polls: an active community helps decide what gets built next. Breed Animals With Real Genetics From tiny chicks to hardworking cows, every animal levels up and gets stronger. Chop trees with a horse, race a wild chicken, or bond with a slime. Breed pets across generations for rare colors and patterns. Why settle for an ordinary cow when you can raise a midnight-blue one? Build the Farm You Want Upgrade your house, barn, coop, and shed, expand your soil, and repair the greenhouse for big bonuses. Build new spots like the Hot Springs, or move into the Beach Villa, the Treehouse, or the Mountain Lodge. Interiors are yours to decorate, and 90+ hats add stat boosts or just look good. 50+ Villagers With Lives of Their Own Build bonds through gifts and chats, and redecorate their rooms once you're close. Villagers gossip, go on dates, and grow their own friendships whether you're watching or not. Some of them will fall for you. 74 Crops, 50 Trees, 15 Berry Bushes, and Living Soil Your soil is alive. Balance nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium with compost to lift growth, yield, and rare seasonal boosts. Chase legendary crops like wasabi and greenhouse watermelon hybrids, or take it slow and let nature do its thing. Season, soil, and compost all shape the harvest. Simple to plant, deep enough to obsess over. Marry, Then Raise a Magical Kid Marry one of 34+ eligible characters, from spirited youths to wise elders, and raise a child who grows from baby to toddler to kid. Your partner decides what they become: wizard kids, kappa offspring, spirit children, goddess babies, and more. Fishing and a 10-Game Arcade Hook quirky catches with a bounce-style fishing system, then hit the Arcade for 10+ mini-games: coin-pushers, claw machines, shooters, and pet races. There's always something small to do between harvests. Collect Cards for Permanent Buffs Cards give you buffs like Double Jump, Underwater Breathing, and Auto-Revive. Finish monster quests and neighbor tasks for rewards, and dig up economic perks like Shop Discounts, Faster Refining, or More Rare Fish. New cards turn up each run. Every Save Rolls Differently Random birthdays and gifts: each save shuffles favorite items and celebrations. Animals with personality: pets arrive with likes, dislikes, and hidden abilities you can breed forward. Unique gear: every weapon and hat rolls its own stats. Procedural dungeons: rare enemies, secret bosses, and treasure rooms in new places each time. A 2.5D World You Can Tilt and Zoom Zoom in on the tiny farming details, or pull back to plan the next expansion. Mix crops, orchard trees, berry bushes, and paths into a layout that's yours. Multiple regions, four seasons, and 72 original tracks that shift with the weather and the time of day. Fast Travel and a Map That Shows Everyone Discover waypoints and zip around town in a tap. The map shows where every villager is right now, so delivering a gift or making a festival is never guesswork. Dungeons, 11+ Weapon Types, and Bosses Take on the caves with 11+ weapon types and gear that rolls its own stats. Explore gently at your own pace, or push deeper for rare loot, unique rooms, and boss fights. Every weapon feels different, and every third hit lands a finisher. Prefer it gentle? Peaceful mode means monsters never start a fight. Festivals Every Season From the Chicken Festival to Fireworks Night on the mountaintop, every seasonal event has its own gameplay and music. Enter contests, bid on rare animals and decorations at the Auction House, and bond with villagers along the way. 200+ Recipes and a Cooking Festival Cook 200+ dishes from ingredients and monster drops. Choose cookware like a Frying Pan, Bain Marie, or Fusion Cooker, each with its own boost to dish value. Then show off at the Cooking Festival in Chef Simon's restaurant for rewards.
