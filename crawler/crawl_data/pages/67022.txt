Vanligvis $2.00 – tilbudspris: $1.80 Legg i handlevogn Vis samfunnssentral Om spillet One direction: up A trampoline in a backyard, and a curious slime on top of it. Every bounce carries it a little higher: over the fence, over the trees, over the clouds, then over the sky itself. Trampoline to Tomorrow is a climbing game played in short runs: each run lasts a few minutes, and each run sends you into the next one a little stronger. Bounce, aim, fall You steer the slime left and right. Land on the mat's sweet spot, thread the rings, read the wind and the gravity. Every landing costs durability; when it runs out the run is over and what you earned goes into the vault. Rings and multipliers The sky is not empty. Boost rings push you upward, rings taken back to back grow your multiplier, and a glowing guide line shows the way. Every meter gained up high is more money, and every coin is more power for the next run. Cards Climbing raises your level, and every level deals you a hand of cards to pick from; the card you pick is permanent. Catch a card pack drifting in the air and you flip a card: its rarity decides how strong the slime's dance will be. Keep the rhythm, multiply the reward. The upgrade tree When a run ends, go back to the vault and open a branch: a stronger mat, a wider sweet spot, more rings, longer runs. Each branch also decides what your cards can improve. Eighty-one nodes, one question: how far this time? The escape line Every stop has an escape line. The moment you cross it, gravity lets go, everything goes quiet, and the route stretches on to the next stop. Seventeen stops Earth, the Moon, Deimos, Phobos, Mars, Europa, Io, Jupiter, Titan, Enceladus, Saturn, Ariel, Miranda, Uranus, Triton, Neptune, Pluto. Each has its own gravity, its own sky, its own ground and its own surprises: Earth hanging in the Moon's sky, Saturn through Titan's orange haze, Charon right next door on Pluto. And at the very end, a moment to turn around and look back at the Sun. Features Short runs, permanent progress: a few minutes each, and every run makes the next one stronger Real gravity: it weakens as you climb, differently at all seventeen stops Day cycle, weather and wildlife: birds, balloons, cloud decks, lamps at night 29 achievements Eight languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese Metric and imperial units No art or audio files: everything you see and hear is generated by the game's own code while you play Opplysning om AI-generert innhold Utviklerne beskriver hvordan spillet bruker AI-generert innhold slik: An AI assistant helped write the code, the translations and the store page texts; everything was reviewed and tested by the developer. All in-game visuals and sounds are generated procedurally by the game's own code. AI is not used at runtime.
