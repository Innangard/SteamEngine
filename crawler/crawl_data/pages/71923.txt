The cursor pulses gravity rhythmically. Place it over the particles to merge them together. Once large enough, the particles will be pulled into the planet. Earn currency and spend it on incremental upgrades via the skill tree. Unlock the 18 upgrades varying from Asteroids to Gravity Beams. Merge enough particles to make a planet. Features 4 main levels, a relaxing endless mode and an experimental 3 mode allowing you to choose any 3 upgrades each round. Playtime around 1-2 hours.
