View Community Hub About This Game Echoes of Mora is an atmospheric narrative adventure about a village lost to the water and a girl looking for her brother. Dive into the sunken ruins of a small, insular community, find hidden objects and traces of its people, and piece together what really happened here. Every discovery brings you closer to Mora and the mystery surrounding her missing brother. After her brother disappears near a cursed violet lake, young Mora sets out to find him. Days later, only the drowned remains of her village tell of what followed. That is where your story begins: drifting through flooded streets and silent houses, searching for answers among what was left behind. But not everything down here was lost. Hidden in the depths lie Echoes, portals into the days before the flood. Each Echo is sealed, its fragments scattered across the sunken world, hidden in quiet corners or behind small puzzles. Find them, and the past opens up to you: see the village as it once was, full of light, voices, and dramatic revelations. There, Mora is the only one who can see you. Answer her questions, point her toward what she needs to notice, and share your discoveries with her across time. Your findings become her guidance, and with every Echo, you follow her brother's trail a little further. How Mora's story ends is up to you. Navigate the flooded landscape at your own pace with keyboard and mouse or controller. There are no timers and no dangers, just a world full of secret places, lost belongings, and stories waiting to be found. Beyond the Echoes, keep an eye out for Traces, lost memories lingering across the village that let you listen in on moments long past. Some follow the events of the story, others reach further back, to the everyday lives of the villagers and the boy at the heart of it all.
