Features Explore a secret-filled land in this 3D Action-Adventure, where every level is hidden in plain sight! Travel and fight through a vast, unmarked overworld. The next surprise is only a single search away! "Bump the World!" with intuitive Bumpslash Combat . Walk into enemies to attack - no button needed! Fast-paced action levels AND exploration-and-lore focused levels will test your skills. Explore fearsome Fae forests, condemned buildings, trap-filled farms, spiritual mountains sites, mines, towns, and more! Fierce and uniquely designed bosses and enemies that will test your Bumpslash skills Follow wandering ex-soldier, Tets Kinoshta, as he allies with the Angel, Arkas Gemini, attempting to breach the storm guarding Throne: the Angel's crashed mothership. Will they unlock its secrets? Plenty of levels, each offering glimpses into the Fae, Angels and Humans living together in the country of Era. Mix up combat with subweapons and a gun, and visit towns to shop for items. Beat levels to get Scales and level up! On average, a first playthrough takes 20-30 hours, depending on how much of the world you want to explore and how fast you play. (Rushed playthroughs may be less - I've seen as low as 15-16, thorough playthroughs may be over 30) Low-difficulty setting for Beginner Bumpslashers, and nightmarish settings for the deranged! From Anodyne 2 and Sephonie's devs, Melos and Marina.
