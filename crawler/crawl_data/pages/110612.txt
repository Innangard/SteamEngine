There’s no steering - just throttle and brake. Every corner becomes a test of timing, precision, and nerve as you balance on the edge of grip. Full Simulation, Focused Control Beneath its minimalist inputs lies a serious racing simulation. Tire wear, heat, fuel load, dirty air, and car balance all shape your decisions. Push too hard and pay the price - hold the limit and gain the edge. Drivers with Personality Race against AI opponents with distinct personalities and behaviors. Aggressive rivals, consistent performers, and mistake-prone drivers all share the same physics as you - creating unpredictable, human-like racing. A Career That Remembers Build your legacy across seasons in a dynamic career mode. Earn your seat, manage your reputation, and navigate the consequences of every on-track decision in a world that evolves with you. Easy to Start, Hard to Master Jump into a race in seconds - but shaving tenths off your lap time takes skill, discipline, and smart strategy. Whether it’s a quick session or a long championship run, every race matters.
