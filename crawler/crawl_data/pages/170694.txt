Build the peasant's house, the wealthy merchant's mansion, and the monumental temple of the gods. From simple mud huts to stone wonders, each project will transport you to a world of sand, the Nile, and eternity. Learn about the everyday life, social contrasts, and spirituality of one of the oldest civilisations. Learn about ingenuity, engineering, and symbolism by building structures that have stood the test of time. Because in Egypt, every stone has a story to tell, and every house has the soul of its inhabitants. ✨ 🏠 Peasant's house – shelter in the sun A simple mud hut of an Egyptian peasant, built of clay, reeds, and wood. One room, a hearth, and a small courtyard show how much can be created with few resources. It is a lesson in patience, resourcefulness, and the everyday life of ordinary people on the Nile. 🏛️ The villa of a wealthy townsman – luxury under the palm trees. The residence of an Egyptian official is full of decorations, rooms, and also with a garden and a swimming pool. Ventilation, a water tank and the coolness of the walls allow you to survive the heat, and the building itself contrasts with the simple peasant's houses in Egypt. ⛪ Egyptian temple – home of the gods A monumental stone structure, decorated with columns, pylons, and hieroglyphs. Dedicated to the gods, it exudes an aura of mysticism. Its orientation and construction teach astronomy, spirituality, and the power of Egyptian engineering.
