One day, a mysterious client enters your shop and hands you an old, dusty journal, pleading for you to uncover the story hidden within its pages. You're the Inkwellers, skilled adventurers who dive into journals to reveal their true meanings. Intrigued by the challenge, you and your partner decide to take on the task and dive into the journal's world. The journal holds dark tales of betrayal, corruption, and a sinister force that has trapped a powerful figure in an endless cycle of misery. As you uncover long-buried secrets, you'll need to work together to solve complex puzzles and reveal the truth. The fate of those trapped within - and perhaps your own - depends on your ability to navigate the journal’s world. Features: Co-op Gameplay: This game is designed exclusively for two players. You'll need to work together, combining your wits and skills to solve puzzles and progress through the story. Hidden Discoveries: Explore the journal's world to uncover pieces of history, identify those behind the events, and piece together the true story. Unveil the Evil: Learn about the malevolent forces that led to the tragic events surrounding journal. Immersive Experience: Communication is crucial—speaking to each other and sharing discoveries will be key to solving puzzles and advancing in the game. Can you and your partner unlock the secrets of the journal and escape its grip before it’s too late? Dark forces are at play, and the fate of more than just the journal's inhabitants may hang in the balance.
