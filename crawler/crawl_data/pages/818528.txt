Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Carry On Together is a physics-based online co-op moving game for up to 4 players. Start a small delivery company with your friends, take on moving jobs and try to get every item to its destination in one piece. Break the furniture, crash the truck, lose all your earnings or hold it together as a team. Can you keep the company alive? LIFT TOGETHER Some items are too heavy to carry alone. Stretch out your arms, sync up with your teammates and lift together with physics-based controls. Couches, fridges, pianos and giant boxes all need teamwork. Watch out for tight corridors, slippery stairs and that one friend who lets go at the worst possible moment. DRIVE THE TRUCK Load the delivery truck, pick a driver and hit the road. Enjoy the view on the way, but stay alert: one sharp turn and the whole cargo flies. The road might be the most dangerous part of the job. Do you trust your driver? EVERY LOCATION IS A NEW DISASTER From suburban houses with wide gardens to multi-story office buildings, from narrow apartment hallways to mountain dirt roads, every location brings its own kind of chaos. Fit the sofa in the elevator, roll the rock off the road, squeeze the wardrobe through the garden gate. GROW YOUR COMPANY Every successful delivery earns money, and every broken item costs you. Upgrade your company, take on bigger jobs and prove you're the best (or worst) movers in town.
