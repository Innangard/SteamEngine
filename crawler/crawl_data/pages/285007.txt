ENLIL-7 is a small robot in a world made of spikes. Every level is a single screen of traps, moving monsters and narrow jumps. Collect all the gems to make the key appear, grab it and reach the door before you run out of lives. Campaign 50 hand-made levels across forests, deserts, skies, space stations and a graveyard, plus a tutorial. Three lives per level – find the hidden extra heart if you need a fourth. Gems speed you up: the more you collect, the faster and slipperier you get. Walkers, floaters, hoppers and sleeping monsters that wake up when you come too close. Checkpoints, trampolines, drop-through platforms and screens that wrap around their edges. Race your best time: clock pickups take seconds off your run. Score Attack & Daily Run An endless runner that gets faster and denser the longer you survive. Gem combos, close-call bonuses and changing worlds as your score climbs. Daily Run: the same course for everyone each day – race the ghost of your best run. Steam leaderboards for both. Map Editor & Steam Workshop Paint ground, place spikes, enemies, trampolines and gems in six themes. Test your map instantly; beat it once to share it on the Steam Workshop. Subscribe to other players' maps and play them from My Maps. Also 90 Steam achievements and Steam Cloud saves. Keyboard and controller support, rebindable keys. Pick your robot's colour. Seven languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
