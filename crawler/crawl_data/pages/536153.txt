This is the sequel to the first episode of Asleep , a psychological horror adventure in the classic 2D point-and-click style, set in the Brazilian Northeast of the 1990s with a unique artistic design. New locations are available to explore in this chapter, gradually connecting Ana's confusing reality. This time, a little girl guides our protagonist through a peculiar village full of people and mysteries, providing clues and raising new questions to further expand Analu's universe. You’ll still rely on your trusty flashlight, using it and your wits to solve more puzzles, discover new types of items, and face even more challenging creatures. The nightmare continues... And much more: Save the game in your notebook and uncover more details about the mind of our protagonist. Find new types of items , like cassette tape recordings that bring back memories and provide clues to solve puzzles. Face five unique creatures in this chapter , including three exclusive to Ato 2, with one returning even more relentless. Explore vast settings, some populated with multiple characters, including a village in the Brazilian backlands and eerie wilderness areas . Solve new original puzzles involving exploration , flashlight use, and environmental interaction. Search for more nostalgic collectible items to dive deeper into the story.
