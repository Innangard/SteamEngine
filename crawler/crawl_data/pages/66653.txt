Something is abducting the residents of Gruesome Grove, and bringing them home means uncovering secrets some of the town would rather keep buried. With a note pointing her somewhere forbidden as her only clue, Nora must piece together what happened and why, before there's no one left to save. She'll encounter the town's revered (and ravenous) moths, and navigate the displaced monsters left wandering the wreckage of her home. The deeper she goes, the less certain it is she'll make it out with everyone she loves. The Gruesome Grove she grew up in has gone. To bring it back, she'll have to break every rule standing in her way. A hand-crafted point-and-click adventure, inspired by the classics A lush, detailed world brought to life through art nouveau-inspired illustration A charming cast of characters, human, monster, and animal alike Combine items and negotiate with characters to solve inventive puzzles An intriguing story full of twists and turns, laced with dark humour An original soundtrack by Kim Miller Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Cartoon blood, gore and mild references to alcoholism.
