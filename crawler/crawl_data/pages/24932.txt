In Where Winds Meet, Chinese martial arts meet a seamless open world. Roam vast landscapes, uncover hidden ruins, and descend into layered underground catacombs. Steal techniques from martial sects, wield an arsenal of classic wuxia weaponry, and master over 30 mystic arts – from the flowing balance of Tai Chi to the force of Lion's Roar. Craft, architecture, astronomy, folk games and performance, medicine and healing – 16 systems come together to form an immersive ancient society. A Living Open-World History Jianghu knows no bounds. Ancient landscapes, traditional crafts, stories drawn from history and realistic graphics come together to build a world true to the spirit of wuxia. Immerse yourself in the captivating Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period of China, where epic battles and legendary tales shape the course of history. From the bustling imperial capital to the hidden wilderness, every path is filled with secrets and adventures. Traverse vast landscapes in wuxia style: scale rooftops with fluid parkour, ride the windstride across miles in moments, or use fast travel points to leap between regions. Adventure Alone or Forge Your Community Embark on a narrative-driven adventure filled with rich solo gameplay, or team up with up to four friends for seamless co-op experiences. Master Your Way of Wuxia Combat Tailor your combat – melee, ranged, or stealth. Build a loadout that suits your playstyle. Switch weapons and employ martial arts and mystic skills like Tai Chi to outmaneuver enemies.
