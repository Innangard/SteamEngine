We're a small team, and extra support helps us keep working on the project and expanding the Frontier universe. Want to help the studio? By purchasing this pack, you're giving our developers and writers a huge green light — and giving us more opportunities to create new content for the world of Frontier. For you, we've put together everything we thought might be interesting beyond the game itself: printable materials, models, and a little piece of its world that you can bring from the screen to your shelf. The pack includes: A high-resolution map of the Oikoumene — a detailed map of the Frontier universe in A0 and A3 formats, prepared for viewing and printing. Two spacecraft models — STL files that you can print yourself or take to a professional 3D-printing service. A miniature of Jay — an STL model of the main character of Frontier: Path of Shadows . And our enormous gratitude to everyone who chooses to support the world we've been working on for many years. The 3D model of Jay was created by Black Bear Miniatures. They also carried out test prints of all three miniatures included in the pack. None of the items in this pack affect gameplay. It contains only additional materials for those who'd like to have a little more of the Frontier beyond the game itself. Please note: all materials are provided digitally. No physical models or miniatures are included or shipped to buyers. To print the STL files, you'll need access to your own 3D printer or a professional 3D-printing service. Thank you for helping us keep moving forward. There's still plenty of the Frontier left to explore.
