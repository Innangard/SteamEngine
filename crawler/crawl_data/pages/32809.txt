Learn more Leaving Early Access: Oct 15, 2026 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We have put all our heart and soul into the development of Enshrouded and we are ready to show it to you! We want to develop the game together with you during Early Access and of course beyond! We are counting on the community’s involvement to help make Enshrouded the best game it can be. We are committed to listening to you, the community.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “First and foremost, it depends on you and your feedback. Our goal is to provide you with the best experience, all the time. While we still have milestones to hit before full release, we aim to bring constant improvements and new content with each update.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Enshrouded is a complete experience in regards to the core gameplay elements. However, during the Early Access phase it is planned to improve and add to the gameplay features in accordance with feedback from the community. In addition to that, it is planned to expand the world significantly. This will include new biomes to explore, filled with new enemies and new types of locations. New biomes also mean more crafting resources and recipes, more tiers of equipment and weapons. While levelling up, the player will be able to explore additional skills in the skill tree. For the player base, more deco furniture and building materials are planned to enable players to express themselves even better with their building creations.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Look at the "About this game" section to learn what the game has already to offer. We are sure that the Early Access version of the game will provide you with a lot of long-lasting fun. The game will be polished and well balanced to ensure that.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, we strongly believe that a higher price once we leave Early Access is fair, and most importantly, it should allow us to develop the game further according to your feedback.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “It's especially important to us to listen and to work with you, the community. We will be gathering feedback from our Discord server, our social channels, and the Steam forums, as well as through a dedicated feature directly in the game. Forge your hero through a deep skill tree - armored juggernaut, deadly ranger, devastating battlemage - or blend them into a playstyle entirely your own. EXPLORE A VAST WORLD OF WILD BEAUTY Discover breathtaking landscapes and the secrets of a fallen civilization. Journey through lush forests, sun-scorched dunes, snow-capped peaks, and ruins swallowed by the Shroud. Swing across chasms with your grappling hook, glide from dizzying heights, and dive beneath the waters to reach places no road will ever lead to. SURVIVE AND GROW Live off the land - hunt, farm, fish, and scavenge rare materials. Rescue survivors from the Shroud, and your lonely hut grows into a bustling settlement of craftspeople and townsfolk, unlocking new recipes and gear. Survival here rewards preparation and creativity. FIGHT YOUR WAY Grow strong enough to face what hunts you. Wield greatswords, channel devastating magic, specialize deeply or mix archetypes through skill-based combat and a talent system that is both easy to pick up and deep to master. BUILD WITHOUT LIMITS Claim your place in Embervale - if you can dream it, you can build it. Raise towering fortresses, carve homes into mountainsides, reshape the terrain, and furnish every room down to the smallest detail. Voxel-based building gives you extraordinary freedom to make this world your home. CONQUER THE SHROUD Take the fight back to the enemy. Descend into the deadly fog that threatens all life. Face its most fearsome creatures, claim materials found nowhere else, and free Embervale from the Shroud's grip. FORGE YOUR LEGEND - SOLO OR WITH FRIENDS Walk this path alone or in co-op with up to 16 players. Fight, build, farm, or explore: every role matters in rebuilding the kingdom. DISCOVER ENDLESS ADVENTURES When the campaign ends, your story doesn't. Dive into worlds and dungeons created by millions of players, or share your own through our Adventure Sharing feature.
