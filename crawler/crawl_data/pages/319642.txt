Exhaustion. Expensive fish and chips. Welcome to the United Kingdom. DIGGERGUN is an oppressive life sim platformer about workers, minimum pay and the environment. You’re trapped on a blustery island, forced to work at Cornwall Mining Corp where it’s shadier than the game’s monochrome palette. To escape, you need to earn £3000 from digging precious lithium. To survive, you need to manage your time, energy and money during the rigid day-and-night cycle. Will you make ends meet while digging for freedom or find another way out and bring down the whole bloomin’ system? Features: An atmospheric world with fleshed-out characters; everyone you meet has their own distinct personality and storyline. Multiple endings: who you interact with (or don’t) will change the outcome of your story. Unique energy system where your actions truly matter as the game tracks your gameplay and punishes/rewards you for your choices. Life sim where wages, food, rent, and much more are based on the current cost-of-living in the United Kingdom. There's even a tax system calculated to real-world rates! Diverse range of randomly generated mines and intricately hand-designed levels to ensure you’re always kept on your toes.
