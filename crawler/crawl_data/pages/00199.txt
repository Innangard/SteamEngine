About This Game QUIET is a 1–4 player online co-op horror game where you and your crew play as alien ducks who crash-landed on Earth. The good news: the ship parts you need are scattered across some very strange places. The bad news: making too much noise might attract some unwanted attention. Every sound matters. Microphone required. DON’T GET CAUGHT Survival requires teamwork, communication, and staying calm under pressure. Unfortunately, you are all alien ducks with terrible survival instincts. One mistake doesn't just cost you — it costs everyone. TAKE EVERYTHING THAT ISN’T NAILED DOWN Search the area for ship components. Move carefully. Don't grab more than you can carry. And for the love of the mothership — don't knock anything over. SHHHHHHHHHHHH Movement creates noise. Talking creates noise. Panic definitely creates noise. All real-world sounds carry risk.  Stay QUIET…you never know what's listening. Cooperation is over: Arcade mode Now the duck next to you is your real enemy! Get into some lighthearted mayhem with your friends. Knock them down and steal their chances to claim victory.
