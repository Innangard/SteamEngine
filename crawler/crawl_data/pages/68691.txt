Sit across from your partner and settle into a familiar game of Spades. Bid your hand, follow suit, and choose when to bring out trump. Every trick matters, whether you're covering your partner, setting the other team, or keeping your bags under control. A classic game, clearly presented Play four-player partnership Spades with a standard 52-card deck, Nil bids, contract scoring, and bag penalties. Choose a race to 250 or 500 points. Computer players keep every seat filled for solo play, with Easy, Standard, and Expert difficulty options. Partners sit opposite one another, with clear bids, scores, and turn indicators. Choose Blind Nil before viewing your hand: +200 for taking no tricks, or -200 if you take a trick. No passing. Large, fanned cards keep your hand readable. Played cards slide onto the table and completed tricks collect automatically. Choose your table felt and background colors. Adjust card size, sound volumes, reduced motion, and faster play animations with saved device settings. Review the score breakdown after each hand and player statistics at the end of the match. Track lifetime games, wins, and Nil results saved on your device. A team loses at -100 in a 250-point game or -200 in a 500-point game. Equal-score finishes continue until the tie is broken. No ads, reactions, or distractions. Just the cards and the next trick. Bring your friends to the table through Steam. Play with one to four human players, with computer players filling every empty seat. A disconnected player can return to the same seat while the host stays at the table. If the host leaves, the table ends; matches are not saved for later recovery.
