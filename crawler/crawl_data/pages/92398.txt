Buy ARK: Dragontopia $29.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Tame the legends and rule the skies! Awaken within a cloudbound paradise where the earth has fractured into a world of floating islands, and myths take flight. High above these magical grounds, Survivors must learn a new rhythm of survival alongside the true rulers of the sky: the dragons. Across a vast aerial ecosystem, ARK: Dragontopia invites you to step off the edge and master the skies. Guided by the echoes of a lost order of dragonriders, enter a land where legends breathe anew. As a fierce new threat claims the horizon, it is up to you to restore balance! LORDS OF THE SKY Between the sun and moon, the skies teem with draconic legends. Encounter titanic behemoths that devour resources and enemies alike, and elegant, wind-dancing steeds that gallop across the clouds. From deadly four-winged assassins, to multi-headed beasts of dual-breathed chaos, and loyal shoulder-drakes that guard you on your journey, every dragon in this realm demands its own path to taming. You’ll need to observe, learn, and master each of these temperamental creatures’ unique rhythms before they ever let you take the saddle. THE DRAGONTOPIA ECOSYSTEM To survive this new high-altitude frontier, you’ll need to grow through the Dragon Skill Trees, a new progression system built around strengthening your bond with these mythical beasts. Craft highly specialized gear and wield potent tools, and expand your base with a draconic tileset, incubator backpacks, and mysterious potion stations. But the skies are actively contested, and you are the realm's last hope to break the vanguard of a ruthless airborne invader! AVAILABLE NOW Eclipsar Umbra , the legendary obsidian terror born to claim the night sky. Bound to your will through the mythic Dragon Horn, this shadow-forged executioner strikes with terrifying, unpredictable speed. Rain precision plasma from the cover of darkness, or slip through the void itself to ambush unsuspecting prey. As his fury builds in the heat of combat, Umbra awakens his ultimate form - a blinding surge of raw energy that shatters enemy defenses and renders him a near-untouchable terror of the skies. Dragon Skill Tree unlocks a brand-new progression path that lets you forge a deeper bond with your dragons. Push the limits of their gravity-defying acrobatics, amplify their shadow-forged plasma attacks, or tailor their defensive auras, as you shape the ultimate apex predator around your exact playstyle. Drake Claw Grappler: Wield this essential new tool for high-altitude traversal and midair dragon mounting. Draconic Armor: Equip this cosmetic set and look every inch the legendary dragonrider. THE FLIGHT PLAN Your journey into the clouds is just beginning. Secure Dragontopia now and you’re locked in for a massive rollout of content over the coming months: July: Lumina awakens! Umbra’s sun-sworn counterpart takes flight to restore celestial balance. Scatter brilliant celestial traps to punish your pursuers, or bend light itself to vanish in a flash and outmaneuver any threat. When pushed to the brink, Lumina erupts into her ultimate sun-flare state - stripping away technological defenses and rising as a blazing sovereign of the clouds. When united in battle with her shadowedkin, their celestial synergy ignites, forging an unstoppable alliance of speed, armor, and destruction. October: A colossal sky-behemoth rises. This heavily armored juggernaut dominates the clouds by inhaling everything in its path, drawing resources, enemies, and even allies into its cavernous maw. It can weaponize its consumed payload, violently expelling it as a devastating barrage to scatter targets below. As it consumes the battlefield, its sheer mass swells to absorb incoming fire, allowing it to anchor down and become an immovable bulwark against any siege. December: Prepare to take flight into a boundless, map-wide skyworld. Explore fantastical floating landmasses surrounded by countless procedural shattered isles, unlock the secrets of aerial phenomena, conquer enemy outposts, gain a tremendous variety of new items, new vehicles, and cosmetics, and most important of all: bond with the complete mythical roster of dragons! Repel the onslaught of a merciless invading legion, and face off against an apex threat unlike anything ARK has ever seen! The dragons are waking, the islands are calling, and the Skies of Dragontopia are yours to claim!
