What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The Fancy Pants games have been developed alongside the community for their full history. We want to bring down the walls on the Classic Pack, and allow the fans to have their opinions heard as it continues to take shape.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We have a minimum target in mind for content in this Classic Collection. But there is also a LOT of additional content from over the years that we want to sprinkle in. The more interest there is, the more we intend to add! I'd expect a minimum of 6 months, but possibly longer.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Our initial launch includes "Classic" versions of the original 3 Worlds. With so much old Fancy Pants material to potentially fold into this Pack, we want future content prioritizations to be community driven alongside general polish. The more levels, Worlds, extra, and secrets we can throw in the better!” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Classic versions of Worlds 1, 2 and 3 are all fully playable! Polishing World 3 will be an immediate focus.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, we do intend to scale the price of this game up over time. Whether that increase is all at once for v1.0, or slowly as more content is added, as well as the final price is all TBD.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “The Fancy Pants Discord has been very active for years now. These games have been in semi-active development for nearly 2 decades. So why keep that process private now? Join Brad on this journey, publicly patching up decades old Flash games, and beefing this Pack up to be the ultimate Fancy Pants fan experience! This collection aims to preserve the legacy of The Fancy Pants Adventures as you remember them , compiled for your convenience, with new quality of life features for a modern context. Silky smooth gameplay - A smooth, reliable experience, untethered from browser limitations.
