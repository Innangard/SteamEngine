Four rooms. A growing army. The frog was just sitting there. The Bugivugis took it personally. Kill The Frog! is a playful pixel-art incremental/idle game about growing an army, turning resources into upgrades, and dealing with frog bosses. Progress through Gold → Gems → Honey → Cheese: each of the four rooms brings a different gameplay loop. Gold room — Build your army Recruit more archers and improve your towers and gold mine. Collect gold as you attack the frog, then turn your earnings into a stronger army and new upgrades. Gem room — Bring in the magic Add mages, ballistas, a mage tower and a zeppelin to your offensive. Earn gems, develop this room’s skills and strengthen your magical units. Honey room — Defend your hive Improve your bees and queen bee, gather pollen, fill your hive and produce honey. Use pollen turrets against the frog’s flower rocks. Hold your cursor over flowers in the sky to trigger a shockwave and clear incoming rocks. Cheese room — Follow every bounce Watch cheese balls bounce through the pins and reach the baskets to earn cheese. Develop golden balls, target pins and explosive pins to explore this room’s different earning opportunities. Skill trees — Choose your next power Every room brings its own skill tree and upgrade priorities. Choose between attacks, production, speed and special mechanics, and decide where your resources will make a difference. BugiVugi King — One king, your build Your king fights beside you in the Gold, Gem and Honey rooms. Discover nine pieces of boss loot: gold-, gem- and honey-themed staffs, capes and amulets. Equip one item in each slot to combine resource income, attack timing and special hit effects. Click his crown to switch between Helmet, Wanderer and Robed appearances. What awaits you? Four rooms with different combat and resource systems A dedicated skill tree for each room A special king unit customized through boss equipment Animated pixel-art characters and a short, humorous opening cinematic 6 supported languages English, Turkish, German, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese.
