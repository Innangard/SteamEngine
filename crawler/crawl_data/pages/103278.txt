Vanligvis $14.99 – tilbudspris: $13.49 Legg i handlevogn Vis samfunnssentral Om spillet Halley's Big Catch Halley is a cartoon cat who takes fishing seriously. He comes home to Hidden Pond for a quiet day on the dock — and finds the start of something much bigger. Relics. Waters all over the world. The old fish stories from childhood start to feel closer than they used to. This is a cozy, readable fishing adventure: warm scenery, great water, and fights that feel like the fish, not a health bar. On the water Cast, hook, fight — timing, drag, and tackle match matter Hidden Pond and beyond — ponds, creeks, reefs, open ocean, and more Dozens of species — keep a bag, fill the index, chase the ones that got away Gear that changes the fight — combos for still water, fly water, and blue water A journal, not a lecture — the story sits in what you find The trail A worn medallion. Strange pieces waiting on distant shores. When Halley finds one, the medallion reacts. The rest you learn by fishing. For players who like Character fishing games with a real fight Exploring a new shore instead of grinding one pond A story that stays a little mysterious A cartoon world that still takes the water seriously Coming soon on Steam. Opplysning om AI-generert innhold Utviklerne beskriver hvordan spillet bruker AI-generert innhold slik: Some Steam store and library artwork (capsules, hero, broadcast panels, and similar marketing images) was created with generative AI, then selected and cropped by the developer. A few 3D props were blocked out with an AI modeling tool and then adjusted by hand for the game. The game itself does not use generative AI while you play. Halley, the story, fishing, and locations are human-authored. Worlds and water use traditional game systems, not live AI generation.
