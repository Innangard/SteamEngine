Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game is already big and fully playable, but a factory game lives or dies on its balance, and we can only test so many yards ourselves. The demo taught us a lot: players build things we never expected, and a good chunk of what's in the game now came from their feedback. We want to keep working like that while we finish the rest.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Roughly 6 to 12 months. It depends on what players think of the later stages and how much needs changing.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to add more content on top of the five stages that are already in: new machines, more diggers, bigger late game projects and more things to build with. We also plan a lot of balancing and polish, especially for the bigger piles, based on what players tell us. Some of these plans will probably change along the way, because We'd rather build what players actually want than stick to a list.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “You can play from your first handful of hay all the way to the ending, where you find all 24 needles. There are five stages of production, from hay bales and bricks up to paper, bioplastic and carbon fibre, with conveyors, pipes, power, scanners, delivery orders, blueprints and building parts. A Standard pile takes around 7 to 10 hours, and the Mountain takes 30 hours or more. There will be bugs. The game sends a crash report on its own, and you can report anything else on our Discord.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. We plan to raise the price when the game leaves Early Access, so buying now is the cheapest way to get it.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community is very involved in development process since beginning, We have discord with dedicated ideas/bugs channels that are revised daily.” Read more Download Find The Needle Demo Learn more about this demo Download This game is not yet available on Steam Planned Release Date: Nov 6, 2026 This game plans to unlock in approximately 1 day and 5 hours Interested? View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game You were a farmer. At least, you think you were. Now you're stuck inside a strange warehouse, standing in front of an endless haystack. You don't remember how you got here, who brought you here, or why the doors won't open. There is only one instruction: Find the needles. There are some in the pile. You start by hand, with a metal detector. You end up with belts, balers, scanners and robotic arms doing the looking for you. Place real machines, wire the power, feed them by belt. Sell the hay, buy more machines, get given a bigger pile. Nobody has said what happens when you finish. The pile Begin By Hand The tools Buy tools like shovels, buckets, wheelbarrows, pitchforks, vacuums. Anything that helps you process hay manually! Start Automating Automate everything! Build robots, drones, conveyor belts, hay wrappers, paper machines, sorting machines, and advanced systems that process hay and search for needles while you focus on expanding your factory. Hay products Turn ordinary hay into valuable products! Process it into hay pulp, compressed bales, eco bricks, hay paper, pellets, and much more. Build new production lines, unlock advanced machinery, and find better ways to turn your mountain of hay into profit. Tech tree Progress through a huge tech tree with over 300 upgrades! Unlock new machines, improve production speed, increase efficiency, expand your factory, and discover entirely new ways to process hay. What happens when you collect all the needles? - Who knows?
