Learn more Leaving Early Access: Jan 18, 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Mr Toilet is a game whose final shape depends heavily on gameplay pacing, challenge level, and how players respond to its unusual sense of humor. That is why we want to develop it together with you from an early stage. Early Access will allow us to see which elements work best and which of our ideas bring players the most fun. ” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect Mr Toilet to remain in Early Access for around 3–6 months. For us, this will be an intensive stage of polishing the game. We want to use this time to improve gameplay pacing, expand the most important mechanics, and add new content. We do not plan to extend this period unnecessarily. Early Access is meant to be a focused production stage that helps us deliver a fuller, better-balanced, and more polished game.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to expand the full version of Mr Toilet on the foundations available in Early Access, but we do not want to reveal all planned surprises just yet. What we can say is that we are working on new mechanics designed to make the gameplay richer and more varied. During Early Access, we also plan to carefully analyze player feedback. Based on your impressions, we plan to adjust the balance, pacing, and overall feel of the game. We also plan to introduce a range of quality-of-life improvements that will improve convenience, system clarity, and the overall gameplay experience.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Mr Toilet already includes the full core gameplay loop. Players can manage a toilet, keep it clean, handle visiting NPCs, react to emerging problems, and grow the business through an upgrade system. At this stage, bugs, balance issues, or elements requiring further polish may still appear. We plan to address them through future updates while also developing new systems based on your feedback.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. The Early Access version will be available at a slightly lower price. As we add new content and approach the full release, we plan to increase the price to reflect the added value.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We want the community to actively help us shape the game. Players can share their feedback on the Steam forums and on our Discord.
