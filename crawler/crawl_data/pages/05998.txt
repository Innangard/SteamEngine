Drop bombs onto concealed enemies to earn currency. Spend your currency to boost passive income, increase bombing power, and unlock new maps. Explore over 12 maps of increasing size and reward as you expand your bombing campaign. Prestige and reset your progress in exchange for powerful, permanent upgrades—including a devastating nuclear bomb. FEATURES Satisfying incremental progression. A short relaxing experience.
