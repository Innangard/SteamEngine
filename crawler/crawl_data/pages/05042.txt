A little town is waiting to be put back together. Collect, sort, and place 18,490 bricks in a relaxing first-person tidy-up game, solo or in online co-op with up to 5 players. Turn scattered pieces into buildings, and watch your work bring the town to life. COLLECT, SORT, AND BUILD Find the bricks you need by their shape and color. Follow each building’s plan and place the pieces in the right spots. Watch the piles get smaller as the walls grow taller. UNLOCK ABILITIES Unlock helpful abilities to make the work easier. Use a magnet to collect bricks, a wheelbarrow to carry them, and scaffolding to reach higher places. Spend less time carrying and more time building. WATCH THE TOWN CHANGE Turn piles of bricks into a cozy little town. Walk through the streets and enjoy the buildings you’ve finished. With every building you complete, there’s more to see. One brick at a time. Better together.
