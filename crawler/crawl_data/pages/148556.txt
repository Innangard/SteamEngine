Planet of Lana II follows Lana and Mui as they step into a new chapter in a changing world. On their home planet, new technology brings progress but also stirs greed and conflict. As harmony begins to shift, Lana and Mui are drawn into a journey to uncover long-buried truths about their world and themselves. About This Game A planet that used to be a place of undisturbed balance between human, nature, and animal has now become something else entirely. The disharmony that had been in the making for hundreds of years has finally arrived in the form of a faceless army. But this is not a story about war. This is a story about a vibrant, beautiful planet –and the journey to keep it that way. Key Features Unique companion-based gameplay. Solve puzzles with diverse challenges and quirky twists. Carefully navigate dangerous situations using stealth and wit. Use your reflexes and survive challenging action sequences. Discover an epic story that stretches across galaxies and centuries.
