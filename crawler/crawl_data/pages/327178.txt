View Community Hub About This Game Hey, you. You're finally awake. It's time to get up, put on your clothes, eat a bowl of sugary cereal, head out the door. You've just started working at the nearest DESTROYER BURGER, America's favorite fast food restaurant. Destroyer Burger is a fast-paced first-person fast food chaos simulator about multitasking, bad decisions, and corporate despair. Juggle multiple customers, learn meal combinations and kick out the most troublesome patrons. Keep your stocks high: prepare fries in advance, always fill up the grill, and be ready for anything. Relax, the worst is yet to come. Struggle through the intensive rush hours when people overrun your little restaurant. Customize your playthrough with over 30 different perks that improve your abilities to create meals from frozen slop. Buy affordable crappy Norwegian furniture to stave off the inevitable existential crisis of working in fast food. Kill the pain with various medical supplies to improve your chances at victory. Features a replayable main campaign with multiple endings. Also included: an advanced longer campaign and a randomizer mode.
