Докладніше Що бажають сказати розробники: Чому дочасний доступ? «Paralives has been developed alongside our community since the very beginning, and we want to keep working with everyone during the project's next steps. As dedicated life simulation game fans, we created Paralives to innovate and offer a new take on the genre, whether it's through flexible building tools, a character creator full of customization, or new and engaging life simulation gameplay mechanics. Releasing in Early Access will allow people to play with the features and systems we have developed, and provide us with crucial feedback to help refine and improve what we have into a truly great and unique experience. Because we're a small team and the breadth and depth of things you can do in a game like Paralives is staggering, this feels like the best option for us.» Як довго гра буде у дочасному доступі? «About two years, considering all the features and content we want to add before we can say that we've hit version 1.0. We'll also certainly have many other improvements and changes to make along the way. In short, this estimated timeframe seems reasonable to us right now, but it's always subject to change!» Чим повна версія гри буде відрізнятися від версії для дочасного доступу? «For the full version, we plan on adding more features such as pets, cars, houseboats, and a town creation tool, as well as more content in each of the game's systems such as more furniture, clothes, personality traits, social interactions, skills, town activities, and more. See our Early Access announcement post for more info: https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1118520/view/520841474254834418» Який поточний стан версії для дочасного доступу? «There's currently quite a bit to do in the game. You can build and decorate houses with various customization options, a color wheel, and resizable furniture, among other things. You can create a diverse cast of characters using our creation tools and extensive clothing catalog, and then make them live their lives in our open-world town where they'll find a job, nurture relationships, improve skills, and more! Some bugs will be present, some features might not be entirely balanced, and some content might be missing here and there, due to the nature of an Early Access release.» Чи буде відрізнятися ціна гри протягом дочасного доступу та після його завершення? «The game will be sold at a discount throughout Early Access, with the price increasing gradually as we add more content until full release. However, we have pledged to never release paid DLCs: there will only ever be free updates!» Як ви плануєте залучати спільноту до розробки гри? «No matter what avenue you use to reach us - through the Steam forums, by email, or through our Discord server - we will listen to feedback, suggestions, comments, and bug reports with the utmost attentiveness, just like we've always done during the development process. Our community is the lifeblood that will sustain our Early Access evolution into a game everyone can enjoy. Paralives is a sandbox life simulation game with a great cast of Townies to meet, as well as a vibrant open-world town to explore and make your own. You will feel right at home, should you choose to embark. What kind of life will you build? Whether you focus on your career, finding love, building a family, or becoming the richest person in town, the story is yours to write in Paralives. But twists and forks in the road await. What will you leave behind? You live and then you die. At least, do it in a nice house! Thanks to an ever-evolving catalog of furniture, decor and clutter objects, staging the perfect homes for your Parafolks will be a breeze: resize, recolor, stack and place items freely for a tactile building experience. Paralives allows truly grid-less constructions with curved walls, split-level floors and more. Recreating your house or building an intricate 8-story mansion has never been this easy. There are lives to create and stories to tell You will be handed an advanced character creator called the Paramaker. Adjust the height of your Parafolks, tweak a wide array of facial and body features, design stylish outfits and create complex personalities. From head to toe, no small detail will be left out!
