Dicealot is a Farkle inspired roguelike dice-builder where you will gather the finest hands of fantastical dice to hit hard and save the kingdom. Find power dice combos within your rolls to rack up points! Be beware, for the more you roll, the greater the risk, so you’ll need nerves of steel to keep rolling when you could farkle and lose it all! But fear not! You will have an array of unique quest dice to help you reach the biggest of big numbers and squash your foes. But finding the right dice for your build is only half the challenge, you’ll want to avoid some of those undesirable rolls! Save Camelot! King Arthur is mysteriously bewitched! Play as Queen Gwynevere rounding up his faithful Knights to root out the source of his majesty's malady. Take on an eclectic cast of Arthurian foes that are sure to keep you on your toes. So come and test your skill and your luck, and see if you have the dice to save the Kingdom! Dicealot is inspired by other amazing games: Balatro, Monster Train, Slay the Spire, Inscryption, Dicey Dungeons and many other amazing deck builders! Check them out too if you have not already!
