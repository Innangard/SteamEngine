It's a short but bittersweet experience that will play on your mind for days to come.” Pocket Gamer About This Game Miniatures: An art house game collection Play four hand-drawn memories from childhood and explore the strangeness and unease hiding beneath familiar moments. Some stories are mysterious, others whimsical, and all are a bit experimental. Each story is supported by its own unique art style and soundscape. A short, open-to-interpretation experience with a total playtime of about 30–40 minutes . What's inside? Open up an old wooden box and discover four different objects: a screwdriver, a lizard, a seashell and a moth. Each one has its own story to tell: 🪛 Familiar Assemble a curious cabinet together with your family. It is not as easy, as it seems. 🦎 The Paludarium Take care of your pet lizard while nature slowly creeps in. 🦋 The House of the Moon Search for the moon in a dark seascape. 🐚 The Last Sand Castle Help the music-loving inhabitants of a sand castle. The experience Miniatures is an atmospheric and slightly unsettling experience for players who enjoy narrative experimentation, stylised visuals and subtle, but poignant storytelling. It features: Hand-drawn and award-winning art with a picture book aesthetic Short and impactful stories that are emotional and open to interpretation. Atmospheric and ambient soundscapes Simple and easy interactions: point, click and explore. Follow the moon, feed the lizard, and discover surreal childhood memories, where the familiar turns mysterious and strange.
