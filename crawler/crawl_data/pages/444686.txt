View Community Hub About This Game Become the ultimate Interior Designer Embark on a creative journey unlike any other in Interior Designer. Unleash your imagination and improve your design skills as you take on various client jobs in the Career Mode . Decorate cozy bedrooms, build sleek gaming rooms, design luxurious bathrooms, and create state-of-the-art home gyms – the possibilities are endless. Plan your architectural masterpiece and bring it to life in stunning detail. Play and Craft Your Spaces In Interior Designer, every space is a blank canvas waiting for your personal touch. Experiment with different layouts, colors, and furniture in the Design Sandbox to create spaces that reflect your unique style. Whether you prefer minimalist chic or eclectic charm, there's something for every taste. Compete in the Design League Think you have what it takes to be the best? Join the Design League and put your skills to the test against other aspiring designers . Show off your creativity and flair as you compete in challenges and earn rewards. With each victory, your reputation grows, paving the way for even greater opportunities. Rise through the ranks and become a legend in the world of interior design. Stay Inspired Inspiration is everywhere in Interior Designer. Explore a vast catalog of furniture, decor, and architectural elements to spark your creativity. Draw inspiration from other budding designers, or be a trendsetter and share your own creations with the world. With regular updates and new content, there's always something new to discover and explore. Innovative Design Tools at Your Fingertips Experience a new world of interior design with our cutting-edge tools that redefine the way you shape your virtual spaces . Interior Designer boasts a wide array of intuitive functionalities, offering tools such as automated roof creation, stretchable furniture, adjustable lighting, and dynamic cabinets. Break free from conventional constraints and let your imagination run wild as you effortlessly manipulate every aspect of your interiors. It's not just a game; it's a design revolution.
