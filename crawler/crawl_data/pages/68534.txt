THE FINAL PARTICIPANT You enter House 12 believing you are investigating an old massacre. That is what the archive wants you to believe. Search the rooms. Examine the evidence. Decode the symbols. Reconstruct the prayers. Follow the instructions left behind by people who should never have written them. Piece by piece, you will complete what the cult began. But the deeper you descend into House 12, the more disturbing the truth becomes. You are not investigating the ritual. You are performing it. Every symbol you decipher is another step. Every object you place is another offering. Every forbidden word you uncover brings the ceremony closer to completion. And every minute you remain inside the house brings Abyzou closer to you.
