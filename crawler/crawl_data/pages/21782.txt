Feed it. Log Drop is a 2D physics automation game with one noble goal: get more logs into the earth's core than is physically reasonable. Chaotic or orderly, the contraption is yours. Fling, funnel and dam up the logs, then let the whole load go at once. Buy new tools as you go, and upgrade the ones you come to rely on. It's a sandbox. The core doesn't care how the logs get there. What's in the demo? Features a campaign mode where every zone is its own puzzle, with its own objectives to complete. (5 zones + 1 bonus zone past the tutorial to complete in this demo) - Demo content is roughly 40min - 1hr+ What to expect in the full game? Many more zones and objectives to work through, with increasing complexity and difficulty. Multiple tree species requiring new tools to harvest and route to the core. More tools in general to add to your arsenal. There will be a sandbox/creative mode with unrestricted access to tools so you can build whatever you want.
