Warning : This software may cause you to unintentionally slam your desk and develop an inexplicable urge to get a real cat. Features designed to ruin your day: Professional-Level Annoyance : Years of research (i.e., watching our cat, Brooklyn) have resulted in scientifically calculated tail movements for maximum irritation. 12 Hand-Crafted Pixel Art Tails : From a fluffy Persian tail to a sleek Siamese one, you can choose your preferred style of disruption. Interactive System : Annoy the cat back by poking its tail with your mouse!
