The American Houses DLC introduces a completely new architectural style to Builder Simulator. Move away from European designs and master the art of building classic American suburban homes from the ground up. Iconic Suburban Architecture Build the neighborhoods you know and love from the movies. This expansion allows you to design and construct classic American homes, featuring everything from spacious open-concept layouts to attached multi-car garages and wide front lawns. Bring the quintessential American Dream to life using completely new building elements. Customize the exterior of your projects with classic wood sidings, asphalt shingle roofing, and iconic, welcoming front porches to capture that unmistakable charm. Authentic Construction Techniques American homes are built differently, and now you get to experience that process firsthand. Say goodbye to heavy masonry and master the art of lightweight timber framing, erecting stud walls, and installing drywall. To build like a true American contractor, you need the right equipment. Expand your toolkit with new, specialized tools, including upgraded nail guns and saws. Tackle timber framing with absolute precision and take your construction skills to the next level. Step Outside with New Porches What is a classic American home without a traditional porch? You will now be able to build iconic porches from the ground up! Whether you are laying down a cozy front stoop for a suburban family home or constructing a sprawling wraparound veranda for a countryside estate, you have the freedom to design the perfect outdoor transition space. Pour the foundations, lay down the wooden decking, erect the pillars, and give your newly built properties that quintessential American appeal. Fresh Furniture and Interior Assets But the structure itself isn’t everything. Once the drywall is up and painted, dive into a brand-new catalog of interior assets to decorate your finished builds with a fresh, cozy style. Stage your properties perfectly using classic American home appliances, massive kitchen islands, and plush furniture. These fresh additions will help you create warm, inviting spaces that look ready to move in.
