How to play With keyboard Forward-w Backward-s Strafe left-a Strafe right- d Open door-e Jump-space Crouch-shift Reload-r Map-tab Flashlight-f Fire-mouse click Game controller is recommended for the best play experience Compatible with most gamepads but control scheme may very Useful tips Déunocta plays like an old school shooter similar to Doom or Duke Nukem 3d most levels contain 3 key pickups, labeled by colors used to open Blue, Red and Yellow doors. Secrets and health pickups are scattered throughout to help you on your journey. Remember if you get lost you have a map (tab). If you can't see you have a flashlight (f). If you're having trouble fitting a space in you can crouch (shift) and jump while crouching (space). If you feel you are being hit and can't find where it's coming from it is best to get behind cover. Enemies , if not visible tend to hide behind bunkers. Levels very in length but each can roughly be completed within 20 minutes for the average player. There is a perilous fight ahead, at times you may think you are lost. Don't give up on this battle. My brother and I did the voice acting, he also mixed the soundtrack. additional sound effects were made with multiple objects and further edited in and added to digitally.
