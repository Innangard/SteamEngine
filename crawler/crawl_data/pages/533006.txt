View Community Hub About This Game CARDS. CARDS EVERYWHERE. Thousands of cards are scattered across the room - and every one of them belongs somewhere. Explore the mess, pick up cards, discover which collection they belong to, and place them into the correct spots in your album. With over 1,500 unique cards spread across dozens of different sets, there is always something new waiting underneath the pile. BUILD YOUR COLLECTION Every card you find brings your album one step closer to completion. Discover completely different themed sets, fill empty slots one by one, complete pages, and slowly turn an empty album into a huge collection. Some cards will be easy to spot. Others might be hiding right in front of you. COMPLETE TASKS AND PAGES TO GET BETTER Completing a page doesn't just feel satisfying - it also makes you a better collector. Earn upgrade points for every finished album page and use them to unlock and improve abilities that make collecting faster, smoother, and more satisfying. But not everything can be bought. Complete special tasks - such as finishing specific collections - to unlock unique abilities and permanent bonuses. Carry more cards. See more. Use magic to find what you need. Deal with duplicates more efficiently. The more your collection grows, the more tools you gain to tackle the chaos around you. PLAY YOUR WAY! Choose between Classic and Discovery Mode, where you uncover album categories as you play and arrange them however you like! Want an extra surprise? Enable Mystery Cards to hide every card's identity until you pick it up. With three save slots, you can enjoy different playthroughs without losing your progress! WHAT ABOUT THE DUPLICATES? Not every card you find belongs in the album. Duplicates have their own place: a special box where you decide how they should be organized. Sort them by color? Go for it. Group them by theme? Absolutely. Carefully build perfect little stacks? Sure. Or don't organize them at all and simply throw them in. That's fine too. As you progress, you can unlock abilities that make dealing with duplicates faster - so whether you enjoy arranging every last card or just want them out of the way, you can play your way. A LITTLE STORY BETWEEN THE CARDS Someone was here before you. Scattered throughout the room are letters left behind by Mira Finch, The Wandering Collector , and the mysterious Grand Collector . Find them as you explore and slowly discover their relationship, their history - and how exactly you became part of it. The story is there for those who want to find it. The cards aren't going to sort themselves either way. ONE CARD AT A TIME. A floor covered in cards. An album waiting to be filled. Dozens of collections to discover. And a lot of sorting ahead of you. Welcome to Cards Everywhere!
