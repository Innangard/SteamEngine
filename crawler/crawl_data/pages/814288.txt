It is time to pick up your pen. Your job is to write the death certificate. England, 1959 A series of unexplained deaths has occurred at Fleming Manor, a country estate outside London. As the investigator assigned to the cases, you will examine each scene, study the condition of the body, question the people involved, and review the available evidence. You must identify the victim, determine the cause of death, and decide whether the death was accidental, self-inflicted, or the result of murder. When you believe you understand what happened, record your conclusions in a death certificate or emergency report. Choosing a suspect is not enough. Missing or incorrect information may lead to the wrong conclusion. Investigation at Fleming Manor Death at Fleming Manor is a forensic mystery adventure built around observation, deduction, and official documentation. The game takes place in a British country manor in the late 1950s. Its hand-drawn visual style is inspired by European crime and mystery comics of the 1960s. If you enjoy Agatha Christie mysteries, methodical investigations, and story-driven games where small details matter, Death at Fleming Manor may be for you. Key Features Complete the Official Records Examine each body, review the evidence, and record your conclusions in death certificates and emergency reports. A Mystery Set in a 1950s British Manor Investigate a series of deaths at an isolated country estate, where the residents may not be telling you everything. Hand-Drawn Ligne Claire Artwork Explore scenes illustrated by hand in a style inspired by European mystery comics from the 1960s. Draw Your Own Conclusions The game will not confirm your suspicions for you. To close a case, you must identify what happened and support every part of your report with evidence.
