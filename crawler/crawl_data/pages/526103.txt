As with every game, much was left on the cutting room floor during the years of the game's development. But in the case of Primordia, and entire soundtrack created by Victor Pflug, the game's artist, was set aside. Now, almost a decade later (and following the successful release of Pflug's soundtrack to Wormwood's sophomore game, Strangeland), the original soundtrack is ready for release. Like Strangeland's soundtrack, this album is a seamless complement to Primordia's haunting mechanical landscapes of rust and ruin.
