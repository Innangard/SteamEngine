View Community Hub About This Game BUILD YOUR UNIQUE FLYING CITY Airborne Empire is a one-of-a-kind Open World RPG City Builder. Build and grow your own city in the skies and keep it aloft by managing its lift, balance, and propulsion. Shape and grow your own unique flying city Manage lift, balance, and propulsion to keep your city airborne Organize your city’s defenses and abilities to defeat pesky pirates Balance resources, infrastructure, and the needs of your people EXPLORE A VAST OPEN WORLD Traverse a vast open world full of dangers, exotic wonders, and many hidden treasures. Travel across 5 diverse biomes such as the lush Aeyrie, volcanic Blackspine and snowy Kingsfell Discover distant kingdoms and forgotten settlements scattered across the lands Meet quirky characters, uncover their stories, and trade with them Complete quests that help liberate towns and rebuild connections between kingdoms DEFEND THE SKIES FROM PIRATES Sky pirates are threatening the fragile peace. Defend your people and hunt down enemy outposts across the world. Build defensive structures to fend off pirate attacks Use a variety of offensive weapons such as Freeze Cannons, Tesla Cannons and Aircrafts Track down pirate outposts and destroy their bases Protect the kingdoms below from an assortment of pirate enemies RESEARCH AND DISCOVER THROUGH EXPLORATION Research, unlock and uncover all kinds of new technology to expand your flying city. Unlock new lift, propulsion, and morale buildings Develop new abilities and weapons tools to defend your city against pirate threats Research upgrades that improve your city and enrich the lives of its citizens Discover relics and hidden technologies across the world NEW WAYS TO PLAY Airborne Empire offers multiple ways to experience life in the skies depending on the challenge you seek.
