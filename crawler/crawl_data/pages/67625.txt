Written by the creators, it contains answers to some of Riven ’s deepest mysteries! This massive, 133 page guide, with a new foreword by one of Riven ’s original Directors, Richard Vander Wende, includes both The Riven Journal and The Riven Quick Guide . Whether you prefer to read the journals of someone taking the journey alongside you, or you just need one hint to get your gears moving again, this digital strategy guide has you covered. The Riven Journal contains many observations that hint at the deeper, never-before-revealed story of Riven , written from the perspective of a newcomer to the Age, just as perplexed by their discoveries as you may be. The Riven Quick Guide lends you gentle hints (or, if you prefer, outright answers), to each of the devious puzzles that may be blocking your way. With this digital strategy guide at your fingertips, you won’t miss a single thing in Riven , even those hard-to-find achievements! ----- PLEASE NOTE: The Riven Companion is a separate PDF file that is automatically downloaded when you download the game and has the filename Riven_Companion.pdf. This DLC does not include any additional in-game content. To access the files, from your Steam's Games Library page, open Properties (the 'gear' icon), then choose Manage > Browse local files, and the window where the game is installed on the computer will open. The PDF file can be found there. If you're still having trouble locating it, please feel free to reach out to support@cyan.com for additional assistance.
