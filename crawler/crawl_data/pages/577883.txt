Managing your meager and precious resources, you need to spend every moment wisely. How can you help your child cope while growing up in a community that hates their existence? Be the one person your child can rely on. My Child Lebensborn is an emotional roller coaster challenging you to tackle events based on true stories. Protect your child – Your neighbors will see the enemy they feared in your child. What do you say to your child when they encounter hate and cruelty? Nurture your child –  Clean, feed, and play with your child and ensure each of their needs are met. How will you manage your time and resources? Listen to your child -  Be there when they need you the most and when they need answers to their past. Your support can make all the difference. Care for your child – Times will be challenging. Help your child when they are bullied. Comfort them when they are hurting. How will you stand up to the hatred? World War II is over, but the hatred for everything that your child’s past represents lives on. Born as a Lebensborn, your child will be confronted with discrimination, cruelty, and hatred by the community around you. Do you stand up to the community and confront them, or do you and your child try to hide from it all? Discover their past, the story of the "Lebensborn". Find hidden links to your child’s biological parents, and confront the realities of the present that your child now faces. The decisions you make will change them forever. My Child Lebensborn has been developed in collaboration with the Norwegian Lebensborn children from World War II and has led to the foundation of The Children Born of War Project, working to protect children of enemy soldiers the world over. Parts of the proceeds of the game are donated to this cause.
