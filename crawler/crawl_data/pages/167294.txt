Two Games, One Legacy The original Chaos Faction set the standard for fast-paced, explosive arena combat. Battle through 15 campaign levels, each filled with weapons, characters, and moves, plus even more explosions. Deathmatch mode lets you create your own rules, offering endless replayability with customizable maps, enemies, and allies.
