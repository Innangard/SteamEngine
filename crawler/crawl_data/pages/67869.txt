Enter the arena and hack and slash your way through relentless waves of enemies. Survive long enough and a powerful boss enters the fight. Defeat it, and the next wave begins — with another boss waiting further ahead. Survive the waves Fight through timed waves of enemies and stay alive until the next boss arrives. Fast-paced melee combat Stay mobile and fight at close range using light and heavy attacks while managing your positioning and avoiding being overwhelmed as enemies close in around you. Choose your style Make each run your own with 35 different character sets and 32 unique arenas. Mix and match character themes and arenas to create your favourite combinations, or let the game choose for you and see what you get. Arenas with their own identity Battle across a huge range of colourful environments, from candy kingdoms and underwater worlds to Viking halls, futuristic cities, icy landscapes and more. Chase a higher score Push further, defeat more enemies and bosses, and improve your score with each attempt. Keep coming back to beat your previous runs and climb the local leaderboard. Achievements and long-term challenges Complete runs, defeat enemies and bosses, and work towards a collection of Steam achievements that reward both individual accomplishments and progress across multiple runs. Colorful arcade presentation Bold characters, vibrant environments, varied themes and constantly changing combinations give every run its own personality while keeping the focus firmly on fast, easy-to-read arcade action.
