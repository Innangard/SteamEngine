View Community Hub About This Game The elections are just around the corner! - will you be able to win them, overcome all the difficulties and reach the final without stumbling and losing everything you have achieved? ONLY UP: TRUMP FOR PRESIDENT - a game in which you will feel like Donald Trump, going through many difficult obstacles and testing your parkour abilities, because the basis of the game mechanics is the OnlyUp mode! Choose the correct answers, go through parkour of varying difficulty, jump up! In addition to the main idea of ​​the classic “OnlyUp” style, choosing the right answers to questions will influence your further path to the top! You need to jump on right platform (it will be easy even if you don't understand meaning of words on platforms). There is no chance for error here - one wrong step and you will fly down to start again. Game features: - gameplay over two hours (if you complete the game faster without cheats, write to us) - over 15 questions, each of which you HAVE to answer correctly (it is better not to know what will happen if you answer incorrectly) - Donald Trump in the lead role - various parkour difficulty levels - you will navigate through moving platforms and other daunting obstacles - easter eggs It's time to rise to the very top, good luck!
