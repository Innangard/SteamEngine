Opened in 2010 the Zhengzhou – Xi'an high-speed railway, is a cutting-edge and crucial transportation infrastructure in China, connecting two major cities in the country's heartland. Serving a vast population in this area the line was created to reduce travel time between Zhengzhou, the capital of Henan province, and Xi'an, the capital of Shaanxi province. The line is known for its exceptional speed, with modern units travelling up to 350 km/h, and picturesque scenery as passengers travel between stations. In Train Simulator you can take control on the Zhengzhou to Sanmenxia section of this route. Enjoy scenic views of Henan Province travelling through agricultural landscapes, past rivers, rolling mountains, and rural villages. Push the throttle through this picturesque route to reach 350 km/h with the modern high-speed CR400BF EMU. Newly built by SimTech Vision for this route, the CR400BF is one of the fastest trains in the world. Nicknamed the ‘Golden Phoenix’ for its striking white/gold livery, this has been rebuilt in detail for this route with realistic cab controls and operation. Discover the thrills of Chinese high-speed as you explore the Zhengxi passenger line for Train Simulator.
