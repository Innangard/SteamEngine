In the beginning of the scenario there is a choice either to play pure sandbox (no goals, endless mode) or play an interaction mode. In the interaction mode you will be asked to complete different quests and tasks. Interaction mode is a variety of a sandbox mode, thus it's also a relaxing, penalty-free, endless mode. This DLC also contains extra Portraits and an additional in-game music track for Deluxe DLC owners. Missions in this pack: My very own metropolis Driven by a story-line feel yourself a mayor. Care about your citizens, satisfy town's needs and lead it to be a great metropolis. The Middle Plains A sandbox map with a relatively flat terrain, which doesn't causes much railroad building challenges, however, it could be suitable for those who like to build big junctions with turnabouts, which require a lot of space. Mission can be played in an interactive mode or as a pure sandbox. Hornet Bay A sandbox map with a terrain cut by a few rivers and a bay. Cities and villages are scattered across the map with resources and free lands available almost everywhere. The terrain provide low railroad building challenge, which can be suitable for players, who want a relaxed game. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. Blue Swirl Lakes A unique coal mechanics sandbox map that require your management skills. Scenario is built on a flat terrain and eight lakes, which are connected with rivers. The terrain provide moderate railroad building challenge, since player have to decide between longer but cheaper routes or shorter but more expensive ones. Cities and villages are scattered across the map with resources and there are plenty of free lands available almost everywhere. Down to the river A sandbox map with an elevated terrain. Megalopolises mostly appear near the big lake. Small towns and mines are on the right side of the map. Wood logging camps are only available at lower left corner. Oil and derivatives are not available on the map. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. Reunion - a cooperative scenario. A story driven cooperative scenario were it is necessary to connect two railroad networks that are located pretty far away from each other. It is necessary to make sure both partners are making enough money to expand their railroad networks and accomplishing the mutual goal. The Old Valley A sandbox map with a big valley surrounded by uplands, which provide moderate railroad building challenge, since player have to reach resources on the uplands. Cities and towns are placed mostly in the valley. Free lands outside the large cities are scarce resource. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. The Partnership Board - an interactive cooperative scenario. An endless sandbox interactive scenario for two players. The map is built on a large plain terrain with a couple of rivers and contains a lot of cities to develop and complete interactive tasks. The Dried River Once fully fledged river provided its waters for a big fleet, now can demonstrate only tiny a part of the former mighty. Settlements along the river won't survive without trade, which became nearly impossible considering the current state of the river. Take this opportunity to connect the settlements with your railroad network for a mutual benefit. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. Extra content: Playing the cooperative scenarios is much more fun with new portraits. Owners of this DLC will obtain five special portraits for multiplayer sessions. There is also a special bonus for our fans. Additional music track is provided in this mission pack for Deluxe DLC owners.
