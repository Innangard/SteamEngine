YK Band is a musical duo that creates exotic soundscapes through an array of rare ancient instruments from around the world, including the West African Kora, the Mediterranean Tar, and the sole existing recreation of the 3000-year-old Tartessian Lyre. Led by their sublime vocals, YK invites you to reexperience the world of OPUS: Echo of Starsong through a playlist of emotions that range from loneliness, hope, love, loss, to rebirth, immersing you in a sea of memories. Composed of Yerko Lorca and Guan Ying, the multi-award winning duo combines ancient instruments with synthetic waveforms to explore the possibilities of narrational music and awaken stories from a long-forgotten past through their unique blend of ancient and exoticism. The album includes 12 arranged tracks that distill the world of "OPUS: Echo of Starsong" into a musical composition, extending the original soundtrack to serve up an all-new auditory feast.
