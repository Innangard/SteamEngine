View Community Hub About This Game Progress through a magic skill tree to unlock the secrets of the glade. Collect items and refine them into powerful magical resources. Use these items to build your own productive base. You can farm crops, slay monsters and automate your alchemical production on your journey to uncover an all-powerful artifact. COLLECT AND MANAGE RESOURCES Forage items from the woods with the power of your mighty cursor. Craft these into ingredients for buildings and incremental upgrades. UNLOCK AN INCREMENTAL SKILL TREE Explore and progress through an incremental skill tree which changes how you play. Pick the best options and become an all-powerful wizard. BUILD A PRODUCTIVE BASE Build machines, self-chopping trees and elemental distilleries. Organize your base to maximize your comfort or to maximize your productive output. Feature List Build structures to refine, store and generate items. Do alchemy. Refine ores into ingots. Craft chaos from the elements.  Concoct and harness the world's primordial matter. Collect in Clicker game style. Chop wood, mine rocks, harvest wheat! You can even prong primordial cubes! Access the blueprints shop to unlock new buildings. Farm monsters and grow crops. Trade resources for gold. Uncover hidden locations deep within the woods. Complete a bespoke incremental experience with constant, clear progression.
