to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game [h1]Dungeons of Uhr – A First-Person Survival RPG[/h1] Descend into the Depths… If You Dare In Dungeons of Uhr, you awaken in a vast, uncharted cave system with nothing but your wits and a strange letter. The air is thick with the scent of damp stone and something far older—something watching from the shadows. To survive, you must explore, scavenge, and fight your way through twisting tunnels, forgotten ruins, and caverns teeming with unspeakable horrors. Game Features: [h3]1. A Living, Breathing Cave System[/h3] Discover ancient ruins, underground lakes, and bioluminescent forests where light is your only ally. Navigate environmental hazards—collapsing tunnels, toxic gases, and pitch-black abysses. [h3]2. Fight or Flee[/h3] Dynamic Combat: Wield makeshift weapons, crafted traps, and rare relics against cave-dwelling beasts and twisted entities. Stealth & Survival: Some horrors are best avoided. Move silently, extinguish your torch, and pray they don’t hear you. Boss Encounters: Face towering subterranean monstrosities guarding the deepest secrets. [h3]3. Craft, Adapt, Survive[/h3] Scavenge Resources: Mine ore, harvest fungi, and loot the remains of lost explorers to craft tools and upgrades. Build Safe Havens: Reinforce crumbling tunnels, rest at camp, and light braziers to keep the darkness at bay. Upgrade Your Gear: From rusty daggers to enchanted lanterns, every piece of equipment could mean life or death. [h3]4. Uncover the Dark Truth[/h3] Piece Together the Mystery: Who built these ruins? What lurks in the deepest chasms? [h3]5. Friend or Foe[/h3] Travel in a company, hire mercenaries with their own goals and lore. Echoes of the Past: Find journals, ghostly visions, and cryptic carvings that reveal the cave’s cursed history. [h3]5. A Haunting Atmosphere[/h3] Stunning, Immersive Visuals: Realistic cave formations, dynamic shadows, and eerie bioluminescence. Sound Design That Chills: Distant whispers, skittering claws, and the groan of the earth itself pull you deeper into dread. Will You Escape… Or Become Part of the Darkness? Dungeons of Uhr is a hardcore survival RPG for those who dare to face the unknown. Every step could be your last. Every light could be your salvation.
