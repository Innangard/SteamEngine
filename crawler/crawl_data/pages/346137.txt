A murky evil distresses the lives of the villagers of a remote region surrounded by snowy mountains. Your mission, as inspector of the King, is to rid these lands from the ghoul that haunts them. Features: Smooth low poly cartoon style graphic, musical elements of traditional Balkan songs, for a deep immersion in the tale. Main game: six-chapter horror adventure, story inspired by ancient slavic folk legends. Follow the yarn to discover the darkness behind the curse of the Upirs. Four extra games included! The first one: hunt wolves in a lonely valley, but beware of the giant daemonic red wolf! The second one: collect in the forest enough wood logs for the winter before the ghoul finds you. The third one: defeat the horde of vampires emerging from their graves by swinging your axe. ... and the last one: you will be the Vampire, looking for revenge! Kill all the villagers and drink some tasty blood!
