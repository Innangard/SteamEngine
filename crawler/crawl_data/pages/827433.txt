Download Farming Simulator 25: Pumps n' Hoses Pack Free Download View Community Hub About This Content Start the pumps and boost your farming operation with umbilical systems, manure separation and configurable biogas plants! A wide range of new machinery, tools and buildings can be used to expand your farm's manure distribution and resource storage infrastructure. Overall, more than 30 items from Dutch manufacturer Schouten, German steel storage specialist Stallkamp, and biogas technology manufacturers BvL and 2G have been included to introduce new gameplay features to Farming Simulator 25. Manure & Slurry Distribution Systems Install umbilical systems on your farm! Attach draggable hoses of different lengths and ultra-light drag hose injectors, rather than heavy manure tanks, to your tractor to protect the soil and fertilise your fields, while enjoying the convenience and high capacity of mobile pumping units, such as wagons and trailers, stationed nearby. Combine hoses spanning hundreds of metres to efficiently fertilise your entire field, and improve pumping conditions with additional water tanks and compressors! Manure Separation Liquid manure separators allow you to separate the liquid and solid components of manure, making your resources more versatile. There are multiple stationary and mobile separators available. Choose one if you want to use the dry parts of manure for animal bedding on your dairy farm, sell them for a profit, or fertilise your fields with them, just as you would with the liquid. Make the most of your manure! Biogas Plant Configuration Upgrade your biogas plant by configuring it to suit your needs and preferences. Various modules can be placed and attached dynamically around the core facility of your biogas plant to customize it. You decide where to build reception areas, fermentation tanks, gas holders and power plants to produce electricity, as well as other components in various sizes, including a gas torch to burn off excess gases from your high-performance biogas plant.
