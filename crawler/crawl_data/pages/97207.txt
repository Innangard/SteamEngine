But upon arrival, you discover the cabin abandoned and your grandfather has vanished, drawing you into a realm of Nordic mythology and long-lost memories of summers past. Embark on a quest to find your missing grandfather and survive the untamed wilderness, facing supernatural creatures from Finnish mythology and the living, animistic forces of Nature. FIND YOUR ROOTS Embark on an empowering journey to uncover your past and solve the mystery behind your granpa's disappearance. Discover hidden truths that will reshape your destiny.
