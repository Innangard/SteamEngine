Master 40 levels of arrow chaos in this fast-paced pixel art platformer! Dive into Arrow Survival: 15 Seconds, a thrilling 2D pixel art platformer where every second counts! As a skilled archer, your mission is to survive 15 seconds per level by dodging deadly hazards and strategically firing arrows. Each of the 40 unique levels introduces new challenges, from bouncing platforms that redirect your arrows to treacherous spikes and evolving mechanics that keep you on your toes. Plan your shots carefully! The angle and direction of your arrows can make or break your survival, as they ricochet unpredictably or trigger new obstacles. Test your reflexes, sharpen your strategy and reach the final level. Can you survive the last 15 seconds of each level? Features 40 fast-paced levels with increasing difficulty and new mechanics. Pixel art visuals for a nostalgic arcade vibe. Strategic and survival arrow-shooting gameplay. Dynamic platform challenges, from bouncing surfaces to deadly traps.
