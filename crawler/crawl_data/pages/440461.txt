View Community Hub About This Game DeathOmen is more than just a psychological horror game, it's a deeply personal expression of my own emotional journey. After dedicating myself intensely to my first game, Horror Story: Hallowseed , I found myself mentally exhausted and plunged into depression. In that dark period of 2022, I wrote a short yet profound story, something meant to invite reflection on life, death, and our fleeting existence. My goal wasn't to create a game that explicitly explained itself, but rather one where players could intuitively grasp the hidden meaning behind the narrative. However, shortly after starting, I lost all motivation and abandoned the project completely. It felt like the story was destined never to be told. Then, after almost three long years, something within me reignited, a newfound desire to finish what I'd begun. Although the game's development was restarted, the story I so passionately wanted to share remained intact. DeathOmen is intentionally brief, a psychological horror experience designed to immerse you in the disoriented mind of someone struggling to differentiate reality from illusion. My hope is that through playing, you'll not only feel immersed in tension and mystery but also connect with the deeper emotional truths that inspired this game's creation. I share this openly, hoping that by understanding my personal journey, you might better appreciate the vision behind DeathOmen . 🚫 Important: I do NOT use AI to generate my games. I code and develop everything myself. KEY FEATURES A Journey of Mystery and Madness:  Expect the unexpected at every turn. Jump-Scares and Microscares: From heart-stopping moments to subtle, creeping horrors, DeathOmen keeps you on edge with relentless psychological scares. Play as Yourself: Step into the shoes of your psyche. The fears your character faces mirror your own. Are you brave enough to face them? Nightmarish Survival Mechanics: - Spend some nights in a house filled with unknown horrors. Dive into an atmospheric first-person experience where every shadow holds a secret, and every sound hints at your doom. DeathOmen isn’t just a game—it’s a test of courage, endurance, and the human spirit. Will you survive the night?
