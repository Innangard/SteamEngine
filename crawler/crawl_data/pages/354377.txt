In celebration of the Steam release, Robot Space Frenzy has been updated with an entirely new Second Campaign! You can access this second episode by selecting New Game in the Main Menu, then choosing the new content in the Chapter Select level. This chapter adds five challenging new levels, plus a new weapon and completely new enemy type, in addition to the common variant of the original game's Armored Cargo Loader Boss! With a focus on open areas and massive encounters, this episode is sure to provide a challenge for anyone looking for more Robot Space Frenzy fun!
