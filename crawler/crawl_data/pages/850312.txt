Project: Simulated Emotional Xenotech is a first-person sci-fi adventure focused on exploration, dialogue, and relationships with female androids. You take part in an experimental program designed to study emotions and relationships between humans and artificial beings. Inside a futuristic facility, you'll meet androids with different personalities, motivations, and views on their own existence. Some are looking for affection. Others want independence, answers, or something they aren't willing to share right away. Get to Know Them Conversations are at the center of the game. Each android has her own story, and the way you interact with her affects how your relationship develops. Your responses matter. Depending on what you say and the decisions you make, characters may become more open, react differently to future conversations, or take their stories in another direction. Explore and Uncover Secrets Move through 3D environments, interact with characters and objects, and complete quests to progress through the story. Along the way, you'll encounter simple puzzles, access terminals, and learn more about the facility, its experiments, and the different factions of androids. Not everyone has the same goals, and some discoveries may change how you see the characters you've met. Your Choices Shape the Story The story develops through conversations, quests, and decisions. Different characters have their own interests, and your actions can affect how their stories unfold. Your decisions can lead to different outcomes, including multiple endings. You won't see every storyline in a single playthrough. Key Features First-person exploration: Explore futuristic 3D locations, interact with the environment, and complete quests. Dialogue and relationships: Get to know female androids, each with her own personality, motivations, and story. Meaningful choices: Your responses influence relationships and the direction of the narrative. Branching storylines: Discover different paths, android factions, and multiple endings based on your decisions. Light puzzles: Solve simple logic challenges and interact with futuristic terminals. Sci-fi setting: Uncover the details of an experimental program studying artificial emotions and human relationships.
