Its citizens are the desperate, the thrill-seeking, the dangerous and the mad. At this height, the legends of the street are made. Airframe Ultra is a fast-paced battle-racer where you compete with friends and foes in split-screen and online multiplayer. You can thrash with pipes, chains and plasma torches or leave them in the dust with boosts and pure white-knuckle cunning. Whatever it takes to win. So load up, make your racer, and settle things like a legend on the cracked asphalt streets of Megacity West! You are gutterborn. Squatters. Smugglers. Pickpockets. Thieves. But with a second hand Airframe, a few custom mods, and a big heavy baseball bat you can become the one thing with freedom in a city of billions: A racer. It won't be quick and it won't be clean but if you've got the will and the wits, you can win a circuit. One circuit becomes two, two becomes ten, and all of a sudden street rats from Ellay to the Bay are chanting your name. Infamy is its own reward. Your key to a new world where first place is all that matters. From the rusting shipyards of Slabtown to the sleek 'scrapers in Vantac... from the dune dens of the Ysidro edgelands to the hulking arcologies of the Texicali ZEGO zone, Airframe racers from all across Megacity West compete for fame, fortune and a chance at glory... three feet above or six feet below. You can have everything you've ever wanted. All you need to do is win. Surviving your first race is just the beginning. You'll rack up kills and cross finish lines. New helmets, new clothes. Make yourself a statement-- a uniform. Win enough parts and you can make a new 'frame. Legends on the track collect their infamy, cash in on their reputation and find new ways to make their mark. And if you want to become a legendary racer yourself, you can't be a nobody. You've gotta find something new, something unique, something never before seen or heard of. In a city where everything is a copy, you've got to become an original.
