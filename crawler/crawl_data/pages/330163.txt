Advanced systems like full shop customization, expansion, hiring staff are only available in the full game In this demo, you can : Open and collect Mystery Eggs , discover new figurines and grow your collection. Complete and sell Diorama collections. Sell Mystery Eggs themes MiniMon / Magical Vault / Victory Vault / Dragon Hatch and figurines, Diorama base sets. Decorate your shop with carpet and various types of plants Manage Your Shop You are in full control. Start with an empty space and build it out. Physical Stocking: Order inventory using your in-store computer and physically place the eggs on your shelves. Price Control: Set the price for each type of egg and adjust based on supply and demand. Checkout & Operations: Scan items, take cash and card payments, and keep your store clean to attract more customers. Expansion: Use your profits to expand your store's physical space, buy more shelving, and upgrade your point-of-sale system. Open Eggs, Find Figures The core of your business offers a strategic choice: Sell Sealed: Sell mystery eggs directly to customers for a consistent, reliable income. Open for Profit: Open the eggs yourself. Inside are collectible action figures with different rarities (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary). A rare figure is worth far more than the sealed egg. Craft and Sell Dioramas Don't just sell loose figures. Maximize their value with the crafting system. Themed Sets: Collect figures that belong to the same theme, like "Space," "Fantasy," or "Dinosaurs." Build Dioramas: Place a complete set of themed figures onto the corresponding diorama base. Sell for High Value: A finished diorama is a premium product that sells for a significantly higher price than the sum of its parts. This is the key to making big money. Grow Your Business Start small and build your way up. Reinvest your earnings to unlock new series of mystery eggs, each with it’s own unique set of figures and dioramas. KEY FEATURES First-Person Shop Management: Physically run your store, from stocking shelves to operating the cash register. Inventory and Price Management: Order stock via a computer and set your own prices for all products. Unbox and Collect: Open mystery eggs to find hundreds of different action figures across multiple themed sets. Figure Rarity System: Figures range from common to legendary, directly impacting their value. Diorama Crafting System: Combine figures of the same theme on a diorama base to create high-value collector's items. Store Expansion and Upgrades: Grow your store's physical size, improve your equipment, and customize its appearance. Day/Night Cycle & Customer AI: Manage your shop during open hours and prepare for the next day after closing.
