Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We have chosen to go with an Early Access release to allow for a community of players to get involved in the final balancing and help us learn more about the game's balance. Astronomics has been in development for quite some time and it features an engaging core loop that we have plans to take farther and we want to make sure that the direction we take is one players enjoy. Early Access allows us to gather that data as we balance the game and figure out the final elements that will make it great.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to remain in Early Access for approximately 8-10 months. The length of the Early Access stage will be driven by the feedback and response of the community.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version is meant to be more balanced, more polished and feature some additional systems that result from our iterations during Early Access. We already have plans to introduce some more mining tools along with some new contract types and we are looking forward to hearing the community's thoughts on what could make the game even greater in its full release.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “We initially had planned for the game to be playable from start to finish but over the course of playtesting have expanded the scope and now this Early Access release will contain only the first 3 regions with the first 12 asteroid classes along with plenty of contracts, tools, equipment and upgrades for players to get into. In total we have 7 regions planned and will be opening up the game to new regions with each content update as we go. This means that the game is currently not playable through to the end as was previously stated in the answer to this question. We will be growing this experience and continue balancing the gameplay through Early Access.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “There is no definitive plan but it is likely that the price of the game will increase when we complete the Early Access stage.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We are hoping to gather player feedback and learn through the forums about how the game is being played and what elements players think would improve their experience. We are interested in quality of life as well as larger system ideas and building Astronomics into the greatest game that it can be. Explore And make money while doing so! Astronomics is an asteroid mining automation game where every operation is temporary, every asteroid is different, and every haul gets you one step closer to home. Procedurally generated asteroids pass through your mining claim, bringing new opportunities (and challenges) with every expedition. Intercept them with your Freighter, pilot your Shuttle to the surface, and establish an operation before the asteroid moves out of range. Time is Cube Corp Credits, you know. Find valuable resources, build mining infrastructure, and dispatch Workerbots to transport materials around the asteroid. As your operation grows, automate extraction and logistics to get more done before time runs out. Then bring everything back to your Freighter for manufacturing before selling your goods at Cube Corp. Build Your Mining Operation Every asteroid is a new operational puzzle. Explore procedurally generated environments, discover resources, and decide how much infrastructure you can build. And how much you can realistically get out before the asteroid leaves your claim. Start by gathering resources yourself, then build toward a fully automated operation. Explore passing asteroids and discover their unique resources, properties, and challenges. Build mining infrastructure to extract resources while the asteroid is within your claim. Deploy Workerbots to transport materials and keep your operation moving. Command your bots with your Command bot and organize increasingly complex logistics. Automate extraction and transportation to get more resources with less manual work. Defend your equipment from the pirates who would rather take your hard-earned resources for themselves. Plan around changing conditions as procedurally generated asteroids put your operational skills to the test. As your mining operation becomes more sophisticated, you'll have to think beyond simply finding resources. What should be automated? Where should your bots go? What equipment is worth bringing? Did you totally forget a Workerbot on that last asteroid? Whoops. Ok, how much can be extracted with the current equipment before the asteroid is gone? Work Your Way Home Complete contracts throughout the belt, taking on jobs ranging from data collection and search-and-rescue operations to equipment manufacturing. Then use your earnings to upgrade your equipment and expand your Freighter. As your gathering capabilities grow, you'll need more manufacturing capacity to process materials. And protect yourself from the pirates who also want your materials. Explore a vast map of asteroid regions, discover new resources and challenges, expand your operation, and take on increasingly ambitious contracts. But don’t forget to keep your eye on the real prize: a Residency Permit on Earth. Because Earth isn't home anymore. Well, not yet, anyway. The asteroid belt is where the work is. It's where the money is. And if everything goes according to plan, it's where you'll earn enough to finally come home...for the very first time. Good luck!
