View Community Hub About This Game Pandora's Toybox blends the boundless freedom of a sandbox God Game with the deep, replayable strategy of a Roguelite. SHAPE A LIVING PIXEL WORLD Every human in your realm is a unique individual with their own name and traits. You are the ultimate architect: play as a passive observer watching them evolve naturally, or actively intervene. Bestow technological revelations, spawn vital resources, or manipulate the world to guide your people from scattered tribes into sprawling, fortified city-states. DRAFT YOUR OLYMPIAN COUNCIL Command a roster of 8 legendary deities, including Zeus, Hades, Apollo, and Ares. In every run, you must draft 3 gods to form your divine council. With nearly 200 skill combinations, discovering overpowered synergies is the key to mastering the game. CULTIVATE FAITH & CRUSH HERETICS Faith is your ultimate currency. Perform miracles to inspire your populace and answer their temple prayers to unlock game-changing artifacts that buff your entire civilization. But growth breeds war. When rival city-states and heretical factions rise to challenge your dominance, how will you respond? Lead your faithful into a bloody Holy War, or simply use your divine wrath to wipe enemy cities off the map. ENDLESS ROGUELITE PROGRESSION Every attempt to build a civilization offers a completely fresh run. Collect "Faith Crystals" across your playthroughs to unlock permanent meta-upgrades. Expand your pantheon, learn devastating new abilities, and tackle increasingly difficult procedural map challenges. BEYOND THE CAMPAIGN Take a break from the main progression with two massive expanded modes: Sandbox Mode: Enjoy pure creative freedom with all powers and artifacts unlocked. Build your flawless utopia (or torture your creations). God-Clash Mode: Trigger a cross-dimensional brawl in a chaotic, auto-battler-style showdown where major pantheons face off for supremacy.
