Embark on a harrowing journey through the shadows of Crimson Ridge in Massacre at the Mirage, where a seemingly innocent trip to the movies transforms into a spine-chilling battle for survival. Set foot in The Mirage, the cherished cinema of this picturesque town, and brace yourself to experience the terror from the dual perspectives of its dedicated staff and unsuspecting patrons. You'll navigate the daily operations, serving moviegoers while desperately trying to thwart a masked killer disguised as a clown. As both staff and patron, each character you embody offers a unique perspective, adding depth to the gameplay. Amidst the flickering lights and hushed whispers of the theater, danger is ever-present. The eerie corridors and dimly lit spaces become a sinister labyrinth where the killer unleashes unrelenting brutality. Thrust into the heart of the chaos, you must balance the demands of your role while combating the relentless onslaught of the bloodthirsty assailant. In this twisted game of cat and mouse, survival is anything but guaranteed. Stay alert and vigilant, for the killer could strike at any moment, turning this cinematic experience into a terrifying fight for survival. 🎬 Immerse yourself in the eerie ambiance of an old, run-down movie theater. 👥 Play as different characters, each with a unique perspective. 🔪 Navigate dark corridors while evading the relentless killer. 🩸 Experience a terrifying journey packed with suspense and horror. 👻 First-person indie horror gameplay intensifies the immersive experience.
