Dating back to the 1840s, the Midland Main Line is a key passenger and freight route which links London to the East Midlands, and en route passes through historical Derby, where trains have been constructed for the past 180 years, and up to Sheffield, the “Steel City” as it is known for its contribution to steel production in the industrial revolution. Today the line sees dozens of trains a day, rushing commuters into South and North of England, and allowing people easy access to the culture and vibrancy of the Midlands. Just Trains have faithfully recreated the 36-mile section between Derby and Sheffield, featuring accurately detailed stations along the way and lush green countryside in-between. Race through the hills on inter-city and local passenger services, and take charge of freight trains on their way across the nation.
