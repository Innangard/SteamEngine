This is Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War! The Arachnid menace has spread far from their home planet of Klendathu, invading human territory across the galaxy. You’ll spearhead humanity’s noble efforts to safeguard the galaxy first hand. Wield the diverse and unique Mobile Infantry arsenal to burn, dismember and cripple the Arachnid threat in first-person, high-action warfare through the eyes of Major Samantha “Sammy” Dietz . This game will immerse you in the most realistic depiction of war ever made! Our Games and Theory department have crafted an authentic retro FPS experience , including high-quality FMV broadcasts from Federation legends like General Johnny Rico . Visit Zegema Beach Welcome to Zegema Beach , one of the most luxurious vacation spots in the galaxy! Explore its beautiful beaches, breathtaking landscapes and prestigious resorts. Thanks to the Federation, enjoy a free trip to paradise! You’ll be granted an entire stage of its in-depth trial by fire : Operation Razorwire. BUGS won't let you take a day off! No time to waste, Trooper! We've got work to do. The Arachnids are swarming the idyllic shores of Zegema Beach as we speak. These enemies of humanity are brutal and possess their own skills . Fast, flying, spewing, and skewering: with this many different types of Bugs in the area, the threat is everywhere . Clean the pristine beaches from the Bug infestation , defend the military outposts and pave the way for reinforcements , and help evacuate the wounded and civilians. Itʼs your choice when and how to tackle each objective. Fights with all means The Federation recognizes your commitment to the cause, Trooper. Choose your rank and difficulty level from the four selectable options in the mission briefing. In this mission, you can count on the support of your fellow Mobile Infantry Troopers and the best the Federation can offer in terms of anti-Bug weapons and equipment . Different rifles can be picked up on the field , each featuring their own range , ammunition , and rate of fire . Turrets and supply crates are placed at strategic points on the battlefield. Thanks to her effective military training, Sammy can also access a full arsenal of abilities like grenades , flares or a trusted melee boot knife to exterminate disgusting Bugs.
