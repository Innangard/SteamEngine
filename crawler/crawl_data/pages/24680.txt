Epic Adventures | Uncover Secrets as Magister Merlin A new adventure awaits with every season! Long ago, the seed of life drifted to Esperia from the stars and took root, giving birth to a land of wonder. However, as the gods fell during a chaotic war, six great factions emerged, each vying for power and dominance.
