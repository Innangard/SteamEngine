N/A View Community Hub About This Game Shelves And Sorcery is a relaxing fantasy organization game about turning absolute chaos back into order. Weapons, potions, spellbooks, fruits, vegetables, magical curiosities, and thousands of other items have been scattered throughout three fantasy shops. Pick everything up, find where it belongs, and slowly watch each shop return to the way it was meant to be. Take your time and enjoy the satisfaction of putting everything in its place, or learn every corner of the shop and race against players around the world for the fastest time. Three Shops to Restore. Four Ways to Play. Choose from three shops of dramatically different sizes, each with its own setting, atmosphere, and challenge. Then return to them in four different modes , whether you want to relax, chase the fastest time, wield every bit of magic from the start, or organize without magic at all. • Casual Mode – No timer and no pressure. Take your time, enjoy the atmosphere, and organize every shelf at your own pace. • Timed Mode – Race against the clock and compete on the global leaderboards . Learn where every item belongs, master your Sorceries and Enchantments, perfect your route, and keep pushing for a faster time. • Archmage Mode – Start with every Sorcery and Enchantment already unlocked. Take on the entire shop at full magical strength and see just how powerful an experienced shopkeeper can be. • No Magic Mode – The magic of the world has escaped. No Sorceries. No Enchantments. No magical shortcuts. Just you, thousands of misplaced items, and the knowledge you've gained along the way. The Hearth & Barrel 1,000+ items to organize A cozy shop tucked away in the quiet village of Burg. Once a welcoming stop for locals and passing adventurers, its shelves have fallen into disarray. With 1,048 items to restore, it's a comfortable introduction to the satisfying world of organizing. The Merchant's Rest 3,000+ items to organize A well-loved shop in the bustling market town of Stonebridge, built through years of honest work by its humble merchant. With 3,085 misplaced items, restoring The Merchant's Rest offers a much larger challenge with plenty of shelves waiting to be put right. The Grand Athenaeum 4,000+ items to organize High above the clouds on the floating isle of Vane stands a legendary shop filled with magical treasures from across the world. With 4,066 items scattered throughout, The Grand Athenaeum is the largest challenge of them all. Take your time restoring it, or master every shelf and chase the fastest time. Sorcery Makes Everything Easier Thankfully, you don't have to organize thousands of magical objects entirely by hand. As you make progress, unlock Sorceries designed to help you tackle the mess. Gather matching items from around the shop, reveal objects you've been struggling to find, or use magic to send items toward the shelves where they belong. The more you organize, the more powerful your magical toolkit becomes. Enchant Your Shopkeeper Magic isn't limited to spells. Unlock Enchantments that permanently improve your abilities and change how you move through each shop. Carry more items at once. Increase your reach. Move faster. Jump higher. Eventually, even take to the air. Combined with your Sorceries, these abilities can turn an experienced shopkeeper into an organizing force of nature and become an important part of chasing faster times. Three Shops. Thousands of Items. One Place for Everything. Whether you're carefully organizing The Hearth & Barrel after a long day, working your way through the thousands of items inside The Merchant's Rest, or fighting for a place on the leaderboard in The Grand Athenaeum, Shelves And Sorcery is built around one simple satisfaction: Finding where something belongs... and finally putting it there.
