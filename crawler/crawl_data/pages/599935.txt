Buy Vambrace: Cold Soul - Soundtrack $6.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Vambrace: Cold Soul is a roguelite fantasy adventure game with a huge cast of characters, an epic story, and a sweeping orchestral soundtrack. Lead composer Hyunho Kim sets the mood of the cursed city Icenaire with additional tracks composed by Sung-woo Hwang and Hakyung Kim . Now, Devespresso Games is proud to present 12 selected tracks mixed and mastered by Norihiko Hibino and Sp3ck . This original soundtrack includes the single Golden Skies by Kino feat. Emi Evans . Tracklist: The Lost Lady Into the Shadowlands Orgram's Drum March of the Green Flame Grr-Chuggah-Grr Flight of the Sorceress Dance with Death Dark Clouds Gather Treachery Afoot Cold Soul The King of Shades Golden Skies Hyunho Kim, Lead Composer: "The Vambrace: Cold Soul Original Game Soundtrack sprung from a collaborative effort between three composers. The result is a collection of tracks that captures the haunting quality of the game's gothic fantasy atmosphere. Its sounds range from the hopeful, warmth of the Lost Lady Inn, to the icy energy of Flight of the Sorceress, and is finally topped off with Golden Skies, featuring the ethereal voice of Emi Evans. It is my hope that listeners will be escorted on an exciting journey through the fantastic world of Icenaire."
