GAMEPLAY Weird anomalies will occur. Spot them , run to get a new ticket, and keep going. If your guess is right, you get closer to your movie room. If you get it wrong, you go back a few numbers—unless you've stacked enough correct guesses to earn a shield in the cinema loyalty program. WELCOME TO OMNIPLEX The Last Screening is a first-person horror anomaly-finding game set in a cinema venue. You will play as Susie, a movie fanatic who has just received a ticket to watch a film she’s been eagerly awaiting. Use your finest perception skills to help Susie get to her film. The first run we guarantee your safety; after that, you are on your own. TERROR AWAITS Not every scare comes from the screen. Monsters, weird voices, blood... You better watch out because it's all coming for you. EVERY DETAIL MATTERS Trick is keeping your sanity whilst doing so. A big scare is not always a jump scare. Notice the subtle shifts or Susie may never make it to her seat. BUILT ON HORROR LEGACY The Last Screening is inspired by find-anomalies games that present liminal spaces and psychological horror walking simulators, like ' The Exit 8 ', ' The Cabin Factory' , ' Hospital 666' , and the old goodie 'P.T.'. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains psychological themes including trauma, death, depression, and self-harm. Players will encounter disturbing imagery, unsettling audio (such as child laughter in eerie contexts), and moments of emotional intensity. The narrative explores loss, memory, identity, and inner conflict through a surreal, cinematic lens. Dialogue and scenes may allude to overdose, suicide, and mental breakdowns, presented in a metaphorical and symbolic environment. Discretion is advised for players sensitive to these topics.
