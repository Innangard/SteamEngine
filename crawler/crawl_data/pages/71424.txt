This demo lets you experience the core loop of managing a capsule shop: stocking machines, opening capsules, collecting figures, and serving customers who are hunting for their next rare pull. It’s a focused taste of shop life, blending management, collecting, and a bit of chaos. Build Your Gacha Collection Open capsules yourself and discover a variety of figures, from giant robots and kawaii cats to meme-inspired collectibles. Keep your favorites and display them proudly in your personal collection, or sell them to fund your growing shop. Manage Your Shop Design and organize your capsule store by placing machines, decorating the space, and optimizing the layout. Keep customers happy with trending capsule series and a clean, well-managed shop environment. Robot Fight Club Manage your cozy gacha shop by day, then enter the underground world of Robot Fight Club by night. In hidden parking lot arenas lit by JDM car headlights, worker robots battle while yakuza, sumo wrestlers, influencers, and other VIP customers place bets. Bet on your favorite robots, win extra cash, and expand your capsule collection. Collect & Sell Spin the machines, uncover rare figures, and decide what to do next. Sell your pulls for profit or keep them as part of your collection. Every capsule series brings new surprises and decisions. Order & Expand (Demo Slice) Restock your machines by ordering new capsule series and tracking what sells best. Earn money to unlock a number of machines and upgrades, giving you a preview of how expansion works in the full game. Customize & Hire (Limited in Demo) Personalize your shop with decorations and visuals that make it stand out. The demo offers a small taste of shop customization and management systems found in the full experience.
