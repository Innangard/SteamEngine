Just a Little Purr suit is a turn-based strategy puzzle game. 65 handcrafted levels , a fun adventure designed for the whole family! With no time limit, you can choose a direction and use cards to perform an available action within the level, such as: Common movement cards: Cards that move the kitten forward by slots. If in doubt, you can undo the action. The card will return to its initial position. Explore different solutions to the challenge! Special Cannon Card: Allows you to choose the direction to launch the kitten, propelling it by the distance specified on the card. Remember, every cat always lands on its feet. Special Trampoline Card: The direction cannot be chosen, but you can determine the distance. However, it will always move forward. Special Portal Card: Some myths say cats travel between mysterious universes. In Little Purrsuit , this myth is true. The portal will always appear ahead of the kitten, allowing it to pass from one portal to another. Special Create new Platform Card: Creates a new platform to aid in navigation. The environment will challenge you! Boosters and totems can either help or hinder you by changing your position.
