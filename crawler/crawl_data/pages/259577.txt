Take on a world full of advanced automation and computing processes brought colorfully to life. Use your powers to solve puzzles, fight digital threats and conquer complex platforming sections. Boti: Byteland Overclocked combines classic 3D platforming with a splash of innovation. Double jump, dash, hover, and glide through vibrant levels, discovering fantastic combos inspired by a world of robots, computers, and technology. Enjoy gaming together with the seamless drop-in, drop-out co-operative mode. Embark on your quest to save Byteland alone or with a friend whenever you want with gameplay that seamlessly adapts between solo and two-players at any point in the game. Dive into Byteland, exploring the secrets of a cyber-world inspired by robots, computers and technology both new and old. You’ll find a plethora of collectibles and data to assemble and relocate to their proper places inside the byte-tastic world of Boti! Experience multiple twists on classic platforming. Master creative arcade elements, such as rhythm-driven musical slides and magnetic gameplay, as you explore and conquer challenges. Joining Boti are his charming nanobot companions, aptly named Zero and One. Zero is the calm, cool, and collected robo helper while One basically has no off switch. Each main character is fully voiced and oozing with charm and personality.
