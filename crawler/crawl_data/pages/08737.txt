Explore a massive open world, customize cars beyond recognition, and compete with thousands of real players thanks to full crossplay support. Key features of the game: Full crossplay and a living world Burst into a unified ecosystem! The PC version offers full cross-platform play with mobile devices. The game servers are always full: communicate via built-in voice chat, organize car meets, pull into gas stations and auto shops, add like-minded people to your friends list, and trade cars right on the city streets. Car hangouts and customization Make your car unique: Interior and detailing: Every car has a fully detailed cabin and interior. Some models have multiple interior options available, and sports cars will surprise you with animated retractable panels. Deep tuning: Adjust suspension and wheel camber. Perform full engine, turbocharger, gearbox, and exhaust system swaps. Visual style: Create a unique look with dynamic vinyls and a variety of body parts. Variety of game formats The game offers content for both single-player enthusiasts and multiplayer fans: Racing mode: Try your hand at four official disciplines — Drag, Circuit racing, Drift , and Rally . Free activities: Head to the Drift and Time Attack tracks, available both in single-player for practice and in multiplayer to compete with friends. Parking and driving challenges: Test your driving skills in 82 real-world parking and driving challenges. Challenges await you across a wide variety of vehicle classes — from classic and sports cars to pickups, tow trucks, and heavy trucks. Auto-life simulator: Try on basic roles in the open world — work as a taxi driver or patrol the streets in police mode, issuing fines to other players. Built for PC Explore the world comfortably with full support for PC peripherals: keyboard and mouse controls, gamepads, as well as racing wheels with Force Feedback for maximum car control. The game is adapted for a wide variety of display resolutions, including modern ultra-wide monitors. Informații despre conținutul generat de IA Dezvoltatorii descriu astfel conținutul generat de IA pentru jocul lor: In game development, artificial intelligence is used solely as a supporting tool. We employ neural networks to enhance and refine 2D art (loading screens and promo banners) created using our in-game screenshots, after which they undergo manual color correction and artifact removal. AI also assists us with translating in-game text and generating the announcer's voice for racing modes. All key game elements, including 3D car models, tuning, and the open world, are created entirely by hand by our team.
