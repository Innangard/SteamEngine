to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Climb to the Peak for Your Son! Samuel is an ordinary lumberjack seeking peace deep within the Bright Forest. His only wish is to teach his son, Jonah, the family trade. However, an ancient terror known as Claw turns this ordinary day into a bloody nightmare. Defeated by the savage attack, Samuel wakes up to find his son missing and his memory of the event wiped clean. Axe and Claw is a father's journey woven with guilt, love, and vengeance. Grab your trusty axe, fill in the blanks in your memory, and tear through everything that stands in your way to Claw's peak! Features: A Personal and Gripping Story: A dramatic narrative that lets you deeply feel the bond between Samuel and Jonah. Trail of Lost Memories: You don't remember what happened. Piece together the clues as you climb and uncover the terrifying truth. A Ruthless Ascent: A breathtaking vertical adventure starting from the peaceful Bright Forest to Claw's treacherous, freezing peak. Hardcore and Technical Gameplay: Master your trusty axe not just to chop wood, but to brutally execute Claw's minions and overcome obstacles. Atmospheric Pixel-Art: A visually stunning pixel-art world, meticulously crafted from the tranquility of the forest to the horrors of the summit.
