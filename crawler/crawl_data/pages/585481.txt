Be on your best behavior and KEEP DADDY HAPPY. You all live together. You, Dad, Mom, Brother, and the rest of the demons. Try not to cause problems so you don't have to be punished. Scrub, sweep, clean, make fries, kill spiders, bring Daddy his pills—essentially, be a good kid. Explore the house and look for treats and action figures scattered around. This might be the only fun part about living here. Visit various parts of the house, face difficult situations, and experience the gruesome reality of a child-mouse trying to maintain a sense of normalcy in an abnormal household. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Mild violence — mostly cartoonish, but with underlying heavy themes of psychological trauma and domestic abuse. Hints of alcohol, alcohol addiction, and intoxicated characters — though never explicit.
