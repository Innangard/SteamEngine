In this demo, you will experience the core fantasy of running your own pizzeria: preparing pizzas, managing your kitchen, serving customers, handling pressure during busy shifts, and taking the first steps toward turning a small family restaurant into something much bigger. 🔥 Try the Core Kitchen Experience Prepare ingredients, stretch dough, manage your oven, serve orders, and keep customers satisfied. The demo focuses on the core cooking and service loop, giving you a clear feeling of how Pizza House Simulator plays moment to moment. More equipment, automation systems, assistants, repair mechanics, advanced kitchen upgrades and interactable NPC's are planned for the full game. 🌦️ Step Into a Living Italian Town Enjoy the warm atmosphere of a Mediterranean pizzeria and get a glimpse of the world growing around your restaurant. The demo already includes the day & night cycle, giving each shift a natural sense of time and atmosphere. For the full game, we're planning to expand this living world further with seasonal weather, rainy evenings, foggy mornings, snowy winters, random events, shifting customer behavior, economic crises, and unexpected disasters that affect your business over time. 📈 Start Building Your Restaurant Empire The demo already gives you a taste of the business side of your pizzeria. Take loans from the bank, invest in the stock market, follow economic news, react to ingredient prices, listen to radio broadcasts, and read local newspapers to predict future events before the market reacts. A tomato farmer strike today might become tomorrow's financial opportunity. In the full game, these systems will expand with deeper progression, more events, bigger risks, and new ways to grow your restaurant empire. 🪑 Customize Your Pizzeria Begin shaping your restaurant and discover how progression can change the look and feel of your business. The full game will expand customization with more decorations, kitchen upgrades, equipment, buffs, unlockable options, quests, and gameplay achievements. Build anything from a small cozy family pizzeria to a luxurious Italian restaurant. ⚡ Shape Your Own Playstyle The demo gives you an early look at the pressure, pacing, and decision-making behind running a busy pizzeria. The full game will let you choose mottos and management styles that affect gameplay. Prioritize customer satisfaction, maximize profits, automate your workflow, or embrace total chaos. Ignore broken equipment, push your staff too hard, and deal with the consequences including fires, thieves, mafia incidents, and unexpected disasters. 👨‍🍳 A First Taste of a Bigger Game Pizza House Simulator is being designed for solo play, online co-op multiplayer for up to 6 players, community-driven chaos, restaurant competition, leaderboards, and Twitch integration. These larger systems are planned for the full game. The demo is focused on introducing the core experience: making pizzas, managing your kitchen, serving customers, and feeling the first spark of building your own restaurant empire.
