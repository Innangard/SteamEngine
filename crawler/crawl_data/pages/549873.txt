Your clone is hiding among the crowd, ready to kill you when you’re most vulnerable. Endure procedural paranoia as you search to find your doppelganger before they find you. Scavenge for weapons, manage limited ammo, and prepare carefully to strike. If your assassination fails, you’ll lose the opportunity to stalk from the shadows. As you progress, you’ll gain a greater understanding of your cursed fate and reveal terrifying new threats. Roguelite elements offer upgrades and improvements, giving you greater flexibility to endure increasingly complex challenges.
