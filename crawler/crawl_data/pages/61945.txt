Now you too can experience ungodly levels of pandemonium with the Explosive Expansions Pass! This epic pass grants Exploding Kittens® 2 Superfans access to three huge expansions. Layer even more havoc upon the bedlam already available in the base game – why yes, we ARE running out of synonyms for “chaos” at this stage! Imploding Kittens Expansion: The Imploding Kittens Expansion introduces a new level of strategy and peril to the game. Imploding Kitten Card: Unlike the Exploding Kittens, this card doesn’t eliminate you immediately. Instead, it’s placed back into the deck face up, creating a ticking time bomb that all players must avoid. When drawn again, it causes instant elimination. New Card Types: Discover new strategies with cards like ‘Reverse’, which changes the direction of play, and ‘Alter the Future’, allowing players to peek at and rearrange the top three cards of the deck. Streaking Kittens Expansion: The Streaking Kittens Expansion adds an awesome new ability to the game – holding onto an Exploding Kitten card! Streaking Kitten Card: With this daring card in hand, when you draw an Exploding Kitten card, you can hold it in your hand without going up in flames! But if someone steals it from you, they won’t be so lucky… New Decks: Discover the Streaking Kittens deck, or ramp up the risk with the Danger Mode deck! Alternatively, chain up a ridiculous number of Attack cards with the Attack of the Attacks deck. Barking Kittens Expansion: Rounding out the Explosive Expansions Pass is the Barking Kittens Expansion! Add a sneaky game of chicken to your next match… Barking Kitten Card: When you play this card, whoever is holding the other Barking Kitten card explodes! But if no one has its counterpart yet…you just made yourself a target… Even More Decks: An incredible eleven new decks! From decks built around seeing the future to decks rigged with unnecessarily aggressive cards, this expansion delivers epic matches by the fistful! INCLUDES: IMPLODING KITTENS EXPANSION – Adds new cards, mechanics and increases the player limit. STREAKING KITTENS EXPANSION – Adds new cards, mechanics and three decks. BARKING KITTENS EXPANSION –  Adds new cards, mechanics and eleven decks. Get ready for CAT-ASTROPHIC CARNAGE with the Explosive Expansions Pass today!
