View Community Hub About This Game Dirt Clicker is a super satisfying incremental digging game where dirt is your currency & buried treasures are just a few scoops away. Start with nothing but a spoon and a patch of dirt. Dig your way through the ground, uncover treasures and build up your fortune by investing in better tools. Unlock a growing collection of upgrades, from shovels and pickaxes to jackhammers and beyond, each helping you dig up even more dirt. You can’t keep digging by yourself forever. Get some pals like the; Mushrooms, Frogs, Golems or any of the other creatures to keep it going while you focus on the more important stuff. Spend your well earned dirt in a massive skill tree, you can upgrade your tools & unlock ways to strengthen your pals. Every upgrade makes digging just a little easier and faster. When you've dug as deep as you can, it's time to Prestige! Reset your progress, turn your hard-earned dirt into precious worms, and spend them on permanent upgrades that reshape the way you play. Unlock new systems, discover powerful synergies, and turn every new run into an even bigger digging adventure. Every new layer has something new waiting for you! Dig up valuable treasures and rare relics. Some are worth selling, while others are harder to find and give you an extra boost. Open chests & equip your pals with hats that have game changing effects. You might stumble upon strange little surprises throughout the world.
