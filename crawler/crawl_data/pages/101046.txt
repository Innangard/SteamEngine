Evil has taken over the ocean, and it's time for these grizzled dogs to grab their guns. Gather your crew, haul the loot and rebuild your base to grow stronger and dive deeper with every run. DOGS WITH GUNS Team up as a pack of badass dog-divers with guns and dive into a rotting ocean. Blast through hordes of dangerous fish and paint the water red with AKs, shotguns and miniguns. LOOT THE OCEAN These waters hide forgotten treasure. Harpoon it and haul it to the sub as a team, before the cable snaps under the weight or a toothy local bites through it. PILOT YOUR SUB The submarine is your main ride and your shelter from the horrors of the sea. Pilot it as a crew, pack it with upgrades and head deeper, where the ocean gets even darker and hungrier. REBUILD THE BASE The hub lies in ruins, but not for long. Rebuild it piece by piece, and residents will move in with new guns, gear and modules for your sub. A stronger base means a stronger pack. EXPLORE A STRANGE OCEAN The ocean isn't as scary as it looks. It's full of bright colors, odd locals and strange encounters, but the deeper you dive, the gloomier it gets.
