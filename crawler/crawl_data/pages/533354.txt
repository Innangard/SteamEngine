Fluffy omelet or boiled rice? Cinnamon roll or vanilla cheesecake? Sausage or tempura shrimp? What does your character want today, and what will you put in their lunchbox? Each order is a card with wishes, preferences, and exclusions. Read it carefully and decide what exactly to put in the lunchbox. Choose items from four categories: meals, sweets, snacks, and drinks. Consider the client’s tastes, avoid what they dislike, and assemble the perfect set! Dozens of levels await you, each offering different ways to assemble the perfect lunchbox! Some levels let your imagination run free, while others require careful thinking to choose exactly the products the character wants. More than a hundred cute and delicious items, a cozy atmosphere, and calm gameplay await you — perfect for relaxing and unwinding.
