The complete original soundtrack to Feed the Scorchpot: 5 tracks by Dragos Nastasie, scoring the whole run from the quiet first year to the one where the dragon wakes up hungrier than you can cook for. Early on it's kitchen music you can properly relax to. It does not stay that way. As the years stack and the meals get more desperate, the music tightens right alongside them, up to the moment a handful of tiles turns into a board-breaking number and the dragon decides whether you're the chef or the main course.
