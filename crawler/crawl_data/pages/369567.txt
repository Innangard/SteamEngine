View Community Hub About This Game The only way out is through. What started as a quiet night camping with friends has become a found-footage nightmare. Military jets roar overhead. A distant siren cuts through the silence. Drawn to a seemingly abandoned bunker for safety, the doors slam shut behind you. Now, you are trapped in the dark, forced to navigate the decaying corridors, uncover the truth behind a long-forgotten experiment, and survive the horrors lurking within. The Bunker is a short PSX-style, VHS horror experience (~60 mins)  that blurs the line between reality and nightmare. Grainy, distorted visuals, realistic first-person movement, and an oppressive atmosphere pull you into an immersive psychological horror experience. Every flicker of your flashlight could reveal something you wish you hadn’t seen. Cryptic notes line the walls, remnants of a failed experiment—and something else is still lurking in the shadows. Will you unravel the bunker’s secrets, survive its horrors, and discover the fate of your friends? VHS Horror Aesthetic: Distorted visuals, analog grain, and eerie found-footage presentation heighten the fear. Immersive First-Person Perspective : Realistic camera movement pulls you deeper into the nightmare. Unravel a Dark Experiment: Cryptic messages, unsettling remnants, and a growing sense of unease drive the mystery  The Bunker keeps. Atmospheric Sound Design : Echoing footsteps, distant whispers, and unsettling silence keep you on edge. Psychological Horror : The terror isn't just what you see—it’s what you think is there.
