View Community Hub About This Game Welcome! You’ve just met the developer who was pulled into their own game. Become a guild master, grow your village, hire guild members, and clear dungeons. With glass-hearted guild members who quit the game after dying in a dungeon, what kind of chronicle will you write? Write your own chronicle. Everything in the game depends on your choices as the guild master. You can focus on crafting, engage in battles, or nurture your village. Or you could simply spend the day watching your lively guild members chatter away. Your choices will become the history of this world. Build your own village. Demolish burned buildings, construct new ones, tend your farm, craft goods, and decorate your village. Guild members stay busy growing herbs, brewing potions, and fishing in the village. The items you create can be sold at the auction house for profit. And what will you do with the gold you earn? The growth of the village is in your hands. Invite guild members to your guild. Guild members with various personalities collect ores, brew potions, and cook according to their abilities. They also join the battles! If you’ve ever played an online game, you’ve probably met these unique and colorful guild members. Invite them to your guild. Dungeons & Raids with Guild Members To return the developer to the real world, you must grow the village. Try battles with various party compositions. During simultaneous battles, party members will level up and earn equipment and gold. Be careful! What’s scarier than bugs is the fragile mentality of a guild member who quits the game after a defeat! If you make your guild members and equipment strong enough in lower dungeons, it will greatly help protect their mentality from increasingly stronger monsters. If you lose a battle, analyze the battle log to find improvements and try again! Trade at the Auction House The Auction House is where you buy and sell the items you need. If you need something immediately, you can pay a high price to get it, or if you have time, you might grab an item someone urgently listed for less than half the price. Your choice will greatly affect how quickly you accumulate wealth. Wise trading is the shortcut to rapid growth. Sell items to visitors, from hundreds to thousands, and earn profits. Complete the Codex The Codex is divided into Alchemy, Cooking, Blacksmithing, and Enchanting, depending on the guild members’ professions. Farming, Mining, and Fishing are also included. The higher the level, the more valuable the crafted items become, and the faster you can acquire resources. Will you be able to return the developer to the real world? We look forward to your story.
