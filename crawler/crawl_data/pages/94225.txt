View Community Hub About This Content The shifting sands whispers and calls A shadow of chaos upon the land falls Beneath a scorching sun, a vista of rolling dunes and massive pyramid monuments rising towards the sky reveals itself. In Kingdom Two Crowns: Pharaoh Lands, you are setting out on a new adventure in the Valley of Kings and the domain of powerful gods in an ancient Egypt-inspired setting, only to discover the Greed now stalks the nights unleashed by foreboding chaos. Features: Desert lands of opportunity: Explore an awe-inspiring setting with grand sandstone monuments and pyramids. Deliver your people through hardships, prepare for the harsh environments and find refuge along the great river Gods among people: Encounter the manifestations of the great power such as the temple of Ra or harness the power of Anubis. Through cunning and courage, discover the true intent of the chaos and journey to its source. Treasures and mythical creatures: Uncover the secrets of places of power, solve the riddles to find powerful artifacts... or mounts like large scorpions, crocodiles and great black cats to aid you on your travels ahead. New threats lurking in the dark: Tread carefully as the enemies of these lands will prove to be a different challenge than you are used to. Expand your strategic options with a new type of troop to defend your Kingdom.
