Fish drift past swaying seagrass while you work, study, or unwind. Move or resize the aquarium to fit your space; when you want to get closer, open the larger management view and make the ocean your own. Meet neighbors that notice you Raise young marine fish, give them names, and choose who swims beside you. Drop in live food and watch the chase, or touch a curious sea creature to see how it responds. In Moonlit Coral Bay, the Pearl Octopus hides in a shell, peeks out, lifts a little stone, and reacts to your touch. Follow the current to new discoveries Drifting treasure chests arrive over time. Choose a voyage route and a discovery wish, then open your finds to meet new residents and uncover plants, scenery, and supplies. Arrange your favorites and gradually restore habitats for fish, seagrass, algae, microbes, and other marine life. Stored chests wait for you; exploration pauses when storage is full. One game, many ways to feel at sea Your ocean can brighten into sunlit ruins or turn toward the included Moonlit Coral Bay. Enter the moonlit theme to use its four fish, two companions, scenery, changing light, and ocean moments. The wider collection unfolds through normal play; there is no separate expansion purchase. Come back when you like Feeding is optional, and your fish never go hungry, get sick, or die. Fish growth and pearl income continue while you are away, within the game's offline limits. Clear drifting litter and maintain equipment to help the habitat recover efficiently, or take a quiet moment just to watch your little sea. A movable, resizable aquarium for the edge of your Windows desktop. Marine fish to raise and name, plus sea creatures with their own behaviors. Drifting treasures, voyage choices, and a collection to discover over time. Habitats to restore and arrange, including the complete Moonlit Coral Bay theme. Gentle progression without hunger, illness, or death for your fish.
