However, as the development progressed, as a story behind the game and a whole world developed, the concept of music also began to transform and adapt to it. Finally, an accompanying album was prepared for the game's world, as if it were the music of a novel or film adaptation. The 13 tracks created this way were structurally transformed to work during the gameplay. The music was written with electronic instruments, mainly synthesizers. The first sketches were ambient electronic melodies, noises, and harmonies made with modular synthesizers. The part that forms the basis of the final tracks was selected from the experimental recordings, which sometimes lasted several hours. These created rhythmic alternations and strange atmospheres in the background of almost all the tracks. The melodies above them were often improvised, played with several synthesizers, and supplemented with drum machines. Gergely Buttinger is a composer, writing music mainly for computer games and several electronic music productions.
