Your niece has a spare key and the best of intentions. The shop opens tomorrow, and every flower you ordered is somewhere on the floor. It's okay. You know where they all go. An armful at a time Pick flowers up until your arms are full. Cycle through what you are carrying, find the basket that wants a rose, and set it in. Each basket takes one kind of flower, and it tells you when it is done. Make it yours Every basket can be painted. Pick a colour, or several, and lay them in bands, alternate them round the rim, or scatter them. The finished shop is one you arranged, not one you were handed. No timers No score, no upgrades to grind, no one waiting. Stop when you like and come back to the same floor. Full controller support.
