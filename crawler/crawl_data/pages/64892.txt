Open a maid café with five unforgettable maids, each with a secret of her own. Fill those empty tables and set your sights on three Maidchelin stars . Welcome, Manager! Another order, another customer—and the next drink won’t make itself. Mix drinks, serve customers, scan receipts, and count out the change. Running low on ingredients? Order on your phone and a Maidroid courier will bring them to your door. When orders pile up, share the workload with your maids. Rush hour waits for no one! What will your maids share with you as you work side by side? Fulfill their wishes and follow their bond stories to discover the secrets behind those bright smiles. Time for the manager to step in. Take on troublemakers yourself and keep your café safe. Once they’re out cold, scoop them up in a princess carry and personally escort them outside. After closing, Dance Video Day lets you choose a maid and an outfit, record a longer dance performance, and share her moment in the spotlight on in-game social media. Today, the spotlight belongs to your maids! More customers? Time to think bigger. Expand your dining area and kitchen, arrange furniture and equipment, and make the café your own. Serve special drinks from the festival menu when festivities begin. Then get ready for your toughest guest yet: the exacting Maidchelin inspector. One rundown café. Five maids. Three stars to chase.
