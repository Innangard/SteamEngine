View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to your new potion shop! As the new owner of the town’s abandoned potion shop, your goal is to build a thriving magical shop from the ground up. Alchemist's Garden is a cozy, incremental crafting game focused on relaxing progression, experimentation, and discovery. Explore diverse locations ranging from lush gardens to frozen peaks and mystical voids. Click to clear obstacles and uncover magical ingredients, recipe scrolls, and unique shop decorations. Take your foraged ingredients to the Crucible to actively experiment and discover new recipes. Once you have unlocked a recipe, use your Grimoire to continually craft potions and enchanted items to stock your shop. Start from humble beginnings and grow your shop. Put your crafts on display and watch your shop flourish. Expand, Upgrade, and Decorate your shop to attract even more customers!
