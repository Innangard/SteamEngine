About This Game Inspired by classic fighting games from the 90's such as Street Fighter, Fatal Fury and The King of Fighters, 'Pocket Bravery' channels the 'Neo Geo Pocket' aesthetic for a modern fighting game with a retro twist! With original characters, vibrant colours and an impressive combat system, 'Pocket Bravery' is designed with high-level players in mind, while making it easily accessible for beginners to pick-up-and-play! Choose Your Fighter! Select from a diverse 12 character roster, each with their own unique abilities and play styles! Local and Online Multiplayer with 'Rollback Netcode Clash head-to-head worldwide with ultra-low latency rollback code in online multiplayer or take on your friends locally! Master An Elemental-Based Combat System Channel your inner 'Ichor', a unique elemental energy blessing each one of the playable characters in Pocket Bravery. Master the elements to open up new  offensive or defensive possibilities! Unleash Screen-Filling Ultimate Attacks! Charge up to unleash your ultimate attack!
