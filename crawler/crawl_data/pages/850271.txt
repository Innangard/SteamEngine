You wake up in an unknown world and slowly start to explore the game like your character explores the world. Awaken the Soulstone by feeding it cards Explore locations, gather resources Research and craft 200+ weapons - but beware, ~80% of combinations are dead ends Find a strange creature and grow it through 140 evolution paths Visit the village, meet NPCs, take on quests Push through 20 dungeon floors of randomized enemy factions Transcend and unlock one of 12 prestige classes Defeat the dungeon overlord Step through the portal into a reshuffled world and do it again A free demo is available. The full version will go deeper - more tiers, more evolutions, more systems, more world.
