Start with a stick rod and a tiny dinghy, then work your way up to bigger catches and bigger boats. If you love the co-op chaos of PEAK or the laid back casting of How to Fish, you'll feel right at home on this boat. Every fish goes in the boat The more you catch, the lower your boat sits. Get greedy and you'll capsize or sink. Grab a bucket, patch the leaks and decide together when it's time to bring the haul home. Fish with your crew Fight fish that run, leap and thrash, then get a friend to land them with the net. Sell your haul for better rods, tackle and boats. Fill your Fish Log, chase rare catches and unlock hats. You can hook your friends, too. Head further out Explore eight fishing zones, from calm lagoons to reefs, wrecks and the Deep. Bigger fish mean rougher water and hungry sharks. Back on the island, cook your catch over the campfire and unwind in a hammock.
