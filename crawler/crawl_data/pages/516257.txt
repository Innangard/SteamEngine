When a mysterious client leaves an old, dusty journal under your shop's door, its powerful magic is impossible to ignore. As the Inkwellers, you and your partner dive into the worlds hidden within enchanted journals, working together to solve puzzles, overcome magical challenges, and find a way forward. Beyond the journal’s pages awaits a world shaped by magic, mystery, and strange challenges. To move forward, you and your partner must rely on each other — sharing clues, connecting ideas, and solving puzzles together. Features: Co-op Gameplay: This game is designed exclusively for two players. You'll need to work together, combining your wits and skills to solve puzzles and progress through the story. Look closely, share what you find, and work together to understand each challenge before putting the solution into action. Immersive Experience: Communication is crucial. Speaking to each other and sharing discoveries will be key to solving puzzles and advancing in the game. Can you and your partner solve the puzzles hidden within the journal and find your way back? Step into a magical world built for two, where communication, teamwork, and careful observation are the only way forward.
