View Community Hub About This Game Start small, dream big, and turn a humble dog-walking business into a thriving global empire. In this colorful idle business game, every venture you build adds another source of income to your growing fortune. Buy more businesses, expand your operations, and watch your empire grow as profits roll in. Hire managers to keep your businesses running automatically, even while you’re away. Invest in upgrades to boost profits and speed up production, then take on quick challenges and timed bonus events for extra rewards. As your empire grows, unlock new industries, achievements, and permanent mogul perks. When you’re ready to start fresh, claim mogul prestige. You’ll reset your businesses and upgrades in exchange for lasting bonuses, making it possible to build back faster and push further with every new run. Key features Build across 14 businesses, from neighborhood services to global industries. Hire managers and level them up to automate production.
