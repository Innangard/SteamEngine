This is an easy contender for game of the year.” 5/5 – So Many Games “Continuing the resurgence of modern games capturing that retro feel, Isle of Reveries knows what it wants to be and achieves it.” 8/10 – Pixel Byte About This Game Take control of an owl named Lief as they make a pilgrimage skybound to the forsaken Isle of Reveries, where it's said that any wish can be granted. Players will traverse a Metroidvania-style world, fight through six puzzlebox dungeons, each with their own unique themes and mechanics, and meet charming anthropomorphic characters along the way in this Zelda-inspired adventure. Use your fairy companion’s magic to duplicate and spawn a variety of objects in the world, and discover how they interact with your robust set of items to solve head-scratching puzzles. EXPLORE A VAST OVERWORLD & CONQUER DUNGEONS Eight different biomes in the overworld. Seven different dungeons, each with their own unique theme, mechanics, and key item. COPY & SPAWN OBJECTS Use Lief's fairy companion to copy and spawn a myriad of objects, using them to solve puzzles and fight terrifying enemies. FORGE A COMMUNITY Construct various buildings and forge a township. Characters that Lief saves throughout the game will move to the township, offering new quests and stories. DUNGEON CRAWL WITH A FRIEND Play through TWO separate multiplayer stages with a friend, either in local co-op or online via Steam Remote Play Together.
