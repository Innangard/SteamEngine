Brrbrrrbr." - Virgil HELL IS IMPURE! Literally. It’s filthy down here. So filthy, in fact, that Dante and Virgil (now trapped inside a broken Rumba) are forced to rely on a dead modern guy named Faust - who’s got nothing better to do than clean it up... or does he? Dante’s Rumba is a clever and comedic route-planning puzzle game set in a cozy, modernized version of Inferno. You’re not here to slay demons - you’re here to tidy up. After all, these poor damned souls deserve to suffer in a clean, orderly environment. For each circle, we introduce nuanced variations of the core route-planning mechanics , adding layers of depth and strategic complexity. These variations keep gameplay fresh, entertaining, and challenging while maintaining the familiarity of the fundamental system. For players who enjoy story-rich experiences, there's a fully optional narrative packed with dark humour, philosophical snark, and awkward encounters with various characters - like Cleopatra, Homer, Mephistopheles, and the endless confusion of explaining tabletop games to a medieval poet. Features: Strategic Route Planning Guide the Rumba through increasingly complex arenas of sin. Optimize your path, avoid traps, and leave no infernal mess behind. Humorous Hellscape Explore a reimagined Hell full of warm taverns, broken metaphors, and deeply unhelpful poets. Replayability and Exploration Strive for high scores, discover secrets, and unlock extra dialogue and content as you revisit and master each level. Bite-Sized Gameplay Designed for quick play sessions - perfect for cleaning Hell on your lunch break.
