As you clean each belonging, you will learn the backstories behind the various objects placed in each chapter. Adora returns home after her mother's passing. She starts to clean long ignored items within the house. As each object is restored to its pristine condition, the items start whispering memories of the past to her. As she cleans more items, she discovers forgotten and even hidden stories. What kinds of stories will she experience? Virtual ASMR Experience the carefully crafted audio that captures the characteristic of each tool you use. The different ambient sounds of each chapter will make you feel as if you are actually in the game. A Touching Story Recall the memories within each object. Where did Adora and her mother's relationship start to fall apart? Will she be able to understand her mother after her passing? A Charming Art Style Appreciate the captivating art style that is uniquely expressed with pixelated textures. Game Features Experience the early chapter of a heartfelt and emotional story about family. Forget about time limits and failure. Just relax and enjoy! The items won't break like they do in real life! Admire the beautiful artwork that elegantly expresses Adora's story. Listen to the sensual original background music, which complements the relaxing gameplay. Enjoy the oddly satisfying sensation you get in ASMR and Restoration videos in a game!
