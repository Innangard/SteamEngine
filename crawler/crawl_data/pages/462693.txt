You are the D-504 developer and your job is to create virtual spaces for next generation video games. Falling asleep in the workplace became commonplace when the 18-hour workday was introduced. Aquarelle is a meditative author's project in which your goals are blurred and nothing awaits. However, you can delve into the wreckage of past projects, search and not find answers, enjoy the authenticity of the game universes, go through hidden tasks and not receive rewards. The development of the plot and the ending will depend on the decisions you make during the game. You have many paths and you are not limited by anything in this world. And the controls based on the simulation of real human movements will open up a completely new gaming experience for you.
