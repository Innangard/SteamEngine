SokoChess is a minimalistic puzzle game that combines Chess with the Sokoban block-pushing formula. The goal of each level is to push black pieces to predetermined positions while making sure they don't capture all your pieces. Push the black pieces The goal of each level is to push all the black pieces to their destination. Careful where you move The black pieces will only move if you give them an opportunity to capture one of your pieces. 70 levels Infinite undos Don't like a level you are playing?
