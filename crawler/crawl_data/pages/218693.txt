Throughout your adventures in Ba Huang, you can participate in their individual stories and uncover their secrets. These characters will also influence the world and its inhabitants in various ways. Additionally, the five characters will provide invaluable assistance during your journey, sparing you from exhausting yourself while fulfilling various needs. The Flower of Mist (胧之花) A dragon maiden who arrives in Ba Huang using the power of the Hetu. To survive and find her way home, she must explore and seek a means of return. However, her bond with the player makes leaving difficult, forcing her to make an ultimate choice. The Flower of Wood (木之花) An animated puppet created by a deity. As her consciousness awakens, she strives to escape her fate. She yearns to become a true individual, not merely a tool controlled by others. The Flower of Ink (墨之花) An ancient being trapped within a book. With the player’s assistance, she overcomes numerous trials to free herself, uncovering a tale of Ba Huang’s past in the process. The Flower of Snow (雪之花) A woman imbued with the power of mystical ice, which often causes snow disasters around her. She strives to rid herself of this power so that she can be with the one she loves. The Flower of Emperors (帝之花) A long-lived demon queen. Her extraordinary perspective allows her to observe the world and its workings, uncovering the ultimate secrets of Ba Huang. However, this knowledge will come at a devastating cost.
