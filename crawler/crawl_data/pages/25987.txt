View Community Hub About This Game 🚀 ✦ PILOT A STARSHIP INTO THE VOID ✦ 🚀 You are the pilot of a starship venturing into a newly discovered dimension filled with vibrant energy and mysterious matter . As you navigate this neon wilderness, you'll encounter distress signals and opportunities to complete side missions that reward you with valuable resources and upgrades. ⚔️ ✦ BULLET-HELL COMBAT ✦ ⚔️ Battle against swarms of alien enemies and colossal bosses . Every fight is a test of your strategy. Collect resources to upgrade your starship in real-time . Master pixel-perfect bullet-hell gameplay and near-miss escapes. Discover 20 core systems , merge them to unlock 16 devastating Hyper Powers , game-changing abilities that can turn the tide of any battle. No two runs are ever the same. 🎮 ✦ PLAY YOUR WAY ✦ 🎮 Choose from four difficulty levels that match your playstyle. Whether you're seeking a relaxed cosmic journey or a hardcore bullet-hell challenge, Void Resurgence adapts to you. ⭐ ✦ PROGRESSION & COMPETITION ✦ ⭐ Death is only the beginning. Earn permanent upgrades for your starship that carry over between runs. Unlock 10 ship classes , each with different starting weapons. Test your skills against pilots from around the world. Compete in over 420 global leaderboards . Prove your mastery and etch your name among the stars as a true ace.
