Content For This Game Browse all (1) Astrophobia Soundtrack $1.29 $1.29 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Astrophobia is  heavily inspired by the novella At the Mountains of Madness by H. P. Lovecraft. STORY In 2001, astronauts Ben Dyer (the player) and Henry Danforth are sent to the Moon as part of a secret mission, Project Kadath, to study ancient megalithic architecture far older than humanity. The civilization that built it did not survive, but something else did. GAMEPLAY & FEATURES This is a slow-burn and narrative-focused experience told through character dialogue and environmental storytelling. The plot will unfold throughout multiple set-pieces and locations, such as the moon base, the moon's surface, and the monolithic city beneath the ground. Immersive PSX-era aesthetics, including affine warping, vertex snapping, distance culling, pixelated textures, and low-poly models. Exploration-focused gameplay centered on finding key items to progress through the story, enhanced by unique set pieces and locations. Optional CRT VFX for even more retro vibes. Horrific monsters based on Lovecraft's deep mythos and creature design. An original soundtrack. Low gravity hops, obviously. Average playtime is 45-60 minutes depending on your pace. You may get even more playtime if you are trying to 100% the achievements! NOTE: Astrophobia is best experienced in one sitting. However, there is an automatic save feature that activates at the beginning of each level, incase you need it. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Blood, profanity, and frightening jump scares.
