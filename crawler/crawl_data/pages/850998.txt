View Community Hub About This Content The Cash is King! Supporter Pack gives you the chance to support us a little extra while unlocking a few cool items in Cash is King! Cash is King! is developed by a two-person team, so every bit of support is welcome. INCLUDED IN THIS PACK: • Money Gun: With the Money Gun, you can really make it rain. Don’t worry, it doesn’t waste your hard-earned cash. It only shoots fake money. • New Furniture: With the Supporter Pack, you expand Sofa King’s selection with cool glow decorations. • Supporter Badge: Every supporter receives a badge that is shown in the main menu. There is also a small trophy that stands in both the trailer and the office. Please keep in mind that this pack is meant only for people who want to support us on top of buying the game. It is more of a thank-you for your support than an expansion of the game. Although we all think the Money Gun is pretty cool, right? ;) Thank you for your support!
