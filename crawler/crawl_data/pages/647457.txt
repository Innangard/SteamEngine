Mr The Tree informs him that the Spikies love to be zapped with Rainbow Guns.  Being rather genial, Rubber Ducky decides to spend his day going around and zapping things. He's a Duck, you're a Duck! Control Rubber Ducky, a square version of everyone's favorite fowl, as he spreads his laser-joy! The Rubber Ducky Gameplay!
