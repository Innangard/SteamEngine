Rotten Flesh is a cosmic horror survival game where you have just lost your dog named Roy and you have to find him by shouting for "ROY" on your microphone. Venture deep into the sewers where your dog was lost - however, something else can also hear your screams in the tunnels... and they are not human. TRIGGER WARNING: This game may be disturbing to some audience as it contains cosmic horror, fear, jumpscares, loud sounds and the enemy can also hear your voice from your microphone input. You as the loving owner that you are have to find your dog by shouting for "ROY" on your microphone. If your dog is near, Roy will bark back to you - however something else can also hear your screams .. and it's not Roy. Go into a terrifying journey to find a man's best friend in this PSX-Style horror game. Solve puzzles, find weapons to survive and fight your way through to get back your beloved dog Roy, only if you dare. That's what good owners do. Features: Weapon system: Find weapons to protect yourself and fight your way to hope for your dog. Microphone Input: shout for 'ROY' on your microphone to call for your dog, if he is close Roy will bark back. However, your dog is not the only one down these tunnels. Terrifying story: Venture the terrifying and disturbing sewers and tunnels that was supposedly occupied by a leading cult. Discover secrets and find your dog, Roy. PSX Gameplay: PSX style graphics combined with cosmic horror that will leave players with hair up their skin . Puzzle Solving: Find clues, solve puzzles and try to advance to the next objectives. Survival elements with full inventory system, objectives, clue-findings and tools to help you in escaping. Puzzles to unsolve, mysteries to uncover. Use your mind to overcome obstacles that you will find in your way. Stealth System: Try and sneak through the terrifying enemies that can only be seen by scanning them. Hiding System: Hide from the enemies under tables, while trying to stay quiet as they can hear you from your microphone when you are in hiding mode. Gut Wrecking Moments: Walking on broken glass can make noises that the enemy may hear, same as moving crates or knocking over objects. all these things can give away your position. Try to evade them or use them as your advantages. Inventory Management: Find supplies, upgrades, health packs and more items will be vital for your survival. Use your microphone to call for 'Roy'. If your dog is near, he will bark back to you. Search the sewers and tunnels for your lost dog, as any loving owner should do. Find guns, ammo and weapons to protect yourself against the bigger threats. Manage your inventory, find supplies and combine them to help you in the journey. Gut-wrecking moments with an enemy that can hear you and your voice. Protect yourself not only from enemies, but also from infected rats that will hurt you. Hide in lockers, under tables and in furniture to stay hidden from the enemy. Creepy environment with narrow and claustrophobic corridors and rooms. Rotten Flesh is a horror survival game made by one developer at Steelkrill Studio. This is objective-finding game with puzzles to solve, and terrifying encounters, inventory system, stamina, pistols, shooting mechanics, microphone input and a lot more features.
