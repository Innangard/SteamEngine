Designed for players who want a flexible way to stream from their own console, this app supports physical controllers, adaptive video quality, and advanced display options. Core experience: • Connect to your own PS4® or PS5® console for remote streaming • Sync app settings and console profiles across your devices • Multiple local profiles for different users or setups • 1080p / 30 fps SDR Remote Play streaming • Automatic bitrate adjustment based on your network • Internet remote streaming support • Automatic and Manual console registration flow for supported consoles • Easy PSN sign in • Support for up to 4 player Multiplayer PS4 and PS5 Remote Play • Support for up to 4 player Multiplayer PS3, PS4 and PS5 Cloud Streaming • 720p / 50 fps SDR Cloud streaming • Optional PS3, PS4 and PS5 cloud-streaming compatibility where available ("PlayStation Plus Premium subscription required for cloud streaming") • Support for Windows, Linux and SteamOS Unlock the full  experience with Asobi Premium with features such as, • Full controller support such as haptics and gyro • Full 1:1 adaptive trigger support when using a DualSense controller •
