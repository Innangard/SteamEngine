View Community Hub About This Game STEP INTO THE FORGE AND CRAFT LEGENDS Become a dwarven blacksmith with big ambitions. Craft the best gear in the realm, automate your forge with goblin workers, deliver on the kingdom's biggest orders, and maximize profit! FROM ORE TO BLADE Delve deep into the mines beneath the mountains to reach the rarest ores. Smelt raw ore and forge everything from plain iron to legendary dwarven steel. Hit the mark with every hammer strike to forge weapons and armor worthy of your ancestors. ENCHANT YOUR CREATIONS Find Elara the elf in the elven ruins and learn the art of elven enchanting. Add magical properties and elemental affinities to the weapons and armor you forge. Enchanted gear unlocks higher-tier orders and helps your heroes survive the dungeons and the arena. HIRE GOBLINS AND FULFILL MORE ORDERS Hire goblin workers and automate your forge. They will mine, smelt, craft, and run the workstations while you focus on the fine work. Expand your crew as your forge grows, and upgrade their workstations for maximum dwarven efficiency. RECRUIT HEROES FOR THE DUNGEONS Visit the tavern to recruit heroes and equip them with your finest weapons and armor. Send them into the dungeons while you keep working the forge. The better their gear, the more gold, rare materials, and high-tier items they bring back. RELAX AT THE ANVIL OR CHASE EVERY COIN Every day brings new customers to your forge. Keep the hammer swinging from sunrise to sundown, or take it slow and craft at your own pace. Give customers what they ask for. They might ask for a plain blade, a sturdy steel chestplate, or a sword with a touch of magic. Display your finished pieces on the racks in your shop and set your own prices. Seasonal festivals bring new faces and special rewards throughout the year. ARENA COMBAT Put your gear to the test in real-time arena battles. Send in the heroes you armed, trigger their skills at the right moment, and earn gold with every victory. TRADE LIKE A DWARF A growing smithy needs a steady flow of raw materials. Head down to the docks and swap the materials you have for the ones you need. A true dwarf never turns down a good deal. UPGRADE EVERYTHING Embrace the ancient greed of the dwarves and upgrade everything. Improve your workstations, your machinery, and the forge itself. Place more workstations, hire more goblins, decorate your shop the way you like it, and expand your home in the village. Play Medieval Crafter: Blacksmith and become the finest dwarven blacksmith the realm has ever seen. The forge awaits you!
