No goals, no management, just vibes. In Ourlands , it's up to you to build cozy and unique little islands free from objectives or restrictions. With dozens of items to discover, your islands can become whatever you need them to be! From chill beachside campfire cookouts to lonely rocky hilltops, items will help you fully explore your creativity. The more  you build and play, the more items you'll find to collect! Who knows what you'll stumble upon while poking around the environment. Whether it's variations on the things you've fallen in love with, or brand new things you wouldn't have even expected, there's tons to be surprised and delighted by. You've heard of photo mode. Well, Ourlands has a postcard mode . Use the postcard mode to capture your favorite creations, decorate them to make your own custom postcards, and share them with the world.
