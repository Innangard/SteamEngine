Fast draw your gun in kill-or-be-killed duels. Dyin’ ain’t much of a livin’, boy. This is an original puzzle game inspired by chess. Using chess Knights, you need to move through the board until you reach the squares where you can shoot and kill other pieces. Kill everyone in your way and duel against the Wanted Ones to become a Legendary gunslinger. Featuring: Story Mode : Hunt and kill the deadliest gunslingers in the Old West. Each narrative features 15 hand-crafted levels with increasing complexity - and a kill-or-be-killed duel in the end! Duel Mode Face 100+ hand-crafted Duel Puzzles and prove yourself to be a legendary gunslinger. Original soundtrack inspired by classical Western movies. All pieces move like in a chess game, but the objective is to use your Knights to kill the other pieces that are in your way.
