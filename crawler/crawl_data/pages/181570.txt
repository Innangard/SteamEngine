View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Dearbnb is a cozy management game where you build a real estate empire one room at a time. Start your journey by renting a small apartment in a modest neighborhood. Carefully decorate rooms, place furniture freely, set prices, and welcome tenants and customers. As you gain experience and reputation, unlock new zones — from poor districts to luxury neighborhoods and expand into residences, shops, and massive commercial properties. Every room matters. Every decision shapes your path to becoming a true property tycoon. Buy, Decorate & Rent Purchase apartments, houses, residences, and commercial properties. Place furniture freely in dedicated room scenes.No construction stress, just cozy design. Interior Customization & Cozy Design Customize every room exactly the way you want. Change wall colors , floor materials , and freely place furniture and decorative items to shape the atmosphere of each space. Every design choice affects comfort, property value, and customer satisfaction. Create warm, minimal, luxury, or playful interiors your style defines your success. Room-Based Gameplay Click on a room to enter a focused decorating scene. When your setup is complete, hit “Ready” and watch tenants and customers arrive. Zone Progression Start in lower income districts and work your way up to elite neighborhoods. Higher zones require experience, skills, and special badges to unlock. Skill Tree & Badge System Unlock new regions, property types, and business opportunities through progression and achievements. Beyond Apartments Manage cafes, shops, theme park, supermarket, cinema, aquapark and more. Expand businesses, increase capacity, and optimize income streams. Lively Characters Watch tenants shower, relax, work, and interact with your spaces sometimes in surprising and funny ways. Cozy Yet Strategic No pressure, no combat. A relaxing atmosphere combined with meaningful management decisions.
