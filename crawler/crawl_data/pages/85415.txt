"one of my favourite short games of the year" - Gay Time's Best And Queerest Video Games of 2023 Top 5 Game in the Queer Games Bundle 2023 - Gamecity Hamburg "hilarious" - jimjimmyjimjim A new life awaits you in The Golden Palace, the world's most exclusive apartment building. But standing in the way of social success is Oscar Wildecard, the witty but cruel socialite. To progress to the top of the building and victory you must co-host a series of dinner parties. Battle by inviting the best guests to your side of the table, each represented by a card. Guests have strengths and weaknesses depending on who they are and with whom they sit! Defend, attack, or throw the rules of the game out the window with special Wildecards. The choices are yours. Just remember, always forgive your enemies, nothing will annoy them more. Story mode with 15 levels and an estimated 90 minutes of play. Conquest mode -- play a series of challenge quests with special rules. Hilarious and handsome images by comic-book artist Joe Phillips. No in-app purchases. All cards are unlocked as you play.
