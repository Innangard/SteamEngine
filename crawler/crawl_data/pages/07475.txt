Starting in the Hidden Landing of the Cathayan Hinterlands, Dechala leads The Tormentors with a cruelly decadent and aggressively mobile playstyle. For centuries, Dechala has waged an unending war of pain and pleasure. More Daemonic serpent than human, her sinuous body rises to a six-armed torso, each hand wielding a sword in a blur of killing strokes. Combining beauty and horror, she embodies Slaanesh’s essence, inspiring her followers to enforce her will with zeal and cruelty. Unique mechanic: Pillars of Cruelty Captured settlements convert into: •	Thrall Camp: generates Thralls and resources, though productivity wanes as they deplete. •	Tormentor’s Hold: enables recruitment, infrastructure, and defence. •	Pleasure Palace: Major Settlement option, requiring Thralls but granting advanced recruitment and economy buildings. Unique mechanic: Decadence Dechala’s actions raise Decadence, expanding Pleasure Palaces and unlocking powers. Benefits: Slaanesh boons (temporary buffs), Corruption of enemy units before battle, and Daemonic Attraction for instantly recruitable Daemons. She may also sacrifice Thralls for In Praise of Slaanesh, an area-damage ability summoning an enhanced Spawn of Slaanesh.
