He comes home to Hidden Pond for a quiet day on the dock — and finds the start of something much bigger. Relics. Waters all over the world. The old fish stories from childhood start to feel closer than they used to. This is a cozy, readable fishing adventure: warm scenery, great water, and fights that feel like the fish, not a health bar. Strange pieces waiting on distant shores. When Halley finds one, the medallion reacts. The rest you learn by fishing.
