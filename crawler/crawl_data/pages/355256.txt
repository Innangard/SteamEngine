Step into the shoes of a MACV-SOG specialist during the Second Indochina War in a turn-based, tactical, old-school 2D roguelite! Current Demo Version Includes: 18 actions 13 story chapters and lore notes 9 terrain types 8 perks 8 items 3 enemy types Every mission is a brutal new challenge! Each raid demands adaptability and cunning, as you’ll need to develop your character from scratch every time, building an invincible tactic. Use every tool at your disposal to complete your objectives: blend in with the surroundings, establish contacts with locals, explore the dangerous world while hiding in the shadows of the jungle, or wreak havoc, crushing enemies along your path with every available means! Remember, the ultimate goal is to survive and make it back to base, because death wipes out all progress made during the raid! Crush enemies in the “signature style” of the U.S. Army in Vietnam Make full use of all the resources the U.S. military has to offer! Air cavalry support, artillery and blazing napalm will not let you or the damn Charlies get bored! Your arsenal includes: a standard loadout of a military specialist, as well as napalm, of course, artillery, C4, combat grenades, tripwires, various types of weapons, and more! Explore the hostile jungle The Vietnamese jungle is filled with dangers and mysteries. A helicopter will drop you into a procedurally generated map covered in dense foliage, where each step could be your last. Carefully survey every section of the terrain—you never know what's ahead: a treacherous trap, an enemy lying in ambush, or a wild animal ready to attack. Missions will require reconnaissance and exploration, so be prepared for a thorough examination of this unpredictable world. Prepare your soldier and collect trophies Carefully review your mission objectives, weigh your options, and consider: should you bring more rations to avoid starving, or a charge of C4 for devastating sabotage? Skillfully allocate ability points and choose perks that will help you achieve your operation’s goals most effectively. This will allow you to experiment with different playstyles for each sortie. Sneak through the night, sabotaging enemy communications, or charge into open combat, mowing down everything in your path. It’s up to you to choose the path to become a legendary special forces veteran! Come back alive, the jungle doesn’t forgive mistakes! If you successfully complete the operation and evacuate alive, you’ll earn well-deserved rewards: medals and new perks that open up more strategies for future missions. Rest at a cozy fire base, chat with your fellow soldiers, and head back into the cursed jungle, because this war will never end otherwise! Come back alive, son, no matter what...
