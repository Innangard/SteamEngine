View Community Hub About This Game Bring the glass to the customer! As two construction workers, you are transporting a glass window for a tall structure. On this journey you have to be careful with the glass because it is very fragile. Can you make it to the top without breaking it? Invite a trusted friend to your lobby to carry the glass with you! Three courses, three difficulties Climb the Skyscraper, reach the top of the Lighthouse or brave the frozen Frosty course. Normal gives you plenty of checkpoints, Hard gives you fewer and Impossible gives you none at all. Skyscraper Cross the city streets and the harbor pier, then climb the skyscraper floor by floor past swinging hammers, rolling red balls, spinning floors and cannons that fire cars, all the way to the top high above the sea. Lighthouse Run from the bombs of a pirate ship along the coast, cross a tropical island on rafts, rails and spinning platforms and beat the giant Crab Boss on your way to the top of the lighthouse. NEW: Frosty, a snowy third course Collect three golden keys to open the arctic base, hop across sinking lily pads, ride the ski lift, survive the 9 Color game and melt the giant Snowball Boss before you fit the glass into the observatory window. NEW: 2v2 Glass Race Two pairs, one course, one winner. Race another team to the finish while the rival pair runs beside you as a ghost you pass straight through.
