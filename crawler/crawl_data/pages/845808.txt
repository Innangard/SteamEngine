The main hub for Lufthansa including Lufthansa Cargo. Features two passenger terminals with a capacity of approximately 65 million passengers per year, four runways and extensive logistics and maintenance facilities. Terminal 1 has a total of 103 gates, which include 54 gates equipped with jetways (25 in Concourse A, 18 in Concourse B, 11 in Concourse C). Concourse Z sits on top of Concourse A sharing the same jet bridges between both concourses. Flights to non-Schengen destinations depart from the Z gates and Schengen flights depart from the A gates. Frankfurt Airport has four runways of which three are arranged parallel in an east-west direction and one in north-south direction. FRA is the busiest airport by passenger traffic in Germany as well as the 4th busiest in Europe after London Heathrow Airport (also available as add-on for Tower! Simulator 3), Paris–Charles de Gaulle Airport and Amsterdam Airport Schiphol. The airport is also the 13th busiest worldwide by a total number of passengers in 2016, serving more than 300 destinations in 5 continents, making it the airport with the most direct routes in the world.
