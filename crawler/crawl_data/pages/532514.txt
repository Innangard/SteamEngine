View Community Hub About This Game Too Many Toys! is a cozy organizing simulator where you'll sort 5,517 toys in a messy toy shop. Put every toy back in its correct place, complete rows to gain points, and spend them on upgrades that make organizing faster and more satisfying! Who Made This Mess?! The toy store is a complete mess! Thousands of toys have been left scattered all over the place. Your job is simple: Pick up every toy, find where it belongs, and shelf it. Organize over 5,517 toys , unlock helpful abilities, and enjoy the satisfying journey from complete chaos to a perfectly tidy store There are no time limits, no customers to rush you, and no wrong way to play, just the relaxing satisfaction of putting everything back where it belongs Every Toy Has Its Place From plushies and action figures to toy cars, guns, board games, books and building blocks. Every toy belongs in a specific section, on the correct shelf. Learn where each belongs, carry an armfuls of toys around the shop, and watch empty shelves fill up as the store transforms before your eyes. Unlock  Abilities As you organize more of the shop, you'll unlock upgrades that make every trip more efficient. Expand your carrying capacity, move around the shop faster, recruit helpful robot assistants, and unlock new ways to organize even the biggest piles of toys. Features Organize over 5,517 toys Sort every toy into its correct shelf or display Unlock abilities that improve your organizing efficiency Recruit robot helpers to assist with the workload Ride the toy train to travel around the shop Enjoy a relaxing, cozy organizing experience Watch the entire toy shop transform from chaos into perfect order No time limits or pressure, you organize at your own pace Oh and you can pet a cat!
