Assemble your squad. Work out the perfect plan. Watch it play out in real time. Study the 3D map carefully and use the innovative timeline system to move and synchronize your team. Coordinate your movements, grenades and room entries to take the enemy by surprise. One wrong move, one corner left unchecked, could be fatal to your mission - there's no plan B! Once you're confident your plan is perfect, the time has come to execute your actions and press that play button. Watch your orders unfold, and get right into the middle of the action with the full-control camera. Planned a perfect mission? Share your replays and show off those tactical skills! Hero or villain? Choose from the 4 available factions and complete different types of missions. Rescue hostages or plan a bank robbery, we're not judging. Equip the appropriate gear and fine-tune it to match your playstyle and adapt to the situation at hand. Customize your operators to elevate the tacti-coolness of your cinematic replays. Challenge yourself with multi-path rogue-like campaign: Select carefully on the map which missions you'll take and which rewards you'll need. Assemble your tactical squad, develop their individual skills, and unlock new weapons, shields and grenades as you progress through the campaign. In a rush? Take on the skirmish missions or tackle the latest daily, weekly or monthly challenge. Already completed everything? Generate new missions or create your own using the built-in editor and share it with others! Plan : Use the innovative timeline system to synchronize your every move and action and meticulously design the perfect plan. Execute : Capture and share epic replays showcasing your best tactics using the full-control 3D camera and cinematic tools. Campaign or Skirmish : Take on brutal rogue-like campaigns with units and gear management, or short but intense stand-alone missions. Infinite Replayability : Generate an unlimited number of missions, create your own using the built-in editor, or tackle challenges crafted by the community. Embrace Your Playstyle : Pick your side of the story, fine-tune the difficulty settings, and use various perks to shape your experience. Extensive Customization : Personalize your characters and weapons in detail to reflect your personal style and give your team a unique look.
