Playing as a brave little mouse, you’ll journey through a vast world shaped by the beauty and wonder of Swedish nature, helping those you meet and restoring harmony one act of kindness at a time. Each small act brings you closer to restoring its harmony and helping the witch who has long cared for you and all the forest’s inhabitants.
