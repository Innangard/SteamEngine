Minimalist Interface with everything you need Escape urban life, and join a couple on their travel through the most poetic places of the north lands. Focused on the beauty of the levels, an inspiring original soundtrack, and a short story that involves the player, Kombi Travels - Jigsaw Landscapes, takes you on a relaxing and surprising experience, with: Gorgeous scenes of Jigsaw Puzzles. A beautiful Story about love, life, and travel. A Unique New mechanic that takes you into the scene after each puzzle. For Jigsaw Puzzle lovers, this game has the best of its genre: Complete 6 Jigsaw Puzzle scenes at 5 levels of dificulty. Enjoy a minimalist interface that helps you focus on the puzzle. Use great gameplay functions that make the experience even better. Relax with an original Soundtrack for each level! For any players who enjoy a beautiful experience, the game is way more immersive than other games of its genre! With each puzzle assembled, the player is taken to a live scene in 2.5D, where it is possible to discover more about the story behind the game, in addition to finding more achievements from Steam. AND also, 2 Bonus levels, that blend Jigsaw Puzzle with the 2.5D scene! At the end of the game, you’ll have accompanied a lovely travel story by a couple that ran away from urban life.
