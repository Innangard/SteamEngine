Tales from Space: Mutant Blobs Attack is a sci-fi puzzle-platformer of cataclysmic proportions! You’re an ill-tempered blob that grows in size by swallowing everything in its path. Evolve from a tiny captive into a world-eating giant. Gain freakish abilities like magnetism, telekinesis, and rocket boosting. Use your mutant blob powers to devour the pathetic humans and all of civilization! Key Features: 24 levels featuring a wide range of retro 50s-inspired environments. 5 top-down bonus levels. Mind-boggling growth! Absorb everything you touch and grow to massive proportions. Jump, slam, roll, and rocket through the air! Push and pull your weight against metal! Move objects with telekinesis! Leaderboards and achievements. Can you become the golden blob!? Pathetic humans beware!
