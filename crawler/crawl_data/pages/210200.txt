Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “我们的游戏融合了4X策略与RPG角色养成的独特玩法，这种结合在设计上需要平衡诸多难点。过去一年里，我们通过DEMO版本收集了大量玩家反馈，这些反馈不仅帮助我们解决了游戏设计中的许多难题，还极大地提升了游戏的玩法体验。因为我们期待能继续收到更多玩家的体验反馈，以便我们的游戏能更快地达到预设的设计目标。” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “大概12个月左右，可能会因为一些反馈带来的修改，会变更实际的持续时间。” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “目前的EA（Early Access，即抢先体验）版本已经能够让玩家领导一个宗门，体验从“引气”境界至“成仙飞升”的基本游玩流程。 我们计划在EA期间，增添更多流派玩法，丰富地图资源类型，强化水域相关玩法，提升AI智能，加入更多美术资源，以及增加更多事件内容等。同时，我们也将积极采纳玩家反馈，持续优化游戏平衡性并修复存在的bug。” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “目前的EA版本我们期望的设计的系统功能基本都已经完成。包含随机地图、实时战斗、4x的基本玩法、门派经营、人物养成、随机事件、炼制系统、外交系统和城市玩法等等。 在内容上，我们已经包含了常规流派的内容，大量的随机事件也已经实现。” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “抢先体验期间价格不会有变化。随着我们内容和玩法的增加，游戏完整版正式上线后价格会提高。” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “我们团队认为和玩家进行交流非常重要，特别是这种题材独特的游戏，这个也是我们游戏一直保持进步的关键。 我们目前的主要的反馈渠道有如下：1，QQ频道，建议和bug都能收到。 2，steam讨论区，建议和bug都能收到。3，QQ群，主要以聊天对话为主，能更快收到消息。我们热切期望收到来自社区的多样化建议、BUG反馈及玩家心声，我们会根据玩家的反馈尽快做出合理的调整，坚信在这样的良性互动中，我们游戏将会一直不断的进步。” Read more English language not supported This product does not have support for your local language. It's a strange new world out there. Travel far and wide, growing your sect into unfounded territories. Stop by cities you've never heard of. Explore mysterious and wondrous lands known to none. Face almighty beasts in battle. Anything is possible here, and each choice you make shifts the threads of your fate. Your sect escaped utter destruction, if barely. It's time to build it back again. Establish a lineage, learn the way of heaven and earth, discover and craft incredible artifacts. Enlist the aid of outstanding cultivators, appoint the greatest among them to lofty positions and guide them—or leave them to their own devices. Danger lurks around every corner, yet with it comes opportunity. Myriad chances and challenges shall arrive as you expand into new lands. You can strive toward immortality, dedicating yourself to meditation and study, or cultivate with a band of friends, and seek to ascend together. Will you walk the path of righteousness, tyranny, or evil? Will you follow the way of heaven, or rebel against it? Only you can decide. Your sect shall grow strong once more. Yet as it does, peril looms, stirring restlessly in the shadows... Other sects, bizarre beasts, evil factions, and even demons have their beady eyes on your resources. It's time to make your choice. Will you practice diplomacy, or let your blade do the talking? Whichever path you take, you must rekindle the glory of your sect.
