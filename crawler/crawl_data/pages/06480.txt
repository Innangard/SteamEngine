In this award-winning, 1-8 player mining sandbox adventure, your choices shape an epic journey. Level up your skills, defeat legendary Titans, and unveil the power of the Core. Upgrade your arsenal and equipment with advanced tools like the mighty Obliteration Ray, and automated machinery to streamline mining, smelting, storage, and more. Build a boat to set sail across the Sunken Sea, race across the Desert of Beginnings, and encounter the remnants of ancient civilizations. BUILD, CRAFT & GET CREATIVE Create a totally unique base using a variety of different materials and customisations. From the ultimate adventurer’s lair to a cozy underground cottage, shape the underground your way. Discover rare and powerful loot to help you on your journey and power up the Core. EXPLORE WITH FRIENDS Enjoy the adventure solo or team up in 2-8 player drop-in/drop-out online co-op.
