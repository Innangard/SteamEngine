Communityhub weergeven Recensies “A lot of fun and surprisingly tricky.” PC Gamer Over dit spel Legion Was Here is a dark-comedy spy deduction game where you study evidence, change your face, and steal identities to complete bizarre missions for all kinds of unusual clients — without getting caught. You are Legion , a secret agent who can modify his appearance at will . Agencies from all around the world call on your chameleon skills to carry out dangerous missions . To approach your targets, you must succeed in passing yourself off as one of their loved ones: become a husband, a brother, a son… The possibilities are endless . But be careful: the slightest misstep can mean… Death . ● Change your look Use the operating room to customize Legion's appearance. Age, hairstyle, nose, eye color… Everything can be changed . Find the right look for your mission, among more than 900 million combinations ! ● Investigate A file detailing your new identity will be provided to you for each mission. By cross-referencing its documents, you will have to find the appropriate appearance. Be thorough, as each document is important … Yes, even this chocolate bar wrapper. ● Go on your mission If you have the correct look, you will be able to approach your target . But be careful, the mission doesn't end there: you will also have to act in accordance with your new identity to gain his trust. This phase uses visual novel mechanics, with multiple choice questions that will test your acting skills…. The file you have studied will help you in your task... As well as the notes you have taken care to write on your left hand! ☞ Legion Was Here is also 3-4 hours of play A wide variety of missions Apart from the usual spy agencies (FBI, MI6...), you will sometimes have to deal with more unusual clients - ghosts from the past or amusement park mascots, everyone can hire Legion! The perfect agent You will always be able to redo missions to improve your score. As long as you don't reach 100%, you weren't totally credible... But stay vigilant, as the missions won't unfold the same way every time ! About the developer Game made entirely solo (except for the saxophone , played by my friend Pierre) and without recourse to AI. Warning : Some sequences in this game contain flashing lights and visual effects that may trigger seizures in sensitive individuals.
