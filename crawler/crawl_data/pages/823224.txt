Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Gun a Rat is a fast-paced asymmetrical multiplayer game to play with your friends. Play as the Rats and wreck the house... or as the Exterminator , and wipe them out down to the last one. Start matches from 2 to 20 players on a variety of maps, each with its own share of surprises, with 3 game modes to choose from… A very special voice chat... Rats can talk to each other through voice chat without fear of being understood. The Exterminator only hears squeaks instead of their words! Your goal as a Rat: Reach the target score by destroying and stealing objects before time runs out, without getting killed! But be careful... every destruction makes a noise that will give you away! If the Exterminator is hunting you down, quickly hide in the furniture to escape their gaze. As the Exterminator: Use your camera to track the rats' movements and find out where they're hiding... Place your traps carefully to slow them down as much as possible Kill them all! A wide range of traps is at your disposal, each one holding its own nasty surprises for the Rats ... 3 game modes to keep things fresh: Classic : smash and steal as many objects as you can to reach the target score before time runs out! Hide and Seek : earn points by looking at the Exterminator ... but stay well hidden so you don't get spotted! Bomb Tag : an explosive game of tag! Pass the bomb to another player before it blows up in your snout! Play match after match to earn Cheese Coins , and spend them on skins to customize your rat! So, which side will you be on?
