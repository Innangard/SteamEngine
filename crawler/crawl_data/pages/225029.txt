Journey through a post-apocalyptic world, solving puzzles with a staggering number of mechanics and object interactions! Welcome to Union Freight Operations What starts as a simple matter of pushing boxes around a warehouse quickly escalates into a perilous puzzle adventure through a world of giant insects and rogue robots. Survive deadly roach-filled grottos, bubbling lakes of caustic goop, and the twisted industrial ruins of the eponymous Depot-13. Join survivors Stella, Todd, and other memorable characters in this comedic retro-futuristic brain-expanding quest through the most puzzling of Depots! Are you prepared for an exciting career in the field of cargo management? Features A classic pixel-art aesthetic and bumpin' soundtrack brings the world of Puzzle Depot to life! Each sprawling level is jam-packed with puzzling rooms, optional goals, and secrets to find. Exploit dangerous fauna to help solve puzzles. Collect useful tools to improve your puzzle solving capabilities. Undo moves, restart rooms, replay levels, and save your game anywhere you like. Convenient! Play as several characters with unique abilities for tackling the Depot's many challenges. Discover deep lore and bad jokes through various notes, datalogs, and dialogue. Create and share your own nefarious puzzles with a fully-featured level editor! 100% created by real-life human beings!
