Welcome to Spice & Grill, your very own cozy idle restaurant that quietly lives at the bottom of your screen. While you work, study, or relax, your restaurant keeps serving happy customers, changing with the seasons, and growing into a cozy little world of its own. Enjoy the following features in the demo version of this game! A level cap of 30. Building your perfect dining space: Build, and arrange hundreds of decorations and choose their color variants to match your vibe. Great for multitaskers: Spice & Grill sits at the bottom of your screen , allowing you to work, play other games, or watch videos while your restaurant serves customers. Staff with personality: Customize every staff member with charming outfits and give them proper cooking skills! Seasons, weather & festivals: Rainy days, sunny afternoons, or festive holidays each bring new bonuses and challenges.
