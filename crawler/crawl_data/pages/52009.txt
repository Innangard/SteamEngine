"Illusion Box" is the first virtual reality entertainment project of the 21st century. It gained unparalleled popularity during the millennium by captivating users with its creative and novel experiences. However, malicious rumors and computer virus infections forced the company to shut down the project. Now, the long-abandoned "Illusion Box" devices are being reactivated... In this game, you will manipulate objects and furniture using your mouse to solve puzzles across various levels. This unique gameplay mechanic, which involves altering the physical environment to find a way out, offers you a fresh and exciting experience. This game creates a mysterious and captivating atmosphere by combining a millennium-inspired retro style with its scene design. Detailed pixel-style art and unique digital glitch effects will evoke a sense of nostalgia. Through its rich scenes, multimedia elements and screen narratives, this game reveals the protagonist's inner struggles and bizarre encounters inside the "Illusion Box". You need to piece these fragmented pictures together to uncover a story where desire and fear intertwine.
