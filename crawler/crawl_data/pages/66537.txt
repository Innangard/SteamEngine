View Community Hub About This Game ZJ the Ball is a Christian-themed, story driven, side scrolling platform video game in the ZJ the Ball franchise by Breakthrough Gaming! You play as ZJ the Ball, who can move left and right, along with jumping and bouncing. Coins are scattered throught the level, and if you collect them all, it will improve your score! At the end of each level, ZJ the Ball meets a robot named Axel, who challenges ZJ the Ball to play a classic styled arcade game. If you make a mistake in the arcade game, you lose coins and your score decreases! Try to get a perfect score in all the levels! Story: ZJ the Ball realizes how much good work his mom does at their neighborhood church, so he decides to get a thank you gift for her. As he travels through the city looking for one, he meet's a robot named Axel! What does he want?
