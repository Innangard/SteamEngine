Break the furniture, crash the truck or somehow keep everything under control. Let's see how your first deliveries turn out. MOVE TOGETHER Some items can't be moved alone. Stretch out your arms, sync up with your teammate and lift together. Watch out for tight corridors, slippery stairs and, of course, your troll friends. TAKE THE WHEEL Enjoy the view on your way to the delivery, but stay alert. The road can quickly become the most dangerous part of the job. If your friend is behind the wheel, there's only one question: Do you really trust your driver? YOUR FIRST DELIVERIES Every delivery can bring a different challenge. Carry items through tight spaces, overcome obstacles and try to get everything to its destination in one piece. Even the simplest moving job can turn into a complete disaster in seconds. image Experience the chaotic co-op world of Carry On Together with your friends in the demo!
