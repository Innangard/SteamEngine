View Community Hub About This Game Desktop Fireworks Festival is a management sim that lives at the bottom of your screen and stays out of your way while you work , launching the fireworks you designed into the sky above your desktop. Grow your festival into a grand venue and light up the sky with a show of your own! Manage Your Fireworks Venue Set up Stalls around the Venue and earn money from your Visitors. Use your earnings to add new facilities, collect Firework Shells, and put on Fireworks Shows. Create Your Fireworks Show Use the money you earn from managing the Venue to host Fireworks Shows. Edit the show timeline however you like, choosing which fireworks to launch and exactly when to launch them. Use the Firework Shells you've collected to create your best show and delight your Visitors. Grow Into Bigger Venues As your Fireworks Shows become more popular, your Fame will grow, allowing you to move on to larger Venues. Bigger Venues give you more room for facilities, attract more Visitors, and make it easier to collect rare Firework Shells. Put on even more spectacular shows and work your way up to the grandest Fireworks Venue. Over a Million Fireworks Once you’ve collected all the UR fireworks—congratulations! You’ll be able to develop your very own unique fireworks! Combine size, core, shape, petals, color, rise effect, and after-effect, and you get more than a million fireworks! Use the money you earn to develop new Firework Shells. Every shell can be previewed with its real effect, so you can see exactly how it will bloom in the night sky before you launch it.
