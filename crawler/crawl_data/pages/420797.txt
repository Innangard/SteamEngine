Everyone’s favourite unlikely heroes are back, and this time they’re caught in a conflict between a coven of Vampires and a band of Necromancers. Grab your sword, pick a side, and do your best to survive the night your brains un-eaten. Features: Step back into the hilarious fantasy parody world, based on the iconic audio series by French author John Lang. A vast haunted environment to explore. A spooky main questline with two factions and alternate routes: choose your side, between the Necromancers and the Vampires! Additional sidequests, including a ‘Frankentroll’ storyline where the heroes must hunt down different troll body parts to combine into a sublime ogrish being. New battle mechanics to diversify your experience Eleven brand new boss fights and an expanded cast. Chickens! DLC 2: Splat Jaypak's Arenas The adventurers are back for more in this new DLC!
