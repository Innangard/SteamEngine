View Community Hub About This Game Veinrider is an incremental game where you upgrade a gravity-flipping mining craft and navigate through the caves of an alien world. Each run yields more resources you can invest into permanent upgrades, turning your fragile rig into an unstoppable mining juggernaut. ♦ Upgrade your ship : mine resources, trade them in, and steadily improve your ship’s shields, weapons, and systems ♦ Satisfying controls : simple gravity-flip controls inspired by classics—easy to pick up, deep to master ♦ Procedural Terrain : every time you play, the alien caverns are different Dig, upgrade, and push deeper—until the planet gives up all it has to offer.
