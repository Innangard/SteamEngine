Use your cards to unleash powerful spells. Wipe out enemy waves and keep the town safe through the night. When daylight returns, you can strengthen your build - buy new cards and artifacts at the shop, upgrade your favourites at the blacksmith, and encounter a variety of events around town - all to prepare for the next onslaught. The longer you last, the stronger your town becomes. Upgraded buildings mean more power, and a higher chance at survival.
