View Community Hub About This Game ShapeNeon Chaos is an old school style space-shooter. You are a ship facing off waves of deadly enemies. This game was made to give you a shot of adrenaline in a couple of minutes. Lots of bullets and neon in an arcade psychedelic shooter! - Based on old-school arcades - Face multiple enemies in a space battle with increasing difficulty - Fight for the highest score - Manage your remaining lives to go further - Use your bombs wisely - Survive the mind-blowing Chaos! - Shots and effects with Neon and insane Lights! - Achievements - Steam Leaderboards - Gamepad and Big Picture support - 4 game modes: "Standard", "Chaos", "Time Attack" and "Slow Motion": ------------- STANDARD MODE --------------------- This is the standard game mode. Start with 3 lifes and 3 bombs. Try to beat the High Score ------------- CHAOS MODE --------------------- This is the "Chaos" mode. Lots of enemies in a crazy fight for survive ------------- TIME ATTACK --------------------- Start with just 1 life and 1 bomb. Try to survive as long as you can ------------- SLOW MOTION --------------------- It's like standard mode, but you don't have bombs, you have Slow Motion ! --- WARNING ---- This game contains fast flashing images It may cause discomfort and trigger seizures for people with photosensitive epilepsy System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 (SP1+) Processor: Dual Core + Memory: 2 GB RAM DirectX: Version 10 Storage: 170 MB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
