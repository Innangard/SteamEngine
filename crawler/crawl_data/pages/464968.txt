View Community Hub About This Game The heroic icon of English ballads clashes with the forces of the nefarious Sheriff of Nottingham in Robin Hood: Sherwood Defenders ! Engage in raid defenses as you fend off wave after wave of incoming enemies both by placing structures and having Robin himself leap into the thick of the fight ! The Defense Set up a variety of defenses to prepare in advance of the enemy fighters' arrival. Towers, walls, traps, and gates all provide nasty surprises for those who would seek to fuel the Sheriff's greed. Hire additional soldiers to man the stronghold you create and help defeat the invaders before they take out its central structure! The Economy However, you'll need resources of your own if you're to withstand the growing onslaught, so don't forget to place production buildings alongside fortifications and then balance them to prevail. With the right resource income , you'll be able to afford upgrades to your current buildings and make them more effective in their purpose. The Fight Robin Hood takes charge of defending the homes of his most loyal allies in the fight against the Sheriff but he can also put his legendary archery and swordsmanship skills to use in the fights. As a wave of hostile soldiers appears, stand side-by-side with the other brave defenders! The different unit types among your allies and enemies have their strengths and weaknesses, so make the right choices! The Rewards As you succeed in battles, you make yourself ready to move on to a new area and unlock rewards in the form of new buildings, new defenses, new warriors, and even new arrows for Robin to use in the fight!
