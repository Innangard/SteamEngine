View Community Hub About This Game 🪨 Bounce. Break. Mine. Pickball is what happens when the satisfying clack of pinball collides with the addictive growth of an incremental game. You start with a single humble pickaxe — and before you know it, one launch is sweeping an entire screen of ore into your pocket. ⛏️ Core Gameplay Launch Your Pickaxe — From the primitive tool that began civilization, to dragon-clawed picks, to mystical instruments imbued with the power of the moon. Collect and upgrade pickaxes, each with their own quirks and special effects. Smash the Ore — Stones, copper, iron, titanium, tungsten, gold, sapphire, ruby, emerald, diamond. The higher the tier, the flashier the shatter — and the fatter the payout. Dig Deeper — From dusty scrap-iron shafts to the golden Tutankhamun tomb and ancient gold-vein fields, every stage hides new ores and richer rewards. 💎 Numbers That Go Brrr At first, even a tiny pebble feels precious. Soon, chain mining, pickaxe explosions, drop multipliers, treasure drops, and coin showers paint the entire screen gold. Slot in attachments for lightning, tremors, lasers, even ghostly phasing — and turn a single swing into a mineshaft-wide earthquake. 🗿 Crack the Boss Rocks At the end of every stage looms a massive Boss Rock — thick, stubborn, and built to take a beating. But when it finally cracks open? There’s nothing quite like it. 🏆 Built to Collect Gem Codex — Fill out your collection as you unearth every mineral. Mastery System — Permanent progression that makes every future swing stronger. Hidden Treasures — Rare prizes buried somewhere in the rubble. You’ll have to dig to find them. 🎯 You’ll Love This If… You love the satisfying thock of a good pinball bounce. Watching numbers get bigger genuinely makes you happy. “Just one more run” has been known to last until 3 AM. You want a game packed with screen-shaking effects and crunchy sound to melt the day’s stress away. Grab your pick. The mine is deep, and the ore is infinite.
