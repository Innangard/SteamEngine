View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to the world of trading used (or stolen) cars! Got little money and nothing to lose? We were waiting for you! In Used Cars Simulator , you’ll fix up cars, put them up for sale, grow your business in an old shed, drive around an abandoned city, drink beer, and wander the desert with a metal detector. And it all starts when you meet a beautiful woman in a red dress at your favorite bar… Are you in? FIND A GOOD OFFER In Used Cars Simulator , you'll learn how to make money by trading cars. First, find a good deal. Check the car’s condition, negotiate the price, and if everything looks good - just buy it. FIX THE CAR Fix the dents, order and install missing parts, wash the car, repaint it, and put it up for sale. If it wasn’t yours, you’d probably want to buy it yourself! BE BAD In Used Cars Simulator, there are many ways to get cars. Are they all legal? Sure - as long as the cops don’t catch you. HAVE FUN After work, have some fun - drift, jump, and compare your scores with other players on the leaderboards. PROGRESS What’s coming in future updates? You tell us! Let us know which features we should add.
