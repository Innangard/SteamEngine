The soundtrack is an eclectic mix of guitar and piano-led instrumentation, with roots and inspiration in ambient and jazz, and some classical elements. The soundtrack begins with establishing guitar notes that swells to a full band ensemble, before settling down to its ambient state. A handful of the tracks from the soundtrack are real-time arrangements from each set of themes as progress unfolds. Each dynamic palette is inspired by the preceding theme as you get to certain points in the game.
