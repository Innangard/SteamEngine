About This Game Lead your clan of dwarves and guide them to victory! Invade cities to restore honor to Valhalla! In Raiders of Valhalla , you take control of your clan of dwarves and lead them into battle. Start with a group of Viking dwarves and initiate wars against vulnerable villages. As your troops plunder these villages, use the loot to strengthen them and advance against locations guarded by formidable enemy warriors! Explore Diverse Dwarf Classes A variety of weapons and equipment await your acquisition to enhance your army. Reinforce your fighters and lead them into war! Various classes of dwarves, including Berserker, Drengr, Valkyrie, Seer, Skald, and Thurl , allow you to expand your group of Vikings. Hire warriors for battle using resources obtained by invading various distinct villages. Master Cities and Reclaim Honor Valhalla awaits those who demonstrate courage and strategy. Invade villages to restore lost honor and claim unimaginable riches. However, beware of the formidable village chiefs who protect their treasures with indomitable fury! Success isn't merely about brute force. Develop clever tactics to overcome enemies superior in numbers and power. Recruit and Strengthen Your Viking Army Start with a small group of fearless Viking dwarves and turn them into an unstoppable force! Hire warriors, enhance their skills, and equip them with legendary weapons and powerful armor to face any adversary. Confront Epic Challenges Brave various biomes, such as forests, fiery lands, deserts, and snowy mountains, where the villages are located. So, are you willing to join the war and become the most feared war dwarf? Get ready to command, conquer, and triumph in a world full of adventure, challenge, and honor. Raiders of Valhalla is the ultimate experience for those seeking strategic and thrilling action. The battle for glory begins now. Join the legend in Valhalla!
