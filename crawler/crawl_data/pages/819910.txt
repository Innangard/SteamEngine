Not inside a window — they walk directly across whatever you have open right now. The base app is free. You can have up to two dogs on screen. With two of them out, they notice each other, come over, say hello, and start a game of chase. Enjoy watching them behave like real dogs. Two dogs you get for free The small, adorable Beagle (tricolor) The big, dependable German Shepherd (black) Paid DLC adds more breeds. How they spend the day changes with the hour Dogs are animals that fall in step with their owner's life. Their activity peaks in the morning and the evening, the daytime passes with short rests in between, and late at night they sleep soundly. They won't tear around at midnight the way a cat does. Now and then, though, a dog will break into a run at dawn or dusk for no reason at all. They talk with their tails A dog's feelings show in its tail. Wagging while sitting, wagging while lying down, wagging faster when excited, wagging hardest of all at a reunion. Even the same "tail wag" looks different depending on the moment. Walking on your windows Dogs use your open windows as footholds — walking along them, jumping down, climbing back up. Whatever state your desktop is in becomes their playground. Staying out of your way A desktop pet, however cute, gets removed the moment it gets in the way of your work. Sill Dogs lets you adjust the following in the settings. Reaction to the cursor / Reaction to clicks — each can be set to "Off / Weak / Normal / Strong". The default is "Weak" . Set it to "Off" and the dogs won't come over to your cursor while you're working An option to keep the dogs off the front of a display while you're playing a full-screen game or watching a full-screen video Dog size, which screen they appear on, and how many dogs (one or two) You can move the dogs by dragging them How to change the settings Changing the breed, choosing which screen they appear on, quitting the app — all of it is done from the icon in the notification area (system tray), or from the bone sign. Find the Sill Dogs icon in the Windows notification area and right-click it to open the menu If you can't find the icon, click the "^" button at the bottom right of the screen to see the hidden icons You can also open the settings screen from the "bone sign" on your screen. From the menu you can choose "Breed selection", "Number of dogs", "Screen settings", "Auto-start settings", "Setting" and "Quit Sill Dogs" About the DLC Adding more breeds requires paid DLC. There is a free toy DLC as well. There is currently no feature for showing three or more dogs at once. About the pixel art The dogs' pixel art uses assets drawn by the pixel artist girlypixels.
