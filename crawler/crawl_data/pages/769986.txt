It plays the way a small box board game plays: a deck of room cards, dice for fighting, and a character with four numbers on it. How a run goes The dungeon is fourteen areas across five floors. Each area deals nine cards from a deck of twenty.  When the area is finished, you climb down and see what the next area has in store for you. What carries over Your character does not carry over runs. What changes is the dungeon itself. At the end of each floor you are offered cards to permanently change what you will encounter on the way down next time.
