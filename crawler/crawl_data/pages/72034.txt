View Community Hub Reviews “The relatively simple gameplay is overflowing with finesse and strategy, the presentation is fantastic, and offline or on, Worms is just incredibly fun.” 8/10 – GameSpot “Worms is a special case. The brightly colored, arcadey artillery shooter puts players in control of a team of worms fighting over a desolate wasteland. It’s wacky where other artillery games are grim, hilarious where others are serious and full of customization where so many of the artillery shooters are stripped down and simple.” VICE “WMD is a whole lot of fun. It brings back the Armageddon experience while still feeling like a fresh take for the series.” PC Gamer About This Game Join the fight and command your worm warriors into battle in the iconic 2D arcade artillery shooter where serious strategy and silly weaponry decide who swims or sinks. Wage Widespread Worm Warfare Unleash tactical, turn-based total chaos in gloriously hand-drawn artillery battlegrounds. Arm your invertebrate platoons with a massive arsenal, command heavy machinery, and use the landscape to your advantage. Outsmart your rivals, blow enormous holes in their plans — and preferably the ground beneath them — to blast them off the map and be the last worms standing. Artillery Combat on a Whole New Scale Ready your Holy Hand Grenade, round up the Super Sheep and prepare to drop the Concrete Donkey. Blast your way through 30 campaign levels with over 80 weapons & utilities at your fingertips. Take the high ground, tunnel to safety, set the perfect trap — or forget the plan entirely and reach for the explosives. The More the Squirmier Worms W.M.D comes locked, loaded and stuffed with ways to wage war. Hone your skills in Training Missions, take on the Campaign and tackle Challenges solo. Train up then head online to wreak havoc in multiplayer modes and take on up to five opponents in hilarious all-out tactical combat in local, online or ranked play. Form alliances. Break alliances. Accidentally bazooka yourself into the sea. Whatever happens, make sure your worms are the last ones standing.
