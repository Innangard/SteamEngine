Quantum Arbitrage is a node-based programming and optimization puzzle game about building algorithmic trading systems under extreme latency constraints. Market data arrives from multiple exchanges. Your job is to process it, compare prices, transform values, route signals, and execute the correct action before the opportunity disappears. A solution that works is only the beginning. Every wire adds latency. Every processing node costs time. Every unnecessary step can make your algorithm too slow. Build a working system, measure its critical path, then redesign it until every millisecond counts. DESIGN YOUR ALGORITHM Construct trading logic using a visual node editor. Connect market feeds to comparison, mathematical, routing, and logic nodes to create systems that react automatically to changing conditions. RACE THE MARKET Opportunities exist for only a limited execution window. Your algorithm may be logically correct and still fail because it is too slow. Reduce latency, remove unnecessary nodes, and find a more efficient architecture. LEARN THE SYSTEM Quantum Arbitrage does not require previous programming knowledge. The opening contract teaches the fundamentals of the editor, while an in-game technical manual documents every available component and its behavior. After that, the solutions are yours to discover. OPTIMIZE YOUR SCORE Contracts are evaluated using multiple performance metrics: Execution latency Logic node count Final rank Replay completed contracts and search for cleaner, faster solutions. EXPAND YOUR TOOLSET New components become available as contracts grow more complex. Work with numeric and boolean signals, mathematical transformations, conditional routing, multi-market logic, and direct execution control. FROM SIMPLE CONDITIONS TO FULL SYSTEMS Begin with basic cross-market comparisons. Later contracts require percentage spreads, multi-market decisions, parallel branches, BUY and SELL execution paths, and increasingly strict latency limits. By the final contract, simply finding the correct answer is no longer enough. You have to build the right system.
