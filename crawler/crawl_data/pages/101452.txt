LATE 2 ESCAPE: CHAPTER 1 includes a a twisted compelling story that gets better through each chapter.While playing Chapter 1 you will collect items that belongs to your daughter Haze.You will have to use matches, an Axe and other limited items to survive. Matches are your main sources of light and your only comfort. Use them to ignite candles that reveal hidden symbols, paths, or ward off the oppressive darkness creeping in around you."Matches don’t grow on body bags, use them like your life depends on it". Found buried in the room of your missing daughter Haze, this axe is more than a tool , it’s a turning point. Use it to shatter obstacles, and a variety of enemies. Is it a weapon? A message? Or just the start of something worse? SecondHand is a recurring presence within the house. He appears without warning, always just beyond clear sight.
