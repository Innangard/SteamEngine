Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We are using Early Access to continue developing Panic Delivery with player feedback. While the core gameplay systems are already implemented, we plan to expand the game with additional content, features, and improvements over time. Early Access allows us to test new ideas, refine mechanics, and prioritize updates based on community input… all while our couriers do their best to survive the job.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect to release Panic Delivery in Early Access around Q2 2026, and we plan to keep the game in Early Access for approximately one year, enough time to expand the experience, refine the systems, and make sure every delivery is just a little less life-threatening.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “New Maps - We plan to add more maps with increasingly crazy settings, each featuring multiple procedurally generated dungeons designed to deliver varied environments and unpredictable challenges. New Enemies - Additional map-specific enemies are planned, each with unique behaviors to expand gameplay variety and keep couriers on their toes. Items - We intend to expand the item pool with more defensive and utility-based tools, supporting different playstyles and survival strategies. Missions - We are exploring additional mission types to create constantly evolving challenges and even more terrifying, yet oddly fun, situations. Delivery Options - Our clients are becoming more and more demanding. We’re adding new types of parcels to satisfy their needs… no matter how questionable the request.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At launch, the Early Access version will include the following features: - 3 maps, each with procedurally-generated dungeons - 3 different difficulty modes - 3 mission types (Standard, Recovery, Smuggling) with randomized objectives - 13 unique enemies - 15 items available in the shop - Character customization with a wide selection of skins, emojis, and dances - 3 delivery package types (Small, Medium, Large) - A challenge system that unlocks additional cosmetics” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will likely increase slightly after leaving Early Access, but rest assured, our deliveries will always remain the most affordable!” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We already have a content schedule we plan to roll out during Early Access, but it’s essential for us to understand what players actually want. How exciting! ✨ Welcome to Panic Delivery - the most wonderful job opportunity for hardworking humans looking for meaningful (but mandatory) work. Let’s talk business - you are a COURIER - teaming up with other COURIERS - to deliver, recover and smuggle packages to our host of beautiful monsters in our ✨ stunning, dystopian, monster-run world ✨! First and likely the most important factor for humans - work-life balance! As part of our employer-employee insurance policy, we’ve invested in a state-of-the-art cloning program! Throughout your employment, you get 99 backup clones. With every death, a fresh clone is deployed to continue your hard work. Run out of “lives”? Everything resets - like nothing ever happened. But you keep your purchased swag! Talk about efficiency! 😊 I’m sure you’re worried already - but fear not! With the Speetchy you get from missions, we’re selling you weapons & tools to help defend yourselves, as well as some "boujee swag" in exchange for Bonus Permits. Besides securing your personal safety, we know you humans love Dopamine! Speaking of not dying - don’t worry! There shouldn’t be too much pressure about it. You can always team up with fellow Couriers. This means two fun things: delivering packages and dying together! Oh, and you won’t be stuck in just one lovely workplace! We take pride in offering a wide variety of delivery routes across our ✨ ever-expanding operational zones ✨.  Haunted houses, panic factories and the Antarctic base bring new sights, new challenges, and new ways to perish - oops! I mean, grow. And our unfriendly neighbourhood monsters! So diverse! So unique! So… hungry! Each one has their own personality, preferences, and exciting ways of interacting with Couriers. We highly encourage you to observe them closely - from a safe distance, if possible! We’re always looking for enthusiastic humans who can follow simple instructions, survive impossible situations, and keep smiling through it all! Apply now. Your shift is waiting.
