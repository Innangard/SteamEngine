Building options may include objects to build custom malls, different forms of parking and/or Movie and Restaurant Drive Ins. We also plan to improve existing features based on feedback and add Quality of Life features where needed. More music tracks are in the works, and a scenario set in Paris. If the desire is there, we hope to integrate Steam Workshop, allowing players to share creations. These plans and the direction may change based on Player Feedback.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Three maps/scenarios with different challenges to play. American City, Pyramids of Giza and Florida with boat-cars. You can level up in each scenario and unlock new cars, building options, Drive Thrus and production chains.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We will be engaging the community through Discord and Steam Forums in order to make improvements to the game. Design the slickest parking lots ever seen, then convince citizens they absolutely need them. Spread glossy pro-car propaganda, outsmart those pesky anti-petrol protesters, and transform quiet neighborhoods into your parking empire! It's time to drive up car sales and oil consumption! For profit.. but mostly for freedom! Features Real-time simulation with hundreds of vehicles and (bald) citizens. Buy land, demolish homes, and build the parking lots of your dreams: Endless flat seas of asphalt or build into the air using the 3D construction tools. Read anyone's thoughts : Are they embracing cars yet, or annoyed by your ever-increasing parking fees and the sight of your oil drills? Use pro-car propaganda to turn everyone into a carbrain. Place shiny statues, billboard posters, and blast catchy slogans from megaphones. Keep the people somewhat happy : If traffic is a mess, distract them with a ride on your glorious Ferris Wheel for cars! Control the anti-car protests before they shatter your reputation. Track everything in the Stat-o-matic : A true tycoon loves watching numbers go up. Drill for oil . A full tank is a full heart. Rival public transport : You don't want trains or walkable towns, you want every space flooded with cars! Car Park Capital fuses strategy and simulation with satire, offering social commentary on car dependency and urban development. Become a real tycoon and start paving your automotive paradise!
