Load up IdleOn the next day, spend your AFK gains earned while you were away, and buy MORE upgrades! Create 11 unique Classes, from Divine Knight to Bubonic Conjuror IdleOn lets you create 11 characters, so you'll have one of each class - each one plays a unique role in your account! Free-to-Play note: IdleOn is free to play, and all classes, maps, skills, bosses, and activities are available without purchase.
