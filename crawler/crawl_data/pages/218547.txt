View Community Hub Out Now, Check out the Bundle! 🏹 About This Game A Cosmic Culinary Adventure Begins! You are the one and only witch chef of Stewpendous Spoons , an intergalactic restaurant like no other. Standing before the legendary Cosmic Cauldron , your mission is to serve the most delectable stews to a host of bizarre extraterrestrial patrons. But beware, failure is not an option. If your dishes don’t satisfy... You might just become the next ingredient! Features 🔥 Deck-Building Roguelike Cooking System Each dish is a high-stakes gamble! With 20 different ingredients dropping into the cauldron every run, watch them collide and combine in unexpected ways to rack up points. Every cooking session introduces new ingredients, pushing you to build a strategic deck and create powerful synergies. 🌌 Cosmic Cooking Challenges Brace yourself for an intergalactic kitchen frenzy! The ingredients won’t just sit still. They’ll tumble, crash, and react in chaotic ways. Hit the target score quickly to earn bonus rewards like Cauldron Expansions and powerful Cosmic Relics that enhance your cooking prowess. 🧙‍♀️ A Whimsical Witch’s Tale Your restaurant attracts customers from the farthest corners of the universe, eldritch beings with impossible demands and unrelenting appetites. If you fail to meet their expectations... well, let’s just say the consequences are cosmic-scale catastrophes. ✨ Charming 2D Cosmic Pixel Art Immerse yourself in a hand-drawn pixel-art world that’s both cute and mysterious! Every detail, from animated ingredients to enchanting space backgrounds, brings your cosmic kitchen to life. Gameplay Highlights 🍲 Ingredient Drop & Interaction Ingredients fall randomly into the cauldron, bouncing, merging, and triggering unpredictable reactions. Adapt on the fly, strategize your deck, and make every dish a masterpiece. 🍽️ Strategic Deck-Building Collect ingredients and craft your unique deck. With a vast array of ingredient types and properties, experimenting with different combinations is key to achieving high scores and mastering the art of cosmic cuisine. 🏆 Rewards & Upgrades Meet your goals to unlock cauldron upgrades and rare relics that amplify ingredient interactions. Discover faster, more efficient ways to cook and dominate the interstellar culinary scene! Are you ready to cook up the universe’s most legendary stew? Don't forget to wishlist  our game!
