AI Park gives you a simple place to try AI-generated media without turning everything into a complicated technical project. Try basic Text-to-Image, Image-to-Video, and Text-to-Video workflows, with supported local AI models including lightweight and larger model options. Already creating with AI? Bring your finished work into AI Park, organize it, and publish it for other users to discover. AI Park is designed to be a home for AI images, videos, comics, series, and other visual media — not just another generation tool. Just here to explore? You don't need an AI model or a powerful GPU just to use AI Park. Browse community creations, subscribe to works you like, and keep your own local library. For creators Publish your work and build an audience through the AI Park community. We also plan to add more ways for users to support creators, including creator support and paid content options . Local AI is optional AI Park itself is free. Local AI generation is optional and runs on your own computer using compatible AI runtimes and models. You can still browse, view, organize, and publish media without installing AI models.
