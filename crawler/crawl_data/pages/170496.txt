Firstly, you need to look at what you need for your next construction. Go to the local warehouse, buy it, and load it on the truck. 🚚 GETTING TO WORK Don’t be late or you can miss your ride. Load up, hit the road, and make sure everything gets delivered. On-site, before the job, you must unload all the stuff needed for the construction. 🌍 TRUE OPEN WORLD Unleash your creativity in a sprawling open world teeming with possibilities.
