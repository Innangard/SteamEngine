SmashCore is a short physics based incremental experience. Smash asteroids with your gravity powers, given to you by a mysterious core. Consume the asteroid dust and feed the hungry core in exchange for riches. Use the spoils to upgrade your gravitational powers and venture deeper into space where ancient technology and new ores are waiting to be discovered. A key element of SmashCore is the realistic physics-based asteroid destruction, there are no health bars, collisions literally remove chunks from the asteroids.
