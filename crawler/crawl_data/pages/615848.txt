You can assemble features like an elevator that takes the exploration bus to the surface, a complete gym for the residents, a large dormitory, a kitchen and dining hall, a control and communication room, and much more! Quantity of pieces \ Series Below we will describe some of the details of the scenario that you will create in this diorama: GARAGE ELEVATOR The elevator is responsible for transporting the bus from external expeditions, from the surface to inside the bunker and vice versa. During these excursions to the outside, the team brings supplies to stock the inhabitants of the bunker. CONTROL ROOM The control room is where all communication with the outside and the inside is conducted (through speakers located in all areas), as surface analysis, and control of all operations and machinery in the bunker. It is here that the inventory of supplies and products, air quality, and other essential information for the bunker’s operation are managed. GYM The gym is quite diverse, with various equipment and resources for the inhabitants of the bunker to exercise and train in self-defense, keeping themselves in shape for imminent danger situations, such as external invasions, for example! PLANTATION The plantation is where various vegetables and greens are grown to feed the inhabitants of the bunker. There is a whole process of irrigation, fertilization, and care to ensure that these essential ingredients for the group’s diet are never lacking. As with all other dioramas in DIORAMA BUILDER : THE MUSIC IS ORIGINAL and made exclusively for this diorama. When completing the diorama, animations will be automatically activated in the scene and INTERACTION MODE will be activated, giving you access to interact with it, clicking on several pre-defined places to see very cool animations. In INTERACTION MODE you can activate NIGHT MODE , turning on all the scene lights at once and thus being able to enjoy a very special view of the diorama. You will have new ACHIEVEMENTS for you to conquer!
