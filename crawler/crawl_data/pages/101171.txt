ROGRID is a single-player dungeon crawler that combines Minesweeper-style deduction with roguelike growth and exploration. Follow the clues, open a path one tile at a time, and build a strategy for the journey ahead. Compare the colors and arrangements of clue blocks with the tiles you have already uncovered. Work out where monsters, traps, treasure, and events may be hiding, then choose where to explore next. When the clues fall into place, a path through the dungeon begins to emerge. Treasure chests, monsters, and unexpected encounters await behind the walls. Collect the treasure you find and consider the choices each event offers as you explore. Finding the stairs leaves you with a decision. Spend your remaining resources searching for more treasure, or head down to the next floor? Use your class abilities, choose skills as you level up, and collect artifacts to develop your strategy. Your active skill slots and available bag space matter, too. Find a way forward with the abilities and items you have on this adventure. Opening walls and attacking monsters cost action points. Keep an eye on your health and action points, and use recovery items and opportunities to camp to keep exploring. Balance the reward in front of you against the journey still to come. Even opening the next tile is a decision that matters.
