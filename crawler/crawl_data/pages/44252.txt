Take on the role of “the gunman”, a professional operative feared in the criminal underworld, working for an illusive order vowed to deliver justice in a world beyond the reach of the law - one contract at a time. From solo developer ANB_Seth, creator of the popular Half-Life: Alyx mods "Gunman Contracts – Chapter 1 & Chapter 2" , this game takes the action to the next level with a completely new, from-the-ground-up developed single-player experience. STORY CAMPAIGN The Gunman sets his sights on a new target: The Collectors - a ruthless crime operation killing and kidnapping innocent people for reasons yet unknown. Pick out your gear, take aim and get ready to kill your way to the dark truth behind the collectors operation. From an old Safehouse outside the city, to a warehouse district and a mob run restaurant with a dark truth kept below. TRAIN IN "THE HIVE" - THE GUNMAN'S BASE The gunman's base features a speed-shooting obstacle course, pop up target practice, a classic gun range, and holographic combat training. Your gun wall has 18+ weapons to unlock and a gun bench to make them your own. CHALLENGE MODES AND LEADERBOARDS In the Takedown-Mode you can select Enemy types, Enemy count and then clear the area. In Last Stand, survive an endless wave of hostiles and disarm bombs as long as you can. Feeling competitive, or just out for a personal best? Every contract, challenge and skill game features steam leaderboards (divided up between VR and Non-VR). EARLY ACCESS The game launches in Early Access with a set of 3 full story missions and challenge maps, with  3 additional story missions to be added with free future updates. However, the price of the game is set to increase as more contracts and content are added up to version 1.0. So feel free to join the party early! LAUNCH PACKAGE The game will release with the following content, with more (free) updates to come in the future: 3 Connected Story Contracts - Safehouse in the city outskirts - Syndicate run harbor warehouse district - Mobster restaurant with a dark underbelly The “Hive” (The Gunman's Home Base) - Gunwall to store and unlock weapons - Gun bench to skin weapons and modify gun sounds - Gun range with skill games and training stations - Holographic enemy combat area Challenge Game Modes (5 Maps Available) - Takedown Mode: Face off against up to 30 enemies to kill in a closed area - Last Stand Mode: Fight against waves of enemies, disarm bombs and survive as long as you can 18 Weapons To Unlock with per-weapon attachments Compound Bow and Knives Custom Music Feature play your own mp3 files in-game via the Gunman’s smartphone Steam Achievements Steam Leaderboards for every contract, skillgame and challenge Unlockable Cheats Optional Low-Violence Mode Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Modeld after gun blazing hollywood action thrillers, this game contains content not appropriate for all ages, and may not be appropriate for viewing at work, featuring frequent violence and general mature content. Note: this game can also be played in a "low violence" mode, which disables nearly all blood effects and splatter, but still keeps the action intact.
