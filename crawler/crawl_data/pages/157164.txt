The game is designed to upset and disturb you. ABOUT MY LOVELY DAUGHTER Return to Salach Village as Faust, the amnesiac alchemy master, in his journey to bring back his daughter to life. To achieve, you must perform forbidden alchemy experiments to restore her soul. You will have to create dozens of homunculi, raise them as if they were your own daughter, build relationships with them ... and then murder them to harvest their souls! But this time, Faust has a special guest, The inquisitor has come to visit him. Uncover the purpose of his presence in Salach Village while trying to revive your daughter using the homunculi souls.
