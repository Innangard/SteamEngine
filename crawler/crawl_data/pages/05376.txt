View Community Hub About This Game Tenebris Somnia is a survival horror adventure game in the same vein as classics like Silent Hill or Resident Evil - but adapted to a retro 2D aesthetic from the 8 or 16-bit era. You will struggle to survive while solving puzzles and fighting horrible creatures. Yet the secrets of the game lie within its cinematics... When you reach certain key points you will see sequences from a completely different point of view, made in live action with real actors and practical effects - created by a professional and award winning film crew from Argentina.
