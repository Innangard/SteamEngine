This expansion brings the long-awaited Type IX U-boat to the game, opening up new strategic possibilities and historical theaters of war. DLC Content Includes: Three Playable Type IX Variants Type IXA: The earliest vessel of this class, deployable as early as 1939. It features three periscopes and enhanced capacity for long-range operations. Type IXC: A significantly improved version with expanded fuel capacity for extended patrols in distant waters and a capability of supporting three conning tower configurations. Type IXC/40: The pinnacle of the IX series. Capable of the longest operational range, this variant in UBOAT comes standard with a quick diving forecastle and an armored conning tower for increased survivability. New Historical Campaigns & Missions Take part in historical operations and lead your boat through new campaigns and missions in distant operational areas. The Monsoon Group: Join the "Monsun Gruppe" and operate from the distant port of Penang, Malaysia, based within Japanese-controlled territory. South Atlantic Operations: Conduct distant patrols inspired by historical Wolfpacks - Cape Town, Eisbär, and Seehund. Operation Kiebitz: Undertake a daring rescue mission off the coast of North America to recover Kriegsmarine officers from a POW camp. Witness History Unfold Participate in authentic historical events including: Rescuing survivors following the sinking of the Laconia. Encountering the auxiliary cruiser Atlantis. Transporting Japanese officials through hostile waters to the Indian Ocean. Additional Emblems Decorate your conning tower with three additional emblems: A victory clover, as on U-68. A crocodile, inspired by a historical emblem of U-504. A tiger eating ship, as on U-518.
