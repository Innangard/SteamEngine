Luke Skywalker, with the help of Wedge Antilles, has blown up the Death Star. But there is little time to celebrate. To save the Rebel Alliance from this Imperial onslaught, Luke Skywalker and Wedge Antilles have assembled the Rogue Squadron, a group of twelve of the most skilled battle-tested starfighter pilots.
