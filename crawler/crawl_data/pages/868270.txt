What Tzelem Is A procedurally-generated dungeon crawler. A game with no permanent upgrades. Your success will be determined by skill, knowledge, and a little bit of luck. A short game, you might be able to finish it in one sitting. How Does The Demo Differ In the demo you'll have only the simplest, least complex version of the dungeon. You'll only have two enemies (thus, only four enemy-based cards), two trap types and one awake boss variant (the simplest). You can play for as much as you'd like, but you will forever only have this dungeon variant. The Weapon You get three orbs, and you will never have more than three. Use them wisely. Enemies Might Drop Cards On Death Different enemies drop different cards, study them as you go. Cards Can Have More Than One Use Most cards can have more than one use. Learn what they do so you may use them to your advantage when needed. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Slight implication of self-harm. You kill enemies and hitting them shoots particles, but there's nothing gory or blood-implying about the hurting and killing.
