Take control of your mining ship and venture into space in search of valuable asteroids. Blast rocks apart with your powerful mining laser, collect their resources, and invest everything back into your ship. Stellarite Miner is a short and focused incremental experience designed to be completed in around 2–3 hours. The more you mine, the stronger you become. Improve your mining power, increase your efficiency, and turn asteroids that once took time to break into easy targets. Upgrade Your Mining Ship Every haul brings you one step closer to becoming a more powerful miner. Spend your resources on upgrades that make your ship faster and more effective. Increase the power of your equipment and watch your ability to tear through asteroids grow with every upgrade. Unlock New Ways to Mine Your laser is only the beginning. Unlock automatic missiles that seek out and blast asteroids for you. Deploy drones that attack nearby rocks while you focus on your next target. As your arsenal grows, what started as a simple mining ship gradually becomes a powerful asteroid-breaking machine. Mine. Upgrade. Repeat. Stellarite Miner is built around a simple and satisfying incremental loop: Blast asteroids → Collect resources → Upgrade your ship → Become stronger → Mine even more. Progress from a small mining vessel to a fully upgraded asteroid-breaking machine in a compact 2–3 hour experience , with a clear beginning, progression, and ending.
