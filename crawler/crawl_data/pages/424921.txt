Expand your humble store into a huge retail company. Co-Op Mode and Single Player Share the duties with your friends in co-op mode (for up to 4 players). The more players in the store, the more demanding customers will visit your store. Also, you can go solo and manage everything in the store. You can invite your friends anytime if you change your mind. Store Workflow Sign contracts with leading brands, order new products with an in-game computer, unpack the boxes, place the products into your store, and set the prices for your products. Consider the market prices while setting up your product prices in the store for customer satisfaction. Always keep your store clean so as not to lose any customers. Also, be careful with the thieves to avoid some box losses that would cause a leak in your finances. Products Please make sure your customers find all their needs in the store. Always expand your product portfolio and satisfy your customers. Shirts, jackets, pants, sweatshirts, dresses, t-shirts, shoes, hats, and more... Be aware of the market prices and purchase more products when they're cheap. Customers Place a sufficient number of checkouts and fitting rooms to reduce the waiting time for customers. Scan products and collect payments whether in cash or with card purchases. Don't forget to fulfill the online orders. Expansions and New Teammates Expand your store and warehouse by leveling up your store and budget. Hire stockers to fill the racks. Purchase more cash registers and hire new cashiers to minimize long queues at the checkouts and to reduce waiting time for customers.
