View Community Hub About This Game Sort The Capybaras is a cozy organizing game. You are in a messy capybara store with 4,800 cute capybara toys. Pick up a capybara and identify the type of the capybara and then place them on the correct shelves. You will earn money for every capybara you sort. Use your money to upgrade sorting abilities, then you can sort faster and faster. Sort Capybaras to Earn Money Put a capybara onto the correct shelf will immediately give you money. Pick a capybara on the floor, look at it and look at the signboard on the shelf, you will easily find the shelf it belongs to. From Chaos to Organized The store starts with compeletely chaos, you can fill the shelves one by one. Finally, the store will be tidy and perfectly organized. Superpower for sorting You can unlock superpower abilities for sorting by spending money. Highlight the same type of capybaras, carry More capybaras at once, automatically highlight correct shelves, immediatelly collect all the same type of capybaras from the whole store to you hand, automatically collect same type of capybaras nearby.
