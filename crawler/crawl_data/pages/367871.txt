Now the house and all plushies are possessed. Can you make it through the maze, activate all symbols and defeat the demon? Plush Madness is a Horror Fighting Game optimized for VR and playable for Flatscreen too. Defeat Plushies In every room, you'll see plushies trying to attack you. You can pick them up, rip them apart or just hit them and make them fly. Activate Symbols You'll have to activate the symbols in the correct order to open the last door in the basement. Time Limit There is a Time Limit on your hands. Whenever you defeat a plushie, the timer is incremented. Can you make it to the end without running out of time?
