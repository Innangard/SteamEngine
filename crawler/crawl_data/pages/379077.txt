The sea is full of fish waiting for you to hunt, but there are also many dangerous creatures out there. You must survive in these perilous waters! Fast-paced gameplay! Each wave of fishing lasts 90 seconds. Hunt down as many as you can! Normal Mode offers classic wave‑based combat – kill enemies, collect gold, and stack stats in 15‑20 minute runs, perfect for quick sessions. Fog Mode adds a spreading purple mist that you must actively clear while dodging foes – it's a real multitasking challenge that rewards sharp reflexes. Missile Mode turns the arena into a high‑risk zone: homing missiles chase you, but you can bait them into monster hordes for devastating chain explosions. Campaign Mode provides a branching node map, letting you strategically choose upgrades that shape your build – ideal for players who love roguelike decision‑making. A variety of animal characters, weapons, and hundreds of items are available for you to choose from. Remember to upgrade your little animal’s abilities to help you hunt fish more effectively.
