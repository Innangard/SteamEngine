Due to the limited energy and time of an independent developer, for the large number of scenes, art resources, and functional implementations involved in the game development process, some content utilized official resources and commercial materials obtained through legitimate channels to ensure the successful completion of the project. Regarding the "Matrix-related content" mentioned by some players, a special explanation is provided here: The relevant demonstration content involved in the game originates from the example projects and demonstration resources officially released by Epic Games along with Unreal Engine 5. These resources are legal resources provided by Epic Games for developers to learn and use. I obtained them through official and legitimate channels and used them within the scope of authorization. There is no act of stealing, cracking, or illegally obtaining any materials from "The Matrix." Thank you for your understanding and support! I hope everyone can focus more on the game experience itself and derive enjoyment from "Kindness Metropolis." The powerful rendering capabilities of Unreal Engine 5 create an extremely realistic and exquisite game world for players. Whether it's the bustling streets of the city, the buildings full of life, or the swaying flowers and trees, everything is presented with细腻的画质 (detailed graphics), delivering an immersive visual experience. As a third-person open-world game, "Nice City" offers players great freedom. Players can roam the city at will, freely choosing paths and methods of exploration. You can stroll through bustling commercial districts to experience the lively urban atmosphere, or delve into quiet alleyways to discover hidden secrets. A rich variety of scenes and locations await discovery, and every exploration might bring unexpected surprises. When facing various challenges, players will use a wide array of weapons for intense shooting combat. The game's shooting feel is smooth, the controls are precise, and different weapons have unique characteristics and attack styles. Players can flexibly choose based on the actual combat situation to formulate personalized combat strategies. From pistols and rifles to rocket launchers, every shot delivers satisfying impact, immersing players in a tense and exciting combat atmosphere.
