Harvest massive amounts of corn and try your luck at finding the 0.00001% rare corn . Plant, Water, and Harvest Your Corn Plant seeds in your fields, water them, and harvest your corn when it is fully grown. Sell your harvest to fund your next upgrades. Longer Equipment, Bigger Harvests! Upgrade your tractor and farm to harvest on a larger scale. Extend your equipment to sweep up several rows at once and fill an even bigger trailer. Buy bigger, faster tractors and expand your farm with new plots of land. Special Corn Needs Special Conditions The corn you can discover depends on the time, the weather, and your active totem. Check the conditions and set your sights on varieties you have yet to find. Every change in the weather brings a new reason to look forward to your next harvest. Complete Collections and Reap the Rewards From gemstone corn to corn containing animals or planets, collect extraordinary varieties to complete your collections and unlock and upgrade new abilities and facilities. As your collections grow, so does your farm. And Then, 0.00001%. Take on the challenge of harvesting corn with a 0.00001% chance of appearing. Features A 3D incremental game focused on equipment upgrades, massive harvests, and collecting rare corn. Driving farm machinery yourself is at the heart of the game. Idle play is not supported. Aerial Watering helps with your work, while the Automated Farm provides supplementary production. This is not a farming life sim with a broad range of lifestyle activities. It does not include NPC relationship systems or story-driven adventures. Use quest rewards to improve your abilities and increase your corn yield at the Seed Lab.
