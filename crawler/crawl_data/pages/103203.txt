Descend into a dungeon that reshapes itself every run. Fight, explore, …and face narrative encounters brought to life through words and imagination. 🎯 Key Features Permadeath: every run is unique and final Procedural generation: layouts, items, and creatures change each time Narrative encounters: NPCs, quests, and lore told entirely through text 200+ creatures, 100+ spells, and countless items to discover 🎮 Free Demo Play the free demo now and experience a roguelike told entirely through words. 🏰 Special Rooms — Narrative Encounters Beyond the fast-paced roguelike exploration and combat, certain rooms transform into story-driven events. These encounters break from traditional roguelike logs, offering narrative depth, unique rewards, and consequences that shape your journey. Every special room is a story waiting to unfold. 🧙‍♂️ Deep Character Building Create a new adventurer with randomized stats, unique traits, and meaningful choices. Six types: Fighter, Rogue, Cleric, Mage, Magefighter, Citizen Homelands with bonuses: Each origin grants different starting advantages Dozens of skills: Combat, stealth, magic, survival, and more 100+ spells: Multiple schools, casting times, interruptions, and Vitality-based costs Mutations and rare abilities: Strange powers that help—or complicate—your survival Classic-inspired attributes: Vitality (VIT), Battle (BTL), Luck (LUK), reimagined from classic gamebooks 🐉 Over 200 Creatures—Each With Personality Encounter more than 200 unique creatures with distinct behaviors: Some pursue you relentlessly once they spot you Some wield a wide variety of spells, from direct attacks to disruptive magic Some inflict poison, disease, paralysis, and other dangerous conditions Some steal your items and flee Some offer quests with strict time limits Even creatures that seem friendly shouldn’t be trusted.
