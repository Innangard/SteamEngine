Content For This Game Browse all (1) Pigeon: A Love Story Soundtrack $5.00 $5.00 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Pigeon: A Love Story is a game where you soar across to-scale replicas of iconic cities in search of your pigeon-shaped soulmate. It's a slow game for fast times. LIFE-SIZE MAPS Fly through full-scale maps of London, New York, Tokyo, Paris and Amsterdam: every building, every street, and every pigeon (we did the math). PIGEON-SIM™ TECH Hundreds of thousands of autonomous pigeon NPCs flying and interacting in a real-time, city-wide simulation. Call out to as many as you can: most will politely reject you, but some may give you the occasional clue.  One of them, somewhere, is waiting for you. MULTIPLAYER "COO"-OP Play with up to eight friends across two different multiplayer modes: in one you must try to find each other, and in the other you must compete to see who can find their pigeon soulmate first. PASSIVE MODE Let the game play itself, screensaver style, if you just feel like birdwatching. SPEAK-TO-COO Optional. Coo into your microphone to call out in-game. ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACK Lock in and zen out to a  soundtrack composed specifically for the game by Madwreck.
