Super Grappling Gecko is an exploration game where you need to help a brave tiny lizard to succeed in his adventure with nothing but a hook. In this small adventure , you'll find challenging areas and dangerous enemies. Grab your hook and enjoy this adventure! Features - Classic Grappling Hook system. - Explore a challenging world with hidden places. - Short gameplay. - 2D frame-by-frame animation. - Speedrun friendly. - Challenging Achievements. What Super Grappling Gecko does not offer - Combat system Super Grappling Gecko is a metroidvania focused on exploration . Some other traits of this game: beautiful pixel art, original soundtrack, and skill challenger. It's perfect for players who like wholesome games and for players who enjoy speedrun. Super Grappling Gecko was developed by 7 game developers from Brazil. Together they have founded Calangames and want to develop many more small, unique, and fun indie games in the future.
