Prepare and serve traditional dishes from British and Irish folklore to satisfy mythical monsters before they feast on you. Every meal could mean the difference between triumph and becoming the next course. BUILD YOUR CULINARY DECK Craft and customise your deck with authentic recipes and spices from across the British Isles. Combine ingredients, chain flavour combos, and serve dishes that match each Horror’s cravings. Feed them full before they reach you, using your growing culinary arsenal to stay one step ahead. FACE MYTHICAL MONSTERS Encounter Black Annis, Jenny Greenteeth, Grendel, and other Horrors drawn from Celtic, English, Irish, Scottish, and Welsh legend. Each one brings its own appetite and personality, challenging you to satisfy their hunger before they devour you whole. MASTER FLAVOURS AND TASTES Each dish carries a distinct taste such as sweet, salty, sour, bitter, savoury, or bland. Combining these flavours in the right order creates powerful combos that can shift the balance of any encounter. Every Horror has unique preferences, from dishes they adore like Bakewell Tart to those they despise like Fish and Chips. Discover their favourites, avoid their dislikes, and serve the perfect meal to satisfy even the most monstrous appetite. EXPLORE LEGENDS AND SECRETS Travel through enchanted woods, peaceful meadows, and spooky bogs across the British Isles. Discover legendary artefacts, epic cookware, and rare spices as you uncover characters and stories rooted in ancient folklore and mythology. UPGRADE AND GROW STRONGER Return to your base after each run to refine your recipes, season your dishes, and unlock new abilities. Each attempt brings you closer to mastering the art of feeding Horrors and reaching the heart of the kingdom’s hunger. Hungry Horrors blends cards, folklore, and British humour in a pixel art roguelite deckbuilder where food, myth, and fantasy collide.
