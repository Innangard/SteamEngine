You begin with empty pockets and your bare hands in a vault containing exactly one million coins. Collect gold directly from the enormous coin pile, carry it to a deposit station, and use your banked earnings to buy better equipment. Every upgrade makes the next haul faster, bigger, and more satisfying. Start by scooping coins by hand, then unlock a growing set of increasingly powerful tools. Buy pouches and backpacks to carry larger loads, invest in a 250+ node skill tree that improves movement, reach, collection speed, tool strength, capacity, and deposit rate, and excavate deeper into the bunker to restore its infrastructure. One Million Coins is designed as a short, focused experience. Most players should be able to work their way through the vault in around 2 hours .
