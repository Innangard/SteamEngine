Plant twisted crops, summon cute but creepy helpers, and attract mutated animals that provide blood for the harvest. Cook and craft raw produce into offerings for the ancient creature in your barn. Unlock and upgrade your helpers to keep the farm running while the Elder God waits for its next meal. Cursed crops and blood-soaked offerings are rough on Petunia’s mind. Plant flowers and decorate the farm to maintain her sanity, but let it slip too far and hallucinations will begin to appear. Push the harvest too hard and Petunia may break. Neglect the farm, and something far worse may stir. Streamers and viewers can build a farm together.
