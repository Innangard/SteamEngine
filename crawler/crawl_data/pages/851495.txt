About This Content Embark on more journeys through the densely packed railways of Suburban Glasgow with a new extension to Airdrie, the classic terminus of the North Clyde Line, in this new expansion for Train Simulator Classic. Operate the night shift with the famed Caledonian Sleeper. Wearing the latest branding on Mk2 and Mk3 coaches, haul snoozing passengers on their long-distance trips bound for the Highlands using the BR Class 67. Also take on freight duties with the BR Class 66, fitting between the passenger schedule and not causing a fuss for commuters! With the ScotRail BR Class 320 from Suburban Glasgow Northwest, experience longer passenger services beyond Bellgrove, further out into the Glaswegian commuter towns that border the outskirts of the city. Authentic stations at Shettleston, Easterhouse, Coatbridge and more await! Included Scenarios A series of Career scenarios are included: Highland Dawn The Helensburgh Rattler Always look on the Sunnyside of Life A Springhill in my Step Made in Monkland More scenarios are available on the Steam Workshop online and in-game.
