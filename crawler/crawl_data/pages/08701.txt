They are influenced by aerodynamic factors such as drag and wind. We have developed a custom physics system, which is based on real fluid dynamics, resulting in realistic BB-trajecotries during gampelay. For some experienced airsoft players, tuning airsoft-weapons is a hobby that complements the sport. The performance of an airsoft gun is dependent on all internal components. Improve the hop-up for increased accuracy. Install a shorter barrel for more stealthy gameplay. Or switch to a red dot on your sniper rifle to show off an aggressive play style.
