In fact, I would even go as far as saying that Flamecraft is on fire!” Tabletop Games Blog About This Game Flamecraft is a cozy, turn-based strategy board game for 1 to 5 players. Become a Flamekeeper and help artisan dragons and shopkeepers delight the townspeople with their craft. Gather and manage resources, enchant the shops using artifacts, and magic your way to becoming the best Flamekeeper. Your main task is to thoughtfully place dragon artisans in the town’s bustling shopping district. They will help you gather various resources from the shops, which you can later use to cast enchantments, triggering powerful ability combos. You’ll also befriend the extraordinary Fancy Dragons, with abilities allowing you to boost your reputation. Your main goal is to earn the most reputation points by the time the game ends. If you manage to do that, you’ll earn the Master of Flamecraft title! Become a Flamekeeper and enter a cozy, vibrant world inhabited by dragons Flamecraft is set in a fantasy world, in which dragons and magic are part of the ordinary. Here, you’ll meet the adorable artisan dragons, who will use their extraordinary flamecraft to help the town’s shopkeepers with their seemingly mundane tasks. The sweets are just waiting to be baked in Guilty Treasures, and Scale Mail Post is ready to send enchanted letters all across the town! Play in a variety of gameplay modes, both single and multiplayer Whether you prefer playing solo, with friends or people from around the world, Flamecraft has something in store for you. Test your skills against AI of varying difficulty or set up a relaxing custom game for your family to enjoy together. Each game is full of charming surprises and opportunities to strategize with how many dragons, shops, and enchantments there are. The order they appear in is random, but how you decide to play them is entirely up to you, leading to each play - solo or multiplayer - feeling uniquely different. Master the art of resource management and create powerful combos to increase your reputation You’ll have to strategize and outsmart other Flamekeepers if you want to come out on top. There are countless ways to victory, and it’s up to you to make the most of your skills. On your turn, you’ll visit one of the various shops in town to either gather resources or enchant the shops using magical artifacts. You could visit Gnome Depot to grab some iron, get freshly baked bread from Drake of Cakes, or brew mysterious potions at Draconic Tonic. You’ll need those, among others, to later enchant the shops - this will let you fire up all of the dragons from a shop and combine their abilities to create amazing combos. This, along with the Companion and Fancy Dragon cards, which offer powerful one-time abilities and additional scoring opportunities, will let you get the reputation points needed to win the game. Meet over 70 beautiful animated dragons and visit 30 unique shops while listening to a whimsical score In Flamecraft, we’ve brought the enchanting world created by Sandara Tang to life. The beautifully illustrated dragons come to life as they use their craft in the vibrant 3D town, full of animated shops, booths, and laboratories. Paweł Górniak, the composer behind Wingspan and Quilts and Cats of Calico, is also back, with another spellbinding score. Will you be able to claim the Master of Flamecraft title with the help of your creative little crafter friends?
