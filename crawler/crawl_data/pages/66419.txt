Explore a procedurally generated world and build an automated industrial complex. Play alone or with friends. Build a factory in motion From hand-gathered ore to a working production line. Connect drills, conveyors and assemblers, follow every item through your factory, and keep the whole system fed. Put alchemy to work Dissolve raw materials, distil their principles and send them through a separate network of conduits. Combine physical components with the hidden properties of matter to create advanced production chains. Engineer your discoveries Scan unfamiliar materials and turn knowledge into technology. Supply your research bench, build directed resonance circuits and test them with calibration pulses. Every connection matters. Control what you unleash Your factory leaves a mark.
