View Community Hub About This Game Little Desktop Runner is a minimal interaction game about going on runs. Automatically level up and run further, collect achievements and reach the end of the world. Minimal Gameplay Press run and get back to work, your little runner will put the work in and let you know when they're ready to go. Upgrade your Homestead Automatically gather resources and build up your homestead between runs to further improve running distance! Background Game Sits in the corner of your desktop and makes sounds when it wants your attention . It's that simple! What To Expect This is not an offline idle game, the game must be open to progress Around 9-12 hours to complete game There is no music, only sounds to occasionally grab your attention Can be completed more efficiency with some active play Continue past the goal forever (the game will probably break eventually) AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: AI is used to assist with coding and general queries related to game development and the Godot engine.
