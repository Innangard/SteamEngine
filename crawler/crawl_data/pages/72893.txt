In Cardaire you take the role of "Mulligan", an intrepid duelist who aims to make a name for herself in a world that revolves around a card game (how neat). Embark on a strange adventure through the Duelist Frontier to prove yourself as a real Duelist. Collect cards, purchase booster packs, duel foes, and build your perfect deck! This game includes: Over 100 unique cards A world packed with things to explore and characters to meet A homemade soundtrack including REAL - ACTUAL - MUSIC Some relaxing fishing content Unique and challenging duels - including repeatable mini-tournaments At least (one) clown This game does not include: AI Art NFTs Microtransactions A second clown (probably) Hey there I'm "aeiou", and I'm the developer behind this thing! For as long as I can remember I've been cursed with the ungodly affliction of "enjoying card games". Growing up, when I wasn't scraping together money to buy more packs I was playing videogames that offered the 'card game experience'. I fell in love with the adventure of starting from nothing, piecing together your first deck, fighting to get more cards, and then finally becoming a powerhouse duelist. I'm making Cardaire to capture that experience for my fellow cardboard enthusiasts.
