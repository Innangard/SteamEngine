Opened in 2018, Istanbul Airport didn't just become the main international gateway to the city—it became a global titan. As of 2026, LTFM officially overtook London Heathrow (EGLL) to become the single busiest airport in all of Europe. It also reigns as Europe's largest airport by land area—spanning a footprint more than double the size of Paris Charles de Gaulle (LFPG) and three times larger than Frankfurt (EDDF). This immense scale features an enormous terminal complex with five concourses, offering a demanding environment where efficient traffic management and careful coordination are essential. Manage a constant, heavy flow of domestic, European, and long-haul traffic. From arrivals and departures to long taxi routes and complex runway crossings, you will need to keep ground operations running flawlessly through Europe’s reigning aviation powerhouse. Features: Six Runways: Includes the brand-new sixth runway, allowing for incredibly dense and complex simultaneous operations. Detailed airport and ground environment. Realistic runway and approach lighting. Unmatched Scale: Navigate aircraft across the largest airport footprint in Europe. Accurate Environment: Background scenery accurately generated using OSM (Open Street Maps).
