Embark on this incredible journey and unlock new cells, medications, and nanotechnology to strengthen yourself in the greatest adventure a living being can face: life! In Survivor Cells, speed is just the beginning to protect your cell from relentless attacks. You'll also need to make strategic decisions by controlling your nanobot and upgrading your medications as you progress. This is a casual and challenging game with elements of roguelite, bullet hell, and tower defense, where survival is the key to victory! In Survivor Cells, you: - Will face and defeat bacteria with devastating attacks and unique abilities; - Advancing in the journey of life unlocks new cells with new special abilities; - Use nanotechnology and control nanobots to destroy viruses and bacteria; - Can use medications to increase your ability to destroy viruses and bolster your defense; - Face different viruses and bacteria with distinct characteristics and abilities; - Face super bacteria "Boss" with special abilities; And much more...
