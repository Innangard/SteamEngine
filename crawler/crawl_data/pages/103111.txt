About This Game Neon Depth is a puzzle game with platform elements, in which to progress mistakes must be made. A block appears where you failed, which can help you on your next attempt. Some blocks have different colors and functions, and it is up to the player to identify which one should be used in each situation. By placing a block in an unwanted location, the player is able to destroy it to reverse his mistake. Be part of this journey about overcoming, full of analogies and reflections on learning how to move forward.
