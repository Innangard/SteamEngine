View Community Hub About This Game Dexterity Minesweeper! Sweep Carefully, Aim Precisely! In DexSweeper , the cursor never stops moving. Use the your joystick or mouse to guide your movement, then time your tile reveals carefully--one wrong move could be your last! Three Game Modes with Three Difficulty Levels each! Dex Style - The full DexSweeper experience, with both crank control and classic Minesweeper strategy. Classic Style - Traditional Minesweeper without the crank mechanic. No Guess Mode - The modern favorite, never encounter a 50/50 scenario. Standard Mode - The tried and true grid style from the origins of the genre. Each mode includes three difficulty levels to challenge players of all skill levels: Beginner - A great starting point to learn the mechanics without pressure. Intermediate - A balanced challenge for experienced sweepers. Expert - The true test of speed, precision, and strategy. Customize Your Game! Choose from a variety of tile and cursor options to personalize your experience: Cursors : 2 animated + 10 static Tiles : 2 animated + 21 static Can You Master the Perfect Clear? With quick reflexes, smart deduction, and precise timing, you'll uncover the board and dodge deadly mines. How fast can you solve the grid?
