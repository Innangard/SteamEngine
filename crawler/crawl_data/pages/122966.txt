View Community Hub About This Content It is the year 1983, a month has passed since the end of the Great War. The situation in the territories of the former Republic of Kagastam remains tense. The remnants of General Morin's army are engaged in bloody battles with representatives of the new regime. As an experienced war veteran, you are appointed as the commander of an outpost located at the very heart of the ongoing conflict. World Explore the conquered territories of Kagastam, draped in an autumnal landscape. Visit new locations such as the Village of Czereniewka, the Abandoned Railway Station, or the State Agricultural Farm. Drivers Post-war legal reforms have allowed women to cross the borders of Acaristan! Additionally, professional drivers and state diplomats, equipped with special document sets, will be visiting the checkpoint. Police Dog Trained dogs will be assigned to all border police teams. These loyal animals will assist in detecting hidden contraband and boost the morale of the units. Contraband To fund their war efforts, Kagastam partisans have developed a complex smuggling network. Recently, their loot has included gold and works of art. They are also involved in smuggling banned propaganda films aimed at mobilizing new supporters. Vehicles In their benevolence, the authorities of Acaristan have launched bus lines in the conquered territories. For these vehicles, all passengers are subject to inspection. Additionally, new passenger cars can be expected at the border. Stealth Attacks Eliminate enemies without raising the alarm! Effectively use terrain covers, such as dense bushes or building ruins, to avoid detection and complete missions with full discretion.
