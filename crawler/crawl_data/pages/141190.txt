View Community Hub About This Game Important note: Please note that Hacker Simulator is not a realistic 1:1 hacking simulation and that the mechanics are only inspired by real-life hacking methods. With Hacker Simulator , I wanted to bring you a fun and immersive experience built around the theme of hacking so that you can get a better idea of some of its aspects (as a solo developer, I certainly can't cover all of them). In order to keep your business running, you regularly need to stay anonymous by hiding behind various Wi-Fi networks. To do this, you will learn how to use the famous suite of tools called Aircrack-ng ! A lot of research has been done to bring you a simplified and enjoyable version of real-life hacking methods such as brute-force attacks , exploits , phishing and more! You will learn a lot during your adventure, but you will also learn that hacking is not without risk. The networks you explore will be secured and the Wi-Fi connections will be monitored , requiring a high level of attention.
