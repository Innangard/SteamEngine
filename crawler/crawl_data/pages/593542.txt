An epic battle between Day and Night pits two opponents — a friend or the CPU — against each other as they race to collect their own time-themed blocks while disrupting their rival’s playfield. But when time shifts, Day becomes Night, Night becomes Day, and suddenly what you can collect — and what you can’t — is completely turned on its head. With multiple game modes, 10 colorful CPU opponents, and over 30 playful costumes to unlock, Day and Night blends classic old-school puzzle action with a fresh, modern twist. GAME MODES: - Versus: Local 2-player split-screen against a friend or the CPU. Chain combos, build bonuses, and unleash seasonal attacks in explosive Day-vs-Night showdowns. - Survival: How long can you last against time itself? Difficulty increases as days, seasons, and years pass, with random modifiers ensuring every run feels different. - Story: Experience 6 unique tales spread across 29 acts. Each story introduces its own rules, challenges, and rewards in these theatrical single-player battles. - Dare: Looking for a real challenge? Take on 10 twisted dares featuring one-off conditions, unpredictable rules, and wild controls. - Rehearsal: Learn the basics and master the rules in this risk-free practice mode.
