View Community Hub About This Game Every. Single. Species. Some help each other. Others give in to their natural instincts. With more than 400 cards to discover, experiment with countless combinations and shape your own path! Ecosystem Builder Cards interact with each other on an expanding hexagonal grid. Each card has a unique effect that influences the animals and plants around it on the board. Inspired by nature, the possibilities are endless. Unleash your creativity and trigger insane combos in a single turn! Rule Wild City Lead your campaign, rally supporters, and outvote your rivals to become the Mayor of Wild City. As you progress through your electoral campaign, you will face Protests and Boss Fights that shake up the rules, forcing you to quickly adapt to new obstacles and debuffs. Exploit and Escalate The more you play, the more synergies you'll discover. Thanks to a keyword-based system, a single card can be used in many different ways as each building block interconnects. As every piece of your build comes together, you will reach heights you did not think were possible! Overview A unique strategic roguelike deckbuilding experience! Deckbuilding meets tile placement. Gather a deck of supporters to fund your electoral campaign and defeat the opposition! Impersonate one of many runner-up Mayors, each with their unique supporters and playstyle. Master strategic placement on grid to trigger insane combos! Unlock new cards and difficulties as you progress through each run. Complete the collection of 400+ cards! Nature meets society in this cute but gory setting! If you like games such as Cat God Ranch, Slay the Spire, Vampire Crawlers, Mewgenics, and 9 Kings you'll enjoy Wild City!
