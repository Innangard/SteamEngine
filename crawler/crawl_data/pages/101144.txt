Each of the 21 characters has its own starting weapon, passive and active skill. The Longer You Last, the Stronger They Get Enemies get stronger as time goes on, and a boss shows up every 3 minutes. From the Giant slamming the ground to the breath of the Bone Dragon, every boss has its own attack patterns. How long can you last? Most first runs end at the Giant. 32 Weapons, Relics From Combos Clear achievements to unlock new weapons. Hold the right two or three weapons at once and a Relic activates, which can turn a run around. A Different Build Every Run Gems : 21 kinds that raise stats such as Damage, Crit Chance, Evasion and Lifesteal. Blessings : 60 of them, found in chests, from Common to Legendary. Active skills : the Ranger rolls and is briefly invulnerable, the Paladin throws Mjolnir and calls it back, and the Bard gets a big buff for hitting the beat. Everything Unlocks Through Play Nothing in the game lets you pay to get stronger. Characters, weapons and relics are all unlocked through achievements and progress.
