No clunky game windows, no cluttered background maps. War of Genesis: Idle Loot is an idle loot RPG with a transparent background that runs smoothly in a small corner of your screen. A 3D hero sets up camp on your desktop, battling tirelessly by your side no matter what you're doing. Just leave it running, and it gets stronger on its own. No controls required.
