Improve your skills, level up your Heroes, and move up the Leaderboard! EXTRAORDINARY HEROES You will encounter both old friends from the Shadow Fight universe and brand new characters. A charismatic demon, an experienced samurai, a queen of the criminal world, or a shadow bot — every player will find their favorite among dozens of unique Heroes.
