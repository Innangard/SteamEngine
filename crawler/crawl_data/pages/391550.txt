Hunt with Precision Skillfully use your harpoon to catch a variety of fish, testing your timing and accuracy with every strike. Upgrade Your Ship & Gear Trade your catch for cash to enhance your ship’s speed and fuel capacity, allowing you to venture deeper into uncharted waters and discover new islands and hidden treasures. Explore the Open Sea Navigate through diverse ocean zones filled with rare fish, hidden finds, and powerful gear. Venture into the Unknown Travel deeper into the ocean where greater rewards await—but only if you can make it back safely.
