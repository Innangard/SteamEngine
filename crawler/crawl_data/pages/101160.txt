Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to WonderLang Russian , where learning Russian becomes an engaging RPG adventure . Explore a living world, take on quests, follow interactive dialogues, and solve language puzzles that turn study into play. This is the Russian-learning edition of WonderLang. Learn Russian from any supported starting language. Other language editions are also available, while WonderLang Polyglot includes all current and future languages in one purchase. Play the demo directly in your browser, with no installation, at Russian is woven into the adventure itself: explore , fight , converse , and investigate so that every quest becomes meaningful practice. WonderLang is designed for beginners , including players starting with no previous knowledge. Key Features Immersive Russian Learning Learn practical Russian through conversations, quests, puzzles, and everyday situations. Enjoy more than 45 hours of combined learning and gameplay. Choose among three learning modes for complete beginners, A1 learners, and A2 learners. Optional Cyrillic Module and Smart Input At the beginning, choose an optional module that teaches the Cyrillic alphabet step by step, or skip it if you already know the letters. Learn to recognize, read, and type Russian letters with clear visual guidance. Use an in-game graphical Russian keyboard and select the on-screen layout that suits you best. Story and Learning as Gameplay Follow a young hero who must learn Russian to lift a magical curse from their brother, meeting more than 60 unique NPCs . Form sentences and understand dialogue to solve puzzles and complete quests. Practise pronunciation through speech-recognition challenges , with an in-game dictionary and contextual help. Spaced Repetition and Real-World Practice Reinforce vocabulary and grammar in language-based battles built around spaced repetition . Use Russian while buying food, shopping, making appointments, and handling other everyday situations. Set daily goals and enjoy crosswords, memory games, charades, and other mini-games. Play with mouse and keyboard, mouse only, or keyboard only. WonderLang also plays great on Steam Deck with an official control layout.
