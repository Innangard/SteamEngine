PANIK is a puzzle game where you have to guide different characters to the goal. Sounds easy... but, can you manage it without panicking? The panikers are scared and can’t move! Find out how to calm these creatures and guide them to safety. Complete over 170 levels spread across 9 unique worlds and uncover all the challenges. Meet the panikers! Have you seen the one wearing a crown? What about the one with the magician’s hat? Or the robot? There’s even a ninja! Each one has unique abilities to help (or not) you solve each puzzle. FEATURES: Over 170 levels spread across 9 worlds. 15 panikers with unique abilities. (Or maybe it's 16?) Minimalist style. A bit of humor. Lots of panik!
