View Community Hub About This Game Seven heroes and the king ended the thousand-year war against the demon race, driving them to the brink of extinction. But over time, the heroes themselves gradually became corrupted. Before the Demon King died, the king received a prophecy: when the end of the world arrives, the heroes will betray humanity. As one of the heroes, what choice will you make when doomsday comes? An unconventional text-based game, dungeon adventure, exploratory turn-based RPG and roguelike with retro-style visuals. Choose the right path, uncover hidden levels within the dungeon, and build the best skill set to reach the highest floor. Choose from 7 unique classes: Alchemist, Mage, Assassin, Berserker, Curse Master, Defensive Warrior, and Believer. Select 1 main class and 2 secondary classes to create your own class combination. 156 brand-new skills, a new talent system, buff system, relic system ...... Choose your own path on randomly generated maps, slay monsters, elites, and bosses, find merchants, upgrade your skills, and craft your perfect strategy... Just one more run!
