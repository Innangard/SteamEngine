The mysterious angler rescues you from death and invites you into this game of destiny. For your father’s unfinished mission, for overthrowing the cruel rule, you will embark on a fated legendary journey. Spanning thousands of years from ancient times to the present, you will experience the intertwined destinies of seven characters, gain the power to change your fate, and create a world truly belonging to "humans." From the creation of the world by Pangu to the establishment of the Xia and Shang dynasties, gods shaped the world, and humans could only rely on the comfort of serving and believing in gods in the face of the unknown and hardships. Until bloody human sacrifices became the norm and slaves became playthings of the ruling class, destined individuals rose up to resist and establish an era of human governance, creating a splendid human world. This story may be different from what you have heard or learned before— Here, wizards are not charlatans who perform tricks, but the pioneers of matriarchal human tribes. Here, Houyi is not only skilled at shooting the sun, but also a wandering traveler with deep love in his life. Here, there are not only wars and conflicts among the Chinese people, but also various forces known as "alien races" — Shebish, Heichi, Qingqiu, mysterious dragons, enchanting foxes, brutal wolves, and various strange creatures described in the Classic of Mountains and Seas… And as the story twists and turns, when you look closely, it all boils down to two people, a boat, a table, and a game of destiny. · Numerous interesting quests and dungeon challenges. · Each character in the game possesses a unique combat style, and as you level up, you can choose higher-level skills to enhance your abilities. · Combine the equipment in the game to create a skill combination that is unique to you. · There are over dozens of equipment options available in the game, and each piece of equipment has its own special effects. · Dice combination + card battle mode, each throw determines the direction of the battle situation. Please make wise choices. · Numerous random events and dungeons are waiting for you to discover and challenge!
