View Community Hub About This Game Meet the size-changing Globs in this colorful puzzle game. Use the curious properties of their bodies at your advantage to solve a variety of levels, by using them to push each other or against walls, squeeze into tight gaps, grow to block holes, and many more surprises, all in a lively and expressive pixel art aesthetic. The puzzles are designed to be approachable, but provide additional optional challenges for players wishing to put their puzzle-solving skills to the test.
