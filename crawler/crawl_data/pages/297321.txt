View Community Hub About This Game This is a simulation game about managing a medieval blacksmith guild, where your goal is to grow it from a small workshop into an influential organization of masters. Forge, temper, and sharpen items ranging from horseshoes to swords, fulfill various orders, and increase your guild’s reputation. Purchase resources at markets, keep track of ore supplies, and maintain uninterrupted workshop production. Send blacksmiths for training and master new smithing techniques. Craft items ranging from horseshoes to swords and armor - by completing every stage of the production process, from mining and purchasing ore to assembling swords.
