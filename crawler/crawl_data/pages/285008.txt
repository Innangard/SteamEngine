THE GAME GET OUT! – A Very Bad Dreams Story is a psychological horror escape game set in the universe of Very Bad Dreams. GET OUT! is a brutal VR horror experience inspired by the wild spirit of 80's slasher movies. You wake up inside a twisted asylum filled with disturbing experiments and nightmarish creatures. Every sound could announce your imminent death. Breathe too loudly… and you might become someone's dinner. With your hands locked in a straitjacket, you must solve disturbing puzzles, survive relentless threats, and find a way to escape. Run. Hide. Fight. Think fast. And whatever happens… try to GET OUT! 👉 Add it to your wishlist… if you dare. STORY You are Dean, trapped in a decaying asylum that looks far more like a death trap than a place of healing. The walls whisper. Shadows move. And the secrets buried inside this place should have stayed buried. Nightmarish creatures roam the corridors — the result of experiments that went terribly wrong. With your hands restrained, you must explore the facility, uncover its secrets, and find a way out before you become the next test subject. Every step brings you closer to the truth… and to the horrors waiting behind sealed doors. CONTENT Includes the "Dean's Dream" DLC — experience Dean’s memories from a very different perspective… about 50 cm off the ground. Includes the "SOUNDTRACK MODE" DLC — a powerful retro soundtrack inspired by 90's horror vibes, crafted by CREATIVE VR3D. Full bHaptics support with more than 40 immersive actions. This build of GET OUT! contains several elements that are important for content reviewers to be aware of before evaluation. The game features graphic horror content, including: Blood and gore, visible in various scenes and interactions. Severed limbs and dismembered body parts, found in the environment or as part of scripted sequences. Human and mutant corpses, sometimes presented in a decayed or unsettling state. Intense and disturbing atmosphere, reinforced by dark, narrow, or confined environments that may create feelings of claustrophobia or discomfort. These elements are central to the intended horror tone and narrative of the game. They are not used for shock value alone, but to support the world-building, tension, and survival experience.
