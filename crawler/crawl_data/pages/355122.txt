A strategy camp sim game where you manage and command your army, training each individual troop and upgrading your camp. Satisfy your King by completing missions and epic battles against enemies. Lead your Lord to legend! Build a living army Manage resources and keep your army's morale high by carefully planning your strategies between missions. Promote captains and assign special roles in your camp improving your commander skills. Don't disappoint the King and carry out his will by completing the tasks assigned to you. Train your troops Each member of the army will have unique characteristics, traits, and specific needs. You can train and equip each individual troop, striving for the right balance in your army. Don't get too attached to them; in every battle, death will be permanent! Explore the maps Explore the various regions of the kingdom where the King will require your services. Noticing points of interest might lead you to rewards or unexpected encounters. Search and loot everything that could be useful for your army. Lead your army on the battlefied Fight in epic battles with various objectives. Each victory will increase the King's satisfaction. Lead your army to glory by gaining experience. Key Features Hand-crafted medieval campaign with regional maps and unique mission modifiers. Deep soldier management: train, equip and specialize every unit. Interact with your followers: rename each troop with the names of your followers. Permadeath & injuries: losses shape your strategy and the camp’s mood. Upgradeable camp: buildings and upgrades for your Camp. Satisfy the King: dynamic satisfaction meter with rewards and consequences. Single-player experience from camp board to battlefield. Customise your troops : More than 50 weapons and 50 pieces of armour will be available in the game.
