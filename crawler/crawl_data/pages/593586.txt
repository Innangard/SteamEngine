About This Demo GROUND ZERO APRIL 2026 SUPER DUPER DEMO The world ended. It got worse. Now it is somehow your problem. Dive headfirst into a radioactive dumpster fire of mutant chaos, cosmic nightmares, and government-level bad decisions in Ground Zero Hero Demo, a retro-fueled adventure where the apocalypse is equal parts terrifying, absurd, and probably avoidable. ABOUT THIS DEMO Put on your mutant hat, grab whatever goo still works, and stumble heroically into the blast zone as humanity’s least possibly-qualified last hope. Science pushed the big red button, reality tore open like cheap pants, and now mutants, goofy monstrosities, and unspeakable horrors are crawling out of places they absolutely should not be. Think Saturday morning cartoon energy slammed directly into nuclear fallout, with just enough cosmic dread to make your therapist nervous. In this demo, you will: Survive the opening chapter of a world gone spectacularly wrong Blast mutants, monsters, and weird flesh things your brain refuses to label Explore irradiated wastelands, broken cities, and suspiciously gooey underground zones Experience the early signs of reality unraveling like a cheap sweater GAME FEATURES Retro Action. Dumb Decisions. Fast-paced mutli-directional combat where every encounter feels like someone crossed toons with forbidden science. Mutants, Mayhem, and Mild Emotional Damage Battle grotesque creatures, rogue machines, and cosmic horrors that look like they were designed during a caffeine overdose. Explore the Blast Radius From crumbling cities to toxic nightmare zones, every location answers the question: “What if everything got much, much worse?” Become Slightly More Heroic Unlock weapons, upgrades, and survival tools because screaming alone will not save humanity. Cosmic Comic Horrors, But Funnier A bizarre blend of post-apocalyptic chaos, dark humor, and existential terror with enough absurdity to keep things only mostly horrifying. WHY PLAY THE DEMO? Because therapy is expensive, and this is Free. Ground Zero Hero Demo gives you your first taste of radioactive survival, mutant-smashing heroics, and universe-breaking nonsense before the full catastrophe arrives. Wishlist now, because the end of the world is coming whether you are ready or not.
