Fight Your Way Feel every blow you land on evil while you fight your way through the haunting abyss. Chase Taram Baba deeper and deeper into the dark and cling to your light, for the walls are moving. Taram Baba focuses on responsive, lightning-fast, satisfying combat. From the variety of Dead Cells to the juicy hits of Scourgebringer, from the atmosphere of Hollow Knight to the speed of Katana Zero, it blends many elements to create a spectacular action feast. Unique level generation algorithm that regenerates every place you have passed through, enhances the fever dream experience.
