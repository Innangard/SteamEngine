Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Dash'n'Drops is, to me, a playground that needs to be explored, experimented with, and shaped over time. That is why I want to go through Early Access first: to share this new vision with players, adapt it based on their feedback and needs, and blend it with my own vision for a project I care so deeply about. The goal is to deliver a rhythmic, varied, unique and content-rich solo platform-fighter.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Between one and two years. I would rather aim wide than promise a date and let people down, but my goal is to reach version 1.0 within that window, taking whatever time is needed to make sure the result is worth it.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Version 1.0 will bring significantly richer content: new game modes, new Diskettes and abilities through new characters that Globine will be able to copy, at least 7 Bosses, additional challenges, alternate costumes, and more developed story elements that will expand the world of Dash'n'Drops. A local multiplayer mode is also being considered.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Even now, the core of Dash'n'Drops already offers a solid experience. Here is what the game includes: .A complete Arcade mode with 2 large Maps, 3 final Bosses, and 4 game modes (Dash, Survival, Game&What, Glomobile) .4 playable Diskette types, each with their own abilities .Permanent rewards/Missions : new game configurations and cosmetics” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will increase slightly when the game moves out of Early Access, to reflect the additional content added along the way. Players who join now get the lowest price and will receive all updates through the full release at no extra cost.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I will be active on the official Discord and Steam forums to gather feedback, answer questions and share development updates. Dash'n'Drops fuses the gameplay of Platform-Fighters (Super Smash Bros., Rivals of Aether...) with the unpredictable progression of a modern Roguelite (Dead Cells, Hades...). Taking place during the events of Decline's Drops , dive into the network's strata to plunder power, stack up Power, Speed, Defense, and Jump, and come out strong enough to bring down the entities locking the system. Improve. Adapt. Update. Build your own arsenal in a race against the clock before the final showdown. Complete. Chain. Discover. Countless Modes and surprises await to be unlocked! Variety is the spice of life, even when you're trapped in a virtual world. Features: Rediscover the feel of a competitive fighting game in a solo adventure where every hit counts. Send your enemies flying or drain their health bar with surgical precision. Fully customize your moveset in real time. Find and equip a wide range of Diskettes to shape your Smash attacks, Tilts, aerials, and more. Every run offers a brand-new combat identity. Optimize your core stats (Speed, Jump, Power, Defense) and collect passive items to become an anomaly the system can no longer contain.
