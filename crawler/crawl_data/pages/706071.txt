An Adorable Job. More than 1,600 adorable plushies are scattered throughout one enormous store, and 90+ shelves are waiting to be filled! Pick up fluffy friends, carry them across the store, and turn mountains of plushy chaos into beautiful, organized displays. Every completed shelf brings you one step closer to transforming the entire store into a cozy paradise. Take your time, challenge your sorting skills, or invite friends to help. There's always another plushy looking for a home! 🤝 Better Together  Online Co-op Why tidy up alone when you can bring friends? Jump into online multiplayer and work together to organize the store. Split up the work, help each other find the right plushies, complete shelves, and watch the mess disappear. Whether you're an efficient team or a walking disaster, organizing is more fun together! 🧩 Two Ways to Play 🌸 Relaxed Mode No complicated rules, no pressure. Place plushies wherever you like, fill every shelf, and enjoy the satisfaction of turning chaos into order. 🧠 Challenge Mode Every plushy has a proper place, but you'll need to figure out where it belongs! Observe colors, animal habitats, plushy types, and other shared characteristics to solve sorting puzzles and complete the shelves. 🎨 Make Your Character Your Own Express yourself with character customization! Choose from different colors, faces, hairstyles, clothing, hats, shoes, and costumes to create your own look. Then show off your customized character while exploring and tidying the store with friends. 📺 Bring Your Twitch Community Along Streaming your plushy adventures? Your viewers can get involved too! Connect the game to Twitch and let your community interact through chat commands, triggering in-game interactions and creating unexpected moments. A cozy sorting session can become a wonderfully chaotic stream! ⚡ Upgrade Your Tidying Skills Completing shelves earns you bulbs that you can spend on useful upgrades. Carry more plushies at once. Increase your movement speed and unlock sprinting. Unlock and improve your magnet. Jump higher and move around the store more easily. Improve your abilities to organize the store more efficiently. The more you tidy, the better you become at it! 🌤️ Watch the Store Come to Life Discover different plushy sections, enjoy soft and fluffy plushies, and watch the enormous store transform as you progress. Changing daylight, cozy evening lighting, and shifting weather give the store a different atmosphere throughout your adventure. Every shelf you complete makes the chaos a little smaller and the store a little more beautiful. 🦩 Can You Tidy Up Everything? Explore every section, complete the shelf puzzles, upgrade your abilities, and reunite the three flamingoes at the central fountain. With over 1,600 plushies waiting to find their place, there's plenty to discover, collect, and organize. One enormous store. Thousands of adorable moments. And a whole lot of tidying to do!
