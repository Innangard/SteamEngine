Simulator 3 - EDDV Airport $19.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Hannover Airport [IATA: HAJ, ICAO: EDDV] is the international airport of Hanover in Germany, with 3 interconnected terminals beautifully modeled. The ninth largest airport in Germany, EDDV is available for Tower Simulator 3, developed by FeelThere in cooperation with SoFly. he airport has flights to European metropolitan and leisure destinations, and serves as a base for Eurowings, Corendon Airlines Europe and TUI fly Deutschland. Hannover Airport opened in Langenhagen in 1952, replacing an airfield situated within the 1952 city limits of Hanover. In 1973 two modern passenger terminals opened as terminals A and B that, as of 2023, are still in service. Terminals A and B each have six boarding gates equipped with jet bridges, while terminal C has eight of them. FEATURES – Realistic modeling with over 100 custom modelled buildings including Terminal interiors. – Detailed ground polygon model. – Realistic Taxi signs and custom 3D taxi lights around the airport. – Runway approach lighting. – Beautiful background scenery using OSM (open street map) – Realistic radar display.
