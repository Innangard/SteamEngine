Content For This Game Browse all (1) Battle Shapers Soundtrack $9.99 $9.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game You play as Ada, a reactivated Shaper tasked with reclaiming New Elysium from the cunning Overlords and their corrupted robots. Using advanced weapons and unique abilities, you will shoot & smash your way through dangerous towers and defeat the Overlords to put an end to their corruption of the city. Gun down hordes of enemy machines with relentless and punishing close-range combat in which every bot destroyed makes you stronger. Face the Overlords and adapt to their evolving powers and tactics by mastering highly customizable weapons, movement, and combat abilities. Steal the Overlords’ Cores and harness their powers to craft and transform both your playstyle and appearance. Unlock new weapons, abilities and talents to permanently increase your survivability and maximize your combat potential with every run. Deploy your own traps, power-ups and hacks in each Overlord’s tower to turn the tides of battle in your favor. Explore vibrant nostalgia-inspired locations to discover hidden upgrades and weapons throughout. Be warned! Just as you grow stronger by defeating Overlords, the journey ahead will also become more challenging. Defy the Overlords and save the city to the might of adrenaline-pumping rock music that will have you diving head-on and fist-first into action.
