What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early access is a great opportunity to get feedback from the community. In true rogue-lite fashion, I strive for every run of Tiny Rogues to be different from the previous. I want to add more content to Tiny Rogues to increase variety even more. I have a list of content and features in mind, which I want to add over time to give players more to explore and more to find, be it new weapons, equipment, traits, enemies, boss fights or big additions like post-game progression. I will tinker a lot with the balancing of the game to make choices matter.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “At least until December 2024, maybe even longer.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan to add more character classes, weapons, equipment items, traits and additional floors with more and harder boss fights! I do roadmap posts every now and then, check them out if you want concrete information on what's coming next. After the game exits early access, I want the game's balance and difficulty level to be more set and not as malleable as during early access.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game offers over 20 unique floors with a custom set of enemies for each floor. There are over 40 boss fights, over 400 weapons, over 500 equipment items, many consumable items, over 30 character classes with special abilities and over 100 traits that can be acquired each run. The dungeon also contains a variety of shop keepers, blacksmiths and a lot of random event rooms that can be discovered. There also are multiple meta game progression systems allowing to progress in both power but also content, such as unlocking new floors over time.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “With each major expansion the game will become slightly more expensive. The full version of Tiny Rogues will be more expensive.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I am very open to feedback and suggestions from the community. Do you have a great idea for a weapon? Do you have a great idea for an enemy or a boss fight? Did you find a bug? The best place to participate in giving feedback is on the community Discord.
