Super Orb Collector is a colorful arcade puzzle game that challenges your precision, reflexes, and timing. Guide your orb through dangerous obstacles, collect every white orb, and reach the exit to complete each level. With 24 levels , increasingly tricky obstacles, and unique puzzle mechanics, every stage brings a new challenge. Features 24 handcrafted levels — Navigate colorful mazes filled with moving obstacles and tricky challenges. Checkpoint system — Death resets your recent progress, but checkpoints let you secure collected orbs along the way. Simple, precise controls — Play with a mouse, keyboard, or controller, including Steam Deck controls. Special puzzle challenges — Discover new mechanics as you progress through the game. 31 languages — Play in your preferred language with automatic language detection. Customizable experience — Adjust display, audio, controls, and accessibility settings. Easy to pick up, challenging to master. Can you collect every orb and complete all 24 levels?
