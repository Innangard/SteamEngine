View Community Hub About This Content Welcome aboard the most luxurious cruise ship ever to sail the oceans! In the "Cruise Ship" DLC for Thief Simulator 2, you take on the role of a thief embarking on a new, exciting mission. This time, your target is a massive cruise ship full of wealthy passengers and valuable treasures waiting to be stolen. Immerse yourself in a world of opulence, where every corner of the ship hides secrets. From luxurious suites to hidden storage rooms and engine rooms – you'll find something valuable worth stealing everywhere you look! In this DLC, you will find: A completely new location with shops, gyms, suites, pools, and more New story missions that will immerse you in the atmosphere of luxurious tropics New valuable items waiting to be looted Motorboats and small boats New jobs Do you have what it takes to pull off the perfect heist in the middle of the ocean? "Thief Simulator 2: Cruise Ship" is your ticket to a world of risk and wealth. Get ready for the most exciting heist of your life!
