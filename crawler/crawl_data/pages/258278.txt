It’s undoubtedly a highlight of the year so far, and it deserves its moment in the spotlight.” 9/10 – Gamespew About This Game Play as a lone gunslinger who arrives in the accursed ghost town in search of a lost friend. The residents of the once-prosperous mining town have been afflicted by madness. Monsters and horrors roam the streets and there are few souls remaining with a sound mind. If the Eldritch beasts of the town can be overcome– there may be hope yet for Tombwater. Dash and slash your way through unsettling Eldritch beasts. Wield an arsenal of firearms, blades, spells, apothecary items, and cursed charms to protect yourself from a madness taking hold. Strike with a cavalry saber before casting a spell, or, equip yourself with a flame-imbued shotgun and a mining pickaxe to just tear the monsters apart. Tread your way through the interconnected world of Tombwater. Metroidvania elements allow you to discover canyons, scrublands, forests, mines, and more. Find many secret, optional areas full of strange NPCs and their quests, or dangerous enemies guarding rewards. A ancient curse, a mining disaster, a missing sheriff, and a darkness permeating everything. Uncover the secrets that Tombwater’s deranged inhabitants are trying to keep buried. 2D Top-Down Souls-Like : Experience punishing Souls-Like combat from a new perspective. Nostalgic, gruesomely detailed pixel art in a top-down view with every classic Souls-Like mechanic. Map & Quest Log : Track your progress through the twisted, Wild West world of Tombwater with a map and quest log. Gruesome Boss Fights: Experience 20+ horrifying boss battles that will push you to madness. Weapons & Spells: Arm yourself with over 50 weapons, 40 spells, and 40 charms to survive  relentless combat and ghoulish beasts. Apothecary Crafting: Helpful tonics, explosives and more can be crafted from materials you find while exploring to help aid you in battle System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 or newer Processor: Intel Core i3 M380 Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Intel HD 4000 DirectX: Version 10 Storage: 900 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 or newer Processor: Intel Core i3 M380 Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Intel HD 4000 DirectX: Version 10 Storage: 900 MB available space Copyright 2026 Moth Atlas and Midwest Games.
