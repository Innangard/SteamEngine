View Community Hub About This Game Features Deceptively simple controls:  Almost every interaction in the game is handled with a simple swipe left or right. Engaging story:  Adventure with a colorful cast of characters that accompany you on your adventures. Rich depth of builds and combos:  Choosing cards to add to your deck will be critical in surviving your adventures.  With over 1000 cards, the combinations are endless. 4 dramatically different characters with their own unique card sets. 1000+ playable cards. 185 unique enemies. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: A majority of the static image content, the character portrait animations in the Character Select screen, and some flavor text on playable cards, had AI play a role in their development. Some notable exceptions to this are: The card frame artwork for all cards, which were created by a contracted artist. Our capsule and header images, which were created by a contracted artist. Some enemy portraits. Marketing materials which include artwork from the capsule and header. All narrative and dialogue text, which was written by the team. We would like to extend a special thank you to the select family and friends who volunteered their likenesses for use as models for some of our main characters. It is deeply appreciated and we enjoyed working with you.
