Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game The aging owner of the beloved neighborhood bookshop you grew up in has fallen ill. You take a gamble on life and decide to embrace your love for books and also help him fight the battle. You quit your city job and decide to do this full time, now, it's up to you to rebuild, reimagine the space and also help the owner with his mounting amount of hospital and other bills, while staying afloat yourself. Manage your bookshop, earn money, nurture rising authors, host community showcases, and as your store's reputation grows, invite literary legends. Expand across cities and maybe, one day, open branches on different cities. Manage Shop Inventory System: Restock books and manage supply-demand cycles. Sales Mechanics: Earn revenue through customer interactions and successful transactions. Dialogue System: Engage in conversations with authors, agents, and publishing houses. Reputation Meter: Grow and track your store’s credibility and influence. Customize Interior Decoration: Change walls, floors, and store themes. Furniture Placement: Move shelves, tables, and seating to enhance customer comfort. Decorative Items: Unlock and place items that improve ambience and customer happiness. Pets: Adopt pets, feed them and watch them roam around your cute literary haven. Scale the Business Multi-location Expansion: Purchase and manage new bookstores in different cities—or planets! Progression System: Gradually unlock new gameplay layers, features, and challenges as your store evolves. Dynamic Environment Day/Night Cycle: Time flows naturally, affecting lighting, customer footfall, and special events. Weather System: Rain, fog, and cozy overcast days add variety, charm, and new customer behaviors. Lo-fi Music: A soothing lo-fi soundtrack complements the changing atmosphere and cozy gameplay loop. Learn & Grow Discover Original Works : Read short poems, book excerpts, and plot teasers shared by visiting in-game authors. Collectibles Library : Save and collect these writings in your personal "Notebook" or "Shelf of Stories", curating a growing literary archive. Unlock Classics : Reach milestones to unlock iconic poems and excerpts from real-world authors and poets. Knowledge + Nostalgia : A feature that rewards curiosity, celebrates creativity, and deepens the player’s connection to literature. Create, setup, decorate and expand your own bookshop. Make it a haven for readers and new authors. Join us in our journey and stay connected by following us on our socials.
