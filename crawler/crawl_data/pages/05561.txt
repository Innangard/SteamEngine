Content For This Game Browse all (1) Pibcremental - Supporter Pack $2.99 $2.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Pibcremental is a cozy, chaotic idle-incremental clicker where you grow a civilization of Pibbles in a giant tree, set in a post-human world. Gather resources, build wacky structures and play through special events to push your production to its absolute limits! At first, there’s just Papa Pib and a tiny tree. Hatch new Pibs, assign them to buildings and watch them generate resources for you. Buy upgrades and grow the tree to unlock new resources and factories. Every time you spend resources to grow the tree, new quirky buildings will appear. Click, click and click them again to boost your resource generation! Buy passive upgrades for your Pibs and gain more and more resources, even when idle. Unlock and play through all special events to win even more resources! Or even relics, which bring greater advantages to the buildings they boost. Gain Prestige points to restart your run and choose powerful, permanent buffs. Optimize your strategy to become more and more efficient with each new run, until you reach the sky.
