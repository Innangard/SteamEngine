Strength and honor is at the forefront as we introduce two new playable characters - The Fallen Knight and the Voidwalker . These characters bring a host of new content to Fahrul, and beyond, to aid you in your journey - new weapons, a new trait, a new merc, and more! The Fallen Knight’s strength has been forged by a code older than the lands. However their fealty is pledged to the people of Fahrul and are ready to charge into battle at a moment's notice to protect and bring honor to the innocent. Don’t let this old knight’s feeble look deceive you as they are a master of arms and bring unique talents to battle. Many an enemy has underestimated them; few have done so twice. The Voidwalker possesses an otherworldly ability to move around their location. At the blink of an eye they can travel across overworlds and battles alike. This gives way to them being incredible bounty hunters. Be wary of becoming their prey for you may end up as a trophy around their neck. Warriors of old and new, foes from near and far, one thing is for certain honor reigns on the battlefield.
