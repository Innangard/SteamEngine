View Community Hub About This Game The addictive bullet-heaven action of the survivors genre comes to the 41st Millennium and the Mortal Realms in an officially licensed game from Auroch Digital (Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun) in partnership with Warhammer and poncle (Vampire Survivors). Experience Warhammer’s epic champions, enemies, weapons and more, meticulously handcrafted in animated pixel glory. - Frenetic Bullet Heaven survival in a grim dark setting - Over 40 iconic playable characters from the Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Age of Sigmar universes, featuring Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun’s very own Malum Caedo! - Multiple modes and stages from across the grim, dark 41st Millennium and the Mortal Realms. - An arsenal of 75+ weapons, such as the Boltgun, Astartes Chainsword, Whirlwind Axes and easter eggs such as the humble pot of Nuln Oil. - Face down the mightiest monsters the universes have to offer, including the disgustingly bloated Great Unclean One. Extremis Bosses pose a challenge even for Survivors-like veterans! - A mighty cache of passives and power-ups to aid in enduring against the monstrous legions. - Esoteric secrets to uncover and collectables to collect! - ...and more! Choose your universe and become a legend of the Imperium or champion of Order in frenetic battles across the open and ashen plains of Aqshy to the tight and confined corridors of an abandoned Space Hulk. Every stage is a new arena of slaughter, each with its own enemy faction... and secrets to uncover. Unlock and choose from over 40 of your favorite characters from both the Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Age of Sigmar universes. Play as the legendary Commissar, Sebastian Yarrick, and the lightning-forged Stormcast Eternal, Neave Blacktalon, among a host of others. Each character comes with unique weapons and starting stats – no two survivors play the same. Wield an arsenal of iconic weapons drawn from the armouries of the far future and the mystical Mortal Realms. Eviscerate Tyranid hordes with a chainsword, or pulverise enemies with the unrelenting Bolter... and, if all that doesn’t work, drown them in a pot of Warhammer Paints  Nuln Oil paint. Mix and match weapons with power-ups and evolve them into devastating new forms to create screen-clearing storms of bullets, blades and magic. Obliterate the skittering, beady-eyed Skaven and their horde of warpstone-addled abominations or stand your ground against the ravenous Tyranid swarm and its myriads of monstrous Xenos bioforms. Survive long enough, and you’ll encounter huge screen-filling bosses such as the mighty, monstrous Ghazghkull Thraka and Vizzik Skour,  Prophet of the Horned Rat. These are the deadliest threats and will require the right champion and loadout to beat! Death is not the end for these champions of endless war. Survive for as long as you can against waves of enemies whilst collecting gold to grow more powerful for future runs. On the battlefield, you’ll also find Warhammer paint pots that will unlock new character forms and secrets.
