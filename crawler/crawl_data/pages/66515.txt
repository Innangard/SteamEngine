Reality was once whole. Then something broke. The Fracture split reality into two opposing worlds — White and Black . You are Shift , an agent capable of moving between them. THE WORLD CHANGES WHEN YOU DO INVERT is a fast-paced 2D puzzle-platformer built around one simple idea: SWITCHING IS SURVIVAL. Change between the White and Black worlds to transform the environment around you. Platforms appear and disappear. Paths open and close. Traps reveal themselves. Enemies shift. A safe route can become deadly with a single switch. You don't fight your way through the world. You figure it out. Observe. Switch. React. Survive. ONE HIT. INSTANT RESTART. ONE MORE TRY. In INVERT, failure is part of the rhythm. One hit is all it takes. But you're never left staring at a Game Over screen. Hit. Restart. Go again. Instantly. Levels are short, focused, and built around a simple feeling: "I know what I did wrong." Every attempt gives you another chance to understand the puzzle, improve your timing, and push a little further. One more try. Then one more. THE RULES KEEP CHANGING The world doesn't stay the same. Every 5 levels introduce a new idea , then build on what you've already learned. Learn it. Use it. Combine it. Then face it in a new way. Across 40 handcrafted levels , simple discoveries gradually become layered challenges that test everything you've learned. You should never know exactly what the next group of levels will ask of you. Learn the rules. Then question them. THE STORY IS WAITING BETWEEN THE LINES The world of INVERT doesn't reveal everything at once. You'll uncover its story piece by piece through: COMICS Story sequences revealing fragments of the world and its history. INVERT TRANSMISSIONS Messages from the organization guiding your mission. THE ARCHITECT'S ARCHIVES Mysterious records left behind by the Architect. And hidden challenges known as Invert Zones offer another way to explore what lies beneath the surface. You don't need to find everything to move forward. But if you look closer... you may discover more than you were meant to. ENTER THE INVERT ZONES Hidden within the fractured world are mysterious Invert Zones . Enter one and leave the normal path behind. Inside, you'll face a separate puzzle before returning to the level exactly where you left it. Solve it. Uncover what was hidden there. Then continue your journey. Some secrets require you to leave the path before you can move forward. THERE'S MORE TO THE WORLD THAN BLACK AND WHITE The White and Black worlds aren't simply different colors. They are two sides of the same fractured reality. Your ability to move between them is what makes you different. And everything you encounter is built around that ability. Enemies. Traps. Platforms. Paths. Puzzles. The world itself becomes the challenge. Switching isn't a power-up. Switching is survival. DESIGNED TO KEEP YOU IN THE FLOW INVERT is built around four things: Precise control. Fair challenges. Fresh ideas. Satisfying feedback. No unnecessary interruptions. No long waits after failure. No combat system to distract from the core mechanic. Just movement, puzzles, timing, and the decision to switch . When you fail, you should think: "That was my mistake." Not: "The game cheated me."
