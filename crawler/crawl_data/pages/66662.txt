Exploring Korean cooking recipes and culture in a cosy environment. Make customers happy with delicious dishes, gain experience and level up! Buy ingredients, craft noodles, engage in Korean food recipes and learn about the Korean Culture in a playful way. Read about Korean History and surround yourself by Iconic Korean Hanok Architecture. Manage your inventory, cook different recipes and serve to hungry customers. A World of Warcraft inspired level up, repetitive grind, cooking simulator, single player experience without combat and all about cooking. Along the way you are able to unlock achievements and pieces in the art gallery. Play as the  girl Dami who wants to for fill her dream becoming a cook and owning her domain. Along with a Skill tree and 8 Talents, temporary buffs and enhancements. Are you ready to hit max level?
