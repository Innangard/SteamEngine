A collection of 16 tracks composed and performed by Composer and Audio Designer Thomas Williams that draw inspiration from orchestral, folk and small ensemble music. Nodding to the instrumentation of the time period but seeking to capture the experience of visiting the Manor through the warmth of contemporary cinematic music. The pieces are ambient, calming, lively and expansive.
