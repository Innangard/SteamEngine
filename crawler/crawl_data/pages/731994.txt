We’re searching for Funfair Experts to travel to the old abandoned park of Gearbox Fairgrounds, on expeditions to uncover new thrilling blueprints and perks for this fresh new museum expansion. Transform the new location Riquetti Gardens into a one-of-a-kind mix of museum-meets-amusement park, or as we like to call it, amuse-euments. Not only will this new funfair-eum boast historic rides from Two Point County’s past, but as our resident Expert, you’ll have the chance to bring history to life for guests, as you build blueprints for immersive ride experiences that complement our existing museum themes. Ideally, you’ll have a good grasp of the technical art of Mascot Entertainment, with proven experience at building corkscrews near fragile artefacts, as well as soft skills such as candy floss making and spannering (that’s Ride Maintenance, to you and me). As our resident Expert, you’ll design the full loop of coastering journeys, creating your own mini-coasters with custom height, tilt and camber to realise your own high-velocity dreams. A background in award applications is a huge plus, as we’re eager to make history thrilling, with lofty goals to achieve lifetime coaster and ride certificates, and the perks that come with them.
