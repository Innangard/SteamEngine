Build and transform the infrastructure network of a self-growing, fully simulated city, where every new building sends fresh trips onto your roads: people driving to work, trucks making deliveries. Every traffic jam has a cause. You provide the creative fix: a few more lanes, a retimed signal, an extra road link, or a whole new highway with an interchange where the crossing used to be. Build it and watch the traffic flow. And when more asphalt stops working, build a public transport network. A bus line with bus-only lanes, a rail link or a metro tunnel placed where people already travel fills up fast, and every rider is one less car in your junction. Design the network Upgrade roads through 11 tiers, from gravel tracks to boulevards and multi-lane highways Cross rivers with bridges and mountains with tunnels in one simple move Rebuild a jammed crossing as a full interchange in one click: 3-way compact or trumpet where three meet, a compact or wide cloverleaf where two roads cross Shape the flow with one-way streets, turn restrictions and per-junction rules, then tune the traffic lights phase by phase Traffic simulated car by car Every vehicle is simulated. Follow a single car across town and see the jam from inside it The people using your network report back, a smooth run, a long wait or a detour twice the direct distance, and a congestion heat map shows where the network hurts Over two dozen live metrics, from queued cars to transit share, each charted across the whole run so you can see what a new interchange did to the numbers Move beyond the car Bus lines with routes and stops, plus bus lanes and bus-only roads so your buses don't sit in jams Passenger rail and underground metro with stations, level crossings and tunnels. Passengers route across your entire network, bus, rail, metro, transfers included. Build connections, and people will use them Freight rides the rails: dedicate lines, connect industry to demand, and the trucks come off your roads. Goods change trains at shared stations the same way passengers do Build it your way Traffic Challenges: puzzles where you build the interchanges by hand, one ramp at a time, then test whether enough cars get through Creative mode: place buildings yourself, no budget and no way to lose Full map editor: sculpt terrain, rivers, mountains and lakes, then play real runs on your own maps and share them on Steam Workshop Large maps up to 18x18 km, with dense downtown districts and suburban settlements System Requirements Windows macOS Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 64-bit Processor: Dual-core 2.4 GHz (Intel Core i3 or equivalent) Memory: 4096 MB RAM Graphics: Any GPU with WebGL 2.0 support (Intel HD 4000 or newer) DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 1000 MB available space Sound Card: Any Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit Processor: Quad-core 3.0 GHz (Intel Core i5 or equivalent) Memory: 8192 MB RAM Graphics: Dedicated GPU, GeForce GTX 1050 / Radeon RX 560 or better DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 1000 MB available space Sound Card: Any Minimum: OS: macOS 12 Monterey or newer Processor: Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel Core i5 Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Any Mac that runs macOS 12 (WebGL 2.0) Storage: 1000 MB available space Recommended: OS: macOS 13 Ventura or newer Processor: Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: Apple Silicon integrated GPU Storage: 1000 MB available space You can use this widget-maker to generate a bit of HTML that can be embedded in your website to easily allow customers to purchase this game on Steam.
