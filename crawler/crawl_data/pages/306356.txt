You will step into the world through the eyes of Chu Hebai, entering a reality that is slowly falling apart. Caught between memory and reality, you will search for what has already been lost— and what has yet to be lost. And in the end, you may come to realize: “This world… is a little kinder than you imagined.” Story “I”—Chu Hebai—lost my memory eleven years ago. In that time, the world has changed beyond recognition. A global catastrophe known as the “Great Collapse” has left everything on the brink of ruin. My family’s corporation thrived in the chaos, gaining immense wealth— and attracting endless conflict in return. And yet, I alone remained abroad, living a quiet, peaceful life for eleven years. But that peace could never last. The news of my grandfather’s death calls me back to my homeland. What awaits me there is not only loss— but long-buried memories, and a farewell that was never completed. “Everyone knows Chu Hebai…except me.” About the “Great Collapse” A global geological disaster caused by increasingly intense tectonic activity. Its effects continue to spread, with no sign of stopping. About the “Chu Group” A powerful corporate conglomerate specializing in extreme-environment protection technologies and biomedical science. While it has profited greatly from the Great Collapse, it has also become the target of both internal and external threats. In recent years, new challenges continue to emerge. About the “Lamplighter,” the “Psychologist,” and the “Consultation Room” Figures and places that have lived within Chu Hebai’s mind for eleven years. Each with their own intentions— yet when danger comes, they will not hesitate to light a guiding flame for her. Yu Lin CV: Qian Mingling Age: 22 “You always say the strangest things…” The current director of Chu Medical. A rare medical genius, disciplined and deeply rational.
