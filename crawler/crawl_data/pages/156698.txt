She seeks refuge from her constant, restless thoughts in a remote cabin. There are no distractions in the profound silence and isolation, but the eerie sound of the cold wind weaving through the trees keeps her awake. In the dark stillness of the night, the silence has begun to take on physical form. Without a Dawn masterfully immerses the player in introspection, intertwining psychological and philosophical elements to create a narrative that blurs the line between reality and fiction. FEATURES The art style and audio are handcrafted and designed with extreme care and love. Changeable color palettes, textures, and frames. Delve into a gripping narrative blurring the line between reality and fiction. Get ready for a delicate philosophical and psychological introspection. Handmade in Finland.
