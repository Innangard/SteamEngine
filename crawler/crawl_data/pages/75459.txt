Gather your sheepdog buddies and guide fluffy flocks through colorful flower fields to dye their wool…and make cool clothes for you and your friends. Take your time and have fun barking, running, and discovering magical places together, or take on challenges to win treats! Master the flock The sheep follow and influence each other, just like in real life. Learn to manage them like a good sheepdog, by positioning yourself strategically and barking at just the right moment! Or, just do whatever: after all, you’re a dog. A relaxing co-op game for everyone Sheepherds! was designed to be a friendly, relaxing co-op experience. No mandatory task-sharing, no peer pressure, no time limit. Just enjoy an experience where your actions can only have positive effects. Stay friends with your friends! Find your perfect herding pace To make sure everyone can enjoy a comfy shepherding adventure, you can freely adjust the game’s difficulty between levels. If things ever feel a bit too hectic or you’re still finding your rhythm, don’t worry. Tweak the settings and settle into a pace that feels right for you. You'll still earn the same rewards and achievements either way, so whether you're a new pup or an old pro, there's always a way to play that fits you perfectly! Spice it up with optional challenges Add an extra layer of depth with optional challenges designed to test your skills and teamwork. Will you become the top dog and master every challenge, or are you just looking to vibe with your friends? Either way is okay! Star Edition is here! Complete Classic Mode to unlock the brand-new Star Edition ! With new friends, new challenges, and new achievements, there’s even more fun waiting for you and your flock. We won’t reveal everything just yet. Can you guess who the new friends are? Not just any dog - your dog Turn your well-earned treats into exciting rewards! Unlock a variety of stylish accessories, colorful outfits and unique skins to personalize your sheepdog. Mix and match your favorite combinations to create a look that perfectly reflects your style and makes your dog stand out in the flock. Kick back in the big field Take a break between two herding trips and enjoy the little things: snap photos with your friends, read the sweet letters from your grandma, or dive into the mini-games she’s prepared just for you. Each new area you unlock brings fresh surprises and joyful moments to share. Meet the Pups!
