Experience the passage of time for three generations Players will join the protagonist, "Xiao Shui," on a profound journey to rediscover lost memories and embark on a soul-searching quest. Through  themes of love, family, and friendship, players will uncover emotional  chapters of life set against the backdrop of a significant era. Exquisite original hand-drawn illustrations Featuring a wealth of delicate and stunning artwork and animations, a script inspired by real-life experiences, and meticulously designed gameplay mechanics, Shui's Odyssey offers a captivating gaming experience.
