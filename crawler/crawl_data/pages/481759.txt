Upgrade your gear to complete levels and beat powerful bosses! Every rock has a number—hit it that many times and boom! it splits into two rocks with half the number and double the speed, turning the field into fast-moving chaos! Play solo or with a friend for double firepower. Beat hundreds of levels, survive endless waves, and climb the world leaderboard! Pick up coins and unlock new cannons: a wide-spread shotgun, a boomerang that swings back and spinning saw blades that slice through rocks as they fall. Upgrade power-ups like a piercing laser beam, explosive drones, and huge shockwaves to wipe out waves in a flash! Features: CO-OP: Team up with a friend and fend off the falling rocks together! Online Leaderboards: Survive as long as you can and get the best score in the world! Powerful Cannons: Choose between basic, shotgun, boomerang, and saw cannons! Devastating powers: Buy and upgrade powerful abilities to destroy everything! Special Levels: Defeat epic bosses and protect the bridge from destruction!
