Over 500 unique toys are waiting for their shelves, and if you want even more, fill your journal with 75 collectible cards tucked away across the levels. And best of all, there’s no rush. Take it easy and play at your own pace. MANY HANDS MAKE LIGHT WORK A store this big could definitely use a few extra hands! Invite your friends into online co-op or play locally in split-screen, sort dolls and dinosaurs onto their shelves and hunt for collectible cards together. Teamwork makes it faster and way more fun! A MODE FOR EVERY MOOD Feel like ignoring the rules, or would you rather challenge yourself? Pick a mode! In Classic, take it nice and easy. In Time Attack, race a timer that tops up with every toy you place. And in Free Play, put anything anywhere you like. Oh, and the first two modes track personal scores for a dash of friendly rivalry! FILL SHELVES WITH A WAVE OF YOUR HAND Sorting toys is so addictive you might not even notice the hours flying by. To find them even faster, level up abilities in the skill tree: they’ll highlight everything a shelf needs, vacuum up nearby toys, send them straight from your hands into place and more!
