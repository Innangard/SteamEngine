Every risk is a thrill. Dicealot is a high-stakes roguelike dice-builder where every roll is a risk, every risk is a thrill, and every thrill teeters between triumph and disaster. Roll in search of devastating strikes, gamble in the hunt for nail-biting payoffs, and push your luck to its very limits as you battle to conquer your foes. Set in the charmingly chaotic realm of Camelot, chance collides with strategy, mastery dances with madness, and one single roll can rewrite your fate. The twist? Keep rolling to stack bigger combos and score more points, but push too far, and you’ll lose everything you’ve gained that turn. Every roll matters. Every choice could be your last. Do you dare to keep rolling when the Kingdom itself hangs in the balance? Your Dice. Your Strategy. Your Skill. You’ll begin each run with a simple set of dice, but between battles you’ll stop at shops to expand and refine your collection. Some dice tilt the odds in your favour, others amplify your scores, and a few can backfire entirely, unless you’ve saved enough re-rolls to turn the tide. It’s up to you to experiment and discover the perfect combinations to save King Arthur and smite his foes! Wield one of 10 distinct weapons that fundamentally transform the way you play. Maybe you favour steady, smaller, but consistent hits, or perhaps you thrive on the thrill of chasing the biggest rolls? Every choice unlocks a unique playstyle and demands a new strategy, putting your fate in your own hands as you forge your path to victory. Master the Madness to Save the King. A strange and sinister fate has befallen King Arthur, cursed by a spell that threatens all of Camelot. Now Queen Guinevere must rally the loyal Knights of the Round Table and lead them through a medieval kingdom that’s both charmingly unhinged and dangerously unpredictable. Master the mayhem on your own terms in this roguelike, where Farkle-inspired combat meets deck-building depth. Every decision you make will shape your fate, and victory belongs to those bold enough to embrace the chaos and risk it all for the perfect combination.
