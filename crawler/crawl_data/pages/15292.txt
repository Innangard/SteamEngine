Content For This Game Browse all (1) Dice Carnival - Supporter Pack $1.99 $1.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Dice Carnival is a roguelike score-chasing game built around dice rolling, strategic banking, and chain reactions that can send your score sky-high. 🎲 ROLL. BANK. EXPLODE. Every round is a decision engine. Roll your dice, choose which ones to bank, then reroll the rest. Banked dice lock in gold when you commit and that's when the real scoring begins. Chain the same number twice for a double bonus. Fill your bank with high-value rolls. Find the moment to stop pressing your luck and cash in. 🎟️ COLLECT TOKENS Tokens are cards that fundamentally change how your dice score. Combine them, stack their effects, and discover synergies that compound into hundreds or thousands of points. Double King — consecutive doubles permanently raise your score bonus Twin — mirror your previous round's bank total for massive returns Last Reroll — your final reroll of the round multiplies your score Lucky 4 — every 4th round hits with a multiplier spike Plus Per Bank — every die you lock adds a flat bonus ...and many more waiting to be discovered No two runs feel the same. 🗺️ CHOOSE YOUR TOUR Before you roll a single die, choose your Tour. Each Tour sets your starting dice pool, move count, and challenge rules. A steady start, a chaotic D4-only run, a high-stakes sprint  pick the Tour that fits your strategy and see how far you can push it. 👹 DEFEAT THE BOSSES Every chapter ends with a Boss who bends the rules against you. One disables a Token. Another halves your moves. Another makes odd numbers worthless. Learn their patterns, adapt your strategy, and survive. Dice Boss - One of your dice becomes ineffective Token Boss - A random Token is disabled Odd Number Boss - Odd numbers don't count toward score Half Moves Boss - Your move count is halved Many different Boss effects - Each requires a different strategy! ⬆️ UPGRADE & EVOLVE Clear a round, hit the shop. Every purchase shapes how the rest of your run plays out: Extra Moves — more rerolls per round Score Multiplier — scale all your points up Bonus Points — permanent flat bonus on every locked die Extra Dice — roll multiple dice at once Upgrade Dice — evolve your D4s into D6s, D8s, and beyond 🎪 ENDLESS CARNIVAL 🎲 Multi-dice system — roll, bank, and chain strategically 🎟️ Unique Tokens — each one reshapes how you score 🗺️ Multiple Tours — different starts, different challenges 👹 Boss encounters — rule-breaking effects at every chapter end ⬆️ Deep upgrade path — build your run your way 📦 Chests & shop — risk vs. reward between every round 🎵 Synthwave soundtrack — groove while you grind 🎵 FIND YOUR RHYTHM Dice Carnival's neon carnival world is built for focus. Roll dice to a synthwave beat, discover Token combinations you didn't expect, and chase that perfect run. Every game teaches you something new, every run is a fresh shot at the high score. Dice Carnival rewards clever play over blind luck. Learn the systems, find the synergies, and let the combinations do the talking.
