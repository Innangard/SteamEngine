Your goal is simple: stay focused, survive the game, and reach 12 points before it’s too late! But you aren’t just playing what’s happening on the screen. You’ll need to divide your attention between playing the game and keeping track of what’s happening around you. Stay alert, react quickly, and learn how to manage the constant pressure as you push closer to 12 points. Losing focus at the wrong moment could bring your run to an abrupt end.
