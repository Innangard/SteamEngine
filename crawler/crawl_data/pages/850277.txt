Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game You're a rookie cat on a borrowed superbike with one goal: become world champion. Every run starts at the back of the grid in the Local Riders League. Score points over three races, beat the division favourite in a double-points final, and move up, all the way to the World Championships and six-time champion Vincent "The Crown" Ashford. Build a bike that breaks the rules Seven sponsors want you on their bike, and each one changes how it works: VOLTEX nitro that throws lightning, EMBER slams that set riders on fire, JACKPOT oil slicks that can wipe a rival out on the spot. Stack perks, team two sponsors up, evolve your best perks, and chase rule-breaking game changers that turn a run upside down. 64 perks across Nitro, Slam, Drop and passive slots 12 joint sponsorships for two-sponsor builds, and Works sets for riding in one sponsor's colours 7 game changers that rewrite a rule, and 6 evolutions for your best perks Plan every division Each division is a route map. Pick races by the reward they pay, then choose your stop: the paddock shop, a pit stop, or a paddock deal that always costs something. Carry consumables like EMP, flares and race fuel into a race, sell perks you've outgrown, and bank cash for interest. Real racing underneath Braking zones, apexes, slipstreams and pack fights decide every race. Rain, night races under floodlights, tunnels and desert heat haze change how you ride. Want more? Sim mode hands you a manual six-speed gearbox: power climbs with the revs, so shift just under the 14,000 rpm limiter and nail the launch. Lose the run, keep the garage Wreck, and the build is gone, but your Rep stays. Spend it on permanent garage upgrades, earn sponsor loyalty to open new perks, unlock tracks, items and riders, and turn up the Grit for a tougher run. Your crew chief and the sponsor reps remember how the last run ended. 12 cat riders , each with a starting perk and a trait that changes how they ride Daily Grand Prix : one seeded run a day, with the same rider and conditions for everyone Time Trial against your own ghost on 24 tracks 33 achievements , unlockable liveries and a full sponsor codex Keyboard or controller, fully remappable, with colour-blind, large-text and reduced-flashing options AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: All code and image and music development.
