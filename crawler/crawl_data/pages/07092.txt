Historically inaccurate.” Antonio López de Santa Anna About This Game Defend the Alamo and rewrite history in this confined bullet hell! Play as Shieldon, a shield who can only parry, who was tasked with rewriting the Alamo's fate. But, the enemies and its commander won't let it come easily. Can you defeat who awaits in the end, or will you be left to be remembered? ITEMS Collect over 60 items in this bullet hell roguelike! Discover even more crazy item synergies! Some items exclusively control your mouse, giving you a brand new ability to use with your mouse! Some of the historically accurate items include: THE Bowie Knife a Polar Bear The city of Roswell, New Mexico an Evil Bird a mentally unstable Buckler and MORE! EVENTS Experience unique events twice a run, to keep runs much more interesting and fresh. They will change all sorts of things, like permanently changing the environment, completely transforming existing enemies, and giving you the ability to swap out old items! A POWERFUL FOE Can you defeat the powerful enemy that awaits you if you survive long enough? The leader of the opposition is not going to let you overcome the odds so easily. Face off against the foe at the end of the battle, who has 8 unique attacks in this challenging boss fight. ACHIEVEMENTS/ENDINGS This game sports challenging achievements for avid achievement hunters, with new items being granted as rewards! You can also discover 3 unique endings. Can you find them all? Runs are about 15-30 minutes long, but the challenge and varied runs will ensure you'll come back for more!
