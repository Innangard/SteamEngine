At first the snail just wants to create a nice little forest. But greed gets the better of the snail and it starts mutating its forest into an abomination of value production. The game has satisfying exponential growth, hundreds of upgrades, and absurd satire of idiot trillionaires. Spread life in the desert! Become an unstoppable growth machine! Upgrade your snail! Automate and optimize! Features Over 100 upgrades (Over 1000 if you count buying the same multiple times…) A wide collection of different snail skins to collect Multiple resources to balance and optimize for, like Juice, Rot, Gasoline and… Blood? Feed the Forest can be completed in about 4-5 hours. Its gameplay does not involve full reset prestiging or long stretches of idle grinding. It has a light and absurd story that evolves in an unexpected direction and concludes with a satisfying ending. Feed the Forest is for you, if you: Want a relaxing and chill game A chill incremental experience focused on gradual, smooth growth and progression Want an extremely satisfying experience Every mechanic you unlock makes the game more satisfying: Paint every corner of the desert green, merge plants into greater plants, automate fruit collection with minions, roll dice to get jackpots and fill your forest with more and more valuable plants Enjoy light optimization challenges Balance the composition and layout of different plants in order to create the most optimal synergies Delight in absurd settings A naive little snail who just wants to have an adventure and make mad stacks, but gets corrupted by a mysterious sand-worm-creature. Together they create a decadent and absurd forest of untold horrors. Like creative, expressive gameplay Paint the desert with a variety of colorful “plant-brushes” to create the forest that you prefer.
