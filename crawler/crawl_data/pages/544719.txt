Solve Music-Driven Puzzles The Lullaby of Life is a puzzle adventure where you encounter multiple timing-based puzzles. Solve them using sound waves that you have to match in sequences of different colors and symbols. Challenge the way you think with each level featuring unique puzzles and mechanics. Awaken new companions, bring life to inert beings, and solve your way in this abstract universe! Become an Adventurer of the Universe You are the catalyst of new life everywhere in this universe. Travel around and avoid obstacles, explore, find secrets, run from enemies and dangers, and behold this journey where each level has its own unique setting, characters, and aesthetics. Bring Life to Dormant Beings While venturing into this universe you will encounter different beings. The main objective and core of each level are Dormant Elders. Bring them to life through sequences of sound waves from you and multiple companions who follow you along the way.
