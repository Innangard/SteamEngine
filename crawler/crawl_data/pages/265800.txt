This demo offers a glimpse into the war-torn wastelands of Siberia, placing you in command of a desperate journey aboard an armored train. Experience the grim atmosphere as you attempt to guide the Czechoslovak Legion through treacherous landscapes and brutal battles, with one ultimate goal: to bring your soldiers home. The demo takes you deep into the heart of Russia, offering challenging RTS missions that showcase the full game’s strategic depth. In the complete version, you’ll face the chaos of the Russian Civil War, trapped between the Red and White Armies where every decision counts. Keep your crew in fighting shape, manage scarce resources, embark on daring missions, and maintain morale as you navigate the dangers ahead—and behind you—as relentless enemies hunt your every move. Can you keep your men alive long enough to reach home? Embark on this unforgettable journey. Download the free demo and discover if you have what it takes to lead your men to safety. The last train home awaits. PICK YOUR BATTLES: Strategically deploy your squad in diverse real-time missions. Use combat skills, tactical planning, and sharp decision-making to turn the tide of battle. EQUIP YOUR SOLDIERS: Upgrade and personalize your soldiers by unlocking skills, earning new equipment, and awarding medals. Discover their personal stories as they rise through the ranks. MANAGE YOUR CREW: Beyond combat, direct your soldiers' roles onboard the train. Craft crucial items, heal the wounded, and boost morale to keep your crew battle-ready. UPGRADE YOUR TRAIN: Enhance your armored train’s capabilities by upgrading carriages, improving the locomotive, and managing essential maintenance to ensure your journey continues. JOURNEY THROUGH SIBERIA: Stockpile supplies, trade with allies, and face crisis after crisis as you traverse the vast, unforgiving landscape of Siberia. BASED ON HISTORICAL EVENTS: Experience a compelling narrative inspired by the real-life struggles of the Czechoslovak Legion during World War I, fighting their way through a fractured Russia.
