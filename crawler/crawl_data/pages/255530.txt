View Community Hub About This Game The Boba Teashop is a cozy horror game where you own and manage a boba tea shop. Prepare boba teas, serve customers, and expand your menu to attract more patrons. You play as Risa, a woman in her 30s who is tired of corporate life and has decided to chase her dream of opening a boba tea shop. As a boba tea shop owner, you will encounter a variety of customers. Your job is to greet them with a smile and take their orders. Making boba tea is simple. The ingredients are just milk tea and boba. Seal the cup with a lid and serve it with a straw for sipping and enjoying the boba pearls. But sometimes, you'll encounter demanding customers with special requests or those who leave a mess in your shop. As the proud owner of a boba tea shop, there's nothing you can't handle. While serving customers and managing your boba tea shop, you start seeing visions and hearing things. Is it all in your head, or is something haunting the place? This is psychological horror game that builds tension through eerie atmosphere, unsettling events, and moments of intense scare when you least expect them. Features: Gameplay: A simple yet engaging loop where you take orders from customers, prepare their drinks, and serve them with a smile. VHS Aesthetics: Experience the game through a creepy VHS filter that adds a nostalgic and eerie atmosphere. Story: Uncover Risa's journey and personal growth as she pursues her dream of becoming a successful boba tea shop owner.
