Spray to Grail is a juice-first incremental game about painting pairs, packing orders, and upgrading a scrappy bootleg sneaker studio. CHASE GRAILS Unlock wild sneakers with animated effects and special reveal moments. Build a collection wall of increasingly ridiculous footwear.
