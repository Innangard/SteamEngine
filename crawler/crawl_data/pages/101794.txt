Break the silence with your intellect! Search for the Truth Between the Pages It Was Not a Suicide is a document-driven detective investigation simulator set in the misty atmosphere of 2005. More than a classic adventure game, it places the bureaucratic burden and dark reality of a genuine investigation on your shoulders. Key Features: In-Depth Investigation: Examine police reports, autopsy documents, interrogation transcripts, and suspects' journals word by word. Find the contradictions and expose the lies. Retro Digital Detective Work: Step back into the technology of the 2000s. Use the POISON-OS terminal system to access confidential hospital records and encrypted files. Detective's Intuition: When you lose your way in the darkness of the investigation and hit a dead end, cryptic notes appearing on the corkboard will guide your thoughts. The system never gives you the answer directly; it only whispers the truth to help you focus on the right clues. Solve the Mystery: Uncover the dirty schemes hidden within the sterile hallways of this psychiatric clinic and expose the secrets the suspects are hiding. Remember: you can't afford to make a mistake you must identify the real culprit on your first and only attempt. Are you ready to uncover the truth? Wishlist it now and be among the first to explore the darkness of Rainier Falls.
