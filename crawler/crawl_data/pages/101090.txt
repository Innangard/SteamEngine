HAUNT is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game where ghosts hide in plain sight while hunters search for them. Ghosts are invisible while standing still, but moving too much can give away their position. Hide and Seek The rules are simple. Players are split into Hunters and Ghosts. Hunters must find and eliminate every Ghost before time runs out. Ghosts must stay hidden and survive until the round ends. Hide in Plain Sight As a Ghost, use the environment to your advantage. Stay still to remain invisible, find a good hiding spot, and carefully choose when to move. You can run when you need to escape, but Hunters will be able to see you if you move too much. Hunt the Ghosts Hunters have several tools to help find hidden Ghosts, including a flashlight, EMF reader, and pistol. Ghosts can take four hits before they are eliminated, so finding one does not always mean the hunt is over. Play With Friends Play with friends or join other players online. Create a private lobby for your group or open your game for others to join. Steam Workshop Paint Presets: Create your own character paint presets directly inside HAUNT and upload them to Steam Workshop from the game. Paint presets you subscribe to through Steam Workshop become available for use in-game. Custom Maps: HAUNT does not include an in-game map editor. Custom maps are created separately in Unreal Engine 5.8 and published to Steam Workshop using SteamCMD. To play a community-created map, subscribe to it through Steam Workshop and select it from the map list in the HAUNT lobby. Videos and Streaming Gameplay videos and livestreams are welcome.
