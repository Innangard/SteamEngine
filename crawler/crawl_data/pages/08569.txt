Explore the first biome, push through dense vegetation, and uncover the path that leads deeper into the unknown. But this is only the beginning! Somewhere deep within the jungle, an ancient temple awaits... A true adventurer knows how to improvise. These cursed temples hold a lot more than just gold, they’re packed with the leftovers of the poor saps who came before you. Check every dark corner for whatever keeps you breathing. Sure, starving to death is bad, but getting squashed by the walls is definitely worse. Every hallway is a literal death trap waiting for the clumsy guy in your squad to mess up. If the traps don’t get you, the locals will. These forgotten temples are the perfect breeding ground for anything that slithers, stings, or bites. It belongs in a museum... but it’ll look way better in your bank account. Deep within these ruins lie priceless artifacts and solid gold idols just waiting to be claimed.
