[A Market Between Memories] Welcome to Oboro no Ichi , a market suspended between memories, where lost souls seek peace. Build, expand, and manage a spiritual market inspired by Japan, welcome wandering spirits, and shape a place that can grow alongside them. [Build and Grow Your Market] Freely design your space, connecting stalls, buildings, and pathways to create a lively and harmonious place. Each structure has a specific role: produce resources, supply your stalls, and grow your market one step at a time. (A Simple Yet Deep Gameplay Loop) Find the right balance between beauty and functionality. Organize production, manage sales, expand your market, and increase its appeal to welcome more and more spirits. A relaxing cycle that is easy to grasp, yet full of choices, strategies, and combinations. (A Calm and Cozy Experience) In Oboro no Ichi , there is no rush. Only the slow drifting of spirits, the sound of the wind… and a market that grows alongside them. Take your time.
