View Community Hub About This Game Run your own Parisian bistro in a chaotic mix of service and management, solo or with up to 8 players in online co-op . Serve crowds of hungry customers, optimize your restaurant, and jump into fun hands-on interactions that keep every shift lively. Grow your business while serving mouth-watering dishes inspired by authentic French cuisine. Divide the workload across the restaurant. With up to 8 players in online co-op , everyone can take on a role and work together to keep the bistro running! Serve delicious dishes, serve drinks, and keep your customers happy. From the kitchen to the bar, every role matters when the restaurant gets busy. Get your kitchen ready for the morning rush and prepare breakfast orders. Put your cooking skills to the test with interactive kitchen mini games. Turn the rooftop into a lively nightlife destination. Bring in a DJ, serve cocktails, and keep your guests entertained as the evening comes alive. Customize and expand your restaurant to create a space that feels truly yours. Unlock new areas, upgrades, furniture, and more as your business grows. Run your bistro your way. Manage finances, inventory, suppliers, and staff while balancing expenses and customer satisfaction to build a profitable business. Hide cash, secure your earnings in a personal vault, and take on shady side activities, but watch your Financial Vigilance before unwanted attention finds you. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Aside from the elements mentioned below, all artistic assets, including the game's store capsule artwork, logo, 3D models, textures, HUD, and 2D user interfaces, were created by 2D and 3D artists without the use of AI in their creation process. AI-generated content was used in a limited number of assets: * Some of the alcohol bottle labels visible on the rooftop were initially generated with AI, then reworked, adapted, and integrated into the final 3D assets by our 3D artist. * The DJ music was purchased from an artist who partially used AI during the creation of some elements of the tracks. * Approximately 20% of the game's sound effects were created using AI tools. The remaining sound effects come from royalty-free libraries or were created by a sound designer without the use of AI.
