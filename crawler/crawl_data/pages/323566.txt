View Community Hub About This Game A roguelite puzzle in which you have to lick and stick postage stamps of different shapes and sizes to collect points onto packages to send them! You are a novice postman, and by order of the Royal Postal Academy, you must restore an abandoned post office. You will be assisted by the inhabitants of the place—the cutest little cockroaches! 💌 Roach-workers Hire moustached workers from over 40 candidates to receive useful bonuses! Choose who goes to work and who gets time off, upgrade the most useful ones and fire those you don't like. 💌 Impatient Customers Colorful characters, varied packages, and a ticking timer. Every order is unique! Welcome to your first shift! 💌 Features More than 40 roach-workers with various hobbies. More than 80 creations with unique advantages. Polymino-stamps and various-shaped packages. Color synergies and conflicts. The progress of the post office and rare cockroaches. Clients with timers and requests, Inspectors' checks. A grade points that needs to be retained and restored. Choosing the starting set and difficulty before the shift.
