The life went on as usual in the Inkland, until the Tower has suddenly fallen straight from the sky! Many heroes tried to solve its mystery, but none of them succeeded! Maybe it's your turn to test yourself? Explore the procedurally generated game world of the Inkland - dungeons, caves, forest and, of course, the Mysterious Tower. Who can reveal all the secrets of the Mysterious Tower? Choose your hero - a warrior, magician or rogue! Each has its own unique ability: to fight with a shield, use powerful spells or avoid enemy attacks skillfully.
