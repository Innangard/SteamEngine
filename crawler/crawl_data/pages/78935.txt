Nobody knows why. What everyone does know is that Chef's patience is infamously low... Every run challenges you to survive chaotic restaurant shifts, unlock powerful upgrades, and adapt to constantly changing kitchens. Every shift is another chance to upgrade, be adorable, and prove you belong in Chef's kitchen. Master Four Unique Restaurant Locations Every restaurant plays differently. Travel across four distinct biomes, each packed with unique layouts, novel mechanics, enemies, obstacles, and challenges that force you to rethink how you approach every shift. Build Powerful Runs If you spot a bird, knock the coins out of them! Then spend loot on upgrades and abilities to experiment with powerful combinations that can completely transform how you play. Every Shift Tells A Different Story Unexpected events, changing conditions, and evolving restaurant layouts ensure that no two runs unfold exactly the same way. Like any good sous chef in a very normal restaurant, success is mostly about anticipating the changing moods of Chef, customers, and marauding hordes of strangely adorable beasts. You Fired! Chef runs a tight ship, and has a peculiar magical ability to burst into flames of fury. This fury tends to target the furry new friend he hired as a sous chef, resulting in an unfortunate return to unemployment (and possibly theft). So... don't get fired! Online Co-Op The kitchen is about to get even more chaotic. letting two players tackle restaurant shifts together and find entirely new ways to succeed. Or fail spectacularly. Decorate Your Room Make your space truly your own. Spend your earnings on decorations and swap them in to give your room a style that's all yours. The Bar & Minigames Take a break from the kitchen and wander around the bar, or play side minigames to earn extra cash for upgrading your raccoon. We know the main game can get pretty stressful, so minigames will give you another way to fund your upgrades. —————— Thank you for playing Sous Raccoon!
