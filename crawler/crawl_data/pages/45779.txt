About This Game Ever wanted to step into the shoes of a packaging expert? In Ship, Inc. , you take on the role of a packaging employee at a fast-paced cargo shipment company. Your job? Sort, package, and ship parcels to their correct destinations while keeping up with the growing demands of the business. How It Works At Ship, Inc., every package counts! Follow shipment orders carefully, choose the right box sizes, and ensure each package reaches its destination safely. Misplacing or damaging shipments could cost you, so be precise and efficient! Key Features 📦 Cozy & Wholesome Packaging System – Select the correct box size, arrange items properly, and seal them securely before shipping. 📋 Order Processing – Keep an eye on incoming orders and ship parcels correctly to maximize efficiency. 🧰 Upgrade Your Office – Earn money to improve your workspace, increase storage, and unlock better shipping tools. ⏱️ Time Management Challenges – Stay on top of customer demands as you balance speed and accuracy in your shipments. 💥 Deliver With Care – Packages must be handled properly! Fragile items require extra attention to avoid damage during transit. 🚀 Dynamic Difficulty – As you progress, new challenges and complex orders will test your multitasking skills. Run a Successful Wholesome Shipping Business Every second counts in the shipping industry! Improve your workflow, make the right decisions, and prove yourself as the best packaging expert in Ship, Inc. . Are you up for the challenge? Different Game Modes Ship, Inc. includes 3 different  modes (difficulty based modes) that invites all players. 🧘 Chill Mode: No timer; less penalties; just chill and pack stuff. 📦 Normal Mode: Standard timer and penalties; intended progression. Good luck on your new job! 🔥 Rush Hour Mode: You have less time to pack and more penalties for your mistakes. I hope they pay you well.
