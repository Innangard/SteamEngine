Complete Contracts and collect power-ups that strengthen you immensely, all culminating in massive boss fights! With inspiration from flash cartoons/games of the 2000s, the visuals are hand-animated with a sharp and bold style. Taking on Contracts will have you exploring large levels, all with their own set of enemies, NPCs, and secrets! Power-ups will prepare you for the Contract's boss battle at the end of the level. Power-ups can increase movement speed, spread-shot bullets, fire rate, and much more! Sub-Contracts will have you taking on smaller challenge levels with a variety of objectives. Earn all 3 stars in each and get rewards! Multiple playable characters , all with unique play styles. Some are focused on pure shoot'em up gameplay, others centered around melee attacks. If you're having trouble on a level, sometimes trying out a new character is the key!
