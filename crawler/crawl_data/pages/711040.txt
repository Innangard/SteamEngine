Kingdoms fall, trade routes vanish, and the world you knew is breaking apart. Lead a small group of survivors across a collapsing world where every decision matters, and survival is never guaranteed. A Story-Driven RTS Journey Follow a narrative campaign centered on a cast of evolving characters, shaped by the collapse unfolding around them. Bear witness as you guide your people through an epic journey defined by hardship, sacrifice, and resilience. Survival Over Expansion This is not a game about building the biggest empire. Resources have to be managed, stability is fragile, and growth comes with risk. Every expansion, every battle, and every decision must be weighed against survival. Building and managing a sustainable economy is as important as commanding your forces in battle. Expanding your army too quickly can put your food economy under pressure. Food is Power Food is the foundation of your civilization. It sustains your population, fuels your armies, and drives your progress. Mismanage it, and everything else will fall apart. A World in Decline The mysterious sea people are bringing destruction and chaos. Drought, famine, and failing trade routes are putting pressure on the great empires. People are starting to revolt against their leaders. The world is being reshaped, and as order breaks down, safety fades and once-powerful civilizations struggle to hold together. Tactical RTS Combat Command historically inspired factions with over a millennium of rich history, including Assyrians, Egyptians, Babylonians, and Hittites. Each brings unique units, strengths, and playstyles to economic domination and warfare.
