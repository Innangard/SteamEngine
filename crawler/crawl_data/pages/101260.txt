View Community Hub About This Game PECADO is a first-person folk horror and survival horror experience set in “España profunda” during the 1990s. Listen to the word of God and step into a grim and fragmented story, where religion, violence, and silence impose their own laws. Explore a rural, decaying, and oppressive environment as you uncover what lies behind a facade built upon silence and faith. Survive. Resources are limited, and sinister presences will force you to decide when to confront danger, when to hide, and when to flee. Hide. Use stealth and avoid the gaze of whatever lurks around you. Some entities can break you simply by their presence. Pray. Do not lose your faith. Hold on to it to stay on the path. And do not question the word.
