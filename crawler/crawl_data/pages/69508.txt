Starting with a handful of biplanes and a dream, build a globe-spanning airline across five historical eras — from the propeller age of 1930 to the Green Frontier of sustainable flight, with open-ended late-game play that reaches 2095. Open routes, buy and lease aircraft, configure cabin classes, invest in airports, hire executives, and crush your rivals — all while history unfolds around you. SkyChart: Airline Executive is a deep management simulation inspired by the classic Aerobiz series, rebuilt from the ground up with modern design, a retro-neon aesthetic, and systems deep enough to lose a weekend in. AT A GLANCE 5 historical eras from 1930 to the Green Frontier (2025–2040), plus open-ended late-game play reaching 2095 — each era unlocks by mastering the previous 533 real-world cities across 7 regions with population-driven demand 201 aircraft from the DC-3 to modern widebodies and speculative hydrogen, electric, and supersonic designs 4 cabin classes (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First) with per-class demand and era-gated unlocks Up to 35 AI rival airlines with 4 distinct strategy archetypes (Budget, Regional, Legacy, Premium) 60+ scripted historical events , reshaped every playthrough — Great Depression, WWII, deregulation, 1973 oil crisis, 9/11, COVID-19 Live weather system with storm cells, pressure fronts, jet streams, and route re-planning 145 achievements spanning operations, finance, history, and diplomacy Full livery editor , 8 UI themes, photo mode, and monthly Casey Cup leaderboards 33-chapter interactive tutorial hosted by Casey the Macaw, chief flight inspector 29 languages (store-page translations rolling out EA → 1.0) Full Steam Deck support with Steam Input API and automatic glyph detection FIVE ERAS OF AVIATION Play through five distinct chapters of airline history, each with unique aircraft, economics, and challenges: Dawn of Flight (1930–1950) — Biplanes, flying boats, and the shadow of World War II. Connect the continents before the world tears itself apart. The Jet Age (1955–1975) — The Bering 707 rewrites everything. Dominate the transatlantic corridor and race your rivals across the globe. The Modern Era (1980–2000) — Deregulation, oil crises, and cutthroat competition. Only the efficient survive. Supersonic Future (2000–2020) — Ultra-long-haul routes, mega-hubs, and the A380 age. Build an empire that spans every continent — then survive COVID. The Green Frontier (2025–2040) — Sustainable aviation arrives. Hydrogen and electric aircraft, carbon offsets, and the race to decarbonize reshape the economics of every route. Each era must be conquered before the next unlocks. Prove you can survive the prop age before you earn the jets — and when you win the Green Frontier, open-ended late-game play keeps flying all the way to 2095. 533 REAL-WORLD CITIES From New York to Nairobi, Tokyo to Timbuktu — 533 cities across 7 world regions with real airport locations, population-driven demand, tourist appeal, and regional diplomacy that gates your expansion. Invest in hotels, theme parks, and infrastructure to boost demand. Upgrade airport hubs to dominate entire regions. 201 AIRCRAFT Fly the planes that made history — and the ones that will. 201 aircraft span the DC-3, Pacifica Skystar, Bering 707, 747, Concordia, Skybus A380, Bering 787, and on into speculative hydrogen, electric, and supersonic designs reaching 2095 — each with stats, retirement dates, and operating costs. Aircraft age realistically: old planes cost more to run, draw fewer passengers, and depreciate on a real curve. Order new-build aircraft on a realistic 1-9 month delivery backlog, lease for instant service, or store surplus frames in your hangar. CABIN CLASS CONFIGURATION Configure every route's seat mix: Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First. Higher classes occupy more cabin space but earn 1.6×-4× the fare. Class availability is era-gated — First Class reappears in 1955, Business in 1960, Premium Economy in 1992 — so strategy shifts as decades pass. Six one-click templates (All Economy, Budget, Regional, Legacy, Premium, Luxury) or hand-tune per route with the stepper UI. AI rivals pick strategies too and stick to them. LIVE WEATHER SYSTEM A Ventusky-style weather map with animated storm cells (tropical round-with-eye, extratropical comma-heads), jet streams, pressure isobars, and cloud shading. Storms drift on prevailing winds — westerlies mid-latitude, trade easterlies in the tropics — and seed from real IBTrACS cyclone data. Route paths dynamically deviate around severe weather at FAA-style distances, and your airplanes' animated tracks update every 8 seconds as storms move. BUY, LEASE, OR SCRAP Every aircraft decision matters. Buy planes outright and build equity, or lease them to preserve cash with flexible 1-year, 3-year, or 5-year terms. Store surplus aircraft in your hangar for reuse. Refurbish aging fleets to extend their service life. Sell at depreciated market value when it's time to move on. Your rivals make these same calculations. OUTSMART YOUR RIVALS Up to 35 AI-controlled airlines compete for the same routes, slots, and passengers. Each picks one of four cabin strategies — Budget (all-economy), Regional (light premium), Legacy (full four-class), or Premium (first-heavy) — and sticks to it, so the competitive landscape feels distinct. They price dynamically, expand into your territory, upgrade their fleets, and invest in hubs. Once per era, you can buy out a rival entirely — absorbing their routes, slots, and infrastructure in one stroke. Ghost carriers from your past campaigns can even return to haunt the map. HISTORY HAPPENS 60+ scripted historical events and World Mega-Events reshape the playing field, and a WorldSeed system perturbs them so no two campaigns play out the same. The Great Depression tanks demand. WWII closes entire regions. The 1973 oil crisis doubles fuel prices overnight. 9/11 grounds the industry. COVID-19 kills 93% of passenger demand (but cargo barely flinches). Earthquakes destroy airports. Volcanic ash clouds close European airspace. You adapt or you fold. DEEP ECONOMICS Every route's profit is calculated monthly from population, regional economics, seasonal demand, your reputation, competitor pricing, hub bonuses, diplomatic relations, fuel costs, aircraft condition, cabin mix, transfer passenger flows, and executive team bonuses. The Strategic Advisor scans all 141,778 possible city pairs (threaded, non-blocking) to find your best opportunities. Quarterly reports track every dollar. THREE GAME MODES Passengers — Fill seats. Maximize yield per passenger. The classic airline business. Cargo — Move freight. Win on price and reliability. Cargo barely flinches during pandemics. Combined — Passengers in the cabin, freight in the belly. Two revenue streams, one fleet. Each mode unlocks after mastering the previous one. FIVE DIFFICULTY LEVELS From Glider (generous capital, slow rivals) to Supersonic (lean budget, aggressive AI, punishing interest rates). Five finely tuned tiers so new players can learn the ropes and veterans can suffer. SCENARIOS & REALISM MODE Beyond the campaign, take on 12 hand-crafted scenarios with specific goals and starting conditions — Hub vs Point-to-Point, the A380 Launch, the Fuel Crisis, COVID Recovery, the Asian Tiger Boom, the Emirates Era, and more, plus seasonal challenges. Flip on optional Realism Mode for country-by-country licensing, 7th-freedom and cabotage gates, an MRO parts inventory, fuel hedging, and a COVID recovery arc — a harder, more grounded simulation for veterans. SANDBOX MODE Completed your first era? Unlock Sandbox. No bankruptcy. No era limits. No time pressure. Pick a starting decade and build forever. MAKE IT YOURS Design your airline's identity with a full livery editor — base, accent, and trim colors, zone overrides, and four patterns (solid, stripe, swoop, split) that render on every aircraft on the map. AI rivals get procedurally generated liveries too. Reskin the whole game with 8 built-in UI themes (Sunset, Midnight Cockpit, Daylight, Forest Cabin, Pure Charts B&W, Aurora Borealis, Tropical Routes, Film Noir), and capture your network in Photo Mode with per-era recap cards. A CAREER THAT CARRIES OVER Win an era and bank Legacy Points toward permanent unlocks. Your operator earns cross-campaign XP, founders get generated bios and a career-timeline panel, and monthly Casey Cup leaderboards auto-submit your best runs. A pull-only community events feed keeps the world fresh between updates. YOUR COCKPIT Strategic Advisor — AI-powered route recommendations, hub analysis, worst-route alerts Route Manager — Sort, filter, suspend, sell, or upgrade any route. Handles 2,000+ routes smoothly Fleet Overview — Active fleet, hangar inventory, and lease management in one panel City Intelligence — Per-city demand, slot availability, investment ROI, competitor presence Competitive Intel — Track rival expansion and retreat across every region Quarterly Reports — Revenue, costs, market share, and 8-quarter trend charts Diplomacy Panel — Improve regional relations through donations and executive hires Strategy Planner — Queue up your next moves with budget tracking and one-click execution Cabin Configuration Panel — Per-route seat mix with era-gated class unlocks and live revenue delta vs all-economy Weather Map Overlay — Storm cells, jet streams, pressure isobars, and cloud layers 145 Achievements — Milestones across operations, finance, technology, history, cabin strategy, and diplomacy A MAP WORTH STARING AT A Mercator projection world map with great-circle flight paths, animated aircraft, weather radar, a day/night terminator, and demand heatmaps. Three visual styles: Retro Dark (neon-on-black default), Satellite (real-world imagery), and Clean Vector . Zoom into any region for OpenStreetMap-style hi-detail tiles. Routes auto-fade as your network grows so even 2,000+ routes stay readable. ERA SOUNDTRACK 44 tracks spanning the 1930s through the 2020s shift dynamically as you advance through the decades. Crossfading transitions, achievement fanfares, and a built-in music player with support for local files and Spotify integration. LEARN FROM CASEY THE MACAW Your chief flight inspector is a macaw with strong opinions. Casey narrates a 33-chapter interactive tutorial — Quick Tour and Full Course tracks — covering everything from opening your first route to configuring cabin classes, managing weather diversions, running a legacy carrier, and mastering the Green Frontier sustainability era. Every in-game changelog entry is written in Casey's voice — he inspects rivets, files paperwork, and takes credit for every bug fix.
