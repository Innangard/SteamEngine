Order your performers before the battle begins, building synergies and powerful combos. Spend gold to hire new performers after each battle, building your troupe's strength. Performers are either Good or Evil. Hire the former to help you out, and the latter to stand as enemy antagonists. Set the stage, and take on the lead role! Battle your way through each stage earning tips from your fans, and put on a show they'll remember. Collect items and hire performers to build a strategy all your own. Curtain Call boasts over 100 Good and Evil performers along with 100 different items, with a range of synergies between them for nigh-on infinite possibilities. Withdraw from your savings, sell performers, refresh or lock in shop offerings for next time around... Push your strategy to the limit as you micro-manage your way to victory. Choose from three leads to play as, each with exclusive items and stratagems of their own for you to explore. Unlock their passives, grow stronger with each run, and take on 10 difficulty levels to push yourself to the limit. Building on the art of Noobs Want to Live, Curtain Call's art design draws on classical history and culture to create an enchanting caricature style. It'll keep a smile on your face, right through to the final act!
