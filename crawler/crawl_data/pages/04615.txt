The scope and priorities may change during development.“ Was ist der derzeitige Stand der Early Access-Version? „The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required.“ Wird dieses Spiel während und nach Early Access unterschiedlich viel kosten? „We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release.
