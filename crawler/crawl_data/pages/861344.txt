This loco pack features the BR218 diesel-hydraulic locomotive, the Bnrdzf driving trailer and appropriate coach rolling stock to set up authentic regional passenger services on your German routes. The loco, driving trailer and coaches have all been recreated in detail for Train Simulator Classic. They feature realistic controls, braking behaviour and safety systems. Using the instrument panels in the cab you can operate PZB Advizory, SiFa Vigilance, instrument / cab lights, windscreen wipers, VR EBuLa system and more. The BR 218 & Wittenberger Regional Express comes with new scenarios for the Pegnitztalbahn route. Run a regional service into the historic city of Nürnberg and take passengers for a day out to the legendary Bayreuth Festival. Add the BR218 & Wittenberger Regional Express to your German collection for Train Simulator Classic. Included Scenarios Three standard scenarios are included for Pegnitztalbahn: Nürnberg - Bayreuth.
