Ler mais Jogar FrontWars Grátis para Jogar Jogar Ver Central Comunitária Comunica a existência de bugs e deixa o teu feedback sobre este jogo nos fóruns Ver todas as discussões Acerca deste jogo FrontWars is a free-to-play real-time strategy game where hundreds of players fight over one shared world map. Start with a single patch of land, grow it into an empire and outplay everyone else, in matches that take minutes, not hours. No pay-to-win, ever. Conquer the world Every match starts the same way: a tiny piece of land and a map full of rivals. Expand into empty land, squeeze your weaker neighbours, and race to become the biggest nation on the map before anyone else does. Build an empire Cities, ports, factories and railroads: trains carry goods between your cities and pay you gold on every delivery. Trade ships sail between your ports and your partners'. Defense posts, SAM launchers and warships keep it all standing. Ally, betray, conquer Form alliances, send gold and troops to friends, or embargo your rivals. Fight on every front: land offensives, naval landings and warship battles. When diplomacy fails, missile silos launch atom bombs, hydrogen bombs and MIRVs that wipe whole nations off the map. Play your way Classic for a calmer game with time to build, Fast for a quicker economy, and Chaos for random twists like No Nukes or Instant Build. Free-for-all, team games (Duos, Trios, Quads) and Humans vs Nations. 29 maps, from Europe and a Giant World Map to Mars, Pluto and Pangaea, plus community maps made in the built-in map editor. Private lobbies for you and your friends, with your own rules. Single player against AI nations on any map, even offline. Watch it back Every match has a replay: pick the speed, rewind, or jump to any moment. Play anywhere Steam players share the same servers as the web version, so there's always a match. Available in 30+ languages. Steam and Discord show your friends what you're playing, and let them jump into your public lobby. Do you have what it takes to rule the world?
