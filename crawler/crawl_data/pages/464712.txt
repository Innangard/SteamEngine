View Community Hub About This Game Vending Machine Business Simulator is a simulation game where you start with very little money in your pocket and build a vending empire that spreads across the city. Work for others and once you have the necessary experience and money, start your own business. Manage Your Machines: Purchase, place, and maintain vending machines across different locations. Keep track of inventory and ensure machines are stocked and operational. Optimize Profits: Follow local events, adjust pricing strategies, and optimize product offerings to maximize revenue and profitability. Expand Your Empire: Unlock new locations and unlock new type of machines to attract more customers and increase sales. Dynamic Challenges: Overcome challenges such as machine malfunctions, aggressive customers, changing consumer trends, and economic changes. Realistic Simulation: Experience a realistic simulation of the vending machine business, including factors like machine maintenance, customer preferences.
