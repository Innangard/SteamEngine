Once plugged, you'll only be able to move within the range of your cable! Space is plunged into darkness, activate the spacesockets to clear a level and travel to the next sector. Choose between 10+ different ships with different gameplay modes! Blast your mega-laser! Form an army of rescued stranded ships that will fight alongside you! Destroy the stages and defeat a large variety of enemies! Encounter dangerous and challenging BOSSES after each galaxy in your quest. Holding the breach, dodging enemy bullets, and piloting through narrow hallways shouldn't be a problem, BUT just in case... Your ship is equipped with a futuristic Slow-Mo System that you can use almost whenever you want! Luckily for you, it recharges by collecting energy from dead enemies! Pick up BATTERIES and discover +250 combinations of bullet modifiers! Get CHIPS of temporary upgrades by destroying enemies! Choose between more than 40 upgrades for your ship. Optimize charge times, improve your firepower, get more cable length... Become invisible to enemies. And more!
