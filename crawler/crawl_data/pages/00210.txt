AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: The main Campaign story, characters, and dialogue were written by a team of human writers and voiced in English by human actors. AI tools were used to localize text and voice overs. The optional Free Ride mode includes AI-generated passenger missions and localized dialogue. Free Ride mode is separate from the main Campaign and can be skipped by players who prefer to experience only human-written narrative content. Both game modes include radio stations featuring a mix of human-made music and AI-generated music. Stations containing AI-generated music are clearly labeled as such in the game.
