Welcome to The Overflowing Flask , a peculiar little potion shop nestled in a town at the very edge of the world. Its owner, an eccentric alchemist, has spent a lifetime traveling across distant lands, collecting every potion, elixir, tonic, and mysterious bottled concoction they could get their hands on. After years of adventures, they've finally decided to settle down and open a shop to share their extraordinary collection with the world. There's just one tiny problem... They've collected over 4,000 potions. And not a single one has been put away. With the grand opening fast approaching, the alchemist needs your help turning this overwhelming mountain of magical mixtures into something resembling a proper potion shop. But unlike the other shops you've visited, this one has a little more freedom. Every shelf can hold any type of potion, leaving you to decide how everything should be organized. Sort them by color, group them by appearance, create beautiful displays, or embrace the absolute chaos of thousands of colorful bottles. The choice is entirely yours. What's Included: One enormous new map featuring over 4,000 potions to organize. Complete organizational freedom! Every shelf accepts every type of potion. A peculiar new shop filled with an eccentric alchemist's lifetime of discoveries. A mountain of magical potions for those who just can't get enough of organizing potions. Play at your own pace. Take your time, relax, and enjoy the satisfaction of putting thousands of potions exactly where you want them. Whether you're a master organizer, a potion enthusiast, or someone who once complained that Shelves and Sorcery had too many potions, this one's for you.
