Uncover the story of humankind's ancestry on your expedition for occult knowledge. You are a driver, plotting river crossings and navigating bogs and rocky slopes to push exploration further and deeper. You are also a pathfinder, a witness, a courier of forgotten truths, and a trespasser in a place where remnants of humanity’s original sin still fester. You carry more than supplies: survival, knowledge, and perhaps the key to awakening something that should remain buried. Features: Survival Logistics - Plan routes, manage fuel, and complete deliveries across treacherous terrain. Artifacts & Cargo - Keep outposts running and transport unique finds, each a fragment of humanity’s forgotten hunt for the divine prey. On-Foot Exploration - Leave the safety of your truck to interact with your environment and uncover ancient secrets. Hand-Crafted Open World - Explore a vast, uncharted land from its coastline to the heart of the island, warped by dreams of god-husks. Upgrade for the Unknown - Upgrade and adapt your vehicle to survive deeper incursions into corrupted lands. Document the Uncanny - Use specialized tools to identify, recover, and record artifacts born of godly decay.
