Guide a leek-haired trader through themed arenas, dodge swarming enemies, collect experience and build a new combination of weapons and upgrades on every run. Build your portfolio Choose upgrades as you level up, evolve weapons and adapt your build to rising pressure. Between runs, unlock more traders and invest in permanent improvements. Push further Take on distinct market arenas and their escalating threats. Bear Mode and Endless Mode give seasoned traders new reasons to keep moving.
