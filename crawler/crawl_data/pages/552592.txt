The crown's authority is crumbling, noble houses pursue their own ambitions, and once-peaceful roads have become the hunting grounds of bandits and creatures best left undisturbed. Meanwhile, dark forces are stirring, and rumours of necromancy are spreading across the land. King's Bounty II is a single-player fantasy RPG that combines third-person exploration with turn-based tactical combat. Journey through the world of Antara, uncover the stories of its inhabitants, and build an army of warriors and fantastic creatures. Your adventures take you across the kingdom of Nostria, but when battle calls, it's your skills as a commander that will be put to the test. A WORLD BEYOND THE BATTLEFIELD Travel through the towns, villages, forests, and forgotten corners of Nostria. Meet its inhabitants, investigate strange occurrences, uncover secrets, and take on quests that reveal the many sides of a kingdom struggling to hold itself together. Some people will seek your help. Others will try to use you for their own ends. And occasionally, what appears to be a simple errand may lead you somewhere entirely unexpected. UNLIKELY HEROES Choose from three unlikely heroes: Aivar, a disgraced knight turned mercenary seeking a second chance; Katharine, a noble-born mage determined to reclaim her stolen inheritance; and Elisa, a peasant girl whose newfound magical powers have inspired her to bring peace to a war-torn land. Each comes to Nostria with their own ambitions, convictions, and reasons to get involved in the kingdom's troubles. As your adventure unfolds, you'll face decisions that put your convictions to the test. The Ideal system reflects your character's beliefs and influences how certain quests and situations can be approached. Develop your hero's talents, acquire new equipment, and decide what kind of leader you want to become. YOUR ARMY? YOUR STRATEGY! A sword may be enough for an adventurer. A general needs considerably more. Recruit and command an army drawn from the many factions and creatures of Antara. From disciplined soldiers and skilled archers to spellcasters and fearsome beasts, every unit brings something different to your ranks. Experiment with different combinations, discover how your troops complement one another, and build a force suited to your preferred tactics. BATTLEFIELD IS YOUR WEAPON Take command of your troops on hex-based battlefields shaped by the world around you. Hills, obstacles, narrow passages, and differences in elevation all influence how a battle unfolds. Position your units carefully, exploit enemy weaknesses, combine their abilities, and use the terrain to your advantage. An army that seems unbeatable in an open field may find itself at a disadvantage when forced to fight through a narrow passage. Ьake the most of your troops, and learn from every victory…and defeat. FEATURES Two Ways to Experience One Adventure Explore the world of Antara in real time, uncover its secrets, and embark on quests before taking command of your army in tactical turn-based battles. A Fantasy World Worth Exploring Venture through bustling towns, ancient castles, mysterious ruins, and untamed wilderness, discovering hidden treasures, intriguing characters, and unexpected adventures. Three Heroes, Three Paths Play as Aivar, a battle-hardened warrior; Katharine, a noble-born mage; or Elisa, an aspiring paladin. Each brings their own story, abilities, and strengths to the adventure. An Army to Call Your Own Recruit knights, spellcasters, undead, and mythical creatures. Discover powerful unit combinations, strengthen your veterans, and build a formidable fighting force. Make the Battlefield Work for You Turn hills, obstacles, and narrow passages into tactical advantages. Every encounter challenges you to outthink your enemies, not simply overpower them. Shape Your Hero's Convictions Follow the Ideals of Order, Anarchy, Power, and Finesse. Your choices influence character development, unlock new talents, and open different approaches to quests. Master the Art of War Combine magic, unit abilities, and clever positioning to overcome challenging encounters. Keep your troops' morale high and turn a well-planned strategy into victory.
