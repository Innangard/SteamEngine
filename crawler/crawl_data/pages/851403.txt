This pack features new crafted scenarios that include a variety of passenger and freight experiences. Included are 6 scenarios for the Chatham Main Line – London-Gillingham route, and an additional 4 scenarios for the Chatham Main & Medway Valley Lines route. Adding a unique new challenge to this popular line, these scenarios include some traffic heavy services for you to contend with. Try to keep to time as you transport a freight load out of London with ‘A Factory Tonne’. Experience a congested approach to Victoria with ‘Running On Empty’, which features 15 AI services. Or follow behind stopping services with the ‘Strood In Line’ scenarios. Each of these features plenty of stop/start manoeuvres as you battle against busy traffic. Along with these you will rescue a failed train, drive through the night with a freight delivery, drive an express service across the full route and more. Enjoy re-discovering Chatham & Medway Valley with this new Suburban Glasgow scenario pack.
