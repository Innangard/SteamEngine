View Community Hub About This Game CAPTURED 2 is an anomaly horror game with a survival horror twist. Escape an endless hallway by capturing anomalies on camera and surviving deadly entities. CAPTURE ANOMALIES Emily, a teenage girl who goes missing during a series of unexplained disappearances, finds herself trapped in an endless hallway within her own apartment, slowly losing her memory. Strange anomalies begin to appear, and by capturing them, she catches glimpses of her forgotten past. SURVIVE ENTITIES Deadly entities, taking the form of Emily's childhood toys, roam the loops to stop her from uncovering the truth. Use your flashlight, camera, and the very doors around you to survive each entity’s unique mechanics. IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE Inspired by analog and found footage horror movies such as Skinamarink , liminal spaces, and early 2000s supernatural horror, CAPTURED 2 offers an eerie atmosphere and unsettling sound design. Experience the horror through the lens of a nostalgic aesthetic, with realistic graphics and immersive camera movement. FEATURES Experience isolation and the eerie feeling of being home alone in the late 90s. Explore a procedurally generated hallway filled with a chilling atmosphere. Capture strange anomalies using your camera and identify their type. Survive encounters with deadly monsters, each with unique traits and mechanics. Immerse yourself in an analog horror experience featuring realistic graphics, a VHS 90s aesthetic, a dynamic camera system, and immersive flashlight movement.
