View Community Hub About This Game Overcome Your Fears - is a psychological atmospheric horror game that immerses you in a world of the unknown. In this game you are exploring the story of 19-year-old Steve Jackson, who takes a job house-sitting for friends while they are on vacation. What starts as a simple favor quickly turns into a chilling experience as unsettling events unfold in the quiet town. Little does Steve know, that these days will reveal dark secrets, and he must make choices that will change everything. The Gameplay This game primarily features a first-person narrative walking simulator with exploration and interaction mechanics. Key Features Atmospheric environment and realistic graphics. Psychological horror elements with unexpected twists. VHS film aesthetic. Interactive dialogues with NPCs. Player voice activity Unique sound effects that create tension.
