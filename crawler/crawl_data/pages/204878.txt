Freya Fellow has been murdered. The lead suspect? A shadowy figure found looming over her body, wielding a knife. The problem? That suspect... is just a painting. Investigate the entirety of Tangle Tower, from the bizarre and eerie gardens, to the cold and ominous crime scene at the very top. Will you be able to uncover the secrets of Tangle Tower? • A fully voiced and animated cast of characters • Atmospheric original orchestral soundtrack • Beautiful digitally painted environments to explore • Unlockable concept art gallery with every character, clue and location System Requirements Windows macOS Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 or later Processor: Intel Core i3 or equivalent Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: Intel HD Graphics Storage: 550 MB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
