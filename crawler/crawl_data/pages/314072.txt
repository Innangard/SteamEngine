Experience Elizabeth's story as you try to escape the cruel schemes of H. H. Holmes. WELCOME TO THE WORLD'S FAIR HOTEL Chicago, 1893. The World's Columbian Exposition is in full swing, a dazzling beacon of human progress. Just blocks away stands a magnificent new hotel, promising luxury and convenience to young travelers. You are one of them. Experience the story of Elizabeth, a young secretary hoping to start a new life in the big city. But the man who welcomed you, Dr. H.H. Holmes is not a hotelier, he is a madman. And his building is not a sanctuary, it is a machine designed to kill for profit. " I was born with the devil in me. I could not help the fact that I was a murderer, no more than the poet can help the inspiration to sing... " — H. H. Holmes Wake up to your worst nightmare. Behind soundproof walls and doors that lead to nowhere, the dark story about America’s first serial killer awaits you. KEY FEATURES Unique puzzles: Custom-designed logic mechanics tailored to each room. Escape Room Mechanics: Interact with objects and use physics-based logic to uncover hidden keys and pathways. True-Crime Story: Search for hidden documents and clues to escape Holmes' grim operations. Stealth & Time Management: Outsmart Holmes by hiding in the shadows and acting fast under deadly time limits. Authentic PSX Horror: Immerse yourself in a gritty, low-poly recreation of the Murder Castle in 1893, Chicago.
