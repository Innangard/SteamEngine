View Community Hub About This Content Taking the Satisfactory gameplay to new depths. Core Values is the first paid DLC for Satisfactory and a major expansion. Pioneers will reach into the crust of MASSAGE 2(A-B)b in a different setting with new resources, flora, fauna and more as you develop an underground factory network with new production lines and buildings, uncovering more about the world the deeper you go as you Construct, Automate, Explore & Exploit while continuing to uphold FICSIT's Core Values.
