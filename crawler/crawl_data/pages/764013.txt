View Community Hub About This Content Explore New Horizons with The Backwood (Free DLC) This new map for Medieval Dynasty offers you an untamed and uninhabited terrain that will become your canvas for creating a village of your dreams. Thanks to the brand new Creative Mode, you won't be stopped by mundane obstacles such as a lack of resources or an unfavourable season. The Backwood is fully yours to shape! No limits, no lore—just pure, expressive creation. This massive, untouched landscape designed specifically for free spirits and true sandbox lovers. Unlike our standard maps, The Backwood is a "tabula rasa"—a clean slate. You are bound by no rules but your own! Whether you want to test a village design for your main playthrough or just have some silly fun, this is your playground. 100% wilderness, 0% distractions. Play solo or invite some friends for a completely new co-op experience! Experiment together without the pressure of survival. Redefine teamwork, break the mold, and build your legacy—or challenge each other in countless new ways limited only by your imagination. Unlock necessary schemes and skills faster than ever. Alter the time of day and weather to suit your needs and many, many more… Hop on and try them all!
