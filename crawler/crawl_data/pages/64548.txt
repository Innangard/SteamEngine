Ver todas las discusiones Acerca de este juego You've been hired by Big Auto to unleash the Freedom of Car Dependency on unsuspecting townsfolk! Design the slickest parking lots ever seen, then convince citizens they absolutely need them. Spread glossy pro-car propaganda, outsmart those pesky anti-petrol protesters, and transform quiet neighborhoods into your parking empire! It's time to drive up car sales and oil consumption! For profit.. but mostly for freedom! Features Real-time simulation with hundreds of vehicles and (bald) citizens. Buy land, demolish homes, and build the parking lots of your dreams: Endless flat seas of asphalt or build into the air using the 3D construction tools. Read anyone's thoughts : Are they embracing cars yet, or annoyed by your ever-increasing parking fees and the sight of your oil drills? Use pro-car propaganda to turn everyone into a carbrain. Place shiny statues, billboard posters, and blast catchy slogans from megaphones. Keep the people somewhat happy : If traffic is a mess, distract them with a ride on your glorious Ferris Wheel for cars! Control the anti-car protests before they shatter your reputation. Track everything in the Stat-o-matic : A true tycoon loves watching numbers go up. Drill for oil . A full tank is a full heart. Rival public transport : You don't want trains or walkable towns, you want every space flooded with cars! Car Park Capital fuses strategy and simulation with satire, offering social commentary on car dependency and urban development. Become a real tycoon and start paving your automotive paradise!
