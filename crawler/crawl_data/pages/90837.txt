Over the course of the story-driven single player campaign, you’ll discover new environments, meet a variety of characters, collect rare treasures and uncover the many secrets hidden at the heart of Botworld.  But make sure you have a strong team of bots, because you never know what's around the corner. ENGAGE IN TACTICAL AUTO-BATTLES Outsmart your foes in a unique tactical battle system. Your bots will automatically leap, charge, stun or blast around the arena with advanced AI. Choose to get your hands dirty and support them by manually deploying abilities like Frost Missile or Firewall at the perfect time - or give the AI complete control and transform the game into a robust auto-battler. Each bot has unique abilities and a powerful Ultimate - combining these with the powerful player abilities is the key to becoming a seasoned Botmaster. Every victory earns your bots experience points to unlock advanced new AI and further increase your tactical options, which you can bring into the online PvP arena and rise up the worldwide leaderboard. CUSTOMISE YOUR BOT TEAM Discover, build and collect rare and powerful bots to form the ultimate team. Search the world to discover new bot blueprints, then collect the rare scrap to build and upgrade your favourites. Customise their powers and abilities as they level up and get stronger. Equip yourself with powerful booster chips to adjust your bots’ strengths and change the course of a battle, and deploy unique gadgets while exploring like Bombardment or Invisibility to get the upper hand on the bandits and wild bots that inhabit Botworld. CHOOSE YOUR CHARACTER Play as one of 5 animals: cats, dogs, buffalo, rats and lizards. Choose a unique look to add your own personality to the character, and complete missions to unlock new cosmetic items like hats and outfits. Find and defeat ultra-rare bots in the open world to collect their alternate skins and customise your own bots’ looks.
