View Community Hub About This Game Features: ·   Simplified RTS + Tribute to Classics ·   Deep Strategy and Independent Controls ·   Unique Boss Fights ❤️Local Co-op As a die-hard RTS fan, I know the complex controls can be daunting for some players. That's why I aimed to create an RTS game that's easy to control yet rich in strategy and action. Here, you control just one mech. It can walk, shoot, kill insects, gather resources, build, and summon. This game is my passion project, and I hope to share that passion with more players. Pre-Battle Phase: Strategize and set up your defenses at your own pace. Combat Phase: Execute your strategy and unleash your firepower. Whether it's resource gathering, base building, or attacking, this game keeps you thinking and acting. This isn't a Roguelike game. It progresses through levels, each ending with a unique boss fight. Defeat the boss to unlock new items and expand your build options. I'm not a fan of reading long descriptions, so I've simplified each module for easy understanding. Plus, you can team up with a friend to fight off relentless alien swarms! One player handles ground forces, the other takes to the skies, complementing each other. In the distant future, humanity faces an unprecedented energy crisis. A new energy source, "Force", discovered in outer space, becomes a coveted treasure. The battle for "Force" leads to a prolonged war between humans and vicious alien swarms. In the war’s final stages, both sides have mastered each other’s genetic sequences, creating many half-human, half-alien warriors. On the battlefield, we might see our mutated former comrades. War devours countless lives, and we seem to have forgotten who our real enemy is.
