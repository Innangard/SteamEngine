Presented in an omnibus format, each chapter unfolds a unique event set in a richly detailed fantasy world. Verify quest completion At the break of dawn, the guild receptionist gets to work — checking every detail to see if the adventurers did their job right. Well, ordinary people don't really know much about monsters, after all. Since most clients aren’t familiar with monsters, it’s up to the receptionist to deduce the target using only the details in the quest. Then comes the real work — analyzing loot, consulting bestiaries, checking maps and licenses — all to ensure the adventurer did their job right. Interaction with Adventurers To make accurate judgments, the receptionist can ask adventurers questions — about the loot, about the monsters, and sometimes about the people who’ve gone missing. Beyond that, enjoy light-hearted conversations and listen to tales of their lives and the many stories scattered across the continent of Eustace. The choice is always yours If you stick to the rules and fulfill your duties as a receptionist, the guild should run smoothly. But what if an adventurer comes to you with circumstances you simply can’t ignore? Or what if you come across a piece of loot you desperately want? The choice is yours. Just remember… that freedom is yours only as long as the guild keeps running smoothly. The Guild Tavern When night falls, the guild tavern opens its doors. Adventurers drop by to unwind after a long day, bringing with them tales of all kinds — from the ordinary to the extraordinary, and perhaps even a story of love or two. Cook up “monster cuisine” using ingredients from defeated beasts, and sit back as you listen to the many voices of the world. Monster cuisine can be dangerous! Cooking with monster loot can be quite tricky. Some ingredients are excessively sticky, like slimes, while others, like Giant Bees, contain toxins. That’s why you need to understand the properties of each ingredient and combine them carefully. The dish you end up with will change depending on which ingredients you use together. Sounds too difficult? Don’t worry. “Kaul, the Magical Cooking Pot” will be there to help! The Thirst and Trials of Adventurers An elf who left the Forest of the World Tree after losing the ability to use mana.  A dwarf who became an adventurer despite being a blacksmith — because he fears fire. Every adventurer carries their own scars, their own quiet struggles.  These are stories of those who simply long for an ordinary day… and of the strength it takes to overcome what lies beyond that thirst. Would you care to listen?
