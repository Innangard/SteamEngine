View Community Hub About This Game Moving Simulator is a small, cozy game with around 4 hours of gameplay. Moving Simulator puts you in charge of your very own moving business! Experience the thrill and challenges of managing house relocations—from packing boxes and disassembling furniture to carefully loading items onto trucks. Each move brings new surprises, humorous accidents, and unexpected obstacles, testing your problem-solving skills and patience. Skillfully navigate furniture through doors, hallways, and tight spaces. Precision is key! Pack boxes smartly to optimize space. Organization is your friend! Load your belongings into the truck and hit the road—be careful on sharp turns and bumps! Secure your load with transportation straps to prevent disasters during transit! Key Features: Realistic Moving Mechanics: Pack and secure furniture realistically to avoid damage during transit. Dynamic Environments: Navigate various homes, apartments, and mansions with unique layouts and quirky residents. Upgrade Your Business: Earn money from successful moves, expand your fleet and improve equipment to tackle bigger contracts. Creative Solutions: Encounter puzzles and logistical challenges that encourage creative thinking. Chaos & Comedy: Enjoy hilarious moments and catastrophic mishaps that keep the job fresh, fun, and unpredictably funny. 8 diverse missions and around 4 hours of moving-day gameplay chaos. Ready to turn moving day chaos into your next big career move? Pack your gloves, strap in, and embrace the chaos in Moving Simulator!
