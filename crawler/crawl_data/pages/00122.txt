View Community Hub FULL RELEASE PREVIEW About This Game Worst Class Ever is an asymmetric party game set in a chaotic classroom, supporting up to 6 players! One player takes on the role of the strict teacher, while the other five play as mischievous students. The troublemakers must blend in among dozens of NPC classmates and secretly pull off pranks like gobbling snacks, passing notes, and playing on their phones... all without getting caught! The teacher, armed with sharp eyes and a no-nonsense attitude, has to spot every last wannabe delinquent hiding in the crowd and restore perfect order to the classroom. What if everyday school life turned into pure classroom chaos? Play as a mischievous student, gobbling snacks in secret and pulling pranks right under the teacher's watchful nose. Or take on the role of the strict teacher, activate sharp-eyed detection mode, and hunt down every last troublemaker hiding in the crowd. Every match is a ridiculous, high-energy showdown between teacher and students. Step into the shoes of a natural-born troublemaker. Blend in, evade the teacher's attention, and go wild the second they turn their back. Your mission? Prank until there's no tomorrow and create pure classroom chaos. Be bold and do your worst... but above all, don't get caught. That constant tension mixed with the pure satisfaction of a successful prank? Nothing hits quite like it. "You're the worst class I've ever taught!" Step into the role of the strict teacher. Use your sharp observation skills to catch every tiny slip: forced movements, panicked glances, or anything that feels off. Nothing escapes your eagle eyes. Identify the mischief makers, shut down their pranks, and restore absolute order. There's nothing quite like the feeling of singling the little troublemakers out. Bright daytime classrooms, late-night study halls, computer labs packed with screens… Multiple maps rotate in, each with its own unique hideouts, exclusive prank tasks, and interactive mechanics. Every classroom brings fresh hiding strategies and brand-new ways to cause trouble. Different settings mean constantly changing matches so that no two games ever feel the same. Smoke bombs, tranquilizer guns, drones… A whole arsenal of wild and creative items that take the pranks and counterplay to the next level! Mischievous students can unlock all kinds of chaotic tools to quietly set traps, create mayhem, and mess with the teacher's vision... anything to evade detection. Teachers get their own exclusive items to quickly lock onto students in hiding, see through disguises, and uncover every trick. Mix and match freely for flexible, ever-changing gameplay, or switch on random combinations for nonstop hilarious moments that'll have you in stitches!
