If you are a fan of games like Against the Storm and Frostpunk, how would you fare when transported into an alternate Southern Song world? — You'll need to dynamically adjust general assignments and worker allocations based on the real-time situation, steadily developing your stronghold amidst resource scarcity and environmental challenges. Manage your main city well, with the goal of repelling foreign enemies and commanding the battlefield! Featuring classic "map painting" grand strategy and card-based combat, lead the Great Song to restore its glory! Story-focused players can unlock rich narrative threads, following the plot to explore the grudges and entanglements between factions. Moreover, multiple female characters are available for deep interaction and romance pursuit, unlocking exclusive storylines and a childbirth system, adding a touch of nurturing warmth. Being a city lord requires managing both internal and external affairs—it's tiring work, so how could you lack romantic companions? 7 romanceable characters await your exploration. Built-in random equipment system: each drop hides surprises, aiding your combat breakthrough. An intuitive "color-the-blocks" style campaign map, paired with a comprehensive transport system and outpost management mechanics, lets you experience the thrill of expanding your territory from scratch amidst the contention of the four major factions: Jin, Song, Western Xia, and Dali.
