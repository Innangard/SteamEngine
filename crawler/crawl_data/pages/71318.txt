This genre-bending release spans from foreboding electronica to pounding progressive metal and features all music from the game and then some! A cascade of synthesizers, guitars struggling to stay in tune, untamable drums, complex polyrhythms, a baroque production, the signature Risk of Rain motif and—of course—solos soaring even higher create the perfect chaos that is this frantic game’s underscore. In addition to being your sonic companion during the game, the music in this album is painstakingly curated to work as a standalone musical experience.
