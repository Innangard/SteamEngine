View Community Hub About This Game Dive into the unique world of Sivers — a dynamic RPG-roguelike where every battle and every decision influence your survival! Face waves of monsters, use the building system and powerful spells to hold your ground as long as possible. Game Features: Variety of Spells: Combine magical, physical and passive abilities to adapt to different situations and defeat enemies. Unique Enemies and Bosses: Foes become increasingly challenging, requiring smart strategies and unexpected ability combinations. Building System: Create and upgrade various structures to strengthen your defenses and expand your tactical options. Flexible Progression: Develop your hero, unlock new abilities and upgrade your buildings to survive longer. Secrets and Discoveries: Explore the world, gather resources and unlock hidden opportunities to aid you in battle. Adjustable Difficulty: Choose a challenge level that suits your playstyle — from a relaxed run to a true test of skill. Sivers continues to evolve, adding new features, enemies and mechanics. Fight, build, survive — and find your path to victory!
