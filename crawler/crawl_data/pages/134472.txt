Since the beginning I built this game based on your feedback and I want to continue to do so. I read all your comments and reviews and chances are your ideas change the game. Early Access will help us to continue our work full-time and stay connected with the community to make Small Spaces the best it can be. With over 100k people already having played our demo, we believe the Early Access version will be even more fun and offer enough content for you to be able to spend hours and hours in a creative flow designing unique and beautiful apartments. With more and more to come!” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “8-9 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Our vision is to set a new milestone for interior design games. To achieve that, we plan to add more city themes, more levels, more furniture, more features and more game modes. Here's what we have in mind so far: Sandbox mode: A mode in which you can create your own apartment layouts. Adding this will truly make players limitlessly creative and we want to offer it as an alternative to the objective-focused story mode. 2 new themes: Tiny Houses and Victorian Townhouse Apartments in San Francisco – Each with 3 new levels. 250 new furniture assets featuring furniture packs that will probably be community-voted. (Our current ideas are: Mid-Century-Modern, Contemporary Maximalist and Modern Farmhouse). We want to work on what players say is important to them! Whether it’s quality-of-life features, performance improvements, content ideas or bug fixes. Our focus goes where you point to! ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “ 3 city themes (New York, Berlin, Tokyo) with 3 levels each Over 500 assets featuring different furniture inspired by: Modern Japanese, Scandinavian, Elegant, Contemporary and Antiquities. ” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, the game is available at a lower price during Early Access to thank early supporters!” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community focus is at the core of our development process. My goal is to make a game that puts you into a creative flow without you even noticing. And a lot of the player feedback has been exactly that. So, if you’re looking for a relaxing game to take your mind off stressful thoughts, Small Spaces might be just the creative escape you were looking for :) What can you expect? Shape the space your way Turn the small spaces fully upside down with your creativity! Modular kitchens? Loft apartments? Find smart solutions and design unique spaces that are functional and beautiful. Add indirect lighting, plants and more to decorate and personalize your space down to the smallest detail. Inspired by real places On Early Access start, you’ll design in 3 different locations: New York – raw industrial factories and warehouses turned into gorgeous loft apartments with high ceilings and expansive windows. Berlin – historic apartments with the typical 1900 european charm and beautiful bay windows. Think vintage charm meets quiet elegance. Tokyo – mid-century inspired simplistic apartments with sliding doors and sometimes warm concrete wall finishes. Together, these offer 9 apartments , each with a distinct layout and creative feel. More locations and styles will be added throughout Early Access. Stories that guide Every space has its own objectives, story and people who live there – ranging from a student in a shared flat to a couple sharing their first apartment together. The objectives inspire to design spaces that are not only pretty, but also practical and give you just enough inspiration to get started while not restricting your creativity. Carefully crafted high quality assets The game already features more than 500 furniture and decor items inspired by different styles – including comfy scandinavian beds, unique modern japanese lamps and decoration items from a record player to sewing utensils. I truly care about original art and all of the beautiful illustrations in the game are hand drawn from an illustrator. Walk through your creations in first person Switch to a first-person view to experience your design from the inside – like someone who actually lives there and view your creations as the sunlight shifts from crisp morning to warm golden hour to cozy nighttime with the glow of the indirect lighting you set up. Unlock new content as you play You start out with just a portion of tools and furniture at your hand and unlock new content as you complete objectives. Get new materials from stone terrazzo to various wood types and unlock new tools to build loft apartments, modular kitchens or wall panels. The more you create, the more options you gain. I recommend buying this game if: You play life sims mostly for home design You like relaxing games that spark your creativity You’re a design lover or architecture nerd It might not be for you if: You want to design floor plans from scratch – we’re building a creative mode where you can build your own layouts, but as of now you furnish existing apartments. You’re expecting to import your own models or textures – that kind of freedom isn’t in the game (yet!)
