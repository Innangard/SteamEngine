Plant seeds, care for your cats, and discover over 40 breeds. Decorate your home, train your cats, fulfill orders, and grow your own cat estate. Swing your hoe to till the soil, sow cat seeds, give them plenty of water, and wait for tiny, fluffy lives to emerge. Growing cat sprouts may occasionally need more nutrients, or become gloomy from boredom—mix the right potion, pick up a cat teaser, and play with them for a while. Every day of loving care makes the final harvest even more delightful. Collect breeding recipes, experiment with different combinations, discover over 40 distinctive breeds, and record every new encounter in your Cat Encyclopedia. Once fully grown, cats can enroll in Cat Academy for training and earn higher certifications through their qualities and hard work. Whether you fulfill limited-time orders or keep your graduates by your side, every choice writes a new story. This is more than a farm—it's a home for you and your cats. Plan each space freely with hundreds of furnishings and decorations, pairing everything from wallpaper and flooring to the smallest tabletop ornaments just the way you like. Some items can be placed at multiple angles or feature animated effects, adding a thoughtful touch to every room and making your home feel uniquely yours. Daily life on the island is about far more than planting and harvesting: craft items by hand, mix potions in the workshop to help your cat sprouts grow, or grab a toy and play to your bored cats' hearts' content. Each task comes with its own interactive minigame, adding lighthearted variety to the laid-back farming life and making every act of care more engaging.
