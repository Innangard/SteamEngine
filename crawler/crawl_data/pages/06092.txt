View Community Hub About This Game What if one ball could break the entire board? Pegfinity is a short colorful, pachinko-inspired physics-based incremental where a single shot can spiral into massive chain reactions, exploding scores, and absurd combos. Launch balls, hit pegs, unlock chaotic upgrades, and turn a simple board into a ridiculous score-chasing machine. You are always one lucky bounce away from your biggest run yet. Between runs, unlock upgrades that push every shot further and make the board more chaotic. Stack multipliers with the Hypemeter Fill the board with explosive, electric, and gold pegs Unlock gadgets that cheat physics: lasers, portals, a gumball machine, even a pinball bumper Launch special balls that shatter, zap, spray watermelon juice, or disco the whole screen Discover pegs that steal the run: chickens, UFOs, mirrors, clowns, and an atomic bomb that hits everything Chase bigger combos, bigger payouts, and bigger score spikes With over 150 upgrades to discover, every run gives you new ways to bend the board in your favor. Every board is randomly generated. Every upgrade sticks. The game keeps handing you new ways to break it. From simple bounces to ridiculous chaos End with a board filled with effects, combos, and massive score spikes. Each run escalates: More hits Bigger numbers More unpredictable outcomes You’re always one lucky bounce away from your biggest run yet. A Satisfying Progression Loop Every hit is a small dopamine boost. Every run builds toward something bigger. Short sessions turn into long ones. You launch one ball… Then another… Then one more to try something new… And suddenly, everything clicks. Permanent Power-Ups Grow stronger over time with upgrades that carry across runs: Multipliers Stronger pegs Bigger payouts Game-changing boosts Each run feeds into the next. Inspired by a 150-year-old game Pegfinity is inspired by a handcrafted pachinko-style board passed down through generations. A simple idea, rebuilt into something faster, bigger, and far more chaotic. Over 150 upgrades Unlock unique pegs, balls, slots and cool gadgets Random generated board - Each run is something new! Permanent unlockable Power Ups! And of course some biiiiiiiiiiig numbers! See you in Pegfinity!
