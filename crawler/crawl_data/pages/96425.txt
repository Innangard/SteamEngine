Pick trumps, follow suit, take control, and win enough tricks to make your bid. Overtrick is a trick-taking deckbuilder based on Bridge, and similar to card games like Hearts, Spades, or Euchre. Each hand, you’ll have to win more and more tricks to advance. But you’ll also acquire special abilities to help you along the way. Build Your Deck Take control and win every trick of every hand, by building up an unstoppable deck of special abilities, including: HONOR CODE. Control the flow of the game by forcing your opponents to play face cards. TREASURE HOARDER. Hoard diamonds to trigger trick bonuses. BRIDGE OVERLORD. Confound your opponents by forcing them to give you their highest card. Ruff, Flush, and Overtrick Earn additional rewards for every overtrick you make above your contract, and acquire even stronger abilities. Outplay High Society In the gilded penthouses of the Roaring 20s, cardplay separates the elite from the out-of-luck. Dazzle high society with your incredible trick-taking. But don’t get carried away! Your opponents are set on winning at all costs, and have a few special abilities of their own. This isn’t your grandma’s Bridge club… this is Overtrick!
