You step into the role of a fresh, unexperienced customs officer at Caldora Airport – a place where the line between duty and greed has never been thinner. You will learn the ropes of being a customs officer from the airport security supervisor. He will guide you step by step through the basics of tasks related to customs. While the full game features an expansive terminal and hangar, the demo is limited to the terminal section of the airport in its basic from without any expansions that will be available in the full game. This setup is perfect to learn the ropes from letting passengers into the queue to checking what they carry on board on their person and in their luggage. A body search is another cool area available in the demo to have a closer look at more suspicious passengers.
