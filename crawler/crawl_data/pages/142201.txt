View Community Hub About This Content Tired of the tedious task of wielding a shovel and the hard labor of a rookie gold prospector?We've got the perfect solution! This add-on makes life easier, especially for novice miners. The mini-machines are more affordable than the larger ones, making them a great choice in the early stages of the game. Mini Mining is a set of four compact devices: Excavator Drill Loader Wash Plant Mini Excavator - enhance your work on the plot, getting the job done much faster than with a shovel. Mini Drilling Machine - with smaller, cheaper drill, you can get straight into rich spot seeking Mini Front-end Loader - load excavated material, faster and more efficiently. Mini Wash Plant – the simplest of the wash plants systems, fully automated and mounted on a handy trailer. After you purchase DLC your Mini Mining Machines will be available to buy in the Machine Shop. Boost your workflow with mini-machines that bridge manual tasks and heavy-duty power—download today!
