What starts as illicit urban exploration becomes a fight for survival when he's captured by a gang of sadistic killers.  Now his only option is to pick up a box cutter, a hammer, a pickaxe, or whatever else is at hand, and unleash a little video violence of his own. FEATURES Use hammers, axes, pipes, 2x4s, box cutters, and more to brutalize anything that stands between you and freedom When all else fails, the level itself can be a weapon.  Kick props into enemies, or enemies into environment hazards.
