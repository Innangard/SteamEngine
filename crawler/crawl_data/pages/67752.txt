Take on 37 missions, from tea parties to “legally ambiguous” deliveries, and earn exclusive Tackle and Puppet Parts you can’t get anywhere else. The locals move around the swamp every day with a new mission of the day, so keep your eyes open. It’s chill… until it isn’t. This swamp hunts back. Fill the Quota, Together Every fish your crew sells fills a shared Dock Quota. Hit it and the whole dock unlocks upgrades like bigger coolers, Sprint Boost, and Jump Boost. Two Fish Mongers each offer their own Bounties and Quota rewards, so pick your prize as a crew and start hauling. Need backup?
