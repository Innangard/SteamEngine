Download Flatspace IIk We Robot Music Pack Free To Play Download View Community Hub About This Content A new and exclusive collection of in-game music tracks to enhance Flatspace IIk, starting with three tracks with the single release of the track 'AI and Celebrity', and later expanded to 6 tracks when the album 'We Robot' is released by Cornutopia Music. Like any in-game music for Flatspace, these will trigger at random when you enter a new sector. This pack contains special Flatspace edits of tracks from the 2023 album We Robot by Mark Sheeky. The pack includes a full description of the music as a pdf booklet. Here is an outline of the music included: AI and Celebrity (Flatspace IIk Mix) An instrumental version of this single release. AI and Celebrity (Adrift Mix) The eerie introduction to AI and Celebrity which seems to echo the feelings of much of The Flatspace Soundtrack. Hydroxychloroquinine Aspartame (FIIk Mix) The ‘B-Side’ to the AI And Celebrity single, the full version of this song is exclusive to the single. It’s also the only track with any links to actual Artificial Intelligence (none of the album, its music or visuals, use AI). This melody was generated using a heuristic intelligence called Oldfield 1; it’s based on a track called Yellow Balloon Attack. The lyrics in the full song jokingly allude to a quest for life-enhancing pharmaceuticals. More tracks are to come in an update when the album is released in October.
