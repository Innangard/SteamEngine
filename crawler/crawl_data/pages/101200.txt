Start with a launch pad, take on contracts, and grow your operation until you're sending crews and cargo to the Moon and Mars. Build rockets and take on missions Manufacture rockets, orbiters, capsules, and ships. Choose the right vehicle for each payload, assemble compatible launch configurations, and prepare the infrastructure for your missions. Expand your factories to keep up with demand, balance production and spending, and decide which contracts to take on next. Deliver satellites, resupply the International Space Station, transport crews, and send scientific missions into deep space. Some customers have unusual ideas about what belongs in orbit. Recover and reuse your fleet Bring your vehicles home, service them, and get them ready for another mission. Unlock new recovery methods, track each craft's condition and flight history, and give your favorite rocket a nickname. Follow launches, stage separations, and landings through in-game webcasts with flight telemetry. Try helicopter catches, propulsive landings, docking, and more in training simulations. Run a satellite internet network Build LinkLink, your own satellite internet service. Manufacture routers and satellites, expand your constellation, and support it with ground relays. Manage coverage, bandwidth, and subscription prices to earn a steady income. As your company grows, automate routine work with production targets and scheduled vehicle programs. Save launch presets for missions you fly often, so you can spend more time planning your next project. Build colonies on the Moon and Mars Transport people and cargo, build housing, and keep your colonies supplied with power, water, oxygen, and food. Set up mining and manufacturing to produce materials locally. Shipments take time, so plan your storage and production to last between deliveries. Build orbital infrastructure, coordinate refueling, and plan flights around planetary transfer windows. On Mars, work toward terraforming and watch the planet change. Choose how you play Follow objectives in career mode or explore in sandbox. Reach milestones to unlock new vehicles, buildings, destinations, and capabilities. Along the way, follow news stories, encounter mission events, and see how people react to your successes and failures.
