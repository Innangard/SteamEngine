Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Mimic Alert is a first-person horror game inspired by the PS1 era. You are a new hire at an agency that clears mimics from a quarantined research zone. PLAY TOGETHER Invite a friend through Steam and play the campaign together, with shared progression and smarter mimics. Or try Challenge mode: larger buildings, more mimics, six rounds and a limited supply of glowsticks to mark cleared rooms. DAY AND NIGHT Every shift starts in daylight. Walk through the building and memorize it: furniture, boxes, chairs and everything else that belongs there. At night, the power goes out. You have a flashlight, a revolver and six precious rounds. Mimics hide among the objects. Some are new, some have replaced something that was there, and some barely move at all. You'll have to rely on your memory to find them. Turn off your flashlight and wait. Some mimics move in the dark, then freeze when the light comes back on. As you progress, some will stalk you from behind and attack when you turn around. THE PEOPLE Scientists, technicians and nurses still live at the research sites. Talk to them during the day and remember what they tell you. At night, one of them might be a mimic wearing their face. If you shoot a real person, the shift is over. If you shoot a mimic, you'll find out what's underneath.
