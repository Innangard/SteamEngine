With so many systems interacting with each other, player feedback is incredibly valuable to us.” Berapa lama game ini akan berada dalam Akses Dini? “We currently expect Invokyr to remain in Early Access for approximately one year. Throughout Early Access, we plan to release regular updates around new themes, with new monsters, events, items and features. This timeframe may change depending on the development process and what we learn from players.” Apa perbedaan antara versi penuh dan versi Akses Dini? “Our goal for the 1.0 version is to significantly expand the variety and depth of Invokyr. We plan to add new boards and themes, monsters, events, dice, cursed rules, items, upgrades. We also plan to expand the narrative mysteries surrounding Invokyr and the Game Master, and continue improving replayability based on what we learn during Early Access.” Apa status saat ini dari versi Akses Dini ini? “At Early Access launch, Invokyr will already offer a complete multiplayer gameplay loop for 1 to 6 players. Players will be able to explore several themed boards, survive more than 15 monsters and 20 events, discover and use more than a dozen different dice and cursed idols, purchase upgrades and equipment, and face increasingly dangerous combinations of rules as they progress toward the ultimate Invokyr Board. The game also includes progression between boards, a shop, environmental secrets, and a rule system designed to make each game unfold differently.” Apakah harga game akan berbeda selama dan setelah Akses Dini? “We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.” Seperti apakah rencana untuk melibatkan Komunitas dalam proses pengembangan? “Community feedback has already played an important role in the development of Invokyr through our demos and playtests, and we want that relationship to continue throughout Early Access. Feedback will help us improve balancing, difficulty, onboarding, replayability, and existing systems, but also identify which monsters, events, rules, dice, and unexpected multiplayer situations players enjoy the most. The house groans around you. Cold. Dark. On the table sits a 1970s board game, dusty but humming with energy. You’d heard rumours, of course. About the board, the house. You roll the dice, and another worldly sound echoes through the house. Will you win your freedom or be dragged into the darkness? Beat the Gamemaster Play the game and survive the unpredictable terrors, traps, and curses that are summoned. Reach the end and you’ll escape. Perhaps. Face the Fear Together Online co-op with up to 6 players. Work together… or leave someone behind. Death is not always the end. Explore to Find Dice Scour the house to recover the dice hidden within.
