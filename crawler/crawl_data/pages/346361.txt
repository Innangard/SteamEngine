This is a slightly sad and fantastical first-person Novel ADV/walking simulator where you try to solve the mystery of the invisible people of Natsugiri and "I". The game includes Story Mode and Free Mode where you can freely roam around the world of Natsugiri. This is a game by an independent indie game creator, Tatamibeya.
