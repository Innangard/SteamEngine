Content For This Game Browse all (1) LawnMower City Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game Lawnmower City is an action-packed arcade-style game that brings the excitement of cutting grass to a whole new level. As the city's dedicated lawn care specialist, your task is simple: mow down all the grass and plants scattered across 40 vibrant levels. But beware—it's not just about landscaping! You'll have to dodge speeding traffic, evade turret fire, and outmaneuver tanks as you race to complete your objectives. Featuring colorful voxel-style graphics and a catchy, original soundtrack, Lawnmower City provides fast-paced fun with simple controls using either the keyboard or gamepad. Each level offers three hidden stars to collect, giving you access to unlock new lawnmowers to enhance your mowing experience. If you're looking for a challenge, track your progress with time records and see how you rank on the online leaderboards! With levels that grow larger and introduce a variety of dynamic obstacles—ranging from busy city streets to enemy tanks and turrets— Lawnmower City offers increasing difficulty to keep you engaged. Whether you're in it for the leaderboard glory or just want a quick, enjoyable time-killer, this game delivers a fresh and exciting experience with every blade of grass. Key Features: 40 challenging levels filled with obstacles like traffic, turrets, trains, and tanks Colorful voxel graphics and an original, energetic soundtrack Simple controls: Play with the keyboard or gamepad Collect stars to unlock new lawnmowers Time-based leaderboards to challenge your best runs Fun for players of all ages and skill levels! Cut through the chaos and claim your spot as the top lawnmower in the city!
