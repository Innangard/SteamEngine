Choose your cute avatar, find the right arena, then beat the tar out of your opponents in point-based battles with a twist, mixing and matching elemental powers! Complete mini-games giving everyone a chance to win, not just those who can kick the hardest! Discover clever combinations of elements and the environment, creating new and unpredictable ways to liven up the party! Oh, and don't get attached to your friends - every round shakes up the teams and you might end up best buddies with someone you just froze and threw halfway across the map into a blazing inferno! Putting the Element in Elemental Every character starts out the same: They can punch and kick, like you! Unlike you, each can collect randomly spawning elemental orbs, granting them new and interesting powers, such as setting players and the map on fire, drenching everything in water and moving them around with spray, or summoning dirt to act as shield. Elements combine, giving you the ability to answer the age-old question: What happens when a superheated rock meets a face? But It's Not All About Violence Oh no. Every round is different thanks to randomly selected mini-games players can complete instead of just racing to get their favorite combination of elements. Don't feel like smacking your best friend across the map? How about a Totem mode, where you need to collect the pieces and defend them from the other team? Or Fire vs. Ice, where you compete to prevent the destruction of ice statues by the fiery fury of the other team? Everyone has a chance to win, not just the ones fastest on the draw! Also, It's the Maps Between the elements and the mini-games are the maps themselves. Forget about static arenas made of indestructibilium! Here you can set things on fire, freeze them, turn puddles into deadly steam traps, deploy turrets and caltrops, and experiment to your heart's content by combining elements with map environments to create fun new ways to get the drop on the other team - or see them backfire spectacularly, which is part of the fun, right? Not a One and Done Elemental Brawl is in for the long haul! We plan on supporting the game with post-release content, including new elements to play around with (and annoy your friends), new environments and maps, new mini-games to enjoy, and of course new skins to kit out your avatars with!
