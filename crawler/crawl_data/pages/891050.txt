Created with endless love; especially for fans of space pirates, sci-fi westerns and alien encounters! Silent Sector takes us to a remote part of the galaxy where several factions fight each other over the profits in a new gold rush - in pursuit of an extraordinarily valuable mineral called Eldorite. Our hero comes into this Wild West universe and very quickly loses both their ship and cargo. You must find a way to repay the debt and take your revenge against Admiral Zed. This truly miserable corner of the universe offers you a chance to try your luck at becoming a successful merchant, rich miner, feared mercenary or a famous adventurer. In the game you will find : Space sim, adventure story, and quick dogfights all in one package. Crawl the large semi-randomly-generated open world space in dozens unique ships. More guns, missiles, lasers and upgrades than a space cow cannot count. Help one of 7 factions destroy their rivals in a series of breathtaking quests and witness the horrible aftermath of your choices! Explore the mysteries of the universe and fulfill countless of random quests and events full of crude humor. Enjoy an atmospheric Wild West inspired soundtrack! Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Shooting and destruction of enemy space ships and starbases.
