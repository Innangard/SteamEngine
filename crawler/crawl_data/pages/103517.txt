Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game She holds the road. You make her stronger. A girl in a red hood, a silver bow shaped like a crescent moon, and a road that monsters pour down until the end of time. She fights on her own. What she turns into is up to you. Moonbow Idle is a dark fantasy idle auto battler. Leave it running on a second monitor while you get on with your day, or sit down and chase the next boss. It works either way. What you actually do Spend gold on seven upgrades, from Damage to Counter Attack, and watch the next wave fall faster than the last. Pick one of three perks every time you level up: pierce more enemies, hit harder, shrug off more damage. Unlock the Arsenal: venom, fire, frost, orbiting souls, chain lightning, arrow volleys, explosions and hail. It's the expensive stuff, and it makes fights look completely different. A long way down the road 210 stages across seven biomes: meadow, swamp, volcano, ice, ruins, desert and the shadow lands. Each one looks and feels different. And the monsters are more than reskins. Some poison you, some heal their friends, some come in swarms, and some get back up after you've killed them. There's a boss every ten stages, and at the very end the Eclipse Sovereign is waiting. Pets, loot, and a good reason to ascend Bosses drop eggs. Hatch them to collect up to 21 companions, take three along, and give each one a skill you can fire yourself or leave on auto. 36 pieces of gear in six sets. Wear two or four from the same set and you get something fun: permanent poison, a freezing wind, a second life. Got a duplicate? Great, it levels up. When a run gets too hard, ascend. You trade it for relics that boost everything for good, keep your pets and gear, and go again. Farther every time. Good to know Idle progress, plus a reward waiting for you when you come back. Game speed up to x3 (one of the gear sets unlocks x3). Full controller support, Pets and Gear tabs included. 20 achievements. English, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese. Pixel art and music by xDeviruchi.
