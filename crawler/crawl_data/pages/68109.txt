It never takes a step. Monsters march down the lanes toward you, your spells fire on their own, and every run comes down to what you build before the line breaks. Build it wave by wave Level up to pick new spells and upgrades, then spend your gold in the shop between waves. Hold up to 5 of 18 spells, and from wave 21 each one can evolve into something much bigger. Over 200 items let you push a build as far as it goes, and plenty of them ask for something back. Then the monsters fight back Elites show up with tricks of their own, 8 bosses stand in your way, and some maps send enemies at you from every side. Turn on Monsters' Ascension and every wave the monsters get a new item too. Plenty to unlock 10 heroes, each with their own weapon and passive. 5 difficulties, Endless mode, meta upgrades, achievements and a codex of everything you've found.
