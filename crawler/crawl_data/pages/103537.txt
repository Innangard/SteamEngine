About This Game The Heirloom is a single-player mysterious thriller adventure with puzzle elements, where players are given the opportunity to make fateful decisions while navigating through a story full of secrets and mysteries . Immerse yourself in the history of the town as you interact with the locals and any items you may find. Uncover the secrets of your family and your grandfather’s old lighthouse as you work to discover the truth behind the unusual happenings. Not everything can be obtained easily! Solve various puzzles to learn a little more than you should and move on. Search for useful items , combine and use them wisely to achieve your goals. Which path will you choose: follow the rules or go against them? Trust your heart or follow your mind? Every word you say and every action you take will definitely have consequences . A mysterious story inspired by the atmosphere of the Hebrides and Scottish folklore Interesting, touching, sometimes eccentric and funny dialogues Each decision you make affects the plot and can lead to unpredictable results Stunning hand-drawn graphics and fascinating interactive cutscenes Unique and exciting combinations of game mechanics MARLA MOIR REBECCA WANG • Guillermo del Toro’s Tales of Arcadia | Jurassic World Camp Cretaceous | Blue Eye Samurai (Netflix) | Steps (Netflix) Marla is the protagonist of The Heirloom , a 16-year-old teenage girl and the eldest daughter of the Moir family . She is sarcastic, stubborn, and determined . Her favorite pastime is writing little horror stories in her notebook, which she uses to scare her younger brother, Jack , when he greatly annoys her . Despite her sometimes seemingly selfish and harmful behavior, is it truly so? JACK MOIR REBECCA WANG • Guillermo del Toro’s Tales of Arcadia | Jurassic World Camp Cretaceous | Blue Eye Samurai (Netflix) | Steps (Netflix) Jack is a loyal companion and an expert in every field , as well as Marla’s 10-year-old younger brother . He is still very young but highly inquisitive and intelligent, which makes him doubly a thorn in Marla’s side . Whether it's rocks, plants, or all sorts of insects, Jack is right there. MORVIN MOIR ANDY MACK • Evil West | Dying Light 2 | Foolish Mortals | Postal 4 Morvin is the sole living relative who agreed to take custody of his two orphaned grandchildren. He is a reclusive, grumpy , and rather peculiar old man who conceals something terrifying within the walls of his house. It appears that the only things to which he is even remotely loyal are chocolate chip biscuits and scary black birds with red eyes, which seem to be exceedingly unfriendly to everyone except him.
