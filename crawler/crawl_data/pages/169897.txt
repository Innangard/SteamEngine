More mechanics will be available in the full version of the game. Weed Supermarket Simulator is a chill first-person simulation game where you run your own weed store. Open and manage your shop as you sell weed, bongs, and other smoking gear. Set up shelves, display cases, and counters to make shopping easy and fun for all kinds of customers. Grow your own weed from seed to harvest. Build a grow room, plant seeds, and take care of your plants. Upgrade and improve your strains to create high-quality weed that sells for more. Order weed and supplies on an in-game computer. Get deliveries, unpack boxes, store items in the back room, and place products on shelves, in fridges, or in locked display cases. Serve customers at the counter. Scan items, take cash, and keep everyone happy - or at least nicely high. Use your profits to expand your business. Make your shop bigger, improve the interior, unlock new strains, and sell premium products.
