View Community Hub About This Game Uncover the mysteries of the box left at your doorstep and experience the difference between reality and hallucination. Be careful, because hallucinations can affect reality too. TRY TO HOLD ON TO REALITY The places you encounter may be far from reality. Don't let them deceive you. Keep moving forward. SOLVE PUZZLES AND UNCOVER THE TRUTH Carefully examine your surroundings to uncover the house's deepest mystery and restore the smart home system. Interact with objects, follow the clues, and solve puzzles scattered across different environments. STAY AWAY FROM THE ENTITY FOLLOWING YOU It is following you. But strangely enough... it is afraid of you too. As the game progresses, the hallucinations will intensify, the traces you leave behind in the real world will increase, and the line between what you see and what you actually do will gradually disappear. FEATURES Walking Simulator Various Puzzles Realistic Graphics High-Quality Sound Design When the line between reality and illusion disappears, all that remains is what you have done.
