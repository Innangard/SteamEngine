A space prison, endless corridors, deadly traps and an all-seeing artificial intelligence ready to tear you apart. You are the last prisoner awakened from a corrective cryo-sleep, your task is to escape from this hell and find out where is everyone else? The only way to escape is to manipulate gravity and adapt to the chaos around you. The Last Prisoner is a hardcore platformer with gravity changing mechanics, an engaging story, and memorable characters. What's in store for you: Gravity Changing Mechanics: Bypass traps by changing the direction of gravity, dodge deadly AI tricks and blast through levels. High difficulty for real hardcore gamers: Each level is a new challenge that requires lightning-fast reactions and precision. Engaging story and charismatic characters: Uncover the truth about humanity’s fate while facing a devious AI and meeting a mysterious ally. An AI determined to destroy you: The merciless AI will do everything to stop your escape, activating traps at the most unexpected moments. But don’t think she’s just a regular opponent. She won’t just try to kill you—she’ll mock your failures with sarcasm and playful taunts. Get ready for her sharp and cheeky remarks. An exhilarating electronic soundtrack: Powerful tracks that immerse you in the atmosphere and pump adrenaline into your every step.
