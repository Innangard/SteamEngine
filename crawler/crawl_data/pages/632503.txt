Here, luck has replaced money as the true measure of wealth, and the lottery has become a vital source of daily necessities. The "Battle of the Big Winners," where luck serves as capital, has emerged as the sole pathway to ascendancy. Only through continuous victories can one break through the class barriers and reach the coveted "Pinnacle of Citizens." The Big Winner - A Clash of Luck and Strategy This unique version of poker, infused with a twist, allows you, equipped with a system, to peer into your opponent's hand using "Causality Cards." At critical moments, you can secretly swap out "junk cards" for winning hands like flushes or three-of-a-kinds, turning the tide of the game! Engage in psychological warfare, leveraging your powerful "verbal skills" to instill doubt in your opponents before the cards are revealed, causing them to fold or impulsively raise their bets, making you the king of mindset in Jiangcheng. Your survival hinges on scratch cards From chocolate-spiced chili to creamy steamed eggplant, whether you can whip up a decent "dish" depends entirely on your luck. Your fashion sense, whether it’s a suit paired with yoga pants or a pleated skirt with a grandpa tank top, is dictated by what you can scratch off. You can also scratch for housing, furniture, electric bikes, and cars... In Jiangcheng, making it this far is all about luck. With hundreds of different hairstyles, outfits, ingredients, and vehicles, boost your luck to play a vibrant dress-up game! Experience a romantic story in a fantastical world Set in a unique worldview centered around "luck," this contemporary urban fantasy diverges from the norm. Across various sectors—work, sports, healthcare, cuisine, and gaming—nine romantic interests from different social strata await. Each character embodies both good and evil, resistance and struggle, with their own intricate backstories. Choose your life partner and join hands with them to uncover the hidden secrets of River Town. The game currently features 9 dateable NPCs, and we will be releasing the 10th NPC in the future. Stay tuned! Exploring New River City to become the ultimate winner Roam freely through the bustling streets and hidden alleys, relying on your luck to scavenge for treasures; even a beggar can survive in River City. If you prefer not to depend on luck or the lottery, you can choose to work hard and earn a living, taking steady steps to accumulate wealth. In the turbulent currents of River City, a miraculous ascent from the bottom to the top awaits your realization.
