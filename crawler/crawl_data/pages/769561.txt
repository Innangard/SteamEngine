Seeking cosmic supremacy, the government harvested stellar remnants to build an artificial star using political prisoners as human test subjects for cosmic-scale weaponry. You are Noven, a former militia leader sentenced to life for defying the regime. When a catastrophic lab failure caused the artificial star to collapse, Noven absorbed its dying cosmic radiation, surviving the blast that vaporized everything for miles. Reborn from the epicenter as Veil , you step out of the fallout armed with stellar power and a singular mission: complete retribution.
