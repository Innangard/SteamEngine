View Community Hub About This Game Six worlds. One enormous mess. Six different worlds have collided in one town square, scattering 4,899 belongings across the ground. A cozy cottage, wizard tower, sci-fi lab, modern apartment, western saloon, and haunted mansion need their things back. Pick up an object, follow the clues, and find where it belongs. Every object has a home First find the right building. Then read its shelf signs, compare what is already there, and find the one row made for your item. Carry a handful into a house and place them one after another. The soft clicks and clinks make each filled shelf its own reward. Turn a pile into a town Each placement earns coins. Complete a row for a shower of them, then spend your haul on upgrades that help you carry more, move faster, and spot matches. As the pile shrinks, shelves fill and each house comes back to life. Take the scenic route Try the cottage's flower puzzle, take aim at the saloon, and explore beyond the obvious paths. Small challenges offer useful rewards, but the cleanup is yours to tackle in any order. There is more to discover There is more to this town than its six familiar doors. Follow odd clues and see what you find. No timer, no rush. Tidy at your own pace. Tidy up!
