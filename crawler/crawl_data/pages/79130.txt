Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We love building games with our community, and Early Access is the best way to do that. With Grounded 2, we’re continuing the same spirit that helped shape the first game. It gives us the chance to hear your feedback early, make improvements together, and grow Brookhollow Park into something truly special with your help.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We aren’t locking ourselves into a hard date just yet; it’ll really depend on how things go and how much we add based on your feedback. We’ll keep you posted along the way!” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full 1.0 version will bring the complete story and will come with more content that won’t be released during the Early Access period. It will also include everything the team develops throughout Early Access to become the ultimate park experience for all.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “There’s already a ton to do! Right now you can: • Play through act 1 of the story • Explore a huge section of Brookhollow Park • Try out three Buggies • Gear up with our new archetype system • Play solo or hop into online co-op with friends It’s a full survival adventure, with more on the way.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yep! Grounded 2 is available at a lower price while it’s in Early Access. As we add more content and get closer to 1.0, the price will likely go up to match the final version, just like we did with the first Grounded. It’s our way of thanking the early community members for jumping in to help us shape the game.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We’re listening! Whether it’s through Discord, forums, social, or our own support inbox, we’re always watching what you’re building, battling, and talking about. We’ll be sharing updates, doing livestreams, asking questions, and making changes based on your suggestions. Traverse sprawling manmade spaces overgrown with nature with buggies that can fight alongside with you, uncover hidden secrets, and fight to survive in a world much larger than the backyard. The playground may seem familiar, but beneath its bright colors and towering structures lie mysteries hidden in plain sight. Are you ready to uncover them? Survive, Adapt, Overcome The world is relentless, but so are you. Shape your adventure with unique archetypes, each offering distinct abilities to match your playstyle. Whether you rely on precision, cunning, or sheer strength, you’ll need every advantage to survive against the threats lurking in the park. Stronger Together The perils of the park are daunting alone, but with friends, every challenge becomes an adventure. Work together to build, fight, and uncover the secrets buried beneath the grass. Whether you’re facing danger side by side or continuing your journey in a shared world, survival is always better with allies. Walking is Bogus - Introducing Buggies They scurry, they fight, and now—they help you survive. Hatch, raise, and ride your own insect friends - buggies! Saddle up and ride through the park, command them in battle, or have them help gather resources. The right partner could mean the difference between thriving and barely surviving. Big Baddies of the Park The park is home to creatures other than the bugs you know and love. AXL, the tarantula, and the Lizard stand as apex predators that demand preparation, strategy, and courage. The snake is a different kind of challenge. To master it, you will need to learn its patterns, ride it, and harvest its scales. And let's not forget about the bird. Every encounter makes you stronger. Claim rare materials, craft powerful gear, and unlock upgrades that push you further into a world built to overwhelm you. Playgrounds Survival is only the beginning. With Playgrounds, you can build your own challenges using powerful tools that shape combat, puzzles, and unpredictable encounters. Download, play, and vote on community creations to discover new ways to test your skills. The park is no longer just a place to survive. It is a place to create. Home Sweet Home The park is yours to claim. Build everything from simple shelters to sprawling fortresses that keep you safe from the dangers outside. But don't limit yourself to just dry land. Create fully underwater bases to support long expeditions, expand your reach into the depths, and make every corner of the park a cozy little home. A Shadow That Follows You The threat is always there—watching, learning, waiting. You don’t know where it’s coming from, only that it never leaves. The deeper you dig, the closer it gets. Some mysteries should stay buried, but it’s too late now. It knows you’re looking. And it’s ready. Every answer drags you deeper, every step invites something closer. You were never alone. You thought you were in control. You were wrong.
