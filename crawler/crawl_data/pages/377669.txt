Learn more Leaving Early Access: Nov 25, 2026 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game is already fully playable, and we’re excited to finally share it with a wider audience for feedback and support as we continue expanding it! You can find and follow the roadmap and planned features in the game's description. Having already added over three times the originally planned content, we see a lot of potential to further grow the cozy cafe concept throughout Early Access leading up to full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “About one year.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “🌱 Several additions are planned during Early Access, including: - Sandbox decor mode: Decorate with no limits - Terrace area: Let your customers enjoy the expanded space - Skill tree: Unlock light progression perks and upgrades - Custom character import: Play as anything you want! - Shop & delivery system: Order supplies and manage stock - Brewing & cafe upgrades: Expand brewing options and layout - Newspaper storylines: 11 mini chapters of game story content - Journal system: Browse unlockable info and cozy tips - Pastry collaboration: Buy pastries from your bunny baker friend and sell them in your cafe - Twitch integration: Let your chat become cafe customers! - Weather system: Atmospheric weather variations that influence customer moods and cafe workflow. - Level rewards Expansion: Extend upgrade rewards from 30 to 50 levels with new items and decor. - Decor item expansion: Grow the catalog from 170 to 500+ items. - Collectible Items: Interact with customers to receive special items. - And more based on your suggestions! These updates will fit naturally into the cozy experience without disrupting its relaxed rhythm.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “☕ Core features - Drink recipes: Brew a variety of cozy drinks - Hundreds of decor items: Furnish your café with plants, furniture, and wall art - Village and creature customers (NPCs): Meet a growing cast of friendly visitors - Day & night cycle: A calm rhythm marking each new day - Bedroom area: Customize and expand your personal space” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. The full release will include a small price increase to reflect added content and polish. Players who join during Early Access will receive all updates at no extra cost.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “💬 We’ll gather feedback through Steam community discussions, social media and our Discord server.
