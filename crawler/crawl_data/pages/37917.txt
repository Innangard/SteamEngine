View Community Hub About This Game You play a young adolescent who is on a journey to find his father. Solve puzzles, uncover mysteries, fight aliens, visit cities to do quests, all while being bound by gravity to the small worlds that you walk on. Our protagonist is of Afro-Caribbean lineage. Adorning his father's jacket, that is inscribed with their family crest (an Adinkra symbol), the adventure entails discovering all that is out there (hidden among the stars). Ancient Statues, Shrine Monuments, Satellite Graveyards, Hidden Messages, a very, very strange (but dedicated) group of Outcasts, and more await you at the edge. In this experience, your choices will matter and your decisions can lead you to one of seven endings! In a totally separate 'Survival' game mode (1 or 2-player couch co-op), you'll survive against waves of enemies with various weapons and abilities. When the coast is clear, discover Ability Anvils that will help you turn the tide against the horde. Destroying enemies may grant you temporary powers or perhaps utilizing various traps might be the very key to your victory. Every match in Survival mode is different. You'll need to develop a strategy if you plan on joining the ranks of other high-ranking Kasslers. Can you make it to the final sector? - Solo or splitscreen couch co-op w/ friendly fire options (Survival) - Twitch integration. Chat chooses wave outcomes (Survival) - Leaderboards. Find out if you have what it takes to be one of the Top Kasslers (Survival) - Endless waves with unique elements in play each run (Survival) - Unlockable Character Skins (Survival) - Open world branching narrative with multiple endings (Story) - 300+ worlds and several unique puzzles (Story) - Keyboard & Controller support + Bindable Keys and SteamDeck In the future, the bravest explorers and heroes find themselves as members to a notorious organization called Kassle Corporation . Are you ready to become a Kassler?
