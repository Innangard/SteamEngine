Crossing the France/Germany border, this bustling 112 km (70-mile) route is a key international service running from Strasbourg to Offenburg and Karlsruhe. Featuring a wide range of rolling stock including locomotives like the SNCF BR 186 in FRET livery, DB BR 406 ICE 3M in DB ICE livery, TGV Euroduplex in SNCF Blue & Grey livery and more. With this pack you will take control of a variety of locomotives across ten new scenarios. Operate the DB Regional Express service #7 through the rain from Offenburg, bring the ICE 2 back onto the mainline for a southbound run, take on intermodal freight with the SNCF BR 186, transport tankers with the MRCE Dispolok and more. Enjoy a mix of high-speed traction, regional express and heavy freight motive power on Bahnstrecke Strasbourg – Karlsruhe with these new Career scenarios.
