View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Eldor’Idle ! It’s a sleepy RPG town like any other. You’ve come here to make your fortune… no matter the cost. Break into houses. Smash pots for coins. Hire gnomes to smash more pots. Break into more houses. Hire more gnomes. Invest your coins, automate your labour, grow your profits, sacrifice your employees to the demonic forces of hell, then reset the loop and do it all again. You can explore the many attractions of Eldor’Idle, beyond the rubes sitting around waiting to be robbed: The Mine : Dig deep and discover a secret! Dungeon : Battle the boss and win rewards! Wizard’s Tower : Brew up a potent upgrade! Cemetery : Make deals with a ghost! Greedy Greedy Gnomes is a satirical clicker-idle RPG where greed is good, might is right, and the end ALWAYS justifies the means.
