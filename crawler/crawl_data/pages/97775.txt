During the journey, the ship collects energy photons that are present in any matter that can be found in the universe, from those close to Earth to those thousands of light years away. You can destroy a satellite, an alien spacecraft, a planet, or a star and collect all the photons emitted during the explosion. If you want to improve your spacecraft, some upgrades are available. Upgrades consume photons to be built, so the more photons you collect, the more improvements you can make. Increase the speed of your ship to thousands of times the speed of light to reach the stars at the edge of the universe faster, improve the range of attraction of photons present in outer space, and enhance the ability to transform photons into energy, turning each collected photon into much more energy that you can spend on more upgrades. Receive gifts and find other surprises floating in interstellar space ready to be collected and converted into energy, which will certainly make your ship faster and more efficient. And if you wish, you can turn the game into a vertical widget for your desktop and leave it running in the corner of the screen while you work, study, or even play other games. Your ship will continue to head toward the unknown.
