Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “There are a few key reasons behind this approach: Unshackled Vision – Teddy’s Haven is meant to be a relaxed, cozy, and ambitious shop simulator that uplifts the genre. Early Access allows me the creative freedom to pursue that vision without compromise. Community Driven Development – I believe the best version of this game will come from building it with the community. By inviting players into the development process early, I can listen, learn, and grow together to ensure Teddy’s Haven reaches its full potential.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I'm currently expecting the full release to happen by late 2026 to early 2027. The development velocity has been substantial, and while the game is already in an incredibly stable state, we want to take the time needed to fully realize my final vision. My estimated time in Early Access is about 18 - 24 months. Even after the full release, I plan to continue offering updates and new content to keep the experience fresh and engaging.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version of Teddy’s Haven will expand in big, meaningful ways. Here’s a peek at what’s planned: New Locations to Explore: The world will grow over time with entirely new regions to discover. Each with their own characters, resources, and secrets. Skills & Minigames: A wide variety of cozy skills like fishing, cooking, crafting, and other optional tasks will be added, letting players engage with the world however they like. Whether you're here to build the perfect shop or just relax by the river with a fishing rod. Shop Expansion & Customization: A major expansion of the decorating and building systems is planned. You’ll be able to construct new buildings and customize your shop to reflect your own personal dream space. Whether that’s a quaint cottagecore storefront or a bustling magical emporium. World Events: I'm designing a system for larger, story-driven world events that unfold over time and leave a lasting impact on your shop and the world around it. These events will evolve the narrative and gameplay in exciting and meaningful ways. These features are designed to enrich the experience for both new players and early adopters. The heart of Teddy’s Haven is already here, but the full release will make the world feel even more alive, magical, and full of possibility.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Teddy’s Haven is already a fully playable and feature rich experience. The current Early Access version includes: A functional, explorable world with the core shop management loop fully in place A seasonal and daily event system that keeps the world feeling ever changing Dynamic world events that evolve gameplay and storytelling Unique and memorable special NPCs to meet. A decoration system to personalize and design your dream shop A leveling system and foundational skill mechanics that enhance how you play Shop enchantments to customize the function and magic of your storefront While the game is still growing, what's here already offers a cozy, meaningful experience. Built with love and ready to evolve alongside the community.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. During Early Access, we’ve made it a priority to keep the price as affordable as possible. This gives early adopters a chance to jump in at a lower price point and be part of the journey from the start. Once we reach full release, we plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features. This change reflects the growth of the game and will help support continued development and future content beyond 1.0.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? I’ve set up a public Discord where players can talk directly with me. Whether it’s to report issues, share ideas, or dream big about what Teddy’s Haven could become. I’m active, listening, and genuinely excited to hear from the community. My goal isn’t just to build a great game, and I believe that journey is best taken together. As a veteran game designer, I’ve seen firsthand how listening to players can elevate a game beyond even its original vision. That’s why I’ve put systems in place to make it easy for the community to be a part of this world’s growth. Your feedback, passion, and creativity will help shape Teddy’s Haven into something truly magical. Welcome to a cozy, magical shop simulator set on the enchanting island of Ursa. Run your store your way and sell what you harvest, craft, or discover. And enjoy the freedom to decorate freely across your shop, home, and the wilds beyond. Level up naturally as you play, grow magical crops, and take part in daily events that bring the island to life. With over 250 decorations , 400 items to sell , and no pressure, every day is yours to enjoy at your own pace. Ursa, A World of Calm and Wonder Ursa is a gentle land filled with peace, memories, and mythical spirits. Your shop is a safe haven of supplies for travelers and magical beings alike. The land is rich and vibrant. You can harvest resources, care for its creatures, and meet friendly creatures along the way. In Ursa there is no conflict, only warmth and calm. This is your relaxing world to make your own. Key Features: Manage  Your Magical Shop – Create the cozy shop you’ve always wanted. With changing seasons , daily surprises , and rewarding leveling systems , your shop grows alongside you. No rush, no stress, just a magical rhythm all your own. Decorate Across Ursa – Over 250 decorations to decorate freely across your shop, home, and the island’s landscapes. With near-limitless placement freedom, express your style and bring every corner of Ursa to life. A Wide Variety of Goods to Keep Your Shelves Full – Stock your shop with over 400 items gathered, grown, or traded across Ursa. Skill Systems – Your skills grow naturally as you run your shop. Stock shelves faster, harvest more berries, and see your abilities improve with each task. Leveling up rewards you for doing what you love, at your own pace. Crafting – Enjoy simple, stress-free crafting that doesn’t keep you waiting. Place your items and return the next day to find something special created. Always growing, crafting will expand with new features and more complex options in future updates. Farming – Farming has arrived in the land of Ursa! Unlock new contracts that allow you to plant and nurture magical crops, each with unique Aether-infused variations. Grow, harvest, and fill your shop with wondrous goods born from the earth and touched by magic. Unique and Magical Employees – Hire magical stockers and cashiers who keep your shop running smoothly. Whether you’re decorating, relaxing, or out exploring the world, they’ll reliably care for your haven as if it were their own. Simplified Inventory Management – Too busy to sort through a warehouse of items? Open your “bag of everything” and stock shelves directly, hassle-free. Spend less time managing and more time enjoying your shop. Exploration – Wander the ever-expanding world of Ursa at your own pace. Harvest berries, care for animals, or simply enjoy the beautiful scenery and discover the magic hidden in every corner. Daily Events – Your shop is a hub of life and wonder. As the days pass, creatures will stop by with stories, gifts, or even magical blessings for your shop. Everyone you meet is here to help you grow and enjoy your cozy world. Bounds Breaking Joy: Teddy’s Haven celebrates curiosity. And sometimes that means climbing too high, falling too far, or finding yourself under the map. Go ahead: break the bounds. Secret items and small surprises await those who wander a little too far from the intended path. It’s all part of the adventure. Auto Saving – Take the game at your own pace, no more requirements of ending your day for your game to be saved. Ongoing Development : Teddy’s Haven is in Early Access, with plans for new locations , minigames , world events , and more, the journey has only just begun. Created In Loving Dedication To The Memory Of My Best Friend Teddy Bear.
