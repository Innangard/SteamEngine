Manage the shared inventory of your party of adventurers and guide them through a perilous journey across a shifting map filled with shops, forges, and dangerous enemies. Your entire team shares a 9×9 inventory grid , where careful placement of gear is crucial—not just for space, but to activate the powerful grid bonuses provided by your chosen heroes. A Unique Grid-Based Inventory System In this game, every item matters. Gear comes in Tetris-like shapes ranging from 1 to 5 tiles, and fitting them together within your limited grid space is a strategic puzzle. Each adventurer contributes distinct bonuses to specific grid tiles, so choosing who to bring—and how to arrange their equipment—is key to success. Heroes with Exclusive Skills and Grid Influence Unlock and experiment with a growing roster of characters, each offering their own exclusive abilities and bonus zones on the shared grid. Build your team to suit your strategy and discover powerful combinations that adapt to each run. Forge and Reshape Your Gear Collect materials during your journey and use them in the forge to upgrade , combine , or even reshape your items . Making a weapon smaller may allow more gear to fit, while expanding it might cover more grid bonuses for stronger effects. Flexibility is essential in managing your evolving inventory. Dynamic Combat and Handcrafted Encounters Every few steps, you’ll face off against enemy parties composed of a mix of procedurally generated opponents and handcrafted challenges designed by the developer. These auto-battles test your strategic planning, equipment synergy, and adaptability. Survive long enough, and you’ll face a powerful boss with unique mechanics tailored to test your final loadout. Multiple Game Modes for Varied Play Experience the game in different ways with a variety of game modes . Play with three characters, take on a minimalist challenge with only one, or face scenarios where the final boss is revealed in advance to aid your preparation. You can even challenge friends by sharing custom combat configurations for them to overcome with their own builds—perfect for testing creativity and skill. Carefully manage your inventory, forge the ideal equipment, and outwit your enemies in this deep and replayable roguelike adventure. Will you master the grid and forge a path to victory?
