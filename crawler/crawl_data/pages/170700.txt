Learn about the electrician job in a relaxing, fun, and safest way possible. New expansion pack featuring electrician challenges presented in a quite realistic still "gaming" casual manner. 🔌May the lights come on! Take on an electrician's role and handle the construction of electrical installations from A to Z. Learn the systems for laying out and connecting wires & assembling the many components of electrical installations. Use your knowledge and skills to create installations that not only meet technical requirements but are also convenient and intuitive to use. Properly selected components and thoughtful installation are the keys to success! 💡 Bet on creativity. A full range of electrical installation tools and components will be put at your disposal so that you can create installations as you wish. Determine the routes of wires, connect lights, and install sockets and switches in such a way as to construct maximum optimal installations. It's up to you how you solve a problem! 🔋 Take on different jobs. As a specialist electrician, you will have to face a variety of tasks. Build installations from scratch, look for faults in existing ones, and fix what some other electrician wired wrong. You'll also have to manage your money wisely to fit within the budget your client has set! 🧹 Don't forget to clean up after yourself! Once you've created the entire installation, it's a good idea to remember to bring the walls up to code before you arrive! Hide the wires in the walls, smooth them out with putty, and then repaint them so that no one leaves any traces.
