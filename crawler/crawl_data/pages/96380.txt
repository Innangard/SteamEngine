This ain't your Grandma's dice game! Diecast is a bag-building roguelite where every roll is a chance to push your luck. Build scoring hands, rack up points with multipliers and crit, and then decide whether to play it safe or keep rolling for something even bigger. Pins can completely change the way your dice work. Reroll bad results, multiply your score, transform your scoring hands, or create unexpected interactions between your dice. Stack the right pins together and discover synergies that get more ridiculous with every run. With dozens of pins to discover, there are plenty of ways to make numbers go very, very big. As you play, you'll unlock dozens of new dice and pins to bring into future runs, giving you more tools to experiment with and more ways to build a completely broken scoring engine. And once you've mastered the basics, unlockable game modes start bending the rules even further. New rules, new challenges, and some truly wacky surprises your grandma could never have dreamt of. You'll have to rethink how you play, and we promise it'll be weird . Demo Features The demo is a large slice of dice-filled pie compared to the full release. Play a full run, as many times as you want! In the demo you'll find: 30 dice (+5 more unlockable in-game) 30 pins (+5 more unlockable in-game) 9 hands (+5 more unlockable in-game) Over a dozen bosses twist the rules between sectors. Adapt your strategy, or crash back down to Earth. 2 game modes System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 Processor: Intel or AMD Dual Core CPU Memory: 500 MB RAM Graphics: Integrated Graphics Storage: 500 MB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
