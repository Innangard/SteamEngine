About This Content This Pack includes: B1 ter tank (Rank 2 France); P-40F-5 Lafayette fighter (Rank 2 France); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. B1 ter The B1 is one of France’s most iconic WWII tanks, thanks to its instantly recognizable appearance and widespread usage during the invasion of France in 1940. This modification is a robust upgrade of the standard B1 bis. Firepower remains the same, but mobility and especially armor have been significantly improved. The sides and front have been upgraded to 70 mm, and considering the angling of the plates, these sections of armor are almost immune to many common cannons at this rank. Space has been made internally for an extra crew member to improve survivability. To compensate for the additional armor, the engine power has been increased to 350 hp, so you can still push through the map effectively and break through enemy lines with the help of the reinforced armor. P-40F-5 Lafayette A modification of the early P-40 models, the P-40F-5 features a Packard V-1650-1 Merlin engine and sports the livery of the famous Lafayette squadron, flown by pilot Constantin Rozanoff, commander of GC II/5. The P-40F model is a highly versatile fighter with strong attributes across the board. It’s equipped with six 12.7 mm machine guns in the wings with a high ammo count — enough to take any aircraft out of the sky and strafe ground targets effectively. It can also carry a pair of 100 lb bombs along with a 500 lb bomb for close air support duties. The F model has a high top speed, strong climb rate, and good turn, giving you everything you need to be a highly effective fighter. All premium vehicles allow you to earn increased Research Points and Silver Lions for each battle and comes furnished with all available modifications. With a Premium account (also purchasable in the game for Golden Eagles) you will earn more Research Points and Silver Lions during each battle for a set amount of days. This is cumulative with bonuses from premium vehicles! Note! Refund is not provided if you purchase 2 different packs which include the same vehicle or you already have the vehicle from this pack!
