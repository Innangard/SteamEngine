It’s a unique puzzle game that fully engages my brain, making it satisfyingly hot.” kotaku “Yet another example of the developer's passion to create unique worlds set around a solid core mechanic” 7 – Play Save Repeat About This Game Play as a woman who's an axe-wielding serial killer in this light-hearted puzzle game about decapitating men In New Detroit, authorities are hunting a serial killer they call Mr. Freezy after hundreds of severed heads are discovered hidden inside freezers across the city. Behind the murders is a mysterious woman kidnapping men and locking them away in her basement workshop. You don’t play as the victims. You help prepare them. Move, swap, and line up heads perfectly so they can all be taken off in a single slice. Every move matters as you try to collect the most heads possible in the fewest number of turns. But the deeper you go, the stranger things become. Different heads completely change how the puzzles work: Pumpkin Heads explode if not collected in time Stone Heads cannot be moved Old Heads move on their own Default Heads shift the position of nearby heads when moved Many More As new head types are introduced, puzzles become more chaotic Do you cash out early and collect a few heads… or risk more turns by trying to build the perfect chain? The more you play, the more you uncover about why these murders are happening and who this woman really is. Features Horror-inspired puzzle gameplay Fast arcade-style combo building Multiple head types with unique mechanics Creepy story elements and unlockable secrets Dark humor mixed with unsettling vibes Replayable levels built around score chasing and efficiency A weird little horror world that gets stranger the deeper you go Disclaimer This game is definitely darker than my past games. It’s still built around a really fun and satisfying gameplay loop first, just with creepy vibes layered on top. We’re a really small indie team trying weird ideas, so if you enjoy it please tell your friends about it. That support genuinely helps us keep making more strange games like this. Also: This game is very much designed to be played a couple of levels at a time before bed or when starting your day. It's way more enjoyable in small bursts. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains stylized violence, including decapitations, severed heads, and themes involving serial murder, kidnapping, and horror. The presentation is intentionally stylized and darkly comedic rather than realistic, but some players may find the content disturbing.
