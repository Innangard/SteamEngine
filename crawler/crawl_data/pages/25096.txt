View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to the market. Your money is fake. The pressure is real. Pixel Trader drops you into a lively stock market where every session is a new test of timing, discipline, and nerve. Prices move, news hits, positions swing—and you decide whether to buy the dip, take profit, or hold through the chaos. This is not a spreadsheet with a skin. It is a day-by-day trading game built around readable decisions: watch prices, place orders, close the day, and see whether your strategy actually worked. Trade your way, day by day Start with cash and a watchlist of fictional companies. Each day opens with fresh market conditions—calm trends, gap opens, earnings surprises, headline shocks, and platform fees that punish careless cash management. Learn when to trade aggressively, when to sit still, and when to cut losses before they cut you. Two ways to play Journey Mode — Campaigns with daily goals, bonuses, and rising market drama. Complete the assigned tasks and prove your strategy still holds on the final day. Endless Mode — No finish line. Build survival streaks, chase bigger gains, and see how long you can stay solvent when the market keeps coming. Built for players who want to feel the market Intraday trading sessions — live price movement and portfolio updates Multiple save slots — experiment without losing a good run Clear day-end reports — see what worked and what hurt Charming pixel presentation — keep the focus on decisions, not clutter Steam achievements — milestones you will brag about—or regret Learn without the real-world bill Pixel Trader is a simulation. The companies are fictional. The sting of a bad day is not. That makes it a great place to practice market thinking: risk control, patience, position sizing, and the hardest skill of all—knowing when not to trade.
