View Community Hub About This Game Build your own yacht empire! Yacht Mechanic Simulator lets you have a taste of luxury as you renovate, redecorate, test, and sell state-of-the-art yachts of various sizes. Renovate old motorboats and give them a new life. You can fix them, upgrade them, or even replace them entirely with better ones! Repair fractured hulls at your workshop and get to work on any damaged equipment. Edit, move, replace hundreds of different elements and improve your yacht. Turn your workshop into a proper shipyard! Cater to your clientele by creating gorgeous interiors! Manage decorative elements and turn your yacht into a temple of beauty. Earn money and start working on even bigger and even more luxurious vessels. Finally, test your yachts and see how they handle the water. Earn money and start working on even bigger and even more luxurious vessels. Features Several different yachts to work on. Various repair mechanics to patch the hull, fix the engines, or change batteries/cables. Plenty of parts to use, which you can either repair or purchase from a store. Test your yachts in a special dock. Career mode with story missions. Randomly generated missions. Grow your yacht fleet. Decorative elements that let you create both cozy and luxurious interiors. Paint tools and various different materials to use.
