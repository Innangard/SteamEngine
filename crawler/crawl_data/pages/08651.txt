View Community Hub About This Game Get the Stores Ready for Opening Megastore: Tidy Up Together is a cozy organization game about transforming messy stores into clean, carefully arranged shopping spaces, either solo or together in online co-op. Products are scattered throughout each store, and every item belongs in a specific department, category, and shelf. Follow the signs, identify where each product belongs, group matching items together, and place everything in its correct location before the store opens. Work together, divide the store between your team, develop an organization strategy, and gradually bring order to thousands of products. Four Stores Available The game currently features four fully playable stores of increasing scale, each offering a different organization challenge, product selection, and atmosphere. The Toy Store: Begin your journey in a smaller and cozier single-story store filled with colorful toys and summer essentials. Sort 3,555 items and place them across 297 shelves while learning how to identify, group, and organize each product. The Electronics Store: Organize an enormous electronics retailer packed with an entirely new selection of products. Sort 6,203 items across 477 shelves, ranging from gaming PCs, consoles, games, monitors, mice, keyboards, phones, laptops, and accessories to TVs, washing machines, refrigerators, dishwashers, vacuum cleaners, coffee machines, and other home appliances. The Supermarket: Tidy up a bustling supermarket packed with fresh favorites and everyday essentials. Organize 7,934 products across 487 shelves and prepare every aisle and fresh-food counter for opening. The Megastore: Take on the challenge of a massive two-story megastore containing 13,380 items across nine departments and 978 shelves. Organize the entire building and restore order across its huge variety of departments. Playable solo, but co-op is recommended. Play through all four stores solo or cooperatively, choose the difficulty that suits you, split responsibilities between players, focus on different departments, or work together shelf by shelf. Sort, Group, and Shelve Organizing each store requires more than simply picking products up from the floor. Pay attention to department signs, recognize product categories, group similar items together, and determine which shelf is appropriate for each product. As the empty shelves begin to fill and the store gradually takes shape, every correctly placed item brings you one step closer to opening. With more than 1,350 unique products and 65 different shelf types , the store contains a huge variety of items to identify and organize. Develop Your Own Strategy Progress through the skill tree and unlock abilities that increase the pace of the cleanup. Improve your movement and organization capabilities, choose upgrades that suit your preferred playstyle, and decide how your team will approach each store. The skills you prioritize can shape your strategy and help you complete increasingly large sections of the store more efficiently. Whether you focus on speed, carrying capacity, or other useful improvements, each upgrade helps you work through the store faster. More Stores Coming One additional store is already in active development and is planned for a future free update, expanding the game from four stores to five. Custom Levels Coming in a Major Update Custom level creation and sharing are also in active development and planned as one of the game's major updates. Players will be able to design their own organization challenges, share them with the community, and play custom levels together in co-op. Development is already underway, and more details will be shared as the update gets closer to release.
