Manage Your Shop: Design your own TCG shop. Organize shelves and card packs to make the customer shopping experience smooth and easy. Expand Your Business: Earn profits to reinvest in your shop. Expand your space, unlock new card sets, and upgrade your facilities to attract even more customers and increase revenue.
