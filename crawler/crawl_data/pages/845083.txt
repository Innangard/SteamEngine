It was also the 3rd busiest UK airport in terms of total aircraft movements, and the 24th busiest airport in Europe. The airport comprises a cargo terminal and three passenger terminals. Terminals 1 and 2 are linked by the skylink , with travelators to aid passengers with the 10–15-minute walk. Terminal 3 is linked to Terminal 1 and the skylink by a covered walkway.
