Multiple maps to choose from : Start your new realm in one of the two beautiful locations available. Organic cities : Forsake rigid grids and shape large cities that evolve naturally with a unique district zoning tool. For all walks of life Each district has its own needs and desires, depending on the people who live there. Some citizens will love a tavern, whilst others prefer the peace of a church. Advisors by your side With 9 companions, bring strength and knowledge to your city’s economy and story alike.
