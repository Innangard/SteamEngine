Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “I chose Early Access because Oboro no Ichi is an experience that grows together with its players. The core of the game is already solid: building, management, and the gameplay loop are fully playable. However, I want to expand the market with new content, systems, and possibilities, while always preserving the relaxing and cohesive tone that defines it. Community feedback will play a key role in finding the right balance between management depth and a cozy atmosphere. I want every addition to feel meaningful, allowing the game to evolve naturally alongside the people who play it.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I expect Oboro no Ichi to remain in Early Access for approximately 6–12 months. During this time, I will continue expanding the game with new content, systems, and improvements, working closely with the community. The timeline may adjust slightly based on player feedback and the direction of development, with the goal of delivering a polished and complete final version.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already includes the full core experience: building, management, and the gameplay loop are fully playable. Throughout development, I will continue expanding Oboro no Ichi with new content, systems, and possibilities, gradually enriching the market and its dynamics. The full version will include: -new interactions with spirits -expanded market customization options -new items and structures -deeper events and gameplay dynamics during market hours The goal is to make the game increasingly rich, polished, and cohesive, while preserving the relaxing atmosphere that defines it.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already includes the full core gameplay experience. You can build and manage your market, connect structures, produce and sell resources, and welcome spirits, all within a gameplay loop that is already stable and satisfying. The game is fully playable and offers various options for development and customization, while continuing to evolve with new content and improvements on the way.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The game will be available at a discounted price during Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Engaging with the community is a fundamental part of Oboro no Ichi’s development. I will actively communicate with players through Discord and other channels, gathering feedback, ideas, and impressions throughout the entire process. As a solo developer, I can quickly integrate suggestions and adapt the game based on what truly works, while always staying true to the original vision. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game [A Market Between Memories] Welcome to Oboro no Ichi , a market suspended between memories, where lost souls seek peace. Build, expand, and manage a spiritual market inspired by Japan, welcome wandering spirits, and shape a place that can grow alongside them. [Build and Grow Your Market] Freely design your space, connecting stalls, buildings, and pathways to create a lively and harmonious place. Each structure has a specific role: produce resources, supply your stalls, and grow your market one step at a time. (A Simple Yet Deep Gameplay Loop) Find the right balance between beauty and functionality. Organize production, manage sales, expand your market, and increase its appeal to welcome more and more spirits. A relaxing cycle that is easy to grasp, yet full of choices, strategies, and combinations. (A Calm and Cozy Experience) In Oboro no Ichi , there is no rush. Only the slow drifting of spirits, the sound of the wind… and a market that grows alongside them. Take your time.
