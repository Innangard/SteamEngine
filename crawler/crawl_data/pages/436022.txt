Placement is Everything Every building placement matters. Place structures to create powerful synergies that generate Souls - your primary currency. Poor placement leads to resource starvation, while masterful synergy chains keep your infernal construction thriving. Buildings unlock dynamically based on what surrounds them: Spider Queens emerge only next to Spider Nests, creating an evolving puzzle of possibilities. Never Truly Fail, Always Progress Running out of Souls? Transmute Hell to convert your entire domain into powerful relics, then start fresh with new advantages. The larger your previous hell, the rarer and more powerful your relic choices become. This prestige system ensures every run advances your ultimate goal. Key Features: Synergy-driven building puzzle - Every structure must earn its place Dynamic unlock system - New buildings appear based on adjacency Transmute mechanic - Convert failed runs into permanent progression Quick sessions - Complete satisfying runs in about 30 minutes Atmospheric pixel art - handcraft 8-bit hellish aesthetic with limited color palette Content: Approximately 5–10 hours of content across 5 Dark Lords, each with a different building list to complete and a unique playstyle.
