Visa gemenskapscentral Om detta spel Intro Anna hides from her mother in an old cursed house and willingly steps into the mystery trapped inside it. Decades ago, a Goldfish granted a couple of wishes and never returned to Lukomorye. So HE brought Lukomorye after her. Now Anna must explore the house, follow its old stories, and find the Goldfish herself, as she has a wish of her own. Gameplay Explore an old cursed house in first person. Search its rooms, collect useful items, solve puzzles, and use what you find to open hidden passages and uncover secrets. Piece together the house’s stories through notes, objects, characters, and the environment itself.
