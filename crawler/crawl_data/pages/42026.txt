View Community Hub About This Game Grow a realm that learns to protect itself Karma Keepers is a chill incremental strategy game about restoring wandering souls, building a Faith economy, and tuning a realm that gets busier as it grows. You start collecting Faith from Totems, restore souls before corruption reaches them, and choose the upgrades that open new jobs. Over time, more of the routine work can be automated, but the realm still needs your decisions when pressure builds. Automate the work, not the decisions Restore Lost Souls into Keepers, then spawn the roles your realm needs next. Some feed your economy. Some keep resources moving. Some defend the realm when things go wrong. Use Prayers and Extractors to move Faith and Aether through Totems. Use Recollectors to gather loose Faith before it fades. Use Keeper Warriors, Evangelists, and Sacred Walkers to fight, restore, and stabilize souls under pressure. Build a setup that keeps Faith flowing, stands up to Corruption Waves, and moves more souls toward becoming Eternals. Keep corruption from turning your realm against you Corruption Waves reduce the Karma of every soul in your realm. If a Lost Soul falls too low, it becomes a Shadow. If an Incursion begins, Shadows enter the realm and start breaking the setup you were relying on. You can intervene and help, unlock better roles, improve your Totems, or push upgrades that make the next wave easier to survive. A calm realm can become a problem fast if you ignore the timer. Push Karma tiers and break Demon gates Collected Faith becomes Karma through your realm's Influence. Fill the Karma Bar to earn Ascension Points and push toward the next tier. Higher Karma tiers bring Demon Incursions. Demons arrive with shields and block further progress until you deal with them. Break their shields, defeat them, and open the next stretch of the run. Use Blessings, Miracles, and Rebirth to scale faster Blessings improve the current realm: better Faith value, stronger Totems, new Keeper roles, safer Incursions, and more automation. Miracles are larger upgrades bought with Aether. They unlock new upgrade branches and improve whole systems at once. Divine Rebirth ends the current realm, keeps your Ascension Points, and lets you buy permanent Ascension Upgrades before starting again with a stronger foundation. For players who like automation with a little pressure Karma Keepers is for players who enjoy upgrade trees, resource loops, automation, and watching small systems grow into a self-running setup. Expect a calm loop with pressure spikes: guide souls, shore up weak spots before Corruption Waves, break Demon gates, and rebirth into a stronger realm.
