I urge you to give this game a go. Don't sleep on Hauntii, for goodness sake.” Rock Paper Shotgun About This Game Play as Hauntii, a naïve ghost on a journey to ascend beyond the afterlife. Explore with your angelic partner, solve challenges, and collect starry fragments to reveal glimpses of your past life. Use the unique Haunt mechanic to possess and take control over almost anything (even trees)! Dash, jump, blast, glide and shake your way through the intricate world of Eternity . Haunt As a ghost, use your haunting powers to command both creatures and the environment itself. Craft an array of solutions to puzzles, and devise various approaches to combat scenarios. Explore An expansive hand-crafted world, with tight yet open-ended environments. Whichever path you take, there'll always be something around the corner, waiting to be discovered. Collect Build the Constellations and uncover the story of Hauntii's past life. Find the pieces you'll need by exploring, solving challenges, and trading with other ghosts. The game employs a unique hand-crafted art style, making use of line art, animation and a striking two-tone palette. Meanwhile, Hauntii’s dynamic soundtrack sways and swells with the action on-screen, creating a captivating audiovisual experience.
