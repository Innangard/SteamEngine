View Community Hub About This Game Introduction Survival driver is minimalistic and low graphic vehicle game. There are 2 different bikes + 2 car. Sport bike which can drive fast and crossbike which player can for example jump and drive in difficult places like mountains. Game hasn’t realistic physics, there is no normal gravity in this game. Player target is to pass level in limited time. Otherwise player has to race track again. So drive fast or Die!
