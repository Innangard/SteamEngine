Let the madness begin! At the edge of despair, lurks the unsettling past, and an unspeakable truth. What lies hidden in the twisted attractions of the secluded amusement park, Yurukill Land? Who will emerge victorious from its cruel, fiendish games? Clear Your Name: Take on the nerfarious attractions of Yurukill Land for a chance to prove your innocence. Partner up with your Executioner and set your sights on emerging victorius from the Yurukill Games. For the victors, absolution awaits. Risk Your Life: Solve puzzles and uncover hints about the crimes you stand accused of, and put it all on the line in Maji-Kill Time—tense life or death cross-examinations. One wrong answer, and all is lost. As you progress, the grim truths of each incident will come to light. Take to the (Virtual) Skies: Pilot your Yurukill Fighter through a series of virtual reality challenges. Put your understanding of each case to the test in High-Speed Quizzes: choose the correct lane, and you'll be rewarded with extra lives. In the Prejudice Synapse, present evidence from your investigations, and destroy your Executioner's prejudices. Each top-down shoot ‘em up section (courtesy of veteran developer G.rev!) ends with a nail-biting Prisoner vs Executioner showdown. Whether you're a shoot 'em up expert or newcomer to the genre, find the perfect challenge to suit your level with a choice of Easy, Normal, and Hell difficulties. Run the Gauntlet: Choose your craft, set the difficulty, buckle up, and blast through shoot 'em up sections from the main game in Score Attack mode. Features global online rankings.
