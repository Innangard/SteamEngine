After installation After purchasing and installing the DLC, the PDF artbook will be in the file "Tiny-Garden_Digital-Artbook.pdf" under the root folder where Tiny Garden is installed. For example, on Windows 10, this folder would typically be "C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Tiny Garden". If you are unsure where Tiny Garden is installed, navigate to Tiny Garden in the Steam library, right click and select "Properties...". Switch to the "Local files" tab in the window that pops up and press the "Browse local files..." button to open the folder where Tiny Garden is installed.
