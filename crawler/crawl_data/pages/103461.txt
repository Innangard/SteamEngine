The Mighty Rar left behind a strange world of floating islands, fragile cities, dangerous ruins, disappointed citizens, and more unfinished business than any Chosen One should reasonably inherit. In Good Heavens!, you will explore procedural islands, fight bizarre threats, build a place of your own, and help the lands of Xant-Rar become slightly less broken than before. Probably... Adventure Together Play solo or team up with up to 8 players online as you explore, build, fight, and recover from Rar’s latest mistakes together. Whether you divide tasks across your group or roam side by side, co-op is built around shared progression, shared exploration, and shared chaos Raise Cities from Rubble Across Xant-Rar, struggling settlements can grow into thriving cities. Accept quests, fix local troubles, deal with angry or disappointed citizens, and help communities recover from the mess Rar left behind. As cities flourish, new opportunities open up. Meet strange inhabitants, unlock more of each settlement, access new city services, and become the savior these people were probably hoping would arrive a few Chosen Ones ago. Build a Home Worth Returning To Start small, gather resources, and turn the wilderness into a home of your own. Expand your base, arrange your workshops, set up farms, decorate your space, improve your equipment, and build the kind of settlement a true Chosen One deserves. Whether you care about cozy layouts, practical crafting spaces, farming, decoration, or chaotic co-op construction, Good Heavens! gives you room to build at your own pace. Shape Your Build Through Six Tech Trees Good Heavens! lets you grow through six tech trees instead of locking you into one fixed class. The three combat-focused trees are: Warfare - light weapons, heavy weapons, armor, and direct combat power Trickery - daggers, bows, mobility, and precision-based combat Sorcery - fire, ice, electricity, and magical specialization The three world-focused trees are: Homesteading - farming, irrigation, cooking, carpentry, and home progression Exploration - smelting, fishing, wayfaring, foraging, traversal, and world tools Influence - weaving, gem crafting, brewing, and upgrades that expand your gear and stats Fight, farm, fish, gather, craft, build, and explore to grow in the direction that fits how you want to play. Craft, Process, and Keep Growing Gather materials, process resources, craft better gear, and expand your capabilities as you push farther into Xant-Rar. From simple tools to more advanced setups, your progression is tied to what you discover, build, unlock, and bring back home. Smelt resources, cook meals, craft equipment, prepare supplies, improve your base, and keep expanding what you and your settlement can do. Discover Strange Biomes, Creatures, and Bosses Every island brings its own mix of monsters, cultures, resources, and local disasters. Push into dungeons, fight strange creatures, face candy-obsessed trolls, and prepare for bosses like Mountain King, a greedy troll who turned Rar’s enormous fork and knife into actual weapons. Survival With Room to Breathe Good Heavens! is built for players who enjoy discovery, building, and RPG progression without constantly babysitting meters. Food strengthens your stats instead of acting as a death timer, giving you more freedom to explore, build, craft, fish, farm, cook, fight, decorate, and experiment. Survival matters, but it does not constantly interrupt the adventure. What You Can Do in Good Heavens!
