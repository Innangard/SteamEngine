Vis samfunnssentral Om innholdet Become a Founder of Global MapleStory Classic World Global MapleStory Classic World revisits MapleStory as it was when it first launched, but with gameplay improvements, balance adjustments, and quality of life changes to polish the experience for modern players. Claim your Zakum Package today and show you were here from the very beginning. This limited-edition bundle brings you Founder's Access on Oct. 6, exclusive Founder's rewards, a striking Manji outfit set, a stealthy Thief outfit set, and several other nostalgic treasures to celebrate your legacy. (All items are purely cosmetic and do not grant any stats or bonuses). Zakum Package: Tradable within account, 30-day duration. Use to receive: Founder's Access on Oct. 6 : Unlock Founder's Access on Oct. 6, and start playing two weeks before the Grand Launch on Oct. 21! Mark of the Founder (Untradable, Permanent) : Show off your dedication with a stylin’ hat! You’ll receive all three versions of the Mark of the Founder hat. (All items are Untradable and Permanent) : Mark of the Founder (Black) Mark of the Founder (Brown) Mark of the Founder (Tan) Founder’s Effect (Untradable, Permanent) : Toggle a floating icon of a Maple Leaf above your character's head so everyone knows a true founder has arrived! You’ll receive all three versions of the effect. (All items are Untradable and Permanent) : Radiance of the Founder Glow of the Founder Glimmer of the Founder Jr. Balrog Invasion (x50) (Untradable, 90-day duration) : Use this to transform into the fearsome Jr. Balrog for 10 minutes! Intimidate friends and foes alike with this powerful disguise. (This item affects visual appearance only and grants no stat increases) . Well-Loved Outfit Set (Tradable within account, 30-day duration) : A complete outfit set evoking MapleStory’s early years! (All items included in this set are Untradable and Permanent) : Well-Loved Frozen Tuna Well-Loved Brown Bamboo Hat Well-Loved Pan Lid Well-Loved White & Blue Sandals Well-Loved Blue Sauna Robe Well-Loved Work Gloves Founder’s Thief Outfit Set (Tradable within account, 30-day duration) : A complete outfit set designed after the original Thief to help you channel your inner rogue! This set can be equipped by any Job (not only Thief) . (All items included in this set are Untradable and Permanent) : Founder’s Thief Hat Founder’s Thief Outfit Founder’s Thief Boots Founder’s Thief Dagger Founder’s Thief Face Founder’s Thief Gloves Manji’s Outfit Set (Tradable within account, 30-day duration) : A complete outfit set to make you look like the mysterious Manji! (All items included in this set are Untradable and Permanent) : Manji's Bamboo Hat Manji's Clothes Reticent Steps Manji's Blade Silent Sea of Clouds Founder’s Ring Coupon (Tradable within account, 30-day duration) : Contains two equippable rings, both Untradable and Permanent: Founder's Chat Ring : Equip this ring to display your chat messages inside a wooden sign bubble when you chat on a map. Founder's Label Ring : Equip this ring and your character name will appear on a weathered sign below your character avatar. Disclaimer: All package items are delivered automatically upon your first login to Classic World, with no manual coupon redemption needed. You can claim these items from your in-game mailbox until November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC . You will need to leave Maple Island before you can open your Zakum Package items, and once you claim it from your mailbox, you will have 30 days to open it. The Zakum Package can be purchased once per account on each platform (once on the Nexon website and once on Steam). Please note that a single purchase will grant all items and Founder's access. Content is only available in MapleStory Classic World . Frequently Asked Questions Q: Do the items in the Zakum Package grant any stat bonuses? A: No, all items included in the Zakum Package are purely cosmetic. They do not grant any stats, bonuses, or enhance your character's gameplay in any way. Q: Can any character equip the outfit set item? A: Yes, all items in the outfit sets--Well Loved Outfit Set, Founder's Thief Outfit Set, and Manji's Outfit Set--can be equipped by any character regardless of gender or job. There are also no weapon restrictions. Q: How do I receive my Zakum Package items? A: All package items are delivered automatically upon your first login to Classic World, with no manual coupon redemption needed. You can claim these items from your in-game mailbox until November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC . Note that the Zakum Package can only be claimed once per account, and you will need to leave Maple Island before you can open your Zakum Package items. After picking up your package, you will have 30 days to open your package. Q: When can I start playing? A: The Zakum Package includes Founder's Access. You can start playing on October 6, 2026. Details regarding the server launch time will be provided at a later date. Q: Can I play in Founder's Access with any Nexon account? A: No, Founder's Access is linked to the account that purchased the Zakum Package. Q: Which platforms and devices are supported? A: Global MapleStory Classic World will be available to play on both Windows and macOS. Q: Will my Founder's Access progress carry over to the Grand Launch? A: Yes, all in-game progress made during Founder's Access (starting October 6, 2026 ) will be carried over as the game officially launches on October 21, 2026. Q: Can I use the Zakum Package items outside of MapleStory Classic World? A: No, the contents of the package are only available in MapleStory Classic World. Q: Do the Zakum Package items expire? A: Yes, you must pick up the items from your in-game mailbox on Classic World before November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC . If not claimed from your mailbox, the items will expire. Please see above for the details on duration and tradability. Note: Outfit sets come packaged in a box that have a 30-day duration and are tradable within your account. Upon opening the box, the resulting outfit items are permanent and untradable but can be transferred to other characters on the same account and within the same world via the Cash Inventory. Q: Is there a deadline to purchase the Zakum Package? A: Yes, the Zakum Package is only available for a limited time! Once the sale window closes at 11:59 PM PDT on October 14, 2026 , the package will no longer be available for purchase. Q: Where can I purchase other packages? Are other packages available? A: Snail and Orange Mushroom Packages are available on our official Nexon website. Q: Is the Zakum Package the same on the Nexon website and Steam? A: Yes, the Zakum Package is the same on the Nexon website and Steam. Both packages have the same items and Founder's Access. You can purchase the Zakum Package on your preferred platform. Q: Who do I contact if I have an issue with my purchase or missing items? A: If you have any questions about your purchase, please contact Player Support on the official Nexon website. **Please refer to the Founder’s Package details on the official Nexon website for more detailed information** Systemkrav Windows macOS Minimum: OS: Windows 10 (64 bit) Processor: Intel® Core™ 2 Duo @ 3.0GHz or AMD Athlon™ 64 X2 @ 3.0GHz Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: GeForce 9600 GT or ATI Radeon™ HD 5670 Hard Drive: 60 GB available hard disk space or more DirectX®: Version 11.0 Network: Broadband Internet Connection Recommended: OS: Windows 10 (64 bit) or later Processor: Intel® Core™ i3 @ 4.0GHz or AMD Ryzen™ 3 @ 3.5GHz or higher Memory: 8 GB RAM or more Graphics: Nvidia GTX 1050, AMD RX 570 or above Hard Drive: 60 GB or more available SSD space DirectX®: Version 11.0 or higher Network: Fiber Internet Connection Minimum: OS: macOS Sequoia 15.0 Processor: Apple Silicon Mac M1 Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: Apple Silicon Mac M1 Hard Drive: 60 GB available hard disk space or more DirectX®: Metal 3 Network: Broadband Internet Connection Recommended: OS: macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later Processor: Apple Silicon Mac M1 or newer Memory: 16 GB RAM or more Graphics: Apple Silicon Mac M1 or newer Hard Drive: 60 GB or more available SSD space DirectX®: Metal 3 or higher Network: Fiber Internet Connection © 2015-2026 NEXON Korea Corp. and NEXON America Inc.
