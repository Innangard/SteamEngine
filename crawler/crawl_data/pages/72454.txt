View Community Hub Reviews “Diecast turns dice rolling into a dangerously addictive roguelite game” The Nerdy Type About This Game Diecast is a bag-building roguelite where every roll is a chance to push your luck. Build scoring hands, rack up points with multipliers and crit, and then decide whether to play it safe or keep rolling for something even bigger. Fill your bag with strange and powerful dice, then combine them with upgradeable scoring hands to create huge combos. Focus on 2s, build around Fibonacci numbers, chase prime numbers, copy other dice, or just throw a bunch of D20s into the mix and see what happens. Experiment until your scoring engine gets completely out of hand. Pins can completely change the way your dice work. Reroll bad results, multiply your score, transform your scoring hands, or create unexpected interactions between your dice. Stack the right pins together and discover synergies that get more ridiculous with every run. With dozens of pins to discover, there are plenty of ways to make numbers go very, very big. As you play, you'll unlock dozens of new dice and pins to bring into future runs, giving you more tools to experiment with and more ways to build a completely broken scoring engine. And once you've mastered the basics, unlockable game modes start bending the rules even further. New rules, new challenges, and some truly wacky surprises your grandma could never have dreamt of. You'll have to rethink how you play, and we promise it'll be weird .
