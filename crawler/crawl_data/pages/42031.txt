View Community Hub Roadmap About This Game You need a operational company to make operational phones. Use your limited funds, choose from renting more rooms or improving your working environment to attract more talented employees. You can also research more advanced phone parts to build your brand and accumulate funds. Your choice will determine the future of your company. Every employee will have their unique stats and abilities. You need to consider your selection and hire the most appropriate. There are many Easter eggs about IT culture in Mad Smartphone Tycoon waiting for you to explore. They will appear in the game in special ways, giving players full of surprises. Carefully design could not win you sales without marketing. You can customize your slogan, hold product launch, place various advertisements, even attack competitors, etc., and use all available means to win this business war! Just be careful not to spend money too willfully and cause yourself to go bankrupt... The game provides a wealth of industrial information and product data for players to keep abreast of market conditions at any time. You can use them wisely to establish new advantages. The game provides players with a comprehensive phone design page where they can customize every aspect of their device, from appearance like size, colour, material and camera, to hardware like processor, storage and battery. Dozens of options in total, all can be adjusted in detail, ensuring that you can create any phone you imagine! The design process is also strategic. You must make corresponding adjustments based on the company's own strength and the market. Taking a blind leap on configuration may create high parameters but low sales, you will lose your money. However, formulating reasonable strategies and choosing some cost-effective combinations may prove to be a more market-friendly approach for your phones. Mad Smartphone Tycoon is the second game I have made. I will keep on updating in the future. I am more than pleased to have your support and feedback.
