Get ready to experience the chilling horrors of Little Vale like never before! Helltown has been remade and drastically improved: New models and animations! Improved environments and level design! Improved gameplay mechanics! New dialogues and an improved storyline! New soundtrack by Ockeroid, composer of the excellent Crow Country ost! The year is 1959, you are a postman and you must deliver mail to a brand new town called Little Vale. Although the sun is shining and the birds are chirping, this town is shrouded in mystery. Something… unnatural is afoot here. Talk to the town's people and explore Little Vale to uncover clues as to what's behind this seemingly idyllic town. However, when all hell breaks loose, you'll have to run away and hide from insidious monsters! Who is that strange woman you see in your dreams? What does she want with you? What secrets are the Little Vale's residents hiding? It's up to you to find out by seeking out the 5 different endings possible!
