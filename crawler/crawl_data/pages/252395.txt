About This Game Stacker Puzzle is a minimalist Stack like game in which you need stack the blocks to reach the bonus line 1 and bonus line 2. You must complete 10 levels to finish the game. Each time you pass to the next level it's more difficult to reach the bonus line 1 and 2. In the level 9 and 10 you have an extra challenge and you will need to stack the block in sync with the moving green block. Believe me: It's hard to finish the level 10! Designed for people who like puzzle game with a touch of action and ability; You must to complete 10 levels to finish the game. Believe me, it's not easy as you can think; Enjoy a minimalist game with simple graphs to keep you focused on the challenge; The game was made with a lot of love and craftsmanship. How to Play On every level you will see a moving block on the screen the you need to stack on previous block; To make the current block stop to move you click the mouse any place on the screen; Every block you stack you win some points; If you stack blocks with the same colors you get even more points; At each 1000 points you increase your life counter; When you reach the first and the second green line your score increases a lot; At level 9 and 10 you will see a green block moving. To overcome these levels you must stack the current block on the previous one in sync with the green block. Will you have the ability to stack all the blocks and overcome the level 10? Give it a try and show yourself that you can.
