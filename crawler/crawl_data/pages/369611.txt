View Community Hub About This Game You’re an operator who is assigned to investigate a signal that is coming from an abandoned facility. For years, rumors have swirled: missing persons, unexplained noises, flickering lights, and the presence of something no one dares to face. You are packed with standard tools that will help you identify which room is the source of the disturbance. Ask the ghost questions using your notebook that will help you discover important information about the ghost. Search the facility's patient records on your computer for the discovered ghost name and year of death to learn more about the ghost's death and complete the investigation. A word is rumored that the place is a portal to another world where rooms are constantly changing. Use the CCTV system (located on the second floor) to report any anomalies before your reality fades away. Cursed We know that the ghost inhabiting the facility is a result of a death that happened inside. Use your cursed tools to determine the ghost room, then conduct your investigation using the notebook. Ask important questions that will lead you to find the ghost patient record. Noise Detection: Ghosts can hear every noise you make using your microphone. Hiding Spots: When you anger the ghost enough, it will start haunting. Only then, find and hide inside a hiding spot quickly! Be careful, they can still hear you! Voice Recognition: You’re equipped with a standard SpiritBox that detects the responses of the ghost! Using the SpiritBox, ask questions like: “Where are you?” “Are you here?” “Show yourself!” Anomalies: The abandoned facility has security cameras scattered all over the place. Use the CCTV system to detect and report anomalies. Object Hidden. Object Movement. Extra Object. and more. Dynamic Ghosts: A new ghost is generated with a different patient record each round. Each round, the ghost has a single type of evidence, such as fingerprints, spirit box responses, candle reactions, and more.
