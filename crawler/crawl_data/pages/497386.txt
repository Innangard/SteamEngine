What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “I chose Early Access because Bonefield is built to grow and evolve with its community. The chapter-based structure allows me to deliver continuous updates, refine gameplay and balance with real player feedback, and expand the game with new content and enemies over time. Early Access ensures that I can shape the experience alongside players while steadily moving toward a polished full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I plan for Bonefield to be in EA for 6-12 months. ” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan for the full version to include multiple chapters with new monsters, locations, and the continuation of the story. I plan to improve mechanics and features of the game, introduce a full lineup of launch weapons, expanded replayability, and make refinements based on player feedback. ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Early Access version includes Chapter 1, where you play as a private investigator following a whistleblower report that leads you to the abandoned town of Bonefield County, now a pine-filled wasteland. The first chapter is set in Grey Manor, the former home of a SparkTech executive, the medical technology company responsible for the county’s downfall. This chapter introduces the core gameplay loop, two discoverable weapons, and the first Tormentor enemy.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “I plan to gradually raise the price as new chapters, features, and content are added, reflecting the game’s growth and value as it moves toward full release. ” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I plan to involve the community by actively engaging through the Steam forums and my social media platforms. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Welcome to Bonefield, Montana, where the lines between reality and nightmare blur. This sleepy little Midwest town holds a dark secret, one you're hired to uncover as a private investigator from out of town. What seemed like a straightforward case takes a turn for the horrifying as nightmarish horrors come to light and start relentlessly pursuing you. It will take all your wits, instincts, and determination to survive the dangers of this twisted town and solve the case once and for all. Captured through the immersive lens of a bodycam, every frantic moment is brought to life as unspeakable horrors pursue you at every turn. BoneField is a bodycam survival horror where realism and tension are paramount. Find resources to stay alive, and combine them to defend yourself against the horrors of backwoods Montana, with firearms, improvised defenses, and everything else - if you can do it in real life, you can do it in BoneField. Are you ready to tackle the challenge and unravel the horrifying secrets of Bonefield, Montana? Bodycam Terror: Experience the dread and immersion of survival horror through the perspective of a bodycam. Every place visited in BoneField is realized with meticulous attention to detail, extending to gameplay  Every heartbeat, every quickened breath, and every split-second decision is captured in real-time, intensifying the horror and putting you right in the middle of the action. Nightmarish Challenges: Each chapter captures new horrors spreading across Bonefield County — different places, different threats, all equally relentless. In the stillness of Montana, you’ll fight for survival with whatever you can find. Weapons, tools, and instinct are all that stand between you and what’s coming next. Horrifying Realism: No easy stashes or safe rooms here. Every weapon, tool, and resource must be earned, crafted, or found in the environment. If it exists in real life, you can use it to survive in Bonefield —but nothing comes easy. Outsmart, Outlive: Whether it's a horrific medical experiment stalking you or other monstrosities determined to end the investigation in bloodshed, you will need to keep a cool head to survive and use all your wits to make it through. Twisted Reality: To find out the truth, you’ll have to survive through seemingly normal pieces of Americana. Each chapter of BoneField will open a new slice of Americana gone wrong — from a mountain retreat to the halls of Bonefield High School — where familiar places become hostile under your lens. Dark Mysteries: Everything connects together, but to unravel the secret of Bonefield, you'll have to stay alive long enough to crack the mysteries of each place the trail leads to. With every new chapter, the story expands. Each location hides clues, stashes, and secrets that piece together the truth behind Bonefield County.
