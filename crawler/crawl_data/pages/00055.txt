Someone should put them back, and there's no one else here. Almost no one. You don't know how long you've been working here, but you know what needs doing: over 200,000 balls are scattered everywhere, and you bring them back to their ball pits. Nobody told you why. Your Job Collect the balls. Bring them back. Fill the ball pits. Full ball pits unlock new abilities, and collected balls are your currency. Eventually you'll have enough for the key to the next area. The work stays the same, only the room changes. Tiles, water, endless corridors, a hallway with more doors than it should have. Tools The more balls you collect, the more equipment you can afford. A scoop or a flashlight. Everything you need to keep working. Observe Working Hours When your shift is over, the lights start to go out one by one. Then there is only one thing that matters: stay in the light! Because you don't want to find out what is waiting for you in the darkness. Flexible Working Arrangements No fear at all, the way it’s meant to be, or no second chances? You decide how dangerous your job gets. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: A horror game set in liminal environments inspired by the Backrooms concept. Contains jumpscares with sudden loud sound effects and extended sequences in near-total darkness. No blood or graphic violence, no sexual content, no nudity, no depiction of drug or alcohol use.
