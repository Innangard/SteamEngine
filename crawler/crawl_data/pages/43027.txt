View Community Hub About This Game NO AI WAS USED IN THE MAKING OF THIS GAME Tidy Up Together is a cozy organizing simulator about restoring your fantasy Adventurer's shop. An evil wizard has blasted thousands of your products all over the floor - and it's your job to put them back! Shop Restoration Item by item you'll build the shop back to its former tidiness. There's an incredible range of products ranging from potions to sushi. Those Greatswords really shouldn't be mixing with the pineapples... Multiplayer It's a mighty big puzzle to solve. Maybe some friends could help you out? Don't worry if you'd prefer to tidy at your own pace; the game is also perfect in single player! Abilities That wizard isn't the only one who knows some magic. As you tidy you'll be inspired to learn new abilities to help maximise your efficiency. Key Features Multiplayer - Invite as many friends as you like to help you out. Character Customization with multiple outfits. 3 Levels of varying sizes. Full Controller support. 650, 2500 and almost 10,000 items across 14 categories and three shops. Ability upgrades. No time limits (but you can see completion time at the end) and no game over. Tidy Up at your own pace!
