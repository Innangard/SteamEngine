View Community Hub About This Game The festive season takes a dark turn in Satan Santa , a first-person horror walking simulator where Christmas cheer turns into sheer terror. Alone in your family’s isolated home, you soon realize Santa isn’t the jolly figure you thought he was—he’s a vengeful, bloodthirsty monster. Explore and Survive: Follow the bloodstained trail through your home and into the snow-covered wilderness, uncovering chilling notes and clues about your family’s fate. All paths lead to a mysterious church, where the ultimate confrontation with Satan Santa awaits. Face the Horror: With no inventory system and limited resources, you must rely on your flashlight and weapon found in the environment to survive. Will you evade Santa’s wrath—or fight back in a desperate struggle? Features: Intense Atmosphere: Blood-splattered environments, sinister soundscapes, and Santa’s chilling laughter immerse you in a terrifying holiday experience. First-Person Action: Battle Santa and his horrifying reindeer in tense, action-packed moments. A Haunting Story: Discover disturbing notes that reveal Santa’s hatred for Christmas and his transformation into a monstrous figure. Immersive Gameplay: Dialogue and key events pull you deeper into the nightmare, culminating in an epic final showdown. This 20-minute horror experience is perfect for fans of dark, twisted tales and seasonal scares. Will you survive the night, or fall victim to Satan Santa’s wrath?
