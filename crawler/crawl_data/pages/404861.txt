On each level, you must find a key and reach the hatch at the very top, collecting scattered jewels and artifacts along the way. But be careful: dangerous monsters, sharp spikes on the floor, and traps that suddenly fall from the ceiling block your path! With each new floor, the game's difficulty increases, and the environment changes completely. Four unique biomes await you, each changing from one to the next: the bright halls of a classic castle, gloomy, dark dungeons, an industrial plant with working fans and flickering terminals, and a toxic sewer. To survive, you'll need to demonstrate incredible reflexes and foresight. Eight different types of enemies patrol the levels, and direct contact with one takes away one of your attempts. However, sometimes life-saving bonuses appear on floors: extra lives in the form of hearts and magic potions. After drinking a potion, the Miser King becomes invulnerable for a few seconds and can sweep enemies out of his way, earning bonus points. Game Controls: A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys — Move the hero. W / S or Up / Down Arrow Keys — Climb ladders. Space, W, or Up Arrow — Jump. P — Pause the game at any time to catch your breath and assess the situation. Esc — Exit to the main menu. Game Features: Classic arcade gameplay: Climb ladders, jump on platforms, and avoid obstacles. Randomly generated floors: The placement of enemies, spikes, and bonuses changes, creating a new, unique experience every time. Four visually distinct biomes: from a medieval setting to high-tech levels with glowing screens and code effects. Dynamic bonuses: Collect a variety of valuable items (6 in total on each level), search for exit keys, and use potions to destroy monsters. Steam integration: Over 30 achievements for the most dedicated players. Challenge yourself by completing floors against the clock, without a single kill, or on your last life. Stylish retro graphics inspired by classics, with vibrant pixel dithering effects.
