View Community Hub Add To Wishlist! About This Game Enter the Cat God Trial for Dried Fish Coco wakes up hungry when a mysterious flyer leads him to the Cat God’s maze trial. If he can pass the trial, endless dried fish await. Along the way, a tiny mouse friend appears throughout the maze, pointing the way and helping you gradually understand each puzzle’s rules. Push, Shift, Squeeze, and Blast! Study the positions of walls, boxes, pipes, and exits. In tight spaces, push boxes, adjust Coco’s position, and find the perfect spot where he can squeeze out of a pipe and explode right away! Fresh Hand-Drawn Mazes, Gentle but Clever Travel from sunny flower fields to midsummer rainforests , late-autumn ruins , and snow-covered wastelands . Each area brings a distinct atmosphere and new mechanics. Beneath the cute hand-drawn style is solid puzzle design for players who enjoy box-pushing, spatial planning, and elegant little brain teasers.
