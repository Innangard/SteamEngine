Enjoy a brand-new adventure featuring multiple chapters, meaningful choices, and an ever-evolving narrative. After the events of Mai Torori Sama Romansu , everything in Tameko’s life seems to be going perfectly: perfect attendance, straight A’s in Philosophy, a great friend group, and the love of his life—Trolley-San. Tameko’s class is preparing for the annual Philosophical Tournament / Cultural Festival… on the beach . Besides being one of the most philosophically romantic activities a couple can experience, the festival also includes a ritual tied to an old legend about eternal love and knowledge . But everything turns upside down when a new classmate enters the philosophy class: a mysterious mecha with a laser-focused goal, to take Trolley-San away from her. Now Tameko must juggle his romantic life and his studies if he wants to maintain his perfect record, keep his straight A’s in Philosophy, and protect his relationship with Trolley-San. How will Tameko’s story end? That’s for you to decide.
