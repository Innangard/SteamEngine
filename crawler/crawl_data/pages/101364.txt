Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Try the auto-battler on wheels Farm & Drive is a chaotic party game on one fenced farm. Up to four farm trucks race around behind shut gates. They cut crops for coins and ram each other, while the crew riding on the truck bed fight the rivals on their own. Between rounds you shop for crew. Between crashes you watch them work. The last team with a truck still in the match wins. What is in the demo The first three stages of a match: fight waves, pickup rounds and loot rounds. All 27 crew members , with their skills, stars and all 10 traits. Items. Grab components in pickup rounds and loot crates, then combine two into an item. Full multiplayer. 1 to 4 players online with Steam invites or a lobby code, in up to four teams. Bots fill any empty seat, and Solo puts you straight into a match against three bots. Drive, ram, harvest During a round you only steer. Everything else comes from where you drive. Drive through ripe crops to cut them. Your load piles up on the truck bed. Ram a rival to spill their load across the farm. Anyone can grab it. When the bell rings, every truck sells what it carries. Coins in the bank are safe. Crops on the bed are not. Build a crew that fights for you After each round the market opens. Spend your coins on crew and seat them on your truck. Fighters shoot, smash and trap rivals. Helpers harvest, plant crops, speed up the truck or cover the load. Collect three copies of a crew member to star them up, and mix crew from the same origin or class to switch on trait bonuses. Level your truck to fit more crew, but every level makes it heavier and slower. Your truck's health lasts the whole match and never refills. A truck at zero drives on as a slow scarecrow ghost that can still get in the way. The full game adds Longer matches with every later stage. Ribbons: three times a match, pick one of three cards that changes how your build plays. The demo shows one card per pick. Wave events that change the crops, the farm or the rules each round. More power-ups and hazards on the farm. Like the demo? Add Farm & Drive to your wishlist to hear when it launches.
