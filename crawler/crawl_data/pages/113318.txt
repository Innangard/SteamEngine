View Community Hub About This Game Polimines 2 combines Minesweeper and Nonograms for a captivating puzzle experience. Immerse yourself in a difficult game where logical thinking is key, and hidden patterns are everywhere. From the different layouts to each of the individual clues, every level has been meticulously hand crafted to be challenging and rewarding. Enjoy 60 levels, each with unique shapes and sizes. Every level in Polimines 2 can be solved without guessing. They have been tested numerous times to ensure that, unlike traditional Minesweeper, you never have to rely on luck to solve a level. With no timers to rush you and its minimalist aesthetic, you can take your time solving each level. Take a break from the noise and distractions and find your own rhythm as you progress.
