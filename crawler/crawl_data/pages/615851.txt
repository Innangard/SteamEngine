Download Diorama Builder - Noir City Free Download View Community Hub About This Content This expansion of DIORAMA BUILDER brings the diorama " NOIR CITY ", which will allow you to set up a central area of a city in NOIR style, representing the architectural style of the 1940s and 1950s. Quantity of pieces \ Series Below we will describe some of the details of the scenario that you will create in this diorama: COFFEE SHOP A beautiful café for that coffee or afternoon tea, in a relaxed and very chic atmosphere! In interaction mode, see who is inside enjoying the place. TAXI A London taxi, with all its charm, fully buildable, seats, steering wheel, doors, windows, lights, etc. Oh, and look how cool it is to see him walking around in interaction mode! METRO STATION The metro station is the gateway to this part of the city, with its posters and benches. A place that makes us think about how many stories have been passed around. TRAIN The metro train is an essential means of getting around in any big city. Here you can assemble it completely and in interaction mode, you will be able to see it in movement, with sound and lights. As with all other dioramas in DIORAMA BUILDER : THE MUSIC IS ORIGINAL and made exclusively for this diorama. When completing the diorama, animations will be automatically activated in the scene and INTERACTION MODE will be activated, giving you access to interact with it, clicking on several pre-defined places to see very cool animations. In INTERACTION MODE you can activate NIGHT MODE , turning on all the scene lights at once and thus being able to enjoy a very special view of the diorama. You will have new ACHIEVEMENTS for you to conquer!
