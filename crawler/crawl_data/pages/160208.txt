Traverse a vast and harrowing landscape together with your companion. Together you will follow tracks, fend off threats and overcome obstacles as you try to make your way home. About Paws : "Dark and grime, twist through the vines, Slip through perils with finesse and toil. Be two for strength, be two of mind, Find the way home to familiar soil." Paws – A Shelter 2 Game combines all the unique elements from the beloved Shelter game series to present a lovingly crafted story about loss, friendship and a search for lost love. Venture out in the world and make your way home. An amazing journey awaits you. Main features: A gripping, narrative experience in a fantasy setting. For the first time ever in the Shelter world, you play as a young lynx cub accompanied by a companion to help complete puzzles and overcome obstacles. New gameplay features include the ability to follow tracks using your sense ability. New original music from Retro Family. A whole new corner of the world of Shelter to explore.
