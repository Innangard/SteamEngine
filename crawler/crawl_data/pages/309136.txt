About This Demo Skeletons are invading and your only defense is a toaster and a chicken! Embark on a whimsical adventure through a land plagued by pesky skeletons! Uncover weird and wacky weapon synergies to fight back against the bony baddies. Along the way, you'll gather a rag tag group of heroes who will aid you on your mission- will you have the guts to stop the necromancer's plans and save the world? CRAZY WEAPON COMBINATIONS Discover an arsenal of crazy weapon combinations, from explosive toast to homing robot chickens, and customize your loadout to suit your playstyle as you wreak havoc on your enemies. ALL ABOARD THE TRAIN Transform your train into a formidable base of operations, also providing a sanctuary for the friends you meet along the way. FORGE UNLIKELY ALLIANCES Befriend a cast of intriguing characters, each with their own unique abilities and motivations, and recruit them to join your quest to defeat the necromancer. ENDLESS REPLAYABILITY Dive into procedurally generated levels filled with surprises and challenges, ensuring that no two runs are ever the same. Test your skills and adaptability as you strive to overcome the ever-growing threats that await you.
