Built on the island of Chek Lap Kok, Hong Kong International Airport is one of the world's premier international gateways, serving millions of passengers and acting as a major global cargo hub. With extensive connections across Asia, Europe, North America, and Oceania, VHHH offers an exciting blend of regional flights, long-haul operations, cargo traffic, and business aviation. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). Control three parallel runways while coordinating continuous arrival and departure streams, heavy wide-body aircraft, and one of the busiest cargo operations in the world. Efficient taxi management and precise runway sequencing are essential to keep traffic flowing smoothly throughout the day. From regional shuttle flights to intercontinental departures operated by some of the world's largest airlines, every session at Hong Kong International Airport provides a rewarding and challenging air traffic control experience. Features: Highly detailed recreation of Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) Accurate runway and taxiway layout Realistic terminal, gate, and parking assignments Diverse mix of regional, long-haul, and cargo traffic Newly constructed Terminal 2 is included, it's still under construction in real life.
