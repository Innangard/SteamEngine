View Community Hub About This Content Head to thriving Pennsylvania and start to build not only your railway empire but your investment portfolio! Travel across this fan-favourite map as you figure out how to complete tickets while also balancing your growing collection of Stock Shares in nine Railroad Companies. Of course, you shouldn’t be investing in everything you come across – pick your companies wisely, invest early and aim for those all-important endgame bonus points! That’s right! You’ll score bonus points for having the most investments in a company, and in the event of a tie, whoever invested sooner will win the tiebreak. You also have to compete for the Globetrotter Bonus! Complete the most tickets in the game to earn a whopping 15 points. INCLUDES: A NEW WAY TO PLAY – Strategise around building your investment portfolio, preventing others from getting ahead and building your railway network! FAN-FAVOURITE MAP – Pennsylvania is one of the most well-loved Ticket to Ride maps, and now, you can play it in the digital game! THEMATIC COSMETICS – A new map to master, beautiful tickets and artful card backs to suit the expansion. Set off for Pennsylvania today on Steam!
