Instead of commanding traditional units, you control the Modu , a highly adaptable native species of water-dwelling creature capable of growing, merging, and splitting their organic structures in real time . By strategically evolving different organs, you create custom units that adapt to any battle scenario. Use resources to grow your Modu starting with the heart, the most vital organ you must protect at all costs. You can expand your Modu by growing tiles off your body and placing new organs on top of them. As you play, you learn to grow more useful and specialized organs to face any battle. Split tiles into singular organ units to scout your enemies quickly and give yourself the strategic advantage. Split and merge multiple tiles to build your armada, combine your organs to unlock unique abilities, send them to war or merge back with your main body to stand your ground! In the single-player campaign, you fight for survival and liberation, unlocking new organ combinations and evolving abilities while commanding one of three distinct factions, but the battle doesn’t end there. Moduwar offers multiple game modes in addition to the single-player campaign: Multiplayer (Up to 4 Players) – Compete or cooperate in dynamic battles. Arena Mode – Test your best Modu builds in intense battles. Survival Mode – Face relentless waves of enemies in a fight for endurance. Classic Skirmish – Engage in tactical battles with customizable settings. Set in the rich and dangerous world of Arkadia, Moduwar immerses you in an alien ecosystem filled with awe-inspiring and terrifying creatures. However, Arkadia is under siege—two invading forces, the technologically advanced Humans and the ruthless Chimera, have enslaved the native Modu, turning them into living weapons of war. You are a Modu forced to serve the ERE - Earth Repatriation Expedition. Will you continue to fight under the ERE, or break the shackles and set yourself free? Your choices will determine the fate of all life on Arkadia, the galaxy and the Moduwar!
