Vis fællesskabshub Om dette spil Sort Them Ducks is a cozy organizing simulator where your goal is simple: pick up rubber ducks and place them on the correct shelves. Discover thousands of unique ducks, unlock helpful abilities, and transform a cluttered store into a perfectly organized collection—one duck at a time. A Cozy Duck Sorting Simulator Thousands of rubber ducks have been delivered, but they're completely mixed up. Pirate ducks, wizard ducks, astronauts, knights, chefs, dinosaurs, and many more are scattered throughout the store. It's your job to sort every duck into its proper category and bring order back to the shelves. No rush. No pressure. Just ducks. From Complete Chaos to Perfect Order Every shelf starts empty, and ducks are everywhere. As you organize the store, shelves slowly fill with colorful collections, making every section feel more complete. Watching the store transform from a mess into a perfectly organized display is satisfying every step of the way. Collect, Sort, Repeat Pick up ducks from around the store, identify their category, and place them on the correct shelf. The better you know your ducks, the faster you'll organize the store. Every correctly sorted duck earns money to help you improve your abilities. Upgrade Your Duck Sorting Skills Unlock powerful upgrades that make organizing even more satisfying. Carry more ducks at once, move faster, highlight matching ducks, reveal the correct shelf, automatically collect nearby ducks, and unlock even more abilities as your collection grows. Thousands of Ducks to Discover With over 4,000 unique rubber ducks, every shelf offers something new. Complete themed collections, learn each category, and enjoy filling the store with colorful displays of quirky ducks. Key Features 🦆 Sort over 4,000 unique rubber ducks into their correct shelves. 🏪 Organize a colorful duck store filled with themed collections. 💰 Earn money for every correctly sorted duck. ⭐ Unlock upgrades that improve carrying, movement, searching, and organization. 📚 Discover hundreds of duck categories and complete every shelf. 🎮 Relaxing, satisfying gameplay with no time limits or penalties. ✨ Watch the store transform from complete chaos into a perfectly organized duck paradise.
