About This Game Connect catapult freely and lay tracks to plan your multiple defense lines, activate elemental field domain, and damage and interfere with enemies. But note that this is the last line of defense of the homeland, and swear to guard the base until the last drop of blood! You need to design tracks and allocate vehicles according to the random monster nests generated in each level to deal with the threat of monsters. As the game progresses, more and more monsters and powerful bosses will come to the battlefield. How to adjust your tactics in real time becomes the key to your survival. Players will set off from a unique Main Base, defeat monsters as they move forward to randomly acquire a wide variety of items, and finally build their own genre system. Vehicles and Synergies, Super Modules and Immediate Orders, random combinations will produce countless wonderful chemical reactions. Every round was a new version that had never been seen before, and the rich Roguelike elements would make you never tired of playing. 1 + 1 > 2? 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 +... Of course it was infinite! 62 distinctive Vehicles 20 main bases with with different features 100+ unique Super Modules Dozens of mysterious objects that reverse the situation Several different styles and interesting bosses, Mummy Chick, Brood Queen, MagiBat ... Explore their unique mechanisms to the fullest More modes - [ Random Endless ] mode is now officially released, and you can compete with other players. Our leaderboard will show the highest scores of players around the world. Come and create your own strongest vehicle system and become a true Loop Master!
