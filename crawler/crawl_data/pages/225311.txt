Pick up an item on your left side, and now that item's ability will be used every time you move left! This simple premise is explored in depth, with many items and mechanical surprises to discover. Items hurt just as often as they help. Clover's Quadrants aims to be a difficult-but-fair experience. Players less experienced with Sokoban puzzles should expect a challenge! If you are a more seasoned puzzler, don't expect a cakewalk either. FEATURES 90+ puzzles with a wide range of difficulty Bespoke art for every puzzle Assist features Movement and parity helpers Visual hints for every puzzle Ability to skip any level at any time "Free Play" option to unlock all puzzles Colorblind accessible by default WHAT ABOUT UDLR_MODIFY? UDLR_Modify was originally created for the Acerola Jam 0 as a prototype with 20 puzzles, where it was among the most popular  games submitted. Clover's Quadrants is the full-length follow up to that game, keeping the best puzzles from the original while adding many more, adding new item types, and modifying some of the base mechanics.
