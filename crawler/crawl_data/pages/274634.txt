After recovering from injuries sustained in his last case, Morley returns to his office where he receives a visit from the enigmatic Lady Margarette Fordside, an English countess haunted by a tragic past. In this demo, you'll experience the beginning of the investigation as Lady Fordside engages Morley to investigate the unsolved murder of her daughter, which occurred on a fateful night 20 years ago. The police had closed the case by arresting scapegoats, but the countess is convinced that the real killer remains at large. What the demo includes: First-person investigation in detailed and atmospheric environments Puzzle-solving system and evidence analysis Immersive 1940s atmosphere with evocative soundtrack A taste of the suspenseful narrative that will leave you wanting more Experience the beginning of this 3-hour mystery and discover if you have what it takes to unravel secrets that have remained hidden for decades. The demo provides a complete introduction to the game mechanics and the opening chapters of this gripping detective story.
