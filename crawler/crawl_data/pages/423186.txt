You want to endlessly roll dice and battle folklore monsters? We got you covered. Roll magical dice, collect rare loot, nudge your fate in the right direction, slay folklore creatures and finally face the King of Undead, The Lich! With Die for the Lich, we set out to recreate the magic of playing a unique deck-builder for the first time. We replaced cards with dice that you need to pair with your gear and the game took on a life of its own, a curious blend of strategy, chaos, and pushing your luck! Will you strike the perfect balance between dice, weapons, abilities and trinkets? Do you wish to always roll what you need, then find a Lucky Die. Do you need custom made dice, then visit the Dice Fusion table. Do you want to re-roll 1s, then grab the Eye Patch ability. Do you think you are strong enough for a tougher battle, then seek the Blood Moon. The options are endless and there is nothing more exciting than having your own unique journey in a chaotic world.
