The interface, performance, controls, and visual presentation will also be improved over time. The exact scope and order of updates may change based on community feedback.» Hva er nåværende tilstand for versjonen i tidlig tilgang? «The current version includes a playable campaign with handcrafted levels, multiple endless modes, competitive online races, progression, and unlockable skins. The built-in Builder allows players to create, test, publish, discover, and rate community levels. The core systems are functional, although players may encounter bugs, balance changes, interface revisions, and ongoing improvements to online stability.» Vil spillet være priset annerledes under og etter tidlig tilgang? «I plan to increase the price moderately when the game leaves Early Access to reflect the expanded content and features. Learn the movement, find the cleanest route, and overcome obstacles across handcrafted worlds. MASTER THE MOVEMENT Jumps, dashes, gravity shifts, and other abilities change the rules as you move. Read the level, maintain your flow, and refine every action. TAKE ON THE CAMPAIGN Discover new mechanics and challenges across a series of handcrafted levels. Each world introduces its own obstacles, routes, and visual identity. CHASE A NEW RECORD Test yourself in endless modes with changing routes. React to incoming obstacles, push your distance, and improve your personal best. RACE ONLINE Join online races and put your movement, reactions, and route knowledge to the test against other players. BUILD YOUR OWN LEVELS Use the built-in Builder to create and test your own routes. Publish levels, discover community-made maps, and rate the creations of other players. The Builder is currently in beta and will continue to expand and improve alongside the game.
