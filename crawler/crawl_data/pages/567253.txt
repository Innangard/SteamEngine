With the music we wanted you to feel the excitement, imagination, wonder, mystery and fun of being a kid and cycling around on an adventure of epic proportions. It also needed to feel unique to Penfurzy island, where the game is set, in the late 1980s. So we created a sonic world as wonderful and crazy as the island itself - roping in our friend’s nine year old kid to sing punk vocals on the thrashy “I Wanna Ride My Bike”; making mad moody synthtronica for “Lazer Thunder”; stoner space dad beats on “Pontefract Rock”; the 8-bit sounds of the Magus video game console; and of course establishing the Knights And Bikes theme across the journey of Nessa and Demelza. ﻿ Track Listing 1 Theme from Knights And Bikes 2:55 2 Lost Parents 0:35 3 I Wanna Ride My Bike 1:50 4 Pontefract Rock 2:52 5 Pipes of Penfurzy I 0:42 6 Luxulyan Warriors 1:32 7 The King's Speech 0:50 8 Treasure Hunters 0:33 9 Caravans Roll 0:46 10 Laser Thunder 4:40 11 The Curse Awakens 1:26 12 Ancient Souls 2:43 13 Captain Honkers 0:43 14 Swinging Cycle 1:32 15 Pipes of Penfurzy II 1:28 16 The Bike Shop 2:13 17 Battle Kazoo (Disco Remix) 2:02 18 Charge of The Puffins 2:50 19 Heading Home 2:34 ﻿ Credits Artist: THE DANIEL PEMBERTON TV ORCHESTRA Composer: Daniel Pemberton System Requirements Minimum: Storage: 252 MB available space © Foam Sword 2019 You can use this widget-maker to generate a bit of HTML that can be embedded in your website to easily allow customers to purchase this game on Steam.
