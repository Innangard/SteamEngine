Content For This Game Browse all (1) Tricky Cat - Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game Step into the paws of a mischievous cat on a colorful journey in Tricky Cat , an engaging puzzle platformer that will test your wit and agility. Your mission? Jump across all the tiles without stepping back on them! Once you clear the tiles, a portal appears to take you to the next level. But be careful—one wrong move and you might fall, though you’ll get a second chance to bounce back! As you advance, the levels get trickier with new mechanics like rotating spike tiles and teleport tiles to keep you on your toes. With simple yet challenging gameplay, Tricky Cat is perfect for players seeking a fun, casual experience that grows more complicated as you progress. Key Features: Jump, clear, and survive as the cat leaps across vibrant, tile-based levels. Unlock portals to progress through increasingly complex puzzles. Navigate new mechanics like rotating spikes and teleport tiles. Enjoy a vibrant, colorful world with easy-to-learn controls. Second chances with respawn mechanics that keep you in the game. Fun for players of all skill levels—complete the game at your own pace! Do you have what it takes to help this clever kitty navigate all the levels?
