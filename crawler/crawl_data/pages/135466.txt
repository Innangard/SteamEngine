About This Game 10 chapters · 8-10 hours of co-op play · Requires two players, no solo mode Once Before Us is a cooperative puzzle adventure for two players. Communication and observation are the only ways forward - every riddle, mechanism, and clue requires teamwork and focus. Together, you’ll explore forgotten places, solve challenges that test both logic and connection. Two-Player Co-op – Built entirely around collaboration. Every puzzle requires both players to act, react, and think together. No solo mode. Challenging Puzzles – Each area introduces new ways to communicate and coordinate. Attention to detail and clear teamwork are key. Environmental Interactions – Push, pull, trigger, and combine elements to solve physical and logical challenges. Narrative Element – The players are guided by someone who speaks to them along the way. They don’t know who he is or why he’s helping - at least, not yet. Occasional Tension – While not a horror game, some moments may surprise or disturb. You and your partner are not just solving puzzles – you're uncovering what was once before you … and perhaps, what’s still ahead.
