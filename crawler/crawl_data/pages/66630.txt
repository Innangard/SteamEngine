Steal. Sell. Expand. Don't Get Caught. Steal valuable items at night, then run your shop during the day and sell your loot. Sneak into warehouses and other interesting places. Pick locks, bypass security systems, and search every corner for valuable loot before time runs out. Your inventory is limited, so every theft becomes a choice - grab the most valuable items. Every  mistake can attract guards, trigger alarms, and turn a perfect theft into a desperate escape. At your shop, every stolen item becomes profit. Use the money you earn to upgrade your setup, improve daily operations, and unlock access to new locations and opportunities. Fill your shelves with rare finds, and keep suspicious visitors distracted - sometimes with nothing more than a fresh donut. As your business grows, so do the opportunities. Unlock new districts, tackle increasingly dangerous locations, hire skilled thieves, and discover valuable tips from local contacts. Features Steal valuable goods from a variety of locations.
