Get on board with your friends and control your direction, timing, and gas to make it through challenging courses. In co-op, every move affects the whole team. To handle sharp turns, squeeze through narrow passages, and dodge moving obstacles, you’ll need to think together and act at the right moment. The secret isn’t farting harder. It’s farting together at the right time. WARNING: THIS GAME MAY TEST YOUR FRIENDSHIPS In Fart Together, coordination isn’t a suggestion. It’s a requirement. One player going the wrong way, reacting too late, or farting at the wrong time can send the entire raft off course. A group of friends that was perfectly calm a few seconds ago may suddenly find themselves questioning physics, directions, and each other’s abilities. If your group has a friend with a questionable sense of direction, suspicious timing, or a habit of saying “What did I do?”, this game may drive you a little crazy. Choose your friends carefully. The game may remember who flipped the raft, but it won’t remember who started the argument. RAFT PHYSICS — SORT OF REALISTIC The raft reacts to the force, direction, and timing of each player. We can’t say it perfectly simulates real-life raft physics. In fact, we probably shouldn’t say that at all. But while arguing about why the raft suddenly flew in that direction, you may find yourselves having some surprisingly scientific discussions. CHALLENGING PHYSICS-BASED COURSES Narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that throw your raft off balance will put your coordination to the test. Sometimes you’ll need to speed up. Sometimes you’ll need to slow down. And sometimes the whole team will need to make the right move at exactly the same time. A perfect maneuver can save the run at the last second, while one small mistake can turn everything into a hilarious disaster. ABOUT US Hi, we’re GamesMrkt . We’re a small independent game studio developing games since 2018 and working on projects that have reached millions of players across different platforms. We’re not new to games — we’re just a little too excited about releasing our first game on Steam. We built the first version of Fart Together during two intense and fairly chaotic months of development. Along the way, we tuned raft physics, designed courses, tested hippo farts, and used the phrase “fart direction” in work meetings far more often than anyone probably should. And yes, if you’re wondering, the original idea for the game came from the toilet. We won’t go into any more detail. Some creative processes are better left mysterious. We made Fart Together so you can laugh, shout, blame your friends, and eventually say, “One more try.” If the game gives you — and anyone watching — a few unforgettable funny moments, then our job is done.
