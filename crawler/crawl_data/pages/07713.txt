View Community Hub About This Game Lux Anima is calm and forgiving. You do not need perfect reactions or stressful micromanagement. Defend the core, grow your garden, and keep moving forward. In Lux Anima, every spirit that threatens the Sacred Core can also become part of your progress. Defeat spirits with your Light Ring, collect the seeds they drop, and grow flowers. Defeated spirits can also slow enemies, clear space, or give extra rewards. Every enemy you defeat helps your garden grow stronger. Your flowers are more than simple towers. They are the heart of your progression. They protect the Sacred Core and generate essence during each run. Use essence to improve your attacks, strengthen your defenses, upgrade the core, and unlock spiritual companions. The longer you survive, the more essence you gather. The more essence you gather, the stronger your next attempt becomes. When a run ends, spend the essence your flowers created on upgrades for your next attempt. Failing does not erase your progress. Each run brings you closer to the next prestige, where new powers and progression layers become available. In Lux Anima, you are always growing, even when you lose.
