What is the limit of our existence? Parallel Shift is a Pixel Art Platformer Runner game where you take control of King and Tess, two scientists stuck in a dimensional loop. Switch between dimensions, close the matching breaches and rewind time to escape the unknown. Inspired by late 90's videogames, the pixel art , the dark synthwave music and retro vibes of Parallel Shift focus on delivering an experience of rush and urgency, while presents a narrative focused in the solitude of the chase of knowledge and discovery. Featuring: Precision and Puzzle mechanics; Synthwave and Retro-futuristic visuals and music; Time manipulation interactions that impact the gameplay.
