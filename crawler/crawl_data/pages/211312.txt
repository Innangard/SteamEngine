[Ember Workshop] New side story: A story depicting the tumultuous past of the Iron Dynasty's Vitalus Vitara and the Ember Workshop. After the Battle of the Black Sun, many people were reported missing all over the desert, and a strange plague gradually spread. Some have even claimed to have seen immortal monsters whose bodies are half steel and half flesh. Some of the elders who have lived long say that this has something to do with remnants of Vitalus Vitara. However, these riots are a small matter for the sultans who are at war. This is even more true for Husnu, the Nasir Sultan. At this moment, he is more concerned about when the artisans of the Ember Workshop will be able to deliver new weapons and equipment. Cherry of the Ember Workshop has also recently been sending assignment letters to heroes from all over the world soliciting their help in some quests. Jewel Salvage - Salvage identified/unidentified jewels into materials for refining and fusing jewels. Jewel Refinement - You can refine jewels with an initial level of 1-3 in the game. Refinement increases the level of a jewel by 1 and can grant them more powerful attributes. Jewel Fusion - Randomly fuse two jewels of the same level into a higher-level jewel. [Child Rearing] Even amidst the war, the need to continue one's bloodline has never stopped. It's just that since the Battle of the Black Sun, the flow of time has become very strange for some. Some young people become old overnight, while some babies suddenly grow up into adults within a day. It seems that God or some other entity has stolen time away from these people. Some say it is a curse, while others call it a blessing. Tavern Girls: Now, after you confess your love to a tavern girl, you can further develop the relationship, and there will be a chance for her to bear your child after some time. Heir Development: There are male and female child characters with fixed avatars. At present, there are two different sets of avatars for each gender. There are a large number of parenting stories from pregnancy to adulthood, which can affect the character's attribute and skill changes. You can raise up to three children in each playthrough. Heir Skills: A total of four brand new sets of skills, designed according to the roles of warrior, support, mage and archer, along with an ultimate linked to the player. When using the joint ultimate, all children will use their ultimates at the same time (similar to Double Musou from the Dynasty Warriors series). [New Class - Among Legends: Xin Qi Ji] A heroic young man from the small village of Tai'an, grew up in a village ruled by outsiders, and so his heart was filled with resentment. He is a little reckless, and came to the desert unwittingly. He is good at counterattack skills and strengthening physique. He has unique skills composed of Profound Sword, Profound Mind and Demon Sword.
