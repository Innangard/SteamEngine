In it, you can set up, for example, a workshop for vehicles, a greenhouse for growing plants, a rocket with its launch tower, an exploration truck, a power station, a control tower, and much more! Quantity of pieces \ Series Below we will describe some of the details of the scenario that you will create in this diorama: WORKSHOP The workshop is the place where "exploitation vehicles" are repaired. It also contains a scanner to analyze the vehicle's situation and two arms that can, for example, charge the battery or carry out welding and repairs on the vehicle. ROCKET The rocket is the means of transporting people and cargo between Earth and the Mars colony. It has an underground structure for repairs and maintenance and an external tower for launches and landings. ROVER The rover is a small robot developed to carry out research and verification of the surface of Mars. It has a retractable claw and a powerful scanner to collect and carry out preliminary analyses of minerals and other components found in the soil. POWER STATION The power station is responsible for generating and distributing all the electrical energy needed for the colony on Mars. It has a panel movement system for perfect capture of solar rays. As with all other dioramas in DIORAMA BUILDER : THE MUSIC IS ORIGINAL and made exclusively for this diorama. When completing the diorama, animations will be automatically activated in the scene and INTERACTION MODE will be activated, giving you access to interact with it, clicking on several pre-defined places to see very cool animations. In INTERACTION MODE you can activate NIGHT MODE , turning on all the scene lights at once and thus being able to enjoy a very special view of the diorama. You will have new ACHIEVEMENTS for you to conquer!
