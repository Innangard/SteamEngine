Find new items and add them to your claw machine to create game-changing builds. Earn gold coins to upgrade or modify your items and unlock new unknown synergies. You will need to use every trick up your sleeve to reach the evil dungeon lord’s lair at the heart of the dungeon and reclaim your lost paw. The ultimate claw machine roguelike experience. Unique characters each with their different strengths and weaknesses. Multiple difficulty levels and unlockable lucky paws to customize your run. Break the game – go crazy with different claw and item synergies. Venture into mystery locations and discover treasures as well as strange and dangerous obstacles. Spend your hard earned coins in the dungeon's pachinko machines. Meet different characters who might help you on your journey.
