But it won't always be easy: you'll need to aim your throws well and launch them before the time limit runs out... Two Campaigns, Two stories Uncover Enid's story as you recover the forgotten fragments, or team up with a friend to unravel all the secrets of the two-player campaign. Each campaign has its own levels! Over 120 Levels to Conquer Each campaign features 4 worlds to explore, with over 60 levels each. But the challenge doesn't end there, as you can replay each level to improve your time and become the best! Epic Boss Fights! At the end of each world, you'll face a powerful boss, each with their own unique mechanics. Get ready to apply everything you've learned during your adventure to emerge victorious in these battles. Find the hidden collectibles and the secret levels! Find the hidden collectibles in some levels to unlock skins and customize your characters. Make sure to explore every corner: to play the secret levels, you'll need to light special torches...
