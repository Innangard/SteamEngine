Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Survive the endless swarm. Build until it breaks. Pick a ship, blast through hundreds of alien enemies, turn level-up cards into a run that melts the screen, and post it to the leaderboard. ENDLESS SURVIVAL, RANKED ON STEAM Survival has no last wave. Cross portals from biome to biome, stack multipliers and relics, and hold on for as long as your build allows. Every run goes to the Steam leaderboards, global and among friends, with the ship you flew and the danger level you picked. The board tells you exactly how many waves you need to pass the next pilot. Survival : highest wave. On a tie, the faster run wins. Campaign : furthest level reached. Kills : every enemy you have ever destroyed. 21 SHIPS, 21 WEAPONS Every ship flies its own weapon, its own ultimate and its own role, from the agile Zephyr to the siege-ram Fragua. The same cards build very different runs depending on what you fly. Unlock the fleet by playing. BUILD UNTIL IT BREAKS Every level-up offers cards; every boss, a shop; every world, artifacts and dark bargains. Push your weapons to their EVOLUTION and chain synergies until the run snowballs out of control. A 100-LEVEL CAMPAIGN Four worlds, each with its own sky, hazards and enemies. 10 bosses with their own mechanics: arenas shrink, safe spots move, and the build that got you there stops being enough. Raise the Threat level at your own pace. BETWEEN RUNS Unlock ships, climb an upgrade tree, earn cosmetics, chase 43 achievements and fill in the Codex. Take risk/reward deals along the way: cursed chests, sacrifice altars and a transit screen with boons and banes. KEY FEATURES Endless Survival mode with Steam leaderboards (global and friends) 21 unlockable ships with unique weapons, ultimates and roles 100-level campaign across 4 worlds, with 10 bosses Builds made of cards, artifacts and weapon evolutions Meta-progression: upgrade tree, records, 43 achievements and a Codex Full controller support.
