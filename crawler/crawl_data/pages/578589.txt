How would you like to experience your favorite genre of music in a new and exciting way? BandRoll creates an awesome mix of captivating gameplay and gorgeous visuals for you to play as you listen to songs you like. Multiple Difficulty Options Slide along the platforms at the correct time to get a higher score and complete levels. Each level comes with its own speed and challenge. If the easier levels don’t satisfy you, the harder ones surely will. Learn from your mistakes and improve your score with each try! Simple Gameplay The only keys you’ll need are WASD or arrow keys. You can move left to right and up to down on the platforms. And some platforms will require you to hold down the key. It’s simple, but remember, simple doesn’t mean easy. Competition Complete the levels and compare your score against others. Don’t fret if you’re not at the top, you can retry the levels you’ve completed to get a better score. Make sure you finish all the levels first to bring your total score up. Different Balls You can buy and use different types of balls with the currency you get from completing levels. Unique Neon Universes for Each Genre Experience different neon universes with the dynamic universe system that changes based on the genre of music! Electronic Rock Metal Classic Level Editor (Coming Soon) Put your favorite song into the game and play with the level editor in the menu. Share your custom levels in the Steam Workshop or download and play custom levels made by other players.
