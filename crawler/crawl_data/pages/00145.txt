A four-player co-op horror game that puts you in the shoes of the chief scientists of a research facility. Your sole objective is to carry out the self-destruct plan and escape the facility. PHYSICAL TRAITS In ZONE L.O.S.T., you don't choose a hero - you choose your limitations. The game features four characters, each with unique physiology: one can't see what's on the table, another can't squeeze through a narrow opening, and one is physically bound to their chair. Your body is your greatest debuff. INTERACTIVE ENVIRONMENT The complex is composed of objects governed by the laws of physics. These aren't just decorations, but the only way to compensate for your vulnerability. Use tables, chairs, equipment, and office furniture to create visual barriers. There are no designated stealth zones in this game - you create your own lines of sight using anything that can be moved, rolled, or knocked over. 5 COMPLEX LEVELS The entire complex is divided into five sectors. In each, you are assigned specific tasks: Complete tasks in each ZONE L.O.S.T. sector. Find and activate the Self-destruction Button. Evacuate before the ZONE L.O.S.T. complex enters the self-destruct phase THREAT WITHIN THE COMPLEX You are not alone in the halls of ZONE L.O.S.T. Creatures that have escaped from their containment zones are tireless. They react to noise and changes in their environment. You have no countermeasures—only physical interaction with the objects and the ability of your team to coordinate actions despite each individual's physical limitations. The self-destruction plan must be executed. The senior scientists must evacuate the complex.
