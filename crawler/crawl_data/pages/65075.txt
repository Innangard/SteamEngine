Humanity had a good run, Now it’s time to take back what’s yours. Dive into a dice-based roguelike deckbuilder where your tactical decisions, not luck, determine the fate of the planet. Build your own Invasion Build a growing alien force on a turn-based grid where every placement matters. Each turn you can acquire different units, artifacts, budges and chrono. Place your invaders strategically in the grid to enhance your scoring potential. Manipulate the Dice Dice aren’t a game of chance here, they’re your most powerful tool. With ‘Chrono’ you can strategically reroll your dice and restock the shop to draft your most vital units.  You can transform a bad roll into an unstoppable chain reaction. Use ‘budge’ to  manipulate your dice throw to build the exact combo you want. Even, align your dice in pairs, triples or quadruple to ignite devastating combos. Be the Commander Each commander brings a distinct strategic approach, specialized synergies and XP scaling possibilities unlocking new ways to dominate.
