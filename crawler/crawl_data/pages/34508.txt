Learn more Leaving Early Access: 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “In Early Access we would like to invite players to get to know the world of StarRupture and most of the gameplay mechanics we’ve planned for them. Basing on our experience from our previous title, Green Hell, we are huge advocates of working along with the Community to create the best gaming experience which - as a result - is almost tailor-made to our Players.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to make StarRupture stay in Early Access for about a year from its initial release. Our goal is to provide the best quality of the game to our players. We want to use the Early Access stage to cooperate with the Community on the feedback, fine-tune and polish the gameplay experience and - when we’re certain that we’ve achieved the best version of the game possible - leave Early Access.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In StarRupture in Early Access we intend to offer the majority of gameplay mechanics we’re planning for the full release to be polished and ready to enjoy. This will include an up-to-4-player co-operation mode in which you will be able to build, fight and explore a vast part of our rich open world. The full release is planned to contain more biomes, enemies, buildings and mechanics, along with a compelling end game. In Early Access we plan on constantly adding new content and enriching the gameplay along with close cooperation with our Community.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current stage of our game already allows for world exploration, gathering resources, building machines that mine, harvest, create and assemble. All of this, along with combat mechanics and survival elements can be played both in single-player mode and in up-to-4-player online co-op.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We reserve the possibility of increasing the price of the game as the game expands.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Gathering feedback is a crucial part of enhancing and tuning our previous title, Green Hell.
