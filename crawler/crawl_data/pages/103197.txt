Fight, explore, …and face narrative encounters brought to life through words and imagination. 🎯 Key Features Permadeath: every run is unique and final Procedural generation: layouts, items, and creatures change each time Narrative encounters: NPCs, quests, and lore told entirely through text 200+ creatures, 100+ spells, and countless items to discover 🎮 Free Demo Play the free demo now and experience a roguelike told entirely through words. 🏰 Special Rooms — Narrative Encounters Beyond the fast-paced roguelike exploration and combat, certain rooms transform into story-driven events.
