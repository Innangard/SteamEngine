The result is an experience that feels like a remaster of the original game. The Hive is a story-driven real-time strategy game with RPG elements where you command an insectoid hive mind. Build your colony, harvest DNA from defeated enemies, evolve powerful creatures, and lead your swarm through a vast, ancient underworld filled with forgotten civilizations, colossal creatures, and untold mysteries. Deep beneath the surface of New Eden, the ruins of lost civilizations reveal a tragic history. As your Hive grows stronger, fight your way toward the surface and uncover the truth behind your existence. MAIN FEATURES Evolve Through DNA – Harvest DNA from defeated enemies and decide which creatures and abilities to unlock first, shaping your swarm to match your strategy. Command an Insectoid Swarm – Take control as the hive mind and lead your creatures into real-time battles against the hostile inhabitants of New Eden. Build and Expand Your Colony – Gather resources, develop your underground nest, and raise increasingly powerful creatures. Experience a Narrated Story Campaign – Journey toward the surface, uncover the fate of forgotten civilizations, and discover the purpose of your existence. Survive Across Missions – In Survival Mode, your creatures carry over between missions, allowing you to develop veteran units whose survival becomes increasingly valuable. Level Up Your Creatures – Your units gain experience, grow in size, and become more powerful as they survive battles. Collect Powerful Loot – Equip your creatures with items dropped by defeated enemies, and challenge optional bosses for rare equipment that carries over between missions. Battle Colossal Creatures – Face massive ancient enemies and adapt your tactics to overcome increasingly dangerous challenges. Uncover a Lost World – Explore ancient ruins and abandoned settlements, discovering the remnants of forgotten civilizations and the mysteries they left behind. Journey Through Diverse Biomes – Travel from lush underground springs to fiery volcanic regions, each inhabited by unique creatures and filled with new challenges.
