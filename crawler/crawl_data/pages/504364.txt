A psychological horror adventure of investigation and deduction. ■ Intervene in the stream. Uncover the truth. Mashiro, your favorite streamer, is missing. Intervene in Tsumimi's livestream, investigate the clues, and uncover the truth. ■ As a viewer, use comments, Mega Chats, and gifts to change the story. ■ Investigate clues hidden outside the stream across fan sites, social media, and more. ■ Connect the clues, make your deductions, and uncover the hidden truth. By day, investigate strange details through social media, fan sites, maps, and search results. By night, watch Tsumimi’s stream and intervene through comments, Mega Chats, and gifts. What will you say? When will you stay silent? How will you use your limited funds? And how will you connect the fragments before it is too late? This is a 15-day psychological horror adventure about stream intervention, investigation, and deduction. 〜 Before You Play 〜 【Basic Controls and Official Guides】 ・Investigation and Deduction Use keywords you learn during streams to search social media and the map. Be sure to check replies to posts and location details as well. You can review the information you have collected in “Investigation Files.” ・Saving and Resuming You can save and load your game from the top of the home screen. Saving before entering a stream makes it easier to try different choices later. ・Fast-Forwarding When Replaying During a stream, hold down the Ctrl key to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice.
