You know that adventurer who spends the entire game picking up absolutely everything? That rusty sword he'll never use? The 37 potions he's saving for “later”? The coins he finds under a table? That book that's been sitting in his inventory for 80 hours? Well, he's back from his adventure. And now all that loot is your problem. Lootkeeper: Tidy Up the Loot is a cozy 2D organization game where you'll have to sort out a fantasy adventurer's inventory. Explore 24 rooms, organize 1834 items, and turn the inventory chaos of an RPG adventure into a perfectly organized space. Because someone has to do it. 1834 items. 24 categories. Zero excuses. Throughout his adventure, our hero has collected all kinds of things. Some have been useful. Others… probably never will be. Now you'll have to organize his collection into 24 different categories: Relics and treasures. Weapons and armor. Clothing and accessories. Coins and valuables. Food and provisions. Ammunition. Magical items. Books. And many other things an adventurer picks up along the way. 1834 items waiting to find their place. From the most legendary treasure to the most useless item he decided to keep, everything has a place. Sort the loot in 2D Lootkeeper brings the satisfying gameplay of organization games to a fantasy adventure viewed from a 2D perspective. Move between 24 different rooms , each filled with items you'll have to sort. At the center of each room, you'll find three shelves where you'll organize the loot. But be careful: not everything you find belongs in the room you're in. An item may belong to another category, so you'll have to observe, decide, and take each thing to the place where it belongs. Organize, sort, and enjoy watching the chaos slowly disappear. Magic and upgrades to speed up the work Sorting 1834 items by hand can be a huge task. Luckily, you won't have to do it all on your own. Unlock magic, upgrades, and power-ups that will let you organize the loot faster and more efficiently. The end of an RPG adventure In many fantasy games, the adventurer finishes their journey loaded with weapons, treasures, potions, and items they've collected over the course of countless hours. But… what happens next? What happens to all that loot when the hero finishes their adventure? In Lootkeeper, we take care of that part. The adventurer appears at the beginning to hand over everything they've collected during their travels. After that, it's up to you to organize it all. And when you're done, they'll come back to see if you've done your job properly. Because defeating a dragon is all well and good. But finding somewhere to store its scales is another story. Can you bring order to someone else's adventure?
