Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Fairy Keykeepers: Tidy Up Together is a cozy first-person game about sorting keys. A huge workshop is full of mess, and your job is to put every key back where it belongs. Play alone or share the work with up to four players. Simple work, big result Pick up a key. Look at it. Fly to the right cabinet. Put it in. There are 3,200 keys and a place for every one. Little by little, the pile becomes perfectly filled trays. Read the key Three things tell you where a key goes: its colour, the shape of its head and its construction. Match them to the cabinet and tray. You are a tiny fairy in a human-sized room, so even a book or a flowerpot feels enormous. Learn fairy magic Fill trays to earn upgrade points. Spend them at the spellbook to carry more keys, fly faster, find matching keys, light up the cabinet you need, pull keys from a distance or throw them straight into place. Bring the workshop back to life Sorting earns restoration magic. Take out your wand to revive wilted plants, repair broken objects and tidy up scattered things. Find picture pieces around the room and assemble the paintings. Earn surprises for the resident cat, then arrange its new things yourself. Tidy up together Open your workshop to friends and sort together in online co-op for up to four players. Everyone works on the same collection; the host's save keeps the shared progress. Work at your own pace, leave and come back to the workshop later.
