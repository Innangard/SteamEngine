Four Twenty Knight Genre: Action , Casual , Simulation , Strategy Developer: ScreamingPie Publisher: ScreamingPie Franchise: ScreamingPie Release Date: Oct 10, 2026 View update history Read related news View discussions Find Community Groups Embed Links & info VR Only Buy 429 $6.90 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Waking up trapped inside a twisted dream, your goal is simple: locate and destroy the Laughing Pumpkins before they mature into massive, terrifying pumpkin monsters. If you let them grow, survival will become a grim and brutal fight for your life! Explore unsettling rooms, shatter objects, fight off aggressive bug swarms, and defeat giant monsters in an immersive, physics-driven Virtual Reality experience. KEY FEATURES & GAMEPLAY MECHANICS: * HUNT THE LAUGHING PUMPKINS Navigate room by room to track down hidden Laughing Pumpkins. Destroy them early, or prepare for intense boss-level encounters when they transform into giant monsters. * PHYSICS-BASED VR COMBAT & DESTRUCTION Use your hands to grab nearly anything in the environment that isn't nailed down! - Smash objects together or break items against surfaces to create improvised tools. - Throw pumpkins, candies, stuffs, or objects at approaching enemies. - Swing items as melee weapons to ward off bug infestations and monster attacks. * SPECIAL WEAPONS & POWERS Discover unique weapons hidden throughout the dream world. Activate special abilities using the trigger/shoot button to unleash ranged projectile attacks or dynamically grow your weapons in size for maximum destruction. * HEAL WITH CANDY Keep an eye out for candies scattered across the environment. Eating candy instantly restores your health during combat! * VERSATILE VR LOCOMOTION & TELEKINESIS Play your way with fully customizable movement options: - Smooth Locomotion using the left controller. - Teleportation using the right controller. - Force Grab / Remote Pick-Up: Grab smaller items from a distance without needing to walk over to them (larger furniture like beds remain fixed).
