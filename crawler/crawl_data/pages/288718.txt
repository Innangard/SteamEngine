View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game BUILD YOUR MEGASTORE Start with $400 ; choose a department from butchery , clothing, toys, bakery, sports, grocery, or music ; buy your first shelves and products, open for business, and grow. EXPLORE 1,000+ UNIQUE PRODUCTS ACROSS 11 DEPARTMENTS Your starting department is just the beginning! Unlock over 1,000 products across 11 departments , from groceries, clothing, and electronics to toys, bakery, seafood, butchery, seasonal goods, and more. Keep expanding your megastore with new products and departments! KEEP EVERY SHELF STOCKED Monitor all departments, refill shortages fast, and don’t miss sales. DEPARTMENT-SPECIFIC FEATURES Some departments offer unique, hands-on mechanics! Bake fresh goods in your bakery, cut and grind meat in the butchery, and prepare seafood for your customers. MORE BOXES, FEWER TRIPS Forget carrying boxes one by one! Use hand trucks, pallet jacks, forklifts, and more to transport multiple boxes at once, reduce unnecessary trips, and make restocking faster and easier. GROW & EXPAND Expand your megastore in any direction by purchasing new sections. UNLOCK THE SECOND FLOOR Open the upper floor to offer more space to your customers. Place departments wherever you like and make sure everything stays accessible. MANAGE YOUR TEAM Hire cashiers, restockers, unloaders, bakers, seafood clerks, butchers, and more! Review job applications, hire the best candidates, and manage shifts to build an efficient team that keeps your megastore running smoothly. WAREHOUSE & LOGISTICS Build and manage a massive warehouse with 8 truck loading docks and cold storage facilities. Equip your warehouse with forklifts, reach trucks, pallet jacks, and pallet stackers to handle your growing inventory. Hire unloaders and restockers to manage deliveries, organize stock, and keep your shelves filled. Monitor your entire operation with stock control panels and warehouse displays as your megastore grows! AND MUCH MORE Attract more customers to your megastore with ads and product promotions . Bring your store to life with talking, reactive NPCs . Boost satisfaction with a returns desk . Buy recycling bins and turn empty boxes into cash . Decorate your megastore the way you like with department-specific decorations and furniture : fitting rooms, various wall and floor customizations, a bakery tray rack , and much more.
