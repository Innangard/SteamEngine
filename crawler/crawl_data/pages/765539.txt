Video Hole is an ongoing, episodic adventure game series revolving around a series of strange completely normal events that transpire in your local, intergalactic video store. In episode one, it’s business as usual as you navigate your soul-crushing job while solving mysteries and meeting new characters. Of course, things are never quite what they seem in the darkly mysterious world of cross-temporal, hard-format film rental. Game Features Explore a bright and colorful world around the only video store this side of the cosmos. Interact with a unique cast of characters with over 1,000 lines of unique dialogue. Uncover the mysteries of this mysterious business by completing tasks for mysterious strangers. Experience both an overt and ambient story by collecting items based on dialogue choices.
