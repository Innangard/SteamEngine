Because our game has been designed as a co-op experience for small communities, we cherish feedback that can help us refine co-op systems, roleplaying features, balancing, and usability as the game grows. By joining early, you become part of the journey to build the best possible version of Wyldheart together.' Hoelang blijft dit spel ongeveer in vroegtijdige toegang? 'We have a strong vision of what our game is, so our goal during Early Access is to add highly requested features from players and fine-tune the game based on player feedback. We hope to finish the Early Access period and release our 1.0 build at some point in 2027.' Hoe gaat de volledige versie verschillen van de versie met vroegtijdige toegang? 'In the full version, we plan to release multiple standalone campaign experiences to increase the length of the game and deepen the player's progression journey over time. The full version will also include additional game features based on player feedback.' Wat is de huidige staat van de versie met vroegtijdige toegang? 'Early Access currently offers the first campaign, three playable ancestries and 19 roleplaying backgrounds and skill trees to specialise in Sword, Axe, Mace, Bow and Staff techniques.' Zal het spel anders geprijsd worden tijdens en na vroegtijdige toegang? 'We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.' Hoe zijn jullie van plan de community te betrekken bij het ontwikkelproces? 'We plan on gathering player feedback across the Steam Forums, Discord, Reddit and other social media platforms to prioritise developing highly requested features and fixing critical bugs.' Communityhub weergeven Meld bugs en laat feedback voor dit spel achter op de discussiefora Alle discussies weergeven Over dit spel YOU ARE NOT THE CHOSEN ONE Start as a humble peasant tossed into a dungeon, tasked with finding one of the ancient Relics of Caerwyn to protect your town. Choose to be a proud Freefolk, a little Mossling, a towering Grimhorn or a stout Stonefolk, with 19 backgrounds and hundreds of customisation options to choose from! CUSTOMIZE YOUR COMBAT STYLE Quickly swap between melee and ranged weapons, casting spells, or throwing deadly concoctions during immersive, fast-paced action combat. Classless, fully flexible skill trees in Adventuring, Survival, Dungeoneering, Weaponry and the Oldways of Magic allow you to specialize or create clever combinations. HANG OUT IN HANDCRAFTED TOWNS Meet local villagers who all have lives, stories and schedules of their own. Reveal quests by eavesdropping, deciphering rumors, and exploring strange ruins. Gather materials, craft items, cook meals for your party, and upgrade your gear before continuing your adventure! SCHEDULING GAME NIGHT HAS NEVER BEEN EASIER Play solo, or up to 4-player co-op with seamless drop-in/drop-out. Cloud-shared saves, group XP, and scaling difficulty all mean the party can continue with someone offline, but nobody gets left behind. Designed for busy gamers, campaigns have about 10 hours of story, and 15 hours of optional content.
