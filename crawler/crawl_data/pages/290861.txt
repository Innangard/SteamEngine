Earth Heals after Devastation Strikes At the end of the '90s, a cataclysm marks the end of the world as we know it. Over numerous decades of humanity's absence, the Earth slowly recovers as nature finally reclaims its rights. Embark on an Epic Journey In this post-apocalyptic world, a solitary young girl stumbles upon an obsolete and defective robot known as a Mech-ute . Together, they embark on a journey guided by the sole information still programmed in the Mech-ute's deteriorated system: a set of geographic coordinates. Overcome Obstacles Together Our duo will have to team up to face various challenges to reach their destination. They will brave the elements, explore lush colorful landscapes, uncover relics from the turn of the 21st century and encounter a handful of peculiar individuals along the way. Throughout their quest, our duo's relationship will flourish in this heartfelt story that promotes one of the most precious things of all: hope . The remaining question is... what will they discover at the end of their journey? Engaging asymmetrical co-op gameplay — Control two vastly different characters to reach your destination. A unique hybrid form — Through the young girl, pilot the Mech-ute to unlock new abilities and create a unique cooperative experience. Special Abilities — Upgrade your Mech-ute to help you out of any predicament. Activate your Abilities Interact with the Environment Collect Lore Meet Cute Robots We are Tempete Studio, a small independent video game studio located in Poitiers, France. Our goal is to bring joy to people through our games! We previously collaborated with Libellud on Dixit World , the mobile adaptation of the popular board game Dixit . Building on this experience, we set out to develop our first original game. We wanted to tell a heartfelt story about a little girl and a big robot centered around hope and friendship.
