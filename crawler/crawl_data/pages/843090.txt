Defend Your Land is an idle auto-battler. Your hero fights on their own in the middle of the arena against ever tougher waves of bugs. You choose the weapon, the gear and what makes them stronger. Two heroes, two ways to play Warrior: 27 weapons, from a stone to a plasma cannon. Mage: fire, ice, lightning, poison and void spells… with their own worlds and bugs. Lots to upgrade Chests with weapons and gear in 10 rarities, from Common to Cosmic. Merge identical pieces at the blacksmith to forge a better one. Socketable gems, superboss relics and weapon evolutions. A skill tree with four paths and endless upgrades. Elves who craft weapons and gems even while you're away. There's always another challenge 4 difficulties: Normal, Hard, Nightmare and Hell. 40 worlds, each ending with a superboss. Challenge worlds with special rules and permanent rewards. 59 achievements, a bestiary with rewards, daily tasks and rebirths. Made to run in the background Offline progress: your earnings keep adding up even when the game is closed. A small always-on-top window or a strip along the bottom of the screen, so you can play while you work. Automate equipping, dismantling, merging and upgrading. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: The game's art (characters, enemies, items, backgrounds and UI icons) was created with AI image generation tools and then edited and cleaned up by the developer. Parts of the in-game text, the English translation and the code were written with the help of AI tools and reviewed by the developer. The music and sound effects are generated by the game's own code. The game does not generate any content with AI while it is being played.
