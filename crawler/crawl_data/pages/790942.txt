To survive in this mysterious land, you must tame creatures both ferocious and cuddly, craft high tech and magical tools, and build your own base… out of cubes! With a robust character creator, an infinite number of voxel based maps and procedurally generated quests, your PixARK adventure will be completely unique. Team up with friends to form a tribe, or play on your own. Spend your time building a towering fortress or go on a quest in a sprawling cavern. Fly on the back of a dragon and smite your enemies with a magic wand, or ride a mighty T-Rex and blast your foes with a rocket launcher. In the world of PixARK, how you play is up to you – as long as you survive! KEY FEATURES Play solo or play with friends! PixARK has a variety of PVP or PVE servers on top of being able to create your own. Gather your besties or make new friends in-game, however you want to play and explore is up to you! In PixARK there's many ways to play! Progress your character using skill trees and customizable stats to suit your playstyle, or build anything you can imagine from your wildest dreams in Creative Mode. In PixARK there are over 100 creatures to tame, train, and ride. Add our complex creatures to your brood and cultivate your team for ultimate survival. PixARK features a complex voxel block building system. You can create a plethora of items and customize your base to suit your personal style and adventuring needs. PixARK maps are procedurally generated and can be customizable based on your needs. If you don't like the world you live in, create a new "seed" and start again! Quests are also procedurally generated to create a variety of gameplay for each user!
