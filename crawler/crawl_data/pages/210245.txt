View Community Hub About This Game Without long-standing worship and offerings, the true deities have been forgotten by the world. Only the faintest remnants of their 【Remnant】 consciousness remain, dwelling within the dust-sealed halls. The cosmos is merciless—all beneath the supreme are but ants. Through cultivation, living beings ascend step by step beyond their physical forms. The world has long descended into madness amidst chaos. At this moment, beneath the Sumeru throne, the sound of sutra chanting reaches you, and you open your stone eyes. Awakening alongside is the resolve to seek vengeance against the heretics and abominations. Ascend to become the 【Fate Commander】, restore the fortune of humanity, and establish a sect devoted to your faith. To the panicked信徒 within the hall, you cast down your first gaze with lowered eyes. You will begin the game with the 【Remnant】 divinity. From guiding disciples to pioneer and explore the unknown, to dispatching them to slay demons and govern the sect. But the path to ascension as the 【Fate Commander】 is fraught with challenges. At each rank you reach, you will unlock a miracle, making each click more powerful. 30+ types of industries and functional buildings: A sandbox-style home construction system offers more choices. Transform the obscure and dilapidated mountain gate into a dominant Daoist immortal sect. 15+ production systems: Internal management combined with arithmetic technology enables automatic management of disciples and production lines. Idle management, gain benefits, easy AFK, and obtain resources even offline. While continuously unlocking new features and divine abilities, enlighten your disciples, turning ordinary individuals into peerless cultivators! As they expand territories, you will manage everything in meticulous detail. When disciples encounter the most vicious demons and monsters, descend and possess them to personally exterminate the enemies. Utilize miracles and your finely honed clicking abilities. In this fiercely competitive end-time world, through ascension rituals, you can unlock massive global bonuses and make the next world more prosperous. However, rituals also come with great costs, and they demand sacrifices and suffering without discrimination. Beware! This may attract the covetousness of ancient deities, and calamities will follow you into the next cycle. Ascension rituals bring various powers and authorities—please carefully choose laws and causality. But more importantly, can you select suitable sacrifices?
