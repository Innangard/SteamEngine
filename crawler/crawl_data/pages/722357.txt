Update dates and content are estimated targets and may change with development and community feedback. About This Game OPEN PACKS. COLLECT ABSURD GROMBOS. BUILD YOUR CARD SHOP. Turn a little card shop into your own collector's paradise. Open packs of hand-drawn creatures, hunt for the card your collection is missing, and decide which prized pulls to keep or put up for sale. Your next sale could pay for your next box. Unless you open all the stock yourself first. 60 shop levels · 4 sets with over 360 collectible cards · 3 store locations OPEN PACKS AND COMPLETE YOUR GROMBODEX Buy boxes, tear open packs, and discover an ever-growing collection of absurd Grombos. Keep your favourites, display valuable singles, or sell them to fund your next terrible financial decision. Look out for: Multiple card sets with their own themes and creatures Rare shiny cards Special premium sets Extremely rare Godpacks A Grombodex to complete Some packs will make you rich. Others will contain yet another copy of the Grombo you already own seventeen times. BUILD THE SHOP YOUR WAY Start with a cramped little store and work your way towards larger locations with more room, more customers, and considerably more things to worry about. Buy furniture and place it wherever you want. Arrange shelves, showcases, tables, decorations, and products to create a shop that feels like yours. There is no single perfect layout. Build an efficient card-selling machine, a cosy collector's paradise, or a complete retail disaster. RUN A SUSPICIOUSLY FUNCTIONAL BUSINESS Running a card shop involves more than opening everything yourself. Order packs, boxes, and accessories Restock shelves before customers complain Set prices for the cards in your showcases Sell unwanted cards in bulk Balance your profits with your own collection Complete requests and deal with themed days The cards you desperately want to keep are usually the ones worth the most money. Funny how that works. CUSTOMERS HAVE OPINIONS Customers visit your store to browse, buy products, open packs, and spend time in the shop. Their experiences can become reviews on Grooble Maps. Stock, prices, and the state of your business all influence what people think of the store. Try to earn glowing reviews. Or proudly become the worst-rated card shop in the neighbourhood. UPGRADE, AUTOMATE, AND GROW YOUR BUSINESS Invest your profits in the upgrade tree and automate part of the work to keep expanding your business. Every upgrade gives you another reason to open a pack. Hire staff to help run the shop while you focus on your collection. You can also use a compact window to keep the shop running in a corner of your desktop while you do other things. It may not always be good business. But there will be Grombos.
