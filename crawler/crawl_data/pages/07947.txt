View Community Hub About This Game Omelet You Cook is a  cozy chaotic roguelike where your cooking skills are put to the test! Create the perfect omelet by combining ingredients to meet the demands of quirky customers. Each round brings new hazards, restrictions, and ever-increasing score thresholds that will push your culinary creativity to its limits. Every customer has a distinct personality and unique tastes, so you’ll need to stay versatile and adjust your recipes on the fly. One minute you're crafting a classic bell pepper and bacon omelet, and the next you're tossing in donuts, pickles and pineapple. Keep your cool and keep those omelets coming! Omelet You Cook is created by two indie devs and draws inspiration from beloved arcade roguelikes like Balatro , Luck Be a Landlord , and Ballionaire . Feature List: 🐔 70+ customers that shake up every run 🥓 140+ delicious (and sometimes ridiculous) ingredients 🧂 90+ synergy-enabling helpers 🧀 A shop to expand your culinary arsenal 🥑 Unlockable ingredients 🌶️ 5 difficulties to progress through 🥗 13 pantries with different starting ingredient combinations Think you’ve got what it takes to become an overworked cafeteria worker? Alright then; Omelet You Cook .
