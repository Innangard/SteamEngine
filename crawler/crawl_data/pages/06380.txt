Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Steve is a busy alien, and they must collect and sort out human fruits, animals, storage or even planets. To do so, Steve will combine the same objects to create better objects, make more points, and make more money! In this demo, you can already play: Tutorial and 1 other arena 3 families of objects, including Fruits, Animals, Storage and Celestials 20+ out of 70+ objects 10+ out of 40+ passives are already available 2 Boss! Discover the ultimate physics deck-builder: Suika meets Roguelike Steve's Warehouse is inspired by the Watermelon game (Suika game) and rogue-like deckbuilding games (such as great games like Peglin, Luck be a Landlord or Balatro ). Physics will do its thing, creating both the most satisfying and the most funnily frustrating situations Experiment with object families and their strange effects Strange effects include animals that eat fruits, strange letters that rotate, and sports balls that create objects when they touch a wall. Each of the 8 families works singularly around physics or other objects! Spice things up with passives With more than 40 unique passives, discover various combinations to have a crazier effect: Transform every object into a car, make every object rotate, win points when using a bomb or TNT... Survive bosses and Challenges Each arena has a  boss with a special effect to give you one last challenge before finishing the game. Additionally, discover 7 unique challenges changing the game rules entirely!
