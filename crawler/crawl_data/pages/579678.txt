After losing his father and being laid off from work, Zainul is forced to find a way to support himself and his family. He decides to continue his father's business: a flower board arrangement service. At first, his days go by as usual. Taking orders, arranging flowers, and delivering flower boards to various locations. Until one night, Zainul begins seeing the ghost of a young child. The ghost keeps appearing and haunting him. It doesn't attack. It barely speaks. As if it only wants to show him something. The longer Zainul continues his work, the more strange events he encounters. Unreasonable orders, places hiding dark stories, and a mysterious child who seems to be connected to all of them. What is the ghost really trying to tell him? What is Zainul's connection to all these events? Gameplay Arrange Flower Boards Create flower boards based on customer orders or design your own creations.
