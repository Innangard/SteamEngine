Others wait until the right person finds them. Abyzou Rituals: The Manson Case presents itself as a psychological horror game. What you are about to access, however, has been reconstructed from a corrupted archive connected to the events of House 12 : degraded recordings, found-footage tapes, photographs, occult symbols, forbidden prayers, ritual objects, and fragments of a satanic ceremony that was never completed. Something happened inside that house. A secret cult gathered there. Blood was spilled. A ritual was performed. And something answered. The ceremony was interrupted before its final stage. The bodies were discovered. The cult disappeared. But the ritual was never destroyed. It was left unfinished. Waiting. THE FINAL PARTICIPANT You enter House 12 believing you are investigating an old massacre. That is what the archive wants you to believe. Search the rooms. Examine the evidence. Decode the symbols. Reconstruct the prayers. Follow the instructions left behind by people who should never have written them. Piece by piece, you will complete what the cult began. But the deeper you descend into House 12, the more disturbing the truth becomes. You are not investigating the ritual. You are performing it. Every symbol you decipher is another step. Every object you place is another offering. Every forbidden word you uncover brings the ceremony closer to completion. And every minute you remain inside the house brings Abyzou closer to you. ABYZOU WANTS TO PLAY WITH YOU Abyzou will whisper. She will follow. She will invade your thoughts. She will make you question whether the footsteps you hear belong to the game - or to the room behind you. And when you finally understand what House 12 was built to accomplish, you may discover why the ritual needed one final participant: YOU. THE PACT Every ritual has a threshold. Every invocation needs someone willing to cross it. In Abyzou Rituals , that threshold is deceptively simple. Wishlist the game: you acknowledge the ritual. Buy the game: you accept possession of it. Install the game: you bring it into your home. Click "Play": you enter voluntarily. Complete the ritual: and whatever happens afterward belongs to Abyzou. You may call it fiction. You may call it a game. You may tell yourself that nothing can cross a computer screen. The people inside House 12 probably believed something similar. Main Characteristics • A deeply disturbing psychological-horror experience built around satanic rituals, occult symbolism, forbidden ceremonies, demonic folklore, and sacrifice. • Explore House 12, the site of a massacre connected to an unfinished ritual. • Reconstruct a forbidden ceremony through symbols, ritual objects, recordings, and evidence left behind by a vanished cult. • A corrupted found-footage aesthetic designed to make the experience feel like something recovered rather than created. • Whispers, distorted audio, infant cries, footsteps, apparitions, and environmental phenomena blur the boundary between the house and the player. • Observation, quick thinking, and mental resilience are your only weapons. • The ritual does not begin inside the game. Clicking "Play" is part of it. • The longer you play, the closer Abyzou gets.
