Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game You are the perfect fit for the job! You've been hired to stock a mysterious dice shop. Pick them up, examine their colours and materials, and rebuild 400+ seven-piece  dice sets. Each set needs one of every classic polyhedral die: a percentile die, D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, and D20. Take All the Time You Need There are no timers and no need to rush. In Story Mode, the next day begins only after you have stocked the required value of dice. Search every corner, plan your sets, and work at your own pace while a cozy ambience and relaxing original soundtrack accompany every shift. Unlock New Skills As the days pass, mysterious floppy disks begin to appear. Use them to unlock strange new powers that make your work easier. Track down the remaining dice needed to complete a set, or pull them directly into your hand as if by magic. The disks are certainly useful. Who keeps leaving them for you is another question... Who Hired You? You were given a job, a shop full of scattered dice, and very little information about your employer. Keep completing your shifts, follow the clues left behind, and uncover the truth behind your unusual new workplace. Key Features Cozy first-person organizing gameplay. Pick up and stock thousands of colorful dice. ASMR, satisfying click-clack dice sounds. Story Mode with shift-based progression and no timers. Mysterious floppy disks that unlock special sorting abilities. Uncover the mystery and the secrets behind the shop. Chill Mode for one uninterrupted, story-free organizing experience. Magical dice, cozy ambience, a relaxing original soundtrack, and a cat.
