Train them, accessorize them, mash (breed) new generations, compete in events, explore randomized islands, improve the town, and discover new species as they grow. Thousands of combinations. Body color, eye color, body part shapes, patterns, sheens, stats, personality traits, etc will all change with each Bobo you find or mash! Distinct Bobo traits and moods will completely change how you interact and care for your Bobos. Bobos start their life as Saplings and grow into a Seedling, developing based on their stats, before finally growing to a full grown Bud . When the time is right, they will then Sprout , giving you the chance to let it evolve! Don't miss the chance to take down the legendary Bobo Stars . If your Bobo beats one of these blockheads then you'll be rewarded with a brand new sapling that is the same species as the Bobo Star. Watch out for those Phantoms... Trials: Scheduled competitions every day that any Bobo can enter. Make sure to come by at night to have your night owl bobos take on different competitions. Train up for relay races as well. Sagas: The main progression of Bobo Bay, where you complete all contests, in order, with the same Bobo. Completing Sagas will bring the biggest rewards, including new maps, new species of Bobos, and special town unlocks! Walk through a cute little town to buy accessories for your Bobos to wear, register a new Bobo, visit the doctor, drop them off at camp, purchase snacks for your Bobo to eat, or even adopt a Bobo from the shelter. Raise money for Public Works Projects to help open up new shops, renovate existing ones, and even buy new things for your gardens. Watch out for the alley at night... Take your state-of-the-art electric Bayfarer out on the open ocean to explore a fresh set of randomized islands each day. Scavenge rare items, race time trials, tackle platforming challenges, and uncover strange new shores no one has ever seen before. See you on Starling Isle!
