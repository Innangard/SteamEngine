View Community Hub About This Content V Rising: Legacy of Castlevania Premium Pack Introducing the Legacy of Castlevania Premium Pack in collaboration with Konami Digital Entertainment (KONAMI). This pack includes new cosmetics that allow you to take on the storied aesthetics of hair, clothing, and forms from one of the all-time classic Vampire franchises. Invoke style and indulge in your Castlevania nostalgia alongside the action-packed V Rising experience you’ve come to know and love. The Legacy of Castlevania Premium Pack contains: 5 Unique Armor Pieces With Alucard’s Regalia The complete set can be crafted in-game when you unlock the tailor bench. It costs cloth and coarse thread to make and is purely cosmetic. Using the new armor customization feature, you can don the garb of the Vampire Prince at any level of your Vampire journey. Sit on a throne that projects power, shadowed only by the great bat wings cresting each side of its towering frame. Unlock Castlevania Music Tracks Set the tone in your ominous abode with two classic Castlevania themes, “Simon’s Theme” and “Bloody Tears”, reimagined by V Rising composer Aleksandria Migova. This DLC unlocks the music to be played with the Music Box in your castle. Bloody Tears (V Rising Adaptation) Simon’s Theme (V Rising Adaptation) The items are unlocked when you research or discover equivalent tiers of items in the game as you progress in your adventure. For instance, you will gain access to the Soul of the Wolf variant after defeating the Alpha Wolf and unlocking Wolf Form. In the Vampire Powers menu, there should be arrows that allow you to change the cosmetic on any of the vampire forms if you have them.
