The maze you remember has been rebuilt from scratch. Return to the classic screensaver era, supercharged with new features for your enjoyment. Watch It, or Play It Auto Mode: Sit back and let it run. Zone out like it's 1997. Features an adjustable camera speed and other maze options. Play Mode: Take the wheel and navigate the maze yourself. No more wondering what's around the corner. Collect coins to level up and move faster! Two Bonus Game Modes! Horror Mode: Find your way out in the dark while avoiding the giant rat. Use your firecrackers to illuminate the spooky halls and scare away the rat. Rodent Rumble: Vacuum up as many rats as you can, build up a combo multiplier, and sell them before the timer runs out. Buy upgrades with each level up. Global Leaderboards Compete for the fastest solve time on every maze size and horror mode, and the highest score for rodent rumble mode. Weekly Competitive Leaderboards: Everyone gets the same seed for each game mode, see how high you can get on the leaderboards every week with a new equal playing field. Your Maze, Your Rules Swap the textures or upload your own images: your pets, memes, favorite photos, whatever you want on those walls. Load your own music to create your own playlist. Adjust the maze generation algorithm, change the size, crank the rat population. Steam Workshop 🎨 Join the community and share your custom texture packs for the maze! Easily browse and install any texture packs you like from the workshop page.
