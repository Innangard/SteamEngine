Each die comes with six sides containing their own actions and abilities that pair together, along with powerful relics acquired during a run, in order to dish out some devastating attacks and combos. Features Build your pool from a growing set of dice Strategize which dice stay, and which ones go as you only have 10 slots available to you at once. Each die over unique ways to win in battle. Forge your dice to your needs If a die just isn't feeling strong enough forge it to upgrade different sides or even swap sides on two dice in order to create the perfect one to suit your current needs. Explore the swamp Discover all that your homeland has in store for you, from dangerous traps to calm fishing holes and everything else in between.
