View Community Hub About This Game What must a chef entrusted with starting a new restaurant in London do? That's right, prepare a menu and come up with a name for the place. Except you've almost missed the deadline. Being out of ideas, you felt super-lucky to see an email offering to solve your exact problem. You clicked on the attached link, and then… Welcome to Wonderland's one and only mobile cafe. A long journey awaits you in the unpleasant company of the smug Cat. Fingers crossed, you'll find a way back home. But firstly, you have to feed the locals. Yet they are eccentric, capricious in their tastes, and tend to interfere in your cooking.
