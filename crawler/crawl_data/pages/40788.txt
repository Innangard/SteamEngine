Pilot Your Spaceship with Real Physics Master the art of physics-based flight. Slam your engines to full power and blast through space or execute delicate burns to thread impossible gaps. Go too slow and the Nova Wave will consume you, but too fast and you’ll lose control. Pause At Any Time to Plan Your Trajectory You’re in control of the pace. Pause at any moment to plot your path, adjust your thrust, and find the perfect angle – or fly by the seat of your pants in real time. Outrun the Nova Wave From the moment you launch, the Nova Wave is coming. Gather energy motes to upgrade your engines and push further into deep space, but be warned – the further you go, the faster the Nova Wave gets. Key Features Real Space Physics : your thrust, momentum, and velocity all matter. Dynamic Hazards : dodge asteroids, explosive mines, and gravity wells in procedurally generated sectors. Play At Your Own Pace : Race through sectors in real time or pause to plan your trajectory. Collect and Upgrade : gather motes to upgrade your ship and pick up powerful abilities to deploy later at the perfect time. Beat Your High Score : Fly as far as you can before the Nova Wave catches up and ends your run.
