Buy Log Away - Easter $1.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Let your Log Away retreat bloom with Easter warmth Come back to Log Away with a collection of decorations inspired by springtime comfort. This DLC adds Easter‑themed items and a mellow soundtrack that keeps everything soft, slow, and easy to return to. How it feels A flower basket rests on the table, painted eggs glow in candlelight, and a little bunny charm peeks from a shelf as music drifts through the room. A meadow painting hangs nearby, a watering can waits by the door, and tiny details, like a rabbit cup or a goose jar, make the space feel quietly cared for. Nothing to keep up with. Just a retreat that feels a little lighter. What you’ll find A collection of Easter‑themed furniture and decorations to mix into any Retreat: garden chairs, flower beds, shelves and cabinets with spring colors, a flower arch, cozy tables, and more. Smaller touches that bring the season close: egg lanterns, egg candles, carrot garlands, fairy lights, kids’ drawings, flower rugs, vases, branches, and a pet bunny companion to keep you company. A gentle seasonal soundtrack with a fresh, hopeful mood that sits in the background while you decorate, explore, or simply pause. A soft invitation The Easter DLC is here to deepen the feeling, not change the rules. No new goals, no timers to chase. Just another way to let your Cozy Heart fill, to add a hint of new life to your space, and to come back whenever you need a quiet reset.
