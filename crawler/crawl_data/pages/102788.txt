Each floor has a large picture with a magnificent painting representing landmarks of Dracula's life. Your goal is to rebuild each painting through a sliding puzzle and get to the top floor! The paintings are cut and shuffled in the jigsaw style, leaving the player to reposition the pieces. The castle keeps secrets Discover hidden items. Explore and discover secrets!
