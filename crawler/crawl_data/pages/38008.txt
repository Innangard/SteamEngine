The game is about pirates and cursed treasure. Choose a captain, gather your crew and defeat monsters by strategically placing units on a 5x5 grid. In the game, you embark on a thrilling adventure as a fearless pirate captain in this captivating strategy role playing game inspired by "Into the Breach" and "Go". Navigate the treacherous seas, assemble a formidable crew and set sail to collect precious treasures in the name of the empire. But beware, for the ocean is envious of those who hoard her riches, and the terrifying denizens of the deep will stop at nothing to claim what's rightfully theirs. Chase was tasked with trying to bring back the sound of the Caribbean for Pirates instead of perpetuating the Western and European sound of what Pirates have been in Hollywood, specifically the Disney film franchise, Pirates of the Caribbean. The strong thematic and historical association of where Pirates in the Caribbean originated has been lost until Chase took up the mantle to revive and incorporate the culture back into game music.
