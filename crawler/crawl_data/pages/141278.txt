Labyrinth: Dracula’s Lair is a dark, first-person exploration game set in a haunting medieval world. You play as a seeker of immortality, drawn into the depths of a forgotten crypt where Dracula once rested. At the core of the experience is the exploration of a vast and silent labyrinth, carved in stone, steeped in darkness and filled with ancient secrets. About This Game Silence is deceptive. Monsters lurk in the fog, hidden in the shadows — only revealed by forbidden powers granted through a boiling chalice of blood. They do not speak. They do not sleep. And they never stop hunting. As you descend deeper, you'll unlock sealed passageways, solve environmental puzzles, and use ancient relics to survive. There are no weapons. Just you, your instincts… and the darkness. Reach Dracula’s tomb and claim the secret of eternal life — if you're strong enough to survive what guards it. Key Features: A deadly labyrinth to explore Explore ancient corridors and sealed passageways in a forgotten crypt, and lose yourself in an oppressive labyrinth carved in stone, drowned in fog and silence. Ever-present monsters The creatures of the labyrinth stalk the fog, emerging from the shadows and relentlessly patrolling the depths. Powerful relics for survival Unlock temporary abilities such as enhanced stamina, improved mobility, or invisibility. Each relic grants a tactical edge — if you dare to use it. No combat, only survival No weapons. Outsmart, outrun, and outlast with only your relics… and your instincts. A slow, immersive descent into darkness From thick fog to ancient stone, each layer of the labyrinth draws you deeper into its secrets… and its dangers.
