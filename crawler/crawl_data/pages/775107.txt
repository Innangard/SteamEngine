A little robot named Josef is thrown out into the scrapyard behind the city. Help him find his way back, deal with the Black Cap Brotherhood and save his girlfriend Berta! World: The world of Machinarium is populated only by robots of various shapes and functions. One of the most remarkable places is the old, rusty and legendary city of Machinarium, where the game takes place. Story: Get rid of the wicked robots from the Black Cap Brotherhood and save the mayor and your robot-girl friend. Puzzles: Solve logic puzzles, adventure quests, brain teasers and mini-games. Graphics: Hand-drawn 2D backgrounds and characters, with more than an hour of carefully crafted animation. Music: A beautiful original soundtrack composed and recorded by Tomáš Dvořák aka Floex. Communication: No lengthy dialogue! Characters talk through comic bubbles with simple animations and symbols.
