Lihat Hub Komunitas Tentang Game Ini Step back into Andy’s room with Woody, Buzz Lightyear, and more classic characters in Toy Story: Retro Roundup! This five-game collection features console and handheld versions of four early Toy Story titles, as well as A Bug’s Life. All the games have been upgraded with new features, quality-of-life improvements, and an overflowing toy chest of behind-the-scenes content. Revisit the ‘90s and early 2000s hits you loved and share these titles with a new generation of Toy Story fans. Play Toy Story (1995), Toy Story 2: Buzz Lightyear to the Rescue! (1999), Toy Story 2 (1999), Buzz Lightyear of Star Command (2000), Toy Story Racer (2001) and A Bug’s Life (1998). Includes 11 total versions of the games across console and handheld. The games have been enhanced with quality-of-life improvements, including Rewind, Rex’s Cheat Codes, and more. Use Rewind to retry mistakes when you miss a jump across Andy’s shelves or take too many hits from the angry, wind-up robot. Unlock game-specific enhancements like unlimited lives or invincibility with Rex’s Cheat Codes. Step inside Digital Eclipse’s behind-the-scenes experience, featuring exclusive interviews withJason Katz – Head of Story, Pixar, Jim Hanks – Voice Actor, Luigi Priore – VP and GM, Disney & Pixar Games, and Jon Burton – Founder, TT Games, who helped bring these adventures to life. Explore a richly-detailed virtual museum filled with rare design documents, vintage development photos, and archival content. Listen to your favorite songs from the games in the Music Player, where we’ve rounded up the soundtracks from every game in the collection. FEATURES - Play five classic games, including four Toy Story games and A Bug’s Life. Includes 11 total versions of the games across console and handheld. - Enhancements and quality-of-life improvements make the games easier to learn and understand for gamers of all ages. - Use Rewind to fix your mistakes. Messed up a jump? Took too many hits? Get unlimited lives, invincibility, or unlock all the characters or levels. - Learn the game with enhanced How to Play instructions, then hone your skills in Practice Mode. - Listen to the Music Player, which includes the complete soundtrack for every game in the collection.
