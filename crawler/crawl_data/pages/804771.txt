View Community Hub Reviews “This is a very good horse racing game, with a solid and robust horse rearing simulator, alongside a story mode that keeps players invested throughout the experience” 4.5 – W.Kohaku Media About This Game Play Rival Stars Horse Racing for the ultimate horse racing and riding experience. Handed the keys to a rundown ranch, it’s up to you to restore your family’s legacy and become an equestrian legend! With the help of a host of colorful characters, you’ll raise charming foals, manage your dream stable of horses, and bring the ranch back to its former glory. Ride your way — take the reins to feel the thrill and thunder on the racecourse, hone your jumping skills on the Cross Country course, explore the ranch with your horses, and so much more. RACE AND RIDE TO VICTORY Feel all the excitement of the racecourse with realistic, motion-captured animations and dynamic race commentary. Race in story mode or online multiplayer in locations inspired by famous race tracks from around the globe. Put your jumping skills to the test in Cross Country and top the global leaderboard. Try your luck by virtually betting on races to earn great rewards. BREED YOUR DREAM HORSES Breed adorable foals with unique coats and patterns. Craft a legendary lineage with the in-depth genetic breeding system. Design your perfect horse with the Horse Creator and share them with the community. ENJOY THE EQUESTRIAN LIFESTYLE Relax and explore gorgeous open locations with your horses, from the ranch to the scenic Swiss Alps. Capture stunning images of your horses with the feature-packed Photo Mode. Earn and manage resources to upgrade your homestead.
