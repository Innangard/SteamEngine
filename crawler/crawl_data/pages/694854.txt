Combine puzzle pieces with operations like +1 or −1 to find the target number. The board may look like a complex circuit board at first glance, but the gameplay is very casual and relaxed. Playable in short bursts and perfect for a quick “brain stretch” during breaks. The World of Electrogical A distant future where interstellar travel has become possible... Several decades after humanity began large-scale terraforming projects, long years of operation and aging infrastructure have transformed the power reactors into labyrinthine "Power Distribution Dungeons." The only one who can resolve this crisis is you, a skilled engineer with specialized abilities. Rebuild the circuits and repair the power grid together with NEKO, a mysterious superconducting lifeform that can control electricity! ●Features: - 250+ puzzles with no time limit - 4-6 hours of gameplay - Many clever mechanics involving numbers - Simple mouse-only controls - No complicated calculations required - Hint system available when puzzles get tough - A mysterious world with a touch of dark humor - Plus, the superconducting lifeform, NEKO, is adorable!
