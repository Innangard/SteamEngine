Meet Leon, a half-human, half-fox, and his companion Rei, a sly naga. Between epic sword battles and rebellion against a ruthless empire, they find time to do what truly matters — crafting and serving perfect ramen. Cook ramen and unlock new recipes The perfect game for a laid-back long play: run it in the background and watch the lively bustle of your ramen shop. Unlock new recipes, earn gold and jade, level up, and return to collect rewards and take the next step in growing your small business. Decorate your stall Start with a simple counter, serve your first guests, and gradually expand your place. Change the stall itself, the roof color, decorations, flowers and plants, and even pets. Collect outfits for Leon and Rei as well as visual themes for the stall. Make your ramen shop truly unique and a reflection of your taste. Improve your time management The game includes a built-in Pomodoro timer that you can set to your preferred time. Start the clock and focus until the signal rings. Add your favorite music tracks to the player and create the perfect atmosphere for relaxation or productive work.
