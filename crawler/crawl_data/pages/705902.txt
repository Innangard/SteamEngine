View Community Hub About This Game WELCOME TO THE CHAOS NOW SORT IT. TURN CHAOS INTO PERFECTION Welcome to a tech Chaos where everything has gone horribly wrong. Phones, keyboards, consoles, laptops, cameras and thousands of other electronics are scattered everywhere and somehow, it's your job to sort it all out. Pick up the mess, figure out where everything belongs, and slowly transform a chaotic electronics store into a perfectly organized shop. The deeper you go, the bigger the challenge becomes. Over 5,027 items are waiting to be sorted and 5 abilities to unlock. Finish the store, earn your place on the leaderboard, chase better times, and challenge your friends to see who can conquer the chaos. One store. How long will it take you? Co Op Available Now!!! You can now Play with your friends Coop Mode Added For 2 Player and share the Chaos together achieve the target and share the Experiment and make the leaderboard score easier Abilities Unlock and upgrade powerful abilities as you organize the store. Use Magnet, Auto Place, Shelf Finder, and Scanner to deal with the chaos faster, while improving your movement speed and carrying capacity. And somewhere along the way, you might discover an ability that isn't so easy to find. Upgrade Yourself Progress through the skill tree and become faster and more efficient as you play. Upgrade your movement speed, increase how many items you can carry, and unlock improvements that make organizing thousands of products easier. Hidden Items & Events Keep an eye out while organizing not everything in the store is what it seems. Discover hidden items, encounter unexpected events, and uncover secrets scattered throughout the chaos. 50+ Unique Electronics Sort and organize more than 50 different types of electronics. From phones and laptops to consoles, keyboards, cameras, and more each product has its own place in the chaos. Leaderboards Turn organizing into a competition and prove you're the faster at cleaning up the chaos. Complete the store, set your best time, climb the leaderboards, and challenge your friends to beat your score. Available in solo/Due Leaderboards Dynamic Feel The store reacts to the chaos around you, making every item you grab, throw, drop, and organize feel satisfying. Watch the mess slowly disappear as the shop transforms into a clean and perfectly organized space.
