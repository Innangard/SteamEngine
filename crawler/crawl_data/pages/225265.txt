Travel across peculiar worlds and help her find the way back home. You are not alone on this journey. Elementalist is a challenging movement puzzle platformer, where you collect elemental orbs that give you powers, such as double jump, horizontal dash, ground slam, and teleporting shot. After you collect a power, it will be the next one you use, so be careful about the order of your actions. Some puzzles will require you to think outside the box. Collect movement abilities in an Elemental Stack. Use the most recently collected ability with just one button. And much, much more! The game has lots of content!
