Közösségközpont megnézése A játékról Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 is the brutal and fast-paced sequel to boomer shooter Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun. Following directly on from the end of Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun, your duty takes you to new locations in a branching, single-player campaign that effortlessly blends the grim, and dark Warhammer 40,000 universe with a fast-paced first-person shooter. Lock in as one of two playable characters, each offering their own unique abilities, weapons, and play style. Dominate combat as the unrelenting force that is Malum Caedo, a formidable Sternguard Veteran with a reputation for purging heretics. Or quickly take control of the battlefield as the swift and nimble Sister of Battle Celestian, Nyra Veyrath, torching enemies and leaving only ashes in her wake. Take the fight to never-before-seen worlds and vanquish the foes that lurk there. From the colossal heights of a hive city to the impenetrable mangrove swamps of a jungle, and even to the heart of the Ark of Omen… You’ll need to overcome the challenges of each location to complete your mission. Built on the explosive combat of Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun, charge headfirst into glorious battle armed with an arsenal of deadly weapons. Shred enemies with your lethal chainsword, blast them into an explosion of pixels and blood with your Shotgun or set them aflame with a torrent of liquid fire from your Flamer! As your arsenal improves, so do the enemies! Eviscerate enemies from the four Chaos god factions (Nurgle, Tzeentch, Khorne, and Slaanesh) in glorious, visceral fashion. The ferocious Bloodletters and their daemonic Juggernauts are a near unstoppable force as they charge into battle hungry for blood! While Daemonettes gracefully leap around the battlefield between the Noise Marines and their devastating sonic weaponry. Features Two Playable Characters – Play as the formidable Malum Caedo or battle-hardened Nyra Veyrath, each with their own playstyle. Explosive FPS Gameplay – Obliterate foes in high-octane combat. Branching Campaign – Choose your own path through the campaign with each branch featuring unique levels. NEW Enemies – Face never-before-seen foes from different factions. NEW Weapons – Eviscerate hordes of relentless heretical scum with devastating weapons. NEW Locations – Discover a range of worlds and biomes. Navigation Tools (Optional) – Effortlessly maneuver across the battlefield with the navigation guide and automap so you’re always at the center of the combat. Mick Gordon Guest Track – Annihilate the final boss to the sound of Mick’s sick riffs. AND MORE!
