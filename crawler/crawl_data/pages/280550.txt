A brand-new homeowner wants just one thing: a pet. A dog, maybe. Or a cat. What he gets instead: two moles. What follows is a chain reaction of questionable decisions, dubious animal diets, overly enthusiastic digging sessions, an ever-growing appetite (for both moles and owner), and a mysterious organization that seems to have waited far too long for exactly this moment. As strange tents appear in the backyard, neighbors suffer from sleep deprivation, and a hole emerges beneath your feet that gives geology the middle finger, one thing becomes clear: This isn’t a pet problem.
