Get ready for an exciting new DLC that adds 5 new USA tanks from World War II to the base game. We are continuing our goals that we set to ourselves on improving the quality of workmanship. The DLC includes iconic tanks such as the M4A1 Sherman (Early production), M4 Sherman (Mid production), M4A2 Sherman (Late production), M4A3E2 Sherman Jumbo, and T34 Calliope. These tanks come equipped with new engines, including the Wright Continental R-975-C1, General Motors 6046, and Ford GAA, adding to the authenticity and realism of the game. Additionally, the DLC introduces a new gearbox, the Spicer Synchro Mesh Gearbox, which can be used for all these tanks. Each of the vehicles has been painstakingly recreated on the basis of the collected technical documentation. The interiors have been nicely detailed with radios, dashboards and more removable parts. Prepare to immerse yourself in the excitement of United States Army's World War II tanks with these new additions to Tank Mechanic Simulator. Whether you're a history buff or just love the thrill of renovating powerful tanks, this DLC is not to be missed. ▶ M4A1 Sherman (Early production) The M4A1 Sherman (Early production) was a crucial tank used by the United States during World War II. It was a medium-sized tank that was highly versatile and adaptable to different terrains and combat situations. It had a crew of five and was equipped with a 75mm main gun, as well as a .50 caliber machine gun and two .30 caliber machine guns. Its armor was thick enough to withstand small arms fire and shrapnel. ▶ M4 Sherman (Mid production) An iconic tank from World War II that played a crucial role in the United States' military campaigns. This medium-sized tank was equipped with a 75mm main gun and two .30 caliber machine guns, making it a formidable opponent on the battlefield. Its thick armor and mobility allowed it to traverse various terrains and combat situations with ease. The M4 Sherman (Mid production) was widely used throughout the war and was praised for its reliability and versatility. ▶ M4A2 Sherman (Late production) A powerful tank used by the United States during World War II. Equipped with a 75mm main gun and two .30 caliber machine guns, this tank was designed for combat and proved to be a formidable opponent on the battlefield. Its improved diesel engine and thicker armor provided superior mobility and protection, allowing it to traverse various terrains and withstand enemy fire. The M4A2 Sherman (Late production) saw action in many significant battles and was highly praised for its performance and durability. ▶ M4A3E2 Sherman Jumbo A heavy tank used by the United States during World War II. This tank was designed to provide additional protection for its crew with its thick armor and improved gun mantlet. It was equipped with a 75mm main gun. “Cobra King” was the first tank to enter the Bastogne perimeter in relief of the besieged American 101st Airborne Division. ▶ T34 Calliope Introducing the Calliope, an American rocket launcher tank used during World War II. This unique tank was equipped with a barrage of 4.5 inch rockets mounted on its turret, allowing it to rain down explosive firepower on enemy positions. The T34 Calliope proved to be a devastating force on the battlefield, capable of quickly neutralizing enemy defenses and providing vital support for ground troops. ▶ Second Supply “Shermans” DLC includes 5 vehicles: M4A1 Sherman (Early production) M4 Sherman (Mid production) M4A2 Sherman (Late production) M4A3E2 Jumbo T34 Calliope ▶ New engines: Wright Continental R-975-C1 (Early, Mid, Calliope) General Motors 6046 (Late) Ford GAA (Jumbo) ▶ New gearboxes: Spicer Synchro Mesh Gearbox ▶ Additionally each vehicle contains packs of new museum stands, posters, ammunition and achievements. 16 posters 5 museum stands 5 achievements (for each DLC tank) Special decal "Cobra King" for Jumbo ▶ Tank ammunition: AP - 75mm M72 (Early, Mid, Late, Jumbo) HE - 75mm M48 (Early, Mid, Late, Jumbo) AP - 114mm M8 (Calliope) System Requirements Windows macOS Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 7 (64-bit) or newer Processor: Intel Core i3 3,20GHz / AMD Phenon 955 Quad Core Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: GeForce GTX 570 / Radeon HD 6970 DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 8.1 (64-bit) or newer Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600 Memory: 16 GB RAM Graphics: 4 GB VRAM  (GeForce GTX 980 / AMD Radeon R9 Fury) or better DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 1 GB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
