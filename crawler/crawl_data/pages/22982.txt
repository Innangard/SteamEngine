Following our participation in Steam Next Fest last October and our recent Closed Beta Test (CBT), we believe the game has reached the quality level required for Early Access. Currently, players can enjoy two Casual game modes: Team Deathmatch (2 maps) and Bomb Defusal (4 maps). The game also features a Weapon Parts Customization System, allowing players to unlock weapon parts through account and weapon progression and customize weapons to match their preferred playstyle. The game also includes a player-to-player Marketplace, allowing players to freely trade eligible in-game items. This system is designed to support a player-driven economy and provide additional long-term engagement." Erken Erişim sırasında ve sonrasında oyunun fiyatı değişecek mi? "No. We intend to continue operating the game as a free-to-play (F2P) title even after Early Access. However, if there are significant changes in exchange rates, we may review our regional pricing. If any adjustments are made, we will notify our players in advance." Topluluğu, geliştirme sürecine nasıl dahil etmeyi düşünüyorsunuz? "We are already actively monitoring player feedback through our official Discord community. As we did during our previous CBT and Steam Next Fest, we will also continue gathering player feedback through Steam Community and discussion forums. Community feedback has already had a direct impact on the game’s development. Based on feedback from previous CBTs and Steam Next Fest, we increased the maximum frame rate and reduced the time required to aim down the scope when using sniper rifles, improving the overall responsiveness and feel of sniper gameplay. Throughout Early Access, we will continue reviewing player feedback alongside gameplay data and player behavior. Based on community responses, we plan to continuously adjust weapon balance and overall game balance, while also prioritizing bug fixes, performance improvements, matchmaking, UI and UX improvements, and other quality-of-life changes. We are also considering adding new game modes based on the types of gameplay and content that players request during Early Access. Community feedback will therefore help us determine which features, modes, and improvements should be prioritized in future updates. While not every individual suggestion can be implemented, frequently raised concerns and well-supported ideas will be carefully evaluated and may directly affect the priority and direction of future updates." Step into the intense battles of Sudden Attack Zero Point, a free-to-play FPS that delivers a fast-paced shooting experience. Experience diverse combat styles through a distinctive gun customization system.  Dominate classic bomb defusal missions with your tactics, and stay tuned for more modes coming soon. Don't forget to explore the Black Market to trade items that you earned through gameplay and collect the items you need for your arsenal. Tailor your weapons to match your style and team up with friends to secure victory! Fast-Paced Combat Nowadays, FPS games can often be overwhelming and time-consuming. We understand that not everyone can indulge in all-day gaming or wander around in an unnecessarily large map only to be eliminated in seconds. Sudden Attack Zero Point offers quick, action-packed battles, allowing you to enjoy intense gameplay and fully embrace the thrill of combat without unnecessary delays. Customization For Victory Unleash your creativity with an extensive weapon customization system, letting you build the perfect gun for your playstyle! Customize various gun attachments to your liking, including weapon recoil, reload speed, appearance and more. Through gun customization, even the same weapon can offer a unique combat experience, allowing you to enjoy diverse and exciting battles. Stand out on the battlefield with a weapon that's truly yours! Classic Tactical Experience Engage in strategic combat focused on bomb defusal modes, where teamwork and tactics are essential for outsmarting your opponents. Each match demands precise coordination and communication to ensure victory. In addition to these thrilling bomb defusal modes, a variety of other game modes await. Strategize with your team and dominate your enemies! Trade & Gear Up! Participate in the Black Market, where you can trade weapon skins, attachments, and other items that you earned in the game with fellow players. Upgrade your weapons and customize your setups to suit your preferences, giving you a competitive edge. With the right modifications, you can truly dominate the battlefield with your favorite weapon!
