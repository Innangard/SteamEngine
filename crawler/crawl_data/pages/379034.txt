About This Game Supercat Survivors is a cute cat-style Survivor-like game—call your girlfriend to play and save Meow Star from crisis! The game offers multiple modes to choose from: Fuse two Supercats for battle in Fusion Mode, push the limits of survival time in Survival Mode, or fight against endless monster waves in Endless Mode. Between waves, upgrade and buy weapons/items to strengthen your Supercats and test their combat limits! Cute Style – Purrfect for Sharing with Your Girlfriend Who can resist sharing this adorable cat-themed game with their girlfriend? Tired of bloody battles and dark dungeons? Here lies a sanctuary of pure feline charm, waiting for you and your loved one to enjoy together, experience cozy cat moments, and lead your Supercats to defeat monsters and save Meow Star! Story – Crisis on Meow Star On the spirit-energy-rich Meow Star, Supercat warriors guard the planet’s lifeblood—the Gemstone Ore—while battling mutated plants for survival. These plants corrupt the land with invasive roots and devour energy with parasitic spores. The cats fight back with spirit-powered weapons, living tense yet peaceful lives. Suddenly, chaos erupts! Alien beasts invade Meow Star, mutated plants go berserk, and the Supercats are caught in a pincer attack. A sinister alliance between the invaders and local mutants hides a secret buried deep in ancient ruins… Unravel the conspiracy—Meow is Justice! The fate of Meow Star hinges on one final battle! Multiple Modes – Fusion, Survival & Endless Fusion Mode : Select a primary and a secondary Supercat to fuse. The fused Supercat inherits all traits of both, with over 1,000 combinations for ever-changing combat, growth, and strategy! Survival Mode : Collect weapons and items quickly through 10 waves. Compete to see whose build strategy lets them survive longer in the final survival stage! Endless Mode : The fight to save Meow Star never ends! Unlock it after clearing 20 waves in Classic Mode to push your Supercat’s combat limits! Classic Mode : Challenge 20 classic monster waves across multiple difficulties and enjoy your own combat rhythm! More modes in development... Survivor-like – Pure Combat Thrills Meow~ Each battle features multiple waves of enemies, lasting about a minute per wave. Control Supercats' movement—their weapons auto-attack, transforming cute fluffballs into ruthless killers. Between waves, upgrade gear and push their power to the limit! Roguelike – Deep Build Synergy Meow~ 35+ unique Supercats, 190+ items, and 45+ weapons offer endless Roguelike combinations. Each Supercat has strengths and weaknesses—mastering synergies and growth paths is key! Unlock more characters, weapons, and items through achievement challenges. Come collect all Supercats in your Encyclopedia! Lick your paws, unsheathe your claws, and face the infinite!
