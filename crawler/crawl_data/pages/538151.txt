View Community Hub About This Game Venture deeper into a dark, ever descending abyss in search of your deepest desires. Your party fights automatically as you progress, striking down enemies with relentless attackscharging mana and unleashing powerful abilities. The deeper you go, the greater the danger… and the reward. Your characters gain experience through combat, allowing you to level up their stats and increase their power. Defeat monsters to earn gold, then visit the blacksmith to upgrade and forge stronger equipment. Every decision shapes how far your team can push into the darkness. Embrace the cycle of power through the Reborn system. Reset your progress to gain powerful permanent upgrades and push even further on your next descent. Master the prestige mechanics and break past your previous limits.
