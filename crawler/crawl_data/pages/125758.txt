View Community Hub About This Game Meet the Auctioneers and the Bidders Dive into the thrilling world of high-stakes auctions, meticulous restoration, and profitable retail in Salvage Shop Simulator. Your journey begins as a humble shop owner turning discarded container cargo into a thriving business empire. Move the Goods Your inventory comes from one place: exciting container auctions. Each massive metal box has been salvaged from the deep, holding untold potential. With only 10 seconds to peek inside and make a split-second evaluation, you must quickly identify valuable finds and hidden junk. You'll be competing against a cast of NPC opponents, each with their own unique bidding personality and style. Learn their habits, anticipate their moves, and use clever tactics to secure the best haul at the lowest possible price. Fix and Clean the Goods Once the auction gavel drops and you secure your win, the real work begins. You'll need to load the winning items onto your transport vehicle. Start small, but as your profits grow, you can purchase new, larger vehicles to handle even the biggest hauls. Master the physics and the art of efficient loading to maximize every trip back to your shop. Maintain and Upgrade the Store Every item needs attention before it hits the shelves. Engage in satisfying minigames to bring life back to discarded goods: Cleaning: Scrub off the grime and dirt with a dedicated cleaning minigame. Repair: Use your workbench to restore items to better condition, boosting their potential sale price. Evaluate: Visit an expert to evaluate the true value of your items. Be wary of bootlegs! Manage Your Business Your shop is your stage. You have full control over the retail space: Custom Shop Layout: Personally design and arrange the shelving units and racks to maximize floor space and create the most appealing layout. Merchandise Display: Carefully place each item on the shelves, deciding how to best showcase your top-tier finds and attract customer attention. Personalize Your Space: Inject personality into your store by painting the walls. A visually appealing shop draws in more clientele! The journey doesn't end with one successful shop. As your reputation and bankroll grow: Unlock New Auction Locations: Gain access to different ports and specialized auction houses, offering higher-risk, higher-reward containers. Expand Your Business: Acquire new, larger store locations in different parts of the city. Reclaim New Property: Each new shop location needs your touch! You'll need to personally clean up and organize the newly acquired property before it’s ready to become your next retail hotspot. Do you have the keen eye of a deal-maker, the steady hand of a restorer, and the business sense of a mogul? Test your skills and build a salvage empire in Salvage Shop Simulator!
