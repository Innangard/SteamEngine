Solve cases in your own way. Interrogate suspects, find clues, or use your flippers to punch your way to the answers. The Story Take on the role of Detective Nooter, a student fresh out of detective school who has moved to Penguin Village in order to get some real life experience. As a new arrival in the village you'll be working for the Penguin Village Detective Bureau. Rex Fontaine, a disillusioned but highly experienced detective, has taken you under his wings and is ready to teach you all you need to know to become a great detective.
