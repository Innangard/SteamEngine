Use a variety of iconic and authentic weapons from the United States of World War II, including machine guns, naval guns, and anti-aircraft guns. Fortify your defenses with mines, explosive barrels, aircraft support, and weapon upgrades! Beach Invasion 1941-Pearl Harbor, the add-on to Beach Invasion 1945-Pacific, will focus on the beginnings of the US involvement of the Pacific front, in particular, the Japanese surprise attacks on Pearl Harbor and Wake Island on December 8th, 1941. Players take on the role of American  soldiers manning machine guns and turrets to protect their Pacific bases. During these huge amphibious and airborne operations, the player's mission is to destroy as many invading forces as possible with the help of machine guns, anti-aircraft guns, naval gun nests and bonuses from supply drops. This DLC introduces two new maps, Pearl Harbor and Wake Island, with new gameplay features! Pearl Harbor scenario will focus on battleship USS Nevada's defense against the aerial and naval attacks, while Wake Island scenario will focus on the Japanese invasion. Hardcore action in an immersive World War II setting with realistic sounds and special effects. Relive memorable and epic moments from your favorite World War II movies and TV series. Non-stop action with continuous naval and airborne attacks in changing weather conditions day and night. A wide variety of new enemies are introduced with this DLC, including Aichi D3A dive bomber, Nakajima B5N torpedo bomber, Type A Japanese Midget Submarine, Mitsubishi T-14 torpedo boat, Japanese melee infantry with katana! Aircraft will start their run in V-formation! You can call in air support which lets you take control of the rear guns of the Douglas SBD Dauntless and rain destruction on your enemies from above! Obtain temporary upgrades for your weapons, including poison gas, armor-piercing shells, explosives, and incendiary bombs. Combined, they create even more powerful upgrades. New weapons: New weapons include: Water cooled .50 cal M2 Browning, Oerlikon 20mm, Bofors twin 40mm, 1.1-inch/25cal "Chicago Piano", 5-inch/25cal gun! Further fortify your defenses with naval mines, anti-personnel mines, anti-tank mines, and explosive gun barrels. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Depiction of killing soldiers with semi-realistic blood which can be turned off via the settings.
