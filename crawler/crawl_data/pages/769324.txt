Idle Reef is a relaxing and captivating ecosystem-building game set in the depths of the ocean. Sit back after a stressful day, dive into the deep blue, and transform a barren seabed into a vibrant, colorful coral reef teeming with life. Create Your Ecosystem Starting with just a small piece of coral, you will eventually attract hundreds of different marine creatures to your reef—from jellyfish and clownfish to sea turtles and majestic whales. Manage your resources to unlock new species, expand your habitat, and set the rhythm of your ocean. Key Features Effortless Progression: Whether you play actively to speed up your growth or simply sit back and watch your reef flourish peacefully on its own, the choice is yours. Rich Biodiversity: Customize your reef with dozens of different coral, algae, and fish species to unlock and upgrade. Explore the Depths: Dive deeper into the ocean to discover hidden secrets, sunken treasures, and rare mythical underwater creatures. Relaxing Atmosphere: Experience a meditative journey with mesmerizing underwater visuals and soothing soundtracks designed to wash away the day's fatigue. A Deep Breath, Free from Stress Whether you are a player who loves optimizing every resource or someone who just wants to enjoy the serenity of the underwater world on your screen... Idle Reef offers an experience you can play entirely at your own pace. Answer the call of the ocean. Add to your wishlist now and start your underwater adventure today!
