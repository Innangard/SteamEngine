What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “This project has been close to my heart for a long time now. I want it to be perfect and for that we would need another year or two to have a result approaching what I really want for this game. It will not be perfect the day of its released in early access but we want to improve Retrowave World while taking into account your feedback and suggestions. It also allows you to support the game and watch it grow over time.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We hope to release the game in final version at the end of 2024.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Honestly, we plan to add a lot of things to the game. New worlds, new cars, new characters, more music, new arcade games and many other things that the community may suggest or ideas that we would have in the future.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “I think that the day of its release in Early Access the game will be 30 or 40% of its final release. In other words, we still have work to do. We can't deny it, the game has quite a few bugs, but we're counting on you to help us find and report them. All info about the status and features of the current game is in the "About this game" section of the store page.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price of the game will remain fixed until the final release, there is a possibility that the price will increase at the end of early access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I think we can agree that the Synthwave/Retrowave/Vaporwave community is clearly one of the best. We are a nostalgic community, for some of their childhood, for others of the good old days and for some, a time they have never known. Welcome to Retrowave World! You're driving your 80s supercar, you stop on the side of the road, you get out of your car, you stay standing, your elbow leaning on the door of your car, the breeze of the wind blows in your hair, the smoke from your cigarette rises and scatters towards the sky as you watch this sunset that seems eternal to you... This is the unique nostalgia experience that Retrowave World wants to give to the player. Forget your job, forget the traffic jams, forget your problems... Imagine yourself living in this world filled with neon lights and nostalgia. When listening to Synthwave / Retrowave / Vaporwave most people have had this feeling of nostalgia for a time that they did not know or that never existed. The Retrowave is heavily inspired by the 80s, but is above all an idealized futuristic version of the 80s. Whether you are nostalgic for your childhood, the good old days or simply for a time you have never known, Retrowave World will offer the player the unique experience of being able to be immersed in this wonderful universe for at least a few moments. .. Alone, with friends or with strangers explore the incredible universe of Retrowave World. Drive your car through the different worlds, stop at the VHS store to see the latest releases, take a trip to the arcade to try to break the record on your favorite game, go to the Synth Shop to buy the cassette of the latest fashionable group, make deliveries and earn money, challenge other players in ever crazier races... The possibilities are numerous... Retrowave World offers above all a visual and sound experience that will delight fans of the genre and will surely introduce this wonderful universe to those who do not know it yet... Features: 🌴 No less than 10 cars! 🌴 More than 5 different universes! 🌴 A unique retro-futuristic atmosphere! 🌴 Over 50 different synthwave music! 🌴 Customize your car! 🌴 Make deliveries and earn money! 🌴 Play your favorite games in the arcade! 🌴 Invest in real estate and earn money with your pensions! 🌴 Check out the different stores and buildings like the VHS Store! 🌴 Pure nostalgia!
