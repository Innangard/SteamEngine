How much is a human life worth? Is it worth sacrificing one for many? And what if humanity as a species is under threat? Organs Please puts you in a situation where you have to answer these questions — in 30 days you need to build an ark to save humanity, and it is advisable to get in there yourself 😉 However, we did not want to turn the game's universe into an average dystopia. Through satire and black humor, our team wanted to better reveal the complexity and versatility of the game world. In the artbook, you can see step by step how we searched for the right concepts to convey the atmosphere, individuality and mood of the game’s characters, factions and locations. Original Soundtrack This soundtrack contains 9 original compositions that were created as a result of a long search for a suitable genre — jazz, acid jazz, doom jazz, lounge, and experimental were all used as reference points. As a result, we created something special that fits Organs Please and supports the game’s theme.
