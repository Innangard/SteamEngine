Reach ever more ludicrious scores to (hopefully) earn your freedom... Grid-Based Bento Goodness Assemble your ingredients on a variety of bento shapes and sizes to maximize space and flavor. Master unique combos and modifiers to raise your score to ever increasing heights. Choose ingredients wisely and optimize your layout to fit as much as possible. Don't despair, there's a light at the end of that tunnel... C-c-c-combos! With 10 unique hats and over 100 tools, there's limitless build possibilities to experiment wtih. Push your luck to the limit and weigh your risks and rewards. Go all in on Bread and fail miserably when you fail to roll your combo. Get gud and try again. There's countless ways to win (or fail horribly). Unlockable Stuff Unlockable hats, challenge modes, tools, and secret puzzles fill the game with dozens of hours of content. Try out weird shit and see what it unlocks. See if you can master the increasingly punishing challenges for rewards and bragging rights. Go infinite even harder than your friends in the challenge mode. Uncover the secret ending. We stuffed the game with content. Features : Master the Combos - It'll take more than just fitting food on a grid to win. Learn the synergies, ramp up your combos, and crank your score into the stratosphere. Meta-Progression – Unlock new tools, upgrades, and run modifiers that change future attempts. 120+ Unique Items & Synergies – Experiment with a huge variety of tools and effects to create deadly-efficient strategies. Bento Building Action! - We all love the Resident Evil 4 inventory system. Now do it with food. Uncover the Secrets - We got tons of secret stuff to uncover. There's like a whole escape room thing. Find the clues and get that juicy L O R E ! Meaningful Challenge Modes - New challenges don't just ramp up the needed score, but they also add new features that work with new tools to create a unique experience that doesn't feel like an afterthought. Endless Mode – Push your skills (and your luck) to the limit in an infinite survival challenge. Tense Atmosphere – You're not alone in the kitchen.
