Originally created for The Dread X Collection 4 , Uktena 64 - Ultimate is a shiny new re-release with extended features and brand new additional campaign. Jebadiah gets a strange call with a job offer from the CDC. Diseased animals are spreading a deadly unknown virus and must be stopped. Jebs got to hunt them down and take photos of their eerie bodily corruptions as evidence. (New campaign with Ultimate) While Jeb is off hunting, his brother Jeeb gets a similar bounty. The CIA wants any infected animals hunted down and the bodies destroyed. Jeeb’s got just the bright idea, He’ll hunt these critters down and then use their meat for his failing BBQ restaurant. In Uktena 64 - Ultimate you’ll find: Atmospheric hunting, in diverse low-poly locales. Multiple difficulties from super easy to not even balanced. A couple silly goofballs as playable characters. Unlockable cheat codes that let you fully break the game. Strange horrors beyond human imagining. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This is a hunting game that involves killing and butchering animals.
