A Relaxing Witch's Cabin Organizing Simulator The cabin is a mess, and the magic only made it worse. Return everything to its proper shelf at your own pace, with no timers and no pressure. Pick up each item: a potion vial, a loose spellbook, or a jar of moth wings, find the room and shelf it belongs on, and set it in place. Work through the cabin one shelf at a time. Earn upgrades as you play. Move faster, reach farther, and unlock a few small spells: a levitation charm, a banishing wand, that make tidying the cabin quicker. Cluttered shelves fill with neat rows of labelled jars, and the tangled herb rack settles into order. Each finished room shows clear progress.
