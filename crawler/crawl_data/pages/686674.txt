Begin with one colony and a single home star, then expand across a galaxy of alien worlds - researching exotic science, designing your own fleets, and bending rival empires to your will through diplomacy or open war. Every game generates a new galaxy, a new balance of power, and a new empire to call your own. Colonize a Living Galaxy Explore procedurally generated galaxies ranging from a dozen systems to well over a hundred. Settle planets, moons, and asteroids - each with a surface you develop tile by tile. Construct more than 50 surface structures and orbital installations: factories, laboratories, hydroponic gardens, defense towers, embassies, and more. Terraform hostile worlds and uncover ancient ruins left behind by a vanished civilization. Hand any colony to a Governor and let it run itself Design Your Own Fleets Engineer warships from the keel up across five hull classes, from nimble scouts to colossal capital ships. Combine more than 80 components - power cores, drives, scanners, shields, and an arsenal of exotic weaponry. No two commanders fly the same fleet. Two players using identical hulls can wage completely different wars. Research Exotic Technologies Advance through a tech tree of 68 technologies, from your first orbital shipyard to the frontier of exotic physics. Harness serious, far-future science: gravity manipulation, frequency-based weaponry, and technologies most 4X games never touch. Unlock late-game breakthroughs that reshape how your empire wages war, negotiates, and wins. Rule Through Diplomacy or War Share the galaxy with 21 playable species - Veylilith, Phasaryn, Oculari, Litharrak, and many more - each with its own homeworld, fleet aesthetic, soundtrack, and signature ability. Run diplomacy as a real economy: allocate your influence across courtship, governance, and espionage, build embassies into full diplomatic missions, and spy on rivals through fleet and diplomatic surveillance. Negotiate non-aggression pacts, trade and research agreements, and full alliances - or declare war, extract reparations, and force a peace on your terms. Rise to lead a Galactic Senate: build toward an electable Premier's seat, or seize the throne as Emperor and defend it through the civil war that follows. Outthink rival empires driven by distinct AI personalities that scheme, expand, and fight each other in character - not just against you. Claim Victory Your Way Triumph through military elimination, territorial supremacy, conquest of every home star, a galaxy-wide peace, or by winning the political game as Senate Premier or Emperor - six ways to win, each demanding a different empire. Shape every playthrough: choose your galaxy size, the number of rival empires, and the temperament of the galaxy itself. Crafted to Be Played A deep 4X wrapped in a clean, modern interface - atmospheric visuals and a sci-fi soundtrack. Characterful AI that makes every rival empire feel like an opponent worth beating. Built-in modding tools let you reshape the tech tree and redesign the game's items. A guided first empire eases new players in, and it's gone the moment you don't need it. The galaxy is waiting. Make it your Imperium. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Imperium is a solo-developed game that makes extensive use of generative AI to create its content: • Visual art and graphical assets — planet surfaces, buildings, species and ship artwork, interface art, and store/marketing imagery — were generated using Midjourney and ChatGPT, then extensively edited, composited, and refined by hand in Photoshop (including Photoshop's AI tools) to fit the game's art direction. • Music was generated using Suno. • Sound effects were generated using ElevenLabs. Every asset is reviewed, edited, and approved by the developer before it ships, and the game's design, systems, and balance are the developer's own. Imperium's in-game opponents use traditional algorithmic AI, not generative AI.
