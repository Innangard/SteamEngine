Once installed, you will experience more detailed character skin textures, sharper environmental visuals, and higher quality special effects, significantly enhancing the overall visual fidelity and immersion of your gameplay. This pack is free for all Marvel Rivals players and requires no additional purchase. This pack is recommended for players with mid-to-high-end graphics cards and sufficient storage space. The High-Resolution Pack is optional and does not affect any gameplay features or combat content.
