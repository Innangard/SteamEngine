Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We believe Farm to Table will become a much better game by growing together with the community. Early Access gives us the opportunity to gather real player feedback on the core gameplay loop of farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Balancing progression, economy, and content pacing is especially important for a management game like this. We want to make sure the player’s journey feels rewarding, that new systems unlock at the right time, and that the overall gameplay flow feels smooth and enjoyable rather than overwhelming or repetitive. Player feedback helps us understand what feels fun, what needs improvement, and how we can create the best possible experience. We also want to shape the game alongside our players, improving systems and expanding the experience based on community feedback rather than building everything in isolation.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Farm to Table to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 to 12 months. However, this timeline may remain flexible depending on community feedback and the overall development process. Our goal is to take the time needed to improve the game properly, polish the experience, and make sure the full version reaches the quality we aim for rather than rushing the process.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan for the full version of Farm to Table to significantly expand both the depth and the overall scope of the experience. This includes more recipes, crops, animals, deeper restaurant management systems, expanded staff management, automation systems, more customization options, seasonal events, and stronger progression systems. We also plan to expand island exploration, allowing players to discover more ingredients in the wild, go fishing, and unlock new ways to grow and produce food. In the full version, players will also be able to sell their home-grown farm produce directly to customers through their own Farmers’ Market, adding another layer to the farming and business side of the game. Many players have also asked about co-op. We’d love that, but it also depends on player support. The first version of the game will launch as a singleplayer experience, and the support and interest we see around release will help us better evaluate the possibility of adding co-op in the future.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Farm to Table already offers the full core gameplay experience, centered around farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Players can grow crops, cook and serve dishes in their restaurant, go fishing, explore the island for wild ingredients, hire staff, manage their restaurant, and customize both their farm and restaurant to match their own style. The game also includes production systems using machines to create more advanced ingredients, as well as the Farmers’ Market where players can sell their home-grown produce directly to customers. Our goal is to provide around 30–40 hours of gameplay in Early Access, with a strong foundation of systems and progression already in place, while continuing to expand and improve the experience based on player feedback throughout development.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to offer Farm to Table at a lower price during Early Access and gradually increase the price as we add more content, features, and improvements throughout development. As the game grows in scope and depth, the price will better reflect the overall amount of content and the full experience. Players who join us early during Early Access will be able to support the game from the beginning and benefit from the lower entry price.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback is one of the most important parts of our development process. Our Discord server is our most active space, where we stay in constant communication with players, gather feedback, discuss ideas, and listen to suggestions directly from the community. We are also active on Steam Discussions, making sure players can easily share their thoughts, report issues, and help us improve the game. We plan to share regular development updates, roadmap progress, and balance changes based on player feedback. Community voting and ongoing discussions on Discord will also help us better understand what players care about most and which features should be prioritized as development continues. We want Farm to Table to grow together with its players, and community involvement will remain a core part of that process throughout Early Access.” Read more Buy Farm to Table SPECIAL PROMOTION! View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Farm to Table is a farming and restaurant management sim where you build a restaurant from scratch and grow food in the backyard. Explore a colourful island where you can grow and gather food, raise animals, go fishing, produce better food with machines and cook delicious recipes for your hungry guests! Build and customise your dream place, hire staff and grow your organic business into a 5-star restaurant! Build From Scratch Build your restaurant from scratch on a coastal hill overlooking a colorful island! Customize each corner of your kitchen and farm, indoors or outdoors. Choose from a wide variety of wall, flooring, window, decoration, and furniture styles. Grow, Raise & Harvest Grow fresh crops and raise animals right by your kitchen! Collect fresh produce and adopt pet companions. One harvest at a time! Cook & Serve Cook and Serve or Sell at your Farmers’ Market. Discover new recipes by exploring ingredients on the island! Create diverse menus, cook, bake, grill and serve flavoursome dishes to raise your reputation and attract even more customers! Gather & Fish Gather all kinds of wild flavours like mushrooms, berries and much more or go fishing in the open seas for rare catches! Produce Get useful machines to turn harvest into more advanced ingredients and unlock new recipes. Manage Manage resources, hire chefs, waiters and farmers, upgrade and grow the reputation of your place.Take it slow on the farm, then pick up the pace in the kitchen, turn your cozy humble spot into a 5-star restaurant!
