View Community Hub About This Game After a long period without finding a job, Jack is forced to join the family business, which he had disowned since he was a child. He begins what will be his first case, simple in appearance, but which will suddenly become his worst nightmare. Embark with Jack Holmes in a macabre story of living puppets, uncontrolled animatronics and more nightmarish beings through places as strange as a house where experiments are done with children, some mines full of huge spiders or a terrifying amusement park while you do everything to survive. You'll also have to manage the bullets and first aid kits you find... oh, and don't forget to get on the rides! YOU WILL FACE A LOT OF THREATS . Empty your enemies' magazines while doing everything you can to survive! You'll face various types of enemies with different mechanics and even bosses - you won't be able to run or hide from anyone here! VARIOUS AND UNIQUE SCENARIOS . Dark and terrifying locations, an abandoned house, a basement where children are experimented on, the old abandoned mines and even an amusement park that you can visit at your own pace as you explore every corner in search of ammo and first aid kits to survive. A fair where you can ride the rides!  Explore each part of the park with its own atmosphere, and enjoy all the attractions. But don't be overconfident, danger lurks around every corner. And ride the roller coasters! Do you dare? Game Features: Spectacular graphics thanks to Unreal Engine 4 FPS Survival Horror with ammo and first aid kit management Save points whenever you want in safe zones Variety of different scenarios Several types of enemies A tragic and macabre horror story Game length 4 to 5 hours.
