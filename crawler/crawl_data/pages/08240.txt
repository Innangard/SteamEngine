About This Game The Dragonvault, a dark floating island-sized fortress, has reawakened. Pieces of the Vault and its artifacts are raining down across the realm, warping the land and drawing rival factions into open conflict. This new Power is bringing the world to ruin. You are a Vaultbreaker , tasked with hunting down these cursed artifacts. Claim their power and use them to break into the Dragonvault itself. Dive into dangerous expeditions, recover powerful relics, and bring them back to your Haven , where you build, upgrade, and prepare for even greater threats. Recruit allies, refine your gear, and grow strong enough to challenge the forces guarding the Dragonvault’s deepest secrets. Every run pushes you forward, but only if you make it out alive. Vaultbreakers delivers top-down action combat that rewards smart decisions over reckless aggression. Positioning, preparation, and timing matter just as much as fast reflexes. Not every fight should be taken. Some enemies are better avoided, at least until you’re ready. Study enemy behavior, exploit moments of vulnerability to deal massive damage , and turn the tide through careful planning. Each victory makes you stronger, but every mistake can cost you everything. Equip your Vaultbreaker with legendary weapons and armor , unlock game-changing perks, and experiment with powerful synergies to shape your own playstyle. In building your Haven, you shape your permanent boons to suit you. Craft and find many useful devices to master, and evolve into a relentless force capable of taking on the world’s deadliest foes. You decide how much you’re willing to risk. Vaultbreakers lets you freely move between PvE and PvPvE modes with shared progression. PvE offers a safer path to growth, while PvPvE raises the stakes, adding other players to the equation and dramatically increasing rewards and experience. Go solo or team up with up to two friends to tackle missions together, facing AI enemies, rival players, or both at once. Trust your teammates, coordinate your actions, and decide together when to push deeper or when to escape before it’s too late. No two missions play out the same. They evolve dynamically based on the actions of other players and NPCs . One group might pollute a massive area with dark magic, another could unlock a hidden cave, while someone else begins an ancient ritual that changes the entire map. Every choice creates new threats and new opportunities. Stay alert and use the environment to your advantage. Launch ambushes with exploding Cacti, lure enemies into traps, harness thunderous hazards, or make a narrow escape when plans fall apart. The world is alive, unpredictable, and always watching. In Vaultbreakers, survival isn’t just about strength. It’s about knowing when to fight, when to flee, and when to risk everything for greater rewards and power.
