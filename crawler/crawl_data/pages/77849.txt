About This Game Smash through the streets of Nuevo-Tokyo in a fast chaotic 2.5D roguelike beat ’em up where every run unlocks new powers, builds and ridiculous Mexican Jutsus. Our story takes place in Nuevo-Tokyo, an offspring of chaos that was born when Japan and Mexico collided in fire, power and betrayal. A land where a new feudal regime was forged between Narcos and Yakuzas, forming a new ruling class, The Narkuzas. They are now the law, and the law SUCKS CULO . Now, in the outskirts of Nuevo-Tokyo, a group of rebels is plotting against the five heads of the Narkuza clan. Enter the Mexican Ninja… Pela el oido, I’m going to tell you a story, eh?  One full of adrenaline-pumping combat, where the streets never give you a break, no matter how many fall before you. Explore the stylized streets of Nuevo Tokyo, a city where Narcos and Yakuzas joined forces and mi pueblo was never the same. Precise movement, positioning and perfectly timed attacks decide every fight. It’s a pick-up-and-play brawler that’s deceptively simple, yet deeply rewarding once you start mastering combos and builds. Unlock ninja skills by training with el Mero Mero Sensei. Gain the favor of powerful Mexican-Japanese spirits and unleash their powers throughout your runs. Experiment with different builds, discover devastating combos and carve your own fighting style. Customize your character with hidden outfits and swag worthy of a true cabrón. Mexican Ninja is a return to the button-mashing arcade classics, but with roguelike chaos and modern depth. Fight your way through gangs of Narkuzas, master outrageous Mexican Jutsus and leave a trail of broken enemies behind you. Because when we fight… we do it CON HUEVOS CABRON . Customize your fighting style using our Way of the Donkey tree. The Way of the Donkey is our skill tree, except it’s 10x cooler and made for people with peluche en el estuche. There’s an inner cabrón in all of us. Be THE Mexican Ninja and show the Narkuzas the true way of the blade.
