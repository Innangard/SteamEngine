A stylish, slightly dark, yet heartfelt horror romance about youth, fear, and self-control. A flashy high‑school girl ghost suddenly appears in your home and begins enjoying life with you—completely uninvited. She will never take your life. After all, for reasons unknown, she has fallen deeply in love with you. But you are a high school teacher. Any inappropriate incident would destroy your career and social standing. Keep your distance, resist her advances, and survive seven nights without letting your life fall apart. Explore your home in first-person and complete nightly missions to make it through each day. The ghost is always looking for chances to get closer. Too much physical contact is strictly forbidden—run away when necessary. Stop her from bringing her belongings into your house and from posting your photos on social media. Protect your peaceful, ordinary life at all costs. March, just before graduation. Without warning, a ghost in a school uniform appears in your home. The uniform belongs to a student from your school— yet you don’t recognize her face. As confusion sets in, the ghost gently takes your hand. Inside is a short message: “Just seven days.” Can you protect your morals and your career? And where will this bold ghost girl’s unwavering affection lead? A strange seven-day cohabitation where fear and affection slowly invade everyday life.
