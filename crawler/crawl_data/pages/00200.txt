Everyone is hunted. Act Normal is a multiplayer game of deception, stealth, and paranoia. Blend into a crowd of NPCs, steal everything you can get your hands on, and figure out who around you is actually another player before they figure out you. Every minute, whoever's stolen the least gets eliminated. Every Theft Is a Risk Everything on the map can be stolen, but every item comes with a risk. You never know if you've stolen enough to survive the next elimination. Do you play it safe and hope your score is enough, or risk another theft and expose yourself? Trust No One There's no way to tell a player from an NPC at a glance. The person standing next to you could be harmless, or they could be armed and waiting for the right moment to make their move. Play Steal or Die in the Demo! The demo lets you experience Steal or Die , one of the three game modes coming to the full version of Act Normal. Steal or Die: Everyone's a Thief with a hidden weapon. Steal the most, survive the longest. The lowest scorer is eliminated every minute. More Game Modes in the Full Game Infection: Guards vs. Thieves. Thieves steal without blowing their cover; Guards study the crowd and eliminate anyone acting out of line. Tag War: Two teams, one twist: get caught, and you switch sides instantly. Survive the clock as a Thief to win.
