HACKAGON is a free-to-play 1v1 cyber card battler built around simultaneous turns. Commit your cards before either side can react, read the plan hidden across the network, and seize control when every play reveals together. Jump into online play for free. Queue for Ranked or Casual matchmaking, browse public lobbies, or create a private room for a direct challenge. Prefer to learn the system first? Fight adaptive AI before taking your deck online. Against the AI, Topology mode turns adjacency into a changing network graph. Energy rises each turn, giving you more ways to spread pressure, reinforce a lead, or bait the other side. Most battles revolve around three contested areas, but the shape of the network can change. Stage your cards, revise the plan while the turn is open, then commit. Rules such as Honeypot, Air-Gapped, Quarantine, and Fortified keep every board tactically distinct. Every card combines cost, power, and a tactical effect. DDoS floods an area and cuts enemy power, Worm spreads into adjacent areas, and Threat Mirror copies the strongest opposing card at its location. Effects resolve in a clear sequence, so a clever setup can overturn raw numbers and a single well-read turn can reshape the entire match. Choose Red offense or Blue defense, then build a 12-card deck from a growing pool of 60 Red, Blue, and neutral cards. Unlock agents with always-on abilities that shape how a deck plays before the first card is committed. Specialize in tempo, shields, disruption, scaling, or combine them into a plan of your own. Enter FARADAY operations, receive CIPHER transmissions, and trace a threat moving through networks that should be dead. Mission paths introduce bespoke objectives, rewards, opponents, and rule combinations, turning the same competitive systems into authored tactical encounters.
