Nightshift Survival is a challenging rogue-lite game where you will face hordes of terrifying and relentless creatures in Millennium Park. What mysteries and dangers does this place hide? Face enemies with various movement patterns, collect powerups to survive, and unlock achievements with new items and ways to play. Be a pacifist survivor or a hunter of the mysterious creatures with the 57 items, including weapons, trinkets, and collectibles available to build your best strategy to survive the insane nights. There are 13 sinister enemies, ranging from harmless little toys to child-devouring mechatronic beasts, all eager to play with you. Face your fears through the nights to make it unscathed until 6AM and confront the bosses of each night. With 60 achievements, unlock new possibilities to make each playthrough unique and showcase your skills as a true collector. Play and replay with different combinations of items to take down enemies! Besides everything already present in the game, get ready for planned new content. Many secrets will still be revealed in the upcoming chapters. Survive the night shift, if you can.
