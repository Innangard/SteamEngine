View Community Hub About This Game Tails of Trainspot is a relaxing logic puzzle game set on the peaceful Packan Islands. Lay railway tracks to connect numbered stations, uncover the island’s secrets, and meet charming animal residents. With a gentle pace, cozy art style, and delightful mini-games, it's a thoughtful escape into a world of whimsy and wonder. Logic Puzzle Gameplay Solve grid-based puzzles where each station’s number tells you how many rails must connect to it. Plan your routes carefully—rails can’t cross or overlap, and every station must be part of a single connected network. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master. A World Full of Stories As you explore the islands, you'll meet curious animal residents and uncover hidden myths buried in ruins. The Packan Islands are more than just a puzzle board—they’re a living world filled with character and charm. Mini-Games for Variety Take a break from rail building with cozy side activities. Assemble bento boxes, run a drink bar, or roll sausage dice with the locals. These mini-games add warmth and variety to your island journey. Relaxing Yet Rewarding With its soft, industrial-age aesthetic and calming soundscape, Tails of Trainspot is designed to soothe while still offering satisfying challenges. Perfect for puzzle lovers looking to unwind without sacrificing depth. Let the tracks guide you through tales, tea, and tranquility.
