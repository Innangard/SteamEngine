Cursed Worlds Cursed Worlds is the first DLC for Stacklands. Travel to three different worlds where the villagers are haunted by tricky curses. Build up their village, then use new cards and card packs to lift the curse! After lifting a curse, you can choose to activate it on your mainland for an additional challenge. World of Sadness 😓 In this cursed world, villagers need happiness every moon. Create enough happiness by letting them read books, building taverns and more. Be careful, as certain actions will also create sadness! Can you help save the villagers from longing for happiness all the time? World of Greed 💰 Here, a greedy royal rules the land. He demands all kinds of luxuries, like wine, socks and paintings. Make sure his needs are met, or he'll send out the Royal Guards! Will he ever be satisfied? World of Death 💀 Within this cursed world, villagers grow old and get sick. Build houses to make new villagers, clean up their poop using outhouses and sewers, and make medicine for the sick. Can you stop them from aging?
