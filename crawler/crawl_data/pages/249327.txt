View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Aboard the great airships reside the last of humanity, survivors of the Grell war that still rages across the earth.  Deploy to the surface with Time-Walker mechs, capable of manipulating time in combat, to defend your salvage teams.  Gather equipment, supplies, and survivors from the ruined surface.  Upgrade, improve, and maybe one day, find the key to revert the apocalypse. Key Features Send mechs back in time, creating duplicate defenders Unique mid-combat mech actions (movement and combat abilities) Mech upgrades and customization Unique mech pilots Upgradable turrets Support turrets, drones, and more Destructible environments Dynamic battlefields with elevation, multiple paths, and more Rewind and Rethink When facing overwhelming swarms, send your mechs back in time to even the odds. When sending a mech to the past, you have the chance to play the wave again, but this time you will have a duplicate mech. If you need more help, send another mech back to that same point. Classic Tower-Defense Gameplay Defend your salvage team from the Krell as you scavenge the ruins of a lost earth. Deploy turrets and position your mechs to hold back the swarms. When space is limited, upgrade your defenses to pack a harder punch. Tower Defense with Tactics Reposition mechs in the middle of combat for strategic advantage Melee-focused mechs can defend roadways and slow enemies they engage. Mechs with jump-jets can quickly traverse the battlefield and jump to elevated areas that increase their line of sight. Mech Abilities A mech’s loadout grants it passive and active combat abilities. Demolish a structure with a gravity hammer to make more room for your defenses. Construct a platform, then use a mech’s jump-jets to give him the high ground. Use the autocannon’s “Bulletstorm” ability to unleash a hail of rounds in a pinch.
