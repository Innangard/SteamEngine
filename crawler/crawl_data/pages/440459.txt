View Community Hub About This Game Colonize: A Peaceful 17th Century Settlement Builder Build and manage a small colony in the New World during the 17th century. Colonize is a calm, no-combat settlement builder focused on resource management, production chains, trade, and keeping your people alive. There are no armies, no enemies, and no warfare. Your challenge is the colony itself: food, shelter, economy, workers, weather, and the needs of your settlers. Build. Manage. Survive. Guide your settlement through three historical-inspired locations: Roanoke, Plymouth, and Bermuda. Each scenario offers different conditions, resources, and challenges. Manage Resources Gather raw materials, refine them into useful goods, and keep your colony supplied. Plan production chains carefully and make sure your people have what they need to survive and grow. Trade with the Old World Use trade ships to exchange goods with England. Expand your economy, bring in missing resources, and keep your settlement moving forward. Play at Your Own Pace Colonize is designed for players who enjoy slower, more thoughtful city-building without combat pressure. Adjust the difficulty to create a more relaxed or more demanding colony management experience. A Note from the Developers: We are a small team dedicated to creating a focused, atmospheric building experience. We value your feedback and would love to hear your thoughts on how to further polish the game. Join our Discord server to chat with us directly and stay updated on bug fixes and improvements!
