Welcome to Picnic Festa, a brand-new seasonal DLC for Real VR Fishing! Travel across 7 beautiful picnic-themed fishing destinations in the United States, Spain, and Japan, featuring relaxing lakes, rivers, beaches, and coastal environments filled with festive spring atmosphere. Discover special “Chubby Fish” that became ridiculously overweight after eating discarded picnic food left behind at fishing spots. Catch these unique fish and place them into your aquarium, where they will gradually recover back to their healthy forms over time. But as the Chubby Fish disappear, the ecosystem begins to change. Rare “Starving Sharks” will start appearing throughout saltwater stages after losing their food source, allowing players to rescue and nurse them back to health.
