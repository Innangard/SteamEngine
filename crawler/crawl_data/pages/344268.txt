Tails Noir: Rebel Rush is a speedrunning precision platformer where you speed through a flooded vision of Vancouver, jumping on buildings, inventing crazy shortcuts, in an effort to become the fastest driver in the city! Push the pedal to the metal, hit those green boost panels and dash through the air with the whooshing sound! Complete the main path and immediately learn that the game has just begun! Study the layouts of the twisted levels and figure out how to complete them faster! Find the hearts that will guide you to secrets and hidden paths, and invent your own crazy shortcuts to beat the global leaderboard (and your friends!!!) Master the chaos that surrounds you! Fall off the road because of one stupid mistake, scream elegantly , and get back on track like nothing happened! Remember : walls are for those who can be stopped by them. So go ahead, break some stuff, pave new roads, and have fun causing chaos in a world full of cops! 4-6 hours of gameplay 19 twisted levels of secret shortcuts, police barricades and surreal mayhem. Highly replayable levels where you beat your times by going off roads and inventing clever shortcuts. That’s not a racing game : our cars can double jump, they dash and bounce off buildings. Friends & global leaderboards , and an official entry on the main speedrunning website in the world. Yes, we really took the gloomy pixel art world of Tails Noir and turned it around into a bright, fun, car driving game. Breakcore & drum and bass soundtrack full of bangers. A good steering wheel that doesn't fly off while you're driving.
