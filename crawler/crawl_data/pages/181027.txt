In this unique blend of 4X strategy, RPG, and roguelike mechanics, you’ll gather resources, craft essential items, complete quests, and protect your chosen from relentless threats. Choose to build up to two thriving villages or embrace the nomadic life as you adapt to the challenges of the land. Engage in tactical card-based combat, make tough decisions with lasting consequences, and lead your people to survival against the odds. Will you restore balance, or watch your world succumb to chaos? The Thea world Thea 2: The Shattering is a follow-up to the successful Thea: The Awakening, which brings the idea of innovative genre hybrid, with elements of turn-based, 4X strategy, survival, RPG and a card game, to entirely new and thoroughly polished level. Key features: Explore rich, procedurally generated and diverse new environments and biomes. Expand your influence in the world, build towns, widen your territories or choose the much tougher path of a nomad. Exterminate your way to victory, battle through the vast array of creatures or simply strive to survive till sunrise. Exploit the many wonders of the land - discover and combine resources, collect materials and craft them into your equipment. Become a deity of Thea and direct your Chosen to victory in a world inspired by Slavic myth and folklore. Play together - co-op mode for up to 3 players allows you to build your pantheon with friends. Choose your playstyle - become a warlord or a diplomat, or simply remain an outsider wandering the world in search of glory. Survive any way you can and choose your challenge style as you resolve conflicts via battle, mental or spiritual challenges. Free DLCs, built-in adventures editor and other modding tools.
