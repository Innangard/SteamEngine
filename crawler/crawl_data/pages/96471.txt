View Community Hub Reviews “A super, arcade-y roguelike that doesn't outstay its welcome.” Wanderbots “Hands down the most unique roguelike of 2024!” Idle Cub About This Game Ever yearn to return to the magic of the first time you played an arcade RPG like Gauntlet 2 or Golden Axe? Recapture the magic in Gorgon Shield , from Oliver Joyce, creator of the beloved Swords and Sandals series! Up to four heroes ride on a giant shield in a tower in their quest to vanquish the mighty gorgon Medusa. Fight, heal, gain treasure, rise upwards. Each hero will eventually fall and make their way for the next heroes, but their sacrifices will not be in vain - for their treasure will pass onto the next, paving the way forward ever onward. Gather your party of expendable heroes in your quest to defeat Medusa ! Control all four heroes as rise through the tower. Our heroes must battle waves of monsters, explore the tower's many secrets and make their way ever upward. Your heroes will gain powerful upgrades and new allies will join you on your quest as you venture further. Gorgon Shield is a world first - a vertical roguelike, top down Action RPG, side scroller and survivor game all in one. Expendable heroes, powerful unlocks, simple to play but difficult to master. You've never played a game like Gorgon Shield. Will you be the first to reach the top of the tower and defeat Medusa? Features: 10 playable heroes including the priest, knight and rogue The Elevator - Stand fast on the shield, protect it from enemies and gather valuable loot The Labyrinths - Fast paced dungeon crawling through fiendish mazes of the tower Fast paced but deeply strategic, with many party synergies and options on each run. Upgrades, collectables and powers to unlock on your way to confront and defeat Medusa! As soon as you reach level 4, you will gain a second hero - then again at certain points in the quest. These extra heroes will really help share the load. Keep an eye on your special powers and use them wisely. Heal when needed, use lightning to clear mobs and haste to gather loot fast. The right party synergies are all important! Four barbarians might sound fun, but who will heal them when things get dire? Expect your heroes to die often and early - but their upgrades will live on. No hero's death is in vain! System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: 2Ghz Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: GLES 3 OpenGL 3 compatible video card with 1GB RAM and up to date video card drivers Storage: 1 GB available space Sound Card: DirectX compatible sound card Additional Notes: *** NVIDIA users - please make sure to  update your graphics card drivers if you experience a crash! *** This game will not work properly on GLES 2 OpenGL Cards! Make sure your video drivers are up-to-date and that your video card supports OpenGL GLES3.
