New Environments & Challenges: Additional stages and environments, offering fresh challenges beyond just boss battles. Enhanced Upgrades & Achievements: A broader selection of character upgrades and in-game achievements. Dynamic Rune System: Introduce a rune system that lets you customize and boost your characters’ abilities, adding a strategic layer to gameplay. Diverse Game Modes: Beyond traditional stages with bosses, the full version will include various game modes. Varied Enemies: A wider range of monsters and bosses with unique movesets to keep the action unpredictable. Refined Mechanics: Improved character-specific upgrades, balance tweaks, and enhanced overall gameplay based on community feedback. Together, these enhancements will create a richer, more varied experience that builds on the solid foundation of Early Access.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “In our latest build, you can experience: 3 Fully Developed Stages – Each with 5 difficulty levels to challenge every type of player. 6 Characters: 2 playable and 4 unlockable, each offering distinct playstyles. Over 50 Upgrades – Enhance your character’s abilities in myriad ways. 70+ Achievements – Track your progress and unlock rewards as you conquer challenges. ” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes! Early Access adopters benefit from a special, discounted price as a thank you for joining us on this journey.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We’re all about community-driven development.
