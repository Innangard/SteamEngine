View Community Hub Reviews “XCOM, but with gyms for firefighters” Kotaku About This Game How would it feel to lead a squad of firefighters and manage a modern fire station? Head into the action, make hard decisions and save people from danger in the real-time strategy game, Fire Commander! RESPOND TO THE EMERGENCIES From extinguishing flames to saving people and valuable equipment, you will experience a variety of realistic environments and dangers, including the risk of a toxic spill and explosions. Manage your people and water supplies. Utilize different vehicles, class specializations, and tools. Discover multiple ways of dealing with any situation by combining the forces at your disposal. Consider all physical factors: backdraft, smoke, or materials with a different combustion temperature. The well-being of your crew and the success of the missions depend on your decisions. GUARD YOUR PEOPLE'S BACK Putting your life on the line together can form strong bonds between you and your firefighting crew. The team becomes your family. Hone your tactical skills and strategies. Send your firefighters on training courses and develop their skills. Be sure that everyone will know what to do when the time comes. FEATURES Different classes of firefighters 30+ thrilling missions Experience system with unlockable skills Numerous rescue vehicles and a growing fire station Story and side missions An enemy you cannot outpower, only outsmart Face the Fire!
