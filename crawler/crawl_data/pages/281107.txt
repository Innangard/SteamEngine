You’ve been trapped for years. Now you finally have a chance to escape. Find your way through a strange building full of unsettling people, dark humor, and strange mysteries. Every floor brings you closer to freedom… or deeper into an ancient, terrible secret. Upstairs has been carefully crafted, late at night and piece by piece, by a single developer using Unity, Adventure Creator and Aseprite. -Short, focused experience (about 1–2 hours) -Classic point-and-click gameplay -Verb-driven interface with a visible inventory -Handcrafted pixel art -A happy ending -A friendly severed head you can talk to (responses are not guaranteed) -No AI-generated content MOUSE - Interact with the world ESC - open the game menu A game by Jaime Herrero Original Music: The Coven "Watch the Skyline Catch Fire" by Of the Wand & the Moon , written by Kim Larsen, distributed by Tesco Distribution Germany Cover art and logo by Clara Soriano. --------------------------------------- Upstairs is a small personal project made during late nights.
