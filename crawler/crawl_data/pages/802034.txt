View Community Hub About This Content The Underkeep Supporters Pack offers additional cosmetic content for the base game for players who want to support Underkeep and the team behind it. Support Underkeep! Many of you have asked if there's a way to support Underkeep beyond buying the game. We couldn't quite fit a tip jar into the dungeon, so we created this Supporter Pack instead! It's a completely optional way to give our small team a little extra support. What's included? 15 alternate character portraits – golden armor versions of existing portraits from the base game. 6 digital wallpapers – featuring artwork by Karel Matějka, one of the artists behind Underkeep. Nothing game-changing, no unfair advantages, and unfortunately, no legendary loot. Just a few small extras to say thanks. Buying this pack is entirely up to you. Simply playing Underkeep and sharing it with others already means a lot to us. Thank you for supporting us!
