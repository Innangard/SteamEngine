View Community Hub About This Game Choose from around 100 magic stones, build your loadout, and wield spells in both hands in this third-person, loot-driven 3D action game. Defeat monsters, earn items, prepare your equipment at your base, and set out for the next stage. Use each element’s unique effects—including Burn and Freeze—to gain the upper hand. Rare magic stones give you even more to discover as you develop your own way to fight. Take on more than 50 stages across four difficulty levels. Play solo or team up with up to four players in online co-op, where the number of enemies and their HP increase. Let Your Viewers Join the Adventure Connect your stream on Twitch or YouTube and let your viewers’ comments influence the battle! Words of encouragement can bring support, while a confident “too easy!” can make elite enemies more likely to appear. Enjoy unexpected twists together with your viewers. Twitch Bits, YouTube Super Chats and Super Stickers can summon a small ally called a Support Pom. It follows the player and helps fight monsters. Streaming integration is optional. All stages can be enjoyed solo or in online co-op without streaming. Features Build your loadout — Choose from around 100 magic stones and find combinations that suit your playstyle. Master the elements — Use different spells and status effects to turn battles in your favor. Fight, collect, prepare — Clear quests to earn items, adjust your equipment at your base, and take on the next stage. Play your way — Choose from four difficulty levels and play solo or in online co-op with up to four players. Stay focused on the action — Items other than HP recovery pickups are collected automatically, while a dodge roll lets you evade incoming attacks. Adventure Together with Your Viewers — Twitch and YouTube comments influence the battle. Encouragement and casual remarks can bring unexpected twists to your adventure. Random Events That Change the Battle — Changing weather, monster reinforcements, unexpected support and bonus time. Even the same stage can unfold differently, encouraging you to adapt your tactics. Story Long ago, conflict between people left the land and seas devastated. A goddess healed the world and granted everyone the power of magic, on the condition that they would end their fighting. Many years later, magic has become part of everyday life. But after an attack in a neighboring kingdom, reports emerge of monsters growing more ferocious by the day. Sou and Kanade, two sisters who make their living hunting monsters, set out to face the spreading threat. Characters Sou Algrette The strong-willed older sister, always ready to take on a monster-hunting job. Her boldness sometimes worries Kanade. She fights with two magical tools: a staff in one hand and a book in the other. Kanade Algrette A gentle but dependable younger sister who looks out for Sou when she pushes herself too far. She wields a large staff fitted with a magic stone at each end. What's New in the Remaster Originally released at Comiket in 2011, Nonet Concerto returns with improved graphics, refined movement and combat, and quality-of-life changes such as automatic item collection. Online co-op now supports up to four players instead of two, and the balance of magic stones and status effects has been reworked.
