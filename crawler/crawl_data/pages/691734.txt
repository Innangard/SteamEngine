A 89 tracks album to celebrate the best game to come out of 1989 - All the classic, infinitely hummable themes from Shinichi Sakamoto re-imagined and exquisitely re-orchestrated as a labor of love by master composer Michael Geyre. A thoroughly enjoyable soundtrack that stands on its own as a must have album made by fans, for fans. The 33 main tracks total over an hour of high quality music featuring live performers across a wide variety of styles and instruments. The 56 bonus tracks offer surprising insight into the composition and arrangement process by including an exclusive extra hour of alternate versions, sketches, unused and or sometimes rejected material.
