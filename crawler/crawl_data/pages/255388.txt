SokoChess Black is a unique chess puzzle game in which you must use your wits to navigate each level, moving, pushing and capturing white pieces until there are none left. Capture the white pieces The goal of each level is to capture all the white pieces. Move and push your own pieces You can move your own pieces but also push them to make them move in a way they normally couldn't.
