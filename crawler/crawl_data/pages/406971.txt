Your new white parrot is full of personality and happy to make it known. Feeling overwhelmed? Open the window. The skies are always there. Meet your new companion for the first time and unlock the exclusive achievement «Pieces of Eight! Pieces of Eight!» . Tips: The rum and sword can be found in your personal chest inside the captain's cabin. The parrot will appear directly in the captain's cabin upon loading.
