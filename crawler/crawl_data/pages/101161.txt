Compete with the other angels to invest into the most perspective startups and climb the top of the rankings. Detailed economic simulation The startup ideas presented in the game are somewhat wacky for the fun factor, but the economic simulation under the hood is realistic. Each startup has starting parameters, which you can improve by assigning advisors. Build your team of experts to help the startup achieve their product market fit. The demo is limited to the Venture Capitalist firm Internship scenario. In the full game you can play more challenging scenarios where you have to pay back the starting capital OR set your own rules in the Sandbox mode. Company market share depends on Product quality Price vs competitors pricing Marketing and PR efforts Sales skills Quality of customer support Features compete in bidding war against other angels to get stake in new companies assign your employees to help startups develop your experts can also help with due diligence and research before investing manage wages and expenses help startups apply for patents vote on making important business decisions adjust the pricing for products trade on the Stock Exchange after companies complete the IPO If you have any questions or suggestions about the game, ask on the Steam discussion forums or join the Discord. Note: the demo ends after your first successful IPO.
