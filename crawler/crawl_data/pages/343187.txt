Be quick, or the next customer won’t wait! Food Cart, Inc. is a fast-food management simulation where you set up your stall in the city streets, race to fulfill orders, and struggle against the chaos of the crowd. From busy downtown squares to carnival grounds, from university campuses to airports, you’ll face customers from all walks of life. Take their orders, prepare the food, manage your stock, and try to end the day with a profit. Remember: the faster and more organized you are, the more customers you’ll serve—and the quicker your cash register fills up. Key Features ✔ Real-time cooking Grill the patties, fry the fries, and pour the drinks. Sounds simple, until the line grows long and chaos takes over! ✔ Turn chaos into fun You’re still the one cooking, preparing, and serving—but as the workload rises, helpers step in. Catapults, robot scoops, and smart boxes speed things up and help you keep more customers happy. ✔ Expand and upgrade your stall Use your earnings to restock supplies, purchase new machines, and make your stand more efficient. ✔ Compete across diverse locations Beaches, stadiums, campuses, carnivals, and more. Each area brings unique customer types and a different pace of play. Fast-food chaos has never been this much fun. 👉 Add Food Cart, Inc. to your wishlist and get ready to set up your stall!
