With countless build combinations, annihilate hordes of terrifying hungry frogs and defeat the Bosses to prove your chicken is UNSTOPPABLE. Evolve your skills into ever more destructive versions and amplify your power on the battlefield. Take your magic beyond the absurd by fusing them to create insane and unexpected combos. Find chests across the map and equip crazy accessories from parallel universes. They change your playstyle, grant unexpected bonuses, twist reality — and above all, make your chicken look absolutely stylish. Find fragments of the sacred relics of the Clucktheon. Once complete, bring them to the battlefield to channel this ancient power and unleash the chicken gods’ energy to obliterate frogs mercilessly. Face insane Bosses, colossal monsters and topple the pillars that hold the frogs’ corrupted reality together. Each battle will push your power to the limit, but for your magical chicken, no Boss is strong enough to stop you from crushing them all.
