Cloud Keeper could not have gotten to its current state without valuable input from players and I hope to continue working with the community.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “While the exact timeline will depend on player feedback, the current roadmap is to be in Early Access for about 6 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan to expand the game with additional content, including additional characters to play, cloud companion types, relics, shrine visitors, maps, enemy mobs, and bosses.
