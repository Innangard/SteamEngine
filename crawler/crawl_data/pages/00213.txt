COUNTLESS CARROTS Welcome to a giant field filled with more carrots than anyone could ever count. Start with nothing but your bare hands. Pull carrots from the ground one by one, carry them to the selling point, and spend your earnings on better tools and upgrades. At first, every carrot takes effort. Soon, you will be clearing entire sections of the field in seconds. UPGRADE YOUR HARVESTING ARSENAL Work your way from simple manual labor to complete harvesting chaos. Use a pitchfork to pull multiple carrots from the soil, collect them with a powerful vacuum, or fire a grenade launcher to dig up everything caught in the blast. Upgrade each tool to make it faster, stronger, and far more effective. CARRY MORE CARROTS Expand your inventory to carry increasingly ridiculous amounts of produce. Once your inventory is fully upgraded, extra carrots are automatically packed into boxes instead of interrupting your work. Keep harvesting while your growing production system handles the rest. PUT A ROBOT TO WORK Activate a helper robot to collect packed boxes and transport them to the selling point. Upgrade its movement speed and carrying capacity to keep your operation running while you focus on clearing the field. EXPLORE SIX FARMING ZONES Travel between six large zones connected to a central hub. Clear at least half of a zone to earn a key, then decide which area to unlock next. Every zone offers a unique bonus. Clear an area completely, and its bonus becomes active across the entire farm. CATCH LIVING CARROTS Not every carrot will wait quietly underground. Living Carrots leap from the soil, run away, and try to bury themselves again. Catch them and offer them to strange totems to open fast-travel tunnels between farming zones. SURVIVE KAMIKAZE CARROTS Some carrots are more interested in destruction than escape. Kamikaze Carrots charge toward you and explode, scattering everything in your inventory. Their blasts also dig up nearby crops, turning every attack into both a disaster and an unexpected harvesting opportunity. BUILD THE ULTIMATE HARVESTING SYSTEM Harvest, sell, upgrade, and discover increasingly powerful ways to clear the field. What begins with a single carrot gradually transforms into a huge and satisfying production system built to handle an impossible harvest. KEY FEATURES Pull, collect, and sell enormous quantities of carrots. Start with your bare hands and unlock a rake, vacuum, and grenade launcher. Upgrade every tool to improve its speed, strength, and effectiveness. Expand your inventory and automatically pack extra carrots into boxes. Use and upgrade a helper robot to transport your harvest. Explore six large farming zones and unlock them in any order. Earn permanent bonuses by completely clearing each zone. Catch rare Living Carrots and unlock fast-travel tunnels. Watch out for explosive Kamikaze Carrots. Turn slow manual work into fast and powerful harvesting chaos.
