View Community Hub About This Game Farming just got a whole lot weirder! Cyber pigs, mutated crops, and a lot of laughs — welcome to 🤖 Cyber Farmer 🤖, a wacky, post-apocalyptic farming simulator where tradition meets the future! Step into the boots of Kazimierz’s grandson, the next in line to inherit the family farm. Set in a world devastated by war, Cyber Farmer blends rustic village charm with outlandish cyber tech, all while cranking up the humor to the max. Welcome to your post-apocalyptic farm☢️, where cyber tech🤖, genetically modified crops🌾, and cyber pigs🐷 are just part of everyday life. Prepare for a game full of laughs, bizarre surprises, and quirky farming fun as you build your very own cyber farm! Key Features: 🤖 Use high-tech cyber gadgets to improve your farm: Harness the power of futuristic gadgets to take your farming to the next level. From robotic tools to cybernetic upgrades, tech is at the heart of your farming revolution. 😂 Hilarious gameplay with strange and funny moments: With tons of absurd moments, unexpected events, and quirky characters, Cyber Farmer is full of humor and fun. Expect plenty of laughs as you navigate through this bizarre farm life. 🌍 Post-apocalyptic world with humor and surprises: Discover a world torn apart by war, where the remnants of the old life mix with futuristic technology. Every corner hides bizarre surprises and quirky characters ready to keep you laughing. 🌱 Grow genetically modified crops and experiment with plant mutations: Explore a farming experience like no other! Modify your plants, experiment with strange mutations, and use cyber-enhancements to boost your crops to extraordinary levels. 🐷 Raise cyber pigs and other unusual animals: On this farm, animals aren’t just animals—they’re part of the future! Grow, modify, and raise cyber pigs and other mutated creatures to help you in your daily farming tasks. 🚜 Continue the farming legacy of Kazimierz’s grandson: As the heir to the family farm, you must keep the tradition alive, but with a twist! Combine the wisdom of the old farm with futuristic innovations to build the ultimate cyber farm. Farm the future, laugh through the past!
