You are a homunculus, created and summoned by him—the Alchemist. He speaks of distant lands and forgotten knowledge, traces of the ingredients for the ultimate alchemical creation: the Philosopher's Stone . He promised it holds the power to grant you a soul. And you? You’d do anything to make that happen. Craft, cook, and brew your way to power—stir the pot just right, and unlock incredible abilities unlike any you've seen.
