Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Wavecrashers has been in development for over two years, and Early Access gives us the opportunity to continue shaping it with the people who actually play it. Because the game is built around cooperative play, build variety, progression, and increasingly demanding challenges, player feedback is especially valuable for balancing heroes, systems, difficulty, and overall game flow. Early Access allows us to refine these elements through real play, while continuing to expand and improve the experience alongside the community.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Wavecrashers to remain in Early Access for around one to two years, though the exact timeline may change depending on player feedback, development priorities, and the scope of improvements we decide to pursue. Our focus is not on reaching 1.0 by a fixed date, but on making sure the game is polished, well-balanced, and feature-complete enough to justify a full release.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Throughout Early Access, we plan to expand Wavecrashers significantly while continuing to refine its existing systems, balance, performance, and overall presentation. Our current plans include: -Expanding the hero roster with many new playable characters, each built around distinct abilities and playstyles. -Adding a large number of new items to create more build variety and synergies. -Introducing additional game modes and ways to play. -Expanding progression systems such as Talents, Perks, Tides, and Ordeals. -Continuing to improve existing heroes, enemies, bosses, towers, maps, game modes, balance, UI, visuals, performance, and overall polish. These plans may evolve throughout Early Access as we learn from player feedback and determine which additions and improvements benefit the game most.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Wavecrashers currently offers a complete and fully playable core game loop across Normal and Tides modes, with progression systems, character customization, and a substantial pool of content already in place. Current content includes: -8 unique playable heroes, each with their own abilities and Talent trees. -Perks for persistent account progression. -130+ items with a wide variety of effects, synergies, and build possibilities. -2 fully playable game modes: Normal and Tides. -A complete combat, tower defense, progression, boss, and itemization loop. Early Access development will focus on expanding this foundation while continuing to improve balance, variety, performance, and overall polish.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, we plan to gradually raise the price during Early Access to reflect the new content and improvements. Early supporters will be able to purchase the game at a lower price as a thank-you for joining us early. All updates during Early Access will be completely free.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback is one of the main reasons we chose to launch in Early Access. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Lane Defense Hordes of enemies spawn in waves to attempt to destroy the core building. Three players team up to defeat them and must endure to the final wave to win the round. Hero Mastery Every hero is built around a distinct combat identity, but none are locked into a single role. Shape them through Talents, Perks and powerful items , creating builds that can bend -or completely redefine- their intended playstyle. Like in a MOBA, heroes continue to evolve throughout each battle as you purchase items, strengthen key attributes, unlock synergies, and adapt your build to the challenges ahead. Across runs, Talents and Perks open even more possibilities, allowing familiar heroes to grow into increasingly specialized versions of themselves. Tides When the normal battle is no longer enough, descend into the Tides -an escalating series of challenges that steadily reshapes the rules of the game. Each Tide introduces harsher conditions, stronger enemies, tighter preparation windows, empowered bosses, disruptive effects, and other dangers that demand increasingly refined builds, coordination, and adaptation. Every Tide run also brings Ordeals : dangerous choices that emerge during battle and force you to decide how the struggle will evolve. As you conquer higher Tides, increasingly severe Ordeals become available, pushing runs into even more unpredictable and punishing territory. Victory unlocks the next Tide, stacking greater pressure onto future battles. Your progression comes with you: Perks and Talents give you the tools to survive these mounting challenges and further specialize your heroes. Rising Difficulty: Each Tide adds new modifiers and increasingly dangerous conditions to the battlefield. Choose Your Ordeal: Face impactful choices during every Tide run, with more dangerous Ordeals unlocking at higher levels. Earn Your Ascent: Defeat a Tide to unlock the next and push deeper into Wavecrashers’ toughest content. Bring Your Progression: Perks and Talents become increasingly important as the Tides grow more unforgiving. Highlights Co-op Gameplay: Form your trio and stand together against the enemy waves. Can be played with bots. No Pay to Win: All progression is earned through gameplay. Replayability: Tides' procedural gameplay keeps every run unpredictable and delivers a fresh challenge. Designed for Fair Play: Game's systems are built to prevent exploits and cheating.
