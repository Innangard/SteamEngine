A recent spike in spirits crossing over from another dimension within the borders of the city has them puzzling over what’s going on. With friends needing help - and a popular band scheduled to kick off their big comeback tour in Demashq - Samar needs to work quickly before the city is overrun with stans and spirits alike! Spirit Swap: Lofi Beats to Match-3 To blends two major sources of inspiration: the joyful, colorful puzzle mechanics of Panel de Pon and the heartfelt and playful storytelling of visual novels/dating sims. Swap to a custom lofi soundtrack by the incredible Meltycanon. Cast powerful spells by arranging spirits into special patterns to unlock potent powers. Bond with a diverse cast of witches and demons … and maybe even smooch. Decorate your bedroom. Earn trinkets and keepsakes as you play, then place them to decorate Samar’s room. Enjoy unique game modes , including Endless and local Versus!
