Mini Airways: Busy Skies brings 3 new mechanics / tools and 5 new maps, inviting you to tackle even busier airspace. 3 New Mechanics / Tools Air Corridor: Open a high-altitude route above congested low-altitude traffic, unaffected by low-altitude aircraft, mountain areas, or restricted areas. Stopover Flight: After landing, the aircraft enters the waiting area and must take off again toward its departure target, with successful landing and completed departure each awarding a score. Overflight: Guide the aircraft to its matching control handoff point to complete the transfer and earn a score. 5 New Maps Germany · Munich Airport: Includes the Air Corridor tool. China · Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport: Includes Stopover Flights and the Air Corridor tool. Türkiye · Istanbul Airport: Includes Overflights and the Air Corridor tool. United States · Chicago O'Hare International Airport: Includes Stopover Flights and Overflights. France · Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport: Includes Stopover Flights, Overflights, and the Air Corridor tool. Map Compatibility *For now, these three new mechanics are only active on maps in the Busy Skies DLC. We will adjust them based on your feedback and gameplay data, and gradually make them available on all maps in some form in the future. *As we are currently reworking multiplayer gameplay, DLC maps do not support multiplayer for now. We will add support in the future. *In the Black Box system, DLC maps will not appear as starred Scenarios (the daily leaderboard maps), but two players who both own the DLC can support each other using a Scenario Share Code.
