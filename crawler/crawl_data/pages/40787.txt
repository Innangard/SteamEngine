View Community Hub About This Game Begin with a single spell. Defeat skeletons to earn gold, unlock new magic, and raise a holy army that fights beside you. Each upgrade makes your wizard and troops stronger as the battlefields become larger and more dangerous. Fight through the Necromancer King's legions, defeat powerful bosses, and keep building your power until the undead can no longer stand against you. Recruit holy troops and strengthen their ranks as the campaign continues. Your army fights alongside your wizard, holds back the undead, and adds more power to every battle. Combine troops with elemental and arcane spells to create a force capable of breaking through the Necromancer King's growing legions. Spend the gold you earn in battle across a massive skill tree. Unlock new spells, improve your damage, increase your attack speed, and choose upgrades that change how your magic works. Each upgrade creates a visible difference on the battlefield. Attacks become stronger, cover more ground, and destroy larger groups of enemies as your build develops. Advance through many different battlefields, each with its own boss. Battles grow in scale as you progress, bringing more enemies, stronger threats, and hundreds of troops onto the screen.
