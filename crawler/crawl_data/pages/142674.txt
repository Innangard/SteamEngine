Parasight is a 2D Action-RPG with souls-like mechanics. In this game, you will embody a little girl striving to save herself from a perilous biological virus, confronting numerous challenges and hardships along the way. Far away in the galaxy, there existed an Earth-like planet called Velasterham. The story begins in the tranquil and peaceful country of Bonrham, formed by the union of 40 cities. Life was simple and sweet for everyone. In a large, old house, a girl lived with her family. One sunny day, she ventured outside the city to visit her friend and play. After a delightful day, as they were heading back home, they stumbled upon a strange plant. From that moment on, the girl's life was turned upside down. A virus seemed to be spreading in Bonrham, and thus began the tale of... - Classes - The game features a souls-like style with six unique classes, each boasting its own special powers. *Knight: A tough, strong fighter with shiny armor and powerful damage, master of great weapons. *Mage: A magic brawler, casting mighty magic and frozen spells from afar to deal with enemies, but frail in close combat. *Priestess: A holy warrior with a high amount of HP, capable of casting holy spells to heal herself or lightning spells to deal bone-breaking damage. *Witch: A warrior of darkness, masterful in casting dark magic that ignores armor, or flame spells that burn the souls of enemies. However, dark and flame spells have consequences, such as draining health. *Thief: A blindingly fast fighter, light and quick, using both hands in combat to deal a lot of damage in just seconds. *Ranger: A ranged fighter, adept at slashing enemies with whips and mastering any ranged weapon.
