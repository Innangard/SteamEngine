Tight Platofrming Test your skills with carefully crafted platforming sections that demand precision and quick reflexes. Each maneuver is designed to offer a rewarding experience for those who enjoy a challenge. Explore the Hidden World Around You Unlock the "Memories" mechanic to shift the environment into a different state, revealing hidden paths, secrets, and new challenges. Use this unique ability to explore alternate versions of each level and uncover the mysteries that lie beneath the surface. Take Control Harness the power of the "Possess" mechanic to take command of special yellow orbs scattered throughout the levels. Use these orbs to manipulate the environment, solve intricate puzzles, and overcome challenging obstacles, giving you a creative edge in navigating the world around you. Race to the Top Compete against the clock and other players in this thrilling Time Trial mode! Collect green cubes scattered throughout the level and race to finish in the shortest time possible. Your final score combines your completion time and the number of cubes collected, adding a strategic layer to each run. Climb the online leaderboards, refine your path, and challenge yourself to become the best! A Scenic Route Immerse yourself in stunning environments filled with vibrant colors, enhanced by a soothing and atmospheric soundtrack that sets the perfect tone for a calming yet captivating experience. Personalize Customize your experience with a variety of unique skins for your ball. Choose from 9 distinct designs available at launch, with more skins to be added in future updates.
