Experience a mix of puzzles and thrilling chases in a colorful world inspired by 90s arcade games. Our heroes Faren, Jenna and Tobi must blast everything in their path to escape the guardian of the artifact they've just stolen: the magic bomb. Push crates and arrange dynamites to create chain reactions and get as much points as possible. But be careful: if the monster catches up with you, it's game over! A FULL SPEED & EXPLOSIVE JOURNEY Travel across 60 levels in 5 different environments with unique mechanics. The chase takes you through Valleys , Swamps and Forests , up to snowy Mountains and the Volcano for a fiery finale. FACE YOUR FRIENDS IN A NEW KIND OF DUEL, LOCAL OR ONLINE Get ahead of your opponent in a head-to-head sprint ! Here, whoever leads sets the pace . Show off your swiftness and brainpower by solving puzzles faster to get ahead. Use your powers to obstruct the way and slow down your opponent. VARIOUS GAME MODES: Adventure: 8 worlds, 60 levels. Time attack: Collect 3 medals in under a minute in exclusive levels. Score attack: Beat the developers' scores. Infinite: Difficulty constantly increases, like in the great classics of puzzle games. Versus mode: local & online! BEGINNER FRIENDLY YET INSANE AT HIGH LEVELS Designed like an arcade game, PathBlasters offers gradual difficulty of challenges. Beginners have plenty of time to get to grips with the game's mechanics, while experts are not left out when it comes to the challenges of the terrifying side levels!
