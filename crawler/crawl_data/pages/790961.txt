Brave the molten fury of an active volcano, delve into dungeon-filled ruins lost to time, or soar above the searing stone arches of Dragon’s Reach: looming like a scorched gateway between worlds carved by time and fire, where a deep cavern pulses with a beautifully eerie ambient glow and jagged crystals line the walls like teeth. Wander the wide, windswept Scotland Hills, find serenity along the shores of a pristine frozen lake, or carve your path through mist-shrouded swamps and the frozen dens of Polar Bears and Ice Wyverns. The Bison roams these lands as a horned juggernaut of highland havoc, its brute strength matched only by its surprising utility. And somewhere between legend and survival... the mythical Griffin awaits. Ragnarok is a playground for explorers, builders, and adventurers alike. This is a land where myths breathe, dangers stir, legends are born and the saga is yours to shape! Forested plains stretch toward half-swallowed ruins while winding rivers carve their way throughout. Redwoods Expansion: colossal trees rooted in rolling plains, their canopies brushing the sky above sheer cliffs that drop dramatically into the sea. From vast, sun-dappled clearings to the ocean-chiseled edges of the land, oversized, ancient, and untamed. It’s a place where giants walk and the wild still reigns. Desert Expansion: A pair of sunbaked specks adrift in the sea. Beyond the arid sands lies a secluded grotto teeming with crabs, weathered ruins, and natural stone formations.
