A princess who would rather rescue herself. A ghost in desperate need of a holiday. Clarice's book is full of unusual characters—and a few missing words. Finding Words is a relaxing word-search game inside an illustrated fantasy storybook. Read each page, look for its missing words in the puzzle and drag through the letters to reveal them. Every discovery fills a gap in the tale, bringing you closer to its ending. Ten tales, fifty puzzles Explore ten short stories set in a playful medieval world, each with five word-search pages and an illustrated ending. Meet awkward knights, mischievous apprentices and monsters with surprisingly ordinary problems. Choose the tale that catches your eye and discover how it unfolds, page by page. Make the book your own Finding a word also reveals a little sticker inspired by it. Watch your discoveries decorate the edges of the book as the story takes shape. Warm illustrations, colourful highlights and gentle magical effects turn each completed page into a small keepsake. Enjoy the next page at your own pace There is no timer to rush you. Take a hint when you need one, pause whenever you like and return to your saved reading. Whether you have a few minutes or a whole quiet afternoon, another tale is waiting. 10 humorous fantasy tales and 50 word-search puzzles. Missing words that become part of the story when you find them. Collectible stickers and illustrated endings. Optional hints, saved progress and individual tale completion tracking. Relaxing music, page-turning sounds and 15 Steam achievements. Play in English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Russian or Simplified Chinese.
