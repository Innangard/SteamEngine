You can raise fish manually or use the Auto - AFK function. Sell the grown fry to make money. You can also keep the adult fish in the fish tank to generate considerable income. Buy fry packs to unlock new fish! No cleaning or water changes—just feed your fish and watch them joyfully scramble for food! Enjoy soothing white noise and deeply relaxing background music. Sit back, listen to tunes, and watch your fish—perfect for a full day of chill "productivity." There are 144 fish species to collect (with more coming!). Purchase packs of various levels to unlock rare shiny and colorful variants—and maybe even hatch a Fish King! Water wisteria, Rotala rotundifolia, Ludwigia repens, Hygrophila polysperma 'Roraima', Apistogramma viejita... A wide variety of aquatic plants, as well as driftwoods of different postures and strange - looking landscaping stones, are waiting for you to arrange.
