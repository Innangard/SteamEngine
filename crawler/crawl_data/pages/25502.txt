Developed by an indie developer who loves volleyball! The sports game made after numerous trial and error to contain the appeal of volleyball! ■ Try out various volleyball strategies! Quicks, pipes, open attacks, and fast tempo attacks in a 3v3 volleyball match! Achieve your greatest spike in diverse scenarios in your screen! ■ Immersive gameplay! Meet the attractive characters that appear in the universe of The Spike! Join Siwoo with his journey in both volleyball and inner growth. ■ Diverse volleyball contents! Is normal volleyball boring for you? Enjoy different types of volleyball contents such as tournament, colosseum, beach volleyball, and more.
