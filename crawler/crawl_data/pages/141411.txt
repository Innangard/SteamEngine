Can you kill her and change your destiny or are you doomed to die? SYNOPSIS The Banshee is a supernatural spirit of unknown origin that announces the death of living beings by shouting or crying. One night, the Banshee announced that you would die, and you decided not to accept it, starting a relentless hunt for the Banshee, to prevent your tragic fate. FEATURES Banshee : A supernatural spirit from Irish folklore that announces death by shouting or crying. Terrifying Enemies : Enemies roam the island looking for living beings to kill. Variety of Weapons : Kill with up to 9 different weapons hidden across the map. Resource Management : Find and manage health syringes and ammo to stay alive. Night Vision Goggles : Wear night vision goggles to see better in the dark. Historical Documents : Discover the history of the Banshee and the island's inhabitants through documents spread across the map.
