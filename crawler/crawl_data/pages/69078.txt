Things are warming up! Well, that’s fine by us, because we know you’re ready to bring the heat. It’s a vital facility where we forge weapons to help defend earth, and it must be protected at all costs. It’s a great opportunity to make use of the scorching environment and submerge our little pink friends in molten iron. This item deals immense damage while holding enemies in place temporarily, making for easy targets. New Item – Napalm Mine Things are getting serious. The general has just dropped us a Napalm Mine. Our scientists have been hard at work, and they’ve provided us with blueprints for a new type of outfit that will protect you against elemental damage types.
