Content For This Game Browse all (1) Tricky Cow - Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game Tricky Cow is an engaging and delightful platformer where players take on the challenge of clearing all platforms and reaching the iconic cowbell to advance through 100 uniquely designed levels. With simple controls reminiscent of classic platformers like Mario , Tricky Cow's levels are compact, one-screen puzzles that pack plenty of challenge and fun. As you progress, the game introduces new mechanics to keep you on your hooves. From timer tiles that require quick reflexes and dodging projectiles to trampolines that launch you into the air, Tricky Cow tests both your puzzle-solving skills and agility. Each level will have you thinking carefully about your moves, sometimes twice or even thrice, before making the right decision! The difficulty ramps up as you progress, offering an addictive blend of strategy and fast-paced action. With flying spikes and tricky tiles to deactivate, every level feels fresh and exciting. And don't forget about the achievements! Can you complete all 100 levels and prove your platforming prowess? Think playing as a fox or a cat was easy? Try being a cow in Tricky Cow ! This charming platformer will have you hooked from start to finish. Perfect for fans of puzzle-platformers and those looking for a challenging yet fun experience. Key Features: 100 levels packed with unique mechanics Tricky one-screen puzzles requiring both thought and quick reflexes Fun new challenges like timer tiles, trampolines, and flying spikes Classic platforming action with a unique twist Achievements to unlock as you master each level Prepare yourself for a hoof-tastic adventure and see if you can make it to the cowbell in Tricky Cow !
