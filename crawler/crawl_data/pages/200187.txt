The zombie apocalypse is upon us and it's up to you to evacuate everyone before they fall prey to the undead. GROUND CONTROL Conduct massive rescue operations with a helping hand from snipers, artillery crews, orbital bombardments, and more. Experience the tactical richness of fully-destructible environments and randomly-generated campaigns. Mercs for hire: Hire and upgrade your ragtag group of snipers, demo teams, orbital bombardment crews, and more. No two cities are the same: Cities are randomly-generated, giving a unique experience with each playthrough. Destructible environments: You can absolutely blow up everything. Play with friends: Up to three people can cooperatively play together. Mod support: Mix and modify the game rules however you like.
