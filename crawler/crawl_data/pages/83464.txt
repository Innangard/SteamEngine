Download Flatspace IIk Golden Age Music Pack Free To Play Download View Community Hub About This Content A new and exclusive collection of 4 in-game music tracks to enhance Flatspace IIk. Like any in-game music for Flatspace, these will trigger at random when you enter a new sector. This pack contains special Flatspace edits of four tracks from the 2023 album The Golden Age by Mark Sheeky. The pack includes a full description of the music as a pdf booklet. Here is an outline of the music included: Welcome To My Gallery A rip-roaring chase down the a musical, and artistic, wormhole. This track also opens the Golden Age album. Nature Morte Nature Morte is the French term for a still life painting. A gentle track, played live in one take on a Yamaha MODX synthesizer. Cotán Juan Sánchez Cotán was a painter of gentle, almost hypnotically sacred, paintings. This track attempts to evoke his monastic mood. Tycho Brahe The Noseless Astronomer In a break from the painter theme, this song is about Danish astronomer Tycho Brahe. This Flatspace edit has something of a bold alien march about it.
