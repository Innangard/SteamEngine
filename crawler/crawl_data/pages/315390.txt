View Community Hub About This Game Butcherman is a psychological horror walking simulator with escape room elements. It's a short game , averaging 30 minutes to complete. Inspired by a real case involving a victim who was held captive for a long period by a serial killer, enduring both physical and psychological abuse. Explore the disturbing world of a serial killer Dive into an atmosphere steeped in the pain, sorrow, and oppression of being controlled by a psychopathic, sadistic mind. Solve puzzles Investigate secrets, search for items, and gather resources that may lead you to an exit. Growing unease Being imprisoned and controlled can drive you to madness, and death becomes an increasingly imminent possibility.
