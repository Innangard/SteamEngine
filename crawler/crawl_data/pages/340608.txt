After years of begging, your parents have finally sent you off to Camp Lilypad, your dream sleep-away camp on a picturesque lake. Surely this will be a summer to remember. But one night, a fellow camper double-dog-dares you to “borrow” a rowboat and sneak into the neighboring camp across the lake. A camp full of clowns. And they don’t take too kindly to trespassers. Run. Hide. Fight ( if you must ). Survive until you can return to the safe side of the lake. Along the way, uncover the disturbing truth behind Clown Camp. Find environmental clues and postcards among the creaking cabins and bristling pine trees. A sharp eye will uncover many secrets hidden in the darkness (some of which may be best left unfound). Carefully manage a small inventory that is essential to survival. Bask in the dithered glow of a design philosophy inspired by the days when surviving horror required both courage AND skill. Low poly 3D models, uncanny animations, grainy filters, tight narrative, and challenging gameplay take center stage once again. Additional modern quality of life features provide wider accessibility options on top of an intentionally retro base experience throughout a 3-5 hour story mode. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Settings in the game are very dark and isolated. Strong atmosphere of fear. Blood and gore is limited to pixelated particle effects and a red screen overlay upon taking damage, but can be toggled off in the options menu (or color swapped to green).
