Step into a thrilling and immersive horror experience based on the SCP universe. Choose from multiple roles, including scientists, Class-D personnel, various SCPs, facility guards, MTF operatives, and Chaos Insurgency members! In SCP: Containment Breach 2, a wide range of terrifying adventures awaits. Immerse yourself in a highly atmospheric, suspense-filled world where every corner holds new dangers, and every decision could mean life or death Experience the Full Story in Single-Player or Multiplayer Co-op! Welcome to Story Mode , a thrilling experience you can enjoy solo or with friends . As a Class-D test subject, escape the site during a facility-wide breach, encountering some of the SCP Foundation’s most dangerous entities and navigating endless corridors. With multiple possible endings, your choices shape your journey as you uncover secrets, evade threats, and strive for survival in the chaos. Can you emerge unscathed, or will the horrors lurking within get the better of you? Fight for Survival or Control in PvP Breach Mode! In PvP Breach Mode , take on various roles—whether as Class-D personnel, a scientist, a guard, an elite MTF unit, or even a member of the Chaos Insurgency. This mode features randomized map generation, making each playthrough a unique experience. Form alliances, cooperate, or betray others in a high-stakes setting where every interaction can lead to intense, dynamic scenarios. Can you survive or secure the facility against other players from around the world? Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Includes gore, violence and drug use. Gore and violence usually occurs in tandem with the use of firearms and the likes.
