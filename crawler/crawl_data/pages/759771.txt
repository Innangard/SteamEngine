View Community Hub About This Game Every click, every keystroke, every button press lets you progress. Create your own little cat hero and embark on an enormous adventure filled with enemies, epic bosses, and LOOT . Go on, little adventurer. Type, type, type! (And don’t forget to grab your loot.) With over 200 unique items , you can create your perfect hero. Want to be a wizard, rogue, or warrior? Easy. Want to land a MEGA bonk with a massive mace? Go for it. Want to wear a helmet that’s way too big for your tiny head? Absolutely. Explore 6 unique biomes , each packed with dangerous enemies: The scorching desert crawling with scorpions A dense forest so thick you can’t see your own paws A mysterious dungeon, filled with traps, living shadows, and ancient guardians Don’t wander alone and bring your friends. Show off your latest gear, tackle tougher enemies, or just get a little extra help. Jump into up to 4-player online co-op and let your friends guide you (or carry you) through your journey. Adventure is better together.
