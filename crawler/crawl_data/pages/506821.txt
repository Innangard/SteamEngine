This absurd adventure follows the mischievous Tom on her delivery rounds, which always seem to go awry. No surprise there, since Tom spends most of her time peeking inside people’s homes whenever she gets the chance. Join Tom in a charming village where every resident has something to hide. You deliver their packages, but what do they do with them once out of sight? Every window, every door, every crack in the wall is an opportunity to learn more about the inhabitants and their nasty secrets: an orphanage that looks more like a kennel, two party-loving priests, a couple of chainsaw-wielding hippies… Human nature in all its dreadful diversity awaits you! Point and Click? Walk and Stalk! Dive into unique hand-drawn scenes and discover countless hiding spots where you can spy on the residents. Take a closer look and witness situations that may be disturbing, or even unholy. Will that stop you from peeking? Quite the opposite. Frame-by-frame animation gives this  world a retro feel and immerses you in a strange village where even the most mundane events turn suspicious. A guilty pleasure Embrace the guilty pleasure of stepping into Tom’s shoes as you adopt her voyeuristic behavior and break every established rule. Your adventure is marked by events as outrageous as they are zany, leading you deeper into the village’s mysteries. From drug-dealing pigeons hiding in the cemetery to a wandering cowboy worm or a grumpy squirrel, it’s not just the humans who deserve your attention. So watch your step… or don’t. Now it’s up to you to decide whether to focus on your work or disrupt public order. Will you resist the temptation to cut down perfectly trimmed hedges, trample flowers in gardens, or steal decorations? Features Unrestrained voyeurism: don’t be afraid to peek, every seemingly mundane detail can reveal the villagers’ madness. Transgressive gameplay: stalk the villagers, open their packages, or destroy their gardens. With Tom, almost anything goes. Somewhere between Adult Swim and a children’s book: the apparent calm of the hand-drawn black-and-white visuals contrasts with the unsettling lack of restraint displayed by the villagers Tom spies on. The joy of rediscovery: with each playthrough, uncover new details you may have missed and experiment with more or less cautious approaches to reveal all the villagers’ secrets.
