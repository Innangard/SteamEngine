View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Turquoise , a cozy collecting and building game that immerses you in the heart of a protected marine area. In this peaceful space, you’re free to imagine, decorate, and shape an environment where underwater life can flourish. Design the seabed with an intuitive decoration editor: place rocks, corals, plants, and objects to create a harmonious habitat. Every detail matters to attract and welcome new marine species, each more fascinating than the last. Build, observe, learn. As you discover new fish and creatures, you unlock their entries in the built-in encyclopedia , where you can learn more about their behaviors, preferences, and origins. A calm, educational, and inspiring experience. Choose from 3 starting zones and build your own reef. Use an intuitive decoration editor with more than 250 assets . Collect over 50 fish and marine creatures . Access an integrated encyclopedia to learn more about discovered species. Enjoy a relaxing and contemplative atmosphere. Care for your corner of the ocean, watch it evolve, and let marine magic unfold. Observe wildlife thriving in the environment you’ve created. 2 game modes : Discovery and Sandbox.
