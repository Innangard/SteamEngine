Discover the bustling settlement of these adorable creatures. See how hard they work, play, and laze around. Find them all! 100 Hidden Pandas: Village is a hidden object/hidden creature game. The player's task is to find cleverly hidden fluffy pandas in a detailed, hand-drawn scene. The game's aesthetic visuals are accompanied by calming music and sound effects designed to relax the player. There is also a hint system implemented, showing where the player might have missed one of the pandas—some of these cute creatures have found really good hiding places. Game features: 100 adorable fluffy pandas to find, a detailed scene depicting life in the village, calm and pleasant music, a hint system, progress saving.
