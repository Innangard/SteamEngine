Simulator 3 – Singapore Changi Airport (WSSS) Take control of air traffic at Singapore Changi Airport (WSSS) in Tower! Simulator 3 and experience one of the world’s most renowned and efficient international airports. Located east of Singapore’s city center, Changi is a major global hub connecting Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and Oceania, offering nonstop action and high operational complexity. Manage heavy international traffic across three parallel runways , coordinating long-haul wide-body arrivals, dense regional operations, and cargo movements. Changi’s layout allows for high-capacity runway usage and continuous traffic flow, challenging controllers to maintain precision while handling peak-hour surges and around-the-clock operations. This detailed add-on recreates Singapore Changi Airport with an authentic runway and taxiway layout, realistic ATC frequencies, and full customization options. Adjust traffic density, weather, and time of day to simulate everything from calm overnight operations to intense long-haul arrival banks, making WSSS a must-have destination for Tower!
