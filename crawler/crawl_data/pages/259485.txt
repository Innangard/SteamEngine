It is enriched by female choirs which add a supernatural and mysterious quality to the music, as well as world music instruments such as Oud, the Banjo or the Duduck to give characters a folkloric feel. Each character has its own melody, which you will find all along your playthrough to ensure cohesion with the game’s scenario.
