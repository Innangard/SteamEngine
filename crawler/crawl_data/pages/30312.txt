A Soul, born without purpose, locked away and forgotten. Her prison served as both sanctuary and shackle. Her fate: doomed by her flawless design. Millennia passed. But the true threat towers overhead… Travel the path of the Colossus and ascend its levels in a race to confront The False Son. Defeat him and unlock the true power of his soul! Through her mastery of soul manipulation, Seeker can exact incredible damage on nearby monsters while simultaneously unleashing potent healing to sustain her teammates. This unique combination of brawling and healing defines Seeker as a Survivor. Players who learn to seamlessly flow between these roles will be rewarded with one of the most versatile Survivors to ever grace an escape pod. Seeker’s presence can turn the tide of any battle, offering both devastation and salvation in equal measure. Embrace duality and lead your team to victory! Those years of primal culinary misadventures have trained Chef to have incredible control over his trusty cleavers. Hurl them at faraway enemies and call them back to slice and dice with twice the pain. Master the art of the cleaver and filet your way to victory. The False Son excels as an immovable object. His massive scale, incredible health, and unmatched regeneration make him a particularly resilient Survivor. In close-range encounters, his hulking club can crush foes into submission. However, outside of clubbing range, fights become far more challenging.
