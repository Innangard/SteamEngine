A little or a lot? With a unique hint system, you control how much of the solution is revealed in each level. Which way should the clock hands point? How to arrange the eggs? Who put so many stickers on this fruit?!? Come enjoy a calming world in an observational puzzle game with surprises around every corner. Featuring charming illustrations and surprising scenarios, A Little to the Left is satisfying and curious with over 100 delightful puzzles to discover. Keep your eye out for a mischievous cat who has an inclination for chaos! In the Daily Tidy mode, you’ll get a puzzle delivered EVERY DAY with variations on our favorite puzzles that’s unique to you.
