This game blends my favorite genres: farming sim and incremental games. I hope I made the incremental part satisfying and not disturbing the overall wholesomeness. Features 🌷 Beauty quantified. Wilhelmina has one ambition - to make her field the most beautiful in the kingdom. Beauty of the field is the number that you grow in the game. All other elements contribute to this main focus. 🌷 Villagers with personality. You neighbours are nice folks. They love to chat and participate in your field life. Their small requests guide you through various field upgrades, and their gifts bump up the beauty score. 🌷 Idle mode built in. Designed from the start to work both as an active game and a hands-off idle experience. 🌷 Gradual complexity. A progression tree introduces new tulip types, tools, and mechanics step by step, keeping things cozy. Plant bulbs, water them, and help them flourish into colourful tulips. Every day the tulips are scored for beauty. The more you score, the sooner you level up. Discover new tulip types as your farm develops. Breed flowers to reveal fresh colour mixes and rare double blooms that add depth and beauty to your designs. Check in to plan your next pattern, or step away and let your tulips grow on their own. Your field keeps evolving even while you’re not playing. Chat with your neighbours who stop by your farm and learn their stories. Completing small favours for them  brings decoration rewards and a few friendly smiles along the way.
