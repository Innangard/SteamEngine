[HERROR] Gas Station Case Dan Cooper, a 25-year-old night shift gas station worker in Ohio, recently laid off from a uranium plant, is trying to put his life back together. But everything changes when his friend Michael discovers a mysterious case by the lake. Soon, people begin to vanish in the surrounding woods, and the cozy gas station turns into a deadly trap. Gameplay This game is primarily a first-person narrative walking simulator featuring exploration, work, and dialogue mechanics, inspired by series like Fears to Fathom and Chilla's Art . Key Features Atmospheric environments and cozy visuals. Light VHS tape aesthetics. Experience working as a gas station attendant. A carefully crafted story full of small details. Optional “Silent Screamers” mode for players who fear jumpscares but still want to enjoy the atmosphere and story of horror. One-sitting episode. Demo 20–30 min. Full chapter 90–120 min. Anthology format — episodes connected by tone, not plot. If you like Fears to Fathom, Chilla’s Art, Zoochosis or Mouthwashing, you’ll feel at home here. Optimized for mid-range PCs and laptops. Scalable graphics options. DMCA-safe soundtrack. The in-game vintage film is public domain — safe for streaming. Everyday setting: night-shift job at a gas station. Job-sim flavor: serve customers, answer calls, keep the shift running. Emotional moments that evoke vivid reactions: fear, laughter, tension, surprise and more.
