Utilize a variety of tools and equipment to interact with a ragdoll chicken under the watchful eye of "your" Uncle Tontine. Commune with other children who have been whisked away from their homes in search of the truth behind your mysterious surroundings. Teach dead rats to smoke cigarettes, play with loaded firearms, throw dance parties for cockroaches. The requests of the carnival are many and strange, but if you are to survive this place you must solve the many riddles and charades revolving around the Forever Chicken. Sometimes it desires to be shot, sometimes it desires to explode, sometimes it merely wants to watch. Whatever the chicken seeks you must determine before the night is through.
