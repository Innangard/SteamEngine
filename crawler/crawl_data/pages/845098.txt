Features: - Realistic modeling with over 50 custom modeled buildings. - Detailed ground polygon model - Taxi signs - Runway approach lighting. - OSM (open street map) background buildings and roads - Beautiful background scenery. The airport covers 1,741 acres (705 ha) and has two parallel runways. It is the West Coast air and truck hub for UPS Airlines and is a major distribution point for FedEx Express. Since Ontario's longest runway (runway 8L/26R) is longer than three of the four runways at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), it is an alternate landing site for large aircraft destined for LAX.
