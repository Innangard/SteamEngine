About This Game A place to unwind, mix drinks, and serve warm meals to charming guests—whether in f ullscreen or tucked neatly at the bottom of your screen as you go about your day. Keep your bar stocked, set the perfect table, and welcome your guests with care. After all, the heart of a great bar is the people inside it. Hire staff to keep things running while you sit back and relax. No rush, no pressure—just the soothing hum of a bar that flows on its own. And... of course, if the mood strikes, you can always jump in. Style your bar, set the mood, and make it the coziest spot by the lake. And don’t forget the little yard above—it’s yours to decorate and shape however you like. Grow and harvest crops for fresh bar ingredients, assisted by magical garden spirits. Store your harvest and unlock special hidden menu items. Special customers are visiting to rate your bar. Serve them well - this bar is yours to run. Unlock new drinks, snacks, and music. Try out fresh recipes to impress your guests. Expand your resources for a smoother flow. Enjoy various mini games Twitch integration And much more, all at your pace!
