You travel to the Squirrel Kingdom – a place full of weird characters, dangerous enemies and lots of treasures to find. King Daniel, party animal and ruler of the Squirrel Kingdom, has got a special job for you! Ring the three bells of awakening! 3D Platformer Jump your way through the Squirrel Kingdom to collect all sorts of valuable treasures! In the Squirrel Kingdom it’s all about the money! You need to find enough moneybags to proceed to different areas of the Squirrel Kingdom. Collect coins to unlock new weapons or buy a homeless cat some coffee! Combat Fight against an assortment of dangerous enemies, all with unique attack patterns. Attack your foes with a handful of different, unique weapons like a dead fish or a 5-string banjo. Avoid incoming attacks by dodging or parrying them.  Lock on to your enemies in this Souls inspired combat system. Parry your enemies by hitting them at the right moment with a thrown item. Once parried, perform a devastating deathblow attack that recovers some of your health. Items Pick up, throw and use tons of different items. Throw bombs, ride on chicken, drink coffee or get drunk consuming “Hobo Brew”. Are you ready to pee-hover while playing the banjo? Jobs There are a lot of ways to make a buck in the Squirrel Kingdom. Meet weird characters, all with even weirder jobs for you to complete. Earn cash for easy tasks or even get a bag full of gold for more difficult jobs. Use this cash to buy new weapons, open new areas or buy a homeless cat some coffee!
