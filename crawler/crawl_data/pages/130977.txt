Download Avorion - Behemoth Event Series Free Download View Community Hub About This Content "The Behemoths are coming! These legendary ships will now attack helpless sectors - unless someone stops them." The Behemoths expansion brings a new series of events to Avorion. Very large and strong Behemoth ships will be attacking random sectors every few hours without previous warning in all 4 directions of the galaxy! The attacks will be called out in the entire galaxy. Defeat them quickly or they will turn the entire sector into ashes. (Player owned sectors are safe from Behemoth attacks) Of course defeating them comes with its own rewards: 4 new, unique and powerful upgrades will be dropped by one of the 4 Behemoth variants respectively! 4 new Behemoth Bosses The Behemoths are a new enemy type of massive ships with never before seen durability and firepower. Gather your strongest ships if you want to take them on! 4 new unique Subsystems Each of the Behemoths drops a unique subsystem bringing a new level of specialization to your crafts. These new systems will provide even better stats to more specialized crafts. Behemoths can be enabled/disabled at will, but will be always disabled when the DLC is not installed. The DLC is not required to play on a multiplayer server which has the DLC enabled.
