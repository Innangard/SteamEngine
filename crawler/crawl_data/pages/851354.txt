For many years, loco-hauled trains were the ‘meat and vegetables’ of passenger rail transport in Germany. Unlike DMUs, which have a driver’s cab at both ends of the train, loco-hauled trains needed a way to be controlled on the return trip, and the driving trailer allows this to happen with no need to uncouple the loco from the coaches. This pack recreates Regional Express services on the Frankfurt – Karlsruhe route, and features the classic BR218 diesel loco, double-decker coaches and DBbzf driving trailer. This is a highly-detailed pack with many features, including variable speed windscreen wipers, switchable instrument lights and realistic operating systems Included Scenarios A selection of engaging standard scenarios for Frankfurt Highspeed are included: 218/2025 - 01: RE 4575 218/2025 - 02: RE 4576 218/2025 - 03: RE38959 Please Note: Frankfurt High Speed: Frankfurt – Karlsruhe Route Extension Add-On is required, as a separate purchase, in order to play the scenarios featured in this add-on.
