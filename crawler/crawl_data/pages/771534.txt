Infinitevania is a 2D platformer in the metroidvania style, heavily inspired by Castlevania: Symphony of the Night and featuring elements from Hollow Knight and Blasphemous. The game focuses on combat, exploration, puzzles, upgrades, and an breathtaking story. Explore different worlds Defeat the most powerful creatures in these worlds, absorb their powers, and grow stronger to destroy the core of Infinitevania and return to your own world. Face challenging enemies Develop unique strategies to overcome them, adapting to each battle as you progress on your journey. Solve puzzles Tackle intriguing enigmas to unlock new areas and hidden secrets. Survive deadly traps Navigate treacherous environments that test your skills and reflexes at every turn. Collect Upgrades to Become Stronger Enhance your abilities and equipment throughout your adventure.
