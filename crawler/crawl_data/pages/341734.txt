View Community Hub About This Game Odd Town is a story-driven first-person adventure that’ll take you around 5+ hours to complete. Take odd jobs to fix your car and discover that the local conspiracy theorists aren’t nearly paranoid enough. Your car has broken down in beautiful St. Sectaire. The locals look at you funny, the phone won’t stop ringing, and you desperately need money to fix your ride. Get to know the peculiar residents and take on their odd jobs. But the longer you stay, the stranger things get. People go missing. The police get more aggressive. Something is very odd here, even by village standards. Explore a bite-sized, handcrafted town. Follow the story from the mundane to the sinister. Find hidden paths, buy useful tools, and poke around in places you probably shouldn’t be. There’s trouble to get into along the way. Steal someone’s lunch and they might fight back. Bring the police into it and your next meal might be behind bars. To uncover what the town is hiding, you’ll need to create a conspiracy of your own. Assemble a team of special specialists, dig up dirty secrets, and try not to get caught.
