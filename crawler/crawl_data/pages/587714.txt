View Community Hub About This Game It's late at night. A little boy finds himself in an empty house. All he see's are toys scattered all over the place. What is going on? He soon remembers the warning his sister told him about the toy monsters! Evil monsters that take away toys from children that don't clean up their toys after playing with them. Be that little boy and find all the toys. Use gadgets in the house, move around the floors using trampolines and elevators. Solve doors puzzles and escape from all the enemies and traps you meet on your way.
