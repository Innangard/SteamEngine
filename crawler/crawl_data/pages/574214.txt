Soundtrack $2.99 $2.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Parkour with a car! Deedlee Doo! Carkour!🚗💥 Have you ever wanted to do parkour with a car? Well then this game is for you! Using explosions you can propel your car into the air and jump back and forth between walls. The jet engine on your car can fine tune your jumps mid-air. If the jump is extra tricky, you can trigger slow motion. This is not an easy game , it takes a lot of skill to reach the end. It also has two multiplayer modes, one where you race other players to the top of the Meowtain and "King of the Catsle" where you fight to stay on top of a cat. Both modes support up to 32 players. Everything is handmade from clay🎨 All of the art in this game was made out of various bits of clay sculpted in real life. My kids even sculpted some of the art in the game. It looks very stupid. I went to art college. My teachers would be proud. The most refined soundscape ever made👄 All of sounds and music are all mouth sounds. That's right. Explosion sounds? PSSCHWWWOOO! Car sounds? NNEEEEOOOOOWWW! Drums? BOOTS AND CATS AND BOOTS AND CATS! Guitars? DEEDLEE DOO!!! If you screw up a jump, children laugh and make fun of you. (Just like real life!😆) Reach the top of the Meowtain!⛰️ The goal of the game is to reach the top of the Meowtain. Which naturally, is in space. The game takes place in one cohesive level with checkpoints. There are the eight sections with different themes such as a forest, desert and space. If you fall you can reset back to the previous checkpoint at anytime. Not an epic open world game or a live service game! Plus it's super cheap! 💰☕️ This game respects your time. It's a few hours long, depending on how good you are at it. You buy it, play it for a bit and have fun. It's less than a cup of coffee. Why is coffee so expensive anyways? Buy more indie games! Deedlee Doo! Carkour!🚗💥 🎸 "Deedlee Doo! Carkour!" is developed by Nate Purkeypile. Nate Purkeypile has been making games for 21+ years and worked on titles such as Skyrim, Fallout 3/4/76, Starfield, Metroid Prime 3 and BloodRayne 2. He was the Lead Artist on Fallout 76 and the Co-Lead/Lead Artist on the Fallout 3 DLC, Point Lookout. After working in AAA games for years and years, he became a solo indie developer. His last indie game was The Axis Unseen, a heavy metal horror game where you hunt monsters from folklore. Now he made this thing. Have fun!
