You have no idea how small our team is, how little our resources are, and how long it has taken us to get this far. Building an entire game that we launch 100% completed day one? That isn't an option for us. Seriously, it just straight up isn't possible. But... if we start small and build this project slowly over time, using community feedback to steer us in the right direction? That works. That's why we're in early access.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The only answer to this question... is that we will be in early access for as long as we need to be. The more that people spread the word, donate, or contribute to development... the faster we'll release out of early access. However, I have no intention to rush that process.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Our ultimate objective is to create a game that has all the same standard features you would expect from a typical multiplayer sandbox shooter. Vehicles, a variety of game modes, a mission editor, and hopefully more.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Currently the game is at our first major milestone... a fully fleshed out first person shooter with a focus on infantry gameplay. The game currently includes single player missions, cooperative game modes, multiplayer servers, mod support, and most other features you'd expect from a standard first person shooter. As time goes on, we intend to continue expanding on these features.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The game will be free. Forever. Patreon members and paid supporters may be granted access to alpha builds of the game, however Operation: Harsh Doorstop will never cost money to play.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Our community IS the development process. Our games have all started as community projects, and they will end as community projects. If you are interested in helping to develop our games, please contact us. Community feedback is critical to our work, and we welcome anyone who is willing to help.” Read more Join the Operation: Harsh Doorstop Playtest Request access and you’ll get notified when the developer is ready for more participants. Operation: Harsh Doorstop is the ultimate tactical sandbox that merges intense military combat with limitless creative potential. Taking inspiration from both classic military simulation and mod-friendly sandbox games, OHD creates a unique experience where authentic warfare meets community-driven content creation. Whether you're engaging in tactical combat or exploring player-created modifications, every session offers something new. Combined arms warfare in Operation: Harsh Doorstop. Tactical Combat Experience warfare where strategy and teamwork define victory. Coordinate with your squad, utilize vehicles effectively, and engage in intense firefights across diverse battlefields. The combat system rewards tactical thinking and team coordination, creating intense moments where every decision impacts the flow of battle. Deployed M240B laying down suppressing fire. Fortification & Supply Systems Adapt to the evolving battlefield with our dynamic fortification system. Establish defensive positions, create supply lines, and reinforce strategic locations with sandbags and tactical barriers. Your team's ability to secure and fortify key positions can turn the tide of battle. Operation: Harsh Doorstop's dynamic construction system. Battlefield Communication Success depends on clear communication and coordination. Our integrated voice system enables: Local voice chat for close-quarters coordination Squad communications for tactical planning Command channels for strategic operations Unlimited Modding Potential At its heart, Operation: Harsh Doorstop is a platform for creativity. Through comprehensive mod support and Steam Workshop integration, creators can build: Custom factions and equipment New vehicles and weapons Original game modes Total conversion mods Unique worlds and scenarios American armored logistics convoy. Key Features Authentic tactical combat with infantry and vehicle warfare Dynamic fortification system Integrated team communication tools Multiplayer, co-op, and single-player modes Comprehensive Steam Workshop support Expansive battlefields Community-driven content creation Regular game updates Step into the future of tactical gaming. Operation: Harsh Doorstop combines authentic military action with unlimited creative possibilities, offering an experience that grows with its community.
