Become an investigator from the mainland sent out to the Isle of Maligree to investigate a missing person case. You only have a few days, as the victims of these crimes are forgotten by everyone shortly after disappearing. In this dark fantasy story, malicious spirits have let magic leak into the world. There has never been a greater need for people like you to control the chaos. In the time you have, collect items and clues, forge connections, and uncover secrets to the best of your ability in order to find who is responsible for people vanishing in Maligree. Key Features: Explore the Isle of Maligree - Forge your own path as the clock ticks away, solve the case your way by discovering where and what you look into. Choose your Fate - Choose one of three starting fates that provide unique options and routes. Collect Items, Discover Secrets - Improve your attributes and find secrets as your choices lead you to discover items of interest. Uncover Multiple Endings - Pick your allies, make your choices, and uncover their consequences.
