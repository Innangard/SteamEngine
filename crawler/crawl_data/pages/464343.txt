At the start of each match, every player receives a hidden role. You might be part of the Town trying to uncover the cult, a Cultist secretly working against everyone, or a Solo role with its own unpredictable objective. Nobody knows who to trust. Lie, investigate, accuse, and survive long enough to reach your victory. When the sun rises, players gather to talk about what happened during the night. Who acted suspiciously? Who might be lying? Players can accuse each other and start a vote. If the town agrees, the suspected player is executed. But be careful. One wrong vote can eliminate an innocent player and bring the cult closer to victory. When night falls, every role secretly performs its ability. Some players investigate houses to gather information. Others protect their allies. Cultists quietly hunt their targets while solo roles move toward their own hidden goals. The events of the night often change everything the next day. 🎭 Unique Roles Each player receives a different role with its own abilities and strategies. Some roles collect information. Some protect others. Some manipulate players or create chaos. Because of this, every match unfolds differently. ⚔️ Three Competing Factions Town Work together to uncover and eliminate the cult. Cultists Hide among the town and eliminate or convert players until you take control. Solo Roles Play your own game. Outsmart everyone and complete your unique win condition.
