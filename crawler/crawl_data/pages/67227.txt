Köp Hansa Forever: Knarrs of Vikings $1.00 Köp Visa gemenskapscentral Om detta material Knarrs of Vikings is a small, optional ship DLC for Hansa Forever. It adds one ship type: the square-sailed Norwegian Knarr, which can be built in Bergen and Oslo. What is included The Knarr ship type for your fleet. Knarr construction in Bergen and Oslo. Distinctive Knarr ship artwork and appearance. A small addition to your fleet This DLC does not add new missions, locations or gameplay systems. The core trading and fleet-management gameplay stays the same. The Knarr uses the same gameplay values as the Brig and does not provide a major gameplay advantage. It is intended for players who would simply like to use Knarrs in their fleet. Hansa Forever is required to play this DLC.
