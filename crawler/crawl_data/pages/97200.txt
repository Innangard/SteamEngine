In an abstract world ruled by numbers, you’ll grow exponentially stronger through a truly infinite, procedurally generated skill tree. You fire automatically. No action is required other than interacting with the skill tree. Your number isn’t just your Health —it’s also your size . The bigger your number, the larger and more powerful you become. But be careful: one defeat sends you all the way back to “1” , though you’ll keep your upgrades when you restart. Unlock new sectors of the infinite skill tree, discover unique upgrades, and keep pushing your growth farther and faster.
