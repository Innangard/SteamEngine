Are you ready to claim your rightful throne? If so, know this - ruling a fantasy kingdom is no easy feat. Welcome to the Crown of Greed Demo , a classic strategy game inspired by old-school traditions, featuring a unique indirect command system, so unusual among other strategy games. The demo version, being just a foretaste of the full version, will familiarize you with the main mechanics and features of the game. Your first steps in Rodovia The Demo version allows you to create and defend your very first foothold. Choose from a  range of basic economic facilities, workshops, military buildings, and hero dwellings to secure the safety and well-being of your people. Surround your city with defensive towers and expand your borders to gain new territories and subjects. Will they agree to serve you? In the Demo version , you will be able to recruit and upgrade three basic hero classes. Heroes, endowed with free will, will gladly undertake your quests... or not! You can't force them to obey you, but you can try to buy their services with shiny coins. The rogue, hunter and warrior await your first offers. Will they agree to cooperate? Realms inspired by European medieval folk culture await Travel through the gloomy, mysterious lands of Rodovia to strengthen your rule. Discover a variety of the Demo version biomes , such as Forests, Deathlands, Swamps or Plains, each with their own locations and horrifying inhabitants. Visit forgotten ruins, caves, graveyards or dens and remember - with great risk comes great reward. No more direct orders Discover that authority extends beyond mere commands, and that a King's power is only as strong as his ability to bend others to his will. In this Demo version of the game , you can try out the unique, indirect management system, in which your subjects will decide for themselves when and which tasks they want to undertake.
