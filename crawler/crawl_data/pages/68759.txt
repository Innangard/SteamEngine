About This Game Embark on an unforgettable journey into a handcrafted open world, where the clang of the hammer echoes with artistic flair.  Explore rich mines, travel through medieval lands and unravel the mysteries concealed within the forge's flickering flames. Delve into the life of a blacksmith, where each stroke of your craft creates not just weapons, but works of art. In this meticulously designed realm, your mastery as a blacksmith will not only forge legendary weapons but also shape the destiny of the entire world. Prepare to immerse yourself in a truly unique adventure where craftsmanship meets fantasy in ways you've never experienced before. Crafting & Smithing Collect materials or buy them at your local market and use them to create various weapons. You can choose from a wide range of metals and parts to create weapons, each with its own unique properties. Mining & Processing Explore various locations around the world to collect resources that will be used to produce various items and weapons. Melt the mined ores into ingots and mix them to make better alloys that can be used to create powerful weapons. Exploration & Journey From the ores you mine to every discovery that fuels your craft, allowing you to forge weapons and shape tools that turn ordinary exploration into a journey. All this is possible thanks to the companionship of your faithful horse. Dreams & Meals Manage your day by cooking meals in your kitchen to restore stamina and when night falls, retreat to your bed for restful sleep, advancing time and preparing you for the challenges of the next day.
