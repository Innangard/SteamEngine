Spice up your grog with Dead In The Water, the first DLC release for pirate tactical turn-based roguelite Rogue Waters . This DLC includes a new storyline, enemies, and an arsenal of new weapons, items, and skills. New Story Campaign! Toxic Glass fumes are forming a dark, unnatural tempest that threatens to spread to every corner of the map, with deadly winds that carry the voices of the dead. Expand the adventures of Captain Cutter as he voyages to the Storming Sea in this terrifying new side story campaign. Play alongside the original campaign New story events New random events Captain Circe! You’ll face a harrowing new boss enemy with undead powers and a burning (and well earned?) desire for revenge against Captain Cutter. Ghosts! These strange new storms bring new enemies to haunt the seas. Their bone-chilling skills are hard to beat. Wraith Wail! Change the tides of battle with Cutter’s powerful new skill that sends ghostly foes fleeing in fright! The Wraithborn! This spectral subclass just blew into the tavern, pledging to help you beat the ghosts at their own game. Bolster your crew with the Wraithborn and their ghostly skills. Their spirited visages have Mr. Wilkes cowering belowdecks. Treasures Ahoy! Buy or steal powerful new magic items and weaponry that will make the ghosts shake in their vaporous jackboots. Equip your crew with deadly new items and weaponry Expand your ship’s arsenal with new cannons and modules Wraith Winds! Don’t get swept up in this unpredictable new environment hazard.
