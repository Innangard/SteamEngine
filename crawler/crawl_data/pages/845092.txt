Features: - Realistic modeling with over 50 custom modelled buildings. - Detailed ground polygon model. - Updated Radar screens. - Taxi signs and hand placed taxi lights around the airport. - Dynamic runway approach lighting. - Beautiful background scenery using OSM (open street map. The central location within Virginia and the mid-Atlantic region attracts over three million travelers each year, enough to keep the “Open V” runway configuration in a steadily busy state. The airport has three asphalt-grooved runways to accommodate traffic, with the longest runway measuring 9,003 feet in length by 150 feet in width. RIC is served by airlines flying primarily domestic routes to cities in the South, Northeast and Midwest, and connecting flights to major hubs for international destinations.
