A growing adventure. Cozy Tales is a single-player idle RPG about automatic battles, collecting equipment, and raising a party of adorable adventurers. Bring three companions into a colorful pixel-art fantasy world, explore different regions, and turn each haul of loot into new possibilities for your team. Automatic battles, your own combinations Your companions attack and use their equipped skills automatically. Choose your party, combine damage, healing, and support abilities, and watch how different builds perform in battle. Find your next favorite piece of gear Equip eight gear slots per character and look for useful random stats and affixes. Collect matching sets to activate bonuses, filter your inventory by attributes or set, and strengthen the equipment you want to keep. Explore regions and challenge bosses Travel through varied scenery and battle the enemies you meet along the way. You can also enter dedicated boss arenas when your party is ready for a challenge. Gather growth and breakthrough materials, adjust your preparations, and return to your journey. Grow alongside your companions Level up your characters, collect materials for their next breakthrough, and give gifts to raise their bond. Alternate between adventuring, sorting your latest finds, and refining your party as you progress at your own pace.
