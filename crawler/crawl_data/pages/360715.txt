This Game is a remake of a mobile game originally released in China in 2015. Enjoy a fully localized experience crafted entirely by a talented Chinese team! ◆In a modern, fictional courtroom, players will defend clients, delve into mysteries, uncover the truth behind murder cases, and exonerate their clients. Play as rookie lawyer Luna Ray or seasoned attorney Charlie Drake, exploring and cracking cases occurring in various unique locations: ◆Chinese-style Haunted House ◆Serene Mountain Village ◆High-Tech Research Center ◆Mine Park ◆World Games Venue ◆Skyscraper ◆With a script exceeding 500,000 words, the game offers a deeply immersive and satisfying experience. ◆◆Over 100 unique dialogues can be triggered by showing the lawyer's license! “Anyway, just present the lawyer's license!” Gather information through testimonies in court and gradually uncover the truth! ◆Use evidence to expose the lies of witnesses! Beyond traditional trials, there are several other exciting gameplay elements: ◆Special Performance: Brainstorm! When you're at a dead end, review all known facts. The key clue might already be within your grasp! ◆Special Mode: Confrontation! Engage in direct confrontations with the prosecutor. It's thrilling to debunk the prosecutor in one go, but it's also fun to see the imaginative wrong answers! ◆Special Mode: Mind Link! Connect keywords related to the case to piece together the truth. The game adopts an anime-inspired art style iterated on the basis of the original artwork. Characters are animated using frame-by-frame techniques instead of keyframe animation, making their movements more fluid and expressive while staying true to the original style.
