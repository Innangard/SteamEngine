The MBT-2000 is an export version of the Chinese-Pakistani main battle tank, based on the Type 90‑IIM design. This vehicle combines high mobility, a modern fire control system, and a powerful 125 mm gun equipped with an automatic loader. The tank is equipped with a composite armor and an early-generation ERA armor, providing reliable protection against most common projectiles. Please note!
