What awaits you in the DLC: Sweet Collection: We’ve added about 100 brand-new items. Instead of rice and omelets, you’ll find appetizing cookies, chocolates, fluffy buns, and fancy cakes. A Journey Through Feelings: 70 new levels await you. The difficulty increases gradually: start with simple compliments and move toward complex compositions where every minor wish or exception on the customer card must be considered. Double Delight: In the final 20 levels, a brand-new mechanic appears — double heart-shaped boxes, something never seen even in the main game! Orders become larger and more engaging as you fill two at once within a single level. Absolute Relax: If you don’t feel like following rules, visit Relax-mode. There are no restrictions or strict orders — just choose your favorite desserts and arrange in the heart everything that looks beautiful and delicious. How to play? It’s just as simple and fun: carefully read the wish card, consider tastes and exceptions, and choose the right food, drink, and snack. Create the perfect gift, meet all conditions — and the level is complete! Fill this world with sweetness and care together with Love Notes!
