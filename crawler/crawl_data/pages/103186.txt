These encounters break from traditional roguelike logs, offering narrative depth, unique rewards, and consequences that shape your journey. Every special room is a story waiting to unfold. 🧙‍♂️ Deep Character Building Create a new adventurer with randomized stats, unique traits, and meaningful choices.
