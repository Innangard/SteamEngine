KAZ is a score-chaser through and through. There's no final boss, no ending screen, only staying in the higher places of the leaderboard. You play to beat your best, climb the leaderboards, and prove you're the fastest, smartest player out there. Weekly and monthly challenges keep the competition fresh  and top performers earn leaderboard fame. Four buttons. Infinite Depth. A minimalist 4-button setup means anyone can pick it up in seconds. But beneath the surface: 110+ items, 6 weapons, 35+ maluses, and active spells that combine into wildly different builds. Find the synergy that pushes your score higher than you thought possible. Short runs. Instant retry. Each run is quick and intense. Die? You're back in instantly! No loading, no waiting, no punishment. Just the pure itch of the next run until you nail it. For fans of Super Hexagon or Geometry Dash, this game will tickle the same senses! Plus... 25+ unlockable themes (each with its own special ability), 145+ quests, and 5 game modes give you reasons to keep coming back, but the real goal is always the same: beat your score.
