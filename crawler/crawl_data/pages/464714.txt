Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Deal Flipper’s core gameplay loop is already playable from end to end: find opportunities on the marketplace, read each seller, negotiate a price, buy items, stock them, relist them and grow your flipping business. We’re using Early Access to improve and expand that foundation with player feedback. Negotiation balance, seller behavior, progression, the in-game economy and content variety are all systems that benefit from seeing how real players approach them. Early Access lets us tune those systems while adding more items, characters and ways to grow your business.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Deal Flipper to remain in Early Access for around one year. This is an estimate rather than a fixed deadline; the exact timing will depend on development progress and the feedback we receive during Early Access.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The Early Access version contains the complete core flipping loop. During Early Access, we plan to expand the game with substantially more item and seller variety, additional ways to develop your business, and further improvements to negotiation, progression and the economy. We also plan to expand the world with additional warehouses and staff systems. Other features, including a possible yard-sale system, will depend on development priorities and community feedback. Co-op is currently planned for the full release. Our goal for 1.0 is not to replace the Early Access game with a different experience, but to make the existing flipping simulation broader, deeper and more replayable.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At Early Access launch, the core gameplay loop is fully implemented and playable. Players can browse the marketplace, inspect seller profiles, negotiate directly with sellers, buy and receive items, stock and relist them, track profits, upgrade their business, run advertising and use the auto-buy system. Early Access development will focus on balancing and refining these systems, fixing issues discovered through broader play, improving quality of life and expanding the amount and variety of content available.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to offer Deal Flipper at a lower price during Early Access. As the game expands and approaches the full 1.0 release, we expect the price to increase to reflect the additional content and features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We’ll collect feedback through Steam Discussions, Discord and our social channels, with particular attention to negotiation balance, seller behavior, progression, economy balance, item variety, quality-of-life improvements and bug reports. We want Early Access feedback to help us decide what needs refinement first and which additions provide the most value, rather than simply adding features for the sake of adding them.” Read more Buy Deal Flipper INTRODUCTORY OFFER! View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Buy low, sell high. Master the art of the deal. Deal Flipper is a first-person flipping sim where the entire market lives online. Scour a marketplace full of underpriced treasure, haggle with sellers in real-time chat, and resell your finds for profit. But here's the catch: every trader thinks, reacts, and bargains differently. Read them right, and the deal is yours. Lowball the wrong seller, and they'll walk away, taking the item with them. 🔍 Hunt the Marketplace Scroll through an endless feed of secondhand listings. A vintage gold watch for $10? A "Ming Dynasty vase (probably)"? The deals are out there, buried between the junk. Spot what's underpriced, judge what's worth your money, and open a chat the moment you see an opportunity. 💬 Master the Negotiation This is where Deal Flipper sets itself apart. Every deal is won or lost in chat and every trader has a profile. Their Knowledge, Patience, Greed, and Stubbornness shape how they bargain. Check a seller's stats before you make your move: a greedy one holds out for more, a patient one won't blink at your lowball, and a knowledgeable trader knows exactly what their item's worth. Push the wrong seller and they'll snap back or refuse to sell at all. Read each profile, adapt your offers, and outdeal them all. 📦 Stock, List & Flip Once a deal closes, your package arrives. Place it on your shelves, then relist it on the marketplace, at your price. That $8 watch becomes an "$89 vintage heirloom." Flipping isn't just about buying low; it's about selling smart. 📈 Build Your Empire Start with pocket change and a single desk. Track every item's full profit history: daily, weekly, and monthly. Study how the market moves, and turn small flips into big margins. What begins as a pocket change hustle becomes a full-blown trading operation. Every item is its own little economy, and mastering them all is how empires are built.
