The Death Howl demo features: Story introduction – Meet Ro, the game’s protagonist Exploration of the game’s two biomes (Forest of Howling Shadows and Creek of Spectral Falls) A deck of cards to craft and use during combat A series of tactical grid-based battles against various opponents In forgotten lands shrouded by myth, Ro—a hunter from a small tribe—is overcome by grief following the death of her beloved son.
