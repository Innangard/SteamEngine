Your grandmother has left you a delightful surprise—a vacation lodge in the heart of a mysterious valley, Jacaranda Mansion! Nestled in a secluded southwest valley, home mostly to ladies, it’s a hidden paradise with a rich cultural heritage. But beyond the familiar scents of home, there are secrets here—secrets tied to the lost myths you once heard from your grandmother as a child... The immediate goal is clear: use the valley’s magical farming techniques and mysterious technologies to revive Jacaranda Mansion’s former glory! But with so much to explore, you’re free to set your own goals and chart your journey as you and the sisterhood discover the countless wonders of Moonlit Valley together. As for your grandmother’s whereabouts? Don’t worry; she’s simply taken off on an impromptu road trip, riding her motorcycle into the horizon! Cultivate southwest specialty crops with creative grafting! Enjoy an alchemical experience that’s impossible to put down! Raise adorable animal friends … and, of course, don’t miss the fishing! Cook to your heart’s content! Choose from a variety of ingredients and techniques. Let your culinary mastery make the restaurant a local legend… (But if it fails, well, it’s all part of the fun!) Furnish Jacaranda Mansion with unique pieces to attract more guests! Run your own souvenir shop! Design one-of-a-kind products inspired by your discoveries in the valley! Overwhelmed? As the boss, you can always hire more hands! Recruit staff to help grow the mansion’s success. Meet up with friends, learn about their pasts, enjoy fun activities together, and build deep bonds. Embrace the joy of roaming! Wander around town and the mountains—who knows, you might overhear some juicy gossip! Embark on wilderness adventures! Discover the hidden stories behind legends and mysterious spirits.
