Build, decorate, and furnish at your own pace, and collect Keepsakes with softly narrated backstories. No grind. No pressure. Just nature, animals, and time to breathe. Quiet places to exhale: seaside coves, inland meadows, and mountain hideaways across fall, winter, and summer—each with a different mood in the light and weather. As lamps glow and corners gather comfort, you feel the room warm around you, the retreat growing cozier with every thoughtful touch. What you love matters: the interests you choose color what appears and which quiet stories surface, so discoveries feel aligned with you. Some Keepsakes carry softly narrated memories; together they settle into your Keepsakes collection—an album‑like place you can revisit whenever you need a reminder. A calming acoustic score and natural soundscapes lift the mood and immerse you in the environment. Choose a retreat and settle in as the time of day and weather drift softly around you. Select up to three interests (from ten). They gently shape decorating and furnishing options and nudge later discoveries, including which Keepsakes you’ll uncover, without locking you in. Decorate at your own pace. Place, rotate, and refine with lamps, chairs, plants, and more, shaping the mood indoors and out. Fill your Cozy Heart. As the space truly warms, your Cozy Heart reaches full; when it does, travel bags arrive with new Keepsakes to unpack and arrange. It’s a reflection of coziness, not a pressure meter. Revisit your Keepsakes collection to browse what you’ve found and hear gentle backstories again, whenever you like. Return anytime to tidy, tweak, and enjoy the quiet of your retreats, or start a new one. Listen to an acoustic soundtrack with over 20 tracks composed by Chris Haugen, crafted to slow the world around you. Personalize every moment with the Cozy Tools: capture breathtaking screenshots in Photo Mode, control the weather, change the time of day, and switch between visual styles for a unique vibe. Log Away is a game about coming back to yourself: simple moments, small comforts, the hush of rain on the window and a lamp glowing at dusk… No meters to chase. No timers. Just a retreat that waits for you, ready whenever you need to breathe.
