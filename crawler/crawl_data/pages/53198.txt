We all know how that one ends.” TheGamer Roadmap About This Game Dungeon Inn (莎拉的黑店) As Sara, the ambitious innkeeper, you’ve struck a deal with a dragon to open an inn right next to a mysterious dungeon that appeared near the dragon's lair. With adventurers from rival guilds eager to explore the dungeon’s secrets, your inn has become the perfect pit stop for them. Your challenge? Keep these competing groups from crossing paths while maximizing profits. It’s a delicate balancing act, but who cares if they’re rivals? As long as we don’t get caught, everyone’s happy – especially your coin purse! Welcome to the art of dungeon-adjacent hospitality, where discretion meets ambition, and every guest is a potential goldmine. Let the games begin! Spread the word of your inn far and wide! Deploy turn-based strategies to attract adventurers to your inn using various tools. Time your tactics perfectly, keeping rival guilds apart while maximizing your inn's success. Manage carefully, prevent clashes, and watch your business thrive in this unique management game! Sometimes, guild clashes are inevitable. If that happens right at your doorstep, you must keep your secret under wraps! Send in covert armored wisps to tip the scales in one guild's favor and reduce suspicion on your double-dealing. Keep the balance and ensure business flows from both sides! Will you serve fine wine or black tea to your guests? How will you handle a rowdy guest who breaks your plates? Your decisions in these special situations will impact your inn's reputation and income—and could either quell or raise suspicions. Every choice counts in keeping your guests happy and your inn afloat! A savvy innkeeper turns gold into more gold! Invest your hard-earned coins in exciting new facilities. Each upgrade attracts more guests, unlocks new events, and makes your inn the ultimate dungeon-side destination. Transform your humble tavern into a thriving adventurer's paradise and watch your profits soar!
