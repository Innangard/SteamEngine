You must nurture her from a clueless goldfish into a qualified human. By working and investing, you strive to resolve financial crises and even turn the tides to bring the entire Pet Street back from the brink of bankruptcy. 【gameplay Introduction】 a.Development： Arrange weekly lessons for Xiaoli, enhancing her base attributes through the acquisition of knowledge and skills, and strive to become an even more exceptional human being. b.Part-time Work： (1).Help out at the fish store by selecting and purchasing fish fry, grow them until they're ready for sale. As Xiaoli was once a goldfish herself, she surely has an innate advantage in taking care of other fish. (2).Take care of animals in the pet store—feeding, cleaning up after, bathing, walking, and listening to what cats and dogs have to say. With her understanding of animal language, Goldfish Princess Xiaoli is bound to become a great companion for all animals. (3).Meet new friends and take up part-time jobs at convenience stores, coffee shops, and second-hand bookstores. Engage in a variety of fun mini-games such as organizing shelves, growing vegetables, cooking, and more—all waiting for you to discover! c.Management： Reach a certain level of proficiency in part-time work at a shop, and you can unlock the management mode! Have Xiaoli rent a shop and become the owner—this is the right way to make big money! Of course, there's a risk of failure if not managed properly. 【Synopsis】 It turns out that the decline of Pet Street is due to the mischief of monsters—go eliminate them!
