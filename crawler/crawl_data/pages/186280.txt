You’re in a vibrant world with a quirky fowl as your only company, nudging you toward beating objectives and never giving up—to Git Gud! Run, hop, take on high-difficulty levels, and achieve ranks few can reach. Unlock The Hardest Levels Level 4: Requires a B+ performance or higher. Level 8: Demands an A+ performance. (Available in full version) Level 12: Unlocks only with an S+, proving mastery over all previous levels. (Available in full version) Co-op Multiplayer and Solo Play Do it solo or team up with friends in co-op multiplayer. Strategize, share tricks, and farm points together to boost your progress. Whether solo or co-op, the struggle is real! Bunny Hopping & Speed Mechanics At the core of Git Gud is its bunny hopping mechanic, crucial for navigating levels and achieving high scores. Hold the jump key to keep jumping automatically and build momentum as you move. After six consecutive jumps, unlock a double jump—a vital skill for advanced levels and improved performance. Speedrunning & Timers Every second counts in Git Gud . Built-in timers track your personal bests on every level. Push your limits, smash your old records, and climb the leaderboard. Secure a top-ten spot, and your name gets displayed on the board for the world to see. Customization and QR (Quick Retry) Points Start with the default outfit and trail, unlocking new items by earning QR points through gameplay. QR points are awarded based on your performance—higher levels and better scores mean bigger bonuses. Playing with friends? Earn extra QR points whenever they beat a level, making co-op even more rewarding. QR points aren’t just for store items—they’re vital for quick retries and overcoming tougher challenges. Each retry costs QR points, and running out means heading back to Level 1 to earn more. As the difficulty ramps up, retries become increasingly costly, making smart QR point management crucial for success in later stages. Tryhard Mode: Double the Risk, Double the Reward Feeling gud enough? Activate Tryhard Mode for 200 QR points to unlock a 15-minute timer that doubles your QR earnings for completed levels. Be warned—retries in this mode also cost double QR points. Use Tryhard Mode strategically to farm QR on levels you’ve mastered. Get Ready to Git Gud Git Gud will test your patience and expose flaws in your strategy, but it will also make every victory feel earned. Ready to claim your S+ and prove your skill? Jump around and find out!
