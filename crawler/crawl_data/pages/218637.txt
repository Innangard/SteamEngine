Player can get the combat experience like a master in Chinese Wuxia novels or movies. In this world of martial arts rich in traditional Chinese cultural elements, you can freely experience different lives in the beautiful world built by yourself! Everything you see can be interacted with, and the world is built out of your own needs. Good person or bad person? Everything has a price. Choose from various Karmas before a new life! Build your exclusive martial arts routines, and defeat the enemy in a unique kind of Deck-building Battle Make friends or enemies with all beings in the wilderness, and experience all kinds of awkward situations during the interactions. Styled World + Smooth Fighting Animation The game's art style is influenced by traditional Chinese ink paintings, as well as stunning terrain. With the performance of the UE4 engine, a refreshing and recognizable scenery is created. Influenced by various Kung Fu/Wuxia movies, the fierce and smooth animation of moves during the battle feels unique and stylish. A Sandbox World and RPG Experience that You Can Play Freely As You Want! The world, or the “worlds”, is built by yourself. Unlock and place different terra blocks to meet your own needs, and build your unique Wuxia world. Practice Qing Gong, jump directly into the air or dive into the lake with a seamless experience. Can also ride a horse, raise your pets and share vision with your eagle friends! No levels, no limits. You can access wherever you want to! But be careful - For those dangerous place might kill you! A Unique Kind of Deck-Building Battle And Gameplay with Chain Reactions. Learn Martial Arts from different clans/genre, build your own battle deck to face various chanllenges! Skills, Gears, Inner Kungfu, Abilities and Items... Use your smart brain and match them to achieve unexpected effects and the most “insidious” builds. You may inadvertently set yourself on fire when practicing, unintentionally burning down the entire sect in desert, but flee to a nearby mountain and sit and watch others put out the fire; You may also trade the evidence of killing someone with your enemy, making them dumped by their friends and become easy to be defeated! The NPCs think!  Well, they are not as smart as you anyway. The NPCs are not simply puppets with well-written plots, but will act spontaneously according to their own personalities, status and goals! Even the bandits have to eat, drink, and like to soak in hot springs in winter! Create your own sect, disguise yourself to deceive, set fire to the clans, ask for advice on skills, fall in love, become the winner of competetion, or discover the hidden secrets of the Matchless world... Experience your own unique Wuxia life!
