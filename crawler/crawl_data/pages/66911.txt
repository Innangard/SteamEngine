"EasyCraft is a user-generated-content VR building sandbox. What makes it fun is the community: the things people build, remix, and share together. We're launching in Early Access because the core creative loop is complete and stable — build with motion controllers, co-create in real time with friends, publish, and remix — but the platform's content library, social features, and onboarding all benefit enormously from real players. Early Access lets us grow the part library and templates around what builders actually want, instead of guessing in isolation." Aproximadamente durante quanto tempo vai este software estar em Acesso Antecipado? "Our goals for the full 1.0 release: - An expanded, continuously growing part and template library - Better onboarding and guided building for new players - More social and discovery features for finding and following builders - Performance and stability improvements for large creations and busier rooms We expect to stay in Early Access for roughly 6–12 months. This is an estimate, not a deadline; we won't leave Early Access until the game feels complete and polished." Qual vai ser a diferença entre a versão final e a versão de Acesso Antecipado? "The full version will offer more comprehensive building tools, including but not limited to: combining multiple bricks into reusable assemblies, spatial bulk deletion, and spatial bulk recoloring. We'll also keep expanding the part and template library, improving onboarding, and optimizing performance for large creations based on feedback gathered during Early Access." Qual é o estado atual da versão de Acesso Antecipado? "The Early Access build includes: - Thousands of true-to-scale brick parts you can place and snap with VR motion controllers - Real-time multiplayer co-creation: build together in a shared space with voice chat - Publish your creations, browse and remix other builders' published works - A full avatar outfit system with hundreds of wearable parts - Friends, notifications, and an in-game store - Support for major PC VR headsets through OpenXR (Valve Index, HTC Vive, Oculus/Meta Touch controllers) A PC VR headset and motion controllers are required to play — there is no flat/desktop mode. It is an online game: an internet connection and a free EasyCraft account are required. This is a single live region during Early Access." Este software vai ter preços diferentes durante e depois do Acesso Antecipado? " The Early Access price reflects the amount of content available right now. As we add major features and content on the road to 1.0, the price may increase. Everyone who buys during Early Access gets every update, including the full 1.0 release, at no additional cost." Como estão a planear incluir a comunidade durante o processo de desenvolvimento? "Our approach centers on collecting player feedback and acting on it. We'll continuously gather ideas, issues, and creative requests through the in-game feedback tool, Steam community discussions, and our contact email. We'll pay close attention to the parts, assembly tools, and social features builders actually want, use that input to prioritize updates, and make it clear through patch notes how community feedback shapes the game. Pick up a part with your motion controller and snap it into place — thousands of authentic brick pieces, precise connection points, and intuitive controller-based assembly make large-scale building feel effortless. Thousands of brick parts with true 1:1 proportions Precise snap-point placement — move, rotate, and align with your controllers in 3D space No grid-box, no block limit on your imagination — build as big as you want Create Together in Real Time Open a room, invite friends, and build the same creation side by side. Voice chat, live collaboration with invites and requests, and automatic conflict-free editing keep everyone building at the same time. Publish. Explore. Remix. Your creations are saved to the cloud and ready whenever you return. Publish a finished design to the community gallery for everyone to visit, like, and favorite — or open someone else's work, make it your own, and publish something new. Free to Play Jump in and start building for free. Optional in-game currency is available if you want expanded building quotas.
