View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Neko Station! Ever dreamed of owning your own train... that's completely filled with cats? Neko Station is a 2D Pixel Art Cozy Desktop Idle game that transforms your screen into an adorable, moving cat paradise! Desktop idle screen Adorable cat actions In this game, you take on the role of a cat train manager. Your duty is to travel to various stations, picking up a wide array of feline customers waiting to board your special, customized train. Build Your Dream Train Attracting feline clients isn't just about showing up. You must decorate your train cars with a huge selection of cute furniture and decorations. Each cat has its own unique preferences! Some might love a soft cushion, others a scratching post, and some might just want a simple cardboard box. The right layout will attract different types of cats, and maybe even rare, special ones! Streaming integration connect to Twitch, YouTube, Kick and let your chat join your train as a cat Befriend Your Feline Passengers Once the cats are aboard, it's time to interact! You can: Pet them: Show your affection and help them relax. Feed them: Offer delicious treats to build your relationship. Take Photos: Capture their adorable moments for your collection. The better you care for them, the deeper your bond will become. Discover Special Cats Beyond decorations, the stations you visit also influence which cats will appear. Pay close attention! You might find special cats in unique costumes or even legendary felines waiting to be discovered, all depending on your train's setup and destination. Key Features: A Relaxing Idle Experience: Runs comfortably on your desktop. Let the train run and watch the cats come and go. Adorable Pixel Art: Enjoy a heartwarming and charming 2D pixel art style. Endless Decoration: Choose from hundreds of furniture items to create a one-of-a-kind train. A Wide Variety of Cats: Meet countless cats of different breeds, each with unique personalities and preferences. Relationship System: Pet, feed, and photograph your way to friendship with every cat. Discovery & Secrets: Find special cats tied to specific decorations and stations. Get ready, and let's begin a truly heartwarming journey on the Neko Station train!
