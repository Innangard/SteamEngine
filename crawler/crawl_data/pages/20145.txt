Follow the lives of occult university students who discover ancient rites and invocations with the help of their passionate Teacher. Face their personal trials, summon new creatures, synergize them, and burn to uncover the depths of obsession. Burn With Me captures a special type of "strangeness" — a comforting one, inviting you to be part of it… while also keeping you on the edge of your seat, never knowing what to expect. However, there are some exceptions that you can definitely expect: Stylish, cozy and eerie narrative deckbuilder Unique position-based gameplay Permanently burning your cards as one of the main mechanics A dark overarching story set in a magical world, where students learn occult arts at university 6 chapters and characters, with gameplay adjusting to reflect their deepest fears and obsessions Almost 100 cards with alternative secret visuals from invited artists Burn With Me is a bold experiment — a visual symbiosis of handcrafted art and real-life videos — spliced with a unique perspective on "deckbuilding."
