View Community Hub About This Game Halu - The Fish is a psychological horror game about a young girl, Hana, living with her strict mother, where her only source of comfort is a small fish in an aquarium. What begins as something quiet and familiar slowly grows into something far more disturbing. The house remains unchanged. The routines continue as normal. But her attachment to that small fish deepens into something she can no longer understand or escape from. This is a story about obsession and emotional dependency. About how comfort can quietly turn into something consuming when everything else feels out of reach. And about a family history buried so deeply that it has become part of everyday life. Horror here is not only built on jumpscares, It is also built on atmosphere, unsettling soundscapes, and the weight of ordinary moments that slowly stop feeling ordinary. ~~~~~ Fish The only place that feels safe. Or so it seems. House Something about this place feels off. Even when it shouldn’t. Mama She’s just trying to keep things in order. Even if it feels too much. Routines Keep your sanity on everyday basis Book You start noticing things. Water The thirst for something more doesn’t go away. It only grows. Grandma She’s the only one who understands. Or at least, it feels that way.
