Key Features Arcade drift physics — smooth, responsive and satisfying slides. 50+ legendary cars — JDM classics, raw muscle and timeless European machines. [b] Story campaign (beginners→elite) — prove yourself and unlock new chapters. Online PvP & co-op — ranked duels, team challenges and casual rooms. Cross-platform competitions — play with friends across platforms. Daily & weekly events with prizes — chase new goals every day. Weekly leaderboards— compete for top spots and exclusive rewards. Visual customization only — paints, liveries, wheels, tires, license plates (no performance tuning, no pay-to-win). Controller support & Steam Deck optimization — pick up and drift anywhere. Low-spec friendly — designed to run smoothly on typical office PCs. Why you’ll love it If you enjoy the style and freedom of Forza Horizon and the high-energy vibe of Need for Speed , this is your drift playground : collect legends, perfect your lines and rule the leaderboards — for free .
