Content For This Game Browse all (1) Super Space Jump Man - OST Free View Community Hub About This Game Super Space Jump Man is an exhilarating gravity-based 2D adventure that will have you leaping across the cosmos! Navigate through space, jumping from planet to planet, and reach the portal to advance. But beware—spikes and lasers are out to get you! Collect enough oxygen to keep your journey going before the meter runs out. With its super-simple one-button control, this game is easy to pick up but challenging to master. Key Features: One-Button Gameplay: Enjoy intuitive, physics-based controls that make the game accessible yet challenging. Stunning Visuals: Immerse yourself in beautiful graphics that bring the galaxy, stars, and planets to life. Epic Soundtrack: Groove to a great electronic soundtrack that enhances your space adventure. Defy Gravity: Leap from planet to planet, defying gravity and dodging obstacles. Achievements: Conquer 24 stages and unlock Steam achievements to showcase your skills. Join the interstellar fun and test your skills in Super Space Jump Man —a game that’s easy to learn, hard to master, and impossible to put down!
