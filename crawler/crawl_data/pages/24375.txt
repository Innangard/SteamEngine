This Early Access phase will allow us to balance the game's parameters more appropriately, refine the gameplay, add new features, and expand the content according to the community's preferences.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Around 6 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We intend the full version of Cyberwar: Neon City to feature 5 different floors to explore, each with its own theme and unique enemies. We also plan the game to include more weapon variations, enemies, mini-bosses, bosses, new upgrades, and new items. Based on player feedback, we intend to make various improvements, fixes, and enhancements to meet their expectations. For more details, check our roadmap for scheduled updates.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At the launch of Early Access, Cyberwar: Neon City will include the first three floors, four playable shark-guns, and a wide range of items and upgrades to unlock. Players will be able to face various enemies, mini-bosses, and one boss on each floor.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We anticipate increasing the price of Cyberwar: Neon City , as more content and features will be available. Players who purchase the game during Early Access will not be affected, and we will notify the community before the price change.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community involvement is a crucial part of the development process. As we are very active on social media, we intend to use Steam and Discord as communication channels. To aid him in his mission, a living shark weapon becomes his most loyal ally against hordes of enemies in a frantic battle. Dive into the characters' memories in a surprising and twist-filled story. A DIFFERENT HERO To become a great hero, Printer, a newborn clone, and his shark weapon, Blue, which can shoot any type of projectile, will face great challenges on each floor of a building taken over by Cyber Monkeys. FRENZIED COMBAT Face dozens of enemies! Cybernetic monkeys, robots, androids, and genetically modified animals. Use your combat skills, enhance your abilities, rely on the help of trustworthy allies, and explore a wide range of distinct projectiles. UPGRADES With your shark weapon, you have the unique ability to store various types of shots. Your cleverness in choosing the ideal shot for each situation will be crucial. Additionally, a skill tree provides permanent improvements, while each room grants you a new upgrade. And to further enhance your options, a merchant will offer new items and drones that can be essential in your combat journey. REPLAYABILITY New surprises await you with each failed attempt. Whenever you return to the start, you can spend resources to improve and become stronger, as well as unlock new allies and expand your base. A MYSTERIOUS WORLD During his journey, Printer will discover that not everything is as it seems. With each new memory about the characters' past, our hero will find himself in a more complex and mysterious plot. New characters may appear, each providing their own perspective on what might be happening in the world of Neon City. Interact with NPCs and discover valuable information.
