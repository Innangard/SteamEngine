Remote Play Together About This Game Save the world. Earth's monuments are under attack, and only the ingenious sports club and Dr. McLabby can stop the extraterrestrial invaders with enhanced sporting gear! Slam And Roll lets you capture enemies inside giant sports balls and kick them across the screen ... ...chaining spectacular combos to wipe out everything in your path. Travel from place to place and explore the world’s most iconic monuments through 100+ unique, action-packed stages spread across 8 tour areas. Unlock new characters, play solo or team up with a friend in local co-op, and uncover hidden secrets in every level. Or dive into total mayhem in the Action Roguelike Chaos Mode , where every run is unpredictable and co-op turns the madness up to top levels. Why you’ll love it Endless variety: baseball, hockey, basketball, soccer, ping pong, and tennis — each with unique characters and styles. Play any way you like: classic hardcore arcade mode or modern adaptive Tour Mode for all skill levels. Challenge yourself: Action Roguelike Chaos mode offers unlimited stage combinations in a rogue-lite experience. High replayability with unlockable characters, items, bonus stages, and secret stages and area. Local co-op fun: defend the world together with a friend! Authentic arcade feel with modern polish, online leaderboards, 50 achievements, and Steam Remote Play Together support. 4 Very Different game modes Cool boss fights Crazy mayhem Action Roguelike chaos mode Sports and Characters from the game: Hockey Uli: The master of ice hockey. He also plays on the streets when the weather is too hot. Pilu: An ice hockey prodigy with lightning-fast reflexes. She’s not afraid of rough matches and loves proving she can outplay anyone on the rink. Baseball Laura: She plays baseball at her school, and she’s pretty good! Thonon: A powerful baseball hitter with a calm mind. He rarely misses a swing and always keeps his cool under pressure. Table tennis / ping-pong Jimmy: A ping-pong master. He can play with two balls at once! Yanfei: A ping-pong genius with perfect precision. Her quick hands and sharp focus make her almost impossible to beat. Tennis Marta: She started playing tennis five years ago, and she can throw balls at lightning speed! Rafa: A tennis all-rounder who never gives up. His endurance is legendary, and he can keep rallies going until his opponents run out of breath. Soccer (AKA: football in Europe) Rachel: She’s been playing football since she was a toddler, and now she can score several goals per match! Hugo: A fast football player. He reads the game well and always knows where to be to score the winning goal. Basket David: Since he was little, he’s been very keen on playing basketball. He learned how to score before learning how to speak English. Sandra: A basketball star with great control and vision. She dominates the court with smart plays and unstoppable shots.
