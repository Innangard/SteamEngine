Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “From the earliest stages of developing Shroom and Gloom, we prioritized sharing playable prototypes with our community. The feedback we've received on these early prototypes and our free demo has played a massive role in helping us make this game the best game it can be. Given how much we enjoy developing the game in collaboration with our players, it feels like an obvious choice to do the final stretch of development in Early Access, working closely with our community to make Shroom and Gloom 1.0 something really special.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to be in Early Access between 12 and 24 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “During Early Access, we plan on expanding the game with a new pathway featuring unique explore and combat cards, new character classes, and additional quests and challenges across all pathways.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Shroom and Gloom is in a good, playable state, with all key features implemented. The game currently offers two distinct pathways with unique characters, ~300 explore and combat cards per pathway, and multiple quests, synergies and unlocks that secure a highly replayable experience.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “No, we do not currently plan to adjust the price of the game between Early Access and Full Release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We've already been working closely with our community and hope that Early Access will provide even more opportunities to continue that. We love chatting to people directly, reading any Steam reviews, and talking about aspects of the game in Steam discussions. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Shroom and Gloom is a first-person, roguelike double-deckbuilder that brings you mobs of monstrous mushrooms and mountains of mega-combos! Unleash cards to hack, slash and roast your way through hand drawn dungeons of fungal foes! Build devastating decks, and grow cards into massive abominations as you fight your way into the heart of The Gloom! Double-Deckbuilding! Build 2 distinct decks! Use the combat deck to survive fierce battles! Use the explore deck to navigate dungeons, modify your cards, and unlock new weapons and abilities! Eye-ball melting art! Hand-drawn art and animations collide with rich 3D spaces, bringing you into  a surreal world of mushrooms and madness! Infinite growth and monstrous mega-combos! Shroom and Gloom offers unique ways for players to grow and modify their cards infinitely. Grow low level starting cards into over-powered attacks, or modify rare cards into game-breaking super-weapons! Less blocking, more eating! Why block your enemies when you could eat them instead! Shroom and Gloom features a bounty of ways for you to season, skewer or roast your foes, then eat them to restore lost health. Better yet, throw your roasted enemies into a pot, and cook up some game-changing soups!
