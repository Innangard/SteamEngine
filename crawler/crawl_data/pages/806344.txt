Fully Integrated Personalized 360 Spatial Audio Immerse Gamepack brings Embody's revolutionary personalized HRTF technology directly into FFXIV at the Object Level. Every single sound effect in the game, from boss mechanics to player SFX, will be rendered with unprecedented depth and positional accuracy in full 360 degrees - including above and behind you. Designed in Collaboration with Square Enix Immerse Gamepack is an official add-on for FFXIV, and was developed in close collaboration with the incredible FFXIV sound team to deliver an unmatched immersive listening experience to FFXIV players everywhere. Compatible with All Headsets The new 360 spatial audio experience of FFXIV outputs as binaural stereo, allowing it to work with any headset. Setup Start by downloading the Immerse Gamepack from the Steam Launcher. Then, enter your email address. Scan the QR code with your phone and follow the instructions to create your personalized HRTF based off your head and ears. Customize your spatial audio experience by selecting Headphone EQ and Preset EQ.
