It's a relaxing job, but not always... Features Make Delicious Ice Cream: Prepare delicious ice creams and milkshakes in different flavors with quick and easy recipes. Serve Customers: Serve a variety of customers with different tastes. Your goal is to make them happy! Psychological Horror: Making ice cream can be relaxing, but be careful, you may not be alone. VHS Aesthetic: VHS filter emulates the look of old movies and adds a nostalgic and spooky atmosphere. Autosave: Autosave at the end of each work day. Game Modes Story Mode: Game mode that tells the disturbing story of Jessica's first week of work at The Sweet Spot. Lasts approximately 1 hour. Endless Mode: Game mode with endless work hours and a time limit to deliver each order. Survive as long as you can. Relax Mode: Same as Endless Mode, but with no time limit to deliver each order. Relax by making ice cream while listening to music.
