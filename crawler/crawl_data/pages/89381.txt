Grab and swing through all-new weird and wonderful worlds with up to four players in online and local co-op and versus play. Take your partners by the hand (or head) and swing your ungainly chain from platform to pillar and beyond. Let go at the wrong moment and meet your splattery end, but a solid grip and pro swinging will take you over the finish line in sweet harmony! Online Pandemonium Adding to the game’s couch co-op roots, Heave Ho 2 takes the chaos online for the first time. Get a grip on faraway friends, then drop them into the void from a safe distance! Wild Worlds Launch yourself and your friends through eight themed worlds of fiendish fun, from weightless space and kitchen chaos, to medieval mayhem, ninja nonsense, and much more. Push, Pull, Shoot, Squeeze Pop guns, drones, space ships, ski lifts, keys, and ketchup are just a few of the items you’ll play with on your swinging travels. And lasers. Of course lasers. Fight Among Friends Take revenge on your underperforming co-op teammates in versus mode through a series of frantic challenges, cunningly designed to start a completely different argument every time!
