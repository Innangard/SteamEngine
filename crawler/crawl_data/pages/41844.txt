Chop trees, collect the logs, and toss them into the chipper to earn cash. Use your earnings to upgrade your strength and efficiency, or sharpen your axe allowing you to cut down increasingly tougher and more valuable trees. Tunnel through trees to uncover the story, discover loot, and work with a friendly gnome who’ll trade tools for a price. Destiny calls, do you embrace it, or defy it? Features: Be the Lumberjack: Chop down trees, and harvest them in the chipper to earn money. Pump Some Iron: Upgrade your core stats to increase your efficiency Hit the Grindstone: Sharpen your axe to improve its effectiveness against harder and more valuable trees Treasures - Find various treasures and other discoverables to earn extra loot and tools. Gnome Guide - Deal with a seemingly friendly gnome who is ready to help you, for a fee. Uncover the Mystery: Seek out the scattered story of the forest to discover your purpose in it.
