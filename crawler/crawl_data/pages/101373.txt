Pick them up, figure out where they belong, restore every shelf, and turn an overwhelming toy-filled mess into a perfectly organized store. But cleaning up is only part of the fun, some toys are made to be played with. SORT THE CHAOS 3,200+ Toys. One Very Messy Store. Pick up toys scattered across the floor and return every last one to its proper place. Fill the shelves, clear the aisles, and slowly watch the chaos disappear. SCAN. FIND. ORGANIZE Where Does This Even Go? Not sure where a toy belongs? Take it to the Toy Analyzer , scan it, and discover its correct location in the store. Then it’s up to you to put everything back where it belongs. PLAY THE CLAW MACHINE Cleaning Deserves a Break. Step up to the Claw Machine , try your luck, and earn special abilities that help you deal with the mountain of toys waiting around the store. You might come for the reward. You might stay because you absolutely need one more try. TOYS THAT ACTUALLY WORK They’re Toys, Play With Them! Not everything is meant to sit still on a shelf. Find interactive toys, place moving cars onto their tracks, and watch them race around the store. Some toys have a little more life in them than you might expect. BUILD IT. PLAY IT. The Piano Is Missing a Few Pieces. Find the missing keys scattered around the store, rebuild the toy piano, and once it’s complete… play it yourself. Putting a toy back together is only half the fun.
