Experience a gold mining process inspired by historical methods from the gold rush era. Break rocks, collect ore, and separate gold from the soil using various tools and machines. Then smelt it into gold bars that you can sell at the bank. During the game you will find different forms of gold: gold dust gold flakes gold nuggets Each type has a different value and requires the proper refining process. As you progress through the game, you will unlock better tools and mining techniques. A System of Mines to Discover In the game you can discover and explore a total of 9 mines. 5 small mines for quick expeditions 3 medium mines with branching tunnels 1 large multi-level mine Each mine has its own unique atmosphere and several to dozens of segments to discover. Working in a mine is dangerous. If you do not place supports correctly, tunnels may collapse. Build reinforcement structures, expand your infrastructure, and explore deeper underground. Upgrade Tools and Equipment Your tools determine how fast and efficiently you can mine gold. Create upgraded versions of your equipment and enhance them using rare materials and gemstones. Possible upgrades include: increased tool durability faster resource extraction ability to break harder rock additional equipment upgrades Alaska, 1896 – A Rich Storyline Thousands of people arrive in northern America in search of fortune. Most will return empty-handed. Only a few will make history. Maybe you will be one of them. In Alaska Gold Fever you start as an ordinary gold prospector with simple tools and a big dream. Explore the wild lands of Alaska, mine gold, hunt, develop your businesses, and build an empire at the edge of 19th-century civilization. In the game you can expect many interesting and engaging storylines as well as unusual characters, such as the Pastor. A large number of fascinating stories and quests await you. The game offers: a main storyline side quests hidden treasures mysterious locations Crafting and Survival Life in Alaska requires strategy and proper preparation. Gather resources and craft useful items in different workshops such as: tool workshop carpentry workshop fur workshop campfire and kitchen Preparing meals and resting provides bonuses that increase work efficiency, cold resistance, and character stamina. Hunt Wild Animals The wild nature of Alaska is both a threat and a valuable source of resources. By hunting animals you can obtain: meat hides furs hunting trophies The quality of the materials you obtain depends on the weapon used and your hunting skills. Fight Extreme Cold The deeper you venture into the Alaskan wilderness, the lower the temperature becomes. To survive in the extreme climate you must: wear warmer clothing prepare proper meals manage your energy and stamina Lack of preparation can quickly end your expedition. Build Your Own Businesses Gold mining is only the beginning. You can develop different ventures and build your own economic empire: new mines farms producing food and milk a hotel for travelers and gold prospectors Hire workers, manage production, and expand your enterprises. Automate Your Mines The largest mines can be transformed into real enterprises. Hire miners who will work for you and generate a steady income. This allows you to focus on exploration, business development, and discovering new lands.
