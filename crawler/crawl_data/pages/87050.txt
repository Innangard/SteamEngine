Program your units, build a fleet, trade, research, and engage in deep tactical combat in this RPG-strategy hybrid. CORE FEATURES Modular Ships - Customize and configure your vessels piece by piece. Get creative and share your designs with the world through Steam Workshop! Automation Through Coding - Program your ships to move and attack smarter. Androids can be programmed using an in-game visual language. Evolving Galaxy - Face procedurally-generated events and crises. The world has dynamic faction diplomacy, economy, and adaptive enemies. Organization Management - Hire pilots, do research and production, build bases, secure funding. Do you ally with pirates or hunt them all? What about being a privateer in a dominion war? Combat Variety - Almost every enemy will have a unique vessel design and behavior. You can even face player-made vessels during your journey! Replayability - Several routes and tons of content, as well as mod support. GAME DETAILS NebuLeet features a two-layered combat system. On the surface: the simplicity of an auto-battler. Deeper down: a complex, programmable combat environment. The victory isn't just about having the best weapons - strategic algorithms can turn the tide even against superior technology. Prefer a more RPG/tactical-style gameplay? Manage your pilots by issuing tactical orders during the pause. Or you can have a simple manual-control program to fly in arcade style. The coolest part is that it works in multiplayer, too. If someone submits their programmed vessels, you can try fighting it using manual controls on your side. Or put your own android squad against it. Every vessel is uniquely yours. Assemble each unit from individual parts, each with its own stats and mechanics. The look of your starship isn't just for show; if it looks like a death machine, it probably is.
