The nation has entrusted you with a vital responsibility: keeping the army fed. Peel the potatoes, prepare the supply, and improve your operation in service of the greater good. Work hard, work efficiently, and remember: the army is hungry. PEEL THE POTATOES Your job is simple: peel the potatoes and get them into the water. Pick them up, peel them by hand, and toss them in one by one. FINISH THE PILE Clear the pile and find faster ways to get the job done. What starts as manual work gradually turns into a much more efficient operation. AUTOMATE THE PROCESS Turn repetitive work into a system. Collect potatoes in bulk, use machines to process them, and automate more of the job as your operation grows.
