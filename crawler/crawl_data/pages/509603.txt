Undertake a new mission to a holy sanctum that's fallen to corruption. Battle hordes of enemies to summon an all-new boss with five variants. Experience the holy power of the new Priest class. Key Features: - Play as the Priest, a new class focused on mana powers and regeneration. - Tackle a survival mission set in the Celestial Ruins. - Battle the five Spirit bosses, each tied to your chosen divine blessing. - Acquire powerful Spirit Boons that open up new build opportunities. - Kit out your characters with new equipment: sceptres,  lanterns and a plethora of new trinkets. - Bring the new Will O' The Wisp and Animated Candle pets along on your adventures. Players who don't own Celestial Ruins can still play the survival mission with owners and earn rewards. However, Expansion rewards cannot be used until it is purchased. You can enter the Celestial Ruins by going to the Chapel in your town and interacting with the five stone statues at the altar.
