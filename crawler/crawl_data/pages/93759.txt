View Community Hub Reviews “A cute, cozy and relaxing game that is perfect to take your mind of work and into tranquility.” Johanna Österlind “An absolutely adorable experience about selflessly putting your life on the line providing for your offspring.” Jacob Lind About This Game Drill Bird is a cozy-yet-tense digging platformer where every trip underground counts. You play as a Bird, fallen from the sky, working to hatch your egg. With a drill for a beak you dig deep for precious ores, and bring them back to to empower your Egg and upgrade your abilities. Outsmart monsters, upgrade your drill, and don’t let night catch you too far from home. DIG DEEP Explore underground caves, mine valuable ores, and uncover hidden treasure. Every block you break or not will bring you closer to helping your egg hatch. FEED AND GROW Collect ores and feed your egg to grow stronger. The deeper you dig, the bigger opportunities - but every choice matters and will bring risks. OUTSMART MONSTERS Dangerous creatures lurk in the dark. Drill, dodge, and outmaneuver them to survive another day. GET HOME BEFORE NIGHT Time is always ticking. Make it back before darkness falls… or face what comes for you. CURSED MODE IS WAITING After finishing the base game once - a demon offers to make your child the strongest bird in your bloodline, but at what cost? Explore a new hand-crafted world featuring new enemies, puzzles and secrets. Cursed Mode doubles the games length and features a new story with multiple endings for everyone who wants more.
