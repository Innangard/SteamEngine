Gravity Snake is an engaging blend of the iconic snake game and platforming puzzles, where gravity is your ally—and your enemy. As you guide your snake to eat all the apples in 20 carefully crafted levels, it will grow in size, adding to both the challenge and fun. But beware—your snake can’t reverse direction and will fall if any part of its body is unsupported, making each move a careful calculation. Your mission is to navigate tricky environments, avoid dangerous obstacles, and reach your ultimate goal—finding your lost love. But with gravity and the length of your snake working against you, you'll need to use strategy to avoid getting entangled in your own body or touching harmful elements scattered throughout the levels. Key Features : Unique Snake-Platforming Mechanics : Experience a whole new way to play snake, with gravity adding a puzzle-solving twist.
