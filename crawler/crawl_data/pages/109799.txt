In the game, you need to build a powerful deck by collecting cards, curses, blessings, etc. to defeat the monsters in the Locking Demon Tower. Game Features - Open levels. You can decide which monster to challenge based on the strength of the deck. You can also arrange resource allocation reasonably according to your own status, without route restrictions. - Curse. Curse can directly improve the player's combat attributes, and the reasonable combination of curse can provide players with powerful combat power - Deck construction. Decks of different schools can be used together or in a single school, so there are many construction possibilities. - Rich props. Including curse, curse forging, cards, card enhancement, card cleaning, blessings, weapons, random events, etc. - Diverse monsters, more than 60 monsters with different mechanisms, you will gradually understand and conquer them in one death after another.
