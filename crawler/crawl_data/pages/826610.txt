Upgrade faster. Race without limits. About This Game Race Max Pro is a fast, stylish arcade racing game that blends Street, Drift, and Drag into one skill-driven experience. Dominate short, high-intensity races with tight controls, deep tuning, and a garage full of real licensed cars. Three Ways to Race Master three distinct racing styles. Street tests your control and racecraft, Drift rewards precision and flow, Drag demands perfect timing and launch execution. Switch between them instantly each mode sharpens a different skill and keeps every race fast and intense. Single-Player Career Climb your way through a full single-player progression. Beat challenging opponents, earn better rewards, and unlock faster machines as you advance through each chapter. Time-Limited Events Jump into rotating time-limited events with unique goals and exclusive rewards. Test Drive, Weekly Events, Time Trial, Air Time, Speed Trap each one pushes a different skill and keeps the game fresh. Seasons Complete seasonal objectives to unlock limited-edition cars, cosmetics, and performance upgrades. New rewards arrive regularly, expanding your garage over time. Authentic Tuning System Build performance your way. Upgrade engine, turbo, gearbox, nitro, tires, and more then fine-tune each setup to match Street, Drift, or Drag. Every part you improve changes how the car feels on the road. Visual Customization Style your car with full visual freedom. Change paint, wraps, rims, spoilers, and more to create a look that matches your racing identity. Legendary Cars Race with officially licensed icons from BMW, Ford, Nissan, Pagani, Jaguar, Audi, Lotus, Aston Martin, Mazda, Volkswagen, and more. Each car is recreated with attention to handling, sound, and performance so every machine feels distinct on the road. Multiplayer League Mod Compete in online leagues built for fast, skill-based races. Climb divisions, earn rewards, and face tougher opponents as you rise through the ranks. Challenge Friends Mode Race head-to-head with friends or rivals in custom matchups. Quick to set up, easy to repeat pure arcade competition. Race to earn Cash, Gold, and Blueprints — unlock new cars, expand your garage, and push your limits with every upgrade. Build. Tune. Race. Repeat.
