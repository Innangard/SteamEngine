View Community Hub About This Game While you were away, a jealous wizard turned your library upside down, and scattered 1,560 items throughout your library: 1,312 numbered books and 248 potions to be precise! Match 14 book designs and nine potion types to the signs on their shelves. Items are nicely highlighted when you point at them, making it easy for you to quickly pick up what you want to organise! If you have trouble locating a particular type of book or potion, visit the wizard cat and use Help Me Find. Your character becomes more efficient as you complete more shelves and you even get a fun sorting spell to help you a bit! There are no timers, penalties or failure states. Sort everything at your own pace, save when you choose, and return later to continue your progress. Features: Sort 1,312 numbered books and 248 potions. Restore 14 book collections and nine potion families. Match book designs and potion shapes to their shelf signs. Carry up to seven items at once. Choose which items to collect and which shelves to complete first. See a placement preview before shelving a compatible item. Use Help Me Find to highlight a selected item type and its shelf. Pet, rename and customize your wizard cat. Play without timers, penalties or failure states.
