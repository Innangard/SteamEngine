View Community Hub About This Content The Gran Canaria Hotel is opening and soon guests will be flying in from all over the globe to visit your subtropical island resort in Hotel Architect - Poolside Paradise. Your job is to provide the perfect getaway for critics and guests alike, but managing a hotel on an island paradise requires more than just sunshine and palm trees. Whether your guests are looking to get active in the wellness areas or seek a dip in the refreshing hotel pool, you will need to create a hotel paradise worthy of a 5-Star rating. Relaxation relies on careful planning, creative logistics, and a seasoned hotel manager ( that's you ). Do you have what it takes? In Hotel Architect - Poolside Paradise you can: Open a pool resort in the brand-new 'Gran Canaria' scenario Build and Design Swimming Pools Hire a new staff type to ensure pools are maintained for top customer satisfaction Build new outdoor gym and wellness areas for active stays Design your hotel and pool area with a whole host of new items and decorations Plus much, much more!
