to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Mine Safety is a retro arcade game where you take on the role of a platform operator in a mining facility. Your job is to control a mobile structure to guide the little miners to the exit elevator — preferably alive. The mine never stops, and the machinery must stay on at all times. High-speed rotating drills are constantly digging in every direction, posing a deadly threat to the little miners. According to your contract, you're legally allowed to cause only a few workplace accidents. Go over the limit? You're fired! Work quickly to maximize your bonus pay — but carefully, as each accident reduces your bonus. This is a game of reflexes, strategy, and high scores. The more efficient you are, the higher you'll climb on the global leaderboard, the friends leaderboard, and your own personal ranking. Claim your spot among the best!
