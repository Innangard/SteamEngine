About This Game In a world that was once thriving with life, a catastrophic and unknown event has brought the end of civilization. The majority of human populations have been wiped out, and the aftermath is a barren wasteland that is now home to all sorts of dangers. You play as one of the few survivors left in this world. As you travel through the ruins of cities and settlements, you will encounter various dangers such as unpredictable weather, hostile creatures, bandits and other factions who will do everything possible to ensure their own survival.
