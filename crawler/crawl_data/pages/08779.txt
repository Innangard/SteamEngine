Early Access allows us to develop the game alongside the community while ensuring the necessary resources to support its growth. Player feedback helps us identify bugs, balance gameplay systems, improve user experience, and prioritize development efforts. Our goal is to build Arctic Drive together with our community. Players who join during Early Access actively contribute to shaping the future of the game. Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? We currently expect Arctic Drive to remain in Early Access for approximately a bit under a year. This timeframe will allow us to add the major planned features, expand the game’s content, improve performance, and refine the overall experience based on community feedback. As with any active development project, this timeline may evolve depending on the needs of the game, player feedback, and new opportunities that arise during development. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? Our goal is for the full version of Arctic Drive to be significantly more complete, stable, and content-rich than the Early Access version. We plan to introduce new vehicles, new biomes, dynamic events, expanded customization options, additional gameplay mechanics, and a more robust multiplayer experience. We will also continue improving performance, optimization, UI/UX, balancing, and overall polish. Some major features currently under planning, such as Steam Workshop or other mod support, may also be added during development. The roadmap will continue to evolve based on community feedback and insights gathered throughout Early Access. Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? We are continuously working to improve stability and overall quality, but buyers should understand they are joining a project that is still evolving over time. Näytä yhteisökeskus Ilmoita bugeista ja anna tälle pelille palautetta keskustelupalstalla Katso kaikki keskustelut Suunnitelma Tietoa pelistä The deep silence of the brutal winter embraces you when you wake up one morning in your cabin. There's no food anymore. A half-empty can of soda in the fridge. A beaten up old car in your garage with all the parts lying around it, and the cold starts to wrap around you, fingers tingling and hunger twisting your stomach around. You know you have to get the car running quickly now and you must leave the warmth of your home. There's no way out but the long and treacherous road into the unknown. Drive, scavenge, upgrade and survive an artic wasteland that is actively trying to freeze and stop you — play solo or with up to 4 players in co-op. Arctic Drive (or Artic Drive for some) is a first-person co-op survival sim built around one fragile thing: your car. In this exploration-survival every run generates a fresh stretch of frozen wilderness and drops you into it with a rusted rig, a thin tank of fuel and a long way to go. The cold kills you slowly, but the icy road kills your car fast, so you better have friends and supplies with you to keep on going. Drive safely onward and keep on gathering items to recycle and turn into upgrades by crafting them. FEATURES Procedurally generated Arctic, different every run Fully simulated damage: chassis and parts deform on impact Every object is physical: if you can lift it, you can load it Trailers and RVs you can convert into mobile bases Hunger, thirst and body temperature to manage Blizzards, whiteouts and a full day-night cycle Scenarios to complete, or open sandbox 1–4 player online co-op A LONG ROAD WITH NO MAP You start with a beat-up car, whatever you can find in the garage, and a road going anywhere and nowhere. On the long drive ahead you will find an odd cabin or gas station that might give you more fuel, an empty cardboard box to recycle later, or nothing but safety from a blizzard. The road narrows, the treeline closes in, and the weather does not care what your plan was. Every mile you drive is a new, big adventure. LOAD IT UP AND HOPE FOR THE BEST Every item in the world is a real physical object. Throw jerrycans in the trunk, stack boxes across the back seat, and tow a trailer filled to the brim behind you. But remember that weight slows you down and burns fuel faster, and one tight turn on the road can spread the whole haul across the snow behind you. But just wait, just around the next hill you just might find a working generator with a recycler and crafting bench hooked up, and you can upgrade your convoy to survive yet another mile, yet another blizzard, yet another crash because your friend had a great idea to engage handbrake at full speed. The Arctic (Artic) will not kill you, but your fuel gauge will.
