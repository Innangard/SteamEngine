View Community Hub About us Twitter: @CottonStudio Youtube: @Cotton Game Bugs&Feedbacks: Feedback About This Game Lilliput's Workshop is a logic puzzle game based on an automated sandbox. If you've played SpaceChem or worked on redstone circuits in MineCraft, then Lilliput's Workshop is also worth a try. The game allows you to assemble a track system using various parts to guide a small train that transports blocks of a specific shape to their destination and complete the task. You can also use the game's built-in level editor to create your own unique levels and share them with players around the world via the internet to challenge their minds. Lilliput's Workshop uses a nostalgic, childlike art style. Even if you're not interested in boring logical thinking, we hope you'll experience the same pure fun from this game as you did when you were a child building castles out of blocks.
