Buy Paratopic Soundtrack $3.00 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content The Paratopic: Definitive Cut Soundtrack includes all the original compositions written and produced for the game by composer & sound designer Lazzie Brown, alias BeauChaotica/Lazarus Audio. Remastered to stand on their own outside the game, this is the Special Edition of the OST, originally only available as a bonus purchase on itch, and consists of two collections. The Standard Edition Files form the main soundtrack. The Special Edition Files include additional un-used demo versions of in-game tracks, the radio broadcasts in full (with a much clearer signal!), and a couple of other goodies.
