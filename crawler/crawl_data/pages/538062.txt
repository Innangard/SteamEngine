View Community Hub About This Game Play Goblin Attack in full screen or switch to a mode designed to sit at the bottom of your screen just like those games you keep running in the background. Your gold and passive income keep flowing while you work, browse, or watch something. Your progress never stops. You start as a humble walking archer. Tap to shoot, earn gold, and grow. Unlock a Mounted Archer , then a Cart with two archers , then a bigger cart with four , and finally a Walking Tower armed with seven archers that even handles melee automatically. Every upgrade changes how your journey feels. Spend your gold on upgrades that boost your income and speed. Build passive income buildings from the menu. Then Ascend : reset your progress, keep your Essence , and unlock powerful new abilities like Rain of Arrows or multi-shot on tap . Each reset gets you stronger than before. You move forward goblins appear in your path. Most go down in one shot. Shield Bearers take several hits depending on their size. Tank Goblins are massive and absorb a lot before falling. Stealth Goblins blend into the background let one slip past and they'll steal your gold. Mushrooms and Slimes are one-tap easy. Ducks fly overhead shoot one for a bonus reward. Missing goblins isn't lethal but it resets your speed buff, so keeping your aim sharp always pays off. The environment shifts every few minutes new platforms and backgrounds keep the scenery fresh as you push forward.
