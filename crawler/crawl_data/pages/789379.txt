The queen of Yamatai, an early Japanese state, Himiko used her political and religious authority as, possibly, a shaman of the sun-goddess Amaterasu to rule. Other accounts claim she had enthralled her subjects by magic. Whatever the truth behind Himiko's legend, her charisma was a powerful force, and resonates until the present day.
