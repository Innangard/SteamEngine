View Community Hub Save Your Spot for the Beta About This Game Craft a synergistic limited pool of dice in this deck-building, turn-based roguelike game where you fight to reclaim your swamp from the corruption that has overtaken it. Each die comes with six sides containing their own actions and abilities that pair together, along with powerful relics acquired during a run, in order to dish out some devastating attacks and combos. Features Build your  pool from a growing set of dice Strategize which dice stay, and which ones go as you only have 10 slots available to you at once. Each die over unique ways to win in battle. Some may seem weak, but paired with the right dice or relics, all can find a way to be useful during your run making sure each one is unique and fresh. Forge your dice to your needs If a die just isn't feeling strong enough forge it to upgrade different sides or even swap sides on two dice in order to create the perfect one to suit your current needs. Explore the swamp Discover all that your homeland has in store for you, from dangerous traps to calm fishing holes and everything else in between. Maybe the inhabitants that aren't trying to thwart you can actually help you on your journey. If all else fails, just hope luck is on your side System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows XP, Vista, 7, 8/8.1, 10 Processor: 2.0 Ghz Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: 1Gb Video Memory, capable of OpenGL 3.0+ support (2.1 with ARB extensions acceptable) * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
