The demo ends when 20 million liters of oil are extracted. BUILD A GLOBAL EMPIRE Build a global oil empire in an engaging 2D clicker game. Start with simple oil extraction and gradually expand your infrastructure by constructing new buildings and managing their operations. Upgrade your facilities, optimize processes, and unlock new resources that become the foundation of your growing power. Every decision brings you one step closer to dominating the global market. BUILD AND EXPAND Progress, level up, and plan future runs. As you extract hundreds, thousands, and millions of liters of oil, you unlock new development stages, buildings, and Perk Points. By ending a run and returning to the lobby, you can spend these points to unlock permanent upgrade cards that strengthen your strategy. Each new run allows you to grow faster, earn more income, and build an even more powerful empire. PRODUCE AND TRADE Produce, process, and trade goods in many different ways. The oil you extract can be sold at your own gas stations, transported by train to other continents, or shipped to global markets via container ships. As you progress, you will unlock gold mining, which can be smelted into bars used for advanced upgrades or sold for quick cash. Develop factories, manufacture ammunition, and prepare to defend your empire in the endgame. MANAGE YOUR BUSINESS Manage your business and political influence. Invest in a bank, issue loans, and choose between high interest rates for maximum profit or lower rates to gain public support. Use an airship to drop cash on cities, increasing your reputation and influence. Knowledge gained in the Library unlocks new possibilities: it boosts worker efficiency at oil derricks, increases factory output, or can be exchanged for other goods. 📈 Features ✔ Oil & business management ✔ Building and production systems ✔ Global trading economy ✔ Reputation and finance mechanics ✔ 2D tycoon / clicker gameplay Can you dominate the global oil market and build the ultimate business empire?
