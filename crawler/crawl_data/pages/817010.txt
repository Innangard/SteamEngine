Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game When the old can no longer protect the land, the small must learn to carry the tune. For as long as anyone can remember, a kind witch has cared for the forest and the creatures who call it home. But now, the world is beginning to fall out of harmony—and the witch who has always protected it is growing ill. Her smallest familiars must venture beyond the shelter of home and learn to carry the responsibility that once belonged to her. Though she cannot join them, her magic is never far away—a gentle hand, ready to lift them back to safety. Playing as a brave little mouse, you’ll journey through a vast world shaped by the beauty and wonder of Swedish nature, helping those you meet and restoring harmony one act of kindness at a time. Hela: of Mice & Magic is a heartfelt 3D open-world adventure about care, courage, and discovering how much even the smallest among us can do. Explore alone or share the journey with friends in local split-screen and online co-op. Carry Her Kindness Into the Wild The witch has spent her life caring for the world around her. Now, it is your turn to care for her and to continue the work she can no longer carry alone. Gather ingredients, brew magical potions, solve environmental puzzles, and lend a helping paw to animals and communities throughout the land. Each small act brings you closer to restoring its harmony and helping the witch who has long cared for you and all the forest’s inhabitants. See the World Through Tiny Eyes Travel through a lovingly crafted playground inspired by the landscapes of Northern Sweden, from rugged mountain trails and magical forests to tranquil lakes and misty wetlands. To a mouse, even familiar places become enormous. Everyday objects transform into obstacles, opportunities, and unexpected playgrounds, while hidden paths and story fragments reward those who stop, listen, and look a little closer. Glide through the air, climb to new heights, experiment with the world around you, and find your own way through an adventure filled with quiet wonder. Share the Journey Some adventures are meant to be shared. Experience the full adventure solo, with a friend in local split-screen, or in online co-op for up to four players. Work together to explore the landscape, overcome challenges, and discover playful solutions. When you play solo, the Shade System lets you create magical copies of yourself to assist with puzzles and interact with the world, ensuring that every part of the journey remains open to you. Meet Froggy Your magical Froggy backpack is more than a useful tool, it is your constant companion on the road ahead. Use Froggy to leap, swing, glide, gather resources, interact with the environment, and solve puzzles. As your journey continues, you’ll discover new ways for Froggy to help you reach hidden places and navigate a world built for curiosity. Game Features: Explore & Discover: Traverse an expansive, lovingly crafted region inspired by Northern Sweden, full of magical forests, tranquil lakes, and misty wetlands. Play Your Way: Experience the journey solo, with a friend in local split-screen co-op, or in online co-op for up to four players. However you choose to play, explore freely and progress at your own pace. Creative Puzzle Solving: Use creative thinking, cooperation, and the special properties of your remedies to solve playful challenges woven seamlessly into the world. Relaxed Adventure: Uncover heartfelt stories that invite you to rediscover the joy of exploration in a world built around small moments, kindness, and wonder. Magical Backpack: Use your trusty Froggy backpack to interact with the world, collect resources, and solve puzzles. Living Playground: Step into a world designed for playful exploration, full of interactive objects, winding paths, and challenges that reward curiosity. Realistic Physics: Experiment with objects governed by realistic physics, opening up a whole range of creative new ways to overcome challenges and solve puzzles.
