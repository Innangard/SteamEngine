View Community Hub Out Now, Check out the Bundle! 🕹️ About This Game 🕹️ Welcome to Replay Value – a 2000s second-hand game store sim! Start from scratch and build your dream retro game shop in this quirky and satisfying job simulator. Buy and sell used games, negotiate with colorful customers, and turn forgotten discs into hot-shelf hits. Whether you're restoring a dusty classic or haggling over a rare gem, your goal is simple: make profit, grow your inventory, and create a store worthy of the name Replay Value . 🛒 Features 🎮 Buy, Sell, Haggle Deal with random customer requests every day; some want to sell their collection, others are after something specific. Negotiate prices, make counter-offers, and decide when to walk away. 🧼 Restore & Reuse Fix up old games through interactive mini-games like CD cleaning, box repair, tag removal, and disk checks. Presentation matters, clean games sell better! 📦 Manage Your Store Physically place games on your shop shelves, tag them with prices, and decide what to display. Want to sell something online instead? Use the digital storefront and check for end-of-day sales. 📈 Upgrade & Expand Invest your earnings to unlock new platforms like consoles and handhelds, buy extra shelves, and decorate your store with posters, stickers, and figures. Even run ads to bring in more customers! 📊 Daily Summary Track your profits, sold inventory, and missed opportunities with a handy end-of-day report, inspired by classic sim games. 📅 Infinite Replayability With randomized customers, negotiation quirks, and hundreds of games to collect, every day is a fresh challenge. Can you master the market and make your shop the ultimate second-hand paradise? Whether you're a collector at heart or just here for the hustle, Replay Value is a cozy, strategic sim full of nostalgia, charm, and unexpected deals. 📦 Time to clean up that cartridge, name your price, and make your shelf shine.
