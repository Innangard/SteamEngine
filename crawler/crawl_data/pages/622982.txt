Composed by Britt Brady. This collection includes all of the hub themes as well as the original demo and release trailer songs. In Shady Knight, you only hear music when you are flawless. It always starts with silence. Every time you get a kill you might hear a glimpse of drums in the distance, but you have to do better than a kill. Reaching A-rank allows you to hear the base layer of a song, you are in the zone, but sometimes is missing. Drums start to appear at S-rank, but they are loudest and clearest only at SSS. Once in the zone, the current song will carry you to the next level, at least until you fall or take damage. This soundtrack is where you can hear the main hub themes in all their glory on repeat.
