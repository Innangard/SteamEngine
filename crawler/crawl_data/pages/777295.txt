Yet even in this era of advanced truth telling, the crimes of humanity remain unchanged. Technology Rules Unravel the mysteries of a vibrant high tech world filled with robots, abnormal creations and experiments that have run amok. But beware, Ancient Lemuria has entered The Age of Restraint and harsh punishments await those who use unapproved technology. Sacred Statute With Sentinels deployed to keep the public in check, their moral compasses are questioned as secret agendas run rife. Forbidden artefacts must be confiscated and those in charge must remain uncorrupted to uphold the sacred statute. Dance of Deception Technological advancements have led to human lives being irrevocably altered, with even the need for the simplicity of spoken language removed. But would technology deceive? When holograms are used to deliver secret messages, do you have what it takes to decode the Spieldance? Four Brand New Scenarios In a world where technology reigns supreme, for the first time you will play in an alien setting. You will need to not only unpick motives from grotesque plots but also learn and immerse yourself in this strange setting. With four scenarios to solve, each click brings you closer to the truth. Forbidden Artefacts Exercise your deductive reasoning to decipher truth from fiction, unravel complex schemes, and analyze extraordinary artefacts to expose how good intentions can pave the road to ruin.
