View Community Hub About This Game 【A Cozy Cellar Organization Simulator】 A once-pristine cellar has fallen into complete disarray. Return 2,448 bottles to their proper racks by identifying their varieties and restoring perfect order throughout the cellar. 【The Forgotten Keeper】 Long ago, the cellar belonged to an eccentric collector who loved his bottle collection more than anything else. Desperate to preserve it forever, he attempted an ancient spell. The cellar survived... but the spell turned him into a chicken and trapped him inside a small cage. Now the cellar has fallen into ruin. Restore every rack, break the curse, and help the forgotten keeper see the sunlight once again. 【Restore Order, One Rack at a Time】 The cellar starts in complete chaos. As you organize each rack, the room gradually transforms into a clean, beautiful collection. Watching disorder disappear is its own reward. 【Learn Skills to Organize Faster】 Unlock powerful abilities that improve carrying, searching, and movement. Choose your upgrades wisely and discover the most efficient way to organize the cellar. 【Two Ways to Play】 Cozy Mode - Relaxing gameplay - Unlock skills - No time limits - Organize at your own pace Speedrun Mode - No skill upgrades - Pure efficiency - Compete on the online leaderboard - Every second matters 【Key Features】 - Organize 2,448 bottles - Sort bottles by their varieties - Unlock powerful skills - Restore a forgotten cellar - Relaxing gameplay with no time limits - Dedicated Speedrun Mode - Online Leaderboards - Atmospheric underground environment AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Generative AI was used only as a supporting tool for the design of some fictional bottle brands and language translation.
