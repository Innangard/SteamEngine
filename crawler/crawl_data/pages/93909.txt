And more.” Game Spew “A tremendously compelling overall experience” PC Gamer “A perfectly formed tower defence game” Rock Paper Shotgun About This Demo Defend against waves of alien monsters in this unique roguelike action mining game. Use the time between attacks to dig beneath the surface in search of valuable resources and relics – deliver them back to your dome to unlock powerful upgrades and useful new gadgets. Demo features: Relic Hunt: Dig into the ground below and find an ancient artifact before the monsters destroy your dome! Play on a medium-sized map with 2 primary gadgets to choose from. Guild Assignments: Add some extra spice to a run by taking on a challenging task from the keeper’s guild, featuring unique modifiers and goals! Hidden Secrets: Each run is procedurally generated with a unique map and useful gadgets to utilise. What will you find in the depths below? Multiple difficulties: Play on our default ‘Brutal’ difficulty setting to get a taste of the core Dome Keeper experience, or take things down a notch to ‘Normal’ for a more relaxed time.
