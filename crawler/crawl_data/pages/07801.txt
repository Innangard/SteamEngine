Discover rare cards, and optimize your earnings as you climb to scratcher supremacy! Scratch juicy pixel art cards and watch the cash pile up Explore the void, unlock forever-upgrades, and gain permanent boosts. Little guys scratch cards for you, stack cash, and help build the biggest empire ever!
