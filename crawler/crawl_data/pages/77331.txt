Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We want to involve players in the creation process as early as possible. Early Access allows us to gather valuable feedback, conduct surveys to determine if systems are fun, and refine or remove features based on player input. Your voice will help shape the game into the best version of itself.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We estimate Vending Dokan! will be in Early Access for approximately a year. However, this timeline may change as we respond to player feedback and refine the game. We’ll keep players informed of any updates to the schedule. ” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version of Vending Dokan! is planned to include expanded content and enhanced mechanics. We aim to introduce additional locations and maps, such as a suburban area with a unique ambiance, song list, and aesthetic distinct from the current map. We’re also exploring the possibility of adding a wide variety of decorative items with diverse themes, more vending opportunities, machines, and items. We plan to incorporate community and social features, as well as streamer-specific tools, to make the game more interactive and shareable. Improvements and potential additions to core mechanics are also planned, and we will rely on player feedback throughout Early Access to help guide these developments.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Vending Dokan! is a fully playable experience with the core gameplay systems in place. Players can manage vending machines, hire staff, decorate locations, and grow their business. The game also supports background play, relaxing music, and a functional economy system. While the foundation is solid, we’re still refining existing mechanics, balancing gameplay, and exploring new features. Some content, like additional maps, decorations, and advanced systems, is planned for future updates during Early Access.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We intend to increase the price of the final version of the game once all the additional content has been added. We do not plan on making incremental price increases during Early Access. When we decide to set the final price, we will clearly communicate this in advance of any change.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We already have a vibrant Discord community where players actively share feedback and ideas, and we’ve been involving alpha testers in the development process even before Early Access. Place your machines in various spots around the city, design each location to match your style, and build a thriving business whether you're playing actively or letting it run passively in the background while you work or play other things. Transform ordinary streets alleys into bustling hotspots and create destinations that the locals can vibe with. Decorate each vending location with various items, plants, and themes that attract more customers and boost sales. Every detail adds a touch of personality and flair to your growing business and creates a vibe that is uniquely you. Hire and manage staff to keep your vending business running like clockwork. Assign different workers specific responsibilities such as restocking, cleaning, or repairs, and balance their needs to keep morale high. A happy team makes for a growing business! Whether you're actively managing every detail or letting your business thrive passively, Vending Dokan! is designed for cozy gaming at your own pace. With soothing music and a laid-back vibe, it's perfect for multitasking or unwinding. Key Features: Build and Customize: Strategically build and personalize vending machines across various locations, each with its own unique ambiance and customer base. Staff Management: Hire and oversee a dedicated team to ensure smooth operations, from restocking to general maintenance. Decorate and Enhance: Adorn your vending locations with delightful decorations to attract more customers and boost sales. Passive Gameplay: Whether you're actively engaged or letting the game run in the background, your business continues to flourish while you enjoy a soothing soundtrack that's perfect for multitasking. Relaxing Atmosphere: Enjoy a serene gaming experience with cozy visuals and calming ambiance sounds, making it an ideal companion during work, study, or other activities.
