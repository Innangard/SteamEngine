View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Super Mini Mart is a wholesome and laid-back take on the management genre, leaving out the stress and only keeping good vibes. As the new owner of the cutest bodega in town, you’ll get to supervise and manage all its daily activities and  transform this budding business into the thriving store all locals talk about! Resupply and fill the aisles before the crowd gets in Use the varied appliances to keep your storeroom neat and your goods fresh Plan your stock and run discounts - all sale, no stale! Grow your business and expand, from micro mart to mini mart - but never wall mart! A colorful cast of endearing characters, each with their hopes, woes, and fav indulgences! From outsider to community : gain the trust of your local customers and be rewarded with loyal friendships and unique storylines. A lovely self-contained world in pastel colors, fat pixels and chirpy music The meaning of life - or at least, the minimart life. Get those walls and floor to reflect your mood , one day blue and the other rainbow Buy new decorative elements to keep things as fresh as a ripe tomato Follow the theme of the seasons , from Spring lushness to Halloween spooks It’s now time to roll up your sleeves and get those doors open o/ And if you’d like to share your super mini creations, see what others made or simply chat with the devs, join our Discord now !
