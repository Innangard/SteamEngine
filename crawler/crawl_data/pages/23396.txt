Lihat Hub Komunitas Tentang Game Ini Museum messed up! Something happened here. Crates are tipped over, and skulls, ribs, and vertebrae lie scattered across the halls. You're the only one around to put nine skeletons back together. Well, you and a cat. TURN CHAOS INTO ORDER Collect - Pick up bones from the floor, the stairs, and wherever the crates spilled them Identify - Every bone starts as “Unknown”. Figure out which skeleton it belongs to Assemble - Find the right slot and click it into place, one bone at a time MAKE A MESS, LEARN THE BONES Open crates, tip them over, or just throw them, and start rebuilding. Each dinosaur's bones have their own look, color, texture, wear, and the more you place them, the faster you recognize them. No skill trees, no upgrades. Just you getting better. WAKE UP THE MUSEUM As skeletons come together, you'll trigger special events around the museum. Each one leaves something behind: little rewards, toys, and surprises that make the place feel like yours.
