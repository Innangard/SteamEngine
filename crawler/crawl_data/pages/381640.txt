View Community Hub About This Game A turn-based roguelike with a deep ability-modification system. Lead a party of three heroes, adapt their skills using modifiers, collect artifacts , and craft unique builds to destroy the source of corruption… or die trying. Features Ability Modifiers Each hero has four abilities that can be customized with modifiers. Combine effects, strengthen synergies, and create unique builds in every run. Unpredictable Routes Every expedition brings new challenges: different enemies, new random events, and unique bosses in each region. Decide whether to take risks or move cautiously. No two paths are the same. Powerful Artifacts Artifacts found in the world grant strong passive effects that reshape your playstyle. They amplify interactions between abilities, unlock unexpected tactics, and greatly increase your party’s power—but some come at a high cost.
