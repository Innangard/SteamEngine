Prepare for the ultimate bullet heaven experience. About This Game Is there a perfect system in the galaxy? The Zogark family is determined to find out. Cosmic Run is a fast-paced space roguelite about a dysfunctional family searching the galaxy for the “perfect” society. Fight through relentless space police, criminal gangs, and powerful rulers as you upgrade your ship and survive fast-paced roguelite combat across a galaxy full of broken ideologies, bad decisions, and desperate hope. 145+ WEAPON & PASSIVE UPGRADES SHORT & INTENSE SESSIONS 14 PERMANENT UPGRADES MULTIPLE PLANETS CHAOTIC SPACE COMBAT UNIQUE ENEMIES & BOSSES AND MORE... DYNAMIC SHIP CUSTOMIZATION Build your ship differently in every run . Choose from a wide variety of 12 Unique Weapons and 12 Unique Modules to suit your playstyle and adapt to ever-changing threats. DISTINCT PLAYABLE CHARACTERS Pick from multiple members of the Zogark family, each with unique strengths, weaknesses, and special abilities. Find the character that best fits your strategy—or master them all. INTEGRATED DIALOGUE SYSTEM Stay immersed in the action with a non-intrusive, real-time dialogue system. Characters speak dynamically during gameplay—offering insights, tips, and story moments without breaking the flow. PROCEDURALLY GENERATED UPGRADES No two runs are the same. Encounter randomized upgrade options throughout your journey, forcing you to adapt your tactics and experiment with powerful new builds each time. PERSISTENT PROGRESSION Earn credits during your missions to unlock and upgrade permanent perks. Even when a run ends, your hard-earned progress carries over—making you stronger with every attempt.
