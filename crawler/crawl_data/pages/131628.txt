Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Space Engineers 2 is a very ambitious game, and we believe launching as a Playable Alpha, In Active Development is the best way to shape its future together with the community. The current version represents an evolving experience that will continue expanding throughout development. During the Playable Alpha, we plan to gradually introduce major content updates featuring new mechanics, gameplay systems, optimization improvements, and additional features. Some systems are still being actively iterated on, and community feedback plays a major role in helping us refine and improve the experience over time. If you choose to join us during Playable Alpha, please expect active development, evolving gameplay systems, ongoing balancing, and continued performance and polish improvements as development progresses.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Our plan for keeping the Space Engineers 2 in Early Access is approximately three years (end of 2027).” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Like the original Space Engineers, the current Playable Alpha version of Space Engineers 2 represents the foundation of a much larger, dynamic experience still in development. While Early Access started with core building tools and Creative Mode, the game has evolved across major milestones: launching full planets and survival systems in the Almagest System, mechanical subgrids (rotors, pistons, hinges, wheels), advanced automation (Event Controllers, Timers, Sensors), destructive combat with warheads and turrets, dynamic wind physics, visual overhauls, and the milestone launch of Hostile Encounters, introducing fully adaptive combat NPCs (Renegades & Boatmen) capable of dynamic pathfinding, cover usage, and coordinated fighting inside fully destructible environments. Looking ahead, the full release will substantially broaden this sandbox across several key pillars: Cooperative & Dedicated Multiplayer: Expanding from initial 4-player, player-hosted cooperative multiplayer to full, persistent dedicated servers and large-scale community networking. Planetary-Scale Volumetric Water: Integrating true fluid dynamics, buoyancy physics, ocean/river biomes, and submersibles/boats across aquatic planets. Living Factions & Advanced AI: Expanding combat NPCs beyond planetary outlaws into high-tech spaceborne factions, dynamic fleet encounters, complex AI behavior, and deep systemic economies. Full Storyline & Campaign: Complementing the open-ended sandbox with structured narrative campaigns, voice-acted missions, and expanded lore throughout the Almagest System. Refined Engineering Depth & Tech: Continuing performance optimization such as native upscaling tech like DLSS alongside expanded block libraries, survival mechanics, and fully integrated production pipelines.* Each milestone adds foundational gameplay and living systems to the destructible sandbox, progressively building toward the complete Space Engineers 2 vision.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Playable Alpha build of Space Engineers 2 delivers an expansive engineering sandbox and core survival loop, powered by the VRAGE3 engine with realistic deformation, destruction, and high-fidelity visuals. The game is fully playable today while undergoing continuous active development and system expansion. Key features currently live in the game include: Creative & Construction Tools: A unified 25 cm grid system, blueprint and projection construction, grid undo/redo, paint tools, symmetry, and voxel manipulation hand tools. Planetary & Celestial Exploration: Full exploration of the Almagest System—including planets like Verdure and Kemik, dense asteroid clusters, orbital rings, planetary stations, and endless dynamic encounters. Mechanical & Automated Engineering: Subgrids with rotors, pistons, hinges, and wheels, supported by comprehensive automation blocks (Event Controllers, Timers, and Sensors) and automated production pipelines (area welding and grinding). Adaptive Combat NPCs (The ‘Hostile Encounters’ Milestone): Tactical ground enemies, the Renegades and heavy Boatmen, engineered to fight inside a completely destructible voxel sandbox. These NPCs navigate player-built interiors, dynamically seek cover (and reposition when cover collapses), coordinate threat callouts, flank, and attack both players and player grids. Combat & Survival Systems: Hand weapons (rifles, pistols) with dynamic recoil and stance-based accuracy, interior defense turrets, vehicle-mounted armaments, and explosive warheads, balanced by suit oxygen survival mechanics, localized weather hazards (snowstorms, sandstorms, meteor showers), and physical wind. Progression, Economy & Workshop: A reworked ore distribution model, colonization contracts, trading stations with Credits and Cargo Ships, Datapad lore, randomized loot, and native modding/blueprint sharing via the VRAGE Hub and Steam Workshop. Performance & Technology: Experimental NVIDIA DLSS upscaling support alongside continuous engine, lighting, and flight HUD optimizations. The game will remain in active Early Access through 2027 as we work through upcoming milestones, including 4-player co-op, volumetric water physics, expanded factions, and a full narrative campaign.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback is an important part of shaping Space Engineers 2 during Playable Alpha development. We’ll keep you updated on Space Engineers 2 through our Steam page, weekly CEO dev diaries, monthly newsletters, and our official Discord, where our team and moderators are always available. For any issues, you can report bugs via our website’s tech support section. About This Game What is Space Engineers 2? Space Engineers 2 is currently a Playable Alpha In Active Development and already lets you begin building, surviving, and colonizing the Almagest System. You arrive in the distant Almagest System on humanity’s first large-scale colonization mission. Your ship is damaged on entry, your tools are basic, and the planets below are beautiful but unforgiving. From here, everything you build is up to you. Explore planets, moons, asteroid rings, and dense clusters. Mine resources, construct bases and starships, complete contracts, discover Datapads, trade, expand into new sectors, and push the frontier forward alone now, and later together with friends as co-op and large-scale multiplayer arrive over the course of Early Access. Creation and destruction never felt this good Space Engineers 2 takes everything we have learned over the past 10 years and reimagines the space engineering experience with more depth, precision, and creative freedom than ever before. Powered by our new VRAGE3 engine, creation and destruction feel better than ever. Build ships, stations, bases, and machines. Mine asteroids, reshape terrain, carve tunnels, damage structures, and experiment in a fully destructible sandbox where engineering decisions matter. Whether you are a veteran Space Engineer or new to the series, Space Engineers 2 gives you a growing sandbox where your creativity can shine while the game continues expanding through Early Access. Build with great precision Explore the next generation of construction with the 25 cm Unified Grid System. No more large-grid vs small-grid separation: blocks fit together seamlessly, making it easier to create detailed and functional builds. Mounting point visualization, grid lines, improved controls, and more intuitive building tools help you design, adjust, and refine your creations with greater precision. Smoother building and better tools Space Engineers 2 introduces major building improvements such as Grid Undo, copy/paste, symmetry, blueprints, Projection Building, Backpack Building, Area Welding, refined CTRL controls, and improved rotation and zoom. These tools are designed to make engineering smoother for both newcomers and experienced players, while preserving the depth and creativity that define Space Engineers. Enhanced mobility and engineering freedom Take your creations to new speeds with upgraded max velocity: characters up to 310 m/s and grids up to 300 m/s. Magboots and automatic jetpack activation help you stay in control while building, exploring, and moving around large constructions, platforms, ships, and asteroids. Mechanical systems are also expanding. Rotors and Pistons are already available in the current Alpha, with wheels, hinges, warheads, and additional engineering systems planned for future updates. Smarter UI and smoother play We redesigned UI/UX to make building smoother and more intuitive for both newcomers and pros, giving more freedom to bring your intricate ideas to life. VRAGE3 is being optimized throughout Early Access; performance can vary by hardware, world size, and build complexity. As Early Access progresses, you’ll be able to build bigger ships, fly more of them, and enjoy epic destruction in a truly immersive ride. Survival, colonization, and progression The current Alpha already includes a real sandbox survival experience. Manage power, oxygen, hydrogen, and health. Mine resources, process materials, manufacture components, build production chains, complete contracts, earn credits, discover Datapads, and begin expanding colonization across the Almagest System. These systems are playable today, but still growing. Long-term survival progression, deeper colonization, richer economy and contracts, NPCs, campaign content, co-op, multiplayer, and many additional systems will continue arriving during Early Access. Next-level visuals Enjoy the beauty of space, industrial designs, planetary landscapes, and destructive engineering with next-level realism. Space Engineers 2 introduces ray-traced global illumination, realistic shadows, a new skybox, tessellated voxel materials, parallax effects for armor, volumetric clouds, and many other visual improvements that bring depth and realism to every scene. Share your creations Bring your ideas to life and share your Blueprints and Worlds with the community through Steam Workshop integration. Explore a growing collection of creations from other players, find inspiration, and discover new engineering challenges. Space Engineers 2 also supports advanced modding through the VRAGE3 Mod HUB and Mod Editor tools, giving creators more ways to shape the future of the game. Living threats in a changing sandbox Experience dynamic combat with the debut of Combat NPCs, built from the ground up to operate inside an entirely destructible universe. Encounter hostile ground factions like the Renegades and their heavily armed leaders, the Boatmen, as you explore Verdure and beyond. Unlike traditional shooter enemies tied to static corridors, these NPCs react and adapt to an environment shaped and reshaped by your engineering decisions. Enemies intelligently navigate complex voxel terrain, explore player-built interiors, open doors, and maneuver around dynamic obstacles. In combat, they take cover behind walls, recognize when that cover has been blasted apart, reposition on the fly, retreat when injured, and communicate threat information across their squads. Fight back with a revamped character combat system featuring directional damage indicators, weapon recoil, and automated interior defense turrets or tune your HUD with customizable NPC health bars to suit your preferred level of immersion.
