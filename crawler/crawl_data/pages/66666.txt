Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game You play as Adam Crimson, the son of a captain in a military unit fighting crime. His father raises him with strict military discipline, moving from one service apartment to another and never staying in one place for long. None of this helps Adam make friends or become a model student at school. As corporations intensify their struggle for spheres of influence in space, a Resistance movement begins to emerge within society. Who will you become in this story? You are not yet a hero - just a young man caught in the heart of a time of change. Will you follow your father's path, or choose your own road? A HIGH DEGREE OF VARIABILITY No fake choices! Your decisions affect not only the course of events, but also Adam himself: his personality, views, and relationships with other characters. Even a small action can change the tone of a scene, open up a new path, or lead to consequences that will only become clear much later. Every visual element is created by hand. In the Gallery section, you can explore the artists’ work - from character concepts and environment sketches on paper to their transfer into digital form, further refinement, and animation. Each illustration was created with attention to the atmosphere of the world: cold cities of the future, tense conversations, and small details that make the story feel alive. No empty characters or overused clichés! Every character reveals the world in their own way, influences the story, and helps you see events from a different perspective. The characters have their own motives, weaknesses, fears, and beliefs. Some will become allies, some will become a challenge, and some will make you question whether a truly right choice even exists. Original music, written by the composer specifically for the game, helps immerse the player in the harsh world of the future. The soundtrack highlights the mood of each scene: from quiet personal moments to tense episodes where every decision carries the weight of what is yet to come.
