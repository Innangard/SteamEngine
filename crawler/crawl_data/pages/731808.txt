Can I get you another round? Set in a dystopian near-future which - a few years earlier - experienced a cataclysmic, global system crash, Another Round is a deckbuilder / slots-style game, where you work to grow your cyberpunk clientele and attract unique and interesting personalities to your cause. How to Play Each night, your customers arrive to do biz, play, chat, and get sloshed. Customers are happiest when they are seated correctly. After the first round of folks arrive and seat themselves, you pick who sits where in the second and third rounds. At the end of the night, you gather resources from every customer seated. If you have made any combos - 3 of a Kind, Straight, Flush, or Straight Flush - then you can generate and update the customer cards in your deck. Every two nights, you will face a random event... many of which require you to make tough choices. And at the end of a month, if you survive, you win! Features Each night is a three-round, tactical experience with a focus on finding poker-based combos to progress. Add, remove, and upgrade/downgrade cards strategically: include too many cards or change your deck too often, and you'll break your combo-making ability! Over fifty unique Events and a dozen interesting Characters, all housed in a rich narrative background filled with cyberpunk-inspired references. We've brought you our absolute best puns, jokes, and other ridiculous cultural callbacks throughout the game. This is classic roguelike gameplay with no game-by-game metaprogression... You're always welcome, though, so keep coming back as long as it takes!
