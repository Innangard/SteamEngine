Content For This Game Browse all (2) Strange Seed - Silly Part Pack $4.99 Strange Seed Soundtrack $4.99 $9.98 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Strange Seed is a creature evolution game inspired by games like E.V.O.: Search for Eden and Spore’s creature stage. You begin as a humble blob: the “strange seed” that fell from a very unusual tree. While many blobs exist, you’re not quite like the others. Evolution Defeat creatures to collect their DNA and unlock new body parts. Reshape yourself and experiment with combinations to build the ultimate form. But remember that balance is key: a strong torso and powerful head might win fights, but what good is strength if you can’t catch your prey? With 7 part slots and over 40 creatures to discover, you’ll have over 10 million possible combinations to play with. Exploration Venture through 10+ unique biomes, from lush forests and sandy beaches to cityscapes and even the heart of a volcano. Each environment brings its own challenges. Too big for a narrow path? Evolve into something smaller. Stuck at a platform you can’t reach? Grow wings and take to the air. The world is full of secrets; can you uncover them all? Combat Don’t let the colorful world fool you - Strange Seed is full of danger. Creatures range from simple lizards to ferocious, Soulslike bosses. And victory isn’t just about reflexes: the right combination of parts could be the difference between survival and defeat. If you’re clever enough, you might even find ways to cheese your way to victory!
