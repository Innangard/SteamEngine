It’s a 3D top-down shooter with a twist: Cute, colorful, and nuts, like your sense of humor. Whether you're taking down undead hordes, showing off your new hamster pet, or just trying to survive long enough to find some milk, this game has something for everyone. 🧟 The Zombie Slayers is packed with fun modes waiting for you! Team up with friends to battle zombies - or see who’ll survive a showdown in the arenas. Stand your ground against relentless zombie waves - or climb the leaderboard. Whichever mode you choose, there’s one goal: survive and have a blast! Survival Stories 👊 Team up with up to 6 friends (or go at it solo if you’re feeling brave) to tackle missions and battle zombies. Oh, and mind friendly fire from that one friend who insists on using the RPG for everything, including close quarters. Brutal Zombie Waves ☠️ Think you’re tough? Try surviving wave after wave of relentless zombies. The more waves you survive, the higher your score on the Steam Leaderboard. Play alone or with friends, but remember: in the end, it’s every baby for themselves! 🎮 Dive into The Zombie Slayers and enjoy a world full of fun features like getting yourself a baby or a pet, bringing them to a zombie fight, and enjoying the chaos that ensues. Dress up in wild costumes, joyride across the map, explore endless options! Dance and discover, experiment with weapons, and embrace the fun! Make Baby 👶 Yes, you read that right. Join up with another player to make a baby, who’ll not only follow you around but also fight zombies in the most hilarious way possible. Just don’t forget to keep that milk bottle handy—nobody likes a cranky tot. Customize Character 👕 Choose between male and female characters, then dress them up with a wardrobe full of costumes unlocked with the XP you earn. Ever wanted to take down zombies dressed as a pirate? Now’s your chance! Dance Moves 🕺 Got a moment to spare? Hit the dance floor—or the battlefield—and show off your sweet moves. Whether you’re flossing on zombies or busting out a moonwalk, dancing is always in season. Use Items 🔧 Turrets, mines, barbed wire, electric cages—if it hurts zombies, it goes in your inventory. Strategize and use these items to boom zombies, zap zombies, win battles - or, you know, just create some more chaos. There’s never enough! Use Gun 🔫 Arm yourself with pistols, rifles, shotguns, flamethrowers, rocket launchers, and of course baseball bats, swords, and everything you can find. Nothing says “I mean business” quite like charging a horde with a sword and a flamethrower. Level Up 🆙 Every kill gives experience, advancing up the ranks of zombie slayers and more importantly, giving you more options to destroy zombies - and more importantly, more ways to look fabulous. Of course, the more XP you have, the harder it gets — who said saving the world should be easy? What are you waiting for? Start your absurd adventure with The Zombie Slayers today. Whether you’re battling zombies, making babies, or just having a dance-off, one thing’s for sure: you’ve never played a game like this before!
