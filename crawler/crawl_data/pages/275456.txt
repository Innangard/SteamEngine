Smash heads in this cooperative platformer game. Play solo or team up with up to 4 players. Inspired by games like New Super Mario , Joe & Mac , Metal Slug , Castle Crashers and Trine . - Different playable characters : Choose one of the playable characters, but don’t worry! You can change your character at anytime in the game and play with them all! - 1-4 players : Play solo, with one, two or three friends. Work together to become unstoppable! - Varied Enemies : Each level has different enemies, watch out as they can catch you unguarded. - Secret Levels : Unlock secret levels inside the game for more excitement! - Challenging : Challenge yourself to unlock all the achievements!
