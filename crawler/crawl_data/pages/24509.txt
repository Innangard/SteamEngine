View Community Hub About This Game Fight against the highly armed army of anti-magic frogs and restore peace in the forest before it's destroyed! "Machick" is a casual game that offers a unique experience where you are the last hope. Using ancient crafting and materials acquired from the wizardry shop, build amazing magic wands with various effects and powers. During the combat phase, face hordes of enemies, accumulate experience, and choose strategic upgrades to progress further in the challenging journey and gather resources to get even closer to your goal. - Players shape their journey by constructing their wand with items acquired in the store. - Strategically choose wand components to optimize performance during combat. - Puzzle challenges that test the player's creativity in crafting magical wands. - Confront the army of anti-magic toads in epic and challenging battles. - Employ your newly crafted wands to defeat enemies and advance the storyline. - Develop unique combat skills as you progress, facing increasingly formidable creatures.
