Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game For years, Lisa has been haunted by recurring nightmares of a burning mansion. One sleepless night, while watching a television documentary about abandoned places and local legends, she recognizes the very same mansion from her dreams. Determined to uncover the truth behind these disturbing visions, she sets out to investigate the mysterious property for herself. Her search leads her to the old mansion at the end of Pineview Drive. Standing abandoned for decades near the small fishing village of Lakeview Bay, the mansion has become the subject of countless rumors and local legends. Even today, the people of the region avoid speaking about the place and its troubled past. Old records tell of a mysterious cycle that once plagued the area. For many years, a violent storm would sweep across the region for exactly thirty days. During this time, people reported strange apparitions, unexplained phenomena, and even mysterious disappearances. Many believed that the old mansion was somehow connected to these terrifying events. Determined to find answers, Lisa begins exploring the mansion and its surroundings. What starts as a search for the origins of her nightmares soon turns into a terrifying journey into the dark history of Pineview Drive, where every discovery reveals another piece of a long-forgotten mystery. As the storm outside continues to rise, so does the turmoil within her mind, blurring the line between memory, nightmare and reality. Pineview Drive 2 - Rising Storm is the official sequel to the 2014 horror adventure Pineview Drive and, for the first time, reveals the story behind the old mansion and the events that have haunted this place for decades.
