Player discretion is advised. Deadly Quiet is a 4-player co-op asymmetrical horror game that immerses you in the unsettling atmosphere of the infamous Hoia Baciu forest . Viewed through the lens of a bodycam , the game delivers an intense and realistic experience as you and your team navigate the forest's haunting depths. Survival hinges on teamwork, strategic decisions, and your ability to adapt to the unpredictable horrors that lurk within. Every step you take and every sound you make could draw attention to your group, so staying quiet isn’t just advice—it’s your lifeline. Will you and your friends work together to outsmart the sinister forces of the forest, or will the silence consume you all? You and your friends embark on a weekend camping trip in the Hoia Baciu forest. A detour along a ravine leads you across a shaky bridge, but disaster strikes as your car plunges into the depths below. Miraculously, everyone survives, but your vehicle is destroyed. With no signal and night quickly falling, you’re stranded in the heart of the ominous forest. Your only hope is to traverse the cursed woods and reach the nearby town of Cluj. As you journey deeper, strange and inexplicable events unfold around you. The forest is a living nightmare, where every step and every sound could mean death. Mistakes are fatal, and the silence conceals an unspeakable danger. Was that chilling sound in the distance just the wind—or something far worse? Can you and your team survive the horrors of the forest, uncover its secrets, and escape with your sanity intact? Or will the silence claim you all? Personalize your character by choosing from a selection of unique skins, allowing you to customize their appearance to suit your style. 4-PLAYER CO-OP GAMEPLAY Team up with friends to survive the harrowing challenges of the Hoia Baciu Forest, where cooperation and communication are the keys to survival. ASYMMETRICAL HORROR Face off against a relentless supernatural predator that adapts to your actions and decisions, ensuring no two playthroughs are the same. IMMERSIVE BODYCAM PERSPECTIVE Experience the terror firsthand through a dynamic bodycam view that heightens tension and realism. PROXIMITY VOICE CHAT Communicate with teammates using in-game proximity voice chat, but be careful—your voice might attract unwanted attention. DYNAMIC SOUND SENSITIVITY The forest reacts to the sounds you make, creating an intense, stealth-focused experience where every noise could be your last. HAUNTING HOIA BACIU FOREST Explore the eerie and mysterious Hoia Baciu Forest, a place teeming with supernatural phenomena and cryptic secrets waiting to be uncovered. ATMOSPHERIC SOUND DESIGN An immersive soundscape amplifies the tension, with every creak, snap, and distant growl adding to the fear. TERRIFYING VFX A mix of realistic graphics and chilling effects brings the Hoia Baciu Forest to life, plunging players into its cursed depths.
