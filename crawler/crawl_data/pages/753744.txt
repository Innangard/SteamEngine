A handful of kick-ass surf-rock tracks that will get you ready to party. But that’s not all! This soundtrack also includes all our recent experimentation in music. Change the vibes by listening to some of the songs translated into chiptune or even Gregorian chants. Ah, Gregorian chants, the music genre you didn’t know you NEEDED. We also spiced the soundtrack up with some lo-fi beats. Because you never know when you may need to relax/study. And finally, the two weirdest pieces of this already weird display of musical beauty: a song full of anime vibes that celebrate our favorite buff blue goddess, and an original song we created to prevent content creators from getting their videos taken down.
