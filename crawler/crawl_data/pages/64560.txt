Car Park Capital will benefit from player feedback and give the team the opportunity to engage with, adapt and adjust the game and future content based on the feedback on the path to 1.0. Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? 10 months with a plan to transition to 1.0 in the Summer of 2027. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? We plan to have more maps, more building and customization options, more strategic depth to features like Oil Drilling and add events and disasters. Building options may include objects to build custom malls, different forms of parking and/or Movie and Restaurant Drive Ins. We also plan to improve existing features based on feedback and add Quality of Life features where needed. More music tracks are in the works, and a scenario set in Paris. If the desire is there, we hope to integrate Steam Workshop, allowing players to share creations. These plans and the direction may change based on Player Feedback. Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? Three maps/scenarios with different challenges to play. American City, Pyramids of Giza and Florida with boat-cars. You can level up in each scenario and unlock new cars, building options, Drive Thrus and production chains. Muuttuuko pelin hinta Early Access -vaiheen jälkeen? We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features. Miten yhteisö otetaan mukaan kehitysprosessiin? We will be engaging the community through Discord and Steam Forums in order to make improvements to the game.
