View Community Hub About This Game Kitty Tom needs to go a long way to reach his home. However, the blocks of land that make up the path are completely out of order, making the journey more challenging. Your task is to slide the blocks on the terrain to create a path that leads the kitten from point A to point B. In each scenario, there are three keys that the kitten must collect to open the door that leads to the next path. Help Tom reach his destination by guiding him through: The forest: The beach: The village: And finally to Japanese territory! Have fun during the 80 stages of the journey!
