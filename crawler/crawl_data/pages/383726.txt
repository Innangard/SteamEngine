View Community Hub About This Game 💧 Every step is a matter of life and death You need to plan every move carefully. Each step consumes water, and your life pool is limited. One misstep, and your droplet will wither away. Terrain changes your strategy - normal ground leaves a trail of moisture you can reclaim, sand doubles your water consumption, and water sources are your only supply. 🚪 Solve puzzles, clear the way A blue button is hidden somewhere on the map. You must step on it to open the corresponding blue door. But here's the catch - can your water reserves hold out if you detour to reach it? Refill first? Open the door first? Or rely on moisture reclamation to sustain yourself? Every decision counts. ✨ A lively water droplet experience Idle breathing - the droplet pulses gently when still, like a living being Bouncing arcs - every move comes with a compression charge, an arcing leap, and a squash-and-stretch landing for smooth, natural feel Vortex suction - upon clearing a level, your droplet is drawn into a swirling vortex at the finish line for a satisfying visual payoff Gradually escalating challenges From basic tutorials to challenging levels. The difficulty scales progressively. Each level teaches you a new mechanic - then turns it into a tricky puzzle in the next. 🎨 Fresh hand-drawn art style Fine visuals built at 2K resolution, with adaptive scaling to fit any screen. Water sources, sand, fire, doors, and buttons - each terrain type has its own distinctive look for easy recognition. 🎵 Immersive sound design Every action - moving, opening doors, clearing levels - is accompanied by responsive sound effects. 4 SFX channels support layered audio, and with future background music, the experience will be fully immersive. Water Spirit Escape - guide the droplet back to the sea with wit and wisdom. This game does not contain any real-time generative AI features. Some game assets, including but not limited to animation effects, videos, card effects, and similar materials, were created with the assistance of AI.
