Featuring fast-paced arcade matches and easy-to-learn mechanics, Bang Average Football is the perfect game for players of all skill levels. Choose from a range of men's and women's teams or design your own. Play with and against friends in local multiplayer or go for glory as both player and football manager in the single-player, RPG Story mode. With simple controls and classic top-down arcade gameplay, Bang Average Football is the ultimate football experience! Story Mode Join a washed-up, rock bottom football club as player… and instantly get promoted to the football manager position! As the player-manager, guide them through the ranks to the coveted top division. Learn how to manage the whole football club: make transfers, upgrade the stadium, secure sponsorships, and engage with fans to maximise the club's all-important Reputation. Visit other towns and football clubs around the country between matches, completing quests for NPCs and undertaking training drills to improve your abilities. Will you become National Champions and conquer the King’s Cup? Tournament Mode Create your own football tournament in a stand-alone mode! Make any combination of groups, divisions, and knockout rounds, using any mix of teams and settings, to construct and conquer your own competitions. Throw all your teams into one giant mega-league or recreate your favourite real-world football tournament from years gone by. Steam Workshop & Custom Content Design your own player databases, and teams for Quick Matches and Tournaments, or personalise your Story mode hero with a variety of hairstyles and facial hair designs. Share your designs and football databases with others… and import the best football creations from around the world in seconds.
