Perform burials and cremations, and fulfill visitor requests with special flowers and gravestones by day. But when night falls, everything changes. The dead may rest, but the zombies don’t. They are coming for your precious work. Will you be able to protect them? Visitor Satisfaction is Key After processing the bodies, visitors will regularly return to check on their loved ones. Your duty is to keep graves clean and intact while providing fresh flowers to ensure their satisfaction. Every visit rewards you with valuable bonuses to help keep your business running smoothly. Build your Defenses Each night, waves of zombies will come with their numbers growing and special zombie types emerging, hungry for freshly buried bodies and the freshest of all, you. From basic defenses like barbed wires and fences to advanced machines like automatic turrets, keep your defenses upgraded, repaired, and fully stocked with ammo to withstand the night. Trust your Arsenal With a variety of weapons, including pistols, rifles, shotguns, and your trusty shovel, you are the ultimate force against the Zombies. As you level up, unlock and purchase new weapons while upgrading your skills to become even stronger. Just make sure to stock up on enough ammo! Even a Graveyard Can Be Cozy Sometimes! Use your Farming Area to grow different flowers for profit and vegetables to simply eat and restore your energy and health. With Upgrades, you can even unlock a Grinder to turn zombie parts into Fertilizer. Decorate your Graveyard with statues, lights, and other ornaments to make it truly your own. Expand and Upgrade Your Facilities Your graveyard isn’t just a resting place, it's a business, and every upgrade matters. Expand your burial area to accommodate more bodies and improve your crematorium with additional furnaces, automated cleaning systems, and faster processing times. Upgrade your farming area for better resource production, and enhance your water supply from a simple well to a motorized pump and water tank to keep everything running efficiently. Every improvement helps you stay one step ahead of both business demands and the undead threat. Key Features Manage and expand your own graveyard while balancing business and survival. Build defenses and fight off nightly zombie attacks. Perform burials, cremations, and keep visitors satisfied. Upgrade facilities like the crematorium, farming area, and water supply. Unlock new weapons and improve your skills. Customize and decorate your graveyard. Experience a mix of management, strategy, and first-person action.
