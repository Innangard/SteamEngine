VANDALIZE Break their piano, clog their toilets, pee on their rug - do anything in your power to ruin the archmage’s day. Every inconvenience you create gives you more points towards your vandalism target for the night. USE YOUR VOICE Use your voice to cast wizardly incantations - enunciation matters so you better keep your voice steady no matter how dire the circumstances. ESCAPE THE TOWER You are not alone in the tower - there are all sorts of creatures patrolling the halls. Some are created to protect their study. Stay quiet, cause distractions, hide and finally when all else fails - run.
