This is the "I'm in" simulator designed for one purpose: making you look like a high-stakes cyber-criminal while you're actually just typing "asdfghjkl" over and over. No networking degree required, just a laptop and crowded cafe. Most of the gameplay involves you typing nonsense commands and pretending to be an "I'm in" type of hacker. And when it comes to hacking, just randomly spam your keyboard at the target speed to bypass fake security and watch as totally-legit-looking code floods your screen. Complete with unnecessary sci-fi bleeps and bloops, because that's definitely how cybersecurity works. Key Features: Effortless Hacking : Feel like a genius by simply typing easy commands on screen! Hack networks, steal valuable data, and navigate through cyberspace with intuitive keyboard controls. Stylized Puzzles: Unlock target security with satisfying visual challenges. Strategic Gameplay: Balance your actions against the rising System Alert. Push your luck to grab more loot, or play it safe and escape with what you've got! Roguelike Sessions : Each hacking run is a unique challenge. How deep can you penetrate before the system locks you out? Unlock and Upgrade : Collect CHIP DATA to unlock new hacker OS types, command lines, and powerful upgrades between sessions. Perfect for: Casual gamers looking for an accessible hacker game yet thrilling experience Sci-fi enthusiasts who've always wanted to live out their hacker dreams Anyone seeking a fun and engaging way to improve their typing skills No coding experience required – just bring your imagination and good keyboard. Can you become the most notorious hacker in the cyberpunk underworld? Boot up Hacky now and leave your mark on the digital frontier!
