The Eclipse Hive New gameplay expansion: A Boss Rush-like gameplay where players hunt bosses, collect clues to exchange for intelligence, directly confront bosses, and receive rewards after victory. In the desert, there's a group of adventurers who bravely hunt monsters and challenge formidable enemies in the terrifying Hazy Realm, all for the treasures possessed by the monsters. As more greedy adventurers flock to the area, an industry gradually emerges. The Sand Lizard Adventurers' Guild gathers many fearless individuals. They scoff at the meager rewards of war, drawn instead to the immense profits and near-death thrills provided by the monsters of the Hazy Realm. Now, some older adventurers have started a new trade. They are spread across the six regions of the desert, collecting various clues from the Hazy Realm and defeating weaker creatures residing there, leaving the most challenging bosses for the fearless "others". Regardless, these veterans always manage to profit handsomely from the Hazy Realm. 24 secret realm bosses, 36 legendary items; Secret Realm Bosses - Powerful bosses with unique skills and combat mechanics, offering rich rewards upon defeat; Legendary Equipment - New weapons, armor, and accessories at levels 25 and 30, each with unique additional skills, which can be combined to create various magical effects. Exotic Wayfarer Character customization expansion: Brought by travelers from beyond the desert, featuring unique costumes and accessories. As the wars between tribes become stagnant, resources in the desert become scarce. Foreign merchants, sensing an opportunity, bring carts full of goods to the Golden Market, where they exchange food, iron, and weapons for gold, silver, and jewels, also introducing various exotic clothes and jewelry. New character customization materials; 48 new options for body, hairstyle, eyes, nose. New protagonist models; adds 4 sets of male protagonist models and 4 sets of female protagonist models. Siege and Conquest With the development of engineering in various regions, craftsmen have once again erected walls to block invading enemies. Now, archers can hide behind these walls and shoot arrows without restraint. Redstone City, Threestone Fort, Black Mud City, and Ice Valley will each have unique walls to fend off invading enemies! Siege defense enhancement; on the basis of the original siege warfare, the last line of defense is replaced with a wall that separates both sides, with the gate needing to be breached to capture the defender's stronghold. Wall system; At the start of the battle, ranged archer units will automatically take positions on the walls for defense.
