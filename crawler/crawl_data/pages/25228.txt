View Community Hub About This Game After... is a story-driven survival horror game with stealth elements, set in a post-apocalyptic world devastated by an unknown epidemic. The cities are silent now except for the sound of the infected wandering the empty streets. You’ll have to explore what’s left, gather supplies, and find a way to stay alive. There are no fights against  hordes here... every infected you encounter means certain death. Silence, patience, and quick thinking are your only weapons. The only person you can rely on is yourself. Every choice you make could lead to your salvation... or your doom. Survive. Adapt. Endure in a world that has already ended.
