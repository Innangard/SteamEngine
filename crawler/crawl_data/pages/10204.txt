Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game already features most of the core systems and mechanics I envisioned, but there’s still so much more I want to explore and expand—both in terms of content and the depth of the experience. As a solo developer working on VOIN, I face the challenge of bringing my complete vision to life. Since VOIN is a hack-and-slash game with a blend of other genres like roguelike, RPG and extraction, there’s endless potential for new loot, weapons, and inventive ways to slay enemies. However, the more layers I add to the gameplay, the trickier it becomes to balance everything without input from a dedicated community of players. That’s why Early Access is the perfect fit—it allows me to steadily introduce new content while gathering invaluable feedback from those who play and engage with the game.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “My goal is to release the full version within a year, though the exact timeline might change based on the feedback from you, the players.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan to expand the game with additional levels to explore, more bosses and enemies to face, and fresh, creative ways to defeat them—since my vision is to continually add new methods for slaying your foes. I also aim to enrich the story and deepen the lore of the world. Naturally, I’ll be striving to ensure the final version is as polished and engaging as possible.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “I’ve worked hard to make sure the Early Access version offers plenty of content to sink your teeth into. It includes two expansive, open-world-style levels to explore, dozens of unique loot and equipment pieces with their own distinct traits, a variety of enemy types, and a challenging boss encounter. You’ll also find a hub area where you can upgrade your gear, craft new equipment, and level up your character.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Depending on the scope of additional content I add to VOIN over time, the price may be increased at release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I’m actively engaged with the community, keeping a close eye on all messages in our official Discord server and on social media, which is linked in the game client. I’ve read every piece of feedback from those who played the demo version, and I fully intend to continue this throughout the entire Early Access period. In addition, I’ll be conducting a survey after the start of Early Access and following each Major Update. I’ll also be regularly checking feedback on the Steam discussion forums, Steam reviews, and any other platforms where public discussions take place. My goal is to create an ongoing dialogue with players, focusing on the game’s balance, content, and progression. With so many interconnected systems in VOIN, achieving perfect balance on my own would be nearly impossible. That’s why Early Access is the ideal path forward—it allows us to collaborate directly with the community to shape the best version of the game together.” Read more Buy VOIN $19.99 Add to Cart Buy VOIN music bundle BUNDLE (?)
