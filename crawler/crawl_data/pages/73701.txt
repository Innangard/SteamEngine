The Book of Nameless is an action-packed novel, written by author L.R. Gaunt, and set in the kingdom of Montelorne, 100 years before the events of 9th Dawn: Remake. Featuring characters from the popular RPG game series, as well as never-before-seen storylines, don’t miss this exciting new addition to the 9th Dawn universe. This PDF download features over 200 pages of immersive fantasy , to be enjoyed by fans of the video game series and new readers alike.  As this novel is a prequel to the events of 9th Dawn: Remake, there is no need to be familiar with the games before reading (although we’ve included some fun Easter Eggs along the way, for those that are). We hope you enjoy this new offering from the team at Valorware. Join Fen Greenbelt and Rena the Teamaster as they battle dragons, goblins, and unknown forces to save the kingdom of Montelorne. While Montelorne is in the grips of an ongoing civil war, Fen Greenbelt and Rena the Teamaster return from a mission to retrieve an object of immense magical power. However, after presenting this relic to the benevolent King Maltyr, a series of grave events begin to unfold, threatening the kingdom of Montelorne and all those who inhabit it. Alongside their allies in the Guild of Valor, Fen and Rena must race against time to uncover the truth and defeat the sinister forces that wish to seize control of the realm.
