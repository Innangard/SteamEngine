View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Zombie Survivor: Undead City Attack - get ready to survive the zombie invasion in this first-person shooter! Get ready for a relentless zombie onslaught in our action-packed shooter. Grab your weapon, take on the horde and unleash a hail of bullets as you fight for survival against the relentless tide of advancing undead! Experience the precision and power of headshots in our intense zombie shooter. Lock and load your assault rifle as you take down zombie after zombie with deadly precision, proving that in the face of the undead, every shot counts! Improve your zombie killing skills by climbing to the top of a building and wielding a sniper rifle. Take aim and shoot down the undead menace in the streets below with calculated precision. The roof is your vantage point; streets, your battlefield! Turn the tables with explosive tactics in our zombie shooter. Throw a grenade into the heart of the undead horde and witness the devastating explosion that clears the way! After a secret scientific experiment goes wrong, a mysterious deadly virus breaks free from the laboratory, turning everyone in its path into crazed zombies! This modern world plague has spread throughout the world, the zombie apocalypse has begun on every continent and every country, the biggest cities have become battlefields between undead monsters and the last surviving zombie killers. The largest zombie outbreaks have occurred in major cities - New York, London, Rio de Janeiro, Mexico and Johannesburg are on the verge of collapse. Several survivors, namely Zombie Survivor: Undead City Attack, had to hide in underground subway tunnels. Your mission is to travel through these places, fight zombies in the streets, protect those lucky enough to survive, and deliver blood samples to the laboratory to create an antivirus. Now only you can give humanity a chance to survive! Only a few people with a rare gene in their DNA are immune to the virus, and their blood can be used to create a vaccine. So, while most of the survivors are hiding underground, the bravest of them come out to meet the crazy zombies on the streets, sneak into the nearest laboratories and create a vaccine. But only the one who can be called a real zombie killer and zombie hunter is able to resist and defeat the hordes of crazy zombies running at them from all sides! And you were destined to become such a zombie killer! Sometimes you'll be left alone at a crossroads in a city, in the middle of a local zombie outbreak, and your mission will be to simply survive there. Hordes of zombies will come at you from all sides, so keep your aim and try not to run out of ammo. Other missions have you sitting safely on a rooftop and your mission will be to protect scientists or innocent people who are trapped by zombies and left for dead. A good sniper rifle will definitely come in handy there! As an elite special forces soldier, you have a large arsenal at your disposal - pistols, submachine guns, assault and sniper rifles, shotguns, grenades and armor - everything a zombie killer could want! Make sure you are properly equipped, spend money on weapon upgrades and extra ammo as you will face several waves of very fast zombies with an extremely strong zombie monster at the end of each level! Game highlights: - Post-apocalyptic zombie first-person shooter at its finest - Huge variety of enemies, new deadly beasts and monstrous bosses on every level. - Wide selection of weapons - pistols, submachine guns, assault and sniper rifles, shotguns, grenades and armor. - Dozens of quests that can be completed for rewards - High-quality graphics and hardcore gameplay - an adrenaline rush is guaranteed! This is not your average zombie vs plant game, pixel game or any other battle royale game - this is a hardcore offline do or die battle during the zombie apocalypse! The zombies ate your friends and now they want to eat you, so get ready to kill them all and escape death! Improve your shooting skills and survival instincts, master headshots for instant kills, upgrade your weapons, buy new armor and ammo - get ready to stop the zombie invasion and clear the city of the undead menace! Game Features: - Furious gameplay, a combination of survival RPG and first-person shooter. - Real zombie carnage, lots of undead to fight at the same time. - An impressive arsenal of weapons, including classic pistols and modern killing machines. - Earn money for killing zombies and medals for completing levels. - Smart system for improving your weapons and equipment. - Lots of equipment and amplifiers, grenades, armor and first aid kits. - Exciting, almost impossible boss fights Be the zombie hunter that people desperately need, you are their last hope, this dying world deserves a hero, so pass all the challenges along the way and maybe you can save the human race from extinction!
