View Community Hub About This Game This is a cooking simulation game about night market life and the stories of ordinary people. You were once living a routine life, working an ordinary job, until you decided to carve out your own path and try starting a street food stall selling kaolengmian. By chance, you meet a warm-hearted girl. Seeing that you are still new to running a stall, she decides to help attract customers by dressing in a trendy cosplay. The two of you quickly hit it off, and thus begins your very own Chinese Street Food Legend ! Fast-paced cooking. The quicker your hands, the more you earn. During cooking, everything has to be done quickly: cracking eggs, brushing on sauce, flipping the noodle sheet, and serving the dish. Within seconds, the oil starts to sizzle, the aroma rises, and steam fills the air. It is a tight, focused kind of concentration, not unlike those small moments in real life where there is no room for mistakes. Meet all kinds of customers, and savor the many flavors of life. On a busy street where people come and go, you will encounter all sorts of customers: some cheerful, some cunning, and some carrying troubles of their own... Different lives gather around this small street stall, where new stories begin to unfold. Pay close attention to their orders, serve them delicious kaolengmian, and connect with them through heartfelt conversations. Who knows, some of them may even become close companions along the way. Sweet and tangy sugar coating, unlocking another flavor of the night market. Beyond the steaming hot kaolengmian, the game will also let you unlock a tanghulu stall. Pick the fruit, skewer it, and dip it into syrup. The crystal-clear sugar coating wraps around the bright fruit, carrying a sweet-and-sour taste that feels like a bite of childhood. One hot and one cold, one savory and one sweet. Managing both stalls side by side makes the night market feel even more lively. Ever-changing decorations, letting you build your own little stall. As business picks up, you can unlock different skins, appearances, and decorations. From a simple roadside stall to a brightly lit storefront, your kaolengmian counter, tanghulu stall, signs, and cooking tools can all take on a brand-new look. Street stories, everyday warmth, and ordinary hearts. The comfort of a simple street-side meal, or a casual greeting exchanged in passing, can be enough to hold all the small tenderness and joy of ordinary days. Perhaps what makes life worthwhile is hidden in the steam rising from a plate of kaolengmian, and in every ordinary yet legendary story of people living earnestly and finding contentment in simple things.
