View Community Hub About This Game Small creatures, but massive destruction! In this colorful survivors-like , summon your trusted allies, the Chuminis , to battle hords of forest demons! It’s up to you to manage each Chumini’s unique behavior to survive for 20 minutes, and defeat the Dark Druidess. There’re 15 different Chuminis to choose from: find the best synergies that suit your playstyle! Gameplay Collect experience from the defeated demons, build your army of powerful allies and upgrade them to survive! Some Chuminis will run in a circle around you, others will follow your every step… Optimize your movement to take advantage of your allies! Content 15 different Chuminis to summon, upgrade, and use in battle An "ability" system tied to each Chumini 10 unique enemies and 2 bosses Colorful hand-drawn graphics A meta-progression system offering replayability and difficulty levels Achievements to collect!
