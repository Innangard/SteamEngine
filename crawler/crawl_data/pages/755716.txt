The dialogue is silly and engaging to read, the puzzles are fun, its a near perfect game imo.” 10 – An Evening With Dylan About This Game Walk The Frog is a cozy, heartwarming point-and-click adventure with simple, unique drag-and-drop puzzle mechanics. Your task is to correctly assemble Froggo’s world on sticky notes. You can solve the puzzles at your own pace and enjoy a gentle narrative journey with no stress or pressure. It is available in English, Spanish or German. Head home for the Froggapalooza festival Froggo only wants one thing: to be back home at his pond so he can celebrate the start of spring with his friend Buddy. But along his path, he will encounter a lot of funny, sassy, and quirky characters who want his time and attention. Maybe they’ll help him, too? Will he make it to the festival on time? Sort the world out Each level consists of an assortment of hand-drawn sticky notes. Your task is to piece them together correctly to create a path so Froggo can continue his journey and set his world to rights. Every puzzle you solve is like a little moment of clarity; the mechanics are intuitive and accessible. Get cozy The lovingly hand-drawn 2D style and ambient soundtrack create a heartwarming world where you can immerse yourself and relax. Friendship and hope Snuggle up in your coziest blanket, brew a cup of your favorite tea, and get to know Froggo and his quirky new friends. Talk to each other, help one another, and work together to overcome an array of obstacles. Walk The Frog invites you into a hopeful, emotional story of homecoming, friendship, and connection. No timer, no stress You can take your time solving the puzzles on Froggo’s journey home. Some encounters are optional, and some dialogues change nuances of the story. A narrative game that helps you unwind Take a break from your day-to-day and immerse yourself in a cozy puzzle adventure that doesn’t push you; it gently takes you along on a journey.
