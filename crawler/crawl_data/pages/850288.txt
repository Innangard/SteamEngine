In Stamperor! , you rule as a jester making fast decisions that shape the fate of your empire. Do everything you can to prevent unexpected events from destroying your empire and leading you to defeat. Each cycle, citizens arrive with requests, but it’s not just about saying “yes” or “no”. You use stamps with unique effects to alter your choices, create unexpected consequences, and deal with situations that spiral out of control. Build and keep your empire standing Expand your empire and manage Money, Food, Happiness, Security, and Population . Every structure shifts the balance of your empire and can either help or accelerate collapse. Proposals are unpredictable, and you will need to think strategically to handle each problem that appears in your realm. Decisions that become stories Engage in evolving storylines throughout the run , meet charismatic characters with their own interests, and face decisions that can change everything. Each character has a different personality and story, and may offer good proposals or threaten you in the most absurd ways possible Expect the unexpected Unpredictable events can turn your run upside down at any moment. One mistake can trigger a chain reaction. A good decision might not be so good after all. Act impulsively, be wise, be a tyrant, be a fool, choose your way of ruling! But don’t forget that your people need you! Survive the chaos or be consumed by it. A decision-driven city builder where every choice matters and can either end everything or lead you to become a glorified emperor.
