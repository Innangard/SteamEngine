Step into The Battlerealm and experience epic 1v1 combat featuring your favorite Super Heroes and Villains from across the Marvel Universe — now fully optimized for mouse, keyboard, and controller on the big screen! BUILD YOUR MARVEL DREAM TEAM As a Summoner chosen by The Collector, assemble a powerful team from a roster of over 300 Champions — including iconic Avengers, X-Men, Guardians of the Galaxy, Spider-Verse heroes, and more! Whether you're teaming up Iron Man and Wolverine or pitting Deadpool against Venom, the combinations are endless and the stakes are high. FIGHT YOUR WAY TO VICTORY Engage in fast-paced battles against enemies and players from around the world. Master intuitive combat mechanics and unleash special attacks as you battle through cinematic storylines, competitive arenas, and time-limited events. Every fight brings you closer to becoming the Champion of The Battlerealm! EPIC QUESTS, ICONIC STORIES Dive into an evolving narrative with thrilling Marvel-style storytelling. Explore new chapters, face powerful bosses, and uncover multiversal mysteries as you quest through The Battlerealm. Every update brings fresh content and new Champions to collect! TEAM UP AND RISE TO THE TOP Join forces with other Summoners in powerful Alliances. Coordinate strategies, take on global events, and dominate Alliance Wars and Alliance Quests to earn valuable rewards and climb the competitive ranks. PLAY YOUR WAY, ANYWHERE Marvel Contest of Champions has been rebuilt for Steam, offering native support for controllers, keyboard, and mouse with customizable bindings. Enjoy full cross-platform support — start your journey on mobile and continue seamlessly on PC. Wherever you play, your progress goes with you. THE CONTEST AWAITS The fate of the Marvel Universe rests in your hands. Will you rise to the challenge and lead your Champions to victory?
