"We believe the best way to build a compelling post-apocalyptic survival game is with the people who play it. Early Access allows us to open a direct line of communication with our players. Your feedback will be essential in helping us refine the core experience and guide the development of Nomad Drive into the game we all want to play." Por quanto tempo aproximadamente este jogo estará em acesso antecipado? "We are targeting a full release within 6 to 12 months. This timeline may shift based on the feedback we receive and the new ideas suggested by our community, as our priority is to deliver a stable and polished experience." Como a versão completa será diferente da versão de acesso antecipado? "The 1.0 version of Nomad Drive will be a more expansive and polished experience. Our roadmap includes new, distinct biomes to explore, a much wider array of RV customization parts, and deeper, more engaging survival and crafting systems. Throughout Early Access, we will be heavily focused on performance optimization, bug fixing, and adding content based on player feedback to ensure the full release is as feature-rich and stable as possible." Qual é o estado atual da versão de acesso antecipado? "The Early Access build provides a solid foundation of Nomad Drive's core gameplay loop. Players can already drive, maintain, and repair their RV, explore a procedurally generated world, gather resources, and manage their survival needs. Online co-op multiplayer is also implemented. While the core experience is in place, players should expect to encounter bugs, and system-heavy updates may require save file wipes as we improve the game." O preço do jogo será alterado após o fim do acesso antecipado? "Yes, the price will be lower during Early Access to thank our early supporters. We plan to increase the price as we add significant new content and features, with a final price increase upon the full 1.0 launch." Como vocês planejam envolver a comunidade durante o processo de desenvolvimento? "Community involvement is the primary reason for this Early Access launch. We will be actively monitoring our Steam Discussion forums and Discord server for feedback, bug reports, and new ideas. We're building Nomad Drive with you, not just for you, and we're excited to see how your suggestions help evolve the game."
