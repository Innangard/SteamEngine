Download Liberated: For the Homeland Free Download View Community Hub About This Content “Great is truth, but still greater, from a practical point of view, is silence about truth.” (Aldous Huxley, Brave New World) Liberated: For The Homeland is the first of two short story addons for Liberated. In this story-driven epilogue, you’ll peek behind the curtains of an oppressive system operated by very ordinary people. It’s a story of the daily grind and crushing violence against individuality. Media on Liberated: "Looks like a noir graphic novel come to life" - Game Informer "There’s really something magical here" - PC Invasion "The story has echoes of Watchmen " - PC Gamer “Blends noir comic art style with Inside -style-gameplay” - Polygon “Feels like an episode of Black Mirror ” - The Sartorial Geek Liberated is an action-adventure game inside a living, hand-drawn comic book. Uncover an alarmingly current cyberpunk reality where human rights are dying away. Take up arms and solve puzzles in a noir rain-soaked city that’s desperate for justice. Seamlessly merging the art of hand-drawn graphic novels with interactivity and side-scrolling action, it’s a revolutionary combination of video games and comics.
