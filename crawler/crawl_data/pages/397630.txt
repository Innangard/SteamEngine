Content For This Game Browse all (2) Machine Mind Soundtrack $2.99 Machine Mind Digital Artbook $2.99 $5.98 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game The world survived a nuclear war after a mysterious virus swept the planet and wiped out most of the humanity. Some managed to find refuge in an orbital station, and Earth has become a wasteland ruled by mad raiders. After your ship crashed, your personality was preserved in a special consciousness module thanks to an experimental programme, but you remain a prisoner of the metal shell. Use the rovers to survive, reconnect with the orbital station, and solve the mysteries of the catastrophe that changed everything. Make weapons, modules and equipment from the resources you gather to upgrade your machines and fortify your base. Build and modify rovers with the help of various modules that you can adapt for different tasks, from mining to fighting. Reconstruct destroyed bases, build defences and structures to survive in dangerous conditions, engage in intense battles with raiders and bosses using advanced weapons and combat modules, and be ready for the challenges of nature: you’ll have to deal with disasters such as sandstorms and radiation. Assign transport rovers to resource gathering, construction, and defence tasks. Plan actions and appoint roles to machines to focus on strategy. Explore a variety of locations, from wastelands to abandoned factories, each full of resources, enemies and secrets.
