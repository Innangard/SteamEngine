B eyond deckbuilding : Create your own cards as you play by combining triggers, conditions, selectors and effects! Over 10 billion possibilities. Build  your cards to fit your playstyle -- not the other way round! Tactical combat with positioning : Manipulate enemy cards' targeting aggro by placing  your cards strategically on the board. Meta Progression Unlock new ability components and base cards after each run to grow your card-creation possibilities from several thousands to several billions. Discover new combat locations with unique gameplay modifiers that can turn the tides of battle. Beat new game modes like constructed gauntlet or random draft gauntlet. Cosmetic card customization : The card-building aspect of Card Coder does not stop at scripting the mechanics. Upgrade the rarity of ability components by merging duplicates to gain stats bonuses and powerful status auras. Addicting gameloop : Fight an opponent, gain rewards, go shopping and create new and better cards to improve your deck. Face the next opponent until you beat the Big Bad.
