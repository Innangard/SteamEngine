View Community Hub About This Game Fulfill your dreams of owning a beautiful vessel with a large crew, teeming with life thanks to all sorts of unique passengers in Cruise Ship Manager! Take over the Mediterranean Sea with your cruise business in this unique management game. Plan out your ship’s interior, make sure everything is running smoothly, set the prices of your services, and become a legend of the high seas! Choosing the layout of the ship is up to you! Decide where to place a club for your passengers, and where the 5-star restaurants should be. Does a casino strike your fancy or maybe you prefer an open pool? And don't forget about the suitably homely cabins for your passengers! Make sure everything is accessible and works well together, and that remember to buy enough food and supplies in port to guarantee a stellar cruise. Pick your crew wisely. Your success or failure depends on their skills. Once you leave the dock, you're stuck with your employees on a long journey. Make sure you won't regret hiring an inexperienced rookie over a seasoned veteran. Once you settle on the amenities and finish all the necessary preparations, you can finally search for passengers to invite onto your ship. But there's a catch - it's not up to you who buys your tickets. So price them accordingly! If you offer them a premium charge, your passengers will feel entitled to the best service. It won't be easy to satisfy them. On the other hand, you could sell the tickets at a low price and improve your budget through other means. For instance, by charging extra for fancy cocktails. As your reputation grows, more people will want to experience the thrill of signing up for your cruise. Build your brand and reach a point where people will pay any amount of money just to brag about sailing on your ship! You will choose the best routes to suit your business. Would you rather make some quick cash on a routine, 1-day cruise? Or maybe a long journey through the Mediterranean is your kind of adventure? You may earn more money by choosing the most attractive long trips, but always be aware of maintaining your ship and paying your crew. Whatever your choice is, there are random occurrences that cannot be helped. A fire may break out, your equipment may need urgent fixing, or passengers may ask you for immediate help in an emergency. It will be your task to end such a crisis before it's too late. Features: A cruise ship of your own that you can customize to your liking Resource management (supplies, fuel, budget, etc.) Incredible variety of unique passengers with different expectations and behaviors Crew members with their skillsets, which they improve during cruises Route selection between well-known Mediterranean tourism hotspots Many unique, luxurious rooms to add to your ship Unlimited fun on a deep, blue sea!
