Calling all bird enthusiasts and animal lovers! We've got a chirpy surprise for you – a flock of 30 adorable birds waiting to be organized (neatly!). Our feathered friends come in all shapes and sizes, each with their unique personalities. But with so many of them, our aviary is becoming a bit chaotic. Please, lend us a helping hand and bring some order to this feathered frenzy. Pick up our little avian friends and gently place them on the grid, making sure each has their own cozy corner. Don't worry if you rotate them – they might squawk in protest, but these friendly birds won't bite. So, put on your sorting hat, grab your bird-loving spirit, and let's create a harmonious haven for our feathery companions. With your help, our aviary will be the envy of the bird world! KEY FEATURES Birds 30 new & unique hand-drawn birds for you to befriend, collect, and organize. Handcrafted Levels 80 brand-new handcrafted levels, which will tickle your brain. Daily Puzzle! Each day there will be a new puzzle for you to solve! Simple Controls Play by just clicking the left and right mouse buttons: an accessible experience for newcomers or seasoned puzzlers of any age. Relaxing Music Birds Organized Neatly has relaxing and upbeat background music! Relax with a puzzle and let your good mood fly free like a bird.
