View Community Hub About This Game Garage Tidy Up is a cozy first-person organizing simulator about bringing order back to a chaotic auto workshop. A once-perfect garage has turned into a disaster. Wrenches are on the floor, sockets are mixed with paint cans, wires are hiding under tires, and nobody knows where the 10 mm socket went. Your job is simple: pick up every misplaced item, find the correct work zone, and put everything back where it belongs.
