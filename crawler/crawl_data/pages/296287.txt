Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We may be just two people, but with your help, this game can grow far beyond what we could create alone. Early Access lets us build, test, and improve the roguelite experience together. Your voice isn’t just heard, it’s part of the foundation of the game’s future. ” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We’re mapping out the next three months as we head toward Early Access, focusing on building the best experience we can for launch. Once we get there, we’ll take a close look together with the community to see if the game needs more content or if it’s already hitting that perfect sweet spot.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We’re exploring new Biomes, Beasts, and systems that shape the path you take on your journey to the end, as well as the possibility of adding PvP. We’re also designing fresh room types and additional features to make the game deeper, more dynamic, and even more exciting for everyone” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current version showcases 1 biome path out of the full 3 biome paths to reach the end of the game. 64+ Beasts in the game. Half the equippable items in the game. Missing: It is missing Coin Toss Inscription Bonuses. A 3 additional Talents. A few more Beasts. A couple of equippable items, Cards, and upgrade options for Talents. The core vision of the game is present. While a few Room types and Biomes are still on the way, what’s here gives you the full taste of the core gameplay loop. Our goal is to keep adding content and new systems that build on this foundation and make the experience even better.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We expect the price to increase gradually as new content and features roll out, but our goal is to ensure it always feels fair for what the game offers.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We’re very active in our Discord, often hopping into voice channels to chat directly with the community. Some Beast designs in the game already come from player ideas, and we’d love to keep collaborating like this throughout Early Access. In the Breaker League, one coin flip can crown a champion… or end your run. You’re the newest contender in an endless competition where every match bends the rules and every rival Breakers wants you gone. Test your team, your nerve, and your luck as you fight your way to the top. Key Features Find, collect, and battle with over 60 unique beasts: From undead teddy bears who refuse to stay down, to shapeshifting marsupials, to leviathans that crush the battlefield in a single strike. Evolve Beasts mid-match for sudden bursts of power, and deeper stratedgy. Then watch them return to normal after the fight. Battle others Online (PvP) for bragging rights! Share your Lobby Code with anyone to have them join and battle against you! Equip special items onto your Beasts to twist their abilities in unexpected ways. Trigger the ultimate move - a TurnBreak - to end the round on your terms. Face your Echo - shadow versions of yourself from previous runs - and prove you’ve grown stronger. Battle your Chat – With Twitch integration: Let your viewers influence battles by voting on your opponent's next move in real-time. Streamers, it’s you vs your chat! Do you have what it takes to become the Ultimate Breaker? Every match is a gamble. Every floor has new dangers. Every turn can be broken. How far will you go before the League breaks you?
