Knock over trash cans, search dumpsters, and scour everything from Animal Control, through the wilderness, on the way to the junkyard for helpful items as well as valuable trash. Climb, scurry, and scamper your way through the world. Wear a hat. Stack your friends. Launch one off a seesaw. Play pattycake with a friend. Eat some junk.
