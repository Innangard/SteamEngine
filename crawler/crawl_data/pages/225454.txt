Use your wits to become a coach, guide a team of adorable pups and solve over 170 hand-crafted challenges! PLAN YOUR ACTIONS Position your players just right, clear their passing lanes, avoid hazards, outsmart the opponents and lead the pups to victory. BECOME A PUZZLE CHAMPION Each chapter brings in unexpected challenges - balls get stuck in the mud, monkeys mimic your movements, bunnies react to passes, and more!
