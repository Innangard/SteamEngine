Összes téma megnézése A szoftverről Build Anything, Brick by Brick in VR EasyCraft is a VR creation sandbox built around real bricks. Pick up a part with your motion controller and snap it into place — thousands of authentic brick pieces, precise connection points, and intuitive controller-based assembly make large-scale building feel effortless. Thousands of brick parts with true 1:1 proportions Precise snap-point placement — move, rotate, and align with your controllers in 3D space No grid-box, no block limit on your imagination — build as big as you want Create Together in Real Time Open a room, invite friends, and build the same creation side by side. Voice chat, live collaboration with invites and requests, and automatic conflict-free editing keep everyone building at the same time. Publish. Explore. Remix. Your creations are saved to the cloud and ready whenever you return. Publish a finished design to the community gallery for everyone to visit, like, and favorite — or open someone else's work, make it your own, and publish something new. Free to Play Jump in and start building for free. Optional in-game currency is available if you want expanded building quotas.
