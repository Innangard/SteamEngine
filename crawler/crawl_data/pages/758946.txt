Buy Star Valor - Base Building $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Build and manage your own stations, with a wide variety of bases and modules to grow your galactic empire. Available station models include: Standard stations that provide basic services like ship repair and trading with NPCs; Weapon turrets and defense walls to provide defenses; Mining stations, to mine large asteroids; Production stations: factories, laboratories and "agristations"; Power plants and Solar arrays that provide energy to nearby bases; Jumpgates; Beacons that provide light or set spawn positions on the sector; Research Centers, to discover blueprints. Internal station modules: Each station comes with a few default modules like a generator or a basic defense setup, then a limited space for expansion, allowing deeper customization. Station Capture: In addition to building your own base, you can now capture enemy stations (limited to hideouts for now). New ship role: Engineer Engineer ships have a built-in 'Builder module', which is required for base building. They also have bonus to repair beams, which are used in the actual building process. New ship models: 3 tier 3 (blue) Engineer ships: 1 Frigate (size 4) and 2 yachts (size 2). New special character: 1 special character to unlock. New perks / achievements 3 new Perks / Achievements to unlock. New game mode: Station defense A new "Tower Defense" inspired game mode where you start with an engineer ship equipped with a repair beam instead of a normal weapon, and basic materials in order to build your base right from the start. Battle enemies in waves and trade with NPCs to grow your empire! Base game compatible Base Building is compatible with old save files.
