Spin the 3x3 slot machine, match the lines, and summon your units. Feeling unlucky? Don't panic. Absorb, Evolve, and Reign Supreme There's no such thing as a useless unit on this battlefield. Weaker units are sacrificed to feed the strong, becoming their flesh and blood. Watch an ordinary 1 star Warrior ascend into a stronger unit. Your branching class choices will completely shift the tide of battle. Infinite Synergies That Overpower Pure Luck This isn't just a mindless slot machine where you leave everything to chance. Every time a unit spawns, it rolls from dozens of 'Random Stats'. Exploit system loopholes, bend RNG to your will, and become the master architect of your own victory.
