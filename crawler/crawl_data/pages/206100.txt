It looks and sounds absolutely beautiful, and its simple but moreish gameplay loop will keep you coming back time and time again.” 9/10 – GameSpew About This Game Join forces with your villagers to battle the malevolent Demon Herensuge and his army of hellish minions & elites, intent on wreaking havoc on your serene homeland. Use your deck-building skills to emerge victorious from the perilous depths of the Pyrenees. Experiment with various characters each with distinct abilities and traits that enable a wide range of gameplay styles. Develop different strategies and optimize the use of items you collect to enhance your abilities on every adventure. Upgrade your deck by unlocking over 200 powerful relics and 100+ cards that you can switch on-the-fly to maximize your deck in between enemy encounters. Fight through distinct biomes such as dark caverns, corrupted swamps, menacing mountains, each with their own set of creatures and terrain modifiers. Between expeditions, return to your village and restore buildings to unlock upgrades for your items and spells . Uncharted Setting: Pyrene is based on Basque mythology, a never-before seen pantheon in video games. Learn more about these untold stories, deities and creatures by playing. Strategic Decision-Making: Balanced between ease of play and strategy, Pyrene presents deep deck-building elements that intertwine with its dungeon-crawling gameplay to force you to think before making any move. Unique Characters: Play as a diverse cast of characters from your village, each one possessing unique skills, mechanics and magic. Tailor your deck to your hero to make the most of each run! Built for Replayability: With a wide range of objects, biomes, relics, talismans & spells, no two runs are the same and you will be able to develop ingenuous strategies to navigate the game’s story and endless modes YOUR way.
