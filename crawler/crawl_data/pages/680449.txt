You have been chosen by the sacred Evertree to bravely delve inside. You will help guide your Fox by attacking enemies, dodging their attacks, and gathering loot , all with just your mouse cursor (or keyboard if you prefer!). As you progress, you will grow stronger. Acquiring new equipment, upgrading them, investing into a skill tree, and choosing permanent major upgrades in the form of Blessings. Key Features: Simple, but engaging gameplay. Click to move your Fox, or change a Setting to make it so your Fox moves to your cursor position automatically. Keyboard and Gamepad movement options are also available. Fighting is done by proximity. The Fox gets near an enemy, they swing their weapon if they can. Combat involves deftly moving in and out of enemy range while dodging obstacles and other attacks. Upgrade your equipment to become stronger. Choose from 1 of 6 different weapons, unlocked gradually as you play through the game. Skill tree with meaningful progression and impactful choices. Choose a powerful Blessing upon level up, a permanent bonus that grants you great strength.
