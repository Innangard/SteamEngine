The OST is comprised of 36 tracks, producing an extensive 4+ hours deep dive into the audio landscape of the mutant soap opera Mutazione, focusing on the original background music composed for Die Gute Fabrik’s wonderful world of mutated plants and wildlife. Process has been as important as product for Coronas, and as well as being heavily influenced by nature, he has used ‘real’ instruments wherever possible.
