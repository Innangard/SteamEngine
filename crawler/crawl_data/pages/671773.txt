FEATURES Cats. Black cats. White cats. Calico cats. Cats in hats. Cats in bows. Cats in hats with bows on them. If you ever, while playing this game, start to feel that there aren’t enough cats, please take a moment and really think about it. 100+ levels to enjoy, with varying levels of challenge and complexity! Relax in cozy greenhouses, cafes, and even outer space! Charming hand drawn art, in a paper-crafts style! A lovely scrap-book to arrange and display all the stickers you collect throughout the game! Fully playable on Steam Deck! The Perfect Scratching-Post For Your Brain! Tricky, but not frustrating. One step at a time, the way all the clues mix and twist and interact; this kind of cat loves being next to that kind of cat– okay. That cat hates being next to the other kind of cat, which also likes being up high, but only in a short box. Okay. Carefully arrange the cats on the screen, one by one. Take your time. Take a breath. Take your little pat on the back when you finally get it– you’ve earned that. Create & Customize A Sticker-book With Unlockable Stickers! Take a break from twisting your brain into knots, and relax with your very own feline-themed sticker-book. As you complete chapters, you will unlock new stickers - cats, backgrounds, decorations, accessories, everything you need to create the perfect sticker-book page! So get your thinking caps on: You'll want to keep playing to see what stickers will unlock next. Your cats will be very pleased with their new accessories. So Many Cats!! Wait, really? Seriously? You still think there aren’t enough cats in this game? And did you– oh. You sat and you thought about it? And even after sitting and thinking about it, you still don’t think there are enough cats? Huh. Well. Okay, then. Guess it needs some more cats. Here are some more cats. Better? Good.
