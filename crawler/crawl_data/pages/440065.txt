Content For This Game Browse all (1) Shop Simulator: Supermarket - Original Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game SHOP SIMULATOR: SUPERMARKET – DESIGN, SELL & EXPAND – YOUR SUCCESS IS IN YOUR HANDS. Put your entrepreneurial skills to the test in Shop Simulator: Supermarket. Immerse yourself in the world of retail and start your own business today! Will your small shop become a successful supermarket? It's in your hands. Playing with friends is even more fun. Coordinate your teammates, give orders via in-game voice chat, and fulfill your customers' wishes. Together, you can make the supermarket of your dreams a success. GAME FEATURES: DESIGN YOUR SHOP Design your store exactly the way you want it. Choose from various shelves and decorations, place products as you like, and set their prices. Build the shop of your dreams! MAXIMIZE YOUR PROFITS Monitor stock levels, analyze sales data, and optimize your inventory to meet your customers' needs. Keep an eye on market prices and order strategically to maximize your profits. EXPAND AND GROW Expand your shop and diversify your inventory to bring even more variety to your business. Hire employees to be able to focus on the critical aspects of your shop. MONEY MAKES THE WORLD GO 'ROUND Scan products, charge the correct amount, and ensure that customers leave your store happy and satisfied. If too many customers come in, you can open a second checkout and staff it with an AI employee or a fellow player. CLICK & COLLECT ORDERS Move with the times and also accept online orders to reduce your customers' waiting time and make additional profits. But be careful: you need to prepare the right products to satisfy the customer and complete the order successfully. PLAY SOLO OR AS A TEAM Setting up the store, stocking shelves, setting prices, ringing up customers, decorating... Everything is easier and more fun as a team! Work together like in real life and coordinate tasks using the proximity voice chat. The multiplayer mode for up to 4 players offers the ultimate supermarket fun!
