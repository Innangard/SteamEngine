Simulator 3 - NZAA Airport $19.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Auckland Airport (IATA: AKL , ICAO: NZAA ) is a major international airline hub serving New Zealand with many domestic and international destinations for visitors to continue on to. It is the largest and busiest single-runway airport in Oceania, able to handle about 45 plane movements per hour. A second runway is planned to be constructed by 2025. Auckland Airport has two main buildings: the International Terminal and the Domestic Terminal. Gates 1–10 are in Pier A. Gate 10 can handle a large A380 plane. Gates 15 to 18 are in Pier B. These gates can handle A380 planes with two jetbridges. They can also handle two smaller planes at once. Gates 4A, 4B, 4C, 4D, 16A, 16B, 16C, and 16D are bus gates. Passengers use these to get to planes parked further away. Stands 19–20, 70–73, 82–84, and 74–81 are remote parking spots for planes.
