Will you accept your fate, or find a way to escape? Abrasion is a first-person horror game set inside a brutal, skyless, megastructure. It takes around 90 minutes to complete. The world is filled with useful items, environmental storytelling, and many secrets. Features A dynamic, stalking AI monster. An interconnected world full of hidden passages and secret areas. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Intended for Mature Audiences – Contains disturbing themes and intense moments. A relentless stalking entity that chases the player through environments. High-tension escape sequences with limited resources. Decaying corpses and unsettling biological growths. Blood stains. Brutalist megastructure with oppressive, claustrophobic spaces. Psychological dread: Flickering lights, distant whispers, and unpredictable sound design. Environmental storytelling with implied violence and tragic lore. Syringe-based mechanics (extracting fluids from grotesque organisms). Jump Scares. Rare, but impactful audio/visual shocks (e.g., sudden bangs, machinery activating).
