The game is built on two distinct roles that are completely interdependent for survival: Silent Guide: Sees how the circuits need to be solved but cannot speak . They must use limited guidance mechanics, such as head movements and hand gestures, to direct their teammate. Circuit Solver: Cannot see the clues, but can interact with the control panel and circuits. They must correctly read the guide's directions, keep their nerves in check, and make the right move at the right time. Solve the Circuits, Cut the Power! The only way to stop the execution is to solve the electrical schematics on the control panel under the deafening noise and heavy psychological pressure of the approaching saw: Direct the Current Correctly: Connect the cables to the right spots, balance the voltage, and divert the energy away from the deadly saw. Combine Logic and Speed: Flawlessly overcome asymmetrical puzzles that grow progressively harder at every stage, from simple current matching to high-voltage maze schematics. Make No Mistakes: A wrong connection or faulty current doesn't just cost you time—it increases the speed of the saw, bringing the lethal end even closer!
