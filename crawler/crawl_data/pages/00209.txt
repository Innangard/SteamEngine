Chaos guaranteed for 1-4 players. Yo, welcome to the gang! In this tantalizing sneak preview of Dumpster Gang, prepare for FULL RACCOON IMMERSION as you grab up to 3 friends (or go solo) in a chaotic co-op sandbox game where rowdy raccoons take on the human world in daring nighttime heists. Vive la raccoon révolution!!! So, what’s featured in the demo? Raccoon-based sandbox fun for 1 to 4 players. Complete ridiculous missions, steal everything you can carry, and outsmart the relentless Exterminator who just HATES raccoons. Invade a suburban home. Ruin the garden, eat trash, make… coffee?! Wreak havoc on a rural farm. Wondering whether you can milk cows or kidnap the chickens? That’s weirdly specific but YES, YOU CAN. Sharpen your raccoon skills in the forest training grounds. Personalize your raccoon with a choice selection of dashing skins and evocative emotes from our cosmetic system. Live the raccoon dream. Knock over trash cans, steal shiny junk, raid snacks, commit tiny crimes, and unleash your inner menace. Sprint, climb walls, crawl through vents, and reach places only a raccoon could. Pick up, throw, steal, or smash nearly everything you find and turn every level into your own chaotic playground. Play solo or with friends. Work together to carry heavy loot, solve puzzles, and rescue captured teammates... but things never go according to plan. Betrayals, disasters, and hilarious accidents are all part of the job. Cause enough chaos and the Exterminator will hunt you down. Outsmart his traps, escape capture, and rescue your friends before your gang ends up behind bars. Return home after every mission to customize your raccoon, unlock hats with unique abilities, and decorate your hideout with every ridiculous item you've stolen.
