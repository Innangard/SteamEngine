Car Mechanic Simulator 2027 returns with new features while staying true to the series’ proven formula. This installment introduces the long-awaited co-op mode, allowing you to run your workshop together with friends. Repair cars and expand your collection, with over 500 available vehicle configurations. Start your journey in any mode — gameplay is now fully customizable. Day and night cycle, deliveries, limited workspace, and job difficulty — everything can be tailored to match your preferred playstyle. The revised skill progression system offers even greater freedom, while the all-new garage customization mode lets you create a space exactly as you imagine it. Key Features: Co-op multiplayer mode – build and manage your garage together 150+ cars in over 500 configurations, with availability based on vehicle rarity 8,000+ car parts to discover, repair, and install New modular engine system – easily swap and mix individual engine components Free driving around the garage and its surrounding area – drive and park wherever you want! Day & night cycle with time skip functionality Fuel system – consumption depends on engine type and driving style Advanced garage customization – decorate with tools, furniture, and lighting Dynamic part deliveries – delivery time and cost depend on time of day and game mode Limited availability of classic car parts – search the used parts market to find what you need Repair jobs with time-based bonuses – complete tasks faster to earn higher rewards Personal diagnostic station – check suspension and overall vehicle condition New car discovery system – discovered vehicles can be viewed in the Carspedia New vehicle selling system – instant sale or trade on the used car market Junkyard – new vehicles available daily for dismantling and parts recovery 3 racing locations – race track, rally track, and airport Abandoned barns with procedurally generated content Multi-level parking system for your collection of cars Car dealership where you can purchase new vehicles Gas station with an automatic car was h Livery & sticker system – create your own custom designs Reworked repair and upgrade system – queue parts for automatic repair or complete minigames to speed up the process And much more to discover during gameplay!
