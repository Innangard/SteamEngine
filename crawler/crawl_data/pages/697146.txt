Reclaim Expansion emphasizes player autonomy: there are no fixed paths or prescribed solutions—only the tools, the terrain, and the consequences of your decisions. From storm-ravaged forests in Central Europe to drought-stricken regions of North Africa, players must assess damage, dismantle what can no longer stand, and reclaim usable resources to rebuild on their own terms. Requires the base game RoadCraft to run. The Reclaim Expansion includes the following content: • Two new 4km² maps with contrasting environments and challenges Autumn Collapse Set in a Central European region inspired by Germany’s Black Forest, this autumn map features dense forests, steep valleys, and unstable terrain. Following a series of violent storms, landslides and fallen trees have cut off access to abandoned industrial sites. Players must clear routes, stabilize infrastructure, and assess buildings at risk of collapse—demolishing unsafe structures to reclaim valuable materials. Summer Drought Located in North Africa and inspired by the Sahel region, this map takes place during an extreme drought. With water sources depleted and infrastructure no longer suited to the environment, players must secure access to water, rebuild critical facilities, and dismantle obsolete structures to prepare the land for long-term survival under harsh conditions. • Seven new vehicles designed for exploration, demolition, and heavy logistics: - 5111Б Dragline Demolisher – Heavy demolition equipment capable of dismantling abandoned or unsafe buildings and recovering salvageable resources. - Torque G-175 – A massive tree crusher designed to clear dense forests, fallen trees, and vegetation, opening new routes through storm-damaged landscapes. - Derry Longhorn 4520 – A heavy search-and-rescue truck built for extreme recovery operations, capable of navigating unstable terrain while supporting large-scale interventions. - Hollander M7 Cable Layer – Specialized equipment used to deploy and manage cables, enabling the restoration of power and connectivity in damaged areas. - Minuteman K370 Explorer – A scout vehicle equipped with an active radar system, ideal for surveying unstable terrain and identifying points of interest in hazardous zones. - Wayfarer OFT96 TS D – A versatile dump truck for large-scale road works, material transport, and terrain reshaping. - Wayfarer OFT96 TS T – A cargo crane variant of the OFT96 platform, enabling loading, unloading, and precise placement of materials in challenging conditions. • New demolition and salvage gameplay For the first time in RoadCraft, players can deliberately demolish abandoned buildings using specialized equipment. Demolition is a strategic choice: dismantling unsafe or obsolete structures allows materials to be salvaged and reused elsewhere, directly reshaping the environment and influencing future operations. • New mission themes and sandbox progression Assess environmental damage and prioritize objectives freely. Clear landslides, fallen forests, and blocked routes. Demolish structures that no longer fit the environment. Salvage materials and reuse them strategically. Operate under extreme conditions with limited resources To be released: A new themed-vehicle pack for industrial, resource, and off-road missions.
