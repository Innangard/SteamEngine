View Community Hub Reviews “MINI CITY: MAYHEM is a city-builder for players in a rush. If you want to enjoy the genre, but don't have a week to play a single session. It's fast, it's clever, and it comes for multiple game modes to keep you occupied with fun bits of play - an excellent proposition for its low price! Mayhem mode is where the game shines, with a unique tempo and challenge that makes for an intense play session.” Good Stuff – Tiny Gaming About This Game Beat the clock and become the city's longest running mayor! It is your job as the freshly elected mayor of a town to turn a little plot of land into a bustling metropolis. Manage your resources, lay the foundation for important hubs for your expanding city, and watch as your population grows. However, in Mini City: Mayhem, every second counts, so there's no time to relax and take pride in your work. A cozy city-builder that will puzzle you! To make sure that your city grows and meets the needs of its residents, you'll need to act swiftly and wisely. In addition to strategically placing and stacking puzzle pieces depending on form to distribute vital structures, roads must be thoughtfully designed in order to connect your buildings in an effective manner. Different games modes for all types of urban planners. Is the fast-paced city building not your thing? Then you'll be glad to know that we have something for all kinds of aspiring Mayors: Mayhem: Prove yourself as the new mayor in a race against the clock to keep your citizens content! Zen: Take your sweet time building your new city, with no pressure and a limited number of blocks. Sandbox: Let your creativity flow freely. Build your masterpiece of a city with no restrictions. Square Stack: Strategically pick one of two shapes and keep building your tower. Crane: Balance blocks with the utmost precision to build the tallest tower possible! Mini City: Mayhem features different starting maps inspired by outlines of iconic real and fantasy cities such as Pisa, Istanbul and New York, each with their most famous landmarks including the torre di Pisa, Galata Kulesi and the Statue of Liberty.
