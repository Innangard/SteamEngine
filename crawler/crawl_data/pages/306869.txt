"What are you searching for?" Upon waking, she finds herself lost in a surreal fairy-tale world. As Alice, she encounters a cast of bizarre characters: factions fighting over names, a bodiless witch, a gardener cultivating world-destroying plants, a lonely collector obsessed with names, and a manager who only speaks when offered alcohol. Everything feels strangely familiar yet utterly alien. "Alice, I’ll always stay by your side. Whatever you wish for, I’ll make it come true." A mysterious girl with long purple hair, Zixuan, stands by her, giving her the courage to face this strange world. To return to the real world, the two of them support each other and push forward. What will they discover in this world? What awaits them in the gap between fairy tale and reality? "Alice, remember this: as you wander through this strange fairy-tale world, never doubt your own common sense. Once you start questioning yourself, you won’t be able to return to reality." Zixuan’s warning becomes Alice’s only hope in this twisted world.
