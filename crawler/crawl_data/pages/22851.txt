Introducing the Knockout expansion for Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor. NEW CLASS: BRAWLER No need for pickaxes when you have fists of steel! Knockout the swarm with up close and personal weaponry! The Brawler brings 11 new weapons to Hoxxes ready to smash, tear and crush his way deep into the planet As a special bonus DRG has decreed that this new arsenal will also be available to to the other classes already fighting the good fight on Hoxxes, via the tag system, in order to help their mining efforts NEW BIOME: SANDBLASTED CORRIDORS Dry and inhospitable, but rich in resources The only vegetation is out to get you and even the environment shifts as twisters scour the landscape, as if to keep you out. But brave dwarves who can find their way and gain the upper hand can gain riches beyond measure Once again more biome choices added to missions, and without increasing progression requirements, you gain even greater freedom to tackle challenges your way. NEW MISSION: BROOD HARVEST Something stirs in the deepest caverns of Hoxxes and Management have acquired samples from new glyphid lifeforms that show great potential, but the R&D department requires more to further the research! Collect as many samples of Kytoplasm from the Broodlings as you can, dive deep and find the source if you can and gather as much as you can before you are overwhelmed The more Kytoplasm you can extract the more support you’ll get during your dive, but be careful, the indigenous glyphids are very protective of the broodlings The few who make it to the last stage, will face off against the Dreadnought Empress and her brood, in a full stage fight for survival!
