After Early Access Launch, AB+ updates will take the form of: Large Content Updates (Episodes or Full Chapters) Gravekeeper Updates (New Arenas) Localisation Updates State of the game Reworks & Bugfixing Customisation About This Game A fusion of splatter-based combat from games like Splatoon and FPS classics like Doom and Quake , ALTEREDBLOOD+ (AB+) is a Movement-FPS-Platformer where Splattered BLOOD is a resource and Positioning is vital to staying alive . Cover arena walls and floors in your foe’s BLOOD . Soak up BLOOD to regenerate Ammo and Health . Stand in that BLOOD to ALTER Yourself and your Weapons. Bring directionality to your ultra-violence: Deal damage to Splatter their BLOOD over the surfaces directly behind and around them. Kill, and they burst into a shower of Gibs , spreading more BLOOD for your exploitation. Soak up BLOOD by Sliding, Dashing and Stomping on BLOOD Splatter. Soaking up BLOOD gives you Health and Ammo , keeping you in the fight. Watch where you stand: Weapons become ALTERED when you stand in BLOOD , gaining powerful effects to dominate your foes. Each weapon comes with its own unique interaction with BLOOD and Movement; master and push them to their limits. YOU move through MOTHER, as though a part of HER. Pass through obstacles like Liquid, fight faster, leap higher. You become ALTERED when standing in BLOOD. Traverse MOTHER’s depths by B-hopping, Air-Strafing, Surfing, Trimping, Rocket jumping and more. Inspired by movement tech in classic FPS, ALTEREDBLOOD+ features a range of movement mechanics to learn and master. Customise your movement experience: CLASSIC and MODERN Air-Control settings are available.
