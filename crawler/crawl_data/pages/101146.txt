Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game A nostalgic tile-based MMORPG where adventure is shaped by the choices you make. Explore a vast fantasy world filled with dangerous creatures, hidden places, valuable resources, and countless opportunities. Whether you prefer exploring alone, fighting alongside other players, building a home, mastering a profession, or sailing across the world in your own ship, Norroth gives you the freedom to create your own adventure. Explore a Living Fantasy World Travel through a vast world with over 300 handcrafted areas filled with towns, forests, mountains, caves, islands, and dangerous lands. Discover hidden locations, dungeons, uncover secrets, and venture into the unknown. Every journey can lead to new adventures. Fight in Turn-Based Battles Face dangerous creatures and powerful enemies in strategic turn-based combat. Choose your equipment carefully, develop your character, and use your abilities to overcome increasingly challenging encounters. Join Guilds and Play Together Norroth is built around a shared online world. Join a guild, meet new players, explore dangerous areas together, trade resources, and take on challenges as a team. Create lasting friendships and become part of a growing community. Build a Home of Your Own Find a place in the world and make it yours. Own your own house, create a home for yourself, and establish your place within the world of Norroth. Build and Sail Your Own Ship The world does not end at the shore. Build your own ship and set sail across the seas. Travel between distant locations, explore new islands, and take your adventures beyond the mainland. Gather, Craft and Master Professions Gather resources from the world around you and turn them into useful items. Master different professions, improve your skills, craft equipment, and become known for the path you choose. Whether you become a skilled gatherer, a dedicated crafter, or a powerful adventurer, your journey is yours to shape.
