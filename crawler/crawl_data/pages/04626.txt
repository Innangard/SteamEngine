Por quanto tempo aproximadamente este jogo estará em acesso antecipado? "We expect the game to remain in Early Access for roughly two months, with a full release targeted for mid-December 2026. Como a versão completa será diferente da versão de acesso antecipado? "We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. We also plan to refine balance, performance, and usability using player feedback. The scope and priorities may change during development." Qual é o estado atual da versão de acesso antecipado? "The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required." O preço do jogo será alterado após o fim do acesso antecipado? "We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release. We have not finalized full-release pricing, and if our plans change as the game develops, we will communicate any change before it takes effect."
