After defeating the Demon Lord boss, the main storyline comes to a close—but your adventure doesn’t end there! You can continue exploring the vast alien continent, build your camp, and enjoy free trade and restaurant management on this strange new world! Freely explore a world full of exotic alien flora and fauna. Start by mining and logging, craft advanced tools, climb the tech tree, and expand your shelter. Try taming alien creatures—not only to farm meat and resources, but also to ride across the planet or fight alongside you against bosses to become the alien overlord. Beyond the alien world above, powerful monsters and bosses lurk underground. Defeat them to obtain various weapons, equipment, and materials, which can be crafted into advanced gear to make yourself stronger. There are hundreds of weapons and accessories with distinct combat styles—swords, bows, staves, hammers, and more, each with unique variations. Weapons can be upgraded and enhanced, branching into even more forms. Accessories also grant different attributes and effects, such as sprinting, dashing, or revival, allowing you to freely customize your combat style. Recruit or capture scattered minions across the alien world. Whether farming 9-to-9-6 or mining 0-to-0-7, you can command them freely. Assign them to smelt iron, cook meals, or stock goods—the planet’s automation runs on the hard work of your minions. Fully enjoy the life of an alien rancher! Collect the genes of wild creatures and use the bio-lab to create gene chips, transforming your minions into powerful weapons. This not only boosts their stats but can also change their attack patterns. Continuously upgrade and enhance your minions, leading them to conquer the planet. From raw materials to crafting to shelving, enjoy a full-cycle service with no middlemen taking a cut. Set up stalls to sell goods, attract merchants lining up to buy, and turn your base into the CBD of the alien world. Personally build and run a trending restaurant, create hit dishes like “Boiled Bug Jelly,” offer full entertainment and lodging services, and attract merchants from across the universe—experience life as an interstellar tycoon!
