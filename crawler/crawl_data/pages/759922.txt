View Community Hub About This Game We wanted to create a desktop idle game that fits naturally into your day. —and thus, Typing Farmer was born! Your keyboard and mouse power the farm! Every keystroke and click helps your crops grow. As long as you're typing, your farm keeps working! Sell your crops to earn gold and unlock new plants to grow. A bountiful harvest will attract adorable animals to settle on your farm! Enter Renovation Mode to customize your farm! Change the designs of UI entrances such as Storage, Shop, and Mailbox, or purchase decorative items to make your farm truly your own. Play together with up to 15 players! Turn into cute little animals, visit each other’s farms, share the joy of harvest, and make your tiny farm world feel even more lively! Stay organized with the built-in to-do list and Pomodoro timer, and plan your day at a glance. The game also tracks your daily keyboard presses and mouse clicks. While growing your farm, you’re also growing your routine!
