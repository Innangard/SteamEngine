Summon the mighty power of gods in this fun incremental game about chopping down trees on a secluded island by using the forces of nature: earthquakes, sunbeams, tornadoes, hail, lightning strikes and tidal waves! It's Chopping Time! CHOP DOWN THOSE TREES! There's no need to click, simply hover your cursor over the trees to chop them automatically. ZEUS WOULD BE JEALOUS! Use the Forces of Nature by hovering the corresponding interest point when it appears. BUY UPGRADES IN THE SKILL TREE! Spend your money and colored wood logs to unlock permanent upgrades in the skill tree. Accessibility options: customize a wide range of settings from the options menu, including the wave movement mode (normal, gentle, or flat), designed for players sensitive to motion sickness. And if you're up for a challenge, you can race against your friends or the world's finest players to be the fastest on the game's various leaderboards.
