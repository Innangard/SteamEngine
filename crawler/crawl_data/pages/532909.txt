View Community Hub About This Game Post Office: Tidy Up! is a single-player sorting simulator. An earthquake has emptied every rack in the depot, and 3,360 parcels are lying on the floor. Each one of them belongs somewhere. Your shift ends when they are all back. Every parcel carries its own address The label tells you everything you need: which courier, which country, which numbered slot. Read it, cross the hall, and put the parcel exactly where it belongs. Size decides first - ordinary parcels go to the tall racks along the walls, oversized ones to the low islands in the middle. Eight couriers, three countries, twenty-four posts Every courier has its own livery colour and mark. Every country has its own code. Every post holds 140 numbered slots. The whole 20 by 20 metre depot is open from the first minute - nothing is locked and nothing is gated. What changes over a shift is how well you know the floor. The right slot answers back There is no punishment for a wrong guess and no fail state. A parcel that settles into its slot answers with a short golden flash, and a finished compartment with a wave of light down the whole rack. No magic Parcels do not fly into your hands, shelves do not fill themselves, and nothing highlights the rack you need. Everything that ends up on the shelves, you carried there yourself. Permits and standards Shelved parcels earn points, and points are spent on the clipboard: running, trolley, stack, pace, payload and arm span. It is not the depot that improves - it is you, your load and your step. Shift report At the end the depot adds it up: clean time, parcels shelved, compartments and racks finished, the share done on the first try, metres walked. Take hours over the floor or plan a route and cut the walking - the report counts the time honestly.
