Simple rules, delicious graphics, upbeat music, and a highscore table you'll be tempted to beat. It's the perfect way to relax for 5 minutes or lose yourself in the game for an hour. How does it work? Slices of different shapes fall from above. Your task is to rotate them and arrange them to fill horizontal lines. Once you've filled them, the row disappears with a pleasant sound, earning you points. If you don't make it, the stack grows. Everything brilliant is simple. What makes Cheese Stack special? 🧀 Cheese instead of boring blocks Each piece is hand-drawn: holes, jagged edges, golden hues. Swiss, Gouda, Cheddar, Parmesan—they're a joy to examine, not just to stack. 🏆 Your Leaderboard The game remembers your best scores. Beating your own record is a special kind of pleasure. The game also features a list of player records from around the world! 🎵 Music that stays in your head Joyful jazz and French tunes that set the rhythm without being overwhelming. You'll find yourself humming them in line at the store. 👶👴 For everyone from 5 to 99 years old No complex plots, violence, or microtransactions. The rules are explained in 10 seconds. Kids master the controls instantly, and adults appreciate the depth—the further you go, the faster the pieces fall.
