It’s also led by a former amateur racer who was inspired by his time racing bikes and playing retro games such as Lotus Turbo Challenge, Jaguar XJ220, Lamborghini American Challenge, Outrun , and many others.😍 These retro games are also the inspiration for the game’s 2D hand drawn visuals, vast spaces, and the exploration.
