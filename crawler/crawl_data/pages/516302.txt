View Community Hub About This Game Forget the agent suit. Grab your toolbox. Go Undercover! 🔧 SOLVE WITH YOUR HANDS Unscrew a panel, reroute the power and grind through a hatch. Smash what's in the way. Cells, canteens, maintenance corridors — all part of the puzzle. Sometimes you crack it with your brain, sometimes with a hammer. Think smart or hit hard. Both migh t work. Seven chapters. Five tools. Zero gadgets. Just you, your wits, and whatever you find lying around. 🤖 MEET WINSTON Your drone partner. Hacker, scout, intel source — and the sharpest mouth in any prison. He'll come through when it matters. Probably. 🔒 THE SETUP Redemption Prison. A model facility where Warden Viktor Kozlov runs his "Born Again" rehabilitation program. Officially — he turns criminals into new people. Unofficially — he brainwashes them. Your mission? Go in undercover as a regular maintenance worker, fix what needs fixing, and gather evidence against Kozlov along the way.
