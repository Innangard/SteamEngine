Merge Emoji themed Tokens together to create more powerful Tokens to defeat waves of enemies. Craft your set with Hundreds of unique Tokens to create Thousands of powerful and wacky combinations. No two runs are ever the same in this bite sized rogue-like you can take with you anywhere! Gameplay Place Tokens on  on the board and Merge tokens of the same type to make ones of a higher Level. Higher Level Tokens are Stronger!! Choose a new Token to add to your set at the end of every wave. There are 100's of Tokens to discover each with unique effects! Synergize your build to defeat the ever more challenging enemies that come each wave across multiple difficulties and challenges! Gain crazy Powers throughout each run, each with game changing, potentially even game breaking effects.
