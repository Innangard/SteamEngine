Tinker, rotate and organize woodeen blocks to discover satisfying little scenes full of cute animals, gentle logic, and soothing sounds. Features: 15 unique hand-painted puzzles Collectible cards Cute animals to pet in every level Soothing and satisfying sound design Relaxing, stress-free gameplay UMAMI is designed to soothe, not stress. Every puzzle is a peaceful invitation to explore a world built around gentle logic, satisfying sounds, and soft visuals. No timers, no wrong moves, just a cozy space to unwind and enjoy playful problem-solving at your own pace. Each puzzle diorama is hand-painted with care, featuring mouthwatering dishes and adorable animal companions. Whether you’re stacking pancakes, arranging onigiris, or petting a cute panda, every scene is full of life, charm, and little moments of joy. Beyond the main puzzles, UMAMI rewards curiosity. Subtle animations, collectible cards, and playful surprises are tucked away for those who look closely. Combined with tactile clicks, warm soundscapes, and visual details, every interaction is designed to feel delightful and immersive.
