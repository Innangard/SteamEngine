Construction of what became the Portsmouth Direct Line began in 1838, and 170 years later the route runs through rural scenery and over steep gradients. Coupled with numerous station stops, there is plenty to occupy even the most expert driver. This scenario pack features services on the Portsmouth Direct Line based on various real-life services set mainly in 2016-17. The majority of services are based upon real life passenger services in peak or off peak, as well as weekend engineering works and winter-based scenarios too. Included are 2 scenarios for the Class 444, 3 scenarios for the Class 450, 3 Scenarios for the Class 455 and 2 scenarios for the Class 159.
