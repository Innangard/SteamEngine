As Princess Amelie, you travel across the lands of Teana in search of the legendary knight Bill Gilbert. Each new region raises the stakes with tougher battles, stronger enemies, and greater rewards, forcing you to explore further, build stronger armies, and constantly adapt your strategy. The world of Teana is filled with new dangers and challenges. You will face powerful monstrous bosses, deadly creatures, and an entirely new enemy race — the bloodthirsty Lizards. As the threats grow, so do your options. Princess Amelie gains new skills and abilities, including the power of flight, opening up new ways to explore the world and approach encounters. Princess Amelie is not alone on her journey. She is accompanied by a pet dragon that grows and evolves alongside her — starting as a small and amusing companion and gradually becoming a devastating ally. Over time, the dragon unlocks powerful abilities, from treasure hunting and direct attacks to battle rage and even summoning volcanic eruptions on the battlefield. Armored Princess further expands the strategic layer with companions, new unit combinations, and more flexible army building. Combined with three hero classes — Warrior, Paladin, or Mage — this creates a wide range of viable playstyles and strong replay value. With its larger scope, new threats, and expanded mechanics, King’s Bounty: Armored Princess offers a deeper and more demanding experience that rewards curiosity, planning, and tactical thinking over dozens of hours. Real-time exploration meets deep turn-based tactical battles Explore the world in real time, then switch to carefully paced, turn-based combat where positioning, morale, unit abilities, and preparation decide the outcome.. A huge hand-crafted world with dozens of locations and battlefields Travel across towns, wild landscapes, remote islands, and dangerous dungeons, fighting battles both on the surface and deep underground. New enemies, powerful bosses, and a new race Face huge monstrous bosses, deadly creatures, and an entirely new enemy faction — the bloodthirsty Lizards — each bringing new tactical challenges. Princess Amelie — New protagonist with new abilities Unlock new skills and characteristics that change how you explore the world and approach encounters, including the ability to fly and reach otherwise inaccessible areas. A pet dragon that grows into a devastating ally Start with a small, amusing companion and watch it evolve into a powerful force, unlocking abilities such as treasure hunting, direct attacks, battle rage, and even summoning volcanic eruptions. Expanded strategic systems and army building Recruit companions, experiment with new unit combinations, and build armies around morale bonuses, abilities, and tactical synergies. Three hero classes with different playstyles Choose between Warrior, Paladin, or Mage, each offering distinct strengths and approaches to combat and progression. A dynamic world that raises the stakes as you progress Stronger enemies, greater risks, and better rewards push you to adapt your strategy and plan every step carefully.
