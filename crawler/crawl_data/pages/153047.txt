The Illustrated Strategy Guide contains four chapters: Combat Basics (pages 7-13) Main strategies (pages 15-19) Minions (pages 21-159) Bosses (pages 161-181) Combat Basics: this is a detailed tutorial describing the subtleties of minions and basic mechanics.
