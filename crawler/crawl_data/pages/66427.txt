Create your company, complete contractor training, and take on two introductory jobs using real-world-inspired concrete polishing equipment and processes. LEARN THE TRADE Begin at your company headquarters and complete Contractor Basics, an introductory training job that teaches you how to operate the equipment and follow the floor-finishing process. Learn how to: Operate a professional floor grinder Use a hand grinder for edges and detailed areas Change tooling as the job progresses Cover the entire work area Follow each stage of the polishing process TAKE ON YOUR FIRST CONTRACTS After completing your training, accept two playable contracts: Josie’s Garage — Restore a worn garage floor and learn the complete polishing workflow. Evans Patio — Apply your skills to an outdoor concrete surface with its own layout and challenges. Work through grinding, edge work, honing, polishing, staining, and sealing as you transform each unfinished slab. DO THE WORK YOURSELF This is not a menu-based job simulator. You are directly operating the equipment on the floor. Push larger machines across open areas, use the hand grinder to reach edges and tighter spaces, change tooling between stages, and carefully cover the complete work area. Every pass produces a visible result. BUILD YOUR REPUTATION Complete jobs, earn money and reputation, review your performance, and return to your headquarters between contracts. The demo provides an introduction to the core gameplay loop planned for Concrete Polishing Simulator’s Early Access release.
