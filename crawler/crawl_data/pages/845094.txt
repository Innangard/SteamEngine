Simulator 3 is a powerful add-on that hands you complete control over your simulation environment. Whether you want to immerse yourself in a stunning visual landscape or challenge your ATC skills with customized weather conditions, MIYO is here to elevate your Tower! Simulator 3 experience. How to Use MIYO Once you start a single-player or multiplayer session, look for the MIYO logo at the bottom left corner of the simulator screen . Click it to access all these customization options and start transforming your Tower! Simulator 3 experience. 1. Visual Customization MIYO enables you to fine-tune every aspect of the simulator’s visuals: Camera Distance: Extend Tower! Simulator 3’s default visibility from 25 km up to 50 km. Color Settings: Adjust the hues of the ground, sky, and horizon to suit your taste. Moonlight & Lighting Strength: Control the intensity of moonlight for more realistic night operations. Airport & Airplane Lights: Independently configure landing/taxi lights and navigational lights for aircraft, along with airport runway and taxiway lighting, to create the perfect ambiance. 2. Custom Weather Scenarios Forge your own static weather conditions to match real-world ops or test your skills under unique conditions: Wind Speed & Direction: Model everything from calm breezes to gusty crosswinds. Cloud Coverage & Rain: Customize precipitation levels and cloud density for added immersion. Visual Range: Dial in visibility levels, from crystal-clear skies to challenging low-visibility approaches. MIYO transforms your Tower! Simulator 3 sessions by giving you unprecedented control over visuals and weather alike. Tweak the lighting for stunning sunrise ops, or push your controlling skills with heavy rain and lower vis. With MIYO, the sky is no longer the limit—it’s whatever you want it to be.
