About This Game Bread is not your average clicker game — it’s a carb-loaded journey of endless bread-clicking madness! The rules are simple: click on a loaf of bread. That’s it. But don’t be fooled by the simplicity! Every click is a step toward bread-clicking greatness. Will you become the ultimate Bread Master, or will your fingers cramp up in the pursuit of glory? Unlock hilarious achievements like “ First Slice ” or “ Baking Bread ” and discover the thrill of clicking your way to fame. Think you can handle the ultimate challenge of “ Timeless Bread Clicker ”? 100,000,000 clicks stand between you and eternal bread-clicking legend status! There’s no baking involved — just pure, unadulterated bread-clicking action. Ready to click your way to the top? Grab your loaf and let the clicking begin!
