View Community Hub About This Game In a world overrun by monsters, the Kaiju Cracking Corporation emerges. As the CEO, you will lead a professional team to process monster corpses, obtain valuable resources, and expand your business empire. In a world overrun by monsters, the Kaiju Cracking Corporation emerges. After defeating a monster, you can organize your team to professionally dismantle 7 types of kaiju across 5 main story scenes and 20 free scenarios. Develop efficient strategies to maximize the acquisition of rare materials and lay a solid resource foundation for your company's operations. The game offers an in-depth mecha R&D system, allowing you to freely customize your own combat robot. Choose from heads (17 types), arms (17 types), torsos (19 types), and legs (15 types) to assemble your mecha, and configure head skills (4 types), arm skills (10 types), and leg skills (4 types). Upgrade, modify, and perform post-battle maintenance in the factory to create your unique war machine. When facing more dangerous monsters, you can deploy your mecha to engage in epic battles across the city. The game features 20 destructible combat scenes, recreating intense showdowns. After the fight, you must still handle battlefield cleanup and monster disposal, completing the full "Fight-Dismantle-Manage" cycle. As the CEO, you can recruit up to 24 employees to build an efficient team. Each employee possesses unique skills, and smart allocation will boost operational efficiency. Additionally, engage in nighttime social interactions at the bar to deepen relationships, manage emotions, and enhance team trust and cohesion. Buy and sell materials obtained from dismantling in a dynamic market. The market offers material futures/contract trading, with prices fluctuating in real time based on in-game events and news, testing your investment insight. You can even influence global material prices through the popularity of monster cuisine in the restaurant, achieving cross-system economic manipulation. Turn monster remains into specimens, build themed exhibition halls, and plan visitor routes to earn ticket revenue. Use monster materials to develop signature dishes, enhance quality through mini-games, and let the popularity of your cuisine influence global material prices. Interact with staff at the bar at night, manage their emotions, and build trust. Three interconnected industries support each other as you construct your monster business empire.
