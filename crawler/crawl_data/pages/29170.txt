Absorb, Evolve, and Reign Supreme There's no such thing as a useless unit on this battlefield. Weaker units are sacrificed to feed the strong, becoming their flesh and blood. Watch an ordinary 1 star Warrior ascend into a stronger unit. Your branching class choices will completely shift the tide of battle. Exploit system loopholes, bend RNG to your will, and become the master architect of your own victory.
