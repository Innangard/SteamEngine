View Community Hub About This Game Cosmic Odds is a roguelike deck builder, that blends board game exploration with slot machine combat. Gamble the lives and sanity of your crew to reach the summit of a mountain full of horrors. CHOOSE YOUR PATH Sure thing or gamble? Safe path or risky one? The mountain keeps raising the stakes, and it gets stranger the higher you climb. SPIN TO FIGHT Every attack, heal, and buff is a spin. Stack your slots with new symbols and power-ups, and hunt for combos that bend the odds your favor. YOUR CREW PAYS The climb drains the health and sanity of the expedition, despite your best efforts, sacrifices will have to be made. FEATURES Juicy slot machine action Hand drawn monochrome art style with a haunting soundscape Fear and Awe of the unknown Board Game aesthetic, with figurines and physical pieces A mountain that changes every run Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Stylized violence and psychological horror. Characters suffer stress, madness, and afflictions that can include self-destructive behavior. Blood and grim injury are stylized, not realistic gore. No sexual content, sexual assault.
