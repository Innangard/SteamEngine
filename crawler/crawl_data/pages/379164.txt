Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We plan to work on the project, develop it and fill it with content. At the moment the story of the main character looks unfinished, the player will still have questions after the passage, which we plan to close in updates. We also want to get feedback from players already now to make the project as comfortable and interesting as possible.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Presumably 1 - 1.5 years.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The planned full version will complete the story of Arina and flesh out the world of Heart of Altai as much as possible. We plan to add a new location, new quests and puzzles to the full version. We also plan to add interaction with animals in our universe. Regarding localization - we plan to add translation into 1 more language - Altai.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Early Access features 4 hours of gameplay and 4 locations. The game ends on an important twist that should make players expect the continuation of the project's development. All the basic necessary mechanics are implemented: movement, dialogs, photography, collecting, quests, controlling two playable characters. The game is translated into 8 languages and voiced in 2 languages.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to expand the project with content, add the next locations and story chapters, and along with global updates, increase the value of the project.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We actively maintain communities: vk, telegram, tik tok, youtube and others. A renowned travel blogger Arina Demidova comes to the Altai Republic to find a lost city based on the notes of her father who was an archaeologist. Her challenging journey through breathtaking mountain landscapes is full of dangers and adventures, but her friends and the nature of this amazing place itself will come to help her. An adventure quest dedicated to the culture, myths and everyday life of the indigenous peoples of the Altai Republic. Now everyone has the opportunity to immerse themselves in the culture of this mysterious region, visiting sacred sites and participating in ancient rituals without leaving home. Immersion in the unique culture and traditions of the Altai region; Exciting gameplay that combines hidden object hunting, puzzle solving, mini-games and exploration of the surrounding world; The project is being developed with the participation of scientific consultants from the Altai Republic Ministry of Tourism and the renowned Altai musician Dobrynya Satin, who provided the appearance and voice of one of the game characters; Interaction with two game characters with different objectives; An intriguing plot and an atmospheric virtual tourism simulation.
