Progress through skill trees and unlock new rooms to expand your options in every run Forge your fate The Lords have corrupted the Gold of the Rhine to craft relics of unmatched power… and eternal curses. In Deified, you play as Sator, determined to confront these legendary figures, learn from their failures, and forge a new future. Try to defeat the game’s 7 bosses and return the Gold of the Rhine to your people. A relic-focused tactical roguelite Deified is a turn-based game where slot management and relic restoration are at the heart of the experience. Restore your artifacts by assembling multiple identical fragments, unlock new abilities, and strengthen your synergies by collecting relics of the same type. Relics need slots to be used so choose them carefully: each one grants a unique bonus but also a drawback that shapes your playstyle. Progress through skill trees to refine your strategies, and unlock new rooms to give you more options in every run. And other surprises Each Lord has their own story and their own reasons for falling. Discover a dark, morally complex world… where everything that glitters is cursed.
