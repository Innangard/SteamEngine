You're the driver of an almost real steam train, and it has exactly one setting: faster. React to quick time events to dodge whatever the track throws at you - every second you survive, the speed climbs, until you're running on pure instinct and one missed key ends it all. Runs take seconds to start and a single mistake to end. Crash, curse, restart, get a little further. Your keyboard may not survive. Your high score might.
