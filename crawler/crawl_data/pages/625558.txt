About This Content On a rainy night two days ago, a terrible car accident happened on a winding road. The driver Dr. Lucrezia Borgia who was the senior researcher of Titan died on the spot as the car fell off the cliff and exploded. But the fellow Benjamin Engle disappeared with the important documents that Dr. Lucrezia carried. After investigating the scene of the accident, the police believed that the missing Benjamin was suspected of committing this crime and issued an order of arrest to all sectors of society. In fact, Benjamin was also the victim of this accident. Since his injury was not very serious, he followed Dr. Lucrezia’s last words to take the important documents to the safe house prepared by Doctor in advance. When he turned on the only computer in the room, someone unexpectedly contacted him with an Internet call: The caller claimed to be an AI created by Doctor, and said that the car accident was not an accident actually… After hanging up the calling, Benjamin temporarily forgot the physical and mental suffering and the grief of losing his closest person, and began to investigate the truth of Dr. Lucrezia’s death.
