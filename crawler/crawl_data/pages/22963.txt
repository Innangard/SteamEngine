Какво имат да кажат разработчиците: Защо „Ранен достъп“? „Prior to the official release, our primary objective is to gather as much player feedback and insight as possible so that we can deliver a level of quality and polish that meets player expectations.“ Приблизително колко дълго тази игра ще бъде в „Ранен достъп“? „We currently plan to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 months. During this period, we will actively incorporate community feedback before transitioning to the official release.“ Как ще се различава планираната пълна версия от тази в „Ранен достъп“? „For the official launch, we are considering introducing major content updates, including a Clan System, new game modes, and more. However, since the purpose of Early Access is to validate player feedback and iterate on the game, these plans remain subject to change. While the core gameplay is somehow ready, we believe additional content is needed before the official launch. During Early Access, we will expand the game's content while continuously improving weapon balance and overall gameplay based on player feedback and gameplay data. “ Какво е текущото състояние на версията в „Ранен достъп“? „In summary, the current build is stable and ready for an Early Access launch. Following our participation in Steam Next Fest last October and our recent Closed Beta Test (CBT), we believe the game has reached the quality level required for Early Access. Currently, players can enjoy two Casual game modes: Team Deathmatch (2 maps) and Bomb Defusal (4 maps). The game also features a Weapon Parts Customization System, allowing players to unlock weapon parts through account and weapon progression and customize weapons to match their preferred playstyle. The game also includes a player-to-player Marketplace, allowing players to freely trade eligible in-game items. This system is designed to support a player-driven economy and provide additional long-term engagement.“ Ще има ли разлика в цената на играта по време на „Ранен достъп“ и след това? „No. We intend to continue operating the game as a free-to-play (F2P) title even after Early Access. We do not currently have any plans to change the pricing of our in-game currency after the official release. However, if there are significant changes in exchange rates, we may review our regional pricing. If any adjustments are made, we will notify our players in advance.“ Как планирате да ангажирате общността в процеса Ви на разработка? „We are already actively monitoring player feedback through our official Discord community. As we did during our previous CBT and Steam Next Fest, we will also continue gathering player feedback through Steam Community and discussion forums. Community feedback has already had a direct impact on the game’s development. Based on feedback from previous CBTs and Steam Next Fest, we increased the maximum frame rate and reduced the time required to aim down the scope when using sniper rifles, improving the overall responsiveness and feel of sniper gameplay. Throughout Early Access, we will continue reviewing player feedback alongside gameplay data and player behavior. Based on community responses, we plan to continuously adjust weapon balance and overall game balance, while also prioritizing bug fixes, performance improvements, matchmaking, UI and UX improvements, and other quality-of-life changes. We are also considering adding new game modes based on the types of gameplay and content that players request during Early Access. Community feedback will therefore help us determine which features, modes, and improvements should be prioritized in future updates. Don't forget to explore the Black Market to trade items that you earned through gameplay and collect the items you need for your arsenal. Tailor your weapons to match your style and team up with friends to secure victory! Fast-Paced Combat Nowadays, FPS games can often be overwhelming and time-consuming. We understand that not everyone can indulge in all-day gaming or wander around in an unnecessarily large map only to be eliminated in seconds. Sudden Attack Zero Point offers quick, action-packed battles, allowing you to enjoy intense gameplay and fully embrace the thrill of combat without unnecessary delays.
