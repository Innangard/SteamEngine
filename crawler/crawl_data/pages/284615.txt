You have money. You have a beautiful home. You have a family. And you're dying. Debt Of Life is a short narrative game about a wealthy man facing the final weeks of his life. Every week, an expensive treatment keeps you alive. Every day, you must decide how far you're willing to go to afford it. Sell the things you own. Give up something your family cares about. Accept help from people you don't trust. Make choices you may never be able to take back. Your decisions shape your relationships, your home, and the story itself. What begins as a struggle to pay for treatment can slowly turn into something much more personal: deciding what matters enough to keep, and what you're willing to sacrifice. And then there is The Visitor . He always has an offer. Each day brings new events, conversations, opportunities, and difficult choices. Some decisions may help you survive another week. Others may change the way your family sees you — and the person you become. There is no perfect choice. Only the choices you make, and the things you're willing to sacrifice for another week of life. Your fortune is finite. Your time is limited. What are you willing to lose to buy one more week? Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Debt of Life contains mature themes including terminal illness, death, financial hardship, moral dilemmas, emotional distress, and difficult family situations. Players are regularly faced with choices that may negatively affect other characters and their relationships. The game does not contain explicit sexual content, graphic violence, or gore.
