View Community Hub About This Game Spring Tales is a cozy 2D puzzle adventure game. Help the charming residents of Blossomdale prepare for their spring festival by solving puzzles, overcoming challenges, and rebuilding after a storm. Experience a heartwarming story filled with friendship, hope, and community spirit. Spring Tales welcomes you to Blossomdale, a cozy town where the community is coming together to prepare for a spring festival after a devastating storm. You’ll follow the lives of five unique characters as they navigate the challenges of recovery and festival preparation. You’ll help them solve puzzles and discover their stories along the way. Spring Tales offers a variety of puzzles that challenge your skills in stacking, sorting, and mixing different elements. From fixing electrical circuits to organizing objects in the right order, each puzzle provides a new, creative challenge that keeps the gameplay fresh and exciting, with endless combinations to discover. Features: Follow the lives of five characters, each with their own story and role in the festival. Enjoy the hand-drawn visuals that bring the town and its people to life. Solve puzzles that are woven into the story and help the characters on their journey. Take a break and immerse yourself in the calming, cozy world of Blossomdale. Experience the warmth of a community coming together to rebuild, heal, and celebrate after a storm.
