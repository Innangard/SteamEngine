About the Game Scam Line is a multiplayer social game for 4 to 8 players where you compete in psychological and social challenges. All players are placed in separate rooms. Communication is limited. Information is power. In Early Access, the game features 22 social challenges, including: • Learn your neighbours’ room numbers before anyone else • Identify who is dressed differently • Play the classic Prisoner’s Dilemma • And many more… Every challenge tests how well you can read people — and how well you can lie to them. Play Your Way There are no right choices. Only consequences: • Form alliances • Make deals • Share information Or choose a darker path: • Bluff • Lie • Manipulate In the end, only one of you will win.
