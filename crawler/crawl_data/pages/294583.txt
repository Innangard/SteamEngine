View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game The fast-paced twin-stick shooter game in the grim post-apocalyptic Renaissance setting. It had been more than a hundred years since the terrible plague devoured the whole continent and turned almost everyone there in the maddened mutants. Only a few survivors managed to find refuge behind the huge walls of the fortress-cities. Although you are one of them, your luck has run out as you got infected while foraging outside the city walls. Now your only hope is to fight through the hordes of mutants and chimeras and to find the cure! Engaging Storyline: Immerse yourself in a gripping narrative that unfolds in the dark and mysterious world of Remedium. Unravel the secrets of a haunting past as you navigate through atmospheric environments and encounter horrific creatures of a terrible Plague along your journey. Dynamic Isometric Action: Experience the adrenaline-pumping action of Remedium's twin-stick shooter gameplay, where responsive controls facilitate dynamic, smooth, and intuitive combat. Engage in thrilling battles against post-apocalyptic mutants, utilizing precision gunplay and alchemical abilities to deftly move, dodge, and annihilate hordes of enemies. Upgrade and Customize Your Arsenal: Acquire a diverse array of powerful weapons and equipment throughout your journey and unleash your creativity in customizing them to suit your preferred playstyle. Utilize alchemical consumables at the workbench to install different elemental attacks, allowing you to personalize your dash, armory, and healing abilities for unique tactical advantages. Discover rare resources and craft powerful upgrades for your weapons and equipment to become a formidable force against the darkness that lurks in Remedium. Challenging Boss Battles: Test your skills and strategic thinking by confronting powerful and terrifying boss enemies. Each boss presents a unique and formidable challenge, requiring precise timing, smart tactics, and the effective utilization of your abilities to emerge victorious and progress in the game. Plagues and Cure System: Brace yourself for the randomness of Plagues, which can afflict your character during the gameplay. Seek out cures, hidden throughout levels, to counter the debuffs inflicted by Plagues and even turn them to your advantage. Be prepared for a variety of effects, from reduced accuracy to uncontrollable firing, adding an extra layer of challenge and unpredictability to your journey. Haunting Atmosphere: Immerse yourself in the eerie and atmospheric world of Remedium, where every corner holds secrets and danger. Explore intricately designed environments, from decaying cemeteries and crypts to alchemical laboratories and ruins of abandoned cities, while the haunting soundtrack intensifies the sense of suspense and draws you deeper into the game's captivating atmosphere. Stunning Visuals and Sound Design: Immerse yourself in Remedium's visually stunning and meticulously crafted world. Experience detailed character and enemy models, atmospheric lighting, and visual effects that bring the dark atmosphere to life. The haunting soundtrack and immersive sound design enhance every moment of your journey, intensifying the sense of suspense. Soon you will know what lurks in the darkness... very soon...
