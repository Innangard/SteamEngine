Become a crazy clown and hunt down your prey, or play as a toy and try to survive the madness! SURVIVE THE MADNESS, STAY ALIVE! As a toy caught in the middle of crazy clowns, hide and try to stay alive without being caught. Increase your chances of survival with special abilities! Use your special abilities to trap them! Trap : Place a trap on the ground and wait for them to get caught! Sense : Feel nearby enemies and locate them! Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: "Toy Scrappers" is a thrilling chase-based horror game where some players takes on the role of a killer attempting to capture other players. The game includes: Horror Elements: Jump scares, dark environments, and unexpected events designed to create a tense atmosphere. Violence: The killer performs attack animations on toy characters. These animations involve mild violence but do not include blood or excessive gore. Psychological Tension: Players may experience heightened tension due to the thrill of escaping or being pursued. The game does not include sexual content, sexual violence, drug use, alcohol, or self-harm. However, it may not be suitable for players sensitive to intense horror or suspenseful themes.
