Take on the role of a rookie angler, driven by the final wish of your beloved partner, as you navigate through the challenges of a town on the brink of bankruptcy. Equipped with only your journal and trusty rod, embark on a journey to uncover the secrets hidden beneath the waves of Moonglow Bay. Embark on an Aquatic Adventure Discover the Depths : Cast nets, set traps, line, and ice fish to discover over 151 aquatic species lurking beneath the surface. Preserve and Present : Cook your catch of the day or proudly show them off at the local aquarium. Explore Enigmatic Locales : Traverse the Glacier and Sulphur zones, unravel the mysteries of the Astral Clouds, and prepare for encounters with the Mythical Monsters of Moonglow. Share the Adventure : Team up with friends in local co-op and embark on a heartwarming journey together. Revitalise the Bay Celebrate the Centennial : Help revitalise the once-thriving fishing town, breathing new life into its weathered buildings. Meet the Townsfolk : Befriend the locals and accept quests through the bulletin board to restore the Bay’s fractured community. Equip for Success : Stock up on bait, tackle, and ship upgrades to prepare for the challenges ahead. Discover the Heart of Moonglow Vivid Voxel Art : Immerse yourself in Moonglow Bay's stunning visual style complimenting your adventure every step of the way. Sumptuous Soundtrack : Lose yourself in Lena Raine's enchanting score that seamlessly flows with the rhythm of your journey. Explore the Picture Perfect Update Photomode Delight : Capture the beauty of Moonglow Bay from every angle with our new photomode feature. Customise Your Haven : Personalise your storefront with new decorations that reflect your unique style and personality. Unearth Ancient Mysteries : Join Marina on a thrilling quest to uncover the skeleton of a long-lost sea creature. Piece together its bones, decode its legend, and embark on a journey that spans land and sea in search of elusive fossils.
