Take the elevator to floor 6. But this is no ordinary elevator... Who knows where you'll end up. A hotel, dungeon, hedge maze, a train? You must travel from floor 1 to 6, working out if each floor is valid or not. If a floor is not valid, you must press the emergency button and don't continue up, or you have to start all over again! How do you know if a floor is valid? Each floor has a numerical clue to indicate what number it is. If this doesn't match the number on the elevator, the floor in not valid. These clues could be a written number on the wall or the number of objects in a pile, but they are often not so simple. Try and try again to reach floor 6, exploring and learning about each floors clues, in order to get the perfect run! Each run is random, with 24 possible floors to arrive at. A marathon mode is included, as the ultimate test, making you go through all 24 possible floors to reach the end. Can you tackle the Elevator Warp?
