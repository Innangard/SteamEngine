With an ability for every situation, you’ll find yourself gliding through levels while weaving in deadly attacks against the vermin. Alternate mech legs unlock the ability to slide across the ground to build up incredible momentum or to hover in mid-air as you rain down destruction. Use the gatling-style heavy fire of the Despot as you approach your foes, until you’re close enough to unleash the terrifying power of its bouncing slugshots to tear through your enemies. Maybe you prefer the rapid bursts of flechette projectiles from the Orchid and its full-auto galvanic heavy fire. Or perhaps all you need is a single well-timed shot from the all-powerful Railslayer to pierce through your enemies Call down orbital strikes, emit static discharge, or spread withering rot with every shot. Grow your strength with classic rogue-like upgrades that affect everything from your movement to your shoulder cannon. Whether you prefer to bolster your defenses, accelerate your movement or amplify your weaponry, there is a path for you. You’re not the only one that’s well-equipped. Whether it’s the leaping Apostate’s razor sharp scythes or the blazing projectiles of the Acolyte, each enemy brings its own deadly weaponry. Anticipate the overpowering barrage of the Carrion Prowler, evade the sweeping lasers of the Asterophore, and focus down the elusive Mnemosynth. Only the truly skilled will reach the sacred Terminus and face off against the vile Hierophant. Finding your favored arms is just the first step on the journey to mastering Marrow Marrow. Each run will bring you more and more in tune with the movement and allow you to traverse more freely and nimbly than you ever thought possible. When gliding through the levels becomes second nature, you become an unstoppable force of vermin annihilation. Music by Karl Casey @ White Bat Audio.
