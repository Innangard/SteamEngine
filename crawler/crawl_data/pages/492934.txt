You're all alone with hundreds of dusty offices to sweep… or so you’ve been told. Strange sounds begin to emanate from nearby rooms, dust bunnies spring to life, and suddenly you find yourself struggling to safely sweep  and survive with your newfound employment intact. Classic Minesweeper With a Twist Clickable floor tiles can be swept clean, or conceal an angry dust bunny to avoid. Place caution signs and tread carefully using classic Minesweeper strategies—but watch out! Tiles and numbers may not be as they seem as hazardous materials emerge throughout the night! 6 Characters with unique play styles and 16 ascension levels ensure you can customize your experience. Ludicrous Item Combinations Stop by a vending machine to restock your janitor cart with items to help you combat the forces lurking in TentaTeq’s maze of cubicles. Defeat bosses to earn powerful keys that grant you new abilities that upend the way you solve puzzles. Discover limitless wacky combinations as you search for the synergy you need to avoid getting fired! Nostalgic 90s-Style Pixel Art Immerse yourself in simpler technological times, with 5 characters, 7 bosses, 100+ items, and millions of tentacles rendered in a quirky pixel art style. Mysterious events reveal unsettling and insidious corporate secrets. What will you uncover at the heart of TentaTeq, Inc.’s towering monument to industry? Can you ascend the increasingly challenging floors and keep your job until morning?
