About This Content Pick up some serious pulling power from Romania! The 060-EA electric locomotive is one of the most iconic locomotives developed to serve the railway networks of Eastern Europe, particularly Romania. It emerged as a result of post-WW2 technological innovation, addressing the growing need for efficient and modern railway transportation. With its 25 kV AC power system developing a whopping 5100kW (more than 6,000 horsepower) the 060-EA can haul heavy trains and reach a speed of 160 km/h (99mph), making it suitable for fast and intercity trains. The 060-EA as recreated by developers RailForge for Train Simulator Classic is a highly detailed tribute to this historic powerhouse, with realistically recreated in-cab signalling and safety systems and numerous controls for lighting, systems operation and more. It also includes scenarios for the Konstanz – Villingen route to let you put this powerful machine through its paces. If you need realism with pulling power, look no further! Please Note: Konstanz-Villingen Route Add-On is required, as a separate purchase, in order to play the scenarios featured in this add-on.
