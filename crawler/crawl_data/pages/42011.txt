Content For This Game Browse all (1) New Fluffy Kitchen Saga - Supporter Pack $1.99 $1.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub Join Our Discord About This Game In this very special diner, you'll have ultra-cozy, fluffy customers as your costumers, including cats, dogs, rabbits, and more! They will arrive at your restaurant full of anticipation, and all you need to do is treat them to your delicious cuisine. Interaction with customers is even more fun—try giving them a gentle poke and seeing what adorable reaction you get! The diner will always reside at the bottom of your PC screen, and these fluffy companions will stay with you constantly, whether you're working, studying, or handling other business. The game features 50+ rich and diverse gourmet dishes , covering everything from global cuisines to exquisite desserts and fragrant milk tea. Serve customers and satisfy their demanding palates to earn coins, which you can use to upgrade dishes and unlock even more recipes. As your restaurant's reputation grows, an increasing number of fluffy new costumer will come seeking out your culinary expertise! In addition to running your restaurant, the game offers various relaxing activities to keep you immersed. Unlock multiple themed scenes and matching decor like tables, chairs, and dessert stands to create a truly unique restaurant space . Check your luck by drawing a daily fortune, listen to music on the Jukebox , and experience the simple joys of the fluffballs' daily lives. Mini-games like Blackjack, Food Combination, and more await your challenge, ensuring you can enjoy some lighthearted fun when you're not busy managing the business. P.S. If you love cozy and simulation management games, this is the perfect title for you. (No Fluffballs No Life!)
