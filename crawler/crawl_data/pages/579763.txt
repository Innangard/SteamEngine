For generations, nothing ever disturbed its quiet nights, Until today... A young man named Cahyadi vanished out of thin air without a trace, This incident puts everyone on edge. As Toro, a newly appointed member of the Ronda Malam (neighborhood night patrol), you'll spend five unsettling nights keeping watch over a small Indonesian village slowly consumed by fear. The police have begun their investigation, rumors spread with every passing night, and everyone seems desperate for answers. Someone knows the truth. The question is... Who? The village never truly sleeps. Walk through narrow alleys, quiet streets, rice fields, and the old cemetery as you carry out your nightly patrol. Interact with villagers whose attitudes gradually change as the investigation unfolds. Your patrol doesn't begin on the village streets, It begins on the village border. The night always begins at the village border. Before you patrol the streets, you'll inspect every vehicle seeking entry into the village. Each has a reason to pass through, and a story to tell... but not every story feels truthful. A routine checkpoint slowly turns into another place where doubt, suspicion, and paranoia begin to take hold. Every patrol uncovers another piece of the puzzle, every villager offers a different perspective, and every night chips away at the line between reality and paranoia. As the village searches for answers, you'll find yourself questioning everything you've seen, everyone you've spoken to, and even your own memories. Somewhere beneath the silence lies the truth, and by the end of the fifth night, you'll have no choice but to confront it...
