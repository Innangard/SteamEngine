Content For This Game Browse all (2) Cash is King! - Supporter Pack N/A Cash is King! - The Official Book N/A View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Miami Beach . Sun, sand, and money are in the air. You start with empty pockets and work your way up in this laid-back first-person simulation. Collect cash, improve your reputation, and enjoy fun, slightly chaotic gameplay with hustles and amusing minigames. In the end, only one thing matters: Cash is King and you want the crown. Build your own street empire You buy and place vending machines, sell lemonade on the beach, and invest your earnings to steadily expand your own little empire. Casual gameplay with a feel-good vibe With hard work, a bit of luck, and a pinch of chaos, you fill your cash register, level up your business, and enjoy that good feeling when your balance grows faster than your sunburn. Improve your reputation & keep the neighborhood clean Your rise depends on your reputation. Keep the neighborhood clean, remove graffiti, and help people stay cool under the sun. The more popular you become, the more opportunities open up for you. Fun minigames & charming chaos Water gun, broom, or newspaper stand: every minigame brings variety, cash, or popularity, plus that wonderful “Just one quick thing …” effect that keeps you playing longer than you planned. Unique characters & quirky jobs Colorful personalities like Siggi Sunshine, Rico Cartón, Selma Slots, and Lenny accompany you on your journey and bring you jobs, tips, and new opportunities in the streets of the Sunshine State. Furnish your apartment Earn enough cash for your own apartment, shop for furniture at Sofa King, and make your home just the way you like it. Your wealth Store your earnings safely in the safe, plan new investments, and keep an eye on every dollar as you rise from a small-time beach hustler to a local mini mogul. Now go restock the newspapers, grab your broom and water gun, head out into the streets, leave your mark on this city, and never forget: Cash is King .
