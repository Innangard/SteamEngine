Go viral or get stranded in the Uncanny Valley! Stream Train is a first-person co-op horror game where your livestream is your lifeline. Up to 6 players can get together to film cryptids, take on dangerous dares and prank their friends to keep a reactive chat entertained. Their donations pay for the fuel and gear you need to keep your train moving deeper into the valley. Keep Chat Entertained Your audience reacts to whatever happens on camera, makes requests, and pushes your crew to take bigger risks. Taunt cryptids, investigate derelict buildings, pull reckless stunts. The better the livestream, the more donations you earn. See What's Waiting in the Uncanny Valley Leave the safety of the visitor’s centre and follow the railway deeper into the Uncanny Valley. Explore abandoned locations, hunt for useful gear, and come face-to-face with cryptids, ghosts, and other strange creatures along the way. Not everything you meet will attack on sight, but if you decide to make something angry, make sure the camera is rolling. The chat will love it. Keep the Train Moving Put the donations to work. Stock up on fuel and gear, then get your steam train moving toward the next stop. Every station brings you closer to escaping the Uncanny Valley, but the farther you travel, the more dangerous the journey becomes. Linger too long and you might attract something you really don’t want to meet. Chat loves a close call, just make sure you’re back on the train before it becomes your last one! Every Run Brings New Encounters No two trips through the Uncanny Valley play out quite the same. Each run reshuffles the points of interest along the railway, changing the locations, creatures, items, and encounters your crew will face on the journey. Some parts of the route may feel familiar, but you never know exactly what’s waiting at the next stop. Make it to the end of the valley and escape with your crew. If everyone goes down, the run is over, and it’s back to the visitor’s center to try again.
