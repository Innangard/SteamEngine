Playing the main game first is recommended, but optional. We are now in 1964. Sophie has taken a new posting aboard the Orpheus , a transatlantic ship , a few years after the events at the Clarington Hotel. What was meant to be a fresh start is disrupted when colleagues and passengers begin reporting missing personal belongings … Set entirely indoors within a small section of the ship, Lost & Found builds on the investigative gameplay of This Bed We Made through an intimate , self-contained narrative experience. Everyday tasks once again become part of the investigation, bringing back more of the cleaning and snooping players wanted more of!
