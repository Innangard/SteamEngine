The goal is to escape by any means nescessary. Santa’s Shadow: a tale of grief, obsession, and madness. After the tragic death of his son on Christmas three years ago, a once-devoted husband begins to spiral. As Christmas grew closer, his behavior grew more erratic. His wife, desperate to understand, confronted him-and the nightmare began. It is Christmas eve and you were invited for dinner. When you arrive, things take a turn for the worse. As you explore the home, you’ll uncover cryptic notes, solve environmental puzzles, and piece together what happened. Key Features Resource Management: Make use of any and every source of light you can before it runs out. Puzzles: Search the house in order to find a way out. Atomospheric fog and Christmas/seasonal decorations. Hiding: Get away from the enemy by utilizing your surroundings.
