If that is the sort of title you want, I couldn't recommend it more!” 8.5/10 – GamerGrin Born Of Bread - Baker Edition About This Game Beings from another age are causing havoc across the land! And with their mischief comes a great threat to everyone. The unlikeliest hero, a golem born of bread, will find himself and his newfound buddies in the middle of a drama that's thousands of years in the making. Together, they will visit incredible locals and meet fascinating characters. Although he may not look like much, Loaf might be the hero this land kneads . Play as a flour golem who possesses a never-ending childlike wonder and a set of odd abilities in this 2.5D adventure game! Team Up With Buddies! Loaf can't do it alone: Meet new friends during your adventure, each with their own abilities and personality. Use their unique skills to overcome threats and obstacles whether in fights or when exploring. Together, you’ll engage with varied NPCs, take on side-quests, and find treasures! A Quirky Battle System! Find equipable weapons and upgrades by exploring and completing quests. Each weapon and buddy attack has a button prompt associated with it that you'll need to successfully execute if you want to deal maximum damage! However, make sure you have the right weapon for the job since enemies can have a resistance and a weakness. While you fight, your dragon buddy Dub, who lets you save your progression while adventuring, will stream your fights to a fake audience with his little laptop! Gather more viewers and answer their demands to get rewards!
