Modules and artifacts turn a good build into an unstoppable one. 6 CHASSIS: Interceptor — speed, combo multipliers, time slowdown Assault — balanced DPS, orbital strikes on clusters Engineer — drones, repairs, protective dome Dreadnought — armor, ramping damage at the cost of shields Vanguard — head-on assault, system overload, heavy tanking Spectre — crits, phase windows, surgical precision 8 WEAPON TYPES — from homing missiles and chain lightning to ricocheting flechettes and triple plasma volleys. 4 COMBAT DRONES — strike, repair, interceptor, radar. 30+ MODULES and ARTIFACTS across four rarity tiers. 192+ base build combinations. With modules and artifacts — thousands. No two runs play the same. FOUR RESOURCES. ZERO MARGIN FOR ERROR. You can't just hold the trigger. Every decision is a trade-off: Shield — regenerates, but every hit resets the timer Hull — when the shield drops, damage goes here. No healing without a drone or shop Energy — powers your dash, railgun, EMP, and ultimate. Spent it all on dodges? Nothing left for the kill shot Heat — hold fire too long and your weapon locks up. At the worst possible moment A good pilot aims well. A great pilot knows when NOT to fire. THREE ARMIES. THREE TACTICS. THREE BOSSES. What works against one faction will fail against another. SYNTHETIC SWARM Dozens of small targets. Rammers, leapers, living bombs. Alone — nothing. In a wave — death. Boss QUEEN: bullet fans, homing missiles, escalating aggression with each phase. GUARDIAN MACHINES Beams, shields, traps, space denial. They don't chase you — they cut off every escape route. Boss PRISM: a puzzle fight. Read the patterns or die. SPACE MARAUDERS Mines, heavy armor, raw firepower. Boss CARRIER: drones, mines, and missile salvos all at once. Multitask or die. 8 elite modifiers — blink, vampiric, berserk, self-repair — make familiar enemies unpredictable every run. Use it wisely. Overkill Explosion — excess damage splashes to nearby enemies 20 combat mechanics in total. Risk more — earn more. 4 GAME MODES CAMPAIGN — 12 sectors, 4 acts, full narrative. Shops between missions, upgrade choices, and a final showdown with Archon. ENDLESS — Infinite waves with escalating difficulty. How far can you go? CONTRACTS — Fixed modifier sets, higher payouts. Specific challenge, specific reward. DAILY SEED — Same seed for every player, every day. Equal conditions. Pure skill. Each mode across 4 difficulty levels: Arcade, Standard, Veteran, Hardcore. 7 MISSION TYPES x 6 BIOMES Missions: Survive, Hold Beacon, Escort Core, Hunt Elites, Destroy Relay, Boss Assault, and special operations. Biomes: nebulae, asteroid belts, dead orbits, magnetic storms, silent sectors, gravitational anomalies. Every combination of mission and biome changes your approach. No two sectors feel the same.
