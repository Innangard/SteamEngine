Demonstração Jogar Chad Billionaire Demo Instalar demonstração Confira o jogo completo Ver Central da Comunidade Sobre esta demonstração Demo VS Full Game The demo will let you get started. You can grow your net worth from $0 up to the first million. This gives you access to the job market, real estate, entrepreneurship and acquisition systems. From there you can continue your journey to a billion dollars in the full version of the game where you will earn your access to the Stock Market, Crypto Launchpad and Mega Projects. Completion records, trust fund and bespoke game modes are exclusive features for the full version. Achievements earned during the demo are automatically unlocked in the full version and your save from the demo carries over in to the full game. They say getting rich is all about the journey. Your journey starts with a job, where you spend your time working hard, getting paid less than you're worth, just to make someone else rich. Net worth determines your step on the ladder. Chad Millionaire takes you to your first million. Chad Billionaire takes you the rest of the way. Job Market: It ain’t much, but it’s honest work. Start by trading your time for cash. Then invest your earnings to escape the wage cage. New jobs are posted daily at 7am. Real Estate: Flip and rent your way to riches. Time the market for quick gains, or buy and hold to collect rent. Property values follow market sentiment while rent provides a steady passive income. Prices adjust and rents pay out daily at 11am. Entrepreneurship (unlocks at $50K net worth): Build your business empire from scratch. Hire employees, choose upgrades then scale each venture to maximize a real time passive income drip. Acquisitions (unlocks at $500K net worth): Buy already profitable and partially upgraded businesses. Negotiate good deals into great ones. Keep your eyes open and your wallet ready for exclusive business ventures only obtainable through acquisitions. New offers appear every five days. Your journey to a million is documented. Visualize your wealth growing, track where your money went, and see which parts of your empire are actually pulling their weight. The Vault : Watch the vault fill as your net worth climbs its way to a billion dollars. Each stack represents $10K, each layer is $1M, and each pillar is $50M. Statistics: A breakdown of your cash, net worth, and the performance of each money making opportunity detailing your investment into it, and the return out of it. Cash History : Review your most recent cash transactions and see exactly where your money came and went. Time, economy, and game modes. One game day lasts 60 seconds in real life, creating a constant flow of opportunities, market changes, income and decisions. Optional offline progression can also keep parts of your empire earning while the game is closed. Every playthrough uses a seeded economy. You can enter your own seed when starting a new game or share one with other players so they can experience the same economic conditions. Difficulty determines how strongly those conditions affect the economy. The demo plays the Self-Made experience, starting from $0, on any difficulty.
