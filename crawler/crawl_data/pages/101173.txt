Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game PenRogue is a hand-drawn 2D turn-based creature-collecting roguelite. Catch wild Pens, build your team, get past Trainers and Leaders, and survive to the end of wave 100. A party of three. Fallen Pens never come back. Each of the 27 Pens has an ultimate of its own. Move uses are limited. Three type matchups. Element (Fire / Water / Grass), Class (Physical / Mental / Normal), Alignment (Order / Chaos / Neutral). Three axes of matchups decide every battle. Nine regions, nine Leaders, and a rival. Choose the next region at every fork and face a Leader every ten waves. Your rival Mei stands in your way to the very end.
