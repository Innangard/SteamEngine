Communityhub anzeigen Über dieses Produkt Needle in a Haystack – Original Soundtrack This is the music from Needle in a Haystack , the game where you and your friends dig through a giant pile of hay looking for one tiny needle. Most of these tracks play on the crew radio while you work, so we wanted them to feel like something you'd actually leave on in the background: a bit of country, a bit of western, and some upbeat stuff for when things start getting hectic. Then there are the tracks for the moments that go wrong, like when a tornado shows up or someone calls in an airstrike.
