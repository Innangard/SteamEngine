View Community Hub About This Game Mineral Defense is a unique blend of tower defense and roguelite gameplay, where the strategic possibilities are endless! Shape the battlefield by unlocking tiles and crafting the ultimate path. Plan intersections and bottlenecks to funnel enemies in the most efficient way. Fight Unique Enemies! Use combinations of towers and abilities to defeat enemies! There are many damaging and support towers at your disposal. Slow enemies, reduce their resistance, melt them with acid, then explode them with missiles! That is only one of many ways to strategize the most effective survival. The possibilities depend on you! Unlock Powerful Upgrades! Draw cards after rounds to unlock powerful upgrades, activate new structures, and improve your chance of victory. 100+ in game rewards, many more to come! Research New technologies! Use collected research points to unlock new technology. Discover the potential of powerful structures, unleash devastating abilities, invest in risky catalysts, and improve your arsenal with upgrades. 184 unique research entries, more added every week! Save Anytime! Mineral Defense offers save functionality, letting you pick up where you left off with ease. The game will automatically save your progress at the end of every wave! Adjustable Game Speed! We understand that not everyone has hours every day to game. Mineral Defense has 4 adjustable game speeds you can use to play the game at the speed that works best for you. Join The Community! The Mineral Defense Discord server is an active community where you can share your thoughts, give suggestions or report bugs, or just hang out and chill. Join us via the link in the info tab!
