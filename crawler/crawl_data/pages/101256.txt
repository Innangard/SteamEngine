View Community Hub About This Game No one ever told you, ”One day you'll run a massage parlor!" And no one ever does... Good Job is an unofficial night-job simulation. Sam is in jail. He left the keys with you. Now you’re in charge of a job you could never put on your resume . Start in a run-down room, manage your team, satisfy demanding customers, sell controversial products online, collect payments, and grow your underground empire without drawing attention.. What you'll do: Build a network of underground massage agencies 🙂. Produce and sell herbal supplements. Manage massage therapists, handle appointments, and oversee deliveries. As a side hustle, build an e-commerce empire for adults . Collect payments. By any means necessary.... Eat, sleep, survive… This city never stops! Get ready for one of the toughest simulations you’ll ever play. In this game, your customers have very specific needs! Build your team carefully, manage them well, and keep your customers happy! Build your herbal supplement business from the ground up. Produce your products, stock your shelves, and sell online to customers with very specific needs. Every pill matters. Every order is valuable. Every delivery is an opportunity. Completely legal, of course. 😇 A tough journey awaits you! There’s so much you need to catch up on! Survival is the most important rule! Eat, sleep, and be sure to earn money! Discover the perfect blend. The right formula keeps your customers happy, grows your business, and builds your reputation. The wrong choice, however, can throw everything off balance. Chemistry is hard. The rest of the business is even harder. Mix carefully. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains implied adult themes presented in a comedic context. There is no explicit sexual content, nudity, or graphic violence. The game includes suggestive humor, mild cartoon violence (slapstick), and references to illegal activities portrayed in an exaggerated, non-realistic manner. All characters are adults. Drug references are limited to fictional herbal supplements used in a business simulation context. The game includes an in-game e-commerce system where players can sell adult novelty products and toys. All products are depicted in a stylized, low-poly, cartoon art style with no explicit imagery or descriptions.
