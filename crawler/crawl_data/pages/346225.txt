View Community Hub About This Game Two Vehicles. One Chain. Zero room for mistake. Chained Wheels is an online co-op driving adventure that challenges two players to control two cars that are permanently chained together. Your mission: survive and traverse a series of harsh off-road biomes, each one designed to push teamwork, coordination, and driving skill to the limit. If one player makes a mistake, both players pay the price. FIND A WAY BACK You and your friend, who love off-road driving, go a little too far and get trapped in impossible terrain. The only solution is to chain your cars together and work your way out. EXPECT THE UNEXPECTED Different biomes and changing weather conditions can completely alter how your vehicles behave. You need to adapt quickly and be ready when things don't go as planned. IT'S NOT JUST DRIVING Every area is filled with obstacles and physics-based puzzles that test coordination and timing. Use your chain to figure out how to get both cars through in one piece.
