View Community Hub Special Offer About This Game Welcome back to Route 66. This story starts a little earlier. Pour beer. Break up brawls. Run pig races. Distill your own liquor. Make deals out back. And when the cops show up, act natural. Roadhouse Simulator is a standalone prelude to Gas Station Simulator , following Uncle as he takes charge of a rundown roadside bar with more problems than customers — and turns it into the rowdiest stop on the highway. You start at the bottom…keeping the peace, watching the door, and dealing with rival gang members and drunks who can turn a quiet night into a brawl in seconds. Step in when you have to. Then build your way up with new stations, staff, and attractions until the whole Road House runs on your terms. Different brews can affect your customers in different ways, and serving the wrong person at the wrong time can get messy fast. Distill your own liquor, keep the beer flowing, the shelves stocked, and the staff paid. And keep some spare furniture around. Fire up the slots. Run pig races. Bring in the Hype Girls. Open the Fight Club…just don’t talk about that one. The more Hype you build, the faster the progression. But be careful - more people means more money, but also more chaos. You can't do everything yourself forever. Hire bartenders, servers, cleaners, and bouncers to handle the daily grind while you focus on growing the business or having fun with the pigs out back. Crime leaves evidence, and when you run the Road House, every mess becomes your problem. Keep things clean, earn the law's trust, pay the price when things go wrong. MAIN FEATURES From Bouncer to Boss - serve, manage, fight, and eventually run the whole Road House. Every Drink Has Consequences -alcohol comes with risks that can trigger new problems around the Road House. Distill Your Own Liquor - process, improve, and bottle your own alcohol. Work Out the Back - stash, package, and deliver questionable cargo. Draw a Crowd - pig races, gambling, entertainment, and an underground Fight Club. Build a Crew - hire bartenders, cleaners, servers, and bouncers. Deal With the Law - manage inspections, fines, and bribes.
