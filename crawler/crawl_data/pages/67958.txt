It tells a story about the fragility of existence and the search for meaning in the inevitable. NARRATIVE You find yourself in a half-remembered, dreamlike nowhere. Its inhabitants are lost in their own regrets, while fragments of their words and memories reveal something painfully familiar. Find a way to bring closure to a story that has already ended. GAMEPLAY Quietly wander through the world, solve puzzles, and converse with its strange inhabitants. Only fragments of conversations and written clues will guide you, leaving you to find your own way. FEATURES Around 3 hours for a first playthrough, 6+ hours to discover everything. Quiet gameplay: walk, read, observe, interact. Non-linear progression with multiple endings. 7 unique characters with their own stories. 10+ puzzles and mini-games.
