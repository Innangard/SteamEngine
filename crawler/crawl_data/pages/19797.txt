Every slot in your spellbook activates a different part of the card, so order matters. Create your own unique spells that a little wizard will shout for you! Combine cards strategically to adapt to every situation. Will you Bibidi Babidi Boo your foes into oblivion, Sim Sala Kadabra yourself to block and draw cards, or Higitus Figitus Presto everyone into tiny, harmless fleas? Game Features Familiar combat with plenty of surprises. Easy to learn for new players as well as fans of the genre, while breaking the mold in unpredictable ways. Mix 'n match different parts of each card to create the ideal spell to get you out of a pickle! Become the Wisest Wizard. Experiment with a myriad of cards, sigils, and characters to become the ultimate magic wielder. Rewards, events, and the shopkeeper all offer potential expansions to your build. Explore an eccentric subterranean dungeon. Entombed deep within the earth, lies the Wishing Well . It promises your heart's innermost desires, but at what cost? Journey through a gauntlet of combat rooms and unusual encounters to reach its core. Unlock content as you play. Your destiny is foretold in the Skymap! Unlock new cards and characters by spending currency you win by getting as far as possible. Handcrafted dungeon synth playlist. These underground halls reverberate with the nostalgic soundscape of 90s fantasy synthesizers. We cooked it ourselves! Will you make it to the end? Can you find out what happened to your brethren?
