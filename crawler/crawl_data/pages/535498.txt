Use the dash mechanic to quickly traverse levels and slice your enemies in half in style, combining your blade with shurikens, teleports and chains that paralyze your opponents. Overcome levels with mechanics that change how you play and face Bosses that redefine what a turn-based game is by using special abilities in real time.
