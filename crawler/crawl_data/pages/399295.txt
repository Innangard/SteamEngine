Each place hides its own secret - just don’t run out of time. Your mission is simple: find the objects you're told to find. But nothing about them is ordinary. Some are buried in darkness. Others whisper forgotten truths. Every item you collect pulls you deeper into the unknown. Each place hides a story no one was meant to hear. The objects you find unlock fragments of it -moments frozen in time, voices left behind. The more you uncover, the less sense it makes. Until it does. Each mission takes you to a different abandoned reality - broken, twisted, and barely holding together. These places weren't just forgotten. They were erased. You’re not supposed to be there.
