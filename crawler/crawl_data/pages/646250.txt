Sune Kølster is a Copenhagen based music composer, with a master degree in symphonic filmscoring, a background in hardcore punk, and an obsession with middle-eastern music - not your typical game-audio-designer. He has been working with Triband since their very first game KEYBOARD SPORTS, and was a part of the WHAT THE GOLF? team from the very beginning.
