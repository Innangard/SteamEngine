About This Game Play as Molly, a successful startup founder who helps people organize their lives by organizing their spaces. 💌 Behind every messy room is a story. In Neat! , you visit clients overwhelmed by chaos, clutter, and emotional weight. Your job is simple. Help them feel calm again. Organize, sort, and place objects with care. Get to know the people who live among them. Turn disorder into neat, comforting spaces, one object at a time. 📚 8 hand-crafted locations. 8 personal stories. Begin in a grandmother's garden, where Molly's tidying service takes its first steps. Help put a neighborhood library back together after a quiet chamber music evening somehow became a rock concert. Bring order to a VTuber's streaming room before her first live show. Some spaces hold a little more mystery. A living room its owner swears is haunted. A room belonging to someone who has everything he needs, without ever stepping outside. Others are part of everyday life. A single mom's kitchen. A Japanese konbini on the night shift. And one last space, the most personal of all, waiting for you to discover it. 🧺 A little tidying. A little understanding. Every client has a different reason for needing a hand. As you sort through their belongings, discover the personal stories told through the things they keep. Organize 8 hand-crafted locations, each with its own client and story Sort and place a wide variety of unique, interactive objects Discover the lives behind the clutter through everyday belongings Bring care and order to gardens, homes, workspaces, and more Relax with a chill, carefully curated soundtrack Steam Deck-verified and well-tested player experience Because sometimes, cleaning a room is the first step toward healing.
