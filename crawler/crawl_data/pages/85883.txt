View Community Hub About This Content Operation: Overlord DLC Storm the beaches and liberate occupied territory in the Operation: Overlord DLC for Operation: Harsh Doorstop . Relive the legendary events of D-Day , as Allied forces launch the largest amphibious invasion in history, fighting to establish a foothold in Normandy and push back the Axis war machine. From the chaos of the beach landings to fierce battles in the hedgerows, Operation: Overlord delivers an unforgettable World War II experience where teamwork and tactics are essential to victory. Key Features: 🌊 Historic D-Day Invasion: Assault the fortified beaches of Normandy in a detailed recreation of the June 6th, 1944 landings. Advance through coastal defenses, secure critical objectives, and push inland through deadly, fortified territory. 🪖 Authentic Weapons and Factions: Deploy as Allied invasion forces or Axis defenders , equipped with period-accurate gear like M1 Garands , Thompsons , MG42s , Kar98ks , and more. 🎯 Exclusive Dynamic Campaign Mode: Fight across a living frontline where your team's performance shapes the course of the invasion. Whether playing solo , in co-op , or multiplayer , the battlefield evolves with each victory and defeat as you fight to liberate—or hold—Normandy. History’s Most Iconic Invasion. The Operation: Overlord DLC brings the brutal, high-stakes combat of D-Day to Operation: Harsh Doorstop . Whether you're storming the beaches under heavy fire or mounting a desperate defense from entrenched positions, every moment is a fight for history.
