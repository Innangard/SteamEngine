Take part in historical battles, set in linear campaigns, alone or with up to three friends. Each player can control his own tank or be a part of your tank crew. Each battle is a separate mission in the campaign, with separate objectives that you need to achieve to win the battle. Plan ahead of your strategy and tactics, use infantry, artillery and air support when it is available. Minimize your losses, to be prepared properly for the final battle of the campaign. While playing the campaign missions, tanks can be damaged or lost during the battles. Try to pull as many tanks as possible back to the repair station, whether they are your own or your enemies. While the tanks are being repaired, work together to get them back into action faster. Repair damages taken during battle Replenish tank ammo (main gun and machine guns) Refuel vehicles Manage your squads. Replace the deceased or destroyed units in squads. Create new squads and assign any units from the pool automatically or manually. Play skirmish or procedural missions with up to 3 friends, use tank radio to communicate and give out orders to coordinate your squad. Play in either total control mode or multi-crew mode. Other players can join your tank and you play as a crew member rather than controlling the entire tank yourself. To be successful in battle, this mode requires good communication and coordination as a crew. Our vehicles are based on construction documents, expert opinions and insights from documentations.
