You'll have to have enough accumulated points to go through each Gates, and reach the top ! Each game is different and each levels have its own rules ! You'll have to trade your tees for items that will change the course of your run ! Play Mini Golf In ROGOLF, each shot costs you points, and each coin collected increases your multiplier. Buy Equipment To achieve higher and higher scores, you'll need to purchase power-ups that will allow you to lose as few points as possible and increase your multiplier ever higher! Reach the Top Accumulate enough points on each level to move from stage to stage, at each Gate, and reach the top!
