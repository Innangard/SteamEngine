You’ve been on patrol for multiple days when you see a fleet on the horizon. Your first instinct is to dive and engage, but it’s a clear day and one of the destroyers has spotted you. Now it’s a fight for survival, a naval game of cat and mouse. Avoid the depth charges whilst you fight back and strike when the enemy is at its most vulnerable, or switch to silent running and lose the enemy in the depths below. Silent Depth 2: Pacific Silent Depth 2: Pacific is a sim-lite submarine game, designed for fans looking for an easy-to-learn, arcade-style naval experience without requiring the use of technical manuals or hours of study to manually calculate torpedo launch vectors. Tailored to fans who fell in love with the original Silent Service series, Silent Depth 2: Pacific is a great choice to ‘get your feet wet’ as we continue to develop new features throughout Early Access. Much like the highly approachable ‘classic’ submarine games from the past, this game allows you to perform important roles on the ship whilst keeping you focused on maximizing the tonnage you send to the bottom of the ocean. Prepare to scout merchant ships, destroy enemy convoys, launch torpedos, fire your deck guns, and then dive to safety in the deep thermal layers from incoming destroyers. Key Features: Approachable ‘cat and mouse’ gameplay set in the Pacific Ocean. Encounter and evade a variety of enemy fleets & merchant ships on war patrols. Realistic water simulations for a truly immersive experience. Intuitive torpedo-based combat with auto-calculated firing solutions, keeping the action fast and furious. Day and night cycles, offering different challenges and opportunities. Varied weather conditions can impair visibility, adding an extra layer of complexity. Sink ships whilst carefully monitoring your limited resources to maximize your daily tonnage score. A layered damage system that challenges you to react and survive when different ship systems become temporarily or permanently unavailable.
