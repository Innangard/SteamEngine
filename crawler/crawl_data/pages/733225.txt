Buy World of Guns: Spec Ops Pack $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content World of Guns: Spec Ops Pack unlocks 10 detailed weapon models for World of Guns: Gun Disassembly . Explore a selection of firearms associated with special operations, close-quarters combat, suppressed fire, and tactical use. This pack includes integrally suppressed rifles, compact carbines, specialized pistols, PDWs, and shotguns — each recreated as an interactive 3D model with detailed parts and mechanisms. Included models: VSS Vintorez — a suppressed special-purpose sniper rifle AS Val — a Soviet-designed suppressed assault rifle M4 Carbine — a compact and lightweight member of the M16 rifle family Franchi SPAS-12 — a Special Purpose Automatic Shotgun FN P90 — a compact personal defense weapon KRISS Vector — a modern submachine gun designed for close-quarters combat HK USP — a semi-automatic pistol used by military and law enforcement units FN Five-seveN — a semi-automatic pistol designed around the 5.7×28mm cartridge GSh-18 — a lightweight 9mm semi-automatic service pistol Welrod — a suppressed bolt-action pistol developed in Britain during World War II Each model can be disassembled, assembled, operated, and studied in detail.
