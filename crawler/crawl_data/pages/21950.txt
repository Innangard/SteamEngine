In Elemental Witches, you command the natural forces of Fire, Wind, and Thunder , combining them freely to unleash a wide variety of elemental spells. Each spell is formed from three elemental orbs , and merging three identical elements summons a powerful Elemental Familiar to fight alongside you. Your choice of element combinations, the abilities of your familiars, and the decisions you make during combat shape entirely different builds and battle experiences. The game follows a Rogue / Roguelite core loop. After every battle, you can upgrade your elements, enhance your base attributes, unlock new items with unique mechanics, and gain meta-progression that persists between runs. Different upgrade priorities lead to dramatically different strategies: Specialize in a single element to unleash devastating spell power. Develop its matching elemental familiar to create a reliable frontline companion. Or balance multiple elements , adapting your build to shifting enemy waves and evolving battlefield challenges. Key Features： Switch characters in real time. All characters share the same HP pool , but each has unique Defense and Movement Speed . Managing who on the field and when is essential to adapting to changing battle conditions. Combine elements to unleash 10 different skills. Each elemental skill interacts differently, and various combinations create completely distinct combat styles. Roguelite in-run growth with random items. Every run brings new builds, new item synergies, and a fresh gameplay experience. Unlock pets through meta-progression. Each pet changes the flow of combat, offering unique strategic possibilities in every run. Allocate resources among the three Elements. Different upgrade priorities create different growth paths and playstyles within each battle. Regular developer updates. Continuous improvements and new content keep the game growing over time.
