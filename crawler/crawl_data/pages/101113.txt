Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Four years ago, a bioweapon leaked on Kish Island. The world closed the door and left it to drown. You go in anyway. This is a free demo of XGUN - one district of the island, and one full raid from landing to extraction. [hr][/hr] WHAT'S IN THE DEMO One district: a flooded five-star resort and the duty-free mall beside it, at night, in the rain. The complete raid loop - deploy, loot, fight, craft, extract. Search containers, bodies, and the infected themselves. Craft ammunition and medical supplies in the field. Attach optics, suppressors, lasers and grips to the weapon you're actually holding. X-Vision - a one-tap pulse scan that shows you what's around you, and where it's weak. THE ISLAND HUNTS BACK The infected are not a texture on the map. They're the reason the map is dangerous. They hunt light. Your flashlight is the most useful and most dangerous item you carry. They hear you. Sprint, or fire unsuppressed, and you announce yourself to everything within earshot. They come in kinds. Bodies that soak a magazine and keep walking. Carriers that detonate. And something larger, further in. AND IT WORKS WITHOUT OTHER PEOPLE Play the demo online with friends, entirely offline on your own, or split-screen with someone on the same couch. No servers to wait for. No lobby to sit in. This demo is PvE. There are no other players hunting you in it. [hr][/hr] WHAT YOU CARRY IN, YOU CAN LOSE Weight matters - overload yourself and you will not outrun what's chasing you. Weapons wear down and jam. Die, and the gear you brought stays on the island. Reach the boat and it's yours. [hr][/hr] If you finish it, wishlist XGUN - that's the single most useful thing you can do for a small team, and it's free.
