Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We’re committed to actively improving our game based on your feedback, with both big and small updates to make sure you get the most balanced and fun experience. We truly believe in seeing every detail of the game—from visuals to balance, items to abilities—through your eyes and making tweaks based on what you think. That’s why we decided to launch Ketz in Early Access. We need your support to make Ketz the best it can be.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to make various improvements throughout Early Access for at least a year to enhance the quality of the game. During Early Access, we will follow our roadmap to actively add content and improve the game’s mechanics. Depending on how development progresses, we might extend the Early Access period or, if we're satisfied with the state of the game, officially release it.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Ketz: We expect the full version of Stellar Sentinels to be more polished compared to the early access version with a complete story ending. We plan to expand the skill pool, add more Galactic Leaders, new items to maximize item combinations, new environment elements and bosses with different behaviors. Our plan is to release a few balance and polish patches after the Early Access release, followed by a major update.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “In the Early Access version, our Game Features; 5 Main Characters with different attack and gameplay types, 10 Galactic Leaders, each offering more than 10 abilities, 500+ In-game items and 7 types of Gems, Meet Tinked to level up items of the same rarity.(The key for unique items) 3 Side Bosses with different behaviors and 14 different enemy types, 1 Main Boss with its own army and unique behavior. 11 interactive environment monuments 10 status effects and more than 15 collectible items to add a variety of effects to your runs, + includes many small features that will enrich your gaming experience.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The Early Access price will be lower than the Full release price. the price will increase with major updates to the development pipeline.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We welcome your feedback in various places, including the Steam forums, Twitter, Discord, and our other social networking sites. We are confident that Ketz will have a much faster development process with the help and feedback of players. Choose your character, collect various gear, and create game-breaking builds using powerful skills from the Galactic Leaders. Find out why the Galactic Leaders chose to help you. Rare Gear Made Easy with Master Tinket! Use Master Tinket to upgrade your gear and reach rare items. Placing the same type of item will increase your chances of obtaining the desired equipment! Different Skill Combinations = Different Strategies Choose from 9 Galactic Leaders who have allied with you against Ketzeror, discover the skill combinations that suit you best, and craft your battle strategy! Hammathor: Lord of the Rats! Are you ready to face Hammathor, Lady Ketzeror’s first commander? The leader of the rats and the ultimate being of his kind, Hammathor crushes his enemies with cunning and brutality. Do you have what it takes to defeat him? Knowledge is Power: Strike a Deal with Orin Vaal! Orin Vaal is passionate about research. Bring him books to support his studies, and in return, claim precious gemstones. Use these mystical stones to enhance your power and upgrade your gear! Unleash Your Full Potential! Reach the maximum power of your chosen character, craft the best build with various abilities, and push your limits! Adapt your strategy, withstand relentless waves of enemies, and survive as long as you can! Lord Synthrex: The Mighty Guardian of Ketzeror! The end of the road is just the beginning… Lord Synthrex! The unyielding ally of Lady Ketzeror and the first great trial you must overcome to reach her! Can you withstand his power, or will your galactic journey end here?
