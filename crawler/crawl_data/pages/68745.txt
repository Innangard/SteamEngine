Buy Sea of Stars - Artbook $4.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content This artbook showcases the concept art created for Sea of Stars by chronicling the conversations between Thierry Boulanger, Sabotage Studio Creative Director, and Bryce Kho, Concept Artist.  Get a rare glimpse into their creative process by reading through their frank, unfiltered conversations as they workshop character designs, enemy attacks, and environmental setpieces. The digital release of this artbook will initially release with 80+ pages, featuring content from the early chapters of the game, to give enthusiasts time to play the game without encountering any spoilers that would be revealed by the artwork.  Coinciding with the eventual release of the physical edition of the book, the digital release will be updated with content from the remaining chapters of the game.
