View Community Hub Our other interesting games About This Game 100 hidden Cthulhu fish is a hidden objects/ clicker game. The game is loosely inspired by motifs from the world created by H. P. Lovecraft, decorated with hand-drawn humorous graphics. There are 100 fish hiding on each map. Your task is to find them all. There are four maps in total, which gives a total of 400 fish to find (wow!). There are many games in which we look for hidden cats, but have you ever looked for the grotesque fish that Cthulhu himself hid? - 4 levels - 400 funny fish - hand-drawn graphics - progress record controls : left mouse button - click on the fish mouse scroll wheel - zoom in/out hold down the right mouse button - map navigation System Requirements Windows SteamOS + Linux Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: 2 GHz Dual Core Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Intel HD 4000 Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Core i3-3220 Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: iGPU / GT 710 Storage: 1 GB available space Minimum: OS: Ubuntu 20.04 or higher Processor: 2 GHz Dual Core Memory: 4 GB RAM Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: OS: Ubuntu 20.04 or higher Processor: Intel Core i3-3220 Memory: 4 GB RAM Storage: 1 GB available space View all What Curators Say 20 Curators have reviewed this product.
