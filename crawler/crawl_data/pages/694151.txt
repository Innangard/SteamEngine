Use cards and keys to unlock doors, chests, and breakable treasures. Face the crypt on your own or descend with a friend. Build separate decks, inspect your teammate's hand, and plan how your strengths can work together. Hundreds of unique cards and dozens of status effects for you to explore. Discover synergies between cards and chain complex combos. Find the cards that bring your strategy together: upgrade cards selectively imbue individual cards banish cards from appearing in your run collect artifacts that reward how you play Master one of several distinct classes. Each class has their own unique deck of cards and additional mechanics. Berserker Build Fury. Unleash Overload. Turn mounting rage into devastating attacks. Build toward explosive turns and decide when to spend your Fury. Alchemist Brew your answer. Gather ingredients, create concoctions, and brew potions during combat. Turn a dangerous hand into an opportunity. Druid Fight with Sheaf & Gob. Strengthen your companions through 'Offering' and choose when to switch who stands beside you. Defeat bosses to choose class-specific 'Depth Rites'. Lean into your strongest combination—or take your build in a new direction. Adapt your strategy to face over 155 challenging enemies, most of which have one or more abilities that will force you to rethink your card-playing decisions. Protectors shield their allies. Summoners bring reinforcements. Bosses change their tactics as the battle unfolds. Read their intentions and decide which threat your deck can afford to leave standing. Rob the tombs of other fallen players in both solo and co-op. Discover the loot they carried, and decide whether to pay your respects or scavenge their remains. Branching paths lead to merchants, treasure, strange encounters, and secrets. Bargain with unsettling inhabitants, try your luck at a minigame, or accept an offer whose consequences will catch up with you later. Make pivotal choices in event encounters, experience witty and ominous creatures in your journey through the crypt, and find new ways of staying alive. No two runs ever play the same.
