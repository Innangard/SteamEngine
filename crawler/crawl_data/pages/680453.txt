Your character automatically travels through the level where you must face increasingly difficult enemies, grabbing power ups and evolving your skills along the way. Attacks and skills are automatically used, but strategy exists in choosing when to use your special ability, which can only be used manually. You must balance survivability and killing as many enemies as possible in order to become strong enough to conquer boss enemies and claim victory. There are 3 levels to play through and 3 difficulties per level. Despite its mobile game aesthetic and nature, it does not and will never have any microtransactions or ads.
