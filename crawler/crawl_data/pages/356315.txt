About the Composer Matúš Jacko(MattJack) born in march 1991 in the city of Michalovce on the eastern side of Slovakia. First hints that he aspired towards the music came when he was 3 years old. He was making drums out of pots and started imitating musicians he saw on television. His mom gave him his first keyboard on his fifth birthday. In a matter of few days, it was obvious that he can play melodies intuitively. Few years later came first attempts at forming a band with his friends. In the year 2010 he joined a Christian band, where he also contributed with lyrics and songs until 2016. From this moment he devoted himself to studio music and to musical corrections for other musicians from Slovak and Czech Republic.Until 2019 movie/game music were only his hobby, until he got in touch with a leader of Centurion Developments(NEUTRAWORKS) and started working on the game Forgotten but Unbroken.His greatest musical influences were games (Battlefield 1, Mafia 1-2, Warcraft series, ANNO series, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt), and films (Star Wars, Cave of the Golden Rose, Hans Zimmer, and the symphonic band Nightwish. ﻿ Track Listing 1 Forgotten But Unbroken 2:32 2 Enemy Force 3:37 3 Stepping on the Strings 3:48 4 Bloody Moon 3:42 5 Alliance Resistance 2:53 6 Alliance Resistance (part 2) 2:52 7 Bad Memories of Prague 3:00 8 On the Way to Victory 3:04 9 Trench Run 1:21 10 At The End There Was A Light 1:38 11 Peter 1:42 12 Fallen Heroes 1:51 13 Another Day 2:03 14 Commander 1:34 15 The US Army is Coming(USA) 1:40 16 For the Country, For the Family (Russia) 2:34 17 Let's Join Forces (Britain) 1:30 18 Ready For Battle 3:41 19 There is Still Hope 3:08 20 The Silent War 3:50 21 Total War 2:00 22 Last Day 2:34 23 Steel Destruction 4:03 24 Flag of Victory 0:48 25 Bonfire, Guitar and Brothers 1:18 26 Goodbye Fear (Bonus Track) 2:41 27 Homeland is Calling (Bonus Track) 2:28 ﻿ Credits Artist: Matúš Jacko Composer: Matúš Jacko Label: MicroProse Software System Requirements Windows macOS Minimum: Storage: 100 MB available space Storage (high-quality audio): Additional 820 MB available space * Starting February 15, 2024, the Steam Client will no longer support 32-bit games or macOS 10.14 or lower.
