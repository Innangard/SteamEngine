About This Game Fadedlands: The Reckless Squad is a challenging, fast-paced turn-based game with roguelike elements. Do you think turn-based means just standing still and attacking? Here, positioning is key ! Predict enemy skills, move flexibly, and dodge deadly strikes! The timing and positioning of your skills determine victory or defeat! Knock back enemies and you might even make them hurt themselves— experience an unprecedented thrill in turn-based combat! With the addition of roguelike elements, you’ll acquire Blessings from shops or random events. These Blessings not only boost your stats but also change the way your skills attack, allowing you to craft a unique combat style and make your squad stronger with every battle! After your adventure, base building is where the real challenge begins! Use the resources you bring back to unlock new buildings and gameplay, creating your very own adventurer’s paradise! You can recruit more companions with unique abilities at the tavern, or improve your adventurers’ skills at the training ground to prepare for the next adventure! Don’t fear death! Every failure is an opportunity to grow stronger! Collect resources to unlock new characters, cards, and buildings, making your squad more powerful with each defeat! Clearing the game is just the beginning. Higher difficulties, stronger enemies, and more bizarre weather and terrain are waiting for you to conquer!
