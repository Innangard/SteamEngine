No clunky game windows, no cluttered background maps. War of Genesis: Idle Loot is an idle loot RPG with a transparent background that runs smoothly in a small corner of your screen. A 3D hero sets up camp on your desktop, battling tirelessly by your side no matter what you're doing. Just leave it running, and it gets stronger on its own. No controls required. Whether you're working or sleeping, your hero's growth never stops. Trade acquired loot on the Steam Community Market. Items you loot can be traded with other players on the Market. Experience the thrill of trading your hard-earned spoils and filling up your Steam Wallet. The real fun is in the loot drops. As you run stages, gear pours in. Weapons, armor, and even gems! Build your own powerful setup through endless item farming. Join an Alliance and grow together. Team up with alliance members to tackle powerful bosses in Alliance Raids. Multiplayer action with players worldwide. Challenge global players in World Boss battles, test your hero's might in the PvP Arena, and chat with the community. Watch your hero grow automatically, trade looted gear on the Market, and compete with players across the globe. War of Genesis: Idle Loot. Right now, on your desktop.
