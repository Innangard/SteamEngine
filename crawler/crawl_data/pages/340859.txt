Assassinated by the senate on the Ides of March, 44BC, Julius Caesar returns to exact revenge on those who betrayed him. Storm a corrupt senate in this relentless slasher. Indulge in the violence. Become the fall of Rome. Thrash through murderous, high-risk gameplay with a bloodlust mechanic that rewards aggression. An assortment of historic weaponry including the Gladius, Composite Bow, Sling, Spear (Hasta), Greek Fire, Sica, Falx, Fire Siphon and the mighty Scorpion! Three distinct acts with more than 30 hand crafted levels across the ancient world. Slaughter your betrayers across Roman fortresses, gladiatorial arenas, untamed wilds, Babylonian gardens, unholy ruins and the depths of the underworld itself. An extensive bestiary of treacherous foes to be slain, both mortal and mythological. Scribe your skill in blood with time trials , secrets and unlockables for a higher skill ceiling and extended replayability. Bone rattling thrash metal soundtrack recorded and performed by the developers.
