A loving tribute to classic first person narrative games and obscure Source engine mods. Are you tired of playing games that actually make sense ? Are you craving something that's equal parts walking, existential crisis, and jokes about the very fact that you paid money to experience an existential crisis? Well, do I have something for you! Presenting DO NOT PRESS THE BUTTON : a walking simulator where the walking is optional, the simulation is questionable, and the comedy is 100% guaranteed to make you chuckle awkwardly while questioning all your life choices. Is there a deep, philosophical meaning to this game? Who knows! Probably not! But it will definitely pretend there is, and you’ll go along with it, because you’re curious like that. Revolutionary mechanics? Let’s just say, if you’ve ever wanted to press buttons with absolutely no clue what they do, THIS is your game. Inspired by classic narrative-driven games,  Do Not Press The Button (Or You'll Delete The Multiverse) challenges the conventions of storytelling and player agency, inviting players into a universe where every action leads to unexpected (and often humorous) consequences. This is Theseus Games' third title, and by now, we've specialized in the advanced field of wacky game mechanics. After all, our previous releases include Pizza Synthwave and Be a Rock . For a more complete and in-depth understanding of what Do Not Press The Button is, check out the free demo. In this game you will have the choice of how to play and what to do, it’s about  you and your misdirected shenanigans against The Developer. We are looking for the person that can set the record for not pressing the button the longest. Don't forget to annoy The Developer along the way! Estimated gameplay time: up to 1 billion years (or however long human civilization lasts). Features - Fun activities such as: > The Room With All The People That Want To Hang Out With You; > The Introspective Therapy room;  > The Boxtopia room; > The Dark Humor room; > The Meaning Of Human Existence room; > Interact with random physical objects inside the facility; > Secret areas with secret content that developer don't want you to find; - Comedic narration and fun story telling; - Secret Do Not Press The Button (tm) lore (tm); - Hundreds of voiced lines; - Annoying The Developer; - Wacky Multiverses; - Accessibility: we value accessibility and strive to ensure that everyone can enjoy our game, regardless of any hearing impairments they may have. That's why we've made sure that every voice line in the game features subtitles, so that all players can fully immerse themselves in the experience. Look, we're not asking you to solve complex equations here. You just have to not press a button. It's an average job for an average person. You've trained all your life for this moment. Go in there and shine my little mediocre moth! P.S.
