Brave the skies on your own or assemble a squadron of friends, and scavenge scrap to engineer and upgrade flying vessels, bringing your airship along for the journey. An Open World Travel through an open world of floating islands and aerial wonders. Discover how this unique environment has shaped the creatures and biomes that inhabit it as you navigate the skies and level up your piloting skills. The Wasp The Wasp is an agile vessel that can both zip through the sky and hover in place. Available in several different classes, each type of Wasp has its own unique appearance, abilities, and aerodynamics. Deploy a portable camp with the Utility Wasp, airlift heavy scrap with the Hauler Wasp, or stun your enemies with the Striker Wasp. It's all about bringing the right Wasp for the job. The Wasps you rely on also rely on you. Make sure they're fueled, well-maintained, and repaired when needed so you're always ready to go when things get dangerous. Upgrade engine parts, hover modules, or entire hulls to get your preferred configuration. Give your Wasp a new paint job to make it look its best. The Cloud Cruiser The Cloud Cruiser is your mobile base: a buildable, fully customizable airship capable of housing everything important to you. Bring your Wasps and fuel supplies aboard by building Wasp pads and fuel tanks, furnish a lounge area to your liking, and pilot the airship yourself from the helm. Expand your Cloud Cruiser as you explore planet Tycos, island by island, adding new sections whenever you need to support increasingly demanding expeditions. Explore, Build, and Survive Live off the land by extracting scrap, processing minerals, and collecting organic materials. Craft items and manufacture resources, cook food, and research artifacts to gain knowledge about new things to create. Your backpack houses a life support system that provides the power you and your tools need. Build camp equipment or Cloud Cruiser modules to expand your reach across planet Tycos. Investigate the events surrounding the collapse of your old colony and uncover the promise of planet Tycos as humanity's final refuge. Band of Vagabonds You don't need to venture onto planet Tycos alone! Up to four players can camp out, hitch a ride on a friend's Wasp, or fly in formation. Work together to build and operate your shared Cloud Cruiser and experience the odyssey together. Deadly Beautiful Flora and Fauna Take cover when day turns to night or when calm weather gives way to unfavorable winds. From howling jet streams to thunderous rainstorms, the treacherous weather on planet Tycos will require you to master your piloting skills as you traverse the skies. Get your bearings on your new home planet and investigate its alien characteristics. Discover a wealth of strange plants, explore lush biomes, and defend against threatening creatures and drones.
