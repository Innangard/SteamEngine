View Community Hub About This Game Hex Blast is a horde survival x roguelike deckbuilder where you strategize on a hex grid, chaining explosive card combos to wipe out cyber hordes. Boot up your robot—each with unique abilities and a customizable deck—to firewall your core systems against relentless viral hordes! Discover devastating synergies: Shatter the battlefield with earthquake builds, transform defense into offense with shield fortresses, or engineer entirely new combos. Build your deck, acquire powerful chips, and refine the ultimate engine to outsmart ever-adapting cyber threats—before they overwhelm you. Key Features: 4 Playable Robots - each with their own unique cards and relics 200+ cards and relics - infinite synergies to discover Tactical Hex Grid - position cards for explosive chain reactions Evolving cyber threats - adapt as enemies mutate into deadlier forms and swarm in relentless waves Endless replayability - no two runs play the same System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 7 Processor: 2.0 GHz Memory: 1 GB RAM Graphics: 2 GB VRAM Storage: 200 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
