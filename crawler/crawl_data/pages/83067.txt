Buy Radioactive Expansion Pack 2 $3.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content A 1980s-Themed Expansion Pack to enhance Radioactive, with 3 new maps, and 3 new colour schemes emulating the look of some classic 8-bit home computers. Expansion Pack 2 contains 3 new maps: HOT FALKLANDS set on the Falkland Islands. GREATER SAXONY is a map of the UK and North Western Europe. PANDEMONIUM is a unique map where units of all sides can appear anywhere rather than in fixed territories. Includes 3 new colour options: COMMODORE 64 emulates the colours of a Commodore 64 computer. DRAGON 32 pays tribute to the Dragon 32 and Tandy TRS-80 computers. BBC MICRO emulates many 8-bit computers (like the ZX Spectrum) which used stark primary and secondary colours. The game is played yellow on red. System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows XP, Vista, 7, 8, 10 or 11. Processor: Any Windows compatible CPU.
