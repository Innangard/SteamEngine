View Community Hub About This Game TCG Football Card Shop Simulator is a relaxing first-person shop simulation where you build and manage your own football card shop. Open card packs, discover rare football cards, stock football merchandise, and create a successful store for your customers. Build Your Own Football Card Shop Arrange your shop as you wish. Place your shelves, organize your products, and create a modern, tidy, and engaging shopping environment for your customers. Open Card Packs Discover hundreds of football cards by purchasing different card packs. Add many different rarity levels to your collection, from common cards to legendary cards. Try your luck and find the most valuable cards in your shop. Sell Football Merchandise In addition to card packs, offer footballs, cleats, trophies, figurines, and many more football merchandise for sale in your shop. Place your products on shelves, manage your inventory, and meet customer demands. Go to the Cash Register Scan the products customers want to buy at the cash register. Take payments, complete transactions quickly, and ensure customers leave your store satisfied. Inventory Management Order new products in-game using your tablet. Unpack delivered boxes, transport products to your warehouse, and boost sales by keeping your shelves stocked. Complete Your Card Collection Add the football player cards you find to your card album. Grow your collection by discovering rare cards and try to collect all of them. Put the valuable cards you collect up for sale along with the card stand. Expand Your Store Use your earnings to grow your store, unlock new areas, and increase your product variety by adding more shelves and card stands. Display more products, attract more customers, and multiply your income.
