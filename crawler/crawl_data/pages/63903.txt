Releasing into Early Access allows us to spend more time VEIN development, and Early Access success means we can hire artists and developers to make VEIN better than ever. (We'll continue work on VEIN regardless of Early Access success). In addition, Early Access allows us to receive feedback from the community and iterate on the current and future features of VEIN, together.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to develop VEIN under Early Access until it’s feature-complete. That means we plan to add NPCs, raiders, settlements, quests, lore, and the full 14x14 kilometer map, alongside hundreds of other features and gameplay and graphical improvements. We estimate that, with full-time work, we can achieve this within roughly 5 years. We intend to work on VEIN indefinitely.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We’ll be expanding the map outwards to 14x14 kilometers. NPCs, raiders, trading, and settlements have yet to be developed. Base fortifications are in currently, but we’d like to expand these to a full range of high-tier defenses. We’re planning on writing lore and quests for the world of VEIN and fleshing out the story and timeline, with quests and things to do spanning a number of in-game years. Finally, we’d like to improve the art over time: better weapons, animations, characters, zombies, and textures will help bring the game world to life.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Character creation, as well as a full RPG stats and leveling system, is present. Multiplayer is functional. Combat, both player-versus-player and against zombies, is present. Looting and scavenging, farming, crafting, cooking, and base building are in. A fully simulated power grid and water system is in-game. There’s a variety of areas and points-of-interest to explore. The playable area is around 4x4 kilometers, covering four towns and the nature around and in-between them. It is continually expanded with updates. VEIN is known for having hundreds of independent and interlinked systems, so we encourage you to play the demo version to see what’s in game. The demo is restricted to two small towns and with a level cap. It continues to and will continue to receive updates with the Early Access version (and beyond).” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We intend to increase VEIN’s pricing over time as the game is developed.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We have been open and transparent with our community since day one. We constantly take feedback from everywhere and everyone, be it from YouTube comments, our Discord server, the in-game feedback reporter, and even via e-mail. We’ll continue to do this during and after our Early Access phase. There are no other restrictions on the demo. The demo gets regular feature updates and bug fixes alongside the main game. About This Game VEIN is a post-apocalyptic survival multiplayer sandbox game. In the near future, the apocalypse has occurred: hostile zombies roam the Earth, most people have died, and society has collapsed. You are a survivor. Gather supplies to survive, explore abandoned buildings, combat bandits, defend your home, and rebuild society with friends. Dynamic Environment As time passes and seasons change, the world of Vein does too. Persistent, long-scale random events affect gameplay. Intelligent AI AI can see, hear, feel, and smell you, and react intelligently to those senses. You're being watched. Terrifying zombie variants make survival that much more difficult. Customizable Characters From your hair to your body type to your stats, you can customize your character and make them truly unique among the survivors. Usable Everything You can interact with nearly everything you can see. Open mailboxes, adjust faucets, and knock on doors. Throw a can of beans at a zombie if you like. Fortification Build over the destroyed world and establish safehouses. Defend yourself from raiders and invasions. Maintain your base by yourself or with friends. Long-Term Survivalism Hunt, fish, and trap. Till land and grow your own food. Chop your own wood. Once canned goods run out, you're on your own. Vehicles and Maintenance Get around the world of Vein with cars and trucks. Refuel and maintain your vehicle. Upgrade parts to repair damage and improve performance. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Violence, drug and alcohol use.
