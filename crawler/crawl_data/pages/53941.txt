Buy MechWarrior 5: Mercenaries - Ashes and Ascension $19.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Lead your Mercenary Company through a New Campaign. Looking to capitalize on the rare show of cooperation between the Great Houses following the Battle of Coventry, First Prince Victor Steiner-Davion has called on all the nations of the Inner Sphere to come together to respond to the threat of a renewed Clan invasion. Lyran Alliance Archon Katrina Steiner-Davion graciously offers to host the international conference on Tharkad. With the fate of the entire Inner Sphere at stake, all eyes now turn to the First Whitting Conference. And everyone, be they house loyalist or independent mercenary, cannot help but be sucked up into the conference’s all-consuming orbit. Experience an 18-mission campaign, our longest yet. Hunt down 10 new High-Reward Crates. Explore the updated Inner Sphere map, spanning 3058–3060. Jump into Career Mode with 5 new faction starts set in 3050. Expand your arsenal Acquire 13 new 'Mechs - Arctic Cheetah, Arctic Wolf, Ice Ferret, Huntsman, Hunchback IIC, Nova Cat, Sun Spider, Marauder IIC, Warhammer IIC, Blood Asp, Supernova, Stone Rhino, and Bane. Deploy 3 new Inner Sphere OmniMechs, the Blackjack, Black Hawk-KU, and Perseus. Discover 9 new Inner Sphere 'Mech variants, including the Annihilator, Hatamoto-Chi, King Crab, Longbow, Nightstar, Phoenix Hawk, Victor, Centurion, and Hunchback. Equip your 'Mechs with 11 new Inner Sphere weapons, including IS ER Small and Medium Lasers, IS Ultra Autocannons, IS LB-X Autocannons, the IS Light Gauss Rifle, and IS Streak SRMs. Face new ground unit variants and threats, including the Po, Ontos, Hetzer, Demolisher II, Patton, and Condor.
