Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Moon Corona is a deckbuilding RPG centered around strategic card combinations, tactical decision-making, and class progression. Games of this type benefit greatly from extensive player feedback, especially when it comes to deck variety, class balance, combat strategy, and the overall gameplay experience. The core gameplay systems are already in place, and Early Access allows us to refine and improve the game with input from a broader community. By working closely with players, we can continue polishing card balance, class identity, game systems, pacing, and the overall quality of the experience before the full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Moon Corona to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 months. This is our current development estimate and may change as development progresses and community feedback is incorporated. Our goal is to continue improving the game while expanding its content at a steady pace.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already includes the game’s core gameplay experience and its major gameplay systems. Under our current plans, the main difference between the full version and the Early Access version will be the amount of playable level content available. We plan to expand the game by adding the remaining late-game regions and levels. Under the current development plan, these remaining levels account for approximately 40% of the total level content envisioned for the full version. Much of this content is already at an advanced stage of development but still requires further balancing, testing, and polish before it can be considered ready for inclusion. Development during Early Access is expected to focus on completing, testing, balancing, and polishing the remaining level content, with player feedback helping to guide that work. The exact scope, order, and timing of updates may change as development progresses.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Early Access version already includes nearly all of the game's core gameplay systems, allowing players to experience deckbuilding, class progression, combat, and the main gameplay loop. Players can expect approximately 16 hours of content during a typical playthrough of the currently available content, while experimenting with different classes and deck-building strategies. Throughout Early Access, we will continue expanding the game's content while improving its overall quality and polish.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “As additional content is introduced and the overall experience continues to improve during Early Access, we expect to adjust the game's price accordingly. If any pricing changes are planned, we will communicate them to the community in advance through our official channels.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback will play an important role throughout the development of Moon Corona. During Early Access, we will actively gather feedback through the Steam Community Hub, Discord, and our other official community channels, with particular attention to card balance, class balance, deck-building diversity, gameplay variety, game difficulty, system design, and the overall player experience. This feedback will help guide our ongoing improvements throughout Early Access.
