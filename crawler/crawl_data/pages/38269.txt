Move your own arms to swim through a flooded, abandoned village: glide over rooftops, slip through windows, and reach out to touch a world frozen in time. Your body is the controller. Every corner of the sunken village rewards curiosity. Dive into flooded houses, search quiet streets, and find hidden objects and traces of the people who once lived here. The relaxed pace and natural swimming make Echoes of Mora VR a comfortable VR experience. No rush, no pressure, just you and the deep. Hidden in the depths lie Echoes: portals into the days before the flood. Each one is sealed, its fragments waiting to be found in secret corners or behind small puzzles. Unlock them, step through, and stand in the village as it once was, with dry land under your feet and voices in the streets. There you'll meet Mora, a girl searching for her missing brother. She is the only one who can see you. Share what you found in the deep, guide her search, and see where her story leads. Echoes of Mora VR supports all PC-based VR headsets and comes with a full non-VR mode. Headset battery running low, or just want to keep playing on the couch? Switch to flatscreen through the ingame menu and continue right where you left off, no restart needed.
