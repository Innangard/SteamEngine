About This Game Embark on a heartfelt narrative road trip game where two companions share a car, conversations, and the fading light of day. In this choice-based adventure, every glance, silence, and detour shapes an intimate story of connection and reflection. Slow down, uncover unspoken emotions, and discover how small moments linger long after the journey ends. Experience a gentle, atmospheric indie game that invites you to: Talk or Stay Silent: Engage in meaningful dialogues or embrace quiet pauses that reveal deeper layers. Make Small Choices: Decide on detours, topics, and paths that lead to multiple endings and branching narratives. Explore and Reflect: Notice the little things, memories, missed turns, and shared glances in a relaxing, emotional story . Replay for New Paths: Drive again to uncover alternate outcomes and hidden discoveries. When the trip ends, you can look back on where you’ve been or take the drive again, and see where a different path might lead. Perfect for fans of story-rich games, Sunset Drive offers a short, replayable experience with beautiful visuals and a calming soundtrack. Supports English and Japanese. The road is yours, will you take the scenic route? Discover more with DLC: Original Soundtrack and Art Collections in the Deluxe Edition.
