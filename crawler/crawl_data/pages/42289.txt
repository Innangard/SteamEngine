Each roll adds or removes from your points, which you need to earn to progress through the game, and determines your fate at the end of the void, where you can earn 1 of 6 endings. Encounter roll-based events and enemies to earn gold to upgrade your Attack, Defense, Luck, and Gold gain as you progress. Collect enemy cards and unlock painted versions of the monster figurines. Travel through multiple areas as you make your way to the Demon King's lair . Complete Achievement Quests and unlock dice skins ! Unlock Auto-roll and 2x speed idler  gameplay once you complete the game, to make your journey towards unlocking all of the endings easier. Gameplay Features Click to roll a 20-sided die to advance. Random events and battles resolved by your roll. Earn points and gold through encounters and outcomes. Move through multiple biomes for unique events and encounters.
