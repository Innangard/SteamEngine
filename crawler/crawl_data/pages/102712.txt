Meer informatie Wat de ontwikkelaars zeggen: Waarom vroegtijdige toegang? 'GeomSurf already has a playable foundation, but I want to develop the game together with its community. Real player feedback is especially valuable for improving controls, level balance, online modes, and the Builder. Early Access allows me to release regular updates and evaluate new ideas before version 1.0.' Hoelang blijft dit spel ongeveer in vroegtijdige toegang? 'I currently expect GeomSurf to remain in Early Access for approximately 2–6 months. This estimate may change depending on player feedback, technical challenges, and the scope of future updates.' Hoe gaat de volledige versie verschillen van de versie met vroegtijdige toegang? 'For the full version, I plan to expand and polish the campaign and endless modes, improve the stability and possibilities of online play, introduce new Builder tools and interactive objects, and expand customization, challenges, achievements, and leaderboards. The interface, performance, controls, and visual presentation will also be improved over time. The exact scope and order of updates may change based on community feedback.' Wat is de huidige staat van de versie met vroegtijdige toegang? 'The current version includes a playable campaign with handcrafted levels, multiple endless modes, competitive online races, progression, and unlockable skins. The built-in Builder allows players to create, test, publish, discover, and rate community levels. The core systems are functional, although players may encounter bugs, balance changes, interface revisions, and ongoing improvements to online stability.' Zal het spel anders geprijsd worden tijdens en na vroegtijdige toegang? 'I plan to increase the price moderately when the game leaves Early Access to reflect the expanded content and features. Any price change will be announced in advance.' Hoe zijn jullie van plan de community te betrekken bij het ontwikkelproces? 'I will collect feedback through Steam Discussions, bug reports, player suggestions, and ratings of community-created levels. Community feedback will help me prioritize fixes, control improvements, Builder development, and online features. Significant changes and new versions will be shared through Steam announcements.' Learn the movement, find the cleanest route, and overcome obstacles across handcrafted worlds. MASTER THE MOVEMENT Jumps, dashes, gravity shifts, and other abilities change the rules as you move. Read the level, maintain your flow, and refine every action. TAKE ON THE CAMPAIGN Discover new mechanics and challenges across a series of handcrafted levels. Each world introduces its own obstacles, routes, and visual identity. CHASE A NEW RECORD Test yourself in endless modes with changing routes. React to incoming obstacles, push your distance, and improve your personal best. RACE ONLINE Join online races and put your movement, reactions, and route knowledge to the test against other players. BUILD YOUR OWN LEVELS Use the built-in Builder to create and test your own routes. Publish levels, discover community-made maps, and rate the creations of other players. The Builder is currently in beta and will continue to expand and improve alongside the game. KEY FEATURES • Fast 3D precision platforming • A handcrafted campaign • Multiple endless modes • Online competitive races • A built-in level editor • Publishing and rating of community levels • Unlockable skins and cosmetic rewards • Colorful geometric worlds and energetic music Informatie over door AI gegenereerde inhoud Zo omschrijven de ontwikkelaars hoe hun spel AI gebruikt om inhoud te genereren: Generative AI tools were used during development to assist with portions of programming and store-page text.
