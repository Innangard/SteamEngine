View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Pre-Purchase Rewards About This Game You’ve inherited a run-down parking garage from your grandfather. A dusty cash register, a worn-out keychain, and a crowd of complaining customers… This place has a terrible reputation in the city, and it’s now up to you to bring it back to life. City residents flood into your garage, each with a unique personality: impatient, elderly, VIP, or grumpy. Take their cars, scan the tickets, and handle payments flawlessly. A single mistake can quickly escalate into big trouble. When things get busy, it’s time to rely on your valet team. Each key must be properly labeled—one mix-up and chaos begins. Hire, train, and manage your staff carefully to keep everything running smoothly. Daytime is busy, but nighttime brings new dangers. Thieves may sneak in to damage or steal cars. Invest in cameras, lighting, and security guards to protect your business. Remember: every damaged car hurts your reputation. Use your profits to expand parking space, improve lighting, and add stylish decorations. The better your garage looks, the more customers you attract—and the higher fees you can charge. Unlock passive income sources and grow into a parking empire. Keys get lost, customers lose patience, thieves lurk in the shadows… Running a parking garage is no easy job. But manage it well, and you can turn a small lot into a citywide legend! 👉 Add it to your wishlist now and start building your parking empire!
