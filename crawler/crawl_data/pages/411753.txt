View Community Hub About This Game STORY: You are Super Clown in this Super Mario styled 3d platformer. Super Clown need avoid different enemies like following slimes and big Mr Wombat enemies. Those enemies can be killed by jumping in their head or hitting Mr Wombat big red button. Also different traps like spikes and water need to be avoided. Traps hurt or kill player immediately. Super Clown main mission is to find pirate flag then level is finished. Also collecting hearths increase player health and collecting enough coins (5 coins) will give player extra lifes. Also falling from map could kill player.  There are also falling and moving platforms. For now 21 different themed levels FEATURES: - 21 levels - Different enemies: Following slimes , bigger Mr Wombat enemies and immortal skeleton enemies - Traps like spikes and dangerous water and frozen water - Steam achievements - Checkpoint system - Hearth which give more energy - Coins. collect 5 coins to give extra life System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: windows 8 Processor: i5 Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: NVidia GeForce 800 series DirectX: Version 9.0 Storage: 9 GB available space Sound Card: Direct x9 Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: Windows 8.1 Processor: i7 Memory: 6 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce 900 series DirectX: Version 9.0 Storage: 9 GB available space Sound Card: Direct x9 * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
