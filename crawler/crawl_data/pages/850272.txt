Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game You are a Primordial, an entity beyond the gods. Your link to Midgard is a lone tower—and the hordes are coming to destroy it. Roll dice to summon Viking defenders, strike enemies with divine magic, and shape your build through an expanding Evolution Tree. Combining incremental progression, tower defense, and auto-battler combat , each run takes you from a fragile foothold to a battlefield alive with warriors and power. Roll the dice. Rally your defenders. Use your attack to send Norse dice tumbling across the arena. Their results summon or strengthen the warriors who stand between your tower and destruction. Build your defense with Viking Warriors, Archers, and Shieldmaidens. Unlock new dice, improve your odds, and discover powerful results as your influence grows. Your first roll starts the battle. Make it count. Your warriors fight. You turn the tide. Your heroes move and fight automatically, but your attention can decide the battle. Direct your magical attack by aiming at enemies with your cursor. Support a struggling warrior, focus down a dangerous elite, or stop an enemy before it reaches the tower. Use your powers alongside your defenders to survive each incoming wave. You watch from above, but you are part of the fight. Grow through the Evolution Tree. Defeated enemies release Souls . Invest them in an expanding Evolution Tree to strengthen your warriors, reinforce your tower, improve your powers, or change the way your dice work. Choose where to grow: Some paths open through battle itself. Defeat enemies, roll dice, and complete objectives to reveal new opportunities for your build. Survive the Realms. Face waves of Draugr and other corrupted foes as you journey through the Realms. Each Realm brings mounting pressure, stronger enemies, and elites that test your defenses. Survive long enough to confront the Realm’s boss . Defeat it to advance and face the next challenge.
