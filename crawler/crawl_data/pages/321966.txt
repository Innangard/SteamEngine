About This Demo Sky Harvest is a cozy farming and life-sim adventure among the clouds. Grow crops, explore sky islands, and build your dream home in a peaceful world above the clouds. A cozy life-sim adventure set in a breathtaking world of floating islands. Grow, explore, and connect with nature and its people as you shape your new home in the skies. Take to the skies aboard your trusty airship. Discover hidden islands, ancient ruins, and breathtaking landscapes filled with secrets and treasures. Plant seeds, care for your crops, and bring life back to the land. From humble sprouts to lush fields — your farm will flourish among the clouds. Relax by crystal lakes, catch rare fishes, and gather ingredients for recipes and crafting. Express your style with unique outfits and accessories. Be who you want to be in your skybound world. Meet charming villagers, share gifts, unlock heartwarming stories, and build meaningful connections across the islands. Turn your harvests into delicious meals and serve travelers from across the clouds. Discover, craft, decorate, and unwind at your own pace in this gentle, story-rich world above the clouds. ⚒️ Crafting - Craft unique tools to enhance your day to day tasks. 💝 Relationship Building - Make friends and unlock unique perks. 🔎 Quests - There's always something or someone in need of your help. 💰 Merchant System - Buy and sell anything and everything through local shops.
