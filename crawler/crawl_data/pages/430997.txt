Over 3 hours of original music were composed for the story sequences and investigations. This release contains the full versions of tracks created for cutscenes as well as the looping cross-examination music, trial segment background songs, and character themes. MP3 files are provided in addition to optional high-quality FLAC files. This OST features a variety of moods and sounds that tie the story together. From the low-stakes ambient background songs that play during dialogue scenes to the intense cross-examination tracks, Burden of Truth's original music sets the stage for Conrad Cross' grand return to the courtroom.
