Why We Made Out of Hand: Deluxe We are a team of two friends from Sweden who played a lot of party games during college. After a while, we got bored of most of them, so we decided to create our own. We released Out of Hand on Steam right after graduating in the summer of 2023. At that time, the game only supported local multiplayer, but most players wanted to play with their friends online. So, we spent the entire year of 2024 developing online multiplayer. We decided to release the game as Out of Hand: Deluxe since we made improvements to every aspect of the game along the way and felt it would be more fun to launch it as a whole new game rather than just updating an old one that probably no one remembers anymore.
