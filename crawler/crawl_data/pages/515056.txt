The rest is physics. Control a rocket and land it on the landing pad. Touch anything else and you're dead. No autopilot, no second chances: Just you, your buttons, and physics that doesn't forgive mistakes. Two ways to play solo Minimal: Two thrusters. Easy to fly, easy to crash. Classic: Five thrusters, more to juggle, and so much more to go wrong. Bring a crowd How many friends will you fly with: just one, five, or 50? Rakete is massively local multiplayer : players share control of a single rocket, each person commanding one or more thrusters. Hand out controllers, divide the keyboard, or improvise. It takes communication, coordination, and a little chaos.
