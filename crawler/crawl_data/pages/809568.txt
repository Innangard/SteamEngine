View Community Hub About This Game Metro Anomaly is an anomaly-hunting horror game where you must identify the anomaly by its behavior. An anomaly can be something as simple as an unnatural head turn or a twisted joint. But it can also be someone staring at you or even trying to attack you. ANOMALY EXAMPLE: Once you’ve identified the anomaly, you can aim and shoot — but you only have one bullet. If you’re wrong, the anomaly will attack you: NOT EVERY ANOMALY WILL JUST WAIT FOR YOU TO FIND IT!
