Weave the magic of focus with a girl from another world. Memory of Memorie : A Chill Story is a story-driven productivity adventure where you work at your desk alongside Memorie, an apprentice witch living in another world. In a quiet room filled with gentle Celtic music, whenever you sit down to work, Memorie studies her magic by your side. What used to be solitary work time slowly becomes something warmer — time shared with someone. ■ The more you focus, the more the story unfolds The game features a Pomodoro-based focus timer. As you accumulate focused work sessions, new story chapters unlock. Casual conversations with Memorie. Small changes happening in her village. And the “secret” she keeps hidden. Beyond each completed session, a new piece of the story awaits. ■ Your focus changes her world As the story unfolds and you earn badges, Memorie’s room gradually begins to change. Her favorite cup. The glow of magic. A space growing warmer and richer. New outfits and hairstyles for Memorie, and more things to place around her room. The more you dedicate yourself to your work, the more her world moves forward too. ■ Make your daily efforts visible Organize your tasks into groups with the To-Do List, and set due dates for them. Link a task to the focus timer, and the time you spend focusing on it will be recorded and accumulated over time. ■ Keep a record of your progress, day by day Use the Calendar to look back on how many focus sessions you completed and which tasks you finished each day. Check your pace with monthly charts, or leave notes about your thoughts and observations from the day. Keep a record of both your efforts and your everyday memories. ■ “Weaving Magic” keeps you going Memorie’s “Weaving Magic” helps protect the progress you’ve made. As long as you have enough mana saved to cover your days off, your streak will be restored automatically. Go at your own pace, and keep moving forward together with Memorie. ■ Features A cozy fantasy workspace with Celtic music and ambient sounds A focus timer based on the Pomodoro Technique Story progression and world mysteries unlocked by your focus time A To-Do List linked to the timer — organize tasks into groups and set due dates A calendar for looking back on each day, with charts for your focus records and notes for your daily reflections More outfits, hairstyles, and room items for Memorie as the story advances and badges are earned “Weaving Magic” keeps your streak going even on the days you rest Perfect for studying, working, or creative projects ■ The World The story takes place in Elmana Village, a small settlement not far from the Imperial Capital. Protected by the sacred “Tree of Light,” it is a peaceful place. But little by little, a dark presence begins to creep into the world. Using a spell that connects distant hearts, Memorie found you. Was it coincidence… or fate? Stay focused, and see the story through to its ending — together.
