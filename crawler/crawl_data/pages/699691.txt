All content within the DLC pack can be accessed by new and existing players throughout For The King II’s campaign and Dark Carnival mode. Arcane artistry is on full display as we introduce two new crafty playable characters - the Toymaker and the Fortune Teller. These characters bring a host of new content to Fahrul to aid you in your journey - new weapons, a new trait, a new merc, and more! The Toymaker is guided by their nimble hands and arcane influence. On the battlefield, their unhinged stare is a window into a fractured mind. An artist that blurs the line between brilliance and madness, the Toymaker is a talent-based character that captures the essence of their enemies into a child’s toy. These creations look intended for the entertainment of children; however, these tortured souls can be summoned into battle to do the bidding of their maker. The Fortune Teller is a mischievous trickster shrouded in mystery. Moving with grace, confidence, and arrogance, the Fortune Teller has the ability to alter fortune and fate in the palm of their hands. Wielding elite-level awareness, the Fortune Teller controls fate through their tarot cards. Just as the fates decide, their alignment knows no equal and the outcomes of these tarot cards can alter the fortunes of Fahrul. Whether you are manipulating destiny or meddling with dangerous devices, fate just got a little stranger.
