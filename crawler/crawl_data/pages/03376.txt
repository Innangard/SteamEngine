This one has you build the dice.” 7/10 – InsertCoins About This Game Sigilfarer is a roguelite adventure where every decision rests on the roll of your dice .  Set in a world broken by a magical disaster, you lead a group of wanderers through dangerous, ever-changing lands. In Sigilfarer , your strength isn’t just your strategy — it’s the dice you create. The game is built around a unique dice system that reimagines deck-building. Your party is your dice set. Every weapon, shield, and piece of armor you equip changes your dice, adding new faces that define your attacks, defenses, and abilities. You'll rely on your wits and the dice you craft to explore procedurally generated, open hexagonal maps. Each region presents diverse biomes, perils, and opportunities . Conducted runs will push you to develop your strategy and enhance your dice set with each attempt. Death is not the end, but rather a fresh beginning , allowing you to become stronger and wiser with each journey.
