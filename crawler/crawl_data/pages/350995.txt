Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We’re launching Slowly Sliding Ducks in Early Access because, well... this is our first dive into online multiplayer and we want the experience to be smooth, fun, and actually work. We’re building an ambitious kind of game, taking on new challenges, and we’re all-in on making it a reality. That’s why we’re moving one step at a time, staying focused on quality, and using Early Access as a way to monitor feedback throughout development. Player input will help us shape and refine the experience, and some features may evolve along the way. But our commitment to the game is already locked in. ” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We’re aiming to keep Early Access short and sweet—less than a year. During that time, we’ll be closely watching how the game behaves in the wild: monitoring networking performance, smoothing out any bumps (or unexpected duck pileups), and making sure everything runs as smoothly as a duck on a waterslide. ” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We’re building a game that’s meant to grow with the community. We're planning monthly content drops: fresh cosmetics, little events, bug fixes, new (free) minigames, and a few silly surprises we’re not ready to spoil just yet. ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Slowly Sliding Ducks is fully playable online with up to 16 players. It includes a selection of core minigames (with more on the way), 100 unlockable cosmetics, and access to the community pool where you can show off your duck in all its glory. While the game is stable and already a ton of fun, we’re still refining online performance and adding quality-of-life features. ” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “There’s a chance the game will have a slightly higher price after release. It all depends on how much new content we’ve added by then. Since we plan to keep Early Access short, the price might stay the same. Players who join us during Early Access will get an exclusive skin as a thank-you from the team. ” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We'll be actively monitoring in-game analytics, the Steam forums, and the game’s Discord channel during development, focusing on player feedback to understand what minigames are most fun and what features need to be added, removed, or reworked. ” Read more Buy Slowly Sliding Ducks INTRODUCTORY OFFER! About This Game Slowly Sliding Ducks is the multiplayer sibling of Placid Plastic Duck Simulator! Relax with friends in a social pool where you can customize your duck, then jump into an online party game for 8–16 players whenever you feel like it. Compete against friends, foes, or bots in a delightfully absurd variety of minigames: race to the finish line, be the last duck standing, or try to come in last in a race where you simply sit back and watch (just like a marble race). And once the competition is over, unlock new outfits and show off your latest acquisitions in the 50-player community pool!
