Learn more Leaving Early Access: Jan 29, 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Last Pirates: Die Together is our first co-op game of this scale – a game built to deliver laughter, chaos and great moments with friends. Games like this aren't balanced on paper; they're balanced by thousands of crews playing together. We're entering Early Access to shape the game with our community: tuning the core loop, difficulty, enemy behavior, physics interactions, co-op flow, replayability and more – based on how people actually play and what they want to see. Our playtests and demo (100,000+ players) have already reshaped key systems, added new content and fixed countless bugs through player feedback. Early Access lets us keep that cycle running all the way to 1.0, with regular content and balance updates shaped by you.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We're aiming for 6+ months. The honest answer: it will stay in Early Access until it's truly ready – we'd rather ship the right 1.0 than chase a date. Our team is based in Ukraine, working in challenging circumstances, so we ask for a bit of patience – and we'll keep doing our best.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Honestly – we have more ideas than we can list. For 1.0 we definitely want: many more locations, new enemies and bosses, character customization, a deeper progression system, more weapons and cursed loot, and overall polish across the board. And this list keeps growing – even as you read this, we're already working on a major update. ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Fully playable online co-op for up to 4 players – everything you saw in the demo, but bigger: more monsters, more locations, more content, more polish. It's already a full, fun co-op experience, and we squash bugs fast – during our playtests we shipped fixes weekly based on player reports. And it will only keep growing with regular updates.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We may increase the price at full release, but we haven't made a final decision yet. What we can promise: every major update during Early Access is completely free, and any price change will be announced well in advance.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We watch every video and read every comment – on Steam and on our Discord. We've run multiple playtests and demo versions, and we collect feedback constantly: there's an in-game survey form, and we get hundreds of responses per build, which we review weekly and turn into real changes. You and your crew will use your unusually stretchy arms to gather as much loot as you can for your captain. Be mindful of your surroundings though. There are others who mean to keep the treasure for themselves. You land lubbers aren’t that tough! You and up to three of your closest mates will work together, communicate, and survive as you try to make your ol’ captain rich and happy! You are quite an unusual sailor! I bet you can grab just about anything with those pairs of hands. Dismantle doors, grab furniture, or destroy the island for all I care! Just bring that money back aboard or the captain will have our head! Remember, scallywags, you are here for gold! Collect all the booties (and protect your own!), shake things down, and don’t come out empty-handed or no-legged. Feel the adventurous drive push you deeper into the mysterious island, but make sure that at least one of you lives to carry out the gold. Make it back to the ship in one piece, would ya? Avoid all the dangers that the island has in store for you. Play the hero or hide somewhere if you find yourself in a pickle. Or maybe one of your crewmembers can provide a distraction… I’m sure they’ll be fine, trust us! Don’t be afraid to look at Davey Jones’ Locker. We all die anyway, so why not create an adventure worth remembering? Grab your crew, grab some booty, and make a story worth telling those bilge rats outside!
