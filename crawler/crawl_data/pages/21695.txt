Danger Humans are intelligent, sometimes, so they will react and find a new ways to counter you. Their houses vary not just by size but also by diffuculty. The richier the house - the stricter it's security systems. You will face fully closed "bunkers" with cameras and detectors and, maybe, even something worse than this...
