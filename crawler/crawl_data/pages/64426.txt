Follow the lives of occult university students who discover ancient rites and invocations with the help of their passionate Teacher. Face their personal trials, summon new creatures, synergize them, and burn to uncover the depths of obsession. Burn With Me captures a special type of "strangeness" — a comforting one, inviting you to be part of it… while also keeping you on the edge of your seat, never knowing what to expect.
