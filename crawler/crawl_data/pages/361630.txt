Buy The House of Da Vinci: The Art Book $5.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Are you curious about the beginnings of the House of Da Vinci? Or maybe you enjoy quality art? Our exclusive digital edition art book will take you by the hand through our art gallery in great detail. Maybe you’ll find a piece of art you’ve missed while playing the game? Book’s highlights The whole book is more than 100 pages long and it’s all written in the original Johann Gutenberg’s font, the creator of the first letterpress back in 1455. It creates the right atmosphere while reading. The book includes several Leonardo da Vinci’s quotes in font inspired by his original handwriting. And not only that! You will see various unreleased images, making-of graphics and even some first images from The House of Da Vinci 3 (yay, there will be another one!). All initials (or drop caps if you will) are hand drawn with ink, just like back in the Leonardo’s times.
