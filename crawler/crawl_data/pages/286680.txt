Grow your colony and create a wide variety of specialized workers of unique shapes and sizes. Produce, gather, transform and combine different types of food with unique attributes, values and effects. Optimize your workforce: find the right balance between production and delivery. Consume more and more food to advance the species to the next step of its evolution. Discover, experiment, and perfect your favorite strategies: Carefully spend your Brain Matter, a rare and limited resource used to unlock the most advanced of upgrades. Unlock and spend DNA points in the genetics talent tree to personalize each colony. Cumulate different types of bonuses through your selection of foods and upgrades. Features a cute hand-drawn art-style and an original soundtrack.
