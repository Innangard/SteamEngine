View Community Hub About This Game STEP OUT OF THE FRAME Run Naked Woman Run is an atmospheric 3D platformer combining fast-paced movement, challenging obstacles, environmental puzzles, and reality-shifting mechanics. Explore one continuous world divided into six unique zones, each with its own atmosphere, challenges, and secrets. One world. Two realities. A different path with every switch. Platforms appear and disappear, bridges change their positions, and barriers block different routes depending on the active reality. Learn to read your surroundings, switch at the right moment, and keep moving. KEY FEATURES Two Realities, One World Switch between Reality A and Reality B with a single key. Reveal hidden platforms, remove obstacles, unlock new routes, and experience changing camera perspectives. Challenging Platforming Run, jump, and survive collapsing platforms, moving bridges, sliding walls, spinning obstacles, and dangerous energy barriers. Every hazard provides a visual warning before becoming dangerous. Environmental Puzzles Activate switches, operate mechanical bridges, restore power, and manipulate light to solve spatial puzzles. Use both realities to overcome seemingly impossible obstacles. Six Unique Environments Travel through a mysterious white void, an overgrown ancient temple, a futuristic bio-laboratory, an abandoned treatment station, dangerous underground ruins, and the final world beyond the frame. Dynamic Cinematic Cameras Experience seamless transitions between side-scrolling, angled, and cinematic camera perspectives as you explore the world. Thrilling Escape Sequences Race across collapsing structures, avoid deadly traps, and escape dangerous anomalies before they catch you. Light and Darkness Use illumination and shadows to reveal hidden paths, activate special platforms, and solve environmental challenges. Checkpoints and Progress Saving Save your progress at checkpoints, continue your adventure, or start a new game whenever you want. Hidden Secrets Explore beyond the main route to discover secret areas and track your discoveries. Minimalist Interface Enjoy a clean, distraction-free interface with contextual hints, clear indicators, and challenge timers. Keyboard and Gamepad Support Play and navigate menus using your preferred controls. Atmospheric Soundtrack Experience ambient soundscapes, dynamic chase music, and a dedicated ending sequence. SIX ZONES. ONE JOURNEY. Awakening - Discover the basics of movement, perspective, and reality shifting. The Forgotten Temple - Explore ancient ruins, activate mysterious mechanisms, and escape collapsing structures. The Bio-Laboratory - Restore power, solve technological puzzles, and overcome automated defenses. The Abandoned Station - Navigate dark corridors, moving platforms, and emergency systems. The Underworld - Survive dangerous platforming challenges, spatial anomalies, and intense chase sequences. Beyond the Frame - Combine everything you've learned in the final challenge. CONTROLS Movement: Keyboard / Gamepad Jump: Space / Gamepad Interact: E Switch Reality: Q Pause Menu: Esc SETTINGS Customize your experience with audio volume, graphics quality, display mode, screen resolution, and VSync settings.
