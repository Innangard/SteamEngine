Man Without Clothes Runner is a humorous 3D endless runner where you dodge obstacles, collect clothes, escape the sheriff, and try to beat your personal best. Run Like Your Life Depends on It! Jump over barrels, slide under obstacles, dodge wagons, and survive dangerous traps. The farther you run, the harder your escape becomes. Every mistake brings the sheriff closer. One too many wrong moves, and you're busted! Find Your Clothes! Start your escape almost naked and collect clothing along the way. Find hats, vests, pants, and boots. Gain useful bonuses from your outfit. Lose your clothes when you crash into obstacles. Try to get fully dressed before the sheriff catches you. Explore the Wild West Run through a variety of environments: Bustling Western towns filled with saloons and wagons. Hot deserts with towering cacti. Dangerous rocky canyons. Railroads with moving trains. Abandoned ghost towns. Every run brings different obstacles, routes, and unexpected challenges. Become the Ultimate Outlaw Runner! Collect coins, upgrade your abilities, complete challenges, and set new records. Take risks for bigger rewards, perform perfect dodges, and do whatever it takes to stay ahead of the sheriff.
