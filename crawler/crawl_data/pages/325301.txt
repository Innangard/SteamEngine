You can support us by adding our main game to your Wishlist. Hello! Custom Corner is developed by a two-person indie couple dev team. Our goal is to let you create that peaceful, personal corner that will accompany you in the background while you work, study, or just relax. Sit back and enjoy this cozy corner! Recreate your own isometric room from scratch with over 70 basic pixel items exclusive to the demo version. Place fundamental details according to your taste, from wallpapers and floorings to plants and PC cases. Additionally, thanks to our in-game clock system, the lighting adapts to the time of day; watch your room embrace a dim and sweet darkness while working in the evenings. Design freely and build your dream corner. Not just a game, but your personal desktop assistant. Manage your time with the Focus Timer and calculator. In the demo version, you can use your To-Do list with a 5-task capacity and a single Memo pad, and keep your desk organized by customizing your devices with black and white themes. Sit back and dive into the atmosphere. Maximize your focus with a carefully selected Lo-Fi album and 4 different background sounds. Express your own style by decorating your music player with selected stickers and a charm from the demo pool. When you switch to the main game, all limitations are lifted, and your room gains much more detail. What awaits you in the full version: Over 300 unique items and unlimited color variations The ability to add your own MP3 music to the game Unlimited To-Do list, multiple memo pads, and dozens of color options for your devices Many more stickers, charms, and various ambient sounds for your music player 1 brand new game mode for the isometric room section Steam Achievements and Workshop Support: The ability to design your own custom items and share them with the community! We invite you to our Discord community to directly contribute to the development of the game and share your ideas with us.
