Both versions are installed automatically. Where can I find the guide after purchase? After installation, Steam saves the PDF in your game directory. By default, you can find it here: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Pioneers of Pagonia\Official Guide If you don’t see the file, make sure the DLC is downloaded. In your Steam Library, right-click Pioneers of Pagonia, select Properties → DLC, and ensure the Guidebook DLC is checked.
