A PUZZLE METROIDVANIA OF MOVEMENT AND MYSTERY Imprisoned in a sinister Venetian villa, use your wits to help Luca Sicomoro — a military man of the Serenissima—escape. Every move is a test of strategy , turning movement into a puzzle. Navigate a dark and intricate world inspired by 1700s Venice and Carnival’s masked mystique, where danger and deception lurk in every shadow . MASTER THE SLIDE Use environmental hazards, weapons, artefacts, and your own momentum to dispatch enemies and avoid deadly traps . Each encounter is a test of skill, where puzzle-solving and combat seamlessly intertwine in an easy-to-learn, hard-to-master movement system. EXPLORE AN INTERCONNECTED WORLD The villa is a maze of hidden passageways, locked doors, and secrets waiting to be uncovered. Unlock new tools that let you backtrack to previously unreachable areas, forge shortcuts , and interact with the eccentric and enigmatic characters who dwell within. A DARK VENETIAN DREAM Step into a beautifully hand-drawn pixel art world steeped in the elegance and unease of the Carnival of Venice . A haunting, original soundtrack inspired by Italian Baroque melodies enhances the atmosphere, as you piece together a story of mystery, fate, and self-discovery . FEATURES A unique puzzle-combat system built around momentum-based movement. Multiple weapons to unlock , each with a specific puzzle mechanic An atmospheric Venetian setting , blending historical beauty with eerie fantasy. Nonlinear Metroidvania exploration —discover new abilities to open fresh paths. Engaging and devious boss fights , where strategy is key. A fast-paced movement system designed with speedrunning in mind.
