EWR is located only 14 miles from Manhattan, serving a critical role for the New York/Newark metropolitan area. The airport spans over 2,000 acres and has multiple runways, making it a crucial global air travel gateway. EWR has three terminals: Terminal A, Terminal B, and Terminal C. The largest airport system in the U.S. is made up of the trio of La Guardia Airport (included in the base game), John F.
