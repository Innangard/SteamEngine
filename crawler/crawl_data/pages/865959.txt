Welcome to the other side of the English Channel! Discover England The England DLC expands the game world with around 15 cities (as well as 4 additional cities: Bremerhaven, Calais, Dover and Folkestone ) and more than 60 distinctive buildings and landmarks from different cities across England. These include Big Ben, the London Eye, Westminster Bridge and Tower Bridge in London, Newcastle Civic Centre and St Thomas’ Church in Newcastle upon Tyne, Carlisle Citadel, St Chad’s Cathedral in Birmingham, Folly Bridge in Oxford and St Peter’s Street in Leeds. Varied routes take you through English metropolises, smaller towns and rural regions, leading you to London, Birmingham, Bristol, Cambridge, Carlisle, Exeter, Kingston upon Hull, Leeds, Liverpool, Manchester, Newcastle upon Tyne, Nottingham, Oxford, Sheffield and Southampton . Along the way, you will also encounter authentic AI vehicles , including emergency vehicles, taxis and the famous red double-decker bus known as the “Routemaster”. Bremerhaven and Calais also include bus stops that can be used outside the England DLC. The France DLC is required for Calais. Travel to England by train or ferry There are several ways to travel to England. Take the train between Calais (France) and Folkestone (England) or the ferry from Bremerhaven (Germany) to Dover (England) . Versatile journeys with the Tourism W410 Complementing the new map, the new Tourism W410 coach (available separately and together with the England DLC as part of a discounted bundle) comes in a total of 8 variants , with 4 designed for right-hand traffic and 4 for left-hand traffic. Choose between the Standard, M2, M3 and L variants, which differ in aspects such as length, number of axles and equipment. Thanks to its different variants, the Tourism W410 can be used not only in the England DLC, but also in the base game and other expansions.
