Step into the shoes of a DIY handyman starting his own small business in a Polish block of flats during the 1990s. This is not about perfection. This is about quick results, smart shortcuts, and making money before the client starts looking too closely. In the demo version, you will get a first taste of the gameplay, try selected renovation jobs, and experience the everyday life of a handyman who always finds a way to make things work… somehow. Renovate, improvise, and cut costs Diluting paint with water to make it last for the entire apartment? Sure, the color may not be perfect, but the wall is painted. Laying tiles without a level? A little unevenness gives the room some character. What matters is that the job keeps moving forward. Not everything has to be done properly. It just has to look good enough… at least for a while. Renovation jobs full of absurd situations Take on renovation tasks, clean up after previous tenants, repair, install, and improvise your way through each job. Every contract is a chance to earn money, but also a risk that something might go terribly wrong. Furniture doesn’t fit through the door? The window is always an option. The client expects quality? Relax, maybe they won’t notice. Cheap tools and accidental professionalism Visit the hardware store, hunt for the cheapest tools, and use whatever you can get your hands on. Not everything will be durable, comfortable, or even reasonable, but a true handyman knows what really matters: getting the job done. What can you find in the demo? selected renovation jobs, a first look at the atmosphere of a 1990s Polish apartment block, basic renovation, cleaning, and improvisation mechanics, humorous situations inspired by shortcuts, bad workmanship, and DIY chaos, a chance to see if the full version is for you.
