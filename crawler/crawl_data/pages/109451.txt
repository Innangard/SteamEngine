As a new member of the Hunter's Association, you'll hunt monsters and face the creeping threat of the Void. Grow stronger with every battle—gathering loot, leveling up, and forging your build—until you're ready to confront the source of the corruption itself. Straight to the Point No bloated story. No filler. Just clean, streamlined combat and build-focused progression. Deep Builds, Minimalist Design Master complex, multi-layered builds where synergy is everything. Smart combinations and dimensional layering are key to becoming a true hunter. Loot, Optimized Go from hands-on farming to fully automated looting as you refine your systems. The grind evolves—your power does too. Descend into the Void The further you go, the harder it gets. Face ever-stronger Void Lords, test the limits of your build, and push into the endless depths. Core Features Minimalist Story - No filler. No lengthy plot-lines. Jump straight into combat and building your hunter. Endless Skill Combination - Mix and match across six archetypes—Warrior, Assassin, Scholar, Ranger, Summoner, and Guardian. Experiment with 18 core skills and their unique variants to forge your own playstyle. True Skill Customization - Every skill can be socketed with 7 Slates, drastically reshaping its form and effect. Layered Progression - Unlock deeper systems as you progress: talents, flasks, the Dark Tree, the Chaos Atlas, and Cognition. Endless Loot - 80 unique relics, 60 distinct Slates, and 35 Abyssal Eyes to combine into your ultimate build. Boss Hunts - Face five distinct bosses, each growing stronger as difficulty rises. Scaling Difficulty - The deeper you go, the harder it gets—unlocking new challenges and rewards with every tier. Custom Hunting Ground - Design your own hunting grounds with 13 different cores. Create endless hunts and test your limits. Automation, Your Way - From hands-on combat to fully automated farming, refine your systems and let the loot flow. Loot & Crafting - Every drop is randomized. Use the deterministic crafting system to perfect your gear piece by piece. Loot Filters & Auto-Pickup - Your trusty companion auto-loots everything. Customize filters to scrap junk and stash valuables. The Abyss - Survive endless waves in confined arenas. Tailor the difficulty and modifiers for bigger risks—and greater rewards. Build Presets - Save multiple builds and swap instantly without recreating your character. Training Dummy - Test your damage and survivability in a safe environment. Challenges - Experience the true dark hunting grounds in Cruel and Expert difficulty modes, racking your brains to face all possible threats. Player-Friendly Options - Customize keybinds, skip reward screens, and adjust skill effect transparency—everything designed to ensure a seamless and fun experience for you.
