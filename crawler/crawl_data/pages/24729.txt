Idle Iktah is a solo-developed project and many of the best ideas for the game have come from the players. More players getting involved earlier on will make sure that the quality of the game stays high and will result in more people having more fun :) Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? I expect the game to be in Early Access for somewhere around 6-12 months. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? The full version is planned to have: ★ A completely reworked Legacy system centered around creating a world for your pets and leveling them up - There may be Legacy “goals/challenges” (to be completed on the next run) which give permanent upgrades to future runs ★ Achievements and achievement rewards - Achievements may be integrated with Steam Achievements ★ Smart window resizing - Resizable windows is a new feature to Idle Iktah. In the full version the UI should smartly respond to being resized as players need ★ Cloud Save - Saving to and restoring from Steam Cloud should be possible and safely allow for multiple save files Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? The game is fully playable with 18 skills, 500+ items, and months of idle gameplay. Muuttuuko pelin hinta Early Access -vaiheen jälkeen? All prices will stay the same. Miten yhteisö otetaan mukaan kehitysprosessiin? The community has always been a huge part of Idle Iktah. In fact, for any update most of the patch notes are usually suggestions by the community. Idle Iktah has an active Reddit as well as a Discord server, both of which are great places to drop a bug report or a suggestion. If you’d rather send an email, feel free to email using the in-game contact button. Or leave anonymous feedback via the in-game feedback button. It all works!
