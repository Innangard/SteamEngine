Every round, one player becomes the seller and only they know the full truth about what they are offering. Each player starts with three items and must choose one to auction. The seller can see the item’s real market value along with two hidden Clauses attached to it. The buyers cannot. Is it a bargain? Or a disaster waiting to explode? Through real-time negotiation, players pressure, bluff, and outbid each other in fast-paced auctions. When the timer ends, the item is sold and the truth is revealed. Every item comes with two randomized Clauses. They might be beneficial. They might be harmful. They might completely shift the balance of power. Clauses can reward bold plays or create unexpected consequences, turning what looked like a perfect deal into a costly mistake. No two auctions feel the same. After each player has taken their turn as seller, the game moves into the Market phase. Here, players decide whether to sell immediately for secure profit or hold onto items for potential bonuses. Timing, greed, and risk management all matter. Fortunes can rise or collapse in seconds. During the Market phase, players may choose to invest in powerful Cards. Cards allow you to shape the game in your favor, strengthen your own position, or disrupt your opponents. Use them wisely to gain long-term advantages or create critical turning points. The system is optional, but the strategic depth it adds can change everything. This is not just about numbers. It is about reading the room. Convince others your item is priceless. Hide dangerous risks behind confidence. Form temporary alliances. Break them when it benefits you. Designed for chaotic friend groups and intense mind games.
