Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game NIPH is a stylish roguelite platformer with risky and intense combat. Slash, shoot, and learn and cast spells with more varied items as you explore a vast and diverse fantasy world! Customize Your Character Choose from various hair styles, outfits, and colors. Make your character look exactly how you want! Create multiple different characters and embark on new adventures! Legendary Arena Duels Test your skills and builds against other players! Arena Mode puts you face-to-face in fast-paced combat where every learned spell and equipped item makes the difference. Who will be the true master of NIPH? Start in the Greenhorn's Haven Every journey begins in the Greenhorn's Haven. Here, adventurers receive basic supplies for their quest, such as an initial weapon of their choice. After solving basic starting challenges, they can hop on one of the ziplines that lead to the four corners of the map and explore NIPH! Explore a Diverse World NIPH's world features 12 different areas to explore. These areas harbor unique creatures, each with their distinct challenges and items that can only be obtained in those biomes. With each new run, the world is procedurally generated, bringing fresh challenges. Exploring NIPH's diverse areas is essential for your character to become a true master! Choose Your Arsenal Every item in the game teaches a unique special ability when the player accumulates enough experience. Learned abilities can then be used even without the item equipped! As items gain experience, they also increase the character's attributes like strength and intelligence, meaning items are the primary means of progression in the game. Choose between swords, hammers, shields, staves, and even musical instruments and more! Face Epic Bosses Each area of the game features its own dungeons with great rewards. Solve fun puzzles, find amazing new items, and confront challenging enemies to learn new spells, since your skills will be tested by the area boss! There are two possible bosses in each biome, and they are chosen by chance, so be prepared to be surprised! Don't Fear Death! NIPH is a challenging roguelite, so getting defeated from time to time is part of the journey. The game's creatures are intelligent, and reaching the boss can be a great challenge, not to mention defeating them, so don't be discouraged if Game Over strikes. Upon death, you lose a bit of progress, but most of your power remains intact, so a new journey is a chance to learn new abilities and become even more incredible!
