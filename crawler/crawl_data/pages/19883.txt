Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub Reviews “I've fallen in love with Steam's latest ingenious card battler, and this one feels like playing with unstable elements that could explode without warning at any moment” Eurogamer About This Demo Bibidi Bibidi! is a wizardcore roguelike deckbuilder with a spell crafting system that enables you to create powerful, wacky spells with unique effects. Combine incantation cards strategically to adapt to every situation. Will you zap your foes into oblivion, enchant yourself for flexible future turns, or draw more cards for a barrage of spells? Game Features Familiar combat with a strategic twist. Easy to learn for new players as well as fans of the genre, while plenty of surprises await you. Mix 'n match different parts for each card to create the ideal spell to get you out of a pickle! Craft the perfect wizarding build. Experiment with a myriad of incantation cards, sigils, and spellbooks to become the ultimate magic wielder. Rewards, events, and the shopkeeper all offer potential expansions to your build. Explore a dark but whimsical subterranean mystery. Entombed deep within the earth, underneath the towering spires your brethren once called home, through twisting tunnels and maddening monsters, lies hidden your deepest wish . Fight your way through the mountain and uncover the secret of the Wishing Well. Handcrafted dungeon synth playlist. The underground halls of the mountain reverberate with nostalgic sounds of 90s fantasy-themed synthesizer ambient. We cooked it ourselves! Will you make it to the end? Can you find out what happened to your brethren? Are you the last wizard? There's only one way to find out. Bibidi Bibidi! Demo Content This Demo contains the following content: 3 Floors to explore, each with their own unique encounters and bosses. 2 Playable Heroes, one of which you must unlock first. 77 Spell Cards for you to find and combine. 55 Sigils to enhance your own personal style of spell casting. 25 Quests to unlock in the Skymap .
