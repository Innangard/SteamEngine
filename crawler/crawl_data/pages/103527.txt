On the remote island of Scirine, the pursuit of ambition takes a dark turn. Majed, a young biology trainee, arrives at the state-of-the-art Al-Bayan Research Center on Scirine Island to start his internship. But what was meant to be an opportunity to make scientific breakthroughs becomes a descent into a haunting mystery. As he explores the eerily abandoned center, Majed discovers that the truth is far more terrifying than it seems. Yet, the truth intertwines with illusions, and every step uncovers a secret while weaving another.
