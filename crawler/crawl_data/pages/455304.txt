Inside the artbook you will find: High-resolution 3D sculpts and renders Creature and character designs Environmental worldbuilding pieces Surreal architecture and decayed forms Development explorations and  ideas Atmospheric illustrations and select digital drawings This collection offers a deeper look into the artistic process behind Necrophosis  a world shaped through sculpting, texture, form, and decay rather than traditional concept painting alone.
