Your store doesn't. In Night Rolls: Cozy Store , you run a small convenience store from 11 PM to 6 AM. Order products, fill the shelves, make fresh sushi, and serve the people who wander in during the night. 🍣 Make fresh sushi Head into the kitchen and prepare sushi, nigiri, and maki by hand. • Pick a recipe from your cookbook • Place each ingredient yourself • Unlock more recipes as you play • Sell your dishes before they expire Fresh food takes a little more work, but it can also bring in more money. 📦 Keep the shelves full Use the store computer to order drinks, snacks, groceries, and other useful items. Your order is delivered outside by drone. Bring it in, unpack it, and find some room on the shelves. • Order stock from your supplier • Restock empty shelves • Unlock new and exclusive products • Make sure customers can find what they want 💴 Watch your money You still have rent, bills, stock, and ingredients to pay for. Change your prices, keep an eye on what is trending, and try to make a little more money each night. • Set your own prices • Follow trending products • Check your sales and expenses • Save enough to buy the store for yourself There is no bankruptcy screen waiting to punish you. A bad night just means you may need a better plan for the next one. ⭐ Read your customer reviews Customers can leave reviews after visiting the store. They notice if they found what they wanted, if the floor was clean, and if there was enough choice on the shelves. • Read new reviews on the store computer • Build your reputation • Learn what customers liked or couldn't find • Unlock more products as your reputation grows 🏪 Make the place yours You can change the colors of the store and kitchen, customize your character, and choose what to listen to during the shift. You can also read local posts, watch the street outside, and pet the cat when things are quiet. 🌃 See you at 11 PM Open the store, turn on the radio, and get ready for another night. Some nights will be busy. Some will be quiet. Both are fine.
