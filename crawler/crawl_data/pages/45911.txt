Out Now, Check out the Bundle! 🐈 About This Game Where is my pet? is a shelter management game about finding, sorting, and selling animals to caring new owners. Start with a run-down shelter, organize incoming animals, fulfill customer requests, search the city for lost pets, and turn your small business into a growing shelter empire. As the new owner of an animal shelter, your job is to take in animals looking for a home and place them in the correct spots. Customers will visit your shelter with specific requests, and it is up to you to find the right animal, complete the sale, and keep the business running. Make mistakes, and you may face penalties that can quickly put your shelter in trouble. Build Your Shelter Business Your dream is to grow from a small shelter into a full network of shelters, earn enough money to never work again, and build a huge home for your family. Every animal you sell brings you closer to upgrading your shelter, expanding your business, and creating the mansion of your dreams. Use your earnings to improve your shelter with helpful upgrades, more space for animals, visual improvements, and buffs that make your daily work easier. At the same time, upgrade your own home from a tiny shack into a mansion with a car, a lawn, and enough room for the whole family. Find, Sort, and Sell Animals Animals arrive at your shelter looking for a new owner. Drag them into the correct cages, keep everything organized, and pay attention to what each customer wants. Buyers are picky and will ask for specific animals, so giving them the wrong one can cost you money and push your business toward bankruptcy. Not every sale happens inside the shelter. You can also search for animals around the city using cameras, bring them into your shelter, and complete online orders for extra profit. Be careful while searching, because some animals hide very well. And if a customer asks for an animal you do not have? No problem. Just flick them away and move on to the next order. Features Run and grow your own animal shelter. Sort incoming animals into the correct cages. Sell animals to customers looking for specific pets. Avoid mistakes, penalties, and bankruptcy. Upgrade your shelter with buffs, more space, and visual improvements. Search the city through cameras to find hidden animals. Complete online orders to earn extra money.
