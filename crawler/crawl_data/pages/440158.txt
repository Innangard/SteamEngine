Content For This Game Browse all (1) Shop Simulator: Pet Shop - Original Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Shop Simulator: Pet Shop – Design the pet store of your dreams! Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of retail with adorable animals and make your customers happy. Order new pets and accessories on the PC and place the animals on cozy sofas or in enclosures so they can be discovered by customers. Make sure you have enough stock so every customer can find their desired pet. Customers bring their purchases to the cash register, you ring them up, and make sure everything runs smoothly. The further you progress in the game, the more cute animal friends you'll get to know to offer your customers for sale. You start with an empty store: Place shelves, decorations, and new animal friends exactly how you want. Choose from various furniture and set up the shop however you like. Expand your business, hire new employees, and automate restocking your animal inventory. Serve customers: Scan, ring up, and open more registers if necessary to keep your customers satisfied. Core Gameplay: Order animals and accessories on the PC, set up your shop, and earn money. Automation: Use automatic restocking systems and employees for more efficiency. Design: Decorate your dream shop with furniture and nice accessories. Expansions: Enlarge the shop and expand your range of animals and accessories. Multiplayer: Play with up to 3 friends, coordinate via voice chat. Let's go: Open your own pet store and make every customer happy!
