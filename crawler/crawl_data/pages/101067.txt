View Community Hub About This Content Included free with ORBIT: Bingo Roguelite ORBIT: Bingo Roguelite — Original Soundtrack Seven late-night jazz pieces, with walking bass and brushed drums, all built on one motif: Mara's. It is stated in full in the hall, stays in the background at the table, turns major at the counter, moves into the bass against the keepers, turns upside down when the lights go out, rises toward dawn in the dome and finally resolves.
