Download Cairn - On the Trail: Deep Water Free Download View Community Hub About This Content On the Trail is a series of free DLCs for the award winning survival climbing game Cairn. Set out on the ascent of a lifetime with pro-climber Aava: climb anywhere, plan your route carefully, and manage your resources to survive the unforgiving ascent of Mount Kami. On the Trail brings climbers new climbing locations and challenges. Find your route freely in the new spots, solve fresh climbing problems and explore iconic places as you follow the trail of a legendary climber. On the Trail: Deep Water, the first of the series, brings you 3 new climbing spots, and introduces water soloing: you can now climb over water, without the consequences when falling! Features: 3 new climbing spots: the cliffs of Haken and Ruka for the thrill of climbing freely in overhang above water! And the Lamu climbing gym with new problems to solve. Climb as Aava or Marco, including on Kami with the free roam mode. Experience how their climbing techniques and styles differ! Follow the trail of a legendary climber and discover the lost history and secrets of the new locations.
