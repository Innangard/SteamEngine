Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game You are sailors running a brand-new fish shop in a small town. Survive in waters teeming with fantastical creatures, catch fish, and sell them at your store. Work solo or team up with your friends to grow your business. Decorate your shop, track down creatures in mysterious waters, brave stormy seas, unearth hidden treasures on scattered islands and battle fierce monsters. Keep your customers happy, and they’ll reward you with plenty of cash. Remember, running your own shop is a blast, but if you're seeking greater thrills, set sail to explore the world and enjoy the grand adventures the open seas have to offer! Key Features: Catch Fish: Use your harpoon to hunt down and catch a wide variety of fish species. Process and Sell Your Catch: Cook, dry, or chop your fish. Sell them in your shop either raw or fully processed to satisfy your customers. Decorate and Expand Your Shop: Upgrade your storefront, crafting stations, and storage facilities. Hire new staff and attract a larger customer base to grow your business. Discover Treasures of the Sea: Battle formidable creatures out on the open seas or track them down for the ultimate hunt. Play Solo or With Friends (1-4 Player Co-op): Your shop needs skilled managers! Thanks to co-op multiplayer, you can team up with your friends to take your business to the next level together.
