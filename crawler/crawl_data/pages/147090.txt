View Community Hub About This Game YOU WORK AS A FLIGHT ATTENDANT Plane Airlines™, 1999. It’s your first day on the job. Board passengers, attend to their needs and make sure their last trip is as enjoyable as possible. Somehow, it all weighs upon you. WE ONLY SERVE HUMAN CUSTOMERS Shape-shifting entities may attempt to board. Check tickets, question passengers, and find a way to remove any unauthorized non-human visitors from the plane. The company will not be held accountable for any mistakes you make. You live with them. NEVERENDING MEMORIES WITH PLANE AIRLINES™ Working as a flight attendant means representing the company at all times. Passengers may require assistance inside the plane, outside the plane, and in places not listed in your training manual. Employees are expected to go the extra mile to save satisfy them. EXPECT THE UNEXPECTED Your flight may encounter turbulence while you are on duty. Whether it is annoying passengers, falling suitcases or [REDACTED], the company expects you to remain calm and continue service with a smile.
