What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We plan to use Early Access to fine tune mechanics and game balance as well as engage with players on all aspects of the game's design. Our ultimate goal is to create a strategy game that is accessible, but also challenging to master with an intriguing story and persistent collectibles. We look forward to hearing community feedback as we continue to ship new content and features.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect to launch the release version of the game Winter 2026 or Spring 2027.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We expect to add the following to the game before release: The fourth and final planned era of the game, the Paladite Era. - Done Additional possible endings triggered by buildings in this final era. - In Progress Advanced mech units to help crush the raiders once and for all.- Done Additional points of interest variants (forest, scrap piles, sink holes, ruins, etc) to add additional variety to the game's randomly generated map. - In Progress Additional Minor Artifacts to collect for more lore and bonuses. - Done Additional Founder Artifacts beyond The Beacon to collect. Like The Beacon, these can be equipped at Town Halls and provide large bonuses. - In Progress Additional music tracks created by the game's composer, Rogue VHS. - In Progress Additional Steam Achievements - In Progress ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “You can currently play all 4 eras of the game and reach 1 of the planned 4 possible endings. You can also begin the task of collecting artifacts. These persistent collectibles provide in-game bonuses to help conquer higher difficulty levels, they reveal parts of the game's story, and they will later unlock a new ending. The 48 of 52 planned artifacts currently in the game will require multiple playthroughs to discover.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price may increase at the end of Early Access when additional content and features have been added.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We will be checking the Steam forums for your thoughts on the game and any issues we might be able to help resolve. SURVIVE a vast frozen wasteland in the far future, 4087 AD. FOUND a new civilization using a mysterious artifact. BUILD a variety of different structures across multiple distinct eras EXPAND your territory to harvest new resources. RESEARCH technologies to improve your structures. DEFEND against raider attacks with defensive structures and fortifications MOBILIZE convoys to defend your towns, attack raider camps, and explore ancient ruins. SALVAGE vehicles, survivors and resources from the wastes. COLLECT powerful artifacts and learn Earth's forgotten history. LISTEN to the original soundtrack composed by synthwave producer Rogue VHS.
