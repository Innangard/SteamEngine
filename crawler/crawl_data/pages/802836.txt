This pack contains a lineup of premium vehicles offering you more spawns in battle, which in turn will net you more Research Points! The pair of premium Tiger H1 heavy tanks are inspired by Tiger tanks on the Eastern and Western fronts, with aesthetic elements inspired by each theatre. These Tigers both feature some additional track armor, and greater gun depression over the corners of the hull due to the removal of the anti-infantry mortars. Aside from this, they both feature the same legendary 88 mm cannon with destructive APHE rounds! Protecting the skies is the premium Ostwind II, complete with camouflage netting and Ostketten tracks, increasing its mobility over rough terrain. Its pair of 37 mm Flak44 cannons can equip both APHE and HE rounds, allowing you to shred lightly armored vehicles and disable heavier ones, and take aircraft out of the sky with only a few hits!
