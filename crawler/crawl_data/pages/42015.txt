Our signature thick-painted hand-drawn art style makes every frame wallpaper-worthy - the game's true visual hallmark. Game Content 10 unique storybook worlds + 1 secret chapter、30+ interactive NPC characters、1,200 collectible items to discover ✦ Gameplay Features ✦ 1. Main Quest Progress through the narrative by collecting yarn balls hidden across environments to unlock the game's true ending. 2. Side Quests Help vibrant NPC characters by completing their item collection requests in different scenes. 3.
