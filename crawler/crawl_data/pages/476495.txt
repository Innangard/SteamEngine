View Community Hub A Hand-painted Style Puzzle Game Escape the Mysterious Circus Woolly boy is accidentally trapped in the "Big Pineapple Circus" and reunites with the yellow dog "Qiuqiu", on his way to find a way out of the circus. One person and one dog help each other in the process of exploring and deciphering the secrets, and finally escape from the circus together. Discovering the Circus Story Meet various characters, learn and experience all the interesting stories they have experienced and are currently experiencing, and help and rescue these people and animals who are trapped in the circus for different reasons. Switch Between Two charactersTo Solve The Puzzle Switch between woolly boy and Qiuqiu freely to handle special puzzles in different scenarios; woollyboy and Qiuqiu need to cooperate with each other and utilize their respective strengths to find objects and solve puzzles in order to solve the ever-changing puzzles. Plenty of Props And Mini-games Hundreds of props and dozens of built-in mini-games, combined with specific game scenarios to set challenges, very much testing the player's thinking and associative skills, so that the player will experience the unique fun of the object-seeking puzzle game. About This Game 'Woolly boy and the Circus' is a hand-painted style puzzle game. The game mainly tells the story of the protagonist, Our woolly boy, who is trapped in the "Big Pineapple Circus" and searching for a way out. Along the way, the player encounters a yellow dog named "Qiuqiu". The two work together, complementing each other and relying on each other to explore and solve puzzles, and ultimately escape the circus.
