But beware! Deadly traps, mad monsters, and even humans infest the labyrinth. Worse, it seems the place is only growing! Dice 'n Goblins is a dungeon-crawler RPG that lets you discover 10 interconnected levels with classic grid-like controls. Walk, run, jump, swim, take stairs, and fall across different floors! Meet a varied cast of inhabitants and maybe make some friends. Use dice in combat Fight with your dice in turn-based combat. Luck is not something you can rely on. Only your wits will lead you to victory. Change the result of dice throws by re-rolling. Transform dice during combat using skills. Unleash unstoppable combos to defeat monsters. Find the best loot Crack open chests and discover powerful new dice to add to your arsenal. Find rare items as you go deeper in the dungeon to upgrade your equipment. Obtain gold from defeated monsters and use it to purchase new gear at the dungeon merchant. Build yourself a unique dice pool and unlock devastating skills! Game Features Classic Dungeon-Crawler : Explore the dungeon using first-person grid-based movement that will remind you of old-school RPGs. Dice-Based Combat : The dice define how you can attack and defend against monsters. Not everything is luck! You can re-roll and change the outcome using skills. Vertical Maps: Each of the 10 maps of the game includes multiple levels. Climb stairs, take elevators, or drop between them to explore. Dangerous Monsters : Defeat over 50 different types of monsters and epic bosses who roam in the dungeon. Infinite Builds : Use more than 60 unique dice and 22 powerful skills to customize your fighting style. Charming Characters : Meet the cast of strange but cute characters as you progress on your quest to find your place in the dungeon. Two Endings : Will you manage to get to the true ending? Your choices determine your fate. Paper Graphics : Fall in love with the hand-painted paper figures that live in a 3D world.
