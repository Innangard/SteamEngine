MYSTERIOUS DISAPPEARANCES AND ANOMALIES The events unfold in the near future. In the Ural Federal District, mass disappearances of people are occurring, accompanied by geomagnetic disturbances and strange atmospheric phenomena. YOU ARE THE SOF COMMANDER You are assigned to investigate the incident sites and uncover the reasons behind what’s happening. But the mission takes a darker turn when unknown creatures launch an attack on your squad. WHO ARE THESE CREATURES? As you progress, you’ll have to uncover who these aliens are, where they came from, why they are abducting people and stealing weapons — and how to fight them. TACTICAL COMBAT AND STRATEGY Use different combinations of weapons and abilities, build your tactics — every decision you make can impact not just the mission, but the fate of all humanity.
