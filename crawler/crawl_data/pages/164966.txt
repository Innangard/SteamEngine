View Community Hub Reviews “I loved Robot Arena Survivors despite a slow start; you need to get through the learning phase and unlock drones to really grasp the essence of the game.” 9/10 – Nihotaku About This Game Robot Arena Survivors is a single-player arena auto-shooter where you're fighting against waves of hostile robots using a powerful arsenal made of hover tank units. Customize your own set of abilities and dismantle your enemies to claim your fame into the leaderboards. Use any of the different available hover tank units to get the job done. They all have their own characteristics and weaponry! Build your own ability deck by choosing from various abilities to unlock, fine-tuning your gameplay experience and creating your own loadouts. Gather the materials dropped by defeated enemies to level-up and unlock powerful rewards while fighting off the swarms of robots! Fight against hostile robots from various locations, in different game modes, and challenge yourself to reach the top 20 of the best pilots! A set of 5 arenas to discover with their own design, hazards and robotic enemies. The choice between 3 different challenges per arena : a standard mode without ranking, and two ranked modes : Endless and Protect the Core, with their own dedicated online leaderboards . A selection of 8 unique hover tank units with their own advantages and weaponry. More than 20 different abilities to choose from in order to customize and build your own ability decks . A total of 13 base stats to permanently upgrade by progressing through the game. Tens of Steam Achievements to unlock! A dual-stick shooter : respecting the traditional bullet-heaven (or reverse bullet-hell) genre, the game doesn't feature any manual aiming or weapon activation. A scenario/campaign based game : the game is meant to be enjoyed for short or long sessions of gameplay where you can freely disconnect your brain and dismantle hundreds of robots with your tanks. A multiplayer game : the only way for you to compare your performance with others is by using the various available online leaderboards associated to the game modes and arenas. The game will not receive any multiplayer capabilities.
