The religion known as Logic Church has convened the Selection to Godhood. Thirteen participants, each bearing a Pathosync, are forced into a game of werewolf with their lives at stake. Whether gathering clues during exploration, exposing lies in debate, or facing death and truth again and again, the music walks with the player every step of the way. DISC 1 | 28 Tracks Opening with the main theme “Psycho-Sleuth,” this disc gathers the unease and everyday moments of the Selection’s early days in “The Invitation” and “New Arrivals,” the tense deduction and debate of “War of Words” and “The Final Debate,” and the tender melodies of character stories such as “Eirys’s Promise” and “Carefree Days.” DISC 2 | 14 Tracks Themes composed for each character’s Pathosync. Tracks such as “World Fabrication,” “Total Recall,” and “Reality Loss” give each ability its own voice, before “God of Logic” brings the album to a close.
