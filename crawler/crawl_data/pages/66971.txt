Stock the shelves with medical supplies, diagnose zombie customers and give them the correct cures for some cash and rewards. Combine ingredients to create potent cures that can be sold and exchanged for a great price. Follow the measurements and get valuable survival rewards if you got it right. Unlock new resources, expand your capabilities, and upgrade your pharmacy through a skill tree that opens up new ways to grow and improve your operation.
