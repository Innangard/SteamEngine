Armed with imagination, spare parts, and just enough optimism, you’ll build unstable machines, solve physics-based puzzles, and explore handcrafted worlds. Whether you're bouncing across planets in a homemade catapult car or launching rockets with no pilot, Astronimo is all about joyful experimentation, co-op chaos, and interstellar fun. Snap together parts to create crazy machines - from rickety lifts to rocket-powered buses. Fail forward, break stuff, and try again. Team up with friends online or on the couch. Work together… or accidentally launch each other into lava. Explore bizarre planets like Turilla, Acara, Zaroth IV, and Nixnillon - each packed with puzzles, hazards, and secrets. Create your head, bling your suit, wave, dance, play music, or just be a dog in space. It’s weird out there - express yourself accordingly. Create your own planets and levels with powerful, easy-to-learn world editing tools. Start with the tutorial to master the art of considered stupidity, then share your contraptions with the universe - or explore the wild inventions of others. Each puzzle has endless solutions - just build what makes sense (or doesn’t). Gravity is optional. Sanity is not.
