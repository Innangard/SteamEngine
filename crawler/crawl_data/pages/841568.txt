In this game, we model internal forces like ethnicity, culture, language, and other internal political struggles. Lead any nation from near the dawn of recorded history in 3000BC, and into the far future. Civitas is an ever-evolving landscape of constant political shifts, misaligned ideas, clashing ideologies, and eventually, their armies! wrestle with the peoples of your land constantly. Upgrade your nation via your governmental policies and technology in order to rule and subjugate the entire planet Utilize the in depth country management and role playing tools to experience both real and fantasy history in ways you've never experienced before. Create rulers, rename and recolor countries, and pursue peaceful or aggressive expansion. Nations will remember their history with you and others, respond dynamically, and constantly change their structure as the world around them also changes. Each nation, at its base level, is a tower built on unstable foundations, forced to constantly change shape while always being at risk of collapsing due to forces both external and internal. The people of a nation may grow angry and rise up against their king! Agents of an enemy nation may implant revolutionary ideas within the people. Your poor choices in managing your nation may bring it to its knees!
