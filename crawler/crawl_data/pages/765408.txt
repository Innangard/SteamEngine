Composed by Eric Daniel Meyer, whose previous works include #1 K-Drama on Netflix spring 2025 "When Life Gives You Tangerines". Using a variety of physical synths allowed Eric to play "off the grid" without a metronome to compose the 4 main tracks of the game. Inspired by the Runescape soundtrack, he wanted to imbue the tracks with as much variety as he could. If you're looking for the mixtape music, check out the artist Carloman!
