Make every shot count Pick your target, judge the arc, and send a parcel home. Different receiving spots, colored parcels, and transport contraptions give you new shots to try and satisfying deliveries to string together. Buy upgrades you can feel Visit the Workshop to improve your cannon, air supply, and transport routes. Faster loading, multi-shot bursts, spread shots, and Rush turn careful single deliveries into a sky full of parcels. Spend your next payout on more firepower or a new way to deliver. Put playful routes to work Unlock rails, lifts, and conveyors that catch, carry, and redirect your parcels to residents beyond your cannon’s direct reach.
