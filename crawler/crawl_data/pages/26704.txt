Gratuito Ver centro de contenido Acerca de este juego ⚔ YOUR HEROES NEVER STOP. NEITHER DOES YOUR POWER. Firestone is an idle RPG about building a five-hero formation and pushing it through endless waves of enemies and epic bosses. Choose heroes whose abilities and resources work together. Equip them, refine your formation, break through the next wall, and turn yesterday’s impossible boss into today’s effortless victory. Battles continue while you are away, with gold, loot, and progress waiting when you return. When you are at the keyboard, use abilities, accelerate combat, and decide which upgrades will carry your army farther. ◆ BUILD YOUR PERFECT FORMATION Collect heroes with distinct abilities, combat roles, and resources. Experiment with formations, combine their strengths, and create an auto battler team capable of defeating increasingly powerful enemies. Level up heroes, equip and enchant gear, enhance jewels, develop talents, and unlock permanent upgrades. Every improvement makes your formation stronger and prepares it for the next boss. A wall today becomes tomorrow’s easy victory. 🌍 EXPLORE THE WORLD OF ALANDRIA Travel through a colorful fantasy world of dangerous forests, frozen lands, pirate territories, ancient ruins, and hostile strongholds. Advance across the campaign map, clear stages, defeat powerful bosses, and unlock new regions, activities, and progression systems as your army grows. ⚙ ALWAYS ANOTHER WAY TO GROW Firestone is an incremental RPG built around satisfying long-term progression. Research permanent upgrades, enchant equipment, command war machines, complete missions and expeditions, discover ancient artifacts, and unlock deeper layers of power. New systems open gradually as you advance, giving you time to understand each one before adding the next to your strategy. Your offline progress keeps rewards coming, but your choices decide how far your army can push. Choose which heroes to invest in, how to build your formation, and which upgrades will overcome the next challenge. 🐾 RECRUIT GUARDIANS. BUILD MORE POWER. Collect and upgrade Guardians, powerful companions that support your heroes and add new bonuses to your army. Each Guardian brings another long-term goal and another way to strengthen your formation. ★ PLAY YOUR WAY Let your heroes battle automatically while you work, relax, or step away. Return to accumulated rewards, upgrade your heroes and systems, and watch your team destroy enemies that once blocked your progress. When you want to play more actively, use abilities, click to accelerate combat, optimize your formation, manage resources, and choose the upgrades that will carry you farther. Whether you prefer idle games, clicker games, AFK progression, or deep incremental systems, Firestone lets you play at your own pace. 🤝 GROW SOLO OR WITH OTHER PLAYERS Play solo or join a guild and progress alongside other players. Take part in guild expeditions, join a fellowship, unlock shared bonuses, and pursue more rewards without losing control of your personal journey. Firestone’s online RPG features add cooperation and shared goals while allowing you to progress on your own schedule. 🔥 SEVEN YEARS OF UPDATES. 2M+ PLAYERS. Regular updates bring new heroes, progression systems, seasonal events, balance improvements, and quality-of-life features. With 15+ interconnected systems, there is always another goal waiting ahead.
