View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to the Lands of Fill! Kingdoms of the Dump is an SNES styled RPG set in a fantasy world of garbage, dreamed up by two full-time janitors. Join Dustin Binsley, the Trash Can Knight, as he travels across the five and half Kingdoms of the Dump. Fight fearsome, filthy foes, brave dangerous, dank dungeons, and take on the totally disgusting toxic grimelin army to save the Dump from all out war! - Turn based, grid combat - Timed Hits and no random encounters - Jumping/Platforming/Elevation (No small bush that blocks a path off that you could easily jump over) - Character Swapping on the field with unique abilities (Rust, Climb, Gun, Bubble, etc.) - Explore a Large Mode 7 World Map - A unique setting with creative environments and bosses Made with love, by those who sweep your floors.
