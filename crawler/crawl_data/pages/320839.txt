About This Game Work for Sunny Morning™ and sell products of the most varied natures! What kinds of products , you ask? Do not worry about it, your role is only to sell them. :) Each step of the conversation can increase or decrease your Rage, Depression, and SALES! :O Choose carefully which house to visit at each time of the day: The mood of each potential buyer varies with time! Balance your spending between Motel daily costs and items that help you deal with Rage and Depression. There are 36 DIFFERENT ENDINGS in the game!!!
