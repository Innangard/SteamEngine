View Community Hub Reviews “The idea of deliberately building a slightly inadequate defence is an entertaining twist on the usual tower defence mentality. There is also something appealing about hopping rapidly between Vikings, Romans, knights and Samurai rather than settling into one enormous campaign map.” Indie-cent Exposure “A satisfying blend of tower defense and incremental mechanics that rewards strategic planning and smart resource management across its god-themed progression system.” TheBigBoisNews About This Game A get in, get out, maximize profit and reputation game loop. No endless waves or grinding to 30 or 100 waves.. just fast contracts vs 5-10 waves, cozy vibes, and storm‑soaked immersion. Great soundtrack (each region has its own battle music). 1. Choose new contract strategically (3 tiers with different waves, enemies, gold and reputation rewards). 2. Save gold on towers, you will need it to buy God/saint spells vs Bosses. 3. Use up to 12 unique powerful God/saint spells with hotkeys 1,2,3 (they are expensive, do not waste them). 4. Complete and stack contracts fast (more reputation, more gold, more perks, more often) 5. Choose perks after each quick Kingdom Contract; 1 of 36 perks blessed by 12 Gods/saints. 6. Upgrade each perk 3 times; click icon to upgrade status to bronze, silver, gold. 7. Unlock region bonus perks when you increase your region reputation [100/200/300/400]. 8.  Increase your reputation with each Kingdom Contract. Become the Legend of the Kingdoms. If you like reading; A chill minimalist single screen contract based min maxing immersion where you deploy just enough magical towers across ancient kingdoms, barely defend castles in super short micro tactical battles, earn perks and hopefully gold, and increase your reputation to get better Kingdom Contracts. Lore; The great kingdoms of the ancient world are always being attacked, every week there is some attack on some castle, and a wandering defender-for-hire can often earn gold, blessings from the Gods and saints, and reputation across the Kingdoms. That defender is you: a mercenary whose reputation will echo from the snowy Viking fjords to the mystical Samurai isles. Choose contracts carefully, face easy to impossible odds, earn the favor of kings, emperors and gods alike.. and carve your legend across every empire. Kingdom Contracts: Tower Defense Incremental. You are the chosen Commander of the Ancient Kingdoms. Rise up. Become the Legend.
