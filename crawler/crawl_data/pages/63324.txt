Ride across the northern reaches, connect colourful cities, and complete tickets from the top of Iceland to the southern border of Denmark! Chase the Globetrotter Bonus, awarded to the player with the most completed tickets. Strategise with 40 train pieces instead of 45 to make connecting the Nordic cities more challenging, and tackle the Murmansk-Lieksa route, a new nine-train-long route!
