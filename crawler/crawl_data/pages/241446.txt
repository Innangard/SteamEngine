Copperhead has established 2 covert outposts right in the heart of Sydney’s bustling CBD. Infiltrate these fortified bases, uncover the Copperhead organization’s sinister plans and sabotage their operations. The Sydney Sneakabouts DLC features 2 new sprawling environments and adds 10 new missions to Espire 1’s virtual challenges game mode. These 10 missions offer variety, catering to fans of the stealth or ‘all guns blazing’ approaches. This pack will also appeal to expert Espire VR Operatives; 2 of these missions are the hardest Espire 1 missions ever made. The environments included in this DLC: Maxim Tower: a large abandoned skyscraper project just south of Sydney’s famous Circular Quay is now home to a sophisticated Copperhead spy network. 5 new missions are set in this environment. Entwine Construction Zone: A construction site that spans an entire city block serves as a front for Copperhead’s own Espire “Control Theater” R&D efforts. The Entwine site is one of Espire 1’s largest and most diverse environments with plenty to explore above and below ground. 5 new missions are included with this environment. The Sydney Sneakabouts Mission Pack adds 10 Virtual Challenge missions to the following Espire 1 game modes: Intel Takedown Elimination Person of Interest Hold Up Sneaking Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains some content that is not recommended for all audiences, including gun violence. There are some topics and scenarios in the story line that some players may have sensitivity to. Namely, Hostage situations, where the player must attempt to rescue hostages from armed guards. Espire is game where violence is not encouraged, and players have a non-lethal approach to every scenario. However, there is gun violence throughout the title. Our gameplay and release trailers give an indication of the type of violence present throughout the title. Players have the ability to disable blood effects in the 'display' options, found via the 'settings' menu.
