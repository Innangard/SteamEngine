Thank you for playing and for your support ❤️ About This Game Manage your resources as you build, survive, and stay ahead of the relentless storm on your tail. Unlock new epic buildings, build shields to protect your structures, or place turrets and go on the offensive. Brave blizzards and fiery tornadoes - and discover ways to neutralize them before they tear your bridge apart. Can you build the longest bridge and stay ahead of the danger? Settlers are the lifeblood of your bridge settlement. They staff buildings, keep production running, and make sure your resource chains don’t fall apart. Keep them fed, warm, happy, and healthy as the storm closes in, and plan housing and essentials so your community can grow instead of panic. As your settlement expands, assign specialized roles like farmers, scientists, and engineers to boost key buildings and unlock stronger systems. The right people in the right jobs can mean the difference between a smooth run and a collapse. This bridge isn’t just a crossing - it’s a moving, living community you’re trying to keep alive! Losing and restarting is part of the experience! Every run teaches you something new. Permanent upgrades carry over between attempts, making you stronger and helping you push farther each time. Recruit adorable companions along the way - whether it’s five unicorns or a whole zoo of critters (dogs, penguins, frogs, chickens, even an armadillo). Each pet grants a unique buff that can shape your strategy. As you progress, you’ll also unlock traits that offer meaningful bonuses, like extra starting resources or faster production. Travel across distinct biomes as you piece together clues about the storm’s origins. Each region brings new mechanics, new tools, and new threats - and forces you to adapt your strategy to survive: Tundra: Find ways to withstand freezing temperatures, manage heat, and keep your settlers from shutting down in the cold. Sky: Launch helicopter expeditions to reach far-off locations, gather valuable resources, and rescue anyone stranded there. Underwater: Mine scrap and salvage lost tech to manufacture repair bots and keep your bridge running even when the environment turns hostile. Every biome changes how you build, how you plan, and what you’ll need to overcome next. And it’s not all pressure: between runs, you can slow down on your own peaceful island - a cozy, disaster-free space where you use the resources you’ve earned to build your dream home base. Unlock cosmetics too, from glowing hair to fancy accessories, and personalize both your character and your bridge settlement in style.
