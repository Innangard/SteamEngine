Scintillating visuals and entrancing music that responds and evolves with your actions make Mixolumia a chill experience for casual players or an adrenaline rush for  hardcore puzzle fiends. Swap the Soundtrack Each included song has unique sound effects that harmonize with the music, giving the game a totally different feel depending on which track you choose. Remix the Colors Switch up Mixolumia's visuals by selecting from a wide range of lovely color palettes, or create and customize your own with the built-in color editor. Choose Your Experience Whether you want to chill out, or challenge yourself to intense, strategic gameplay, Mixolumia has a game mode to suit your mood. Accessibility Mixolumia has options to adjust or turn off all of its effects individually so as to be as accessible to players as possible.
