Slip & Skid combines precision driving and methodical planning to create your path to victory. Up to four friends can compete. It's up to you to alter the track and create the secrets that will make you finish first: Race, then earn points based on your finishing position. Choose from what the game will offer: a game modifier, an obstacle, a surface change, a trap, etc. Then place it wherever you want. Repeat until a player reaches the required score. To win, they must finish first in the next round . The other players must do everything they can to prevent it: finish before them or team up to destroy them. MAKE EVERY ROUND UNIQUE It's up to you to decide: ruin your opponents' lives, or make your own easier. Choose and place obstacles in between racing rounds, ranging from simple objects to complex animated weapons. Each object can be precisely placed to make it a devastating trap or a narrow shortcut. Simple yet advanced tools for quick and accurate obstacle placement in three dimensions, using a controller or a keyboard and mouse. Several obstacles allow you to transform the surface of the track and objects: asphalt, ice, dirt, mud, concrete, magma, and sand. The environment, scenery and music change during the game depending on the surfaces that make up the track. DRIVE FULL SPEED Will you show honor by trusting your driving skills? Aerial control allowing flips, rolls, and driving on walls. Cars behave differently depending on the surfaces they drive on. Do you prefer more radical methods? Push your friends off the track and into traps with arcade-style, physics-based driving: drifts, accelerations, jumps, flips and more. Use obstacles and their various effects to create insane shortcuts. Driving altered by various modifiers : increasing reverse speed, adding bonuses when doing a flip, etc. Track disrupted by various modifiers : swap the start and finish lines, transform all surfaces, etc. CONTENT A wide selection of tracks, many obstacles and modifiers to choose from. Customize your vehicle with accessories, designs, and paints that you can unlock throughout the game and in different game modes. Game statistics: obstacles placed, and content unlocked. SINGLE PLAYER Time trial mode with a ghost to beat on each track, unlocking an accessory. WITH FRIENDS Editable rules. Up to 4 players online and/or locally in split screen. Note: There is no matchmaking system. Friend lobbies only.
