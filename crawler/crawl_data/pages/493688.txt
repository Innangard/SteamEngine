The long and bloody conflict of Veridian Succession left the Empire of Veridia scarred, its people subject to brutal hardships. Under the reign of Empress Florina, the vicious cycle seemed to be broken, until a rogue General abducted her from the capital, mustering his own armies and raising the flag of rebellion. You, a young academy graduate, will command an expedition against these forces… a conflict that will set off a chain of events and shake the very foundations of the world. 2D Turn-based Strategy RPG Squad-based player army Story-rich 30-40 hour Campaign with huge replay value 50+ Character classes for your troops Massive depth of tactics you will learn through natural play Limitless possibilities for how to build your characters and squads The gameplay focuses on grand-scale operations, outwitting and out-planning the opponent, while individual battles are carried out automatically and are thrilling to watch unfold.
