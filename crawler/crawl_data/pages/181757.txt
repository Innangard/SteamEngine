Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Wonderia is an open-world survival sandbox game with online co-op, base building, exploration, combat, puzzle dungeons, and a strange sense of humor. Sandbox and co-op games are shaped by how people actually play them. Through Early Access, we want to better understand what players enjoy, what they need more of, and which parts of Wonderia should grow first.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Wonderia to remain in Early Access for approximately 12 to 18 months. It is difficult to give an exact timeline for a sandbox game, so this may change depending on development progress and community feedback.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version will include more content, more NPCs, more strange discoveries, more building options, more weapons and equipment, and more reasons to keep exploring the planet. We also look forward to hearing players’ suggestions during Early Access, especially around co-op, home building, NPC housing, progression, balance, and future content priorities.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version includes the core open-world survival sandbox experience across three complete biomes, with exploration, gathering, crafting, building, combat, equipment progression, creature transformation, and online co-op for up to 8 players. Players can explore large alien environments, discover ruins, challenge handcrafted puzzle dungeons, collect and try different weapons and equipment, meet strange creatures and NPCs, and build a home base alone or with friends. The game is still in development. Some areas, such as NPC housing, base-building progression, economy progression, balance, UI/UX, and overall polish, still need more real player feedback and continued improvement.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. We plan to increase the price when Wonderia reaches its full release. During Early Access, we will continue expanding the game and improving the overall experience. By the time of the full release, Wonderia should include more content, features, and polish, so the price will be adjusted accordingly.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Early Access means building Wonderia together with the community. We will stay in touch with players through the Steam Community Hub and Discord, and we sincerely welcome everyone to share their feedback, ideas, and expectations with us. Tell us how you play, what surprised you, what felt frustrating, and what you hope to see next. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Roadmap About This Game Explore 'Wonderia', a unique open-world game set on an alien planet. Your journey combines survival, creativity, and exploration in a dynamic, procedurally generated environment. Discover a vast, ever-changing sandbox world. Uncover resources and secrets hidden across diverse landscapes, each offering unique challenges and opportunities. Build and customize homes for space travelers, influencing them to make this world their new home. Your constructions directly affect the planet's landscape and ecosystem. Delve into mysterious dungeons scattered across the planet. Navigate through complex labyrinths and uncover hidden treasures in these subterranean realms. Cultivate an alien garden, planting seeds collected on your travels. Delve into the art of fishing, capturing exotic aquatic species in hidden nooks of the planet. Experiment with your harvests and catches in the kitchen, concocting culinary creations that offer surprising benefits. Enjoy 'Wonderia' solo or with up to 7 others in multiplayer mode. Work together or compete in constructing, exploring, and surviving on this alien planet. 'Wonderia' offers a blend of adventure and creativity, where your decisions shape the world and your experience in this alien landscape.
