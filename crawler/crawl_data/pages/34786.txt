Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “In Early Access we would like to invite players to get to know the world of StarRupture and most of the gameplay mechanics we’ve planned for them. Basing on our experience from our previous title, Green Hell, we are huge advocates of working along with the Community to create the best gaming experience which - as a result - is almost tailor-made to our Players.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to make StarRupture stay in Early Access for about a year from its initial release. Our goal is to provide the best quality of the game to our players. We want to use the Early Access stage to cooperate with the Community on the feedback, fine-tune and polish the gameplay experience and - when we’re certain that we’ve achieved the best version of the game possible - leave Early Access.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In StarRupture in Early Access we intend to offer the majority of gameplay mechanics we’re planning for the full release to be polished and ready to enjoy. This will include an up-to-4-player co-operation mode in which you will be able to build, fight and explore a vast part of our rich open world. The full release is planned to contain more biomes, enemies, buildings and mechanics, along with a compelling end game. In Early Access we plan on constantly adding new content and enriching the gameplay along with close cooperation with our Community.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current stage of our game already allows for world exploration, gathering resources, building machines that mine, harvest, create and assemble. All of this, along with combat mechanics and survival elements can be played both in single-player mode and in up-to-4-player online co-op.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We reserve the possibility of increasing the price of the game as the game expands.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Gathering feedback is a crucial part of enhancing and tuning our previous title, Green Hell. Similarly, during Early Access we would like to invite the Community to join our development journey and listen to their feedback and suggestions on StarRupture given through Steam forums, our Discord server and other social media channels.” Read more Downloadable Content This content requires the base game StarRupture on Steam in order to play. Supporter Pack for StarRupture grants you extra items for the game and lots of digital goodies. Supporter Pack includes: - In-Game Bonus Items; - Digital Artbook; - Digital Soundtrack; - Digital Goodies Pack. Digital Artbook (in a PDF format) provides an in-depth look at StarRupture’s production process. From early sketches and concepts to nearly final renders, this vast album will certainly awaken your imagination about the world of StarRupture and possibly unveil some secrets about things that are yet to come to the game. StarRupture Original Soundtrack , composed by Adam Skorupa (“Green Hell”), is an intergalactic journey through the futuristic, electronic music to another planetary system, that is both as familiar as it is eerie. The digital version of the Original Soundtrack contains over 1 hour of music in 19 tracks in 3 formats: MP3 (for convenience) and WAV / FLAC (for those eager to “hear more”). By purchasing the Supporter Pack, you’ll also receive the following extra in-game bonus items: - 1500 x Basic Building Material; - 1000 x Intermediate Building Material; - 150 x War Bonds; - 50 x Quartz Ore; - 20 x Supporter Survival Package; - 10 x Meteorite Heart; - 5 x Supporter Introductory Data; - 1 x Supporter Shieldgiver LEM; - 1 x Lesser Shieldgiver LEM; - 1 x Lesser Lifegiver LEM; - 1 x Lesser Endurancegiver LEM. After purchasing, the Digital Artbook, the Digital Soundtrack, and the Digital Goodies Pack will be available in the game’s installation directory. The in-game bonus items will be available in the player’s inventory, after finishing or skipping the tutorial section, upon launching each new game session.
