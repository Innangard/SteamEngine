Design and expand your new recycling center in this first-person simulation game. Take care of unloading, sorting and compacting the waste. The new level editor allows you to freely design the yard, place upgrades and set it up the way you like. Completely new shaders for containers and vehicles bring realistic graphics and more possibilities.
