View Community Hub About This Game Last Term is a spot the difference horror game. Trapped in a school, each floor appears the same. But is it? Look out for any anomalies to determine if it's different than the last. Progress to floor 0 to uncover how you ended up here. The final bell has already rung, but something won’t let go... Navigate through the school and familiarize yourself with the surroundings. There's 3 areas the game revolves around: Hallway Classroom Bathroom Uncover a variety of anomalies. Some  are subtle, others far more disturbing. Certain anomalies are interactable, leading to unexpected moments If you spot any anomalies, go downstairs If everything feels normal, head upstairs Making the right decisions will put you closer to reaching floor 0. The anomaly book allows you to track which anomalies you have encountered and work towards spotting them all!
