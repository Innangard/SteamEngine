In 2006, The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion was released onto the unsuspecting masses. Hailed as a groundbreaking RPG of its era, it was also well-known for its inscrutable Persuasion Wheel mechanic, which captured the hearts and minds of dozens, while causing immense confusion for the rest. In 2025, some game developers got together and hosted the first WHEELJAM, a game jam centered around the persuasion wheel mechanic. In 2026, WHEELJAM became an annual event with the second iteration, which included a version of Oblivion's lockpicking minigame. This collection represents a selection of 22 games from both years of the jam, all with marginal updates in order to make them a little bit more polished for release. Games Included Chamelon 'n' Ghouls Wheel Crisis Mission Un-Patented Grimoire of Oblivion Daemon Resources Department Scrap Race Backseat Driver SUPERSTARBUNNY Break the Wheel Wheelfox 64 SIDELANING Dawn of the Wheel Bewheelderment Sentine WheeGob A Contrario Dungeons of W'he'el Steering Conversations Wheel Big Time Fishing 3: Recast Handful of Lockpicks Wheelest Dungeon The Big Cheese Made by Humans, Released for Free Using generative AI at any point during the jam is forbidden and always will be. We don't just prefer human-made games: we mandate it. The goal of this bundle is to provide more visibility to the developers participating in the jam, so please check them out. We also feel it's against the spirit of a game jam to charge for these games, so we're releasing them for free, even with the updates we've added.
