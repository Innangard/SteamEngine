Află mai multe Ce au de spus producătorii: De ce acces timpuriu? „Scam With Your Friends brings together co-op teamwork, unpredictable conversations with fictional AI callers, and increasingly chaotic workdays. We want player feedback to help shape how these systems develop. Early Access gives us a way to learn which scenarios players enjoy, where the progression needs work, and how we can make the call-center experience more fun and reliable.” Aproximativ cât timp se va afla acest joc în acces timpuriu? „We expect the game to remain in Early Access for roughly two months, with a full release targeted for mid-December 2026. This estimate may change based on player feedback, the development of content and progression, and the stability of the experience. We plan to share updates as the timeline becomes clearer.” Cum se va diferenția versiunea completă de cea disponibilă în acces timpuriu? „We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. We also plan to refine balance, performance, and usability using player feedback. The scope and priorities may change during development.” Care este starea actuală a versiunii aflate în acces timpuriu? „The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required.” Jocul va avea prețuri diferite pe durata accesului timpuriu și după părăsirea acestuia? „We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release. We have not finalized full-release pricing, and if our plans change as the game develops, we will communicate any change before it takes effect.” Cum intenționezi să implici comunitatea în procesul tău de dezvoltare? „Players can share feedback, report bugs, and suggest ideas through our Steam discussions and Discord community. We plan to use that feedback to help prioritize improvements to the co-op experience, AI conversations, scenarios, and progression, and to share development updates as the game evolves.” Află mai multe Nu este disponibil în limba: Română Acest produs nu este disponibil în limba ta locală.
