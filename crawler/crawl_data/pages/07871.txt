Content For This Game Browse all (1) Leafy Corner Original Soundtrack $1.99 $1.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Run the plant shop of your dreams in Leafy Corner, a cozy shop management game where you grow, sell, and care for real-life houseplants! Step into the role of a plant shop owner. Grow plants, serve your customers, and create a warm, inviting space filled with greenery and charm. Watch your plants grow over time, unfolding leaf by leaf as you care for them. Not everyone knows what they’re looking for. Sometimes customers bring unusual or tricky requests. Finding just the right plant to fit their needs adds a fun challenge. Decorate your shop with a variety of unlockable items, from shelves to plant pots. Pick colors that fit your mood and create a space that reflects your unique style. KEY FEATURES Grow, care for, and sell 100+ unique plants. Discover plant variegations. Build and decorate your shop with a variety of pots, furniture, and decoration options. Fulfill orders and help customers. Complete tasks to progress your shop. Increase your shop’s reputation to unlock new content. Customize your character. What Leafy Corner does NOT offer: Hardcore management (deep economy, hiring staff). Advanced plant care. Storyline.
