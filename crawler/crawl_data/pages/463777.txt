And only a bear cub named Cherry blossom, named after the ancient wise tree, will be able to overcome the darkness. Before the last petal falls from the wise tree, the bear cub must have time to find and overcome the darkness and its inhabitants. One day, darkness overtook Japan, as the prophecy said. Many residents fell into despair and joined the ranks of darkness. In one of the remote villages, a bear cub was born - plump and nimble. He was born on the day of Sakura blossoming, and it was decided to name him in honor of this event. The little bear was mischievous, he liked to jump, when he went out into the yard with his friends, he jumped every now and then - sometimes he played tag with jumping over obstacles, sometimes tag, sometimes hopscotch. One day, the mother bear went to the temple to find out what kind of bad weather had come, why there was no harvest, why the sun was shining less and less often. The wise old man in the temple said that darkness had come, the prophecy had come true. You are about to go on a great adventure and learn history. The game is based on the short story "The Legend of a Little Bear Named Cherry blossom" by Sveta Sky. Some images, backgrounds, characters, and styles are created by artificial intelligence. Translations into other languages ​​were done manually using a translator. The developer and text author is Sveta Sky. The game is built using the Godot 4.5 engine. Detailed information about the freely available assets can be found in the description in the game itself, as well as in the game folder. Game controls: WSAD - forward, backward, left, right. Left mouse button - weapon. Space - jump. R - restart. The game doesn't have saves, but there are several autosave checkpoint slots in the main menu so you can return to a scene with a story choice.
