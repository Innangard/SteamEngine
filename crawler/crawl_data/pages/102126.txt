This time, it’s real. Play through Verity’s game while they come to life right beside you. Your goal is simple: stay focused, survive the game, and reach 12 points before it’s too late! But you aren’t just playing what’s happening on the screen. While you work toward your objective inside the game, Verity is waiting for you to get distracted.
