Contains 63 tracks from the game's dynamic soundtrack. The almost three hours of music has been mixed and reimagined for standalone listening. The soundtrack was recorded and composed at E-Studio in the mystical Sipoo forests, as a collaboration between the Finnish psychedelic/fusion rock group From Grotto and composer Niilo Takalainen . Vol. 1 contains selected tracks from the first areas of Noita, along with some sweet out-takes! Vol. 2 delves deeper into the Snowy Depths, Hiisi Base and more, without forgetting side biomes, secrets and additional out-takes! Vol. 3 takes you through the Underground Jungle, the electronic grooves of The Vault, the doomy Temple of the Art and The Laboratory, in addition to some less explored areas and themes.
