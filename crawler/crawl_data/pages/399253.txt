View Community Hub About This Game Retrieve what no one else dares to touch. As an agent of Secret Seekers, you explore forgotten dimensions and recover cursed artifacts. Each place hides its own secret - just don’t run out of time. Your mission is simple: find the objects you're told to find. But nothing about them is ordinary. Some are buried in darkness. Others whisper forgotten truths. Every item you collect pulls you deeper into the unknown. Each place hides a story no one was meant to hear. The objects you find unlock fragments of it -moments frozen in time, voices left behind. The more you uncover, the less sense it makes. Until it does. Each mission takes you to a different abandoned reality - broken, twisted, and barely holding together. These places weren't just forgotten. They were erased. You’re not supposed to be there. But you are. You’re not alone. Interdimensional guardians roam these places - and they don’t like intruders. Fight if you must. Hide if you can. But whatever you do… don’t get caught. You can’t control the mission. But you can control how you enter it. Every item, every perk, every decision matters. Load up, lock in, and hope it’s enough. Time is your greatest enemy. The more objects you choose to find, the less of it you’ll have. Explore fast. Decide faster. Fail, and it’s all lost. Discover your personal archive - a mysterious museum that preserves only the most important artifacts you've recovered. It’s a silent gallery of failures and triumphs. Watch it evolve with each expedition... • Challenging but Fair – No hints, no hand-holding. Fail often. Learn more each time. • Relax Mode – Explore without time pressure. • Dynamic Loadouts – Customize perks and items before every run. • Fragmented Storytelling – Piece together the past through objects and environments. • Reactive Spaces – Your museum evolves with each mission. • Tactile Interaction – Pick up, inspect, and sometimes mishandle what you find.
