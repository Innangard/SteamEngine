Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Like our previous opus Pirates Outlaws we have grown its world progressively. Its storyline is based by chapters and regions. EA informed players that the game is still in development and not completed fully. We chose Early Access to refine Pirates Outlaws 2 with real player feedback while we expand the game’s content and systems. Throughout development, we plan to add new chapters, regions, classes, companions, cards, relics, and Arena challenges, and Early Access allows us to balance and polish these features as they grow. We are committed to updating the game regularly and working closely with the community to ensure the final release meets our full vision.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Our forecast for adding the major planned content is estimated at around 1 year of development.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version is plan to expand within 3 chapters, divided by regions and maps to navigate. Each region has their own contents and secrets to discover, as well as dedicated cards & relics to collect. Major extensions and updates will contain additonal contents as hero, classes, companions, cards & relics.... . At least 6 playable heroes with 15+ Classes (Cursed, Alchemist, Chef, Musician, Herbalist, Explorer, Fortune Teller, Inventor, Pet Tamer, Wild...) . 7-8 Companions . 700+ Cards (Approximately) . 180+ Relics (Approximately) with new Outfits . Card "fusion" and "invention" mechanics . Hero "cursed", "specter" and "wild" classes functions . New races "Cursed" and "Machines" features” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game offer up to 3 playable heroes with 7 classes and 4 companions. Navigate the first region, with 5 varied areas through progressive difficulties, defeat the 9 sea masters, discover all secrets and challenge legendary creatures. Collect up to 250+ cards and 80+ Relics. Reach stage 10 of the Arena to challenge its Champion. Almost 70 achievements and trophies to complete. The game's playability has already reached 90%, while the final content, which is intended to be progressive, is approaching 30%.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “After the game is updated with enough content, we will increase the price.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “The community will play an active role in shaping Pirates Outlaws 2 during Early Access. We will collect feedback and suggestions from players, to discuss balance, share ideas, and report issues. Conquer the battles of the Arena to reach and defeat its Champion. ● Heritage Talent Tree - Strengthen the initial ability, boost each adventure journey ● Workshop - Craft Golden Cards, extend base cards effects ● Black Market - Ban and manage unwanted cards using your gemstones ● Equipment - Elegant or wild, customize your own pirate style In Pirates Outlaws' sea quel, a new era of piracy begins in New Elysia. The knowledge of inventors and alchemists shapes both the universe of the game and its game mechanics. Heroes have unlockable classes, each with a unique appearance, attributes, and starting deck (including ability cards). A companion can also be selected, and their ability card added to your initial deck for more advanced strategies. In Navigate mode, sail without limit from isle to isle to explore hidden islands, to collect cards and relics in the region. Defeat the 9 sea master’s bosses and legendary creatures. In the Arena, you fight against strong fighters to challenge its Champion. To dominate and pass each stage, you must improve your deck, optimize your relics, and choose powerful effects: Runestones. New combat system for improved gameplay experience. Mastery the Countdown assigned to end turn based on your hand cards. Manage enemies’ actions trigger whenever you Redraw or play cards. Upgrade your deck with the new three-in-one card upgrade system. Collect 3 identical cards to choose between two evolution paths, refining your deck while boosting power. Additional Contents In Pirates Outlaws 2: Heritage has extended experience with additional features and new mechanics to progressively discover. Gain level of Repute and knowledge, invest earned Gold and Gemstones at the Workshop to craft enhanced Golden Cards or banish cards at the Black Market. Discover your Heritage passives to improve strategies and attributes to defeat legendary creatures and become a Reputed Captain.
