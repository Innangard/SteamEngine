Swords fly in arcs, spears shoot straight, axes are heavy but hit hard, and scythes spin through the air. Each weapon also comes with its own unique abilities. Master them all and find your perfect arsenal! Even the slightest change in angle can dramatically alter your weapon's trajectory. Stop the Shot Gauge at just the right moment to deal extra damage! Master your angle and power to land the perfect shot! Each time you throw a weapon, the enemy's action counter decreases. When it reaches zero, they attack! But if your weapon returns to you, you'll trigger ONE MORE CHANCE! Throw the same weapon again without advancing the enemy's turn! Level up and clear stages to earn rewards and grow stronger. Use active skills and items, or raise your shield to block incoming attacks. Combine everything you find and create your own way to fight! You awaken with no memories—and a mysterious gauntlet attached to your right arm. Who are you? Collect Memory Fragments throughout the labyrinth to improve your starting abilities as you uncover the truth behind your lost memories and the mysterious gauntlet.
