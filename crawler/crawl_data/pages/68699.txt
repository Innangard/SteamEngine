Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Floating Paradise takes classic seek-and-find into full 3D. Instead of scanning a flat illustration, you're exploring an entire floating island diorama. Spin the camera, zoom into tight corners, and peek into every nook and cranny to track down the objects tucked away across the landscape. 360° orbital camera – Inspect the island from any angle, high or low, near or far. Handcrafted dioramas – Each island is packed with detailed hidden objects and little micro-scenes to discover. A full island, free – A complete, fully playable island experience at no cost. Calming soundtrack – Relaxing lo-fi music made for stress-free searching. Easy to pick up – Simple mouse controls and no fail states. The base game includes the first complete floating island at no cost. If you fall for it and want to keep hunting, more themed islands are on the way as DLC, each with a fresh environment, new hidden items, and new music tracks.
