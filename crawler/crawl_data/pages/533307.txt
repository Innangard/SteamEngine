About This Game WARNING: Intense visual motion and moving objects on the screen may cause dizziness or nausea. At the first sign of discomfort, it is recommended to pause the game and take a break. Dive into Perfect Order Are you familiar with that pleasant feeling when chaos turns into harmony? Welcome to a meditative game where sorting groceries becomes a true art form. Forget about the hustle and deadlines: take items from the conveyor belt and jewelry-accurately send them to the shelves, turning chaos into a benchmark display. Let every jar of strawberry jam, pack of aromatic tea, crispy buns, delicate cheeses, and neat packages of meat take their strictly designated places. And thanks to the random generation of products on the belt, you can restore this perfect order again and again — each level launch will give you a new meditative experience and dozens of hours of pure anti-stress. 🛒 Core Game Mechanics 1. Tame the Chaos In front of you is an endless conveyor belt of chaotically placed products that crave order. Control the flow in the way that is most convenient for you: scroll through the belt by turning the mouse wheel , use dragging with RMB (right mouse button), or simply press the A / D keys. 2. Find the Perfect Spot Empty racks and huge refrigerators await you all around. Click on the on-screen arrows or use the Q / E keys to quickly switch between them. Your goal is to find a specific slot for each item. Don't worry, you will definitely recognize it by the neat, semi-transparent silhouette hint on the shelf! 3. Arrange and Complete Levels Found the right spot? Grab the product from the belt and drag it to the shelf! Every precise placement not only brings you closer to perfection but is also instantly recorded on the progress counter in the upper left corner . Watch empty spaces fill up with even rows, step by step. 4. Stack Products Be attentive: some shelves are designed so that products on them must be stacked on top of each other in several tiers. Some items are placed strictly one by one, others in pairs, and some can be built into a stack of three pieces. You will easily understand this intuitively and through trial right during the arrangement process! Identical items can be gathered into neat stacks right on the conveyor belt and then, in a single motion, move the entire vertical stack to its rightful place, or combine them directly on the shelf itself. 5. Organize Space Without Limits Move products in the way that is most convenient for you, as there are no strict penalties for mistakes in the game. Swap items on the shelves, put them back onto the moving belt if you change your mind, or do several things at once. You can grab a product with the mouse and, while holding it in your hand, switch between racks and refrigerators using the Q / E keys. Total freedom for your inner perfectionist! 6. Create a Benchmark Display Feel true satisfaction when confusion retreats before your perfectionism. Perfectly aligned packages, even rows of delicacies, and aesthetically arranged sweets. You will experience that exact mental "click" of pleasure when absolutely everything takes its rightful place, and the refrigerators and shelves become a model to follow! ✨ Inside Awaits You: High Replayability: Every new level launch brings a completely random order of chaos on the belt. Products are shuffled anew, so you can replay your favorite levels dozens of times, and each time it will be a new meditative experience. Dozens of Types of Favorite Products: Sort aromatic breads, meat products, delicate cheeses, yogurts, jars of jam, cereals, tea, seafood, and vibrant gummies. Pure Visual Delight: Cute hand-drawn art, a soft palette, and kawaii, incredibly pleasing animations. Absolute Anti-stress: No timers, penalties for mistakes, or rushing. Play entirely at your own pace to a soothing soundtrack. The Magic of Sounds: A cozy audio accompaniment that turns every precise placement of a product on a shelf into a small victory.
