to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Bloodwatch, a short horror survival game developed by Gamingscape. When going out in a solo adventure, your ship has been sunken by an unexpected storm. You have been washed ashore by a wave. Waking up, you have realized you have been stuck in an unknown island. You search around to see is there any animal or people living in here. After a while, you see a group of people are torturing and beat one of the person to death. They have caught you watching them. You run away as fast as you can, hiding behind a lighthouse. You can hear the cult leader ordering every of his soldiers to find and kill you. You open your backpack, equipping the nightvision. Now you must plan a tactics, find the weapons on the island, kill the cult leader and all of his soldiers to escape this place. Find 5 types of weapons scatter around the island : Knife, Axe, Baseball Bat, Handgun, Crossbow. Each types of weapons have different amount of damage. You can be tired when running and attacking with melee weapons, so be careful. Enemies are very strong, don't fight them if there is 2 or more. Collecting apples to restore your health, each will restore near 1/3 of your health bar. Finding batteries to replenish your nightvision. Ammo and arrows are scarce, use it wisely. Finding 3 types of keys to open locking doors. Controls: I for Inventory, click on the weapon icon when you find them to equip or changing them. E to pickup items. Reloading gun and crossbow: I to Inventory, click the gun and the bullet magazine icon to reload with gun, click the crossbow then the arrow icon to reload with crossbow Replenish batteries: I to Inventory, click on the battery icon in the Batteries. Heal health: Go to Inventory then click at the apple icon. O for Ingame options, save and exit game. N for Nightvision, can clear a good amount of heavy fog. F For Flashlight. Left and Right Mouse for attacking with melee weapons, shooting gun, crossbow. W+ Shift to run, W, A , S,  D to move. The saving system: After save, if you die or exit the game, you will be back from the beginning, but still keep all your weapons and items. If you die without saving, the game ends. Press ESC to exit the game immediately. Use the tiny dot in the middle of the screen for easy pickups.
