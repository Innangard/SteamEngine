View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game GODS & GORE A roguelite tower defense game set in Greek mythology. Pick your god, collect mythological creatures, and defend you city. Expand each round to grow your defenses, powers, and combos to stop increasingly strong enemies. COMMAND Collect mythological creatures that persist between runs. Zeus' golden eagle and minotaur. Level them up so they grow stronger with every attempt. FREE BUILD Place your defenses and strategically build a deadly path for your enemies. Position matters: create chokepoints, stack upgrades, and turn your city into a fortress. Each wave, draft powerful building cards. Towers, barracks, walls, boosters - all have different strengths. VARIETY Artifacts, different map layouts, and randomized cards means no two runs are identical and you must adapt to the challenge with the hand you are dealt. Features: Card-based building system with infinite stacking upgrades Creature collection and power progression across runs Multiple gods with unique abilities Waves that scale until you die Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Contains stylized, low pixel violence and blood effects during combat.
