Altre informazioni Cosa dicono gli sviluppatori: Perché l'accesso anticipato? “Scam With Your Friends brings together co-op teamwork, unpredictable conversations with fictional AI callers, and increasingly chaotic workdays. We want player feedback to help shape how these systems develop. Early Access gives us a way to learn which scenarios players enjoy, where the progression needs work, and how we can make the call-center experience more fun and reliable.” Per quanto tempo questo gioco rimarrà in accesso anticipato? “We expect the game to remain in Early Access for roughly two months, with a full release targeted for mid-December 2026. This estimate may change based on player feedback, the development of content and progression, and the stability of the experience. We plan to share updates as the timeline becomes clearer.” Quali saranno le differenze fra la versione completa e quella in accesso anticipato? “We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. We also plan to refine balance, performance, and usability using player feedback. The scope and priorities may change during development.” Qual è lo stato attuale della versione in accesso anticipato? “The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required.” Il prezzo del gioco varierà durante e dopo l'accesso anticipato? “We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release. We have not finalized full-release pricing, and if our plans change as the game develops, we will communicate any change before it takes effect.” Come pensi di coinvolgere la Comunità durante il processo di sviluppo? “Players can share feedback, report bugs, and suggest ideas through our Steam discussions and Discord community.
