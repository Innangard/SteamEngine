Putting is easy and natural, the physics are completely realistic, and a wide variety of courses provide the perfect challenge for hardcore golfers and casual players alike. COURSES Each 18-hole course is carefully crafted to replicate the feeling of classic mini golf in a larger-than-life location. Interconnected holes encourage you to explore the entire course, search for lost balls and try out your own trick shots using anything and everything in the environment. ⠀ HARD MODE Once you master a course, you're only halfway there! Hard Mode isn't just cosmetic, every hole has been modified, and sometimes completely redesigned to provide an entirely new course. In the redesigned courses, there will be difficult challenges, a Foxhunt leading you to a specialized putter, and a completely different aesthetic than the standard version of the courses. MULTIPLAYER Walkabout Mini Golf is better with friends! Full crossplay lets up to 8 players join a private room with voice chat no matter what platform they're playing on (some limits to Pocket Edition do apply). Join our Discord for regular tournaments, or host your own by simply entering the same room name as anyone you want to play with! FEATURES 14 unique 18-hole courses to master (plus additional DLC courses available). Unlock Hard Mode for more challenging versions of all courses. Play solo, a 1v1 online Quick Match, or create a private room with up to 8 players. Play DLC courses with those who own them for free with GUEST PASS . Extremely accurate physics that feel just like the real thing. Over 250 collectible golf balls hidden throughout the courses (plus more in additional DLC courses). Support for Teleport and Smooth locomotion, as well as the ability to fly. Hone your skills with a driving range, practice green and other features. Unlockable commemorative putters for each course through Foxhunts on Hard Mode. Customizable avatars featuring lip sync and over a QUADRILLION combinations. Full crossplay between all VR platforms. Supports English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Steam Frame Verified. System Requirements Minimum: OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Core i5-4590/AMD FX 8350 equivalent or better Memory: 12 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970, AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent or better.
