But be cautious; the deeper you venture into the Norček facility, the more questions begin to surface. What is Norček trying to achieve? Why were you brought here? Key Features A shared adventure: When fate takes an unexpected turn, two Explorers find themselves trapped inside a mysterious facility. With only each other to rely on, they must uncover its secrets and decide what Tomorrow will hold. Asymmetrical co-op puzzling: No two Explorers experience the facility the same way. Trust your partner, communicate through your walkie-talkies, and combine your unique perspectives to solve puzzles that challenge the rules of time, gravity, and space. Two players, two distinct roles: You and your partner each have a different role to play, with your own high-tech tool at your disposal. Work together and use your equipment in creative ways to overcome obstacles that neither of you could face alone. A mystery in an imagined 80s future: Explore Norček Industries and uncover the secrets behind its experiments and missing research team. Journey through a retro-futuristic world brought to life by striking environments and a nostalgic synthwave soundtrack. The We Were Here Series Every game in the We Were Here Series is a standalone online co-op adventure – the stories are connected but you don’t need to play them in order. Playing with your friend and communicating well is key to the escape room series: asymmetrical puzzles give players different perspectives and can only be solved together.
