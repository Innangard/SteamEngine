Gemblades is a single-player deckbuilder inspired by classic tabletop deckbuilders where you draft a deck of powerful heroes called Gemblades and battle against the forces of Onyxion, the Dragon Lord. Each Gemtype has their own identity! Every Gemblade is empowered by the magical properties of their own Gemtype. Emerald: Growth and balance in everything. Ruby: First on the battlefield and selfless to a fault. Sapphire:  Seekers of knowledge, legendary tacticians. Show your skill on the Global Leaderboards! Hire Gemblades and combine their power to defeat the minions of Onyxion to save the world from his invasion! There's also a Global Leaderboard where you can compete with others in the multiverse of Gemblades! Join our Discord to stay up to date with announcements and learn about future tournaments. See you there, Gemblade!
