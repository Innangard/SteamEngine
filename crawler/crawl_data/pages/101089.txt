View Community Hub About This Game Moomin: Midsummer Madness is a delightfully playful point-and-click adventure inspired by Tove Jansson’s cherished Nordic fantasy novel, Moominsummer Madness . Play as Moomintroll and Little My as they explore a curious old theatre caught in a great flood. The journey will be filled with endearing encounters, familiar faces, and a touch of mischief. A hand-painted Moomintale Lovingly crafted against richly illustrated backdrops inspired by Tove Jansson’s original artistry, Moomin: Midsummer Madness ’s story unfolds across chapters filled with charming details for Moomin fans with a keen eye. An adventure for all ages Light puzzles and fun mini-games infuse each chapter with whimsy and small victories. Don’t worry! The hint system will make sure each challenge is relaxing and approachable. An uplifting fable To find their way home, the Moomins need to rely on kinship and open hearts, not to mention a little theatrical magic. Above all, Moomin: Midsummer Madness is a tale of curiosity, sympathy, and the small wonders around us. Game features Point-and-click adventure starring Moomintroll and Little My Beautiful hand-painted world inspired by Tove Jansson’s Moominsummer Madness Rich music soundtrack lovingly composed for this game Relaxing, cozy gameplay designed for all ages Variety of beginner-friendly puzzles and charming mini-games Accessible built-in hint system Nordic storytelling brought to life by memorable characters and scenes Moomin: Midsummer Madness is equally enjoyable for longtime fans of the Moomins or fairy tale lovers about to set foot for the first time in their wondrous valley.
