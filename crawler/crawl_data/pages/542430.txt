In The Hive , a strategy game infused with RPG elements, you’ll guide your swarm through a landscape filled with ancient civilizations, colossal creatures, and hidden wonders. Strengthen your colony, evolve your brood, and uncover the secrets buried beneath the ruins of a lost world. As your forces grow, so does your understanding of the tragic history lurking within these forgotten caverns—until the echoes of the past reveal what truly lies beyond. In this hostile yet mesmerizing realm, survival is never guaranteed, and every strategic choice will determine the fate of your swarm. DEMO FEATURES First Story Mission: Play through the opening chapter to learn the basics of commanding your swarm, gathering resources, and expanding your nest. Build & Expand: Construct and fortify your underground nest, assembling powerful forces to conquer new territory. Harvest & Evolve: Collect DNA from fallen foes to unlock advanced units, unique abilities, and fresh strategies. Equip Your Creatures: Scavenge relics of ancient civilizations to outfit your swarm with powerful items and gear. Will you endure the trials of New Eden and reshape its destiny, or be devoured by the dark secrets within? Play the demo now to find out!
