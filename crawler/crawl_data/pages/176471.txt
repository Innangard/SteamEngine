View Community Hub About This Game Destruct Blocks is a captivating isometric puzzle game that challenges your strategic thinking and precision. Your goal is to destroy blocks of the correct color while avoiding others, making each move count. As you progress, the puzzles become increasingly complex, requiring sharp problem-solving skills and careful planning. With intuitive controls and vibrant, colorful visuals, Destruct Blocks offers a fun yet satisfying experience for puzzle enthusiasts. Unlock achievements as you progress through the levels, and enjoy the game's seamless experience with cloud saves and controller support. Key Features: Strategic Puzzle-Solving : Destroy blocks of the correct color while avoiding others, in puzzles that challenge your logic and precision. Increasing Difficulty : Face progressively more challenging levels that test your spatial awareness and problem-solving skills. Vibrant Isometric Graphics : Enjoy colorful and visually appealing levels that make the gameplay more engaging.
