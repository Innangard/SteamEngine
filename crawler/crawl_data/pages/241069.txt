Using the mighty Creation Glove, build structures, clone objects, paint buildings, and lay down roads, then use the human waypoint tool to populate your creations. Share your creations via Steam Workshop or visit islands from other players. You can choose to be benevolent rulers, or wreck havoc on your newly formed civilizations – it’s your choice!! Pick from a huge selection of more than 400 objects to create and populate your little (or big) islands. Tentacular Playgrounds let`s you experience the sandbox part of Tentacular.
