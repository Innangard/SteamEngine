These are just a handful of features that define the helicopters in Airport CEO, a new type of vessel that can now traffic your airport! Featuring iconic models like the Bell 206 and the Boeing CH-47 Chinook in a passenger configuration, the Airport CEO Helicopters DLC showcases a quintet of distinct helicopter models that have played a pivotal role in aviation. This DLC also enables you to operate a new type of airport: Helicopters only! Of course, a hybrid configuration is also supported per your airport's requirements. New features: Five new helicopters Two new airlines The small helipad The medium helipad Helicopter only airport operations Helicopter research projects Helicopter incidents The following helicopter models are included in the DLC: Robinson R44 Bell 206 Eurocopter AS350 Sikorsky S-92 Boeing CH-47 Chinook (passenger configuration) The helicopters are brought to life in Airport CEO complete with stunning, authentic and accurate graphics and are accompanied by two new airlines. Downloading this DLC will add the helicopters to the new airlines HeliJet and BladeLift. The helicopters can, just like any other aircraft in Airport CEO, be modded with custom liveries using the official MDK. In order to traffic the helicopters you will need to research the helicopter projects and sign contracts with the airlines HeliJet and BladeLift.
