The Void has plenty of wildlife of its own—all that's left is to grab your gear, try your luck, and see what you can catch. Catch different species of fish and earn Scales with every one you reel in. Upgrade your rod and reel, and use familiar resources as bait. The Scales you earn can be spent not only on fishing upgrades, but also on new decorations for your train. Big Catch adds a new theme for walls and cabins, along with several new interior items to give you even more ways to shape the look and feel of your train. And once you've unlocked every upgrade and reward, those leftover Scales won't go to waste. Spend them on Reward Bags of different tiers to stock up on extra resources. Traveling with friends? Then a good catch is also a chance to show off. The leaderboard will reveal who really knows their way around a fishing rod and who's left telling stories about the one that got away.
