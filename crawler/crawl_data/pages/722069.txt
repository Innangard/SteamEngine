View Community Hub About This Game The incremental auto battler. Build an orc army, watch it fight, and make it unstoppable! Orc Incremental puts you in command of a growing warband of dark forces. Recruit all kinds of units, set formations, draw powerful cards, and ascend for permanent power bonuses to make every run hit harder than the last. Idle progression meets real tactical decisions, and it never stops scaling. DRAFT YOUR ARMY Draw cards to add units and totems to your warband. Units come in multiple rarities and fill different battlefield roles, and the right mix is what gets you past the next wall. Totems provide passive bonuses that can change how your whole army performs. Every card you pick shapes your composition and pushes your run in a different direction. SET YOUR FORMATION. LET THEM LOOSE. Your army marches forward and fights on its own. When your strategy is working, you watch it rip through wave after wave. When you hit a wall, rethink your lineup or swap in new units. CAST SPELLS. BREAK THROUGH. You can support your army with spells. Drop a Blizzard to slow a boss that's tearing through your frontline, or land a Fireball at the right moment to close out a fight you were about to lose. ASCEND. RETURN STRONGER. At some point your army stops being enough. That's when you ascend (Dark Rebirth). Reset your run, keep permanent bonuses (for damage, health, gold, etc.) and unlock new units and challenges that weren't available before. The early game opens up as your power grows, and the later milestones keep pushing you further. Every Dark Rebirth makes you stronger and opens up new goals. KEY FEATURES - Build and scale an orc army from a single grunt to an unstoppable warband - Set up your formation, push until you hit a wall, then rethink and reorganize - Draft units and totems with different rarities, roles, and synergies to shape your composition - Cast spells during tough battles to turn the tide at critical moments - Ascend for permanent power bonuses, new unit unlocks, and challenges that change how you play. Every ascension you get to choose a chieftain that makes every run unique. - Go offline. Come back to a pile of gold and a stronger army waiting for you. - Boss milestones that put your army to the test - Handmade assets. No AI used!
