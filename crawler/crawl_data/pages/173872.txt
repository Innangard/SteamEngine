Controller or keyboard highly recommended. Mouse alone won't get you far here! F.A.Q. - How do I access this content? Enter the leftmost door in the hacker street (the one with the "Grand opening soon" banner). If you have finished the game at least once with a "main ending" on any save file, the banner should be gone and the door should be open. Just interact with it. - Do I absolutely have to finish the main game before playing this content? No, you can also access the DLC through the main menu, or by clicking the door in the hacker street twice and choosing to enter. - What save file should I choose? It doesn't matter. The DLC progress is kept independent from the main game. EXP and Unis in particular are not used. - How hard is it? You can play all challenges on "relaxed" difficulty if needed. Additionally, you can skip challenges that are still too hard for you by spending C-Tokens (the bar currency). - I'm not interested in combat; should I play this anyway? Combat is the main focus of this DLC. However you'll still get to talk with various characters in the bar and experience a short story in the game universe. It is recommended you play if you enjoyed combat in the main game, but don't if you skipped all the fights. Accessibility note This DLC offers a similar level of accessibility as the main game. However, due to the concept and often the big number of enemies and hazards on screen, it might be difficult for blind players as it requires precision and fast reflexes.
