View Community Hub About This Game A devilishly addictive retro arcade video game that goes into 3D and beyond. TimberMan VR is a simple game whose charm lies in its lack of storyline, while the advantage - is in your skill set. EASY TO LEARN HARD TO MASTER The game offers a simple and straightforward concept but with increasing difficulty. It is easy to learn but tough to master, providing a challenging and rewarding experience for players. 4 maps (autumn, winter, spring & halloween) 15 levels campaign arcade mode SHOP FOR A UNIQUE ITEMS Timberman VR features an in-game shop where players can purchase new tools, such as axes, machetes, and hammers, to enhance their gameplay experience and set higher records. The shop offers a variety of stylish and powerful tools: axes (stone Age Axe, tomahawk, Bat) machetes (futuristic, Viking, candy) hammers (meat Hammer, Shark, Thor) CUT YOUR WAY OUT Players must overcome obstacles while chopping down trees. The game requires quick reflexes and precise movements to avoid branches, adding a layer of challenge to the already difficult task of chopping down the tree. The obstacles increase in difficulty as players progress, providing a test of skill and endurance. COMPETE AND BECOME A CHAMPION Timberman VR features an in-game leaderboard where players can compete with others by setting high scores. The game tracks and displays scores, providing a platform for players to showcase their skills and compete with friends and other players from around the world. ENJOY A SPECIAL EVENT CONTENT The game features spooky and festive elements, including bat axe, and decorations, to get players in the Halloween spirit. This special content adds an extra layer of excitement and provides a unique experience.
