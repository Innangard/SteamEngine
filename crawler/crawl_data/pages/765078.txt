The rules are simple: each turn, place a bomb from your hand and set it off. Blast coin blocks to earn money, defeat enemies, and dig deeper into the dungeon. 💣 Build a strategic bomb deck with a variety of explosives! Each bomb has different power, blast range, and special traits, so choosing the right one is key. Collect coins to level up and obtain powerful new bombs! ⚙️ Fuse bombs to create the ultimate explosive! Combine bombs to increase their blast radius or inherit special effects. Keep fusing and craft your very own ultimate bomb to obliterate the dungeon! 🐉 Defeat the monsters guarding the dungeon and aim for total destruction! Monsters lie buried within the dungeon! They gradually advance, and if they reach the bottom, you’ll take damage. Destroy the required number of blocks before your HP hits zero. Your bomb placement skills and explosive strategy will determine your victory!
