Rhythm Animal Cafe is a charming collection of hand-drawn rhythm mini-games where everyday life becomes a musical adventure! In this lively little town, going to work, cooking, playing games, and all sorts of ordinary activities turn into hilarious rhythm challenges. No complicated controls or overwhelming note charts here. Just listen to the music and press a button at the right moment to enjoy the simple, pure fun of rhythm gaming! ✨ Game Features Doug Busy. Dog. Age 26. Office Worker. His name is Busy, and he certainly lives up to it. He works nonstop, sleeps only four hours a night, and doesn't earn a penny in overtime pay. Here comes his boss with another empty promise! His coworkers know better, but Doug opens wide and swallows it whole, right on the beat. Pan Youxian. Panda. Age 42. Jack-of-All-Trades. Despite his laid-back name, he's never got a moment to spare. Cooking, fixing pipes, playing in a band... there's nothing he can't handle! Watch him work his magic in the kitchen as onions, potatoes, and eggs fly his way. One perfectly timed flip after another, and a delicious plate of fried rice is ready! Burmilla Cait. Cat. Age 24. Cafe Employee. Her name sounds cheerful and lovely, but she's not always as happy as it suggests. Today, she's appearing on a TV show! The cameras make her terribly nervous, but as she finds her rhythm, she slowly relaxes and finally shows off a sweet smile. Tucked away on a street corner in this bustling animal town is a cozy little cafe. And you're the Manager! You'll get to know the locals, share their joys and troubles, and lend a helping hand whenever you can. Work, cooking, deliveries, part-time jobs, sneaking in a little break... Even the messiest days can become wonderful memories when you follow the beat. Turns out, everyday life has a rhythm of its own. Welcome to Rhythm Animal Cafe , a heartwarming and hilarious collection of rhythm mini-games! 🎵 Feel the Beat, Even Without Watching the Screen! Ever find yourself tapping along to your favorite song without thinking? That's the idea behind Rhythm Animal Cafe . Listen to the beat. Press the button. That's it! No complicated button combinations or endless streams of notes. Just follow the rhythm, and you'll quickly get the hang of it, even if you've never played a rhythm game before. Every beat, melody, and character animation is carefully designed to let you rely on your ears as much as your eyes. But hitting every beat perfectly? That's another story! 🐾 Everyday Life, Extraordinary Rhythm Challenges! Every animal in town has a job, a story, and a rhythm of their own. An office worker struggling with his boss's empty promises, a panda cooking up a storm, and residents juggling one part-time job after another... These ordinary moments become a colorful collection of rhythm mini-games, each with its own music and surprises. Nail the beat for a satisfying performance, or miss a few notes and enjoy the animals' hilarious reactions! After all, a Perfect isn't always the funniest outcome! 🎶 35 Tracks That Bring Everyday Sounds to Life Clattering dishes, bustling street stalls, neighbors chatting... Here, even the sounds of everyday life become part of the music! Featuring 35 tracks across a variety of styles, including Jazz, Pop, and House, the soundtrack brings every scene to life. Music, gameplay, and animation work together to make you feel like you're part of the animals' everyday adventures. Some tunes are sweet and relaxing. Others are so catchy they'll stick in your head all day! Don't be surprised if you find yourself tapping along long after you've stopped playing. 🎨 A Cozy, Hand-Drawn World Full of Life Warm sunlight, leafy rooftops, a vintage record player, and a whole town of busy, fluffy residents. With its lively 2D hand-drawn animation, Rhythm Animal Cafe feels like a storybook brought to life. There's no world to save and no evil overlord to defeat. Just work, meals, friends, everyday worries, and that wonderful moment when you can finally relax after a long day. It's these little moments that make up the rhythm of life. 🛠️ Create Your Own Rhythm! The story of this little town is far from over! A Level Editor is planned for a future update, giving Managers the chance to create rhythm mini-games of their own. Design your own challenges, combine music, sound effects, animations, and performances, and even tell your own stories! We can't wait to see the creativity our community brings to this world. The Level Editor is currently in development. Its release date will be announced later. ☕ Managers, Welcome to the Cafe! Life can get busy, and things don't always go as planned. But with a little music, there's always a bit of happiness to be found. If you're feeling tired, why not stop by our little cafe and stay a while? The coffee's ready. All we're missing is you, Manager! ☕🎵 System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 Processor: Intel Core 2 Duo @ 2.00 GHz or equivalent Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: Intel® HD Graphics or better Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: OS: Windows 10, or Windows 11 Processor: Intel Core 2 Duo @ 2.00 GHz or equivalent Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX750 Storage: 1 GB available space * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
