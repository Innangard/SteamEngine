View Community Hub About This Game Become a Medieval blacksmith in this interactive blacksmithing simulator. Upgrade your forging equipment, unlock new exotic crafting materials, grow your business, and manage your customers - in addition to engaging forging mechanics. Strike while the iron is hot and hop in front of the forge now! Story In the uncertain times of the Middle Ages, every town needs its blacksmith! Pick up your hammer and heat the forge as you prepare to keep your customers pleased. From wandering knights-errant to the local butcher, everyone needs your metalworking skill. Use your knowledge to craft the finest weapons and tools around. Upgrade your workshop, expand your business, and get access to even better materials and techniques as you gain more customers. Forging Forging is the core of all metalworking. To excel as a blacksmith, you must master furnace control, heating, hammering, tempering, and more! The interactive environment of Blacksmith Simulator makes forging a game of fast-paced multitasking, skill, and order management. Your forging prowess will affect your business, as better forging produces higher-quality items. Keep your product quality high to grow your business and attract more customers! Materials As your forge grows, so will your selection of materials to forge with. Better weapons can be crafted as access to more exotic materials becomes available. Every day, you can venture to your own ore cave to mine what you need. When you have the money, expand your cave to uncover better ores! Customers Reputation will make or break your blacksmithing career. Keep customers happy and provide high-quality workmanship to raise your reputation and receive more lucrative contracts! Happy customers are the key to success. Upgrade As your business expands, so will your workshop needs. Upgrade your equipment as you progress to support your growing reputation. Better equipment is a must to use exotic ores and to complete more rewarding contracts!
