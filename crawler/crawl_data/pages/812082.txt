Or they'll eat each other. Your passengers are hungry. Some are turning radioactive. The engine is burning, and the baby won't stop screaming. You're the crew. This is your problem. One plane. Up to six idiots. Play solo or bring up to five friends to split the work. Serve food, cure radioactive passengers, climb onto the wing to repair engines, and patch holes before half the cabin gets sucked out. Please don't eat the other passengers. Hungry chickens will eat each other. Radioactive ones can explode. Neither is great for business. Choose your parenting style carefully. Try to calm the baby. If that fails, you can throw it off the plane. If you let it crawl away, it might reach the cockpit and start flying. If you can hear screaming, you still have passengers.
