This is very high quality” Idle Cub About This Game AHOY, CAPTAIN! Embark on a thrilling voyage with NAUTICAL SURVIVAL, an intense survivor-like auto-shooter set in a vast, unforgiving ocean. Command a heavily armed vessel, navigate through treacherous waters, and fend off relentless hordes of sea monsters. With rapid gameplay and extensive customization, every skirmish is a test of skill and strategy. HIGH-SPEED NAVAL COMBAT Take the helm and dive into action-packed battles against fearsome oceanic foes. Equip your vessel with a formidable arsenal, hire seasoned crew members and adapt your tactics to survive. Journey to uncharted islands to find upgradable outposts to bolster your naval might. The seas are treacherous, so manage your cargo carefully! ADVENTURE CALLS NAUTICAL SURVIVAL offers a fresh, action-packed oceanic experience with high replayability, unlockable content and a variety of possible builds. Whether you're a seasoned sea dog or a newcomer braving your first gale, welcome aboard! Nautical Survival is a high-speed arena shooter featuring quick gameplay and high customization. The Early Access version will feature the following content on release: ~20 minute runs Procedural maps 10 unlockable seacrafts with different abilities and gameplay styles 14 crew members that can be promoted to customize your build 17 specialized auto-firing weapons 5 upgradeable buildings can be found at sea 17 enemy types 3 boss battles Your ship awaits, Captain!
