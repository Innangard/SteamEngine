Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “After having a strong reception with the demo and gathering feedback from the community on system improvements and Ideas, it made sense to bring that same energy into early access release where we can gather feedback from the community and actively improve on the game as we wrap up the story.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Comatose is planned to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version is planned to expand Comatose beyond the currently available content by completing Laura’s full narrative arc. While Early Access includes the first Chapter of the story, the 1.0 release is planned to add additional Chapters that continue and conclude the campaign. The full version is also planned to include: .New enemy types and encounters .Additional puzzles and narrative sequences .Expanded environments and areas to explore .Further balancing and gameplay refinements .Performance optimizations and visual polish Early Access represents a complete and polished portion of the game, while the final release will deliver the full intended narrative and expanded gameplay scope.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version includes the first Chapter of Laura’s story, offering over 2 hours of gameplay. .This portion of the game is fully playable from beginning to end and includes: .Exploration-focused gameplay .Combat encounters .Environmental puzzles .Core narrative cutscenes .Retro-inspired visuals and atmosphere The experience is stable and polished. We plan to continue improving performance, balancing, and overall quality based on player feedback, though our plans may evolve over time” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The game will have a lowered price during early access, but once the story is complete there will be a price increase.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback plays a central role in development. Player input will be gathered through Steam discussions, community channels, and direct feedback from players. Updates will reflect balance adjustments, performance improvements, and quality-of-life changes influenced by player experience. Retro Survival Horror Find supplies and survive as you navigate a deserted town with monsters that will haunt you around every corner. Psychological Nightmare The environment will change as you progress, becoming more corrupted as you unearth previous buried trauma. Fight your way out Equipped with various weapons, Laura is not utterly hopeless against her pursuers. Fight through the nightmare and earn your life back, or die trying. Solve puzzles and uncover the truth. Some truths hide in plain sight; others require key pieces of your memories to unlock. Solve these puzzles to unlock new areas and peel back the curtain of painful memories you forgot.
