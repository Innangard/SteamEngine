Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Every die has its place. A spell from a tabletop game has escaped into the real world, multiplying thousands of dice and scattering them across a basement straight out of the 1980s. Roll for Order is a cozy first-person sorting game. Search through the mess, identify every die and return it to the correct display until the entire collection is perfectly organized once again. Search, sort and complete every set The basement is filled with themed dice collections, each divided into matching sets and arranged inside labelled display cases. Search for every missing D2, D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 and D20. Pay attention to their colors, patterns and designs, then work out exactly where each one belongs. Dice can be hiding anywhere: beneath furniture, behind boxes or mixed together in enormous colorful piles. Pick them up, inspect them and carry them back to their proper display. Complete full rows, finish entire sets and gradually transform a chaotic mountain of dice into a perfectly catalogued collection. Choose your class Play as the Warrior, Barbarian, Mage or Rogue . Each class has its own magical abilities designed to make finding, collecting and sorting dice easier. Use brute force, arcane magic or clever tricks to manipulate dice, move them across the basement and deliver them to their displays in different ways. Every class offers its own approach to bringing order to the chaos. Sort dice and grow stronger Every die you place in its correct slot brings you closer to earning a new Ability Point for your chosen class. Use those points to unlock new abilities or upgrade the powers you already have, making it faster and easier to recover the dice still scattered throughout the basement. The more dice you correctly sort, the more powerful and efficient your class becomes. More awaits in the full game The full version of Roll for Order expands the basement into a much larger sorting challenge, with thousands of dice to organize, countless sets to complete and co-op multiplayer so you can tidy up together with friends. Welcome back to the 1980s Step into a basement lit by the glow of CRT screens and colorful string lights, filled with retro consoles, fantasy posters and the remnants of countless tabletop game nights. Surrounded by old electronics, board games and cozy clutter, you can take the job at your own pace, enjoy the satisfaction of completing every display and restore the basement in time for the next adventure. Can you sort them all?
