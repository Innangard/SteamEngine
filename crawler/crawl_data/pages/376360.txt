As a sandbox game, it’s all about making your own fun while discovering each and every challenge on offer” Gameshub About This Game Doggy Don’t Care is a comedic 3D adventure where you step into the paws of an adorable but mischievous pup! When Charlie’s owner leaves him home alone, it’s his chance to explore, collect, and smash his way through four vibrant, interactive environments! Solve puzzles and dig into a howl-arious list of fun-filled shenanigans in an attempt to impress Charlie’s feathered friends, who reward you with ✨Mischievous Badges✨ Charlie has one main mission: to cause as much adorable chaos as possible. By grabbing a roll of toilet paper, he can leave a trail of TP in his path, or by stepping in oil, he can leave paw prints over the roof of his owner’s car. She won’t be mad, right? Across four vibrant cartoon-style levels, Charlie is free to run amok to his little heart’s content. Be sure to leave no pillow, chair or photo frame unturned! It’s fun to cause adorable chaos, but what’s the goal? Each stage features plenty of badges that reward Charlie’s shenanigans. Each badge is a puzzle for Charlie to solve, all relying on his ability to cause as much mayhem as possible. As Charlie explores, he’ll find plenty of new and adorable pawjama outfits for him to wear. Dress Charlie in an entire puppy closet full of costumes, including a giraffe, a unicorn, or even (heaven forbid) a cat! Ever wondered what goes on in our dogs' minds when they’re sleeping and their legs start twitching? Explore Charlie’s dreams and uncover their hidden meanings. 🐶 A pawsitively chaotic single-player adventure 🌈 Vibrant 3D cartoon-style graphics 🧦 Adorable pawjama outfits 🧠 Puzzles to solve using found items ⭐ Earn mischievous badges for being naughty 🐦 Impress your feathered friends ✨ You can pee everywhere! ✅ Fun for all ages Experience the world through a dog's eyes in this game of exploration, problem-solving, and causing mischief. "Doggy Don't Care" is your invitation to unleash your inner pup!
