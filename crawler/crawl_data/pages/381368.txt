Connect. Chain. Explore. HEXA WORLD 3D is a relaxing 3D puzzle game about placing colorful hex stacks, creating chain reactions, chasing high scores, and unlocking new worlds. BUILD & CONNECT Place hex stacks on the board and connect matching colors. Watch chips move between stacks, combine, and disappear as the board changes with every move. CREATE CHAIN REACTIONS A single good move can trigger a much larger reaction. Plan ahead, build bigger combinations, and push your score higher. PLAY YOUR WAY Play handcrafted Levels, build endless runs in Infinite Mode, or compete for better scores in Competitive Mode. EXPLORE DIFFERENT WORLDS Play the same core puzzle across a growing collection of atmospheric locations, each with its own visual style. Unlock new worlds, Hexagon Skins, and customize the look of your game. One puzzle. Different worlds. Endless combinations.
