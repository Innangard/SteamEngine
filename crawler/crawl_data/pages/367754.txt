MOVIEHOUSE is the game film-fans have been waiting for. Ready the camera, unleash your creativity and pile up the profits! Grow your operation with your razor-sharp business sense and usher in a new era for filmmakers worldwide…or fail trying. After all, this business is like a box of chocolates—you never know what you’re gonna get. What is this? A studio for ants? Experience the decades – start with a small film crew in the 80s, develop your first short-film, and taste the sweet nectar of box office revenue. When your enterprise expands, you’ll create better movies, hire a larger crew, and, of course, need a bigger boat! Drink their milkshake Use your wits to outsmart the competition and leverage your intuition to spot the next hit-genre at the box office. Win prizes at events, take the biggest slice of the pie, and become the most successful studio Hollywood’s ever seen. That’s a bingo Make movies your way  — pick from a dozen different genres and plot the perfect storyline. Make a spy flick, a romantic comedy, a bloody slasher, or something entirely different. It’s up to you to strike the perfect balance and make a true blockbuster,becoming the godfather of cinema. Cinderella story. Outta nowhere. Assemble the dream team — hire directors and writers, nurturing their creative talent and skills to ensure optimal creative output. A good crew can bring viewers to seats, while the director charts the movie’s course. Make unknown actors famous overnight, or put them on the sideline if they don’t live up to the hype. After all, you’re the boss now!
