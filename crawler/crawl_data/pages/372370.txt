Deep within a spooky dungeon, witches have set aside their cauldrons in favor of sizzling grills and toasted buns. The world craves the finest enchanted burgers, and only you and your coven can deliver! But beware—meeting the quota is no easy feat. With orders piling up, ingredients flying, and time slipping away, teamwork is the only spell that will save you. Burger Witch blends cooperative chaos with a whimsical witchcraft twist. Take orders, assemble burgers with style, and serve mystical customers before time runs out. Earn gold to upgrade your tools, expand your kitchen, and prepare for even greater challenges. But remember—witches cook with magic, not madness, so don’t throw food (unless absolutely necessary)! Are you ready to don your witch hat and master the mystical art of burger-making? Magical Kitchen Madness – Work in a haunted dungeon-turned-burger-joint, where every round brings new challenges. Fast-Paced Co-op Chaos – Team up with friends to cook, assemble, and serve enchanted burgers before the clock runs out! Upgrade Your Tools – Earn gold to enhance your kitchen, unlock new magical cooking gadgets, and improve efficiency. The Witchcraft Book – Follow the ancient (and slightly absurd) rules of mystical burger assembly. Style matters! Whimsical Witchy Vibes – Spooky, charming, and filled with humor—because even witches need to have fun in the kitchen. The orders are in, the grill is hot, and the customers are waiting. Fire up the magic and start cooking—before it’s too late!
