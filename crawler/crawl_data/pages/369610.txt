You’ve been selected for the Night Shift at the finest pizza establishment this side of the void. Your duties are simple: sweep up, hand out pizzas, rearrange the chairs, and keep an eye on—well, never mind that part. It’s just three nights. Just you, the pizza, and whatever that was in the corner of your eye. If you survive, you get paid. If you don’t… hey, you won’t be hungry anymore. Inspired by the golden age of lo-fi horror, Night Shift blends first-person survival mechanics with PS1-era visuals, atmospheric tension, and a dash of pizza grease. Tasks are randomly assigned each night, so stay sharp. Or don’t. It’s your funeral. Hand out pizza to the night delivery crew. Take out the trash before something else does. Put the chairs up on the tables. You’ll want a clear line of sight. 🍕 Three-night survival horror experience in a haunted pizza parlor with a twist. 🪑 Interactive task system — clean up, organize, deliver. No two shifts are exactly alike. 🕹️ Retro PS1-style visuals and analog horror vibes straight from your favorite nightmares. 🔦 First-person perspective keeps you close to the action—and everything else. 🧠 Minimal guidance, maximum paranoia. Figure it out. Quickly. 📻 Atmospheric sound design that may or may not be whispering to you. You wanted a job? You got one. Now hang in there. Don’t look too long at anything you can’t explain. And above all? Happy shift!
