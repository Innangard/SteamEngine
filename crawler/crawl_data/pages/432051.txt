To access this content please do the following : Go to your Steam Library and on the dropdown box just below the search bar on the left-hand side, click on the dropdown arrow and change from GAMES/ALL to SOUNDTRACKS. This will list all of the soundtracks you own on your Steam account. Right-click on Tavern Talk Soundtrack in your Steam game library and choose "Download Album." Once installed, click on Tavern Talk Soundtrack and all of the songs installed will be listed on the song list to the right of your library screen. You can also right-click on Tavern Talk in your Steam game library, choose "Properties", click on the "Local Files" tab and choose "Browse...". This will open up your Tavern Talk folder in Steam where you'll find the folder for your Soundtrack.
