The inhabitants of this island use the energy coming from the laser beams to survive and to transport this energy they use the repeating cubes. Your mission is to help the inhabitants of Rayland transport the energy of the Domus through the different lands contained in the path. To do this, you will have to correctly position the repeatodes cubes and rotate them so that the energy rays reach the Domus of their respective colors. Use the resources on each level to make the reflected rays reach the Domus. Use the mouse to move and the keyboard to rotate the Repeater Cubes. Use blocks with passages to reach the Domus. Hit each target with lasers of their respective colors. 50 challenging levels.
