View Community Hub About This Game A K-pop drama – and you make the calls You run a tiny agency with a rented practice room and a big dream. You discover unknown talent and form your own K-pop group. But backstage it is never quiet: fights in the practice room, secret feelings, a stalker, scandals, rivals, homesickness and injuries. How your group deals with it is up to you – and every decision has consequences. And debut is only the beginning: three acts take you from the first stage through the national breakthrough to the world tour – then carry on in free play or with the next generation. Fresh drama every day Something new happens every day – in group chats, story scenes and comics. Your answers change trust, mood and careers. Not every wound heals. Live streams on the fan network Glimr – in Korean with subtitles. Whatever the fans comment, the group hears about it. Story arcs that span many weeks: a secret romance, a stalker, a baby at the office, a bitter rival, the solo question, the world tour and the farewell – with humour and heart, but also serious topics like pressure, exhaustion and loneliness. Seasonal stories that come back every year – like "Night of the Monsters" at Halloween. Rivals: PULSAR Starline Entertainment sends its boy group PULSAR into the race. The story arc "Law of Physics" is about rivalry, respect – and maybe friendship. It peaks with a battle in the Countdown Arena: PULSAR dances first in 3D, then your group. The audience decides, and the result changes the story. Take the stage Your idols perform on stage in 3D – on the beat, with more than 30 dances from hip-hop and jazz to the moonwalk, dedicated choreographies for story songs, changing formations and a killing part that crowns every comeback. Music videos with their own concept, budget, premiere and locations per song, a music show week against your rivals and a photo mode for the perfect moment. Your headquarters comes alive – and so does your loft From a rented practice room to your own headquarters: dance studio, styling room, management office – and a music room in the basement with guitar, keyboard, drums and a rhythm mini-game. Your group lives together in the loft: every idol has their own room to visit and decorate. Your idols live here: they train, text on their phones, listen to music, laugh on the sofa or pack parcels in the merch center. Design your own T-shirts for the fans – your group wears them on stage. Collect photocards of your idols in an album. Seasonal themes for office and loft: Halloween, autumn rain and First Snow. People, not numbers Every member has their own strengths, quirks, dreams and worries. Watch the chemistry – who sticks together, who clashes? Assign roles like main vocal, main dancer, rapper or center, launch solo projects and units – and see what it does to the group. Mix tops, bottoms and shoes from many looks, plus hair colours, make-up and accessories. Run your agency Plan training, media appearances and breaks every week – or risk someone collapsing. Choose song, concept and budget for every comeback – and after every release, find out exactly why it became a hit or didn't. Three save slots, autosave, free play after the finale and four difficulty levels. Aurora Edition – more stage, more stories Aurora collection: holographic outfits for all 13 idols that light up with the beat of the show – earned through special milestones. Two new idols: Kai (popping/locking) and Dara (hip-hop/waacking), each with their own voice, story portraits, signature look and Aurora look. Six new songs with their own stage sets for shows and music videos. Story archive and endings: reread scenes and comics you have played at any time; an epilogue shows how your band's stories ended. Free play and records, four difficulty levels from "Relaxed" to "Agency Hell". Fair price, fair play No ads, no microtransactions: everything in the game is unlocked with IDOL Coins earned by playing. Steam achievements, Steam Cloud for your saves, playable offline, in 9 languages. All characters, groups and broadcasters are fictional.
