In order to make her dream come true, she must develop a bond with her reassuring little buddy "Homu", brush up on her strategy, and overcome the challenges she faces! The player controls "Solaris". However, in order to defeat your enemies, you must command "Homu". To do so, you need to successfully complete QTEs (Quick Time Events). However! If you advance the story and raise your "Reliability", you will be able to easily activate difficult skills! The bond between the two characters is the key to victory! [Plan your strategy with personalized equipment] Save up money by working as a “shop keeper” or in “battle” to make new equipment! Solaris's equipment: expensive and powerful. You can buy it anytime you have money. Homu's equipment: Inexpensive and can be equipped in bulk. However, what you are able to craft is random. The best choice for you is a matter of preference! Let's prepare the best equipment according to the skills that Homu can learn now! [A Tale of 100 Days] You get to choose your action from three cards every day for 100 days. Battle? Rest? Learn a skill? Earn money? Every action will affect the outcome of your trial! Sub-scenarios unfold as you select specific cards or accomplish objectives! It's up to you what kind of story you want to weave and with whom! The ending will change depending on how you proceed! The other characters are "Tetia", a young but brilliant former classmate, "Croise", a strict but kind mother, "August", a lazy shopkeeper , "Melchior", a wise woman who has high expectations for Solaris and Homu, and a suspicious character who is plotting something... Let's make their adventure a success with strategy!
