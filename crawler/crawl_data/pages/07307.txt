View Community Hub About This Game Play poker hands in this idle deckbuilder that lives quietly in the corner of your screen. Deal cards, gain chips, buy score-multiplying buildings, complete achievements, and grow stronger across ascensions while you go about your day. Levels and unlockables Gain XP, level up, and earn rewards such as deck modifications, permanent upgrades, or hand bonuses. Hunt for loot Find rare gear with huge bonuses. Equip the best and create broken builds. Ascend and grow stronger Reset your run to earn Ascendium, unlocking cross-ascension upgrades that boost your progress. Always on, never in the way Runs in a small corner of your screen so you can work, browse, or game while defending. Customize your play Adjust size and position to fit your setup. Stay passive or play actively. It's up to you!
