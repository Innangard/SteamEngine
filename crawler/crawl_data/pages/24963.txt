View Community Hub About This Game 🌵 A debt you can’t escape It’s 2046. Water is scarce. Land is barren. Corporations rule everything. You are bound by a contract you never truly understood - a massive debt owed to a ruthless megacorporation. The only way out? Extract. Build. Expand. Survive. Every unit of cobalt you mine brings you closer to freedom… or deeper into the system. ⛏️ MINING & RESOURCE EXTRACTION Start with basic tools and carve your way through a harsh, dry landscape rich in valuable minerals. Mine cobalt and rare resources Discover new deposits across expanding regions Manage limited energy and equipment durability Sell resources and crafted goods in settlements 🤖 AUTOMATION & PRODUCTION You weren’t meant to do this alone. Build a growing industrial network in the middle of nowhere: Deploy automated drills and extractors Use drones to transport and manage resources Expand with storage unit and power system Design your own efficient production system - or watch it collapse under its own weight. 🔧 CRAFTING & UPGRADES Turn raw materials into survival. Craft tools, machines, and infrastructure Upgrade your gear, extraction speed, and efficiency Improve your electric motorbike for longer expeditions Unlock advanced technologies as your operation grows Every upgrade is a step toward independence - and a bigger target on your back. 🏍️ EXPLORATION & TRAVEL The world is vast. And it doesn’t care about you. Travel across desolate terrain: Explore new zones filled with resources and secrets Ride your electric motorbike across long distances Manage battery levels - always carry spares Freedom is out there. But reaching it won’t be easy. 🏙️ STORY & WORLD You’re not alone in this broken system. Meet people trying to survive under corporate control Uncover stories hidden beneath contracts and machines Decide how far you’re willing to go to clear your debt Not everyone believes in freedom anymore. 🎮 GAME FEATURES 🌵 Pixel-art 2D world set in a  futuristic USA (2046) ⛏️ Mining, refining, and resource management 🤖 Automation systems with machines and drones 🔧 Crafting and upgrades 🏍️ Exploration with battery-based vehicle mechanics 🏙️ Narrative-driven world with NPCs and trading 📈 Build, optimize, and scale your industrial ⚠️ PAY YOUR DEBT Or become part of it.
