A $100,000 fee. No sign of an exit. Welcome to a very Normal Golf Game. HOW HARD CAN IT BE? From the twisted well-adjusted  mind of Luke Muscat (Fruit Ninja, Jetpack Joyride), Normal Golf Game is a sadistically accurate recreation of the noble game of golf. Use physics to manipulate the path of your club, while simultaneously managing your stance, direction, and the angle and path of the face. OKAY, QUITE HARD IT TURNS OUT. Explore a giant golf course that is both a trap and a playground. Shoot from anywhere, aim at anything. So long as you don't try and leave, we will have a fun, normal time. OTHER FEATURES A nice day out Learn a fun new sport Meet  a game developer Have a great, very normal time WAIT, WHO? Normal Golf Game was built almost entirely by me, Luke Muscat. The real Luke Muscat that is, not the fictional Luke Muscat that traps you in the game. Confusing, I know.  I’m a solo dev, Youtuber, and have the worlds worst golf swing.
