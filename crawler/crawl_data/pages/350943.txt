Oh, and one more thing… They also have to overcome the obstacles ahead of them. TWO STEPS FORWARD, ONE STEP BACK Players can move the lumberjacks forward and backward, allowing them to maneuver the log. Be careful though, rolling the log in the same direction can turn it into something as fast as a race car, and that speed can easily throw off your balance… A PHYSICS-DRIVEN WORLD The balance of the log you roll is simulated by a physics system that closely resembles real life, making the experience much more challenging. Characters react realistically to collisions, falls and other physical interactions thanks to ragdoll physics. I LOVE HANDLEBAR MUSTACHES Throughout this adventure, you can collect coins from chests and use them to customize your character. Lumberjacks, for example, really like handlebar mustaches. What do you like? LOCAL MULTIPLAYER AND CO-OP This is a two-player adventure. You can play solo or together on the same device using different sides of the keyboard or connect to a game hosted on another device and play cooperatively. DIFFICULTY LEVELS AND CHECKPOINTS The difficulty levels affect how often checkpoints appear. As you pass checkpoint flags on the map, the game is saved automatically, allowing you to continue from where you left off whenever you want. No need to make things too hard, right? LEADERBOARD Players can compete via the global leaderboard and see who completes the game in the shortest time.
