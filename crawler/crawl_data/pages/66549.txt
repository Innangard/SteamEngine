5 of 11 View Community Hub About This Game A Classic Hardcore MMORPG Gather resources through hunting, warfare, and cooperation with other players. Craft useful items and powerful gear from what you collect, then unleash your strength in massive wars where skill and strategy decide victory. Meet players from all around the world, build lasting friendships, and create your own story in a vast world of adventure and conflict. World of Iveria In the world of Iveria, a powerful empire has collapsed, and in its place have risen two divided and troubled empires. What was once a peaceful continent is now torn apart by war between the two most powerful factions, Craxion and Defugel. War is everywhere. Small and large battles erupt in every corner of the continent. Yet the roots of this conflict trace back to the ancient era of the Iverian Empire. It is only a matter of time before the two greatest factions must unite and stand together to confront the darkest scheme of all. Will the continent survive at all? Join us in Iverian Wars: Craxion vs Defugel at the most exciting moment in the history of Iveria—and stop the continent from falling. Here’s what you can expect in the game. Large-Scale Faction Warfare Players can engage in warfare at every scale, from small-scale skirmishes to massive field battles. Faction-based territory occupation, along with content such as Battle in Chaos and the Dark Portal , creates a dynamic battlefield where strategy, positioning, and coordinated faction play ultimately determine victory or defeat. Action-Oriented Combat with Hidden Skills Combat goes beyond standard skill rotations. Discover hidden skills that let you roll to evade attacks and perform powerful jump attacks, rewarding timing, movement, and mastery. Freedom-Driven Equipment Progression Build your character your way with a progression system centered on freedom and choice. Players can freely name their gear, customize and combine all item attributes, and create unique builds without being locked into fixed paths. If you want it, you can get it. Party-Focused Gameplay & Dynamic PvE Party-focused gameplay lies at the heart of the experience, featuring Party Wave , large-scale faction battles, and dynamic party-based encounters. In addition, the Scavenger System introduces Spirit Scavengers that create unpredictable PvE situations—even during combat—requiring players to constantly adapt their strategies. Shape the Fate of Iveria Iverian Wars: Craxion vs Defugel is a living battlefield where faction conflict, strategy, and player choice define the world.
