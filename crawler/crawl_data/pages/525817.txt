A 65 page PDF that details the journey of developing Rainswept. From my own history detailing my shift from Architecture to game development, insight into everything that influenced and inspired the game to early notebook sketches and scribbles that were the first steps towards making Rainswept a reality, this art book goes behind the scenes of the complete development process. Note that due to my unusual method of work, this book doesn't contain concept art you might be used to seeing in most art books. Which is why this is more of a “making of” book!
