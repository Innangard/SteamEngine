By joining early, you become part of the journey to build the best possible version of Wyldheart together. Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? We have a strong vision of what our game is, so our goal during Early Access is to add highly requested features from players and fine-tune the game based on player feedback. We hope to finish the Early Access period and release our 1.0 build at some point in 2027. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? In the full version, we plan to release multiple standalone campaign experiences to increase the length of the game and deepen the player's progression journey over time. The full version will also include additional game features based on player feedback. Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? Early Access currently offers the first campaign, three playable ancestries and 19 roleplaying backgrounds and skill trees to specialise in Sword, Axe, Mace, Bow and Staff techniques. Näytä yhteisökeskus Ilmoita bugeista ja anna tälle pelille palautetta keskustelupalstalla Katso kaikki keskustelut Tietoa pelistä YOU ARE NOT THE CHOSEN ONE Start as a humble peasant tossed into a dungeon, tasked with finding one of the ancient Relics of Caerwyn to protect your town. Choose to be a proud Freefolk, a little Mossling, a towering Grimhorn or a stout Stonefolk, with 19 backgrounds and hundreds of customisation options to choose from! CUSTOMIZE YOUR COMBAT STYLE Quickly swap between melee and ranged weapons, casting spells, or throwing deadly concoctions during immersive, fast-paced action combat. Classless, fully flexible skill trees in Adventuring, Survival, Dungeoneering, Weaponry and the Oldways of Magic allow you to specialize or create clever combinations. HANG OUT IN HANDCRAFTED TOWNS Meet local villagers who all have lives, stories and schedules of their own. Reveal quests by eavesdropping, deciphering rumors, and exploring strange ruins. Gather materials, craft items, cook meals for your party, and upgrade your gear before continuing your adventure! SCHEDULING GAME NIGHT HAS NEVER BEEN EASIER Play solo, or up to 4-player co-op with seamless drop-in/drop-out. Cloud-shared saves, group XP, and scaling difficulty all mean the party can continue with someone offline, but nobody gets left behind. Designed for busy gamers, campaigns have about 10 hours of story, and 15 hours of optional content.
