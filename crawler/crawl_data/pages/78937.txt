About This Game Idle Battle x Chill Adventure A JRPG pixel-art idle battler where your Guild grows through automatic combat. Running on your taskbar , it is the best companion to keep you company during your working days. Various Hand-drawn JRPG pixel art Roles Master 7 main classes and 21 sub-classes while recruiting from a total of 91 unique roles . Every duelist features individual abilities and animations to help you find the perfect fit for your playstyle. Discover the most powerful combos from a wide range of roles and group them into your own legendary team. Casual Adventure, grind! grind! grind! Grind for over 200 different equipment in your idle adventure across the Continent. Master your build with 60+ class-exclusive Legendary Equipment. Customize your equipment through enchanting to craft a perfect build for every squad member. Operate camp and Manage your team and facilities! You can support your Duelists with essential resources while they journey across the Continent. Recruit new talents in the Tavern where you can also earn revenue from their wine consumption. Upgrade the Blacksmith to provide your squad with superior equipment and produce extra goods for your camp’s commerce. Let the Shopkeeper sell your spoils of fight to secure the funds needed for more supplies. Gain Followers and become the ultimate Duelist squad! Defeat regional Duelists to win the allegiance of their Followers and gain unique regional Combat Styles . These Combat styles allow you to further refine your team’s battle strategy and playstyle while enabling truly unique Builds. As you gain more Followers, you will unlock more progression content and new challenges. Defeat every team on the Duelist Road to prove your strength and become the ultimate Duelist squad on the Continent! Deploy 3 Teams, Immersive Idle! The new mode is now live! Simultaneously deploy 3 squads for multi-front battles! Rake in 3x EXP and massive resources, and enjoy the thrill of lightning-fast growth ! Switch between Idle/Immersive modes with one click, perfectly adapting to your work and leisure pace!
