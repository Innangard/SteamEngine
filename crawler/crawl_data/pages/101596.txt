A flashy high‑school girl ghost suddenly appears in your home and begins enjoying life with you—completely uninvited. She will never take your life. The ghost is always looking for chances to get closer. Too much physical contact is strictly forbidden—run away when necessary. Stop her from bringing her belongings into your house and from posting your photos on social media. Protect your peaceful, ordinary life at all costs. March, just before graduation. As confusion sets in, the ghost gently takes your hand. Inside is a short message: “Just seven days.” Can you protect your morals and your career? And where will this bold ghost girl’s unwavering affection lead? A strange seven-day cohabitation where fear and affection slowly invade everyday life.
