Through quiet days and turbulent events, you'll meet colorful characters, uncover hidden truths, and carve your own path in a living, breathing wuxia world. With immersive storytelling, rich character interactions, open-ended development, and a unique combat technique system, step into a sprawling martial epic like no other. ◆ Key Features ◎ A Massive Story to Uncover Over 500,000 words of original narrative and 30+ hand-drawn CGs. Tons of side quests and bonus content deepen your journey through the martial world. Multiple endings await — will you become the “Master Healer,” the “Wanderer,” the “Jack-of-All-Trades,” or unite the land under your banner? ◎ Build Your Character, Your Way Train your martial skills on the field, study ancient texts in class, and rise to the top. With 12 development paths, live a balanced academic life of both mind and body, and shape your own martial identity. Grow herbs, cook meals, grill over an open fire, go hunting, play strategy games, take on bounty quests — the choice is yours. ◎Turn-Based Combat Classic turn-based battles enhanced with a unique Technique Stage and Technique Branching system. Mix and match over 100 martial arts techniques and inner styles to create your own signature fighting style. Master timing, adapt to the flow of battle, and unleash devastating combos in one fluid strike. ◎ Forge Bonds in the Martial World Meet 8 distinct characters, each with their own backgrounds, personalities, and stories — including 3 romance options and 2 potential party members. From busy market streets to quiet teahouses, your relationships will grow through shared encounters and heartfelt conversations. Raise your bond with each character to unlock unique storylines, exclusive CGs, and meaningful choices that shape your journey. ◆ Character Profiles A native of Kaifeng, raised in a decent household (with branching options during character creation). Calm, responsible, and quietly drawn to the world of martial heroes. Not born to be a legend — but determined to walk the path. Sang Ling'er A cheerful girl from the Miao tribe of Yunling. Known for her mountain songs and riddles, she commands twin snakes with the bells she wears, and is skilled in poison and medicine. She leaves her village in search of the truth behind a certain event. After solving a poisoning case, she’s discovered by the academy and granted the chance to take the entrance exam. Wenren Yao Owner of the Fan Pavilion, warm and wise, always seen with her round fan. As the eldest daughter of the Wenren family, she’s managed the family business since youth. Her eyes light up when she sees account books. In her free time, she enjoys reading storybooks and trying her hand at cooking. Duan Hongtang Daughter of the Xuan Dao Sect leader. Wields ring blades with grace. Gentle in appearance but strong-willed and fiercely loyal. Raised to uphold the image of a sect leader’s daughter, she maintains elegance and composure — though she secretly longs to explore the world beyond. Shen Tinglan A disciple of Sword Hearth Sect, a young senior sister of her generation, and a skilled Sword wielder. Gentle and composed, humble and courteous to all, she is already one of the finest heroines in the jianghu. Should you be fortunate enough to see her draw her blade, you will understand that “Little Senior Sister” has never been merely a respectful title among fellow disciples. Ye Luo Hailing from the royal court, already trusted to carry out missions outside the palace at a young age. Efficient, quiet, and emotionally reserved — but never lacking in ability. Fang Yuchi Son of a local magistrate from a scholarly family. Strong-willed and sharp-eyed, with a passion for law since childhood. She’s devoured every legal text in her home library. Deng Xiaoyu A young disciple of the Lantern Shadow Pavilion. Skilled beyond his years, but blunt and outspoken — often leading to minor “misunderstandings.” Huiguang A bright, witty disciple of Xiangguo Temple. Polite and always smiling, with a silver tongue that persuades even the most frugal to offer incense. He serves as the temple’s charming public face. Zhe Guanshan Martial arts instructor at Mingjing Academy. Calm and disciplined, with the air of a seasoned veteran. Though strict in training, he’s known for his bold spirit and generosity — and is beloved by generations of students.
