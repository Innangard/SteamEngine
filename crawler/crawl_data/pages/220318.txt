Episode 2 - "Cafe and the Stone Port". - Length: 15:29 . A Peaceful café. Photo album. A place for ourselves. Upon leaving the school, Sachiko and Takako walk down the street making small talk. As they reach the broad avenue far off downtown, they find the road lively with traffic for the early afternoon. Takako raises her voice for Sachiko to hear her over the loud sound of a large passing truck, and of the wind blowing in the sand. The two girls enter an alley leading to the shopping district in an old part of the town. There, you can find shops and stores with age-old signs, and they always attract the two girls on their way home. The episodes are delivered in both a subtitled video format, and a Japanese audio-only track in .wav format. The video supports Japanese, Japanese (closed captions), English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean subtitles which can be switched at will.
