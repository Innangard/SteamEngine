Let's find out how you control the 12th largest airport as of 2023 in Europe in terms of passenger volume. Features: – Realistic modeling with over 100 custom modelled buildings. – Detailed ground polygon model including 3D modelled taxi bridge and terminal entrance. – Numbered gate parking stands. – 3D parallax rooms on most buildings simulating an interior. – Taxi signs and hand placed taxi lights around the airport. – Runway approach lighting. – OSM (open street map) background buildings and roads, along with illuminated street lights in the distance. – Beautiful background scenery including the 17km-long Vasco da Gama Bridge and Estádio José Alvalade (José Alvalade Stadium). Nowadays, Lisbon Airport features 2 terminal buildings and 2 runways. It is the main European hub to Brazil, the largest European Star Alliance hub to South America and also an important European hub to Africa, TAP Portugal. Lisbon lying along the edge of the Atlantic Ocean, the city is a unique assemblage of colors, mixed cultures, and impressive landmarks, but also sandy beaches.
