View Community Hub About This Game Gather. Craft. Build. Explore. Relax. Outpath is a colorful first-person sandbox that combines resource gathering , base building , automation , exploration , and incremental progression . Start on a small island, gather resources , craft new tools , and expand your world . Build your base , unlock powerful upgrades, and automate production as you discover new possibilities. Play at your own pace, solo or with friends in online co-op . No time limits, no pressure. Whether you prefer actively exploring and building or watching your automated systems do the work. ONLINE CO-OP: Explore, gather, craft, and build together with friends! GATHER & MANAGE: Collect resources and turn them into useful materials. CRAFT & UPGRADE: Create tools, unlock blueprints, and improve your abilities. BUILD & AUTOMATE: Expand your base and automate resource production. EXPAND YOUR WORLD: Purchase new islands and discover new environments. EXPLORE & DISCOVER: Uncover hidden secrets throughout the world. FISHING: Take a break and catch some fish! PARKOUR MOVEMENT: Jump and move freely across the islands. ACTIVE OR IDLE: Play your way, whether you prefer clicking or automation. DYNAMIC MUSIC: Enjoy music that reacts to your actions.
