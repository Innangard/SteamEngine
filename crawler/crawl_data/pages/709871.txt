Through Early Access, we're excited to collaborate with our community to refine and expand the game together. Your ideas and suggestions will directly shape the evolution of this project as we work side-by-side to create the best possible experience. As our game fully supports Steam Workshop, we are also looking forward for passionate members of our community to expand and refine the game with awesome mods.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We estimate the Early Access period will take around 18 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We want to give players a complete RTS experience. Our surveys indicate (and this matches perfectly with our vision) that this includes a story-rich campaign mode, flying units, naval units, additional map biomes, additional hero items and hero activities, as well as multiplayer improvements. Please refer to our Roadmap posted below for more details!” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game is fully playable both singleplayer and multiplayer in several game modes: - Classic RTS mode - Hero mode - Custom scenarios (includes RTS survival and MOBA scenarios) All core features are implemented, like hotkey rebinds, game chat, game replays, game difficulty settings. We also have a basic ranked 1v1 leaderboard and a basic matchmaking system. Game also features the AI assistant - for players who are looking for an easy chill-out time. Early Access includes about a dozen skirmish maps (up to 8 players, any team alliances configurations) and a few scenario maps. But most importantly, the game fully supports Steam Workshop where members of the community can upload, share and download additional maps, units, game modes and even custom AI opponents and AI assistants!” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will remain the same.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “At every stage of development, we maintain a close connection with our Discord community, carefully considering all feedback shared there. Wield magic, unleash cannons, and outsmart foes in dynamic battles. Defend from waves of undead at night! Sounds familiar? In a unique mode of The Scouring, become a powerful hero, crushing foes with devastating skills. Gain experience, collect items, and customize your playstyle, blending action RPG combat with strategic army control. Shape battles with spells, weapons, and tactics to claim victory. Enter dungeons to look for precious items. BE THE HERO. Control the battlefield. MULTIPLAYER MODE The Scouring delivers epic multiplayer battles with team skirmishes, and free-for-alls for up to 8 players. Сustomize matches, and test your skill in dynamic modes. Forge alliances or dominate foes in the ultimate multiplayer RTS experience. USER GENERATED CONTENT The Scouring unlocks endless creativity with Steam Workshop integration. Download custom maps, modes, and AI scripts or create and share your own. With powerful tools and a vibrant community, every game offers fresh challenges and infinite variety. ACTION RPG STRATEGY The Scouring introduces a new genre: Action RPG Strategy. Customize your hero, equip gear, and unleash powerful abilities in fast-paced combat. While you dominate the battlefield, AI manages your army, resources, and bases, blending action and strategy seamlessly. The Scouring started as a solo developer’s dream to create the ultimate RTS. Inspired by classics and built with passion, it thrives on community feedback. Join our Discord, follow and read X, get in touch on Reddit! Together, we’ll refine, expand, and create something special. Thank you for being part of this journey. Attention to Detail : Immerse yourself in richly detailed, visually striking environments. Watch battles unfold with dynamic destruction, lifelike unit movement, and spell effects. Smooth Gameplay : Fluid and polished controls that allow you to fully utilize your skills. Action RPG Strategy : New gameplay experience and new genre. You control a powerful hero while AI manages your army. Multiplayer Modes : Engage in ranked matches, free-for-alls, or team battles with custom games. User-Generated Content : Explore and share maps and modes via Steam Workshop. Create AI scripts to automate strategies and watch them play in real-time. Community-Driven Development : Shaped by players and their feedback.
