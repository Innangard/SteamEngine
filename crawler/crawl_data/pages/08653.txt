Your fable begins when a dragon challenges you to organize 5,648 papercraft treasures and tally every last  item. Level up and unlock new skills along the way in this Tidy Up game. Play solo or tidy up together with friends. Supports up to 32 players in online co-op. Handmade, No Gen AI Our game contains no generative AI content. Some people may specifically wonder about the art used for the items because  tidy-up games need a lot of unique items. We are using art from two of our games: Crusaders of the Lost Idols and Bush Whacker 2. Each and every one of the 500+ unique items in Folded Fables was drawn by one of our artists over the past 14 years. Physical Paper Transformed Into A Video Game Many of the in-game pieces, such as the pop-up book sequence, started as physical paper models before being recreated digitally. Even the Folded Fables logo was physically hand-folded with paper before being photographed for use in-game. Once Upon a Time... Did you know a dragon nearly roasted us alive? Yes! Your Grand Ma and Pa, and a few fellow adventurers, were caught trying to steal from a dragon! Gather round to hear the tale... The dragon’s hoard was vast! Gems, swords, and fancy trinkets were everywhere. Surely the dragon wouldn’t miss a few small things? But we were caught! And rather than roasting us alive, the fearsome creature gave us a choice: organize every last treasure in the cave and determine the total value... or become part of the collection ourselves. A World Of Papercraft Step inside a pop-up storybook and discover a beautiful handmade world where flat paper creations spring to life. Earn Experience, Level Up, And Unlock New Skills Unlock new sorting skills to organize faster, carry more treasure, and sort every last item scattered throughout the dragon's cave. Explore three skill trees: Collection, Placement, and Utility. Collection helps you locate treasures faster. Placement helps you identify where treasures belong and place them more efficiently. Utility improves your movement, carrying capacity, and overall efficiency. Tally Every Coin Place every item correctly to earn the dragon's favor and unlock new powers. Transform the chaotic mountain of treasure into a perfectly organized hoard worthy of song. Developed with the participation of Creative BC & Canada Media Fund.
