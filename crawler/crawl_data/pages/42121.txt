Clean up the grounds in this cozy Tidy-Up-lite, where there are only two things to sort: good apples go in the Market Crate, wormy ones go in the Animal Food Bin. Earn coins, buy upgrades, unlock animal helpers and uncover the orchard's secrets. Let's tidy up! Apples Here, Apples There Apples lie scattered across the ground and in the trees. Pluck them one by one, use a crate, or summon them to you in an instant. Watch Out for Worms! Inspect your apples for worms and sort them into the right bins, or launch them straight in from across the orchard to earn some coin. Furry & Feathered Friends Unlock cute and helpful animals that will gather and harvest apples for you. Form efficient harvesting chains, and let them do the hard work while you relax. Features 10,000 Apples to Clear: Pick them off the branches and up off the ground, check each one for worms, and toss it in the right bin. Upgrades and Abilities: Grab several apples at once, throw them straight into the bins from across the orchard, or summon a hungry pig to gobble up the wormy ones. Animal Helpers: Unlock an alpaca that gathers apples (and that you can ride), plus ducks that fish apples out of the pond. Upgrade them so they tidy up for you. Crates: Buy crates, fill them with apples and carry them wherever you need them. Hidden Keys and Treasures: Find keys to open treasure chests that grant passive skills. Secrets to Uncover: Discover hidden ways to change the weather, the time of day and the seasons, and light up every gnome.
