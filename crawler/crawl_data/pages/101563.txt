The board contains only one thing: arrows made of connected cells. Slide an arrow and it flies straight out of the board along its direction — but if another arrow blocks its path, it bounces back and costs you a life. Simple rules, deep puzzles One board is one maze: which arrow you fire first decides what you can fire next The longer the segment, the more it matters — the longest can span a full edge Comb patterns, side-by-side walls and ultra-long arrows all force you to re-plan Sliding arrows builds Spirit; click a sun to fire and bring a Golden Crow down Suns sink over time and scorch the board — balance solving against racing 200 hand-tuned levels + endless mode Levels 1–200 are generated from a fixed seed, identical for every player. Past level 200 you enter Endless mode where every board is new. Every 10th level is a Ten Suns celebration with a choice of 11 stackable blessings. Race the whole world Every level from 2 to 200 has a global time leaderboard, shown live on the right side of the screen while you play Daily Challenge: one board for everyone, every day — fastest solve wins Speedrun codes: generate a code, send it to a friend, and race the same board Global / Friends / My rank views, any time Normal and Hard Normal mode is for exploring and enjoying the puzzles; Hard mode (unlocked at level 50) reduces lives, speeds up the sinking suns and removes assist blessings — built for hardcore solvers. Achievements and codex 20 achievements (including hidden ones), a sun codex, a blessing codex, and three forms of Yi to unlock as you progress. Controls Mouse-only playable; keyboard and gamepad supported (move the cursor with arrow keys or the stick, confirm to fire).
