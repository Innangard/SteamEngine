View Community Hub About This Content Assetto Corsa Competizione GT2 Pack is finally bringing the higher power of GT2 vehicles to sim racers, together with the perfect playground of the Red Bull Ring Circuit. GT2 cars are a new grand tourer racing class maintained from SRO Motorsports Group, the class inserts between GT4 and GT3 in terms of on track performance. They are well known for their high power, which is extremely demanding and fun to control thanks to the less invasive aerodynamic package they come with, when compared to GT3. The Red Bull Ring circuit is one of the most challenging tracks around Europe, well known for the wide variety of 2 and 4 wheels racing happening there, it is particularly demanding for it's ultra high speed corner sequence towards the end of the lap and its 3 crucial braking points. This track suits perfectly for GT racing, allowing for a never resting experience. Surrounding this pack, players of Assetto Corsa Competizione will also benefit of the 2023 liveries pack, released for all players on the same day of this DLC pack.
