View Community Hub About This Game Hocus Focus is your cozy background companion for work, study, or relaxation. Gamify your focus with built-in tools and gentle progression - no stress, just enchanted vibes and original ambient tracks to keep you company. Get Things Done Summon your focus with Pomodoro timers, daily tasks and weekly planner, habit tracker, journaling, gratitude & mood tracker. Level up by staying focused, and unlock new cosmetics and environments to explore. Build Your Cozy Magical Workspace Personalize your dorm room with hundreds of hand-drawn items​ Change time of day and weather to fit your mood Style your avatar and your adorable shadow fox companion for that perfect cozy setup.​ Music & Sound Over 7 hours of original music composed for the game, with playlists ranging from sunny lofi and inspirational ambient to rainy dark academia vibes - all 100% stream-friendly. Enhance your experience by mixing your own perfect brew of ambient sound. Explore Level up and experience Adventure Mode, unlocking 60+ environments all across 3 magical realms. Collect & Progress Gather herbs and craft magical potions. Find, tame and collect adorable flying creatures called Mitzkins. They make for great deskmates!
