View Community Hub About This Game The thrill of 90s arcade action is back! Experience the complete saga of Gaelco’s cult-classic platformers with Thunder Hoop Collection, featuring the original 1992 arcade hit Thunder Hoop and its explosive 1994 sequel, TH Strikes Back. Go back to the golden era of coin-ops and test your reflexes against a bio-organic nightmare. When the mad scientist Professor Genbreak unleashes an army of uncontrollable, genetically engineered creatures upon the world, humanity’s only hope lies in a powerful bio-engineered hero developed in a secret laboratory: Thunder Hoop! Climb ladders, leap across hazardous platforms, and smash dangerous bosses to halt a global catastrophe. Whether you are controlling the young hero in the original classic or dominating the battlefield as a powerful adult warrior in the sequel, prepare for pure, uncompromising arcade fun. Master your combat skills, unlock devastating weapon power-ups, and enjoy the definitive way to experience these forgotten retro gems. Features: Two Games in One: Play both the legendary Thunder Hoop (1992) and its simultaneous 2-player sequel TH Strikes Back (1994). Rewind Function: Undo costly mistakes and perfect your platforming runs. Save States: Save and load your game at any moment so you never lose your progress. Retro Screen Filters: Relive the genuine arcade atmosphere with customizable CRT and classic TV effects. Local Multiplayer: Face the mutant hordes with a friend in alternating turns or simultaneous co-op action.
