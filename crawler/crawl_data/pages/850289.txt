Are you the secret genius of the group, or the one who loses their phone while holding it? It’s time to find out. Gather your friends (and potential future enemies) in a fast-paced, chaotic co-op party game where your reputation is entirely in the hands of the lobby. How It Works The rules are simple, but the social stakes are high. Every round, the game drops a burning question. Predict what the majority thinks to score points: The Plus (+) Questions: "Who has the absolute best taste in music?" or "Who is the most reliable in a crisis?" If the crowd chooses you, get +1 point and a massive ego boost. The Minus (-) Questions: "Who is most likely to click a scam link?" or "Who forgets everyone's birthday?" If the finger points at you, take a -1 point and some heavy emotional damage. Key Features Hive Mind or Tactical Liar?: To win, you must think like the crowd. Do you vote honestly, or betray your thoughts to match the majority? Pure Social Chaos: Perfect for casual game nights, Discord hangouts, or streaming with your community. Infinite Replayability: A dynamic deck packed with modern, relatable, and brutally funny questions that guarantee no two games are the same.
