Your little angler quietly fishes from a tiny island on your desktop, waiting for the next bite while you do other things. Check back whenever you want, reel in your catch, grow your collection, and keep progressing at your own pace. Catch, collect, and complete your album Discover dozens of fish and sea creatures, each with multiple collectible rarities. Every catch can become part of your growing collection, and duplicates can be sold for coins. Fill your album, uncover missing creatures, and chase the rarest versions of your favorite catches. Some fish are easy to find. Others will take patience. Explore new fishing regions Progress through different areas using the Map , each with its own collection to complete. Discover every species in a region to earn special collection rewards, including unique islands and other cosmetic items that cannot simply be purchased from the shop. There is always another creature to find and another collection to finish. Build your perfect little fishing spot Spend the coins you earn from fishing to unlock and customize your setup. Choose from different: Characters Islands Bobbers Fishing rods Vests Cards Mix and match your favorites and create a fishing setup that feels like your own. Upgrade your angler Use the Upgrades system to improve your fishing progression over time and make your little desktop companion even more rewarding to keep around. Earn achievements and unlock rewards Complete challenges, fill collections, discover rare catches, and unlock achievements as you play. Some rewards are bought with coins. Others have to be earned. Made to stay out of your way Little Angler is built to coexist with everything else you do on your computer. Keep your angler small and unobtrusive, move the setup to whichever side of the screen you prefer, resize it, mirror it, or minimize the main menu entirely. Your angler keeps fishing while you focus on something else. No pressure. No rush. Just one more catch.
