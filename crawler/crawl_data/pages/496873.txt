A 90’s-Inspired Slasher Survival Experience The Scarlet Harvest is a horror survival game inspired by classic 90s slasher films. Step into tense, atmospheric locations pulled straight from a late-night horror flick. A silent gas station in the middle of the woods, sinister cornfields, ramshackle barns, and a remote farm hiding more than one terrible secret. Enjoy the Party… While You Can Before the nightmare unfolds, you’re free to live the moment. Dance with your friends. Grab a beer. Talk, explore, and light a few firecrackers under the Halloween sky. But when the horror begins, there’s no standing still. You’ll have to run for your life. Fight back against your enemies. Survive the night. With dark environments, constant tension, and moments of gore, The Scarlet Harvest delivers a slasher-inspired horror experience where not everyone makes it to morning.
