Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The decision to adopt an Early Access model stems from our belief that player feedback is crucial for the final refinement of the game. Currently, the core experience and most of the gameplay mechanics are quite mature. However, we recognize that the game's background story and the communication and interaction experiences with other townsfolk still need optimization to enhance player immersion. This includes enriching the main storyline, creating home environments for the townsfolk, and introducing mechanisms for townsfolk to invite players to participate in events. Through the Early Access model, I hope to collaborate closely with the player community during the final stages of development. This way, I can improve various elements of the game based on player feedback and ensure that these changes truly meet the expectations of the players. I believe that through this approach, I can create a more engaging and deep gaming experience.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We anticipate that the Early Access phase for this game will last approximately 3 to 5 months. During this time, we will make adjustments and improvements to the game based on player feedback, ensuring that the final version provides the best possible gaming experience.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “To distinguish the full version from the Early Access version, we plan to expand and improve the following areas: Enrich the Game's Background Worldview: We will deepen the game's world-building by crafting richer and more cohesive background stories, providing players with a more immersive gaming experience. Enhance Interactions with Townsfolk: We will optimize the interactions between players and townsfolk, adding more dynamic dialogues and events to make each encounter more meaningful. We place great value on player feedback and will continuously improve the game based on player suggestions. While we cannot make specific commitments about future events, we are committed to making the game a fun and creative space. As development progresses, we will share more information and roadmaps within our community to ensure players are involved throughout the development process.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Currently, the overall experience and most of the core gameplay mechanics are well-developed, as detailed in the game description under "About This Game." To stay updated on the latest development progress, please follow: • Twitter :JiaoHu • Discord • YouTube : Gamer JiaoHu About This Game Leave the stress of city life behind and start over in a peaceful seaside town. In Little Life, you can build your own cozy life by the ocean — farm, dive, explore islands, decorate your home, meet townsfolk, and slowly settle into a relaxing new routine at your own pace. Whether you spend your days growing crops, driving around town, collecting rare items, running small businesses, or simply watching the sunset by the sea, every moment is about enjoying the simple pleasures of island life. A Cozy Seaside Life Escape the noise of modern life and move to the charming coastal town of Housai. Experience slow living in a warm and welcoming community where every day is yours to shape. Take things slowly, build relationships with local residents, discover hidden places around the islands, and create a life that feels truly your own. Farm, Fish, Dive & Explore Grow crops, raise animals, go fishing, dive beneath the ocean, gather resources, and uncover hidden treasures scattered across nearby islands. From peaceful farming to underwater exploration, the world is filled with activities to discover at your own pace. Meet the Townsfolk Build friendships with the residents of Housai through conversations, gifts, daily interactions, and shared experiences. As relationships grow, townsfolk may offer discounts, invite you to events, share stories, or even become someone special in your life. Customize Your Home Create a home that reflects your personality, from cozy apartments to seaside villas. Decorate your living space with furniture and decorations collected throughout your journey, and slowly turn your house into your dream island home. Live Freely There are many ways to spend your life in Housai Town. Become a street vendor, run small businesses, deliver goods around town, collect rare items, or simply enjoy peaceful days near the sea. You are free to choose your own lifestyle without being forced down a fixed path. A Relaxing Life with Hidden Depths Beyond the peaceful atmosphere lies a world filled with systems and discoveries. Use negotiation cards while shopping or selling goods, upgrade tools and vehicles, explore distant islands, and uncover new ways to improve your life. Little Life blends cozy daily living with deeper simulation and exploration mechanics, giving players freedom to play however they like. Slow Down and Enjoy Life Little Life is a cozy open-world life sim about freedom, atmosphere, and the joy of everyday moments. Take your time, enjoy the ocean breeze, and build your own little life by the sea.
