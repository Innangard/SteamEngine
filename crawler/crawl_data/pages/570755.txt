On the way there, you will need to explore and traverse the islands ahead. You will have to find new origami shapes, that you can shapeshift into and use their abilities to traverse the world. While linear gameplay lasts about 30 minutes , embark on a sweet and short journey to rescue your lost friend. Different objectives like speedrunning or collecting crowns provide additional challenges. Bite-Sized Brilliance: Dive into a 20-30 minute origami adventure, perfect for a quick, scenic escape from reality. Origami Odyssey: Transform into unique paper shapes to unlock special abilities, unveil secret paths and find hidden crowns. Crafted World: Immerse in a stunning 3D platformer landscape inspired by paper, origami, and cardboard artistry. Speedrunner's Delight: Challenge yourself in "Hardcore"-mode, race against time, and climb the leaderboards. The Human Paperthin and versatile, the Human can traverse obstacles with ease and adapt to any challenge that arises. Glide through carboard landscapes with this shape, effortlessly surfing the winds. As the Crane, you'll master the art of navigation, mapping your own path through the skies, and discovering hidden secrets that await. The G.O.A.T. Unleash chaos and power with the Goat , a shape that's all about raw, unbridled energy. As the Goat, you'll thunder through obstacles with unstoppable force, leaving destruction in your wake.
