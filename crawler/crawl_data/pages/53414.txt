Master unique loop movement , build your dream frog with shiny upgrades , and you might be able to survive the ongoing waves of angry enemies and strong bosses ! Skate that loop! Tired of moving by simply walking? Good news, that frog doesn't even know how to walk, but she sure as heck can skate ! Ride along the loop arena, turn around, jump, crouch, accelerate, do whatever you need to evade enemies attacks and give them a taste of your weapons! Choose cool upgrades! A simple ollie is cool and all, but how about doing a kickflip that's so fast that it summons thunder while swinging a huge sword and throwing fireballs? You could do that, or a backflip while blasting loud music and launching a bowling ball, that's up to you to decide! Choose your weapons , change your skateboard tricks , upgrade your stats and become unstoppable! Survive waves of cute enemies! You thought you could skate peacefully today, but that frog army won't let you. They'll come bouncing, flying, rolling, and they'll even send their big bosses . Do anything you can to keep them away, and be warned: they might be cute, but they sure are angry and many !
