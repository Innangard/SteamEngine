Play as a goose to survive or a duck to wreak havoc, with proximity voice chat, unique roles, custom cosmetics, and diverse environments to explore. As a Goose, complete tasks, report bodies, and vote out the ducks. As a Duck, call sabotages to hinder, and eliminate the geese. As a Neutral, pursue your own secret objective to win.
