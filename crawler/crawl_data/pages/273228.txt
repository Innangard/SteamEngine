It's so small that you can also complete the game in a single session! Explore the depths of planet Micronus Traverse and backtrack through meticulously crafted pixels of caves, jungles, labs, ruins, and more. Almost every room has a hidden item or a secret path.  Can you find them all? Find and make use of classic upgrade tropes Missiles, Claw, Ball-Mode and more! Customise your play style Equip Microcards to modify your weapons and abilities.  Create your own play style.  Just make sure you've found enough Microcells to power them. Microvania respects your time Don’t worry about saving your progress - the game will create a checkpoint every time you enter a room, and you will spawn back here if you die. And quitting to the main menu will save your game where you are, so you can jump right back in on your next session. Accessibility & Full Controller Support Designed for controllers, with fully customisable buttons. Including accessibility options like rapid fire or toggle modes for various functions.  If a boss gives you trouble, you can use the "Respawn at Boss" setting to  make them easier to fight. ... and you can pet the snail!
