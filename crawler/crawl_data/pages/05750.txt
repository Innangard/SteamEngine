Humanity is gone. All that remains is dust, silence, and one small, frightened creature . Your only prompt: Take care of her. The Last Cat in the Universe is a short, narrative-driven incremental game. Your directive is clear: salvage what's left of a dying ship to build a warm home for the final survivor. Key Features Find Your Flow: Experience a mining mechanic designed for relaxation. Hover your mouse over debris fields in a satisfying, rhythmic flow. No frantic clicking required. Meaningful Progression: Every upgrade matters. Unlock tangible leaps in power that let you tear through deeper layers of the ship instantly. As the station decays, you must constantly balance your desire for stronger tools against the rising cost of keeping the air breathable and the nest warm. Recover the Lost Lullaby: Discover the story of the station and its crew as you collect the fragments of a very important song. A Soulful Soundtrack: Music is the emotional core of this experience. The original score, composed in collaboration with Venezuelan singer-songwriter Diora Salazar , evolves from meditative rhythms to deep emotion, intimately tied to the narrative's climax. A Focused Experience This game is designed to be played in one or two sittings (approx. 2-3 hours) . It does not feature infinite prestige loops or idle grinding. It is a complete story about the quiet joy of fostering a life in dire circumstances.
