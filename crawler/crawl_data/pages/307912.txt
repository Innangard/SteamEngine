View Community Hub FOLLOW US About This Game Feng Shui Meowjong is a unique light roguelike card game that fuses traditional mahjong mechanics with roguelike progression and mystical Feng Shui-inspired tactics. In this strategic turn-based system, you reinterpret classic mahjong gameplay using a fixed set of 14 tiles to form combinations such as Chi (Chow), Pong (Pung), and Pair. At the same time, you activate powerful Feng Shui treasure effects, manage skill cards, and take on increasingly challenging enemies. Every run tests your planning, adaptability, and strategic thinking. Easy to Learn, Deep to Master No mahjong experience required. Simple to pick up yet deeply strategic. A seamless blend of Eastern tradition and modern roguelike design. Difficulty and complexity scale as you progress, constantly evolving your strategy. Unlock new treasures, cards, and effects by defeating enemies. Boost score times fan through treasure synergies to unleash massive score explosions. Collect and use skill cards to manipulate tiles, increase scores, or modify treasure effects. Treasures interact to create powerful synergies, enabling varied deck-building paths and playstyles. High replayability. Every run offers new combinations, new builds, and new challenges. Drag and arrange tiles to form Chi (Sequences), Pongs, and Pairs. Every adventure is different. Randomized tiles, enemies, treasures, and card choices keep each run fresh. Break traditional limitations and experience the joy of playing all 14 East tiles at once. Place mystical treasures across the eight directional slots while minding Feng Shui Dos and Don'ts. Internet-famous cat memes meet pixel art aesthetics. “Wait, that cat has this ability.” Feng Shui treasures link together to create powerful synergies and signature playstyles.
