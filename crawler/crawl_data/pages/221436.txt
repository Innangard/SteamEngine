Jiangshi x Daoshi is an epic story of Chinese vampires, dao priests, assassins and immortals, told in a spectacularly animated visual novel format. When former assassin Luan is attacked by man-eating creatures, he is saved from certain death by a wandering dao priest, Ling Ling. At that time, neither of them know what tribulations yet await them. Features Breathtakingly animated action Explosions that cut mountains in half Sexy assassins of both sexes Blood, gore and humor Includes Jiangshi x Daoshi Episode I - Boy Meets Girl, and Episode II - The Fable of the Fake Elixir of Life.
