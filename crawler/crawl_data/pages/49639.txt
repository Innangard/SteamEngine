The Bloods, Crips, and Vatos fight to regain control of the city. Choose your side, spin the block, and come out on top. Hood Warfare features chaotic and humorous FPS gameplay. Spin the block in different parts of the city including downtown, the suburbs, the police station, detroit's abandoned mall, and more. Each of these maps work with different playstyles depending on the map and mode. Some of Hood Warfare's gamemodes Include: Gang Warfare: A round-based mode where your team will clash in the streets of large maps with large teams. Each player only gets one life per round. Come up with strategy's with your team to win rounds. Turf War. A gamemode where gangs fight for control of turf surrounding a map. Gangs will get points for every piece of turf they own. Eventually, the gang with the most turf points will win. Chaos. Every man for himself. There is no loyalty to a gang in this mode. Kill or be killed to win. Gangbang: Classic Bloods vs Crips firefight with the most points win. 3 Gang mode variants: Tired Of 2 teams? Switch it up with three. These modes feature the Bloods vs Crips vs Vatos, a new gang from Mexico that found their way into Detroit. *NEW* Zombies mode. A zombie outbreak has plagued the streets of Detroit. Experience a classic round based zombies mode in Detroit's abandoned school map, and the newest addition, Detroit penitentiary featuring objectives and a boss battle. KIllstreaks: Get enough kills and call a drive by to help your gang out on certain maps. Dial up the gang and they will come support with a armored lowrider with a machine gun. Hood Warfare features online play, however offline play is also supported with bots. Bots can also fill private or public lobby's if the host chooses to enable them. Experience an advanced dynamic weather system featuring every season and weather condition imaginable during gameplay. Hood Warfare features built-in voice chat, to chat with your team along with enemy's. Level up your players reputation by playing, and you will unlock various weapons and skins to flex on the opposing gang as you progress. Hood Warfare is currently in Early Access, and will receive various updates featuring more weapons and modes over time. *DIsclaimer* Hood Warfare nor Trapstargames promotes nor condones any form of real life gang violence. This game does not attempt to portray any real life events and is merely a work of fiction for entertainment purposes. The issues of gang violence are a complex issue that Hood Warfare does not attempt to replicate or promote. Like all video games, this is a piece of media for entertainment.
