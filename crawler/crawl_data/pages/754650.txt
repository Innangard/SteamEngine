View Community Hub About This Game Start a new life on an overgrown farm in the rural and peaceful Clover Town. You’re not alone this time—raise adorable blob companions that help you farm, explore, and make new friends. Ova Magica blends farm-life simulation with creature-collecting JRPG gameplay for a cozy yet adventurous experience. BLOBS In Clover Town, blobs are part of everyday life—but most of all, they are friends. You’ll raise them and grow together as you train for the Clover Cup, explore for treasure, breed your very own unique blobs, or simply enjoy life on the farm. They’ll help you with daily tasks like watering crops or producing goods such as milk. RELATIONSHIPS Along your journey, you’ll meet many different characters, each with their own deep stories and struggles. These aren’t just brief encounters—through your help, you’ll become part of their lives as they grow and change. And maybe, you’ll even find love along the way… TOWN LIFE In Clover Town, the changing seasons shape everyday life. As nature transforms, so does the town itself—filled with traditions, celebrations, and a strong sense of community. Throughout the year, seasonal festivals bring everyone together, offering special activities, heartfelt moments, and unique rewards to enjoy. EXPEDITIONS If a peaceful farm life isn’t enough, the Adventurers' Club is waiting for you. Explore uncharted worlds filled with wild blobs, hidden treasure, and valuable rewards. With your blob companions and friends by your side, you’ll set out on thrilling expeditions.
