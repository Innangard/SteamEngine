Shape the fate of Elester, a Magin and veteran hitman in an underworld syndicate, as well as young Tolen, who is about to realize that he too can use the essence to his advantage. Step into a mature, magical world standing on the edge of an industrial revolution and utter chaos. Overcome obstacles, play your cards wisely, and find your destiny in this immersive, story-driven adventure. Explore atmospheric and mysterious locations by investigating the environment, and interacting with items. Battle horrific creatures and meet numerous complex characters - potential allies or foes. Manage your essence by utilizing emotions, both in combat and during exploration. The emotional state of your character influences the story and various gameplay mechanics. Immerse yourself in a cold and harsh fantasy world where medieval culture is confronted with the invention of industrial, magic-powered machines, and the forces that seek to control them. Follow the main characters on their journey of lost and redeemed hope and decide its outcome based on your choices. Manage and upgrade your deck as the story progresses. Gain special cards from story choices and defeated enemies. Craft unique double-sided, emotion-infused essence cards fitting your strategy.
