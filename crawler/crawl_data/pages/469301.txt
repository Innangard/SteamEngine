Scale the Depths is a casual fishing game built around this simple but satisfying loop. Feed hungry customers, upgrade your gear, and uncover the many secrets hidden beneath the surface. Cast Your Line! Catch a variety of fish and discover hidden secrets! Prepare Your Catch! Scale fish just right with precise timing and control. Going too fast may damage the scales! Feed Hungry Customers! Hungry, aquatic customers of all shapes and sizes! Upgrade Your Gear! Upgrading your gear allows you to fish for longer, catch more fish, and scale fish more efficiently! Demo Features: Explore Loch Ness , the first of the four worlds in the full game. Catch a variety of fish under the depths. Scale fish with just the right speed and timing to prep them nicely for your customers. Meet and feed friendly customers your catches and get paid for your efforts. Figuring out a customer's favorite fish will net you an even higher pay! Use your hard earned cash to buy various upgrades to help you go even deeper into the depths, prep fish with more efficiency, get more storage, and net bigger and better catches. Explore secrets underwater such as hidden gates, money, and secret bottle messages. Change settings to adjust screen resolution, audio options, reset your game, and also try different control types such as Mouse Mode.
