This demo covers the early-to-mid loop of We Build Below: gather resources, lay down your first production lines,  build out a good tower defense against parasites and place the foundations of a crew base. You'll get a solid taste of the unique genre blend that defines the full game. What's included: Full co-op support (1-3 players) Core resource gathering, crafting and automation systems Core tower defense system 3 Biomes 3 Playable Characters A good range of machines and turrets A good range of perks and build styles The basic structures for building a base A progression arc that runs up to crafting your second Diamond Drill What's not (yet): This is a slice, not the whole game. Progression in each run is capped at the second Diamond Drill craft and not every upgrade is unlockable here, there is a lot more waiting underground for Early Access. Consider this your first descent. The full experience starts later this year!
