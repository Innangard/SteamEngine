Mewseum: Film Festival is a relaxing pixel art puzzle game set in a charming feline universe. Pick a featured movie, solve each grid, and gradually reveal the hidden poster. With every artwork completed, the museum fills with charm and sparkle! SOLVE THE PUZZLE AND REVEAL THE IMAGE Complete each grid by drawing rectangles or squares following two simple rules: Each section you draw must contain exactly one number. The number indicates the exact size of the section you need to draw. A VARIETY OF FILMS ON EXHIBITION Discover 6 famous movies reimagined in the feline universe, with hundreds of puzzles to solve. Each grid offers quick sessions perfect for a casual break, or long hours of relaxing gameplay. RELAX AND HAVE FUN Puzzles come in different levels of difficulty depending on the grid size. If you need help, you can use hints or undo moves at any time. Relax to a cozy jazz soundtrack while discovering amazing pixel art masterpieces!
