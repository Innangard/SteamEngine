The village is full of these dolls, and those who enter the village are never able to leave. Your goal is to investigate the village and uncover the secrets behind Martha's dolls and the curse that plagues the village. However, you must be careful, as the dolls are not to be underestimated. They are said to be possessed by Martha's vengeful spirit, and they will stop at nothing to protect their village and its secrets. As you explore the village, you will encounter a variety of different dolls, each with its own unique abilities and behaviors. Some dolls will simply wander the village, while others will actively hunt you down. You will need to use your wits and survival skills to avoid the dolls and stay alive. In addition to the dolls, you will also need to be wary of the village itself. The village is full of traps and hazards, and it is easy to get lost in its maze-like streets. You will need to carefully navigate the village and avoid the traps in order to find your way out. Martha's Dolls is a challenging and suspenseful horror game that will test your nerves. If you are looking for a truly terrifying experience, then Martha's Dolls is the game for you.
