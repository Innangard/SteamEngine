Navigate deadly caves solo or with friends in this chaotic co-op adventure. Team up with friends and watch your step on this journey full of traps, hazards, and the most deadly force of all: gravity. Will you jump into the unknown to rescue your dog? RESCUE YOUR DOG Wait, why is there even a construction site in this dog park? Your best friend fell down a hole and needs your help! Grab a helmet, a pickaxe, and some friends to head after your buddy. DON'T FALL (TOO MUCH) There’s a huge system of caves down there, and this rescue mission might be tougher than expected! Luckily, someone ordered a bunch of items that are scattered and buried all over the place. Find food to recover from injuries, use ziplines to cross crevasses, or create your own path with your pickaxe, drill, and magic beans. Don't forget to light the way with flares, it's dark down there! GO DOWN TOGETHER Turns out, it's not only you who is missing their best bud! All the dogs are gone! Watch out for your friends, catch them if they fall. Too clumsy to catch? Revive them if they're down. Or don't. Feed them weird stuff, toss them a bomb, and see what happens.
