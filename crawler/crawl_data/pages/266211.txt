You’ll play as Marshal, who is a powerful psychic that works for the Condatis Arcadian Government. Under the guidance of your mentor, Zoey Peyton, you’ll combat the STROL virus, Saffron’s army, and encounter new mutations of the Hunter. However, Condatis is never alone! Riles and his crew will also join you as you travel across the Martian surface in Riles’ VLAK HQ. We’re All Going To Die! (Please note, you can join another player’s Condatis Group content without owning it. But you cannot host a Condatis Group campaign without owning the DLC.) NEW CONTENT: NEW CAMPAIGN STORY Experience a new story line with new and old characters and settings. Take on the role as Marshal and survive by any means. PSYCHIC CLASS Become a psychic and destroy your enemies in new and interesting ways! From flinging objects, teleporting enemies, and psionic blasting your foes, your mind is your weapon. PLATOONS The condatis group is never alone! They might lack sophisticated weapons and gear, but they’ve got the numbers! Call-in soldiers to fight by your side as the psychic class and also with various upgrades in the campaign to help bolster your numbers. HUNTERS They lurk in the shadows, watching your every move. Fight and survive against all new hunter class enemies! VLAK HQ You're not here to win the war, but to survive. Stay on the move with the new VLAK HQ mechanic. However, from the world map to the combat layer, your HQ will follow and take damage.
