View Community Hub About This Game A Gentle Reminder - This game is easy to start but hard to master. It is more difficult than it looks like. In this minimalist style game, you will choose a hitter in each run, dodge the enemy's bullets or strike them back. Use the enemy's attacks to destroy them! You have to rely on your excellent skills to defeat as many enemies as possible in the hail of bullets to get higher scores! There are over 10 powerful hitters, each with a unique striking form and exclusive super abilities that can be unlocked. Each hitter means a brand new gaming experience! Strike back the enemy's crazy attacks, improve your hitting skills, use different hitter to challenge the global leaderboard, and finally become a living legend among all the hitters!
