A: No, all items included in the Zakum Package are purely cosmetic. They do not grant any stats, bonuses, or enhance your character's gameplay in any way. A: Yes, all items in the outfit sets--Well Loved Outfit Set, Founder's Thief Outfit Set, and Manji's Outfit Set--can be equipped by any character regardless of gender or job. A: No, Founder's Access is linked to the account that purchased the Zakum Package. Q: Which platforms and devices are supported? A: Global MapleStory Classic World will be available to play on both Windows and macOS. Q: Will my Founder's Access progress carry over to the Grand Launch? A: Yes, all in-game progress made during Founder's Access (starting October 6, 2026 ) will be carried over as the game officially launches on October 21, 2026.
