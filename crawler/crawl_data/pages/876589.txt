Prepare the aircraft, handle passengers and baggage, deal with problems during the flight and fly the plane all the way to landing. THE WHOLE FLIGHT IS ON YOU Prepare and refuel the plane, load the baggage, seat the passengers and get ready for takeoff. Once you’re in the air, your main goal is simple: get everyone to the end of the flight. WORK TOGETHER One player can fly the plane while another takes care of the cabin and someone else fixes whatever has already broken. How you divide the crew’s jobs is entirely up to you. TAKE CARE OF YOUR PASSENGERS Passengers get hungry, thirsty, need to use the bathroom and won’t always stay calmly in their seats. Ignore them for too long and you may end up with even more problems to deal with. BE READY FOR TROUBLE Turbulence, storms, birds, mechanical failures and running low on fuel can quickly turn a normal flight into a disaster. Solve problems as they happen and keep the aircraft together until landing. MOST IMPORTANTLY, LAND THE PLANE Reach the end of the route, lower the landing gear and try to make it safely onto the runway. The more passengers and baggage you deliver, the better your result.
