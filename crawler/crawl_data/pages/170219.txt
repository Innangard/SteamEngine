In the Pizza Slice demo, you can experience 5 days of Tonio running his grandpa's family restaurant. Step into the shoes of a young entrepreneur trying to revive an old pizzeria in the heart of a 1960s-inspired New York, brimming with Italian flair and nostalgic charm. Key Features: Interactive Tutorial – Guides you step-by-step through gameplay mechanics, teaching how to run a restaurant from ingredient prep to customer service. In-Depth Pizza Creation System – Craft your own dough from scratch, select fresh toppings, and bake the perfect pie in a traditional oven. Serving Customers and Restaurant Upgrades – Deliver your culinary masterpieces to hungry patrons, then enhance your eatery with new furniture, decorations, and amenities. Vibrant 1960s New York-Inspired City – Explore streets alive with Italian culture: from bustling markets to local festivals and hidden cultural nods. Introduction to Tonio's Story – Meet the young Italian who inherits his grandfather's rundown pizzeria and fights to get it back on its feet, uncovering family secrets. This demo is the perfect teaser for the full game – test your management skills and dive into a delicious tale of food passion and family legacy!
