View Community Hub About This Game Kitty is unable to reach her destination due to some rocks in her path. He could jump over the rocks, but he just doesn't want to do that, as he claims to be very tiring. So it's up to you to carry out the heavy labor for the comfort of this little kitten. Move the stones intelligently to make way. He should reach the place indicated with the color red. You just can move the stones forward and backward. Test your skills with the 40 challenges in Unlock The Cat! Can you move the stones in the way that will solve each puzzle? Kitty hopes so.
