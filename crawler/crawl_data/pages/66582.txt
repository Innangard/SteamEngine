Build your pickleball empire Pickleball Tycoon is an idle clicker game about building a pickleball empire across four venues: your hometown, the coast, the city and the Moon. Buy your first business, reinvest, hire a manager to run it even while you sleep, and watch the numbers climb. Every business you own makes the pickleball dream possible. What you'll do 30 businesses , from loaner paddles and dink clinics to broadcast rights and a world championship. 30 managers who take over a business and run it while you are away. Each one levels up as your club grows. 88 upgrades to speed up production, raise profits and improve your venues. Four venues with their own economies: Hometown, Sunset Shores, Metro Heights and Moon Courts. Every venue plays differently The town fair fills the local courts. The coast has a high season and a low season. Metro Heights hits rush hour and the occasional media frenzy. On the Moon, gravity is low and meteors fall. Each venue has its own upgrades that change how its events work. Four minigames Ball Hunt, Dink Rally, Third Shot Drop and Serve Pattern. Play them for a cash prize when you want a break from the numbers, or ignore them completely. Your progress never depends on them. A rival who does not go away Preston Baseline III owns the tennis club across town and does not think pickleball is a real sport. He challenges you out of nowhere, and The Rivalry runs ten matches across the whole game before it settles. Things to collect and read 214 achievements and a Medal Wall with 29 pieces. 24 paddles , each earned by a different feat. Equip one for a small edge. The Kitchen Line Gazette , 21 editions that report on your club as you grow. A logbook that keeps every story moment, manager line and milestone you have seen. No strings One purchase. No ads, no microtransactions, nothing to unlock with money. Runs fully offline. No account and no internet connection needed. Eight languages: English, Latin American Spanish, French, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. Interface scaling, a high contrast mode and a reduced motion option in Settings.
