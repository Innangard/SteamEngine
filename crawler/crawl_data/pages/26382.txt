About This Content This Pack includes: T-126 tank (Rank 2 USSR); Zhukovsky's I-153-M62 fighter (Rank 1 USSR); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. T-126 The T-126 is an experimental light tank built in the summer of 1940 at Experimental Automobile Factory #185 in Leningrad, with the intention of creating a modern light vehicle for use in future conflicts. Despite being functionally similar to the T-50, the T-126 comes with some key differences. The T-126 trades speed for significantly more armor. It’s protected by 55mm on the front plate and 45mm on the turret and sides, giving it protection comparable to the T-34. It’s equipped with the 45 mm 20-K cannon, featuring powerful APHE rounds and a fast fire rate. While still classified as a light tank, the combination of speed, fast reload, and strong armor allows it to be played on the front lines as a conventional medium tank, making it quite versatile. Zhukovsky's I-153-M62 An advanced Soviet biplane fighter developed from the experience gained during the Spanish Civil War, where it was realized that the I-16 struggled to outmaneuver enemy biplanes. It wears the livery of pilot Sergey Y. Zhukovsky, 127th Fighter Aviation Regiment. The I-153 is an exceptional biplane, one of the most capable examples of all time, with very few enemy aircraft able to even think about outturning it. It has a strong climb rate and four fast-firing ShKAS 7.62 mm machine guns equipped with incendiary rounds, enough to take down even heavier aircraft. It can also equip bombs and rockets for light close air support duties. While fragile, the Chaika allows you to run circles around your enemies with ease and take them down quickly with a strong burst of fire. Note!
