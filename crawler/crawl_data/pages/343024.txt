Open mysterious boxes to collect rare and legendary warrior figures. Use these figures to expand your collection and participate in table battles with people around town. In this world full of strategy, excitement, and action, defeat your opponents to become the strongest collector. Unlock and sell different figure series. Each series has different figure warriors. Set up battle tables in your store, offer players the opportunity to battle at tables, and earn money from these battles. You can join the battles too! Build your collection and join the battles. Display your figures on shelves, set their prices, and sell them to collectors. If you wish, keep rare pieces to complete your collection. Design your own figure store. Unlock figure series, order surprise boxes, and sell them by placing them on your shelves. Choose a unique name for your store. Create the perfect store for collectors. Expand your collection with rare and valuable warrior figures from surprise boxes. You can earn money by selling these figures or complete your collection. Grow your store and attract more customers! Gain new customers and increase your profits with new shelves, race platforms, and figure boxes. ⚔️ Warrior Series - Four warrior series have joined the battlefield. Shape your army with different playstyles and strategic options. 🃏 Add-on System - Customize your team with add-on cards and gain advantages during battle. The right combination is the key to victory! 🏰 Battle Tables - Battles are now more tactical and spectacular with Tower Battles and Grand Battle tables. It's not just about power, but strategy too. 🧬 Race System - Bring together warriors of the same race and activate race bonuses. Make a difference on the battlefield with the right strategy. 👥 Employees - Employees are ready to assist you in the store. Simplify management, increase efficiency, and deepen your simulation experience. Table Battle Simulator offers you a collecting experience, legendary warrior figures, strategic tabletop battles, and an exciting adventure.
