View Community Hub About This Game Little Ghosties is a cozy idle desktop companion game where your ghosts live inside a terrarium on your desktop. Hatch, raise, and collect unique ghosts while they keep you company as you work, study or browse. Keep your ghosts happy with food and toys, or let them nap and do their own thing. Collect ghost slime, discover new ghost types, unlock terrariums, and grow your collection from tiny wisps into fully grown ghosts. The menus disappear when not in use, letting your ghosts live quietly on your desktop without getting in the way. Features 🐣 Hatch and raise ghosts – Watch your ghosts grow from Eggs into Wisps, Juveniles, and finally Fully Grown ghosts. 👻 Collect them all – Discover common and rare ghost types, each with their own adorable designs. 🧪 Collect Ghost Slime – Use your Hoover to gather slime and exchange it for new ghost eggs. 🔘 Idle economy – Your ghosts can press the button, but you can't! The happier they are, the harder they work. 🌍 Unlock new terrariums – Match the season or your mood. 💻 Lives on your desktop – Move and resize your terrarium anywhere on screen while you work, study, or relax. How to Play A ghost egg hatches and out pops a tiny Wisp Your Wisp is hungry, so it presses the button to earn Button Currency Spend Button Currency on food, toys, and new terrariums Feed your ghost and watch it grow through each stage of its life As your ghost grows, it produces Ghost Slime.
