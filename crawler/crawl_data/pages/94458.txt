View Community Hub About This Game Build your ultimate sanctuary alongside mysterious creatures and defend it from the hidden perils of this world. Join forces with your companions to uncover the realm's ancient secrets. Explore Awaken in a breathtaking, uncharted primitive world. Bond with powerful companions to conquer the land, sea, and sky, turning a beautiful but perilous wilderness into your ultimate playground. Resource gathering Harvest wood and stone to secure essential survival resources. Bring the right companions along to maximize your efficiency—they are always eager to lend a helping hand! Crafting Utilize gathered resources to forge advanced gear and weapons essential for survival. Assign your creatures to processing stations, channeling their unique abilities to power your entire camp's infrastructure. Building Enjoy unrestricted building freedom across the wilderness. Design a safe, thriving paradise where you and your creatures can live and survive side by side. Taming This world is full of mysterious monsters waiting for your protection. Set up traps, lull them to sleep with a Dream Brew, and tame them to welcome them home. Farming Leave the watering and fertilizing to the ones who do it best. Share the crops you’ve grown together and enjoy a cozy, moderately automated slow life. Player combat Danger is always around the corner in this primitive world. When enemies threaten your beloved family, you must take up arms yourself and lay your life on the line to protect them! Boss fight The ultimate test of your family's bond. Launch an all-out assault alongside the trusty companions who survived this harsh world with you, and strike down mighty threats!
