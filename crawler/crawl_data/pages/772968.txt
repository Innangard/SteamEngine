Grab your trusty d6, arrange your backpack so one lucky roll sets everything off, and see how far it goes in snappy 30-minute runs. Craft new degenerate combos to break the game, over and over and over again. Over 150 Cards, including a Rusty Nail and a Sock Full of Nickels Over 40 Memories to make battles easier… or weirder Over 35 tasty Snacks to boost your runs Pick any two Aspects (our word for classes) and draft Cards from both. For example: Tactical + Sk8er: stack Vulnerability on the enemy, then use Tricks to trigger your Cards again and again. Gamer + Tinker: stack the odds with extra dice while you upgrade your Cards. Every pair mixes a different set of Cards into your backpack, so no two runs build the same machine. Fall asleep and the school day follows you in. The Lunch Lady is serving seconds whether you want them or not. Your Geology Homework is still due. The Werecoach will make you run laps until you howl. The Alarm Clock is furious that you're still in bed. Plus plenty more Knightmares to discover and unlock as you play. Spend your Allowance on nostalgic ’90s memorabilia : Retro video games Lava lamps Boomboxes Inflatable chairs Sick skateboards Everything you need to survive Y2K.
