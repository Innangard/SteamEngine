The scientific community is on the verge of a major discovery and they need you to lead the expedition into newly uncovered caves. The secrets hidden there could change the history of mankind, and your skills in hostile terrain will allow you to take this unique challenge up in a previously unseen biome. The Season 1: Cave Odyssey includes: •	The new Sedona map, located in Arizona. Explore uncharted nooks in the heart of the desert. Whether you're helping a scientist simulate life on Mars or recovering a rocket's lost flight recorder, there's a whole new range of missions. •	A huge underground biome. Learn to make your way through the depths of the earth. •	Two new vehicles: The Shiba Overlander, an agile scout sneaking easily in narrow places, and the STEP-7249, a six-wheels truck which can confront any terrains. •	Two new specialists: an operator enhancing the drone, a jaeger specialized in mechanics.
