AND YOU'RE NOT ALONE. The Transference is a first-person mystery thriller escape room that combines puzzles, a story of family secrets, and moments of tension where you'll have to hide to survive. WHO LOCKED YOU IN? AND WHY? You wake up in your home and discover that the doors and windows have been boarded up from the inside. Someone has placed strange riddles throughout the rooms and turned your home into a prison. Explore, search for clues, and find a way out while trying to answer one question: who is behind all this? PUT YOUR WITS TO THE TEST Observe the details, connect clues, and experiment with mechanisms to make your way forward. Not every challenge is solved in the same way: you'll have to put your logic, attention, and even your stealth to the test. Take on challenges inspired by classics such as the Tower of Hanoi and the Dining Philosophers problem . Play Seven and a Half against a peculiar automaton using a very special Spanish deck of cards. Decipher enigmas inspired by Spanish poets, well-known stories, and their authors . Overcome challenges where observing and moving carefully will be just as important as thinking. Inventions, traditional games, and historical and cultural references from Andalusia and Spain intertwine with the puzzles, environments, and narrative, giving the mystery surrounding you its own identity. YOUR HOME HOLDS MORE SECRETS THAN YOU THINK Your captivity is only the beginning of a story of family secrets and betrayals. Every room, object, and recording can reveal a new piece of the mystery and change your perception of what happened. The story isn't just told: it's also investigated. Explore your surroundings, listen to testimonies, compare accounts, and connect the clues to reconstruct events that have remained hidden for years. With full Spanish and English voice acting , the voices bring the characters to life and turn every conversation and recording into a fundamental part of the mystery. YOU'RE NOT ALONE You won't always have peace and quiet to think. There are other inhabitants in your captivity, and to escape them you'll have to find somewhere to hide, observe their movements, and seize the right moment to get out. The tension comes from having them nearby and avoiding being discovered. Adjust the tension level to your liking and step into an experience without jumpscares , where hiding and escaping are part of the path toward the truth.
