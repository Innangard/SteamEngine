Jump into the egg-ceptional arcade action of Go! Go! Mister Chickums with this complete original soundtrack! Composed by Zane Little , this collection features all 17 tracks from the game, plus a bonus disc with an additional 20 tracks of pure poultry power! Behind the colourful chaos of Go! Go! Mister Chickums awaits you a soundtrack crafted with pure joy and dedication. Composer Zane Little explains: "I had an absolute blast working on the music for Go! Go! Mr. Chickums! I was given a lot of freedom with this project, and so I just had as much fun as I could with it. My favorite part of this project was probably developing a little voice for Mr. Chickums to sneak into the game's music and jingles to make it sound as though he is singing along. I wanted to maintain an authentic chiptune style for the game while also leveraging modern synths to accentuate the energy within each track, as well as to create Mr. Chickums' quacky singing voice from scratch! All in all, I had as much fun making the music as I did playing the game."
