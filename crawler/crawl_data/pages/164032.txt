View Community Hub About This Game An arcade classic is back! The Magic Pendulum that brings toys to life has been stolen by the villain Scrubby! As Inguz, your mission is to fight your way through vibrant worlds, rescue allied toys, and face a relentless biomechanical army to restore order to the kingdom. Inspired by the intensity of classics like Metal Slug, Biomechanical Toy is a platformer and shooter experience with incredible pixel art visuals, created by the legendary Zeus Software in 1995. Modern features and nostalgia This definitive version preserves the original arcade experience and adds essential tools for today’s players: - Rewind: Made a mistake? Go back in time instantly. - Save States: Save and load your progress at any time. - Screen Filters: Relive the feel of original CRT monitors. - Jukebox and Gallery : Explore the soundtrack and exclusive concept art. Key features - Non-Stop Action: Fast-paced 2D platforming and shooting gameplay. - Diverse Arsenal: Use explosive ammo, automatic fire, and full-screen bombs. - Epic Bosses: Face giant toys and challenging combat mechanics. - Living World: Interact with the environment, use mushrooms to jump high, and destroy chests to collect items.
