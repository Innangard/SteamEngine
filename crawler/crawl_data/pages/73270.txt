The entire game is free to play, with a cosmetic pass DLC available to those who want to support development. Work together with your friends to build, craft, and train to progress in this fun to play RPG. Now arguably most importantly, what exactly can you do with your friends? The answer is everything! Every non pvp battle that occurs in the world can be joined by 9 players! This is done through the Link System. When you are linked in a battle, all damage, jamming, and healing to the enemy is replicated, as well as half the damage to your monsters and half of friendly healing. This allows you to strategically work with your friends to take down strong enemies. Work together to clear the 10 Towers that are spread out across the map, and the final boss that comes after that!
