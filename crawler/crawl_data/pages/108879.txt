Please speedrun this game. I'm begging. About This Game Descend through absurd layers of history as a rat-dog-witch trying to escape employment and transcend reality. Explore weird museums, cosmic hallways, and your own bad dreams in a chaotic mash-up of art, humor, and a playable existential crisis. Fuzz Dungeon invites players into an evolving funhouse of human absurdity. Enter into a playable collage of forgotten centuries, workplace dread, and divine nonsense. Each floor feels like a fever dream of culture itself, only it's been put through a broken xerox machine and dunked in a concoction of strange chemicals. The deeper you go, the more the systems collapse-- time becomes malleable, enemies become jokes, and your inventory fills with objects that shouldn’t exist. But you're not just exploring the dungeon; you're travelling through an absurdist version of human history. Funny, sad, and endlessly strange, Fuzz Dungeon is a pilgrimage through the chaos of creation. A game about getting lost, laughing about it, and realizing the exit might just be the punchline.
