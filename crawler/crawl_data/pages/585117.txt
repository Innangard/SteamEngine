View Community Hub About This Game A cozy relaxing farming sim with a cute simplistic art style with vibrant colors, characters, and classic gameplay. 🌿 About the Game Escape the noise of city life and start over on a colorful tropical island full of secrets and possibilities. With a cute minimalist art style, vibrant colors, and relaxing classic gameplay, Sunset Sprout offers a peaceful farming and life simulation experience for players who love cozy games , crafting , and island adventures . 📖 Story After realizing your 9–5 job isn’t making you happy, you decide to make a change — to become a farmer! In the southern seas of Kur, you discover an oddly affordable island for sale. You put everything on the line — your savings, your car, and even most of your clothes — to buy it and start fresh on the Island of Sunset Sprout . Build your dream farm, meet new friends, uncover the island’s mysteries, and become part of its close-knit community . Care for animals, decorate your home, and help rebuild the island. Whether you’re tending your fields, exploring for hidden treasures, or chatting with the locals, Sunset Sprout offers a relaxing cozy farming sim experience perfect for fans of Stardew Valley , Harvest Moon , and Animal Crossing .
