UniDuni is a tale of two tiny friends that have their lives turned upside down in a blink of an eye and suddenly have to challenge themselves and their beliefs. An introspective adventure of acceptance and change. Explore this odd perky world made by hand with a friend in a seamless local co-op experience just like old times or play online using Steam's Remote Play Together. Support (or mess with) each other by solving exotic trials or take the challenge to the limit making all the work yourself! Key features: As you wish: Approach every challenge, collect all guaranas and perfectly avoid the hazards before the time runs out to unlock the astonishing gold medal, or play in a more casual and relaxed way enjoying every subtle detail. It's up to you! Unique environments: Dive into exciting landscapes with peculiar obstacles and designs that tell a story. Progress through forests with gigantic trees, dense slimy swamps, cold introspective mountains, frantic scorching volcanoes and gloomy complex dungeons. Funny problem solving: Play with mechanics that interact with each other. Push boxes, jump on springs, shoot the cannons, avoid the spikes, keep the cool on the balancing board, find secrets and much more! Simple and cozy story: Discover a wholehearted story of emotions and actions. Help Uni and Duni to bring hope to the most unexpected places and foes. Engage with cute friends and weird enemies by trying to restore what was once stolen.
