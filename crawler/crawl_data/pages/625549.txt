The game spans a 50-week time limit, and you play as a humanoid AI awakened by the resistance organization GOLD. Leading a squad in the cybercity "Hanport City," you run a bar on the surface while secretly taking on commissions, gradually changing the city's fate to ultimately overthrow the Eastern Han regime. Core Combat The game features the most engaging aspect of Darkest Dungeon's turn-based combat, where action order is determined by each character's speed stat. When a character's HP drops to 0, they enter a near-death state—female characters in this state will have a clothes-breaking effect. While near death, characters can still use their skills, but every hit they take carries a chance of permanent death. Sanity points are consumed during dungeon exploration and combat. When a character's Sanity drops too low, they will enter a cyber "psycho" state. This "psycho" state puts the character into a completely randomized condition—they might taunt the player, or they might receive a massive boost in combat power, introducing a significant degree of variation and unpredictability to the combat experience. Exquisite Illustrations + Expert Writing L2D animated art + CGs + professional CV voice acting + nearly 500,000 words of text. And every female character has a clothes-breaking effect when near death! Each character has their own dedicated bond questline—reach the maximum bond level to unlock their ultimate secret story CG! Character, Skill & Equipment Synergy — Build Your Own Playstyle In the mid-to-late game, a large number of equipment pieces with special skills will become available. You can use these equipment skills alongside your characters' own abilities to lower the difficulty of battles. Equipment can also be enhanced and reforged multiple times, which, when combined with character talent development and different skills, allows you to deal massive damage and craft a build that is uniquely yours. Simulation Management Gameplay You have 50 weeks—be sure to become a master of time management. The gameplay revolves around running a bar, with time advancing through turns. Each turn, you have a limited number of actions and can assign your characters to stat training, equipment R&D, work dispatches, bond building, and commission fulfillment. A total of 13 young men and women will join your squad. You can deepen your bonds with them through gift-giving, chatting, and fighting alongside them, while crafting exclusive skills and unique equipment for each individual. Every companion has their own personal storyline, and your nurturing and choices will ultimately determine their fate. A Classic Reimagining Told in Nearly 500,000 Words: A Three Kingdoms Tale That Seems Worlds Apart Yet Resonates in Spirit You play as a humanoid AI, leading a group of young men and women in an alternate cyberpunk Three Kingdoms world, fighting side by side and growing together, each searching for their own path. You will personally experience fresh reimaginings of iconic Three Kingdoms events, and confront ideological conflicts and intensifying contradictions with historical figures and major factions: Zhang Jiao’s utopian Great Peace, Liu Bei’s virtue-driven leadership, Cao Cao’s claim that Heaven’s timing is with him, and Sun Ce’s reliance on strategic terrain. Are you a puppet to their ideals, or the master of your own destiny? You must choose between love and destruction, and on the boundary between AI and humanity, repeatedly question where your “true heart” truly lies. In summary: A Three Kingdoms tale that's familiar yet fresh. Rebellious punk that defies the mainstream. A super-frankenstein mishmash of various game elements. Perhaps perfect for someone with uniquely refined tastes.
