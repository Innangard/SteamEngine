The Curse of Resthaven Concept Art Go behind the scenes and see how Resthaven came to life. This digital concept art booklet collects artwork from throughout the development of Curse of Resthaven , tracing the game's evolution from its earliest ideas to the finished world. See how the people of Resthaven evolved from rough sketches and initial concepts into the characters you meet in the finished game. The book also features backgrounds and environment art, showing the process of building Resthaven itself, from early visual ideas and exploratory sketches through to the richly illustrated locations of the final game. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Curse of Resthaven is a narrative horror game intended for mature audiences. The art book contains illustrated black-and-white depictions of violence and murder. The game itself also includes references to suicide and themes of war and colonial oppression, as well as references to alcohol and substance abuse, psychological distress, and other subject matter that may be disturbing to some people.
