You have been forcefully stranded aboard The Avarice in order to participate in an underground tournament. Find a way to win several rounds of a sadistic poker dice game that the elites have concocted on their luxury mega yacht. Will you be able to break the cycle, or will the body count keep rising? Roll your five dice to get the highest poker hand. Each round gives you 3 re-rolls. The player with the highest hand wins the round. Play until you've cleaned out your opponent - or your opponent cleans you out. Place bets by cutting off your own fingers - or watch as the boss loses theirs. Lose the game, lose your life - get tossed overboard and replaced with a new poor soul, and start again. Another match lost is another notch on the body count. Remember to hide away your best resources in the hidden compartment of your cell for the next run. Rig your dice up with demonically sealed special abilities crafted using materials and fingers you've won through poker games. Unlock charms and curses for your dice that allow you to build a strategy towards defeating your opponents and rigging the game in your favor. Unlock multiple endings by deciphering a codex. Find hints and clues to the code scattered throughout emails and your cell.
