Pick cards, place gates, and collect charms. Settle into the loop as numbers go up and dopamine fills your brain. See how far you can go in the pursuit of infinity. Big Variety: With a heap of different game-changing cards, create synergies and manipulate the ways you reach the goal. Will you go for big scores or will you chase insane speeds? Draft your build: Pick your cards, discover synergies, and put together a winning build. Watch the cards jump as they trigger from many different sources. Unique Effects: Cards can give you a bunch of different effects: from increasing passive multipliers, to spawning ghost cars, to rolling the dice for a jackpot, to triggering other cards altogether. Gate Madness: Place gates on the track to enable even more synergies and enhance the craziness that unfolds. Optimise Placement: Each track comes with a number of gate placement slots. Figure out the best combo order of gates to get the most out of each gate. Chain Reaction: Trigger a chain of reaction of passive multipliers, unique effects, and compounding synergies that turn a normal run into a fireworks show. Collect Charms: Discover and stack powerful passive Charms, which further change how your run plays out. Zero to Hero: Tip the scales from a modest drive into a screen tearing powerhouse of dopamine. Unleash full potential: Synergize your build between cards, gates, and charms to unlock the true ecstasy of The Loopler Push the car beyond its limits. Fine-tune each aspect of your build, max out your cards and gates, and assemble the god run to see if you can reach infinity. Prepare for the challenge accordingly, you will need it. 9 Cars, each with their own identity and effects 9 Tracks with different looks and layouts to fully challenge your build 5 difficulty modes to make it even harder to go infinite Daily and weekly challenges, with a variety of optional leaderboards 50 Cards to draft 30 Gates to place 70 Charms to collect Chill soundtrack by Pulsewith (Justin Skomarovsky) Infinity awaits System Requirements Windows macOS SteamOS + Linux Minimum: OS: Windows 10, 11 x64 Processor: Intel Core i3 Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: no Storage: 500 MB available space Recommended: Memory: 2 GB RAM Storage: 500 MB available space Minimum: OS: macOS 12.0 Processor: Apple M1 or Intel Core i5 Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: no Storage: 500 MB available space Recommended: Memory: 2 GB RAM Storage: 500 MB available space Minimum: OS: Ubuntu 20.04 Processor: 4 hardware CPU threads - Intel® Core™ i5 750 or higher Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: no Storage: 500 MB available space Recommended: Memory: 2 GB RAM Storage: 500 MB available space View all What Curators Say 25 Curators have reviewed this product.
