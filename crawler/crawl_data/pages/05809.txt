Hover your cursor to extract resources and synthesise fuel cells to explore previously inaccessible sectors and earn your ticket home. Just remember - machinery doesn't need sleep, and efficiency will be rewarded. Simply hover your mouse over organisms to harvest resources from them. No clicking needed. No timers. A nav-malfunction has dumped you and your crew in uncharted space. You decide to take refuge on a nearby planet. Instead of a rescue party, Head Office has a new directive: begin a full-scale harvest of the planet's mysterious, energy-dense organisms. Features Unique combo mode : When activated grants a multiplier to the resources harvested. Aim for higher value targets whilst in a combo to maximise your harvest - efficiency will be rewarded. Skill Tree Progression: Spend your resources in a large skill tree with over 170 upgrades that will improve your extraction beams, influence organism spawn rates, unlock unique abilities, automate your harvesting and boost factory efficiency The Fuel Factory: Deposit harvested Orbs and Spores to synthesise Fuel Cells . The factory can be automated and the efficiency improved with upgrades in the skill tree Idle mechanics: You can unlock idle harvesting , and factory automation , so, you can sit back and watch those numbers go up Discover New Areas: Spend Fuel Cells to unlock new areas with higher organism densities and new organisms with unique abilities to harvest from and upgrade Aesthetic & Atmosphere: Art, SFX and music made by me (No AI) :0 Steam Achievements: 20 Steam achievements to be completed Music for Sporbs: A complete unique soundtrack for Sporbs is available (again, no AI used) System Requirements Windows macOS Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 or later Processor: Dual Core 2.0 GHZ or better Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: Any graphic card Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system Minimum: OS: 10.15 Catalina or above Processor: Apple M1 or Intel Core M Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: Metal-capable GPU (Integrated or Dedicated) Storage: 1 GB available space Recommended: View all What Curators Say 4 Curators have reviewed this product.
