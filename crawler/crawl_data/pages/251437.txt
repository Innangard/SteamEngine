For the Tilt Five™ system, modifications designed to improve immersion in the game have been added, including: Navigate the Diorama using the Tilt Five™ wand, with full control of the scenery view, rotating, zooming, and panning; The fourth, which is the game's MENU, was positioned below the Tilt Five™ board, giving the feeling of interacting with a model and choosing options on the objects inside it; After finishing the Dioramas, interact with the Tilt Five™ wand on some objects in the scenery and watch them animate and come to life.
