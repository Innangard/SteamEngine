YOU'RE STILL BEHIND THE BAR. The city is in the middle of an outbreak, but the bar has bills to pay. So you keep working. Across several shifts, regulars come in to drink, complain, overshare, and occasionally make your job much harder. Take their orders, talk to them, and choose who gets more of your time. Alcohol is supposed to offer extra protection from the virus. The best way to get someone properly pickled is to drink with them after work — but every contest costs money, and you can't afford to help everyone. KEY FEATURES: Work the Bar Take orders, pour drinks, keep impatient guests happy, and earn enough to stay afloat while your regulars bring their problems to the counter. Get to Know Regulars Choose who you spend time with and what you ask them about. Different conversations reveal different sides of their lives, and sticking with someone long enough leads to the end of their story. Decide Who You Can Help Spend the bar's money on drinking contests with the people you want to protect. There isn't enough for everyone, so sooner or later you'll have to choose. Drink After Work Play pub games inspired by Never Have I Ever , beer pong , and more. Drink with your regulars and hear the things they don't usually tell you from the other side of the counter. Last Pill Bar is a short first-person narrative bartending game about alcohol, other people's problems, and trying to help during a virus outbreak without running your bar into the ground. A playthrough takes around 1 hour. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains strong language, depictions of alcohol abuse and addiction, and disturbing psychological themes.
