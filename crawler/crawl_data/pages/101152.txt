Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game In Nekomancer you play as a cat fighting through swarms of enemies. Perks Perks unlock at each level-up, spanning offense, defense, and economy categories, each with a rarity tier and an elemental type (Physical, Fire, Ice, Earth and Electricity). Builds vary from run to run based on what's offered. Weapons Weapon drops let you swap in new guns during a run, each with its own stats offering vastly different playstyle. Once equipped, a weapon stays for the rest of the run. Each cat can carry 3 weapons. Maps Maps are procedurally generated, increasing in intensity as they progress. Clearing one opens a portal for a short window to collect loot before moving to the next, with some maps ending in a boss fight. Each map has various objectives to complete, some being extremely rare.
