View Community Hub About This Game -"I'm obsessed with this game; the builds are insane. 10/10"- Custom loadout Choose your Class, Equipment, Upgrades and Artifacts to discover each time new powerful synergies . Carry an infinite number of weapons at once ( guns + artifacts ) and improve them by eliminating ever stronger enemies . Equipment Classes Gameplay 112 upgrades, 30 unlockable equipment, 19 deck cards, and 11 artifact weapons to build your OP arsenal. Eliminate all enemies, face mini-bosses, and win by defeating the Archon of Red or the new Icon of Tyranny . 7 different mini-bosses that teleport, grab, summon enemies, and much more. 4 classes, each with 4 weapons to suit any playstyle. Matches lasting 15 minutes. 9 difficulty levels, including Madness , One-Shot , Endless Elites , and Infinite Survival with a Public Leaderboard . Collect souls to unlock new upgrades and fragments to enhance your weapons. Destroy minerals scattered across the map for small stat bonuses. Auto-aim/Manual-aim and manual/auto fire, with faster movement when not shooting. Dash through almost anything without taking damage. Unlock pets that automatically gather resources, block, attack enemies, and more.
