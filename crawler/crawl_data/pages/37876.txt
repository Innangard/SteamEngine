About This Game Chasing after her assailant, Tashia discovers a bustling market, hidden under a sprawling cotton tree, the Halfway Market. Quickly realizing that she has lost much more than a cell phone. Tashia is distraught to discover that somehow she's become enmeshed in a murder mystery.  She must now find the culprit among the ghostly denizens of the market. Explore a Hidden World! Navigate a beautiful world that blends the magical with the mundane. Explore the various vending stalls as you conduct your investigation. Visit each location at different times throughout the day as the suspects move about. Investigate the Halfway Market! A magical place populated by Duppy, mischievous spirits who trick and teach in equal measure. Collect clues , uncover mysterious motives and confirm which suspect has the means to conduct this heinous caper! Solve the Mystery! Interrogate 7 suspects based on creatures of Myth & Legend. Cross reference statements based on the facts you've gathered and deduce the culprit responsible!
