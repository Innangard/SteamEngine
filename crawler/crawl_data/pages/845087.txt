Features – Realistic modeling with over 100 custom modelled buildings. – Detailed ground polygon model including taxiway bridges – Modelled Taxi signs. – Updated taxi light models that follow the curve of the lines and wigwag lights placed at runway entrances. – Dynamic runway approach lighting with runway sequenced flashers. – Beautiful background cityscape from OSM (open street maps). Fifteen major airlines (and several connection brands) service travelers at PHL. PHL is the primary international hub of US Airways. Southwest Airlines and US Airways are the largest competitors at PHL. Over 30,000 passengers travel through the airport each year. Philadelphia International Airport has six terminals with a total of 126 gates. Non pre-cleared international arrivals are processed in Terminal A. The B/C connector and Terminal F. Terminal A is divided into two sections, east and west. Terminal A West has 13 gates, while Terminal A East has 11 gates. Terminals B and C have 15 and 14 gates respectively. They are the two main terminals used by American. Terminal D has 16 gates; E has 17 gates and F terminal has 38 gates.
