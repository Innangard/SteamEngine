Zero Colour is a roguelike card game where every run is different. Play cards that share a colour or a number to build longer and longer chains — the longer your combo, the harder you hit. But careful: the enemy chains too. Four ways to play Story Mode — Three sectors, each guarded by its own boss. Learn the rules at your own pace and discover the special cards （it is a mode for progressing through the levels, not dialogue or the story itself）. Corruption Zone — The pure roguelike mode. Choose your path across the map, buy upgrades between fights and push as far as you can. Ascension levels await when you want it to get serious. Infinite Mode — Endless "waves" and a high score to beat. Easy to learn, hard to master. Battle Royale — You against three rivals. Last one standing wins. Five decks, many strategies Unlock five decks, each changing the way you play. Collect cards, build your own deck in the editor and find the combination that suits your style, theres hidden combinations that does extra effects.
