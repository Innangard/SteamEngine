View Community Hub About This Game Make money. Buy gears. Make more money. Buy more gears. That's the whole idea, and it's dangerously satisfying. Idling Gears is an idle clicker where your machine spreads across a map full of hidden machines waiting to be found. Click the golden gear, place gears that earn for you, and watch the numbers get silly. How it works Click the golden gear for your first coins Spend them on gears that produce money on their own Buy upgrades to boost every gear you have When progress crawls, Ascend : reset for permanent boosts and go again, faster Explore the map and uncover the hidden machines scattered across it Simple to learn, hard to put down, with lots of achievements to chase!
