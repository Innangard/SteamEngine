FPS + roguelike: an explosive mix Divine Wish is a high-speed first-person shooter where each run is a unique challenge. Explore random combinations of weapons and artifacts, adapt to new threats, and prove your skills in boss fights. Speed, reaction, and accuracy are key skills for your survival. No saves - every death brings you back to the beginning, but with a new experience. Each attempt is unique thanks to a system of classes and random artifacts. Stickers that change your abilities There are no classic character classes in Divine Wish - you choose how to play! Before each race, you get a sticker that gives you unique effects and changes the gameplay. But it's not that simple - each sticker gives you both a powerful bonus and a weakness, so you have to choose wisely. Boss battles Every 10th wave is an epic boss battle that will test your skills to the brim. Unique attacks and mechanics make you adapt to each battle. The gradual increase in difficulty ensures that you will not have an easy time. Only the best players will be able to defeat all the bosses and complete the game. Death is only the beginning As in any roguelike, each run is a chance to become stronger. Explore enemies, experiment with stickers, and find your perfect playstyle. Divine Wish is an explosive combination of roguelike and FPS. Ready to take on the challenge?
