Kosárba tesz Közösségközpont megnézése A játékról HippoHop is a psychedelic jazz-club flyer inspired by the music and art of the 50s. You tap to flap through handcrafted levels of colorful musical instruments rendered in a flat, bold-color, screen-printing aesthetic. A variety of drink-themed pickups grant you extra points or temporary abilities, enabling you to traverse a range of unexpected situations. As you progress from track to track, not only does the game require tighter flying and the combined abilities of multiple pickups to succeed, but intrusive thoughts of unpaid bills emerge to block your path more and more often.
