We've developed a cozy idle game just for you. Now, you can enjoy birdwatching from the comfort of your desktop, too! Place your postcard — a window to another world — right at the corner of your screen. Decorate your favourite birding spot to your heart's content. If a bird happens to fancy your humble abode, it just might stop by for a visit! Join us on Discord! Unlock a variety of birding spots! The docks, beaches, deserts, even rainforests… Each place has its own unique scenery, and new bird species to discover! (The demo version is currently limited to the Balcony.) Your Birding Area can be freely manipulated! Zoom, move, and arrange your favourite birding spot however you like — the birds will keep you company while you work. The Blueprint System is now live! You can save your builds, share them, and quickly switch between different designs! Sparrows aren't the only birds that want to hang out. There are dozens of other bird species, too! They have their own preferred times to visit — maybe you can capture something wonderful! (The demo currently features around 20 bird species!) The more a bird visits, the more comfortable they'll feel! You might be able to discover new behaviors over time… Snap! Immortalize your favourite moments with your camera. Once you've documented every unique behavior from a bird, you'll earn a statue of them! Any new lifers today? Your handbook also keeps a record of Birding Areas, decoration, photographs, achievements, and more! If you enlarge the main illustration, you'll be able to discover just what traits make that bird special! You can change the time of day and weather of your Birding Area as you desire. Regardless of time, or weather, you'll meet a new friend who enjoys it, that's for sure! In the night, nocturnal birds await… Your bird friends will bring all sorts of trinkets for you to collect! They'll drop all sorts of colorful feathers, and maybe more… Boss Crow loves anything that sparkles — whether it be shimmering feathers, or the bright glint of bottle caps! He'll return the favor in Crow Coins!
