Experience diverse combat styles through a distinctive gun customization system.  Dominate classic bomb defusal missions with your tactics, and stay tuned for more modes coming soon. We understand that not everyone can indulge in all-day gaming or wander around in an unnecessarily large map only to be eliminated in seconds.
