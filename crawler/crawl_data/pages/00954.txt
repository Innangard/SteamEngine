View Community Hub Reviews “For those on the hunt for their next multiplayer obsession, may I turn your attention to Dumpster Gang?” Polygon “Embraces that playful silliness of some of the best co-op experiences out there” TheSixthAxis About This Game Play as a gang of mischievous raccoons in this chaotic co-op sandbox for 1-4 players. Complete ridiculous missions, steal everything you can carry, and outsmart a relentless Exterminator determined to throw your gang behind bars. Live the raccoon dream. Knock over trash cans, steal shiny junk, raid snacks, commit tiny crimes, and unleash your inner menace. Sprint, climb walls, crawl through vents, and reach places only a raccoon could. Pick up, throw, steal, or smash nearly everything you find and turn every level into your own chaotic playground. Play solo or with friends. Work together to carry heavy loot, solve puzzles, and rescue captured teammates... but things never go according to plan. Betrayals, disasters, and hilarious accidents are all part of the job. Cause enough chaos and the Exterminator will hunt you down. Outsmart his traps, escape capture, and rescue your friends before your gang ends up behind bars. Return home after every mission to customize your raccoon, unlock hats with unique abilities, and decorate your hideout with every ridiculous item you've stolen.
