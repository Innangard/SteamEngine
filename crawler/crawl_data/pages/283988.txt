Idle Fields is a relaxing Idle game where you grow crops, fish, cook, mine, smith, decorate your house, take care of animals, and complete everything possible by leveling up skills and unlocking rewards. Level up Farming, Husbandry, Fishing, Cooking, Woodcutting, Mining, Smithing, and Constrution! This game offers you: 50 levels in 8 different skills Workers to automate skills Many achievements with more to come A relaxing atmosphere Offline Progression. Beautiful outfits for all your workers This game does NOT offer you: An insane depth like other idle games. There are few activities with more to come. A ton of replayability. All skills are simple. Cross skill interactions are planned. Is This Game For You? If you like a simple short-experienced game you won't have to focus on often, yes! If you want something super engaging, maybe not.
