As a drone taxi pilot, soar over the city, pick up customers, and safely deliver them to their destinations. With its simple controls but deep strategy, it appeals to both casual and competitive players. The difficulty increases with each level: you have to cope with rotating mines, passing planes, and dynamic weather conditions. Feel the effects of rain and snow from level 3 onwards; the wind hits your drone and makes it more difficult to control. Be careful when your customer is next to you — a collision could cost your customer their life and you will receive a -100 TL fine. Key Features • ⭐ 1 or 2 Player Mode — Play alone or race with a friend on the same screen to grab customers. • 🎮 Full Gamepad Support — Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, and other standard controllers are automatically recognized. Control everything from the menu to the game with the gamepad without ever touching the keyboard. • 🌤️ Dynamic Weather — Rain, snow, and wind effects are activated from episode 3 onwards. Weather conditions directly affect the drone's speed and direction. • 🌙 Day/Night Transition — Random day or night in each episode; automatically changes in the next episode. Stars and city lights await you in night episodes. • 💨 Detailed Effects — Smoke changing from yellow to gray, multi-colored explosions, gold coin particles, screen shaking, and more. • 🔊 Web Audio Synthesis — No external audio files. Engine sounds, music, explosions, and delivery melodies are generated in real time. • 🌐 Dual Language Support — Turkish and English interface, clients speak to you, currency changes automatically (₺ / $). • ⚙️ Customizable Controls — Set your own key settings; all preferences are remembered in your browser. • 🗺️ Mini Map — All platforms, customers, destinations, and drones are displayed live in the bottom right corner. • 📱 Play in Browser — Single HTML file, zero setup. Compatible with Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Game Mechanics Control the drone with three buttons (or a gamepad): push up, move left/right. Gravity is constantly effective, and your fuel is limited. You can rest by landing on platforms. Customers appear on the map with yellow balloons and attract attention with an audible call (“Hey Taxi!”). When the drone approaches, the customer automatically boards. Fly to the green destination marker to make deliveries and earn +50₺ and +100 points. In two-player mode, customers are shared — whoever catches them first gets them! Complete the level by delivering 10 customers in each section. Mine and aircraft collisions cause loss of life and customer death. Running out of fuel or losing health means the game ends.
