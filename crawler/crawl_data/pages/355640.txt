You'll have the opportunity to uncover and control the major events of the 1960s, from the mysterious assassinations of Marilyn Monroe to John F. Kennedy. Manage your empire like a seasoned tycoon, making critical decisions that will shape the course of your operations. Engage in RPG-style interactions with a diverse cast of henchmen, gangsters, and iconic personalities from the vibrant 1960s. But beware, your enemies are determined to oust you. Cultivate your invaluable collection of secret files to always stay one step ahead. Take charge of Jey's Empire and become the enigmatic puppet master of an era defined by power, intrigue, and strategic prowess. Are you ready to make history?
