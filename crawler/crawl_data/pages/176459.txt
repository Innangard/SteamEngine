View Community Hub About This Game Connected Towers is a strategic puzzle game where you control a little robot on a mission to connect and disconnect towers from their power supply. Navigate through a variety of challenging levels, each filled with obstacles like gates, mazes, moving platforms, and treacherous lava pits. With every tower pushed into place, you’ll need to use logic and timing to overcome the puzzles and reach the end goal. Featuring 27 increasingly difficult levels , Connected Towers starts off easy, allowing players to grasp the core mechanics before ramping up the complexity. Each level is packed with its own unique challenges, and upon completion, players are rewarded with achievements. Race against the clock to complete levels as quickly as possible and compare your times with other players on the global leaderboards. Set in a world of charming voxel art , and accompanied by an atmospheric soundtrack, Connected Towers delivers a visually appealing and mentally stimulating experience for fans of puzzle and strategy games. Key Features: Challenging Puzzles : Solve increasingly difficult levels that test your strategic thinking. Single Player Adventure : A solo experience designed for puzzle lovers. Unique Obstacles : Navigate through gates, mazes, moving platforms, and avoid dangerous lava. Leaderboard Integration : Compete with other players for the fastest times. Achievements : Earn rewards as you conquer each level. Voxel Art Style : Enjoy vibrant, minimalistic graphics with a cute, colorful twist. Engaging Soundtrack : Immerse yourself in the game with an awesome, fitting score. Step into the world of Connected Towers and take on the challenge of guiding your robot through an array of intricate puzzles!
