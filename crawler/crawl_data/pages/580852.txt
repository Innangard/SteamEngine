About This Content Agent, an urgent mission requires your immediate attention! In the course of Operation Withering Dusk’s 5 missions, you’ll investigate a major data breach against emission-tracking company Kotok Analytics. As the climate crisis intensifies, governments and large corporations are quickly learning the economic value of climate data analysis, and bad actors have taken notice. A mysterious group is targeting major scientific organizations, and you must determine who is behind these attacks before they can strike again. Along the way, you’ll encounter a bizarre series of intelligent tools, learn the increased strength of a familiar foe and take an entire city off the grid. Key features of Operation Withering Dusk includes: Uplink51 Module: AI Chatbots, capable of text interaction, decoding tools and executing StingerOS commands. 5 new missions (2 - 8 hours of gameplay) 2 new Hydra terminals (Video Surveillance Looping and POWERNu Control Panel) Increased intrusion tool: 2-Factor Authentication Attack via Phone CID Backdoor 2 exclusive skins, 7 unique achievements, plus a promotion (if you can complete the operation successfully!) The first mission is available to all Agents, and will grant access to the Uplink51 Module with an interactive version of FAY. The rest of the Operation is available with purchase of this DLC.
