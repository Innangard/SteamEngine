Fine-tune your survival experience to suit your playstyle, whether you want to take on a hardcore survival challenge or enjoy a more laid-back journey. Use the custom difficulty settings to adjust individual survival needs, crafting costs, death penalties, and combat encounters whenever you like, as often as you like, without affecting your progress or achievements. BUILD, CUSTOMIZE AND PILOT YOUR AIRSHIP Your high-tech airship will become your mobile base, workshop, laboratory, and lifeline as you navigate and explore an ecologically ruined Earth. Unlock new technologies to upgrade and expand your airship, allowing you to reach new heights, venture further, and thrive in this desolate environment. Fully customize the interior and exterior of your airship to make it uniquely yours and one of a kind. SCAVENGE RUINED SKIES Discover the remnants of humanity's attempts to escape the dust engulfing the surface below. Scavenge abandoned makeshift towers for supplies, materials, and clues to the past. Harvest drifting debris caused by a strange anomaly to craft tools and upgrade your airship. Uncover the story of humanity's struggle and ultimate fall, and piece together the fate of the crews sent on missions before yours. HARNESS SCIENCE TO THRIVE Use science and technology to expand your capabilities and unlock new possibilities. Craft essential tools and devices, scan and analyze lost technology to reverse engineer it, and develop new ways to make survival easier. Discover innovative ways to use science to your advantage, from harnessing the power of lightning storms to automating resource extraction or streamlining the production of essential food and water. OVERCOME A CHANGED WORLD In Forever Skies, danger lurks both above and below the toxic dust blanketing the planet. High above the surface, protect yourself and your airship from powerful lightning storms, hostile drones, and other uncharted threats. Venture beneath the dust to explore a strange new world, transformed beyond recognition in humanity’s absence. Face evolved flora and fauna as you uncover the mysteries of Earth’s collapse and search for the cure that could save what’s left of humanity.
