View Community Hub Expect To Die. A Lot. About This Game With boundless sea at your back and a towering gate ahead, you have no choice but to walk into the maze. Hunt, gather, and run to survive, alone or with friends. In this survival horror, you're not the only one watching. You're not the only one starving. Find Your Own Way No one is waiting around the corner to guide you. The only allies are the ones you bring: your friends. So choose wisely, plan carefully, and think outside the box. Tear branches for torches. Break crates for scraps. Build crude defenses, light your path, and hold your ground when the maze closes in. Everything you carry, you earned. Gather and Endure Just because it’s quiet or the view looks good doesn’t mean it’s safe. Craft weapons from wood, stone, and scrap to defend yourself. Hunt for food, light fires, pick berries, and collect anything that helps you survive. Do not linger in one spot for too long... Face What Haunts You The maze isn’t empty. It's alive. You'll encounter twisted beings shaped by hunger, curiosity, or something worse. Some like to crawl. Some like to wait. Some mimic what once was human. You have to keep moving. No. Matter. What.
