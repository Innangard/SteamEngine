Buy Log Away - Halloween $1.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Turn your Log Away retreat into a playful Halloween hideaway Return to Log Away with playful spooky décor, warm candlelight, and a calm seasonal soundtrack. This DLC adds Halloween‑themed items that let your retreats feel a little more mysterious, without ever losing their coziness and beauty. How it feels A pumpkin lantern lights the hallway. A web shelf holds tiny curiosities. A knit pumpkin sits beside a crystal ball, as if the room is telling a quiet story just for you. Green firelight dances in the fireplace, and the soundtrack keeps everything slow, soft, and safe. It’s a Halloween night where nothing is chasing you, and nothing needs to be done. What you’ll find A set of Halloween‑themed furniture and decorations to mix into any Retreat: pumpkin and spooky tables, ghost seating, shelves, cabinets, curtains, and cozy nighttime pieces. Seasonal accents that complete the mood: pumpkin plates and mugs, lanterns, rugs, garlands, spider lights, magic potions, a big pumpkin centerpiece, and a pet owl companion. A calm Halloween soundtrack with a gentle, moody feel that never interrupts your pace. A soft invitation This Halloween DLC keeps Log Away exactly as it is, only a little more glowing and enchanted. No new goals, no timers. Just playful spooky warmth you can mix into any retreat whenever you want the world to feel quieter and a little more magical.
