Download Ghetto Zombies: Graffiti Squad - Artbook Digital Free To Play Download View Community Hub About This Content This is an artbook compiling a large portion of the artwork created for the development of the game Ghetto Zombies: Graffiti Squad. Within these pages, you will find not only concept art in various styles, but also several explanatory texts about the game's universe! The artbook brings together artwork produced by Brazilian artists of different styles such as: Lucas Ponce, Gabriel Rocha, Oberas, Mity Dias, Daianderson (Dradra), George "Seelvart," and Mayke Arth, in addition to various pixel art assets created by Samuel Aragão, Elder Júnior (macapixel), and Victor Athayde. The artbook is available for free in Brazilian Portuguese as a way of democratizing access to the content produced for the game through the PROAC 33/2022 and SPCine 05/2023/LPG grants. The game Ghetto Zombies received funding from the PROAC grant.
