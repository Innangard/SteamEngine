Explore a forgotten place buried beneath the earth and uncover the story hidden within its halls. Solve puzzles, search for clues, and slowly piece together what happened here but be careful. The deeper you go, the more the place begins to reveal things that were never meant to be found. Uncover the Story The story isn't given to you all at once. Discover letters, hidden details, solve puzzles. Each discovery adds another piece to the mystery, gradually revealing what is Ashworth and why you were brought there. Solve. Explore. Survive. The place is filled with puzzles designed to challenge your observation and understanding of the environment. Some answers may be right in front of you. Others require you to connect pieces of information you've discovered along the way. But solving puzzles isn't always safe. Something else is moving through the place. When you're being hunted, there may be no way to fight back. You'll have to run, hide, escape, and use the environment around you to survive. Your Choices Are Simple Explore the unknown.
