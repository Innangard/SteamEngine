Προβολή κέντρου Κοινότητας Σχετικά με αυτό το παιχνίδι Endministrator, Welcome to Talos-II! Arknights: Endfield is a 3D real-time strategy RPG from Gryphline. On the planet [Talos-II], visitors to this world, the earliest settlers, braved wars and disasters. Through over 150 years of relentless effort, they rebuilt a foundation for humanity: the [Civilization Band]. Yet most of this world remains untamed. Vast wildlands and uninhabited territories stretching toward the horizon still await exploration. The shadows of the past still linger, while new threats have quietly emerged. As the Endministrator of Endfield Industries, use AIC Factory technologies and collaborate with your operators to build and expand a new homeland for humanity. Explore Talos-II, Build a New Homeland Venture into the soaring peaks and snaking waterways of Talos-II, where discoveries lie at every turn. Neutralize Ankhor threats, purify the Blight, overcome crises through exploration, and continue AIC construction to bring prosperity back to this land. Meet Operators, Journey Together No one stands alone. As you explore Talos-II, you'll meet operators with distinct personalities. Work alongside them to build a new home for humanity. Unleash Your Creativity, Build an AIC Factory Take control of Endfield Industries' versatile facilities. Follow existing production layouts or devise your own sophisticated, highly efficient automated production lines. Let your industrial capacity power every exploration and battle. Savor the joy of building from scratch. Coordinate Combos, Fight Side by Side Lead your team into battle, moving them freely and chaining their skills to shatter enemy defenses. Put both your strategy and control skills to the test on an ever-changing battlefield. Immerse in the Story, Trace the Path of Civilization Awakened from a long slumber, your memories may have faded, but your mission remains clear. As the Endministrator of Endfield Industries, you will journey alongside your operators. Across this vast and uncharted land, the hope of rebuilding your home still burns bright. Go forth and prosper. Reignite the flame of civilization.
