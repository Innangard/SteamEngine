SAVE YOURSELF, SABOTAGE FRIENDS You and your friends are thrown into a gauntlet of lethal mini-games, and only one walks out. Survive the competition however you can – dodge the traps meant for you, and shove your friends into the ones you can't. There are no teammates here. Only survivors and corpses. BUCKETS OF BLOOD EVERYWHERE This is a horror party game, and it doesn't hold back. Limbs fly, blood sprays across the low-poly arenas, and every brutal death is rendered in glorious PSX-era grit. Come ready to get messy. DEADLY MINI-GAMES Every round drops you into a new twisted mini-game, each with its own deadly rules and its own way to wipe out the competition. One moment you're dodging blades, the next you're racing to bury a friend first. More games mean more chaos – and more ways to make sure you're the last one standing. LOOK GOOD, WIN BIG Death is inevitable, so look good doing it! Unlock a growing roster of crazy skins by conquering the game's toughest challenges. Stand out in the arena, flex on your friends, and make every gruesome loss look stylish.
