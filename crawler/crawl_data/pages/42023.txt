Build and decorate your own cosy MMO village in this relaxing city-builder where life goes on, even when you’re away. Watch your village grow, fill with adventurers, and slowly transform into a lively fantasy haven at your own pace. MAKE ADVENTURERS HAPPY See your village flourish as adventurers arrive and make themselves at home. Recruit skilled craftsmen, create places of entertainment, and build small dungeons while Paladins, Mages, and Thieves happily wander through your town. Train your adventurers, help them gain experience and gold, and live the adventurer’s life they all deserve. A VILLAGE FULL OF PERSONALITY ☕ A Latte Café Tavern ? You got it ! 📚 Book staircases ? Absolutely ! 🐴 A collection of fantastic mounts ? For sure ! 🥘 Customisation for every building ? Yes ! DECORATE YOUR LITTLE VILLAGE Create a charming village where adventurers can craft items, cook delicious meals, collect mascots, and explore cosy dungeons. Decorate the environment freely, place small details, and design a welcoming world that feels truly alive and truly yours.
