Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Hi, I'm Kaleb! I work as a 3D artist in the film industry, but my heart kept pulling me toward a small, very personal game idea. One of the reasons I started developing this game was that I wanted to bring my children’s drawings to life 💖. Drawquarium is a relaxing aquarium sandbox where your drawings come to life! Draw your own fish and plants, feed them, raise them, and collect golden fish scales to unlock new decorations and tools. Create your own cozy little underwater world and bring it to your desktop as an animated wallpaper! And don't worry, you don't have to be an artist to create something beautiful! You can always choose from a variety of ready-made fish and simply enjoy watching your little aquarium come to life. Draw Your Own Fish COMPANIONS Draw anything you want and watch your creations come to life inside your aquarium. Start with simple shapes, use templates for a little inspiration, or get as creative as you like. There are no rules, just you and your imagination! When you place new creatures in your fish tank, they will interact with the others, forming friendships, rivalries and even relationships. They can become enemies and quibble or find their soulmate and have cute little babies 🥰. Decorate With Your Own Plants Any decent aquarium will need an abundance of thriving plants. Create the perfect habitat by drawing your own vegetation and placing it wherever you want. Of course, if you’d rather be drawing sea mines or skyscrapers… Who are we to stop you? Wallpaper mode! Miss your babies whenever you go and close the game? Simply turn on the Desktop Wallpaper Mode and all of your creations will stay right there with you. Whether you’re crunching numbers for the boss or browsing a forum, you can observe, feed and play with your fish right there on your desktop. Unlock Limitless Customization Change the way the sand looks, customize the lighting, and place decorative rocks, driftwood and more. Your aquarium is a tiny living world, filled with creatures made and cared for by you. Your Cozy Little Space to Focus & Unwind Journal your thoughts, organize your day with a task manager, or use the timer to stay focused. Customize your clock and set your aquarium as an animated desktop wallpaper, so your little fish friends can keep you company while you work, study, or simply unwind. Upload Your Own Drawings Upload any PNG image and watch it become part of your aquarium! Whether it's your kid's latest masterpiece or something you created yourself.
