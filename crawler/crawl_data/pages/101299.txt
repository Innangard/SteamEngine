Ready to Become a Whale? A "whale" refers to someone who spends an outrageous amount of money on online games. They may be scorned for wasting money, but in the game world, their overwhelming strength earns admiration. This is a simulation game where you can experience life as one of these legendary whales. Take on part-time jobs, start a company, earn money, and sink it all into a virtual social game! Work and Earn Money! Plenty of job options are available. Find the one you like and start making money! Run Your Own Business! You can also start and manage your own company to generate higher profits. Recruit Talented Employees! Hiring skilled workers will boost your business’s earnings even further. Spend Big in Online Games! Use your earnings to spin the gacha and collect powerful characters! Claim Victory in War Events!
