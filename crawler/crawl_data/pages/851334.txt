The Engadin Linie: Pontresina - Scuol-Tarasp Route developed by Rivet Games is a 57 km long Swiss narrow gauge line from Pontresina to Scuol Tarasp which follows the Inn river. Experience the unique charm of the route in this scenario pack as you transport passengers and goods reliably to their destinations. Get on board and experience special trains in the challenging timetable during the Engadine Ski Marathon. This exciting and extensive scenario package also features the use of freight trains. Sometimes containers must be brought over the mountain or unusual, non-standard trains with large loads must be handled. Included Scenarios A selection of engaging Standard scenarios is included: JTG – R 1957 to Samedan JTG – Marathon Express JTG – Oil over Oil JTG – Container, Container, Container Please Note: Engadin Linie: Pontresina - Scuol-Tarasp Route & RhB Enhancement Pack 02 are required, as a separate purchase, in order to play the scenarios featured in this add-on.
