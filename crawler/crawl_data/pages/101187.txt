View Community Hub About This Game Messa Anomaly is a first-person psychological horror game about observation, memory, and the unsettling fear of the unknown. You are trapped in a museum during a night shift that never seems to end. Walk through repeating corridors, study the exhibits, and notice what has changed. When something feels wrong, trust your memory and choose your path carefully. WATCH THE MUSEUM Each loop may hide an anomaly: a displaced object, a broken pattern, an impossible sound, or something watching from where it should not be. MAKE YOUR CHOICE Decide whether the corridor is normal or has changed. Correct choices bring you closer to the exit. Mistakes draw you deeper into the museum.
