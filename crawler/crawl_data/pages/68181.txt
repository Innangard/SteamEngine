Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We chose Early Access to work closely with our community and shape the game together. Lunars is built around fast paced mini games and dynamic boards, and Early Access allows us to gather real player feedback on what is fun, what needs refinement, and where the game can grow. Early Access also gives us the opportunity to thoroughly test multiplayer systems, balance mini games, and fine tune board events at scale. Player feedback will help inform updates, new content, and quality of life improvements, helping us deliver a more polished and enjoyable experience by full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect Lunars to remain in Early Access for approximately 8 to 12 months. This period allows us to gather meaningful feedback, refine gameplay balance, improve online features, and continue adding new content. The exact length may change depending on development needs and community feedback.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to expand the game with additional mini games, new boards, and possibly new game modes. The full version is also intended to include more unlockable items for character customization, further board customization options, and additional quality of life improvements, including smoother reconnection to ongoing matches in most situations. Player feedback during Early Access will help shape updates, balance changes, and overall improvements.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version is fully playable and built on a stable foundation, with a strong set of features already in place. It currently includes four boards, around thirty mini games, board customization options, and a wide range of unlockable outfits and cosmetic items. While we aim to ensure a stable experience, increased player participation and feedback during Early Access will help identify areas for improvement, particularly around balance, performance, and online features.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan for the game to be priced lower during Early Access. As development progresses and more content and features are added, we plan to slightly increase the price when transitioning to the full release. This approach allows early supporters to access the game at a lower price while reflecting the expanded scope and additional content planned for the full version.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We plan to involve the community through ongoing feedback and open discussion throughout Early Access. Player reports, suggestions, and gameplay ideas will help us identify bugs, improve balance, and refine existing content. We also value feedback from live streams and recorded gameplay, as watching players interact with the game often highlights issues and opportunities that are not always reported directly. Gather your friends for Lunars , the zodiac party game where cute chaos meets fierce competition! Customize your characters, dive into hilarious mini-games, and outplay your friends with items and power-ups across three exciting game modes : 🎲 Classic Mode - Roll the dice in classic party board game style, competing in mini-games at the end of each turn. ⚡ Blitz Mode - No waiting, just non-stop action as everyone takes their turns simultaneously! Race to strategize, collect power-ups, and compete in mini-games at the end of each round for extra rewards. 🎮 Mini-Game Mode - Get ready for a non-stop mini-game marathon! Knocked out? Not really! Come back as a mischief-maker and shake things up for the remaining players. MULTIPLAYER MAYHEM Play with up to 4 players in couch co-op, 8 online, or mix both with online couch co-op - where two groups of up to 4 local players can team up online for an 8-player experience! EXPLORE DYNAMIC GAME BOARDS From mummy-infested deserts to a cardboard kingdom with a cranky dragon, each board is its own adventure! Play through 4 wildly different locations, each packed with quirky characters, unpredictable challenges, and a unique spin on the game! MINI-GAMES, MAXIMUM FUN Brace yourself for 30+ over-the-top mini-games! Work together, battle it out, or throw strategy out the window - anything goes! And if you’re knocked out? No worries! Come back as a troublemaker to keep the fun going! STYLE YOUR ZODIAC PLUSHIE Choose from 12 cuddly zodiac-themed plushies, each with its own unique personality, and dress them up with an ever-expanding library of outfits, accessories, pets, and styles. Mix, match, and express your vibe as you play with your favorite zodiac and claim the top spot! FAST, FUN, AND FOR EVERYONE Casual fun or high-stakes competition? Lunars strikes the perfect balance between easy-to-learn gameplay and deep strategy, so everyone can jump in and enjoy!
