Get comfortable on your trusty pallet truck and hit the gas hard! Overtake your rivals, collect power-ups and rush to the finish line, leaving a trail of falling boxes and sparks from metal wheels. Key Features: Crazy Racing: Race alone or challenge a friend in split screen mode!
