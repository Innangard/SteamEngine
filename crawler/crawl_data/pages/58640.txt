View Community Hub About This Game Prophet Margin is a cozy board game-inspired city builder where you design trade routes to please the gods. Clean decisions, meditative vibes and satisfying strategy puzzles. Inspired by the deep logic of "Eurogame" tabletop classics, it’s a thinky sanctuary where you weave a perfect system of trade routes to grow your settlements and your purse. Can you master the art of the divine margin? Place with purpose. Position complementary settlements so inputs feed outputs without waste. Weave profitable trade routes. Chain together multi-stop caravans that snowball in value with every city they pass. The gods hunger for tribute! Each temple demands a single sacred resource associated with its god. Your job is to weave long, efficient trade routes that end with the right goods at the right shrines. Optimize your web of settlements to deliver the exact resources each god demands then watch divine gifts reshape your possibilities. Unlike traditional strategy games or supply chain sims, Prophet Margin challenges you with a single, elegant task: perfect route design and resource management. Serve the pantheon. Deliver the exact resource each temple wants to receive divine gifts. Grow by connection. Every incoming road upgrades a settlement, unlocking tougher choices and bigger payoffs. Build and optimize at your own pace. Through Real-Time with Pause gameplay and the dedicated, limited Undo button, you are free to experiment with complex logistics and automation without the fear of a permanent misstep. Strategic, Not Frantic: Take all the time you need to weave the perfect web. Board Game DNA: Clear systems, readable resource chains, and strategic depth. The 30- to 60-Minute Session: High-impact strategy distilled into a rewarding run respectful of your time. Every settlement is a gear in a celestial machine. Link them together through complex automations, grow your towns, and deliver the sacred tribute the gods demand. Input & Output Loops: Manage growing complexity as your settlements evolve and demand different goods. Value-Add Logistics: The longer and more efficient the route, the greater the reward. Divine Boons: Please a God to receive game-changing gifts that twist the rules and open entirely new lines of play. With unlocking new winning conditions and procedurally generated maps, no two ascensions are the same. Whether you’re building to beat the turn limit or aiming to achieve a tricky set of quests in your run, Prophet Margin rewards the strategic planner in you. Relaxing & Stylized: A quirky, high-contrast art style that makes your logistics easy to read and beautiful to behold. High Replayability: Adapt your strategy to the cards you're dealt and the terrain you're given. Different god combinations demand novel resource chains. Unlock new quests and win conditions for you to beat. This game is for you: If you spent hours upon hours playing games like "Dorfromantik", “Let them Trade”, "Islanders", “Slipways”, "Mini Settlers" or "Mini Motorways", or you enjoyed weaving trade routes in classics like “The Settlers” or “Anno” then Prophet Margin is the god-like, boardgame inspired lovechild you'll enjoy!
