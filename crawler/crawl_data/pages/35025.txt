Settle your intergalactic homestead, build a farm, and craft a machine able to provide for the struggling frontier colonies. Work at scale using your powerful mech. Plant vast fields using the rapid-firing Seed Shooter and haul dozens of logs on one shoulder. Crop rotation and fertilizer reward the farmers who plan ahead. Build your ideal farm, whether that’s a cozy homestead or the ultimate resource generator. Decorate a home to call your own and unlock useful effects. Improve your quality of life by laying down roads and automating upkeep, like crafting and watering. Up to three friends can join you on your farm. Pool your resources to create the perfect homestead, and share the rewarding feeling of a good harvest with your pals. Use the Shared Saves system to keep playing even while the host is away. No enemy raids, no hunger, no lost progress - no pressure. Kick back, get creative, or challenge yourself, with lots of tweakable options to fit your unique playstyle. Upgrade and tune the capabilities and look of your trusty mech. Take care of it through repairs and fuel, and it'll take care of you.
