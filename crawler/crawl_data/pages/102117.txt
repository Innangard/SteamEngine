Kup Verity's Revenge $4.99 Dodaj do koszyka Zobacz centrum społeczności O tej grze You’ve heard the classic horror story a million times: someone brings home a cursed game cartridge. The difference? This time, it’s real. Play through Verity’s game while they come to life right beside you. Your goal is simple: stay focused, survive the game, and reach 12 points before it’s too late! But you aren’t just playing what’s happening on the screen. While you work toward your objective inside the game, Verity is waiting for you to get distracted. You’ll need to divide your attention between playing the game and keeping track of what’s happening around you. Stay alert, react quickly, and learn how to manage the constant pressure as you push closer to 12 points. Losing focus at the wrong moment could bring your run to an abrupt end. Features include: • A horror experience built around managing your attention between the game and the threat beside you • Arcade-style gameplay with the clear objective of reaching 12 points • A constant risk-and-reward challenge where focusing on your objective can leave you vulnerable • A cursed-game horror premise where the danger extends beyond the screen • A tense atmosphere designed to keep you watching both the game and your surroundings • A simple goal that becomes increasingly difficult when something is waiting for you to make a mistake Can you beat Verity’s game without taking your eyes off what really matters?
