View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game VILLAGE OF THE MIRROR An abandoned village. A forgotten past. And a place where the line between the living and the dead is slowly fading. Village of the Mirror is a first-person psychological horror game that blends Japanese and Anatolian folklore. Explore the village, search the old mansion and its surroundings, and follow the traces left behind by those who came before you. As you dig deeper, you'll discover that the boundary between the real world and the afterlife may not be as distant as you thought. EXPLORE THE VILLAGE AND THE AFTERLIFE Explore abandoned houses, old buildings, and places that have been forgotten for years. But your journey won't stay in the village. Pass through Torii gates and step into the afterlife. Here, the boundaries between the living world and the other side begin to change. Look around carefully, follow the clues, and uncover the secrets hidden throughout the village. FOLLOW THE TRACES OF THE PAST The village hides a dark story that goes back generations. Discover the connections between Akira Karaca , the village, and the events that have been hidden for years. There won't always be someone there to tell you what happened. You'll have to pay attention to your surroundings, find the clues, and piece the story together yourself. LISTEN TO THE WELL Some answers aren't hidden in old notes or forgotten objects. Talk to the well. Listen to the voice coming from its depths and uncover what it knows about the village, the afterlife, and the events that took place here. But how much can you really trust what you hear? EARLY ACCESS Village of the Mirror is an Early Access game, and its story will continue to grow over time. The current version is the beginning of your journey into the village, the afterlife, and the mysteries hidden within them. Future updates will bring new story chapters, locations, afterlife areas, mysteries, and gameplay content as development continues. We'll keep expanding the world and story over time, while also taking player feedback into account. The story has only just begun. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains horror themes, depictions of blood, corpses, and disturbing scenes. Some areas contain blood and hanging bodies with no visible facial details. A humanoid creature and other supernatural horror elements may also appear later in the game. The game does not contain sexual content, nudity, or drug use.
