Other countries have added traps and turtle and bee enemies, so that President Trump need to use all his skills to get more tariff trade moneys. Levels contains different traps like firetraps, spikes, moving spikes, cannons etc. Levels contains also 2 different moving and deathly enemies bees and turtles. Trump can avoid enemies or shoot them with flames. There are also some moving and jump platform which Trump can use. Some levels player need find key to open doors to continue level and also falling to lava will kill player and after death player will respawn to checkpoint area.
