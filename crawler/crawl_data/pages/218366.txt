View Community Hub Follow us Message from the producer:2752720 About This Game "Dice Player One" gives dice more possibilities. Here, you will strategize and control dice with different attributes to ultimately achieve victory. Ingenious Combinations, Ever-changing Dice Sets In this game, you combine dice skills to break through one score limit after another, depending on different situations. Each skill has its place, where you can meticulously plan and make the most of all the dice. Or you can carefully select and hone, making your favored dice unbeatable. Resonate with Your Heart, Surprises Await In numerous random challenges, besides the dice themselves, various resonances are waiting for your discovery. Attach suitable resonances to the dice to unleash their stronger powers! Later in each game, you will also unlock the forging workshop, relying on abilities like "copy" and "delete" to further strengthen your control over dice sets. Destiny is unpredictable, whether it's selecting dice, matching resonances, or using the workshop, every step you take could change the course of the entire game. Simple Yet Infinite Fun Step 1: Roll the dice. Step 2: Fill in the scores. Step 3: Embrace victory in the game! As long as you use dice skills well, you can create unimaginable high scores under simple rules! Move towards higher scores! Packed with Content, Challenge Yourself 300 dice with different skills, each appearance bringing you new inspiration. 50 selectable resonances and 7 processing skills allow you to customize your strongest dice as you wish. 29 starter dice sets, each offering a different game experience. 50 rhythm dice, customize difficulty, and strive for all rhythm rewards! 20 challenge levels, defeat enemies under special rules, and enjoy different fun! Daily refreshed random challenges, compete with players worldwide, and continually climb the leaderboard! More will be updated on the way! Accumulate Experience, Explore and Discover The real failure is not death but forgetting. The screen statistics will visualize each of your challenges. Dice resonance, the more, the merrier. With each game, you may unlock more new dice to perfect your dice museum!
