About This Game Ahoy there! Welcome to Cross Blitz , a unique deckbuilder RPG where heartfelt adventure meets fierce, strategic battles! Whether you embark on a thrilling Story Campaign or a ruthless Roguelite Run, Cross Blitz will push your deckbuilding skills and strategic mind to their limit! Engage in exhilarating turn-based fights where any move can turn the tide of battle! For each encounter you face on the 4x4 grid battlefield, you’ll need to carefully consider your card’s effects and placement , while strategizing around your deck’s synergies. Draw out the master tactician within you : learn your enemy’s moves, deploy clever counterstrategies, and time devastating “Blitz Burst” ultimate finishers ! Experiment with new and exciting ways to crush your foes: hurling bombs, tinkering with deadly bots, cooking up powerful dishes, conjuring demonic groupies, or even releasing shrubasaur stampedes! It’s up to you to mix and match complementary archetypes to draw out the most powerful combos. With over 500 cards, 160 relics, and 20 archetypes across 5 factions , infinite ways to Blitz await! Arrre ye a hardened adventurer thirsting for challenge? Set sail for our roguelite mode: Tusk Tales! Choose from 20 powerful mercenaries , each with their unique playstyle and starter deck, and challenge treacherous randomized maps. As you progress through each encounter and peculiar locales, strengthen and customize your deck to face increasingly difficult opponents! Engage in captivating battles that require strategy and cunning - with more than 200 cards to experiment with - as you build powerful decks to stomp your foes into dust! Easy to pick but chock-full of challenge, you'll have to harness the strength of your minions alongside a plethora of spells, traps, and match-finishing Blitz Bursts if you're to claim victory! Each run will reward you with precious Husks, a currency to invest in your mercenary or to unlock useful items you’ll find in future runs! From the treacherous Swindler’s Wharf to the chilling Frostfall Peak, Crossdawn Isle is a vibrant world packed to the crow’s nest with colorful characters, intriguing locations, and delightful secrets. Follow 5 determined heroes in the Fables Story Mode, and experience each full-length story campaign packed with action and intrigue! From hilarious moments to epic showdowns, witness each hero overcoming their struggles and achieving profound personal growth . Redcroft - A mighty pirate burdened by the weight of legacy, forced on a perilous errand to regain his freedom. Violet - A charming popstar running from her past, on the hunt for her evil doppelganger. Quill - A pint-sized thief hiding a heart of gold, trying to break free from his crippling debt. Seto - A noble martial artist searching for his long-lost master and the true meaning of his teachings. Mereena - A sheltered but dutiful sprout elf on a journey to bring peace to her divided people.
