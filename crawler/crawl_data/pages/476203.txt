Upon entering this mysterious villa, Orange encounters the bizarre disappearance of the homeowner, "Boss." The animal companions living under the same roof, each with distinct personalities, can all speak and clearly possess wisdom beyond that of ordinary animals. "Wait, how did I become so smart too?" After "Boss" goes missing, the villa's living facilities come to a standstill. Orange decides to work with the new companions to restore calm to the "home." You will collaborate with other animal friends, gather clues, use your four nimble paws to climb up and down the rooms, and employ the sharp mind of a cat to solve puzzles and explore the secrets and truths hidden in every corner. "Boss" set up a series of unique puzzle challenges at home—covering search, observation, logical reasoning, text interpretation, and route finding. The clever Orange just needs to observe calmly and think meticulously to solve them effortlessly. Your task is to restore the operation of the home's living facilities and unravel the mystery of "Boss's" disappearance, while also speculating: "Boss" didn't turn his own home into a spy movie set, did he?! Yes, the large villa with more than a dozen rooms hides too many secrets! Each room has a unique scene and a series of puzzles. You never know what surprise—or more often, trouble—lies in the next room, but none of this can stop you, the adventurous little cat. Immersing yourself in exploration from a cat's perspective, this fantastic world awaits your discovery. The villa is not just a puzzle game venue; it is also everyone's "home," with each room telling a different aspect of "home." Through solving puzzles and exploration, you will gradually piece together the stories of "Boss" and the animals: how he built this home and why he left. And the final mystery may not just be "where is Boss," but about the bond between you and your companions. As you explore the villa, you will learn the reasons for "Boss's" sudden departure and also discover that the secrets of this home are slowly awakening. Ultimately, you will unravel the mysteries one by one, find "Boss," or at least find some answers. Because you have a strange premonition that "Boss" seems strangely familiar to you... Are you ready to live an extraordinary cat life, to play the symphony of adventure and wisdom with your animal companions?
