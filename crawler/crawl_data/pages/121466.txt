Customise them by upcycling and more to meet the eccentric requests of your endless colourful, customers. Spend your savings to upgrade your shop, buy new and better tools, and customise your space! There is no pressure , no risk, no right way to play - it is not a simulator - it's just good vibes and nice customers to serve. DISCOVER PECULIAR TRINKETS Unearth knick-knacks of all kinds by chipping away at the dirt and cruft they're embedded inside, wrestling with different types of dirt that yield in different ways. RESTORE FORGOTTEN CURIOS TO GLORY Scrub all these strange items up nice and clean with your sponge to get them ready for sale! UPCYLE INTO IMAGINATIVE NEW FORMS! Customise these curios by combining Trinkets together, turning them into strange and wonderful new items! REMIX & REIMAGINE TRINKETS WITH COLOUR SWAPPING! Use "Hugh" to extract the materials from any trinket and apply them to any other. Get your aesthetic locked in . SELL TO A PARADE OF COLOURFUL CUSTOMERS A wide range of intriguing, odd and charming customers will frequent your store, some with interesting stories to tell, others after a particular object, some just browsing... UPGRADE YOUR WORKSHOP Spend your savings to expand your tool set, and unlock more space to work in. Buy a display mat to tempt customers with specific wares, or a soak tub to clean up overnight, an honesty box for getting rid of unwanted trinkets, and more! TREAT YOURSELF Customise your space, decorate your shelves however you like, get some nice plants, change up the wallpaper and bedclothes; make it your own! MAXIMUM CHILL, ZERO PRESSURE There's a gentle limit to how much you can do in a given day but customers will return, you can potter as much as you like, and there are no fail states. FEATURES: Hundreds of Trinkets and Accessories with 1000s of combinations and variations to find, clean, upcycle, sell and collect Multiple Tools to buy, upgrade and use across 4 Trinket-care minigames Make life easier with the Coffee Pot, Soak Tub, and other handy gadgets Expand your shop with shelves, storage, and more 4 distinct locations to visit and sell in Customise your shop with cosmetics covering bedclothes, wallpapers, and more 40 unique NPC characters to get to know and sell to 120+ Quests to complete 45 Achievements to earn 20+ hours of story content Endless randomised customers Endless gameplay System Requirements Minimum: OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Pentium 2GHZ or AMD equivalent Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: DX11, DX12 capable Storage: 3 GB available space Trash Goblin © Spilt Milk Studios 2025 View all What Curators Say 76 Curators have reviewed this product.
