Simulator 3 puts you in control of busy airports around the world, where every departure, arrival, runway crossing, and taxi instruction is your responsibility. Whether you’re managing a quiet regional airport or coordinating traffic at a major international hub, every decision matters. Built for aviation enthusiasts, aspiring controllers, and industry professionals alike, Tower! Simulator 3 combines realistic airport operations, advanced voice communications, a fully interactive 3D tower environment, and extensive customization tools into the ultimate ATC experience. Key Features Voice-Controlled Air Traffic Control Communicate with pilots using advanced voice recognition technology and experience realistic ATC interactions from the tower. Realistic Airport Operations Manage departures, arrivals, taxi movements, runway crossings, gate assignments, and ground traffic using authentic ATC procedures. Immersive 3D Tower View Watch aircraft operate from a fully rendered control tower environment while maintaining complete control of airport operations. Multiplayer Support Work together with other controllers in real-time and divide responsibilities across ground, tower, and departure operations. 11 Airports Included Start your ATC career immediately with eleven detailed airports included in the base game, ranging from regional destinations to major international hubs.
