View Community Hub About This Game DEFEND AGAINST HORDES OF DEMONS Flock of the Seraphim is a horde survival roguelite bullet heaven where you defend against hordes of enemies that grow in power over time, but so do you!  Gain the strength to catch up to the threat's growing power by leveling up, gathering weapon-wielding holy allies, collecting loot, and repeat. Hell has risen and its up you and heaven's cutest soldiers to repel the demonic invasion! UNIQUE AND UPGRADEABLE GUN WIELDING ANGELS Acquire unique angels with unique weapons and control a specialized squad of god warriors. Bows, Rifles, even RPGs? They use them! Each angel comes with a unique passive and stats that improve their fire rate, reload speed, damage, pierce, and health recovery. The currency earned fighting hordes of demons can be used to upgrade your holy power outside of the crusade and join the fight again with increased divine power! Key Features Fast runs, 10 minutes to reach the boss.
