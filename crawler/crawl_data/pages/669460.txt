Gunslingers & Zombies is an action-adventure strategy game hilariously mixing two beloved pop-culture themes: the Wild West and the undead. Become one of the triggermen facing the zombie apocalypse. Plan your tactics, and bring it to life-fighting turn-based duels. Enjoy the Wild West with a twist in a lightweight, post-apocalypse scenario, where nothing is normal. Face a variety of themed undead such as the mighty Buffalo Zombie! Let those zombies taste your pistols, rifles, canons, or knives, and make sure they’ll regret the day they were… erhm… they went undead. You have a choice, shoot, or get eaten. Gunslingers and Zombies: "Go West", they said. "Adventure and easy money await", they said. The problem is the only green things that matter now are trying to eat us alive. A Great Adventure like hell! Zombie Apocalypse hell! Survive in the unique post-apocalypse scenario and help Gang of Gunslingers to not die like Greenhorns. Shoot first, ask questions later, Main Features: Tactical Turn-based combat - have time to aim, shoot and watch Weird West World - lightweight post-apocalypse scenario. Let's yeeehaaw those zeds!
