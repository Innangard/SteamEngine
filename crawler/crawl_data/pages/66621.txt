He was rushed to a specialized medical facility dedicated to soldiers who had fought in those fierce battles. Now, he is tormented by terrifying nightmares; Your mission is to help him escape this labyrinth of nightmares that feeds on his deepest psychological and mental fears. Game Features: Gameplay revolves around escaping the nightmare in each stage. Creatures and enemies from the war will pursue you, the soldier. You will face puzzles, hidden keys, and scarce resources such as weapons and ammunition. An atmosphere steeped in psychological horror, featuring unsettling sound design and sudden jump scares. Explore locations from his past, some of which may transform into labyrinths, and encounter characters etched in the soldier's memory. Most bizarrely, the soldier is stalked by a massive, terrifying creature that ambushes him and attempts to kill him, perhaps the embodiment of the nightmare itself; you will discover the truth for yourself.
