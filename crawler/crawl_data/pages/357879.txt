The classic iM1A2 Abrams delivers a highly detailed, immersive, and tactical simulation of modern armored combat. Developed by Imagic, this groundbreaking title puts you in the command seat of the legendary M1A2 Abrams Main Battle Tank—along with full control of up to three additional armored vehicles as you execute coordinated battlefield operations. You’ll operate in hostile combat zones across real-world-inspired conflict zones such as Eastern Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. As a tank commander, you must master both firepower and battlefield strategy. Seamlessly switch between tank crew positions—including driver, gunner, and commander—or issue tactical orders to your platoon and supporting units. Key Features Full M1A2 Simulation – Engage with a realistic fire control system, thermal imaging, and modern tank optics. Crew Station Switching – Take direct control of driver, gunner, or commander positions—or leave it to the AI. Tactical Command Interface – Issue orders to your entire tank platoon and supporting infantry or helicopters. Three Campaign Theatres – Fight across varied combat zones in Eastern Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Dynamic Mission Design – Engage in scripted and semi-random missions with variable objectives and AI behavior. Smart Enemy AI – Opponents utilise real-world tactics, attempting to flank, ambush, or call for support. Historical Accuracy – Detailed modelling of U.S. and enemy armour, terrain, and weapon systems. Destructive Environments – Use your cannon and machine gun to level obstacles, structures, and enemy armor.
