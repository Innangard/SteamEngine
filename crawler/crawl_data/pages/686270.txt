View Community Hub About This Content The fifth and final expansion of the Imperiums series plunges you into the twilight of antiquity, a time of monumental change when the old order collapses, giving rise to a new world. Rome stands divided, torn between East and West, facing crises on every front. Along its borders, restless tribes gather, hungry for land and wealth, while from the East, the ruthless Huns advance like an unstoppable force, feared as divine retribution. Yet the greatest threat lies within—the very heart of the empire is crumbling. With an economy in shambles, politics plagued by corruption, and an army losing its loyalty, mighty Rome teeters on the brink. Will you take on the daunting task of saving the Roman Empire—East or West? Or will you embrace the role of a barbarian chieftain, breaking through Roman defenses to establish a new realm? Perhaps you’ll seek to fulfill the dreams of your ancestors as the leader of the Sasanian Empire, aiming to create the greatest empire the world has ever known. For those drawn to power and freedom, lead the merciless Huns and embark on a campaign of conquest that will shake Europe to its core. Prove to the world what it means to rule from the saddle! Political Power: A New Resource A powerful new mechanic introduces a resource akin to Influence, but turned inward, not outward. Use this rare resource to curb corruption in cities and boost the loyalty of your troops. In a fractured Rome, only the emperor’s authority can rebuild the empire’s efficiency, pride, and the strength of its once-unbeatable army through new State Decisions, actions, and city improvements, offering additional strategic opportunities. Can you master your limited supply of Political Power and lay the groundwork for Rome’s revival? With each decision, your strategic prowess will determine the fate of the empire. Inflation: The Economic Challenge Face the consequences of every financial decision with a new economic mechanic. Short-term gains, like debasing the currency for instant funds, could lead to long-term ruin. As a ruler, you must navigate the delicate balance of keeping your treasury afloat without plunging your state into chaos. Will you find the balance to thrive, or will you, like the emperors of old, be trapped by impossible economic dilemmas? Dive into this richly detailed system, complete with new State Decisions and trading dynamics, and prove your worth as a ruler of the ancient world. Weather and Seasons: Nature as Your Adversary With seasons returning from Rise of Caesar and revamped for this new expansion to suit the different historical and geographical context, snow shortens supply lines, winter weakens armies, and summer heat diminishes your soldiers’ strength. These natural forces now extend to Custom Worlds, challenging you in every campaign. Plan your offensives wisely—your fiercest enemy might not be human but the unforgiving hand of nature itself. Can you adapt and turn the elements to your advantage? Asymmetrical Gameplay: Every Faction Is Unique No two factions start alike. Each offers a unique combination of strengths, weaknesses, and strategies. Success depends not on size or population, but on your ability to leverage your resources and exploit your enemies’ vulnerabilities. Even the smallest tribe can topple a mighty empire if led by a cunning and determined ruler. Will you lead a band of savage warriors to overthrow a corrupt colossus? The fate of your faction lies in your hands. Legendary Leaders: Uldin and Stilicho History’s greatest commanders are now at your disposal. Make use of these legendary figures to ensure your army’s supremacy and lead it to ultimate victory. Special " Commander " units, such as the relentless Uldin or the brilliant strategist Stilicho, are more than just symbols—they are game-changing forces capable of turning the tide of battle. But they are not alone. A host of other legendary leaders stand ready to join your ranks. Can you harness their unparalleled skills to lead your armies to immortal glory? Tailored Governments: Rule Your Way The asymmetry of the campaign is reflected not only in the different starting conditions, but also in the unique forms of government tailored for Rome, the Huns, and various tribes. Each government type brings its own challenges, from the corrupt yet low-maintenance Roman bureaucracy to the vastly different recruitment costs for each faction. This variability forces players to adapt their strategy and make the most of their regime’s unique properties while managing its weaknesses. Will you master the intricacies of your government and wield it as your greatest weapon on the path to victory? The time has come to shape the destiny of an era.
