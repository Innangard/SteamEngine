Trapped deep underground during humanity’s final war, you are pulled into a shifting reality built from memories that aren’t yours. Each place is different, yet they all share a tragic similarity: dried bloodshed. MEET THE MEAT MAN You aren't alone. The Meat Man feasts on evil and death. A trickster, the animalistic Meat Man likes to play with his food. The Meat Man enjoys causing you pain and suffering more than anything. REPAIR YOUR BODY PARTS The Meat Man will try to rip you apart piece by piece. Individual body parts can be injured, altering your abilities. When heavily damaged, be wise! Medicine bottles are scarce. Be smart about the body parts you heal first. When your body is entirely exhausted, any extra injury will be fatal. EXPLORE A PLACE BELOW NOWHERE Descend into the darkness and voyage through strange memories that aren't yours. The vicious Meat Man will be hunting you. Use the environment to your advantage and hide in the darkness where he can't see you. Mysterious white glowing doorways mark your escape. Reach them to be spared by the Meat Man. You have no way of defending yourself. Your only choices are to run, hide, or die. Features an original dark ambient soundtrack composed by The Nomad Communion. Built with Unreal Engine 5.
