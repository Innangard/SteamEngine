Over dit spel Strikers Club is your ultimate multiplayer football simulation game where you take control of a player and compete in action-packed matches against others. Master dribbling, passing, and shooting while working with your team to outplay opponents. With gameplay that rewards precision and timing, every move counts, delivering a fast-paced, skill-driven experience that keeps you engaged until the final whistle. Free-to-play Strikers Club is free-to-play and always will be. Cosmetic elements and additional features are available for purchase and obtainable through gameplay and events. We always provide a fair online playing field. Multiplayer Every player on the field is a real person. Work together to win your association football match using dribbling, shooting, skill moves and other mechanics. Skill-based gameplay There is no luck or randomness factor involved in Strikers Club . The better team will win by having better team-play and individual skill. Real football Strikers Club implements all of the game rules. Throw-ins, corners, penalties, fouls, and numerous other elements that make the game loyal to the sport. Choose your position The game's name may be Strikers Club , but every position is represented in the game. Choose which one you prefer playing at the start of the match. If you play as a goalie, you have an extra set of mechanics to make the difference. You can even run up to participate in plays up the field! SEE YOU ON THE FIELD!
