Collaborating with Jonathan Figoli, they have crafted compelling soundtracks for War on the Sea , Second Front , Task Force Admiral , and other unannounced projects across various media. Aleksi gravitates toward orchestral compositions and the creation of unique sound textures, while Jonathan excels at crafting powerful melodies and deriving distinctive sounds from real instruments. This complementary dynamic makes their collaboration exceptionally effective. In their recent work on Sea Power , they remained faithful to the game's musical genre while introducing original twists to give the soundtrack a unique character. In addition, some tracks are more NATO-focused, featuring heroic and bright themes, while others lean toward the Warsaw Pact, offering darker tones imbued with a sense of power and mechanical danger. The soundtrack has been mixed by the exceptionally talented Joël Dollié, elevating the music to an even higher level of quality. This musical score not only enhances the gameplay but also stands alone as a unique musical journey. Listeners can look forward to exploring the dynamic contrasts between the NATO and Warsaw Pact themes.
