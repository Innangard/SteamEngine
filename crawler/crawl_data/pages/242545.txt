Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game is mainly developed by one person, which means getting feedback early, is very important in making it the best experience it can be. The core gameplay loop that has been developed over various betas has been finished now. The game now features enough content and runs stable enough to release it to a broader audience.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The current plan is to stay in early access until all features that are planned have been developed. Since the start of early access, a lot of new features have already been announced that we never thought we would add, like Multiplayer for example. This is why we can't give a perfect estimate and will need to adjust the release date of the full version once more features have finished development. Furthermore, since this is a roguelite game, there are endless possibilities content wise. We have a lot of ideas that we want to realize which may take a considerable amount of time. We expect to have most of the content finished around end of 2025.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “There are a lot of things that still on the todo list, which have not yet been implemented into the game which include: More dungeons and more alternate paths More items, achievements,enemies,bosses,journal pages and general gameplay mechanics A hardmode that makes the game significantly harder for all players that want a real challenge A true final boss with epilogue lore Daily runs with a leaderboard Better documentation on the modding tools and more modding capabilities General polish and replayability improvements ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Please refer to the "About This" section for more details.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “There are no plans to increase the price of the game after the early access phase.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We listen to all feedback the community has about the game to improve our development process. Players that want to leave feedback, suggest new features, or report bugs are always welcome. Swing your sword, grab loot, and explore rooms full of traps, secrets, and monsters. The dungeons change every time you play! It’s one of the best-rated VR games out there with Overwhelmingly Positive reviews, and you can play for many hours with your friends! Full Online Multiplayer Team up with friends in multiplayer. Trade blows with dungeon bosses, share loot, and revive each other… or let them get caught while you grab the loot. Your choice. Multiplayer supports up to 4 players, cross-play with Quest native and soon PSVR2. New Dungeons Every Time No two runs are the same! Every time you play, the dungeon looks different, with new monsters and loot. You can unlock secret rooms, new floors, and cool surprises. Real Combat. Real Movement. Real Fun. Slash, block, throw, and dodge, all with your real hands in VR! You can even cut down plants if you’re feeling silly. Permanent Unlocks & Upgrades Earn permanent rewards like new weapons, floors, relics, upgrades, hard mode, and more! Every adventure helps you build power and unlock fresh challenges.
