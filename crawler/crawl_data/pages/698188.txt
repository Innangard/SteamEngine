The album blends warm and adventurous cues, capturing both the comfort and the tension of the mouse’s journey. In Winter Burrow, the soundtrack grows from a dialogue between fragile home life and the wild outdoors. As composer and sound designer, I wanted the audio to reflect the character’s journey, not just sit in the background. Key inspiration came from Nordic folk melodies — old songs like “Mørk er November” and “Drømte mig en drøm i nat” bring a voice that feels familiar and strange at the same time. I re-recorded and adapted these tunes using playful instruments like toys and music boxes alongside soft symphonic layers, creating soundscapes that shift between comfort and tension. In safe moments — inside the burrow, crafting, resting, knitting — the music is soft and intimate. Outside, in snowy landscapes and strange terrain, it becomes sparse and open: pulses, shifted field recordings, and cinematic energy that relates to the  vulnerability to the wild. Beyond the instruments, I focused on how the music changes with the player: motifs return in new shapes, echoing the mouse’s journey.
