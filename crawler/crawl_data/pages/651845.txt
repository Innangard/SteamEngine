The art is gorgeous, the design is clever [...] and it's fun to try and break the game.” Fizgigs About This Game Islantiles is a cozy roguelike deckbuilder where players craft, combine and strategize their way to success! Starting with a single island, you’ll gradually expand your settlement into a thriving town. As you settle a chain of beautiful islands, you’ll unlock, swap, and combine powerful technologies that reshape your entire strategy. How far can you go? Place buildings, gather resources, shape your deck and trigger satisfying card combos. Technologies don’t just improve your deck – they twist the core rules, changing how tiles interact, how you score and what kind of strategies you can build. Buy new technologies or sell old ones to adapt to special challenges and pivot your strategy. Every island is unique, and no two runs are the same. Master the art of cozy efficiency and grow your settlements to glory! Islantiles offers: A unique blend of city puzzler and deck builder Cozy but compelling roguelike gameplay Develop and refine different strategies Six different difficulty settings 100 hand-crafted islands to develop More than 100 technologies, each with unique rules Highscores & Leaderboards Roguelite progression: no two runs are the same System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: Quad Core I5 or AMD equivalent Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: GTX 960 or AMD equivalent Storage: 2 GB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system © 2024-2026 Thunderrock Innovations GmbH.
