Setup Creator offers a wide range of tools to bring your dream room to life. No limits—just you, your imagination, and a vast collection of highly customizable elements. Setup Creator is a simulator of interior designers, visionaries, capable of turning bravest  ideas into reality. Experiment with interior colors, room shapes, lighting, and create the setup of your dreams. Room Planning In Setup Creator, you have the opportunity to design a room that you can later transform into something beautiful. You can adjust the shape of the room at any time. This flexible tool allows you to effortlessly bring your envisioned projects to life. Wallpaper Customization Transform your walls to match your vision! Choose from a variety of textures, patterns, and colors to create the perfect backdrop for your dream room. Your walls, your canvas! Floor Customization Add the finishing touch to your room by choosing the perfect flooring! From warm parquet to stylish tiles, the floor becomes the foundation for creating a unique design. Your style starts from the ground up! Placing Interior Elements After creating the room's shape, it's time to fill it with content—and rest assured, nothing will hold back your creative impulse. Place interior elements as you see fit by simply clicking on an object and selecting the move function. Customizing Interior Elements If the color of your furniture doesn’t match your current mood, nothing stops you from changing it. You have access to the full visible color spectrum for customization. And this applies not only to furniture but to anything you can find in the game. Experiment, mix colors and styles, and create the setup you’ve always wanted. Resizing Interior Elements Think of the models you find as clay in your hands. Anything you discover can be made wider, longer, or BIGGER. A giant sofa for the whole family? Sure, why not. A tiny fridge for drinks? Make it two. Component Overlapping You’re also free to break the chains of hitboxes and place one plant inside another to create something entirely unique and personal. Your room, your rules. Lighting Customization We haven’t mentioned this yet, but every light in your room is fully controlled by you directly from the interface. Try it—adjust the color and brightness of the lighting. Don’t hold back—indulge yourself! Weather and Outdoor Appearance Control Who says you can’t move? Of course, you can! Choose one of four locations and customize the weather outside. Turn on some rain, set the weather to night, brew yourself a cup of tea, and enjoy watching the rain patter down in the game—just as it does outside your window in real life.
