Hiển thị trung tâm cộng đồng Về trò chơi này When the old can no longer protect the land, the small must learn to carry the tune. For as long as anyone can remember, a kind witch has cared for the forest and the creatures who call it home. But now, the world is beginning to fall out of harmony—and the witch who has always protected it is growing ill. Her smallest familiars must venture beyond the shelter of home and learn to carry the responsibility that once belonged to her. Though she cannot join them, her magic is never far away—a gentle hand, ready to lift them back to safety. Playing as a brave little mouse, you’ll journey through a vast world shaped by the beauty and wonder of Swedish nature, helping those you meet and restoring harmony one act of kindness at a time. Hela: of Mice & Magic is a heartfelt 3D open-world adventure about care, courage, and discovering how much even the smallest among us can do. Explore alone or share the journey with friends in local split-screen and online co-op. Carry Her Kindness Into the Wild The witch has spent her life caring for the world around her. Now, it is your turn to care for her and to continue the work she can no longer carry alone. Gather ingredients, brew magical potions, solve environmental puzzles, and lend a helping paw to animals and communities throughout the land. Each small act brings you closer to restoring its harmony and helping the witch who has long cared for you and all the forest’s inhabitants. See the World Through Tiny Eyes Travel through a lovingly crafted playground inspired by the landscapes of Northern Sweden, from rugged mountain trails and magical forests to tranquil lakes and misty wetlands. To a mouse, even familiar places become enormous. Everyday objects transform into obstacles, opportunities, and unexpected playgrounds, while hidden paths and story fragments reward those who stop, listen, and look a little closer. Glide through the air, climb to new heights, experiment with the world around you, and find your own way through an adventure filled with quiet wonder.
