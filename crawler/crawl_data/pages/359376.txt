Surviving is only the first step. To craft a way out, you'll need to navigate the prison hierarchy using social RPG mechanics, tactical combat, and a whole lot of space rats. Can you escape where others have failed? DEATH IS NOT AN EXCUSE FOR A SHORTER SENTENCE. Game features: Choose your prisoner from 3 races and criminal skills Join one of two gangs: Gravity Fist or Hypernova - rise up their ranks 18 unique inmates - build relationships with and learn their stories Feed, tame and name different kinds of adorable space rats Craft, equip and conceal items to aid your escape and upgrade your cell Manage your squad and lead them into brawls for resources and respect Fight in turn-based brawls against all kinds of prison residents If you die, get reborn in a new body JOIN A SPACE GANG Rise up the ranks of a prison gang, with unique alien members who have their own stories and fighting styles. Unlock a gang hideout that will expand your tools and means of escape. Climb up the ranks by fighting your way up the hierarchy to become the new boss. TACTICAL TURN-BASED COMBAT Fight for your life in fast-paced, tactical turn-based brawls. Use improvised weapons and armour, as well as your skills and allies to successfully explore the facility and avoid becoming another unrecoverable corpse. MAKE ALLIES TO SURVIVE There are up to 18 different inmate stories to discover as you gain their respect and build up your squad from adorable space rats to hardened criminals. Doing jobs for them will provide you with much needed intel and materials as well. CRAFT CONTRABAND Scavenge for scrap, destroy prison property, upgrade your cell - craft illegal tools, weapons and materials to equip you and your squad. Keep contraband concealed to not get caught at security checkpoints. DEATH IS NOT AN ESCAPE Dying in Space Prison doesn’t mean you can dodge your life sentence. Should you be taken out, the rogue-lite recovery system will bring you back. However, your mind must be transferred into a new body, perhaps more alien than before. At least your cell is the same, all your hidden contraband exactly where you left it.
