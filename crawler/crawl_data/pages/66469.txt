Choose the effect you like, change the atmosphere of your screen, and discover mini-games designed around cursor control. From natural phenomena and scientific experiments to magic, technology, and animals, Magic Cursor lets you find a style that matches your mood, game, or desktop. Seven categories — countless different effects The Magic Cursor collection is divided into seven themed categories: Nature — fire, water, snow, lightning, leaves, minerals, and other natural phenomena. Mini-Games — small games where mouse movement becomes your main tool: arcade challenges, reaction tests, dexterity, and precision. Science — atoms, molecules, chemical processes, physical phenomena, astronomy, and space-themed effects. Technology — digital effects, neon, holograms, electronics, engineering, aerospace technology, and futuristic visuals. Magic — runes, spells, potions, portals, fantasy glow, and other mystical effects. Animals — real, fantasy, and mythological creatures, tracks, insects, and other effects inspired by the animal world. Screen Effects — full-screen atmospheric scenes featuring weather, leaves, lighting, space, magical, and technological visuals. More than cursor effects Magic Cursor can change more than just the trail behind your mouse. Enable screen effects that work independently of the cursor and transform the atmosphere of your entire desktop. Combine cursor and screen effects — for example, a magical effect with a night background, snow with a winter atmosphere, or a technological cursor with a futuristic environment. Play with your cursor Whenever you want a break, launch the built-in mini-games. Control a snake, catch fish, herd sheep, defend the planet, dodge obstacles, and take on other challenges using your mouse movement.
