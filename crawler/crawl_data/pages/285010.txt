View Community Hub About This Game Classic Minesweeper gameplay combined with gambling mechanics: Use numbered diamonds to deduce mine locations, but instead of just clearing the board, each safe card you reveal exponentially increases your coin winnings. Decide when to cash out and secure your earnings, or push your luck for massive payouts - hit a mine and lose everything you've earned that round. 60 unique power-ups that change your strategy each run: Risk/reward modifiers like High Roller and Golden Touch turn mines into profit opportunities, Powerups like Fortune Teller and Lucky Peak reveal hidden dangers, Shield and Armor Plating protect you from mistakes, and multiplier boosters like Lucky Charm and Coin Magnet maximize your earnings. Combine power-ups to create devastating synergies and develop new strategies every playthrough. Important Note: Coinsweeper is a single-player puzzle game with gambling-themed mechanics. No real money wagering. No in-game real-money purchases. Pure skill-based entertainment.
