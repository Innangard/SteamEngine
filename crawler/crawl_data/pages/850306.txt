Gold Miner Online: Claw Chaos brings the classic gold-mining arcade game into the online era. Time your throw, hook the biggest nugget and reel it in before the clock runs out... or before three rivals snatch it from under your claw. ONE FIELD. FOUR CLAWS. TOTAL CHAOS. Jump into online matches against real players. Duel 1 vs 1 in a best-of-three, or throw down in a 2-4 player free-for-all where everyone digs the same field at the same time. Every nugget you grab is one your rivals don't get! CLIMB THE RANKED LEAGUES Quick Match is ranked. Win matches to climb from Bronze to Silver , Gold , Platinum and Diamond , and show off your league badge in every room. PLAY WITH FRIENDS Create a private room and share the 5-letter code with up to three friends, or grab a second player and battle on the same keyboard in local Versus . Friend far away? Invite them to your local match with Steam Remote Play Together . Cheer and tease with quick-chat bubbles like "Nice catch!", "So close!" and "Well played!" A FULL CAMPAIGN, NOT JUST A PARTY Dig through 60 levels across 6 worlds : Prairie Mine, Sunscorch Desert, Frostvein Caves, Ember Peak, Sunken Reef and the Lost Temple. Every world brings new tricks: ice blocks to crack open, red-hot gold that slips off your claw, drifting treasure chests, clams that snap shut, cursed idols and moles carrying diamonds. A BOSS AT THE END OF EVERY WORLD Each world ends in a boss fight where you hook the boss again and again, and every hit breaks off loot, from the Mole King to the Treasure Octopus . SHOP, UPGRADES AND STYLE Spend your coins on Dynamite , Strength Tonic , Lucky Clover , Gem Polish and the Pocket Watch . Earn stars for permanent Workshop upgrades, and unlock 24 hooks, hats and winches to make your miner your own. Your rivals will see them too! ENDLESS AND DAILY How long can you last when every gold catch adds time to the clock? Or take on the Daily Challenge : the same dig for everyone, with a new twist each day, such as Heavy Gold Day, Diamond Rush, Mole Madness and Dynamite Day. BUILT FOR STEAM 20 Steam Achievements : clear every world, collect stars, land a jackpot catch, keep your daily streak alive and win online. Steam Leaderboards : compare your Endless best, ranked rating and total campaign stars with everyone, your friends, or the players right around you. Remote Play Together : share your local Versus match with a friend online. Only you need to own the game. Your Steam name is used automatically in every mode. EASY TO LEARN, HARD TO PUT DOWN One key or click to throw, one to blast with dynamite. Quick tips teach you as you play, and options like game speed, colour-blind friendly shape markers, text and interface size and full key rebinding let everyone join in. Play in 26 languages . Grab your claw, call your friends and start digging!
