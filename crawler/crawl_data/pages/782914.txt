Overview: Welcome to "The Last Customer," a gripping survival horror experience set in an isolated gas station. As Mike, the dedicated clerk, you and your missing friend John face nightmarish beings disguised as customers. Your mission: identify who’s there for gas and who has deadly intentions. Story: The gas station, once a safe haven, is now haunted by grotesque monsters. With John mysteriously absent, Mike must navigate a chilling night filled with deceit. As reality blurs with nightmare, will he find John and survive, or become the next victim? Gameplay Features: Interactive Storytelling: Your choices shape the narrative and influence outcomes. Identify the Threat: Use your instincts to distinguish between harmless customers and deadly monsters. Atmospheric Horror: Experience a tense environment filled with dark corners and chilling visuals. Multiple Endings: Your decisions lead to various outcomes—will you escape or succumb to the terror? Real-Time Decision Making: Quick judgments are crucial for survival. Dynamic AI: Monsters adapt, keeping you on edge. Psychological Horror: Fear that challenges your sanity with subtle cues and unexpected moments.
