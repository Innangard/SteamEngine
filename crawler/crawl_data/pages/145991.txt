These additions are designed to offer a more immersive preview of the full game experience. Master the way of the shinobi, execute the ninja arts with precision, and journey through a stylized world. ・Play as the legendary Shinobi Joe Musashi, master of the ninja arts. After finding your village burned to the ground and your clan turned into stone, you must set off on a quest for vengeance, ready to face an unparalleled evil and avenge your clan. ・Enjoy a thrilling challenge battling enemies and platforming obstacles through the hand-drawn environment. ・Demo enhancements include enhanced ninja abilities, greater enemy variety and a modified shop system. ・Once you've conquered the stage, push your skills further by replaying it in Arcade Mode to aim for the highest score and uncover the hidden path. *The demo version is not representative of the final game. Progress will not carry over to the full version, but the demo can be played multiple times.
