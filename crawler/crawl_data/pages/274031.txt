The curse awakens at midnight You are the seventh son of the seventh son, born into a humble 19th-century farming family. When you reach adulthood, the truth of your bloodline finally reveals itself. Every night at midnight, you transform into a werewolf. The villagers discover what you are… and now they want you dead. Forced to flee, you must travel across isolated villages, dark forests, abandoned churches and dangerous roads, searching for the truth behind your curse while surviving the relentless hunt. But time is never on your side. Cards after Midnight is a dark roguelike card adventure combining strategy, survival and deckbuilding, where the passing of time changes how you play. The Clock Is Your Curse Your power shifts with every passing hour. 🌙 Midnight: Unleash the Beast When night falls, you become a feral werewolf. Brutal, relentless and driven by instinct, you gain devastating abilities and unstoppable momentum. ☀ Daylight: Survive the Hunt When the sun rises you return to human form, but you are far from helpless. Arm yourself with weapons, tools and tactics to fight hunters, villagers and the dangers lurking along the road. Master both sides of your curse to stay alive. Strategic Card-Based Gameplay Build and adapt your deck of survival cards Level up and unlock new powerful perks Use weapons, abilities and tools to overcome threats Manage resources and plan your escape Survive unpredictable encounters and deadly enemies Experience a roguelite adventure where every run is different A Dark Journey for Answers Explore villages, forests, churches and hidden locations Discover clues about your curse and your origin Make difficult choices that shape your journey Escape the villagers determined to destroy you About This Game History The infection is advancing by the minute and getting the vaccine is humanity's only hope. Do everything in your power to fight off the hordes of zombies piling up in the streets to make your way to the biological complex and retrieve the sample. Live the story of Rick, Selena and Dan, three interconnected stories on which it depends to get the vaccine to stop the infection. Features Multiple weapons : Get all the weapons at your disposal to take out the undead, find everything from broken bottles to chainsaws, spiked bats, hunting shotguns, rifles, futuristic experimental weapons... Survival strategy : Each level is a battle for survival, control your vaccine to avoid giving in to the infection while you stock up on food to recover your health and weapons to defend yourself, manage your turns using all the equipment at your disposal, binoculars, explosive barrels, tools, lanterns... and watch out for traps! A nightmare story : Unravel the role of the survivors and how their lives are intertwined in the same destiny. Acquire the documents scattered throughout the game to learn more about each of them. Zombie Hordes : Take on the army of the undead, explosive zombies, zombie dogs, zombie soldiers or zombie nurses are some of the creatures that have mutated due to the viral infection. Each creature has a strategy for attacking and defending itself. The zombie apocalypse will show you no mercy. A flight forward : On your way, you will visit places in the twilight, Chaos in the city. The dangerous escape in the suburbs, the infamous experimental biological complex, supply shops, dangerous gas stations where a spark can blow everything up, and much more. Extra mode: Complete the story mode to unlock Deadly Night mode, an extreme survival mission against the clock with the goal of recovering a vaccine sample. Face multiple hordes of enemies with your base equipment, enemies have new mechanics and you have the unique dodge ability A deckland tales production.
