Slotbound is a solo-developed roguelite defense game that fuses explosive 3x3 slot machine jackpots with deep autobattler strategy. Pull the lever to summon units, trigger massive synergies, and crush endless waves of enemies. It's time to put your luck and intellect to the ultimate test! Pull the Lever! Say Goodbye to Ordinary Defense Games! Tired of the same old tower defense? We've injected pure dopamine into the very core of combat preparation. Spin the 3x3 slot machine, match the lines, and summon your units. Feeling unlucky? Don't panic. Absorb, Evolve, and Reign Supreme There's no such thing as a useless unit on this battlefield. Weaker units are sacrificed to feed the strong, becoming their flesh and blood. Watch an ordinary 1 star Warrior ascend into a stronger unit. Your branching class choices will completely shift the tide of battle. Infinite Synergies That Overpower Pure Luck This isn't just a mindless slot machine where you leave everything to chance. Every time a unit spawns, it rolls from dozens of 'Random Stats'. Exploit system loopholes, bend RNG to your will, and become the master architect of your own victory.
