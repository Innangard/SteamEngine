View Community Hub About This Game Step into The Vessel. In The Vessel, you'll take control of two characters who must work together to reach the exit of each room. Both characters must arrive at each room's exit after solving a series of puzzles, creating a constantly evolving mind-bending experience where every movement and action matters. With carefully designed challenges, you must master timing, positioning, and strategy to overcome obstacles and uncover the mysteries of who they are and where they came from. Across 150 puzzle-filled levels, you'll encounter a variety of mechanics designed to test their problem-solving skills, including blocks with different properties, timed doors, teleportation systems, and other interactive puzzle elements. Beyond its unique gameplay, The Vessel features a deep fantasy sci-fi narrative exploring themes of identity and self. Get ready to uncover a story brought to life through fully voiced dialogue from a range of characters, with multiple endings that offer different outcomes and encourage multiple playthroughs Key Features ● Unique dual-character gameplay — Control two characters simultaneously as you navigate challenging puzzles where every action affects both. ● 150 puzzle-filled levels — Discover a wide range of handcrafted challenges and mechanics. ● A story about identity and self — Explore a deep fantasy sci-fi narrative filled with mystery and discovery. ● Fully voiced dialogue — Experience character interactions brought to life through full dialogue audio. ● Multiple endings — Make choices and discover different outcomes across multiple playthroughs. ● A constantly evolving puzzle experience — Solve challenges featuring unique mechanics including blocks, timed systems, teleports, and more. The Vessel invites players to experience a puzzle adventure unlike any other — one where success depends not on controlling a single character, but on understanding the connection between two.
