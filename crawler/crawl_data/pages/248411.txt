Your goal? Popping all of your opponents’ balloons with your trusty weapon. Be the last vacuum cleaner surviving and claim victory in crazy battles with plenty of unique maps, minigames and game-modes! Roombattle is designed for all types of players and can be played with just 2 buttons and a stick. Don’t have a controller? No problem, you can also play using a keyboard or smartphone as a controller! Call your friends, customize your vacuum, and get ready for some good old fashioned party gaming! Battle your friends in detailed and diverse rooms filled with interactivity, with dangers, traps and obstacles making every setting feel truly alive. Drop a fish tank into the ground, crash into all the office supplies, or smash a 240-million-year-old dinosaur skeleton, all in the name of healthy competition. Learn every map’s unique quirks and be the master behind the chaos, taking advantage of the game’s unique environments to ensure you’re the last vacuum standing! Dance, score goals, have a nice time in the pool or as any good vacuum does, clean your actual room. Play minigames between battle rounds to secure power-ups, or ditch regular mode completely for minigame mode, adding a whole new layer of excitement to the game. Want your vacuum cleaner to look like a pirate, unicorn, or kappa? How about a Viking with a magic wand? Pick your color, hat, eyes, and your favorite sharp object, ready to pop everyone else’s balloons! Every match contributes to a full progression system, unlocking new gameplay elements and cosmetic rewards. With nearly 200,000 customization combinations, players can personalize their vacuum to create their own distinctive cleaning machine, and the more you play, the more you unlock!
