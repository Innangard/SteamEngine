Play Together offers more convenience by letting you link your mobile device with Steam. Log in and start making friends with various people from all over the world in Play Together! Key Features Create a character that is uniquely you and make all kinds of friends. Customize your character from head to toe in your unique style. The possibilities are endless with a variety of skin tones, hairstyles, body types, and costumes to choose from. Maybe, you'll find that special someone in your life as you chat with and befriend various people from around the world! Turn your humble abode into the home of your dreams and invite friends over for a Home Party! Choose a house from a wide range of styles and concepts and a countless number of furniture pieces to make your dream home fantasy a reality right before your eyes. Invite friends over or visit their homes to catch fish, play games, chit-chat, and role-play together for hours of fun! Have a blast playing fun-filled minigames with friends. Demonstrate your mad gaming skills in minigames such as Game Party, where the last one standing from 30 players wins, Zombie Virus, Obby Race, Tower of Infinity, Fashion Star Runway, Snowball Fight, Sky High, as well as an assortment of additional minigames found only in school. Go around various fishing spots to catch new species of fish and show them off to others! Catch over 600 species of fish in places such as a pond, the sea, and even a swimming pool. It's never a dull moment as new fish to catch are constantly added to the game. Every fishing spot has fish not found in other spots, so visit them all to complete the collections listed in the Illustrated Book and show people what you've caught! Catch insects and lizards with your friends in various locations or go excavate rare ores and fossils. Over 300 species of insects are thriving throughout the in-game world! Also, look forward to a unique and fun experience digging up dinosaur fossils and rare diamonds. Sell your findings directly or show off your accomplishments to your friends by beautifully displaying them for double the satisfaction. Take good care of pets of all shapes and sizes. Add a spoonful of happiness to your life in Play Together with one of many adorable and ever-present pets that follow you around, such as puppies, kittens, ponies, and more. Take walks with your critter friend or cruise the streets together in your dream car. Also, don't miss your chance to own special limited-time pets available only during specific periods. In-game, AI provides conversational responses during NPC interactions, enhancing immersion and social gameplay. All AI-generated content is reviewed and controlled to ensure it aligns with the game’s design and provides a safe player experience.
