View Community Hub About This Game I have friends I want to see I have family I want to see Frendy lives a peaceful life with her beloved dog Jazz. One day, in front of a flower shop, she spots a dog that looks just like Polka, the dog Frendy had in her childhood. Before she knew it, Frendy and Jazz were chasing after it ー A cozy interactive adventure where you find Polka somewhere on the map. A simple world drawn in ballpoint pen welcomes you with playful humor. Unique characters await you at the end of your adventure! You can high-five them and become friends. Friends are reliable allies who will help and cheer you on when you face trouble. No dialogue, no spoken words. The characters convey their feelings through expressions, subtle gestures, and sound effects. Even without words, they laugh, they get surprised, and they become friends ー Various mini games are available throughout the area, including putt-putt golf, whac-a-mole, soccer shootout, boat racing, watermelon splitting, and volleyball. These are not required for story progression and are optional side activities you can enjoy freely.
