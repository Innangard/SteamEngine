You can join 'Discord' via the link on the Steam store page. About This Game Time To Usher In a Life As Ellie the Little Apprentice Witch! Step into the shoes of Ellie, a dispatched apprentice witch, and experience her journey of self development at Lucerine Ortu—a land of witches! Explore brand-new areas, craft potions and help the villagers. A heartwarming and cozy adventure awaits you here. Fish, gather, decorate your house, and interact with cats! We have all kinds of activities that will make every day an unforgettable experience. Activities and Explorations as a Witch Navigate forests, caves, cliffs and lakes on your broom to meet adorable life forms and complete your Encyclopedia. Gather materials to craft problem-solving potions, discover hidden treasures and unlock new paths. Stories of the Village and Its Inhabitants Work as an apprentice witch. Help out at the village and solve problems for the villagers. Make friends with the villagers as you restore the village. By establishing bonds, you can unlock their stories and even learn about their secrets. Collections and Home Decoration Gather recipes and materials to craft furniture, which will give your living space a personal touch. Collect and customize brooms to add extra enjoyment to your explorations. Laid-back Moments Spend your peaceful free time fishing and chatting. Interact with cats to deepen your cross-species friendships. Create your exclusive story in this magical yet ever so relatable world!
