Originally built between 1859-1873, and electrified throughout in 1959, this double track main line is one of the most important lines in Southern Germany. Running 120 km (74 miles) the route connects two historic cities and features 11 detailed stations. Start your journey at Passau, the ‘city of three rivers’ and follow the Danube to Vilshofen, Plattling, the historic city of Straubing and on to Regensburg, the capital of the Upper Palatinate subregion. Your duties on Passau - Regensburg include a mix of freight and passenger services aboard the powerful and efficient DB BR 147. Take the controls of this electric workhorse across five Career Mode scenarios, and continue exploring the route with Quick Drive. Experience the enchanting beauty of Bavaria on the Passau – Regensburg rail line, where scenic landscapes and historic towns seamlessly connect for an unforgettable journey. If you like this route, make sure you also check out Regensburg – Ingolstadt from RSSLO, also available separately on the Steam Store.
