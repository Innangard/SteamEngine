Only one way to fix it and escape the wrath of your alchemy master - clean the laboratory. Gather 1000+ ingredients , process them at multiple processing stations, brew almost 200 unique elixir recipes and plant them into proper shelves. Fully enjoyable alone or with friends. Use your magic wand shine you way forward, levitate or throw any of the ingredients scattered around the laboratory. Everything you see is physics-based and can be controlled with magic. You have the absolute freedom to enjoy the game any way you seem fit. Do you want to relax and slowly pick up the mess or optimize by streamlining your process of potion brewing by throwing stuff around wildly to save time? Maybe create a chain with your friends where you pass items to each other to speed everything up even more? Or just immerse yourself in the magical alchemy laboratory and enjoy your cleaning journey. It's totally up to you. The laboratory begins as an absolute mess. Empty shelves hidden beneath ginormous piles of various ingredients covering up the ground and even the balconies. As you proceed, you see the whole laboratory transform - tiny and shiny wisps floating in the hall, birds fluttering and shelves covered in beautiful flowers upon being filled with elixirs. You're not only cleaning up the space. You're transforming it into a place filled with beauty, peace and harmony. The game is fully enjoyable solo or with friends in co-op. More hands mean more fun and more cleaning up. You can scatter around and fetch the ingredients individually, create chains where one player gathers ingredients, another processes them, the third brews the elixirs and the fourth places them in their proper shelving place. Or, should you want to, you can even cause some mischief. Whatever you choose, it's your journey.
