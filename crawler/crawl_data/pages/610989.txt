No puzzles, no perks, no upgrades, and definitely no time wasted on fancy mechanics. Just raw, relentless action inspired by gritty 90s action movies. Wade through endless waves of Yakuza in tight, intense corridors where every shot counts and survival depends on quick reflexes and guts. Use an arsenal of brutal weapons and even spit in enemies’ faces to assert your dominance. This game is tailor-made for fans of old-school shooters who want pure, unfiltered gameplay: no aiming down sights, no character builds, just pure shooting mayhem. If you crave high-octane, corridor-based FPS action with zero filler, COP BASTARD delivers. Built with a modern engine yet dripping with retro atmosphere, the game throws you into an immersive world full of gore, pounding music, and chaotic combat. Get ready to run, shoot, and spit your way through the criminal underworld—and don’t expect anything less than non-stop, brutal fun.
