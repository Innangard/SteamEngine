This demo contains the entire FIRST CHAPTER of Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School. Enjoy a preview of this year’s upcoming curriculum right from the start of the game! In this NEW Demo you will: Buy a ticket and take a ride to the enigmatic Escape Academy. Escape from a death-defying introductory course on the Academy’s history. Explore the sights and sounds of the game’s living, breathing open campus Earn Extra Credit from puzzle-stuffed side quests! Rendezvous with Professor Solange atop the school’s mysterious Clocktower in a new escape room. Get familiar with Escape Academy 2’s new quest system! Track quest progress and prioritize your classwork as you see fit. Play the demo singleplayer or with a friend! As always, splitscreen couch co-op and online co-op are both fully supported! ABOUT THE FINAL GAME: Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School is the sequel to the award-winning Escape Academy, enjoyed by over 4 million players! Welcome to Escape Academy, the world’s foremost clandestine educator in the art of Escape. Whether you’re a returning student or a new pupil, you’ve come to the right place. Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School evolves the escape room format of the first game into a whole new GENRE: This is the first open world escape room game, and it's available right in the comfort of your own home. Escape Academy 2 Key Features: Puzzle your way through an all-new campaign boasting handcrafted, best-in-class escape rooms built by our team of veteran puzzle designers. Explore a sprawling, secret-filled campus with mysterious passage ways, hidden tunnels, and dangerous traps. Or chill out with your pals and take in all the *vibes* that student life has to offer. Discover a dark mystery surrounding the school’s founding, leading to shocking new revelations about the very nature of The Academy. Connect with new teachers and familiar faces from the first game with an expanded curriculum offering character driven-storylines and puzzle-stuffed side quests! Grab a classmate and experience ALL of Escape Academy 2 in both online or splitscreen couch co-op. And thanks to the new optional No-Timer Mode, students of all backgrounds can puzzle at their preferred pace. Evil genius DJ Doseone is back with a sick round of fresh beats to chill and study to. So whatcha waiting for? It’s time to go…Back 2 School!
