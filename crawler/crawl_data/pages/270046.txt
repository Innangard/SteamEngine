Enjoy a captivatingly sweet story with over 20 hours of content, set in a world full of charm and warmth. You play as Nico, an adorable cat on a journey to turn the peaceful island archipelago of Ostara into a place to call home. Along the way, Nico will make new friends, experience heartfelt moments, and uncover the many little secrets hidden across Ostara. Procedurally generated islands across four unique biomes offer endless variety and plenty to do. Grow crops, harvest plants, care for animals, mine precious resources, forage for goodies, buy and sell your wares, decorate your home, go fishing, or simply head out and explore at your own pace. A dynamic weather system and day–night cycle create unique in-game events, making each passing day feel alive and natural.
