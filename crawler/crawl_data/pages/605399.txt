Business is booming. ARMS DEALER is a deep strategy trading simulation where you start with owing money to people you should never owe money to. End with enough wealth that nobody can touch you. You don't command armies. You don't fight battles. You supply the people who do. Track market fluctuations, manage debt, evade law enforcement, and make strategic choices as you climb the ladder of the international arms trade. Expand your operation with powerful Weapons Packs, Transport Packs, and Network Upgrades that increase your reach, efficiency, and profitability. Progress from smallscale firearms dealing to advanced military hardware, unconventional weapons programs, and strategic technologies that can reshape the balance of power. Every decision carries risk. Corrupt officials demand payment. Gangs want a share of your profits. Loan sharks expect repayment. Rival dealers are waiting for you to make a mistake. ·      Deep Black Market Economy: Buy low, sell high, and exploit global instability. ·      Scale Your Operation: Buy in units. Buy by the container. Today's shipment is tomorrow's fortune. ·      Unpredictable events and violent attacks can change your fortunes overnight. ·      Weapons Progression System:  Unlock and trade increasingly sophisticated military equipment. ·      Armoured Vehicle Packs:Unlock and trade increasingly powerful military vehicles and heavy equipment. ·      Advanced Weapons Programs: Access biological, chemical, nuclear, and other highrisk technologies as your influence grows. ·      Criminal Underworld Simulation:  Deal with gangs, militias, corrupt authorities, financiers, and smugglers. ·      Permadeath Campaigns:  One bad deal can end everything. ·      Loan Sharks and Debt Management:  Borrow money to grow fast, then survive long enough to repay it. ·      Strategic Risk Management:  Balance profit, exposure, reputation, and survival. There are no heroes. There are no good outcomes. There are only margins. Some say nobody wins this game…. How rich can you become before the world catches up with you? Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains mature themes including weapons trafficking, organized crime, corruption, armed conflict, and illegal activities. The game includes occasional strong language and text descriptions of violence and criminal activity. Violence is implied through events, narrative content, and gameplay systems, but is not graphically depicted.
