REPEAT LESS. ENCYCLORA is a desktop content design and management tool built for game developers, designers, writers, and other creators working with large collections of ideas. Create structured libraries for perks, items, enemies, abilities, characters, quests, mechanics, or any other content your project needs. BUILD YOUR OWN CONTENT LIBRARY ENCYCLORA doesn't force your project into a predefined structure. Create your own card types, groups, tags, fields, and templates to match the way your project actually works. Organize everything from a small prototype to a large content library while keeping information easy to find and maintain. FIND DUPLICATES AND OVERLAPS As a project grows, ideas begin to overlap. Duplicate Audit and configurable Similarity Rules help identify cards that may be too similar. Compare names, text, tags, numerical values, mechanics, and other fields to spot repetition before it becomes a problem. Mark intentional differences when similar content is deliberate and keep your audit focused on the cases that actually need attention. ANALYZE YOUR CONTENT ENCYCLORA helps you understand the structure of your project, not just store it. Use Content Health, Diversity, Coverage, Mechanic Reuse, and other analytics to discover overused ideas, underused mechanics, design gaps, and areas that may need more variety. CONNECT YOUR IDEAS Create relationships between cards to document synergies, conflicts, requirements, dependencies, drops, and other connections across your project. DESIGNED FOR REAL PROJECTS Work with large content libraries using search, filters, groups, table view, bulk actions, quick creation tools, and pagination. Customize templates and duplicate-detection rules for different types of content instead of forcing every card to follow the same structure. YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS ENCYCLORA is a local-first desktop application. Your project data is stored locally on your computer. Export projects to JSON, export card data to CSV, create backups, restore previous versions, and move your projects without depending on a cloud service. BUILT FOR • Game developers • Game designers • Indie teams • Writers and worldbuilders • Tabletop and RPG creators • Anyone managing a growing library of interconnected creative content Create.
