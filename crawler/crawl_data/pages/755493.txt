Gideon and Jeremy are the founding members of the post-americana band Briars of North America, and have spent many years working together leading the collaborative international music exchange OneBeat. Gideon, a vocalist, keyboard player, and composer, is currently a member of the renowned Meredith Monk Ensemble and performs with Bread and Puppet Theater. Jeremy, a french horn player, producer and multi-instrumentalist, was a touring member of Neutral Milk Hotel and the National. Gideon and Jeremy both find inspiration in music ranging from traditional to experimental, and from the sounds of the natural world.
