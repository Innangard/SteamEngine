A single hopper bulk tipping wagon, the Fas 126 has been designed for transport of a variety of goods including ballast, gravel and more. An indispensable workhorse this wagon was originally built in 1993 when 2 prototypes were built by FEW Blankenburg before the final production run of 250 units started in 1994. Almost all wagons are still in use on the rail network. The tipping mechanism makes them unique, along with the ability to be controlled locally with remote shunting. Rebuilt in authentic detail for Train Simulator Classic this wagon includes four steering variants and 16 wagon variants with authentic DB branding. Included with the pack are two scenarios on Bahnstrecke Riesa – Dresden that teach you how to use the remote operation. The DB Fas 126 is ready for service, add this unique freight wagon to your German railroads.
