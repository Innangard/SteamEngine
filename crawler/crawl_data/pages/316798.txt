Use your huge arsenal, out-maneuver your enemies, push your piloting skills to the limit and defeat the invaders at all costs. Missiles Away delivers non-stop action, intense gunplay, and strategic decision-making. Fight your way through wave after wave of enemies that will do anything just to get a chance to hurt you. Equipped with a cutting-edge arsenal, it's up to you to pilot your heavily armed helicopter through deadly enemy attacks, decimating enemy hordes and unleashing destruction upon the invaders. As you progress, gather powerful upgrades, unlock devastating weapons, and develop unique strategies to turn the tides of battle in your favor. Features Intense First-Person Combat: Action never stops! You will find yourself rushing the chaos of an alien invasion as you engage in lightning-fast gunfights and aerial maneuvers. Rogue-Like Progress: Embrace the unpredictability of each run with randomized power-ups, and upgrades. Adapt on the fly and discover new strategies to survive against overwhelming odds. Upgradeable Arsenal: Unlock a diverse range of devastating weapons and equipment, from high-caliber machine guns and missile launchers to experimental alien tech. Customize your loadout to suit your playstyle and maximize destruction. Strategic Decision-Making: Make split-second choices that could mean the difference between life and death. prioritize targets, exploit enemy weaknesses, and maneuver precisely to secure victory. Grapple Hook Movement: Up against faster, highly maneuverable enemy vehicles, you are equipped with a grapple hook to turn the tide of the battle. You will need to use this tool visely to get the edge over your enemies. Missiles Away is the ultimate fusion of rogue-like progress, movement oriented first person shooting and high-octane action. Against overwhelming odds, and show the invaders that humanity will never back down. The fate of humanity rests in your hands.
