Immerse yourself in a meditative world where a tiny painter colors tiny figurines, decorates their home, and learns to take care of themselves. Decorate the room, buy new geeky items, and boost your little painter’s creativity. The more creativity they have, the better their miniatures will turn out. Each painted miniature brings you closer to your ultimate goal: a long-awaited vacation... Or maybe skip the vacation and just keep painting? Features: Unhurried progress: take your time creating the perfect geeky room. Work and rest balance: don't overwork your artist, or he'll burn out. But don't let him relax too much either, or he'll lose his motivation. 24 unique miniatures : Unlock packs of figures, each one a direct nod to a famous character. Skill growth : The more experienced your character becomes, the faster they work. Pixel art in isometric view : That warm 2000s vibe, right on your desktop. Around 70 hours of meditative gameplay.
