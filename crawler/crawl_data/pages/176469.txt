View Community Hub About This Game Dive into the captivating world of Vacuum Ball , where colorful cubes and spheres create a delightful puzzle experience! In this unique game, you control a small ball that attracts various cubes, navigating through creatively designed levels filled with vibrant colors. Your goal? Collect the white cubes while skillfully avoiding the colored ones (red, blue, and more) that stand in your way. Key Features: Challenging Yet Relaxing Gameplay : Enjoy a well-balanced difficulty that strikes the perfect chord between easy and normal. Vacuum Ball is designed for those looking to unwind and enjoy a laid-back gaming experience without excessive challenges. Unique Levels : Each of the 100 levels presents a new layout of cubes and spheres, providing a fresh puzzle experience every time you play. The thoughtfully crafted designs ensure that no two levels feel the same! Collect and Customize : After successfully clearing a level, your total collected cubes are tallied up! Spend them to customize the look of your ball and express your unique style as you progress through the game. Achievements and Cloud Saves : Unlock achievements as you conquer levels, and enjoy the convenience of cloud saves, allowing you to pick up right where you left off across devices. Relaxing Soundtrack : Immerse yourself in the soothing background music that complements the easy-going vibe of the game, enhancing your overall experience. Intuitive Controls : Whether you prefer using a gamepad or keyboard, Vacuum Ball supports multiple control schemes to ensure a comfortable gaming experience. Why You'll Love Vacuum Ball : Perfect for casual gamers seeking a fun and engaging puzzle experience. Minimalist art style and clear design make it visually appealing and easy to navigate. A great way to unwind while challenging your mind and reflexes. Join the fun and discover the joys of collecting and avoiding in Vacuum Ball ! Whether you're a seasoned puzzle enthusiast or just looking for a relaxing game to enjoy, Vacuum Ball offers a delightful journey through vibrant levels and satisfying challenges.
