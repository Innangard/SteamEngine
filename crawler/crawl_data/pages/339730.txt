Leaving his home colony while tracking a mysterious glitch, an android finds himself in the midst of a vast and dangerous structure, completely hidden from his people's knowledge. Unable to go back, you must keep going to find out where he is being led... And why he left in the first place. Dark foreboding atmosphere Retro 90s PC graphics , with a modern flair. Uncover mysterious lore as you travel into the depths. Jump, climb, slide, and scan your way around difficult challenges, using simple mechanics . No "Parkour Ledges". Climb almost any vertical wall face using your pickaxes! It's a tough game, and sometimes frustrating . Fast and slow paced. Take in the sights as you traverse, and sometimes play as fast as a speed runner !
