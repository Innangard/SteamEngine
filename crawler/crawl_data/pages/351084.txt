The heroes won the fight. The city survived. Now it’s your shift. You are a Harvester — a post-battle worker sent to clean up the aftermath of kaiju attacks. Rev up your saw, carve up fallen monsters, collect meat, bones, organs, and rare kaiju amber, then use them to upgrade your equipment. Upgrade your saws, improve damage and critical hits, unlock collector robots, helper drones, supply drops, critters, and booster drinks. The better your gear, the faster the harvest. Work your way through various kaiju, from smaller monsters to massive body parts too large to fit on screen. Travel through five disaster zones inspired by famous places around the world: Los Angeles, Rio de Janeiro, Istanbul, Siberia, and Tokyo. Cut, collect, upgrade, automate, and move on to a bigger monster. Heroes saved the world. You clean up the mess.
