Find your winning line. Decks of Valor: Fantasy Card Battles is a fantasy card game built around predefined decks and tactical decisions. Command units, cast spells, equip weapons and use your hero power to defeat the rival hero. Different decks, different plans Explore aggressive armies, patient control strategies and combinations across three deck tiers. Each deck has its own hero and complete 30-card list. Browse every deck and card, then unlock the decks you want with Gold earned by playing. Choose your challenge Learn the basics in a short interactive tutorial. Play a Friendly match against a chosen or random opponent. Travel through a three-tier Campaign with branching encounters and deck rewards. Enter a 16-player knockout Tournament or challenge ten contenders in King of the Mountain. Watch computer-controlled matches and save progress in longer modes. Play with other people on the same local network. Inspect cards, decks and the action history. English and Spanish interface and card text are included. Solo play works offline; local network play requires a connection to the other players. There is no custom deck building. Meet the heroes and decks Explore a selection of the heroes and their different deck plans.
