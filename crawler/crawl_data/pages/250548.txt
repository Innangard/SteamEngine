With Matraquinha PAIR, matching everyday items becomes a playful exercise in logic and memory. Featuring charming illustrations and sound effects designed for player well-being, the game provides a welcoming and motivating environment. Players start with simple challenges in the initial levels, and as they progress, the difficulty gradually increases. There are 99 different images to explore and learn, stimulating associations across categories such as food, clothing, school supplies, and toys. With a scoring system that rewards progress and unlocks achievements, Matraquinha PAIR adapts to each player’s pace, celebrating every victory. Ideal for use with parents, educators, and healthcare professionals, Matraquinha PAIR is more than just a game—it’s a tool for inclusion and development for those on the autism spectrum.
