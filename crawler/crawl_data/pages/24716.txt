Ver todas las discusiones Acerca de este juego Your adventure begins in the heart of a lush forested wilderness, where the simple act of fishing by a  river, mining rocky outcroppings, or chopping local pine trees will become the foundation for a thriving existence.  Idle Iktah  blends traditional RPG elements with the satisfying progression of an incremental game, allowing you to craft tools, level up skills, and unlock the secrets of the land at a pace that works for you. Leveling up is deeply rewarding, offering powerful rewards and abilities. Whether you're offline or actively engaging, your journey continues. The offline progress feature guarantees that your community grows, resources accumulate, and your story unfolds, even when you're not actively playing!
