View Community Hub About This Game Frog Holm is a narrative-driven game about a young frog named Filip who returns to his home flock of islands to take over the old family pub. But instead of settling into quiet days behind the counter, he finds himself drawn into the mysterious disappearance of one of the archipelagos residents. Explore the different islands of the community, each one holding its own small secrets and traces of the past. Along the way, gather ingredients to craft froggy cocktails for your guests back at the pub. The customer is king: your guests have very different tastes and will constantly challenge your skills with new requests. Mix over 20 unique recipes and become a truly great host.
