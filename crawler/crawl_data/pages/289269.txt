View Community Hub About This Game CLASSIC SNAKE. UNDEAD HORDE. TOTAL CHAOS. SnekromancY twists classic snake movement into a chaotic action roguelite. Slither through maze-like dungeons, devour your enemies, and raise an undead army that fights from your back. SLITHER. DEVOUR. GROW. Weave through maze-like rooms, gobble up food and treasure, and summon zombies, skeletons, ghosts, and other undead minions to join you as you grow. Your minions can auto-attack or be manually aimed if you have the skills. BUILD SOMETHING COMPLETELY BROKEN Stack powerful upgrades and relics to transform a humble undead snake into an unstoppable dungeon-clearing monster. Runs start simple, but they don't stay that way. FIGHT YOUR WAY TO THE TOP Survive escalating hordes, explore unpredictable dungeon floors, and defeat powerful bosses standing between you and the safety of your tower. FEATURES Classic snake movement meets bullet-heaven combat Undead minions, fully automated or manually aimed Procedurally generated mazes, full of secrets and hidden treasures Game-breaking upgrades and relic combinations Massive enemy hordes and powerful bosses Persistent unlocks that expand future runs with new content, not stat grinding MASTER THE ART OF SNECROMANCY.
