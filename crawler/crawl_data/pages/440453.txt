Where your adventure with the world of culinary enterprise awaits! In this management simulator, you take on the challenge of running your restaurant. Key Features 🍽️ Menu design 👨‍🍳 Restaurant management 👥 Staff management 💰 Sales system 📊 Finances 🎨 Decor customization Put your business skills to the test! Take your first steps as an owner of a humble diner and work your way to the top as the crème de la crème of restaurant tycoons! Serve your customers, organize your storage space, order ingredients, and furnish your eatery. There are plenty of tasks awaiting an aspiring restaurant owner, and it’s your job to make sure they’re done and everything’s kept running smoothly. Your starting capital allows for a small fast-food place at first. Compose your first diner menu snacks and progress towards fancier food in no time! You choose the dishes served at your restaurant. Can you find the best cuisine? From collecting tips to managing a team of world-class staff. As you progress, you’ll have opportunities to control your finances and automate ingredient purchasing, and design a work schedule for your staff. Make sure the bills are paid, and your restaurant makes a tidy profit. You'll be hooked for good once you get a taste of success! Expand your venue, hire more employees, and get fancier equipment. Watch as the world outside your restaurant’s doors responds to its success by getting a bigger crowd queuing for a table at your place. Dive into the challenge of culinary enterprise with a Restaurant Owner!
