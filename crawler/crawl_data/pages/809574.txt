One shredder. You. A gigantic bank vault is buried in money right up to the ceiling: mountains of bills, bundles, coins and gold bars. Your job is simple and glorious: tear through all that wealth and feed it to the shredder. Every bill pays out, every purchase makes you faster, and the mountain keeps getting lower. Dig, scoop, suck it all up Start with your bare hands: grab bills one by one and carry them to the shredder. From there it only gets bigger: a bucket, a shovel and a powerful vacuum that pulls money in with a swirling vortex. When your arms get tired, launch drones — they'll dig up and haul the bills without you. Upgrades you can't put down Dozens of upgrades for every tool, the shredder and the drones A proficiency tree with branches for your play style Boosts, relics and gold perks for the long run Numbers climb from your first dollars to trillions — and beyond A secret at the bottom of the vault Somewhere beneath the mountains of money, a golden key lies hidden. Dig down to it, open the massive door and step into the Golden Reserve — a hall full of gold bars where a whole new level of the game begins. Better together Play solo or invite up to three friends to online co-op. Shared progress, one shared mountain of money, voice and text chat — dig together, argue over who buys the next upgrade, and shred a billion as a team. Features A cozy first-person incremental game Every bill is a real object you can pick up, not just a number Online co-op for up to 4 players with voice chat (full game) Drone automation — the money keeps rolling in while you're busy with something else 100 Steam achievements (full game) Fully localized into 30 languages Free demo This is the free demo of SHRED A BILLION: roughly the first 30–40 minutes of the game. You get your hands and the bucket with all their upgrades, the jetpack, boosts, a few relics and the first tiers of the proficiency tree — and at the end, a short test drive of the vacuum. The shovel, the vacuum, drones, the Golden Reserve and online co-op are waiting for you in the full game. Demo progress doesn't carry over to the full game. Enjoyed the demo?
