You have found yourself in this reality, and now it’s your path. Entering the tower, you find yourself in fierce battles that require valuable loot items - armor, magic rings, weapons, and much more. These are essential tools for developing your unique builds while clearing the tower's floors. Defeat enemies and earn gold, which can be spent on new loot items. Continuously manage your loot to become stronger right in the battle by absorbing the mythical power of the items you discard - even if for a mere moment, but with dedication it’s enough to ascend to near godhood and kill whatever twisted creature stands in your way. Use potions and tomes to boost your power, seek out reforged items for unique and enhanced gear, and banish excess loot to refresh the shop with better options. Make the most of every item and be ready for any enemy. Combine loot strategically to craft dominating builds and become the ultimate power hunter. Balance between strengths and weaknesses to maximize your power to the fullest extent.
