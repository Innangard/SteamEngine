Build colonies on every side of asteroids through smart placement that maximizes synergies, balances resources, and shapes efficient layouts. Watch trains roll around your colony as you trigger satisfying combos by connecting stations. Once you complete the tutorial, an expedition level awaits. Your expedition score will appear on the leaderboards, so expect a little friendly rivalry! This demo includes: Expedition Mode (preview) - Plan your colony carefully, earn points from synergies, and reach score thresholds and custom objectives to achieve expedition goals. Procedurally generated environments (limited) - Try a sample of unique asteroid layouts to get a feel for the full game’s variety. Core building set - Experiment with a reduced roster of structures designed to highlight the main mechanics (includes trains and orbital stations ). Collectibles and customization - a few customization items that let you add a personal touch to your colony. Achievements - Unlock a small set of achievements as you explore the available content. Not included in this demo: Freebuild Mode (creative mode without score or objectives). Full building roster and all environment presets. Unlimited procedurally generated environments.
