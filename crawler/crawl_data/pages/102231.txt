Læs mere Hvad udviklerne har at sige: Hvorfor tidlig adgang? “We’re choosing Early Access because we want your experiences as players to be part of the development process. Over the past five years, we’ve been working to bring mothership construction, crew life, fleet exploration, and planetary base building together, giving you the freedom to shape your own journey through space. These systems interact in complex ways, and our perspective as developers alone isn’t enough to tell us how enjoyable, intuitive, and flexible they are across different playstyles. Early Access gives us the opportunity to hear from more players while there is still room to make meaningful changes. How you design your mothership, organize production, manage your crew, and make decisions during exploration can reveal issues that internal testing hasn’t uncovered—and introduce approaches and ideas we never anticipated. We hope to use that feedback to refine game balance, improve usability and the connections between systems, and better understand which areas deserve the most attention. For us, Early Access is more than making the game available sooner. It is an opportunity to develop it alongside our players. We have a clear creative direction, but we also want to listen to different perspectives and, through ongoing discussion and iteration, help this universe grow into a deeper, more vibrant place to explore.” Cirka hvor lang tid vil dette spil være i tidlig adgang? “We expect the game to remain in Early Access for around one year. During this time, we’ll continue expanding and refining the game’s content, adjusting its balance, and improving usability and stability with feedback from our players. The exact duration may change as development progresses, as we want the game to reach the level of quality and completeness we envision before moving to a full release.” Hvorledes kommer den fulde version til at være anderledes end produktet i tidlig adgang? “The current Early Access version has the game’s core framework in place, but there is still a lot of content we want to add and expand upon. During Early Access, we plan to release several major updates focused on faction warfare, an evolving universe, and modding tools, alongside substantial content additions and expansions to existing gameplay. For the full version, we aim to build on this foundation to deliver a richer experience with more content and more fully developed systems. We plan to move to a full release once these major updates are complete and have been thoroughly refined with player feedback. The specific content and update plans may change based on development progress and community feedback.” Hvad er den nuværende tilstand for versionen med tidlig adgang? “The game’s core systems are already in place, including planetary surface generation, multi-deck starships, fleet combat, and space travel, establishing the foundation of the gameplay experience. The current Early Access version features more than ten types of landable planets, a customizable sandbox mode supporting universes with 120 star systems, and two long-form story scenarios. Players can chart their own journey through space in sandbox mode or get an initial taste of the game through its story scenarios. While the core framework is established, there is still substantial content to add, and both the individual systems and the overall experience need further refinement. Throughout Early Access, we’ll continue expanding the game’s content and refining the existing gameplay with feedback from our players.” Ændrer spillets pris sig under og efter tidlig adgang? “At this time, we have no plans to change the game’s price when it leaves Early Access for a full release. Step into the role of a legendary captain and lead your elite team to forge an epic tale on the desert planet of New Terra. You are a legendary captain from the United Stellar. While leading your team to suppress a galaxy-spanning rebellion, you’re pulled into the Initial Rift—an isolated star region lost to time. Here, the last known civilization—the Terra Kingdom—has long fallen. Its homeworld destroyed two centuries ago, its people now scattered across broken worlds: surviving in hiding, raiding for scraps, or clinging to fading strongholds. Seamlessly connected star system maps Derelict space facilities to explore Building an Interstellar Transportation Network Unknown planets waiting for discovery Surface resources ripe for collection Colonies built from the ground up Will you rebuild order, watch it crumble, or plunge the stars into deeper chaos? Harness advanced United Stellar technology to gather resources, unlock research, and expand your domain. Design your base room by room. Assemble space fleets. Construct planetary hubs and system-spanning megastructures. Explore scorching deserts, frozen tundras, volcanic wastelands, ruined ground cities, orbital relics—and twisted alien hives choked in bio-contamination. The fate of the Wastespace begins with your first outpost.
