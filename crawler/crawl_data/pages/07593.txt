Tavern Keeper 🍻 - Original Soundtrack Composers Phil French and Tom Puttick of Cedar Studios (the team behind the soundtracks of Two Point Hospital™ , Two Point Museum™ , and more) take you on a feel-good tour through the world of Tavern Keeper. Created over many years of close collaboration with Greenheart Games, the Original Soundtrack features 46 tracks across almost 2 hours of original music.
