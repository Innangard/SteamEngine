You won’t just question suspects, you’ll examine the environment and collect every piece of evidence needed to reconstruct what really happened. Each of the nine cases presents a different scenario, ranging from violent confrontations to incidents that appear to be accidents. Nothing is explained in advance. About This Game Grab a pencil and paper to write down what you need because no one is going to help you. You play the role of a detective who has been assigned a case. When you arrive at the crime scene, you find a hotel with several rooms. At that moment you will have to discover what has happened, but it will not be easy. The evidence is not visible, the residents have secrets and they do not want anyone to know them. You will also find some puzzles that will test your determination and your ability as a detective. DETECTIVE - The Motel is a realistic deduction game where only your detective instinct will help you. Inspect all the rooms, find evidence, understand the relationships between the residents, their motivations and you will be able to solve the case. To solve the case, you will need to answer these questions: Names of the suspects. What room does each of them live in? Approximate time of the crime. Crime day. Who died? Who is the murderer? You will have to be very attentive to your surroundings to find all the possible evidence, it will not be easy, but if you are a good detective you will find the truth that is hidden behind the walls of each of the rooms in "The Motel".
