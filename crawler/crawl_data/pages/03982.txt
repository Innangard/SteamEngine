Buy Liberté Soundtrack $6.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Filip Kazimierczak 's stunning digital score brilliantly accentuates the game's environment and mood. A blend of inspirations will give a distinctive and captivating aural experience. It took an open mind to delve deeply into these many sources of inspiration, as well as a good amount of scores from which we pulled. We looked at a variety of materials, from sci-fi classics like "Alien" to the captivating film scores written by Trent Reznor & Atticus Ross, Daniel Pemberton, Ben Salisbury & Geoff Barrow, or Michael Abels, as well as revolutionary songs written during the French Revolution. The soundtrack created as a result of this varied mash-up of inspirations is atmospheric and evocative, perfectly enhancing the game's story and environment. As you set out on your quest, Liberté's soundtrack will be by your side, accompanying you through diverse character encounters, heart-pounding combat sequences, and even the spine-tingling ambiance of Bliss.
