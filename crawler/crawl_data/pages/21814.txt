Neighbors: Suburban warfare is a chaotic free-to-play party brawler, where you battle for suburban dominance. The neighborhood is the battleground, your neighbors are the enemy and their property is the target. Team up with your friends in chaotic PVE and PVP battles, defend your house with traps and gadgets, and destroy your neighbors’ houses with whatever tools you have at hand. Fast-paced matches, wacky combos and pure chaos make every game of Neighbors: Suburban Warfare crazy, unpredictable and wild. Pick your suburban hero → Grab a garden tool → Barricade your house → Invade your neighbors’ and cause chaos → Make it back for a lovely home cooked meal! Bring the fight to the Suburban Neighborhoods Have you ever dreamt of wrecking havoc on your annoying neighbors? Now’s your chance! Take the battle to the idyllic suburban streets and bring long-overdue justice down on your next-door rivals. Triumph and become a local legend! Gang up with your Friends for Free Looking for your next free-to-play party game? That’s right, Neighbors: Suburban Warfare is going F2P! And that’s not all; the game is releasing on Xbox Series X/S and PS5 with cross-play support. This is a perfect time to get in the game! Fight in Chaotic Family vs. Family Battles Are you looking for a thrilling team experience? Neighbors: Suburban Warfare features a bunch of exciting game modes to play with your friends: Destruction: Smash your neighbors prized possessions before they wreck yours. Brawl: No houses, no rules, just pure team fighting! knock out the enemy to win. Prop hunt: Think chair, become the chair! Hide as everyday objects or hunt down that sus couch. Robot Invasion: Team up in this PvE mode and defend your house against waves of invading robots. Barricade your House and Destroy your Neighbors’ Do you want to defend your homestead while sabotaging your enemies? This is the place to live out that fantasy! You’ll get to pick your house design, barricade it with everything from bear traps to alarm cans, and invade your neighbors, but beware! They’ll be awaiting you… Customize your Suburban Warrior Would you like to choose from a roster of your local neighborhood heroes? In this game, you can pick between the “friendly” grandma, the “cute” boy, the “kind” grandpa, the “perfect” dad and many more. Unlock epic skins by playing the game to ensure you will be noticed. There’s nothing better than asserting your suburban dominance in style. Embrace the Chaos Want to try full-blown free-to-play party Mayhem? Then try Neighbors: Suburban Warfare today! Every tool is a weapon, every piece of furniture is a target, and you never know when you will get shot down by a machine-gunning seagull. No showdown will ever be the same, so prepare accordingly or face imminent humiliation! Season your cast iron pans and don your rubber gloves - the battle for suburban supremacy has begun!
