About This Game Gameplay To progress through each level, the player must possess specific essential items that are necessary for its completion. In order to obtain these items, you must meticulously arrange your items and  treasures within your inventory. After that you'll need to engage in the process of selling your treasures to the merchant. Now, armed with a substantial amount of pesetas, you possess the means to procure the items you require! To successfully complete the level, all that remains is to place the purchased items back into your inventory. Features Use your organizing skills to make all your items fit. Use your logical thinking to balance your finances and be able to buy the items needed. Understand the format of several items to find the best fitting. 40 unique handmade levels. Relaxing music and sound effects made for the game!
