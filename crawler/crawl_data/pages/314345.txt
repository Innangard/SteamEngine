About This Game Holbrook Hotel is an atmospheric horror game that embodies the dreams and passion of a young developer. The 14-year-old developer used his imagination and love for retro style to create this unique horror world, dedicated to those who love games and horror experiences. In the atmospheric horror game Holbrook Hotel, you will come to the hotel as a guest, and after hearing strange noises from the next room, you can't help but embark on an adventure. As you explore, the dark secrets of the town of Holbrook Oaks gradually surface. Under the quiet surface of the town, there is anxiety and fear, and you must find a way to survive and escape the unknown danger that is about to come. Game Feature Immersive escape experience: In the process of avoiding being chased, players need to collect items, use tools in the scene, and find an effective escape route. Exploration and puzzle solving: Not only escaping, there are also hidden clues and puzzles in the hotel waiting to be solved to reveal the shocking truth of the town. Retro visuals and sound effects: The classic PS1-style low-polygon graphics and eerie sound effects make people feel like they are in a horror masterpiece of the last century. Deep horror atmosphere: The combination of weird sound effects and dim environment creates an immersive horror experience. Self-challenge: Explore, escape, solve puzzles, and test your wisdom and courage. The game creates a unique environment in a classic retro style, combined with weird sound effects and dark scenes, to bring you an immersive horror experience. You need to use all your wisdom and courage to escape from danger and pursuit, get rescue, and escape from this dangerous place!
