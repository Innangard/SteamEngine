Lihat Hub Komunitas Laporkan bug dan berikan masukan untuk game ini di papan diskusi Lihat semua diskusi Tentang Game Ini YOU ARE NOT THE CHOSEN ONE Start as a humble peasant tossed into a dungeon, tasked with finding one of the ancient Relics of Caerwyn to protect your town. Choose to be a proud Freefolk, a little Mossling, a towering Grimhorn or a stout Stonefolk, with 19 backgrounds and hundreds of customisation options to choose from! CUSTOMIZE YOUR COMBAT STYLE Quickly swap between melee and ranged weapons, casting spells, or throwing deadly concoctions during immersive, fast-paced action combat. Classless, fully flexible skill trees in Adventuring, Survival, Dungeoneering, Weaponry and the Oldways of Magic allow you to specialize or create clever combinations. HANG OUT IN HANDCRAFTED TOWNS Meet local villagers who all have lives, stories and schedules of their own. Reveal quests by eavesdropping, deciphering rumors, and exploring strange ruins. Gather materials, craft items, cook meals for your party, and upgrade your gear before continuing your adventure! SCHEDULING GAME NIGHT HAS NEVER BEEN EASIER Play solo, or up to 4-player co-op with seamless drop-in/drop-out. Cloud-shared saves, group XP, and scaling difficulty all mean the party can continue with someone offline, but nobody gets left behind. Designed for busy gamers, campaigns have about 10 hours of story, and 15 hours of optional content.
