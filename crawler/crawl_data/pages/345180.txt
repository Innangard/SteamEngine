Take control of advanced weapon systems, build powerful synergies, and then blast away as aliens attempt to conquer Earth. Between rounds you'll earn unique augmentations, purchase and upgrade a huge variety of weapons, make scientific breakthroughs, and unlock powerful synergy bonuses. With auto-battler style drafting, procedural battles with multiple enemy factions, and light roguelike elements, Cosmic Raze offers endless replayability. Simple controls, and tactical depth make this the perfect game for any device, from desktops to laptops and especially the Steam Deck! Features Tower defense gameplay Auto-battler inspired drafting and synergies Roguelike style upgrades, randomization, and perma-death. Each run is unique! 3 unique enemy factions, with their own strengths and weaknesses. Each run combines 2, with 1 faction becoming your primary opponent. 11 difficulty settings including a more relaxing easy mode Piles of unique weapons, active powers, augmentations, and synergy bonuses to customize your builds 46 unlockables to expand your arsenal even further 40 achievements to unlock Play with any gamepad, or mouse Pick Up And Play: A full game lasts around 25 minutes.
