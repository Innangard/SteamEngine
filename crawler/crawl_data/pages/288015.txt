Discover the strange secrets in this part of the world ​A volcano started being active again. Water in the city became more and more scarce. Mining in the search for water was the only option. While digging, strange artifacts were found. Archaeologists and explorers started being interested. Learn more about the world and the dark secret behind some peoples actions. In the spirit of old school 2d dungeon crawlers. Travel multiple levels with different enemies and puzzles. Discover items to gain new abilities which allow to go even further. Features Explore 4 different dungeons each with 3 sections. Each sections have a maze of 10 floors. Battle enemies in the right order with a simple combat system. Solve different type of puzzles using acquired items. Normal and hard mode with a secret for those that can do the impossible.
