A group of mystics from across the globe gather in the west of Ireland to rip open the veil between worlds and speak directly with the dead. On the run up to the séance, Evelyn Deane goes missing. Detective Ward is summoned to find her and uncover the culprit before the séance takes place in 48 hours. Every action he takes brings the event closer – can he save her in time? In The Séance of Blake Manor Demo, you will experience the first two playable in-game hours of the game. You will be introduced to the investigation, interrogation, and deduction mechanics, introduced to the cast of characters who will be staying with you, and introduced to the haunting Blake Manor. In the Demo you will: Arrive at Blake Manor,check in and begin your investigation by solving the first mystery (October 28th: 23:00 – 00.00) Explore a small section of the manor grounds and tackle an environmental puzzle Meet the cast of characters and investigate two of them (October 29th: 8:00 – 9:00) Confront a character after investigating them Influence other characters in the story This demo is available only in English.
