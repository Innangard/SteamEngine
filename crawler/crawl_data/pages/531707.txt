Inspired by Eastern miniature art, mythology, and ancient tales, the soundtrack accompanies the game's journey through historic cities, festivals, mysteries, and puzzles. Traditional instruments including santur, hang drum, and bendir combine with melodies influenced by Hicaz, Nihavent, and Rast makam traditions to create a unique musical atmosphere. The artbook consists of two sections: the first covers the game's development process, while the second features all the artworks included in the game. The soundtrack is provided in MP3 and WAV formats, and the art book is included as a PDF file.
