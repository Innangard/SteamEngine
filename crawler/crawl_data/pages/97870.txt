In Road Defense Racing , speed alone won’t win the race. To come out on top, you’ll need to survive the chaos of the track. Armed drivers fight for every corner while the crowd around the circuit turns the race into an unpredictable arena. Earn money by destroying rivals and smashing track objects, unlock new weapons, and upgrade your car to take on increasingly intense circuits. VEHICULAR COMBAT RACING Enter races where speed and destruction go hand in hand. Fire at rivals, dodge incoming attacks, and fight your way to victory as cars exchange gunfire at full speed. THE TRACK IS DANGEROUS TOO The threat doesn’t come only from rival drivers. The crowd around the track can also attack vehicles, turning every race into a hostile environment where a single mistake can cost you the win. DESTROY YOUR RIVALS Use a wide arsenal to dominate the race. Equip rifles, missile launchers, land mines, and shotguns to destroy opponents and clear your path to the lead. UPGRADE YOUR VEHICLE Earn money by smashing objects, running through obstacles, and surviving the race. Invest in engine, tire, nitro, and armor upgrades to make your car faster and more durable.
