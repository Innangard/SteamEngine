View Community Hub About This Content Houses sometimes need to be put up and sometimes need to be torn down - we present to you a demolition game like never before!👷‍♂️👷‍♀️ Key features: 🏗️ Demolition - now you can demolish prepared houses! We enable you to do this in various ways - demolition with a ball, excavator, or maybe explosives? Choose what you are most satisfied with!💣 🔨 Deconstruction - demolition isn't just about knocking down walls, breaking windows, and scraping off tiles - it's the conscientious removal of door frames, panels, and priceless ornaments. Use a hammer, drill, and torch to do it perfectly! 🛠️ 📖 Delve into the story - demolition always has a purpose - sometimes the building poses a danger to outsiders, and sometimes it reveals amazing treasures or secrets🕵 ♻️ Ecology - ecology is a hot topic these days - and we support it too! - interact with the environment by twisting things to recycle them for use in future projects✅ 🚚🚛 Moving out - in our old house many things remind us of our most beautiful moments spent with our family or with ourselves - sentiment is very important - it would be a shame to leave everything behind. Bring out your belongings to make a full-fledged move. House Builder has a story mode. Deconstruction levels will unlock as the story progresses - that is, if you reach a level from the storyline of, for example, Lighthouse, you will automatically unlock a second level - Deconstruction of the Lighthouse.
