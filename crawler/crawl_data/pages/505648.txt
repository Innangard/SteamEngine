Use and master our Computer, the most powerful time machine ever created! Thanks to this marvelous device, you will be able to travel centuries back in time: from the Jurassic period to the Space Race, or from Ancient Greece to the French Renaissance, you can explore thousands of years in just a few seconds! Change the past. To complete your assignments, you will have to make decisions at key historical moments. Stop JFK from being assassinated, give the nuclear bomb to Gandhi, send humanity to Mars rather than to the moon… and analyze the consequences to make even better choices afterwards! Just… please avoid causing a premature apocalypse, if you don’t mind. " Working at A Better World Company means being both a witness to AND an actor in history! ” - Anonymous former employee Climb the ladder! Each of your completed assignments will be evaluated by our HR Team and given an employee score. When your score is high enough, you will be authorized to access the upper floors of our office building… unless you manage to really screw things up. As we encourage employee empowerment as part of our corporate culture, you will be able to restart assignments if you think you can achieve a better ending and thus, a better employee score. So if you’re just dying to see the building’s 7th floor… you know what to do. #RocketEmoji The power of technology. ABW Company promotes technology as well as individualism. Rather than exchanging boring gossip and useless tips with soulless colleagues at the coffee machine, you will be assisted by Globos, our cutting-edge virtual assistant! She will be your dedicated guide throughout your assignments, answering any questions you may have. She’s even programmed to tell JOKES! Who needs friends when you have Globos? A flexible culture. ABW Company will give you objectives for each assignment, but YOU get to choose how to achieve them. If we want you to change someone’s life from ordinary to extraordinary, we won’t mind if you make them a movie star, a cut-throat businessman or, why not, the first ever female president of the USA! An office dedicated to excellency. Improving the world takes some skill, right? At ABW Company, you’ll find a professional workspace designed by the biggest names in retro-futurism, helping you dedicate yourself to our success! Enjoy an office away from plants, organic fruit, or foosball tables. We took care to remove every possible source of distraction to help you complete your assignments. #NoRoomForMistakes We’re looking forward to working with you. If you have any questions regarding our corporate culture or our long-term goals, keep them to yourself! Let’s get to work, together, FOR A BETTER WORLD!
