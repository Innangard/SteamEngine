View Community Hub About This Game Game Description Every night, the nightmare begins. Your room is no longer a safe place… The moment you lie down on a strange bed and close your eyes— you are no longer alone. Is this reality? Or are you trapped inside someone else’s nightmare? Key Features That game you’ve seen somewhere before — now on Steam! Run from terrifying monsters and hide in safe rooms to survive. Lie on your bed to earn money, then build turrets to fight back against vengeful monsters. Tap empty floor tiles to open the building menu. Place buildings strategically and manage your resources wisely to survive as long as possible. As time passes, the tension keeps rising, and new strategies and upgrades become the key to survival. Stop the monsters and you win. But if they catch you… it’s over. Take Control of Your Nightmare Don’t fear the nightmare. It might be the perfect way to blow off some stress. Overcome the challenge and prove that you can wake up from this nightmare. “The moment you lie down on the bed… everything begins.” Can you escape this nightmare? Survive the monsters and climb your way to the top rank!
