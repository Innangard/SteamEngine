View Community Hub About This Game BUILD YOUR STOCK CAR RACING EMPIRE Start with a modest garage, a limited budget, and one goal: build a team capable of dominating the championship. In Stock Car Tycoon, you control every part of a growing motorsport organization. Develop competitive race cars, recruit talented staff, attract sponsors, expand your headquarters, and make the strategic decisions that determine what happens on race day. BUILD AND EXPAND YOUR TEAM Transform a small racing operation into a professional motorsport empire. Upgrade your garage, workshop, offices, pit facilities, and transport fleet as your reputation and finances grow. Every improvement opens new opportunities—but expansion costs money. Invest carefully, balance your budget, and build the infrastructure your team needs to compete at the highest level. DEVELOP THE PERFECT RACE CAR Research improvements, install better components, and fine-tune your cars for different tracks and racing conditions. Raw speed is important, but reliability, handling, tire wear, and fuel efficiency can decide whether you finish on the podium or retire early. Build multiple cars, test different setups, and keep developing throughout the season to stay ahead of your rivals. HIRE THE RIGHT PEOPLE A championship is never won by one person. Recruit drivers, mechanics, engineers, pit crew members, and business staff with different strengths, weaknesses, and salaries. Train your employees, improve team chemistry, and decide when promising talent deserves a chance—or when an expensive contract is holding the team back. MANAGE SPONSORS AND FINANCES Secure sponsorship deals, meet performance targets, and keep your racing operation profitable. Bigger sponsors offer greater rewards, but they also expect stronger results. Decide how much to spend on staff, facilities, research, new cars, and race preparation. One bad season can create serious financial pressure, while smart investments can transform your team into a motorsport powerhouse. MAKE THE CALLS ON RACE DAY Choose your strategy, react to changing conditions, manage pit stops, and make crucial decisions while the race unfolds. Push for position, protect the car, change your tire strategy, or take a gamble when an opportunity appears. Every decision can be the difference between victory and defeat. CREATE A MOTORSPORT DYNASTY Compete across multiple seasons, develop rivalries, chase championship trophies, and grow your reputation throughout the racing world. Will you build a sustainable organization that dominates for years—or spend everything chasing one final lap of glory? The track creates legends. The business behind it creates champions.
