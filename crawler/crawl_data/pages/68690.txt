Huiyu AI 3D Video is not a regular video player, and it is not a “convert first, watch later” offline tool. During playback, AI analyzes each frame, generates depth information for the current image, and immediately reconstructs the flat video into a depth-driven 3D mesh scene with Three.js. Foreground elements feel closer. Background elements move farther away. Ordinary 2D footage becomes a realtime 3D video space with distance, layers, and depth. ⚡ Core Feature: Realtime AI 3D Video Huiyu AI 3D Video is designed for realtime 3D video viewing. The depth result is streamed to the frontend in realtime and used to drive 3D rendering. This means you are not watching a static converted image. You are watching a video continuously reconstructed by AI into a realtime 3D scene. 🎞️ Per-frame depth estimation —— Generate depth information for ordinary videos 🧩 3D mesh reconstruction —— Use depth maps to create spatial displacement 🌊 Realtime depth updates —— Continuously update depth while the video plays 🖼️ Depth map preview —— View the AI-generated depth result in realtime ⚡ TensorRT-Accelerated Inference To make realtime 3D video as smooth as possible, Huiyu AI 3D Video supports TensorRT-accelerated inference and uses a lightweight, fast depth model configuration by default.
