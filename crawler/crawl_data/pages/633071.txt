View Community Hub About This Game In Magic Runestones you will play as different characters fighting their way through various rooms. You can create a unique character by matching different skills and equipment and choosing unique and exclusive skills. Rich skills and equipment, unique blessings and treasures will bring you an exciting and thrilling gaming experience. Each character in Magic Runestones has its own unique attack pattern, and you can experience a different action experience for each character. Every character has an exclusive skill slot, and different characters have different exclusive skills. Each character also has an exclusive exclusive skill upgrade page. Players can unlock exclusive skills of different genres by choosing different blessings. Each character has 4 generic skill slots. Generic skills can be equipped by selecting different skills through skill stones during gameplay. Each generic skill can be upgraded through blessing. Upgrading a skill can significantly increase its power and performance, and some skills can also change their mechanics. Each character can carry up to 9 pieces of equipment. There are 4 different rarity levels, with higher rarity levels providing higher attribute bonuses and some having special effects. In addition to providing attribute bonuses, equipment also provides blessings, players can choose different types of equipment according to their own needs to freely match. Jumbo is a treasure used in the game to enhance the player's output or survival. Players can use the effects of treasures with equipment and blessings to try out different routines and genres.
