Lihat Hab Komuniti Tentang Permainan Ini Forge a path through the ashes Embersworn is a single-player roguelike deck-building adventure. Build a deck as you travel a branching map, then put your choices to the test in turn-based battles against monsters, elites, and act bosses. Make every card count Choose attacks, defenses, and special abilities to create combinations that fit your run. Adapt your deck as new cards appear and decide which risks are worth taking. Find power between battles Collect relics and potions, visit shops, and face events along the route. Each reward can change your strategy; each encounter asks you to use your resources wisely. Rise again Explore new paths and experiment with different builds across repeated runs. A successful journey takes you from the first fight to the final boss, with an optional Hard difficulty for a tougher challenge.
