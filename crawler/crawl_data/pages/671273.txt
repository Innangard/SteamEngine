View Community Hub About This Game Help save the kitties from the zombie apocalypse!!! 3 huge maps 300 objects to find (cats and zombies) 30 special objects (milk bottles) Hint system Progress saving Progress reset Hand-drawn graphics Cats VS Zombies is a hidden object game – the player's task is to find cats hidden on the map and eliminate the zombies. The game is designed in a zombie apocalypse style, but with a touch of humor. Due to contamination, an epidemic has broken out – zombies have taken over the entire town and are wreaking havoc. You are the last survivor, once a member of an elite cat sniper unit. The cat army has dropped ammunition supplies – these can be found in wooden crates. Your mission is to eliminate all the zombies and rescue the cats. Go, cat soldier! Meow!
