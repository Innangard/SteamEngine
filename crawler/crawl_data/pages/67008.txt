Trampoline to Tomorrow is a climbing game played in short runs: each run lasts a few minutes, and each run sends you into the next one a little stronger. Bounce, aim, fall You steer the slime left and right. Land on the mat's sweet spot, thread the rings, read the wind and the gravity. Boost rings push you upward, rings taken back to back grow your multiplier, and a glowing guide line shows the way. Every meter gained up high is more money, and every coin is more power for the next run. Cards Climbing raises your level, and every level deals you a hand of cards to pick from; the card you pick is permanent. Catch a card pack drifting in the air and you flip a card: its rarity decides how strong the slime's dance will be. Keep the rhythm, multiply the reward. The upgrade tree When a run ends, go back to the vault and open a branch: a stronger mat, a wider sweet spot, more rings, longer runs. Each branch also decides what your cards can improve. Eighty-one nodes, one question: how far this time? The escape line Every stop has an escape line. The moment you cross it, gravity lets go, everything goes quiet, and the route stretches on to the next stop.
