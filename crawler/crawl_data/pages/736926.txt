Forget the classic top-down management — here, you command your servants directly, leading them from the front lines. Build your own kingdom, erect mighty fortresses, and raise an army to withstand hordes of enemies. During your wanderings, you will encounter multiple magical creatures that you can tame to serve as combat units or even mounts. Riding a dragon over the battlefield or storming enemy walls on a giant elephant will completely change the tide of war. Explore wild lands, discover mystical artifacts, and decide the fate of mankind! 🛡️ First-Person RTS – Deeply immersive gameplay with direct, hands-on control of your units in battle. 🏰 Local Base Building – Construct walls, defensive towers, farms, and barracks to secure your domain. 🌍 Global Expansion – Build your kingdom on the global map by establishing villages, mines, sawmills, and plantations. ⏳ Full Real-Time World – Time never stops, forcing you to think and act quickly both in local skirmishes and on the global stage. 🎲 Endless Replayability – Generate unique sandbox maps with a wide variety of customizable options. ⚔️ Hiring & Progression – Recruit diverse troops, perform upgrades, and unlock brand-new units as you progress through the game. 👹 Epic Battles – Fight off massive hordes of aggressive monsters and defeat powerful, challenging bosses. 🐉 Unique Mounts – Ride Dragons, Elephants, Men??? 🐍 Creature Taming – Bend wild beasts to your will, including giant Golems, deadly Spiders, and massive Snakes. 🎭 Avatar System – Take full control of your army by stepping into the shoes of any unit to use them as your personal avatar. 👥 Local Co-op (Split-Screen) – Play with a friend! Player 1 acts as the ruler, while Player 2 plays as a loyal companion who can carry the king, gather resources, heal units, and fight side-by-side in battle. 🔧 About the Developer: This game is entirely created by a single independent developer.
