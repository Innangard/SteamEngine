The Christmas market is almost ready to open... except for one tiny problem: everything is a complete mess! Thousands of gifts, sweets, decorations, fruits, toys, and festive treats are scattered across the market. The stalls need to be restocked, the presents need to be sorted, and every item needs to find its proper place before the Christmas crowds arrive. Roll up your sleeves, grab your friends, and bring some holiday order to the chaos! A CHRISTMAS MARKET TO TIDY Explore a bustling Christmas market filled with cozy stalls, snowy streets, and thousands of items waiting to be sorted. Gift Stalls: Sort presents of all shapes and sizes, organize boxes, toys, ribbons, and festive surprises. Sweet Treats: Put chocolates, candies, cookies, pastries, and other Christmas goodies back where they belong. Festive Market: Organize fruits, decorations, handmade goods, ornaments, and everything else you'll find at a Christmas market. Every area has its own collection of items and its own sorting challenges. The more you tidy, the more beautiful the Christmas market becomes! PICK UP, SORT & ORGANIZE Pick up objects, carry them around, toss them across the market, or carefully place them exactly where they belong. Thousands of individual items can be scattered throughout the market, creating a satisfying mess for you and your friends to clean up. Some objects are small and easy to carry. Others are bulky, awkward, or just waiting to cause trouble when you throw them across a stall. And remember: you can always make the mess worse before you make it better. COOPERATIVE CHRISTMAS CLEAN-UP Play solo or bring your friends along for some chaotic holiday teamwork. Split up and tackle different stalls, work together to move larger objects, or simply throw presents at each other when nobody is looking. Whether you're carefully organizing every shelf or creating an even bigger mess with your friends, there's always something to tidy. MAKE THE MARKET MERRY AGAIN Watch the Christmas market transform as you work. From scattered boxes and overturned stalls to beautifully organized displays, every completed area brings the market one step closer to opening night. Untidy → Organized → Christmas-ready! FEATURES Satisfying Sorting Gameplay: Pick up, carry, throw, and organize thousands of festive items. A Cozy Christmas Setting: Snowy streets, glowing lights, decorated stalls, Christmas trees, sweets, gifts, and all the holiday atmosphere. Cooperative Clean-Up: Play solo or team up with friends. Chaotic Physics: Throw things, knock things over, and occasionally create an even bigger mess. Thousands of Unique Items: Gifts, candies, fruits, decorations, toys, treats, and much more. A Satisfying Transformation: Turn a chaotic Christmas market into a beautifully organized holiday wonderland.
