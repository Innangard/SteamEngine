View Community Hub About This Game Core mechanics: Combat system " Click-and-Hold " Upgrade your character Variety of enemies More than 129+ different quests Dozens of living NPCs with their own history Interesting and exciting plot Control two heroes throughout the game 4 different locations with their own puzzles Abandoned dungeons with puzzles and monsters Stylish, hand-drawn pixel art What is Hammer Of Pain? In Hammer Of Pain , you step into the worn boots of an exhausted father, left alone in a world that has forgotten its history and plunged into darkness. This pixelated 2D platformer immerses you into the grim corners of the Middle Ages, where the once vibrant spark of life has been replaced by the cold shadow of madness. Your daughter has vanished into the darkness of this world, leaving behind nothing but echoes of her memories. You are to navigate through myriad trials to decipher her fate and unravel the ghostly threads of history that led to the collapse of kingdoms. Each step you take will be an imprint on the bloody annals of history, each leap - a desperate reach for hope in the abyss of despair. You'll face intricate puzzles, intriguing quests, and clashes with creatures dwelling in this insane world. You are to uncover the mystery of this world, find your daughter, and regain peace once more. In Hammer Of Pain , every step forward is a stride towards truth, every hammer strike is a shout in silence, every sigh is a prayer in the quietude. Welcome to Hammer Of Pain - a journey awaits you! Combat system "Click-and-Hold" Learn to master your strikes. Hold the key and release it at the moment when the world stands still in anticipation. Your blows can shatter stone and cleave the wind, obliterating all enemies in your path. But power is only one side of the coin. In a world where danger lurks at every turn, the ability to react swiftly and dodge at the decisive moment becomes the key to survival. Evade enemy strikes, glide smoothly between the streams of arrows, avoid traps. Variety of enemies Embark on a journey where you will face a diverse world of creatures, from the undead lurking in the shadows to bandits roaming the wilderness. You'll encounter forest beasts whose eyes glow with wild power and survival instinct. Magical beasts that lurk in the depths of forgotten places. Plants that threaten even the bravest traveler. In each encounter, each confrontation you will learn your enemy , learning their weaknesses and strengths . You will have to apply all your art and skill as a blacksmith and a father. In each battle, you will improve your skills, learning more and more about this world and those who inhabit it. On this journey, you will face challenges that will force you to overcome yourself. Art Style Meet a world where every pixel has been carefully hand-drawn, bringing Hammer Of Pain's unique style to life. Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of the Middle Ages. Every pixel reflects the hard work, love and passion put into this game. Here you will find a world where light and darkness battle for dominance, creating extraordinarily colorful and vivid scenes. Soak up this world where the smallest details matter. Here every pixel comes alive, adding its own touch to the multifaceted palette of Hammer Of Pain . It's not just a game, it's a canvas on which a gripping story opens up, and you become its protagonist. Enjoy every moment, every glance, every step in this uniquely hand-painted world. History and Plot Enter a world where history and legend intertwine into one, and immerse yourself in the lives of the blacksmith Silver and his daughter Nora . They are the soul of this game, lively and vivid characters, their stories and challenges mesmerizing and their fates touching the deepest strings of the soul. Let every step you take be a new spin on this incredible story. Unravel the tangle of mysteries of the kingdoms, look into the most hidden corners of the lands of Arkon and Riven . Here every stone, every leaf, tells its own story, and every one of them is waiting for you to uncover it. Discover a world where myth and reality merge into one, where past and present are closely intertwined. Discover the secrets, drowned in time. In the world of Hammer Of Pain each moment opens a new chapter, each step leads to new discoveries. Are you ready to walk this path and discover the full extent of Silver and Nora's story? Upgrading your hero Your journey is not just a journey, but a constant self-improvement. In Hammer Of Pain you will face incredible challenges that will require your quick reflexes and keen intellect. You'll engage in upgrades and enhancements, improving your hero . Collect valuables scattered throughout the game's world. Allocate these resources wisely, invest them in the development of your hero, honing his skills and improving his abilities. In this world, every item, every resource has its value. But only your decisions determine how you will use those values. Be wise, be determined. Your skills, your power, your destiny are all in your hands. In the world of Hammer Of Pain , you are the smith of your own destiny. We are a small, passionate team working tirelessly on this game. Each day, we pour our love and dedication into this project, fueled by our deep affection for the world we're creating.
