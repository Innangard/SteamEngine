Welcome to Dino Dome, a colourful indoor world where thousands of dinosaur toys are waiting to find their homes. Dino Dome: Tidy Up! is a single-player, first-person organising game about turning a mountain of scattered toys into a collection you can be proud of. Pick up a dinosaur, find where it belongs, and watch the mess disappear one satisfying placement at a time. Whether you enjoy carefully finishing one cupboard or gathering matching toys from across the dome, you decide how to tackle the tidy-up. Find a home for every dinosaur There are 4,096 dinosaur toys to put away. Look at their appearance and outfits, follow the collection labels, and bring matching toys together in the correct cupboards. Each row holds eight toys. Fill it with the right dinosaur type to complete the row, then keep going until the whole cupboard is finished. Your dinosaurs stay on display, so every completed row becomes a visible part of your progress. Put something in the wrong place? You can still set it down, but incorrect placements do not count towards completion or earn money. A red row label helps you spot the mistake. Pick the toy back up and return it to its proper home—nothing is permanently stuck. Discover themed collections Classic Dinosaurs are only the beginning. Explore collections including Jungle Expedition, Volcano Rescue, Pirate Dinosaurs, Space Sauruses, Dino Knights, and Bedtime Babies. Across 32 categories and 256 dinosaur-and-outfit combinations , familiar prehistoric shapes take on playful personalities. There are explorers to gather, pirates to reunite, and sleepy little dinosaurs to put away. Themed decorations and labelled sections give each collection its own corner of the dome. As you organise, scattered favourites become neatly arranged groups. Make every trip count Start by carrying one toy at a time. Correct sorting earns in-game money that you can spend on upgrades at reception: Ranger Satchel: Increase your carrying capacity one toy at a time, up to 15. Expedition Knee Pads: Unlock crouching to investigate low spaces. Trailblazer Boots: Unlock sprinting to travel across the dome more quickly. Improved Smell: Temporarily highlight loose toys matching the dinosaur selected in your hands. Dino Tracker: Follow a trail of dinosaur footprints towards a matching loose toy. Search abilities have cooldowns, so choose when to use a little help and when to follow your own eye for a familiar dinosaur.
