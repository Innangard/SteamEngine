View Community Hub About This Game Description: Embark on an epic journey as an adventurer in " Choice Matters ," a unique resource management card game where every decision shapes your destiny. Navigate through a series of events, manage your resources wisely, and uncover the unique stories of each character you play. Will you make the right choices to reach the end of your adventure, or will you falter and face the consequences? Key Features: Decision Making: Encounter a variety of events and make crucial decisions that will impact your journey. Choose wisely to ensure your survival and success. Resource Management: Balance your resources carefully to avoid dire situations. Every choice you make affects your available resources. Unique Stories: Play as different characters, each with their own distinct storyline and quests. Unlock new characters and delve deeper into their adventures. Rogue-like Elements: Experience a challenging and dynamic gameplay experience where each run offers new encounters and opportunities. 80+ Unique Encounters: Discover and navigate over 80 unique events, each offering different challenges and rewards. 30+ Relics: Collect and utilize over 30 powerful relics to aid you in your journey. Unique Character Stories: Explore the unique backstories and quests of five different characters, each with their own narrative and challenges. Important Note: Please note that "Choice Matters" is still in development. We are committed to keeping the game updated with new relics, stories, and encounters. If future updates create any issues, please contact us directly for support. About NUE GAMES: NUE GAMES is dedicated to creating engaging and innovative gaming experiences. With "Choice Matters," we bring a fresh take on resource management and deck-building, offering players an adventure full of choices and consequences.
