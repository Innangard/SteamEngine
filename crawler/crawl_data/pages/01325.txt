Every day, lost items are brought to your warehouse. Your job is to help people get them back! Pick up a form with a detailed description, find the lost item, and return it to its owner. That's gonna be easy, right? CAN I BUILD IN THIS GAME? Sooner or later, you will realize that without proper organization of your workplace, everything you do is pointless. That's why you have to take care of it yourself! Arrange your warehouse with various shelves and containers so that it makes your job easier. Office space is no less important! What would it be without the ability to set your favorite cat as wallpaper or plaster the entire office with its photos? Does it serve any purpose? No, but it's fun! JUST ADD MULTIPLAYER BRO I already did! If you think working at LOST & FOUND is chaotic, wait until you discover arcade mode.
