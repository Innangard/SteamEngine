Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We believe Early Access is the best way to make the game as fun and well-balanced as possible. Community feedback is essential to refining gameplay, improving balance, and adding meaningful content. During development of the demo, we maintained a consistent update schedule, releasing new versions several times per month. This experience has shown us that we can sustain a solid development pace throughout Early Access, even though we’re only a two-person team. We plan to expand the online co-op mode to support up to 3 players during this period. Our goal is to use Early Access to: - Gather feedback and implement community suggestions - Continuously improve gameplay balance and overall fun - Add new content and features based on player priorities” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We are aiming for around 5–6 months of Early Access, though this duration may be adjusted based on the needs of the game and the feedback we receive from players.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to add more content and improvements  to the full version to expand gameplay depth, variety, and replayability, such as: - new skills, Soulbounds, items, and augments - Additional Guardians - More difficulty modifiers - Greater build variety - Improved synergy between characters and Guardians - A fourth biome with a final boss - New enemies and levels - Increased replayability - Overall polish across UI, sound effects, and gameplay And of course, a few surprises along the way!” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current version already offers a complete core experience with plenty of content and replayability. It includes: - 4 Guardians - Permanent Guardian progression - 4 playable characters - 18 enemies - 150+ skills to unlock - 9 Soulbounds (character meta-progression) - 40+ items - 3 biomes - 24 levels - 4 currencies - 16 story quests - 30 achievements - resonance system (difficulty modifiers) This version represents a solid foundation for the full game, and we’ll continue expanding and refining it throughout Early Access.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. The price will gradually increase as new content and features are added throughout Early Access to reflect the game’s growing scope and quality.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We actively gather player feedback and bug reports through the in-game report tool, our Discord server, and the Steam Community Hub. Community input plays a key role in shaping the game. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game For 1-2 Players The deeper you go into the Haunted Swamp , the more you feel Witch Maeve’s influence. She has bent the creatures here to her will , turning them into cursed abominations . If you want to break free, you’ll have to become stronger. Take control of a water spirit in this co-op action roguelite for one or two players . Explore a haunted swamp where decay and beauty intertwine, battle relentless enemies, and adapt to ever-changing challenges while evolving your powers with every run. Get ready for thrilling boss battles that will put your skills to the test. Summon and command Guardians , natural turrets that fight alongside you, turning the battlefield into a dynamic mix of strategy and chaos. Experiment with unique abilities, adapt your tactics on the fly, or combine powers with a friend to overcome increasingly dangerous threats as the swamp itself fights back. Guardians: Deploy and modify nature’s guardians to change the course of the fight Unique Playable Heroes: Each with their own weapons and special abilities Skills & Items : Stack absurd builds, experiment with upgrades, and break the game in the best way Permanent Progression : Improve your character and guardians over time, unlocking new powers and upgrades that carry across runs Online Co-op (1 or 2 players) : Team up with a friend and fight through the chaos together Endless Replayability : Every run brings new enemies , challenges, and surprises Difficulty System: An unlockable difficulty system keeps every run fresh and challenging once you finish the game Join our Discord: Share feedback, get updates, and be part of the development.
