View Community Hub About This Content Set out on a new journey with The Wandering Village: The Last Leviathan ! Build your settlement on Levi, the all-new marine creature , develop your relationship and take care of its unique needs or advantage of its new resources. Key features New creature : Levi emerges from the sea and sets the stage for a new adventure. New mechanics : Maintain Levi's moisture levels while also making sure your villagers have enough water. New resources : The ecosystem on Levi's back provides your villagers with new resources and forces them to improvise with old ones. New buildings : Over a dozen new buildings for you to construct such as the Drying Shack, the Kiln and more. New techtree : A techtree incorporating the new buildings and resources will turn the way you progress through the game on its head.
