Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game A tropical island. A villa full of valuables. And you, armed with a pair of scissors. Money Burner is a first-person incremental game where destroying valuables earns you the means to destroy even more. Start small, cutting through banknotes, then turn your rewards into new weapons and upgrades. Every material calls for a different approach. Slice through paper, bring a hammer down on gold, or trade careful cuts for a stream of fire. Tougher treasures demand better tools: improve your damage and penetration, then return to smash what once stood in your way. Visit the workshop to expand your arsenal. Scissors and hammers are just the beginning: flamethrowers, saws and a massive laser cannon let you make an increasingly extravagant mess. Break through sealed passages and explore more rooms of the villa, finding new valuables to put your upgrades to the test. Take apart a pile one piece at a time, or set an entire house built from money ablaze. Watch paper split, metal crack and burnt scraps drift away as a room full of riches becomes your next round of upgrades. How much destruction can you get out of one little pair of scissors?
