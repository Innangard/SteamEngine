Your cursor stays at the center of the action as you actively destroy, collect, and grow stronger every run. Unlock upgrades that transform your build with chain lightning, orbiting orbs, explosions, beams, healing effects, and powerful chain reactions. Each evolution layer expands your possibilities and pushes your scaling further. Spawn helper units, grow biomass-producing Minicores, and maintain your evolving network to accelerate your progression. Simple mouse-only controls, constant active gameplay, and steady progression designed for both short sessions and long optimization runs.
