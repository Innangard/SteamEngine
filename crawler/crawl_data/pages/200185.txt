Dial tone. Cassette decks. Sabotage. Suitcase decks. Brainbox injectors. Quadrilateral Cowboy is a first-person adventure in a cyberpunk world. Tread lightly through security systems with your hacking deck and grey-market equipment. With top-of-the-line hardware like this, it means just one thing: you answer only to the highest bidder. 🏆 Winner of the Independent Games Festival Seumas McNally Grand Prize 🏆 Winner of the Independent Games Festival Excellence in Design award 🏆 Winner of the IndieCade Grand Jury award PLAY YOUR WAY Do you want to infiltrate quietly, low and slow? Or smash and grab, loud and fast? The choice is yours. Each heist is a systemic clockworld world of security systems waiting to be broken. A HACKABLE WORLD Type commands into our intuitive hacking interface to hijack doors, weapons, cameras, robots, and more. Subvert burglar alarms, master your hacking deck, and devise your own unique route like a smooth operator. TOOLS OF THE TRADE Use your Deck to hack. Blast yourself across the sky with the Launcher Pad. Remotely control the high-powered rifle in the Autocase. Pilot the tiny but mighty Weevil robot. The only limit is your creativity. GET IMMERSED Explore the retro-futuristic world of Quadrilateral Cowboy and experience the many highs and lows of your cyber-heist crew. Saddle up, cowboy.
