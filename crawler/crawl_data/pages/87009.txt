Content For This Game Browse all (1) Recommended Echoes of the Architects - Supporter Pack $4.99 $4.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Gameplay Echoes of the Architects is a real-time strategy game that rewards creativity, strategic depth, and adaptability. Victory lies in your ability to design and deploy customizable, programmable units to dominate the battlefield. At its heart is a robust unit customization system, letting you build armies tailored to your playstyle. Combine modules like locomotion systems, weapons, and repair tools, and define your units’ roles—whether gathering resources, defending key points, or executing precision strikes to outflank enemies. Battles are fast-paced and dynamic, demanding sharp strategic thinking and quick adaptation. With programmable units capable of executing complex maneuvers autonomously, you can focus on your tactics rather than high actions-per-minute gameplay. As you advance, unlock new modules, unit classes, and tactical options, continuously evolving your forces. Explore diverse planets, each with unique environments and challenges, as you expand the fight across the galaxy. In Echoes of the Architects, every decision matters. Success depends on how you configure your army, time your deployments, and outmaneuver your opponents in the relentless chaos of war. Key Features Design with Precision : Craft modular, programmable units, each tailored to play a specific role in your customizable army. Shape your strategy, one unit at a time. Adapt Strategically : Analyze your opponent's strengths and weaknesses to deploy the perfect combination of units for any challenge. Flexibility is your greatest weapon. Deploy Tactically : Master the art of timing and positioning in dynamic skirmishes. Outsmart your enemies with clever builds and adaptable tactics. Dominate the Battlefield : Unlock powerful modules and advanced tactics to upgrade your army, overcoming tougher foes and securing victory through superior innovation. Asynchronous Multiplayer : Enjoy stress-free gameplay with pausable battles and unlimited, always-on replayability Story The galaxy was ours to conquer—or so we believed. In our unyielding quest for dominance, humanity delved too deeply into the void, awakening a force beyond comprehension: the Architects. Silent, enigmatic, and devastatingly efficient, they seized our greatest creations—turning humanity’s own advanced technologies into instruments of annihilation. Entire worlds fell in mere moments, their skies darkened by the machines we once called progress. Now, you stand as one of the last sparks of resistance, seeking shelter on a scarred and broken Earth—a planet consumed by the wars and greed of its past. With only the remnants of human ingenuity at your disposal, you command the battlefield from a distance, deploying what’s left of our scattered forces. Every battle is a desperate gamble, a flicker of hope against the Architects’ impenetrable grip. But there’s something more—whispers buried deep in the ruins of our shattered worlds. Clues that hint at the true nature of the Architects, their purpose, and why they were waiting for us to awaken them. Understanding them might be the key to victory—or a revelation too dangerous to confront. This is our last chance to reclaim the galaxy, dismantle the Architects’ empire, and unearth the secrets of their origin. The final war begins, and humanity’s fate rests in your hands.
