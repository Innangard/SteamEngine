View Community Hub Reviews “...combat twists that transform Hotline Miami's crests and lulls of situational disempowerment into a sort of weaving tactical bloodlust.” Rock Paper Shotgun “Games like these need to play immaculately, and I can confidently say that it does just that.” Fingerguns About This Game SLAUGHTER VOID is a brutal  heavy metal dark fantasy slasher where speedrunning meets blood-spattered dark fantasy. Built around tight handcrafted levels, lethal combat, and constant forward pressure , it throws you into a savage run through cults, beastmen, necromancers, and broken realms inspired by 80s arcade action and retro fantasy paperbacks. In the Void, blood is cheap, but Bones are the only currency that matters. Violence rewards you with the remains of the fallen, and the faster your clearence, the richer the harvest. Return your spoils to the Astrarium to trade with the Smith , the Huntress, and the Twins to unlock the sacred instruments of slaughter. Carve through packed enemies in wide, bloody sweeps, crush anything in your path with brutal forward charges. Hook into prey and drag yourself straight into the kill before the first body hits the floor. Secondary weapons let you kill beyond arm’s reach , while relics change what your body can do: stronger kicks, reflected shots, explosive force, and second chances when the Void should have taken you. The right build does not just help you survive - it lets you reach maximum slaughter. You die in one hit. Most things you fight do too . The difference is who reaches the killing blow first. Stack kills quickly enough and your speed condenses into momentum, a sliver of grace that eats the next fatal blow for you. Lose it, and you are back to the same fragile thing the enemies are.  However, as long as your style and execution is flawless the gods won't let you die and you can truly be INVINCIBLE .
