Set sail on a thrilling, high-seas adventure like no other, where time itself bows to your command. Inspired by the likes of Quicksilver, The Flash, and that one time Fry drank a bit too much coffee, "You Have No Time" takes you on a fast-paced pirate odyssey, where every moment is a treasure in the making. About In this swashbuckling action puzzle game, your wits and creativity are your most valuable assets. Prepare to embark on a quest where every choice matters and every challenge offers multiple paths to success. Solve puzzles by slowing the flow of time itself, all the while dodging bullets and navigating perilous explosions with cunning strategies. The more you delve into this time-bending adventure, the more it rewards your cunning and curiosity. Each level is a sandbox of possibilities, beckoning you to explore, experiment, and surpass your own limits. What Awaits Ye? Captain Silva: Meet our daring rogue, a swashbuckler extraordinaire with a knack for pilfering treasure. Colonial Foes: A crew of colonial soldiers stands between you and fortune, and they're not too keen on your looting. The Vengeful Kraken: Beware the deep, for the Kraken hungers for what's rightfully his, and he's not taking any prisoners. Over 40 Puzzles: Immerse yourself in a seafaring voyage with more than 40 intriguing puzzles that'll keep your curiosity afloat. Leaderboards: Prove your mettle by ascending the ranks and showcasing your piratical prowess. Level Editor: Craft your own treacherous trials and share them with your fellow mariners. Steam Workshop: Dive into a treasure trove of community-created content, expanding the adventure beyond the horizon. The Pirate's Code Now let me divulge the pirate's code for this adventure: You must strategically maneuver enemies, relocate crates, and rescue your crew from the onslaught of bullets and explosive mayhem. Ah, but the true mark of a cunning corsair lies in the pursuit of hidden treasure, stowed away behind locked doors. Can you muster the skill to claim these glittering riches? Get ready to experience time in a way you never have before. The open sea awaits, and the winds of destiny are at your back. "You Have No Time" is your ticket to a world where wit and daring shape the story, and where every second counts. Hoist the anchor, and set your course for adventure!
