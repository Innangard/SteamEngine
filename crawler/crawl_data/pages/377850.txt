Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Mazey Village is a game centered around exploration, village building, and cozy interactions, and we want to shape that experience together with the community. Early Access allows us to expand the world while gathering feedback on gameplay systems, progression, customization, and overall making sure it's an enjoyable player experience. Community feedback will help us improve existing features, polish the game, and guide future content as we continue building the world of Mazey Village.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Mazey Village to remain in Early Access for around 1–2 years. During this time, we plan to expand the game with additional content, features, polish, and at least two brand-new biomes for players to explore. Development timelines may change depending on community feedback and the scope of planned updates.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version of Mazey Village is planned to include: - Additional biomes and maze regions - More villagers, quests, critters, and interactions - Expanded customization and decoration options - More puzzles, secrets, and activities - Additional polish, balancing, and quality-of-life improvements - A more complete village restoration experience and story progression Our goal is for the full release to feel like a larger, richer, and more polished version of the core experience already available in Early Access.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already contains the full core gameplay experience of Mazey Village. Players can explore cozy maze regions, recover lost belongings, restore homes, decorate the village, catch critters, fish, solve puzzles, and meet villagers throughout the world. While the game is already fully playable and regularly updated, we plan to continue expanding Mazey Village with additional biomes, content, features, polish, and quality-of-life improvements during Early Access.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. We plan for the price to increase as major content and features are added throughout Early Access and the game moves closer to full release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We intend to use a range of different methods to gather player feedback and data throughout the early access period. These include engaging with users directly on our official communication channels and our Steam forum. Community feedback will play an important role throughout development. Explore nearby mazes of winding paths and hidden routes, recover what was lost, and help restore the village one step at a time. Explore Charming Worlds ⭐ Travel through peaceful forests, snowy paths, forgotten ruins, and cozy hidden areas filled with small stories and secrets. Every region holds lost items, friendly creatures, tiny critters, and small moments waiting to be discovered. ✏️ Fill your journal map as you journey through the world 🧩 Solve cozy puzzles using levers, lights, weights, portals, rotating paths and more. 🐾 Discover and catch adorable critters 🎣 Enjoy fishing, wandering, and relaxed exploration Restore & Customize Your Village 🏘️ Bring life back to the village by rebuilding homes, decorating freely, and shaping the town into your own creation. Every recovered item and restored house helps the community grow again. 🍃 Decorate interiors and outdoor spaces 🧱 Edit tiles and paths to design your village layout 🛒 Visit shops and collect furniture, decorations, and more 🏠 Restore homes and welcome villagers back A Living World Full of Cozy Moments 🌸 Mazey Village is filled with small, heartwarming interactions that make the world feel alive again.
