There is now a major difference in the aim of the game, as "noble" tiles have been replaced by "city" tiles. When a player meets the required conditions, they pick up a "city" tile. Their opponents must then do all they can to get their hands on the remaining cities before the end of the round, which signals the end of the game. Become the only one to conquer a city or take over a city of greater value than your opponent's to win the game!
