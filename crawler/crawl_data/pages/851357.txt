Especially for freight traffic, this route has steadily increased in importance over the last twenty years. Experience the special features of the line in this scenario pack. Drive a TGV from Strasbourg to Karlsruhe and experience French high-speed technology on German tracks. Even a TGV has technical challenges from time to time, so you must step in as the driver of an ICE 3 M and bring your passengers to their destination with as few delays as possible. Since this is one of the most important freight lines in Europe, you will experience in this scenario pack what it means to move in the tight rhythm of the line - if it weren't for the regional train in front of you... You will also jump into the driver's cab of a BR146 and take your passengers along the stretch to Offenburg in the morning rush hour.
