About This Game Uncover the truth behind every crime scene Step into the role of a detective in DETECTIVE Scene Crime , a challenging mystery game where you’ll investigate five gripping crime scenes. Each level presents clues, testimonies, and puzzles to analyze carefully in your quest for the truth. Do you have what it takes to solve them all? Five Unique Crime Scenes THE HOSPITAL A person has died in a hospital. Was it a tragic case of medical negligence or a calculated murder? THE SUBWAY A subway car smeared with blood after an altercation. Whose blood is it? How many people were involved? THE ROAD A highway crash with three vehicles and four fatalities. The pieces don’t add up... What really happened? SUPERMARKET A shooting at a supermarket leaves two dead. Was it a random act or a crime with a hidden motive? THE HOTEL A wedding turns deadly. Two families, multiple victims, and a killer on the loose. Can you unravel the chaos? An Immersive Detective Experience Grab a pen and paper to jot down clues, connect the dots, and develop your theories. Examine every detail, as even the smallest hint could be the key to solving the case. Key Features Five unique levels, each with its own captivating story and twists. A compelling narrative that keeps you on edge until the very end. Designed for mystery lovers and fans of investigative gameplay. Discover what happened, who did it, and why. Do you dare to step into the detective’s shoes? In DETECTIVE Scene Crime, the truth is always within reach... if you know where to look.
