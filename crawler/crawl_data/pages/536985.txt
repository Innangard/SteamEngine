View Community Hub About This Game Sokocat: Castaway is a cozy 3D Sokoban-style puzzle adventure with an isometric view, where you play as a shipwrecked kitty exploring a mysterious island filled with secrets, varied biomes, and relaxing brain teasers. After washing ashore on a forgotten island, your journey begins across sunny beaches , lush forests , dark caves , and the ruins of an ancient temple . Each area introduces new puzzle ideas, hidden paths, and rewarding discoveries. Push boxes, interact with the environment, and think a few steps ahead to solve handcrafted challenges. Every puzzle is designed to feel thoughtful and satisfying, while the island itself invites you to slow down, explore, and enjoy the atmosphere. There are no enemies, no combat, and no time pressure — just pure puzzle solving and peaceful exploration. Sokocat: Castaway is perfect for players who enjoy cozy games, relaxing adventures, and smart environmental puzzles without stress. Along the way, you can discover hidden collectibles , complete optional challenges , and unlock achievements . Curious players who pay attention to every corner of the island will always find something extra.
