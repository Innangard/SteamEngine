From the creators of Stifled comes Muffled Warfare, a brave new entry into the echolocation-game genre. Muffled Warfare is a multiplayer first-person echolocation shooter where everyone is blind and uses sounds to SEE , SHOOT and SILENCE their competition. You've heard of that blind superhero that dresses up like a devil in red right? Yea, think that, but with guns. ECHOLOCATION - Use sound to SEE, SHOOT and SILENCE the competition.
