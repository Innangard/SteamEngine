View Community Hub About This Game The main character of the game is a journalist named Konstantin, who specializes in the field of paranormal phenomena and investigates various mysterious cases. Especially those related to aliens and the search for extraterrestrial intelligent life. One day Konstantin himself became the hero of an amazing and unusual story... Embark on a fantastic journey full of mysteries and riddles. Become a hero who has been to extraordinary, amazing places and experienced very unusual adventures. Get acquainted with the unique feature of the game, which is the so-called "System of Thoughts" - a kind of generator of quests and various thoughts located in the head of the main character, which directs and motivates the player to explore the outside world. Learn how to defend yourself from various dangers, uncover the secrets of the plot and find answers to many questions that the mysterious story of the game asks. Explore fantastic devices that are not the creations of human hands. Witness various anomalies and mysterious natural phenomena. Use alien technologies and devices to achieve important goals. Meet and interact with unusual creatures of the game world. Fill your head with various thoughts, exploring the game world, and make your plans come true. Overcome various obstacles and traps that get in your way. Solve various puzzles using your logic, observation and wit. On this difficult path, going through all the tests, try not to die and not to injure the psyche with a stream of negative thoughts. Will you be able to solve all the mysteries, overcome all difficulties and find a way home by controlling the mental state of the hero? You will find out the answer to this question by playing the game “Mental State. Game One”. Game features: A combination of different game genres An unusual and mystery atmosphere A cryptic, shrouded in secrets plot An unusual "System of thoughts" that affects the mental state of the hero and the gameplay as a whole Diverse interaction with objects and fantastic devices Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Battles with monsters.
