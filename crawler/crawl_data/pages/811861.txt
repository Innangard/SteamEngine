Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Can you make a profit from leasing a pond and draining it for fish? Hard to say! In A Pond Hunt , you start from scratch—draining muddy puddles by the riverbank to earn your first pot of gold. Then, level up and lease real fish ponds in the countryside, from classic grass carp ponds and monster-sized lakes to snapping turtle ponds ! Step by step, you'll grow your empire, bigger and stronger. Search for fish-filled wild puddles, drain the water, and catch the fish! Lease farmed fish ponds and try to turn a profit—but a poor catch could mean a heavy loss. With over a dozen different fish ponds to choose from—ranging from crucian carp, grass carp, and common carp, to turtles, snapping turtles, and the ultimate final challenge: Monster Lake! Your ultimate challenge? The abyssal Dragon Pool, where legendary treasures await the fearless—if you dare to drain it dry. What? Loch Ness? Is there really a monster in there? Drain it and you'll find out the answer! 🎮 Co-op draining – Invite friends to pump water faster and tackle bigger ponds together!
