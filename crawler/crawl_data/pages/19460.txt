View Community Hub About This Game One pogo stick. One ridiculously jacked animal. Absolutely no dignity. Pogo Together is a 1-4 player physics-based climbing game where you and your friends take control of absurdly muscular animals and attempt to bounce your way to the top. SUFFER TOGETHER Bring your friends along and try to make it to the top together. Work together through difficult sections and climb as a group (or kick someone off a platform and send their progress down with them). Play nice or make things worse. It’s up to you. Prefer to suffer in peace? You can play solo too. MASTER THE POGO Learn a physics-driven movement system where balance, timing, momentum, and control determine whether you stick the landing or go flying back down. The controls are simple. Getting good at them is another story. MEET THE CREW Choose from a cast of bizarre, suspiciously jacked animals, each equally unqualified to operate a pogo stick. Unlock new characters as you play and find your favorite way to look ridiculous while falling from extreme heights. ONE MISTAKE CAN COST EVERYTHING Climb through increasingly dangerous environments filled with tiny platforms, moving obstacles, traps, shortcuts, and jumps that looked much easier from below. There are no stairs. There is only pogo.
