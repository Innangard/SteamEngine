Engage in action-packed combat, where the exciting and dynamic spell casting combo system is at the very core, find mythical items, and fight epic bosses and elite monsters on your journey to escape the evil wizard's lair without dying even once! Demo features The demo features 5 levels, where players can get a good grasp of the game, including an epic boss fight to finish off the demo. The progress of the game will not carry over into the full game. Engage in action-packed dynamic combat Combine abilities into powerful attacks - for instance the Fireball and Shield turns into a Fire Shield , while the Fireball and Arcane Sphere turns into the Fire Globe . Add another Fireball and you fire off a devastating Meteor : Explore Margoq's devious lair Find your way out of a procedurally generated lair filled with deadly traps & horrifying monsters, by completing the procedurally generated quests to unlock the door to the next floor. Every floor is randomly cursed by Margoq. This means that each floor is a unique combination of level layout, curse and quest, making sure that you never play the same level twice! Find tons of mythical loot to become a powerhouse Slay monsters, break vases or find your way to the many hidden treasure chests found in the depths of the lair, where you can find mythical loot that makes you even more powerful. Encounter epic boss battles Throughout the lair, you'll meet difficult boss encounters that needs to be defeated before you can continue. All of which are powerful lieutenants of Margoq, and of course the demonic wizard himself. They will pose a great challenge for any one in their path. Random elite and super elite monsters But it's not just the bigger boss fights that might be difficult for the adventures. All minions in the lair can be an elite or super elite version of themselves, posing great risks to anyone not giving them the proper respect. Permadeath and metaprogression power system Every death in Margoq's Lair is permanent, however you earn souls during each run which can be used to gain perks in-between each playthrough, making you progressively more powerful, enabling you to get further into the lair.
