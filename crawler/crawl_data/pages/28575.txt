Play through the Prologue, where Aidan follows Sunny into the Labyrinth and discovers a terrifying, ever-shifting realm of nightmares. Then, experience a portion of Episode 1, where Aidan must escape the clutches of the Scarlet Church, who have taken the Genesis Configuration into their possession. This portion of the game introduces the basics of puzzle solving, crafting and combat while showing you a brief glimpse into the twisted machinations of the story’s main characters. The entire gameplay experience, including Genesis Configuration powers, an arsenal of weaponry with their upgrades, as well as more complex puzzle solving can be found in the full game.
