We will NOT monetize the game at any point during Early Access. 2. We will NEVER monetize in-game cash or gold bars, they must be earned through play. 3. We will NEVER allow you to directly purchase camos. ( camos should show that a player has worked hard to unlock that item not how much money they have) Thankyou!
