Watch as limbs fly off from a single cut of your energy blade! You are Blink, a teleporting robot who is the newest “looprunner” gladiator to compete in the Crashloop, a deadly roguelike tournament to the death. Die and reawaken in an endless loop, leap over lava pools, avoid turret fire, and take down waves of enemy defendroids. Battle your fellow looprunners for a chance to fight the Captain in the Hyperdome, and claim his power for yourself! In between runs, get to know the other looprunners and learn the secrets of Crashloop. Take sides in their rivalries. Let your sword do the talking to win their favor. Defeat other looprunners to claim their Turbofists! Shoot flames, launch shurikens, slow time, summon zombies and more! Upgrade the turbofists with power from other defeated looprunners to become unstoppable. Swing lots of different weapons to find your favorite style . Two handed spears, a katana, an axe and shield? If you’re daring, fight with your bare metal fists! Feel the power of voxel destruction. Learn to wield your laser sword. The Hyperdome awaits !
