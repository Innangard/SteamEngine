A New Journey Begins A legendary treasure. A risky bet. Set sail into uncharted waters and uncover a mysterious island shrouded in secrets. What begins as a simple gamble soon leads to a brand-new chapter in your martial arts journey. New places, new faces, and new challenges await beyond the sea. Mystic Fan Arts Shrouded in mystery, a young girl arrives from afar—her fan a weapon, her pearl a wager. She seeks the one fated to help unlock the secrets of her past. As the long-lost Qingyun Fan Technique returns to the martial world, its graceful strikes hide lethal intent. Can you uncover its true power and master the art within? A Hidden Haven Beyond the chaos of the martial world lies a place of peace. On a secluded island, carve out your own paradise—manage your land, assign a steward to gather resources, and trade with passing merchants. For those still seeking action, a private arena awaits where you can assemble your team and engage in exhilarating tactical battles. A Fresh Journey Begins Head to the Inheritance Vault’s [Appearance] tab to unlock stylish new outfits for Shangguan Hong and Sima Ling using a small number of inheritance points. You’ll also gain two playful new mounts—the White Seal and the Gray Seal—to accompany you on your adventures.
