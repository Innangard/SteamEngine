Choose the Frostwood Ranger or the Runeblade and carve a path through a world gripped by endless night. Shape every run Follow branching routes, face events, and choose when to fight, recover, or take a risk. Collect cards, relics, and potions to build combinations that suit your hero and the threats ahead.
