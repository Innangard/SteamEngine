Simulator 3 - KDAL Airport $15.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Dallas Love Field (DAL) is the 31st-busiest airport in the United States and busiest medium-hub airport despite its federally mandated cap of 20 gates. In 2018, Love Field surpassed 8 million enplaned passengers for the first time. The airport is the major operating base of Southwest Airlines. Dallas Love Field has a single terminal with 20 gates, numbered 1-20. Alaska Airlines and Delta Air Lines lease one gate each, while Southwest leases the remaining eighteen gates. FEATURES * Detailed custom buildings with highly detailed textures * Airport taxi signs * Ground markings * Dynamic lighting, light sources individually placed, for a spectacular night view experience. * Most buildings remodeled with high detail * Beautiful background scenery In June 2022, the airport completed major renovations that included converting the old runway 18/36 into a taxiway and resurfacing Runway 13R/31L. The airport played an extensive role for the military back in World War I and World War II.
