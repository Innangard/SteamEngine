Προβολή κέντρου Κοινότητας Σχετικά με αυτό το παιχνίδι Konbini Cleanup is a cozy organizing simulator about putting a messy Japanese convenience store back in order. Sort 2,928 products across 15+ sections: drinks, noodles, candy, chips, and more. All at your own pace. A Relaxing Konbini Organizing Simulator The store is a mess and needs to be cleaned up. Return items to their shelves at your own pace, with no timers and no pressure. Pick up each product, find the right section, and place it under the correct price tag. That's all there is to it, just work through the store one shelf at a time. Earn upgrades as you play. Increase your movement speed and reach, and unlock a couple of unique abilities that make clearing the store faster. Empty racks fill with neat rows of onigiri, and the messy magazine stand settles into tidy stacks. Each finished section shows clear progress. Key Features Sort more than 2,900 products across 15+ konbini sections Simple gameplay: pick up items and put them back where they belong Skill upgrades for speed and reach, plus a few unique abilities No time limits and no game over.
