Set in a fantasy world, you, the player, are invoked as a protection deity by the queen to help her protect the land against a new threat. Lead your forces in an adventure spanning the whole continent, exploring mysterious and dangerous locations, and going to battle with your troops and your magic. There will always be four female companions by your side – as powerful as they are charming, helping you both in battle and with their wisdom. Build a deck that adapts to your playstyle and strategic approach, choosing between +300 unique cards. Every card has its own strengths, weaknesses and strategic value, so choose carefully to create the most powerful deck you can! Even though your troops will fight for themselves, it’s your tactical skill as a general that will make the difference: choose when and how to deploy your soldiers to make the most of their forces, and cast powerful spells and skills to turn the tide of battle. An army is as powerful as the logistics that sustain it! Build facilities that will allow you to gather resources and recruit soldiers for your armies to lay a solid foundation for your kingdom that will let you advance to new conquests. An adventure that will last hundreds of hours: with a procedurally generated map, every playthrough will be unique; you decide where to explore next, how to develop your territories and when (and how) to engage in battle. Four charming female characters as your companions. Enjoyable Roguelike map exploration and territory management gameplay. 300+ unique cards and over 100 collectibles. Turn-based strategic battles that combine auto-battler and card combat.
