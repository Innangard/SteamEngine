Forgotten histories buried at the foundation of existence resurface, some seeking the truth, and some asking questions that were never meant to be answered. As millennia pass and her power grows, she begins to question whether her fate was truly ever her own – or if it was chosen for her. As the ages pass, an unknown threat looms at the edge of reality.
