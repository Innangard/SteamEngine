Get ready to embark on an action-packed adventure where you stomp, launch, deflect and bash your way through Kutala, a land of demons, on a quest to defeat the Demon Lord Yalda and link Okko’s isolated world with the heavens! After 1,000 years of concealment, Okko is ready to wield the Mighty Fang and take down Kutala’s lords one by one. Defeat powerful foes, overcome obstacles and unleash your heart as Okko fights to fulfill an ancient oath. Enjoy meticulously hand-drawn 2D animations that bring Kutala’s characters to life in retro-inspired pixel art glory! Explore side-scrolling stages with precision platforming, link attacks to form devastating combos, send enemies flying and face down enormous bosses that require quick-thinking strategy! As capable as Okko may be, he won’t be alone in his quest. A colorful cast of allies will sell you valuable items, fill your belly and provide useful information at The Old Throne, a place of respite with its own buried secrets to uncover. Upgrade and unlock new abilities to give Okko an edge in combat, collect Deevols to unlock Underground challenge levels and more. A devilish world of discovery is at Okko’s fingertips! Game Features Vibrant, hand-drawn pixel art brings Okko’s world path of destruction to life as he explores the demon lands of Kutala. Captivating soundtrack composed by an industry veteran to immerse you in Okko’s epic journey! Focus Mode: consume Heart Energy and time Okko’s attacks to inflict extra damage on enemies and break their shields! Launch, Stomp, Bounce, Shoot, Deflect, and more, as you send enemies flying across the screen, pop them into the air, and link attacks to form devastating combos! Fill your belly, purchase goods, and chat up a colorful cast of characters at The Old Throne, a place of respite, while also uncovering the secrets of its depths. Expressive character portraits bring Okko's dialogue to life as the story unfolds! Upgrade Okko’s stats and unlock new abilities to give Okko an edge in combat and exploration! Collect Deevols to unlock Underground levels that put Okko's combat and platforming skills to the test!
