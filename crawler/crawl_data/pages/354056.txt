I need more Reverend like right now” Ruby Ranger About This Game From the twisted mind of TROMA Shorts Creator Emil Levin comes a new old-school first-person shooter paying homage to genre classics such as Blood, Duke Nukem 3D and Rise of the Triad. REVEREND puts you in the boots of a disillusioned former reverend, tasked with fighting back against the evil lurking beneath the church he once called home. Battle against Eldritch and Demonic beasts that would cripple the average mind to witness. Visit varied nightmarish locations ranging from crematoriums to flesh-pits. Use powerful and distinctive weaponry, including forbidden fruit which explode upon contact with the horrors surrounding you. Hunt for keys and scavenge for resources to keep you alive and get you closer to your goal. Also, keep an eye out for secrets, as easter eggs, resources and secret pathways make exploration a strong recommendation.
