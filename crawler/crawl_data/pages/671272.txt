View Community Hub About This Game Due to the contamination, an epidemic has broken out – zombie cats have taken over the entire town and are wreaking havoc. You are the last survivor, once a member of an elite cat sniper unit. The cat army has dropped ammunition supplies – these can be found in wooden crates. Your mission is to eliminate all infected cats and cleanse the city. Go, cat soldier! Meow! Find Kitties: Zombie Hunter is a hidden object game – the player's task is to find cats hidden on the map. The game features a zombie apocalypse style, but with a touch of humor. There are 200 zombie cats hiding in the ruins, and your task is to find them all. As a bonus, there are also 30 Halloween pumpkins scattered throughout the area. All graphics are cute & cozy and also hand-drawn.
