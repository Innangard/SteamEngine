The level revolves around the past experiences, imagination, and traumas of, as the name implies, the reclusive do-nothing Trevor. Or are they really like that? Perhaps there's more to this timid soul than meets the eye. Maybe you were way to quick to judge them. Do better. While this is the very first "level" you will enter in Videohole: Episode II, it is not exactly where you will begin the full game, however, the content contained in this demo is largely spoiler-free as far as the overarching story is concerned. Game Features A first-person adventure game with visual novel-depth storytelling and light puzzles. A contained episode with many callbacks and references to Episode I and the larger Episode II story, but does not require previous knowledge to enjoy. An enigmatic mystery that spans a dark forest, with many secrets and Easter eggs to be uncovered while you attempt to escape a looming horror. A story both overt and ambient with collectable lore pieces and documents.
