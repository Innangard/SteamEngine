Докладніше Що бажають сказати розробники: Чому дочасний доступ? «We believe Farm to Table will become a much better game by growing together with the community. Early Access gives us the opportunity to gather real player feedback on the core gameplay loop of farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Balancing progression, economy, and content pacing is especially important for a management game like this. We want to make sure the player’s journey feels rewarding, that new systems unlock at the right time, and that the overall gameplay flow feels smooth and enjoyable rather than overwhelming or repetitive. Player feedback helps us understand what feels fun, what needs improvement, and how we can create the best possible experience. We also want to shape the game alongside our players, improving systems and expanding the experience based on community feedback rather than building everything in isolation.» Як довго гра буде у дочасному доступі? «We currently expect Farm to Table to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 to 12 months. However, this timeline may remain flexible depending on community feedback and the overall development process. Our goal is to take the time needed to improve the game properly, polish the experience, and make sure the full version reaches the quality we aim for rather than rushing the process.» Чим повна версія гри буде відрізнятися від версії для дочасного доступу? «We plan for the full version of Farm to Table to significantly expand both the depth and the overall scope of the experience. This includes more recipes, crops, animals, deeper restaurant management systems, expanded staff management, automation systems, more customization options, seasonal events, and stronger progression systems. We also plan to expand island exploration, allowing players to discover more ingredients in the wild, go fishing, and unlock new ways to grow and produce food. In the full version, players will also be able to sell their home-grown farm produce directly to customers through their own Farmers’ Market, adding another layer to the farming and business side of the game. Many players have also asked about co-op. We’d love that, but it also depends on player support. The first version of the game will launch as a singleplayer experience, and the support and interest we see around release will help us better evaluate the possibility of adding co-op in the future.» Який поточний стан версії для дочасного доступу? «The Early Access version of Farm to Table already offers the full core gameplay experience, centered around farming, cooking, building, and restaurant management. Players can grow crops, cook and serve dishes in their restaurant, go fishing, explore the island for wild ingredients, hire staff, manage their restaurant, and customize both their farm and restaurant to match their own style. The game also includes production systems using machines to create more advanced ingredients, as well as the Farmers’ Market where players can sell their home-grown produce directly to customers. Our goal is to provide around 30–40 hours of gameplay in Early Access, with a strong foundation of systems and progression already in place, while continuing to expand and improve the experience based on player feedback throughout development.» Чи буде відрізнятися ціна гри протягом дочасного доступу та після його завершення? «We plan to offer Farm to Table at a lower price during Early Access and gradually increase the price as we add more content, features, and improvements throughout development. As the game grows in scope and depth, the price will better reflect the overall amount of content and the full experience. Players who join us early during Early Access will be able to support the game from the beginning and benefit from the lower entry price.» Як ви плануєте залучати спільноту до розробки гри? «Community feedback is one of the most important parts of our development process.
