Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game A tiny treehouse lives in the corner of your desktop. Prince Tomato times your focus sessions, Todudu keeps an eye on your to-do list, and Lily plays you something soft. They don't take up much room and they won't nag. They just stay close while you work, study, or take a break. Focusing, finishing tasks, and playing with the fairies all earn wish points. Spend them on new outfits, playlists, and treehouse decorations. As your wish points add up, the treehouse grows, and that's how Todudu and Lily move in. When it's time to focus, ask Prince Tomato to keep time. While you work, he reads, paints, or knits beside you. When time's up he lets you know and drops a few little gifts. Sweep your mouse over them to turn them into wish points. Write today's tasks on the to-do board and Todudu will keep watch. Each time you check one off, she hops over, pulls up a carrot and eats it. If a task sits for too long, she'll give you a gentle reminder. Music in the treehouse belongs to Lily. Over a hundred gentle tracks are spread across more than a dozen albums, from music-box lullabies and Chopin nocturnes to lo-fi, vaporwave and medieval tavern tunes. Save your favorites, press play, and she sings along. Finish several pomodoros in a row, leave a task sitting too long, or let the music go quiet for a while, and the fairies will notice and pop up to say something. Sometimes Todudu hides in the grass and waits for you to find her. Put up the rainy-day gate and you'll find a watering can hanging on it. Drag it to water the fairies, or the ground to make flowers pop up. Keep poking the decorations and a tiny hat might fall off for a fairy to wear all day. Each fairy has a short comic story of their own. It unlocks as the fairy levels up with you, or you can spend wish points to open it early. Once you've saved enough wish points, make a wish. Outfits, playlists, backgrounds, plants, and new looks for the timer and to-do board are all in the pool. There are no microtransactions; wish points come only from spending time together. More fairies will move into the treehouse later on. Everything you collect, from backgrounds and plants to gates and trinkets, lives in your collection book, ready to put on display whenever you like.
