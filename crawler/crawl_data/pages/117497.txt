Summon the Goddess. ​​You are a dark wizard performing the ultimate ritual of vengeance. Every rune you draw with precision channels forbidden magic, and every second matters as the filthy peasants storm your sanctuary to stop you. This demo lets you step into the opening stages of the ritual. Invoke the first gods, draw runes up to level 3, and survive the early waves that test your execution under pressure. The Training Room is also available, allowing you to practice rune drawing freely before facing the full ritual. Your hands are the weapon. Execution is everything. Hesitate, and the ritual collapses. Succeed, and you summon the goddess of death herself. Master the Ritual Loop. Master the Runeomicon. DRAW intricate runes under pressure. Every line channels power. Every mistake has consequences. DEFEND the ritual by unleashing magic, summoning creatures, and holding the line as waves of filthy peasants try to stop you. SUMMON ancient gods when you gather enough power. Command six dark entities, each unlocking spells and abilities that radically reshape your strategy. UPGRADE your ritual through branching paths. Fortify your defenses, unlock new skills, and draw runes faster and more accurately as mastery takes hold. Your Hands Channel Forbidden Magic Draw intricate runes in precise patterns while chaos erupts around you. The runes are simple to learn, but nearly impossible to perfect under pressure. Break the pattern and the rune fails. Lose control, and your defenses begin to crumble. As enemies close in, your execution must become faster and more precise. As your power grows, the runes become more complex, leaving less room for error. What begins as frantic survival transforms into mastery. This is not about speed alone. It is about precision. Six Dark Gods. Six Paths to Darkness. As your mastery grows, you gain the power to summon six ancient dark gods, each unlocking a radically different way to perform the ritual. Every god grants its own spells, defenses, abilities, and advantages, reshaping how you draw, defend, and survive. Mastering these powers is the only path forward, bringing you closer to the final ritual: summoning the goddess of death herself. Your Choices Shape the Ritual Runeomicon is built on constant tension between mastery and survival. Every moment forces a decision: draw faster to push the ritual forward, or invest in defense to stay alive a little longer. Every rune you complete earns you arcane energy. That power fuels the summoning, but it is also your lifeline. Spend it to unleash spells, reinforce defenses, or invest in upgrades to survive the next wave. Every choice pulls power away from somewhere else, forcing you to decide between progress and survival. Will you summon fast or defend hard? The ritual answers only to your decisions. You Are the Villain of the Story They scorned you. Then they killed your family. That was the moment the ritual began. The goddess of death brought you back to avenge what was taken from you, and the power she grants grows with every rune you draw. The ritual is not a choice. It is what you were brought back to complete. There is no redemption waiting at the end. The ritual must continue. No matter the cost.
