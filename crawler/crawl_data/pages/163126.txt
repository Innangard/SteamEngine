FarmTD combines the charm of farming with the strategy of tower defense. BUILD YOUR TOWERS Many aren't aware, but your farm-animals pack a serious punch! Get access to a varied range of animal towers to prevent the incoming waves of insects from harming your crops. Use stuns and slows to limit the insects' movement, and don't forget to provide proper illumination at night! GROW YOUR CROPS Someone has to pay the bills. While defending yourself from seemingly endless hordes of insects, you have to remember to keep the money flowing! Grow different kinds of plots to not just earn money on your investment, but also win favor with the townsfolk. PLAY YOUR WAY We're all individuals! Are you someone who plays for lategame? Power through the early? Win the favor of the town? Play with permanent upgrades? Play whatever playstyle you prefer in one of the four difficulty settings.
