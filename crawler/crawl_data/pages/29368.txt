Procedural characters add endless variety, while our in-house matchmaking adapts to skill and regional liquidity for consistently sharp, high-quality matches. FREE TO PLAY A football game with no paywalls, no barriers, and no locked-off modes. Fully accessible from the start and built as a global live service. New content, features, and events are introduced continuously, making the game grow alongside the community and staying fresh for every type of player. CROSSPLAY FOR REAL True crossplay across PC and consoles, one unified playerbase with no platform barriers. UNIQUE PLAYERS & PROGRESSION Every player is truly unique, no duplicates, no clones. Your squad is yours alone.
