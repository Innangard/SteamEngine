Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “A closer connection to the community before our official launch would be great for both the final state of our game and for dedicated fans who want to try it out early. As a roguelike, players can provide feedback and iteratively experience the game's narrative and gameplay without ruining or compromising their own journey through the story.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Until we reach our final release goal - all planned bosses, arenas, weapons, combos and upgrades. We estimate the game will be completed roughly by early 2026.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Our final release goal is planned to have four bosses, eight arenas, six weapons, 10+ combos and 30+ upgrades. We'll also be updating the game's narrative as we introduce new bosses with their own story arc and contribution to the overall story. Our current version is pretty similar to our final release, in terms of overall experience - we'll basically just add more content.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Early Access version includes two bosses, six arenas, four weapons, eight combos and 20 upgrades. We plan to launch new updates on a somewhat regular manner, progressively adding more content.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes - our Early Access version has its price reduced to reward early adopters for their feedback and trust in our studio.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Feel free to send feedback to our Steam forums and Discord! Focused on movement abilities, split-second decision-making and stylish strings of attacks, BloodRush encourages a creative approach to combat , combining abilities to demolish your foes! String together new combos by combining weapon attacks with movement abilities; Always a chance for comeback - rush to recover Blood by executing enemies ; Waiting is dying - as your health constantly drains out , you must move quickly to escape death! A variety of enemies and bosses stand in the way of Death's liberation, forming the Cult of Eternity ; Dance in eight combat arenas , each with different mechanics and challenges to interact with; Sharpen a different build every run with diverse weapon combinations, abilities and talents. Delve into Death's domain in multiple runs, unlocking the Stranger's vast arsenal of 6 weapons, 10+ combos, 20+ talents, and more ; Uncover insights into Death's imprisonment, progressively learning more of her captors' motivations and philosophies at every run; Fight in a beautiful pixel-art world, accompanied by a gothic-inspired soundtrack and atmosphere . Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This is a combat-heavy game, so naturally there's violence. Blood can be disabled through settings. The game's narrative also deals with themes of personal loss, death, animal death, physical and mental pain, and depersonalization.
