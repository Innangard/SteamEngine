Wield unique and satisfying weapons such as a double-barrel shotgun, a napalm launcher, and even a skull infused with a strange energy. Fight enemies ranging from cannibal cultists and terrorists to grotesque aberrations — and even a walking bomb built by a seven-year-old child. In 1985, after the fall of the military dictatorship in Brazil, an unknown cult begins to form. They are known for their belief that there is another world inhabited by an unknown being who lives inside a skinless humanoid, hopeless and empty. After two years, the cult manages to open a portal to this world in the center of a small city known for its people having no faith or hope. The portal is opened with the goal of granting this being more power, giving it more life and strength. In a forest near the city lives a man who fought against the dictatorship. After the conflict, he isolated himself from society because of the crimes he committed.
