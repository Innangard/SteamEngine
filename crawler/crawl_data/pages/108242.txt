About This Game Potatoman Seeks the Troof will put your reflexes and worldview to the test as you journey through scorching deserts, dark forests, busy cities, the tallest mountains and eventually your own potato-consciousness in search of the ultimate answer. Precise Platforming Think just as quickly as you move, dealing with unique challenges! Move your spudsy avatar in and out of obstacles paths! Train your fingers to dodge rocks, eggs, and anything else thrown at you! Run, run as fast as you can to find the troof! Mind-Bending Challenges! Never take anything for granted in this strange landscape! Test your wits against ever-changing challenges with strange rules! Laugh alongside Potatoman at his bizarre and humorous journey! Prepare for outlandish adventures and an odd sense of peace that comes with finding the troof. Join Potatoman on His Journey! Do you have what it takes to find the troof, little spud?
