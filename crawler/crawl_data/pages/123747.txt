Good Job is packed with absurd humor, questionable decisions, and unexpected situations. If you're looking for a serious, realistic simulator, this probably isn't it. But if you enjoy dark humor and a little chaos, welcome aboard! Nobody ever told you, "One day, you'll be a p*mp!" Of course they didn't... Good Job is an unofficial p*mp simulator. Your good friend Sam is in prison. He left you the keys. Now you're running a business you could never put on your résumé . Start in a run-down room, manage your crew, keep demanding customers happy, sell questionable products online, collect payments, and quietly build your underground empire. Manage your "massage" therapists (you know what we mean), schedule appointments, and oversee deliveries. Build an adult-oriented e-commerce empire as your side hustle. Collect your payments. Whatever it takes... Eat, sleep, survive... This city never stops! Get ready for one of the toughest simulations you’ll ever play. In this game, your customers have very specific needs! Build your team carefully, manage them well, and keep your customers happy! Build your herbal supplement business from the ground up. Produce your products, stock your shelves, and sell online to customers with very specific needs. Every pill matters. Every order is valuable. Every delivery is an opportunity. Completely legal, of course. 😇 A tough journey awaits you! There’s so much you need to catch up on! Survival is the most important rule! Eat, sleep, and be sure to earn money! Discover the perfect blend. The right formula keeps your customers happy, grows your business, and builds your reputation. The wrong choice, however, can throw everything off balance. Chemistry is hard. The rest of the business is even harder. Mix carefully.
