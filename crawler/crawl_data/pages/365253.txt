What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We chose Early Access for Recharge to actively involve our players in the development process. While the core gameplay mechanics are already polished, Early Access allows us to gather invaluable feedback from the community to refine features, improve balance, and introduce new content. This collaborative approach helps us work towards delivering the best possible experience for our players.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan to remain in Early Access for around 6-12 months. However, the timeline may change based on the feedback we receive and the additional features we decide to develop. Our aim is to take the time needed to deliver a well-polished experience.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan for the full version to include: Additional tracks to offer more diverse environments and challenges. Expanded customization options for RC cars and accessories. Improved physics, refined with community feedback for smoother gameplay. New gameplay modes, such as time trials and enhanced multiplayer features. Further polishing of graphics and audio to maximize visual and audio fidelity.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of Recharge includes: Fully functional RC car racing and exploration mechanics. A selection of detailed and challenging tracks. Initial customization options for RC cars. High-quality visuals showcasing the level of realism we aim to achieve. Players can enjoy a solid and fun gameplay experience, while helping us identify areas for improvement and shape the game’s future.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, we plan to increase the price after Early Access as we add more content and features. Players who join during Early Access will get the game at a lower price and have the opportunity to contribute directly to its development.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We plan to actively engage with the community through: Steam forums for feedback, suggestions, and bug reports. Our Discord server, where we will host discussions, polls, and direct Q&A sessions with the development team. Regular updates to share progress and plans with the community. Beta testing opportunities for players to try new features before they are fully implemented. Enjoy realistic physics, impressive graphics and deep customization options. Race solo or with friends across diverse tracks, from professional RC circuits to real-world inspired environments. With broad controller support, including RC-style controllers and gamepads, Recharge delivers a dynamic and authentic RC racing experience. Recharge caters to both solo racers and competitive spirits, offering an experience that suits your preferred playstyle. In singleplayer, face AI opponents at different difficulty levels or challenge your ghost to improve your best times. Multiplayer supports up to 12 players in online races, with private lobbies for friends or public lobbies for wider competition. Each mode brings intense racing action, whether you’re honing your skills alone or battling it out with others. Recharge lets you build an RC car that feels uniquely yours, blending performance upgrades with personalized style. Enhance your car’s performance by upgrading the motor, ESC and chassis to meet any challenge. Customize the look with paint, stickers and body choices, creating a distinctive appearance on the track. Find the perfect balance of speed, control and style that matches how you want to. Recharge offers a wide variety of tracks, each crafted to deliver a distinct RC racing experience. Race in professional RC circuits inspired by real professional tracks. Take the action to the beach, amusement parks and skateparks, bringing the thrill of real-world locations into the game. Each track challenges you in a unique way, offering fresh racing experiences that keep you coming back for more.
