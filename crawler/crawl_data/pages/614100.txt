Silence is your ally. Darkness is your friend. Move like a ghost. No room for mistakes. Chess Knights: Shinobi is an original turn-based puzzle game inspired by Chess. Featuring: All pieces move like in a chess game . You play as a Shinobi Knight who needs to move stealthily through a game board full of Samurai enemies; 50 hand-crafted levels with increasing complexity (+ 10 secret "surreal" levels. Can you find them?); Play in beautiful environments of ancient Japan in the Edo era; Originally composed soundtracks; A chess game without any chess. This is an original game concept for chess lovers and newcomers alike (you do not need to know any chess to play it).
