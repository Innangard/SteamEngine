View Community Hub Reviews “Vibe City Islands Architect is a gridless tropical city builder where you design island paradises, unlock 200 unique buildings, drive golf carts, and jam to 10 curated radio stations.” The Biggest Lake Giveaways “Build your island city however you want, balancing housing, jobs, and parks while choosing which of ten gangs to support with their own radio vibes.” TheBigBoisNews About This Game Welcome to Vibe City  Islands Architect! Design and build the your island paradise in this tiny gridless indie city-building game. Create Your Dream City : Build a thriving city across picturesque islands surrounded by the tropical ocean. High Score Mode : Take on 4 important city objectives: Housing, Jobs, Parks/Nature, and Tourism. Unlock and place 40 new buildings per rank as you climb from a Rank 1 to Rank 5 Architect! 200 unique buildings. Sandbox Mode : Unleash your creativity with no budgets, targets, or restrictions- just pure building bliss. Find your favourite radio station and create with 200 buildings. Diverse Soundtrack : Jam out to various music genres with 10 radio stations to choose from. The game will launch with 10 radio stations of carefully curated vibes (inspired by GTA vice city and Tropico 4). Island Adventures : Explore your city by driving around in a golf cart (collide with buildings but not with people like Manor lords). Instant Construction : Watch buildings rise instantly with mesmerizing blueprint effects. Growing Population : Add life to your city as more citizens arrive with every new building. Craft the tropical city of your dreams! Or just chill and vibe. This will be one of those small games for a nice price you can get immersed in within minutes. Get busy in the details of what your tropical island city people need, press spacebar to hide UI , then zoom out and marvel at your creation. Get in your cart,  cruise around. Put the radio on (I spent 1000+ hours curating the 10 radio station playlists :) (220 songs is ~11 hours of unique soundtrack!) Inspired by Islanders, Tropico 4, Urbek, Manor lords (cruise mode) and GTA vice city (best soundtrack ever?).
