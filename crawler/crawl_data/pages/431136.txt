Question the lineup, learn what makes each of them tick, and work out who did it. Every case happens somewhere that makes no sense. Banana peels, broken printers and other junk are scattered  everywhere. It looks like nobody in this town has ever cleaned up. Meet a cast of unique suspects. Each one has their own quirks, fears and questionable life choices. Play silly minigames to earn their trust and find out what they are really like. The more you learn, the easier it gets to rule them out... or not. Rule out suspects one  by one. Use what you have learned about each suspect to cross them off the list, Guess Who style. Keep going until only one is left. Step into a world of   pure nonsense. People wander, dance and argue in ways that don't make sense. Everyone you meet is bizarre, unpredictable and a little bit suspicious.
