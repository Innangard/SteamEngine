Buy Vagrus - The Riven Realms At the Heart of Ruin $19.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content In a land more perilous than most, stripped of any semblance of civility, a host of new opportunities and perils await. Remote, inhospitable, and mountainous, this realm is cursed with unbearable heat, ravaged by mad scavenger gangs, and prowled by abominable creatures. Those who brave the suffocating air and deadly terrain that characterizes the landscape are oft-called fools. Yet there are riches aplenty here, and a buried history spanning millennia to boot. In recent years, the land was isolated from the rest of the Realms, which only gave rise to more bloodshed and despair. Thus, the fate of the region hangs in the balance, waiting for an outsider to affect its future for decades or even centuries to come. In the meantime, a great journey into the very heart of this ruined realm is being organized, seeking riches and secrets against all odds. Vagrus ’s second expansion, At the Heart of Ruin , offers a tight but multi-layered and dense region fraught with conflict and rich with opportunities. Vagri who decide to brave the forsaken paths of this broken realm will be challenged not only by new foes and villains, but also by the very nature one must traverse the mountainous terrain.
