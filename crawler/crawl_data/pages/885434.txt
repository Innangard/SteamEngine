Designed by the creator of the mega hit video game Plague Inc. - this is the digital version of the best selling physical board game. Question : Is this really a video game of a board game of a video game? Answer : Yes it is! :) I (James, the creator of Plague Inc.) originally made it to help test out new rules for the second edition of the print run - but had so much fun playing it digitally that I decided to infect everyone else with it too! Play full single-player games with intelligent bot players Full Legacy campaign with unique game modes, rules and rewards Global Daily Challenge - see how  your disease holds up Use the tutorial to learn the rules in a safe space Try and beat the infamous PlagueBot game mode from the physical game. Multiple online game modes let you share diseases with people all over the world. Online multiplayer - jump into games fast. Online Turn Based mode - take your time and play asynchronously Use Legacy mode to turn games into a living world that is shaped by your actions Have you always wanted to impress your friends with your  nosebleeds, pustules and diarrhea?
