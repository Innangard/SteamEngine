View Community Hub About This Game Enter the World of Half Grip Start your engines and embrace the chaos in this high-octane roguelike combat racer that forces you to juggle between steering, shooting, changing gears and more - all with one arm. The Real Deal The game balances between realistic and arcade car handling as you'll drive through different road conditions. Depending on your car upgrades, your tires will either grip the road, or you'll slide right off it. Lock and Load Win more money through skill-based driving and combat to unlock a variety of upgrades and weapons - rams, shotguns, magnets, mines - they'll all get you ahead of the competition. No Race Feels the Same As a roguelike, each run will feel different. Build your version of the optimal car - be it a tricked out drifter, an armed stormchaser or an aerodynamic speedster. Car Customization Choose your colors and hit the road in style.
