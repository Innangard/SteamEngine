Experiment with countless combinations to find the most effective way to loot more! Upgrades & Progression Loot coins and items to unlock powerful upgrades in the Skill Tree. Each upgrade strengthens you and your heroes to defeat more skeletons and gather even greater loot. Forge weapons in the Blacksmith, every weapon features its own unique attack animation. Recruit new heroes and upgrade them in Guild, each with different upgrade paths and abilities. Heroes & Party Building Recruit, bless and lead a team of heroes through darkest catacomb dungeons. Dungeon Exploration Explore new dungeon rooms, each with its own drop rates and challenges. Unlock new enemies that drop unique items, use them at the Blacksmith or sell them for coins. Enjoy the massive variety of content and discover endless ways to grow stronger in Bones and Coins!
