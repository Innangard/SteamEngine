But there is one problem - everything around is captured by alien bugs - lithoarachnids. Gravity Beams: Your character has gravitational beam technology. With their help, you can interact with objects around. Swarm: Hordes of aggressive bugs captured the corridors of the station. Carefully plan your battle tactics with them, otherwise you will be crushed by numbers. Debris: Along the way, you will encounter various pieces of debris that prevent you from passing. Everything is in business: You can throw literally anything you can get your hands on at enemies. Tear off paneling from walls or pick up various debris from under your feet to throw at enemies.
