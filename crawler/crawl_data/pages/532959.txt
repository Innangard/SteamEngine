View Community Hub About This Game Tidy Tails is a relaxing organization and collection game where you restore order to a messy cat plushie store. Explore the shelves, learn to recognize hundreds of designs, and slowly complete each section as you improve your abilities and your cat’s abilities, discover minigames, and search for collectibles. Find the Right Place for Every Plushie Each of the 5,555 plushies belongs to a section and has its own place in the store. Learn to recognize their designs, find the right shelf, and put them away one at a time. Fill the displays with Christmas cats, space cats, dinosaurs, superheroes, mythological creatures, and many more. Transform the Store The store will not always look the same because it changes with the weather. Cover everything in snow, fill the air with cherry blossoms, unleash a thunderstorm, or change gravity to make it feel like you are in space. Each type of weather transforms the store and brings new mechanics, interactions, and secrets that change the way you play. Get Better at Organizing As you progress, you can unlock abilities that change how you deal with the mess. Carry more plushies, move faster, find what you are looking for more easily, and get your cat to lend a paw. The more your collection grows, the more tools you will have to take on the chaos. From Chaos to a Collection Before you can fill the shelves, you need to find the plushies among all the mess. Explore the store, pick them up from the piles, and carry them with you as you learn to recognize their designs. What starts as a floor covered in cats will slowly clear up and make room for a huge organized collection. What Is Hidden in the Garage? Even with so many plushies to organize, there is still time to search for collectibles, complete tasks, and play different minigames. But something will keep waiting while you restore order to the store. There is a locked door at the back of the garage. What will be behind it when everything is back in its place?
