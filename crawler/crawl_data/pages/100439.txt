Be the resistance! Starexcess is a retro-looking vertical shoot 'em up inspired by the 16-bit era. Take on enemy armies and dodge frenetic shots in this shoot 'em up for one or two players in a local ofline co-op. Features: Each ship has two weapons, main and secondary, which can be alternated. But beware, changing weapons influences your speed! Supports 1 or 2 players (local - offline coop). Play side by side, like old days. Different ships with different weapons, health and speed. Choose the one that suits you best. Seven levels with waves of enemies randomly chosen. The waves will not appear in the same order as previous games, encouraging the development of player skill. Pixel art style. Drawn pixel by pixel.
