The game will be a bit cheaper during Early Access to reward those who want to support it early on. The price will likely increase due to the amount of content added during this time.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Moonfrost is all about community, and so are we! A second moon appeared in our skies and with it came constant meteor storms that rained chaos upon our homes. We called these icy meteorites MOONFROST. In this ruined world, some of us learned to harness the power of this mysterious substance, transforming its shards into all sorts of structures, creating order from the chaos. They call us Frost Hunters, and we have a mission: ✨To rebuild the world, one shard at a time. ✨ This is Moonfrost, and your adventure is about to begin! Moonfrost tells the story of a broken world and the broken people who live within it. Form friendships and potential romances with a cast of deep, colourful characters. Help them face their fears and flaws as you unravel your own forgotten past and uncover the truth of Moonfrost and the ominous Second Moon that looms over us all. Cottage, castle, or cozy villa! It’s all up to you! Use Build Mode to design, construct, and decorate your perfect home, wall by wall. Bring life back to your village, cleaning the streets, restoring old buildings and designing unique homes for the other villagers! Gather resources, grow crops, fish, cook, explore dangerous temples and decorate your homestead as you expand your Frost Hunting abilities. Sit back, relax and lose yourself in a next-gen pixel art world featuring dynamic lighting, weather and wildlife systems as well as a haunting, original soundtrack. Unlock a growing collection of crafting recipes, from tools and decorations to outfits, hairstyles, and more. Mix and match gear to shape your own unique style, both in how you play and how you look.
