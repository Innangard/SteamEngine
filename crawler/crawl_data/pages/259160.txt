View Community Hub About This Game Holy Shoot is a fast-paced roguelike FPS packed with chaos, style, and comedy. Start as a Sanctum warrior and battle through sin-themed levels filled with traps, demons, and bizarre bosses. Upgrade your powers, unlock new heroes, and blast your way through hell one ridiculous run at a time. Fast-paced Rogue-lite FPS Holy Shoot is all about speed, chaos, and humor. Blast your way through randomized level layouts filled with cursed enemies and explosive surprises. Every run offers a fresh challenge and new ways to bring the pain. Play as Sanctum’s Finest Misfits Start your journey as Supersonic Samuel, a lightning-fast warrior with divine reflexes. As you progress, unlock new heroes like Toshiko Techno. From divine speed to tech-powered tricks, every character brings something different to the battlefield. Their strengths shape how you play, and the rest is up to what you find along the way. Build, Upgrade, and Adapt Unlock powerful perks mid-run and earn currencies to buy permanent upgrades back at HUB. Mix and match upgrades to suit your style, and build your own flavor of divine destruction. A HUB Full of Sanctum Oddballs After each chaotic run, you return to your home base. This lively headquarters is where angelic blueprints are drafted, cakes are sliced, and tea is definitely preferred over coffee. Chat with quirky NPCs as you regroup, unlock permanent upgrades, and prepare to dive back into hell even stronger. Weapons with Personality From chunky shotguns to nail-spitting rifles and boom-happy grenade launchers, every weapon feels unique. Each one packs a punch, looks cool, and delivers comic-style chaos with attitude. Stylized Visuals and Comedic Chaos Each level is themed around a different Deadly Sin, with hand-painted environments, stylized hazards, and comic-style effects. From crumbling ruins overflowing with gold to wrath-filled lava chambers, every setting cranks up the absurdity. Combat stays fast and flashy, delivering chaos with a chuckle.
