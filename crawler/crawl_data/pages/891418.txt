Buy The Battle for Japan $4.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content This DLC takes us to 16th-century Japan, when bloody battles for power were at their peak.  Choose one of the most powerful clans and try to subjugate the entire Japanese islands and unify the country. In addition to  the most powerful Japanese clans, you will also encounter the Joseon Empire and the Ming Dynasty, which are fighting on the nearby mainland. On two maps, you can choose one of up to 10 factions and try to conquer Japan by force or politics. Unlike the medieval units in the base game, here you will also encounter the most modern soldiers of the time with firearms and the precursors of rockets.
