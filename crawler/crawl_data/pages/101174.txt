The boxes are stacked by the door, the room is empty, and what happens next is entirely up to the two of you. Small boxes open on your own. Big ones take two: grab an end each and pull. There is no grid and nothing snaps into place. You drag each piece by hand: the heavy ones scrape across the floor and need two people, the light ones you can carry anywhere. Where things end up is your decision, not the game's. No friend around tonight? You can play alone too. No score. No timer. No stars. When the room feels right to both of you, you call it done. The camera takes a slow turn around what you built and hands you a polaroid. Every finished room becomes a polaroid on the wall of your main menu. Not everything in a moving box is furniture. Sometimes it's the cat, and it makes itself at home. In the demo Online co-op for two, with voice chat Solo play No fail states, no timers, no scoring The full game has more rooms.
