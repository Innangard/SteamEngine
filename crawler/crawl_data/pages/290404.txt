View Community Hub About This Game In Zombie Town , you venture alone into a fallen lakeside town. Rely on your wits, firepower and courage to fight for survival amid a zombie‑ravaged apocalypse. Desolate abandoned streets, scattered weapons and supplies, and deadly threats lurking in the shadows await you. But remember — death follows every step. Stay alert and stay prepared, or you will fall into the horrifying abyss of Zombie Town . We aim to recreate the tense, oppressive atmosphere of apocalyptic survival for new‑generation players. Veteran players will also find familiar end‑times peril within this overrun town. Whether you are a newcomer to the apocalypse or a battle‑hardened survivor, grab your weapons, lock and load, and step bravely into the death‑shrouded lands of Zombie Town . Kill Zombies Press Tab to open your inventory. Right-click to select your weapon of choice and click [Equip] to hold it. Right-click to aim, then left-click to shoot! Save Point You'll find a computer inside houses throughout the game. Use it wisely to save your progress — it'll make exploration much easier.
