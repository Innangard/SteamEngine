An underground research facility located in the Earth sector B-7, nicknamed COLD CALL . Here at Northbridge, we study biological anomalies and research ways of saving the Earth from destruction. Play as Roman, an intern's first day here at Northbridge, and it just so happens to be the day of reckoning, where hell breaks loose. Escape the research facility through many dangers and flee COLD CALL. You can't stay here forever. Explore The Large Facility of Northbridge Northbridge is a sprawling, multi‑level research labyrinth filled with secrets, forbidden experiments, and anomalies pulled from a broken Earth. Experience the many anomalies captured from the broken Earth and understanding the behavior of creatures out of this world. SURVIVE NORTHBRIDGE Everything wants to kill you, flee, or hide from dangerous threats Roman comes along the way. Collect keycards and documentation to understand the threats. Manage your inventory and take what is necessary with you to carry on your escape.
