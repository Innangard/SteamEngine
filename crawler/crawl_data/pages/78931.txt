Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Deep Blue Sushi combines several interconnected systems, including online co-op, ocean exploration, fishing, sushi preparation, restaurant management, progression, and character customization. Early Access allows us to improve how these systems work together with feedback from real players. We want to use this period to refine the co-op experience, balance the progression and economy, improve usability, and better understand which parts of the game players enjoy the most before moving toward the full version.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Deep Blue Sushi to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 to 12 months. However, this timeframe may change depending on player feedback, development progress, and the amount of additional polish and content the game requires.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to expand the game with additional ocean content, fish and sea creatures, sushi recipes, restaurant upgrades, equipment, customization options, progression systems, and quality-of-life improvements. We also plan to continue improving multiplayer stability, balancing the economy and progression, refining existing gameplay systems, and adding more variety to both ocean exploration and restaurant management. These plans may evolve throughout Early Access based on community feedback and development priorities.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version includes the main gameplay loop and can be played alone or in online co-op with up to five players. Players can explore the ocean, catch fish using diving equipment and harpoons, prepare different types of sushi, serve customers, manage and expand their restaurant, upgrade equipment, customize their characters, and progress through the game. The game is fully playable, but some systems, content, balancing, animations, user interface elements, and multiplayer features may continue to receive improvements during Early Access. Players may also encounter bugs or unfinished areas while development continues.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We are not currently planning frequent price changes during Early Access. However, the price may be adjusted when the game leaves Early Access to reflect the additional content, features, and improvements added during development.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback will be an important part of the development process. We plan to collect feedback through Steam Discussions, our Discord server, playtests, reviews, bug reports, and player suggestions. We will use this feedback to identify technical issues, improve multiplayer stability, balance progression and the in-game economy, refine existing systems, and help determine which areas of the game should receive the most attention. Build and run a sushi restaurant with your friends but don’t expect everyone to stay in the kitchen. Dive into the depths, hunt for fresh seafood, prepare every order, and work together through each chaotic service. MAKE YOUR CHARACTER YOUR OWN Choose your look and stand out from the crew. Customize your outfit, colors, hairstyle, and accessories to create a chef that truly feels like yours. SLICE IT. ROLL IT. SERVE IT. Prepare sushi step by step. Add the ingredients, follow each recipe, and turn your fresh catch into handcrafted exotic sushi. From precise cuts to finished rolls, every plate is made by your own hands. THE DEEP BITES BACK Not every catch comes easy. Dive with your crew, track dangerous sea creatures, and fight sharks, piranhas, squid, electric fish, and octopuses to bring home your next big catch. BRING THE RIGHT TOOL Use specialized tools above and below the surface. Catch fish, explore the ocean, clean up disasters, and choose the right equipment before diving into deeper waters. EXPLORE THE OCEAN The ocean is full of life. Discover colorful fish, strange creatures, and rare sea animals as you explore its deeper layers. Some are easy to catch. Others are better left alone.
