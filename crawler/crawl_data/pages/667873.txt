Fluffy Cats Pack introduces four brand new, ultra fluffy cat breeds to Animal Shelter 2 that can show up at your shelter at any stage in their life, from kittens to senior cats! Each with luscious, elegant fur and incredible personality, these furballs are for you to find them a new home! Norwegian Forest Cat These majestic creatures were once companions to a Nordic goddess. Now, they can be companions in your daily life. Independent and a little wild, these beauties need a home that can give them freedom to roam! Persian The ancient Persian cat, beautiful and calm, needs a loving home where they can be appreciated and worshipped as they deserve. They love to be lavished with attention and need to be close to their human, despite what their little grumpy face might suggest. Maine Coon The largest breed of cat, the Maine Coon is the definition of majestic. They may be a little intimidating, but in reality, they are a little more like a puppy than a cat - loyal, friendly, and playful! Bobtail Cat You can easily recognize the Bobtail by its pompom-like tail, short and curled. Playful and intelligent, these kitties deserve a loving, warm home where they can be both silly and cuddly to their hearts’ content! Help them find a home! These beautiful cats found their way to your shelter for one reason - to find a forever home. Help them find a family in Animal Shelter 2 - Fluffy Cats Pack!
