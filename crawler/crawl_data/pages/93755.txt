The thing is fueled by a mix of high-tech nonsense and Vampire blood. As her ship rounds the moon, the Vampires' space-fortress comes into view… Bullet Hell Deckbuilder Moon Watch mixes deckbuilding strategy and bullet hell action, where time itself becomes your weapon! Control the flow of time to think strategically and weave impossible odds in your favor. Complete challenges to unlock new cards and characters with unique attacks and abilities. Refine your deck to build devastating combos. Discover rare relics that completely change your run. Hunt the darkest threat the world, and the moon, has ever known. Wield Three Unique Characters Unlock  their specialized playstyles, decks, and powerful relics! Lucia the Nun Your go-to starter character! She uses garlic to control space and projectiles to attack enemies. Straightforward and dependable. Nina the Ninja A melee queen! Nina thrives in close combat with dashes to evade enemies and powerful sword slashes. She also has a “curse” ability to weaken foes, making her a great pick for those who love risk and reward. Encio the Engineer A creative powerhouse! Encio uses pinball ability to move around the level, relies on electricity and turrets as offense. Also has a “charm” ability that he can use to turn enemies against each other. This is it. Our chance to strike back at the Vampire Lords who haunt our planet from above!
