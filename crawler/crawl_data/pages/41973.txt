You and your crew are criminals sentenced to die. But you were shown mercy: travel down the cursed badlands instead, and get to the bottom of The Depths. Take the reins and drive the wagon yourself, or guard it on foot with a bow while a teammate drives Keep it running: swap broken wheels, grease the axles, chop trees for lumber, and feed the horse Push through four cursed biomes, each sealed by a puzzle you must solve to pass 1 to 8 player online co-op with proximity voice chat, solo and offline supported Drop-in co-op, with disconnected players rejoining right where they left off, gear intact Fully playable in 13 languages. THE BIOMES The Forest - Storm-lashed woods where goblins raid the wagon by night. An abandoned witch village demands its ritual be finished before the gate will open. The Faelands - Was that there last time? Walk the fairy ring and remember everything, because if you judge wrong it's back to zero. The Frost - A mansion buried under the snow. Feed the creature behind the grate, align the cipher, and escape the room. The Depths - Descend into the catacombs, answer the altar in a dead tongue, and find the power that will seal away a great evil for good. Then run!
