The OPUS: Prism Peak Original Soundtrack brings together orchestral, electronic, and genre-crossing tracks into one complete collection. Composed by Triodust (KUSHIH), Bo-Xun Lin, Chia-Wei Hsu, and more, the soundtrack captures every note of wonder, sorrow, and warmth throughout the game. Featuring a lineup of high-profile collaborations, the album includes the main theme " I Am Farewell Itself, I Am Life ," written and composed by Kevin Penkin and performed by Sarah Àlainn . Martin Johnson , Zooey Wonder , Paroto , and other acclaimed artists also lend their distinct voices to bring each moment of the journey to life.
