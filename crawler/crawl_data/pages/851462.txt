The detailed Lake Constance route is part of the Swiss “Seelinie” (Lake Line) in the Thurgau region. Running from Schaffhausen to Kreuzlingen around the lake this modern-day route features “Thurbo” branded multiple units that run every half hour in both directions. Get commuters and sightseers to their destinations on time as you take over driver duties on these new challenges. In this new scenario pack you will drive a morning train from Stein am Rhein to Schaffhausen, tour the full route from Schaffhausen to Kreuzlingen using the request stop feature, take over a stopping service in the snow to Etzwielen and test out a new timetable service stopping at all 18 stations. Climb back into the cab of the SBB RABe 526 2/8 EMU on Lake Constance: Schaffhausen – Kreuzlingen with this new scenario pack.
