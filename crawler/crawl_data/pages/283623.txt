In Professional Cats , adorable felines dress up as doctors, firefighters, chefs, astronauts, and many more. Your mission? Master four unique mini-games that test your observation, memory, and strategy skills in a cozy and charming experience. Four Unique Mini-Games FindCats A fresh twist on the hidden object genre. Instead of searching for dozens at once, you’ll receive a list of 3 specific professional cats to find in each round. Stay focused — every detail matters! Cat Harmony Bring balance to the feline world by matching pairs of cats. Some appear in vibrant colors, others in black and white. Can you reunite every perfect pair? Memory Cats An arcade-style memory challenge that increases in difficulty every round. Flip, remember, and match the professional cats as the game gets faster and more intense. Cats on Shelves Organize the chaos! Match 3 identical cats on the same shelf to clear them all and win the level. Extra Features Radio System – Choose your favorite music and set the perfect mood while you play. Individual Leaderboards – Compete globally in each mini-game.
