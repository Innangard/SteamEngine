Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game The map is the prize. Ancient Monoliths stand across Veyra, the Flux Moon. Infuse your troops with Flux to push beyond the limits of your technology, survive the moon, and prepare to expand. Three civilizations. No way home. Veyra's ore is the key to crossing the stars. Three civilizations have each sent a small seed force to claim it. None of them can go home, and none can bring more until a Flux relay stands. The moon is not empty: its native life reacts to every mine. Cindral Vanguard , from the industrial world Kharos. The anvil: units that stand still near their own completed structures take less damage. Zephyrine Court , from Aurelion, ruled by scientific houses. The knife: every unit is faster than its rivals, with thinner hulls. Pelagic Choir , from the ocean world Neryth. The chorus: no speed or armour trade-off, dependable everywhere. All three share the economy, the Monoliths and the core roster. Doctrine is what sets them apart. Build fast, fight everywhere Metal and energy stream in every second and are spent as you build. Queue up to 10 orders per factory, loop production, and spread a queue across every factory you select. Siphon Flux, the rare third resource, from shards to field elite units. Fight on land, at sea and in the air with 15 unit classes, and upgrade field structures to MK2 and MK3. Features Three game modes: Ascendancy, Annihilation and Classic RTS, plus optional Horde Attacks, where waves of native creatures hit every base, sooner and bigger each time Skirmish against AI on 32 hand-built maps for 2 to 8 commanders, plus a map generator with nine terrain styles Teams, start positions, 16 player colours, army size and bot difficulty for each seat Online multiplayer through Steam for up to 8 players: host with a 6-digit join code or invite friends If the host crashes, the match can be resumed with everyone back in their seats Basic Training: three missions that teach the controls, the economy and how to take a Monolith. The three faction campaigns are coming soon. Watch All Bots: turn any setup into a spectator battle at 1x to 16x speed Saves and replays, built on a deterministic simulation In-game Getting Started guide covering controls, factions, modes, settings and lore AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: Generative AI tools were used during development to help create some of the game's art, textures and icons, audio (including music and voice), in-game text, and store and marketing materials.
