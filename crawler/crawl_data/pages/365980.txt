View Community Hub Roadmap About This Game Dollhouse Corp. is the world's leading manufacturer of toys with AI. The company's business is thriving, but there is one problem. Sometimes smart toys get out of control and take over company factories. But Dollhouse Corp. is also a frugal company! They employ squads of Scavengers who go to captured factories and collect materials that the company needs. Are you ready to become a Dollhouse Corp. scavenger and risk your life for $10? Congratulations, you are hired! Gameplay Your team arrives at one of the abandoned Dollhouse Corp. factories. It is captured by smart toys, some of them are neutral towards you, while others are madly eager for your death. Your task is to look for various trash, extract materials from it and gradually open new areas of the location Toys Rabbits are mostly harmless. You can even trade junk with them. But if you take more from them than you give them in return, they will bite your head off! And this is the last good news for today. Because the rest of the toys: Thumper, Spider, Teddy Bear, Doll and many others will kill you as soon as they see you Hide and seek Does everyone want to kill you? Don't worry, scavengers. You can hide from bloodthirsty toys in toilet rooms and closets. But remember, there are not enough places to hide and seek for everyone! So choose a place to hide and before your mates and stay alive and with money Money Sell excess materials and save money for improved equipment. Maybe one day you will even have enough money to retire!
