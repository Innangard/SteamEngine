About This Game Twenty years, time flies, while it takes a lifetime to say goodbye to a sunny day. "You know what? When I was little, you told me that my mother was a fairy, a star, an alien queen. But I kept thinking, I don't want those awesome moms. I've been thinking... I don't want an awesome mom. I just want an ordinary mom to be by my side every day. " "The last photo, a group photo of you both, may have been taken with the help of a passerby. Your wife is laughing in your arms, and you look down at her, not at the camera... You see a line of small words at the back of the photo: Youth as a companion to return home." Under a roof, day after day of living, the missing outline of the family is redrawn. "In the world you know, a father's duties seem vague compared to those of a mother. But in your case, these duties are clear.” Life is like a long-lasting song in the wilderness, re-raising the inner child , and irrigating the fragrance of the garden with the intimacy of friendship. "To be honest, I'm envious of these people because I'm different from them, and I'm missing half of my path. All the while, I’ve been limping along it.” "The storyteller said that our little princess had to be brave, then she became brave. So convenient! Has anyone asked the little princess for her opinion? No! Because she's just a character to be manipulated! ” "I used to think that only princesses in bedtime stories deserved certain things: respect, friends, happiness, love... But we were born to enjoy all of this. Rest when you're sick, cry when you're in pain, stop and sleep when you're tired. Then get up to see whether you feel happy with your mind at ease.. ” A moving story composed as a poetic collage of the ordinary moments we all experience in our daily lives. An old bicycle, the familiar path back home. Farewells to friends, and meeting new neighbors. A life filled with laughter and tears, where you can always move on with the love of your family. A text-based game with delicate emotional descriptions of nuanced situations. Your child left a light to “guide you back home” after work – you’re moved by her warm gesture, but in a humble home like yours, electricity bills can be crippling. How to manage this as a parent is up to you. As you live your life and decide which paths to follow, you fill a customized album with the memories of your experiences in the game. Whenever you flip through the pages, it will be like reliving a lifetime of memories and experiences. As a young father hustling in the workplace, facing inequities in corporate culture, meager salaries, and the escalating expenses of rising a daughter, many challenges keep cropping up. Caution! Every decision threatens to shatter the fragile balance between your mental well-being, health, and focus. Want to be a successful father? You've got a long way to go! With a beautiful hand-drawn art style and emotional music, Fragment offers an immersive gaming experience designed to make you feel cozy and warm, like sharing someone’s life story over a steaming cup in the middle of winter.
