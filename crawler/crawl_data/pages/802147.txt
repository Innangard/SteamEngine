If you treat them well, they will gain a metallic shimmer as they age. Will you be able to raise a golden fish? Feed your creations, celebrate their birthdays, discover their relationships and watch them grow. You Draw The Fish Whatever that beautiful mind of yours can dream of, draw absolutely anything and watch it come to life inside your aquarium. Use template shapes if you need a little inspiration or go wild with unfettered creative energy. The canvas is yours. Decorate With Your Own Plants Any decent aquarium will need an abundance of thriving plants. Create the perfect habitat by drawing your own vegetation and placing it wherever you want. Of course, if you’d rather be drawing sea mines or skyscrapers… Who are we to stop you? Unlock Limitless Customization As you progress in the game, you will unlock additional ways to customize your aquarium. Change the way the sand looks, customize the lighting, and place decorative rocks, driftwood and more. Your aquarium is a tiny living world, filled with creatures made and cared for by you. The environment will reflect your journey together. Feed Your Fish and Watch Them Grow Your special little creations will start small and grow with time, as you feed and get to know them. Make sure to pay them regular visits and take good care of them, lest your relationship turn sour. If you prove yourself to be affectionate and persistent, you may even find a golden fish inside of your aquarium one day! Your Fish Interact With Each Other When you place new creatures in your fish tank, they will interact with the others, forming friendships, rivalries and even relationships. They can become enemies and quibble or find their soulmate and have cute little babies Your Drawquarium, Always By Your Side Miss your babies whenever you go and close the game? Simply turn on the Desktop Wallpaper Mode and all of your creations will stay right there with you. Whether you’re crunching numbers for the boss or browsing a forum, you can observe, feed and play with your fish right there on your desktop. Additional Features But wait, there’s more!
