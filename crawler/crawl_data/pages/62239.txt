Most of these tracks play on the crew radio while you work, so we wanted them to feel like something you'd actually leave on in the background: a bit of country, a bit of western, and some upbeat stuff for when things start getting hectic. Then there are the tracks for the moments that go wrong, like when a tornado shows up or someone calls in an airstrike.
