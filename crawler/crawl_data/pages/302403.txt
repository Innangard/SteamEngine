View Community Hub Reviews “Tactically on par with the best deck builders” Play!Zine “A fresh take on the genre” Gamezebo “A slick, sporty spin on Slay the Spire” PCGamesN About This Game Clutchtime is a deck builder centered around basketball plays and team synergies . Double Team your opponents and force Turnovers or create a dream team of Superstar shooters who Splash three pointers even while getting fouled. Different game modes: Elimination tournaments in true roguelike style, or Classic regular season where you can lose some games, but have to qualify and win the playoffs to become a Champion. Each team has a starting deck of 5 starter players with 3 cards representing their skills. Random events happen during the season, which allow you to: trade with other teams upgrade cards remove cards that don't fit your strategy duplicate your favorite cards and more... You can also get team cards like: Timeout , Coach Challenge or even Complain to the Referees to get a Tech . Can you help your favorite team win the Championship?
