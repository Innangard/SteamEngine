View Community Hub Reviews “There’s a ton of heart and depth to be found here” 8.5/10 – GameTyrant “Fatal Claw builds on familiar ideas to deliver a solid, visually stunning Metroidvania packed with content.” 8/10 – GameBlast GET INVOLVED About This Game Absorb the Power of the Infected and Discover New Ways to Attack! A fallen ancient kingdom has awakened once more through the technology of the Commoners. In this mysterious underworld where the Ancients and Commoners coexist, ferocious Infected, sealed exits, and countless secrets await. Join Kisha, a cat brought back to life with the help of his ancestors, to rescue survivors, absorb the power of the Infected, and find a way back to the surface. Discover Hidden Fusion Recipes and Unleash New Attacks! Hidden throughout the underworld are Fusion Recipes that transform Engraved Skills into entirely different attacks. Explore to uncover these recipes, then fuse the Engraved Skills you’ve absorbed to create attacks unlike anything you’ve used before. From devastating area attacks and adorable transformations to bombs with unpredictable results! Discover and experiment with a wide variety of Fusion Skills to unlock new possibilities in combat. Dynamic Combat with the Silver Claw and Golden Claw Use the Silver Claw’s Blink to break through enemy attacks and exploit openings, then strike back with the Golden Claw’s powerful charge attack. Use Engraved Skills absorbed from the Infected alongside Inherited Skills to unleash a variety of attacks, and develop your own combat style based on each enemy’s traits and attack patterns. Explore Freely with New Abilities Find your own path through an interconnected underworld. Squeeze through narrow passages, climb walls, and float through the air—use newly acquired movement abilities to open previously inaccessible paths and uncover hidden secrets and rewards even in areas you’ve already explored. Explore an underworld filled with ancient castles, mysterious natural environments, dangerous modern facilities, and hidden ruins, each with its own atmosphere and challenges. Powerful Bosses and Massive Threats Face powerful bosses, each with their own unique attack patterns and abilities. From tense chase sequences against massive enemies to intense one-on-one battles, a variety of challenges await Kisha. Learn your enemies’ attacks and use your newly acquired abilities and skills to overcome each threat in your own way. Uncover the Secrets of a Reawakened Ancient Kingdom Meet survivors who continue to hold on to hope even in this desperate underworld, and rescue those in danger. Follow the traces left behind throughout the underworld to uncover the secrets surrounding Kisha and the truth behind the corruption. You can also customize Kisha’s appearance with a variety of costumes as you continue your adventure.
