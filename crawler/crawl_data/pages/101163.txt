View Community Hub About This Game Cat Rooms: Sticker Decor! is a calm sticker game about cats, cozy rooms, and tiny spaces that come together on the pages of an album. Open new rooms, pick up stickers with tweezers, and gently find the right place for each one. A little café fills with cups, plants, soft light, and cute cats drinking coffee, reading, working behind the counter, or simply sitting somewhere cozy. Everything is made to help you relax and unwind: soft music, pleasant sticker sounds, and the small joy of adding each new detail. One sticker at a time, the room becomes a little more alive, warmer, and kinder. Features: Rooms for cats Create cozy cafés, bedrooms, book nooks, little shops, and other tiny spaces where cute cats live and work. Warm relaxing atmosphere No rush — just cozy rooms, calm music, and small pleasant actions. Cute cats The rooms are home to cats: baristas, visitors, little owners, readers, and helpers. They make every place feel warmer and more welcoming. Tiny joys in every detail A cup on the table, a lamp above the counter, a plant on the shelf — every sticker adds a little warmth to the room. Cat Rooms: Sticker Decor! is a small warm place to visit after a long day, where you can breathe out, step away from the noise, and simply create something kind and cozy.
