Organize 2000+ potions on shelves after the witch's cats have knocked them over, using clues around an enchanted cellar. Learn strange symbols, decipher cryptic notes, and uncover the correct shelf orders in a cozy organizational simple puzzle game. The witch had to leave on urgent business. By the time you arrive for your very first day as her apprentice. Her mischievous cats have knocked every potion off the shelves, turning the workshop into complete chaos. To leave a good first impression, we might as well put them back up. Gameplay Each shelf is its own handcrafted puzzle with unique rules waiting to be discovered. Sort and organize over 2,000+ potions , learning how every order works through experimentation, logic, and observation. Some puzzles can be solved at a glance, while others require finding hidden clues, using special items, or connecting information you've learned elsewhere in the workshop. Whenever you feel stuck, Pet the magical cats, then ask them for hints. They'll point you toward missing potions, highlight important clues, or even reveal part of a puzzle's solution when you need a little extra help. Features 20+ handcrafted potion-sorting puzzles with unique rules to discover. Organize and sort 2,000+ unique potions throughout the workshop. Relaxing gameplay with no timers or penalties. Cozy fantasy atmosphere inspired by witches, and alchemy. Cute cats!
