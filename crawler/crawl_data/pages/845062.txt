PHNL offers a unique air traffic control experience, combining inter-island, domestic, international, and cargo operations in one of the most scenic aviation environments on the planet. Manage a constant flow of arrivals and departures, coordinate complex ground movements, and keep traffic moving efficiently across a busy multi-runway airport. Background scenery created using OSM (Open Street Maps). With its diverse mix of aircraft, oceanic arrivals, and high-frequency regional traffic, PHNL provides an engaging challenge for controllers of all skill levels. Whether handling short inter-island hops or long-haul international flights, every session requires careful planning and precise coordination. Take the tower at Honolulu and oversee operations at the crossroads of the Pacific.
