Improve your skills, level up your Heroes, and move up the Leaderboard! EXTRAORDINARY HEROES You will encounter both old friends from the Shadow Fight universe and brand new characters. A charismatic demon, an experienced samurai, a queen of the criminal world, or a shadow bot — every player will find their favorite among dozens of unique Heroes. VARIETY OF COMBAT STYLES Each Hero has their own style and abilities. As you level up, you unlock special talents for your Heroes, allowing you to actively experiment with tactics and strategy! STORY MODE Stories is a PvE mode that provides a great opportunity to dive deeper into the world of Shadow Fight. You can learn more about your favorite Heroes in a rogue-like style. It's also a great way to take a break after intense ranked battles! DUELS WITH FRIENDS You can always challenge a friend to an online duel. It's a great way to practice or find out who's the best! APPEARANCE Skins for Heroes, weapons, fighting stances, taunts, victory poses, and even phrase packs. You will stand out! ..AND MUCH MORE!
