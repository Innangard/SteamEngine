About This Game In Swimpossible! you are Craig a reverse mermaid with an ambition to wiggle and flail swim upstream. Control each limb separately while dodging worms, ducks, waves and whatever else the river might throw at you. Hilarious to fail butt satisfying to beat, put your patience to the test in this swimming based rage-game. Find the momentum in your swimming movement. Every stroke counts... and so does every mistake. Master your leg and arm coordination... Or don't, we're not your boss. Race the clock and achieve enviable glory by setting new records on the global leaderboard... Until someone faster ruins it. Well, at least you might get temporary bragging rights. Prepare to lose progress, maybe parts of your dignity, but never lose that smile on your face... Probably. You will get better with practice... and also look and feel ridiculous the whole time. Failure is always an option... But hey, at least you have a great butt while doing it. Explore beautiful, vibrant yet serene environments... that might treacherously drag you back downstream. Become Craig and enjoy a lovable small story of belonging... that is , if you set aside all the bonking into worms or getting zapped by eels. Catch your breath in quiet and calm checkpoint areas where nothing can hurt you... Yet.
