Solve tasks that need real teamwork, survive monsters that each hunt differently, and figure out who you can trust before they decide for you. Full friend group or public lobby, doesn't matter; the chaos is the same either way Scream with each other Scream at each other Screw around with silly items and difficult co-op tasks Infected players have unique abilities that will help them cause chaos.
