You and up to 5 friends have to prevent a total meltdown while producing as much power as possible. WATCH OUT! All objects are physics based, and fuel rods are fragile, bump into a doorway or a coworker too hard, and you’re both gone. MONITOR THE SITUATION The reactor is under constant change, cooling needs to be adjusted, fuel rods need to be replaced and thrown out, power will cut off and fuses will blow. But keep your cool in the control room and everything will be okay (probably). COMMUNICATION IS KEY The only way to keep the reactor stable is to work together. If you are in the control room, you can’t take action in time, so grab a phone and connect with your friends running around in the reactor. IMPROVE AND UPGRADE Upgrade your reactor with improved fuel rods, or new equipment.
