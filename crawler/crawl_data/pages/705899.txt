View Community Hub About This Game What is Minatro? Minatro brings the famous gambling game Mines into a roguelite experience. Open tiles to earn money and reach your target. Item Synergies Making the right choices in the Black Market can create powerful item synergies, allowing your items to work together and generate even greater profits. Buy Items to Multiply Your Earnings Purchase powerful items that multiply your earnings and help you reach your target faster Boss Levels Diffrent bosses you have to defeat. Black Market Upgrades Use your coins to purchase permanent Black Market upgrades, such as reducing shop prices or increasing the number of items you can buy per visit. Active UI Elements Minatro features active UI elements that enhance the player's experience. Cursed Items Opening the Mystery Box comes with high-risk, high-reward items that can either push your run much further or bring it closer to an early end. Permanent Upgrades When your run ends, spend your earned permanent upgrades to make your next run easier. Is This Gambling?
