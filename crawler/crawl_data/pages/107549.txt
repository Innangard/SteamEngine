Grid Ranger is an arcade style shoot-em-up with laser sharp aesthetics and a retro-futuristic soundtrack. Practice your mouse dexterity as you blow through obstacles and dodge projectiles in a game reminiscent of the arcade. Trade your Blows Use your projectiles to target objects in your path, creating new ways to move through the grid. Linear Targeting: Use the grid against them by firing along it. Line up your character to take aim at enemies. Dodge and Weave: Avoid obstacles that might end your run while targeting the opponents in front of you. Power Up your Escape. Dodge and weave between enemies to pick up extra lives in preparation for the boss at the end of each level. Destroy your Enemies: explode different targets and collect the glittering diamonds they leave behind. Extra Chances: Salvage a run by grabbing extra lives from destroyed enemies. Be Prepared: Each level ends with a unique boss and attack sets. Do your best to grab new powerups and gain an edge on each level. Advance Beyond the Grid Play across three levels and an infinite mode in a difficulty of your choosing, all while immersed in the arcade game atmosphere. Featuring 8 original tracks and precise retro graphics, this is a nostalgia hit you won’t want to miss. Do you have what it takes to escape the grid?
