An entire generation has been born and raised underground, and their besieged Metro Station-Cities struggle for survival; with each other, and the mutant horrors that await outside. You are Artyom, born in the last days before the fire, but raised underground. Having never ventured beyond the city limits, one fateful event sparks a desperate mission to the heart of the Metro system, to warn the remnants of mankind of a terrible impending threat. Your journey takes you from the forgotten catacombs beneath the Metro to the desolate wastelands above, where your actions will determine the fate of mankind. But what if the real threat comes from within? UPDATED FOR STEAM Experience the post-apocalyptic depths of the Moscow Metro like never before with the Next-Gen Update. METRO 2033 Redux has been enhanced with numerous visual and technical improvements, including 4K Textures; TSR, DLSS and FSR support; increased Geometry and Texture details; improved PBR materials; spatial audio support; improved Animations and sound quality, and more! METRO 2033 Redux also adds supports for the Xbox Controller Impulse Triggers and DualSense controller Adaptive Triggers, immersing you further in the fight for survival.
