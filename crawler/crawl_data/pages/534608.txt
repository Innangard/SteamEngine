Go Slimey Go! doesn't rely on massive marketing or big promises, but rather on a simple yet effective design and gameplay that hooks you from the very first moment.” Kotaku About This Game Become Slimey, a tiny blob with an unstoppable bounce, and make your way through colorful worlds full of platforming challenges, hidden paths, dangerous hazards, and plenty of secrets. Build momentum, master your movement, and discover just how far a slime can bounce. Every Bounce Counts Simple controls with plenty of room to improve. Bounce across huge gaps, slide under obstacles, launch yourself through the air, and combine your abilities to reach places that seemed impossible at first. Whether you're aiming for the finish, every collectible, or a new personal best, there's always another route to find. Explore. Experiment. Improve. Travel through 5 unique worlds, each with 5 handcrafted levels and its own mechanics and surprises. Collect enough treasures to unlock 10 bonus levels filled with even tougher challenges. Every level rewards exploration. Hidden rooms, optional paths, and secret collectibles are waiting for players willing to look beyond the obvious. And when you've mastered everything else, there's one final boss waiting for you.
