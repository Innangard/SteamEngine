Everything is gone. Only these two brave capybaras remain, determined to raise their community from the ashes. Can you click your way to survival, growth, and a future among the stars? Gather Resources Increase your resources either by clicking or by assigning capybaras to rooms in your colony. Unlock Technology and Upgrade your Colony Research new technology and innovation to advance your colony. Watch your shelter evolve as you transition through different levels. Achievements and Outfits Conquer achievements to gather Capycoins . Use your hard-earned currency to buy adorable outfits and raise up the style of your colony. Features ✅ What Capyvarias offers: A short clicker : Demo is 15 minutes long and full-game is around 2 hours of playtime, dynamic resource management and light decisions. Simple game mechanics : Increase your resources, upgrade to get new ones, and advance through time. A post-apocalyptic cozy vibe : Hand-drawn capybaras, cute art and lovely music! ❌ What Capyvarias does NOT offer: No idler : This is an active experience. Leaving your capybaras unattended for too long can lead to losing the colony! No prestige levels : The game does not offer permanent upgrades that are kept over different colony runs. No deep narrative and many hours-long objectives. No multiplayer of any kind. Dev Team Hi! I'm Kirani , and this is my first game.
