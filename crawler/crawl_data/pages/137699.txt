Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Content Years have passed. Another winter has come to the gas station, and with it, new room to grow. Return to familiar ground as a new hero, a raccoon, and see how much has changed. Turn a quick roadside pit stop into a popular winter resort where tourists come to spend their holidays! Take the old business further with new winter services: clear the snow off the grounds, convert the store's back rooms into a rental counter and a diner, build an ice rink, and carve ski runs into the nearby slopes. Grow your infrastructure to draw more tourists and keep the place running through the harshest winter. TURN THE GAS STATION INTO A WINTER RESORT Set up an ice rink and ski slopes, run guided winter tours, and put your guests up in cozy cabins. Expand and upgrade your new services and keep them in good repair, so the resort grows more appealing and can host more vacationers. Dress up the grounds with themed decor and make the familiar gas station a place where travelers will want to stay a while. TAKE CARE OF YOUR GUESTS Clear out the cluttered back rooms of the old store and convert them into a rental counter and a food prep area. Fit your guests with the right gear for winter fun, keep an eye on your stock, order ingredients, and cook up dishes for hungry vacationers. And don't forget the register and the pumps: even after all these years, not everyone out here in the backwoods has switched to an EV. EXPAND AND AUTOMATE The bigger the resort gets, the more power its services draw. Grow your infrastructure along with your business and make sure everything you add has the electricity it needs. And once you have energy to spare, bring in automated helpers. Put them in charge of cleaning, restocking, and helping out with guests.
