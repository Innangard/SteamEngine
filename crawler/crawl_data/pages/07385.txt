Free Demo preview available NOW: ^ This demo is VERY DIFFERENT from the version of the game you can buy right now ^ Spellbook Demonslayers is a fast-paced roguelite bullet heaven game inspired by my favorite parts of Vampire Survivors and Path of Exile !  Customize your playstyle with magic, charms, abilities, and demon summoning to become the most overpowered slayer the Library has ever seen! Do you have what it takes to destroy the demonic horde, or will the Cosmic Library be lost forever? Creative Builds — A huge variety of upgrades, equipment, and abilities make every run unique! Take risks with the Shady Smuggler — Break the rules and completely change how your character works with Illegal Upgrades! Steal Powers from Enemies — Stack massive amounts of buffs! Slay demons the way you want — Choose between Autoaim, Manual Aim, or Directional Aim Customize your Upgrade path — Evolve and Mutate your auras to unlock unique effects! Customizable Difficulty and Rewards — Earn powerful charms, demonic essence, equipment, and more! 💖Thank you for all of your support!
