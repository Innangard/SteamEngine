Only the 1st day is playable. The Plant Shop is a cozy horror game where you manage a small plant shop. Craft plants, serve late-night customers and grow your plant collection to attract more people. You play as Silvia, a 26 years old girl who decides to open her own small plant shop on a late-night shift because she has a corporate job during the day. Your job is to serve the customers coming to the shop. Depending on the order you will have to craft flowers, plants or cacti. Crafting plants is easy. You just need a plant pot, soil, a seed and water. The customers talk about a curse that struck the town 30 years ago. As you manage your shop, you begin to see and hear strange things. Is the curse real—or just an old legend? FEATURES Gameplay: Narrative walking simulator with a simple game loop: take orders from customers, craft plants and serve them. VHS film aesthetics: Emulates the look of old CRT screens that adds an eerie atmosphere. Immersive Story: Experience Silvia’s journey and uncover the truth about The Plant Shop and the strange events happening in town.
