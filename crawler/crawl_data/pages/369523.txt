Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We want to have players begin playing the experience midway through development in order to make sure we create the best experience possible.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We don't currently have a planned launch date, however as we continue to add content and features we will set a solid launch date once we are comfortable with where things are in the development process.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to add additional chapters, SCPs, and content that the community has suggested. We also plan to improve the existing features and mechanics of the game to bring it to its full potential. One major feature we plan to add during Early Access is PvP and co-op, where players will fight for survival in "The Hunt"—a 4v2 mode where players can choose to play as the legendary MTF Zeta-14 or side with dangerous anomalies.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current state of Early Access is Chapter One, which is the introduction to the world of SCP: Fragmented Minds and teaches the main mechanics, introduces the story, and gives players an introduction to combat and stealth mechanics with various enemies and puzzles.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will change during the early access period after a significant amount of new content has been added. The price will likely change upon final release out of early access however we will always give players a warning before making any pricing changes.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “The community will be involved closely in the development process and can provide feedback and many different suggestions in the Steam Discussion section or through Discord and various other social media outlets. I don't know how much time you have, so I'll get straight to the point. You're at Mars Site-113, one of our largest anomalous research and containment facilities. Or at least, it used to be. We lost contact with Earth after the Near-Extinction Event (NEE). Whatever happened, it wasn't good. The SCPs we transferred here for safekeeping have broken containment, and the situation is... dire. We estimate there are only a few hundred personnel left, down from the tens of thousands who were stationed here. Communications are barely holding up, and you're the only one we've been able to reach. This isn't a rescue mission. This is the last line of defense. You are humanity's last hope. Secure. Contain. Protect. This message will not repeat. Dr. ████████ SCP Foundation ======================================================================== Immerse yourself in the largest Foundation facility in existence. Hack, weld, and parkour your way through a crumbling complex, stretching across distinct zones each with varying levels of danger, ██████, potential resources, and even ███████ Use your own blend of stealth and combat to weave your way through Site-113’s increasingly unpredictable SCP inhabitants. Adapt your strategy and tactics using weapons and even SCPs themselves to claw back the upper hand. Make choices, learn the consequences, and use your tools to access new and unique paths throughout the facility. However, in a world marred by deception and lies be very careful who you trust and why – you never know how large the consequences may be. ----------------------------- Content Warning Section SCP: Fragmented Minds is a first-person action-survival horror game where players encounter terrifying anomalies known as SCPs. The game features intense violence, graphic depictions of gore, and disturbing events affecting both survivors within the facility and the player character’s team. Players will use a variety of weapons and unique tools to combat hostile creatures, face stressful situations that demand tough decisions with grim consequences, and confront the unsettling realities managed by the SCP Foundation.
