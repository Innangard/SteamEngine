We want to integrate the feedback from you into the game in a good way and develop it with you.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “There are updates that we think should be in our game. In the full version of the game, we want to release the game with different mechanics and a different loop of events waiting for you every time you enter the game. We don't want to keep this process too long. A period of about 6 months will be enough.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In the full version, we plan to make sure that there are no bugs in the game, that you don't get bored while playing and that you can play for a long time. We plan to add additional mechanics to our game and add more based on your feedback. We also plan to make our community better until the full release and to be intertwined with our community in the full release.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current state of the early access release offers a game with basic systems.
