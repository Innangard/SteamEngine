As an investigator unexpectedly drawn into a labyrinthine plot, you'll delve deep into the heart of the mystery, navigating bustling restaurants, solemn police stations, cluttered grocery stores, and even a hidden, enigmatic laboratory. In the game, you'll need to repeatedly switch scenes, interact with various characters, and solve a series of interconnected puzzles to eventually uncover the secret behind it all. In this demo, due to the intricate connection between different game processes, not all puzzles can be solved with the existing information. We apologize for this. The demo currently includes three task cards and three scenes, allowing you to experience our intended gameplay and fun. The game is still in development, and if you find it intriguing, we highly recommend adding the full game to your wishlist. We will keep you updated with any news or demo updates. Employ your wit and courage to unravel intricately designed puzzles, acquire essential items, and gradually uncover the secrets that have ensnared you in this bizarre world. As you explore, you'll need to meticulously untangle the complex storyline, map out character relationships, and skillfully select and use items to solve seemingly impossible puzzles. Stay alert and actively engage with the environment by clicking, interacting, and conversing with characters to gather clues and items that will lead you closer to the truth and the hidden forces at play. While Shattered Dimension Demo features a distinct art style compared to other CottonGame titles, it maintains the studio's commitment to innovation and unique interpretations of fantastical worlds. Embark on a mind-bending adventure filled with imaginative elements and a romantic atmosphere. Join us on this challenging and captivating puzzle-solving journey!
