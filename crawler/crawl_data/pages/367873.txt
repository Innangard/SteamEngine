Times Tables Hunt combines an educational game that is simply fun with the purposeful repetition of math exercises. After all, who doesn't like popping balloons? Why parents and kids love Times Tables Hunt: * 3D Physics Gameplay: Practice math by aiming and throwing darts at moving balloons – learning through action and interaction! * Adaptive Help System: If a child gets stuck, the game doesn't just show the answer. It guides them step-by-step (e.g., splitting sums, reversing order) to foster true mathematical understanding. * Stress-Free Mode: Enable the "Simple Mode" to make balloons bigger, slower, and fixed forward so younger children can read numbers easily and aim at their own pace. * Additional Motivation: Solving exercises unlocks new, colorful sceneries, balloon shapes, and arrows to keep the learning experience fresh and engaging. --- Pedagogically Valuable & Safe We have developed this game in close cooperation with trained teachers as well as parents of elementary school children. Times Tables Hunt is perfect if you want to give your children your PC or laptop and have a clear conscience. In line with our mission, we refrain completely from additional purchase content, advertising, and any data collection from your children.
