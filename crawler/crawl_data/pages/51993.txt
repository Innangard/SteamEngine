About This Game In the mystical world of “Abyss Fantasia”, players will take on the role of the “missing” girl, Eugaly, as she embarks on an expedition into the Abyss. Here, you'll encounter a diverse cast of characters and unexpected adventures. The treasures you find in your exploration and the choices you make can be collected in the “The Bok of Fate” and transformed into cards. Utilize these cards to unravel complex mysteries, alter the destiny of the characters you meet, and confront the perilous monsters dwelling in the Abyss. The Abyss, a massive chasm on the continent, is deep and mysterious, with no end in sight. Those who venture into it are affected by the Abyss, with the weak-willed losing their sanity and humanity, twisted into unrecognizable forms. Yet, the precious treasures hidden within lure explorers to journey into its depths. In the depths of the Abyss lies a tale for every soul, with multiple outcomes to every story,all hinging on your decisions. You can alter others by placing cards, thereby influencing the plot, or employ unexpected cards to achieve a certain purpose. Exploring within the Abyss, where every scene is a hand-drawn work of art, singular and mesmerizing. Discover new landscapes and Abyssal monsters, admire the surreal beauty of the world below, and wield various abilities and cards to uncover what lies hidden. Retreat to the city for rest before you lose your sanity, and immerse yourself in the charm of “Cynthia,” a town perched on the edge of the Abyss. Gather provisions, forge new equipment, and select select your very own unique “relics” to assist in your exploration. As you delve into the Abyss, you will face strange and formidable monsters. Engage them with cards and relics, discovering unexpected effects from different combinations. The same card can take on various forms under different circumstances; wield them wisely and unleash your creativity to triumph in battle.
