Raise livestock, cultivate new crops, and plant bountiful orchards as you expand your village’s rural economy with new production chains and resources. Key Features New Aspiration: Become the Master of Harvests , developing your agricultural economy and hosting an increasingly prestigious Harvest Festival. Common Crofts: Raise chickens, pigs, and geese in modular Common Crofts to sustain your growing population. Bountiful Orchards: Grow apples, plums, and hazelnuts across three new specialized Orchard Farms. Vegetable Farming: Diversify your fields with dedicated plots for cabbages and turnips. The Ultimate Celebration: Expand your Tavern with the customizable Festival Grounds and prepare the perfect Harvest Festival. Cook feasts, serve drinks, hire entertainers, and grow a modest gathering into the most prestigious celebration in the realm. DLC Content Includes 10 New Buildings: A modular Common Croft, 3 Orchard Farms, 2 Vegetable Farms, Drying Kiln, Cider House, the Tavern’s Festival Grounds, the Monastic Croft, totalling more than 120 new building parts . 14 New Resources: Including Pigs, Chickens, Geese, Eggs, Apples, Plums, Hazelnuts, Cider, Cabbages, Turnips and more. A Grand Harvest Festival: The Harvest Festival, featuring unlockable decorations and unique rewards. New Progression: A dedicated Aspiration and a prestigious new Village Title. New Characters & Animals: Pigs, piglets, chickens, chicks, roosters, geese, and goslings—plus traveling minstrels, jugglers, and performers. Expanded Gameplay: Unlock new production chains, cook Festive Meals and design your own Festival Grounds. 20 Minutes of Brand-New Music: Immerse yourself in the festive atmosphere with an original soundtrack featuring the lute, a new instrument making its debut in the game’s music.
