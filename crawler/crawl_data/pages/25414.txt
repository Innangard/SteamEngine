View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Idle Shuguang's Alchemy Store ! This is a pleasant and relaxing idle management game. You can place it in the corner of your screen to accompany you during work and study, allowing you to enjoy a tranquil shopkeeping experience amid your busy life. As an apprentice alchemist, Shuguang has studied diligently for a long time and finally received the first internship assessment from the mentor. The mentor hopes that Shuguang will grow steadily while running the shop, evolving from a trainee with solid theoretical knowledge into an outstanding alchemist who can skillfully and proficiently wield alchemy. In your spare time, you may watch Shuguang run this tiny shop with dedication. Manage your store, craft alchemical goods, raise your alchemy level, and unlock a wealth of new recipes. Along the journey, you will also meet distinctive companions, build friendships and create unique bonding stories together. No complicated controls are required. Fully idle-friendly, Shuguang will automatically take orders, conduct alchemy production and deliver all items, bringing you a casual and soothing shop life. In the end, raise your alchemy level to Lv99 to complete the first alchemist assessment. While progressing through tasks and growing stronger, you can freely decorate your shop and create your own warm and one-of-a-kind alchemy store.
