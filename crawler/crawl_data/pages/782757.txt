SWEYET (etc.) is a single player (always) , first person (generally) , game (mostly) . SWEYET uses optional eye tracking technology utilizing your everyday webcam. Through the physical action of real-life blinking, the world changes around you, drawing inspiration from the unpredictable nature of the films that play in our heads between each day. THE FAMILIAR DISCOMFORT Dreamscapes are connected, with your path branching based on the outcome of each world. The puzzles within opt for a new approach, typically obscuring controls, forcing one to think outside the box and confront their relationship with gaming and their expectation of "control".
