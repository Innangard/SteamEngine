View Community Hub About This Game Chef Pinball blends the delicious chaos of pinball with roguelike mechanics inspired by the greatest deckbuilders. Discover incredible synergies, recruit colorful chefs, and get your dopamine fix every run! Will you be able to turn your moving train into the ultimate restaurant? Discover all kinds of ingredients, recipes, and events throughout your runs and explore their countless synergies. Prepare juicy burgers, crispy pizzas, steaming bowls of ramen, and much more. Grill your ingredients, recruit new chefs, and trigger increasingly outrageous bonuses. When rats invade the kitchen, grab your hammer! And if everything gets out of control, a rain of burgers might just save your service. Jackpot! Unlock a variety of items and upgrades that will completely transform your runs. Strengthen your favorite recipes, recruit new employees, increase your income, and discover increasingly absurd combinations. Will you invest in an army of hyperactive cooks? Upgrade your kitchen to serve meals faster? Or rely on ridiculous combos capable of flooding your train with food and money? Travel through a variety of environments and expand your restaurant on rails throughout your runs. Upgrade your kitchen, recruit new chefs, unlock powerful bonuses, and grow your business while keeping your chef-ball rolling. FEATURES 20 unique items and upgrades with distinct effects. 30 ingredients and recipes to discover. 25 train environments and themes to explore. An endless mode for limitless replayability. A one-of-a-kind cooking and pinball adventure packed with dopamine.
