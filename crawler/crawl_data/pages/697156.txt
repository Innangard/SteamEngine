Buy RoadCraft - Foundations Expansion $14.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Foundations Expansion introduces two new maps, seven vehicles, and new construction-focused gameplay systems centered around concrete work, logistics, and infrastructure projects. From the Dutch countryside to a desert oasis recovering from a devastating tornado, players must restore critical infrastructure, prepare construction sites, and support rebuilding efforts using new tools, materials, and workflows. Requires the base game RoadCraft to run. The Foundations Expansion includes the following content: Two new 4km² maps featuring unique construction challenges and rebuilding opportunities. Spring Renewal: Set in the Netherlands, Spring Renewal takes place in a long-abandoned agricultural region recovering from decades of decline. Following a powerful storm, roads, bridges, and industrial facilities require extensive repairs. Players must restore infrastructure, rebuild damaged sites, and support the area's transformation into a new green-energy hub. Oasis Delta: Once a prosperous trade and agricultural hub, Oasis Delta blends desert landscapes, waterways, and industrial infrastructure damaged by a devastating tornado.Players must restore ports, reconnect isolated locations, rebuild key facilities, and support recovery efforts across a region shaped by water, sand, and extreme weather. Seven new vehicles designed for construction, logistics, and infrastructure projects: ALCES PL30C Pile Driver – Specialized tracked equipment used to install concrete piles and prepare foundations. Voltrack Mk11 Concrete Pumper – A dedicated vehicle designed to pour concrete foundations. Voltrack MX  – A concrete mixer used to transport and supply concrete to construction sites. EPEC MAG240 – A magnetic crane capable of handling multiple units of metal debris and recyclable materials at once. Derry Longhorn 4520 HH – A heavy hauler equipped with a wider trailer for transporting larger vehicles and equipment. Isonzo M1 Service Vehicle – A compact support vehicle providing field access to resources and vehicle management functions. Aramatsu R1 Asphalt Roller – A roadwork vehicle designed for surface preparation and finishing operations. New foundation-building gameplay: For the first time in RoadCraft, players can take part in additional stages of the construction process through new foundation-building mechanics. Pile Driving: Produce, transport, and install concrete piles to create durable foundations for future construction. Concrete Foundation Laying: Use concrete mixers and concrete pumpers to pour foundations for buildings and infrastructure projects, managing materials and logistics along the way. New mission themes and sandbox progression: Restore ports, bridges, roads, and windmills. Prepare construction sites, develop new infrastructure, manage material supply chains, and support ambitious rebuilding projects using specialized machinery and logistics networks.
