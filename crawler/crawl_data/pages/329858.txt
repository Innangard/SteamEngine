View Community Hub About This Game Be Your Own Boss! You rented a small shop with the last money in your pocket. Now it's time to show off your art and rewrite the rules of this town! Listen to customer requests, choose the right design, and get to work. Earn money by providing tattoo and piercing services, but be careful; every mistake lowers customer satisfaction. Use different levels of tattoo machines, manage ink stocks, and keep your equipment in working order at all times. Don't settle for just a tattoo shop! Use your earnings to buy cafes, pizzerias, and hotels in the city to generate passive income. Create extra revenue streams by acquiring hotdog and ice cream selling licenses. Roam freely with your motorcycle and explore the city. Track every detail of your business. Manage your inventory, monitor your shop's popularity and cleanliness. As your business grows, hire employees who each possess different skills. Maintain your financial balance through the banking app and keep your shop safe from bankruptcy. The city's thugs are not open to change, so they might scare away your customers. Grab your bat and defend your shop! Stop these thugs from damaging your business. The city isn't always quiet and sunny. Changing weather conditions and city events can impact your entire business. Letters from the municipality can change your fate. Always be prepared for events happening in the city. Return to the comfort of your home after a busy day. Leave the stress of work behind, take care of your pets, and make time for yourself. Find balance in your life within the day and night cycle.
