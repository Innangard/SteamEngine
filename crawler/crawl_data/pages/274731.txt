About This Game UFOPHILIA is a first-person psychological horror game where you play as an investigator obsessed with the UFO phenomenon. Explore remote locations full of anomalies, use specialized technology, and face the unknown. Your goal: discover which alien is present… and manage to photograph it. Each mission is random—you never know what type of alien you’ll encounter. But with the help of the UFOPHILIA software, you’ll have all the necessary tools to search, identify, and photograph any alien. But it won’t be easy—they're watching us too. You'll experience terrifying phenomena. Some aliens are aggressive, others curious, even playful. Embark on this unsettling adventure, as fascinating as it is dangerous. WHAT’S YOUR MISSION? Phase 1 – Select the mission: Missions are locations with reports of close encounters. The more missions you complete, the more missions you'll unlock. Phase 2 – Identification: Use the available tools to determine what kind of alien is in the area, but don’t overuse them. Just a tip. Phase 3 – Spawn zone: You must identify the spawn zone, a smaller area where the alien first appeared. It’s variable and essential for gathering the required evidence. Phase 4 – This is not a game: In Phase 4 the alien becomes visible—it’s time to take the photo. Each alien behaves differently, and you must complete their unique objectives to get close enough. Read their descriptions carefully. KEY FEATURES Atmospheric horror: Missions are unpredictable. Spawn zones and alien types vary. Aliens can be curious, aggressive, try to abduct you, or simply play with your fears. Nine unique alien types: Each with distinct behaviors, signs, reactions, and weaknesses. Remember—this is not a game. Abductions are terrifying events. Specialized tools: EMF detectors, night vision cameras, remote microphones, microwave meters, or heart rate sensors are just some of the tools at your disposal. Real device synergies: Certain tools enhance others when used strategically. Not everything is in the manual. Roswell Points and level-based economy: Unlock upgrades, new tools, and scenarios by earning Roswell Points and prestige levels. Full replayability: Aliens spawn randomly. Every mission is different and unique. Many factors can make your run short or long. Endings and ranks based on performance: From “Forum Conspiracy Theorist” to the prestigious “Roswell Agent.” Achieve the highest rank in every mission to become a legend.
