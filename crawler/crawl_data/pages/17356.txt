About This Game Enjoy a pure single-player experience with zero in-app purchases, build a powerful defense with heroes and turrets, and protect your castle from endless monster waves! Your heroes and turrets will continue fighting in the background while resources accumulate automatically over time. As monsters grow stronger, you'll need to upgrade your defenses, adjust your strategy, and make the most of your ultimates. Every battle strengthens your lineup and fortifies your castle, helping you build an unstoppable stronghold that keeps monster waves at bay. The game also supports a boss key, allowing you to instantly hide the game window whenever needed. Recruit new heroes, pair them with turrets, and build the ultimate defense team. Every unit brings unique abilities and strengths to the battlefield. Mix elements, attack styles, and crowd control effects to discover powerful combinations. Whether you prefer steady firepower to hold back monster waves, devastating area attacks to sweep the battlefield, or explosive bursts to bring down elite foes, you can shape a playstyle that’s uniquely yours. Experiment with different lineups, adjust your defenses, and find the combination that works best for you! Every idle reward helps fuel your growing army. Gold, experience, and talent points can be invested into upgrading your heroes, turrets, and castle. Skill trees, treasures, and equipment provide even more ways to customize your lineup and strengthen your defenses. Resources continue to accumulate while you’re away, letting you return ready for greater challenges. There’s no need to constantly watch the battlefield—your castle keeps advancing with every battle. As your defenses grow stronger, even the bosses that once challenged you will fall with ease! Your fortress stands ready, but the monster waves never stop. Keep growing your defenses, gather resources through countless battles, and take on whatever challenges await.
