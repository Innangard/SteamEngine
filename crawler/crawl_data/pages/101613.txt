Stop her from bringing her belongings into your house and from posting your photos on social media. Protect your peaceful, ordinary life at all costs. March, just before graduation. And where will this bold ghost girl’s unwavering affection lead? A strange seven-day cohabitation where fear and affection slowly invade everyday life.
