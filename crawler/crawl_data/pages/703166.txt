Depending on the cards you select, Lazriel will evolve into either an angel or fall victim to the influence of demons. To survive, you'll need to utilize the powerful Demon Cards from time to time. But be wary—overusing these evil powers may lead to Lazriel's physical and mental decline. Do you have what it takes to resist the demons' temptation and continue on the path of holiness? STORY Lazriel is a star pupil at the school in the sky, in the heavenly world ruled by goddess Emelia. One fateful day, however, on her way back from a school ceremony, Lazriel is thrown down into the demon world. There is only one way to return: by climbing the demon-infested tower. Can Lazriel successfully climb the tower and return safely to the heavenly world? HOW TO PLAY This game uses a turn-based system in which you and your enemy take turns. Lazriel can use two types of cards to inflict damage on enemies: Angel Cards and Demon Cards. However, overusing the Demon Cards' evil powers may lead to Lazriel's physical and mental decline.
