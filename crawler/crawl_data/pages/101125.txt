Player 1 drives the front wheels and Player 2 drives the rear, each with their own gas, gears and steering. Move together and the car flies. Disagree and it spins out of control. One Car, Two Drivers The car only moves when you both commit. One of you hesitates and nothing happens. Steer the same way to turn, and count down every jump together, or fall together. Climb the Stunt Tower A giant course reaching into the sky, packed with swinging hammers, spinning gears, laser grids, wind, oil and boost pads that launch you across open air. Everything is timed, so watch it, wait for it, then go. Race Up to 4 Cars Up to 8 players, two in every car, so up to 4 cars on the tower at once. In the lobby, pick your car and your seat (front or rear), then race to the top. A live leaderboard shows who's leading and who just fell. The first car through the finish wins. When It Goes Wrong (And It Will) Miss a jump and you're back at your last checkpoint. Crashes send your drivers flying with full ragdoll physics, and the results board remembers every fall. Play Online With Friends Each player on their own PC, with their own camera and full voice chat. You're going to need it.
