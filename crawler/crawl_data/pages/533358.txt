View Community Hub About This Game Inhale the aroma of fresh bread and become the most thoughtful chef in Make my Sandwich ! This is a cute, relaxing puzzle game where every sandwich is a tiny masterpiece crafted specifically for your guest. Study the notes, decipher preferences, and mind the exclusions. Your task isn’t just to assemble a snack—it’s to truly listen and understand. Every customer arrives with a unique order card. What’s on the menu today? "I want something light, but no fish" or "Anything but cheese, I have an allergy" ? Experience Every Ingredient. The gameplay is built on tactile drag-and-drop mechanics. Choose your base: Soft white bread, crispy egg bread, or healthy seeded bread? Drag it onto the cutting board. Build the filling: Layer by layer, stack fresh vegetables, mouth-watering meats, fish, or cheese. Add a drink: The perfect sandwich needs the perfect companion—be it aromatic coffee, tea, or a refreshing soda. In Make my Sandwich , there are no timers and no rush. This is your personal cozy corner where you can enjoy the process of creating beautiful food to the sound of calm music. Progress through dozens of levels, from simple orders to complex culinary puzzles where every detail matters. Meet every requirement, and every sandwich will be perfect! Key Features: Care in Every Card: Solve your customers' wishes by taking their diets, allergies, and taste preferences into account. Tactile Assembly: Simply drag and drop ingredients and bread onto the board to create appetizing layers. Rich Variety: Dozens of cute, detailed items—from various types of bread to fish and meat fillings and drinks. Cozy & Relaxing: Enjoy aesthetic art, peaceful gameplay, and a pleasant atmosphere with zero pressure. For Everyone: A heartwarming game that is perfect for unwinding at any age.
