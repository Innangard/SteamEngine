A turn-based puzzler with a teleporting mechanic about sneaking through an ancient crypt, while avoiding its traps and the undead. The Story The prophecy speaks of an ancient evil that slumbers within a crypt. It foretells its awakening which will lead to our world being consumed by its dark shadow. Your brothers at the monastery dismiss it as a pagan heresy, however you know better than that. The world's fate rests on your shoulders. It's up to you to find the slumbering evil and seal it, before it awakens. Can you save our world? What awaits you 60 challenging levels Boss fights Thinking with teleports Infinite undos Skeletons, Vampires, Spikes and more!
