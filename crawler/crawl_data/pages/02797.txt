About The Frozen Trail Reward Pass The Frozen Trail Reward Pass consists of 60 levels of in-game rewards, and two tracks: the Free Track, available to all players, and the Premium Track included in this bundle (can also be obtained separately). The Premium Track allows access to up to 50 additional rewards within the Frozen Trail Reward Pass, including exclusive Raider cosmetics and up to 1,150 total Raider Tokens.
