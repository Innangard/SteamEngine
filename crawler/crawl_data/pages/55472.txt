A disgraced medium. A gallery without windows. An elixir which permits the most final of feasts. A gnawing hunger which cannot be denied. All reflected in the snow-coloured eyes of Miss Naenia, gentlest of the Elegiast's Names. Dig in cemeteries. Desecrate tombs. Consume the dead to unearth their secrets or commemorate them. Comfort or betray the living. Eternize the lost Hours in the Palest of Paintings. Just be careful to feed your hunger before it grows beyond you. The GHOUL DLC includes: Make a shadowy living as a disgraced medium. Hold imaginary conversations with paintings. Serve the purposes of the Elegiast, though they may cross the purposes of the other Hours. Seek a beautiful end. Become a Winter Long, immortal and matchless beneath claw of the Ivory Dove. Or lose yourself to a power that grows Nowhere. Find and rob graveyards. Consume the lesser and the greater dead to learn their secrets. Share those secrets with the living, or paint them into your canvases. Consume the corpses of your followers, and gain a little something extra from their more interesting scars.
