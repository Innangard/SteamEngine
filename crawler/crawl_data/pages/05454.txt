Your home kingdom is in danger, and it's up to you to recruit and expand an ever-growing band of brave warriors, wizards, and all sorts of adventurers who relentlessly attack a single, gigantic dragon. The game is a numbers-driven idle experience: buy more troops, upgrade their power, automate your economy, and click the dragon yourself for bonus damage. This game expands on the idle incremental genre while keeping its most important feature: NUMBERS... GOING...
