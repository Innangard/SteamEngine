Find out everything about dinosaurs in mini-games and discover the secrets of the prehistoric age. Dino Dino is designed to playfully introduce big and small dinosaur fans to the world of dinosaurs. In a modern design and with hand-drawn illustrations, Dino Dino sheds light on the way of life, appearance, social and hunting behavior and conveys basic knowledge about the different dinosaur species. Thanks to a cooperation with the Paleontological Society of Germany , the latest findings await you, as they have never been found in any other dinosaur game before. What did the dinosaurs actually look like? Did Spinosaurus eat fish? Which one is heavier, an excavator or Tyrannosaurus Rex? How big did Brachiosaurus get? Find out in our game – and even create your own coloring dinosaurs! These dinosaurs can be discovered: Allosaurus Ankylosaurus Archaeopteryx Baryonyx Brachiosaurus Deinonychus Dilophosaurus Diplodocus Gallimimus Iguanodon Maiasaura Microraptor Pachycephalosaurus Parasaurolophus Plateosaurus Psittacosaurus Spinosaurus Stegosaurus Titanoceratops Triceratops Tyrannosaurus Ubirajara Utahraptor Velociraptor Mini-games that are waiting for you: Paint dinos and give them a feather coat! Feed dinosaurs and find out what they liked to eat! Which one is heavier, an excavator or Tyrannosaurus Rex? Dig through different layers of rock and soil until you find the bones of the biggest dinosaurs! Put dinos back in one piece correctly, like a puzzle! Compare dinosaurs in the size parade! Discover how dinosaurs are related to one another in the family tree! Take a closer look at the special features of the dinosaurs in the detail view! Deepen your knowledge in the dino card collection! Rummage through an extensive catalog of questions anwered by real paleontologists!
