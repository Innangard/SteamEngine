You're not just taking a test; you're taking a psychopathy exam, and the results are far more disturbing than a label. Presented through an eerie VHS aesthetic , this experience immerses you in a chillingly nostalgic environment . Answer a series of questions designed to probe your deepest thoughts and impulses. Every choice has consequences , each more unsettling than the last, as the screen flickers and the tape warps under the weight of your decisions. Are you ready to find out if you're truly a psychopath? Or will the test itself drive you to madness? The only way to know is to press play. Retro 80s Analog Horror: Immerse yourself in a true 80s horror experience complete with VHS aesthetics, spooky noises, and unnerving atmospheres. Psychopathy Test: Respond to a series of frightening and penetrating questions intended to delve into your psyche. Will the results show something sinister, or is this just a game? Dynamic Consequences: Each decision has an impact on the atmosphere and your final ending, guaranteeing that no two playthroughs are identical. Immersive VHS Filter: Play via a distorted lens, complete with realistic VHS effects, tape errors, and visual distortions that worsen as you advance. Psychological Horror: A story that plays with your mind, questioning your perspective of reality and blurring the borders between fiction and truth. Surprise PS1-Style Gameplay: Once the assessment is completed, the game unexpectedly shifts into PS1-style gameplay. It is up to you to decide how the story unfolds. Short, Intense Experience: A spine-chilling excursion that will leave a lasting impression in less than an hour. Discover a terrifying blend of psychological horror and classic gameplay in which your decisions impact the result, and the story does not finish with the assessment—it is merely the beginning.
