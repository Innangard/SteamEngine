Thousands of torn fragments now cover the floor of the King's Map Tower, and as the King's personal cartographer, you have been given the impossible task of piecing them back together. You are not allowed to leave until the map is restored. Gather the scattered pieces, and rebuild it before the blame falls on you. Royal Cartographer: Restore the Map is a single player simulation game where you gather, rotate, place, and stitch together thousands of scattered map fragments. Discover exactly where every fragment belongs by matching coastlines, connecting rivers, following roads, and rebuilding mountain ranges. What begins as an overwhelming pile of torn paper becomes a complete map of the Kingdom. Use the magical winds of the sea to guide your work and restore the map. As you progress, unlock new abilities that transform the way you gather, inspect, and place fragments. 【Key Features】 Satisfying Restoration: Watch a floor covered with scattered fragments slowly transform into one enormous, complete map. Learn the Lands: Use coastlines, rivers, roads, borders, landmarks, and other details to determine where every piece belongs. Grow Your Efficiency: Unlock magical abilities and discover new ways to gather, sort, connect, and help restore fragments. A Tower Full of Atmosphere: Work high above the Kingdom as wind, ambient sound, and music surround you while you restore the map.
