Like any in-game music for Flatspace, these will trigger at random when you enter a new sector. The first, original music pack was created in 2005, and included a mix of unused game music, such as the epic theme to The Heart of Aorkhan, and some tracks from my solo albums up until that time, such as a track from the first version of The Spiral Staircase. I'm now a full time artist and my music has changed considerably since 2005, so I wanted to include some recent music, as well as the best quality versions of those original tracks. This pack includes a full description of the music as a pdf booklet. Here is an outline of the music included: The Heart of Aorkhan: Main Theme Created using the first generation of my music software, Noise Station, in 2001, this was the main theme for a massive 3D rogue-like game that didn't make it to final release. Catacombs This was part of The Heart of Aorkhan soundtrack. Catacombs made it onto one of my earliest albums, The Incredible Journey. Fall in Green: Barefoot (instrumental version) Fall in Green are an act of poetry and live instruments. Barefoot is from 2018, and was released only as a bonus track with their first single, Who is Afraid/She Floats. Insanity A simple fugue. Oceanic Oceanic appears on Animalia, one of my early albums. Reliquary I: Angel on the Moon A new release, this music was composed to accompany a work of art, The Love Reliquary. Sea Monsters Sea Monsters is part of my first album on a symphonic scale, a conceptual electronic works called The Spiral Staircase. Space A short track which pre-dates the original Flatspace game. The 7th Bell Inspired by 1970s electronic music, such as Pulstar and Alpha by Vangelis. The full version of this track is on Flatspace II (The Official Soundtrack).
