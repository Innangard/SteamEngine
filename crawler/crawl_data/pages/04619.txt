En savoir plus Ce que l'équipe de développement a à dire : Pourquoi choisir l'accès anticipé ? « Scam With Your Friends brings together co-op teamwork, unpredictable conversations with fictional AI callers, and increasingly chaotic workdays. We want player feedback to help shape how these systems develop. Early Access gives us a way to learn which scenarios players enjoy, where the progression needs work, and how we can make the call-center experience more fun and reliable. » Pendant combien de temps environ ce jeu sera-t-il en accès anticipé ? « We expect the game to remain in Early Access for roughly two months, with a full release targeted for mid-December 2026. This estimate may change based on player feedback, the development of content and progression, and the stability of the experience. We plan to share updates as the timeline becomes clearer. » En quoi la version finale sera-t-elle différente de la version en accès anticipé ? « We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. We also plan to refine balance, performance, and usability using player feedback. The scope and priorities may change during development. » Quel est l'état actuel de la version en accès anticipé ? « The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required. » Le prix du jeu sera-t-il différent pendant et après l'accès anticipé ? « We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release.
