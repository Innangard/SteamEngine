View Community Hub About This Game Explore Palan Battle in turns: Gain EXP to become stronger with each victory as you calm agitated creatures. Defeated in battle? Don’t worry — besides recovery skills and items, your companion Feyrie can restore you. Collect, buy, and sell: Talk to Traveling Merchants or visit markets to buy and sell items. Side Explorations: Go beyond the main story. Optional and additional experiences await you, explore to find them. Collect Stamps: Find Stamps and trade your duplicates to complete your Album. Craft Potions in the lab: You’ll need Formula and ingredients. And a Pouch to collect ingredients in the field.  Ingredients can also be found in markets. The Kingdom Surrounded by woods and vegetation, Palan is home to restless creatures seeking battles. But of course, this kingdom also has coastline. You can even collect Shells on the beach. There are also rocky tunnels waiting to be explored. When you find a fragile wall, break it with your Hammer to uncover a tunnel entrance. Butterflies can be found in various places and if you manage to touch them, they share Pollen with your companion Feyrie. Believe it, Pollen is useful! There’s more to explore than what’s been said here. Now, put on your Royal Explorer outfit and experience everything Explorers of Palan has to offer! How long is this game? Focusing on the main story: average of 10 hours. For completionists: 15+ hours.
