A World of Adventure and the Star That Grants Wishes The secrets bound to the stars, the journey of a boy and a girl—and a story of growth along the way. The tomboyish warrior “Star” and her childhood friend, the mage “Yu”, set out on an adventure! What awaits them at the end of the road? Vivid Pixel Life Brought to the Screen Full sprite animations paired with cinematic direction create a deep sense of immersion, as if you were adventuring in a living world. Strategic Depth That Carries on the SRPG Tradition Building on the fundamentals of turn-based tactics, a card system expands combat depth and variables even further. Experience refined strategy that both SRPG veterans and newcomers can enjoy. Thrilling Turn-Based Battles Forged by Counters and Combos Read your opponent’s moves and turn the tide with counters and combos.
