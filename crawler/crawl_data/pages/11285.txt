View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Welcome to Cozy Keep, where you can be a shopkeeper in a fantastical realm. Engage in commerce with merchants, strive to buy low and sell high, handle thieves, interact with customers, pay taxes(or don't) and ensure you always have the best products in stock. Remember, at the end of the day, it's all about profits! Clean up the place, build furniture and structures using raw materials and decorate as you wish to increase the appeal of your shop. Unveil the mysteries of this world! Chop down trees, collect berries or, if you’re a brave explorer, delve into the depths of the mine where no human has set foot in hundreds of years to grab the cursed gold. Utilize raw materials to craft tools, weapons, gear, and anything else you can imagine. Put these crafted items to use for your own purposes or sell them for a profit! Purchase seeds from merchants, plant them in high-quality soil, add water, and patiently wait to harvest the results of your labor. You can sell your crops, use them in cooking, or even create special potions. Hunt wildlife and gather their meat to prepare food and open a new specialization. Your survival depends on filling up basic needs and protecting the store from bandits, ghostly soldiers, and cursed skeletons. Renovate, operate, and grow your own shop in a medieval-inspired setting. Manage prices, attract customers, and work to increase the shop's reputation. Enjoy a variety of customization and decorating options. Specialize in areas that align with your preferred gameplay style. Explore the game's world, explore a hand-crafted map and delve into abandoned mine shafts. Offer services to the local community and complete quests. Take advantage of special in-game events for added opportunities and challenges. Raise your own animals and use their products to increase your profits.
