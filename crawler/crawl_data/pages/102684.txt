That's exactly what you get for having several kittens at home. Kittens and Yarn is a cute and minimalist puzzle game where it's up to you to untangle the yarns by simply swapping the pieces. Features: Relaxing - No scoring, no timer, and no pressure. Solve the puzzles in your own time and enjoy the game experience. Simple control - You can play by just clicking on the pieces with the left mouse button. Level progression - Unique levels, where the challenge increases with each level with more pieces and yarns. Great soundtrack - Music and effects exclusively designed to give you the best experience.
