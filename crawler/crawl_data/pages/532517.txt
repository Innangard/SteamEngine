View Community Hub About This Game Black Friday Janitor is a organization game that hands you the biggest clean up job of the year. Over 30,000 things to tidy up: misplaced products to return to their correct spots, rubbish to collect, dirt to scrub, and fallen furniture to set back upright. Don't expect to finish it all in one go! Over 30,000 Things to Tidy Up! Get ready for the biggest clean up of your life, in the aftermath of the craziest shopping frenzy of the year! You, and you alone, can save the mall from total ruin and return it to the sparkling, tidy mall it once was! 21,000+ Items to shelve 10,000 trash to collect 500 spills to clean 300 fallen objects to put back in place Wide Variety of Stores and Products! Multiple different stores, each with their own range of items to correctly organize and tidy. Unlock Abilities to Tidy Up Faster! Unlock abilities and strengthen your janitorial skills to allow you to tidy the mall up faster! Be Immersed in a 2000s-Era Mall! Immerse yourself in the cozy, nostalgic atmosphere of a mall straight out of the 2000s, complete with familiar lighting and ambient sounds! It's a mess right now, but maybe you can make it look how it used to!
