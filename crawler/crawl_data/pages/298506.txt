Customize the Lofi music, ambient sounds, and scenery that reflect Satone’s emotions to enhance your focus! The more time you spend working together, the deeper your bond will grow—perhaps you’ll even connect with Satone on a deeper level…? Emotionally Expressive Music to Maximize Focus Whether you want to relax and work at a leisurely pace or boost your motivation with emotionally engaging melodies, Chill Talk offers a variety of original songs tailored for different work scenarios. Immerse yourself in deep focus while listening to high-quality music from talented sound artists. A Wide Variety of Nature Sounds to Awaken Your Instincts Sometimes, it’s best to turn off the music and listen to the soothing sounds of nature. Let the sound of wind, rain, and other natural elements sharpen your senses and create a deeply immersive work environment. Customize the ambient sounds to suit your preferences. Pomodoro Timer for Maximum Productivity Set up a Pomodoro timer before you start working to manage your break times effectively. Taking short, regular breaks helps maintain maximum concentration and efficiency. Satone’s Hidden Feelings During your break, take time to interact with Satone. The “precious moments” you share while working together will deepen your relationship, and if you build enough trust, she may eventually open her heart to you.
