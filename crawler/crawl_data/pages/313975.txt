Story You play as 19-year-old Olivia, a college student living with her boyfriend, Jack. One night, Jack suggests sneaking into Creepwood Wonderland Carnival after closing hours to have the entire park to yourselves. Olivia hesitates, but against her better judgment, she agrees. What starts as a harmless thrill soon takes a terrifying turn. The empty carnival feels off. It’s too quiet, too still. Then come the distant footsteps. The rustling in the dark. The unsettling feeling of being watched. At first, it seems like paranoia. But as the night drags on, it becomes clear: someone else is here. And they’re getting closer. Will you make it out to tell Olivia’s story? Key Features Fear-Inducing Atmosphere. Step into a fear-inducing atmosphere where you'll find yourself constantly checking your surroundings, knowing danger could lurk anywhere. Experience a Relentless Pursuit. What begins as a casual night of fun soon takes a dark turn. The tension rises as you realize someone is hunting you, their presence growing more intense with every passing moment. Interact With NPCs. Connect with the game's world through interactions—call, text, and engage in one-on-one conversations with NPCs. Multiple Creepy Environments To Explore. Journey through a variety of unnerving locations, including an creepy apartment, a unsettling convenience store, and the ominous Creepwood Wonderland Carnival.
