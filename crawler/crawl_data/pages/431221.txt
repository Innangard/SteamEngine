About This Game Oh Roots and Branches! Your Kingdom needs you. Winter creeps across the forest, and only the cunning will survive. Welcome to Tree Kingdoms, a minimalist 4X strategy game , with fast, bite-sized matches perfect for short play sessions . As the Great Tree Spirit , you must guide your Treelings to expand, defend. And outwit rival kingdoms before the frost devours the land. Will you conquer, endure, or outsmart your foes in the race against winter? Turn-based strategy, Bite-Sized Battles Explore, expand and upgrade: Claim territory, fortify your borders, and grow your cities. Dynamic map changes: Winter spreads turn by turn, freezing the land and forcing you to adapt. Conquer or survive: Win through domination, diplomacy, or simply outlast the cold. The Clock is Ticking Each match is a race against time. As ice closes in from the edges of the map, resources dwindle, enemies grow bolder, and your choices become more desperate. Build tall for survival, or strike fast before winter claims the battlefield. With infinite replayability and no two winters alike , Tree Kingdoms offers a fresh and strategic puzzle every time you play. Will your kingdom thrive, or freeze beneath the ice? It’s in your hands now Great Tree Spirit, lead on.
