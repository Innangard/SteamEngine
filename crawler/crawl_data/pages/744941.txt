Inspired by Harlow creator Arman Nobari's journey through fighting cancer, a brain tumor, and grief, the songs find a resolute, tender, and enduring optimism from which uplifting journeys can grow. Studying, coding, writing, and adventures of your own will no doubt shine alongside these songs. High-Quality Audio & Production OBFUSC, who's features on the Monument Valley OST and who created the entire NeoCab OST, brings his ethereal, textured, out-of-this-dimension production to the Harlow Original Soundtrack. Arman Nobari, creator of Harlow, also co-produced the soundtrack. The soundtrack was mastered by Steven DePalo (Blacklist, Voxtrot). 1 Hour And 20 Minutes Of Vibes Each of the 26 songs are carefully composed and full-length experiences.
