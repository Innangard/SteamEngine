Sort 5,313 curios across 6 rooms: the apothecary, the library, the greenhouse, the ritual chamber, and more. All at your own pace. A Relaxing Witch's Cabin Organizing Simulator The cabin is a mess, and the magic only made it worse. Return everything to its proper shelf at your own pace, with no timers and no pressure. Pick up each item: a potion vial, a loose spellbook, or a jar of moth wings, find the room and shelf it belongs on, and set it in place. Work through the cabin one shelf at a time. Earn upgrades as you play. Move faster, reach farther, and unlock a few small spells: a levitation charm, a banishing wand, that make tidying the cabin quicker. Cluttered shelves fill with neat rows of labelled jars, and the tangled herb rack settles into order. Each finished room shows clear progress. Key Features Sort more than 5,300 curios across 6 rooms of a witch's cabin Simple gameplay: pick things up and put them back where they belong Skill upgrades for speed and reach, plus a few magical abilities No time limits and no game over.
