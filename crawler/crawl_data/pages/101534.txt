View Community Hub About This Game Survive the Collapsing Space Running out of room! In Space Smash, the playable area constantly shrinks. Collect blue energy orbs to gain more time and space — but avoid the red ones. Inspired by classic arcade games, Space Smash is a fast-paced retro shmup focused on survival and high scores. Fast Arcade Action Easy to learn, hard to master. Survive as long as possible and compete for the highest score on the global leaderboard. Every second counts. One mistake and you'll be crushed by the space storm. Unlock Unique Ships Collect coins in the arena and unlock ships with different traits. Each one offers a new playstyle. Minimalist Retro Arcade Experience Simple graphics, retro sound effects, and pure gameplay inspired by 80s arcade classics.
