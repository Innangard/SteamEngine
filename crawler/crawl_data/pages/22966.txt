During this period, we will actively incorporate community feedback before transitioning to the official release.“ Wie soll sich die Vollversion von der Early Access-Version unterscheiden? „For the official launch, we are considering introducing major content updates, including a Clan System, new game modes, and more. However, since the purpose of Early Access is to validate player feedback and iterate on the game, these plans remain subject to change. While the core gameplay is somehow ready, we believe additional content is needed before the official launch. During Early Access, we will expand the game's content while continuously improving weapon balance and overall gameplay based on player feedback and gameplay data. “ Was ist der derzeitige Stand der Early Access-Version? „In summary, the current build is stable and ready for an Early Access launch. Following our participation in Steam Next Fest last October and our recent Closed Beta Test (CBT), we believe the game has reached the quality level required for Early Access. Currently, players can enjoy two Casual game modes: Team Deathmatch (2 maps) and Bomb Defusal (4 maps). The game also features a Weapon Parts Customization System, allowing players to unlock weapon parts through account and weapon progression and customize weapons to match their preferred playstyle. The game also includes a player-to-player Marketplace, allowing players to freely trade eligible in-game items. This system is designed to support a player-driven economy and provide additional long-term engagement.“ Wird dieses Spiel während und nach Early Access unterschiedlich viel kosten? „No. We intend to continue operating the game as a free-to-play (F2P) title even after Early Access. We do not currently have any plans to change the pricing of our in-game currency after the official release. However, if there are significant changes in exchange rates, we may review our regional pricing. If any adjustments are made, we will notify our players in advance.“ Wie werden Sie versuchen die Community in den Entwicklungsprozess miteinzubeziehen? „We are already actively monitoring player feedback through our official Discord community. As we did during our previous CBT and Steam Next Fest, we will also continue gathering player feedback through Steam Community and discussion forums. Community feedback has already had a direct impact on the game’s development. Based on feedback from previous CBTs and Steam Next Fest, we increased the maximum frame rate and reduced the time required to aim down the scope when using sniper rifles, improving the overall responsiveness and feel of sniper gameplay. Throughout Early Access, we will continue reviewing player feedback alongside gameplay data and player behavior. Based on community responses, we plan to continuously adjust weapon balance and overall game balance, while also prioritizing bug fixes, performance improvements, matchmaking, UI and UX improvements, and other quality-of-life changes. We are also considering adding new game modes based on the types of gameplay and content that players request during Early Access. Community feedback will therefore help us determine which features, modes, and improvements should be prioritized in future updates. Step into the intense battles of Sudden Attack Zero Point, a free-to-play FPS that delivers a fast-paced shooting experience. We understand that not everyone can indulge in all-day gaming or wander around in an unnecessarily large map only to be eliminated in seconds. Sudden Attack Zero Point offers quick, action-packed battles, allowing you to enjoy intense gameplay and fully embrace the thrill of combat without unnecessary delays. Customization For Victory Unleash your creativity with an extensive weapon customization system, letting you build the perfect gun for your playstyle! Each match demands precise coordination and communication to ensure victory. In addition to these thrilling bomb defusal modes, a variety of other game modes await. Strategize with your team and dominate your enemies! Trade & Gear Up! Participate in the Black Market, where you can trade weapon skins, attachments, and other items that you earned in the game with fellow players.
