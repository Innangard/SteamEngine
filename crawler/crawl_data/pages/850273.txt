Coverage is a board game for two players similar to Othello and Ataxx. The rules are simple and easy to learn, you can pick it up in a single session, but the game contains surprising strategic depth! Challenge your friends Coverage can be played by up to two people, both online and by taking turns at the same computer . Online play is fully cross-platform , so no matter where your friends are you can challenge them. Simultaneous Move Rules Try a new fast-paced parallel-move mode in which both players move simultaneously ! If you both move to the same location at the same time, your pieces turn to stone ! You'll need to find new strategies and think hard about where your opponent might move to be able to excel in these modes! Boards to choose from Tired of the same old grid? Think hexagons are bestagons? Try out some additional board designs! Different starting conditions, obstacle arrangements, and board dimensions can all require different strategies! You can also design and share your own boards with the built-in editor! Computer Opponents for all skill levels Learning to play or just want to practice? We include a collection of AI opponents with different skill levels and strategies to play against. From very basic and predictable to challenging bots which plan ahead you should always be able to find an opponent for your level. Customizable Look & Feel Prefer a much more basic and relaxed presentation or want to see a lot of hints and animations? Want to play as your favorite color or customize the visuals? We got you :) Created with Human Hands This is a passion project. No generative AI tools were used at any stage of development.
