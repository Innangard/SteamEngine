Leave your safe zone to gather the resources that will help you craft better gear. The further you go, the more dangerous it gets but the resources are also better. Dying means losing everything that you are carrying. In this Action-Adventure game, you ascend into a world you know nothing about. Outside the safe zone, everything will try to kill you. And you won't be handheld in any way. No tutorial. No direction. No map. No goal given. Minimal user interface. You can only count on yourself. FEATURES Explore a handcrafted interconnected open world with different biomes. Discover a wide variety of enemies each with unique abilities. Understand the mechanics that will help you progress. Complete missions to gather resources. Get back alive to the safe zone to not lose your gear and loot. Survive dangerous random events as you explore. Craft better gear with your loot. Customize your loadout by choosing between different weapons and abilities. Exterminate what stands between you and the answers you are looking for. EXPLORE. UNDERSTAND. EXTERMINATE.
