Tokoyo no Sakura is a visual novel about loss, encounter, and farewell. Set in a world where virtual reality and reality intertwine, you will meet a cast of unforgettable characters and uncover a truth that has been forgotten for years. Story The story takes place in Japan after the Third World War. To restore the ancient capital of Kyoto, which was destroyed by war, a virtual reality project called "Tokoyo no Sakura" was launched — a perfect replica of the ancient city, rebuilt on the far side of the moon, in an eternal night. You play as Haruka Watanabe, a young man haunted by his past and chosen as a tester for the game. During an accident, you become trapped in a "Closed Domain" within the virtual world, where you meet a mysterious shrine maiden named Inori Sakuraba. She is gentle, enigmatic, and seems to know everything — except for her own past. Her arrival will change the course of your life forever. Features · Immersive Eternal Night Kyoto — Explore a beautifully crafted ancient city that never sees daylight, with a unique visual atmosphere. · A Touching Story Across Time and Space — Unravel the mysteries of the "Closed Domain," "Spacequakes," and the legendary "Yamata-no-Orochi" as you delve deeper into the truth behind this virtual world. · A Cast of Vivid Characters — Meet a diverse cast of characters: the gentle yet clumsy Inori, the sharp-tongued but loyal Nagi Isozaki, the quiet and ever-supportive Shizuru Takanashi… each with their own complete story arc. · Multiple Endings — Your choices will shape the fate of everyone around you. Will you protect, let go, or chase a miracle that seems impossible? In this eternal night, who will you meet — and who will you lose? (This game was made entirely by a solo developer.
