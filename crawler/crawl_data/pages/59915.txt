THE GAME OF LIFE will take players on a journey where fortunes can be won…and lost! Choose a college or career path and start down the many roads of life, make money, buy a house or start a family -- will you take the safe route or the risky road? The choice is yours as you navigate life’s rich tapestry! Do whatever it takes to retire in style with the most wealth at the end of the game to win!
