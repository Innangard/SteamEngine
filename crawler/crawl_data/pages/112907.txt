View Community Hub About This Game Infinite Ruins is an incremental mining game where you command a team of golems, destroy elemental blocks powered by ice, fire, lightning, poison, and more, and descend layer after layer into the depths below. As you progress, the entire world transforms into a self-sustaining machine under your control. Freeze, explode, clear entire sections, and do it all again, faster than before. The layers were here long before you The ruins existed long before your arrival. Who built them? Why were they abandoned? No one knows. Your job is simple: dig deeper. One layer, then another. Gather every resource you can find and keep descending into the unknown. The golems await your command A growing roster of golems is ready to be unlocked. Ice, Fire, Rock, and more, each serves a unique purpose. Place them manually and direct their actions, or let them operate automatically while you focus on optimization. Even when you're not actively mining, resources continue to flow. Chain reactions vs random events Lightning can cut through entire layers. Ice spreads across neighboring blocks. Resource Vortices may swallow valuable materials before you can collect them. But every challenge can be overcome. Build the right team of golems, unlock powerful upgrades, and create devastating chain reactions capable of handling anything the ruins throw at you. There is no end, only deeper layers The deeper you descend, the stranger the layers become and the more meaningful your decisions are. Choose which upgrades to prioritize, when to unlock new golems, and how to tackle the next stage of progression. Beyond every layer lies even greater growth and ever-increasing numbers. Features Endless downward mining : Break blocks, gather resources, and accelerate your mining and destruction with powerful upgrades. Specialized golem teams: Build your own underground operation with 8 unique golem types , each fulfilling a different role. Deep upgrade progression : Unlock nearly 100 upgrades and feel a clear sense of growth with every purchase. Elemental synergies : Combine 6 different elements to create powerful interactions between fire, ice, lightning, poison, and more. Collapse the Ruins through a deep Prestige system with permanent Rune upgrades. ~4–5 hours to complete the main story, with around 7 hours to reach 100% completion. Continue your journey in Endless Mode with infinite progression. If you enjoy watching numbers grow, making meaningful progression choices, or building systems that keep working while you're away, Infinite Ruins offers a world worth digging into one layer at a time.
