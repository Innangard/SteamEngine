Download Settlement Survival - Tourism Free Download View Community Hub About This Content Settlement Survival - Tourism contains a whole new tourism system, with 9 attraction structures and 10 commercial structures. You can earn Silver Coins and Access Points from tourists, and increase Town Attractiveness to bring more tourists immigrating to your town. Key Features: The basic gameplay is building scenic attractions and using the item Souvenir to promote your town and attract tourists. You can gain Access Points by satisfying tourists' requests. Use Access Points to unlock more attraction structures and commercial structures to increase Town Attractiveness. Finally, you will earn more Silver Coins and attract more immigrants from tourists. The Town Attractiveness is composed of 4 positive factors and 1 negative factor: 1. Happiness and Health. 2. Tourist Satisfaction. 3. The number of built attractions and commercial structures. 4. Decorations in scenic areas. 5. Pollution buildings in scenic areas. Tourist Satisfaction varies by the categories of tourists. Access Points will be given when requests are satisfied, but will not be given if not. After building attraction structures, you can start promoting to attract tourists. Higher-level attractions will have higher-ranking tourists visiting who can provide more Access Points and spend more silver coins. Commercial structures and accommodation structures can attract more tourists. Commercial structures need supplies so tourists can shop at the place. During peak tourist seasons, the number of tourists will increase significantly, and to keep them satisfied, it is important to have enough commercial structures and supplies. You can unlock attraction structures and commercial structures in the Access Point Shop. And redeem items with Access Points, such as Farmer Mystery Box, Rancher Mystery Box and Architect Mystery Box.
