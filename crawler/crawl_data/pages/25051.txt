View Community Hub About This Game Roku and Rei is an engaging puzzle game where the protagonists move in reverse directions. Synchronize their movements to help them reaching their crosses! Roku and Rei possess the ability to interact only with specific colors. Utilize this distinctive trait to guide them through a multitude of levels filled with colorful elements that can either help or hinder your progress! Within the world, you'll find a variety of interactive objects, such as buttons, brushes, and portals, to navigate and alter the state of the levels. Explore 7 unique worlds, each with its own distinct features, ranging from basic challenges to complex puzzles that test your deep understanding of the game's mechanics. The game features a built-in editor that allows you to create your own levels and share them instantly on the Steam Workshop. Moreover, you can download and play levels created by the community directly within the game! Game content Over 100 levels with various mechanics and objects. An integrated level editor. Worshop integration for uploading and downloading levels directly withing the game. Over 30 achievements to unlock! Roku and Rei, conceived during GMTK Game Jam 2023, secured a top 100 spot and received positive feedback from players.
