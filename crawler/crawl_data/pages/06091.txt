View Community Hub About This Game Place pegs, drop balls. ploink is all about building fun and creative Rube Goldberg style plinko boards to pass the score requirement. DISCOVER WACKY ITEMS With over 200 pegs and 100 balls, there's many crazy items to discover: Pocket Dimensions you can place pegs inside of Knight Helmets with built-in skill trees Vaults that let you save pegs for another run Virtual Pets you can feed to increase their score Amalgamations that can combine multiple pegs together Alternate Universes that open entire new runs DESIGN CREATIVE SETUPS There are no forced item interactions, every item is an open-ended component you can utilize to create unique builds: Summon a bunch of balls and guide them to specific pegs using walls and obstacles Generate money and shop items to buy whatever you want Trap a ball to let your automatic pegs grant you score over time Create a network of power giving pegs that all connect to a single peg MAKE IT harder Trinkets add modifiers and increase the score requirement, making the game harder. Boards add even more variety by switching up how you design your builds. Unlock trinkets and boards by winning runs: 20 Trinkets 6 Boards ENCOUNTER BOSSES Every few levels a boss will try to stop you, and with a colorful cast of 20 bosses you'll surely grow to hate at least one of them: Hot serial killer Cute frog summoner 🐸 Disturbing cheese god Cool kamikaze uncle Horrifying void entity Busty goblin woman System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10+ Memory: 1 GB RAM Graphics: 200 MB Storage: 200 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 11 Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: 400 MB Storage: 200 MB available space View all What Curators Say 8 Curators have reviewed this product.
