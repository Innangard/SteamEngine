Is that really what you're asking? Not how to escape from here? Run— You were kidnapped in a terrible way while on your way to meet a friend at a cafe. It may be sudden, but one thing is certain—this is not the Human World. On the outskirts of the Warp World, run from the hostile entities that are after you. Collect the Keys— The one who kidnappedyou said that escape from this place is possible. After wandering for a long time, you discovered a strange, massive door… but it's locked. Only a few are willing to talk, choosing conversation over charging at you with a shriek. If you manage to earn their favor, you may even receive some help. Or… you can just ignore them. You are human, after all—you don’t rely on monsters or gods like them, right? It’s not like you plan on making new friends in a place like this. Why were you kidnapped? What is keysaster? Why you, of all people? As you wander through the Warp World, you may uncover a few facts that aren’t all that important. …instead of simply escaping back to the human world as quickly as possible.
