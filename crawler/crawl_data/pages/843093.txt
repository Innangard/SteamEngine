ASTERIS is a 1–4 player action roguelite that combines roguelite buildcraft with MMO-style co-op raids. Reshape your chosen skills into entirely different techniques and take on the forms of five gods to turn the battle. Your gear and growth survive each run, becoming the strength behind your next challenge. THE SAME HERO. A WHOLE NEW WAY TO FIGHT. A slash becomes a dash, splits into three attacks, or unleashes a shadow clone. Reshape each of the 33 active skills across four heroes in 24 ways . Combine 50+ passives, transform techniques with runes, or choose the path toward True Name awakenings. The skills you choose matter. How you complete them matters just as much. TURN THE TIDE. TAKE A GOD'S FORM. Sweep through enemies with fire. Strike with the storm. Consume them with the void. Take on the forms of five gods and let divine power flow through your skills. Each god brings distinct powers and a signature ultimate. Turn a moment of danger into your chance to strike back. YOUR FRIEND'S ULTIMATE IS YOUR CUE. When one player unleashes a god's ultimate, join with your own. DIVINE RESONANCE sends a blast out from each resonating hero and strengthens their damage until their transformation ends. Four distinct builds become one decisive moment of power. A TANK WHO HOLDS. DAMAGE THAT HITS. A HEALER WHO KEEPS YOU FIGHTING. Dodge the telegraphs, cleanse curses and pull enemies off your allies. When everyone plays their role, your party can break through fights that damage alone could not win. Test your build solo, or invite Steam friends to the Asterian Deep, a raid dungeon with six bosses . Azhdarak, the Last Dragon, awaits at the end. THE RUN ENDS. THE STRENGTH YOU EARN STAYS. Gather loot, enhance your gear and push into higher difficulties. Collect appearances to make your hero your own, and find the equipment you need in the player marketplace. The progress you earn in this challenge becomes the starting point for your next. TAKE YOUR DEMO ADVENTURE INTO NEW BATTLEFIELDS. Your demo characters and progress carry over to the full game on the same Steam account. Five stages and the Asterian Deep await in the full game. Take your hard-earned growth and a fresh build into your next battle. FOUR HEROES. FOUR WAYS TO FIGHT. Dark Slayer — Melee damage built on timing: blade dashes, chains and eclipse executions. Tank or damage. Vanguard — A tank with golden gauntlets who splits the ground open. Tank or damage. Windrunner — Arrows that strike and heal, cyclones and storms of arrows. Damage or healer. Aether — Support powered by a star core: shields, relics and supernovas. Damage or healer.
