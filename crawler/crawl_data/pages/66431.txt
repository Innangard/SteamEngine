Not a video on loop: these are scenes that read your hardware in real time. Your graphics card heats up and the sky reddens. Your music plays and the ocean moves with it. Snow falls and settles on your real desktop icons. 600+ wallpapers, 14 categories Fractal — endless mathematical patterns, Julia/Mandelbrot, orbit traps Visualization — flowing music visualisation patterns, hundreds to choose from Playground — scenes you can touch with the mouse that live on their own Desktop-Aware — scenes that read the real icons and windows on your desktop HTML Boards — single-file scenes; text, colour and layout change from the right panel 3D Scene — three.js scenes, glTF models, real depth Particles & Flow — flocking, fluids, gravity, cursor interaction Shader — raw GLSL scenes, raymarching, procedural textures Scene & Landscape — sky, ocean, mountains, day cycle Minimal & Clock — clean typography, clocks, single-colour compositions Audio & Spectrum — visualisers that live with music: spectrum, waveform Data Dashboard — CPU, GPU, RAM, disk and network on your desktop Retro — CRT, VHS, 90s screensavers Images & Video — your own photos and videos It reacts to your machine This is the part a video wallpaper cannot do. Scenes can read: Processor load, per-core load, clock speed and temperature Graphics card load, VRAM, temperature, power draw and fan Memory usage and the top memory-consuming process Every physical disk: free space, read/write traffic Network speed, up and down, and the active adapter The audio coming out of your speakers — spectrum and waveform The real icons and windows on your desktop Clock, date and uptime 288 of the wallpapers listen to audio, 203 read the processor, 192 the graphics card, 189 memory, 175 the disks, 89 the network, and 38 read your actual desktop icons. Tune everything, without writing code Every wallpaper exposes its own settings panel — 5,500+ settings across the library. Colour, speed, density, which data source drives which effect. Save your tuning as a preset. Each monitor can run a different wallpaper with different settings, and your layout survives cable swaps because settings are bound to the monitor's hardware identity. Your own photos and videos Point it at a folder and every image or video inside — subfolders included — is listed. Click one and it becomes the wallpaper of the selected monitor. Fit, brightness, playback speed, volume, blur are all adjustable. Your files are never copied and never moved; they stay exactly where they are. Built to stay out of the way When a window covers a monitor, rendering stops completely Full-screen games pause every wallpaper automatically Each wallpaper declares its own frame-rate cap A per-wallpaper memory badge tells you what it actually costs Anyone can build one — there are no limits The editor is the other half of this product. Open it and you are looking at plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a live preview next to it. Save, and the wallpaper reloads instantly. No build step, no account, no upload. 2D — canvas, SVG, CSS animation: anything a browser can draw 3D — three.js and Babylon.js templates, glTF models, real depth Shaders — write GLSL straight onto a full-screen quad HTML boards — one single file is enough Everything the platform reads is one line away. Write pw.subscribe('hw.gpu.temp', v => ...) and your scene knows how hot the graphics card is. The same goes for the sound coming out of your speakers, memory, disks, network, the clock, even the real icons on your desktop. Don't write code? Describe the wallpaper you want to any AI assistant, paste the JavaScript it gives you into the editor and press save. The API is small and documented, and every one of the 600+ bundled wallpapers can be opened as a copy and taken apart — the copy is yours, the original stays untouched. 31 languages The interface is fully translated into all 31 languages Steam supports. On first run it follows your Windows language. Everything stays on your machine Wallpaper reads your hardware locally and shows it to you. There is no account, no telemetry, and no network connection in the core product. Wallpapers run in an isolated browser context that cannot reach your file system, cannot start processes, and cannot reach the network unless a wallpaper declares it and you approve it. Every permission is listed before you apply a wallpaper and can be revoked at any time from the Permissions page.
