Easy Red 2: Blood on the Danube - Hungary 1945 is an expansion focused on some of the final and most brutal battles of the Eastern Front of World War 2. It takes the conflict to Hungary, ranging from large-scale armored operations to bloody street-to-street urban warfare that pushes base Easy Red 2's systems further than ever before. CAMPAIGNS The DLC features three campaigns , each covering a major historical scenario: The Siege of Budapest , sometimes known as "the Stalingrad of the Waffen-SS", a 102 day vicious urban battle where German and Hungarian forces were encircled by the Red Army. The Konrad Offensives , the failed attempts by various elite Axis forces to free Budapest from Soviet encirclement. Set in rural Hungary east of the great Lake Balaton during a harsh winter, huge battles of armor and troops await. Operation Spring Awakening , the final Axis offensive of the war, fought also near Lake Balaton, its failure marking the last ground gained by the Third Reich. One of the largest tank engagements of the late Eastern Front. Across all three of these campaigns, experience 30+ missions across two maps. FACTIONS New Hungary and Romania factions are added with dedicated voice acting, along with well over 100 new uniforms and helmets, including late-war German and Soviet equipment. WEAPONS Over 25 new weapons and grenades are added, covering the standard-issue rifles, submachine guns, light machine guns and support weapons of the added factions in the region. Among these include: PPS-42 and PPS-43 Mannlicher 35M and 43M for standard Hungarian infantry Mannlicher 95/30, 95/31, and G98/40 Danuiva 39M and 43M FÉG 37M M.08/12 Schwarlose Orița M1941 31M./43M. Solothurn , Madsen 24/43M and ZB-30 LMGs ROKS Flamethrower Mosin-Nagant M44 Carbine and AVT-40 soviet rifles ZB vz. 24 & sniper variant. Panzerfaust 100 and 44M kézi rakétavető anti tank rocket launchers Various additional small arms, grenades, bayonets, etc. Late-war experimental equipment is also represented, such as the Vampir ZG-1229 night vision scope for the STG-44, used by German forces in limited engagements and appearing during Spring Awakening. VEHICLES Over 40 new late-war vehicles & variants are represented in this DLC, ranging from simple support and scout cars to some of the heaviest tanks of the entire war. Among those include: Tiger II / "King Tiger" Panther Ausf. G (As well as Panther Ausf. G with "Sperber" Infrared night vision system) Flammpanzer 38(t) flamethrower tank IS-2 , the iconic Soviet heavy tank ISU-152 , the heavy assault gun unofficially nicknamed "Beast Killer" ISU-122 and ISU-122S assault guns SU-85M SU-100 OT-34-85 , the flamethrower variant of the late-war T-34 Hungarian tanks such as the 40M Turán I , the 41M Turán II and the 43M Turán III 40M Nimród self-propelled gun 39M Csaba Armored Car Yak-3 and various late war plane variants DSHK Machinegun and 44M Buzogányvető rocket launcher German conversion tanks such as Flammenpanzer 38(t) and Panzerjager 35R(f) Various additional logistic and support vehicles for each side Blood on The Danube: Hungary 1945 delivers large-scale combined arms combat in the brutal conditions of the late-war Eastern Front.
