You find yourself at the end of a grand tabletop campaign, facing an undead king who wields the power of time itself. However, every defeat sends you back to the gates of the final dungeon, where your experience began. But here's the twist – you retain your memories, particularly knowing which die yields what number. Armed with this information, guide your characters strategically through the final battle. Role dice to cast spells and launch attacks, but be cautious – the world's fate hangs in the balance, and so does the friendship among your party. Key Features: Strategic Dice Rolls: Remember the outcomes of your dice rolls to make crucial decisions. Your success hinges on choosing the correct paths and actions to overcome challenges. The game is designed to be completed in roughly 20 minutes, offering an engaging experience that fits into a short gaming session or 1 hour if you want to get all the endings. Bite-sized tabletop RPG Journey: Immerse yourself in the final moments of a tabletop RPG, complete with an undead king who controls the very fabric of time. 4th Wall Breaking Fun: Delight in the unique experience of playing a bite-sized tabletop RPG journey within a game. Enjoy the subtle humor and surprises as you navigate the game world and the meta-narrative. Friendship and Fate: Every decision matters not only for the fate of the in-game world but also for the bonds between your characters. Can you break the cycle, defeat the undead king, and ensure a triumphant end to your campaign? Explore the intricate blend of time travel and tabletop RPG in Time Glitch – where the past is your greatest weapon.
