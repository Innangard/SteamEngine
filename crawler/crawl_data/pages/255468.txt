(It's a short puzzle game) Be cautious and observant, because a sinister psychopath lurks within these rooms, hidden in the shadows and monitoring your every step. Authorities in Romania discover a very unsettling record detailing, the horrible murders committed by a single person during a police raid on a home. Approximately 60 videotapes, recorded by the psychopath and documenting the graphic details of his killings , are among the discoveries. Law enforcement teams from both state and federal agencies carefully examine the graphic footage to find crucial hints such as the name of the murderer, the victims' identities, and possible leads regarding his whereabouts. Even after numerous viewings, the tapes don't provide much information other than the terrifying reality of the atrocities. The powerful effect of the gruesome visuals unnerves detectives as they probe further into the insane mind that produced the recordings. After months of investigation, one tape, in particular, draws the attention of the authorities despite the abundance of others . Florentina, the victim , recorded this tape, which unintentionally revealed the identify of the attacker and shows her frantic attempt to break free from the grip of her attacker. Through close examination of the video, the authorities are able to reconstruct Florentina's terrifying experience inside the house as well as the identity of the criminal. With the potential to make significant progress toward catching the offender and providing the victims with justice, this vital evidence acts as a ray of hope for the investigation. George Dinescu imprisons you within his home , refusing to let you go. A pounding headache wakes you up in a dimly lit room, reminding you of a hard blow to the head. Your flashlight, is the only source of illumination. The main exit is sealed behind a series of locks, forcing you to search the house for keys and other items required to unlock the door to freedom. However, caution is advised because the psychopath has acute hearing; any sound, whether a dropped object or a creaking floorboard, indicates his imminent arrival. Beware of his bear traps. Choose from three difficulty levels: Hard, Normal, and Easy. True Survival Horror Experience: You are no fighter, and your only chance of surviving the horrors of the house is to escape... or hide. Immersive Graphics: RETRO VHS quality graphics provide gamers a complex, horrifying environment to explore. Hide & Seek: Stealthy gameplay with parkour areas and hide zones. Unpredictable Enemy: Players have no idea when, or from where. The game has speech recognition, use your microphone to distract the enemy or trigger certain game events. In game shop, beat the game on certain difficulties, earn coins and customize your game. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: The game contains, flashing lights, loud noises, jumpscares and blood.
