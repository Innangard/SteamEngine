Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Anomaly President is built on three core pillars: Action, Base Building, and Exploration. We’re putting significant effort into each of these elements, but in a game with high replayability like this, player feedback is crucial. Your input will allow us to refine even the smallest details in the game. More importantly, it will help us implement our full vision more effectively in the final release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan for Anomaly President to be in Early Access for at least one year. However, this duration may be extended based on player feedback and needs. While it's challenging to provide a precise timeline, we will keep you informed about our plans at every stage.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In the full version of Anomaly President, we aim to refine and expand on everything you see in Early Access. We will regularly add new content to enhance both the action elements and the variety of choices you can make. You’ll also be able to explore the rest of the map. By the time we reach the full release, we hope to deliver a product that both you and we can enjoy with peace of mind, knowing it's well-polished and complete. Throughout this process, we will address any potential issues with updates and patches.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Anomaly President is currently fully playable from start to finish. As a presidential candidate, you can conduct campaign activities and battle a variety of enemies and bosses. You also have plenty of options to build and customize your bus. However, the story’s ending is not yet complete, and not all areas of the map are currently accessible.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Our primary goal is to gather feedback from our Discord and social media channels. We will compile the most requested features from the community and integrate them into our development plan. We’ll then share these developments with the community to gather further feedback. The main reason Anomaly President is launching in Early Access is to make the community a full part of the development process. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Roadmap About This Game Anomaly President is here with its unique presidential rogue-like gameplay where you can destroy your enemies in the craziest ways and lead an anomaly-fueled revolution for the oppressed and the silenced! Take on the mission to overthrow a president pushing the world toward disaster. But fighting is not enough. To save the world, you must also run for presidency! Presidential Candidate by Day, Deadly Warrior by Night Lead a double life in a world of chaos and corruption. By day, you're a presidential candidate, winning the hearts and minds of the people. By night, you transform into a vigilante warrior, using your weapons to take down the criminal gangs controlled by the current president. In a world filled with anomalies, you will encounter various enemies and vice president bosses, each with their own unique anomaly power. For example, did you know the president's PR chief has the power of HEART, making people fall in love with them?! The good news is, by defeating these bosses, you can acquire their anomaly powers! You Can't Become President By Yourself; You'll Need a Team of...FLUFFIES! You need a trustworthy team to run campaigns and empower you on your path to presidency. Choosing the right team is crucial in Anomaly President! They'll be the ones to help you win the race! Recruit the oppressed Fluffies to fight for their rights by joining your team. You can assign them to different departments: some will campaign for you during elections, others will upgrade your equipment, and some will assist you in battles. But remember if you want to benefit from them, you need to take good care of them. Otherwise, they might abandon you or worse, lose their lives. You'll Need a Base on Your Journey to Presidency! To combat hidden crime syndicates and also run your presidential campaign simultaneously, you need a base that moves with you. Enter the campaign bus! Design your campaign bus to suit your style and needs! Start from scratch, add unique sections, and create a personalized mobile base for your campaign. Choose Your Weapon and Anomaly Power! Special anomaly upgrades you'll encounter while fighting will shape your warrior identity. You can become a sword master who parries enemies at the right time or transform into a cyber mage dealing mass damage with anomaly powers. In Anomaly President, the choices are plenty! The best part is, the rogue-like structure lets you experiment with all options. Try out different powers and progress with the one that best suits your style! There are many ways to evolve in Anomaly President. Win the People, Crush Your Enemies, and Lead the Anomaly-Fueled Revolution Travel the country, explore new horizons, win the people's hearts, destroy the current president's gangs, liberate the people from tyranny, and ultimately get your revenge! Anomaly President is calling you to action!
