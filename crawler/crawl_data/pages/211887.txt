The Seven-Star Pavilion is an ancient sect deeply attuned to the celestial patterns of heaven and earth. Established in the northern reaches of the Arid Zephyr Continent during an era so ancient that it defies tracing, its location offers an optimal vantage point for observing the heavens. the pavilion's most esteemed members, have attained mastery over the celestial realm. Their profound understanding of the stars and skies has reached a transcendent level, allowing them to harness the boundless power of the constellations to their will. In recent times, the Seven-Star Pavilion appears to have uncovered revelations from the celestial omens, prompting them to dispatch disciples to explore the Celestial Realm in search of answers.
