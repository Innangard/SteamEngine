View Community Hub About This Game After finishing your studies and working non-stop for five years, you still feel stuck in life. Should you look for a job or pursue your dream project? The choice is yours, but it's a hard one. Suddenly, a notification pops up on your computer, waking you up - your friends want to meet up tonight to help you clear your mind. Is this a waste of time? Is this just a distraction? One thing is clear - you're now stuck at home, overthinking whether to go out or not today. You have 7 days to think about it . KEY FEATURES A house with randomised furniture - memorise what you see and answer a "Yes" or "No" question; Atmospheric 3D aesthetic with an eerie, retro vibe; 3 endings to discover; 2 unlockable extra game modes + an endgame Secret Story.
