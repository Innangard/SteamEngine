Every shop swears it used to be organized, before the last delivery, or the last accident with a self-stacking bookshelf. You're the new pair of hands the street's been waiting for - part cleaner, part sorter, part miracle worker. 5 shops. 6,666 items that all ended up in the wrong place. Shop by Shop Each shop has its own personality and its own mess... humming wands in the wand shop, whispering tomes in the bookshop, jars of things nobody can quite identify in the apothecary. Sort every item onto the shelf it belongs on, and watch each shop click back into place. Key Features Visual Satisfaction: There's a real joy in watching a chaotic shop resolve into order - clutter thinning and shelves filling. Cozy Charm: Wands hum. Tomes whisper. Every shop is packed with little details, in-jokes, and clutter that rewards a closer look. Efficiency Through Mastery: The faster and cleaner you sort, the more you're rewarded - unlocking abilities that turn a slow tidy into a satisfying rhythm. A Street Worth Getting Lost In: No two shops are alike. Each one is a small, self-contained slice of a bigger, busier magical world.
