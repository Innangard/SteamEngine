Pattern Survivors: Bullet Hell (Roguelike) is a dynamic action game where you take control of your cosmic tower and design attack patterns yourself to stop hordes of enemies! This is not another Vampire Survivors clone – this is a bullet hell where YOU create the chaos. The Patterns appeared suddenly. They were not alien, they were not ours… they were fundamental. As the Tower Operator, you must adapt, create, and survive waves of cosmic threats using the most powerful weapon: imagination. In Pattern Survivors you don’t just play a bullet hell – you design it . Set emitters, modify angles, speeds, rotations, and spread to create your own bullet formations. Every wave is a test of creativity and reflexes. ⚡ Fast action – get into the game in 3-5 seconds Don’t wait, don’t read tutorials – just play! 1️⃣ Click New Game -> Quick Run -> add an emitter (or choose a ready preset) 2️⃣ Configure the emitter (optional) 3️⃣ Start the wave, upgrade your tower, and watch the chaos unfold Done – from start to battle in less than 5 seconds! And the best players will cut that time down to 2–3 seconds! Campaign or quick session? Play how you like If you’re not interested in the campaign, you can pick another mode and play the campaign later – or the other way around, play the campaign first and then the other modes. Or maybe all at once? Campaign – Action-packed storyline with dialogue choices, main and side missions, 14 unique bosses, diverse enemies, and the mystery of the Patterns to uncover. Survival – Endless waves, increasing difficulty, and global leaderboards. You start with no abilities – how long will you survive? Sandbox – Full freedom, test builds, create experimental patterns, and have fun with God Mode. Quick Run – Intense action for a short 10–20 minute session, global leaderboards, perfect if you’re not into the campaign but want a quick game. Tower Defense – A classic tower defense mode with progressive difficulty. Enemies move along set paths, and each round becomes increasingly intense. The final stage turns into an endless survival battle. Perfect for fans of traditional tower defense games. Finally… a bullet hell roguelite with SAVE ANYTIME Unique and complete Save State System (First in the world?) – Save & Quit at any time, resume exactly where you left off: with every bullet, enemy, and ability. Key Features (Cherry Picks) 🎯 Create your own bullet hell – Intuitive slider-based pattern editor with ready presets, random layout generator, and the ability to save patterns as JSON files. 🌌 Choose your game mode – From full story campaign to limitless sandbox. 🚀 Advanced progression system – Tower shop, artifacts, and abilities to purchase. 🪐 Explore the galaxy – Planet scanner, anomaly hunting, and Quantum Data Cores to collect. 🔥 Epic abilities – Nuclear bombs, drones, deflecting shields, time slowdown, and more. 👾 Rich bestiary – At least 14 bosses and 9 different enemy types, each with unique attacks and behaviors. 🎮 Full player support – Mouse, keyboard (shortcuts), gamepad, and touchscreen support. 💻 Cross-platform – Available on Windows, Linux, and Mac . 📀 Retro audio vibe 🏆 Steam integration – Achievements, online and offline leaderboards, cloud saves. ⚙️ Full settings – Graphics profiles, sound/music adjustment, smooth gameplay with seamless transitions and music crossfade, difficulty levels. 🎥 Cutscenes and narrative – Campaign intro and ending scenes. ♾️ High replayability – Every run is different thanks to the pattern system and random upgrades. Visit the Armory Buy and unlock powerful towers, rare artifacts, and unique abilities that can completely change the course of battle. Experiment with different combinations, create deadly synergies, and adapt your strategy to your playstyle – every decision can turn the tide of the fight. Active abilities are your strategic tools: place mines anywhere on the battlefield, summon drones and support ships, set up flanking units or ambushes. Every use is a tactical choice that can decide whether you survive the next wave. Who is this game for? Pattern Survivors is for you if: You enjoy games inspired by Vampire Survivors , Brotato , or Hades , but expect a fresh approach and new mechanics – this is not another clone of the well-known titles . You love bullet hells but always dreamed of designing the attack pattern yourself. You want a game that gives you the choice: quick sessions or a deeper campaign with a story. You value high replayability and the ability to experiment with strategies. If you like supporting independent solo developers without a publisher or PR – this game is made out of passion and for players just like you.
