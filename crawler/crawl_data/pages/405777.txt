Yesterday you drove a chair to Tomato Street, and today - a mirror on Lenin Avenue. And again the car broke down - which means that all the daily earnings will go to repair. The only thing that will brighten up these working days is music. Every week a single track is added to the radio in the rotation. What will be a hit in February? And in October? A realistic courier simulator - every day is similar to the previous one. Routine. Only addresses and cargo for delivery differ. Everything, as in life. Minimalistic text interface. Get a check-list with assignments for each day and execute them for income. A random list of cases for each game day - a lot of combinations of subject-address, there will be almost no repetitions. Music written by a neural network is a unique soundtrack for every game week. But you can choose manually in the settings you like the melody, and it will not interrupt during the game.
