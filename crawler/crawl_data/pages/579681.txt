View Community Hub About This Game Five months have passed since the horrifying tragedy at Arya’s fried rice shop. The place where Valeria, the daughter of his late close friend, mysteriously vanished. Trying to rebuild his life, Arya now works as a nighttime courier. But the guilt and his obsession with uncovering the truth continues to haunt him. One night, a routine delivery brings him to the home of Viktor, a mysterious figure with ties to that dark event. From that moment on, strange things begin happening. Every truth Arya uncovers pulls him deeper into an inexplicable terror, caught between dreams, delusion, and reality. Is Valeria truly gone? Or has Arya’s guilt awakened something that should have remained buried? Play as Arya A man ridden with guilt and loss after tragedy struck his late friend's daughter whom he swore to care for Deliver Packages Deliver packages in Arya's nighttime job and experience a brand new driving mechanic!
