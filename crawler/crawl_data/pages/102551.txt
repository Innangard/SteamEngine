Go Beyond. ⚡ Enter the Wattverse , an incremental game where electricity is your currency and exponential growth is the goal. Start with a tiny amount of power and build it into an energy production empire capable of generating unimaginable amounts of electricity. Upgrade your systems, unlock new technologies, and keep pushing your production higher and higher. Build Your Power Start small and begin generating electricity. Invest your power into upgrades that increase your production and unlock new ways to grow. Every upgrade brings you closer to the next major milestone. Exponential Progression Watch your electricity production grow from a handful of watts to numbers that become almost impossible to comprehend. Each new milestone opens the door to even greater levels of production. Powerful Upgrades Spend your hard-earned energy on a wide variety of upgrades designed to improve your production and accelerate your progress. Find powerful combinations and optimize your path toward maximum output. Automate Your Energy Empire As your power network expands, automation becomes increasingly important. Let your systems generate electricity for you and keep your progress moving even when you’re not actively playing. Enter the Wattverse Electricity is only the beginning. Push beyond ordinary power generation and explore increasingly powerful systems as you progress through the Wattverse. There is always another upgrade, another milestone, and another massive number waiting for you. How far can you take your electricity production? ⚡ Generate. Upgrade. Automate. Repeat.
