We own the company together, and if we ever expand, we want new people who join to also become co-owners. We want to be a small push towards a more fair way of running and owning companies - we believe worker-coops to be a powerful transformational force to creating a more democratic society. We try to create games that are first and foremost entertaining, great games. Then, we try to infuse them with the values that we believe in (democratic ownership, solidarity, resistance, sustainability). We want to make games where you feel welcome, if you don't like the feeling of power concentrating away from you and into fewer and crazier hands. Let's explore, question, and fight that. And let's do it in a way where everyone can be included without being judged for being early in their journey, or for disagreeing, or for not knowing the right words, or for not being perfect. Let's create weird and fun and kind video games for weird and fun and kind people. Anyway, that's a lot about our little studio, thanks for reading.
