In this FPS/Tower defense game,  players defend the beach fortifications against invading forces who will attack your position with troops, armored cars, biplanes, planes and more! Use a variety of iconic and authentic WWI weapons stretching from machine guns to coastal artillery, and field cannons to anti-air guns. Beach Invasion 1915-Gallipoli brings  modern FPS realism to arcade style on-rail shooter game mechanics. The players can choose to play two scenarios; in the first as a Turkish soldier   defending the beaches of Gallipoli, in the second one as a British soldier defending the Suez Canal. Moreover they can play together with friends in online CO-OP gamemode . During these immense amphibious  operations,  player's task is to destroy as many of the invading forces as possible in continous waves, utilizing a variety of weapons and bonuses from the supply drops, such as Poison Gas, Armor Piercing, Explosive and Incendiary ammunition. Compared to previous games in the franchise, players can now determine the precise location of where they want the artillery fire to hit. New enemies include infantry who will  charge with their bayonets , armored cars , motorcycles , bi-planes and more!
