View Community Hub About This Game Trenches VR – A Psychological WWI Survival Horror, where enemies hear your microphone. Trenches VR puts you in the boots of a World War I soldier trapped behind enemy lines. In this claustrophobic nightmare, fight for your life — and your sanity — as you navigate the horrors of war. Your only goal is to return to your family. Your only tools is an old trench whistle and your instincts to survive. Warning: This game contains disturbing imagery, intense jump scares, and psychological horror not suitable for all players. Play at your own risk. Enemies can hear your voice, breathing, and every move that you make through your microphone. Stay quiet, stay hidden… or they’ll find you. Collect clues, uncover the truth, and use your whistle to guide your way - but beware, every sound could give away your position. Insanity: Just because you passed somewhere already, does not mean that there is nothing there. The environment may change with your sanity. Repeatability: Experience may change for each player. Fog type, some parts of the environment, jump-scares and objectives location will be randomized on each play-through. Plan Accordingly: Plan your next move as it may be your last. Peak around corners, watch your footsteps as some wooden floors creak, and be always on the move. Microphone Input: Enemy can listen and hear your microphone, screams and sounds. Make sure to not scream. Mark Walls: With the chalk you find in the trenches, leave your mark on the walls—your drawings will guide you when you’re lost. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains jumpscares, loud sounds, fear, blood and gore that some audience may find disturbing.
