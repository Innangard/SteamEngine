Simulator 3 and experience one of Southern Europe’s most important aviation hubs. Located east of Athens, LGAV serves as Greece’s primary international gateway, connecting Europe, the Middle East, and beyond with a constant flow of passenger and cargo traffic. Operate two long parallel runways and manage a diverse mix of short-haul European flights, busy seasonal charter traffic, and long-haul international operations. Athens is well known for its strong summer traffic peaks, requiring precise sequencing, efficient runway usage, and careful ground coordination to keep operations running smoothly. This detailed add-on recreates Athens International Airport with an authentic layout, realistic ATC frequencies, and full customization options. Adjust traffic density, weather conditions, and time of day to recreate anything from calm off-peak operations to intense summer rush scenarios, making LGAV a rewarding challenge for every Tower!
