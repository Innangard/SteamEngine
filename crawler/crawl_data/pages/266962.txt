We do believe we managed to create a fun old school and authentic adventure game and we hope you will forgive us some production shortcomings. In this alternate reality, the humankind has already conquered space travel by the mid-19th century. Get ready to embark on a journey filled with quests, conspiracies and general chaos. Become a part of this hilarious story and reveal the great Space Age conspiracy. Kaptain Brawe's methods are not for everyone. He is... well, he's a unique guy misunderstood by people around him for most of the time. But, he's brave and he's incorruptible and he'll do everything to bring villains to justice. Step into this authentic old school point n' click adventure game and follow the trail of two kidnapped alien scientists. You'll get to play with three different characters: captain Brawe, agent Luna, and Danny and explore four colorful planets while meeting intriguing and unique characters like space pirates, Interstellar government, secret agents and more. Who this is for: fans of Monkey Island, Vampire Story, Broken Sword or anyone who loves story-led puzzle adventures. Whether you're new to the genre or a seasoned adventure gamer, Kaptain Brawe offers a warm, comedic ride through space-age conspiracy.
