Long, a high school senior, accidentally discovers the existence of the Dream Corridor —a realm that bridges the living and the dead. Within it lies an endless hallway lined with doors, each one connecting distant corners of the world. That is why, in dreams, we find ourselves transported across vast distances in the blink of an eye. But the discovery comes with an unintended side effect: Long gains the ability to see wandering spirits. What seems like a miracle at first quickly reveals itself to be more of a nightmare, as he realizes that restless Ghosts have always Surrounded Us —unseen, until now... More than just your typical story-driven game, this is where action meets introspection. Beyond battling wandering spirits in the mortal realm, you’ll also be answering delightfully absurd quizzes that reveal hidden sides of your personality—even the ones you didn’t know existed.
