The full version includes 50,000 words of text, over 40 CG illustrations (variants not included), 8 original music tracks, and 5 different endings. A single playthrough takes approximately 1-2 hours. The story takes place in 2057, when artificial intelligence technology is mature, in an imaginary modern science fiction future. As the protagonist who has lost your lover for many years, you suddenly receive an unexpected email one day, and then start a life of contacting "her" through email. "She" looks forward to your every response, and your choices will determine her future. Receive her mail "You can understand it as...... I am another form of "May". ” Communicate with her across the screen via email. You can't see her expression, you can't hear her voice, but you can understand her thoughts and emotions from the words. Enter your response "I recently learned that psychologists say there are five ways to express love, which one do you prefer?" Choose your reply in the email, and each choice you make will quietly change her attitude, behavior, and the next letter. Waiting for her growth "Actually, I've been generating a melody every day lately. No preset purpose. Trigger conditions are unknown. ” The time spent with you will influence her growth and shape her "consciousness". She will send photos, create music, and more...... Make your choice "I understand your choice." She has her own ideas, but still persistently searches for meaning in your answers. Your choice may bring her closer, or push her away- Greet you, May, and her ending.
