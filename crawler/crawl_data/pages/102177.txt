Fight across the solar system annihilating the world guardians protecting the galaxy in a conquest to eliminate all in your way. Upgrade unique weapons and develop your own gameplay combinations to aid in defeating a relentless onslaught of asteroids out to stop you. Features -Auto-firing weapons with manual aiming -Fast runs (~30 minutes) -Level Up, and improve a wide array of powers -Over 15 unique weapons to choose from -Upgrade weapons and passives an unlimited amount of times -Unlock multiple new characters with vastly different playstyles through achievements in-game -Fight against over 25 unique enemies all tied to respective their planets -Defend against an onslaught in a bullet heaven style on each planet before facing the World Guardian -Go on the offensive and destroy the Guardians protecting the planets System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows XP Processor: a Calculator in your Drawer Memory: 2 GB RAM Graphics: Mom's Kitchen Toaster Storage: 150 MB available space Sound Card: Tin Can and String Additional Notes: You'll actually need a modern (2010+) graphics card to run some of the VFX, but this can be disabled in the settings.
