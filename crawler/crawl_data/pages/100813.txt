to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Ancient Demon Shadow is a game that combines side-scrolling and exploration. In the battle between gods and demons 60,000 years ago, the world gathered top mages with the help of the Giant Modao clan to seal the Evil God King in Ancient Demon City. However, over the years, the population in Ancient Demon City has dwindled and the magic has weakened. The Evil God King has transformed his power into several major demons, seducing many government officials from different countries into soul trades to control them and break the seal of Ancient Demon City. Therefore, a mysterious hero has summoned players to eliminate him.
