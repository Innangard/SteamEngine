When completing the diorama, animations will be automatically activated in the scene and INTERACTION MODE will be activated, giving you access to interact with it, clicking on several pre-defined places to see very cool animations. In INTERACTION MODE you can activate NIGHT MODE , turning on all the scene lights at once and thus being able to enjoy a very special view of the diorama.
