There’s just one problem: your customers are anything but ordinary. In a neighborhood full of zombies, vampires, werewolves, and cyborgs, everyone has different needs. It’s up to you to keep the shop running. Place your shelves and equipment, order your products, and arrange your shop just the way you want it. As you earn money, unlock new products and upgrades, expand your space, and grow your little shop step by step. Stock unusual products for your supernatural customers. Place orders through WolficOS on your shop’s computer, and make room for the delivery. But around here, deliveries don’t always come by road. Sometimes they come from the sky. Goblin thieves may grab products from your shelves and try to run. Your answer is right under your feet. Your shoe. Strike up close or throw your shoe. Stop the thief, recover the stolen item, and put it back on the shelf. Wholesale prices change. Stock up when prices are low, decide what to buy and how much, and set your own selling prices. If your prices are too high, customers may walk away. Keep your shelves stocked, your prices right, and your service good to boost both sales and reputation. Customers pick their products, line up, and head to the checkout. The rest is up to you. Scan the items, take the cash, and count out the right change. As the shop gets busy, keep customers moving while watching for shelves that need restocking. Step out from behind the checkout, wander the neighborhood, and meet its unusual residents. Drop by the bar, join the dancing crowd, or investigate the strange things happening nearby. Your supermarket isn’t the only odd place in this neighborhood. Special shop events can turn your daily routine upside down in an instant. Keep up with the rush of customers during Golden Hour . When Sticky Fingers begins, keep an eye on your shelves: thieves may target your shop. Each event tests your stock, your checkout, and your priorities in a different way. Adapt your plans to keep your shop going.
