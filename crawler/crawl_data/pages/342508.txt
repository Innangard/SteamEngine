Learn more Leaving Early Access: Q1 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We want to build a deeper and more enjoyable game together with our players. Early Access allows us to listen to feedback, understand what the community really wants, and make better decisions about future features. Instead of guessing, we prefer to shape the game based on real player feedback. Our goal is to use this process to create a higher quality and more fun Tailor Simulator over time.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We plan for Early Access to last around 8 to 12 months. However, this timeline may change depending on player feedback, feature requests, and how the game evolves during development. We want to take the time needed to do things right rather than rush the process.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version is planned to include many new features shaped by your feedback. Tailor Simulator has a lot of room to grow, and we want to decide what to add or change together with you. We have shared a public roadmap, and our decisions about adding or removing content will be based on your feedback. Planned additions include a clothing factory, all seasons with changing clothing preferences throughout the year, random events and themed events, a second city and region, deeper customer relationship systems, and more content developed during Early Access.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already offers an engaging experience focused on management, fashion, tailoring, and progression, and is fully playable with a stable structure designed for long play sessions. The game includes many mechanics and options across production, selling, upgrading, and shop decoration, and in its current state, Tailor Simulator provides a relaxing and immersive experience that can be enjoyed for hours.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The sale price may increase depending on the content will be added.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We plan to stay in close contact with the community throughout development. We will have an active community manager in Steam discussions and our community channels to keep communication ongoing. Players will be able to leave feedback across our social media channels, which we will continue to improve and expand. The in-game Early Access hub allows direct communication with us, where players can share feedback and follow updates. We will also host developer broadcasts where players can talk with us live, and regular Discord sessions where they can share ideas, requests, and suggestions directly with the team. Use different fabrics from cotton to linen to create unique collections with thousands of combinations. Every corner of your workshop, from the smallest scissors to the largest shelf, is the reflection of your art.
