Rise from the ashes and rebuild your castle as you embark on a journey to shroud the land in darkness. MULTIPLE VAMPIRE CLASSES Discover powerful classes, each with its own unique playstyle. Tame monsters, brew potions and craft magic weapons to gain the upper hand in combat. Tailor your strategy around your undead army. Unique synergies arise depending on whether your minions are slow, sturdy juggernauts or swift, delicate fighters. EXPLORE THE LAND Journey through a changing world filled with hidden villages and kingdoms to raid. Each area offers new resources to find and different enemies to fight. Random events like traveling merchants, bandit raids, and snowstorms keep each playthrough fresh and exciting. In addition, Mysterious artifacts lie hidden across the realm. Complete special challenges to uncover their locations. REBUILD YOUR CASTLE Your castle lies in ruins. Only you can restore it to greatness! Build walls, towers, gates, and decorations to customize your stronghold in this sandbox mode. Design a unique castle that unlocks lasting bonuses and powerful upgrades. VARIATION IN STRATEGY Discover a large number of powerful effects embedded in weapons and equipment, allowing you to shape your own unique playstyle. Experiment with different combinations and uncover exciting synergies, ensuring every battle stays fresh and rewarding.
