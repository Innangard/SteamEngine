View Community Hub About This Game Snap the Cat Get your cameras ready! Snap the Cat is an incremental game about taking photos of cats. Improve your equipment, upgrade your account, discover different biomes, and unlock new cats and costumes to make your photos the most viral. Your main resource is likes. The more interesting your photos are, the more likes you'll get! As time goes on, people will start following you, and you'll be able to monetize your account. The more popular you become, the more opportunities you'll have to improve your skills — with an extensive skill tree, you'll be able to unlock new abilities to get the maximum benefit! Open packs to get new, unique cats and outfits for them. Rewards are guaranteed! Features 8+ biomes 40+ cats 25+ costumes Progress through resource gathering Skill tree of upgrades to explore Simple and relaxing gameplay Could you get everyone on the planet to follow you?
