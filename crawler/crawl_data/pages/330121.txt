View Community Hub About This Game Mystery Egg Shop Simulator is a simulation game where you manage every aspect of a shop that sells collectible mystery eggs. Open Eggs, Find Figures The core of your business offers a strategic choice: Sell Sealed: Sell mystery eggs directly to customers for a consistent, reliable income. Open for Profit: Open the eggs yourself. Inside are collectible action figures with different rarities (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary). A rare figure is worth far more than the sealed egg. Expand Customize Manage Your Shop You are in full control. Start with an empty space and build it out. Physical Stocking: Order inventory using your in-store computer and physically place the eggs on your shelves. Price Control: Set the price for each type of egg and adjust based on supply and demand. Checkout & Operations: Scan items, take cash and card payments, and keep your store clean to attract more customers. Expansion: Use your profits to expand your store's physical space, buy more shelving, and upgrade your point-of-sale system. Craft and Sell Dioramas Don't just sell loose figures. Maximize their value with the crafting system. Themed Sets: Collect figures that belong to the same theme, like "Space," "Fantasy," or "Dinosaurs." Build Dioramas: Place a complete set of themed figures onto the corresponding diorama base. Sell for High Value: A finished diorama is a premium product that sells for a significantly higher price than the sum of its parts. This is the key to making big money. Grow Your Business in New Ways Earn more than the usual market price by exploring several ways to buy, sell, and trade: In-Store Auctions – Put valuable figures up for auction and let customers compete with higher bids. Black Market Merchant – Meet a mysterious merchant offering a rotating selection of limited themed eggs and exclusive dioramas. Online Orders – Fulfil simulated customer orders for specific items and earn premium payouts above their standard market value. KEY FEATURES First-Person Shop Management: Physically run your store, from stocking shelves to operating the cash register. Inventory and Price Management: Order stock via a computer and set your own prices for all products. Unbox and Collect: Open mystery eggs to find hundreds of different action figures across multiple themed sets. Figure Rarity System: Figures range from common to Epic, directly impacting their value. Diorama Crafting System: Combine figures of the same theme on a diorama base to create high-value collector's items. Store Expansion and Upgrades: Grow your store's physical size, improve your equipment, and customize its appearance. Online Marketplace: Real Player to Player online marketplace, where you can sell your rare findings to other players or buy the items you need to complete your collection Online Order System: Besides in-store customers, you can receive  in-game online orders, which you can fulfill for extra cash Day/Night Cycle & Customer AI: Manage your shop during open hours and prepare for the next day after closing.
