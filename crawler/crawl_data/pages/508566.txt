View Community Hub Reviews “The best Survivors-like so far” 8.2 – Video Chums “Entropy Survivors easily earns a spot among my favorite roguelikes, largely in part due to its impressive depth of customization and the satisfying sense of progression with every run.” Gamerheads Podcast “Entropy Survivors successfully carves its place among the best bullet-heaven games, offering an addictive and exhilarating experience that’s hard to put down.” 8 – Geekgasm About This Game A sword-wielding mech. A sharpshooting space frog. Control both characters simultaneously against the forces of Entropy in this bullet heaven Survivors-like. You’ll bring these two unlikely heroes home using nothing but the unstoppable power of friendship (and an arsenal of mind-blowing cartoon weapons)! Play by yourself, or bring your friends along for a genre-bending online co-op experience! Become best buddies with synergetic builds or prove yourself champion in the contest for high score. Is your shotgun out of ammo? Throw it at your enemies! Feeling hungry? Use your spear as a shish-kabob. Unlock dozens of customizable options to power up and create your own playstyle. From weaponized garbage trucks to a gun that shoots bees, Entropy Survivors is overflowing with unique weapons, abilities, and upgrades that’ll make you ask “What?” Beautifully crafted alien landscapes meet colorful bomb bursts and delicious chain reactions. Find the sights and sounds of an entire carnival arcade within one 20-minute level! Entropy Survivors is a prequel set in the Shoulders of Giants universe–but don’t worry: this game stands completely on its own.
