Begin with only a cheeseburger to sell, but grow your menu so that your customers can choose from a variety of food. Update your menu If your menu remains the same for too long, customers will grow tired of seeing the same food, and will leave without ordering anything. To fix this, you can swap out menu items and increase customer satisfaction! Different foods will have different stats and effects, but also might become stale faster, so change your menu often to keep up efficiency. Grow the skill tree Upgrade your production speed, happiness, and more with the skill tree. Vital for keeping up with the growing demands of customers, this can drastically change the pace of the game . Meet new customers In the beginning, you'll only get one type of customer, the capybara Chase. Over time, though, you'll meet new faces who can give a variety of effects like speeding up all the customers behind, increasing the happiness level of all your food, or buying out your whole menu. If you can make enough money quickly, you might even run into the elusive golden flamingo , who can unlock powerful rewards. Unlock new restaurants One of the golden flamingo's rewards is the ability to open up new restaurants, which will have more expensive food, and new effects that can alter the gameplay . Some restaurants might have more slots open for food, more choices, or require much faster production in order to run effectively.
