View Community Hub About This Game Real Zombie Survivors is an intense 2D post-apocalyptic survivors-like and top-down arena shooter that blends fast-paced action with procedural world generation powered by real OpenStreetMap (OSM) data. Choose any location on the globe - your hometown, the streets of New York, Paris, or Tokyo - and face unstoppable zombie hordes in a dark, atmospheric night setting. Play solo or team up with friends in Steam P2P co-op. Tactical positioning, map awareness, and squad synergy are vital - if even a single teammate falls, the entire team is defeated! Key features of the game: Real-world OpenStreetMap map generation - Play anywhere on Earth: The game engine pulls real-time data from OpenStreetMap (buildings, streets, sidewalks, water bodies, street names) and immediately generates a playable world complete with physical obstacles, 2D light occlusion, and navigation meshes. - Tactical urban terrain: Narrow alleys, intersections, abandoned car wrecks, and trash/rubble barricades force continuous maneuvering and using urban architecture to funnel enemy hordes. Unique character classes and active abilities - Soldier (DPS): Firearms specialist dealing high damage. Active skill: Combat Fury (massive temporary boost to movement speed and fire rate). - Medic (Support): Crucial squad member deploying a Medkit healing circle to restore injured teammates during intense combat. - Bodyguard (Tank): Heavy frontline defender with increased health, damage reduction, and a powerful knockback Shockwave. - Engineer and Grenadier: Area control via automated defense turrets, damaging auras, and devastating fragmentation grenades. - Dash: Every character has a dynamic dash with invulnerability frames (i-frames) to escape deadly traps in clutch moments. Extensive arsenal and supply crates - Auto-firing and line-of-sight targeting: Primary weapons continuously fire at the nearest visible targets while accounting for terrain obstacles and building walls. - 9 distinct secondary weapons: Discovered in weapon supply crates (including Auto Grenade, Ricochet Sawblade, Mortar Strike, Cryo Nova, Orbital Shield, Combat Drone, Flamethrower, Lightning Strike, Toxic Aura). - Levels II-V: Each secondary weapon features a 5-tier upgrade tree that enhances damage, range, fire rate, and special effects. Dual-layer progression system - In-run upgrades: Collect gold coins dropped by defeated zombies and immediately purchase stat upgrades (damage, speed, fire rate, projectile count, magnet radius, maximum health) in the side panel. - Permanent meta-progression: Collect green crystals by slaying bosses, rescuing trapped civilians, and claiming achievements. Spend skill points across extensive class-specific skill trees in the main menu. Technological support and map events - Combat robot airdrops: Dropships deliver devastating mechanical allies onto the battlefield: Goliath (heavy combat mech with pulse laser) and Tarantula (10,000 HP mega-tank fortress with a continuous lock-on laser beam). - Civilian rescue missions: Randomized, timed rescue events on city streets rewarding players with valuable crystals upon successful extraction. - Crate sonar radar: A 45-degree tactical scanning radar built into the interface to detect and locate ammo, health, weapon, and magnet supply crates. Steam co-op and Sudden Death - Steam P2P multiplayer: Server lobby browser, public and private games, and direct friend invitations through the Steam overlay. - True team survival: Cooperative matches feature Sudden Death - the death of any player instantly triggers defeat for the entire team, demanding close teamwork and mutual protection. Atmospheric setting and dynamic lighting - Night atmosphere: True survival tension with limited visibility, player flashlights, and real-time shadows cast by building walls. - Clean interface: Modern industrial UI featuring off-screen objective indicators, meta XP progress bar, and sonar readout.
