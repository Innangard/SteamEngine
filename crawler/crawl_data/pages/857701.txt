The Western Maryland Railway Retro Pack, developed by High Iron Simulations, includes everything you need to step back in time to the late 1960s and early 1970s and go to work for the famed railroad that was nicknamed “The Wild Mary.” The Western Maryland Railway Retro Pack includes three types of Wester Maryland diesel locomotives – the Electro-Motive F7, GP9, and GP35 – along with the railroad’s noted C-13A steel cupola caboose, and ten era-authentic types of freight equipment! All three locomotive types are provided in WM’s traditional black “Speed lettering” livery and the F7 and GP35 are also dressed in the railroad’s flashy “Circus Colors” scheme. The C-13A caboose is featured in a trio of Western Maryland liveries and the freight equipment wears a variety of Western Maryland, Baltimore & Ohio, Chesapeake & Ohio, and generic liveries. Created to bring the Western Maryland era to full life on the highly acclaimed CSX Hanover Subdivision route (which for more than a century was a key route of the WM known as “the Dutch Line”), the retro pack includes a selection of ten realistic career scenarios based on actual Western Maryland operations and train symbols and which use “Form 19” train orders to add extraordinary authenticity to the operating experience. Note: The locomotives and freight equipment included in the Western Maryland Railway Retro Pack can be used on a variety of Train Simulator route; use of the included scenarios requires the CSX Hanover Subdivision route. Take the throttle of Western Maryland’s magnificent motive power and enjoy 1960s and 1970s-era railroading amid the historic farmlands and scenic mountains of Maryland and Pennsylvania -- with the Western Maryland Railway Retro Pack for Train Simulator! Included Scenarios Ten realistic and challenging career scenarios appropriate to the Western Maryland-era are included, putting you at the throttle of Western Maryland motive power for mainline, local, and switching operations on the Train Simulator CSX Hanover Subdivision route. The pack is also Quick Drive compatible and features a variety of WM Quick Drive consists.
