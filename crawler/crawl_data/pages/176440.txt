Content For This Game Browse all (1) Enchanted Path - Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game About This Game Enter the magical world of Enchanted Path , an atmospheric isometric puzzle game where every step matters! Guide your character across floating pathways, collect glowing green crystals, and unlock the magical portal to the next level. But watch your step — one wrong move could send you falling into the abyss! Each level introduces new challenges and clever tile mechanics. Some tiles disappear after you leave them, others teleport you across the puzzle, and some reveal entirely new pathways. You'll need careful planning, precise movement, and a little creativity to find your way forward. With 40 handcrafted levels , Enchanted Path offers a relaxing atmosphere combined with increasingly challenging puzzles. Key Features 40 Unique Puzzle Levels – Explore handcrafted challenges with new mechanics and obstacles introduced as you progress. Clever Tile Mechanics – Encounter disappearing platforms, teleporters, cages, and tiles that create new pathways. Collect Crystals & Unlock Portals – Gather the glowing green crystals to reveal your path to the next level. Atmospheric Visuals – Enjoy colorful isometric environments with subtle animated backgrounds and four distinct atmospheric themes. Camera Zoom & Fit to Level – Zoom in for a closer look or fit the entire puzzle on screen for a better overview. Customizable Gameplay Experience – Enable optional movement indicators and exit previews, hide the HUD, or switch back to the original background style. Improved Settings & Pause Menu – Adjust audio, display, and gameplay preferences without leaving your current puzzle. 31 Supported Languages – Enjoy Enchanted Path in your preferred language. Steam Achievements & Cloud Saves – Earn achievements and keep your puzzle progress synchronized through Steam Cloud. Every Step Counts Whether you enjoy solving puzzles at your own pace or challenging yourself with increasingly complex levels, Enchanted Path invites you to think ahead and discover the right route. Collect the crystals. Unlock the portal. Find your path.
