View Community Hub About This Game Lead the Invasion. Master the Dice. Break the Game. Humanity had a good run, Now it’s time to take back what’s yours. Dive into a dice-based roguelike deckbuilder where your tactical decisions, not luck, determine the fate of the planet. Build your own Invasion Build a growing alien force on a turn-based grid where every placement matters. Each turn you can acquire different units, artifacts, budges and chrono. Place your invaders strategically in the grid to enhance your scoring potential. Manipulate the Dice Dice aren’t a game of chance here, they’re your most powerful tool. With ‘Chrono’ you can strategically reroll your dice and restock the shop to draft your most vital units.  You can transform a bad roll into an unstoppable chain reaction. Use ‘budge’ to  manipulate your dice throw to build the exact combo you want. Even, align your dice in pairs, triples or quadruple to ignite devastating combos. Be the Commander Each commander brings a distinct strategic approach, specialized synergies and XP scaling possibilities unlocking new ways to dominate. Each equipped with unique perks and artifacts so each run gives you new ways to send your attack score into the stratosphere. Earth won’t be destroyed without a fight. Beware, the legendary Pilots from StarVaders will show up to protect the planet, each armed with unique abilities designed to disrupt your invasion. Break the game Experiment with absurd synergies and push the game’s systems to their absolute limit. Discover a broken combo that launches your score into orbit and out smarts the pilots.
