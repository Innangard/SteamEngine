Hatch unique birds, sell them, decorate their home, then watch them fly around your screen and perch on top of your other windows! Designed to be played while you're doing other things, the game can be zoomed in/out and moved up/down so it doesn't get in your way. DISCOVER NEW SPECIES Complete your collection of over 40 bird species + unique color variants! CUSTOMIZE Customize your aviary with a variety of decorations for your birds to perch on! BREED NEW LIFE Breed your best birds to maximize profit. LEARN Learn fun facts about each species you discover! FEATURES Build the perfect aviary for all your birds!
