Think carefully, because these specialized rounds will push your reflexes, skills, and your build! Feeling lucky and want a D20 that buffs or screw you over ? Maybe a beige ball that judges how stylish your combos are ? Or a ball dressed like a certain laser-eyed psychopath ? No matter what, every ball you unlock adds something new to your run. 6 playable characters , each with unique mechanics. 6 stages filled with dynamic hazards. 50+ upgrades and special moves with stacking synergies. 10+ balls that changes your runs. High-skill juggling mechanics with spectacle-fighter combo depth. Roguelike build crafting and score-chasing replayability.
