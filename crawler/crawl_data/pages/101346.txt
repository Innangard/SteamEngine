SMOKE HAVEN: Smoking Area Simulator is a comic 3D management, building and defense game about a smoking area visited by fictional adult characters. Build, grow and earn Place ashtrays, furniture, appliances and cafe equipment. Rotate, move and expand to create a comfortable space. As customers use amenities, their satisfaction grows, bringing more regulars from each profession and more frequent visits. Cafe income is separate from admission fees. Keep an eye on cleaning, repairs and expenses, too. From the roadside to outer space Learn the basics with NT at the Roadside, then choose the Office, Park and Home in any order. Clear all 4 Earth stages and a hidden stage awaits... Aliens want a smoke too, and surely even outer space has people who hate smoking. Take on stage objectives, climb the ratings and aim for a world where 100% of the population smokes. Defend their moment of peace Counter raids by fictional groups with barricades, defense equipment, click attacks and missiles. Plan your layout and supplies for bosses such as the dragon. Raids and events can reduce the in-game smoking population. If it reaches 0, it is game over. Play freely, or continue from the demo Free Mode lets you choose unlimited money and turn raids or events on or off. The free demo includes only the Roadside stage, where you can keep running your business after clearing it. Free Mode is not included in the demo. On the same PC, import a demo save into an empty slot from the full game's title screen. 31 languages Choose your language in the game settings. Saves are stored locally on your device. Includes smoking depictions and comic violence. All in-game shops, monthly expenses and advertising use in-game currency, not real money. Tobacco products cannot be bought or delivered in real life through the game.
