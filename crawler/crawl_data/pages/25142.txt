A provincial town wrapped in quiet stagnation. In one corner stands a fading secondhand bookshop. The five childhood friends who gather here unravel the small scandals brought to their door. Their only weapons are the books on the shelves. Confronted with evidence, people will not let go of their own story. Only when books from distant fields say the same thing does the other's claim fall apart. During Investigation, decide where to send each of the five friends to gather information. Where you dispatch whom changes how the case unfolds — reading each friend's chemistry with the people involved is the key to cracking a case. Once the clues are in hand, it's time for Confrontation. Armed with what the books have taught you, choose your words and break down the suspect's walls. The goal isn't to corner them — it's to reach the real reason hidden behind their story. Each case runs about 5–10 minutes. Across these easygoing short stories, the five leads, the world of books, and the lives of the townspeople quietly unfold. Features - 15 cases, 60 books across 14 shelves, 21 suspects — every case rewards a different combination of clues - Three difficulty levels (Easy / Normal / Hard).
