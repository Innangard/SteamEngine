Composer and musician Priscilla Snow has created a wide variety of sounds using both live and synthesized instruments, vocals (human and feline), and a healthy dose of good old experimentation. Additional credits: M Gewehr provided additional arrangement and co-produced the brass heard on the theme song Nina Pasadena (Skin Deep) , the drums on the theme were played by Josie Brechner, and the theme’s vocals were performed by Aricka Lewis. Isaac Schutz and Lysandra Nelson contributed additional vocals for the song Productivity (Presented by MIAOCorp) . Josie Brecher’s drumming can also be heard in both the vocal and instrumental versions of Little Lion’s Song . The clarinet for the SFPR Theme was performed by Jack de Quidt.
