As the forces of reality influence and intervene in the lives of mortals, myths and legends are told. Forgotten histories buried at the foundation of existence resurface, some seeking the truth, and some asking questions that were never meant to be answered. In one fledgling universe, Elissa encounters a primordial power older than existence itself, forever altering the course of her life. As millennia pass and her power grows, she begins to question whether her fate was truly ever her own – or if it was chosen for her. As the ages pass, an unknown threat looms at the edge of reality. Powers stir to life, setting in motion a convergence of events that will echo until the end of all things.
