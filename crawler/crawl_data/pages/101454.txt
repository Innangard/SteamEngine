I decided to release LATE 2 ESCAPE: Chapter One in Early Access because I want to continue developing the game while allowing players to experience it before development is fully complete. Creating a horror game involves more than just building environments and adding scares. I want to keep improving the gameplay, atmosphere, enemy behavior, and overall experience as the project develops. Early Access gives me the opportunity to work toward that vision without waiting until every planned feature has been completed. I also want to learn from the people who play the game. Their experiences can help me understand which parts are enjoyable, which areas need more attention, and what could make the game more engaging. This is an ongoing project, and I am committed to continuing development, working on improvements, and moving the game toward a more complete release. Early Access is an important step in that process, and I look forward to seeing how the game develops over time. Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? My current estimate is that LATE 2 ESCAPE: Chapter One will remain in Early Access for a few months. However, this is only an estimate, and the timeframe may change depending on development progress and the amount of additional work required. During Early Access, I want to focus on improving enemy AI mechanics, adding additional features and minigames, fixing bugs, and polishing the existing gameplay. I also want to take time to evaluate player feedback and identify areas that need further improvement. The length of Early Access will depend on how development progresses and how much work is needed to reach the quality I want for the full release. I would rather take the time necessary to improve the game than rush it out before it is ready. I will work toward a full release as development progresses, while keeping the focus on delivering a more polished and enjoyable experience. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? The full version of LATE 2 ESCAPE: Chapter One is intended to offer a more developed and polished experience, with additional features and minigames introduced throughout development. One of my main goals is to improve the enemy AI mechanics so that encounters feel more engaging and better support the game's horror atmosphere. I also want to expand the gameplay with additional activities and features that give players more to experience. Alongside these additions, I plan to refine the existing systems and improve the overall quality of the game. The exact scope of the final version may change as development progresses, but I want the finished game to build meaningfully on the Early Access release rather than simply remain unchanged. My aim is to deliver a more complete experience that better reflects the vision I have for the game. Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? LATE 2 ESCAPE: Chapter One is currently playable, but it is still under development and requires further polishing and improvements. The game follows Michael Salvatore, a father searching for his missing daughter, Haze, as he explores his surroundings and uncovers clues connected to her disappearance. The current gameplay experience is built around first-person horror, exploration, environmental puzzles, and survival elements. The store page describes several core gameplay mechanics, including collecting items connected to Haze, using matches to light candles and reveal hidden symbols or paths, and using an axe to break obstacles and fight enemies. The game also features a dark atmosphere and threatening encounters intended to build tension as players explore. Although the game is playable, some aspects still need additional work. I plan to continue improving enemy AI, refining existing mechanics, adding more features and minigames, fixing bugs, and polishing the overall gameplay experience. Players should understand that this is an Early Access version rather than a fully finished product. They may encounter bugs or mechanics that still require balancing and refinement. Development will continue through future updates, with the goal of improving the game based on testing, development progress, and community feedback. LATE 2 ESCAPE: CHAPTER 1 includes a a twisted compelling story that gets better through each chapter.While playing Chapter 1 you will collect items that belongs to your daughter Haze.You will have to use matches, an Axe and other limited items to survive. Matches are your main sources of light and your only comfort. Use them to ignite candles that reveal hidden symbols, paths, or ward off the oppressive darkness creeping in around you."Matches don’t grow on body bags, use them like your life depends on it". Found buried in the room of your missing daughter Haze, this axe is more than a tool , it’s a turning point. Use it to shatter obstacles, and a variety of enemies. Is it a weapon? A message? Or just the start of something worse? SecondHand is a recurring presence within the house. He appears without warning, always just beyond clear sight.
