Use your magical powers to manipulate walls and guide your skeletal minions to freedom. Enjoy a mix of strategy and rhythm as you uncover the secrets of your bodiless origins. Grooveyard features six unique worlds, each with its own aesthetic and challenges. Start in a traditional graveyard, master wall-jumping in a ruined cathedral, unlock doors in an underground prison, navigate broken tiles in a crystallized cave, and defy gravity in a hellish palace.
