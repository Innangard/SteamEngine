View Community Hub About This Game From the two-person team behind Captain Contraption’s Chocolate Factory comes a pixel-art run based park management sim. You’re a robot historian. Humanity is long extinct, and you are studying it the only way you know how… by cloning the creatures you found on a hard-drive, releasing them into a safari park, and selling tickets to your robot friends. There are no fences. There are no enclosures. Nothing stays where you put it. Every creature acts on its own instincts. Herbivores graze and breed while Predators hunt them. Populations rise and fall, and rise again. Put a dragon nest downwind of the field your goats call home and watch what happens. Your job is to stay one step ahead of an ecosystem that doesn’t do what it’s told. You don't control the animals. You control the visitors. You build the paths, your guides follow them, visitors follow the guides. Get the route right and you have paying customers. Get it wrong and you have free food for the new dinosaur exhibit. Every park ends in ruin. So you can learn and try again. Sooner or later the food chain inverts, time runs out, or something gets loose in the gift shop. When a park falls that park ends, but the study doesn’t. This is a run based game, meaning you earn currency each run to unlock: new creatures to clone, new attractions to build, and new systems that promise a little more control. The archive of creatures is extensive… but poorly labelled. As you get deeper into the game you’ll unlock more of what humanity left behind. Lions, tigers and other creatures that behave roughly as you expect. Medieval knights and orcs who settle their disagreements in public. Sentient push bikes. All of it runs on a mysterious green goop. Feed goop to a cloning vat and you get a creature. Feed it to a field and you get wheat. When something dies your Recycler Robot turns it back into goop (if something else doesn’t eat it first). Run out of goop, or money to buy goop, and the whole park starves.
