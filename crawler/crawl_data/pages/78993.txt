Buy American Truck Simulator - Iowa $11.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Set out on an epic journey to Iowa in American Truck Simulator! Explore the Hawkeye State as you drive along the iconic I-80 to the capital city of Des Moines and discover charming towns along the Mississippi River. The Hawkeye State is a blend of natural beauty, rich agricultural heritage, and vibrant cities steeped in culture and history. The state is also dotted with plenty of roadside attractions. If you take the time to explore, you'll find Iowa is home to many unique landmarks, which we encourage you to discover and even take a photo or two. Make the most of your visit to the Hawkeye State and enjoy every moment as you: Truck through 12 cities including Des Moines, Cedar Rapids, Dubuque, Burlington, Iowa City, and more! Discover beautifully recreated landmarks like the Iowa State Capitol, Historic Cedar Covered Bridge, Tree in the Middle of the Road, or the world’s largest grain bins. Deliver cargo to or from new depots – the agricultural factory and agricultural dealer. Visit the biggest truck stop we have ever built in ATS on Interstate 80! Become a vital part in transporting farm produce from the Hawkeye State all over the USA. Explore more than 15 scenic towns (settlements) on your travels such as Spencer, Clear Lake, Dyersville, Oskaloosa, or Traer! Pick up and deliver equipment and goods to the Aluminum Factory, a brand-new prefab depot featuring custom-designed assets. Admire the state’s stunning expansive hills with terraced fields and picturesque farms. Keep goods flowing in and out of Iowa’s huge ethanol plant depots, supporting its lead in U.S. ethanol production. Uncover Iowa’s hidden gems, find hidden roads, viewpoints, and more! The Atlas exploration feature introduced with the Road Trip DLC series is not currently available in Iowa, but will be added in future updates.
