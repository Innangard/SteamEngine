Finish matches with a single click, chain win-streaks into combos, collect buffs and unlock skill upgrades as you claw your way to Rank 1. How to play? Each match on your board has a winrate, a role, special effects and makes your MMR change by a certain amount. Finish matches with a single click - your results trigger chain reactions across the board: dodges, resets, winrate swaps, and more. Read the board, pick the right match at the right time, and turn the odds in your favor to gain MMR and reach Rank 1. Features One-Click Matches . Every match resolves instantly. No loading screens, no queues, no surrendering at 15. Season System . Each season has a limited number of rounds. Race to hit your peak rank before the season ends! Many different Match Effects . Matches chain together through effects like dodging neighbors, swapping winrates, triggering flashbacks on completed matches, duplicating results, and unleashing chaos across the board. Buff System . Collect persistent buffs through periodic Patch Notes events. Buffs activate based on conditions like win-streaks, rank-ups, or roles played. Upgrade them up to level 5 for maximum impact. Tokens . Unlock consumable abilities between runs like Spin, Chaos, Reset and Lucky Day tokens that let you manipulate the board in your favor. Skill Upgrades . By completing achievements you can unlock Skill Upgrades that give you an edge on your next run.
