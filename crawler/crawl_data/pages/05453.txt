It is a short, escalating experience about discovering synergies, experimenting with builds, and creating chaotic chain reactions. Grow The Fire Throw objects into the fire and keep it alive as the sun tries to drown it out Really, Grow Adapt your strategy across 3 phases as your fire grows from a tiny campfire to a celestial force. Start your own cult to feed the flames, then expand your operation to the Moon. Upgrade Discover and unlock 200+ upgrades to create unique builds that change how your fire grows, spreads, and reacts. Strategize Specialize in one flammable type, discover cascading reactions, or craft the perfect automated layout. However you choose to grow, the Sun must be defeated.
