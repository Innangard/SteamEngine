Join-Together’s Konstanz - Villingen Scenario Pack 01 brings a varied collection of new operations to the beautiful Black Forest Railway for Train Simulator. Explore a variety of engaging scenarios across the Konstanz–Villingen route, featuring scenic sections of the Upper Rhine and Black Forest Railways as they appeared between 1998 and 2002. Operate through the picturesque landscapes of Baden-Württemberg, from the shores of Konstanz and the busy junction at Singen to the steep gradients, sweeping curves and forested valleys of the Black Forest Railway, complete with detailed stations, varied signalling and authentic regional atmosphere throughout. From early-morning local services to demanding freight work, this pack offers a fresh set of immersive drives across a picturesque and challenging route. The pack includes four scenarios covering a range of duties between Konstanz, Villingen and Singen. Take control of a Regional Express replacement service to Villingen, operate a regular passenger run to Konstanz, haul a simple freight train through the region, or move a single locomotive through wintry conditions to its destination. Passenger scenarios also include appropriate station announcements, helping bring each service to life and adding extra atmosphere to your journey. Whether you prefer passenger operations, freight haulage or scenic route running, this scenario pack offers a compact but varied Black Forest Railway experience. Spanning approximately 149 kilometres, the historic Konstanz - Villingen route is one of Germany’s most famous railway lines, linking the shores of Lake Constance with the dramatic landscapes of the Black Forest. Included Scenarios A selection of engaging standard scenarios is included: Take the locomotive to Singen Regional Express (replacement service) to Villingen Regional Express to Konstanz Simple freight train to Singen Please note: The scenarios can be found in the standard tab of Train Simulator.
