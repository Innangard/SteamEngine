View Community Hub About This Game Ever dreamed of owning the ultimate comic book haven? Now's your chance! Step into the shoes of a comic book store owner and transform your humble shop into a thriving bookworm’s paradise in Comic Book Store Simulator! Manage every aspect of your business, from buying comic licenses and hiring staff, to hosting prestigious book signings and dealing with daily challenges like troublemakers. Explore the vibrant town and engage with the quirky townsfolk as you build your empire! Your ultimate bookworm's haven! Transform a humble shop into a thriving enterprise by managing every operational aspect of your business from start to finish. Curate the definitive collector's vault! License and stock over 300+ comics from more than 75+ titles, covering everything from Western releases to Japanese mangas and Korean manhwas. Meet the diverse townsfolk and patrons! Discover the fun and sometimes weird stories from people around town. Each unique encounter may reward you with special gifts. Build your dedicated team of experts! Hire more than 10 dedicated staff members, including cashiers, shelf restockers, cleaning crew, and security guards, to manage the daily workload. Reflect your personal style! Decorate and design both the interior and exterior of your store using over 50+ decorations, and utilize custom paint tools for walls, floors, and ceilings. Unlock new possibilities! Expand your comic book store by unlocking new areas, including the highly coveted second floor, opening up new business opportunities.
