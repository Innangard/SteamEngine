Can you survive in this merciless new reality? 🔴 Survive at all costs Hunt or take risks in the ruins of the old world, getting rare supplies. The choice is yours: hide and build or take risks in search of a better life. 🔴 Cooperative mode Play alone or with friends. Together, survivors are stronger, but you can't trust everyone... 🔴 Leveling and loot system Customize your character with skills and Soviet equipment. Strengthen your positions and defend yourself from threats, be it hungry beasts, mutants or marauders. 🔴 Realistic fight for life Hunger, thirst and radioactive zones – even without mutants and bandits, this world will try to kill you. How long can you hold out in a world where hope is a scarce commodity? Soviet Survival is your story in a harsh alternative reality. Are you ready to fight?
