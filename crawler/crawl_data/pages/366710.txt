Negotiate the price. Repair, upgrade, and sell it to the right customer for a profit. Car For Sale Simulator combines the excitement of vehicle trading with in-depth business management. Discover used cars and motorcycles, evaluate their true value, and build your story from a small office to the largest vehicle dealership in the city. You will begin with a limited budget and a small business. Every decision you make, every deal you negotiate, and every vehicle you purchase will directly affect your growth. Can you find the right vehicle? Explore the Market and Find the Best Deals Visit car markets and neighborhoods to discover used vehicles for sale. Every vehicle comes with a different price, condition, and profit potential. You do not have to accept the seller’s asking price. Negotiate, make an offer, and try to purchase the vehicle at the best possible price. But be careful: Not every seller will be honest about the vehicle’s true condition. You can request an inspection report to identify potential problems or take the risk and trust the seller. Sometimes, the worst-looking vehicle can become your greatest business opportunity. Motorcycles Have Arrived Your vehicle business is no longer limited to cars! Buy, ride, restore, and sell 15 different motorcycles . Build a larger and more diverse collection by adding both cars and motorcycles to your dealership. Make a quick sale or keep your favorite motorcycle for yourself. The choice is entirely yours. Buy, Repair, and Increase Its Value Not every vehicle you purchase will be ready for sale. Repair damaged parts, perform maintenance, fill the tank, and wash away the dirt. Repaint the body, change its appearance, and customize it with a variety of modifications. Restore a worn-out vehicle from the ground up and turn it into something far more valuable. With the right investments, a cheap purchase can become a highly profitable sale. But remember: Not every expense guarantees a return. Calculate each vehicle’s value carefully and manage your budget wisely. Create Your Own Style Do more than simply repair your vehicles. Personalize them with different colors, parts, and customization options. Make upgrades that increase their resale value or build your dream vehicle and add it to your personal collection. Sell it or keep it? The decision is yours. Negotiate with Customers Customers visiting your dealership will make different offers for your vehicles. Evaluate their bids, make counteroffers, and negotiate your way to the highest possible profit. As your negotiation skills improve, you will gain access to better purchase prices and more profitable sales. Building a successful business requires more than understanding vehicles—you also need to understand people. Grow Your Business Start with a small office and reinvest your earnings into your business. Move into a larger dealership, expand your capacity, and display more vehicles for sale at the same time. Manage your bank transactions, keep track of taxes, and handle the financial responsibilities of a growing business. Every successful sale brings you one step closer to building the largest vehicle dealership in the city. Explore the City Explore car markets, neighborhoods, businesses, and new trading opportunities across a larger, expanded city map. Get behind the wheel of the cars and motorcycles you purchase and freely explore the city. Find new opportunities, test your vehicles, and access the services you need to grow your business. Key Features More than 50 cars with countless vehicle combinations 15 different motorcycles to buy, ride, and sell A new and expanded city map Used vehicle buying and selling Negotiations with sellers and customers Upgradeable negotiation and character skills Vehicle inspection and evaluation system Vehicle damage, accident, and repair mechanics Vehicle painting and customization options Fuel and car wash systems Business management through your tablet Banking and taxation systems Dealership upgrades and business expansion The ability to build your personal vehicle collection Every Great Dealership Starts with a Single Vehicle Follow the market, find undervalued vehicles, and turn them into profitable investments. Restore cars and motorcycles, negotiate with your customers, and build your own vehicle-trading empire.
