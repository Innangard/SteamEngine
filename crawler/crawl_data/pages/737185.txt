Originally released in 2000, this is the Dungeons & Dragons journey that led countless players into the frozen North—now thoughtfully refined for modern adventurers while preserving every bit of its old‑school charm. Customize a full party of up to six heroes and brave the icy wilderness of the Spine of the World, battling fearsome monsters, delving into ancient dungeons, and uncovering the long‑buried secrets hidden beneath the snow. Original Campaigns & Unseen Quests Experience the full breadth of the original 2000 adventure, including the base campaign, both official expansions, and restored quests that were left on the cutting‑room floor. It’s the most complete version of the classic ever assembled. Original Icewind Dale Campaign Heart of Winter Expansion Trials of the Luremaster Expansion Customize Your Party Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition preserves the deep party‑building that made the original a standout among classic cRPGs. Shape a full adventuring company with expanded class options, new spells, and powerful gear to help you survive the frozen North. Expanded Class Options: Over 30 new character kits and adventuring classes Carry Over Your Heroes: Upload characters from previous playthroughs New Spells & Gear: Dozens of additional abilities and items to strengthen your party New Magical Equipment: Fresh armor and weaponry to aid you in the harsh surroundings Classic Gameplay 2D Isometric Graphics: The timeless perspective of classic cRPGs Real‑Time‑with‑Pause Combat: Strategic encounters with old‑school pacing 2nd Edition AD&D Rules: Faithfully adapted for authentic Dungeons & Dragons play Enhanced for Modern Adventurers Thoughtful refinements make the classic smoother to play today, while preserving its unmistakable atmosphere. Updated Interface: Includes a new quick‑loot bar for easier exploration Polished Experience: Scores of bug fixes and improvements to the original game Cross‑Platform Multiplayer: Adventure together across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS Evil Stirs in the Frozen North Far beyond the warmth of civilization lies Icewind Dale, a stark and unforgiving stretch of land where only the hardiest souls endure. Journey deep into the Spine of the World, a land of snow‑shrouded peaks and ancient dangers, where beasts grow cunning to survive the endless winter. Beneath the carved glaciers, a sinister force gathers strength—an evil whose schemes threaten to spill destruction across the face of Faerûn. This is the world of Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition, a classic Dungeons & Dragons adventure reborn for a new age of heroes.
