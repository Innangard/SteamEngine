This particular percussion ensemble, composed of tuned and untuned instruments like the Kendhang and the Reyong, easily lends itself to catchy melodies and epic percussion lines. It's all we need to accompany the player in this crazy runner game, which I had as much fun playing as I did creating the soundtrack for.
