View Community Hub About This Game In EON Trooper, you join a galaxy at war, assuming the role of an elite soldier on missions behind enemy lines. Each mission is a unique challenge that requires careful planning and swift execution. Whether you’re sabotaging enemy bases, stealing vital information, or rescuing allied soldiers, you’ll need to utilize all the tools and tactics at your disposal. EON Trooper blends intense action and tactical strategy into an unforgettable experience. Dive into battle and choose from a wide variety of weapons; EON Trooper has something for you. Key Features: AI-Controlled Soldiers: Recruit and command up to 4 soldiers with unique special powers to aid you in battle. Extensive Arsenal: Packed with options to suit your playstyle. From firearms to knives, flamethrowers, and more. A wide variety of devices and traps to use to your advantage. Violent Cartoon Style: Experience gameplay with a cartoon visual style that contrasts with its intense and bloody violence. Variety of Enemies and Locations: Face a broad range of foes, from conventional armed forces to aliens, in diverse locations including deserts, snowy areas, and spaceships. Unlock and discover! - 8 unique characters with their own weapons, special attacks, and stats. - 10 aspects that can be improved for each character. - 5 zones with different biomes, each with 5 levels and 10 variants. - Day or night mode for each zone. - 32 reinforcements with unique and upgradeable weapons during the game. - More than 35 types of enemies with unique attacks. Face the intergalactic challenge with EON Trooper. Every mission is crucial in the battle for the fate of the galaxy.
