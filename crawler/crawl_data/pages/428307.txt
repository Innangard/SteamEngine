Immerse yourself in the chaos of battle, where every decision can mean the difference between victory and defeat. Experience the intensity of multi-front warfare, and feel the adrenaline rush as you face relentless enemy waves. NETD deploys you in big battles of +20 different units, +77 unique upgrades, 4 passive powers, and 5 special abilities. Command a diverse array of units, upgrade and customize your units to adapt to the ever-changing battlefield conditions, and secure your victory. From beach landings to desert trenches, Northend Tower Defense's rich level design allows players to continually gain unique gameplay experiences each round. NETD Zombies introduces an entirely new gameplay experience. Deploy a variety of newly designed traps and pick up powerful airdrops, and face off against massive zombie hordes with your army. There are over 12 new powerful upgrades at your disposal. Each upgrade features 4 different levels, allowing you to shock, burn, electrify, and blow up your enemies in increasingly devastating ways.
