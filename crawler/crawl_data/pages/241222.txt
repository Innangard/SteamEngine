From the makers of Sairento VR comes a first person action-combat game that descends you into hell. In Hellsweeper VR you traverse the demonic dimensions, wield weapons with deadly precision, command the forces of telekinesis and muster mystical powers to wipe out your twisted enemies. Reap your rewards, upgrade your skills, and choose your fate as you experiment with unique combat variations and start to discover what being a Hellsweeper means to you. INTENSE LIMITLESS MOVEMENT A daring, no-holes-barred locomotion system that pushes the limits of even the bravest of VR players. MASTER YOUR COMBAT STYLE Summon, wield & master various weapons and magic combinations. SURVIVE IN VARIOUS GAME MODES Various singleplayer modes and 3-player co-op mode. Survive together, Die together. CONTENT RICH WITH HIGH REPLAYABILITY Conquer 18 maps, over 10 enemy types with elemental variants and multiple mission types, offering a unique gameplay experience with every run. Remember to follow us on our Discord channel or Twitter to get updated on our latest developments!
