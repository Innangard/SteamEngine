Slash through hordes of horrific creatures and fight your way to the top of the Tower . Discover new weapons and upgrades, and confront the evil cult that turned you into a Hollow. FACE POWERFUL ENEMIES Unleash your fury, crush your enemies and triumph over menacing bosses that stand in your path. Nothing can stop you from achieving your goal. CHOOSE YOUR WEAPON Pick a weapon that best suits your combat style. Use a swift scythe to quickly cut down your enemies or wield a great axe to overwhelm them with power. GROW STRONGER Death is not the end in Hollow Survivors. Utilize the souls you gather to purchase permanent upgrades from other Hollows, ensuring that you grow stronger with each run. BEFRIEND OTHER HOLLOWS Grow your relationships with other Hollows, uncover dark secrets, and rebuild the settlement. UNIQUE BUILD = UNIQUE LOOK Each soul-card you select directly influences your character's appearance . Every unique combination creates a distinct look for the player. CUSTOMIZE YOUR CHARACTER Buy cosmetic upgrades to craft a unique look for your character. From subtle tweaks to dramatic transformations, you have the freedom to customize your hero exactly how you envision. Experience an exciting action-packed adventure where your choices and skills determine your fate. Will you rise to the ultimate test ?
