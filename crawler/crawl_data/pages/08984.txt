Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We’re dropping BRUTAL JOHN 2 in Early Access so you can help shape it. Your feedback is gold - whether it's a game-breaking bug or “hey, this gun needs more impact,” we’re listening. Let’s build this beast together. We’ve still want to squash every bug, polish every thing, and make sure the game runs smooth like a headshot montage.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan more story missions. Deleted scenes. And more brutallity. We’re wrapping up the full story and packing in new features that we couldn’t cram into Early Access. We plan it to be bigger, meaner, and more brutal.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Right now, you'll get the Complete Story Campaign + "The Vault" - featuring 20 main Story Levels and 13 restored cut levels. That's roughly 7–16 hours of gameplay, depending on how thoroughly you explore and hunt for secrets. It's already packed with intense action, massive explosions, and brutally fun combat - and we've made sure every moment delivers!” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yeah – the final release will be about 20% more expensive. Why? Cause we’re packing in extra missions, new weapons, more content… the works. So if you want in early and save some cash, now’s your shot.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “This game wouldn’t be the same without your help. And only two real men can stop this madness — BRUTAL JOHN and  BRUTAL DOG. Experience BRUTAL JOHN 2, a mind-blowing fusion of the best old-school FPS shooters, like Duke Nukem, Serious Sam, Doom, and Half-Life. Immerse yourself in a heart-stopping, ultraviolent gaming experience that's sure to get your adrenaline pumping. Prepare for a mind-blowing adventure in the year 2090, where the entire world is controlled by the ruthless crypto-mining mutant, Luzilian. With humanity's fate in his merciless and greedy grip, he has brought the global economy to its knees and left the earth teetering on the edge of disaster. Key Features : Crazy Storyline. Save the world from the crypto-mining madness. A true Old-School FPS, featuring retro-inspired graphics and gameplay that pays homage to the legendary FPS classics of the '90s. Play as Brutal John and his loyal companion Brutal Dog, each with their own unique storyline levels. Wield a devastating arsenal of weapons, and experience an ultra-detailed gibbing system where every shot dismembers enemies with gory details. Epic Boss Battles. Face off against giant brain machines, and grotesque alien overlords. Explore the universe! Uncover secrets and hidden Easter Eggs across the entire game. Dive into amazing locations: sci-fi space stations, cities, wild west, caverns, casino, crypto-mining factories, and more. Superior dynamically changing old-school soundtrack.
