Show Us Your Bubble Chaos!! About This Game Stick together, bounce apart. Grab soap, mess with your friends.A cute but chaotic multiplayer party game where jelly creatures compete, collide, and betray each other. Play with up to 6 players online or 4 players locally, and jump into wild matches—or build your own with the map editor In this chaotic playground, soap is power. Pick it up from the ground. Steal it from others. Or lose it the second you grab it.That’s just how it goes.More soap = stronger abilities. Bigger size = more control. Bring your soap back to the scoring point and climb the leaderboard — if you can make it there alive. Your bubble is both shield and weapon.Same colors attract—get close, steal resources, or even absorb your opponent. Different colors repel—bounce enemies away or save yourself at the last second.Switching colors at the right moment can turn the fight around. Soap doesn’t just make you stronger.It can blast enemies away, swap positions, or save you in critical moments.Every piece of soap is a tactical choice. Supports up to 6 players online or 2–4 players in local multiplayer. Dorm room gaming? Friends’ party? Just gather your crew and it’ll quickly turn into a chaotic friendship-destroying showdown. With multiple game modes and fun-filled challenges, every match brings a fresh experience! The game features a DIY map editor. Create: A trampoline-only arena A full-speed boost racetrack Or something even more ridiculous Then share it with friends and see what happens.
