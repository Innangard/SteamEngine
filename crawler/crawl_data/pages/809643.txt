A gigantic bank vault is buried under money up to the ceiling — mountains of bills, bundles, coins and gold. Your job is simple and glorious: dig through all that wealth and feed it to the shredder. Every bill pays out, every upgrade makes you faster, and the mountain keeps shrinking. Grab, scoop, suck it up Start with your bare hands: grab bills one by one and carry them to the shredder. Then go bigger: a bucket, a shovel and a powerful vacuum that pulls money in a swirling vortex. When your hands get tired, launch drones — they mine and haul the cash without you. Upgrades you can't put down Dozens of upgrades for every tool, the shredder and your drones A skill tree with branches for your play style Boosts, relics and gold perks for the long run Numbers climb from your first dollars to trillions — and beyond A secret at the bottom of the vault Somewhere under the mountains of money lies a golden key. Dig down to it, open the massive door and enter the Golden Reserve — a hall full of gold bars where a whole new layer of the game begins. Better together Play solo or invite up to three friends to online co-op. Shared progress, one shared mountain of money, voice and text chat — dig together, argue about who buys the next upgrade, and shred a billion as a team.
