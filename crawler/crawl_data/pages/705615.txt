View Community Hub About This Game Gear up for your fishing adventure across a chain of unique islands! Whether you're casting lines off the coast or hauling in the big catch deep at sea, you'll have plenty of tools to make every trip count. Fishing Traps: Set it and forget it! Perfect for passive fishing while you explore or take on other tasks. Fishing Rods Casting Rod Equipped for accurate long-distance casting, these rods target larger fish species in deep seas. Boats: From dinghies to decked-out deep-sea rides, your boat is your best buddy out on the water. Some are fast, some are sturdy, and some have room for a lot of fish. Upgrade them at Outposts to turn your humble hull into a fishing machine! Outpost: Dock your boat and gear up! Upgrade your boats here to improve speed, storage, and more. Shovel: Dig up upgrades or uncover hidden treasures. More useful than it looks! The Ultimate Fish Wagon: Your Reliable Catch Companion No fishing adventure is complete without a reliable way to transport your precious catch. Introducing the fishing wagon – an essential tool for transporting and storing your fish as you explore new waters and expand your fishing empire. Selling the Fish: Got fish? Turn those fins into funds! Wheel your haul into town, strike a deal, and watch your wallet grow. Save up for better rods, bigger boats, or just blow it all on a shiny new shovel—no judgment.
