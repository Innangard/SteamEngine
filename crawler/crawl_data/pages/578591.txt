Embark on rhythmic fun with Hailing From the Abyss! Explore a variety of distinct stages, each offering unique enemies and original soundtracks, meticulously crafted to match the mesmerizing pixel art style. Adventure through the world of Hailing from the Abyss while our protagonist faces off against her own memories in familiar settings with each distinct tune of melodies after she falls asleep on a train ride back to her hometown while wearing her headphones on. Gameplay & Features -Over 20 hand-crafted stages with unique songs. -More than 6 chapters with interesting plots and 15 unique enemies. -Power-ups and duel system. -Consumables that the player can use during duels. -2D-pixel art style alongside a vibrant atmosphere.
