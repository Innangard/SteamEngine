Play as Elm Myrkwater, a recently laid off adventurer who was gifted the bodega by a distant uncle and trying to turn this cold prison into a welcoming supply spot for adventurers and locals. In this demo you'll be able to experience the first 6 days of the full game, with an "endless" mode so you can continue as long as you'd like. The full game will feature 20+ days, quests, upgrades, and more! Grow crops and process them into items for adventurers. Sell items to friendly customers and dungeon dwellers. Brew potions and turn your crops into sought after elixirs. Day/night cycle with different demands each day you can adapt to and make more gold. Get dungeon slimes you can keep as pets. Enjoy the start of the storylines from the main game. Retro Style graphics Farm 3 different kind of crops (and one secret crop) in the demo! In the full release you'll be able to enjoy 12+ different unique crop types that you can turn into a variety of potions and products for your customers. The dungeon is full of creatures both nice and gnarly, and your customers will need some assistance! Brew different potions and sell them for a significant amount of gold. Get to know your regulars, learn about the dungeon, and take care of your customers needs while you run your shop. In the full release you'll be able to unlock new types of customers as you improve and upgrade your bodega. The dungeon may be home to many dangerous creatures, but your pet slimes are exceptionally sweet! These lil guys also serve a valuable purpose, turning unused items into potions. Slimes are not picky eaters but are quite hungry! We couldn't forget the most important part about running a bodega, petting the bodega cat!
