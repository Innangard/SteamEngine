Your creations have escaped from the mad Electro-Doctor, and now chaos begins in the abandoned house Play as the Escaped Test Subjects (Hiders) Your invisibility is not endless. Gather valuable resources throughout the house to activate camouflage mode and blend in with your surroundings. Signal your friends to coordinate actions, and run when the hunter gets too close. Your task is to survive and avoid capture until the timer runs out. Play as Mad Doctor (Hunter) Your test subjects have scattered throughout the house, and you must find them. Arm yourself with a rifle and begin the hunt. You have limited time to track down all the hiders. Hear maniacal laughter? That means they're nearby. Shoot, corner them, and win before time runs out.
