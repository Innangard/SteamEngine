About This Content This Pack includes: Crusader II “The Saint” (Rank 2 Great Britain); Spitfire Mk.IIa Venture I (Rank 2 Great Britain); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. Crusader II "The Saint" A light cruiser tank developed in the early 1940s that saw extensive action in the deserts of North Africa. This version sports the livery of the 10th Regiment of the Royal Hussars, 2nd Armoured Brigade of the 1st Armoured Division. The Crusader II is a highly effective light tank equipped with a rapid-firing 40 mm cannon with access to high-penetration AP rounds and a destructive APHE round. The gunner also has a shoulder stabilizer for the cannon, keeping it accurate at low speeds. In addition to being a fast light tank, it is very reactive with a quick turret traverse. This, in tandem with the stabilizer and rapid reload rate, provides all the assets needed for a swift and aggressive playstyle. Spitfire Mk IIa "Venture I" The Spitfire Mk IIa is a direct development of the Mk I, featuring an improved Merlin XII engine with higher horsepower, designed to increase high-altitude performance. This variant carries the livery of No. 411 Squadron of the Royal Canadian Air Force. Undeniably a legendary fighter, the Spitfire Mk IIa offers high speed, a strong climb rate, and a very fast turn rate. It's armed with eight 7.7 mm Browning machine guns loaded with incendiary ammunition. The Spitfire gives you options in every scenario: most enemies you can’t outrun, you can outturn, and most enemies you can’t outturn, you can outrun. While it may lack heavier ordnance for close air support, it excels as a nimble and destructive fighter. All premium vehicles allow you to earn increased Research Points and Silver Lions for each battle and comes furnished with all available modifications. With a Premium account (also purchasable in the game for Golden Eagles) you will earn more Research Points and Silver Lions during each battle for a set amount of days. This is cumulative with bonuses from premium vehicles! Note! Refund is not provided if you purchase 2 different packs which include the same vehicle or you already have the vehicle from this pack!
