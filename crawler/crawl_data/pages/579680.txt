View Community Hub About This Game Play as Valeria, a high school student that must work alone and earn enough money to buy more medicine for her sick mother. Experience a paranoia inducing scenario in this PSX-inspired horror game! VALERIA Play as Valeria, a young student who recently lost her father, and now has to care for her sick mother by her lonesome. And  with the medicine running out, she must earn enough money to buy said medicine. MOTHER Valeria's mother has been suffering from a chronic illness for a long time and is bedridden. Although she's bedridden most of the time, she only gets up if she wants to go to the bathroom. THE SHOP Luckily, Valeria's neighbor gave her an opportunity to work at his Nasi Goreng shop late at night. Now, she must cook and serve Nasi Goreng to customers and save enough money to buy her mother's medicine. Key Features : A PSX Inspired horror set in the quiet neighborhood in Indonesia Multiple Endings depending on how you play the game Mix of Horror and Cooking Simulation System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 (SP1+), Windows 10 and Windows 11 Processor: x86, x64 architecture with SSE2 instruction set support Graphics: DX10, DX11, DX12 capable * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
