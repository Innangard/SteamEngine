View Community Hub About This Game Shuffle World is a refreshing take on the classic side-scrolling platformer, blending puzzle mechanics with exciting platforming action! In this unique adventure, you'll control your character as they run, jump, and collect fruits while avoiding obstacles like spikes and enemies. But here’s the twist – the levels themselves are puzzles, and to succeed, you'll need to shuffle pieces of the level to create new paths. Key Features: Innovative Level Shuffling Mechanic: Rearrange segments of the level to make unreachable areas accessible. Swap pieces with a simple click and solve each puzzle to collect all the fruits. 42 Handcrafted Levels: Experience a variety of challenges that progressively increase in difficulty, from easy to mind-bending puzzles. Platforming Fun: Use your agility to jump over spikes, outsmart enemies, ride moving platforms, and unlock blocks with keys. Achievements & Progression: Unlock achievements as you master each level and push your skills to the limit. With its charming pixel art style and a unique twist on traditional platforming, Shuffle World offers an engaging mix of quick reflexes and brain-teasing puzzles. Whether you’re a fan of platformers or puzzle games, this game offers a fresh, delightful challenge.
