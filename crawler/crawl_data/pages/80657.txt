Buy Vagrus - The Riven Realms Ruinous Supporter Pack $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content This pack is primarily a way to support our small team, helping us to continue creating stories and games set in the Riven Realms. If you enjoy our content and want to see it expand, this is a great way to endorse the effort. As a token of our gratitude for the support, this pack unlocks a single alternative appearance for all Companions* in the game (unique to each Companion). These cosmetics are purely visual, do not alter gameplay in any way, and do not represent “canon” looks for the characters. Instead, they are cool alternatives themed around the At the Heart of Ruin DLC’s content and factions. The contents of the supporter pack are: “Crimson Reaper” skin for Harvek “Stalker Supreme” skin for Criftaa “Sardakron Retainer” & “Sardakron Upstart” skins for Renkailon “Expedition Sapper” skin for Garrik “Venomous Fang Brave” & “Arachnid Warlord” skins for Gor’Goro “Waterseeker” skin for Finndurarth “Reeve of Varnurud” skin for Skornar “Conflagrator” skin for Nedir “Raptor Champion” skin for Eylani “Hollow Exile” skin for Vorax* “Faergal Psion” skin for Javek* “Ironclad” skin for Sedarias* As always, your support is much appreciated. - The Lost Pilgrims Team *Renkailon and Gor’Goro receive two skins due to their unique, storyline-related changes in their appearance. For Javek and Sedarias, only owners of the Old Acquaintances DLC can access their respective cosmetics. To be able to access Vorax’s cosmetics, you need to download the free Vorax DLC.
