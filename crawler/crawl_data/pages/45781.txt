Stock your machines, set prices, explore new areas, and enjoy lots of fun minigames along the way. Explore, Interact, Grow Your Business! Start small with just a few machines and limited items. Pick your first area. Will you begin in the calm streets of Komorebi Homes or the busy, colorful Otaku Alley ? Grow your business across the city. Each area has different people and different needs. Office workers want quick drinks, tourists look for fun items, and every spot gives you new ways to earn money. Core Gameplay: 🗾 Unlock new areas with different customer types 🛠️ Place and upgrade vending machines around the city 🛒 Buy items like ramen, drinks, gadgets, and gachapon toys 💸 Set your prices or let the game help you 📦 Upgrade machines with heaters, coolers, and new features 🎮 Play minigames and side activities to earn more money As you grow, you will unlock more items and better strategies. Put the right products in the right places and keep up with daily trends to earn more profit. Features & Systems: 🚚 Manage your truck and restock machines in a simple and satisfying way ⚡ Shift + Click or Double Click boxes to restock vending machines or quickly move them into your truck 🎨 Change your truck color and add fun stickers 🏮 Add special upgrades in areas like Yakuza Row for unique effects 🎴 Buy charms in Temple Grounds that give daily bonuses 🐟 Go fishing at Harbor Market to earn extra money during slow days Play your way. Focus on making the most money, or relax and enjoy the simple loop of stocking, selling, and upgrading. With better balance, smoother progression, and more ways to earn money through contracts and side activities, Vending Machine Co. is easy to pick up and fun to master. Build your vending machine business, grow at your own pace, and enjoy a cozy and relaxing experience.
