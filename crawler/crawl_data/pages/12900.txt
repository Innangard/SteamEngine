Buy Log Away - Christmas $1.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Wrap your Log Away retreat in a Christmas fairy‑tale Step back into your Retreats as the season turns to twinkling lights and quiet snow. This DLC wraps Log Away in gentle holiday warmth, adding festive decorations and a calm Christmas soundtrack that keeps the pace soft and pressure‑free. How it feels Candles glow against the dark, garlands spill over shelves, and a small tree sparkles in the corner while soft music drifts through the room. Toy trains, gingerbread houses, nutcrackers, stockings, and reindeer bring back the feeling that everything is ready and nothing is urgent. Just a holiday evening waiting to be enjoyed. What you’ll find A collection of Christmas‑themed furniture and decorations to mix into any Retreat: trees, wreaths, cozy seating, festive dishes, arches, wrapped gifts, and more. Smaller details that make the season feel near: mugs, snow globes, candy canes, gingerbread figures, ornaments, and a baby reindeer companion to warm your heart. A gentle seasonal soundtrack with a soft, wintry mood that sits in the background while you decorate, explore, or simply take in the magical surroundings. A soft invitation This Christmas DLC does not change how you play Log Away; it deepens how it feels. No new goals, no timers to chase; just another way to let your Cozy Heart fill, to sit with the glow of the season, and to give yourself one more reason to come back and breathe whenever you want.
