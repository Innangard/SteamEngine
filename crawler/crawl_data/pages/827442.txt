The Plains & Prairies Pack brings two classic Ford tractors to the game, alongside legendary machines like the Versatile 1080 “Big Roy”, the modern Fendt Rogator 900, and more. 3 New Brands & 21 Machines With over 20 vehicles and implements spanning from cult classics like the Ford 976 & 1156 Versatile to modern innovations, you’ll farm the open plains and vast prairies in style. The Plains & Prairies Pack features three new brands Ford, GEA, and Trout River, as well as returning manufacturers Brandt, Fendt, International, Salford, Väderstad & Versatile. Moving Covers, Lifting Axles! As the fleet grows, so does the attention to detail: Pneumatic sprayers from Fendt and Salford let you spread solid fertilizer, realistic trailer covers roll over your stored crops, and working lift axles satisfy your hydraulic-loving heart as you maneuver the game's largest manure tanker. Together, these details add to the authenticity of virtual farming.
