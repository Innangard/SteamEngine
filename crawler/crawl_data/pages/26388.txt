About This Content This Pack includes: Tank M3A3 (1st PTG) (Rank 2 China); Fighter Curtiss Hawk H-81A-2 (Rank 2 China); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. M3A3 (1st PTG) The M3A3 Stuart is primarily a Lend-Lease model and saw service with China as part of the 1st Provisional Tank Group, where it saw conflict in Burma. A quintessential light tank, the Stuart boasts impressive speed and a rapid-firing 37 mm cannon. The gunner is also equipped with a shoulder stabilizer, which keeps the gun steady while slowing down. The M3A3 can reach a top speed of 58 km/h and maintains speed well off-road, making it great for getting into a strong position early in battle. It’s also equipped with smoke grenades, and while only a light tank, it offers enough armor to keep you safe from several weaker guns at longer ranges. Curtiss Hawk H-81A-2 The H-81A-2 is an export model of the famous P-40, developed by Curtiss-Wright. It was a prolific fighter in the CBI Theater and flown by the legendary Flying Tigers. A very solid fighter, the H-81A-2 is equipped with four 7.62 mm guns in the wings along with two 12.7 mm machine guns in the nose — you can also equip a 500 lb bomb under the fuselage. Due to having less weight in the wings from carrying lighter guns, this P-40 variant is nicely agile, able to dogfight effectively while still maintaining a good top speed of just over 560 km/h. It climbs well, has a good turning radius, and is overall an exceptionally versatile fighter. All premium vehicles allow you to earn increased Research Points and Silver Lions for each battle and comes furnished with all available modifications.
