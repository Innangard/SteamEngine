Cats Around Us is a series that introduces wholesome silent comics as a new medium for gameplay. "Giant Cat" unfolds across four pages of silent comic panels by Kathrinna Rakhmavika, offering a heartwarming tale about a giant cat causing chaos in a village. A young witch must step in to resolve the situation. Throughout each page, we've hidden numerous cats for you to find while enjoying the story. Additionally, we've included a photo mode for players to capture intriguing moments from the comic, allowing them to colorize and share their creations with the world.
