A 2D Fighting Action Game featuring Side-Scrolling and Roguelike combat. Enjoy a visceral cosmic adventure where every punch lands hard—team up for explosive Combo chains and unleash Overdrive Burst attacks to defeat powerful enemies. The two Cosmic Hunters are standing by for deployment! Game Modes The game features the following core play modes: Story Mode｜Explore the treasure planet, unlocking the story and character mysteries. Battle Rush Mode｜Every run starts fresh! Build unstoppable power routes with hundreds of enhancements Ultimate Challenge｜Face the hardest battles and prove your true strength! Characters and Enhancements 【Core Experience】 Become one of two uniquely skilled Cosmic Hunters: Muu — A close-combat robot fighter using energy claws, capable of agile movement to knock enemies into the air and chase them with continuous Combo attacks for massive damage. Kelly — An enhanced human specializing in firearms and tactical martial combat. A technical, high-skill character capable of commanding the battlefield entirely. V.T — An otherworldly visitor wielding runic swordsmanship and dark magic. A mid-to-close-range swordmaster built around the blade, capable of summoning otherworldly entities to assist in battle. Deep Build Customization：50+ combat Tech Arts × Hundreds of Chips per character allow you to forge unique playstyles Support System：Revive using portable recovery ship checkpoints + battlefield Support allies to turn the tide in desperate moments! Battlefields & Enemies Utilize elemental offense to exploit weaknesses: Machines are vulnerable to electricity, and biological enemies fear fire. Gain combat advantage by using environmental objects! Boss Enemies These powerful entities are equal in strength to the player characters. They can also accumulate energy and unleash Overdrive Burst attacks. To counter their powerful offensive, focus your attacks during the openings between their moves to break their 'Stance', interrupt their offense, and gain the upper hand! Rich Content & Replayability • 50+ Tech Arts and special actions per character • Customize performance via flexible Chip layouts — multi-air-jump / infinite energy / multiple HP layers, and more • Collect all Support allies to enhance tactical depth • Dozens of enemy types and diverse BOSS encounters • Battle Rush Mode includes hundreds of upgrades for destructive power escalation Whether playing solo or co-op, you’ll enjoy fast-paced, thrilling action every time you start the game!
