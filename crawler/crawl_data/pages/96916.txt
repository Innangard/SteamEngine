Collect data. Unlock upgrades. Watch the algorithm grow beyond your control. An active incremental where you feed an evolving AI with data and shape what it becomes. Will it save the world… or take it over? You explore a dark network with limited vision, gathering Data and feeding it to an evolving AI. At first, it listens. Then it starts to respond. Eventually, it starts to change. Every upgrade matters and helps you gather more Data. Each upgrade increases your AI’s capabilities, pushing it closer to something beyond human control. With AI Perks, you decide how it evolves. Will it protect humanity… optimize it… or dominate it? The game blends active incremental gameplay with a reactive narrative. The AI comments on your actions, questions your intent, and reacts to the way you play. Sometimes it helps. Sometimes it judges.
