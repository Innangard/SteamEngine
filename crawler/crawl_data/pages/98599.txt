Look the level carefully, there is no time limit, no resources to manage, so you can calmly focus on solving the challenges. Features 55 handcrafted levels. Stack boxes. Catchy soundtrack. Jump between platforms, like a good cat would. Minimalist interface and gameplay. Restart level. Fun and sweet sokoban with relaxing. Mimi, the cutest one that will warm your heart ♥.
