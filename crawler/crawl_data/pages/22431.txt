Neighbors: Suburban warfare is a chaotic free-to-play party brawler, where you battle for suburban dominance. The neighborhood is the battleground, your neighbors are the enemy and their property is the target. Team up with your friends in chaotic PVE and PVP battles, defend your house with traps and gadgets, and destroy your neighbors’ houses with whatever tools you have at hand. Fast-paced matches, wacky combos and pure chaos make every game of Neighbors: Suburban Warfare crazy, unpredictable and wild. Pick your suburban hero → Grab a garden tool → Barricade your house → Invade your neighbors’ and cause chaos → Make it back for a lovely home cooked meal!
