Each entry is a compact experience. Some are minute-long challenges, while others tell a complete mini story. Many have been remastered with achievements and quality-of-life improvements, optimized for seamless Windows play. The collection grows over time. New jam entries, unreleased projects, and refreshed browser originals will be added as free DLC, turning the launcher into an evolving anthology you can return to whenever you want something fresh. The current build already includes several playable titles spanning platformers, puzzles, artful experiments, and action bites. Games Currently Included: So Much Money : Collect valuables while dodging traps like spikes and saws. Going Up : Guide a ball through tricky obstacle courses. Swordvania : A metroidvania-style platformer. Simple Square : Collect tokens, doge saws and flying rectangles. Eridu : An artful, turn-based exploration with random islands. Ancient Orb: You are in a Ancient Temple. Collect orbs to fuel the ancient apparatuses.
