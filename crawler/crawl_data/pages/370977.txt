You’ve inherited a dusty little storefront and a mountain of debt. The landlord? Unforgiving. The rent? Rising every single week. Your only hope? Sell the right treasures to the right customers, and let the cards decide the rest. Features Hands-on Inventory Management Arrange your antiques Tetris-style to make the most of your limited storage space. Every slot matters, the wrong layout could cost you your life. Sell What They Crave Read your customers, match their tastes, and score huge payouts. The better the match, the bigger your profit. Tarot-Based Upgrades Draw cards between days to gain powerful new abilities. Choose wisely, the deck can bless or betray you. Spiral into debt Weekly rent hikes keep the pressure high.
