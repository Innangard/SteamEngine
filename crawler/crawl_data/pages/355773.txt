In the wake of the Afghan war and the Soviet Union’s dissolution, nationalism, religious fervor, and ethnic tensions ignite the southern flank of the Russian Federation. What’s in this DLC? This DLC expands the timeline of the game to include the years 1989 to 2001 , for both the USA and the USSR. As usual, you’ll find a mix of smaller scenarios and massive campaigns (1949–2001). Playing through the final decade of the 20th century, you may often feel like you’re fighting against yourself, or against the system, rather than competing with the other superpower. You’ll find that this has its own unique charm.
