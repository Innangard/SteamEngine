Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We envisioned Chickenauts as a game with a strong emphasis on fun-per-minute. Easy to jump in when you want to decompress and play through one or two super intense runs - without needing to commit an entire evening. You can absolutely enjoy longer sessions of slaying mutated robo-ufo poultry, but every single run should be this juicy, bite-sized, flavour-packed experience. And it’s fun to expand the menu, right? We believe we’ve built a solid system that allows that. We want to do it. And we want to make sure that the way we do it is tailored to our community's tastes. So if you want even more replayability with the tools that YOU want, please, do chime in! We’re here to listen. Which combat mechanics are your favorites? What would be the evolution of your worst enemies? What crazy rooms should await you further down the ship? Join us now to make sure the vision stays true, but it has even more special flavors!” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We expect Chickenauts to stay in Early Access between 6 months and a year. Of course, for how long exactly depends on the features and ideas that you and other community members may bring to the table. We want Early Access to be the time for you to explore the game, see what feels right, and what could use some of that good, old elbow grease, and what additions to the game you’d like to see. It’s also time for us, a time to assess all your suggestions, see what is possible and what is not, implement it, and make sure that everything added to the game is as polished as its foundations.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to expand the full version on the systems already in place for Early Access. Based on community feedback and playtesting, we’ll decide which areas require extra attention and care as we grow the game. We want to balance our own ideas and vision with what the community wants. Basically, we want to add more stuff to the toolbox, so your runs are even crazier, longer, and feel even more different: more companions, more biomes, more enemies that haunt them, more weapons, and upgrades/mutations so you have even more build options! Of course, throughout the Early Access, we’ll continue polishing the already existing game by fixing bugs, improving performance, and ensuring that everything is as smooth and responsive as you’d like it to be.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version includes two biomes of randomly generated rooms and the item/ reward pool, one Big Boss Encounter, 4 different weapons, meta-unlocks for your character, and three different chicken companions, each of them with a unique ability.  While these systems and features may change during development, they are already tested, smooth, and highly playable.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “That’s correct, the game will be priced differently! If you dare to join us now, not only will you have a chance to shape the game’s direction with us, but you’ll also become a Chickenaut at a lower price than when the game’s finally fully released with full content.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We’re excited to hear your feedback and suggestions at all times, so we’ll be scouring Steam forums, our Discord server, social media, and - last but not least - the feedback forms you can access through the game’s menu. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Chickenauts is a companion-based, action roguelite. Blast your way through a massive, randomized alien mothership with an assortment of lethal, mutated chicken companions at your side. It all starts when some goshdarn pesky aliens land right outside your farm and abduct all your chickens to run all sorts of insane genetic experiments on them! Grab your gun and mutated chicken companion of mass destruction to teach these alien scum a lesson about trespassing and private property. Create unique companions of mass destruction with each run. While teaching alien scum a lesson or two, you’ll free, arm, and mutate various chicken companions, equally hell-bent on revenge and destruction. Each chicken companion is inspired by pop-cultural icons and brings very different traits and abilities. With multiple, stacking mutations to pick from along the way, you will create overpowered, game-breaking chickens of mass destruction on each run. Every run brings something different from the last. Fast and varied runs deliver short but intense sessions made up of compact, randomized rooms that take under a minute to clear. You’ll face multiple chicken abomination enemy types and bosses, ensuring no encounter is the same. Rooms, enemy spawns, plus all mutations, upgrades, and loot drops are all randomized, so each run is unpredictable and encourages experimentation. But failure doesn’t mean total defeat. Collected eggs, weapons, and unlocked companions all carry over when you go again. - Short but intense rounds. Each room takes under a minute to clear. - A collection of pop culture-inspired companions with different abilities - Mutate companions with randomized, stacking abilities on each run. - Multiple enemies and boss types - Randomized rooms, enemy spawns, mutations, upgrades, and loot drops - Make progress with each run.
