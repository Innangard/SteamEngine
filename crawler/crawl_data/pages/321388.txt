Forge custom dice , harness wild loot synergies , and dive into the cursed realms of Eldralith; each run a new roll of fate. Outsmart brutal foes in turn-based combat , embrace high-risk modifiers, and uncover dark secrets in a world trapped in cycle. Victory changes you. So does failure. Dialogue hits as hard as the dice in this pixel-powered showdown. Nostalgic Roguelike With A Dice Building Twist Play as one of three heroes, each haunted, each hunting meaning. Customize your dice with powerful, swappable faces as you forge the perfect build through each high-stakes run. Every choice counts. Every roll is a risk . Every face is a choice—build your dice, shape your fate. Dynamic Dice Building Forge your strategy with interchangeable, upgradeable dice faces that define your power—and your fate. Sacrifice weaker sides to unlock potent traits , and adapt your build on the fly as the dangers of Eldralith deepen. But choose carefully: each die holds only so much power , and every decision edges you closer to mastery… or ruin . Choose your curse in color—each palette a new shade of doom. Pixel Peril & Chiptune Charm Descend into a dark fantasy where every pixel drips with dread and every chiptune echoes with myth . Luck & Loot blends retro style with eldritch horror , inviting you to brave haunted forests, corrupted keeps, and cursed melodies alike. Will you break the cycle, or become one more echo in the void? The dice are cast. The fate is yours .
