So, you want to know about Lacey’s games? Ok, But just so you know, I have nothing to do with what people have been saying out there. Lacey’s Flash Games are the real girl games - or at least, that’s how they were marketed back in the day. Most of them are simple and intuitive point and click games. You interact with the environments, make choices, and unlock different endings depending on what you do. Sounds harmless, right? Just follow my instructions Each game takes place in its own themed environment. You might work at a pet shop, ride a skateboard, bake and decorate cakes, or help Lacey and her friends deal with one of their many problems. Your job is usually pretty simple. Read my instructions. Click on things. Make the right choices. Although sometimes doing the wrong thing is more interesting.... Cute, colorful and completely normal The games are bright, friendly and full of cute little details. At least, they are supposed to be :) Look closely and you might notice that some things do not quite belong. A character might act strangely. An object might appear where it should not. The instructions might stop making sense. If something feels out of place, maybe it is better to ignore it.... More than one ending Your choices can change how each game ends. Some endings are predictable. Others are not so much. Even I have no idea what Rocio hid inside some of these games, and I am not sure I want to know :p So, that is basically it Or at least, that is what I remember. There might be a few things Rocio added without telling me, but... oh well, If you REALLY want to search through all of it, then be my guest on this reflection of her twisted mind.
