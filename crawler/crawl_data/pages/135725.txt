Up. Up. And then down. Paddle Paddle Paddle: Up Up Up is a brand-new rage map that flips everything on its head. Instead of paddling forward, you are climbing straight into the sky (...even though you're technically still paddling forward). You can take on this challenge alone or together with a friend. Solo paddlers will need perfect control and patience. Coop crews will need communication, trust, and nerves of steel. Either way, one mistake can end the run for everyone involved. There are no checkpoints. Not a single one. One bad jump. One mistimed paddle. One moment of panic. And your boat falls all the way back to the start. The map is packed with new mechanics, moving hazards, and vertical obstacles, all wrapped in a vibrant tropical theme. Hidden throughout the climb are 9 brand-new skins and new voicelines, waiting for those who refuse to give up. Playing together turns this into the ultimate friendship challenge. Playing alone turns it into a personal nightmare. Both options are equally valid. Both will hurt. Features Completely New Vertical Map Paddle upwards, not forwards, through a towering tropical challenge. No Checkpoints. Ever. One mistake sends you straight back to the beginning. Hardcore Coop Rage Gameplay Built to break your teamwork, patience, and friendships. 15 New Game Mechanics Including elevators, paragliders, frogs, minecarts, boss fights, and more chaotic surprises. 9 New Tropical Skins Hidden across the map for those brave or stubborn enough to find them. New Voicelines Fresh audio reactions to celebrate your victories and your inevitable failures. Vibrant Tropical Theme Beautiful, colorful, and completely unforgiving.
