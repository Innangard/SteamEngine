About This Content This Pack includes: SdKfz 140/1 (Rank 1 Germany); Ju 87R-2 (Rank 1 Germany); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. Sd.Kfz. 140/1 The Sd.Kfz. 140/1 (or Aufklärungspanzer 38(t)) is a light tank designed for recon duties. Being fully tracked, it's more suitable for traversing rough terrain quickly, where contemporary wheeled vehicles would struggle. Equipped with the fast-firing KwK38 20 mm cannon with HVAP rounds, the 140/1 can quickly tear through light vehicles from the front and many stronger opponents from the side and rear, which it can reach comfortably thanks to a 160 hp engine. The cannon can also point almost directly upwards, allowing you to use the Sd.Kfz. 140/1 as an anti-air vehicle if the situation calls for it. The 140/1 excels at fast close-range engagements where its cannon has the most power, alongside quickly capturing strategic points from the outset thanks to its impressive speed. Ju 87 R-2 A long-range variant of the Stuka and a modification of the B model. The “R” in the name stands for Reichweite, meaning range. This R-2 version is able to carry several bomb types: a 250 kg, 500 kg, or even a 1,000 kg bomb under the fuselage, capable of taking out multiple tanks in one strike if they are close together. It can also carry a large napalm bomb, ideal for open-topped targets or for destroying bases. It's equipped with a defensive 7.92 mm MG 15 machine gun, along with two offensive 7.92 mm MG 17 machine guns in the wings. These can comfortably defeat light targets and even aircraft, as the Ju 87's large wings allow you to turn fairly effectively. Note!
