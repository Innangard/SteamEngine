A round of Belote with the family, a quiet game of Solitaire, a quick Four in a Row over coffee. Belote , Whist and Hearts : the great trick-taking games Up & Down the River and Crazy Eights , also playable online Rummy , President and Golf Solitaire and FreeCell for playing on your own Four in a Row , Checkers , Reversi and Backgammon Horse Race , for 2 to 4 players Sea Battle and Radar Yatzy , Sudoku , Pairs and 2048 Word Guess , Quiz and Riddles Solo against the computer , with three difficulty levels: easy, medium or hard. Pass-and-play on one screen : everyone takes their turn, and the other players' cards stay hidden in between. Online with a friend (Up & Down the River and Crazy Eights) using a simple game code. If the connection drops, the game picks up where it left off. Quit whenever you like : long games are saved and can be resumed later. The rules of every game are always one click away. One purchase, every game included. No ads, no shop, no account to create. Everything plays offline, except online games with a friend. The whole interface, the rules, Word Guess, the Quiz and the Riddles are available in both languages; switch any time in Settings. Le Tapis Vert is made in France by Petit Vaisseau, a one-person independent studio.
