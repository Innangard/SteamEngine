Solve tasks that need real teamwork, survive monsters that each hunt differently, and figure out who you can trust before they decide for you. Full friend group or public lobby, doesn't matter; the chaos is the same either way Scream with each other Scream at each other Screw around with silly items and difficult co-op tasks Infected players have unique abilities that will help them cause chaos. Call a Lockdown to accuse someone and force a vote. The "Lockdown-Captain" will see one player's true role. Fight back with guns, knives, or a boxing glove when monsters break containment. Don't take too long, or make too much noise... Note from the devs: Built by four friends, start to finish. Every task, ability, and monster made by hand. No AI-generated content. Made in Godot.
