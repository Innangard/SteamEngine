In your workshop, combine mysterious aquatic ingredients and enchanted crops to craft hundreds of delicious dishes. Whether it’s farming, fishing, or baking—no complex controls are needed. Just assign the right tools, ingredients, and staff, then let time do the rest. Watch as your automated production lines spring to life! Nothing here works by ordinary rules. Grab a pot, add some soil, count to five… and somehow you’ve grown a sunglasses-wearing cat from a magic cube!? Before you cast your line, remember to bait your hook with a worm, some grass, or maybe even a depth charge! From ordinary Bread Fish to dangerous Nuke Fish—all kinds of curious creatures are waiting beneath the surface, watching you 👀 The town’s Barter Market fluctuates daily. Sell the day’s hottest items at the perfect moment to maximize profits! Use your earnings to unlock more workstations, buy better ingredients, and make even more money! Don’t forget to decorate your cabin! Buy all sorts of uniquely styled furniture from the shop to create your ideal layout while boosting your "Beauty Rating." Save different design sets and switch between them instantly to match your mood. This is your home in the magical town—where the in-game day/night cycle syncs with real time, immersing you in the charming rhythm of island life.
