Hit the open roads of American Truck Simulator like never before with the Road Trip: Ford DLC! Step away from your big rig and get behind the wheel of iconic Ford vehicles, bringing a fresh way to explore the world of ATS while staying true to the spirit of the open road. Take the wheel of: The iconic 1967 Ford Mustang Fastback The all-purpose 2023 Ford F-150 The modern 2023 Ford Bronco and the legendary 1998–2012 Ford Crown Victoria Discover the world around you with the Atlas feature and its Tourist Boards - currently available in California, and the Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Montana, Wyoming, and South Dakota DLCs, with the remaining states to be gradually included in future updates. Visit iconic landmarks, learn more about them, and collect beautifully designed postcards along the way. And don't forget that if you own this DLC, you can also enjoy the Atlas features from behind the wheel of a truck. Alongside these new ways to explore, the Road Trip: Ford DLC unlocks other features available in Car Mode. Buy personal cars and customise them with a variety of accessories and paint options. Take on smaller deliveries through Quick Jobs or the Dispatch Market and build your courier reputation, adding another way to experience the open road and make the most of your journey. You can also team up with your friends in Convoy mode and hit the road together, with trucks and cars sharing the highways! We're thrilled to have partnered with Ford to bring these iconic vehicles to life in American Truck Simulator, and we can't wait to see where your next road trip takes you!
