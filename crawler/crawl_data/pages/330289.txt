View Community Hub About This Game In Coffie Simulator , step into the world of coffee and experience the thrill (and chaos) of running your own café! Your dream? To own the best coffee shop in town. But it won’t be easy—you’ll need to master the art of coffee-making, keep your customers happy, and manage your shop like a pro. Design & Customize – Create the coffee shop of your dreams! Stock Up & Manage – Keep an eye on your supplies or risk running out when you need them most. Handle Your Finances – Every bean counts! Spend wisely and invest in upgrades. Expand & Upgrade – Improve your shop to attract more customers and build your coffee empire. Over 500 Unique Customers – Each with their own personalities and preferences! Your Choices Matter – Treat customers well… or badly! Your reputation will shape how they react. Stay on Your Toes – Follow every order carefully; slow service or mistakes could drive customers away forever! From Simple to Complex – Learn and master recipes, from a basic espresso to the most intricate latte art. Realistic Brewing Mechanics – Use professional barista tools to craft the perfect cup. Master the Espresso Game – Precision is key! Extract, steam, and serve like a real barista. Every Save is Unique – No two playthroughs are the same! Customers, events, and challenges change with each game. Weather Affects Business – A rainy day might mean more customers—how will you adapt? Surprises Await – Uncover hidden events and unexpected twists as you grow your café.
