UNMEMORY COMBINES TEXT-DRIVEN MYSTERY, CHALLENGING PUZZLES, AND IMMERSIVE NOIR THRILLS. unmemory tells the story of someone hunting down his girlfriend's murderer, a member of a lady thieves' gang. Hindered by a rare, untreatable form of memory loss, he relies on notes, pictures, and recorded messages to unravel the mystery and possibly uncover an unsettling truth in this captivating 90s thriller story about a girl gang and a fractured mind. KEY FEATURES - Innovative gameplay: unmemory combines interactive reading, engaging story, and puzzle mechanics across 8 chapters with 8 hours of gameplay. - Stunning aesthetics: every element in unmemory is carefully crafted, from editorial design to sophisticated photographs and nostalgic devices. - Groundbreaking storytelling: unmemory explores the intersection of gaming and literature, redefining what games (and books) can be. - Nostalgic vibe: unmemory is filled with 90s references to noir thrillers, graphic adventures, and iconic devices.
