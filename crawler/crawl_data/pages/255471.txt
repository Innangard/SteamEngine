You take on the role of Amelia , a loving mother and wife who has traveled to the remote cabin to celebrate Christmas with her husband, Adam, and kids. But when a murderous maniac starts stalking the cabin, your vacation plans take a terrible turn. Dynamic AI – The killer reacts to your environment and actions. Phone Mechanic – Read messages to uncover the dark lore. PSX / VHS Graphics – Choose from 5 filters or combine them for the ultimate retro experience. Custom Killer Masks – Switch up the killer’s look for added variety. Adam and the kids are stuck outside while you are alone in the cabin, and a snowstorm ensnares your family, miles from assistance. You can feel the loneliness, and your main responsibility is to keep the place safe and secure. However, you understand that you are not alone as night falls. Someone is observing, waiting for the ideal opportunity to enter. Outwitting this vicious murderer and getting away before they destroy everything is the only way to live... But it's not just the psychopath that you should be afraid of. Adam and Amelia's relationship has never been easy, and as the game progresses, a terrifying revelation could ruin everything you believe to be true about him. Christmas Mutilator™ is about surviving your own house, employing every tactic and tool available to you to stay hidden, stave off the invader, and discover the truth about the man you believed you knew. It's not simply about escaping a killer. One incorrect move could result in certain death in this game, which blends heart-pounding stealth techniques with psychological strain. Every creak of the floorboards or shadow in the corner may be interpreted as the psychopath's next move as the atmosphere grows oppressive while the storm rages outside. Will you be able to survive the killer, uncover the dark secrets of your husband, and escape this nightmare? Or will you become another victim of Christmas Mutilator™ 's brutal house invasion?
