Content For This Game Browse all (1) Arise from Shadows Soundtrack $3.99 $3.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game You are Ka-Hool, a ghost, that remembers nothing but their name. Awaken in a cave, your goal is to look for your past, to look for your identity. Throughout this adventure, you’ll rediscover your own abilities, stroll through the remains of an ancient world, and continue your journey for memory. Remains of a mysterious civilisation lay ahead. Who were they? How did they disappear? Your quest for memory will cross a quest for history. Ka-Hool is nothing of an ordinary ghost: take control of the beasts that creep in the shadows to progress in your adventure. Each creature has unique abilities, use them wisely! Yet beware, the depths have their own ecosystem, and not all creatures can be fooled. Travelling through the world will take you to different sublime sceneries, and your quest for knowledge will be shaped by challenges and fragments of the past. This world is beautiful but filled with radiant dangers. In your spectral form, the light is your greatest foe, and you must avoid it at all costs. Choose your path wisely, or risk a shining demise.
