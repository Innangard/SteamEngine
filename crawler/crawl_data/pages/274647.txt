View Community Hub About This Game DO YOU HAVE AN EYE FOR DETAIL? Forensic M.E. Protocol puts you in the shoes of a forensic investigator. Your goal is clear: arrive at the scene, find the clues, and decipher what happened. You have 9 distinct scenarios, each with its own beginning and end. From a dark alley to a seemingly quiet neighborhood. No two cases are the same, and each one challenges you to adapt your approach, follow new leads, and connect the dots in unexpected ways. This is not a child's game. You will encounter crimes of passion, settling of scores, kidnappings, and mature themes. Each scenario tells a dark story that you will have to reconstruct clue by clue. Is the clue on a roof? Take out the Drone . Is there something under the car? Send in the Ground Robot . You have many tools at your disposal, and only you decide how to use them. Nothing is as it seems. Or is it? Discovering every little clue is essential to understanding each case. Any detail can change the entire plot, and it won't be easy. YOU SET THE PACE There are no stressful timers or "Game Over" screens. It's just you, your tools, and the mystery. Examine the scene at your own pace, analyze samples, find loose ends. Who is lying and who is telling the truth? Find out if you have what it takes to be a forensic detective. Ready for your first case?
