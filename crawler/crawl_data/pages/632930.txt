View Community Hub About This Game Pixel art meets medieval nostalgia in a modern roguelike turn-based game. You are a fledgling adventurer from the Duchy of Lyle. Faced with a crumbling kingdom overrun by goblin hordes, you must traverse randomly generated deadly dungeons and rise as the hero who saves all. Forests, swamps, snowy plains, deserts, abandoned ancient cities... every step unveils a new path and unknown events. Swordsman crushes foes with heavy blades, Assassin strikes from the shadows, Mage bombards from afar, Alchemist concocts arcane potions, Guardian stands unbreakable. Each class offers a completely different combat style. Mix and match skills, items, and sub-classes freely—no restrictions, imagination is the only limit. Battle greedy goblin tribes, savage orcs, and eerie undead armies—every step demands careful planning. One mistake can ruin everything, but a perfect combo will send your adrenaline soaring with an explosive rush of satisfaction! Random events, mighty artifacts, hidden bosses, sub-classes... you never know if the next encounter will be a blessing or a disaster. Death means rebirth, with countless build combinations and surprises waiting to be conquered.
