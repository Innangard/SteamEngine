Angry and sneaky Bosses crept up and knocked him all the way down! Now you must help Cuboy collect every star, discover all secrets, solve every puzzle, and defeat three wicked Bosses to claim the golden trophy! Platforming and More In Cuboy Adventure, you’ll face platforming challenges filled with dangerous obstacles such as spikes, disappearing blocks, and crushing presses. Solve puzzles with buttons and boxes, escape fast chases, and take part in epic yet adorable battles! Alongside classic levels, the game features dynamic stages inspired by Geometry Dash , where Cuboy automatically runs forward and you must jump at the perfect moment and use your abilities wisely. There are also intense Boss levels ! The game includes 3 Bosses and 3 zones under their control. Each Boss has unique mechanics, and defeating them won’t be easy! Awesome Abilities A unique part of the gameplay is that Cuboy starts with no abilities at all – he can only jump. But as you progress, you’ll find upgrades that grant one of three special powers: Double Jump Box Pushing Dash When Cuboy picks up a new ability, it replaces the previous one. This means you must choose abilities in the right order to solve puzzles and reach the end of each level. The Cute Little Square Cuboy Adventure is presented in a charming cartoon style, and you play as the lovable main hero, Cuboy . He is always positive – no matter what happens, he never gives up and keeps moving toward his goal! Cuboy loves stars, watches your cursor, falls asleep if you stay idle for too long, and his body wiggles like jelly when he jumps and lands. Step by Step In every level, your goal is to reach the finish line, collect as many small stars as possible, and find one big star. Secret locations can only be discovered by players who explore carefully. Complete all 45 levels to earn the silver trophy. To claim the golden trophy, you must finish every level with 3 stars – only then will you achieve 100% completion !
