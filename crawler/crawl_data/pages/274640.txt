About This Game Detective Rainy Night places you in the shoes of Iker Carmona, a police officer tasked with investigating strange disappearances in the area. After driving all day, Iker decides to spend the night at a motel called “Holiday.” The next morning, he hears an argument outside his room. Upon stepping out, he discovers there is no electricity, mobile phones and computers aren’t working, vehicles won’t start, and it has begun to rain heavily. Seven guests are staying at the motel alongside you, each with their own story. As the weather worsens and vehicles remain unusable, no one can leave the motel. Over five days divided into 12 narrative-driven levels, you will experience unexplained phenomena, disappearances, deaths, and cryptic symbols—all unfolding within the motel. As a police detective, your role will involve speaking to guests, interrogating them, and piecing together the truth behind these events. Detective Rainy Night is a guided narrative adventure with elements of deduction. Throughout the game, events will unfold that you’ll need to recall later to solve the case. Violent crimes, mature stories, and psychological terror await you in the Motel Holiday. Grab a pen and paper because cracking this case won’t be easy.
