The Feather River Canyon Enhanced route for Train Simulator Classic is an expanded and richly detailed 130-mile edition of the famed Western Pacific crossing of the Sierra Nevada Mountains – and this premium scenario pack includes two additional diesel locomotives and ten new and authentic career scenarios! With the Feather River Canyon Enhanced — Premium Scenario Pack, you’ll climb aboard for memorable passenger, freight, unit-train, local, and switching operations across the length of the legendary line from Oroville to Portola, California. Included (as complete locomotives) are two potent Western Pacific freight diesels, the Electro-Motive GP35 and General Electric U30B, both wearing “Perlman era” green and orange liveries! With the Feather River Canyon Enhanced — Premium Scenario Pack, you will: Take the throttle of a classic and stunning Western Pacific EMD FP7 to lead the famed California Zephyr across the length of the WP’s famed Third Subdivision. In a three-part scenario, you’ll make the westbound descent of the Feather River Canyon with the “Silver Lady,” and make station stops at Portola, Keddie, and Oroville. Climb aboard a pair of Western Pacific General Electric U30Bs wearing green and orange liveries to handle challenging local and switching duties on a run through the heart of the Feather River Canyon west of Keddie. Work a priority freight – Train BN 138 – in a three-part scenario from Oroville up the canyon to Keddie and onto the rugged Inside Gateway, calling upon a typical mix of Burlington Northern and Western Pacific second-generation diesel power. Handle heavy Western Pacific symbol train UCW loaded with Utah coal as it eases its way westbound down the grades of Feather River Canyon with a trio of Western Pacific GE U30Bs providing the horsepower – and dynamic braking – for the tough descent. Call upon a pair of veteran Western Pacific Electro-Motive GP9s to work an eastbound local turn along the east end of the WP Third Subdivision, with switching duties en route at Spring Garden and Sloat. Serve as engineer of EMD GP35-powered Western Pacific hotshot freight APRG (auto parts bound for Rio Grande interchange) as it makes the eastbound climb of the Feather River Canyon and Beckworth Pass en route to a crew change at Oroville. The ten career scenarios of the Feather River Canyon Enhanced — Premium Scenario Pack are based closely on actual railroad operations and train numbers and deliver superb railroading realism and authenticity to your Train Simulator Classic experiences. American mountain railroading at its challenging best, as the remarkable Western Pacific Railroad battles the rugged and beautiful Sierra Nevada Mountains, awaits you now with the Feather River Canyon Enhanced — Premium Scenario Pack for Train Simulator Classic!
