Relive Defining Moments Experience key events from the life of Jesus and witness moments whose meaning still resonates today.  See these scenes unfold from a personal perspective and engage with the story in a way that feels immediate and immersive. Journey Through the Holy Land Travel across diverse locations from Jesus’ ministry, from deserts and mountains to towns brought to life with atmosphere and detail. Discover the Teachings Explore the foundations of the Christian faith through an interactive journey inspired by the life and message of Jesus. Gain a deeper sense of the ideas, values, and events that continue to shape millions of lives around the world. Resist the Tempter Stand firm against Satan’s temptations and face the spiritual trials that lead to the fulfillment of Scripture. Read the Scriptures Encounter selected Bible passages throughout the game and connect each moment of the journey to the words that shaped it. Are you ready to resist temptation in the desert, heal the sick, and uplift those in need? Perform over 30 meaningful miracles - from feeding the 5,000 and healing lepers to calming the storm and restoring sight to the blind.
