What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Plane Accident is already a substantial game, but we're far from done. Early Access is our canvas, and we're eager to add exciting features based on your input. Your feedback is crucial; it's our guiding star, ensuring the game meets and exceeds expectations.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Our production plan is making the game leave Early Access in 9-12 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In future we plan to add new features: - New maps with new plane crashes - each map will contain different plane models, which presents new challenges. -Various Planes and flying machines - We want to give player chance to investigate the crashes of the various machines - each with it's own challenges - New localisations That's not all that we have planned!” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Investigative Core Loop: Explore crash sites for vital clues. Head back to your office to investigate and piece together the story. Functional Menus and Options: User-friendly menus for seamless navigation. Easily adjust game options to tailor the experience to your preferences. Localization Functions: Designed with localization functions for a global reach. Initial localizations are already in place for accessibility. Playable Maps: Five unique maps offering diverse investigations including tutorial. Unique investigation and challenges in each map.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Currently, we do not plan to increase the price after the Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We're committed to evolving the game mechanics based on your Early Access experiences.
