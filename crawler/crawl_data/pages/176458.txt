View Community Hub About This Game Roll, color, and solve your way through 27 relaxing isometric puzzles! In Color Cube , your goal is simple: roll your cube across the board and transform grey cubes into matching colors. Plan your moves, experiment with different routes, and discover the most efficient way to complete each puzzle. With no enemies, no time limits, and no game over screens, you can enjoy every puzzle at your own pace. Features 27 Unique Puzzles – Explore a variety of isometric cube layouts, each offering a new challenge. Relaxing Gameplay – No time pressure, no deaths, and no move limits. Take your time and enjoy solving puzzles. Undo Your Moves – Experiment freely, correct mistakes, and try different strategies. Challenge Your Best Score – Complete levels in as few moves as possible and compete on the global Steam leaderboards. Flexible Controls – Play with a keyboard, mouse, or controller, with customizable controls and alternative keyboard directions. 31 Languages – Enjoy Color Cube in your preferred language. Steam Achievements – Earn achievements as you progress through the puzzles. Whether you're looking for a peaceful puzzle experience or enjoy perfecting every move, Color Cube offers a colorful challenge that's easy to pick up and satisfying to master. Relax. Roll. Color.
