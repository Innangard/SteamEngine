View Community Hub About This Game Party Madness is a 2-4 player online party game. Compete with your friends in wacky mini-games, collect points, and claim victory! UNIQUE MINIGAMES! Every mini-game in Party Madness is pure chaos! One moment you're escaping an angry octopus in the ocean, the next you're in the middle of a bank heist dodging lasers — and just when you think it’s over, you’re driving through a desert with meteors raining down on you. Dodge, laugh, and grab the cash! EVERY GAME IS A NEW CHALLENGE! As you face off against your friends, each game throws a different challenge your way. Race with cars, battle on boats, or outsmart your opponents in a variety of mini-games using different strategies to come out on top. COSTUMES & CUSTOMIZATION! Customize your character with a wide range of costumes inspired by pirates, astronauts, samurais, and more! Unlock new outfits as you play and show off your unique style in every match. Get ready to dive into a world of crazy fun! Party Madness is packed with non-stop action, laughs, and unexpected adventures. The madness awaits!
