A 1-4 player co-op airline comedy. You and your friends run a tiny island airline with one very old plane, and it starts falling apart the moment the wheels leave the ground. An engine catches fire at cruising altitude. Clip in, climb out on the wing and get to work. Load the bags, fuel up, board everyone and take off. Then figure out whose job it was to check the engine. The autopilot won't hold forever. When the nose drops, someone had better be in that seat. Every seat is sold and every passenger expects a smooth ride. When they don't get one, you'll hear about it. Not every problem starts inside the plane. Keep an eye on the windows. Island weather turns in minutes. Rain on the glass, gulls everywhere, and you still have to land. Lights down, passengers asleep. Enjoy the quiet, it never lasts long on this plane. Whatever happened up there, you made it down. Dress up your crew, pick an emote and celebrate on the apron. 1-4 player online co-op or LAN, or play solo Proximity voice chat Fly the plane by hand and fix it mid-air A full cabin of passengers to keep in line Changing weather and night flights Character customization and emotes AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: The store page text, in-game text and translations were written with the help of AI tools and edited by our team.
