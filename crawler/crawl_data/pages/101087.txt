Thousands of VHS tapes are scattered across a chaotic 1980s-inspired video store. Pick them up, identify their genres, and return every tape to the right shelf. Clear the floor, fill the empty shelves, unlock new abilities, and slowly bring the store back to life. Play alone or with friends in online co-op and turn complete chaos into a perfectly organized video store. Sort Thousands of VHS Tapes Horror, Sci-Fi, Western, Romance, Action, Comedy, Adventure, Drama, Fantasy, Japanimation and more. Every VHS has its place. Explore the store, inspect the tapes, find the correct shelves and watch the entire store transform as you make progress. Features 📼 Thousands of VHS tapes to pick up, sort and organize! 🗂️ Sort movies into the correct genres and shelves! 🧹 Restore a completely chaotic 1980s-inspired video store! ⚡ Unlock upgrades and abilities to sort tapes faster and more efficiently! 👥 Play solo or with friends in online co-op! ✨ Watch the store transform as the floor clears and the shelves fill up! 🌈 Explore a colorful retro environment filled with VHS covers, neon lights and nostalgic details! 🕹️ Discover additional activities and interactive elements throughout the store! 🏆 Compete for the top spot on the Steam leaderboards! 🏅 Unlock Steam achievements through sorting, exploration and progression! 🧼 Enjoy a satisfying tidy-up game at your own pace!
