Please Wishlist. Conspiracy! is a detective game that takes you on a journey into the heart of the Deep State. Only you can figure out the hidden truth. An investigative puzzle game that asks you to piece together a hidden conspiracy. The game is a detective puzzle scenario, in which players may inspect sources, documents, audio logs, and records in order to solve the puzzles contained. The game takes place on one screen containing all the resources. The player will piece together the story chapter by chapter, receiving validation every 5th correct chapter. The player will experience an uproarious, satirical comedy, while also engaging in the sort of logical deduction found in Return of the Obra Dinn and Her Story. The game combines written, audio, visual and meta-game puzzles to create an enthralling experience. It plays for roughly 2-3 hours.
