View Community Hub About This Content 《Voice of Belldona - Artbook》 is the official artbook for 《Voice of Belldona》, featuring 【60】 pages of beautifully presented content. This artbook brings together a wide variety of materials created during the game's development, including character concepts, costume designs, expression variations, background artwork, and promotional illustrations. It also features selected design drafts and production materials that were not fully shown in the game. Through these artworks, you can gain a deeper understanding of the world of 《Voice of Belldona》, as well as the creative process behind its characters and visual style, from initial concepts to their final presentation. Included Content Main character illustrations and character profiles Character costume, expression, and pose designs Background artwork and environment concepts In-game CGs and promotional illustrations Drafts, line art, and early design concepts Selected previously unreleased artwork Messages and additional notes from the development team This artbook is provided in PDF format .
