View Community Hub About This Game Gallery Organizer is a relaxing sim where you arrange an art gallery featuring works by real artists. [Note: This game has not been tested on the Steam deck] Sort and Hang Sort paintings by category and find the right spot to hang them. Power up Discover handy tools that help you sort and hang faster and focus on creating the perfect exhibition. Your Gallery, Your Challenge Choose the size of the gallery to play a quick session or a game that lasts hours. Each gallery you start is unique. Choose one of four difficulty levels, and enjoy a fresh challenge every time you play. Slow Down and Enjoy Take your time, admire beautiful artworks, relax to soothing music, and watch an empty gallery transform into an exhibition you'll be proud of. Key Features Experience paintings by real artists. Enjoy relaxing gameplay with no time pressure. Create your own hand-picked art collections. Replay with customizable gallery sizes and difficulty. Choose from a variety of soundtracks.
