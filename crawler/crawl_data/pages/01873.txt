Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Looks Fine on Paper is a logic puzzle game built around interconnected spatial constraints. Each level presents different area requirements and spatial clues. Starting with a blank blueprint, you must reason your way to a floor plan that satisfies every condition. Design Your Own Layout Rooms have no predefined shapes or positions. Use area requirements and spatial clues to determine the shape, size, and placement of each room, gradually completing the floor plan. Complex Spatial Relationships Room-to-room adjacency, the placement of doors and windows, interactions between furniture, and how furniture relates to room boundaries, doors, and windows must all be considered. These constraints are interconnected. Every decision can affect other requirements. Every Change Has Consequences A room with exactly the right area might leave no space for another. Moving a piece of furniture could resolve one conflict while creating a new one. You must untangle the relationships between all the conditions, reasoning, testing, and adjusting until you find a layout where every requirement is satisfied. The real challenge is making everything work at once.
