Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “With Roadside Research we want to make sure that the game not only follows our vision, but also take into account community expectations. The game features simulation-heavy systems which are adaptable and modular, meaning these systems can be expanded upon over time, and even make them more interconnected so the game feels more rewarding the deeper you go.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We are keeping the Early Access period to around a year. We plan on providing regular, smaller, updates on a frequent basis with large systems-wide updates every 3-4 months. Regularly updating the game every so often and creating new features for players to try are planned and vital to the game’s development.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The current version of Roadside Research is fully playable and cohesive in its own right. However there is a lot of room for expansion as well. This means: alien research tech, multiplayer interactions, extending and upgrading the gas station, new NPCs and behaviors. There are still many areas we want to explore and having the community involved every step of the way is important to us.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Currently Roadside Research features fully playable areas and systems. The gas station is customizable with over 25 items, each placeable wherever in the station. Key items and store-based mechanics have upgradable tech trees for players to unlock. NPC customer behaviors are implemented, they come into the store to purchase items, use the facilities, and refill gas. There is also the customer satisfaction and suspicion meters in-game already. Players will have to balance accelerating their research without humans finding out, or else agents come to the gas station to investigate.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes, Roadside Research will be at a lower price during Early Access. As more major systems and features are added the price will also reflect the time and effort put into developing the game! ” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We've already implemented certain requests from the community, like being able to control how many toes the aliens have in-game. Yes that really did happen, please rejoice at being able to choose from 1, 2, 3, or 4 toes (per foot) now. Player feedback and opinions are very welcome as ultimately it is what matters to make the game the best it can be. Our Discord has reached over 5000 members, so please join us there if you'd like! We run events there like: drawing your own alien mask, sharing gas station layouts, and we’ll be running more activities there during the development of Roadside Research. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions 2026 Roadmap for Early Access About This Game Aliens have landed on Earth… to run a gas station. Play solo or up to 4 players and do human tasks to blend in. People depend on you, it’s your job to make sure that the shelves have the correct items, refuel customers passing by, and provide food for travelers. But most importantly, you must conduct your research. Stay Undetected Your mission is simple. Gather all data you can from humans without their knowledge. Scan, steal, and test various methods on customers while they stop by the gas station. Be careful not to raise the “suspicion meter” too high, as that will trigger the visit of government officials. Be Normal, Be Human During the day, take care of the gas station as an ordinary employee. Restock the shelves, order wares for the shop, refuel passersby, and exchange goods for cash. At night you will be able to gather your thoughts, and plan for the next day. Upgrade and Customize Balance between two missions, upgrading the gas station to attract more customers and upgrading your alien tech to process data and gather information more efficiently. To blend in, draw a very normal human expression on your state-of-the-art disguise. During closing hours, you can also upgrade the station, inside and outside to make sure everything is going according to plan. Features Chaotic solo or co-op fun: Gather friends or play alone to manage the gas station while pursuing your alien mission. Dual Upgrade Path: Manage upgrades for your gas station and for your alien technology at the same time Satisfying Simulation: Clean, cook, and stock your workplace with all necessary supplies and items to make sure every customer leaves satisfied. Alien Deception: Make sure to stay disguised and don’t get caught by human government officials. If you are too suspicious, they will turn you into goo.
