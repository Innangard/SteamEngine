View Community Hub About This Game Red Pistol is a roguelike bullet-hell set in a dark and twisted Wild West, where survival demands precision, strategy, and skill. As bounty hunter Reed Ransom, you must battle relentless hordes of undead, hunt notorious outlaws, and confront the vampire Varius Valentine—the mastermind behind the undead plague consuming the frontier. With procedurally generated maps, evolving combat strategies, and an ever-growing bounty on your head, no two runs are the same. Unlock powerful upgrades, experiment with unique builds through the skill card system , and engage in fast-paced shootouts where every shot matters. The wanted system increases the challenge dynamically—raising your notoriety means stronger enemies will come for you. Meanwhile, the camp evolves as you progress, reflecting your journey and offering new upgrades to push deeper into the nightmare. Beyond the chaos of battle, Red Pistol delivers a rich narrative rarely seen in the genre. Meet intriguing characters, uncover secrets of the Dark West , and make crucial choices that shape Reed’s fate. Will his newfound power be his salvation… or his downfall? Features: Fast-Paced Bullet-Hell Combat – Dodge, shoot, and adapt in intense, skill-based battles against relentless foes. Roguelike Progression – Experiment with different builds using the skill card system, evolving weapons, and permanent upgrades. Wanted Poster System – The higher your bounty, the more dangerous the enemies that come after you. Four Treacherous Worlds – Explore RedWood, Desolation Sands, Whispering Pines, and Crimson Mansion—each with distinct enemies and challenges. Challenging Boss Battles – Face deadly foes like Iron Heart, Soul King, and Blazing Crown, each with their own unique attack patterns. An Evolving Camp – Upgrade your base of operations with new tools, skills, and weapons as you progress. Immersive Storytelling – Experience a rich, atmospheric narrative that unfolds with every run. Take up your pistols, embrace the chaos, and survive the Dark West. The hunt begins soon.
