THE YARD Lay out the intake zone, the shredder, a warehouse and the drone hangar, then grow the line: depollution plant, crusher, dismantler, compost shredder,  battery neutraliser. Six waste families arrive as the waves go by, scrap, drums and tyres, rubble,  e-waste, green waste, batteries. Each one needs its machine and the right drone  tool: magnet, clamshell bucket or grapple. Conveyors link every machine to a warehouse, which sells on its own. Let one fill up and everything behind it stops. THE PRESSURE Trucks queue on the access lane and none of them can be turned away. Four bays: if the queue stays blocked for twelve seconds, the yard shuts down. Every five waves a heavy convoy is announced a wave ahead : a wrecked car, a bus shell, a digger, a plane wreck, and your drones strip it where it lands. Overdrive doubles a machine's throughput for fifteen seconds. Do it three times in a row and it breaks down at the worst possible moment. BETWEEN WAVES, BETWEEN RUNS Every wave you clear deals three cards and you keep one: technologies, grants, contracts, and pacts that trade a sharp bonus for a cost you accept. A run lasts twenty waves, then carries on in endless mode. The debrief shows exactly where it broke, wave by wave. Reputation unlocks new sites, starting kits and six difficulty levels that stack their constraints. The finds album and the achievements carry over from run to run. GOOD TO KNOW Mouse only.
