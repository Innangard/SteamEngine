Bull-Type Terriers Dogs Pack introduces four new dog breeds to take care of in your shelter. The bull-type dogs are loving and lively companions, strong of both will and body, who deserve a family and home! Meet these smiling canines as puppies, adults, and senior dogs. Pit Bulls The mighty pit bull stands strong and muscular, but don’t let that fool you! These dogs are gentle and loyal, often used as emotional support animals. They are a great family dog as they are both gentle and caring, but can be protective and scare away unwanted guests when necessary! Bull Terrier The Bull Terrier is a striking dog with a unique, long face and small eyes that can be easily recognized at one glance. Due to their willful and energetic nature, they need a family that can train them well. In return, they will receive a loving, loyal companion who loves to run around and play. Staffordshire Bull Terrier Also known as The Staffy, a strong, muscular dog that is always eager to play! Full of joyful energy, these meatheads need a lot of activity and contact with their owners to stay happy. With training and love, these dogs can be extremely loving and gentle. American Staffordshire Terrier The Amstaff is a strong and loyal dog that, with proper training and socialization, can be the perfect companion for any family. They are very attached to their owners and make great competitors at agility contests. More dogs to love! These dogs may look tough and strong, but they need a loving, caring family just like any other pup! It is time to find them a home that will take care of their needs! Help them find a family in Animal Shelter 2 - Bull-Type Terriers Dogs Pack!
