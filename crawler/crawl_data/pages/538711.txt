Buying commercial frame mockup templates gets expensive and it is time consuming. Less savvy users need photo editing software such as photoshop which adds to the expense. Instant Frame Mockups will save your budget and reduce the time you spend on making mockups for presenting your wall art. Use for commercial purposes however you like, no attribution required, no royalties, no accounts or logins capturing your personal data, nothing except awesome frame mockups. Instant Frame Mockup comes with a library of 80 beautiful backgrounds and you can load in your own custom background! Just pick a background, or your own custom one, and in seconds plugin your artwork, move and resize the picture canvases around and hit the camera button. You'll get images of 16:9 and 4:3 aspect ratios that you can use on your digital art store or where ever else you are showing your wall art. Instant Frame Mockup is easy and intuitive. Instant Frame Mockup canvases are a typical 2:3 and 3:2 aspect ratio used for printed art so you just need to plugin images in those ratios to get the best result.
