View Community Hub About This Game Birds Watching is a short psychological horror about bird watching. An endless global fire destroyed the world, and you are now alone, the last human being. You survive on the top of a mountain, the only place still untouched by the flames, and your only occupation is watching with your binoculars the wild birds escaped up there with you. It is a peaceful, isolated, desperate life. Until you are not alone anymore, and the only ones who can save you are the birds; but they are picky. Satisfy them, or lose everything. Be honest. Be ruthless. Be careful. Birds are watching. Features A short but impactful open-world horror experience (~60 minutes). Go birding! Observe, catalog, and find every bird in the reserve. Explore a handcrafted open world full of secrets and hidden locations. Follow your animal companion. Not only birds are present in the mountain.
