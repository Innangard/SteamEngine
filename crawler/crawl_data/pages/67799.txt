Jogar Attic Antiquities Grátis Jogar Ver Central Comunitária Acerca deste jogo Apply tools with surgical precision to exorcise haunted dolls in your workshop, but be careful, because it's your life on the line . The spirit within the doll can be restrained, but each mistake you make is an opportunity for it to escape. Follow the story of Elias Beaufort as he learns about the spirit within each doll and discovers why they've suddenly appeared on his doorstep. Enter the Workshop Use your mouse and keyboard to change to the correct tool in the workshop for each limb on the haunted doll. When looking at the doll in the workshop, press A or D to switch tools Left-click on a limb to focus on it and begin working on it You can unfocus by right-clicking , returning your attention to the workshop Some limbs may require the use of multiple tools to fully remove the evil within. Cut It Open Left-click and drag the scissors across the limb to reveal what's inside. Stay in line when cutting; go too far or hit an obstacle might be your last mistake. Extract the Objects Pull on the strange objects away from the doll to remove them. Sew to Seal It Up Special stitches are required to seal up the doll. Use A or D to turn the needle and charge it by holding left-click. The longer the charger, the further you push the needle forward. Don't push it too hard or you'll poke into something else, loosening the seal. Solve the Puzzle Eradicate the remnants of the spirit from each limb as you go along. Move your cleansing coin across each fragment of the spirit on the board, leaving no trace behind when you reach the final fragment. Anything you leave behind is another chance for it to escape. About Elias Beaufort Not all is peaceful in the night, and the Beaufort family has known this all too well for centuries. The year is 1908;  you are Elias Beaufort, who had long forsaken the family traditions capable of cleansing haunted objects. It is after all, a dangerous yet rewarding hidden profession that he swore he'd not return to. One night, he found a box outside his shop and inside was a dangerous haunted doll. Unravel Elias' history and learn who and why this doll has appeared.
