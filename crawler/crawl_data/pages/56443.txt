Key Features Cooperative Multiplayer Mayhem: Join forces with players from around the globe in real-time co-op gameplay. Whether you're delving into dungeons or engaging in epic boss battles, cooperation is key to survival. Form guilds, strategize with your allies, and communicate to outsmart treacherous foes. Perma-death Intensity: Experience a unique gameplay mechanic where death is permanent. Every decision matters as you navigate a high-stakes environment. Can you rise to the challenge and conquer the Mad God's realm? Diverse and Dynamic World: Explore an expansive realm filled with diverse dungeons, menacing monsters, and thrilling surprises. Engage in non-stop action as you traverse a meticulously crafted world, rich with opportunities for discovery and adventure. Distinct Character Classes: Choose from 18 unique character classes, each possessing their own strengths and abilities. Whether you prefer close combat as a Knight, magical prowess as a Wizard, or agile strikes as a Rogue, find your perfect fit and playstyle. Unleash the Power of Your Pets: Nurture your companions to unlock their hidden potential. Feed and fuse pets to create stronger, more powerful allies! With hundreds of unique skins to collect, your pet army will be as stylish as it is fierce. Each pet comes with its own unique set of abilities, ready to aid you in your quest for victory. Epic Loot and Customization: Vanquish enemies to earn a variety of loot, including weapons, armor, pets, and exclusive items. Customize your character's appearance and gear to optimize your combat prowess and showcase your style. Community-Driven Development with Seasonal Content Updates: Engage in a thriving community of players who contribute to the game's evolution.
