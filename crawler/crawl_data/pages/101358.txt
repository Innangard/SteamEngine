Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Fear: Nightmare Citadel is an immersive first-person psychological horror game set within the dark and oppressive halls of a realistic medieval castle. Plunged into total darkness, you must rely on your perception and a single flickering lighter to navigate the unknown and survive the suffocating atmosphere. KEY FEATURES: Photorealistic Visuals: Detailed medieval architecture, realistic lighting, and deep shadows create an intense, claustrophobic atmosphere. Flickering Lifeline: Your lighter is your only source of light. Use it wisely to cut through the darkness and spot details in the shadows. Immersive Exploration: Traverse abandoned halls, claustrophobic corridors, and ancient chambers as you attempt to find a way out. Constant Psychological Tension: Every creak, sound, and shadow plays with your perception as you navigate the eerie silence of the citadel. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: The game contains psychological horror elements, jump scares, intense hallucinations, and mature dark humor that may not be suitable for all ages.
