Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Pawsome Resort is launching in Early Access so that players can begin enjoying the heartwarming animal hotel experience we’ve crafted so far, while also helping shape its future. Your feedback will guide us as we expand the world, polish the systems, and bring the story to life in preparation for version 1.0.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We’re currently planning for Pawsome Resort to stay in Early Access for around one year. During this time, we’ll roll out frequent updates including new animals, gameplay systems, quality-of-life improvements, and story content.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan for the full version of the game to include more animals, quests, customization options, and expanded storylines. We're also considering new villagers, seasonal events, mini-games, and additional late-game content.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? ““There’s already plenty to enjoy in Pawsome Resort! Here's what’s currently available in Early Access: Gameplay Manage your own animal hotel and take care of over 25 different animal species. Build and decorate both indoor office space and outdoor animal habitats. Handle daily needs like hunger, cleanliness, and happiness for each guest. Earn money, gather items, and expand your business through quests and events. Seasonal day-night cycle with dynamic weather and cozy visuals. Villagers & Story Interact with a growing cast of villagers, including the vet, lumberjack, and a mysterious witch. Unlock the main questline and complete side missions to and improve the town.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “No, we do not plan to increase the price when the game leaves Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We want to build Pawsome Resort with our community. Throughout Early Access, we’ll be actively reading player feedback on Steam, social media, and our Discord server. We'll also run regular surveys and polls to help decide what features and animals to prioritize in future updates. Your feedback means the world to us. From cats to crocodiles, every guest is unique! • Meet and interact with quirky villagers, build friendships, and uncover charming stories as you discover the Pebble Town! Game Features: • Customize your office and build spacious outdoor habitats for animals of all kinds. Design the perfect resort just the way you want. • Feed, love, and care for a wide range of animals, from fluffy cats to exotic wild ones. Keep them happy and healthy during their stay at your animal hotel! • Strange events are affecting the village, and it’s up to you to uncover the truth. Complete quests, meet mysterious characters, and restore harmony as the story unfolds. • Get to know the charming residents of Pebble Town, each with their own stories, quirks, and secrets. Strengthen your bonds through heartfelt conversations and meaningful interactions.
