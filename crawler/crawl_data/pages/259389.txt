Chill Fox is a small, silly, and fun stealth game where you play as a clever fox with a mission to steal chickens and eggs from a quiet countryside farm. There’s no timer, just take your time, explore the beautiful surroundings, and enjoy the laid-back vibes. But don’t let the calm fool you! Two guard dogs are always on the lookout. Stay sharp Fox! Ten golden eggs are scattered across the area, each one is worth five hens. Find them all! And whatever you do… don’t forget the elephant. He deserves his freedom too!
