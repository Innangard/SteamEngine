Explore the house by following clues to solve puzzles, experience supernatural events and uncover the horrifying truth in the end. Have you ever been haunted by your own memories? A man finds himself standing before the house where he once lived with his family many years ago. At first, he doesn’t recognize it since it looks like a plain abandoned house, surrounded by a dense forest and an eerie atmosphere. Driven by curiosity, he steps inside and begins to uncover the tragic story of a family. But he needs to find the answer for the most important question: Why is he here? Notice: This game contains psychological horror, flashing lights, noise and a few jump scares, which may prove disturbing to some players. KEY FEATURES: Short gameplay: Experience the terror within a short time span Hallucinations: Were there this many stairs? Where's the corridor? It was here a few seconds ago... Simple horror-driven puzzles: The puzzles might be simple, but are you brave enough to get to the other side?
