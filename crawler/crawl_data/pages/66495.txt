About This Demo Working With Montabi is a desktop companion where curious Montabi visit your space and keep you company through your days. Every time you press a key, your avatar presses theirs too, reacting to your activity as you work and browse. Give visiting Montabi a little pet and watch them enjoy their time in your space. Productivity with Company Whether you're working, studying, gaming, or browsing, Montabi is there to keep you company with built-in productivity tools to help you stay on track. Stay organized with a to-do list, jot down notes with the memo, and manage your work sessions with a Pomodoro timer. Along the way, your avatar will offer gentle words of affirmation, encouragement, and little reminders to help you keep going. Meet Curious Montabi Visitors Look who came to visit! Curious Montabi may wander into your space when you place something they like. Some are easy to attract, while others need a little extra effort. Let them play, give them a gentle pet, and they might leave you a gift before they go. Keep typing to speed up their visits and see who comes next! Discover All Montabi! Meet every Montabi, from everyday visitors and special companions to elusive Montabi that almost no one ever meets. Fill in their Montapedia entries and collect their unique customization rewards to expand your customization collection. Personalize Your Avatar & Space Mix and match outfits, furniture, decorations, and accessories to create a space that feels uniquely yours. Whether you’re feeling cozy, playful, relaxed, or just want to try something new, make your little world your own. Collectible Montabi Discovery — Experiment by placing different items to discover a variety of collectible Montabi. Fun & Productive — Turn your desktop into a fun little space with Montabi. Enjoy their company while using productive tools to keep your day on track. A Free Experience Without Microtransactions — Enjoy the full Working With Montabi experience for free. No microtransaction walls, no locked gameplay, and no pay to win mechanics. Starting Small, Updating Occasionally — We’re starting small with a limited amount of content and features. We plan to update the game occasionally with new content and features while continuing to listen to our community and improve the experience over time. No Marketplace Scams! — Enjoy a cozy desktop companion without worrying about marketplace scams, bots, or other activities commonly found in similar games. If you’re looking to make money by collecting and selling things in this game, this probably isn’t the game for you. DLC to Support Development — So, how do we keep maintaining and updating the game? We offer optional DLC, starting with Donation and Supporter Packs , for players who love Working With Montabi and want to support our development. The game itself will always remain free and purchasing DLC is completely optional.
