Ollie, kickflip, and grind your way through Japan's biggest cities, as well as a world of meadows, volcanoes and oceans. Rack up points and chase that sweet high score as you flip, trick and stick the landing in your customisable ride. Face off against a lineup of reimagined Japanese trains, gain the respect of an underground network of gangs and rebels, and turn rivals to allies as you master your skills. Journey from naive beginner to seasoned pro as you strive to race the fastest train in existence and become a legendary Denshattacker! From mecha magical girls to moving castles, and mechanical worms to a Denshattacker army -- face a flurry of madcap bosses that get wilder and wilder the further you get. Use what you’ve learned to stop them in their tracks. Travel from the countryside of Kyushu through the metropolises of Osaka, Tokyo and more, now sealed under domes by Miraidō. Venture across Japan to the snowfields of Hokkaido... and beyond! Each new region holds fresh challenges to beat and rules to break.
