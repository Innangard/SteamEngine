Investigate signs of an ancient presence and uncover a forgotten chapter of humanity’s past. MAIN FEATURES: Survive deadly encounters Face unpredictable dangers and stay alive as you navigate hostile environments where every step may lead to a new threat. Hidden secrets Uncover the alien statuettes hidden in the most remote corners of each level. Some can be spotted from afar, challenging you to figure out how to reach them, while others lurk in the darkest and most unsettling places. Find as many as you can: the final reward will reveal deeper lore and shed light on the darkest secrets of this unsettling universe. Compelling story Dive into a story filled with mystery and terror, drawn from the darkest corners of Lovecraft’s mind.
