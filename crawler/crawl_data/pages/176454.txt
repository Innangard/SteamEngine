View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to Maze Slider , a captivating ice slide puzzle game that challenges your wit and reflexes! Inspired by the iconic ice slide puzzles from Pokémon, Maze Slider expands this beloved concept with innovative mechanics and diverse levels that will keep you engaged and entertained. Navigate through a Variety of Unique Levels In Maze Slider , each level presents a fresh puzzle to solve. Control multiple characters simultaneously or outsmart cunning enemies that follow your every move. You'll encounter brain-teasing challenges, including trigger blocks that toggle doors on and off, and special blocks that can only be touched a limited number of times. As you progress, the stakes rise with obstacles like menacing saws and collectible gems that unlock the exit portal. Intuitive Controls for Everyone Whether you're a seasoned gamer or new to puzzles, Maze Slider makes it easy to jump in! Use the WASD or arrow keys , the left stick on your gamepad, or on-screen buttons to maneuver through the map. For a more tactile experience, swipe vertically or horizontally to slide your way to victory! Features : Challenging and Fun Puzzles : Experience a mix of thought-provoking brainteasers and fast-paced gameplay. Multiple Characters : Master the art of controlling more than one player at once for complex challenges. Dynamic Obstacles : Evade enemies and avoid hazards while collecting gems to reveal the exit. Flexible Controls : Enjoy seamless gameplay with multiple control options, suitable for all play styles. Join the Adventure Today! With a total of 27 thrilling levels , each offering unique challenges and mechanics, Maze Slider promises hours of fun and excitement. Perfect for casual gamers and puzzle enthusiasts alike, this game invites you to think strategically while sliding your way to the exit. Are you ready to slide into an unforgettable puzzle experience? Play Maze Slider now and start your icy adventure!
