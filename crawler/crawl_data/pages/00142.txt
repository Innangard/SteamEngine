View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to LYSSA: Goddess of Rage, a tactical card RPG set in a world run by mercenaries, where three factions are locked in war and every fight is short, sharp, and brutal. Pick Your Favorites. Make Them Unstoppable. In LYSSA, every mercenary has a price — and a past. Recruit the ones you trust, gear them up, and turn them into legends. Then let your Commander tie it all together: their skills power up your whole deck, opening thousands of possible combinations to build a squad that fits your playstyle. Grow Strong. Then Prove It. Follow the twist-filled story campaign of LYSSA: Goddess of Rage and adapt to arenas where random obstacles change every fight. Then comes the true test: real-time PvP . Climb the ranked ladder, jump into battles and tournaments, and fight your way to the top of the leaderboards . Play at your own pace : quick bursts or marathon sessions — how you get there is up to you. Just make sure you get there first . Find Your Crew. Take the City. Join an alliance , swap strategies with players from around the world , and team up for community events . Then go to war together: seize territory alongside your alliance and hold it against everyone who wants it back. In LYSSA: Goddess of Rage, the city belongs to whoever takes it. Get Close. Get Personal. Off the battlefield, your mercenaries have plenty to say. Chat with them , get to know the stories behind their contracts, and build relationships that unlock exclusive moments in your personal gallery . So hire your mercenaries, build a deck no one sees coming, and fight your way to the top of a city that belongs to whoever takes it — your story in LYSSA: Goddess of Rage starts now!
