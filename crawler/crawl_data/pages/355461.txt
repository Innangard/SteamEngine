Play as the USA or the USSR , manage Prestige, Budget, and National Policies , and bend history through policies, proxies, diplomacy, and brinkmanship. Easy to pick-up but hard and deep enough to offer a challenge, this is the kind of game you will keep coming back to. How Does the Game Work? The objective is simple: earn more prestige points than your opponent. You gain prestige by improving diplomatic relations and signing treaties with countries across the globe. At the same time, your opponent will work relentlessly to undermine the nations in your bloc and shift the balance of power in their favor. Beyond diplomacy, you must also manage your nation’s internal policies and carefully plan how to exploit the geopolitical landscape to weaken your rival over time. Are you ready to lead your ideology to global dominance—or will history repeat itself? If you're familiar with my work, you know I focus on crafting games you can enjoy in a single evening, yet with enough depth to offer endless replayability. I’m confident the game's simple mechanics paired with historical events that force you to make impactful choices, will provide over 100 hours of entertainment. Now: Have fun! You are looking for a deep simulation of the Soviet Politburo: Probably not. This game focuses on Cold War geostrategy rather than the internal politics of a single country. If domestic politics and government management are what you enjoy most, there are better games for that. You love planning military campaigns: Probably not. Military intervention is rarely the objective. If you find yourself putting boots on the ground, something has probably gone wrong. You don't enjoy reading: Probably not. The game expects you to read tooltips, event reports, and occasionally the in-game wiki. Much of its strategic depth comes from understanding the information the game provides. You enjoy making long-term plans: Then this game is probably for you. Success often depends on thinking years ahead rather than reacting to next month's events. You enjoy indirect strategies: Then this game is probably for you. The best move is not always the most obvious one, and achieving your goals often requires patience, subtlety, and careful planning. You enjoy adapting to uncertainty: Then this game is probably for you. There is rarely a perfect solution, and strategies must evolve as the geopolitical situation changes. (If 90% of your actions are economic aid because every other option feels too risky, you're probably missing a large part of what the game has to offer.)
