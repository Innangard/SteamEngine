In the new Twinkleby demo, you'll discover the first two islands of the Twinkleby archipelago. You'll unlock items as you play, like houses, scenery and other decorations, and there's a chance that your islands will catch the attention of five different spacefaring neighbours, who will try to make themselves at home. Cater to their wishes to earn rewards, or evict them at anytime, and send them flying into space! The save files for the Twinkleby demo are fully compatible with the main game, so you can stop playing the demo at any time, and pick up where you left off in the full game! And that's not all -- Twinkleby players that have the demo in their library will be rewarded with a unique wallpaper design. Prepare to encounter five whimsy residents who want to make themselves at home. These characters have personality traits that affect how they interact with the environment and each other, and like everything else in Twinkleby, any neighbour can be evicted at any time – just throw their luggage off the side of the island, and they’ll take the hint. In Twinkleby, the world is your inventory. Throw anything you don’t want off the side of the island, and it will be returned to your collections. Rebuild, redesign, and unlock new ways to customise using colours, wallpapers, and sceneries. As you change the scenery, you change the soundtrack and gradually gain further control over the ambience of your dreamscape. In the Twinkleby demo, you can: ✦ Start out exploring the star chart, and discover the first two islands of the Twinkleby archipelago. ✦ Visit Molligan's Antiques, where you can peruse a selection of items, and get tips from Molligan on what the archipelago has to offer. ✦ Build tiny neighbourhoods on your islands, and decorate the houses and gardens with a selection of items. ✦ Unlock the summer and autumn seasons, as well as backdrops featuring stars and fluffy clouds, opening up many scenic customisation options – including full control of day and night! ✦ Attract five whimsy characters to your island, and see how they make themselves at home and interact with your decorations. ✦ Make your residents happy and get rewards in return – or evict them from your islands! ✦ Compose the soundtrack of your diorama by changing the weathers, seasons and backdrops. ✦ Enjoy escapist, relaxing gameplay without the stress of lose conditions and game overs. ✦ Carry over your Twinkleby demo save file to the full game at any time and continue playing where you left off, and – collect the unique demo wallpaper in the main game, available only to players that have the demo in their Steam library! In the full Twinkleby game, you can continue exploring the star chart and unlock a wide variety of islands, including mountains and islet clusters; you can meet a vast cast of neighbours, including some very rare visitors; you can unlock dozens of scenery settings, including mercurial storms, midwinter snow, and colorful deep space backdrops; you can explore the full range of Molligan's Antiques once the store is fully stocked, and discover the hidden secrets of Molligan's island.
