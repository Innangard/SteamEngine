Dungeon Destroyer is a fresh puzzle-roguelike experience where players strategically reshape dungeon layouts like a puzzle, combining tactical planning with the thrill of trial and error. ■ Key Features Reshape the Dungeon Freely redesign the dungeon’s structure to optimize your path for collecting coins and items. Once you press the play button, your character will automatically head toward the stairs along the shortest route, battling enemies and picking up items along the way. Endless Puzzle-Like Trial and Error On the same floor, you can retry as many times as needed—even if you are defeated by enemies. By rebuilding the dungeon like a puzzle, you can discover the best strategy and enjoy the process of solving it. Just like a roguelike, how you make use of items collected on previous floors is the key to survival. New Strategic Mechanics from the Web Version Compared to the earlier browser edition, this release adds brand-new features for deeper strategy. Traps such as explosions, speed boosts, or falling rocks create new challenges and tactical opportunities. Dungeon Shop for Smarter Resource Management You can now spend the gold you earn to gain advantages. Use gold to increase item slots, boost the number of coins placed in the dungeon, or restore your life. Since gold is limited, careful choices are required to balance between powering up and recovering HP. ■ The Appeal of Dungeon Destroyer Dungeon Destroyer combines the replayability of roguelikes with the satisfaction of puzzle-solving. While many puzzle games require players to follow the “intended solution,” this game invites you to discover your own optimal strategy among countless possibilities. Born from the idea of “What if puzzle logic were expanded into a roguelike?”, the game lets you approach challenges through repeated experimentation and reconstruction of the dungeon. Even if you cannot clear a floor on your first try, each attempt builds your skills and sharpens your thinking. The combination of an evolving player and a reconstructable dungeon creates a brand-new kind of addictive gameplay. Find your own best solution and experience the unique thrill of Dungeon Destroyer .
