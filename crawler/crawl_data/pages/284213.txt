MINE. UPGRADE. MANAGE. REPEAT Mine and defend your gold while managing your time. Once your time is up the day will end and send you to the management menu where you can buy upgrades and manage your kingdom. Keep your people happy and the gold flowing. DEFENCE Defend against hordes of enemies and prevent them, from reaching your gold mine. Prioritize faster enemies with low health or work down tanky enemies that move slower. Once enemies are killed collect the XP they drop to work towards your next level up! MINING Click your gold mine to gain gold! Watch out, if the enemies make contact they will damage your ability to mine and if you love too much health it ends your day early! Prioritize mining if you have a few seconds of downtime before the enemies get too close! UNLOCK UPGRADES Use your experience points to unlock more damage, better mining, AOE attacks, charge attacks, critical hits, health to gold transfusion, increased XP drop rate, and much more! BECOME POWERFUL Utilize your upgrades to become the most powerful king the realm has ever seen! BUY TROOPS Increase your defensive capabilities by buying troops that can do the dirty work for you! But be careful, the more troops and upgrades you buy for them, the more your operating costs increase. Which means you need to make even more goals the next day or risk losing favor with the people of your city MANAGE YOUR PEOPLE Keep the people of your kingdom happy by making them feel safe and the economy booming. If the people's opinion of you drops too low, you might be overthrown! MANAGE YOUR KINGDOM Invest in buildings like banks and bazaars to keep your economy flourishing and your kingdom growing while also allowing passive income to be gathered while you are out fighting! HANDLE RANDOM OCCURRENCES While you are away, random occurrences might happen that you must deal with once you return! Make smart choices to ensure you have enough resources for the next day or that your people are happy enough not to revolt against you!
