Drawing inspiration from the likes of Lovecraft , 2001: A Space Odyssey, and John Carpenter’s The Thing , this game pulls you deep into a decaying wilderness where something incomprehensible is rapidly spreading through the surrounding environment. You must discover where this fleshy entity came from, armed with only a flashlight and handheld camera. This game is an environmental and psychological horror that will have the player on the edge of their seats. You can expect  lots of twists and turns and maybe a couple of jump scares! You will also find a lore-rich and linear story with reliance on high tension and fear of the unknown. The game's story is told through the players' inner dialogue and through notes scattered around each level. Triggering dialogue events and notes via exploration will unlock new areas, allowing the player to progress through the game. This game includes: > Handheld/ VHS-like post-processing and realistic movements, creating a nostalgic, yet uncanny experience > Two fully fleshed out (pun intended) levels which includes mountains, caves, labs, and...? > An original soundtrack composed by myself > A LOT of fleshy entities, huge eyeballs, and mutated remains > Completely custom mountain Ranges and landscapes > Toggleable flashlight for dark areas > One insane twist that you don't want to miss out on!
