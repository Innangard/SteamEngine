What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We’re a small studio, with only two people on the core team. Despite the full-time dedication to the development of Be Hero, we need time to implement all the planned features. At the same time, we believe that the best way to develop a game is with the engagement and opinion of the players. With the support of the community during early access we can make the game more interesting and fun, refining and polishing the game according to player feedback.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We believe that early access will last for approximately one year. We need this time to bring all the planned features without losing quality.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version will bring what's missing from the main quest, which tells the story of the game, and many more side quests. Also, the tavern improvements up to level 25 and character upgrades up to level 100 will be implemented. We’ll add the missing areas, more levels to the mines and forests, as well as two new semi-procedural instances. We will implement the remainder of the mechanics, buildings and characters to the city. Some other mechanics, workstations, items and complements that have not yet been released will be implemented. We also want to polish the game with the help of players' opinions and suggestions.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current version has most of the areas and mechanics planned for the game. The player can already grow and harvest crops, fish, collect wood, mine, craft items, repair and upgrade the tavern up to level 8, advance the character to level 30, fight enemies and explore two of the four planned semi-procedural instances. We believe that the player already has a good range of activities to do, places to explore, NPCs to meet and much more. And that will be increased as we update the game.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We’re still analyzing it, but the price may be higher when the full version is released.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Even before the release, we have tried to maintain daily contact with our community through Discord, Twitter and Twitch. We’ve created, in our Discord server, dedicated channels for reporting bugs and suggestions from the community, many of which were already implemented during the development of the alpha version. We want the community to continue giving us suggestions to make the game even more polished, interesting and fun.” Read more Buy Be Hero $12.99 Add to Cart Buy BRundle BUNDLE (?)
