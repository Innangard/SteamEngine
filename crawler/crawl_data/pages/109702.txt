Why not have fun while doing it? The Trolley Solution is a compilation of minigames based on the famous trolley problem, originally proposed in 1967 by British philosopher Philippa Foot. Put your thinking power to the test  in humorous iterations of the problem. Unlock a variety of minigames where you can put your gaming skills to the test. Challenge yourself and learn important and useful philosophical knowledge while expanding your mind! Features: - 20 trolley problem variations (plus the original) - 20 minigames inspired by each variation Disclaimer: You might not actually learn anything useful or important, nor expand your mind, by playing this game.
