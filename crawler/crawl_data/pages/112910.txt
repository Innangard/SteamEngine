View Community Hub About This Game Loop Miner Deep beneath the dark underground, a small mole miner begins an endless journey of excavation. Loop Miner is a mining incremental game where you gather resources, sell minerals, collect artifacts, and push deeper into the mine. At first, all you have is a tiny pickaxe — but with every mining loop, the mole grows stronger, and the mine reveals greater treasures and new possibilities. Gather resources with simple controls, choose upgrades, and improve your efficiency. Even when one loop ends, your progression never stops. Each new run lets you go faster, deeper, and farther than before. Join the little mole miner and dig toward the bottom of the endless mine. Game Features Endless Mining Progression Dig through the mine, gather resources, and grow your mole miner. The more you repeat the cycle, the faster your mining speed and profits become, allowing you to reach deeper areas. Simple Yet Addictive Incremental Gameplay Focused on the core fun of mining, selling, and upgrading without complicated controls. Make progress even in short play sessions, and achieve massive efficiency gains the longer you play. Upgrade the Mole Miner’s Equipment Enhance your pickaxe, turrets, drones, mining abilities, and more to mine resources faster and more efficiently. Your upgrade choices directly affect your growth speed. Loop System That Grows Stronger Every Run Even when a mining expedition ends, your progression continues. Start each new loop stronger than before and challenge even deeper mines. Adorable Mole Miner and Ever-Deeper Mines Explore the mine alongside a small but hardworking mole miner. The deeper you go, the more valuable resources and new goals await you.
