Every action, every event, every discovery is determined by... a slot machine . Dive into a procedurally generated dungeon , explore its mysterious depths, and let fate pull the strings of your adventure. Attack, defend, loot, trade, or flee — it all depends on the spin . An unpredictable adventure Every action is handled through a one-armed bandit . It's not just luck — try to stop the reels at the right moment to trigger favorable outcomes. Combine symbols to unleash powerful effects… or devastating disasters. Adapt your strategy based on the spins and the artifacts you unlock along the way. Spend energy to re-spin certain reels and try to improve your results. What to expect?
