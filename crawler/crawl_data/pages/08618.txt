Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game The manager is clueless. The players can’t tie their own shoelaces. But there’s one role that keeps the team together, one role that makes the difference when it counts. The real hero. The Kitman. It’s up to you to take your club from a small time outfit, up through the echelons, and into the big leagues! Off-Pitch Action Take on the role of the unsung hero of every football club – the humble Kitman. It’s your job to ensure the team aren’t hitting the pitch in their underwear. Polish the boots, sew up the torn shirts and keep the energy drinks chilled. Manage available resources and the player fitness ratings, all while ensuring that the floors are clean and the bathrooms sparkle. Steer from the Shadows The players are spoiled brats, and the manager hasn’t got a clue. Take charge by scouting new talent, choosing the starting lineup and planning the formation. Steer your club from the dregs of the amateur leagues all the way to the top of the beautiful game. Match Day Mayhem Support the players on Match Day by solving problems as they occur. Supply fresh kit and clean damaged gear on the fly to ensure victory. A single untied shoelace could be the difference between victory and defeat. Play With Friends This is a team sport, so you don’t have to do it alone. Invite up to three friends to join in and share the workload. Or go it alone, and prove once and for who really makes a football team succeed. Chase Victory, Your Way Upgrade your club to play in the big leagues and unlock advanced equipment to make your job easier. Customize your Kitman, and decorate the backrooms with mementos of your club’s success. Just don’t ask for any credit for all your hard work.
