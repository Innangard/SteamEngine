From avenging your fallen sect to unraveling the "Eternal Dragon" conspiracy, master fluid builds to cut through a world of intrigue and shatter the eternal scheme. 【Legend Against Fate】 Protagonist: With the embers of the fallen sect still warm, the Jianghu's web of schemes is already unfolding. Trample rotten wood into blades; tear apart the web woven from countless lies. Use fragmented letters and broken blades as your eyes; seek light through a sea of ink and mountains of corpses. When the myriad conspiracies finally meet the edge of the blade, you will realize: we are all but pawns. Only the long sword in hand can cut through the illusions. Eternal Dragon Usurper: Is the flesh weak? Then dismantle the bones to forge armor! Is fate immutable? Then burn the heavens and change destiny! Complete the forbidden ritual, ascend as emperor, and use secret arts to control the major factions and court officials—so that strife may cease forever. 【Countless Arts, Limitless Builds】 Four sets of weapon skills (more to come), hundreds of divine arms, thousands of inscription combinations, paired with a variety of unique martial arts and internal techniques for free build construction. Rich Roguelite elements ensure endless fun. Switch freely between different build combinations without starting over. Challenge all levels freely; different builds will offer unique experiences against different bosses. 【Rich Non-Combat Progression】 Every effort in the game is retained. Collect meridian points and martial arts points to develop your exclusive combat talent tree. Forge divine arms tailored to your build. Explore ruins, cliffs, and caves to acquire classic martial arts legacies. Study medicine and alchemy to enhance your combat, making it even more exhilarating. 【Free and Dynamic Hidden Weapons System】 Wield various forms of hidden weapons to strike enemies in diverse ways. Combined with exclusive hidden weapon talents and unique tempering techniques, hidden weapons can be used independently of weapon skills. Coordinate hidden weapons with weapon skills for linked attacks and enjoy combat pleasure that belongs only to you. 【Gather Clues, Unravel the Truth】 In this martial world drowned in a storm of blood, every sword scar, half-burned page, and even the posture of a corpse is a clue in the vast web of intrigue. You will: see through disguises, decode secrets, and listen to the whispers of the dead. In the end, countless broken blades will merge into the Dragon-Severing Sword—what you uncover is not just the truth, but the entire Eternal Game itself.
