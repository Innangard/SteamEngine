A relaxing idle RPG about crafting, building, and conquering new islands at your own pace. Click when you want, idle when you don't : click trees, mines and fishing spots to gather (up to 5 clicks per second), or sit back and let your workers keep the village running. Grow a small village into a thriving kingdom by assigning workers to gather resources, craft useful items, and keep production moving while you expand at your own pace. Unlock new islands, strengthen your economy, and prepare your leader with gear and simple RPG classes to face dungeon battles, bosses, and earn valuable loot to further improve your island.
