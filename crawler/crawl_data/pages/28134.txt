Story Overview In the waning years of the Northern Wei Dynasty, in the town of Dunhuang, a rumor circulates about a stone statue that bestows blessings in the mortal realm. Lured by the rumours, Anu, the Royal General of the Northern Wei, accompanied by her Han captive Chu Qingqing, is led to Dunhuang, only to discover that the merchant holding the stone statue has already met his demise. A mysterious murder, a hidden thief among the crowds, and the sudden manifestation of the stone statue… What does the spirit within the statue seek, and what does it have to offer? In the darkness, the two are forced to reminisce about their past, choosing once again the paths that lead them to the present and the next moment. The Strange Case in Luoyang Chu Qingqing, the son of a wealthy merchant, grew up with his younger sister and his cousin. The three were inseparable, until his sister married their cousin, and a hidden desire began to stir within Chu Qingqing. Lost in confusion, he returned to the old mansion of his childhood, where a secret awaited him behind the plum tree in the garden... "You must feel that you love her, otherwise you'll become a joke." Turmoil in the Garrison In the sixteenth year of Taihe (AD 492), Anu, daughter of the Tuoba family, followed her sister-in-law, a nomad shaman, to Wuchuan to partake in her first war. Yet, soon after, her sister-in-law died unexpectedly, the capital relocated to the south, and a decade flew by… Six years after the death of Emperor Xiaowen, Anu returned to the turbulent town of Wuchuan. There, she saw the garrison ravaged by plague, a Deerhead shaman walked through fire. What answers would the spirits provide to the truths Anu once sought? In the woman’s green eyes, burgeoning ambitions began to shine… "I am no longer your pet... I am a hunter now, just like you." Game Introduction This game is a fantasy family drama set in the Northern Wei Dynasty, with a choice-driven visual novel AVG format. The game features about 100,000 words of text, with two main storylines and six endings. After completing the first playthrough, players can choose another storyline for the second playthrough. The total playtime for the entire story is approximately 2 to 5 hours. The story includes non-traditional intimate relationship plots, including LGBTQ+ content and polyamorous relationships. Content warning This game contains depictions of violence, sexual innuendo, unethical and criminal behavior. The viewpoints of characters do not necessarily reflect those of the author. If you feel uncomfortable, please close the game promptly. This story is purely fictional and has no connection to any real individuals, events, groups, or organizations.
