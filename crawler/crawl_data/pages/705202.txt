Manage your staff, increase document output and advance through promotion stages that constantly reshape the game. Collect unique items, adapt to new policies and discover absurd synergies that can completely change your strategy. Your office runs on people, paperwork and increasingly questionable management. Hire and organize staff, grow your workforce and push document output higher as your bureaucracy expands. When productivity drops, restructuring begins. Trigger mass layoffs, remove large parts of your workforce and turn ruthless corporate decisions into new opportunities. Every promotion stage introduces new policies that change the way you play. Combine them with unique items to create powerful, strange and completely absurd synergies. Advance through promotion stages and unlock new possibilities along the way. Your research tree expands with every stage, opening new branches, strategies and ways to optimize the chaos. Manage. Restructure. Synergize. Get promoted. The paperwork never ends. That is the point.
