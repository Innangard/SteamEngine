View Community Hub About This Game The house is yours. The garden wants it back. The apocalypse ended a while ago — you're the Supreme Rotten, and the house behind you is the last thing worth defending, while every night the surviving vegetation crawls up the lawn to take it back. Plant your own Rottens to hold the line, then wade in yourself — melee strikes, an area-denial roar, and two single-use weapons for when a wave gets out of hand. Features: 9 escalating levels , from a gentle backyard skirmish to two no-economy gauntlets of endless random Rottens and the toughest fights in the game. 21 enemy plant types , including 6 unique bosses , with several levels pitting two against you at once. 14 Rottens to command — 10 unlocked from the start, plus 4 premium units unlockable with Gold. Two single-use weapons for when a wave gets out of hand. 6 languages : English, Română, Español, Français, Deutsch, Русский. Every sprite, sound, and menu is generated at runtime from code — no external assets.
