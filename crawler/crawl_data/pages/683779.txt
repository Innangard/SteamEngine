The information and timeline described by the developers here may no longer be up to date. What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Lotus Mountain Creative is a tight and passionate crew. We are working to create a game that will truly impact the next generation by creating an experience that sparks joy, brings people together, and elevates consciousness! You can aide us in that goal by playing the Early Access and helping to infuse the final product with your love and feedback.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Lotus Mountain Creative aims to take Narcalid out of Early Access and into full release after some time for feedback, development, and refinement.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The Early Access version is expected to be a 7 hr experience, more or less when in multiplayer. The full version aims to be 20 hours in length. The full game aims to have 6 major biomes to explore, each with a unique boss and mini-boss experience (heal the boss or pass its challenge!). The Early Access version will have a reduced number of biomes to explore, as we focus on the polish and experience on the early portion of the game. We expect to have about 2/3rds of these biomes for players to explore, along with their corresponding bosses and mini-bosses. The lore in the game teaches about meditation. The final game aims to teach 3 forms of meditation with invitations to meditate up to 30 minutes in length! The Early Access version will have a limited amount of depth, offering shorter meditations.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The focus for early access is polishing the experience of the first half of the game and validating that experience to inform the final act of the game. While the experience is ideally very close to the final game, there may be improvements based on feedback. The current state contains about 2/3 of the final game map area to explore, along with bosses, mini-bosses, and story experiences. Art and effects are close to final, but may be adjusted. All 4 characters are playable, with all abilities available to unlock. Face the Bison Stampede-a formidable challenge, the Slime Menace-a big gooey problem, and the Spider Queen-who lurks in the cavern shadows. Each boss will test your skills, reflexes, and quick thinking.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The Early Access version of the game is priced at a discount as bonus for your early investment and vote of confidence!” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Join our community here on Steam or on Discord to share your feedback to help make the final product the best it can be.” Read more Download Narcalid Demo Download Buy Narcalid $19.99 Add to Cart Buy Journey Beyond BUNDLE (?) Immerse yourself in the beautiful and broken remains of once great civilizations, but stay aware… the Narcalid is a powerful threat that should not be underestimated! From the ancient texts that move along the narrative to the multiple environmental hazards of an unknown territory, Narcalid challenges you to see the universe differently. The deep spiritual principles embedded in the gameplay offer intense focus and flow stats throughout your adventure into this new world. Reclaim lost abilities. Read what the ancients left behind. Heal the bosses instead of putting them in the ground, share the planet with some very odd creatures, and sit down to meditate when the world asks. With Seemless co-op, bring up to four friends to explore a beautiful and dangerous new world. Narcalid is built to offer a compelling solo-player experience and an adventure that can be enjoyed as a team. Each ambassador character in the game comes equipped with a unique multi-tool, from a wall-shattering projectile to a projectile-shunting shield. Narcalid offers precise controls, challenging puzzles, tricky platforms, and epic bosses. Get ready to unlock all your chakras and conquer all the dangers and threats that Mother Narcalid holds for you.
