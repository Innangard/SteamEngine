Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Gun a Rat is a fast-paced asymmetrical multiplayer game to play with your friends. Play as the Rats and trash the house... or as the Exterminator and wipe them out to the very last one. Jump into matches for 2 to 20 players across varied maps, each packed with its own set of surprises… A unique voice chat... Rats can talk to each other via voice chat without fear of being understood. The Exterminator only hears squeaks instead of their words! Your goal as a Rat: Reach the target score by destroying and stealing items before the timer runs out, without getting killed! But watch out... every piece of destruction makes noise that will give you away! If the Exterminator gives chase, quickly hide inside furniture to escape their sight. As the Exterminator: Use your camera to track the rats' movements and uncover their hiding spots... Place your traps strategically to slow them down as much as possible Kill them all! A wide variety of traps are at your disposal, each with its own nasty surprise in store for the Rats ... Play match after match to earn Cheese-Coins , and spend them on skins to customize your rat! So, which side will you be on?
