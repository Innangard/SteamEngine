The goblins keep coming. You have no choice. Crush goblins with your magical stamp, collect their coins, and meet the king's quota before the deadline. Coins Must Be Made is a single-player active incremental roguelite where every coin goes toward a stronger stamp or your next tax payment. Crush goblins. Make gold. Guide your magical stamp with the mouse and choose which goblins to crush. Make every blow count before your stamina runs out and the shift ends. Spend money to make money Turn your earnings into stronger tools and new upgrade branches. Improve your power, stamina, speed, and income, then return to the workbench to put those choices to work. Pay your taxes Upgrades and taxes come from the same purse. Invest too much and the next deadline can end your contract. Hold too much back and your production may fall behind. Decide when to grow and when to pay. Return stronger A failed contract is not the end of your progress. Build mastery across contracts and unlock permanent prestige rewards that give your next attempt a stronger start. Discover new materials and expand your collection along the way. Active, mouse-guided goblin crushing with a magical stamp. Short shifts shaped by stamina, target selection, and tool upgrades. A branching upgrade tree to grow your power and income. Tax deadlines that make every purchase a decision. Persistent mastery and prestige rewards between contracts. This demo contains limited content.
