About This Content This Pack includes: Tank Strv m/41-S/I (Rank 2 Sweden); Fighter Fokker D.XXI (Rank 2 Sweden); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. Strv m/41-S/I A modified variant of the Czech LT vz. 38 light tank, produced under license by the Swedish Scania-Vabis company in the early 1940s. It features 50 mm of armor on the hull and turret face along with impressive speed and mobility. As the Strv m/41 is a Rank II vehicle, it also has access to the scouting ability which allows you to mark enemy targets for your team. It’s equipped with the 37 mm m/38 cannon, with access to APHE and a very powerful sabot round with over 100mm of penetration! While APHE is great for explosive damage, the sabot will allow you to slice through even the toughest tanks at the rank with ease. Fokker D.XXI A Dutch aircraft developed for the Netherlands Air Force in the mid-1930s. Later on, Finland began to develop their own license-built versions with various differences. This Fokker is a special Finnish variant with impressive performance. It offers a strong climb and fantastic turn rate, making it one of the most maneuverable monoplane fighters at its rank. What it lacks in speed, it makes up for in punch, with two FN-Browning 7.7 mm machine guns and two Oerlikon FF 20 mm cannons in the wings! With these, you’ll be more than equipped to bring down any fighter or bomber you come across, with its maneuverability helping you get behind a target effortlessly. All premium vehicles allow you to earn increased Research Points and Silver Lions for each battle and comes furnished with all available modifications. With a Premium account (also purchasable in the game for Golden Eagles) you will earn more Research Points and Silver Lions during each battle for a set amount of days. This is cumulative with bonuses from premium vehicles! Note! Refund is not provided if you purchase 2 different packs which include the same vehicle or you already have the vehicle from this pack!
