View Community Hub About This Game About the Game Coffee Keeper is a cozy first-person organizing simulator set in a two-storey coffee house. The night's delivery has been scattered across the shop, and 4,179 bags, tins, bottles, cartons, brewing tools, pastries, chairs and more must be put back where they belong before morning. Every item belongs to a marked family, and every fitting carries an engraved brass reference. Read the stock, find its bay and shelf, and turn complete chaos into perfect order. Gameplay Pick stock up by the armful, cycle through the stack in your hands, and carry each piece to its correct place. The coffee house itself is your guide: brass bay letters, engraved shelf plates, painted setting-out marks and a detailed plan help you navigate the building rather than follow a quest list. Across 4,179 places, 267 rows and 76 fittings, you will restore everything from the wall displays and island gondolas to the working counter, glazed pastry case, green room and mezzanine. Even the chairs and window stools have a proper place. Make the Coffee House Your Own In Cozy mode, completed rows earn skill points. Unlock and improve seven helpful abilities across five levels to carry more, move faster, find missing pieces and make every route through the shop more efficient. There is no fail state: nothing breaks, nothing is lost and nothing chases you. Prefer a challenge? Hardcore mode removes the help, starts the clock and records your finished nights on the leaderboard.
