Click the shrine bell to receive Foxfire and Offerings. Spend those resources on upgrades that make each ring stronger and gather more automatically. As your income grows, you can welcome new fox spirits, restore their Areas, and deepen your Bonds with them. You can also press and hold the bell to keep it ringing at a steady pace. Each welcomed fox opens a new Area to restore, new upgrades to pursue, and new Bond moments to share. The sanctuary continues gathering Foxfire and Offerings for a while after you close the game, so its restoration can carry on while you are away. Choose what to improve next Switch freely between the sanctuary's residents and decide where to spend your resources. Deepening Bonds grants Trust, restoring Areas strengthens resource gathering, and each step brings the sanctuary closer to life. Accumulated Trust allows more fox spirits to be welcomed. Shared upgrades strengthen resource gathering across the whole sanctuary. Use the resources you gather to celebrate seasonal Festivals, opening further shared upgrades and allowing the sanctuary to gather for longer while you are away. Meet nine fox spirits Nine fox spirits live across nine distinct Areas, from an ember-lit kitchen to a moonlit archive. Each has her own personality, profile, visual design, and sequence of short Bond moments. Make memories together Deepen your Bond with a fox to share brief character events and learn more about her. Bond milestones and Festivals turn these moments into 50 illustrated Memories, which are recorded in your album for you to revisit with their short stories at any time. Undiscovered spaces remain visible and offer clues toward the moments still waiting ahead. Celebrate the changing seasons Restore the required Areas, deepen the featured Bonds, and gather the requested Foxfire to hold four seasonal Festivals. Each Festival changes the sanctuary's season, unlocks a group Memory, and opens further shared progression. Completing every fox's Bond and Area journey reveals one final Festival and the game's conclusion. Afterward, the completed sanctuary remains available in free play.
