Defeat skeletons to earn gold, unlock new magic, and raise a holy army that fights beside you. Each upgrade makes your wizard and troops stronger as the battlefields become larger and more dangerous. Combine troops with elemental and arcane spells to create a force capable of breaking through the Necromancer King's growing legions. Spend the gold you earn in battle across a massive skill tree. Unlock new spells, improve your damage, increase your attack speed, and choose upgrades that change how your magic works. Each upgrade creates a visible difference on the battlefield.
