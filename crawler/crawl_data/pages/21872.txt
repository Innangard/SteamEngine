View Community Hub About This Game SHARKS UNDERCOVER You and your friends are sharks masquerading as dolphins to dupe and snatch the clueless fish who wander into your shop. Keep up the act and don't let those teeth show! MANAGE YOUR SHOP Stock the shelves with seashells and fresh seaweed, serve the locals, and keep the place spotless. Work your fins off to earn their trust - it’s the perfect cover for your shady business on the ocean floor! GIVE WHALES A GLOW-UP Win the trust of the ocean’s behemoths by buffing their bellies and fins until they sparkle. Gaining whale loyalty is your ticket to winning over the smaller fry! THE PREDATOR'S SECRET KITCHEN Snatch your own customers and turn them into gourmet delicacies in the hidden basement beneath your shop. Your shop is the bait, and they’re the main course! THE FISH POLICE ARE WATCHING Underwater cops are on your tail! Scrub the crime scene, lie through your teeth during interrogations, and don’t let the fuzz catch a whiff of what’s cooking in your kitchen.
