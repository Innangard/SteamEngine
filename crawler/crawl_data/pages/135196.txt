Defend the Kingdom of Asteria from the corruption of the Glimmer Curse in this roguelike tactical RPG. Build your best deck, obtain super-powerful relics, and choose the best sidekick to help you in your quest to overthrow the evil King Ogma . Unleash your best combos in strategic turn-based battles , but beware the curses that will weigh down on you throughout your journey! Outwit the Corrupted Dwellers with cunning battle tactics and razor-sharp positioning. Whether you are a veteran player or a neophyte, you must think strategically and use all the tools Shuffle Tactics offers to prevail in your ordeal. Will you lean on your martial skills or choose to harness the dangerous powers of the Glimmer? Each Hero has their own set of mechanics that you can mix and match through state-of-the-art deckbuilding. Unlock hundreds of formidable cards and build a deck worthy of a Herald of Legend! Hire unique sidekicks with their specific sets of cards, grow your bond with them to upgrade their abilities, and design devilish combos to maximize your combined efficiency on the battlefield. Complement your playstyle with the long-range prowess of the Ranger , the sneak attacks of the Assassin , the dark magic of the Necromance r, and much more! The path ahead of you is difficult, and you will die over and over again... But it won't be the end. A force beyond your understanding keeps bringing you back to this cursed world. Can you uncover the secrets that will allow you to break this perpetual cycle? Deckbuilding Roguelike with Tactical 3D Grid-Based Positioning : A superb blend of environmental tactics and deep deckbuilding. Iconic Art Direction : Pixel art with high-quality shading, lighting, and effects. Lots of Content : 3 heroes, 10 sidekicks, 27 enemies, 10 bosses, 6 regions, 300 hero cards, 150 sidekick cards and hundreds of relics. Huge Replayability : Each run is a new experience thanks to roguelike features, unlocks, secrets, and meta-progression. Infinite Tactical Combos : Vanquish your enemies by combining the power of your cards, sidekicks, relics, and the terrain itself!
