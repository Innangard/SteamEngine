Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Corner Market is being built with players in mind and I mean that literally. Early Access gives you a direct voice in shaping the game. Your feedback will influence what gets added, refined, or rethought. I believe the best version of Corner Market is one built alongside its community, not just delivered to it.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I expect the Early Access period to last no longer than 3 months.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The core gameplay loop is already in a solid state. By the time we reach full release, I plan to add features like kick integration, leaderboards, and other community driven additions. Beyond that, I plan to use the Early Access period to polish the overall experience and ship meaningful updates based on the feedback I receive from players. The goal is a tighter, more refined game shaped by the people actually playing it.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “All core mechanics are up and running. You can build and grow your shop, hire staff, including cleaners, stockers, and cashiers. to automate operations, stock a variety of products, and expand your market over time. The game is playable and enjoyable from day one of Early Access.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Yes. I plan to raise the price when the game leaves Early Access. If you're interested, getting in during Early Access is the best time to grab it.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We have an active Discord server and I closely monitor Steam Discussions. Every bug report, suggestion, and piece of feedback gets noted. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Corner Market is an idle market simulator designed to sit at the corner of your screen. Letting you run your own cozy shop while getting on with your day! Order products and watch your market come to life as items get restocked, and sold. Unlock new product categories as you grow. Grow your business from small store to supermarket Every sale fills your register, use your profits to expand your store. Open new aisles and attract more customers to keep the foot traffic flowing. Bring on staff to handle restocking, checkout, cleaning, and deliveries. Expand your team to run a smoother, faster, and more profitable corner market. Corner Market is built to sit comfortably at the corner of your screen so you can focus on other work while your shop runs itself. Zoom in or out for a less distracting view, or drag it anywhere on your screen when you need it out of the way. Twitch Integration available: Streamers can run their market live while viewers join as customers, showing up in your shop with their own Twitch names.
