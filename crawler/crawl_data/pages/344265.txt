Learn more Leaving Early Access: 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “I am releasing SPR into Early Access as I want the community feedback to make this an amazing top down racer that fellow fans of the genre deserve. I have big plans for SPR and will be releasing new content updates as well as a roadmap of what content you can expect.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I hope to have Super Power Racing out of early access by 2027” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “It is my aim to add reversed night versions to the other tracks already in the game which would make the total 12, I have designed a further 5 tracks which I aim to add into Super Power Racing and the aim after that is to make reversed night versions of those too. I hope to make the track count in the full game 22. I have 18 vehicles that have been tested on all the tracks currently available and I aim to add more ways to unlock those extra vehicles. I hope that if I complete all of the above I will be able to move onto working with the community to build the career section of Super Power Racing.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At present Super Power Racing has 10 tracks which includes 7 unique tracks and 3 reversed night versions. The current total of vehicles in the game is 12, 3 available from the start with a further 9 unlockable. The game currently has 4 game modes which are, standard race, Power Race which adds missiles, mines, speed boosts and oil slicks, Timebomb mode which is a checkpoint dash done with a bomb attached to your car and finally Hot Lap that players requested, it is you Vs the clock for as many laps as you want so you can practice the tracks and get the best lap you can. I have now released 2 seasons of the career mode and a Driving School which adds 17 events including a two special events called "Zombo Blast" and "Koi Most Wanted" There is now 33 achievements with more coming and online Leaderboards” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “I plan to gradually raise the price as I add new content and features” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I am hoping to use community feedback to improve every area of SPR, especially the career mode.
