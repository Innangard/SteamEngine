In Teardown, anything can be broken, moved, or turned into a shortcut. Take your time and use creative problem-solving, brute force, and everything around you to create an efficient path through the level. Run, jump, drive, slingshot. Do whatever you need to collect targets, avoid robots, or steal whatever your clients ask for, alone or together with friends in multiplayer. And whatever you do… don’t get caught. Features Fully destructible voxel environments Realistic physics, fire, smoke, water, debris, and vehicles Multiplayer chaos - up to 12 people Huge arsenal of tools and explosives Story-driven campaign with 40 missions, fun in single player, a blast in multiplayer. Sandbox mode for unlimited resources and destruction, alone or together with friends Thousands of community made mods to enjoy! Campaign After accepting a shady offer in hopes of saving your demolition business, you’re pulled into a murky soup of revenge, betrayal, and insurance fraud. You soon find yourself stealing cars, demolishing buildings, blowing up safes, avoiding trigger-happy robots and more. Upgrade your expanding arsenal of tools by searching for hidden valuables scattered around the environment. Modding Use the same editor the developers use to create custom maps, tools, vehicles, and game modes. Complete with built-in mod support, Lua scripting, and Steam Workshop integration. Teardown includes detailed example mods you can learn from or build upon, making it easy for aspiring modders to get started. Or just dive in and enjoy the wide range of community-created mods. Multiplayer Up to 12 people can play together in an online multiplayer session. Set up a private session for you and your friends or join a public lobby. You can play the campaign, have fun in sandbox mode, or play mods created by the community. Sandbox Create chaos in sandbox mode with unlimited resources and total freedom. Experiment, destroy, spawn objects, and play by your own rules. Art Vandals A free mini campaign expansion for Teardown featuring five new heist missions set in the quiet village of Tilläggaryd. Introducing new tools like the Paperboy Gun and Jackhammer. Created as a mod, Art Vandals also serves as an introduction to Teardown’s powerful modding systems.
