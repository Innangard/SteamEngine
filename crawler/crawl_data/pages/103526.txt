View Community Hub About This Game The Abyss Break is a hardcore first-person co-op horror game where you and your team venture into dangerous parallel worlds born from the Abyss. You wake up with no memory in an underground complex scarred by disaster. There are no people left here — only a semi-autonomous hub and a path into foreign worlds. There, beyond the Abyss, you will have to gather resources, search for gear, and piece together the truth of what happened to you. Every venture brings you closer to the answers: what happened in the complex, who you really are, and why realities keep collapsing one after another. But the farther you go, the clearer it becomes: the Abyss has rules of its own. Key Features Survival Favors the Prepared You have full control over how you arrange your pouches, armor plates, and support gear. Customize your weapons with attachments like suppressors, magazines, sights, and more to suit your playstyle. Time Is Against You The Abyss is unstable — an alien reality is about to tear you apart. Return before it’s too late. But remember, rushing blindly might attract too much attention. Stronger Together Form a team of up to three players, combining stealth, combat, resource gathering, and preparation to withstand relentless threats. Total Immersion The world reacts to your actions — tracks, open doors, and missing resources won’t go unnoticed. With dynamic weather, a day-night cycle, and detailed environments, every mission feels unpredictable and immersive. This is just the beginning!
