View Community Hub About This Game From the developers of Old Market Simulator comes a satisfying market-cleanup game where every item belongs somewhere. Search the market for misplaced products, return them to their correct crates, shelves, and stands. Earn reputation by placing products correctly and spend your reputation to unlock helpful skills and upgrades that make restoring your market faster and easier. Your market has been left in complete disarray by an employee whose wages you could not afford to pay. Now it is up to you to bring the market back to its former glory. Look carefully for products hidden beneath stands, behind counters, stuck on the stairs, or scattered anywhere throughout the market. Pick them up, figure out where they belong, and gradually transform the market from a total mess into a clean, organized, ready-to-open business. Play alone or work together with friends as you search every corner, organize every shelf, and clean up every last misplaced item. No pressure of running a market, just the pure, deeply satisfying joy of turning an absolute disaster into a flawless market that is ready to welcome customers.
