Experience over four decades of Cold War history, from the ashes of WWII to the fall of the Berlin Wall. Why Play as France? Playing as France in a Cold War strategy game from September 1945 to 1989 offers a uniquely dynamic and challenging experience. You're not leading a superpower—but a proud, ambitious nation determined to punch above its weight. From navigating post-war reconstruction and decolonization to asserting independence from both Washington and Moscow, France’s path is never linear. Will you follow de Gaulle’s vision of a “third way” and leave NATO’s command? Will you hold onto your crumbling empire—or redefine your global influence through diplomacy, nuclear power, and culture? As France, every decision matters: from handling the Algerian War and the May 1968 protests to building the bomb and shaping the European Community.
