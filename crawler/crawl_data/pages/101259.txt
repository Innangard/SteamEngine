View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Don Pepe is the host of the hottest game show on the air, where contestants from all over the world clash in intense water balloon fights! What started as a simple plan to scam children has now grown into a legitimate sport, and there can only be one winner. Do you have what it takes to become Champion? Intense water balloon fights in a 2.5D style with colorful characters and a catchy soundtrack. Choose from a variety of characters and stages, with new content being released periodically. Duke it out with your friends both locally or online in Battle Mode ! Take your favorite character to the top in Arcade Mode and become the new champion! Discover the secrets behind Don Pepe and this whole water balloon sport thing in Story Mode (coming soon). Throw water balloons at your opponents, run and roll to dodge theirs! Each character moves and throws a bit different. Positioning is key! Use obstacles and be careful when refilling your balloons at water fountains. You automatically aim at a single opponent, change your target stratefically or use manual aiming for more precision. Use items to get an edge in combat! Recover health, instantly refill balloons, get a stamina boost, and more. Try Battle and Arcade modes right now in our free demo ! Play by yourself or with your friends both locally or via Steam Remote Play ( full online play not available in demo ).
