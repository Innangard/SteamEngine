Dicey Words is a dice-based word-building roguelike where strategy, vocabulary, and a little luck collide. Spelling words isn’t always enough! Roll letter dice, lock and reroll like Yahtzee, and craft five-letter words for big points. Every decision matters—what letters you keep, what words you build, and how you use the powerful badges you earn along the way. Each run is a fresh challenge packed with risk, reward, and ever-evolving mechanics. Think fast, spell smart. Every roll is a puzzle. FEATURES Build your loadout with over 83 unique badges that bend the rules, boost your score, or twist the way dice behave. Take on 21 challenging bosses across 6 levels of difficulty . Trade letters for permanent perks with a unique letter sacrifice system. Discover 6 specialized dice types that add layers of strategy to every roll. Customize your runs with 23 powerful perks to build your perfect word engine. Whether you're a word game fanatic or a roguelike addict, Dicey Words delivers fast-paced, brain-twisting gameplay with endless replayability. Each roll is a gamble.
