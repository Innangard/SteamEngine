Gratis Spill spillet Vis samfunnssentral Om spillet You work as a security guard monitoring cameras of a haunted mansion. Find the anomalies in time to fix them and keep the mansion—and yourself—safe! The game features over 40 different anomalies to find and  3 game modes to try- easy, normal and hard! in the game you move between multiple cameras back and forth and try to figure out what are the changes in the cameras, your goal is to report the anomalies before your sanity runs out, which gets lowered when anomalies are active.
