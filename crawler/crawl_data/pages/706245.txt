View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Just Updated About This Game HeroInc is a tactical auto-battler RPG about building a hero from nothing. Start with no powers, no fame, and no money. Manage your time, train your stats, earn money, unlock new abilities, and prepare for the threats waiting in the city. FROM ZERO TO HERO Start as a nobody and build yourself into a powerful hero. Train your stats, earn resources, and make the most of every day. Every upgrade brings you closer to taking on stronger enemies. BUILD YOUR HERO Choose your class, unlock powerful skills, and shape your build around your playstyle. Experiment with different upgrades and create a hero capable of handling whatever the city throws at you. MAKE YOUR CHOICES Not every problem can be solved with a fight. Meet strange characters, face unexpected events, and make choices that can change your stats, resources, and progression. Take risks for powerful rewards or play it safe. PREPARE. FIGHT. IMPROVE. Your hero fights automatically. Your job is to build them for the battle ahead. Choose your upgrades, improve your stats, and prepare for increasingly dangerous enemies in tactical auto-battler combat. PROTECT THE CITY The city is under constant threat. Enemies spread Fear across different areas, forcing you to decide where to focus your strength. Clear threats, defend the city, and keep Fear under control. FEATURES • Incremental hero progression • Tactical auto-battler combat • Multiple classes and skills • Skill tree and character builds • Random events and meaningful choices • Resource and time management • City defense and Fear system • Noir pixel-art world • PvE progression and increasingly dangerous enemies HeroInc is actively being developed in Early Access. The game already features its core progression, combat, hero-building, events, and city defense systems. We will continue expanding and refining the game with new content and improvements based on player feedback.
