Tavern Keeper has received plenty of polish already but understanding how players engage with the game and working with the community to incorporate feedback will ultimately lead to an even better experience for everyone. Early Access also gives us the chance to bring the later-game scenarios up to the same level of polish as the early-game content before the full release. Miten kauan peli tulee arviolta olemaan Early Access -vaiheessa? We plan to be in Early Access for roughly a year, though this could change based on player feedback. Millä tavoin lopullinen versio tulee eroamaan Early Access -versiosta? We plan to add three more campaign (and freeplay) levels, along with new tavern features up to a 5-star tavern. See our Early Access Roadmap for more information on our intentions. Mikä on Early Access -version tila tällä hetkellä? The Early Access version is fully playable and has all the content described on the Steam page. It features an expansive narrative campaign across three unique locations, nearly a hundred stories, tavern-progression to 2.5 stars, as well as additional Freeplay and Item Workshop game modes. There’s a lot to experience already! Muuttuuko pelin hinta Early Access -vaiheen jälkeen? We don't intend to increase the base price during Early Access, but it might increase at full release. As an additional thanks for the early support, the “Supporter Pack 💚” DLC is automatically included for free during Early Access. The DLC contains the high-quality original soundtrack, as well as an extra in-game “Developer Commentary” mode (which will become available during Early Access). Whether you keep a cosy neighbourhood taphouse or a lean, mean gold-minting machine, the choices - and consequences - are yours. Chaos Comes Knocking Dozens of intertwined game systems create unpredictable emergent situations . Dirt, heat, noise, flammability, sickness and of course, a parade of colourful customers all keep the unexpected coming. Today’s situation: Half of your staff are in bed with sleep-sickness. Orders are piling up and patrons’ tempers are getting heated. So is the tavern - because it’s on fire! A spark from a broken lantern landed on just the wrong patch of dirt with no one around to notice. Oops, perhaps it’s time to call the fire brigade... An Unusual Journey Begins An expansive narrative campaign lets you build taverns across a vibrant world full of your favourite fantasy cultures. Each new location brings fresh challenges and surprising stories . Meet immortal elementals, outwit halfling philosophers and aid a crazed orc inventor. A Witty RPG Full of Choices Experience a magical storybook fully-narrated by Steven Pacey . Encounter hundreds of bite-sized stories with a surprise at every turn. There’s always someone needing the guidance of a Tavern Keeper like you. What choices will you make? Design Mode - Get Inn-Spired Decorate every nook and hollow of your tavern to create everything from bustling taprooms to relaxing reading corners. Place and manipulate thousands of items anywhere with complete creative freedom and make the tavern of your dreams. Your Own Item Workshop Go beyond decorating and become a master artisan by using Tavern Keeper’s powerful Design Mode to craft unique furnishings. Combine pieces, add magical effects, then share your creations with the Tavern Keeper community. Your Game, Your Way Play the single-player campaign your way with adjustable game mechanics and storyteller settings or play in Freeplay for a custom challenge. Pick Challenging and strategize over every decision, barely making it through each day, or play a Relaxed game running the tavern of your dreams without pressure. The choice is yours. Our Love Letter to Management Games Made by Greenheart Games 💚 (Game Dev Tycoon), a tiny independent team , Tavern Keeper is a passion project more than a decade in the making. Every detail is handcrafted , from the art, sound, writing, animations, down to the 3D user interface and many quality-of-life features . We took no shortcuts to bring you an experience like no other . Come on Inn!
