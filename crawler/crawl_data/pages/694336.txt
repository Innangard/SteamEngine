Cards and keys can be used to unlock doors, chests, and breakable treasures in the Crypt. Build your deck from hundreds of unique cards and dozens of status effects. Discover synergies between cards, chain complex combos, Play as the Berserker class and attempt to conquer the entire first depth of the Crypt. Berserker - Generate and spend Fury as a second resource, unlock a special card when reaching threshold. Alchemist - ( unavailable in demo) - Brew unique potions during combat. Collect ingredients and form concoctions. Druid - ( unavailable in demo) - Call upon your allies (Sheaf & Gob) to assist you in battle. Each ally has its own diverse set of abilities and actions. Adapt your strategy to face over 30 challenging enemies in the demo version, most of which have one or more abilities that will force you to rethink your card-playing decisions. Every descent into the crypt is unique. Procedurally-generated maps with branching paths, a variety of encounter types, including secret encounters that keeps you at arm's length from imminent death! Make pivotal choices in event encounters, experience witty and ominous creatures in your journey through the Crypt, and find new ways of staying alive. No two runs ever play the same. The full release includes: Solo & co-op deckbuilding Over 475 unique cards and effects across three classes Interact with other player tombstones that have fallen in previous runs Over 150 enemies to encounter that have their own passive abilities, card actions, and unique playstyles Over 200 items (potions, artifacts, secrets) to help you explore the crypt Over 70 engaging and choice-filled Events Unique mechanics baked into deckbuilding: active artifacts, class Rites, ally assists Three depths to conquer which are fully procedurally generated so no two runs feel the same Encounter variety: combat escape, secret encounters Leaderboards, daily delve, achievements, full save system The Crypt awaits you!
