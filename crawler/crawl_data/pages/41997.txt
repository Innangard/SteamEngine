Imagine the thrill of sweeping mines with 10,000 players on the same screen! The game retains the classic Minesweeper gameplay, where the numbers on the tiles indicate the number of mines in the surrounding eight tiles. Players need to deduce the location of the mines based on these numbers, mark them, and gradually clear the entire area. Join forces or compete with players from around the globe to clear all hidden mines on the map and transform the minefield into a safe zone. In this vast minefield world, every flag you plant will be recorded. Whether you're a newbie or a seasoned Minesweeper veteran, your efforts will leave an indelible mark on this world. Don't worry about stepping on the wrong tile. Even if you lose, you can continue to play after a few seconds. Any location on the map is a new zone of adventure. There is always new area for you to discover. Unlike traditional Minesweeper games, players can earn points by "selecting safe tiles" or "correctly marking mines". These points can be used to redeem unique flag styles, tile backgrounds, and more. With over 100 different decorative skins to choose from, players can freely customize their interface and leave their unique stylish moves in the minefield.
