What awaits you in the DLC: Cinema Menu: We’ve added 70 brand-new items. Crunchy popcorn, soda, a variety of dishes from golden fries to spicy nachos and sweet pies, plus salty and sweet snacks — everything you need for the perfect movie night. 70 Levels to Complete: Enjoy a calm progression through new levels. In some tasks, the customer will ask for any set to your liking (for example, just something with popcorn), while in others, you must strictly follow their order: like cheese popcorn, large fries, and a medium soda. "Combo" Mechanic: Now customers can request specific sets, such as a "Double Meat Combo." To understand what’s included, check the new special Cinema Almanac — your guidebook with recipes for all combo orders. Find the name and assemble the set according to the instructions! Relax-mode: If you don’t feel like following the rules, just jump into relax mode. There are no strict cards here — choose any snacks and fill the tray however you like. How to play? It’s still just as simple and intuitive: read the customer’s card, consider their tastes and exceptions. If the order mentions a Combo name, check the Almanac, pick the right popcorn, drink, and snack. Assemble the perfect set, meet all the conditions — and the order is ready!
