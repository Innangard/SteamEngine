Vanligvis $9.99 – tilbudspris: $8.69 Legg i handlevogn Vis samfunnssentral Om spillet Déunocta is a mixed media solo development art project by Aaron H. J. using a combination of digital and practical effects featuring art from my extensive portfolio, new age and old.  A cut and punch 90's style tactical action First Person Shooter. You play as Kathayra Cassidy Chrome, An armored Psych Witch fighting in a corrupted Dimension. In a realm where witchcraft evolved along side technology, a lone marauder, Kathayra Cassidy Chrome seeks vengeance against a tyrannical demi god who enslaves souls to conquer worlds and prolong his immortality. Cassidy, trained in the black arts fights against undead corrupted entities in a forsaken world. FEATURES • Classic cut and punch retro style tactical boomer shooter fps action • A combination a digital and hand made practical graphics presented in a psychedelic goth style • Over 20 different obscure creatures to encounter and destroy, 3 bosses that push the limits of your tactical skills • 8 original brutalizing weapons that ensure the dead stay dead • A secret weapon, red eye coffees and bonus rooms for the most keen of cryptic seekers • A sleek and immersive soundtrack . Save feature can be activated at any point during gameplay • 18 dynamic mazes across three hellish chapters listed as follows: CHAPTER 1 THE CITADEL CATACOMBS DEATH'S EDGE THE FELLED WODE INSANITY'S CHAPEL SALEM'S CRIPT CHAPTER 2 SEAS OF SANDS THE MALACE LABRINTH PART 1 THE MALACE LABRINTH PART 2 ESOTERIC CENOTAPH ATAVISTIC OSSUARY AVALON CHAPTER 3 NECROPOLIS THE FROZEN BASTION RED EYE STRONGHOLD ATEMKALT RIDGE THE CITADEL (NANOINFECTED) COVEN MOON CASTLE THE GALLERY (accessed after completing main campaign) Thankyou for your purchase contribution of Déunocta. It helps the time and resources it takes to fund more practical fine art to be utilized in the upcoming sequel. Déunocta 2 currently in development. Until next time, happy blasting! How to play With keyboard Forward-w Backward-s Strafe left-a Strafe right- d Open door-e Jump-space Crouch-shift Reload-r Map-tab Flashlight-f Fire-mouse click Game controller is recommended for the best play experience Compatible with most gamepads but control scheme may very Useful tips Déunocta plays like an old school shooter similar to Doom or Duke Nukem 3d most levels contain 3 key pickups, labeled by colors used to open Blue, Red and Yellow doors. Secrets and health pickups are scattered throughout to help you on your journey. Remember if you get lost you have a map (tab). If you can't see you have a flashlight (f). If you're having trouble fitting a space in you can crouch (shift) and jump while crouching (space). If you feel you are being hit and can't find where it's coming from it is best to get behind cover. Enemies , if not visible tend to hide behind bunkers. Levels very in length but each can roughly be completed within 20 minutes for the average player. There is a perilous fight ahead, at times you may think you are lost. Don't give up on this battle. How the art of Déunocta was made I used a wide range of different art mediums including hand painted ceramic clay and plastic sculptures, ethylene-vinyl acetate hand painted figure models, spraypainted hand crafted weapon props, multiple photography techniques with filter editing, hand drawn digital art/pixel art, upscaled digital textures, watercolor, acrylic, oil paintings, Copic marker drawings, color pencil drawings, original tattoos and recycled materials. My brother and I did the voice acting, he also mixed the soundtrack. additional sound effects were made with multiple objects and further edited in and added to digitally. I hope to learn more and integrate more advanced gameplay into Déunocta 2!
