What is The Rise The Rise is a precision platformer game focusing on the adventure of two characters seeking to escape the terrifying Abyss where they find themselves. Who are Sam and Elicy Sam is the older rabbit wearing a cloak with a pendant that seems to have some kind of power, apparently he relies on it. Elicy is the youngest bunny who woke up in a place she doesn't remember after a strange dream. Something is calling her to the top, could it be the way back home?
