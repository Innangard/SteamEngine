Step into the secret game of an ancient order - a single-player roguelike auto battler played on a 3×3 grid. Recruit strange allies, position them with care, and empower them with occult artifacts to survive each battle. Every run brings new enemies, unpredictable events, and the chance to uncover fragments of the ritual’s true purpose. Simple rules. Infinite strategies. Order Automatica’s battles are fought automatically - your role is to prepare. Spend gold, place units on the grid, and combine them with items, cell effects, and indulgences to create powerful synergies. Victory comes not just from raw strength, but from clever placement and timing. Lose all your candles and the ritual resets - but with every run, you gain knowledge and new ways to bend the rules.
