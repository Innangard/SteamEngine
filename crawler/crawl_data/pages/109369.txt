Desperate for a way out of financial ruin, you start spinning. But this game isn’t just about money. It’s about survival. Treasure Tiger is a first-person horror game where every spin brings you closer to fortune… or to something far worse. Spin to Survive Your only tool is your phone — and the cursed roulette app installed on it. Use the mouse to spin and earn cash through randomized outcomes: Double or nothing Partial refunds Complete losses Sudden anomalies in your environment Each result affects your surroundings. Power outages. Sudden darkness. Unnatural sounds. And the Tiger… getting closer. Keep Treasure Tiger Out As the nights go on, the odds become increasingly stacked against you. A sinister creature known only as Treasure Tiger begins to appear — tapping at windows, lurking in shadows, and waiting for a chance to strike. You'll need to: Monitor your environment carefully Close windows quickly using the E key Conserve flashlight power React fast when danger hits Fail, and he’ll enter your home. Game over. Five Nights. One Shot at Salvation. The game unfolds across five increasingly intense nights. Each night escalates the tension with new challenges, harder odds, and deeper revelations. Clues begin to appear — messages on walls, distorted phone calls, and strange figures in the dark. What starts as a gambling app slowly reveals a much darker truth. Key Features A unique blend of gambling mechanics and survival horror First-person gameplay Dynamic environment changes based on spin results Reactive AI: Treasure Tiger adapts to your behavior Atmospheric storytelling with escalating psychological horror Multiple endings depending on your performance Short-session design — easy to pick up, hard to master Will you get rich — or get devoured? Treasure Tiger is watching.
