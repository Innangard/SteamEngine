Grow your own mini metropolis: place buildings, unlock powerful synergies, and trigger cascading combos. With 21 possible guild combinations, Combolands offers strategic depth and a 'number-go-up' rush in satisfying 30 minute bursts. Unlock new guilds as you play, each with unique identities, strengths and drawbacks. Every run will see you picking 2 different guilds to play with. You'll start with a brand-new, procedural map, and grow a tiny hamlet into a thriving city. Break the game, refine your strategy, and build a city for the ages! Features: Satisfying roguelike blend of deckbuilder and citybuilder mechanics A pantheon of 7 guilds with unique buildings and playstyles - pick 2 every run Procedurally generated islands 120+ buildings to construct your town 100+ passive heirlooms to further enhance your build Upgrades to manipulate your buildings' behaviour, and potions to manipulate the map Recruit from 13+ city councillors, each with their own progressions and abilities Work your way up 10 increasingly challenging ranks Systemkrav Minimum: Krever en 64-biters prosessor og operativsystem OS: Windows 10/11 Prosessor: Intel Core i5-7500, AMD Ryzen 5 3600 Minne: 8 GB RAM Grafikk: Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 3gb, AMD Radeon R9 Fury Lagring: 1 GB tilgjengelig plass Anbefalt: Krever en 64-biters prosessor og operativsystem OS: Windows 10/11 Prosessor: Intel Core i5-7500, AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or better Minne: 8 GB RAM Grafikk: Nvidia GeForce RTX 2060，AMD Radeon 5600XT or better Lagring: 1 GB tilgjengelig plass Vis alle Hva kuratorene sier 30 kuratorer har anmeldt dette produktet.
