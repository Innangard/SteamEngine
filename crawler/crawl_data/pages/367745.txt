View Community Hub About This Game 🏝️ Welcome to Kioku island, Asti’s new home! Ride your bike, wander off the path, and let the days unfold at your own pace. There are secrets tucked everywhere, and the best moments usually happen when you’re not rushing. 🦀 Fishing on warm summer days Cast your line, catch fish , and pull up crabs to add to your collection. Every catch feels like a little win, especially when something rare surprises you. 🎴 Marubi is the island’s latest obsession Open packs, meet new marble monsters and build a team you love. Challenge the locals and watch your Marubï slam into the arena with a satisfying clack. 👨‍👩‍👦 Meet the locals of Kioku Get to know the locals, help them out, and earn your place in the community. The more you connect, the more the island opens up - new stories, new places, new little routines. 🚲 Wander freely across Kioku Island Kioku is a cozy island made for wandering, with secrets tucked into every path and corner. Drift from town streets and the pier to forest trails, the mountain, and places that feel a little mysterious. If you follow your curiosity, you’ll always find something new. KEY FEATURES: 🏝️ Exploration: Roam as Asti a cozy open island packed with secrets, shortcuts and surprises 🦀 Minigames: Go crab fishing, battle in Marubi and enjoy this 2000s childhood summer 🤝 Villagers: Meet memorable locals, build friendships and shape your summer story 📸 Memories: Capture moments and relive your adventure through in-game photos ⛩️ Japandi: Discover a world blending Japanese and Scandinavian charm ☀️ Please Wishlist and Play The Demo!
