Drive a bathtub on wheels through steep, unforgiving terrain where every mistake costs you progress. Master tough physics-based gameplay, sharpen your patience, and uncover Jeff’s journey of self-doubt, family struggles, and emotional growth as he finds strength, self-acceptance, and hope for a brighter future. Features Drive a Bathtub : Navigate Jeff in a physics-based bathtub on wheels Select Your Experience : Easy Mode for a fun, relaxed time or Hard Mode for the original challenge. Explore 7 Worlds : Each with unique challenges and mechanics. Easy to Play, Hard to Master: Simple controls, challenging mechanics. Emotional Journey: Jeff faces his father’s expectations and finds his own path. Customizable Bathtub : Unlock and use different car skins. Collect Soaps : Find 21 soaps as you play. WARNING! This difficult game tests your patience—falling is part of the challenge. Will you rise or quit when it gets tough?
