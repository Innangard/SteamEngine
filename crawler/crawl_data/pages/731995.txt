As our new resident Art Expert, you won’t just be in charge of standing around looking pensive (although that is 70% of the role), you’ll also be responsible for establishing a brand new Art Studio in the museum, creating new works to evoke a broad range of emotions in guests to the museum gallery… a gallery-um as it were! Not only will this new gallery(um) feature Famous Works from Two Point’s greats, as our resident expert, you’ll immerse guests in interactive experiences, alongside creating your very own original pieces, made right in the museum Studio, from sculptures to paintings. Experts will need an eye for detail to distinguish the genuine arty-facts from the geneuin-ish works you might find in the back of a Roach Burger car park. Your talent will be curated on inspiring expeditions, where you’ll (probably) bring back ideas and emotion ready to evoke in the medium of your choosing. Perks to the role mostly include a free beret and hazard pay for the inevitable, however rare, paint-related incidents, and the salary expectations are set to the competitive industry rate of "mostly exposure".
