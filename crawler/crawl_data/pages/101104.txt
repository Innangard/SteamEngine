Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Software Your coworkers hate your mechanical keyboard. We can fix that. You bought the board. You agonized over switches. You stayed up a little too late lubing the stabilizers. Then you carried it into the office and received The Look. So now you type on a membrane keyboard that feels like pressing a damp sponge, and you miss your thock a little more every day. Let DogaakDogaak help. Put on your headphones, type on whatever keyboard you've been given, and there it is — your sound, right where it should be. Nobody around you hears a thing. What it actually does Works everywhere — lives quietly in your tray and plays in any app. Your editor, your browser, that spreadsheet you're pretending to work on. Multiple samples per key — press and release, cycled, so fast typing doesn't turn into a jammed machine gun. Near-zero latency — a dedicated audio engine keeps the sound glued to your fingertips. No lag, no uncanny valley. Steam Workshop — browse community sound packs and subscribe in one click. Someone will definitely make a silly one. That's half the fun. Make your own — the built-in editor pulls key sounds out of your own recordings and publishes straight to the Workshop. Immortalize your board. No, we are not logging your keystrokes A fair thing to ask — and worth asking of every app that touches your keyboard. To play a sound the instant you press a key, DogaakDogaak listens for key press and release events, and does nothing else with them: It never records, stores, or logs what you type. No history, no buffer written to disk. It never transmits anything. No server, no analytics, no telemetry. Key events live in memory just long enough to pick a sound, then they're gone. The full privacy policy is linked on this page. Go read it. We'll wait. Type loud. Bother no one. Pick a pack, put your headphones on, and let the office keep its quiet. We'll take care of the sound — your fingers will thank you.
