Card Collector Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Congratulations, recruit. Team Ballistic has selected you for an extremely important assignment: collect cards. Lots of them. From your desk, you’ll rip packs, sort through your hits, and build the finest collection of Critkin cards. Drew a duplicate? Sell it! New card? Flip through the binder pages to find the right spot and lock it in! Celebrate god-tier pulls, enjoy the thrill, and make your collection grow. 1,809 Cards. One Binder. Start simple: open packs by hand, reveal the best cards of your pull, and find a home for every new addition. Discover variants like ultra-rare and mythic and buy higher value types of packs like the First Edition Boosters to rip, slot, and then fill each binder page. Can’t get your hands on special cards? Take your chances to the Black Market and check what’s on sale or earn upgrades with Gadget Points to hunt down those missing cards more easily. For extra income, shake your piggy bank and make it rain! Unlock a Better Way to Rip Your humble desk won’t stay humble for long. Grow your arsenal of strange gadgets by spending Gadget Points: Load packs, boxes or even entire cases into the Rip Rig, slot cards faster using the Binder Bot, and keep expanding your setup with increasingly excessive ways to get through more cards. For every new card you place into your binder, you gain XP with rare cards getting you much closer to the next level and earning a new Gadget Point! Your Rhythm. Your Playstyle. There are no timers rushing you forward and no pressure to get the job done quickly. Take your time as you inspect card art, vibe to the music, and find your groove. Play your own style: randomly draw cards from a huge pile to move through your latest haul, arrange your cards neatly on your desk before you slot them, or upgrade all gadgets to automate the process. Let’s scratch that collector’s itch! Features Fill your binder and complete sets one card at a time Rip packs, sell duplicates and reinvest the profits Upgrade a retro-futuristic workstation that expands with your collection Relaxing, low-pressure incremental progression 1,809 hand-drawn cards to collect Wacky gear available through the black market A radio, how retro!
