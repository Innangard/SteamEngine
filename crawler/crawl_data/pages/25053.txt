Your gym gets busier, dirtier, and increasingly ridiculous. Visitors leave sweat on the floor, special events bring mustard, PIPI, and other disasters, and it is your job to keep the chaos under control. MOP. EARN. UPGRADE. Clean messes, collect resources, and invest them into increasingly powerful upgrades. Improve your mop, bring more visitors into the gym, and unlock systems that gradually change the way you keep everything clean. AUTOMATE THE CLEANUP Eventually, you will not have to handle everything yourself. Unlock AUTOMOP, improve your cleaning robots, and let them take over part of the workload while the gym becomes more chaotic around them. THE TREE OF MADNESS A large skill tree unlocks new options for cleaning, visitors, special messes, AUTOMOP, DNA mutations, and increasingly ridiculous endgame effects. From stronger mop upgrades to Rush Hour, Sweat Tsunami, Moppzilla, and Mustard Asteroid, the further you progress, the more absurd your gym becomes. RARE RESOURCES AND DNA Not every mess is the same. Discover special resources, rare DNA drops, and new ways to improve your long-term progression. REBOOT AND GO AGAIN Gym Reboots reset parts of your progress in exchange for permanent advantages. Every new run becomes faster, stronger, and more chaotic. TWITCH INTEGRATION Optional Twitch integration lets viewers interact directly with the game. Viewers can join regular gym visitors, trigger messes and events, or vote together for larger chaos events. YOUR GYM. YOUR MOP. YOUR CHAOS. What begins as simple floor cleaning grows into an overcrowded gym filled with robots, special messes, ridiculous events, and increasingly huge numbers.
