From their cozy mountain onsen, the Peaks of Yuzuyama, they slip through hot springs to the fragile moments of the past and quietly fix the flow of time. Nobody thanks them. Nobody knows. They are fine with that; the water is warm. You are the newest keeper. You have a towel, a good heart and the most important job in history. No pressure. (A little pressure. The good kind, like a warm soak.) Every mission is a board of springs, stones and channels. Draw warm-water channels to connect the matching spring stones and let the flow run. No lives, no ads, no clock ticking in your ear. Hints nudge, they never answer. More than six hundred missions across the ages, from the first rivers of the Stone Age to the far future Over 2,500 hand-tuned maps in twelve elemental settings Fences, spring gates, pressure plates, sun mirrors, bridge decks, ward plates, treasure nooks and chests, each explained on the board the moment you meet it Nothing bad can ever happen: redraw as often as you like The puzzle is sacred: nothing you can buy ever solves one Each mission visits a real moment in history and is hosted by the person who was actually there, as a capybara. Tutankhamun runs his bath as Tutbarahamun. Nikola Tesla harnesses Niagara. Florence Nightingale carries her lamp through the long ward as Florence Nightinbara. Julius Caesbara fixes the calendar, James Wattwhisker listens to a kettle, Joan of Bara lights a lantern, Albara Einstein loses a pencil, Ada Lovegraze writes the first program and Amelia Earherd checks the weather twice. Fix the flow, hear their story, and learn what almost went wrong that day. Every fact in Professor Liliane Llama's archive is true. Only the whiskers are added. The Peaks of Yuzuyama : your home outside time. The Pool of Time, the Sacred Yuzu Tree, a waterfall, and a village that talks back. The River of Time : the mission map through the ages, revealed stop by stop as you fix the flow. Scroll into the past, scroll into the future, pick a moment, dive. The board : the puzzle itself. Mouse, keyboard or controller, your choice. Grandfather Kapi , the Elder. Has soaked in more centuries than he admits and forgotten none of them. His tea is always exactly the right temperature.
