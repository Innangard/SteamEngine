The idea is simple: pick a missile silo, set the angle and range to your target and click fire. Missiles will creep across the map of the world to destroy the enemy. The game is a mix of turn-based and real-time play. An easy-to-learn game of strategy and skill for 1 to 4 players. Featuring Missile Silos, Airbases, Aircraft Carriers, and Submarines to fire from, plus Factories, Cities, and ABM Air Defences. Radioactive is easy to learn yet has hours of playability. Every game be highly customised, with 6 maps to choose from, and total control over the types of unit you play with, the difficulty level, the game speed and more. Best played with a mouse, 1 to 4 players take turns to play. You can even set the game to play itself and watch the result. Rendered in glorious wireframe, Radioactive was designed to emulate the huge displays of a 20th century war-room. System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows XP, Vista, 7, 8, 10 or 11.
