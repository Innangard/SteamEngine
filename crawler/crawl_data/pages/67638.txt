Unreservedly yes.” Punchboard About This Game Red Rising is the official digital adaptation of the critically acclaimed board game, based on Pierce Brown’s bestselling sci-fi saga for 1 to 6 players . Players will step into the color caste system, forming their own House of unique followers, and fighting for dominance. The digital version faithfully recreates the original tabletop rules while adding stunning new visuals and convenience. Take command of followers from rival houses in a game of hand management. Trigger powerful card effects, and battle for dominance across Mars and beyond. Every decision can help — or hinder — your opponents in the fight to be the house with ultimate power. Draft unique characters from the Golds, Reds, Obsidians, and beyond, then chain their abilities into devastating combos that can turn the tide of battle in a single turn. The physical game brought to life with stunning upgrades only possible in digital form. Watch every card come alive with stunning gold foils and refractions, dynamic effects, and custom voice-over performances. Enjoy exclusive digital tools that make the game faster and more immersive: instant scoring, rules on command, and a rich in-game codex that dives deeper into the Colors, Houses, and lore. This is Red Rising as you’ve never experienced it before. Every rule. Every card. Every strategic choice  as you remember it from the tabletop. Emerald City Games is happy to partner with Stonemaier Games to bring the complete, board game experience to PC with all 112 unique character cards, beautifully recreated, and ready for deployment.
