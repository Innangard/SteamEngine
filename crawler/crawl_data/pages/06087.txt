View Community Hub Wishlist Now~ About This Game 30 Seconds a Round, Easy to Pick Up! This is a relaxing and addictive fishing-themed incremental game that combines two gameplay modes: incremental fishing and arcade-style boss battles! Across five seas, catch fish, train your meow squad, and challenge the mysterious Overlords hidden beneath the waves! Fishing & Seas Cast your net alongside your Meow Squad across five seas and frantically catch 18 types of marine life. Gather resources and build your strength. Accumulate bountiful resources and gradually unlock new seas from shallow waters down to the abyss, until you’ve conquered the entire ocean! Talent Tree Resources earned from fishing can be used to light up a massive talent tree, boosting your fishing abilities. Every choice makes your fishing journey more unstoppable, letting you deeply experience the joy of incremental progression! Boss Battles You’ll encounter Overlords within the seas, each with unique battle mechanics, and plunge into intense, arcade-style boss fights. Each Overlord demands a different strategy. Defeat them to unlock new seas and push your adventure forward! Nets & Sailors Continuously upgrade your fishing net to enhance your catching power, while four cat sailors each take control of a powerful turret, automatically blasting schools of fish. The seamless teamwork between sailors and nets puts your fishing firepower into overdrive! Collectibles While fishing, you have a chance to obtain 16 curious collectibles scattered across the seas. Gather them all to uncover the secrets of this ocean!
