An idle RPG that runs in a small window next to your taskbar, with an arena you can scale from 0.5x to 1.5x. Your party fights on its own while you do other things. Every so often you check in, equip what dropped, spend some points and send them somewhere harder. Build your party Pick up to 4 heroes from 10 classes. Six unlock with gold, and the Barbarian, Monk, Witch and Paladin unlock with Crystals, which you buy on Steam. Heroes you bench keep all their progress. 300 stages Three maps with ten biomes each, and a boss at the end of every biome. Clear a map and the next one is harder. 110 different monsters in all. Chests full of loot Weapons, armor, jewelry, materials and gems. Gear comes in seven rarities, from Common to Eternal. And nothing is junk: everything has a use at the workbench. A new look every 10 levels The same chestpiece, from level 1 to 100. At the workbench Forge gear, brew potions, sell items, transmute, enhance up to +10, socket gems and enchant. A passive tree with 2,224 nodes Each hero picks their own path, up to level 100. The black holes are portals: reach one and you can jump to any other. In green, one hero's build. 20 skills per class Each class has its own skill tree, so spend your points wisely. This is the Warrior with 40 points invested. Skills in battle Heroes cast on their own. You choose which skills to use. Pets You unlock pets by hunting specific monsters, and each one gives a permanent bonus. There are 11 to hunt and 4 more that unlock with Crystals. Your house Build a home for your party on an empty plot: walls, floors, furniture, a garden, even a pool. Your heroes and pets live there. Visit your Steam friends' houses and leave a stamp in their guestbook. Crystal Shop Crystals are the premium currency, bought on Steam. Spend them in the shop on the four extra classes, the four extra pets, premium stash tabs and furniture for your house. The shop changes every day. The Codex Upgrades for the whole account: more hero slots, more skill slots, more stash tabs, chests that open themselves, more gold and XP. One of its runes keeps your party earning some gold and XP with the game closed. 100 Steam Achievements From the first monster defeated to the complete Codex.
