Hunt Together is a multiplayer VR game offering two Player vs Player modes, in which you take on the role of the bold Hunter or the terrifying Ghost. Your task will be to defeat your opponent with the help of numerous skills, items and usable environmental elements 🛠️ The arenas of your battles are spooky, chilling locations, such as an abandoned mine, a haunted mansion or a forgotten orphanage. In addition to being able to face your friends in the Elimination Mode (1v1) or the Hide and Seek Mode (1v3), you also can acquire new skins for your favorite characters and develop their skills through a progression system. • Challenge your friends in a spooky horror PvP system with 1v1 and up to 1v3 modes • Two unique classes with dedicated skills and items • A suspenseful vibe of psychological horror • Haunted locations abound with possibilities and usable environment elements • Skins to customize your favorite character • Events and updates with new content to the game System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 or 11 Processor: Intel i7-4790 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600 or greater (only Oculus Rift, for Vive see Recomended spec) Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1060 or greater (only Oculus Rift, for Vive see Recomended spec) Storage: 4 GB available space VR Support: SteamVR or Oculus PC Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 or 11 Processor: Intel i7-7700K equivalent or greater Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1080 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 or greater Storage: 4 GB available space VR Support: SteamVR or Oculus PC It is hereby confirmed that Hunt Together (the “Software”) does not collect any User data or personal information.
