A MODE FOR EVERY MOOD 🎃 Fangtopia Mode 🎃 Jump right in and craft delightfully spooky worlds in short, restful sessions 🧛 Ghoulish Mode 🧛 Settle in and build bigger, bolder worlds with a wider spread of tile types 🏝️ Island Mode 🏝️ Connect an ocean of archipelagos by laying down railway track tiles for the 🚂Ghost Train🚂 HOW TO PLAY MATCH TILES Place land tiles from a stack, connecting terrain of the same type to score points and expand your kingdom. BUILD LANDMARKS Meet score goals to acquire a wide array of weird and wonderful landmarks, then complete their special objectives to unlock more tiles and expand your world. What new and devious discoveries will you make as your kingdom expands? DISCOVER MONSTERS Complete portal objectives to draw friendly monsters into your world, then place their habitats! Where does the werewolf's forest belong? Which mountain cluster is just right for the yeti? All manner of quirky creatures are out there, just waiting to find their perfect home!
