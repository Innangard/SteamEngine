View Community Hub About This Game Strange things are happening around the city. The police are powerless. They've called in the only experts equipped to handle the unknown. The Supernatural Investigation Agency. They decided to send one of their squads from the C.C.T.V. (Critical Containment Threat Verification) division to investigate, and you are the captain of the squad. Your mission is to monitor live surveillance feeds and report any anomalies you find for the night. Your life, and the safety of the squad, depends on your keen eye. Stay vigilant and don't miss a thing. Features: Scour CCTV feeds in real-time, identifying subtle anomalies that grow increasingly disturbing. Different levels with distinct environments and anomalies. Play through three nights at each location. Each night, the anomalies become increasingly more active and aggressive. Clear each location to unlock secrets.
