Learn more Leaving Early Access: March 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early Access is a way for the development team from Danger Thumbs to focus on a successful deployment of one body of work at a time. This is important because each major feature that gets implemented will have the full attention of the team, meaning that we can make adjustments quickly. We want to develop our game together with you to make sure we create the best arcade Footy game. Having community feedback will allow us to iterate on the mechanics and design of the game, and help us choose what features to prioritize.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The game will be in Early Access for approximately 5 months, which will give each portion of work enough time to be implemented properly.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “When the game hits full release, it will be a 'full game', meaning that what we advertise the game as having, will be implemented to the best abilities of the team. The full version will allow the player to create custom teams and players, complete a series of challenging 'towers', play online with friends, and have the ability to play online with strangers!” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The first iteration of Early Access will have a focus on the local couch-coop portion of the game, as well as the single player aspects of it! ” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We are not planning on changing the pricing when we leave early access, and still intend to have a free version of the game for people to try.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “The best way for us to get feedback is through our Discord, or via our Steam discussions. Even though we do read and note as many comments from our social media as we can, our Discord allows the team to converse directly with our players!
