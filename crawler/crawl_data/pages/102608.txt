Design and expand your new recycling center in this first-person simulation game. Take care of unloading, sorting and compacting the waste. The new level editor allows you to freely design the yard, place upgrades and set it up the way you like. With increasing experience, multiple customers will show up simultaneously and might create a queue if unloading does not happen fast enough. Completely new shaders for containers and vehicles bring realistic graphics and more possibilities. You can not only clean containers with the pressure washer but also restore worn paint jobs with the paint gun. If you don't take care of the compactors, they can wear off and stop working properly. New ways to sell used goods like furniture and electronics: The enlarged second-hand shop and new advertisement signs attract customers. Unlock the municipal services and take over trash routes in a realistic city that can be explored. Empty trash bins and dump the truck contents at the waste processing plant. Upgrade your recycling center to the XL expansion with a large modern roof and loading ramp for easy unloading. New human and vehicle 3D models with many variations in clothing and style will keep unloading and sorting the trash interesting.
