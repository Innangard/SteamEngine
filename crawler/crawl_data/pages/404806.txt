Forget about boring history textbooks, it's time to study real culture! In this turn-based tactical strategy game, you will have to assemble an elite squad of Internet legends and go from an ordinary "cupcake" to a master of memology. The world has been taken over by strange creatures and old unfunny jokes. Your task is to lead the resistance, correctly distribute your fighters across the battlefield and prove that classics (and fresh trends) never die. Game Features: ● Legendary Hero Roster: The most charismatic fighters are at your disposal: from the muscular Dog and the indestructible Gigachade to the eternally sad Banana Cat and Dr. Livesey. Everyone has their own unique characteristics and fighting style. ● Deep turn-based tactics: Plan every move on the chessboard. Distribute attack points, use the terrain features (be it a school notebook or a gym) and combine the abilities of the heroes for maximum damage. ● Crazy Opponents and Bosses: Fight against an army of haters, strange creatures and huge bosses. Each level is a new test of your tactical thinking (and sense of humor). ● The atmosphere of the "Same" Internet: Enjoy the vivid visual style, absurd animations and cool soundtrack that will make you dance right during the fight. ● Wave System: Survive the onslaught of ever-increasing enemies. Reach the end of each chapter to gain access to new locations and secret characters.
