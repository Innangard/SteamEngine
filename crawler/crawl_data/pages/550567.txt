Match elemental tokens on your wristcomp to generate mana, then use that mana to unleash elemental fury on the guards, robots, and bizarre bio-cybernetic creations of an evil corporate tower. Your goal? The CEO at the top, who you'll be showing the express elevator to ground level, via a window. En route, you'll need to slot new spell chips into your wristcomp, grab relics from your enemies' smouldering remains, and engage with dozens of nuanced text encounters. Encourage laborers to unionize, cheat the guards in a card game, sabotage experiments, and trade for gear and info in an illicit black market.
