In this experience, you'll get lost in a huge maze. You'll obviously want to find your way out, don't you ?  Well, actually... maybe you won't. Dive into a contemporary Greek tragedy paying homage to modern dramatic theory while challenging it at the same time. Lavrynthos is a humorous take on a 3000 years old myth. Navigate an infinite non-euclidean maze that shifts space and time. What will you find?  Lavrynthos will distort your sense of direction, space and balance and you might even find yourself lost inside your own bedroom . Created with support of the Venice Biennale College, Lavrythos premiered at Venice Film Festival and in 2022 won the Stereopsia Crystal Owl Award for Best Creative Experience .
