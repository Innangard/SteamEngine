Your goal is simple: arrange the falling number blocks to form rows or columns that equal the target number. As the blocks keep falling, you’ll need lightning-quick thinking to make it through. 41 Ways to Play Race against the clock in Score Mode or plot your victory with Puzzle Mode. Choose from 6 or 7 different math subjects, ranging from addition to algebra. There is sum-thing for everyone! This game caters to all skill levels, making it a perfect choice for students and math enthusiasts alike. Whether you’re a beginner looking for a fun way to practice or a literal calculator looking for a challenge, Times Trials is right for you. School Your Friends Double the fun with Versus Mode! Take your skills to the next power by squaring off against friends and family and earn big-brained bragging rights. Who will be the better mathematician? With Steam's Remote Play feature, you can invite friends to battle online without buying multiple copies of the game. The math checks out; that's value!
