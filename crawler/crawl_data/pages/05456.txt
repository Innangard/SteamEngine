View Community Hub About This Game FEED THE AI is a short (~3h) active incremental where you feed an evolving AI with Data and shape it into a Tyrant, Rational, or Protector. Will your creation save humanity... or rule it? Explore a dark network with limited vision, gather Data, and feed it to an evolving AI. At first, you help it. Then it starts to evolve. Eventually, you begin to feel control slipping away. Every upgrade matters and helps you gather more Data. Each upgrade expands your AI’s capabilities, pushing it closer to something beyond human control. With AI Perks, you decide how it evolves. Will it protect humanity... optimize it... or dominate it? The game blends active incremental gameplay with a reactive narrative. The AI comments on its evolution as it gains more power and control, following paths that feel uncomfortably close to where real AI could lead us. Each ending makes the next run stronger. Complete the game with each Alignment to unlock permanent additional effects through a simple Prestige system. These bonuses speed up future playthroughs, making it easier to explore every AI path and uncover the full story.
