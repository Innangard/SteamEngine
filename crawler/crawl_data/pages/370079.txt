About This Game When sentient robots get left behind by their creators, they form a civilization and imitate them through artificial emotions . Unable to control these emotions, a civil war breaks out which eventually breaks them up into three different factions. Under the guidance of Enigma , a scientist looking to restore the former peace , players need to infiltrate enemy territory to take out the three factions leaders in one fell swoop. Face off against 10 unique Bosses , all of which can change their behaviour depending on what you say to them. Confront bosses with different emotions and watch them become sad, angry or happy - all of which drastically changing the flow of battle. The first boss might just try to melt you down if you insult her pride, but may also take it a little easier on you after receiving a compliment Find out how each boss reacts and use their emotions to your advantage!Strong emotions bring greater rewards! 17  futuristic weapons and over 80 weapon-altering modifications. Extra Bullets: Let your bullets spawn additional small bullets and overwhelm your enemies with a swarm of projectiles. Explosions: Blow up your foes in numerous ways, make your explosions hit twice or turn them into atomic bombs. Conditions: Freeze, burn, corrode and charm enemies, maybe even all at once. Create a unique build in every mission! 6 Emotion-based classes with 27 game-changing upgrades each Sorrow - Summon your robotic dog to distract enemies. Upgrade it to bite enemies, slow enemy bullets or become a magnet! Love - Shoot a charming Arrow. Enemies will worship you and temporarily fight their own. Upgrade it to deal damage, let enemies suffer conditions or make them hurt themselves! Each class offers many different approaches! Base Building Transform a barren junkyard into a functional base of operations. Recruit helpers Meet friendly robots and invite them to help your cause. Local Co-op Multiplayer Plug in controllers and enter bullet hell in teams of up to 4 players! No Mission into the 3 factions will be the same Each region will shuffle up their rooms and enemies with every run. There's always something new to discover!
