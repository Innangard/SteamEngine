View Community Hub About This Game In an alternate reality, the Cold War ended with an escalation of nuclear attacks. In such circumstances, we find our heroine — known from Alice Mesmerizing Episodes of Neurosis. Her struggles are far from over. In one of the post-Soviet countries, a resident of a prefabricated concrete apartment block tries to piece her identity back together and find a way out of this traumatic situation. As the world teeters on the brink of collapse, everything ceases to make sense, and what was once familiar comes to an end. For we know that the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until now. And not only the creation, but we ourselves... For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the sons of God\ AMEN 11 11 is a hidden object adventure game. The player's task is to find objects hidden on the maps. The game consists of seven maps, each with a specific number of hidden items to find before progressing. The maps are large and can be explored by moving the screen with the arrow keys or by holding down the mouse button. Zooming in and out allows for a closer look at the map's content. Players can use the hint function if they get stuck at a certain level; it shifts the view to the area where the hidden object is located. The game stands out with its hand-drawn graphics, maintained in a tone of unease and unreality. It features the atmosphere of a dystopian apocalyptic story, psychological horror, or even outright psychedelia. The graphics, combined with atmospheric music, allow the player to fully immerse themselves in this surreal experience. The game touches on themes familiar from Alice in Wonderland, but also delves more into mental instability and spirituality.
