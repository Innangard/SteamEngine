Cheater’s Table is a 2–4 player online card game of cheating and bluffing. Empty your hand. Cheat. Catch cheaters. Don’t fall for traps. Control the table and stay alive. HIDE YOUR CARD Want to play a card that doesn’t match the table? Hide it in your sleeve or pant leg. Pull it out and play it when the time is right. If someone challenges you and finds it, you pay the price. If they fail, they do. DON’T TAKE THE BAIT But not every suspicious move is a cheat. Challenge someone and you might walk straight into their trap. Ignore it, and the trap backfires on the player who set it. PICK YOUR PUNISHMENT Cards, dynamites, or a Cadillac... What kind of trap do you want to set? The host decides what kind of chaos takes over the table. Every punishment carries its own risk, and one wrong decision can completely change the course of the match. PLAY THE MILK. END THE MATCH. Milk is your final card. Play it to win the match instantly. If nobody manages to play Milk, the last player alive wins. SET UP YOUR OWN TABLE Choose total chaos with cheats, traps, and deadly punishments. Change the rules in Lab Mode and shape the game your way. Want a calmer table? Choose Old School and remove cheating entirely. Set the rules. Pick your punishment. Invite your friends. Trust no one.
