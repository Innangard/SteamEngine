Build and expand your settlement, shape your strategy, harness the Gods’ favor, and prepare your warband for the battles to come. Place new buildings, gather resources, and develop your economy while forging synergies between your structures and your warband. Your settlement’s layout is your greatest weapon. Construct economic buildings near resources to boost their production, raise military structures to recruit warriors, and build mythical sites to unleash powerful creatures on the battlefield! In Northgard Battlegrounds, every placement shapes your clan’s strength in combat. Plan wisely, your warriors will reap the benefits when the battle begins. The Old Gods still watch over Northgard, and their blessings can reshape the fate of your clan. Choose divine powers that influence your strategy and transform the way your settlement and warband interact.
