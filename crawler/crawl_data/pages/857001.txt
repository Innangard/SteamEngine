Penn Central endured a troubled existence until rolled into Conrail in 1976, but nonetheless the giant railroad hosted an extraordinary variety of operations and called upon one of the largest and most diverse diesel locomotive rosters in history. And now, Penn Central railroading comes to Train Simulator – with Penn Central Pack 01 from acclaimed High Iron Simulations! Penn Central Pack 01 delivers the realistic experiences and challenges of railroading in the late 1960s and early 1970, as first- and second-generation diesels haul passengers and handle tonnage on one of Train Simulator’s classic routes – the double-tracked and bustling ex-New Haven Springfield Line in New England. Penn Central Pack 01 includes six types of diesel locomotives, in multiple variations and a variety of authentic Penn Central and Pennsylvania Railroad liveries: The classic streamlined Electro-Motive FL9, created by EMD for the New Haven, is featured in Penn Central and New Haven liveries and the model reflects the FL9s “as built” 1960s configuration and details. Another first-generation classic, the Alco FA (FA1 and FB2) is featured in cab and booster configuration and Penn Central livery. The landmark Electro-Motive GP9 is included in multiple Penn Central and Pennsylvania Railroad liveries and with a range of detail variations. Liveries include PRR early and late liveries and standard and “red P” PC schemes. The unique Electro-Motive cabless GP9B is included in Penn Central and Pennsylvania Railroad liveries. The potent, six-axle, second-generation Electro-Motive SD35 is provided in Penn Central livery The veteran workhorse Electro-Motive SW1200 diesel switcher is featured in Penn Central livery. Each of the locomotives in Penn Central Pack 01 feature the advanced and authentic air braking systems as developed by Mike Rennie of Smokebox. Joining the diverse motive power in Penn Central Pack 01 is a selection of freight rolling stock, including Penn Central’s noted Nb5 (ex-PRR) and NE-5 (ex-NYNH&H) cabooses, Penn Central (ex-NYNH&H) streamlined parlor-baggage-lounge and coach, and a selection of nine types of period-authentic freight rolling stock in multiple liveries and variations, including Penn Central, New York Central, Pennsylvania, and New Haven liveries. And Penn Central Pack 01 will put you right to work with ten realistic career scenarios on the busy and now visually enhanced Train Simulator Springfield Line: Springfield – New Haven route, handling passenger, main line freight, local, and switching duties across the length of the line. Note: The locomotives and equipment of Penn Central Pack 01 can be used on a variety of Train Simulator routes. The included scenarios require the Springfield Line: Springfield – New Haven Route Add-On (route available separately). Climb aboard and take the throttle of Penn Central’s memorable and diverse diesel motive power and relive challenging 1960s and 1970s railroading – with High Iron Simulation’s Penn Central Pack 01 for Train Simulator! Included Scenarios Ten realistic and challenging career scenarios representing Penn Central operations of the late 1960s and early 1970s are included, putting you at the throttle of Penn Central motive power for passenger, mainline freight, local, and switching operations on the Train Simulator Springfield Line: Springfield – New Haven Route Add-On. The scenarios are closely based upon actual Penn Central operations and train symbols of the era.
