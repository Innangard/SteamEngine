View Community Hub About This Game "Imagine Sky: Children of the Light with a dash of Animal Crossing's homey charm" - MMORPG dot com Welcome to Spirit Crossing , a whimsical multiplayer game set in a storm-swept world above the clouds. Build a home, help spirits, and cross paths with other players, all on your own terms. Make a friend, or just quietly share the same little corner of the world. Our game is built around small acts of kindness, and the way even brief connections can make us feel a little less alone. Spirit Crossing is launching this November; wishlist on Steam today! Restore a World of Spirits Magical storms sweep across the Crosslands, wreaking havoc on spirits and humans alike. Each day brings new quests to rebuild your community, heal troubled spirits, and restore joy through music. As you learn the language of the spirits, they will reward you with unique items and surprising revelations about their stories and struggles. Show Off Your Style Show off your unique style with hundreds of designs for every fashionista. Customize your look with hairstyles, dyes, and mix-and-match outfits, then bring it all to life with dozens of expressive emotes. Come as you are, or try on someone new! Decorate Your Space A mountain peak escape all to yourself? Now we’re talking! Design the ultimate space to host your friends, from cozy cottage to full-blown castle. Then fill it with enough furniture, plants, rugs, lamps, and tiny trinkets to ensure no shelf goes tragically undecorated. (Oh, and your home might come pre-furnished with a fuzzy, friendly roommate. 🐻) Cozy Campfires & Impromptu Jam Sessions Just like in the real world, storms occasionally roll in and interrupt your exploring. But that's a feature, not a bug. In Spirit Crossing , take shelter around communal bonfires with other wanderers. Share a song or conversation, or just simply enjoy the flickering firelight during one of the most magical parts of the game. Play Together or By Yourself In Spirit Crossing , other players appear as shadows. Share a high five or an activity, and they'll bloom into color, revealing the person behind the silhouette. Maybe you make a new friend. Or maybe you just fish with each other for ten peaceful minutes and call it good. Not in the mood to meet or chat with new people? No pressure, you can opt out of meeting others for however long you might want to. Stay a mysterious shadow, lend a hand when you feel like it, and disappear before anyone can make it weird. 🤗 What Will it Cost? Spirit Crossing will launch with an upfront price that gives players full access to the complete game experience in perpetuity. We will also offer a completely optional monthly “Cozy Club” membership for players who want to continue supporting our studio and future Spirit Crossing development. Our Cozy Club members will be able to customize their flying pet fluffalo, collect exclusive flairs, access a limited resource exchange shop, and more! We’re intentionally avoiding microtransactions in Spirit Crossing , so you can focus on enjoying your game, not stressing about your wallet. 💸 What’s With the Bears? Our studio has been making games for almost twenty years. We invented the Merge game genre with Triple Town , and have since released many other original games, including the hit life-sim, Cozy Grove , and the delightfully silly word puzzle game, Alphabear . Spirit Crossing has been in development for over five years. At the heart of this project is our belief that games should bring people together, encourage kindness, and reduce loneliness in this chaotic world we live in.
