Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early Access allows us to release the project in stages while continuously improving it based on player feedback. At this stage, we already have a clear creative vision for the full version of the game, as well as an internal development roadmap. During Early Access, we plan to: • add new story episodes as they become ready; • work on fixing technical issues and optimizing performance; • take community feedback into account while further polishing the game.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The game is expected to remain in Early Access for approximately 3–5 years. The game will leave Early Access after the release of the final episode. A total of five episodes are planned.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “Currently, the Early Access version includes only the first episode of the story. We plan to release a total of five story episodes in the full version. This will allow players to experience the story in its complete form over the course of future updates.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Currently, the first episode is fully playable from start to finish.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price of the game may increase as new story episodes are released and as the game approaches its final release. Players who purchase the game during Early Access will receive access to all planned content at no additional cost.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We strive to improve the gameplay and technical aspects of the game based on player feedback. The primary channel for collecting feedback will be the Steam Community forums for the game, where players can share feedback, suggestions, and bug reports.
