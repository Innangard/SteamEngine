From the charming lakes of Germany to the stunning coastlines of France, Italy, and Spain, this DLC introduces a wealth of new destinations for anglers to enjoy. Begin your journey in Germany, where you’ll fish in the serene Binnenalster and Kleine Alster, or visit the historic Castle Anholt. Each location offers a unique fishing challenge and an opportunity to enrich your aquarium with new and exciting species. Whether you’re fishing solo or with friends in multiplayer mode, the Europe DLC Vol. 2 invites you to discover Europe’s incredible waterscapes and create unforgettable fishing memories.
