Make your perilous ascent through the various handcrafted and procedurally generated challenges of Mount Om, all the while following a silly story with eccentric folks along the way. Master your own unique style of climbing either alone or with a friend, upgrade and customize your climber to stand a better chance, and maybe just maybe, you'll be the first ever to reach the top! Procedural & Handmade Levels Be the first to reach the top of Mount Om, the highest mountain in the world. Discover an endless amount of unique climbing routes along the way. Physics-Based Climbing An approachable physics-based climbing system that is endlessly replayable and not overly punishing. Meet eccentric characters On your way to the top of Mount Om, you'll meet a variety of eccentric characters. All with their own reasons to journey to the top. Items & Upgrades Unlock a variety of permanent and equipable items that will boost your stats. Customize Your Character Be the most fashionable climber out there by customizing your character's look. Daily Mountain Test your skills and compete with your friends in the Daily Mountain challenge! Solo or Duo! Play on your own or tethered to a friend in local co-op.
