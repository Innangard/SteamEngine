Buy Red Solstice 2: Survivors - INSURGENTS $3.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content The Elysium Corporation has always told everyone that they had humanity's best interests. However, the Insurgents knew the truth. Secret STROL research labs, kidnappings, and even human experimentation on the less fortunate. It seemed like Elysium could get away with anything, until now. Rise up and become an Insurgent! Play as the Governor and take the fight to Elysium at every turn. Build up a resistance force and overthrow them. (Please note, you can join another player’s Insurgents content without owning it. But you cannot host an Insurgents campaign without owning the DLC.) NEW CONTENT NEW CAMPAIGN STORY Experience a new story line with new characters and settings. Play as the Governor, who is in-charge of going after Elysium's remnants. Even though they call themselves the Phalanx Initiative, the innocent blood they have spilled will never wash away from their hands. TUG OF WAR It's not easy taking down a powerful corporation. Experience the new strategy layer tug of war mechanic. Gain points in bringing down Phalanx, but be careful. You can lose those points which will make it not only harder for you, but can lead to failure. The war of attrition starts now! WEAPON MODIFICATIONS Fighting against well-funded corporations with better gear just means you have to fight back harder and use anything at your disposal. This DLC introduces weapon modifications from stocks, barrels, under barrels, frames, sights, and magazines. Your weapon is now an even greater tool to continue the fight against all odds. STROL MARINE The STROL Marine is making a comeback! Packing an autocannon and some new tricks, the STROL Marine will keep you on your toes. FACILITY LEVEL Experience the new gameplay revolving around tight corridors and facilities in new skirmish maps that also appear in the new campaign which includes new missions with rector core, escorting a nuke, a small puzzle game and terminals connected with turrets that you can power up. THE TANK If you thought the HEST vehicle was rough, wait until you fight against an actual tank! Donning 2 barrels, a top mounted MG and a rocket pod, the tank vehicle is an unstoppable force. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: Blood from killing creatures and player deaths.
