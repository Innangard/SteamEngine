To do so, you’ll need to venture deeper into the workers’ area of the complex, an industrial sector filled with dark corridors, blocked passages, and traps. Maxin is roaming the complex, lurking around every corner. Move carefully, avoid making noise, hide when necessary, and escape his pursuits.
