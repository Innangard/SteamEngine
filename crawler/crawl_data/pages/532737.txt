View Community Hub About This Game A gang of mischievous cats has turned every store upside down. Shelves are empty, products are scattered everywhere, and chaos has taken over... Luckily, you arrived just in time, Shiny! They didn't name you Employee of the Month for nothing. Your task is simple: organize hundreds of products across 5 different stores, decide where everything belongs, and create your own perfect layout. ORGANIZE THE SHELVES Pick up products, place them in the correct aisles, and restock every empty shelf. ENJOY THE SATISFACTION OF ORGANIZING Experience the relaxing joy of creating the perfect supermarket by arranging every product just right. EXPLORE DIFFERENT STORES Journey from a tiny corner shop to a massive supermarket, with an ever-growing variety of products to organize. A RELAXING EXPERIENCE No time limits. No impatient customers. Just a few guilty cats. (Just kidding—we locked them up before you bought the game.) Just you and the unmatched satisfaction of putting everything back in order.
