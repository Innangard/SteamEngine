They want to be our friends and to communicate with us. They captured images of common objects and animals, and want to know their names. Let's help Pathfinders discover basic words in English, in this unique mix of logic puzzle and word search. In each level: Observe the images of day-by-day objects or animals; Find their name on the board, using all letters on the board, without repeating or crossing paths. Listen to the words spoken in English - using automated text-to-speech technology from AWS (Amazon Web Services). The newest game in the Mini Words series presents simpler challenges compared to the other instances, so English learners of all levels can have fun while practicing vocabulary! It offers 100 levels, in the English language only. See the previous Mini Words games on our studio page.
