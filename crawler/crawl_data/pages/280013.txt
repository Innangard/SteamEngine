Cloudgazer: A Game About Watching Clouds is a sky-gazing simulator where your only goal is to relax, watch the clouds, and discover the hundreds of hidden shapes drifting above you. No rush. No enemies. No explosions. Just you, the sky, and your imagination. And yes, before you ask: we also recommend watching real clouds whenever you can. The graphics are amazing, and most of the time, they work pretty well. But we know that's not always possible. Maybe there aren't any clouds where you live today. Maybe your window faces the building next door. Maybe you can't go outside. Or maybe you just feel like enjoying a perfect sky from the comfort of your couch. That's exactly what Cloudgazer is for. Features: Hundreds of shapes to discover: From animals and everyday objects to mythical creatures and plenty of surprises. Realistic, dynamic clouds: A natural cloud simulation ensures every sky is different. A full day-night cycle: Experience sunrises, sunsets, starry nights, and every color the sky has to offer. Relax at your own pace: No pressure. No timers. Just you, the sky, and a cloud that insists on looking like a cat. Train your pareidolia: How many shapes can you find? Real life will always be the best place to watch clouds. But when the sky doesn't cooperate... we'll lend you one.
