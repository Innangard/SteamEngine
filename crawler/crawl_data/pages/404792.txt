Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo The elections are just around the corner! - will you be able to win them, overcome all the difficulties and reach the final without stumbling and losing everything you have achieved? Will you be able to prove your power at least with knowledge of Trump in the first 5 levels on the way up? ONLY UP: TRUMP FOR PRESIDENT - a game in which you will feel like Donald Trump, going through many difficult obstacles and testing your parkour abilities, because the basis of the game mechanics is the OnlyUp mode! In addition to the idea of ​​the classic "OnlyUp" style, in this DEMO, choosing the right answers to the first 5 questions will affect your further path to the top, because in the main game you will already know a small part of the path to the presidency! You need to jump on the right platform. And most importantly - do not stumble, because one mistake can cost all the progress of the game! (however, your knowledge of Donald is always with you) Game features: you will move along moving platforms and other complex obstacles; 5 questions, each of which you HAVE to answer correctly (the MAIN difference between the main game and the DEMO is that you can go through all 15 questions, and not just part of them in the test version); Donald Trump in the leading role (Kamala Harris is also planned as a special guest, you can write your options in the game discussions. It is fair to note that Biden was also in the plans at one time ...); It's time to rise to the very top, good luck! Note that the game does not have any political subtext and is a humorous performance without any attempt to evaluate specific individuals. Please, don't forget to add the main game to your Wishlist to buy it in case you want to try yourself as Trump completely!
