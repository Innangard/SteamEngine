Take a run-down 1-floor building that looks more like a motel than a hotel and flip it into a place guests actually want to stay. The demo covers your first week: the lobby, one rooms floor and everything up to level 5. No time limit, and your save carries over to the full game, where your hotel grows into a 100-story luxury tower. Build, Expand & Design Your Dream Hotel You're the boss. Design your hotel from the ground up, add your first rooms floor and decorate each room with customizable furniture. Pick from 4 floor plans and 150+ furniture and decor items, from budget beds to proper hotel rooms, to raise your hotel rating, attract more guests and boost your profits. Manage Guests & Keep Them Happy Running a hotel isn't easy! Work the reception desk, hand out room keycards, answer room-service phone calls, solve guest issues and keep your popularity up. Guests run on a schedule, and when peak hours hit, the line reaches the door. Strip the beds, wash the sheets and mop the floors before the next guest walks in. Happy guests mean better reviews, and more income for your next expansion. Your Front-Desk Computer Is the Control Center Use your in-game computer to manage every part of the business: Manage rooms and reservations Purchase furniture & supplies Hire a cleaner, a storage worker and a receptionist Run marketing campaigns Apply for your first loan Track daily goals and milestones Play fun mini-games Side Jobs With Style Forget stacking supermarket shelves or running a gas station. Here the side jobs are the hotel cafe and room service orders over the phone, on top of running the front desk. It's a tycoon game where you do the work yourself. Relax After a Long Day Need a break from managing the hotel? Visit the club at night, unwind, socialize and even try your luck at finding a girlfriend. You can also deal cards at blackjack or spin the slots right from your hotel computer. What the Full Game Adds Up to 100 floors, so your hotel can actually reach its peak Luxury furniture for a 5-star hotel More staff for every role The gym floor, bigger loans and levels past 7 Long term goals and milestones Your demo save carries over to the full game. Pick up right where you left off.
