Make the burger is a fun game where just do a good burger is what matter. In a depreciated neighborhood, a gastronomic Chef decides to sell his beautiful burgers, with the wish to improve decadent reality of the neighborhood and do people happier. Nothing to kill hunger with delicious food to solve all problems. The Game: Make hamburgers for different types of customers, with various combinations of ingredients. Do not miss your orders, they will not be happy with this. Customers don't like to wait too long so be quick and get it right. Also, do not let the burger fall, otherwise it will only leave the neighborhood more depreciated and will bring you more work having to clean everything. Unlock the ingredients to receive more happiness. Meet police and bandits who sit in the same environment. Buy more tables to be able to serve all customers. You don't want your precious customer to leave, right?
