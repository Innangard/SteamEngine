Explore, fight and build to shape the land to your will. Conquer bosses and collect valuable loot to improve your character. Explore winding cave systems with secrets and structures to discover. Build a base to defend yourself at night. Allumeria strives to harness the full potential of what a voxel sandbox game can be. Allumeria provides advanced building tools not present in other games. You can change the shape of any block on the fly. Want glass stairs? Want a dirt fence? You can do it! Allumeria has a full RGB light engine . Create dimly lit cozy cabins, or bright neon cities. Monsters only spawn on natural blocks, so you are free to experiment with dark and light! The Paint Roller allows you to paint ANY block in the game, with no limitations. Want to use bricks, but grey isn't your style? Just paint them blue! Multiple palettes are available, from colourful, to earthy, to pastel. Allumeria is a procedurally generated world, packed full of content. Each biome has unique gameplay, environments, items, weapons, enemies and loot . The game generates procedural dungeons and structures , filled with traps and treasure . Mine in a vast interconnected cave network filled with shiny ores and perilous danger . Caves are bountiful and frequent , meaning you will not have to strip mine for resources. They are filled with more than just ore, unique biomes, plant life, enemies and structures await you in the depths... Demo Features This demo features the first biome in the set of progression. It also includes online multiplayer. You will be able to load your demo world in the full version of the game.
