Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Breathedge 2 is planned as a large story-driven survival game. Since we're still a small team, developing the game in stages and releasing new chapters over time is the most practical approach for a project of this scale. The previous part followed the same approach, and we're grateful to our community for their continued support and valuable feedback.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Breathedge 2 to remain in Early Access for roughly 16–20 months, though that may change depending on how development progresses, player feedback, and the scope of any changes we decide to make along the way. We plan to leave Early Access once the game is close enough to the full Breathedge 2 experience we have in mind and we’re happy with its overall state. We’ll also do our best to polish everything to a blinding shine and deprive players of at least some of the bugs they love so dearly.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “During Early Access, we plan to gradually expand Breathedge 2 through major updates. Our current plans include continuing the main story, adding new areas to explore -including gameplay on a planetary surface - along with additional characters and quests, further development of the existing survival and exploration mechanics, and more items, equipment, and ways to interact with the world. Another major part of the game will be its different forms of transportation: small flying and ground vehicles, as well as a personal spacecraft and a warp-jump system. And, of course, building. The exact scope and order in which these features appear may change during development and based on community feedback.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At the start of Early Access, Breathedge 2 already offers a substantial playable experience featuring the first major story chapter and a large area to explore. The game includes its core survival and open-space exploration mechanics, resource gathering, crafting, upgrades, repairs, combat, main and side quests, NPCs, and plenty of optional locations, events, and assorted junk that you’ll inexplicably feel compelled to pick up.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “As with the first game, the price will increase by the time of the full release. It will most likely rise gradually as major updates are released. We don’t plan to make Breathedge 2 an expensive game, but the lowest price will be available at the start of Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Player feedback is one of the main reasons we’re releasing Breathedge 2 in Early Access. We don’t just want to fix bugs - we want to see how people actually play: which mechanics work well, what feels awkward or unclear, which parts of the game players would like to see developed further, and where our brilliant ideas turn out to be slightly less brilliant in practice. We’ll be following feedback and discussions on Steam, Discord, and our social media channels, collecting bug reports and suggestions, and occasionally running polls on individual mechanics and possible directions for the game. Community feedback may influence balance, UI and usability improvements, quality-of-life features, the development of existing mechanics, bug-fixing priorities, and the content of future updates. That said, the overall direction and story of the game will remain in our hands, as we have a very clear vision and a specific story about the Man that we want to tell. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Roadmap About This Game Breathedge 2 is a humorous survival adventure set in space, following an ordinary Man and his perfectly ordinary space problems. It’s a direct sequel to Breathedge, but don’t worry if you never played the first game: everything will be equally confusing to Breathedge veterans and complete newcomers alike. The Breathedge Corporation unexpectedly started a galaxy-wide war, built an enormous army of Coffinbots, and attacked the largest human settlements. The Man and his faithful companion, an AI living inside his spacesuit, accidentally derailed the Corporation’s plans for a swift takeover and instantly became the number-one target of every hostile Coffinbot out there. Apparently, fate still wasn’t satisfied. Having barely survived, our heroes suddenly find themselves under attack by their own allies. Who attacked whom? Who is Babe? Why does the Man need a Chicken? Where were we even going? And what are we supposed to do with all this duct tape? Figuring that out is now your problem. EXPLORE Breathedge 2 is a story-driven adventure where exploring the world is a major part of the experience. Along the way, you’ll get to know the Man and his crew, learn more about life on the distant fringes of inhabited space, meet new characters, and gradually untangle the forces carving up the galaxy between them. Explore abandoned stations, ruined settlements, shipwrecks, and plenty of places no reasonable person would ever think of entering. Discover other people’s stories, strange devices, useful junk, and completely useless one-of-a-kind treasures. Some discoveries will be funny, others strange or unsettling, and a few may help you understand what is actually going on and what part you have to play in it. SURVIVE Hunted from every direction, the heroes of Breathedge 2 are forced to survive in some of the harshest conditions on the fringes of inhabited space. There are no 24-hour stores or banana smoothies out here, but there is an ocean of duct tape, tons of wreckage, and the Man’s remarkably capable hands, ready to turn all that garbage into surprisingly useful contraptions. Learn new blueprints, craft tools and equipment, and upgrade both your gear and your spacesuit. Repair and dismantle machinery, gather resources, find ways into inaccessible places, and venture farther and farther from anything resembling safety. Out in open space, you’ll need to keep an eye on your oxygen, temperature, and various other little details capable of bringing your adventure to a premature end. Occasionally, you’ll also have to fight, run away, and shove aside the bodies of less fortunate astronauts. EARLY ACCESS Breathedge 2 is currently in Early Access and is still actively being developed. At the start of Early Access, the game includes its first major story chapter, a large area to explore, and its core gameplay systems. The story is not yet complete, parts of the world are still inaccessible, and some systems will continue to grow and evolve throughout development. During Early Access, we plan to release major updates that continue the story and add new areas, mechanics, characters, items, and other features. At the same time, we may continue refining existing content, balance, UI, performance, and fixing issues as they are discovered. Player feedback is an important part of this process. We’re incredibly grateful for your support and for all the comments that give us both inspiration and a fresh perspective on the game. Please keep sharing your feedback with us wherever you can. It helps us make Breathedge 2 even better. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: The game contains sexually suggestive content in the context of adult comedy.
