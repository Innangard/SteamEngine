Here, you will take on the role of a dedicated air traffic controller, providing professional ATC services at airports around the world. High-Quality Airport Scenery Experience flight-simulator-quality 3D airport scenery, packed with detail and realism. Fully Animated Jet Bridges: Single and dual-bridge configurations with realistic aircraft docking. Complete Airfield Lighting Systems: Approach lights, runway lights, and taxiway lights built to real-world standards. Accurate Airport Markings: Taxiway markings, runway designators, and stand guidance lines faithfully reproduced from real-world airports. More airports are continuously being added. Realistic Radio Communications Authentic air-to-ground communication procedures based on real-world aviation standards. ATC instructions and pilot readbacks synchronized with your commands in real time. Multiple pilot accents from around the world, with optional Chinese or English voice communications. Highly Realistic Operations Regularly updated procedures based on real-world airport operations. Authentic STARs, APPs, SIDs, altitude restrictions, and speed restrictions. Runway usage restrictions, taxiway pavement limitations, wingspan restrictions, and other taxi route constraints. Flexible Ground Control Freely choose pushback positions. Create and assign custom taxi routes with a single click. Queue Commands: Follow the aircraft ahead, follow a follow-me vehicle, and give way at intersections. Runway Operations: Line up and wait, takeoff clearance, and runway exit assignment. Special situations fully supported, including holding position and waiting off the taxiway. Tug vehicles can be dispatched from any location. Built for Hardcore Players 4K & Ultrawide Support Steam Workshop — Share your custom liveries and flight schedules with the world.
