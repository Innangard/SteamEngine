Features Yuri Mystery Visual Novel 90,000 Words 3 Endings Cel Shading Backgrounds 25+ CGs Extra The perspectives of the gentle Queenie and the brooding Xin Rui are now for you to explore. In these newly unlocked narrative branches, you will re-experience the established storylinbe through their first-person viewpoints. Step back into the familiar path of events and choices, but this time, you will capture the unspoken emotional undercurrents that once flowed beneath the surface. Listen to the silent conflict within Queenie—the constant weighing of reason and struggle hidden behind her soft exterior. Confront the long-suppressed solitude and obsession deep within Xin Rui’s heart. The buried thoughts and fleeting trembles of emotion that remained hidden in the main storyline will now, one by one, come to light. ※ How to unlock: Upon completing a specific character route, their "Omniscient Perspective" will be made available. Following the ending and the final credits, a system prompt will appear asking if you wish to enter this perspective. Simply select "Confirm" to begin the experience. Besides, our game includes a short extra story on time travel of about 20,000 words. The extra story is set in Iceland 1980, where the characters travel to the future and bump into another side of destiny. In this part of the story, you will unlock the elderly images of the characters as time goes by, and witness an extended version of their stories.
