View Community Hub About This Content 🎉 WORK HARD, CELEBRATE HARDER 🎉 Celebrate the year in Farmer’s Life with Easter traditions, Harvest Festival competitions, Christmas preparations and a wild New Year’s Eve. Compete, feast, drink, dance and make every celebration one to remember. Life in the countryside isn't only about fields, animals, and surviving another hard season. Sometimes it's time to put the tools down, gather the whole village and celebrate properly. Farmer's Life – Time to Celebrate brings four major seasonal celebrations to the game: Easter, Harvest Festival, Christmas and New Year's Eve . Each comes with new quests, competitions, traditions, activities, and plenty of opportunities for Kazek to make some questionable decisions. 🐣 EASTER Spring has arrived, and the village is getting ready for Easter. Help the priest with the preparations, deliver boiled eggs, decorate the village, and take part in new story quests inspired by local traditions. Then comes the serious competition. Search the area for hidden bottles and collect more than everyone else to claim the prize. On Śmigus-Dyngus, grab a bucket and prepare for an all-out village water fight. NPCs can soak Kazek too, so keep moving if you want to stay dry. And don't forget the Easter palm competition. Build the biggest palm you can and somehow deliver it to the church without destroying it along the way. 🌾 HARVEST FESTIVAL After months of work, it's finally time to celebrate the harvest. Help decorate the village centre and set up Kazek's own market stall , where you can sell products from your farm. Leave the stall alone and your goods will sell automatically — or stay behind the counter and try to get better prices yourself. But the Harvest Festival is about much more than business. Take part in a whole collection of village competitions: 🎯Race against the clock in a shooting contest featuring Tadeusz's homemade exploding scarecrow targets. 🥊Fight your way through a classic boxing tournament. 🎒Compete against other villagers by throwing sacks at randomly selected targets. 🐔Bet on Chicken Bingo , where chickens wander across a giant numbered board and the winning number is decided by... well, where they decide to do their business. 🍻Eat, drink and dance with the rest of the village. 🐖Race pigs over obstacles. 🤠And when everyone has had enough to drink, see how long you can survive a drunken pig rodeo . Win competitions, climb the rankings and prove that Kazek is capable of doing more than just farming. Probably. 🎄 CHRISTMAS Winter transforms the village, bringing new preparations, traditions and activities. Prepare Christmas dishes, make presents and deliver them to the people around you. Your relationships matter — the way you've treated your neighbours and the gifts you've given them will determine who joins the village Christmas Eve celebration. Decorate your own Christmas trees, build snowmen and start snowball fights with NPCs throughout the winter. You can also head out to catch the most important fish of the year: the Christmas carp . If Kazek needs some extra money, there's always carolling. Team up with the twins from the sawmill, visit the villagers and perform for them. Do well in the minigame and you might actually get paid. 🎆 NEW YEAR'S EVE The year ends exactly as it should in Farmer's Life: with alcohol, fire and homemade explosives. Craft fireworks from whatever questionable materials Kazek can get his hands on and help the twins prepare the midnight show. The more fireworks you make, the bigger the show. There is no sensible limit. Bring a few, and you'll get a nice celebration. Bring a hundred and see what happens. Build a huge bonfire, invite the villagers, drink and dance until midnight. And when walking is no longer a good idea, take the horse cart instead. New Year's Eve also introduces a special sleigh ride challenge: pick up villagers and deliver them to their destination before time runs out without crashing your low-friction, winter-ready, definitely-not-designed-for-drifting horse cart. Four celebrations. One village. A whole lot of bad ideas. From Easter water fights and Harvest Festival boxing to Christmas carolling and an unnecessarily large New Year's fireworks display, Time to Celebrate adds new reasons to experience an entire year in Farmer's Life. Work hard. Celebrate harder. And try not to kill Kazek before the next holiday.
