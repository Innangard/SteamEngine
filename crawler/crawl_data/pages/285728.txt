View Community Hub About This Game Your only job? Stack food towers as high as they'll go. Sandwich +1 is a satisfying idle clicker where every click adds to the pile, and soon your kitchen runs itself. Stress is not on the menu. 🥪 Click, Stack, Grow Every click drops a random ingredient. Build wobbling, towering sandwiches and send them off to thirsty, happy customers. The taller the tower, the bigger your payout! 🧑‍🍳 Automate Your Kitchen Tired of clicking? Hire cute flying workers to do the work for you. Upgrade their speed, buy more boards, and watch your kitchen turn into a self-sustaining sandwich empire while you sit back and relax. ✨ Discover Legendary Ingredients Unlock dozens of new ingredients to add to your random drop pool. Start with simple cheese and lettuce, and work your way up to legendary steak and caviar. Hit a special combo? Watch your customers react! 🛋️ No Stress. Seriously. Zero Stress: No timers, no failing, no burnt food. You can't lose. Play at Your Pace: Jump in for a 5-minute break or chill for an hour. Satisfying Progression: Constant upgrades, new boards, and numbers that only go up. Chill Vibe: Warm colors, satisfying "plop" sounds, and nothing but good vibes.
