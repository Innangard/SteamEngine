Make every card count Choose attacks, defenses, and special abilities to create combinations that fit your run. Adapt your deck as new cards appear and decide which risks are worth taking. Rise again Explore new paths and experiment with different builds across repeated runs. A successful journey takes you from the first fight to the final boss, with an optional Hard difficulty for a tougher challenge.
