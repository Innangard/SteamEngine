Content For This Game Browse all (1) Tricky Fox - Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game Take on the role of a clever little fox in Tricky Fox , the sequel to the popular Tricky Cat . Jump, walk, and puzzle your way through 100 increasingly challenging levels as you clear all the tiles by stepping on them—just be careful not to fall into the bottomless pit! Once all tiles disappear, the exit portal opens, but getting there won't be easy with spikes and tricky moving tiles in your way. As you progress, levels become more complex with timed jumps, spikes that retract and extend, and tiles that need to be cleared in specific sequences. Every level requires quick thinking and precise moves, making Tricky Fox a perfect blend of platforming and puzzle-solving. With its fresh gameplay, catchy soundtrack, and fun level design, this game will keep you on your toes.
