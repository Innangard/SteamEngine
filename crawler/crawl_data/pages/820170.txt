Let them wander across your desktop while you work or study, then hop into their world to decorate homes, play mini-games, and hang out with friends! Bring your Weyrdlet to your desktop while you work, study, or browse. Feed them, play micro-games, or just watch them wander and chill beside you! Help your pet grow fresh produce right on your desktop or in-game. Use the harvest to make juices for Sports Day and keep the loop going with seeds for future crops. Decorate your Weyrdlet’s home with fancy furniture and style your pet with cute accessories and stickers. Make both your pet and their space playful, cozy, and totally your own. Visit shared spaces like the Playground and Cozy Cove to spend time with friends (or alone if you like). Go fishing, throw water balloons, climb the lighthouse, or just relax by the beach. Take your Weyrdlet for a spin in Worble , a fun and chaotic mini-game where rolling through obstacle courses is half the fun. Play solo or with friends in weekly races with a fresh map every week. See who tumbles, rolls, or somehow makes it to the finish line first! Get your Weyrdlet ready for Sports Day and watch them learn new skills. Turn the produce and fish you’ve gathered into energizing juices that give your pet a chance to pick up new abilities. Take on Sports Day challenges to earn seeds, grow more produce, and keep the training going! Use the Pomodoro timer, track your wellness, and manage tasks with the to-do list! All available both in-game and on your desktop. Let your pet idly dig for loot on your desktop or on the island while you're busy. Unlock fancy hats, home decorations, and snacks to keep your pet happy.
