Fight Nights Fight for your Faction every week, and you’ll find yourself earning Allegiance at a vastly increased rate! Each Tuesday, both Factions will be offering doubled rewards to crews who pledge themselves to the cause as they dive into battle using the Hourglass of Fate. Emissaries of Fortune The Trading Companies aren’t going to miss out on grabbing your attention either. Every Wednesday, they’ll be offering a full 24 hours of increased rewards for crews flying an Emissary Flag – including extra gold and reputation to bring your next promotion that much closer. Bilge Rats Weekenders Determined crews will have more chances to earn Doubloons throughout Season 21 as Bilge Rats Weekenders continue to liven up the Outposts and beyond. Last Ship Standing Faction fighting isn’t going to be limited to one-on-one battles this Season as Last Ship Standing makes its long-awaited return! Crews will once again be able to venture into the dangerous waters of the Sea of the Damned to fight for survival against a fleet of rival ships. Season Pass Rewards There are rewards to be earned throughout the Season Pass, including Voyages and a bountiful purse of Doubloons to save for the next Bilge Rats Weekender! Every five levels, you’ll also get to choose between three hand-picked items from the Pirate Emporium archives. The final reward for completing each Season Pass is a very special Star Prize – for Season 21, that’s the decision-making Fate’s Light Lantern! About This Game Sea of Thieves offers the essential pirate experience, from sailing and fighting to exploring and looting – everything you need to live the pirate life and become a legend in your own right. With no set roles, you have complete freedom to approach the world, and other players, however you choose. Whether you’re voyaging as a group or sailing solo, you’re bound to encounter other crews in this shared world adventure – but will they be friends or foes, and how will you respond? A Vast Open World Explore a vast open world filled with unspoiled islands and underwater kingdoms. Take on quests to hunt for lost loot, forge a reputation with the Trading Companies and battle foes from Phantoms and Ocean Crawlers to Megalodons and the mighty Kraken. Try your hand at fishing, make maps to your own buried treasure or choose from hundreds of other optional goals and side-quests! A Game That’s Always Growing With each Season bringing in new game features every three months alongside regular in-game Events, Sea of Thieves is a service-based game that’s still growing and evolving. Check back regularly to see what free content has been newly added, and see how far you can climb through each Season’s 50 levels of Renown to earn special rewards. Become Legend On your journey to become a Pirate Legend you’ll amass loot, build a reputation and define a unique personal style with your hard-earned rewards. Adventurer. Explorer. Conqueror. What will your legend be?
