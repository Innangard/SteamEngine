A 4-axle wagon with a 25-ton weight capacity, the Facs124 is a hopper wagon with gravity discharge. Introduced in 1978, it is used to transport bulk materials that do not need to be kept dry. Materials such as sand, ore, lime and coal are held inside 4 chambers, each with its own gravity unloading doors. This pack features script-controlled train lock plates and dynamic car numbers, and includes the vR feature RandomSkin. This pack is set up for Quick Drive.
