You'll be able to enjoy around one hour of content and learn the basics. Plus, you can invite your friends to play with you or join a multiplayer session, through Steam and inside the game. Keep in mind: There's no Save and Load of games, everytime you start, it's a new save game from scratch. Deep Dish Dungeon is a dungeon crawler and exploration game that rewards curiosity and resourcefulness. In an atmospheric environment, explore corridors, rooms and passageways solo or with up to two friends, and gather resources to improve your camping site. Craft tools and cook nourishing meals to bolster strength throughout the journey, and unravel the enigma that lies within the shadows. Build, craft, cook Forage and scavenge as you traverse through the dungeon's bountiful depths. Between your journeys, return to camp and plan your next steps by the fire. Use the resources you find to craft new tools and cook a hearty meal. Remember that food is progress: over time, unlock recipes that have different effects on your stats. A hand-crafted dungeon to discover Fortune favors the bold. Take your time to investigate every shadow, solve every puzzle, and open every door. The dungeon will reveal its secrets only if you are patient and persistent. Learn how to defeat enemies with diverse behaviors, discover coveted treasures, and never take a hint for granted. Explore together Any adventure is better with pals! Play with up to two friends and collaborate to unveil the secrets of the dungeon. Remember, co-op mode in Deep Dish Dungeon is not about competition, but connecting, sharing knowledge, and taking care of each other.
