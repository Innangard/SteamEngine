View Community Hub Join Our Community About This Game Restore Your Island is a slow, cozy restoration sim about healing a damaged island and building a peaceful home. Revive nature, and watch wildlife return as the ecosystem comes back to life. Explore at your own pace with your loyal dog by your side. Explore & plan explore the island, spot areas that need care, and choose what to restore next. Clean & Repair remove trash, mend structures, Heal trees actions that let nature begin to recover. Rebuild habitats use tools to rescue wildlife, and bring nature back. Sustain through your journey revived trees and gathered fruit provide resources and energy to reach new parts of the island. Bond with your dog pet, play quick mini-games, and get the occasional helpful cue or small reward. Relax unwind by the fireplace with music, water ambience, sunsets and other cozy features Grow & discover upgrade tools, unlock new areas, and uncover hidden secrets across a brighter island . KEY FEATURES Clear, satisfying progress: every cleaning, repair, and re-greening shows immediate results. A companion with heart: your dog reacts to pets, plays, adds warmth to every session. Low-pressure cozy play: no combat, no harsh timers, play in short bursts or long, relaxing sessions.
