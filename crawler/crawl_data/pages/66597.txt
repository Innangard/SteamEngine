Set up your equipment and let the station idle, generate power, charge products, and sell them automatically. No constant watching or repeated clicks to collect earnings: let coins build up while you work, study, or do something else, then return to grow your station. Idle power, automatic charging, automatic sales Install rooftop solar panels, energy storage, and charging equipment. All rooms share power without wiring. Fully charged products sell automatically, and the next charging cycle begins right away. While the game stays running, your station keeps working unattended, even with the interface collapsed, another app in focus, or you away from the computer. Earn, reinvest, and expand your power station Reinvest your automatic sales income in new products, stronger solar panels, more storage, extra charging slots, and additional rooms. Progress from small cells to vehicle battery packs and large energy-storage products in a satisfying loop: produce, sell automatically, earn, and expand. Grow to up to ten rooms and scroll sideways to explore your expanding station and rooftops. Build your business and repair your spaceship Repair the hull, power system, and navigation in stages as you prepare for the journey home. Once repairs are complete, you can stay at the station, keep expanding, and unlock the remaining equipment. Check in at your own pace Expand the station for a quick look between tasks, then collapse it while production continues. The game sits on the desktop layer, beneath ordinary application windows. Solar output stays steady regardless of real-world daylight. Production does not accumulate while the game is closed or your computer is asleep or hibernating.
