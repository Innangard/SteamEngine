About This Game Loot Frog is an incremental action bullet hell where you smash pots for loot, fight an evil cult, and grow stronger with every run. There are no resets and no idle mechanics. Every upgrade is permanent, and every run pushes you forward. Start small. Build momentum. Become unstoppable. A tightly designed 3 hour experience that keeps you coming back for "just one more run" . Break pots, defeat enemies, and collect resources to fuel your progression. Unlike traditional incremental games, Loot Frog is fully active . You'll be constantly moving, dodging, and making real-time decisions in chaotic bullet hell combat, not just watching numbers go up. The better you play, the faster you grow. Every run makes you stronger , pushing you deeper into the dungeon. Every level introduces new enemy types with distinct behaviours , keeping combat fresh and challenging. Face aggressive elites that test your skills, and take on unique bosses with different bullet patterns and mechanics at the end of each biome. Progress through 25 handcrafted levels across 5 biomes , each with its own challenges, hazards, and enemies to master. Unlock a large branching skill tree with 200+ meaningful upgrades and abilities that drastically change how you play. Focus on survivability, explosive power, or resource efficiency and create a build that fits your style . There's no single correct path. You play at your own pace .
