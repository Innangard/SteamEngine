Please review the supported language list below before purchasing Buy Sonders: Beginning of an end $1.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Game A classic RPG game with an original and absurdly unpredictable story set in a strange fantasy universe. Four human friends end up there by accident... or perhaps not, and soon discover that finding their way back home will be far more difficult than they expected. This world is part of the so-called Orbital Paradigm, a vast network of interconnected worlds linked by a mysterious force known as Orbital Energy, capable of creating magic, impossible creatures, and powers that defy all logic. As they journey toward the legendary and long-abandoned Orbital Portal in hopes of returning home, they will encounter magical beasts, unpredictable clans, bizarre settlements, and situations that are as ridiculous as they are dangerous. And although the universe around them is completely unfamiliar, their sarcasm, dark humor, and constant teasing remain exactly the same. Sonders combines exploration, turn-based combat, and character progression in an adventure focused on storytelling, discovery, and worldbuilding. Throughout the journey, you will strengthen your party, unlock new abilities, acquire equipment, solve puzzles, and make decisions that influence the knowledge you uncover about the world and its inhabitants. Game Features 🌍 Explore more than 600 locations throughout the Orbital Paradigm. ⚔️ Battle over 150 unique enemies in strategic turn-based combat. 🧠 Discover more than 300 abilities, including hidden techniques and secret skills. 🎒 Collect over 200 items and more than 250 pieces of equipment to strengthen your party. 🧪 Use or resist more than 30 different status effects that can dramatically change the course of battle. 🧩 Solve puzzles and overcome situations that will challenge your observation skills and common sense. 🎭 Follow four protagonists filled with prejudice, sarcasm, and unfiltered commentary. 📖 Experience a 5–10 hour adventure packed with mystery, provocative humor, and absurdly unpredictable events.
