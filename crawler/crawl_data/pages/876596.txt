Unless the city is made of yellow walls, empty pools, and existential discomfort. It’s about falling through a shed floor and ending up somewhere you were absolutely not trained for. You and your team (1-4 person coop) start what should’ve been a normal mission. A quiet road. A random shed. Five seconds later, you’re dropping into a dimension that feels stitched together from forgotten textures and bad dreams. Level 0 is endless yellow walls and something that doesn’t blink. Level 37 feels calm, empty, almost relaxing. Level Fun looks like a party and absolutely isn’t. Level 94 gives you peaceful hills during the day. Level THE END is exactly as comforting as it sounds. The rules are different here. Sound matters. Light matters. Staying low suddenly matters a lot. The new crawling system lets you drag yourself across the floor to stay less visible. Sometimes it’s the only reason you’re still alive. Physics still exists. And it will betray you here too. Someone will panic. Someone will run in the wrong direction. Backrooms traps your usual SOS OPS! chaos inside tight spaces, strange architecture, and levels that feel just a little too quiet. You’re still a team of emergency responders. You’re just not sure who you’re responding for anymore.
