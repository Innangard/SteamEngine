Use a variety of tools to interact with the Forever Chicken, a living ragdoll at the heart of a mysterious midnight carnival. Under the watchful eye of Uncle Tontine, speak in whispers with other children taken from their homes and uncover the writhing truth behind the carnival. Teach dead rats to smoke cigarettes. Play with loaded firearms. Throw dance parties for cockroaches. The carnival's demands are many and absurd. Solve riddles, charades, and bizarre physical challenges built around the Forever Chicken. Sometimes it wants to be shot. Sometimes it wants to explode. Sometimes it merely wants to watch. Whatever the chicken seeks, you must determine before the night is through.
