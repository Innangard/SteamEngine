What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “While the core mechanics of Himno - The Silent Melody are ready, the game still can grow and evolve, and Early Access is just perfect for it to reach its true atmospheric experience potential. Himno TSM is a huge world and I have lots that I want to add, and Early Access is a way for us to receive constant valuable feedback, collaborate with our players and keep them in mind at every step of the development cycle.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The most important thing for me is quality, and it requires time, so for at least one year. Depending on player feedback and the amount of content I choose to put into the final game, it may take longer.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan to add more content such as new biomes/zones with unique themes, new enemies, new bosses, new guardians, new materials to find and base objects to personalize your camp.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game looks and plays as a finished product. All the basic mechanics work well and most of the features are in place. It has hints of a sandbox game so can be played indefinitely. You can play it for dozens of hours, traveling through districts, discovering new items and places, and expanding your base and optimizing your creations even at the current stage of development.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “No definitive plans here yet, but it is likely that the price will be increased after the Early Access period.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I currently use our Discord server and the Steam forums as primary ways to talk to the Himno's community. Upcoming changes are openly discussed with the community for early feedback.
