Aircraft War invites you to an epic fight for domination of the skies! Defeat enemy planes and collect points for victory. Achieve success by neutralizing the enemies you encounter in each level. But beware, you must complete your mission before you run out of fuel and the opponents shoot you. The game offers you three different exciting experiences: single player combat, double co-op co-op mode and double match mode. Prove your mastery by beating challenging levels in single player mode. Fight alongside your friend on-screen against enemy planes in double-player co-op mode. In the double match mode, compete with your friend to achieve the highest score. Take control of the skies, challenge your friends and experience an unforgettable airplane combat with Aircraft War. Play now and enjoy the victory!
