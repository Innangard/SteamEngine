View Community Hub About This Game You’re a moose on a diving adventure. Swim into the ocean and vacuum coins from the seabed. Sell the coins and earn money to upgrade and buy new gear. Swim deeper and find treasure chests to collect power boosting artefacts, but don’t forget to keep an eye on your oxygen level. Dive into the water and vacuum coins from the seabed with your coin vacuum. Sell the coins to Coin Collector Sir Floaty II Seahorse of Kiddie Pool he will pay a fair price for them. Use the money earned to buy upgrades to your gear and more in Barkie McBarkie’s Divin gear n Stuff . He has the best prices on the island. With your upgraded gear you will reach deeper parts of the ocean which hold more valuable coins, treasure chests and maybe some hidden caves. But mind your oxygen level so you don’t drown and lose the coins you have collected. Features: 16 coin types and 16 gem types appearing as you get deeper into the ocean A large map of ~67 sq km with millions of coins and gems to vacuum up Lots of gear upgrades to unlock Many different artefacts to find and collect with different power boosts System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Core i3 or AMD equivalent with SSE4.1 support Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: Intel UHD Graphics 630 Storage: 700 MB available space Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS: Windows 10 Processor: Intel Core i5 or AMD equivalent with SSE4.1 support Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: Nvidia GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 560 Storage: 700 MB available space View all What Curators Say 2 Curators have reviewed this product.
