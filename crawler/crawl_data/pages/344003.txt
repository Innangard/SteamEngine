Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early Access allows me to release the game in chapters, as they are completed. Since I’m a solo developer and unable to work on the game full-time, each chapter takes several months to create. Also, it helps to get feedback so I don’t accidentally make something completely unplayable. If something’s broken or not hitting right, you can yell at me early enough that I can fix it.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “It really depends on how much time I can put into it. Worst case, the full game should be done by the end of 2026 (hopefully sooner). ” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “I plan to include 4 chapters in total, fully completed and polished. Chapter 1: Decay Chapter 2: Dog Days Chapter 3: The Grid Chapter 4: A World To Erase” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “As of right now, Abyss Veil has 1 out of 4 planned chapters available to play. Chapters 2 and 3 are already pretty far along in development, so expect them soon(ish).” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will go up a bit once the game leaves early access. ” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “I’ll be reviewing feedback on typos, bugs, balance issues, and any other features that need work.
