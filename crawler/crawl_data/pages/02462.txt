Pick a side, customize your weapons, and fight other players across a variety of action-packed game modes. Spin the block and take over the streets in this chaotic multiplayer FPS. Hood Warfare 2 brings back the chaotic FPS gameplay and humor of the original, now built in Unreal Engine 5 with updated visuals, new weapon animations, and a new set of maps. Fight through downtown, the suburbs, The Trenches, and Chicago Mall. Hold down the streets or take the fight indoors, with different routes and positions to learn on each map. Weapon customization gets an upgrade, too. Change out sights, magazines, grips, suppressors, and other attachments to get your gun how you want it. Set up your loadout with perks and equipment before heading into a match. Hood Warfare 2 features six game modes: Turf War: Gangs fight for control of turf around the map. Earn points for the territory your gang holds, and push the other team off theirs. GangBang: A classic team shootout. Two gangs go head-to-head, and the gang with the most kills wins. Chaos: Every man for himself. There is no loyalty to a gang in this mode. Get the most kills to come out on top. Hit A Lick: Take the bag from the enemy’s hood and bring it back to yours. Keep an eye on your own bag while your crew goes after theirs. Destroy The Block: One gang has the bomb. The other has to stop them. Plant or defuse to win, with only one life per round. Chain Snatch: Getting the kill is only half the job. Snatch the chains enemies drop to earn points, and collect your teammates’ chains before the other gang gets them. Build your reputation by playing matches and leveling up to unlock more weapons and equipment. Attachments are earned through weapon challenges, while kills with each gun unlock new skins to flex on the opposing gang. Play online with friends and other players, or set up a match against bots.
