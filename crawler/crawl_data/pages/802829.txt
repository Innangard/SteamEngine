About This Content This Pack includes: Leopard 2A4M (Senrai Maidens) (Rank 7 Germany); Premium account for 20 days; 2500 Golden Eagles. Unique camouflage: "Elsa" Unique portrait: "Elsa" Unique title: "Fan of Elsa" Unique decal: "Elsa" A true professional of the Senrai Maidens, Elsa leads her customized Leopard 2A4M (Senrai Maidens) into battle — a perfect machine for those who value precision and control. She acts coldly, deliberately, with absolute composure. For Elsa, every battle is a chess match — and her enemy’s mistake is always their last. The Leopard 2A4M CAN is a modernized Canadian variant purchased from the Royal Netherlands Army, currently in service with Canada’s only tank regiment. This variant is drastically more advanced than the standard 2A4 model, featuring a coating of composite armor protecting the sides of the hull and turret, complete with camo netting. The rear is protected by slat armor, which can defeat chemical rounds, while the underside is protected with extra plating to guard against explosions under the tank. You also have the option to remove these kits to reduce weight. Firepower is also improved; this variant can fire the advanced KE-W APFSDS round and comes with high-quality thermals, helping you identify and dispatch enemy tanks with ease. A powerful modernization, Leopard 2A4M sports excellent armor, mobility, and firepower, making it ideal for effectively all combat roles. Please note! Senrai Maidens’ characters are not added to the game and appear in the images next to the vehicles solely for visual purposes.
