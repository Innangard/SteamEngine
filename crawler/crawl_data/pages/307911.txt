On a whim, the younger sister Gracie purchases a coastal tavern in a small town. To their surprise, they discover an adorable baby dragon inside. Together with this little dragon, they run the tavern, befriend new companions, and gradually immerse themselves in a tranquil new life. You may choose to play as either sibling or create your own character to begin a journey uniquely yours. With plenty of customization options such as hairstyles, skin tones, facial expressions, and more, you can shape your character just the way you like. Dress up before setting out on your adventure! On the picturesque seaside farm, you can plant crops, fish, and gather resources. Beyond the shore, set sail from the dock to explore uncharted maps, collect rare treasures, uncover the world’s mysterious legends, and experience extraordinary stories. Cute and full of energy, magical pets are here to help you. Whether it’s boosting production or shortening crop growth time, they’ve got it all covered. After gathering ingredients and materials through exploration, return to the island to brew wine, craft handicrafts, and manage your tavern using these carefully prepared items, welcoming every customer drawn in by fine drinks and cuisine. Too busy to manage everything on your own? Team up with a partner. Work together to explore, manage the tavern, and build your very own shared island. With teamwork, the workload is halved and the fun is doubled. May you delight in the joys this world brings. Add to your Wishlist now and embark on a journey that is both cozy and adventurous.
