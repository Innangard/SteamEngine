Together, you will synergize your builds and work together to vanquish those who would stand in your way. TAKE OVER THE WORLD Auto-attacking Horde Survival Roguelite with a unique twist, 2 characters means 2 builds, and many skills interact with the other player directly. Play with an AI-controlled Ally, or with a friend using Local Co-Op (compatible with Steam RemotePlay )! Unique skill level ups - most filler is removed in favor of giving skills more interesting effects when leveling them up! Each run is unique, with each room presenting various risks and rewards! Acquire relics to aid you in various ways throughout your playthrough, modifying the behavior of your abilities and more! Take on a boss fight that awaits you in the final area of every zone. Each boss has their own special attacks and abilities! Many elemental skills interact with each other. For example, some Water skills make puddles, and Storm abilities electrify them! Become stronger between runs through meta upgrades in the form of a skill tree!
