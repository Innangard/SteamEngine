Step into a nightmarish hand-drawn world where every corner hides grotesque horrors — from writhing tentacled beasts and blood-soaked eldritch abominations to hulking horned demons and swarms of hellspawn. Armed with devastating weaponry, you’ll blast, shred, and obliterate your way through brutal levels drenched in blood! In this world, humanity's greed and avarice have led to the loss of their home—Earth itself. Now, the planet is ruled by a diverse array of beings: aliens from distant worlds, demons from the depths of hell, intelligent machines, and wild animals that have gained consciousness. The wealthy have retreated to the Moon, establishing bases, cities, and scientific laboratories. There, they conduct horrific experiments to alter the human body and indulge in perverse pleasures of all kinds. Meanwhile, those remaining on Earth struggle to survive amidst countless monsters that outnumber and outmatch them. Some of these survivors have formed militarized resistance groups to fight back against the alien, demonic, and monstrous threats. One such group is known as the Resistance, led by a fierce warrior known as Whale Man. Earth is being terraformed by aliens seeking to make it more suitable for their survival, while demons strive to summon their mighty ruler onto the planet. Meanwhile, intelligent animals fight to protect their homeland from destruction. Long before this endless conflict began, a beautiful creature descended from the sky. Many believed it was a god. But the world’s governments, unable to understand or control this being, saw it as a threat and destroyed it. That act left Earth vulnerable - exposed to the invaders that now threaten to annihilate what remains of humanity and its world.
