GREAT IDEA, RIGHT? It was supposed to be just another crazy adventure for your crew: hop on your bikes , ride deep into a remote national park, and find out how much truth there really is behind the legends of Bigfoot . The problem is, the deeper you venture into the forest, the less this feels like an ordinary trip. Follow strange tracks, leave the beaten path, discover forgotten places, and solve puzzles together. All of it to catch legendary Bigfoot. Just don’t stray too far from the rest of the crew - it’s very easy to disappear here for good... YOU’RE NOT THE FIRST CREW TO MAKE IT THIS FAR... As you venture deeper into the forest, you begin to notice traces left behind by the crews who came before you . Other crews came here looking for the same monster. So what happened to them? Stay alert - you don’t want to disappear like they did. NEW FRIEND LITTLE BIGFOOT - TRY NOT TO LOSE IT... Your search eventually leads you to someone you definitely weren’t expecting to find - Little Bigfoot . Don’t let that adorable face fool you. This little troublemaker has quite a personality, knows a bit of magic, and can turn your expedition into even more chaos in no time. Take him with you and find Bigfoot . He might also be your only chance of making it back home... BIKES, PUZZLES & BAD DECISIONS Spooky Ride is a co-op adventure for 1–4 players , combining exploration, puzzles, a bike trip, supernatural mystery, and the kind of chaos that always appears when the whole plan depends on your friends. 1–4 Player Co-op + Voice Chat Ride together, communicate, and help each other along the way. Sometimes the biggest threat isn’t what’s hiding in the forest — it’s your friends’ ideas. Your Bikes Are Part of the Adventure Cross rough terrain, discover new trails, and keep an eye on your two wheels. Walking back through this forest is a very bad idea. Puzzles Built for Cooperation Activate mysterious mechanisms, search for hidden clues, uncover secret passages, and work together to figure out how to move forward. A Supernatural Forest Full of Secrets Ghosts of previous crews, strange rituals, monsters, and unexplained phenomena mean you never know what’s waiting around the next corner. Little Bigfoot Find him, keep him close, and try to get him safely back to his family. Easier said than done. Chaos, Physics & Bad Decisions Even when the forest gives you a moment of peace, your friends probably won’t. 1–4 Players | Online Co-op | Voice Chat Bikes | Bigfoot | Exploration | Puzzles | Supernatural Encounters GET YOUR CREW READY TO GO LOOKING FOR BIGFOOT.
