The legendary islands of Aldramar are waiting with monumental ruins, a coarse desert landscape, and the remains of a long-forgotten civilization. A new story campaign with four maps, a desert landscape, impressive monuments, new enemy type, and new gameplay challenges are waiting for you! Explore the story of the Galdrar, restore monumental buildings made of marble and revive a land that was split into several islands. Story Campaign The new story campaign with four maps tells a story of a lost civilization. Restore the monumental buildings of Aldramar in several successive stages. Step by step you piece together Aldramar's legacy. What has been a legend for generations becomes a story you can experience. Stone Warriors, Obelisks and Altars Colossal stone warriors who guard the ancient monuments are out of control. With their unique unit behavior they challenge you to think strategically. You can either face them in battle or evade their patrols with clever tactical maneuvers. Activate the mysterious obelisks with magical key stones and turn the landscape fertile again. Altars can give you combat boosts and open new strategic possibilities to face new and familiar enemies. New Landscape: Turn Desert into Fertile Land The new desert landscape provides new challenges for your settlements. Sand dunes and quicksand form the environment; water is scarce, fertile land is rare, and some passages are blocked. Stranded Pagonian adventurers depend on your help. If you succeed in the multi-level restoration of the monuments and activation of the obelisks, new life will return to Aldramar: Parts of the desert turn back to fertile land and change the conditions of your settlement. Out-of-Territory Construction The Expedition Camp and the new Expedition Builders are now able to restore the monuments and obelisks outside of your territory. Regular buildings from the settlement progression can only be built in your in your own territory as per usual.
