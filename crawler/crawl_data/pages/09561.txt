Using a fully gridless , intuitive snap-and-rotate designer, you balance weapons, thrust, energy and armor, use advanced mechanical parts like pistons and hinges to build transforming warships , or install hangars to deploy swarms of custom-designed drones . Take your custom vessel into Bounty Hunt mode, navigating asteroid storms, pirate ambushes, and random encounters as you evolve your ship from a basic drone into a true dreadnought to survive intense, bullet-hell boss fights. Mission-based game loop : complete missions to increase in rank and build even bigger ships to hunt more dangerous targets. Gridless spaceship designer that lets you easily snap parts together in any angle, allowing you to quickly try out new designs or upgrade your ship. Design your own drones and deploy swarms of them from your hangars. Intuitive engineering constraints : balance thrusters, weapons, energy and armor to create an effective warship. Bullet-hell boss fights : each mission ends with an epic phased boss-fight. Find weak points while dodging projectiles.
