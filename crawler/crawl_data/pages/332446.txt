Battle Visualizations Completely optional but highly immersive, this DLC introduces visualized battle reports through a series of images that depict the entire course of a battle. These visualizations will include: A simple battlefield overview, showing whether the fight took place in open terrain or before the walls of the capital. Regiments and their positions relative to each other during different stages of the battle. A system of arrows illustrating the sequence of individual attacks. Tooltips providing detailed updates on changes in the number of warriors in each regiment after every attack.
