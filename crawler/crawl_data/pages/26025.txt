Step into the dugout in Soccer Manager 2027, a realistic football manager game where you take charge as manager of elite clubs and world-class players. KEY FEATURES • Build your ultimate dream team from real players in this immersive soccer manager experience. • Master formations and player instructions as you manage elite teams like Manchester City, Bayern Munich, and Borussia Dortmund. • Compete across 54 football leagues globally, including the official Bundesliga. • Develop your football club through training facilities and youth academy investments. • Experience stunning 3D football games powered by the upgraded Match Motion Engine. • Step onto the global stage and lead one of over 100 national football teams. DEVELOP YOUR CLUB Upgrade stadium facilities, improve your youth academy, and craft a long-term sporting strategy. Make every administrative decision count as you secure the future and financial success of your club.
