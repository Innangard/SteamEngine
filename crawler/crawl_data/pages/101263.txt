View Community Hub About This Content The Blood Moon Edition unlocks the Intermediate and Full role sets for Three Nights: Moonfall: 14 new roles on top of the 5 in the base game, 19 in total. Only the host needs it. When you host, everyone in your room plays with every role you pick, whether they joined through Steam or from a phone on your Wi-Fi. Intermediate set: 8 new roles Robber : on the first night, swap roles with another player and become them. Troublemaker : secretly swap the roles of two other players. Drunk : swap with a random player every night without knowing what you became. Witch : one potion to save the night's victim, one poison to kill. Mayor : your vote counts double. Tanner : wins alone if eliminated. You want to die. Cupid : link two lovers. If one dies, the other dies of heartbreak. Minion : knows the werewolves and wins with them, even in death. Full set: 6 more Alpha Werewolf : on the second night, recruits a villager into the pack. Mystic Werewolf : hunts with the pack and peers into one player's true role each night. Detective : learns whether a player reads as clean or suspicious. Guardian Angel : saves their chosen player from the first vote against them. Necromancer : brings one fallen player back to life on the final night. The Fool : wins alone by getting voted out as a werewolf. Requires Three Nights: Moonfall.
