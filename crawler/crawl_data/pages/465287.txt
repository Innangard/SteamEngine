You can hire and manage employees to do your dirty laundry too! 🧽Clean With Detail🧽 You can load dirty clothes in the washer, wash them with the washing program of your choice, dry them to save some time, iron the clean clothes for a final touch and detail clean the clothes that your customers bring in. Chop chop! 🧹Take Care of Your Shop🧹 You need to clean the dirt your customers or wet clothes make and repair the machines that need fixing. You can decorate your laundromat and buy items how you like. You can upgrade your shop to become the biggest laundromat. Maximize your income and make famous people your regular customers. 🍀Find Forgotten Item of Customers🍀 Your customers often forget some of their items in their basket. You can take them or leave them. One man's trash is another man's treasure. 🛠️Manage and Upgrade Your Equipment🛠️ Manage your machines and equipments. They need love and attention too. Unlock and buy newer and better equipment to manage your time and maximize your profit.
