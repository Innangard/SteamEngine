Like any in-game music for Flatspace, music will trigger at random when you enter a new sector. This pack contains a range of music, from alternative ‘Flatspace’ versions of commercial recordings, to new music written for this pack. A full description of the music is included as a pdf booklet. Here is an outline of the music included: Flatspace Nocturne A gentle piano variation of the main Flatspace theme. Cat Covid (Flatspace Mix) A special remix taken from the 2024 album ‘A Drive Through The Town’ by Mark Sheeky. Widespace A new track for this music pack. Excessive Consumption Has Laxative Effects (Flatspace Version) The longest title of any Flatspace track! This is an instrumental variation of this single. Cycles III Piano Version Cycles & Shadows was a 2017 album of piano music in two halves. The album was partly re-recorded in 2024, with a new version and video of this tune, Cycles III: Perpetual Change. The version here is simpler, without the strings present on the album version. Riding Pi (Flatspace Version) Riding Pi is a track from the 2020 concept album 'Burn of God'. I, Robot (Extended Version) There will probably not be a soundtrack album of the 2004 game Taskforce: The Mutants Of October Morgane, but the 2020 extension of that game added a huge amount of brilliant new music. This is an extended version of one of the game tracks, 'I, Robot', from one of the levels. Lost My Telephone A new track for this music pack. Flatspace Cavatina A gentle acoustic guitar version of the Flatspace main theme. Burning Meat Outdoors (Flatspace Version) A rip-roaring race of a tune, this is a Flatspace variation of a track from the 2024 album ‘A Drive Through The Town’.
