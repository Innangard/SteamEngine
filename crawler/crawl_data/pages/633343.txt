View Community Hub About This Game In the fractured continent of Aetheria, an abyssal corruption from the depths of the earth is silently spreading. This ancient dark energy twists all life, transforming living beings into bloodthirsty monsters. The only things capable of resisting the corruption are the magical gems scattered across the world – relics of antiquity containing pure, uncontaminated magic power. These gems not only grant the power to manipulate elements and strengthen the body but can also be synthesized to create ultimate weapons against the abyss. Features classic turn-based combat, allowing for calm planning with every move. No penalty for failure; freely adjust gem combinations and repeatedly optimize tactics until you build the perfect build. Defeating enemies drops gold and elemental gems (Fire, Frost, Lightning, etc.). Hundreds of gems can be freely combined to create high burst, sustained damage, or special effect builds, offering endless combinatorial fun. 40+ enemy types, each with unique mechanics (gem devouring, evolution, damage reflection, etc.), require targeted gem and relic搭配. 400+ gems, preset groups, and buff systems work in synergy to form earth-shattering ultimate combos.
