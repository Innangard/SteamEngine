View Community Hub About This Game In Hunted Within: Terminal Rust , you traverse a harsh wasteland consumed by blinding sandstorms, deadly gravity anomalies, and grotesque biomechanical horrors. Armed with makeshift rusty industrial weaponry and your trusty dual-detection terminal , Pilot your own survival train through the desolate desert and pitch-black caverns, and abandoned areas. Monitor your terminal for environmental threats, conserve your momentum, and fight back against relentless mutations to survive. THE STORY In this chapter of Hunted Within , your hunt for your ultimate nemesis continues. The Psycho Doctor has executed all of his test subjects -except you- and unleashed a catastrophic bio-virus onto your final remaining companion in the Metro. The MISSION Your mission is simple: track the psycho doctor down across the desolate surface and put a permanent end to his nightmare experiments. KEY FEATURES The Survival train: it's your mobile base, your refuge, and your key to reaching new areas, The Dual-Detector Terminal: Keep your terminal scanner close. Track invisible, crushing gravitational field anomalies and incoming sandstorms in real time to navigate safely through lethal terrain. Rust-Tech Warfare: Wield and upgrade heavy, makeshift industrial weaponry—from high-torque saw-blade melee weapons to devastating triple-barrel, break-action shotguns. Adaptive Biomechanical Horrors: Face blind photosensitive stalkers, mutated titans, burrowing scorpions, and savage cavern dwellers and more creepy and deadly experiment subjects.
