View Community Hub About This Game Roll, dodge, and race against the clock! Take control of your marble and navigate 144 challenging levels packed with deadly traps, moving obstacles, and tricky paths. Keep your marble on track, avoid falling into the sea, and reach the finish as fast as you can! With simple directional controls and increasingly difficult courses, Marble Trap is easy to learn but challenging to master. Every movement matters, and every second counts! Features 144 Challenging Levels — Roll through handcrafted courses filled with obstacles that test your precision, timing, and reflexes. Deadly Traps & Obstacles — Dodge rotating axes, spike cubes, rising spears, trap pits, shooting cannons, and even chainsaws! Race Against the Clock — Improve your personal bests, discover faster routes, and compete on Steam leaderboards. Daily & Weekly Challenges — Take on new challenges and compete with players around the world. Collect 432 Stars — Master every level, collect stars, and unlock achievements along the way. Customize Your Marble — Visit the Marble Garage to choose your colors and unlock 6 special marble skins , including Metallic, Striped, Galaxy, Gold, Lava, and Champion. Made for Precision & Speedrunning — Simple controls, challenging courses, and plenty of opportunities to perfect your runs. Can You Master Every Level? Whether you're collecting stars, overcoming tricky obstacles, or chasing the fastest times, there's always another challenge waiting. 144 levels. 432 stars. Countless ways to improve your time. Are you ready to roll?
