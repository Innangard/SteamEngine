About This Game Sultan's Game is a dark fantasy resource management RPG inspired by the extravagant beauty and brutal intrigue of the Arabian Nights. As a lowly minister serving an insane Sultan, you are trapped in a deadly game. Every week, a card is drawn: Carnality, Extravagance, Conquest, or Bloodshed. You have seven days to obey its demand — or face the executioner's axe. Time is your enemy. Allocate allies and manage resources before the sand runs out. Accumulate wealth, secrets, and influence to survive the court's deadly politics. Every hour wasted brings the headsman closer. Survival demands sacrifices. Will you slaughter innocents in the name of a Bloodshed Card? Will you betray your marital oaths for the Consort's favour? Watch in horror – or glee – as the consequences of your choices ripple through the story. With 50+ endings, determine your legacy: die a loyal sycophant, rise as a dragon slayer, or summon the apocalypse. Strategy, not luck, dictates your survival. Build a deck of allies, deadly tools, and dirty secrets. Deploy assets wisely to counter assassins and supernatural threats lurking in the palace shadows. The Sultan is not the only horror here. Ancient gods watch from beyond, promising power and salvation. Piece together the cosmic truth across multiple lifetimes. Claim powers no mortal should. Bring down the apocalypse.
