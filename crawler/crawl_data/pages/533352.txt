The game is clear without words — even a child can easily play it! Organize my Shop 2: Pet Shop is an ultra-cozy and meditative sorting puzzle designed for complete relaxation. Turn on your favorite podcast, music, or movie, turn off your mind, and immerse yourself in the satisfying process of bringing order ✨ In the second installment, a brand-new pet shop awaits you with unique levels and a huge variety of pet supplies! 🐱🐶 🐾 What's new in this part? In the second installment, a completely new pet store and the feeling of a full-fledged, deep sequel await you: 🛍️ Hundreds of pet supplies: From delicious pet food, canned goods, and treats to soft beds, collars, rodent houses, and even a full veterinary department! ✨ Zero monotony: 17 unique levels with carefully designed balance. Forget about endless repetitions — every level feels fresh, and completing the game takes from 3+ hours of pleasant meditation. 🌀 Chaos Mode included right away: Everyone's favorite mode is integrated directly into the main campaign without any paid DLCs! ⏱️ Grand finale with a timer: For those who love a challenge and achievements, an optional timer has been added to the final Chaos level (featuring 1302 items). 🛠️ Maximum comfort: The quick jump button now collapses empty spaces on the conveyor belt so you don't have to scroll through it needlessly. 💾 Smart autosaves: Progress and timer data are saved separately for each level. You can comfortably switch between levels or take breaks at any time. 💡 How to play? Take items from the conveyor belt and carefully place them on shelves and slots. Some items stack into neat piles, creating the perfect storage system. And if you want to extend the fun, any level can be replayed, as items on the belt always appear in a random order!
