From your capital city you lead your Kingdom against the invading Void Army that threatens all life. Your match-3, tower defense, and strategy skills will determine the fate of your armies, but the many political decisions you make as a leader affect the story and the culture of your kingdom. As your capital city improves, you will have access to more powerful spells, mightier elite troops, and better battlefield gear and armaments. Along the way you will also earn achievements, complete quests, and fill your Bestiary with enemies. 50 towers across 3 continents. Millions of story possibilities determined by your policy choices. Deep strategy elements. 4 cultural spectrums: Martial, Druidic, Elemental, Republic. 20 spells. 10 types of troops. 12 implements of war. ReignMaker: Saving the World One Tower at a Time.
