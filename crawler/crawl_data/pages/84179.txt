Download Anomalies - Dungeonomalies Free Download View Community Hub About This Content Anomalies - Dungeonomalies is a nod to the dungeon-synth genre.  It includes two main features: 1. New background art with various dungeon-like ancient ruins 2. New sounds from Erang's Free Dark Dungeon Synth Sample Pack (on Bandcamp) I tried out some of the sounds and they were really great, better then my own sound effects.  So I thought it would be nice to provide these as an option for Anomalies users.  All thanks to Erang for making them available to use! The photos of ancient ruins were from a recent trip I was fortunate enough to have gone on.  They include images from Jerrash, a Roman ruin new Amman, Jordan, as well as some images from Miletos, a Greek ruin in western Türkiye.
