It's almost as if... they cannot escape this room. || A visual novel about an endless therapy session - developed, directed, produced and written by a psychologist! || GAMEPLAY FEATURES: - 50k words of content , including many optional and hidden dialogue that only the keenest of players will unlock. - Choose how to respond to the patient's revelations and lies, what questions to ask, and when to keep quiet - Read the doctor's notes to delve into his line of thinking and his suspicions about his mysterious patient - Four total endings unlocked based on your choices , two being 'True Endings' available at the very end of the game - Nearly 50 achievements to unlock. - Point-and-click mode : Take a breather with Noel and interact with the room around you. - Affinity System : Unlock additional conversations and therapeutic interventions by increasing the doctor's bond with his patient. ART & SOUND FEATURES: All original art and sound was created by Brazilian creatives. Metahorror Therapy Session is proud to showcase and celebrate Brazilian talent. - Original Soundtrack by Guilherme Alexander - Highly expressive sprite art with various poses and in-engine animations by Hetsan - 3 CG illustrations by MirukiMary - Background Art by Felipe Daher - Unique interactive user interface, themed after a therapist's notebook, by Cim - A cinematic parallax camera that zooms in and out, rotates, and follows your mouse - Additional sounds, drones, and effects to further immerse you in the psychological metahorror Are you ready to unravel the truth behind Noel's claims?
