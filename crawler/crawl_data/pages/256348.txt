Listen to Circles EP: South of the Circle Edition Free To Play Play Soundtrack View Community Hub UPCOMING GAMES About This Content Step inside the circle of extraordinary music interpretations What happens when two composers meet and unleash their creativity? A sound feast of music! Dive into an extraordinary collaboration of Piotr Musiał - the creator of Frostpunk Soundtrack - and Ed Critchley, who has made the ethereal music to South of the Circle game. The Circles EP album contains two covers of each composer's music re-arranged by the other, thus blending the feel of the original mood with the new sensibility. This package includes: 2 tracks from Frostpunk OST in versions by Ed Critchley 2 tracks from South of the Circle OST in versions by Piotr Musiał 3 exclusive digital postcards (postcards differ between versions. Check Frostpunk version) All of them in popular formats: wav, flac and mp3.
