This final release brings a lot of new content, including the long awaited final two chapters that complete the story, as well as two brand‑new multiplayer modes supporting up to 8 players. You’ll also find new maps, mutators, and monstrous behaviours that will make each round more thrilling than the last. IT HAS YOUR FACE . Now it wants your life. Hide, seek, and try to kill your clone in this deceptively simple first-person horror roguelite. Your clone is hiding among the crowd, ready to kill you when you’re most vulnerable. Endure procedural paranoia as you search to find your doppelganger before they find you. Scavenge for weapons, manage limited ammo, and prepare carefully to strike. If your assassination fails, you’ll lose the opportunity to stalk from the shadows. As you progress, you’ll gain a greater understanding of your cursed fate and reveal terrifying new threats. Roguelite elements offer upgrades and improvements, giving you greater flexibility to endure increasingly complex challenges. In multiplayer, step into the chaos with up to eight players who all share the same face. Compete in teams or go solo as you try to figure out who’s who before it’s too late.
