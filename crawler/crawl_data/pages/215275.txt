Buy RuneScape: Dragonwilds - Deluxe Edition Upgrade $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content The Deluxe Edition Upgrade for RuneScape: Dragonwilds unearths a range of exclusive cosmetic and decorative items to enjoy as you carve out a foothold in this magical new frontier in the world of RuneScape. You'll unlock: Gilded Armour and Cape: Exclusive craftable armour to wear or display on your adventure. (Comparable to Level 4 armour that is crafted at the Armour Bench.) 6 Old School RuneScape-inspired helmets: these iconic low-poly helmets are craftable items that you can wear instead of the standard helmets at each material tier. 4 decorative Dragon head wall mounts to build and place around your home. 10 exclusive in-game paintings you can place to decorate your home inspired by the art and designs of Ashenfall. 2 animated animal decorations: Muncher (Death's Best Friend) as well as a Chinchompa, each dozing in a bed as an adorable companion waiting at home. The gear you unlock with the Digital Deluxe Edition is statistically equal to existing in-game gear, and can be upgraded using Ascension Cores earned through gameplay and the Nightmare Crucible in Umbral Sands. The OSRS Medium Helms you know and love from RuneScape are also side-grades, giving you the option to enjoy the aura gain, without a stat loss alongside it.
