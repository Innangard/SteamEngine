View Community Hub About This Game Rideshare "Stimulator" is an immersive driving simulation game that puts you behind the wheel as a rideshare business owner. Dodge traffic, follow the rules, and race the clock to drop off your quirky passengers, each with a story that's either captivating, hilarious, or just plain weird. Because you never know who you’re going to pick up next… Expand Your Rideshare Business: Your driving style and the condition of your vehicle directly influence how passengers react, affecting your overall rating, reviews, and tips. Have Stimulating Conversations: Each ride presents players with unique and unpredictable passenger stories, ensuring a diverse and engaging gameplay experience. Dodge Real-world Traffic & Pedestrians: The city comes to life with dynamic real-world traffic. Wait for traffic lights, yield to pedestrians, and navigate the intricacies of a well-regulated traffic system. Experience a Living, Breathing City: A visually stunning game world, crafted to offer immersive environments. Cruise through streets inspired by both European and American cities, each with its unique charm and beauty Master Intuitive Car Controls: The game provides intuitive and accessible car controls, designed to offer an enjoyable driving experience for all players. Maintain Your Vehicle: Between rides, keep the car clean, fix what needs fixing, and make sure it’s ready for whoever gets in next. AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: The main Campaign story, characters, and dialogue were written by a team of human writers and voiced in English by human actors. AI tools were used to localize text and voice overs. The optional Free Ride mode includes AI-generated passenger missions and localized dialogue. Free Ride mode is separate from the main Campaign and can be skipped by players who prefer to experience only human-written narrative content. Both game modes include radio stations featuring a mix of human-made music and AI-generated music. Stations containing AI-generated music are clearly labeled as such in the game.
