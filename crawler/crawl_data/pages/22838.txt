View Community Hub About This Game Hellforged is a loot-driven, extraction bullet-heaven game where you are in control of the chaos around you. Battle through hordes of bloodthirsty demons and loot epic gear with powerful modifiers and effects that offer exciting new ways to fight. Unlock new sources of power and become the master of chaos to break the Blackpyre once and for all. Every run is a tense loot heist. Explore the map to capture locations, unlock chests, take on challenging quests, or risk capturing rifts for endgame resources. In order to secure your hard-earned gear, you have to decide if you are willing to risk it all for even greater rewards or if you would rather recover and return to fight with new equipment next time. The choice is yours! Play as one of the legendary survivors of the shattered expedition. Choose from distinct character classes, each wielding wildly unique and devastating powers. ‎ ‎ ‎ Take your build to the next level by spending talent points in the Eternal Tree, specializing in paths that perfectly synergize with your combat style. Push your limits to conquer hundreds of achievements that are far more than mere cosmetics, as they also unlock new abilities, relics, and powers, alongside elusive secret achievements waiting to be uncovered. Control your fate by using the Arsenal to literally build your deck before every run, handpicking exactly which upgrades and abilities will drop for you during the expedition. ‎ Every run brings back resources to help you prepare for the next expedition. Rebuild the ruins of your camp and unlock new ways to improve your character. Enhance your equipment in various ways and craft a combat style that is uniquely yours. Discover overpowered combinations through impactful synergies that dynamically shift your strategy. Set an enemy ablaze and strike them with a lightning ability to trigger massive explosions that clear the screen when combined with the correct talisman. Channel your gathered chaos to summon devastating bosses that test the absolute limits of your arsenal, rewarding you with greatly boosted loot. Prove your supremacy to the world by climbing the online leaderboards, where you can fight for the top spot against your friends and the best players globally. Only the strongest will survive. Tread lightly, adapt quickly, and strike hard. ‎ ‎ Every month, a new Trial puts your build through a different sin-soaked gauntlet, from greed to wrath and beyond. Climb the live leaderboard and claim exclusive in-game titles if you make it to the top.
