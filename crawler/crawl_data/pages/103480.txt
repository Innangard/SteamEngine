Design your dream château from modular architectural pieces, decorate every detail inside and out, and bring the valley back to life as you harvest grapes and craft artisanal wine blends in your own countryside retreat. After enduring an earthquake, the once-bustling Vineyard Valley now rests in quiet disrepair. Restore its beauty, rebuild its homes, and bring warmth and life back to the countryside. Each home you bring back to life helps the valley bloom again, turning forgotten places into reflections of peace and care. Build from the ground up using intricate, customizable pieces. Create ivy-covered walls, elegant arches, and gabled rooftops that reflect your personal style. Whether you dream of a rustic cottage or a grand estate, every home in Cozy Builder tells its own story. Furnish interiors with art, cozy furniture, and handcrafted details that make your spaces feel alive. Step outside to design gardens, terraces, and greenhouses bursting with flowers and greenery. Every decoration adds warmth and charm to your vineyard retreat. With abundant sunshine across the valley, no home is complete without a greenhouse. Create elegant glass structures filled with light, color, and life, seamlessly connected to your home interiors. Collect rare plants, nurture flowers, and design lush indoor gardens that blur the line between inside and out. Your greenhouse is both a sanctuary and a living work of art. Bask in the sunlight as you walk through rolling vineyards surrounded by gentle hills and golden light. Harvest ripe grapes, olives, and other produce nurtured by the valley’s rich soil. Ferment, blend, and bottle your own artisanal wines! The valley is abundant with life. Gather heirloom produce, forage for herbs and wildflowers, and craft artisanal blends, preserves, and rustic goods to share with the townsfolk or sell to fund your next creation. Each harvest connects you more deeply to the valley you’ve helped revive. Step inside your château in immersive 3D and enjoy the peaceful life you’ve built. Explore scenic hills, unwind under the warm vineyard sun, and find joy in every detail of your creation. Cozy Builder is a relaxing, creative escape for players who love to design, decorate, and bring beauty to the world.
