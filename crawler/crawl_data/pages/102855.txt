A hundred years ago the valley of Gemhaven went dark. Its magical Heartstones, gems that turn into brick, timber, glass and gold at an Architect's touch, fell silent, and the city crumbled to dust. Swap a Prism with a Blast Stone for a chain reaction, or two Prisms together to wipe the whole board. Earn three stars. Finish with moves to spare. Leftover moves turn into bonus points.
