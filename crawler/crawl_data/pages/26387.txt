About This Content This Pack includes: M14/41 (47/40) tank (Rank 2 Italy); Re.2001 Gruppo 22 fighter (Rank 2 Italy); 120.000 Silver Lions; Premium Account for 7 days. M14/41 (47/40) The M14/41 was a modernization of the standard M13/40 and saw universal upgrades across the board, featuring improved armor, mobility, and firepower. Chiefly, it features an improved 160 hp diesel engine which enhances its maneuverability nicely. It’s also equipped with a reinforced driver's hatch and fitted with an improved 47 mm cannon with a fast reload rate. Unique to this variant, a 13.2 mm Breda machine gun is placed in the hull with 25mm of penetration at 100 meters, allowing you to take down lightly armored vehicles with ease. Re.2001 Gruppo 22 The Re.2001 is an upgraded variant of the Re.2000, complete with the more powerful Alfa Romeo R.A.1000 RC.41 engine. As a fighter, it boasts impressive attributes with a strong climb and an even stronger turn rate. The Re.2001 could be regarded as something of an Italian Spitfire. It’s equipped with two 12.7 mm and two 7.7 mm machine guns in the nose and wings respectively. The 12.7 mm guns feature powerful HEF rounds in certain belts, allowing you to tear aircraft out of the sky with a good burst. All premium vehicles allow you to earn increased Research Points and Silver Lions for each battle and comes furnished with all available modifications. With a Premium account (also purchasable in the game for Golden Eagles) you will earn more Research Points and Silver Lions during each battle for a set amount of days. This is cumulative with bonuses from premium vehicles! Note! Refund is not provided if you purchase 2 different packs which include the same vehicle or you already have the vehicle from this pack!
