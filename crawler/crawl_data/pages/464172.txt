Content For This Game Browse all (1) Bogos Binted? - Fashion Pack $0.99 $0.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to a sinful night with the galaxy’s craziest aliens! Not every alien visits Earth to witness the indomitable human spirit. Some just want to chill on the Moon's surface and play games. But of course, these aren’t normal games. Alien humor is... how should we put it... a bit off. In this first-person, four-player, online multiplayer space adventure, every alien at the table must do whatever it takes to win in a variety of tabletop games. You’ll craft sneaky strategies, tell outrageous lies, and blow up the heads of so-called “friends.” Four Game Modes in Early Access! Each player has a deck of number and special cards. Players take turns adding to a shared total without going over the target. Special cards can be used to trick opponents. If a player goes over, their head inflates. If they survive, they stay. If not, they're out. Each round, a card is selected for the table, and players must take turns placing matching cards face-down. Lying is allowed. If someone suspects a bluff, they can call “beeble meep.” If it’s a bluff, the liar inflates their head. Could survive, could explode. If not a bluff, the accuser suffers instead. After any inflation or explosion, a new round begins. Watch for the Deadeye card, it may let the accused shoot another player. Might hit, might backfire. Each player starts with 3 cards. Play a card equal to or higher than the one on the table. Same-number cards can be played together. Draw a new card for each one you play. Always keep 3 in hand. Don’t have any cards to play? Your head inflates. Special cards can change everything. Use them smart or chaotic. Play smart. Keep your skull safe. At the start of each round, one player becomes the VORP. A topic and secret word are chosen. Everyone sees the word except the VORP. Players write clues. The VORP fakes a clue and tries to guess the word. Then, players discuss and vote on who they think the VORP is. If the real VORP is caught, they must guess the word. Fail, and they lose a life. Lose all lives, and you're off to meet your alien god. See You In The Game! Take your seat in Bogos Binted? right now and dive into chaotic fun with the galaxy’s most disposable, big-eyed companions. Just remember: no one here tells you to “play fair.” They’re aliens. They don’t even speak English.
