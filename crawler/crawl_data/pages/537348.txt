View Community Hub About This Game Humanity has angered the gods by ravaging the Earth. Now, they are punished with a colossal arena of death, where all species must fight to survive. Faced with ferocious creatures, deadly traps, and a harsh environment, can humanity redeem their mistakes and prove their worth before the cold judgment of the gods? Gameplay Become a legendary warrior : Choose your preferred character class, each with unique skills and combat styles. Face countless monsters: Battle against swarms of terrifying creatures in fierce encounters. Make wise choices: Every decision you make impacts your strength and chances of survival. Choose your weapons, upgrade your skills, and make strategic choices. Fight to survive: Only the most intelligent and courageous warriors can overcome this trial. Prove yourself and secure humanity's survival. Features Automated weapons and skills A lot of playable characters Stat strengthening system when leveled up Face off countless of enemies from many directions Get more item from equipment shop by killing enemies Death is not the end; the power stones will help you become even stronger. Flexible playstyle, with no limitations of weapon and skill combinations System Requirements Minimum: OS *: Windows 7 or newer Processor: 2.5Ghz or better Memory: 3 GB RAM Graphics: Nvidia GeForce GTX 750 Storage: 2 GB available space Recommended: OS *: Windows 7 or newer Processor: 2.5Ghz or better Memory: 3 GB RAM Graphics: Nvidia GeForce GTX 950 * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
