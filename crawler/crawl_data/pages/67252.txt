Demoverze Hrát Ball Busters Demo Nainstalovat demo Prohlédněte si plnou verzi tohoto produktu Zobrazit komunitní centrum Informace o demoverzi Ball Busters is an action roguelike all about busting balls using combos, special moves, and game-breaking synergies . Wanna play as a billionaire with a literal suitcase of weapons ? How about a former assassin turned martial artist influencer ? Maybe a water goddess turned maid is more of your fancy? Either way, these characters and more bring their own unique abilities and playstyles to your run. A city with cars that don't care you're on the road . A jungle with chimpanzees that throw poop at you . A desert with sandstorms that temporally blind you . All stages want one thing from you, for you to drop the ball. Want to hit the ball with a random lightning strike ? Summon a giant ghost fist from the ground? Or you just want something simple to make the big numbers go up? No matter what, get to roll for power ups and upgrades at the end of every round. Can you build a run winning kit from what you got? Can you time your attacks to avoid getting parried ? How about making sure your attacks stay in the ring ? What about avoid getting frozen by a giant snowball ? Think carefully, because these specialized rounds will push your reflexes, skills, and your build! Feeling lucky and want a D20 that buffs or screw you over ? Maybe a beige ball that judges how stylish your combos are ? Or a ball dressed like a certain laser-eyed psychopath ? No matter what, every ball you unlock adds something new to your run. 6 playable characters , each with unique mechanics. 6 stages filled with dynamic hazards. 50+ upgrades and special moves with stacking synergies. 10+ balls that changes your runs. High-skill juggling mechanics with spectacle-fighter combo depth. Roguelike build crafting and score-chasing replayability.
