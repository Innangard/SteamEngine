Composed by Pau Damià Riera, the NETBREAK Original Soundtrack feel like late-night terminals, CCTV snow, and the quiet space before a crime goes wrong. He delivered a lean, moody score built for headphones and dim rooms. What it sounds like. Slow, heartbeat pulse, that keeps the tension moving without getting in the way. Deep sub-bass and low, rounded synths that sit under everything instead of shouting over it. Detuned, misty pads and drones for that “neon rain on glass” vibe. Clicks, ticks, and tiny glitch hits, like keys, relays, or a distant hard drive, used as percussion instead of big drums. Sparse minor-key motifs on a thin, glassy lead, simple figures that feel more like codes being entered than melodies being “sung.” Gentle filter sweeps and sidechain “breathing” that make the whole thing inhale/exhale while you play.
