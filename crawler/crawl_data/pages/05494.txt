ΕΚΔΟΣΕΙΣ ΤΟΥ We Were Here Tomorrow Σύγκριση εκδόσεων Περιεχόμενο παιχνιδιού Περιήγηση σε όλα (2) Προσεχώς We Were Here Tomorrow Soundtrack N/A We Were Here Tomorrow: Fan Edition N/A Προβολή κέντρου Κοινότητας Ειδική έκδοση Σχετικά με αυτό το παιχνίδι On an expedition to save your fellow Explorers, you and your partner become the victims of bad timing, and are trapped beneath the ice of Antarctica. Once awakened, the world around the two of you is no longer the one you remember. With little recollection of what happened, you find yourselves inside a facility of Norček Industries, where the charismatic Director Kathleen Bowe is waiting for you. Her team was on the verge of a breakthrough, but they have mysteriously disappeared, leaving their work unfinished. With a global crisis looming, the Director turns to you and your partner to continue where they left off. To complete their work, you must take on a series of complex tasks throughout the facility. Often separated, you will rely solely on your walkie-talkies to communicate. Describe what you see, share what you discover, and combine your perspectives to solve the puzzles that stand in your way. But be cautious; the deeper you venture into the Norček facility, the more questions begin to surface. What is Norček trying to achieve? Why were you brought here? And what happened to those who came before you? Only together will you unravel the mysteries surrounding Norček Industries and discover the truth. Only together will you decide the fate of Tomorrow. Key Features A shared adventure: When fate takes an unexpected turn, two Explorers find themselves trapped inside a mysterious facility. With only each other to rely on, they must uncover its secrets and decide what Tomorrow will hold. Asymmetrical co-op puzzling: No two Explorers experience the facility the same way. Trust your partner, communicate through your walkie-talkies, and combine your unique perspectives to solve puzzles that challenge the rules of time, gravity, and space. Two players, two distinct roles: You and your partner each have a different role to play, with your own high-tech tool at your disposal. Work together and use your equipment in creative ways to overcome obstacles that neither of you could face alone. A mystery in an imagined 80s future: Explore Norček Industries and uncover the secrets behind its experiments and missing research team. Journey through a retro-futuristic world brought to life by striking environments and a nostalgic synthwave soundtrack. The We Were Here Series Every game in the We Were Here Series is a standalone online co-op adventure – the stories are connected but you don’t need to play them in order. Playing with your friend and communicating well is key to the escape room series: asymmetrical puzzles give players different perspectives and can only be solved together. NOTE: This game is co-op only. It requires both players to have a working PC-compatible microphone and an internet connection.
