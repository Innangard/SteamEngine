A new episode of the Tales Beyond The Tomb series — colder, more isolated, and more disturbing than ever. Inspired by real events that were never fully explained, this chapter takes the player to a remote mountain range haunted by wind, ice, and secrets buried beneath the snow. With a suffocating atmosphere, immersive audio design, and moments of pure psychological horror, this is a journey back to a place that was never meant to be rediscovered. Important: This is a slow-paced walking simulator with a narrative focus. White Silence is about atmosphere, immersion, and discovery at your own pace. It’s for players who enjoy exploring, observing, and piecing the story together through the environment. Episode 4: White Silence Years ago, a group of young explorers vanished during an expedition into a remote and frozen mountain region. Weeks later, their bodies were found scattered across the terrain — barefoot, wounded, and frozen in expressions of sheer terror. The case was quickly closed. No answers. No survivors. Now, you play as a determined investigator, returning to the scene in search of the truth. Accompanied only by a local guide, you must retrace the steps of the doomed expedition, confronting the freezing cold, growing isolation, and the eerie feeling that something — or someone — still lingers in those mountains. Every step brings you closer to the truth. But some truths should never be uncovered. Main Features VHS Aesthetic – Go back in time with visuals inspired by classic horror films from the 80s and 90s. Photorealistic Graphics and Immersive Environments - Feel the terror with realistic visuals and terrifying environments. Exploration - Discover tapes, documents, and remnants of the original expedition. End - An ambiguous, disturbing ending — just like the case it's inspired by. Dialogue Text System – Immerse yourself in the story with dynamic, narrative dialogue. Cold and Insanity - Sometimes the freezing winds of the mountains can cause some disturbances and hallucinations. Gameplay Basically an immersive first-person walking simulator with an engaging story. Investigate and explore a cold and inhospitable region... Take care of your sanity and be careful not to freeze to death.
