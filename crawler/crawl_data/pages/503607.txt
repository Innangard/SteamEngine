Explore, admire and progress throughout surreal landscapes and labyrinth structures that should not exist. Navigate through endless labyrinthine complexes and vast spaces filled with eerie waters and surreal yet familiar architecture. No entities, just pure liminality, nostalgia and the fear of being lost. Rewind feature. Since the perspective is from found footage, you can rewind at any moment for as much as possible, as long as the tape is not empty. Swimming mechanic. You can jump, swim and dive freely in any deep water pool. This mechanic also features an easy pool exit, meaning you can pull yourself out without the need for a ladder. VHS mode is supported without any unnecessary effects. Additional post-process effects can be configured separately. Immersive and realistic experience with minimal user interface. Dynamic player movement system with immersive camera.
