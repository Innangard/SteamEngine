Each biome - from burning deserts to haunted swamps and frozen peaks - presents unique threats and rare opportunities. Sub-biomes, hidden routes, and unpredictable enemies keep every run fresh. Will you outlast the wild? BRING YOUR BASE CAMP WITH YOU You don’t find safety - you build it. Grow and customize your mobile Base Camp to follow you through the frontier. Upgrade workbenches, secure resources, and expand your shelter as you push deeper into the wild. In a world that never stops hunting you, your camp is your only constant. MASTER THE ART OF GUN FIGHT Face ambushes, predators, and bounty hunters in high-stakes ranged combat. Use rifles, throwables, and quick instincts to survive escalating firefights. As your bounty rises, enemies grow tougher and more aggressive. Will you thrive under pressure - or fall to the hunt? HANDCRAFT YOUR TOOLKIT Stay alive by scavenging, crafting, and planning ahead. Manage hunger, thirst, temperature, and health as you move through deadly terrain. Every resource counts. Every mistake costs. Adapt, improvise - or die. Are you prepared to survive?
