View Community Hub About This Game CozyMining is a cozy idle game that lives right on your desktop — a tiny mining world you can glance at anytime, between work, study, or just kicking back. Point your miner somewhere. They'll dig there. You'll feel great about it. ⛏️ Simple to Start. Satisfying to Master. Click to send your miner to a destination. They'll walk over, start digging, and automatically collect everything along the way — ores, treasure chests, the works. Come back when you're ready, open a pile of chests all at once, and feel that rush. No punishing fail states. No constant clicking. Just the cozy rhythm of a pickaxe hitting rock and a little buddy doing their best. 🪨 Dig Deep. Get Strong. Go Further. The underground stretches 1,000 floors deep, with 20 unique minerals waiting to be discovered — from humble Copper near the surface to legendary Orichalcum in the deepest dark. Forge your pickaxes from the ores you collect Reforge pickaxes to roll for perfect buffs Equip accessories across 6 stat categories — mining power, move speed, dig speed, crit rate, luck, and max HP Unlock permanent upgrades that carry into every new run, so you're always growing 🐾 Build Your Little World Unlock five adorable animal miners: Mole, Dog, Cat, Hamster, and Rabbit. The ones you're not actively mining with? They roam freely on the surface — wandering, resting, celebrating. The more friends you unlock, the more alive your world feels. ✨ Made to Live on Your Desktop CozyMining is designed as a true desktop companion: Transparent background & adjustable opacity Always-on-top display mode Fits naturally over any wallpaper — it's yours to decorate Quietly digging. Quietly growing.
