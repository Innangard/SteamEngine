Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Rogue Belt is a top-down space roguelite you can play solo or in online co-op. Fly into a storm-wracked asteroid belt, break asteroids for valuable Fulgurite, and build a ship that can survive the fight out. Ten minutes to build. One boss to escape. Harvest the belt while rival ships hunt you and asteroid storms close in. Choose an upgrade every minute, then face the boss guarding your escape after ten minutes. Read its attacks, find your opening, and break through with your haul. Spend your earnings on permanent upgrades and take newly unlocked ships and weapons into your next run. Your ship. Your weapon. Your rules. Every ship can equip every weapon. Cut through the belt with an energy beam, send homing missiles after enemies, or fill the field with pulse fire. Your ship changes how you fly, how much punishment you can take, and how long you can sustain your weapons. Experiment with different pairings and find the combination that fits the way you fight. Build around every choice. Choose your ship, primary weapon, and boss mission before launch. During the run, pick upgrades that strengthen your weapons, defenses, movement, and utility. Commit to heavier firepower, give yourself room for mistakes, or change direction when an unexpected upgrade offers something better. Weapon evolutions add new ways to fight as your build takes shape. Brave the belt together. Fly solo or bring a friend in online co-op. Take different loadouts into the same fight, combine your firepower, and face the belt’s dangers together. Play the free demo Try two ships, three weapons, and two boss missions, solo or in online co-op. Earn permanent upgrades and unlock the Veteran challenge.
