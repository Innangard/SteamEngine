Welcome to the Squeakross: Home Squeak Home program! You just got into a fresh new home for your tiny rodent friend, but… it’s empty! Solve puzzles from the Home Squeak Home Catalog and transform them into splendid furniture and accessories to dress it up! Relaxing Puzzle Solving : Learn how to play in 5 minutes, and solve logic-numbers puzzles at your rhythm , no time pressure. Scared of puzzles? Worry not! Your rodent friend is here to help. Customize your game experience: Toggle hints , Solve a random cell, or use the brand new Logic-Assist system that lets you preview possible solutions! No assistance needed? You can disable all those options if you prefer to be challenged. Our Home Squeak Home catalog features many different styles of interior decor, from basic housing supplies to extravagant ones to match a broad range of tastes. You will also find many accessories to dress your rodent friend. Aaah… The joy of seeing a Squeakross puzzle transform into a beautiful object is so satisfying! Shape your rodent to your liking in the world's first Rodent Editor (we think??). From snout to tail, decide what kind of friend you want to have by your side! Made for rodent lovers by rodent lovers, we made sure to offer plenty of options for you to be able to recreate your beloved pets in game. FEATURES: A Nonogram game like no other. 800+ puzzles to solve! Relaxing puzzle-solving, with no time limit, mindful of new players. Logic-Assist system that lets you preview possible solutions. Decorate your home with hundreds of unique furniture. Create and customize your own rodent with accessories. Customize your user interface with unlockable stickers made by Eon. Receive tips and lore from Nini, the owner of Home Squeak Home Inc. "Streamer mode" to turn off Content ID tagged music Note: NO generative AI was used during the making of the game. We have a firm no-AI stance for ALL of our games, past, present and future.
