Avoid obstacles and traps until you reach the portal that takes you to the next level. Find the ideal launching point and perfect path to eliminate all musical blocks in one single throw. Use your physical intuition to find the best release and bounce angles (all those days playing pool were not in vain). Avoid obstacles and traps that prevent you from performing the perfect path.
