A game like this relies heavily on fine-tuning, balancing, and discovering what makes the gameplay loop the most satisfying. By opening up the game early, we can gather direct feedback, experiment with mechanics, and ensure we are steering the development in the exact direction the community wants to experience.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently plan for Stonewards to remain in Early Access for approximately 9 to 12 months. We aim to introduce new maps and environments, expand on the ressources,treasures, secrets, and rewards found while digging, and add more diverse enemies, challenging boss fights, additional playable characters, and more intricate upgrade systems. We also plan to deepen the game's lore, expand the central hub, and add more cosmetics for character customization. Throughout the Early Access period, we plan to continuously refine the progression and balance based on player data.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version already features a rock-solid core gameplay loop that has been shaped by continuous playtesting and our participation in the June 2026 Steam Next Fest. Grab your pickaxe. Tighten your beard. Try not to blow up the kingdom. Stonewards is a solo and 1-4 player co-op first-person roguelite defense game where a band of battle-ready dwarves leaves their base camp to answer distress calls from nearby dwarven kingdoms. Each mission sends you to a threatened stronghold on the edge of disaster. Your job is simple: dig fast, forge smart, and defend like your beard depends on it. Monsters are coming. The walls are cracking. The horn is about to sound. DIG After each wave, rush into procedurally generated mines to gather precious metals, rare materials, and whatever shiny rocks look important enough to steal. Explore twisting tunnels, uncover hidden caves, and haul your loot back before the next attack begins. Take too long, and your friends will be defending the kingdom one dwarf short. And yes, they will complain about it. FORGE A dwarf without a forge is just an angry short man with excellent facial hair. Between waves, spend your mined resources to craft arrows, repair defenses, and unlock randomized upgrades. Stack raw strength, strange powers, tougher walls, or trust your team’s latest “brilliant” plan. Nothing could possibly go wrong. DEFEND The monsters have one goal: breach the stronghold and bring the kingdom down. Hold the walls, cover the gates, coordinate with your crew, and use every crafted tool at your disposal. When the enemy pushes through your outer defenses, fall back, regroup, and remind them why attacking a dwarven stronghold was a terrible idea. If the kingdom falls, the mission is over. If you survive, drink later. Dig now. FEATURES 1-4 Player Co-op & Solo - Rally the crew or go full lone-dwarf. Whether you fight alone or with up to three friends, the monsters are coming either way. Answer the Call of the Dwarven Kingdoms - Travel from your base camp to defend nearby dwarven realms against incoming monster attacks. Each mission is a desperate stand for survival. Mine the Mountain - When the wave ends, the clock starts. Dive into procedural mines, gather valuable resources, and get back before the next horn sounds. Stockpile & Forge - Bring your loot back to craft arrows, repair walls, and unlock randomized roguelite upgrades that shape each run. Hold the Line - Take your place on the walls, defend the stronghold, and stop the horde before they overwhelm the kingdom. Dig Deep. Forge Hard. Defend Harder.
