View Community Hub About This Game Drive. Harvest. Upgrade. REPEAT! Start the game with a vehicle equipped with only a massive saw blade. Drive across planets, harvest resources, and upgrade your vehicle and tools. Every run is a step forward — more tools, better vehicle, bonuses, unexpected upgrades, and of course, bigger numbers. Deep Upgrade System Spend your hard-earned resources on an upgrade tree. Improve your vehicle, unlock drones, upgrade tools (lasers, rockets, cannons, lightning, and much more), and discover powerful synergies that make each run more satisfying than the last. Explore Unusual Worlds Travel between vibrant, beautiful planets — each with its own look, soundtrack, and resources to harvest. From a Candy planet dripping with sweets to a harsh Cyberpunk desert! Upgrade Your Vehicle Customize your crawler and upgrade its speed, power, capacity, nitro and more. Watch it evolve from a humble harvester into an unstoppable resource-collecting machine.
