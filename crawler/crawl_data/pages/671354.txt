The game is designed in a zombie apocalypse style, but with a touch of humor. Due to contamination, an epidemic has broken out – zombies have taken over the entire military base and are wreaking havoc. You are the last survivor, once a member of an elite cat sniper unit. The cat army has dropped ammunition supplies – these can be found in wooden crates. Your mission is to eliminate all the zombies and rescue the cats. Go, cat soldier! Meow!
