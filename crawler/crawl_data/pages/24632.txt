Equip accessories, cast devastating spells, and defend your hive in this chaotic and personality-packed roguelike tower defense. In each run, explore maps filled with clever references, traps, and unique mechanics. Face unpredictable bosses and unlock magical cards of accessories and spells that can completely turn the tide of battle. Manage your resources, build strategic defenses, and experiment with millions of combinations to survive the endless Zombee swarms.
