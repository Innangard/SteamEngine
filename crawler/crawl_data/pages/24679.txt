Content For This Game Browse all (1) Kitty's Last Adventure - Supporter Pack $1.99 $1.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Play as Kitty or her friends and survive the wave of cat nightmares in this roguelite survivor. The cat automatically throws its toys at enemies; all you have to do is control its path! As you progress, you'll unlock an array of hilarious yet powerful cat-based weaponry that evolves with you. Each weapon starts as a playful toy and transforms into more impressive and dangerous forms. Each weapon you acquire is associated with one or more families, each offering distinct bonuses that amplify with every point in it. Will you be a playful cat dropping cute ball, or a nasty one throwing poop at your enemies? Control one of the different cats, each with its characteristics and starting weapon! After each run, you can purchase permanent upgrades or new cats to play with! The entire world unfolds within a book created by Kitty's grieving human so she can experience one last adventure. This book contains multiple worlds to explore, each representing a stage of grief. I am Woum, a one-man army that did everything except the game's sound design!
