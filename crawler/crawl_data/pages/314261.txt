(can disable it) In Link Cameras , you begin your shift as a moderator for a mysterious camera website, presented through the interface of a late 90s / early 2000s computer. At first, the job seems simple: review cameras submitted by users, decide whether they should be approved or rejected, and follow the instructions sent by the site administrator. But each day brings new rules, new cameras, and new actions to perform. Some feeds look normal. Others should not be online. The entire game takes place from your computer screen. You will inspect camera feeds, read messages, follow moderation rules, make decisions, and progress day by day as the website becomes increasingly disturbing. Gameplay Features Monitor live camera feeds from different users. Approve or reject cameras based on the site’s rules. Receive instructions from the administrator. Discover new mechanics and events each day. Play entirely through a retro computer interface. Psychological horror focused on observation, moderation, and decision-making. Includes jumpscares, which can be disabled in the settings. Inspired by old internet aesthetics, webcams, and moderation systems.
