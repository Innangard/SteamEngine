to Cart Add to Account View Community Hub About This Game Kingdom Rules is a cozy strategy game about rebuilding a fragile medieval realm after years of decline. Step by step, you will restore prosperity, repopulate empty lands, and prepare your garrison for the dangers ahead. Build farms to feed your people, raise houses to grow the population, and construct barracks to defend the borders. Manage taxes and expenses, watch your food reserves, and keep development, safety, and public stability in balance. Every turn changes the fate of your kingdom. Peaceful days bring growth and opportunity, while random events may reward you with rich harvests and new settlers or strike back with fires, disease, and raids. Your choices decide whether the realm survives and rises again.
