Infiltrate megacorp servers, reroute connections, collect valuable data, and outmaneuver adaptive defenses, all while racing against the clock and the relentless threat of reactive sentinels. KEY FEATURES Real-time tactical puzzles – Every move matters as adaptive defense systems react dynamically to your actions. 20-second infiltration window – Plan carefully and execute quickly before the trace monitor locks you out. Reversible routes – Your movement leaves behind a visible trace; retrace your steps to erase it and reduce your exposure. Stack memory system – Store, reorder, and rewrite data nodes to alter the environment and exploit the system. Adaptive defenses – Sentinels, gates and firewalls respond to your behavior, turning obstacles into potential tools if used cleverly. Emergent gameplay – Discover loopholes, bend the rules, and transform threats into opportunities through creative play. Challenging progression – Over 125 handcrafted stages across four powerful megacorporations, each with unique security systems. Ghost runs – Complete servers without detection to record your best times and prove mastery of the system. Cyberpunk aesthetic – A minimalist, high-contrast style inspired by classic terminals and retro hacking culture.
