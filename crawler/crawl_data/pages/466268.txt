Yeah, sign me up.” Kotaku “I'm a fan of Akatori's look.” PC Gamer About This Game Welcome to the amazing world of Akatori — an action platformer with metroidvania elements. Discover the story of Mako, a guide to the amber worlds who grew up in the Akatori Monastery. Embark on a journey to save these worlds from amber storms that destroy and distort all living things in their path. A mysterious companion — a red bird — will join you on your adventure. Its past and motives may be unclear, but it will be indispensable throughout your journey. The bird can transform into a staff: it can become a weapon that crushes enemies, or a companion that helps you explore every corner of the world. Key features Unlock new staff abilities to explore the world. Glide through the air, slide down steep slopes, climb walls, discover hidden places — the staff can do it all. In the hands of the battle monk Mako, the staff becomes a powerful weapon against a variety of amber-infected monsters. Explore picturesque amber worlds Your challenging journey will take you through several worlds infected by amber, yet still preserving their unique appearance and history. Against the backdrop of colorful landscapes, floating structures, and dark catacombs, you will face dangerous trials and uncover a bitter truth. Amber storms Since amber storms swept across the worlds, all living things have lost their original form. However, priceless amber crystals also appeared. Amber is both the foundation of civilization’s prosperity and its curse. Will you be able to uncover ancient secrets and save the worlds? Enjoy the scenery The beauty of the amber worlds is breathtaking. They were created with great attention to detail, combining classic 2D pixel art with 3D models. Take a moment during this dangerous adventure to admire the beautiful landscapes and capture a few screenshots. Fight amber-infected monsters and bosses Prepare yourself: you will face more than twenty enemies and several unique bosses. Enjoy the sound The soundtrack of Akatori is beautiful and diverse, helping you immerse yourself in the atmosphere of the events unfolding throughout the game. Join Mako’s captivating story Take on the role of a young battle monk and, together with her mysterious companion, experience the twists and turns of a story about inner strength, trials of fate, and a great mystery.
