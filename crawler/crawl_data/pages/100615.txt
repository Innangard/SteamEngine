The story centers on Linh, a fragile schoolgirl who nonetheless possesses a strong sense of justice. Despite her moral backbone, she is relentlessly tormented by a group of bullies who subject her to humiliations such as public shaming and being locked in a classroom overnight. At her breaking point, Linh encounters a demon that offers her unconditional power. Can a girl fueled by hellish power ever be a true hero, and what "unconditional" debt will eventually come due? Key Features Experience the story through a Visual Novel–style narrative. Explore atmospheric dungeons, scavenging for items, equipment. Fight grotesque monsters and urban horrors using classic turn-based combat. Answer quirky, humorous quizzes that analyze your personality.
