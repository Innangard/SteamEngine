Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early Access is a fundamental step in the development of OUTBRK. Putting the game in the hands of passionate players to gather early feedback is an invaluable step of game creation. The additional funds will help us achieve the true vision we have for the game, push the quality of the game, add all the features we want to add, and improve performances so that players with lower-end PCs can also chase storms.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “It’s very hard to estimate at this point, we don’t want to rush too fast to 1.0, nor want to stay in early access unnecessarily long. OUTBRK is still very early in its production cycle, and we want to add a lot more features, cars, car accessories, polish and bug fixes. Our primary focus is to keep our game scope in control for 1.0, deliver new content that players want, and improve stability and performance.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version of OUTBRK will have a lot more online and coop functionalities, lot more customization options for cars and characters, more storm scenarios variety and obviously better optimization and debugging.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The Early Access version of OUTBRK currently represents what we consider the “minimum viable product” of the game. The complete game loop can be played from start to finish, online, on dedicated servers, using our innovating scientifically accurate storm scenario system, and players can purchase and customize their cars and characters. We integrated basic settings such as graphical, sound and language settings, and more advanced accessibility settings such as controller and steering wheel support and keybind remapping.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We’re not currently planning any price increase for OUTBRK. We’ve been transparent about our eventual plans to add cosmetic microtransactions in the future to help alleviate our dedicated server’s infrastructure costs, but our short-term plans are very much focused on increasing the main-game content offer before exploring this.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Our community has been involved in the development process we launched our Kickstarter back in February 2020 and we’re not planning to stop! Experience the wonder of nature and compete against other chasers as you put yourself into random scenarios based on real life weather events. Prepare to have too close for comfort encounters with tornadoes, fight your way through torrential downpours, while avoiding hazards of extreme weather. Take the role of a professional storm chaser and make use of your risk management skills to gather valuable data from severe weather. Become the chaser you’ve always wanted to be by buying new vehicles, upgrading them, and customizing them. OUTBRK features a 625 square kilometers map with various landmarks such as rivers, canyons, plains, towns and forests. So tighten your seat belt, and get ready for OUTBRK: The Multiplayer Storm Chasing Experience.
