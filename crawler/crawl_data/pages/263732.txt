View Community Hub About This Game Missi is a cozy point & click game in a watercolor hand-drawn world. Find hidden objects and solve puzzles using clues in sketches of past adventures. Try to trick the Mushroom Spider and the Crow King by collecting all medicinal herbs for the sick little mouse. Join the brave little mouse Missi on an enchanting adventure, where she encounters magical creatures and solves a wide variety of unique puzzles. Bring a dragon statue to life, brew potions with a swamp witch, and immerse yourself in other fairy-tale adventures in this atmospheric 2D point & click game. Friendly inhabitants of the colorful world are always ready to assist you in finding items or solving puzzles. Missi offers a wide array of puzzles, some of which may be quite challenging. Be sure to consult the sketchbook, where episodes of your past adventures are recorded, and thoroughly explore every corner of the magical world for clues. The characters and the world of Missi come to life thanks to frame-by-frame animation, completely hand-drawn. We created over two thousand frames to capture the warmth of a living illustration and immerse players in the atmosphere of a magical journey. This makes the game feel like the pages of a children’s book unfolding right on your screen. The game features cute illustrations in a cozy watercolor style. There's no need for competition or combat here. Simply enjoy a relaxing experience as you uncover the secrets of the mouse's burrow, help a frog astronomer, or save a thirsty mandrake on Fish Island. The game contains minimal text, with all dialogues conveyed through adorable drawings.
