Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game There are things beyond our control. Changes that cannot be resisted. Sometimes, all we can do is leave, or stay and watch everything we loved disappear. In this world, leviathans, colossal creatures of Lovecraftian scale, have long since become part of everyday life. They cannot be stopped. Humanity can only track their movements and get out of their way in time. But for some, there is nowhere left to go and no reason to leave. Explore a small village from a first-person perspective, talk to its residents, and carry out simple everyday tasks. Feed the cow. Visit your neighbor. Listen to the news. Remember the past. Wait. Leviathan: Rhino is an existential horror game about inevitability, attachment, loneliness, guilt, and the fragile hope that even at the very end, there may still be light.
