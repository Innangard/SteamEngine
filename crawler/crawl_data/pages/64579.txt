All of these weave together in The Music of Car Park Capital . The game itself is a sort of mise en abyme , a postmodern take from our 2020s vantage point on the rapid urbanization of the 1950s and 1960s through the fun-house lens of the 1990s tycoon sim: a dream within a dream. The soundtrack follows suit, its pseudo-corporatized sheen of cartoonish realism driving through each track with one foot, uh, tire in the musical styles of the midcentury, the other in the musical hardware of the millennium…and the third in the musical production standards of the modern day.
