Desperate to preserve it forever, he attempted an ancient spell. The cellar survived... but the spell turned him into a chicken and trapped him inside a small cage. Now the cellar has fallen into ruin. Restore every rack, break the curse, and help the forgotten keeper see the sunlight once again. 【Restore Order, One Rack at a Time】 The cellar starts in complete chaos. As you organize each rack, the room gradually transforms into a clean, beautiful collection. Watching disorder disappear is its own reward. 【Learn Skills to Organize Faster】 Unlock powerful abilities that improve carrying, searching, and movement.
