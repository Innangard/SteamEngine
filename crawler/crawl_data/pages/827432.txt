New crops, animals, production chains and more than 25 new machines await. Catuaí: Welcome to South America! Explore a lush, humid valley with narrow roads, dusty streets, scattered rocks and a winding river running through the landscape. A small rural town with colorful buildings, shops and agricultural depots connects the region, while stilt houses line the river shore. New Gameplay: Coffee, Bananas & Animals Cultivate coffee and bananas and expand your livestock with alpacas and Brahman cows. Bananas introduce an all-new cultivation system to design your plantation layouts, and connect transport rails to washing stations. Alpacas add new hands-on interaction through shearing, while wild animals like crocodiles and parrots bring more life to the environment. Productions, Projects & Collectibles Create new products such as pigment and resin, and repaint houses for the community, or start production chains for crop processing. Take on the Research Center construction project, find dinosaur skeleton parts and collect your discoveries in the collectible museum. Machines, Brands & Details Operate 25+ new machines from Case IH, Ford, John Deere, KUHN, Mercedes-Benz Trucks, New Holland, Valtra, and many more. Colombo Industries and Southern Spreaders join the series as new brands, while enhanced ground tool animations and improved effects increase immersion.
