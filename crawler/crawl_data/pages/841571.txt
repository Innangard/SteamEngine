Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We could keep this game behind closed doors for another year chasing "finished" with every feature and all content complete. But for a small team like ours, we think it's more productive to get the game into players' hands early. The core is there—what we're missing is the player feedback that will help us shape it into the best version of itself. Early Access makes that possible. ” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We don’t intend to stay in Early Access for long, but we also know that shaping the game we and our community want takes time. We’ll keep up a steady pace of new content and features as we work toward a full release, which we currently estimate will come about 18 months after the Early Access launch.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “In the full game, the core progression loop stays the same—just bigger. We're working on more upgrades: more shops to expand into, new equipment, and achievements, along with more ways to reflect your in-game actions in fun, surprising ways. And of course, we'll keep balancing and adding content like decorations and customizations along the way.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Right now, most of the core mechanics and progression are in place. This includes cooking and cleaning, upgrading your kitchen equipment, exploring the area around your shop, discovering and unlocking new recipes, decorating your ramen shop, unlocking new decorations, buying new decorations from different shops in the game world, and completing in-game achievements and daily quests. We’re continually adding content and tuning the experience based on community feedback. Whether you’re here for a cozy, relaxing session or a deeper grind, we aim to offer something for both.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “As a thank-you to our community for helping us develop Ramen Shop Simulator into the best game it can be, it'll be sold at a discount throughout Early Access.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We want to involve the community in as many aspects of development as possible, whether that’s game balance, suggestions for new decorations and customizations, or even ideas for new ramen shop locations and themes. Your feedback will be essential as we continue to perfect our recipe for the full game! Japan is beautiful this time of year Create life again in a worn out, forgotten alley. Explore your surroundings and wander through cozy, lantern-lit backstreets and find inspiration for the task ahead. Maybe visit your local convini and look for exiting new ingredients or that unique decoration. You never know where an old recipe might be laying around. Decorate for days Roll up your sleeves, fix up equipment in your kitchen and paint all your old walls. Complete challenges and unlock a wide array of unique decorations, customizations and recipes. Whether you prefer a quirky, bustling diner with mismatched charm or an elegant, fine dining establishment that oozes sophistication, the choice is yours. Don’t forget to check out the gacha machine to have a chance to pull that special item! Clean your way to the top Clean, paint, and cook your way to the top. Hard workers have the best equipment and can afford the best ingredients. Or maybe you want to splash all the cash on 50 darumas? Just make sure to be on top of cleaning - no one enjoys a shop with dirty floors and a full trash can, your customers will notice! Sweep the floor, make sure that all your lights work and keep an eye on the toilet to maintain a clean shop. Everything starts with a good bowl The most important thing in a ramen restaurant is a really good bowl. Prepare all the ingredients, your amazing broth and have the best protein in town. Unlock receipts to extend you menu and earn more cash to level up your ingredient-game! Handle your menu on your restaurants kenbaiki and attract different type of customers, do basic ramen bowls really good or dive in to different types, just make sure your up to serve your customers what they ordered.
