Join the ever-curious Molly on a brand-new adventure through the beloved world of Alice in Wonderland. Features: 🌳 Delightful diorama-style immersive environments to lose yourself in 🙀 Hours of whimsical gameplay with perplexing puzzles 🦋 Mingle with marvelous characters from the world of Alice in Wonderland 🌳 Delightful diorama-style immersive environments Prepare for hours of gameplay, brimming with brain-teasers, enchantment, and just the right dash of delightful nonsense. Will Molly navigate her way out, or will she unearth the curious truth that sometimes the best way out is further in? Step into this whimsical whirlwind and discover what Molly is really escaping from and help her recover her memory—if you dare! 🙀 Whimsical gameplay with perplexing puzzles Puzzle your way through perplexing challenges and lose yourself in an enchanting landscape where up is down, left is right, and nothing is quite as it seems (except when it is, and even then, it might not be). Escaping Wonderland is a new chapter in the Down the Rabbit Hole saga, and an all-new story from the same developers who brought you the original, Cortopia Studios.
