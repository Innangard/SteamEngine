The soundtrack tracks the story of Momento closely, with different themes and motifs mapping out the different rooms and paths you can explore in each playthrough. The soundtrack is 47 tracks long with a runtime of over 2 hours. This OST includes all of the different variations of each theme from the game. The first set of themes represent the compositions in their fullest form, with all instruments and layers active. Version II is slightly stripped back, with fewer layers but still keeping much of the core instrumentation from each theme. Version III is very stripped back, often with just a few solo instruments and minimal texture and layering.
