Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game A board-game collection with dozens of different board games from around the world. Board Games includes chess families, territory and alignment games, draughts and captures, chase-and-block hunts, goal dashes, piece allocation, territory splits, and more classics—plus original board games of our own. Want a brain workout, a match with friends, or a slow night learning the rules? You can start right away. How to play Online multiplayer (create a room) — Host a room and sit at the same table from anywhere Local two-player — Take turns on the same PC, face to face Challenge the AI — Several difficulty levels so you can learn before you go online Every kind of board — its own flavour Chess families Classic checkmate games. Chess , Lewis Chess (medieval bishop leap), Xiangqi (river and cannon), Shogi (drops and promotion), Shatar (camel for bishop)—one family, many rulebooks. Territory and alignment Classic Go fights for liberties and points, Gomoku races to five, Reversi flips whole lines, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe sends you across nine boards, Four in a Row falls with gravity—simple to start, deep to master. Plus Amazons (move then shoot) and Lines of Action (connect your army). Draughts and captures One hop can decide it. English Draughts , International Draughts , Turkish Draughts , Alquerque , Kōnane , Fanorona , Surakarta , Hasami Shogi , Halma , Yoté , Latrunculi , Fighting Serpents … forced chains, sandwich takes, loop captures—fast and crisp. Hunts and sieges Asymmetric thrills. Hnefatafl races the king to a corner, Tiger Game pits four tigers against twenty goats, plus Fox and Geese , Hounds and Hare , Asalto , Breakthrough —surround or break out. Animal tactics Jungle leaps rivers; Arimaa pushes, pulls, freezes, and traps in four steps—animal boards with real depth. Races, dice, and sowing Luck meets planning. Senet , Royal Game of Ur , Chaupar , Yut , Puluc race home; Oware and Toguz Kumalak sow and reap. Plus top-down 8-ball and flip-to-reveal Banqi . Mills, blocks, and folk miniatures Nine Men's Morris , Achi , Tant Fant , Mū Tōrere , Dots and Boxes , Fidchell , Seega , Dara , Five Field Kono … short rounds you’ll want again. Original rules Only here: Tidepull , Starnet , Rift Path , Shade , Ripple , Vine , Shove , Triad , Football , Oppose , Comb , Tripod , Funnel , Ringturn , Shunturn , Gourd , Starpoint —familiar board feel, brand-new win conditions. Table and atmosphere Restyle frames, outer plates, corner icons, and painted skies Multilingual UI; switch games anytime from the hub Solo puzzles too (xiangqi endings, horse swap, eight queens, peg solitaire) Pick a board from somewhere in the world. Open a room. Make it a match.
