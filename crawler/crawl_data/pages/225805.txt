An enigmatic cataclysm has fractured reality, opening portals which lead to other times. Explore the past and future versions of an expansive city. Manipulate Black Holes to Travel Through Time Solve Escher-like puzzles through the use of black holes, traveling back and forth through time to make the city's architecture whole again. Challenge your spatial thinking skills while navigating distorted realities as time becomes increasingly more unstable. Use Technology from a Lost Era Activate holograms to move massive portals between floors of crumbled cathedrals, allowing you to traverse structures which only partially exist at one time. Save the City from Ruin Partner with the city's mysterious guardian to prevent a cataclysm threatening to merge past and future. Unravel a haunting narrative to save the City of Desolus from complete annihilation. Desolus has been selected for several festivals, including an exhibit at the Smithsonian American Art Museum.
