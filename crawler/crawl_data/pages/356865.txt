Dominate rival gangs by seizing buildings, summoning unique fighters, and unleashing devastating special attacks. Whether you're battling alongside friends in co-op or unleashing chaos in PvP , the game offers shared split-screen, online multiplayer, and free-for-all modes to keep the action fresh. Sneak Peek from Community Feedback: “Simplified Street Fighter-style combat... heaps of fun and very much skill-based.” Key Features: Four Distinct Gang Leaders Master the unique micro-mechanics and personalities behind each hero. Six Combat-Ready Unit Types Deploy thugs, kung-fu masters, sumo wrestlers, ninjas, and more for tactical depth. Fast-Paced Action RTS Seize enemy territory, fortify your position, and unleash a relentless assault of fighters. Crush their champion in high-stakes 1v1 boss fight to claim ultimate victory. Upgrade & Specialize Customize and upgrade captured buildings, yourself, and your gang. Build your perfect loadout by choosing 4 of 12 special attacks, from defensive stands to aggressive strikes and unleash them to turn the tide of battle. Versatile Multiplayer Modes Team up or face off in a variety of 4-player modes. Battle it out in King of the Hill, Horde and Total Conquest. All modes are PVP or co-op  playable online or in local split-screen. Horde Mode Survives Defend your turf against relentless waves with up to three friends. Custom Map Editor (WIP) Forge your own maps and challenge the community! With full Steam Workshop support, you can share your creations and download endless new battlegrounds from other players. What's Next? This Early Access title is actively evolving—expect smarter AI, fresh maps, and game modifiers. Join our Discord to help shape the future, test new modes, and build maps for upcoming console versions.
