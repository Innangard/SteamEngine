The campaign consists of 7 unique opponents, each increasing in difficulty. Floating Isles is the third campaign in Legion TD 2. The first campaign, Crystal Coast, is included with the base game. Overview Prepare yourself for a challenge. Floating Isles is harder than Crystal Coast. However, opponents have prominent weaknesses that you can press to your advantage. Approximately 10-15 hours of gameplay to complete the full campaign (Normal + Hard mode combined), depending on your skill level. Every match is unique. The campaign is enjoyable to replay even after you've beaten it. If playing co-op, only one player needs to own the DLC for everyone to play! Features Each opponent has its own playstyle, special abilities, rolls, and AI for building & sending. Each opponent has its own soundtrack, dialogue, reactions, and voice acting. The in-game Game Coach gives you hints for each opponent. Beat an opponent on Normal difficulty to earn the first star. Beat an opponent on any difficulty without any 40%+ leaks to earn the second star. Beat an opponent on Hard difficulty to earn the third star. Beware: Hard difficulty is designed to be extremely challenging , even for experienced players! Floating Isles Opponents Reek-a-deek: A mischievous rat thief who liberates the possessions of passers-by. Her Pack Rats gain extra mythium from treasure hunts, and she uses that to her advantage. On Hard mode, her mercenaries all cost 50% less mythium, but give -50% income, deal -25% damage, and have -25% damage reduction. Her economy is snowbally, but you can get fed off her weak sends, too. Fabulous Freddy: A once-legendary wizard who is enjoying retirement perhaps a little too much. He always takes All Out Assault and sends a free Witch on Wave 11. On Hard mode, he now sends two free Witches on Wave 11. Plus, all his units have 50% increased mana regen. Take a defensive legion spell and build Magic damage dealing fighters that are strong against Wave 11's Natural defense. Declan Stormhammer: A rare dwarf, more at home in the skies than underground. Plays Grarl into Eggsack opening. Plus, in these fertile lands, waves 4, 9, 13, and 17 spawn 50% more enemies! On Hard mode, his flying fighters & mercenaries gain 20% damage and damage reduction. Will you try to break his egg, or scale your economy? Consider sending Fortified/Natural tanks as his builds tend to be Pierce heavy. Jill McDonald: A cheerful nature-lover whose friendliness belies a competitive spirit. Plays Sakura opening. All your fighters lose 0.9% current health per second. On Hard mode, this is increased to 1.8%! Consider using healing to mitigate degeneration, and mount offenses on Arcane waves to exploit her Magic-heavy builds. Rex Ribbitor: A smooth talking frog tyrant who exploits his underlings for the "greater good." At the start of every wave, 30% of your current gold is...redistributed... to the royal coffers. On Hard mode, he also steals 5% of your hard-earned income. Perfectly fair, of course. Do your best to spend all your gold before the wave starts! Utilize Grarl if you want to save for more expensive fighters. chaoslord2468: The ultimate supervillain - at least in his own mind. Forces you to play Chaos along with him. Be warned: every wave you clear, your King loses 12% current health! On Hard Mode, this effect is increased to 20%! Plus, if that weren't enough, he also starts with 23 income. Try to pro-leak to keep your King from being drained, but it's a fine line between pro-leaking and leaking too much! Try to survive until late-game, where your Chaos synergies will pop off. Drachia: A godlike dragon with the power to cross between multiverses. All waves spawn 2x enemies, and everyone starts with a choice of extra resources! On Hard mode, your fighters attack 30% slower and have 30% reduced mana regen. Area of Effect fighters excel against the 2x waves. Just make sure you're ready for boss waves, too! Why a DLC? Legion TD 2 takes time and money to develop and keep the servers online. Periodically releasing DLC's ensures that the dev team can continue to pump out monthly patches with new units, new features, balance tweaks, quality of life improvements, and bug fixes, all free of charge. Supporting the dev team with your purchase also ensures the game can continue running for many years to come, without any subscription fees!
