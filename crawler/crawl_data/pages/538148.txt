View Community Hub About This Game BUILD YOUR SYNDICATE. EARN YOUR LEGACY. Start with a few dollars and nothing but ambition. Build a network of criminal operations across Prohibition-era New York, work the odds at The Action, fight rival hitmen, and climb through 10 ascensions toward the top of the organization. Streetlight Syndicate combines idle and incremental progression with a complete noir crime story. Your decisions shape the legacy you leave behind - and if you become the Don, your completed campaign can continue in Empire Mode. Build and upgrade 20 rackets with unique owners and stories Discover over 5,500 words of character dialogue Progress through 10 ascensions with narrative cutscenes Fight rival hitmen using an expanding arsenal Play three gambling minigames that unlock as you rise Spend Notoriety across four permanent campaign skill trees Reach multiple endings based on your decisions Continue after the good ending in the optional Empire Mode BUILD YOUR CRIMINAL NETWORK Invest in 20 illegal operations spread across the city. Run speakeasies, gambling halls, smuggling operations, protection rackets, and more. Every operation is run by someone with a story to tell. Spend Influence to uncover their histories and earn permanent bonuses while learning about the people building your organization. WORK THE ACTION Track the Ace in Three Card Monte. Place your bets at the horse track, where insider tips and fixed races can tilt the odds in your favor. Rise high enough to operate your own Numbers Racket. The games are not there to empty your wallet. Play the angles correctly and The Action can bankroll your next expansion. ASCEND THE HIERARCHY When your operation reaches its limits, Ascend. Your rackets reset, but your reputation remains. Earn Notoriety and spend it on permanent upgrades in the Ascension Market: Composure : Delegate work, automate collections, and identify profitable opportunities. Connections: Protect your fortune, expand your network, and keep the right people paid. Capo: Strengthen your crew and master the violence required to survive. Chicanery: Improve the odds, unlock harder games, and expand your gambling operation. After every ascension, Don Salvatore Russo judges your performance. Whether he sees a worthy successor or a dangerous liability depends on the choices you make. SETTLE ACCOUNTS Starting at Ascension 1, rival hitmen begin coming for your organization. Upgrade your arsenal from the Colt 1911 to the Thompson submachine gun and defeat increasingly dangerous opponents before they dismantle everything you built. A COMPLETE STORY - AND AN OPTIONAL POSTGAME Your campaign has a beginning, middle, and end across 10 ascensions. Your decisions determine whether you build a lasting legacy or leave behind a trail of betrayal. Earn the good ending and become the Don to unlock Empire Mode. Your completed syndicate is preserved while rival hitmen return in escalating cycles. Earn more Notoriety, purchase long-term Empire expansions, raise racket and weapon limits, and continue growing for as long as you choose. The city is waiting.
