View Community Hub About This Game Kill thousands of Bugs in Entomophobia, a roguelite reverse-bullethell, gather exp from slain bugs to level up, and choose from 3 upgrade cards to try and build the craziest combo. Features Auto-firing weapons as well as a controllable gun. Collect goop to gain experience, level up, and snowball to murder all the bugs. Quick 20 minute runs. Kill Bugs, Level Up, Pick Upgrades, and Repeat. Currently, the cards consist of weapon cards, some buff cards, and some infinitely stacking stat cards.
