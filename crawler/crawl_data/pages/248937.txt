Designed for fans of atmospheric, non-linear gameplay, Dreamcore invites you to roam vast, interconnected worlds where every corner holds a mystery, and every path leads to discovery. Experience the Dreamcore Demo—a taste of the enigmatic Dreampools. Dreampools : Dive into a surreal maze of endless pools, dimly illuminated by an ethereal glow. Reflective tiled walls amplify the profound stillness of the water, creating an atmosphere of captivating tranquility. Only the distant drip of water and the soft echo of your own footsteps accompany you, intensifying the isolation. Narrow hallways lead to grand open chambers, connected without obvious logic, beckoning your exploration deeper into the unknown. The sense of calm is overwhelming yet unsettling—as though an unseen presence watches, lingering forever at the edge of perception.
