View Community Hub About This Game This is a first-person organization simulation game where you shelve the videotapes, unlock new skills, and use your unique methods to tidy up the TV archive. Game Modes Solo, together, or competition... Enjoy the different game modes: Single player, online co-op, and online PvP. You can also try "Sandbox Mode", where all your skills are unlocked and always ready to use, or "No-Skill Mode" to challenge yourself. Videotape Categories Your task is to shelve 3,180 videotapes into the correct categories in the archive. Action movies, drama movies, TV series, cartoons, horror movies, comedy movies, athletic games, football cups, romance movies, sci-fi movies, baseball, tennis, news, game shows, basketball, motorsport, cooking shows, documentaries, boxing, and talk shows. Skills Sorting Numerically: Sorts the videotapes in your hand in numerical order Highlighting Shelf: Highlights the correct shelf for the topmost videotape in your hand Highlighting Videotapes: Highlights the same videotapes as the topmost videotape in your hand Auto-Placement: Automatically places your videotapes on the shelf in the correct order Auto-Collect: Automatically collects the same videotapes as the topmost videotape in your hand AI Generated Content Disclosure The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this: AI was used only as a supporting tool for the design of fictional images on the videotapes.
