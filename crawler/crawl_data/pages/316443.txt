Bunch Of Idiots. One Huge Mountain. Dangerous Mountain Together is a physics-based driving game for 2 to 4 friends who think their friendships can survive being literally tied together. You’re tethered by a rope, driving wobbly cars on roads that are way too thin. The Goal? Reach the Top. The Reality? Gravity. Tethered Chaos: You move as one. If your friend drives off a cliff, congrats you’re probably going with them. The "Save Me" Winch: One of you fell? No worries. Use the winch to haul your screaming friends back up before the whole squad plummet. Wobbly Physics: These cars don't drive; they bounce, tilt, and slide. It’s "controlled" driving... mostly. 3 Biomes of Pure Misery From "Hey, this is nice" to "Why are we doing this?" Grasslands: The "easy" part. (It’s not actually easy). Snowy Peaks: Sliding is mandatory. Traction is a myth. Swamp: Slimes, exploding Tikis and murky water designed to ruin your momentum. Look Good While Falling Customize your ride with funny cosmetics. If you’re going to explode for the 50th time today, you might as well look cool doing it. Grab 2-4 friends and see how high you can get before someone ruins everything.
