View Community Hub About This Game Lead the Horde. Destroy the Kingdom. Rescue your Warchief! The humans have captured your Warchief and locked him inside a heavily fortified castle. There is only one way to free him - tear the fortress apart. The Horde of Gorak is a pixel art incremental strategy game where you command a growing orcish horde. Gather resources, construct buildings, recruit increasingly powerful warriors, unlock devastating technologies, and lay siege to castle after castle in your relentless pursuit of your imprisoned leader. Every strike against the enemy fortress sends Castle Shards flying - your most valuable resource. Spend them wisely to expand your economy, strengthen your army, and prepare for the next, even tougher siege. But the humans won't surrender so easily. Each time a castle falls, they evacuate the Warchief to an even stronger fortress deeper within their kingdom. Your horde must continue growing if you ever hope to catch them. Build Your Horde Transform a small orc camp into a mighty war machine. Construct  production buildings. Expand your economy to support an ever-growing army. Unlock powerful structures, laboratories, and military facilities. Optimize your resource production to sustain endless warfare. Raise an Army Recruit a wide variety of brutal orc units, each contributing to the assault. Unlock stronger warriors as your horde evolves. Upgrade existing units to increase their power and efficiency. Deploy devastating trebuchets to bombard enemy walls. Combine different unit types to maximize destruction. Master Orc Magic Victory isn't achieved by brute force alone. Cast powerful spells during battles. Enhance your attacks with magical abilities. Discover new combat options through laboratory research. Research New Technologies Your laboratory unlocks powerful improvements for your horde. Research new military upgrades, economic bonuses, magical enhancements, and advanced technologies that dramatically increase your horde's strength. Prestige and Grow Stronger Every successful campaign rewards you with Prestige Points . Invest them into a permanent skill tree to unlock lasting bonuses, accelerate future runs, and push your horde further than ever before. Each new assault begins stronger than the last.
