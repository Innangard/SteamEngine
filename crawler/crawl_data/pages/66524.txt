Cast into an ocean of lava in a first-person fishing adventure where your dinner has other plans. Reel in a catch, survive the fight, and haul your prize back to shore. Every sale brings you closer to better equipment, stranger islands, and something much bigger on the end of your line. THE FISH BITE BACK SS ROD TO FIREPOWERSs Work your way from close-up brawling to pistols, automatic weapons, a sniper rifle, and a bazooka. Buy weapon upgrades, fit sights and lasers, and add a suppressor to your M4A1. Inspect your weapons, drop or throw your gear, and recover your lost inventory after death. BAIT SOMETHING BIGGER Earn special bait and summon the creatures guarding your next destination. Dodge lunges, bouncing attacks, and heavy slams as the encounters grow more demanding. Bring back their spoils to keep your journey moving. TAKE A CHANCE WITH YOUR CATCH Find rare fish for reward cases that roll and eject collectible knives. Put a valuable catch on the blackjack or roulette table and risk losing it for a larger payout. Then head back to the shore for one more cast. Catch. Fight. Feed. Upgrade. Set sail.
