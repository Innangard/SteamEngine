DYNASTY WARRIORS 3 was the first game in the DYNASTY WARRIORS series to feature mechanics that would become a mainstay for the series, such as True Musou Attacks, two-player simultaneous play, and the officer development system. It was also the first game in the series to sell more than one million copies.
