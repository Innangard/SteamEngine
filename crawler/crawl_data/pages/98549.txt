With the ball, you need to move around the grids until you finish your movements limit, find the correct position to make shots and then finish your turn by scoring a basket! Featuring: Characters move according to their ability, but the goal is to use your movements to reach the basket; 50 creative levels with increasing complexity; A basketball game without basketball. This is an entirely new game concept for basketball lovers and newbies (you don't need to know basketball to play it); Enjoy the beauty and dangers of Mars and many cats; Soundtracks originally composed.
