View Community Hub About This Game One war, two sizes WARTIDE runs as a small always-on-top widget in the corner of your screen while you work, browse or watch. When the swarm arrives, go full screen and take command. Same battle, same save, your choice of size. Hold the line from every direction No lanes. Enemies close in from 360 degrees, wave after wave, with all-out assaults, enemy commanders and night attacks. Your army has a cap, so where you deploy and what you field matters more than how much Evolve your army through three eras Start with WWII halftracks and Shermans. Evolve eight vehicle lines (recon, carriers, tanks, tank destroyers, artillery, air defence, helicopters and jets) through the Cold War to modern armor, open every vehicle in your garage, and file every unit you meet in the codex. Call in the big guns Strafing runs, cluster bombs, carpet bombing, rocket artillery. Commander abilities (artillery barrage, smoke screen, reinforcements) unlock and grow stronger as you prestige. Conquer the campaign, survive the outbreak Take 14 regions on the campaign map for lasting bonuses, all the way to the capital at wave 150. Then switch to Outbreak mode: build turrets, hold the shelter and survive a horde of thousands. Good to know Single-player, Windows. Runs as a resizable desktop widget or full screen. Cloud saves. Idle-friendly: your army fights on its own, auto-command keeps the line manned. All-out assaults and the later eras still need your decisions. Every run pays: Micro Conductors from a lost run buy permanent upgrades, and each one takes you further. Nuke a strong run (prestige) for new headquarters buildings and commander abilities. Pin it. Play it. Let the war rage on.
