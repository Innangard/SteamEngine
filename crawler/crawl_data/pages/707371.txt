You will love Cozy Paintings! Famous paintings from all times and all places got their colors all mixed up and it's up to you to restore them! Drag colors  to spread them across your canvas and put them all back where they belong to reveal new artworks. Optimize your moves to aim for the golden paw and collect each artist's cat to keep you company. Cats are wandering on your screen, but you can also pick them up and move them as you wish. You can even customize them with 18 hats to unlock. What You’ll Find More than 50 levels of varying difficulty to discover,  through 6 different painting categories Each painting unlocks a new cat to accompany you while you play The cats are named after famous painters, with some tweaking because they are cats!
