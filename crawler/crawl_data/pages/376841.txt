View Community Hub About This Game 'Pieces of the Past' is a cute, sentimental adventure where you restore family keepsakes to uncover touching stories. Blend puzzle-solving with the joy of preserving memories in this delightful journey! Step into the life of Lily, a young adult gifted with the extraordinary ability of "repairing memories." Fix broken items in this cozy, heartfelt puzzle adventure to uncover hidden family secrets. Explore themes of family, reconciliation, personal growth, and nostalgia in this captivating story. Utilize Lily's unique skill to repair items and reveal the memories they hold. Each repaired item reveals a new piece of the family's history, offering insights into the motivations and experiences of each family member. Memory Repair Mechanic: Fix broken items to uncover the memories within and piece together your family's history. Emotional Storytelling: Dive deep into a narrative rich with themes of family, reconciliation, and personal growth. Detailed Environments: Explore a beautifully crafted antique shop filled with historical and atmospheric details. Character-driven Experience: Witness Lily's journey of understanding and healing as she confronts the past. Immersive Gameplay: Engage with a variety of items and puzzles to unlock new memories and advance the story. Join Lily in Pieces of the Past, where every repaired item brings a new revelation, and every memory helps her rebuild the bonds of her family and shape her future. Rediscover the past to shape your destiny.
