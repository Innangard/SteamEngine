Meet new friends and foes, visit strange lands, and outfit yourself on a quest to keep the entire land from collapsing underfoot! Jump, slash, and dig your way down an ever-changing chasm of mystery in this standalone Shovel Knight adventure. Shovel Knight Dig also includes two free expansions, Wicked Wishes and Fate and Fortune, adding new quests, challenges, characters, bosses, Relics, secrets, and more! WICKED WISHES Step into Hoofman’s Guild Hall, a secret hideout where elite adventurers and their illustrious patrons gather! Take on new quests, climb the guild ranks, collect powerful Relics and buffs, encounter new bosses and enemies, and uncover the mysteries surrounding Hoofman and the Guildarch. But be careful what you wish for. Wicked consequences await deep within the well! FATE AND FORTUNE Something truly evil is brewing underground! After completing the main adventure, take on the challenging Knightmare Mode, earn Mementos to display in the hub, and enjoy a wide range of quality-of-life improvements throughout your journey. FEATURES An all-new Shovel Knight story! When Drill Knight and his dastardly digging crew steal Shovel Knight’s loot, he grabs his trusty Shovel Blade and starts tunneling after them! The first Shovel Knight adventure in glorious high-color pixel graphics and sound. Fluid animation, scaling, rotation, and parallax create the most convincing Shovel Knight world yet! Every adventure is different! Shovel Knight Dig levels have been meticulously crafted, then stitched together using proprietary generation techniques for endless replayability. Jump, slash, and dig your way in an all-new direction: down! Rely on your trusty Shovel Drop, then dig through huge swaths of dirt using the all-new Speed Shovel mechanics. Grow in power and wealth as you descend into the depths of the earth. Emerge with untold riches, unlock permanent items and upgrades, and outfit Shovel Knight for the journey ahead. Take on additional quests, challenges, mini-games, bosses, enemies, Relics, secrets, and more across the Wicked Wishes and Fate and Fortune expansions. New Knights, characters, and enemies abound, but fear not! Shovel Knight Dig features the series’ signature storytelling, humor, action, and maybe even some familiar faces. Another pulse-pounding soundtrack in a new style by astonishing virtuoso Jake Kaufman! The first collaboration between Yacht Club Games and Nitrome, pixel masters!
