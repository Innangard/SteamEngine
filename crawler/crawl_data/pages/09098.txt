Ver todas las discusiones Acerca de este juego FrontWars is a high-stakes PvP battle-royale where the fight for global domination never stops. Hundreds of players collide on one massive map, battling to seize land and outmaneuver their rivals. Build alliances through trade, grow your fortune in gold, and unleash catastrophic nuclear strikes to crush your enemies. Only strategy, power, and ruthless ambition will decide who rises to rule the world—will it be you?
