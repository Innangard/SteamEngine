daripada 9 Lihat Hab Komuniti Tentang Permainan Ini THE DYNAMIC ACTION GAME ABOUT COMBAT VEHICLES OF PRESENT AND FUTURE Modern Warships is a cross-platform arcade online action game where modern naval battles look like they do in reality. Surveillance aircraft rush forward to detect the enemy from distance, cruisers launch volleys of guided missiles and try to reset enemy missile defences, submarines sneak up to surface vessels and jet fighters provide cover to the allied fleet from the air. MODERN WARFARE Take part in modern naval and air battles with combat vehicles that perform the way they would do in reality. With drones and precise weapons. ALL THE TOP VEHICLES 200+ types of modern and prospective ships and aircraft including carriers, submarines, drones, stealth fighters and strategic bombers. DYNAMIC AND PRACTICAL Play comfortably thanks to simple controls and arcade battle mode, switch smoothly between different types of vehicles during the same battle. Be in the most fun parts of battlefield! DO IT YOURSELF Use the potent customization system that allows you to change and modify almost all components of ships and aircraft. Bring the full power of supersonic missiles down on your enemy! THE GAME NEVER FAILS TO IMPRESS Modern Warships regularly receives new battle maps, combat vehicles and weapons while in-game tournaments, events and other activities provide new gaming experience and cover the subject of modern naval and air battles from different perspectives.
