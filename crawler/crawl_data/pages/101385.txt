The main character wakes up in his house and lives an ordinary life of a person who survived the end of humanity. Helps his friends, goes hunting and repairs equipment. The main character is an unusual person, he has abilities that help him survive in poisonous spores. You need to help the main character's friends, update the filters in their houses, solving the problems of other acquaintances along the way.
