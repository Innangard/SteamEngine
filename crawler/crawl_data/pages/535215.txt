View Community Hub About This Game FURNITURE FU is a casual home-deco roguelike where you slay waves of demons, buy furniture, and design your dream home. Every piece of furniture gives you a unique power - and the smarter you arrange your home, the stronger you become. In other words, the better you Furniture, the better you Fu! Descend the underworld Take on the armies of hell who are bent on stopping you moving into your lovely new neighbourhood, and take on huge swarms of enemies with just a sword, a tasteful home and a dream. Build OP synergies Build awesome furniture synergies to create interesting builds, and become OP. Make an eccentric, pretentious mansion and become a bullet shooting machine, or design a cosy, rustic cottage to make explosions of fire and death. Crush the underworld within your iron fist. The urge to build beautiful, cosy homes, and the urge to kill everything you see. Build carefully arranged maisonettes, or embrace chaos and play optimally. The world is your oyster. Mix and match from a huge range of furniture, from the pretentious chaise longue to the globe to the sofa to the printer to the humble cardboard box.
