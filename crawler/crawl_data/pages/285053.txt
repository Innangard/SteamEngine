View Community Hub About This Game Catch fish! More fish is a simple incremental idle clicker game. EXCEPT YOU DON'T USUALLY CLICK. Hover your cursor over the fish for catching them. Your cursor will turn into a fishing buoy when you are on ocean tiles. At the end of each fishing round, you upgrade many things and continue fishing in the next round. Events - Something fishy going on Events change the reality in different layers. They make  game mechanics slightly more challenging and give extra rewards. Pierce the fog... Feel the nordic lights... Beware of sea sickness... Listen for a good catch... Hold still during heavy storms... Party together with THE FISH MASTER... Skill tree These upgrades permanently boost your skills, letting you catch MORE FISH. The left hand side of skill tree is about auto fishing game mechanics, the right hand side of skill tree is about game economy. Fishing buoys and runes Better fishing buoys and runes will help you catch MORE FISH. Some runes can change how you interact with mouse. If you remember, I said you don't usually click above. :) Well... Sometimes you will have to click in different ways. The statistics The statistics will tell you the strength of your fishing buoy including all possible upgrades. For example: How is your performance of collecting coins and pearls over time? Which fish did you catch the most? Fish Collection There are 64 different fish from different places in the world. You can read fish details. You can select the fish you want to catch in the next round. They are also a source of passive income. Talent cards Levelling up means  earning a talent scroll. Spend talent scrolls to reveal 3 random talent cards and pick one to keep! Everyone loves a bit of card game. Soooo, why not, right? Right. Event upgrades Upgrade your event abilities to turn harsh seas and foggy nights into massive fishing opportunities. Events add a bit of spice to your gameplay, and give  rewards. You will love them even more after some upgrades! Lost  Items Lost items provide you with permanent upgrades. Lost items can be about game mechanics, game economy or utility.
