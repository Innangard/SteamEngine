Amiga About This Demo Descend five levels into the Dungeons of Mordecoom in this bite-sized taste of Roguecraft DX - a leisurely roguelike where every step can still get you into trouble. Choose your adventurer: the sturdy Warrior, the sneaky Rogue, or the dangerously fragile Wizard. Each class plays completely differently, rewarding clever tactics, risky decisions, and just a little bit of panic. A full run takes around 20–30 minutes… assuming you survive long enough to defeat the menacing cultist and steal his key. And because this is a roguelike, one run is never enough. The dungeon shifts, the monsters keep coming, and every failed escape becomes another excuse for “just one more run.” Feeling brave? Try the extra-hard Chicken Run mode… where YOU are the chicken. If this little descent hooks you, the full game goes much deeper. The award winning and critically acclaimed modern Amiga classic comes to Steam! 15 procedural generated levels (every play-session is unique) An endless dungeon mode, with a seperate highscore! Lots more monsters! More potions and fun effects! An achievement system. A highscore feature! More music! Play at your own pace. Every move counts, and each decision could mean survival - or a swift end. In Roguecraft DX, each of the three playable heroes brings a unique play-style and special abilities to the fight. They also represent different difficulty levels, so choose wisely! The Warrior delivers crushing blows, the Rogue blinks behind enemies for deadly backstabs, and the Wizard may be fragile, but unleashes powerful magic missiles. Every descent is unique. The dungeon reshapes itself with every run, ensuring a fresh challenge and new surprises each time you play. Designed to respect your time, perfect for a focused session or a quick escape. Enjoy beautifully crafted isometric pixel art, celebrating the golden age of retro visuals. For time immemorial a legend has been told of a dark, deadly dungeon containing unimaginable horrors and riches beyond belief. Enough riches and magic to make you a king, THE king even. Tales of the dungeon was found in an ancient Egyptian library many years ago. The scrolls told rumors of an ancient dark presence and a magic amulet. The legend also tells of a wakening and of horrors to come. Your search carried you across the world - one riddle leading to the next. Finally you are standing at the entrance of a dark and mystical dungeon. You, Zendar, a fighter, a mage, or uhm, a rogue… are ready to defeat the monsters and gather the riches waiting below! You just need to take a leak first. Step into the dungeon. Embrace the unknown. The depths of Mordecoom await! Originally developed in Assembly for the Commodore Amiga, Roguecraft DX has been faithfully ported to PC, preserving its authentic retro charm while bringing the experience to modern systems. Whether you're a seasoned roguelike veteran or new to the genre, Roguecraft DX offers a respectful, tightly designed experience for all adventurers.
