Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Aethermancer has been in development for over three years, with several successful playtests and demos. The early access version of the game already features a rewarding loop with a significant amount of content. As a result, we believe now is the right time to put the game into players’ hands so that we can generate feedback and build towards making the 1.0 launch as polished as possible. ” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “Approximately 1.5 years. ” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “With the full version of the game, we intend to build upon the foundation we’ve built with the Early Access launch. We plan to add polish, quality of life improvements, more monsters, more biomes, a fleshed-out end game area, Monster Deity boss fights, and player classes. Our hope for the full version is to have endless builds to experiment with, hours of exploration, as well as rewarding endings (and true endings).” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “As it stands, the game contains 2 player classes, 4 difficulty modes, 40 monsters (each with a shifted variant) across 19 types, 500+ abilities for your monsters to learn, 4 biomes to explore, 4 avenues of metaprogression, town decorations, and two Monster Deity boss fights.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We are planning for a price increase once 1.0 launches. ” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We value our community’s feedback highly and have experience in working closely with the community from our last game Monster Sanctuary. We have already implemented a host of improvements and fixes suggested by our alpha, beta, and demo players before Early Access launch, and we will keep a close eye on what the community has to say during development. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Encounter the monsters of Terastae that defied the virtues of this world and are trapped in a cycle of death and rebirth. Use your special ability as an Aethermancer to form bonds with monsters to guide your party of three through a sprawling world and dangerous battles. Make smart skill choices as your monsters grow stronger and find synergies between your monsters - making every one truly unique! Fight ferocious enemies and divine bosses in engaging turn-based battles where every action matters! Manage and harness the four elements of Aether to unleash powerful spells. Consider your synergies and plan your monsters actions. Purge enemy Aether, grind your opponents down with poison, protect your allies with shields, focus on attacking with the right elements - you can pursue different strategies! But whatever you do, keep a careful eye on the HP of your monsters… If a monster dies in combat, you’ll lose it. Permanently. But you have the power to defy death by rebirthing their souls. Don’t lose hope! Your monsters might change in the process, but will still be the same companions you once established a soul bond with and will grow stronger from their previous lives. With every level up you get to decide in which direction your monsters will grow. Every monster has a dynamic skill pool that reacts to previous choices and other monsters you have in your team, enabling you to create powerful teams. Experiment with a myriad of unique team builds with every run! Meet various characters who will help you and your monsters throughout your journey! Uncover their stories and dig into the lore of this world - all fueled by your choices and encounters. Help liven the village up by gathering townsfolk, hearing them out, and doing what only a true Aethermancer can do! The Aethermancer has many faces - and each of them come with entirely new mechanics and abilities. Unlock new player classes to shake up the way you play and how you decide to build your party. Use your Aethermancer abilities to explore, sneak and fight your way through procedurally generated levels while your monsters gain Worthiness. Keep going until you lose your monsters… then come back stronger and try again! Special thanks to Path of Pixels (logo), HeavyMetalHanzo (key artwork) and Steven Melin (soundtrack)!
