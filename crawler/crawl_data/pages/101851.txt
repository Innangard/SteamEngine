Choose the Frostwood Ranger or the Runeblade and carve a path through a world gripped by endless night. Shape every run Follow branching routes, face events, and choose when to fight, recover, or take a risk. Collect cards, relics, and potions to build combinations that suit your hero and the threats ahead. Fight with intention Plan each turn, balance offense and defense, and adapt to enemies that demand different answers. Elite encounters and powerful bosses test the deck you have built.
