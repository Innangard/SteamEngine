Kandungan Untuk Permainan Ini Layari semua (1) Among the Trolls Soundtrack N/A Lihat Hab Komuniti Tentang Permainan Ini Among the Trolls is a first-person narrative-adventure game, with survival elements, where the secret mysteries of Finnish forests are unraveled. Step into Anna’s shoes as she travels from the U.S. to Finland to visit her grandfather in a remote and isolated cabin deep in the forest. But upon arrival, you discover the cabin abandoned and your grandfather has vanished, drawing you into a realm of Nordic mythology and long-lost memories of summers past. Embark on a quest to find your missing grandfather and survive the untamed wilderness, facing supernatural creatures from Finnish mythology and the living, animistic forces of Nature. Follow in your grandfather’s footsteps and uncover ancient secrets of Finnish mythology. FIND YOUR ROOTS Embark on an empowering journey to uncover your past and solve the mystery behind your granpa's disappearance. Discover hidden truths that will reshape your destiny. WITHSTAND THE NATURAL AND THE SUPERNATURAL Face a relentless world shaped by harsh weather, wild animals, scarce resources, and supernatural encounters. Balance Väki (Magic) and Lykky (Luck) to survive, and discover how these ancient forces intertwine. FINNISH MYTHOLOGY Experience and explore a vast world inspired by Finnish mythology and ancient shamanistic magic. Meet extraordinary creatures rooted in the classic Kalevala myths. SURVIVE THE WILD Survive and thrive in the breathtaking yet unforgiving Finnish wilderness. Build, craft, and adapt to the environment as you unlock the ancient secrets of your ancestors. AUDIOVISUAL EXPERIENCE Immerse yourself in a beautifully crafted 3D world that blends nostalgic and otherworldly aesthetics. The stunning visuals are paired with an enchanting soundtrack and the authentic sounds of Nordic nature, making it hard to leave the world of Among the Trolls.
