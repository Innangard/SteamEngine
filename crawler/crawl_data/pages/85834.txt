Play against the AI in a team battle with 3 starships per side to choose from, customize starship load-outs, and give orders to your wingmates. The demo includes: The prologue campaign with seven story based missions acting as both tutorial and introduction to the game universe Skirmish: choose and outfit ships for your computer team mates and opponent in Team Vs Team skirmishes Keep your enemy against your strongest side while you attack their weakest. All warships use a quadrant-based damage model for 8 sides to protect or take advantage against. Ship positioning will matter. Familiar present-day weaponry The majority of weaponry available will be familiar to present-day and mountable on turrets with limited firing arcs. Warship Customization Customize your ship's weaponry with a range of projectile and missile turrets. Turrets slots have a maximum supported size and fitting the biggest guns in each slot is not an option. Then enhance the abilities of your warship by fitting modules, impacting offensive and defensive ship systems to gain an early advantage over your foes.
