Challenge your skills to the limit. Manage the garage, dealing with parts and funds. Fix damaged rally cars, tune them and go to rallies. Deal with bad weather and limited time. Conquer all of that and your competition. Become the mechanic of the best rally team out there. See for yourself if you have the skills and mindset needed for the job. If you want it, you can achieve it Your goal will be to handle time pressure and to turn a complete wreck into something that will drive and win a special stage. The entire team’s performance during the rally will depend on your decisions, mechanical prowess, time and budget management. Get your tools ready! Creativity and the right items and tools will allow you to conquer all obstacles. Always take them all with you. Windshield replacement? Not a problem. Bumper repair? Piece of cake! Your workshop is your kingdom Take advantage of your well-stocked workshop with useful tools for fixing cars. Forge your career While fixing and modifying cars will be the main part of your experience, there will be much more to do. It's not just a random car, it's a rally car You are dealing with a rally car - a purpose-built machine. Turbos, coilovers, proper tires and performance transmissions will sooner or later be installed in your vehicle. The car has a lot of extra features that you need to keep an eye on. Make it as good as possible and earn fans and money as a part of a champion rally team.
