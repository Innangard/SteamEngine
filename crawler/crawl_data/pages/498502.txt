They dedicate themselves to great ideas and work hard to make them a reality. People have begun to dream; they look to the future full of wishes but also with high expectations. Lead this hopeful generation into a period of prosperity and new inventions. Make the former pioneers into citizens of a newly blossoming civilisation.
