Now, for the first time, it's available on PC in a full digital edition – featuring single-player, local multiplayer, and online PvP modes . Take command of one of the factions and face off against rival armies – including the legendary Moloch , a network of automated machines that have rebelled against humanity. Every match is a fast-paced, intense battle of tactics, planning, and anticipating your opponent's moves. Step into the role of a faction leader and lead your units to victory. The reason for the fight? The classics: water, fuel, weapons, survival – everything that’s precious in a world engulfed by rust, dust, and toxins. The game’s mechanics revolve around strategically placing unit and module tiles on a hexagonal board. Choose, combine, deploy, and activate – every decision matters . It’s a fast-paced yet mentally demanding turn-based game where good planning is key. At launch, you’ll have access to 10 diverse armies , each with its own playstyle, strengths, weaknesses, and unique mechanics. Every faction offers a different tactical approach – from brute force to precision counters and mechanical traps. Base game armies include: Moloch, Hegemony, Borgo, Outpost, Steel Police, Smart, Vegas, New York, Beasts, and Mississippi . Additionally, we’ve added 3 more armies as DLC: Neojungle, Sharrash, Uranopolis . Over time, we plan to add more expansions known from the board game version . Whether you're playing for relaxation or in competitive mode – there's something here for everyone. The game includes: Advanced solo mode with AI Story-driven campaign Local multiplayer on one computer Online PvP battles That makes Neuroshima Hex perfect for both casual home play and intense online combat. The game is available in 17 languages , including Polish, English, German, French, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese – making it accessible to players around the world. A must-have title for: fans of board games and turn-based strategies players who value deep tactics and asymmetric factions fans of the Neuroshima universe – now also on PC!
