About This Game Everything was peaceful in the city of Chagas , where humans and other creatures lived together in harmony. Peace reigned… until Isaac laid his hands on a strange chainsaw , this act triggered a chain of events that would change the world forever. Death, violence, and murder became part of everyday life, bloodthirsty monsters now roam the city in search of more victims, and now, the only person capable of putting an end to it all is… YOU . STORY TOLD IN CHAPTERS CHAINSAW will be released in chapters. By purchasing the game, you will receive all future chapters as free updates. YOUR TEAM Claim your POWER and become a GOD , face your ENEMIES and grow STRONGER . Your influence spreads across the world, and four apostles answer your call: An unstable and violent individual, strangely kind. A cowardly, moralistic rabbit. He only thinks about his work. A naive yet determined cat. Her greatest wish is to find a friend who was hospitalized a long time ago. An emotional elephant who seems to share sentimental memories with the other apostles, despite apparently not knowing them. In this world, your ENEMIES will show no mercy and will not respect your turn, they will ATTACK your apostles even during your own turn. THINK fast to avoid as much DAMAGE as possible. Do not let your apostles down, or they may turn against you. The violence of this world affects everyone, trust NO ONE . CHAINSAW is a real-time turn-based combat RPG where you must create strategies and prepare your team before each battle, because once combat begins, enemies will keep attacking without stopping. Explore a dangerous world filled with powerful enemies and uncover secrets; Find items and equipment to improve your team; Change your team’s formation to gain different effects with certain weapons; React quickly in combat or perish.
