Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We've been working on Colossus: Eternal Blight for several years, developing the core systems and laying the foundation for the game. As we're building a story-driven action-RPG, we have many planned features and future story content to we aim to include, as well as class variety and build customization. As such, enabling our community to test, and provide feedback on our content and balancing is crucial. This, alongside being a small indie team, is why we feel Early Access is a great way for us to expand upon the game, whilst players/our community can get their hands-on available content as its released and help shape future development! If you decide to participate in Early Access, we'd love to hear your feedback and ideas in the Steam Forums or in our Community Discord.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We want to provide the best experience possible with Colossus, and aim to release the final version in its best state. As such, we expect Early Access to continue until around the end of 2027. This gives us time to develop features, whilst also letting our community play and provide feedback for us to incorporate.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “With Early Access, we aim to gradually introduce the main story, alongside various side quests/content (including new dungeons and Purification Quests!) As Early Access progresses, we aim to include more content and gradually expand upon the story as well. Our aim is to try to have saves made during Early Access to carry through with future updates. We estimate the final game to have around 15-20 hours of content (which may be greater if players choose to engage with side content).” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The initial Early Access release features the Prologue, and the early story chapter proceeding the Prologue. This gives players access to our first playable class, with two tiers of unlock-able skills (which can be upgraded). There are also optional side quests, as well as our first 'Purification Quest', which tasks players with saving an NPC from their Blight. There is also a mini 'roguelite dungeon' section toward the end of the current story content - with 10 tiers of increasing difficulty. This offers more replay-ability for those looking to get more, as well as offering a challenge! We will be providing a roadmap soon, so you can see when we aim to release major patches and story beats.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We plan to increase the price of the game at full launch, upon leaving Early Access, to reflect the available content.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “After our successful Kickstarter campaign, various playtests and our Demo release - we are thrilled to have such an incredible and supportive community. We often host events, particularly on Discord to involve our Community. We also have dedicated channels for feedback, bugs and content ideas. A creeping darkness called the Blight devours everything it touches and the one man sworn to stop it, your brother Lance, has vanished without a trace. You are Lucian. Not a chosen hero. Not a legend. Just a brother who refuses to give up on someone he loves even as the world around him collapses into nightmare. Colossus is a story-driven action RPG with precision combat, a hand-crafted pixel art world, and choices that leave real scars. The Blight will test you. How you answer is up to you. Every fight in Colossus is a test of timing, positioning, and adaptability. Parry a blow at the last moment, counter with a fluid combo, dash through an opening, or use the environment itself to turn the tide. Switch freely between a varied arsenal each weapon changing the rhythm of how you fight. Button-mashing will get you killed. Mastery will set you free. Explore a Hand-Crafted World: Journey through a beautifully animated pixel art world inspired by classic JRPGs. Discover hidden treasures, solve Zelda-style puzzles , explore ancient ruins, and visit tightly-knit towns spread across four distinct regions of Eireos. The Blight isn't just an enemy it's a temptation. As the corruption spreads across Eireos, you decide how to respond: fight it, contain it, or leave people to their fate. But if you're willing to walk a darker path, the Blight itself offers power. Corrupted abilities that can turn the tide of any battle. At a price. But Beware... Those lost to the Blight are gone forever. Your choices are permanent. Choose from three distinct classes , each with their own skill trees and specialisations then switch freely between them as your strategy evolves. Lean into a pure discipline, or forge a hybrid approach that suits your instincts. And if corruption doesn't scare you, the Blight's abilities are waiting brutally effective... but at a cost... At its core, Colossus is about what we do for the people we love and whether that love is enough. Lance believed in duty above all else. Lucian believes in Lance. As the truth behind his brother's disappearance unravels, you'll face questions the game doesn't answer for you. Strength won't be enough. Neither will hope. How far will you go to bring him back?
