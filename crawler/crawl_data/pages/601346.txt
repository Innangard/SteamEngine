Buy Deleveled Soundtrack $4.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Deleveled features an original chiptune soundtrack composed by Patrick Nance. Jazz, blues, and funk form the basis of each track, which creates a distinct downtempo sound not often heard in the realm of chiptune. The entire soundtrack was composed with FamiTracker, a tool designed to emulate the sound capabilities of the NES.
