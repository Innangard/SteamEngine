View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Craft amazing musical instruments. Tune, test and sell them, then reinvest your profits to upgrade and grow your music business. Step by Step to Perfection From assembly to painting and setup, every step counts. The happier your customers are with the quality and the cost, the more often they will come back with new orders. Just remember to meet your deadlines! The Music Business The quality of your musical instruments depends on your experience, so the more you practise, the better you get. Your tools will wear out with use and sometimes need replacing. Upgrading your equipment, such as buying a better workbench, can speed up certain tasks and reduce production time. Use your profits to buy more tools and unlock additional rooms to expand your shop. Keep an eye on your stockroom, too: it needs a steady supply of instrument parts, paints, adhesives and tools. Worldwide Fame Grow your small local brand into a name recognised around the world! Famous artists will seek out your services and promote your brand on their concert tours. Launch advertising campaigns and open branches across the globe to become an international musical instrument manufacturer. Your Private Collection Ever wanted to create your dream musical instrument? In Music Store Simulator, you can! Build your dream instruments and add them to your private collection! What's in the Game? Graphics and models designed to faithfully reproduce every detail of the musical instruments. Over 40 instruments to build, including acoustic and electric guitars, acoustic and electric basses, bowed string instruments and amplifiers. Over 1,000 parts to work with, all customisable in terms of materials and finishes. Build instruments to meet specific orders from customers around the world. Carry out authentic production tasks, including joining parts, fastening screws and gluing. Build your own private instrument collection. Realistic production times: painting and glue drying each take different amounts of time. Improve your skills to keep raising the quality of the instruments you make. Manage your stockroom. Play the musical instruments you create, once you have tuned them, of course! Expand your shop with new rooms: a production room, two stockrooms, a drying room, a paint room and a photo studio to capture your finest creations! Grow your brand by keeping customers around the world happy. Open new branches in any city where your brand has become popular. Use the loan system to finance your shop's expansion. Customise the background playlist and listen to your favourite music while you play.
