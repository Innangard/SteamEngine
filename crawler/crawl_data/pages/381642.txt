View Community Hub About This Game Beaconfall - is a dynamic strategy game about defending a settlement on an island surrounded by darkness. Build structures, gather resources, and assign villagers between work and defense. Every building either boosts your economy or strengthens your fortifications - but you’ll never have enough hands. When a wave begins, enemies emerge from the gloom and attack from multiple directions. Staff your towers, place traps, and make decisions under pressure. The lighthouse is the heart of your settlement. It holds back the darkness - and it can become a powerful weapon if you choose to invest in its upgrades. Each run lasts around 20–30 minutes and takes place on a procedurally generated island. Different scenarios, unique island layouts, and bosses demand new strategies and builds. Survive the final assault and prove your light is stronger than the dark.
