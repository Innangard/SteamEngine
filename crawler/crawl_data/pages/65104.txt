View Community Hub Reviews “A relaxing treat for the senses.” 80 – Entertainment Focus “A great game for casual puzzle players who want something fun but not too challenging.” Screen Hype “Every transformation requires a good amount of prep and momentum, rendering Origament as one of the most original origami platformers.” Mirror About This Game An adventure platformer where a precious letter journeys across time and continents in search of the one still waiting. Each world tells a unique story, and each quest reveals a clue to the greater secret. From a quiet moonlit garden to raging tornadoes in the Wild West - what a journey for a tiny piece of paper! And the letter? That’s you. Origament is your escape after a long day - a lighthearted, joyful adventure where gameplay can be relaxing yet sometimes test your skills. With light puzzles, chill quests, stunning views, and a mysterious story, it’s less about the challenge and more about enjoying the journey. Be a paper boat drifting down a quiet stream. A paper plane gliding over the rooftops of old Venice. Or a paper shuriken smashing stalactites to send your mine cart racing ahead. Some worlds feel like meditation, others like wild journeys full of excitement. Each level opens a new era, a new place, a new mood. The challenge doesn’t come from enemies, but from the environment and its clever puzzles. Change form to overcome obstacles. Every transformation means new physics, abilities, and vibes. This is not about combat - it’s about curiosity, observation, and clever adaptation. The levels themselves become both your biggest challenge - and your greatest ally. It all began in the Archive of Memory - a place of fading thoughts, unsent words, and letters that were never read. Forgotten, but not lost, they sleep in endless drawers. Among them was one special letter. No one remembers who wrote it anymore, but inside was a strange spark. Enough for the paper to tremble… and awaken. That same night, a Time Glitch flared up - and the letter vanished from the Archive. Not stolen - escaped. Somewhere out there, the addressee is still waiting. Time to go.
