After you and Dr Grimble experience a rough day at the Elektro factory, you find yourself transformed - with a blue, stretchy, and sticky body!? Fellow denizens may be befuddled by your new look, and to make matters worse, the Elektros have escaped containment and are now scattered around the Isles! Harness your newfound stretchiness to climb, swing and stick across this bizarre world. Track down the hidden Elektros to restore power and unravel the mystery of what happened to you. Why are you so stretchy, and blue?
