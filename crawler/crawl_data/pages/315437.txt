View Community Hub About This Game Lost Luminosity is a first-person psychological horror game with a linear storyline. Brief Story You will take on the role of Sam, whose mother passed away shortly after being released from a psychiatric hospital. He still doesn't know why she began to lose her mind, which led to her mental disorders, or who is behind it. Gameplay The gameplay will involve exploring an apartment where you can uncover the true events of the past, unravel your father's fate, and understand the reasons behind your mother's psychological disorders. The game also addresses relevant social issues related to society and family, offering a perspective on your character from the outside. You will experience the presence of supernatural forces. Will you be able to confront them?
