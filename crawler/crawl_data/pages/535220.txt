Explore four different worlds while collecting golden flies to advance and face the final challenge, which lies behind the gigantic gate! Help the little frog reach the end of the level and collect lots of golden flies. This way, he can open the portal to the next level, where he'll find more challenges and golden flies to catch! Face seed-spitting plants, explosive-egg-throwing chickens, giant-axe-wielding ladybugs, soda cannons, and even space missiles. Each world presents unique challenges! The frog's control scheme is simple and easy to learn, but becomes more complex through interaction with powerups and objects in the environment. Use everything from jars that give you triple jumps to anti-gravity bubbles in space! Open the giant gate in the main area when you've collected enough flies and discover its secret. Be prepared for the final challenge! And that's not all!
