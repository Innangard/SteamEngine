Explore Talos-II, Build a New Homeland Venture into the soaring peaks and snaking waterways of Talos-II, where discoveries lie at every turn. Neutralize Ankhor threats, purify the Blight, overcome crises through exploration, and continue AIC construction to bring prosperity back to this land.
