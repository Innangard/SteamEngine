Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We believe a competitive trading card game is strongest when it grows alongside its players. Early Access will allow us to improve Origins and enhance the game with our community in a live setting. Feedback from real competition with real rewards will help us refine balance, progression, card accessibility, and the overall experience as we work toward full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We are currently aiming to leave Early Access roughly March 17th, 2027. This is an approximate timeframe and may change based on player feedback, development progress, and when we believe the game is ready for a full release. We will continue to share updates with the community as development progresses.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The core Origins experience will be available during Early Access. As development continues, we plan to introduce new features, more content, and make a wider variety of cards accessible to players. Community feedback will help us refine how that content is introduced while we improve balance, progression, and the overall experience.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “Origins already features a robust competitive experience built around fast-paced online matches, simultaneous turns, three dynamic lanes, and a large pool of rotating locations with abilities that shake up the game. Early Access will build on that foundation by releasing new features and giving players access to more meaningful, tradable collectible rewards. We will continue to expand our playable card pool, refine balance, and improve the overall experience with help from the community.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “No. Origins Trading Card Game will remain free-to-play and free-to-compete forever. Both during Early Access and after its full release.  Early Access will feature some premium paid features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Community feedback will continue to be central to the development of Origins throughout Early Access. We will keep asking for feedback through Discord, Steam discussions, and playtest feedback forms. That input will help us improve the competitive metagame, refine balance and progression, expand card accessibility, and shape future content and expansion sets. Outsmart opponents, collect iconic characters like Mulan & Robin Hood, and trade to build the ultimate collection. Every turn is both players' turn. Outplay your rivals in ~7 minutes. The best competitive games start with a level playing field. That’s why every Origins player can earn all the cards they need to build competitive decks, for free. Spending money will never unlock stronger cards or give you an advantage in gameplay. Collect rare, hand-drawn cosmetic variants of your favorite cards, then trade them with other players in the marketplace. Rip limited-edition collectibles, chase legendary pulls, and discover grail cards prized for their artistry and rarity, not their gameplay power. Both players move at once. Simultaneous turns means no more waiting for your opponent to take their turn. Draw, predict, and battle across three dynamic locations. Outplay your opponent in ~7 minutes. Origins is an original world inspired by history's most iconic characters like Robin Hood, Queen of Hearts, & Winnie-the-Pooh. Earth got their names right and everything else wrong.
