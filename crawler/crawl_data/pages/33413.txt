Ipsen and additional composers. Embark on a musical journey through a vibrant collection of 38 original tracks, spanning 2 hours and 28 minutes of warmth, creativity, and adventure. From tranquil melodies to uplifting rhythms, each piece captures the heart and soul of Planet Zoo 2's living, breathing world. Whether you're designing breathtaking habitats, managing your thriving zoo, or simply taking a moment to admire the animals you've released into the wild, these enchanting compositions provide the perfect accompaniment as you build, nurture, and protect wildlife across the globe. Let the soundtrack's signature blend of folk-inspired charm transport you to a place where nature flourishes and imagination knows no bounds. Led by J.J.
