View Community Hub About This Content ABOUT THIS CONTENT Dig in and give your party a fighting chance with the Dust to Dust Character Pack! The Dust to Dust Character Pack introduces two new playable characters - The Gravedigger and The Pugilist. These characters bring a host of new content to Fahrul to aid you in your journey - new equipment, a new trait, a new pet, and more! The Gravedigger toils the lands and is the keeper of souls for those who pass on from the living realm of Fahrul. Carefully treading the fine line between brilliance and madness through the mysterious and powerful Soul Lantern, the Gravedigger has two paths they may walk. Will you favour the Ardent glow, or steep yourself in Gloom and Doom to wield the powers of Dimwick? The Pugilist was born fighting in the streets and takes up arms… literally. Favouring unarmed combat, the Pugilist builds up combos through strength and speed to unleash their limit break ability. Always seeking out the sweet science of fisticuffs, the Pugilist yearns to be the Champion of Fahrul. Adventurers of Fahrul, the souls of our heroes have never been in safer hands. There has never been a greater time to take up arms in the fight for Fahrul.
