Vai all'hub della Comunità Informazioni sul gioco Intro Anna hides from her mother in an old cursed house and willingly steps into the mystery trapped inside it. Decades ago, a Goldfish granted a couple of wishes and never returned to Lukomorye. So HE brought Lukomorye after her. Now Anna must explore the house, follow its old stories, and find the Goldfish herself, as she has a wish of her own. Gameplay Explore an old cursed house in first person. Search its rooms, collect useful items, solve puzzles, and use what you find to open hidden passages and uncover secrets. Piece together the house’s stories through notes, objects, characters, and the environment itself. Exploration and puzzles are at the heart of the game, but sometimes you’ll have to pick up a weapon and defend yourself Features First-person exploration Puzzle solving Exploring environments for important objects Occasional light combat Alchemy-based crafting used in puzzles and combat Dark fairy-tale atmosphere Storytelling that rewards attention Original soundtrack Approximately 4 hours of gameplay Descrizione del contenuto per adulti Ecco come gli sviluppatori descrivono questi contenuti: The game contains themes of domestic abuse, murder, implied suicide, and references to multiple deaths.
