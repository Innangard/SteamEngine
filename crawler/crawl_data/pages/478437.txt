The "Child's Heart" train-mobile special train to CTcity is now officially open to traffic. Let's set off together to start a fantasy adventure in this city! "CTcity" is a casual puzzle game. The screen adopts the style of cartoon picture books, and the clues of the grid puzzle are also full of childlike interest. Paired with carefully arranged pure music, it creates a refreshing and healing experience. After completing the main chapter of the game, the map editor will be unlocked on the main interface. In the editor, players can freely edit and build various city modules. Each module can be personalized according to the player's design. They can also use various decorations to beautify the city to make it more suitable for the city they envision. In addition, the editor supports players to upload their own levels to the creative workshop to experience the creative results of other players around the world. After completing the main chapter of the game, the "City Fragments" mode will be unlocked on the main interface. In this mode, each map is composed of randomly generated road grids. Players must maximize the collection of props and obtain scores through carefully designed routes within a limited number of steps to ensure that they can effectively explore the map. And collect as many "urban fragments" as possible. “Dear passengers, welcome to CTcity. I sincerely hope that you can find your own pure happiness in our city and fall in love with this charming small city.” System Requirements Minimum: OS: Windows7 Processor: i5-4670K Memory: 4 GB RAM Graphics: GTX 960 DirectX: Version 10 Storage: 4 GB available space Sound Card: None Additional Notes: None Recommended: OS: Windows10 Processor: i7-7700K Memory: 8 GB RAM Graphics: GTX 1660 DirectX: Version 11 Storage: 4 GB available space Sound Card: None Additional Notes: None View all What Curators Say 2 Curators have reviewed this product.
