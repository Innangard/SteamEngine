Welcome to Peel The F*king Banana, a game dedicated to the noble art of removing the outside of a banana to reveal… a banana. Pick a banana. Peel it. Watch your count go up. Do it again. Different bananas. Same important mission. Explore different banana types and give each one the peeling it deserves. Find your favorite, spread your efforts across the bunch, or dedicate yourself to one type with a level of commitment your friends may struggle to understand. Make every banana count. Keep track of how many bananas you’ve peeled with in-game statistics. Every peeled banana is another contribution to a number you will become surprisingly invested in. Your peeling career includes: Banana peeling. Lots of it. Different types of bananas to peel. In-game statistics tracking your peeling efforts. Planned features: bigger banana ambitions. The following Steam features are planned but are not yet implemented: An overall Steam leaderboard for total bananas peeled. Separate Steam leaderboards for each banana type. Steam achievements celebrating your peeling milestones. The goal? Give your increasingly questionable banana obsession a little recognition.
