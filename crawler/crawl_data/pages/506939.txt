Buy Looking For Fael Soundtrack $9.99 Add to Cart View Community Hub About This Content Looking For Fael's original soundtrack is composed entirely by Yann Van Der Cruyssen, known as Morusque and Nurykabe. A beautiful soundtrack occilating between calm lonely melodies and tense atmospheric tracks.The score's style that shifts deliberately throughout the adventure, echoing the different atmospheres of the each apartment. Recurring motifs and symmetries punctuate the game like musical echoes of the puzzles, as if each melody were itself a riddle to solve. A true chiptune expert, Yann comes from the world of video games, where he moves just as comfortably between creating games and composing original scores. His credits include the music for A Game of Thrones, Aarklash Legacy and Confrontation. He has also released four EPs, forms one half of the duo “The Cheat Code,” and loves sharing his passion for chip music and circuit-bending.
