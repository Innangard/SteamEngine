Forge your guns with randomized gun parts, collect passive abilities, and embed gun-changing runes into your guns to create the ultimate build. Enter the arena, lay waste to hordes of enemies, eradicate bosses, and extract with gold. Forge your guns Forge your guns from randomized gun parts. Get the right damage, fire rate, and range for your build. Add runes to your guns to completely change their behavior. Make enemies explode into gold on hit. Add ricochet to bullets. Spawn pillars on enemy death. Collect passive abilities Choose from dozens of passive abilities after each wave. Call down lightning storms, spawn "Blood Brothers", use your stamina as health, and more! Create the perfect build by choosing the right passives for your guns, or by forging the right guns for your passives! Create wild builds & fight challenging enemies Become overpowered. Shoot bananas. Forge a cow-gun. Get private security. Become a glass cannon. Become the opposite of a glass cannon (obsidian paperweight?) Slay waves of challenging enemies. Encounter and destroy 6 unique bosses. Fight through each of the 3 arenas to encounter more difficult and unique enemies. Lock in your progression Use the gold extracted from each run to purchase permanent upgrades. Gain the Loot Goblin as a companion. Increase your max health. Unlock the rocket gun. Increase your Prize Wheel spins. Enhance weapon customizability. And more!
