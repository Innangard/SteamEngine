Control and Master mutiple experimental Hydra Helicopters to destroy this new threat to the nation! Direct various weapons to destroy battleships and earn shards. Spend Shards to boost damage, increase firepower, and unlock new regions. Can you find a way to protect your country from the Island Legion?
