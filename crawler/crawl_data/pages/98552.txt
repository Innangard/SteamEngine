What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Early Access gives us the ability to receive and implement community feedback in a way that helps us build a better game faster. The Devil's Chosen is the type of experience where community feedback will be key, and Early Access will allow for that feedback loop from the start. Our game contains some mechanics that are not present in other games, especially in battle, we would like to have contact with the player and analyze any and all feedback he has about the project to better balance these mechanics.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We want to make the best experience possible and putting an exact timeline on a game early in development is very challenging. That said, we also want to be transparent with our community, so we'll give you our best guess knowing that these plans may change. The current target for the full release of The Devil's Chosen is sometime in 2023.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “There is a ton of great content we want to deliver with The Devil's Chosen but more importantly some of the ideas and features may change dramatically based on the feedback we receive from the community. Our current plan for the full release is to have the following features: Complete storyline Steam Achievements and Cards. More Monsters, Demons, NPCS, Survivors. More maps and locations. More Weapons, Items, Collectibles. Additional features and updated systems to improve the whole experience ” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “At the start of Early Access, the following features/content will be available in The Devil's Chosen , but all content should be considered work in progress: Groundwork laid for the intended plot 1 map and 2 locations Stealth system Crafting Single player game ” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We greatly appreciate our community's willingness to support and engage with the development of The Devil's Chosen . Therefore, early supporters will receive a benefit as we will gradually increase the price of The Devil's Chosen in early access as we add more content and features.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We will be active on all social media responding and speaking to game buyers who speak up, trying to keep the community active, and we will respond to every review on Steam, and will listen, discuss and incorporate your feedback from start to finish.” Read more Buy The Devil's Chosen $7.99 Add to Cart Buy Opala Project Games Collection (FOR GIFTS) Includes 8 items: BasCatball Mars , BasCatball Jupiter , BasCatball Saturn , Panqueca & Paçoca: Friendship Jigsaw , Thats Life of Ralph , Matching Cats , The Devil's C hosen , Fatec's out Show less … Show more Bundle info -50% -54% $21.92 $10.17 54% off. $21.92 normally, discounted to $10.17 Add to Cart Add to Account Buy Opala Project Games Bundle BUNDLE (?)
