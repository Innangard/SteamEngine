More than a classic adventure game, it places the bureaucratic burden and dark reality of a genuine investigation on your shoulders. Key Features: In-Depth Investigation: Examine police reports, autopsy documents, interrogation transcripts, and suspects' journals word by word. Find the contradictions and expose the lies. Retro Digital Detective Work: Step back into the technology of the 2000s. Use the POISON-OS terminal system to access confidential hospital records and encrypted files. Detective's Intuition: When you lose your way in the darkness of the investigation and hit a dead end, cryptic notes appearing on the corkboard will guide your thoughts. The system never gives you the answer directly; it only whispers the truth to help you focus on the right clues. Solve the Mystery: Uncover the dirty schemes hidden within the sterile hallways of this psychiatric clinic and expose the secrets the suspects are hiding.
