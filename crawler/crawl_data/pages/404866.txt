Five Nights with Dino is a tight little horror game about the night guard at the «Dinozavrik» family diner, where everything hangs on a power meter and your nerve. The shift starts at midnight and ends at six. You get eight security cameras, two doors, two floodlights and a tablet — all running off a single supply. Every system you switch on drains the meter faster, and once it hits zero you are left in the dark with nothing left to do. You start budgeting from the first minute. Three animatronics, and no two of them are handled the same way. Green Dino walks to the left door in the open — hear the footsteps, flick the light, shut the door. A door will not stop Yellow Gosha: while he is on his way the cameras push him back, and once he is standing in the doorway only the floodlight saves you, because he is shy. Purple Triceraptych sits in the storage room and stays patient exactly as long as you keep an eye on him — neglect him and he bolts through the kitchen, rattling the dishes, and reaches the right door in seconds. Between all that, sunflower seeds turn up on the cameras. Collect them, then spend them in the shop on a thermos of tea that gives you back a little power at the worst possible moment. Your boss Mikhalych calls in every night and explains exactly as much as he feels like explaining. Five story nights, a sixth overtime shift and a custom night where you set each dinosaur's aggression yourself. Plus leaderboards, if you feel like measuring up against everyone else. KEY FEATURES: RESOURCE-DRIVEN HORROR. Doors, lights and the tablet all feed off one meter — survival is deciding what to switch off right now; THREE DISTINCT THREATS. Doors, cameras and floodlights each work on one of them, and working out which is part of the job; EIGHT CAMERAS, ONE OF THEM BLIND. The kitchen is audio only: rattling dishes are your only warning; CUSTOM NIGHT. A separate aggression slider for every dinosaur, from a warm up to the maximum; SEEDS AND A SHOP. Collect seeds on the cameras and upgrade your thermos; RUSSIAN HUMOUR. Pager calls from the boss, posters on the walls and dinosaurs who comment on how your night is going; 12 STEAM ACHIEVEMENTS AND 2 LEADERBOARDS; TWO LANGUAGES: Russian and English. Six hours until morning. The power is dropping. Someone is already in the hall.
