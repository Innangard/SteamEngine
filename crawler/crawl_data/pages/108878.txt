Ganglian Travel: Slip through a space-time ganglion and visit wildly different alien colonies, each stranger than the last. About This Game Awaken as an alien. How did you get here? No idea! What's your name? No one knows! Explore the strange colonies of a spa-cult civilization to find your way home. Unravel cosmic jokes, befriend digital ghosts, and discover what “you” means in a universe built on absurdity. In JEF, you’re dropped into the middle of a cosmic walkabout that went off the rails centuries ago. The world is beautiful but meaningless — alien cities glitter with purpose that you'll never comprehend. Through exploration and interaction, maybe you can piece together who you are and what you're doing here. Or maybe not! The world hates you! It happens. You don't come here as a conqueror. You are a visitor. A drifter. A wanderer. And here you'll find a bathhouse cathedral. A religious resort. A simulation of life. You desperately want to understand every encounter but it feels like you're overhearing a half-jumbled secret instead. At its heart, JEF is an existential comedy about memory, faith, and the weirdness of being alive. You’re a tourist in your own consciousness, just trying to laugh before the void logs you out.
