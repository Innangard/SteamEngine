Hand drawn, animated, coded, designed, etc, etc by (mostly) one dude. Enjoy simple controls to shoot and melee in different directions. Shoot to stun enemies, melee them at close range! Hit stunned enemies for MASSIVE DAMAGE! Fight in locales ranging from one of oldest cities in Mexico to An Alien Spaceship. Pick up and play gameplay. Pick the controller, punch aliens! Play solo or with a friend in local co-op. About 20 short diverse stages for 45 to 60 minutes of breezy, gutsy action! Replay to get better score! Bring a friend! Two Characters to choose! Featuring music by Tokyo Based Music Producer Ghostpops, and City-Pop Sensation Neko Milkshake and the Mex Lo-Fi Legend Palmasur. * Note on coop: It requires two controllers connected to the same pc for local coop.
