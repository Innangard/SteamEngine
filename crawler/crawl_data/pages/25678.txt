View Community Hub About This Game Visit our camp to seek help from one of the fantasy fighters, and delve into the brick-breaking adventure. Collect gems and coins to upgrade your heroes, aiding you in facing dangerous enemies along your journey. Break barricades and get to the enemy side to beat them once and for all. Tiny Breakers Camp is an indie game that combines the classic Brick Breaker action with Rogue-lite experience. The game features meta-progression for heroes and a linear campaign where players can unlock new spells and skills—all set in an adorable, fantasy pixel world. A mix like no other. Choose your hero. Every hero has his own special attack, health pool and talent for magic. Break the barricades on the battlefield. You will face many obstacles on the battlefield, boxes, chest, books or gems. Destroy them to open you a way and defeat your enemy. Plan your attack carefully because some obstacles are indestructible. Collect all goodies. Try to catch all falling treasures, spells and buffs. You can spend coins and gems to upgrade your heroes. Cast powerful spells or even bring a support on the battlefield. Beat your enemies! Enemies have their own spells and minions. If you want to survive the mission, use all your arsenal to deal with them quickly. Liberate your land in challenging campaign. Uncover spells and buffs by beating campaign missions in this tiny fantasy world. You can also play random missions to collect more gems and coins. Go and get them Hero! BUM! BAM! ...and Win! Controls: Enjoy your game playing with keyboard or game controller if you like, Tiny Breakers Camp supports both.
