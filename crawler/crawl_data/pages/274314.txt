Discover the four interconnected psychological horror stories. Explore different retro pixel art 1 bit to 8 bit environments in each of the stories. Discover the mysterious relationship you have with your psychiatrist. From a third person view in the purest retro style to a first person system reminiscent of old labyrinth games. In a mysterious and strange consultation with the psychiatrist, he analyses you by making you play disturbing video games, with different endings. You experience past traumas, occult cults, a cursed bunker and much more.
