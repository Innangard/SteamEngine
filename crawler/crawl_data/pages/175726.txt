Unlike classic bullet hell shooters, here YOU decide how your attack looks. Place emitters, adjust patterns using sliders, and create unique projectile sequences to dominate the battlefield! You can modify your patterns in real time to adapt to new enemy waves. After each wave, you gain experience — level up and choose from powerful upgrades to enhance your tower or emitters! ⚔️ Sample Upgrade System: Tower Reinforcement – increases your tower's max health. Emitter Spikes – damage enemies that come close. Electric Aura – zaps nearby enemies. Reflective Shield – returns a portion of damage to the attacker. Railgun – a powerful beam that pierces everything in its path. Emitter Fortification – emitters become obstacles that block enemy movement. Auto-Repair – your tower regenerates health over time. 🚀 Game Modes Available in Demo: Normal Mode – a classic survival mode to test your patterns and skills. Includes a short campaign (1–3 missions) and the beginning of the 4th mission — continue your progress in the full version! Sandbox, and Survival modes are available only in the full version. Tower Defense – A classic tower defense mode with progressive difficulty. Enemies move along set paths, and each round becomes increasingly intense. The final stage turns into an endless survival battle. Perfect for fans of traditional tower defense games. ✨ Demo Features: You can save anytime — even mid-battle — and resume exactly where you left off, with every bullet, enemy, and ability preserved. (Works in the demo too!) Controller support (gamepad), mouse and keyboard. Unique pattern editor – bullet hell attacks created with sliders! Save and load your patterns (shareable via JSON). Real-time pattern editing during combat. Tower and emitter upgrade system. Roguelike elements with high replayability. Retro graphics inspired by 90s SHMUP (Shoot 'em up) style. Auto-battler with special active skills — mines, time-slowing, sniper shot, and more, triggered via hotkeys and aimed directly at enemies or zones. Time control – speed up or slow down the game to your liking. Projectiles can have different patterns and colors – like pink! 🧪 Demo Limitations: The demo ends after wave 9 – the full version continues beyond that and includes a final boss . Sandbox, and Survival modes are not included in the demo. The demo will not receive future content updates – new abilities, artifacts, and features will be exclusive to the full version. This demo gives you a taste of Pattern Survivors and lets you explore its core mechanics and chaos!
