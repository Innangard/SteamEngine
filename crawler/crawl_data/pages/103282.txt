Adaugă în coș Vezi centrul comunitar al jocului Despre acest joc Halley's Big Catch Halley is a cartoon cat who takes fishing seriously. He comes home to Hidden Pond for a quiet day on the dock — and finds the start of something much bigger. Relics. Waters all over the world. The old fish stories from childhood start to feel closer than they used to. This is a cozy, readable fishing adventure: warm scenery, great water, and fights that feel like the fish, not a health bar. On the water Cast, hook, fight — timing, drag, and tackle match matter Hidden Pond and beyond — ponds, creeks, reefs, open ocean, and more Dozens of species — keep a bag, fill the index, chase the ones that got away Gear that changes the fight — combos for still water, fly water, and blue water A journal, not a lecture — the story sits in what you find The trail A worn medallion. Strange pieces waiting on distant shores. When Halley finds one, the medallion reacts. The rest you learn by fishing. For players who like Character fishing games with a real fight Exploring a new shore instead of grinding one pond A story that stays a little mysterious A cartoon world that still takes the water seriously Coming soon on Steam. Halley, the story, fishing, and locations are human-authored. Worlds and water use traditional game systems, not live AI generation.
