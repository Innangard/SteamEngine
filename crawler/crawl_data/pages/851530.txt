The CSX Hanover Subdivision route for Train Simulator extends from Porters, Pennsylvania (east of Hanover, Pa.) to Hagerstown, Maryland. The eastern section of the line passes through rolling and classic "Pennsylvania Dutch" farmlands, while the western portion of the route is dominated by the crossing of the towering Blue Ridge Mountains and features grades of up to 2 percent and two horseshoe curves! Across its full length, the route is loaded with more than 20 line-side industries, including massive quarries and cement plants which generate heavy tonnage. And the route passes through a famous and historic part of America, including Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, site of the largest and most important battle of the American Civil War, where the rail line passes directly through a portion of the preserved National Battlefield. The CSX Hanover Subdivision route includes a superb and diverse selection of freight rolling stock and four locomotives, highlighted by a unique CSX SD40-3 rebuilt locomotive along with a CSX GE Dash 8-40CW in YN3b livery, CSX EMD SD40-2, and a short line EMD GP9. As an engineer on the 62-mile CSX Hanover Subdivision route, you’ll face the challenges of heavy-haul railroading on roller-coaster grades! And in addition to main line operations, you will tackle a variety of local and switching operations at lineside industries and amid the urban settings and railway yards of Hanover, Gettysburg, and Hagerstown! Experience challenging and realistic modern railroading amid the historic and scenic American countryside of Pennsylvania and Maryland – with the CSX Hanover Subdivision route for Train Simulator! Included Scenarios Nine realistic and challenging career scenarios are included, putting you at the throttle of contemporary CSX motive power for mainline, local, and switching operations on the Hanover Subdivision. The route also includes a free-roam scenario and is Quick Drive compatible. CSX D796, Baltimore Bound CSX D797, East Slope Descent Short Line Switch Job CSX D795, Gettysburg Bound CSX K943, Rock Runner Duty CSX D778, Hanover Evening Duty CSX D795, Destination Hagerstown CSX D797, West Slope Climb CSX D997, Interchange and Grain Gettysburg Free Roam More scenarios are available on the Steam Workshop online and in-game.
