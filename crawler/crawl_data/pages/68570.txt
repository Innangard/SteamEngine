Közösségközpont megnézése A játékról One cannon. A whole neighborhood waiting for its parcels. Line up a shot, watch your parcel fly, collect the payout, and put it toward your next upgrade. Parcel Cannon combines hands-on physics shooting with the satisfaction of a delivery business getting bigger, faster, and a little more ridiculous. Make every shot count Pick your target, judge the arc, and send a parcel home. Different receiving spots, colored parcels, and transport contraptions give you new shots to try and satisfying deliveries to string together. Buy upgrades you can feel Visit the Workshop to improve your cannon, air supply, and transport routes. Faster loading, multi-shot bursts, spread shots, and Rush turn careful single deliveries into a sky full of parcels. Spend your next payout on more firepower or a new way to deliver. Put playful routes to work Unlock rails, lifts, and conveyors that catch, carry, and redirect your parcels to residents beyond your cannon’s direct reach. Each new contraption brings another way to keep the neighborhood’s deliveries moving. Deliver somewhere strange Travel beyond the sunny courtyard to vampire, rainforest, and magical neighborhoods. Try coffin launchers, bat relays, leaf chutes, and portals. Where will your next parcel go? Complete delivery contracts, earn your next upgrade, and come back ready to send more.
