Take on multiple difficulties for virtually every track, each level hand-choreographed to sync to the music every time. Push your skills even further to collect the hidden hearts for some extra insurance against tougher sections. Carry them all the way to the end to unlock extra tracks and modes. And when you've mastered everything there is to dodge, craft your own masterpiece in the Level Editor or check out the hundreds of community levels made over the last decade to see what it really means to play an "impossible" level.
