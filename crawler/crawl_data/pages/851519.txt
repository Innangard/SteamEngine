This freight loco from developers RSSLO introduces an up-to-date diesel-powered version of the Traxx locomotive platform to Train Simulator. The Traxx platform is a widely-used locomotive series which can be found at work across Europe. The BR285 mainly operates in Germany, where its 140km/h top speed and ability to work non-electrified routes makes it a very useful member of the loco fleet. As recreated for Train Simulator by RSSLO, the BR285 runs in a clean paint scheme without operator logos, such as it might have been produced at the factory, and before the fleet operator applies their own branding.  The loco features PZB, SIFA and a realistic train length system, as well as three career scenarios for the Mosel Valley: Koblenz – Trier route. Included Scenarios A selection of engaging Career, Free Roam and Quick Drive scenarios for the Koblenz – Trier route are included: Night Freight Train Passenger Train to Bullay Evening Rush More scenarios are available on the Steam Workshop online and in-game. Train Simulator’s Steam Workshop scenarios are free and easy to download, adding many more hours of exciting gameplay. With scenarios being added daily, why don’t you check it out now!
