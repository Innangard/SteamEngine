You start with nothing, no home, no money, no safety, and must fight to survive each day. Hunger, debt, and danger are everywhere, but so are the chances to rise again. Every choice matters, and the streets will test your will to endure. From begging, recycling, and stealing to studying, working, and even taking risks in casinos or arcades, your path is your own. Will you remain trapped in poverty, or climb from the cold pavement to wealth, pride, and freedom? Expect unique features such as: ♻️ Recycling System On the streets, every piece of trash has value. Gather bottles, cans, and scrap, and trade them for cash to survive another day. 🤲 Begging Mechanic Not everyone shows mercy. Beg for food or cash, but be ready to face rejection, insults, or worse. 🎰 Arcades & Casino Life on the streets is tough on body and mind. Escape for a while in arcades or test your luck at the casino. Will fortune lift you up, or take what little you have left? 🕒 Living the City Day and Night The city changes with every hour. Work during the day, find shelter at night. Manage your time and money wisely to survive in this vast urban world. 💼 Study & Work Education and effort can change everything. Take small jobs, study new skills, and work your way out of poverty toward a brighter future. 🚗 Cars & Housing Life on the streets won’t last forever. Save money to buy your first car or even a place to call home. Every step takes you closer to stability and success. 🕶️ Theft System Desperate times call for desperate acts. Steal discreetly to survive. Fail and the police will make you pay.
