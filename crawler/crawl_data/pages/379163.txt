The familiar world disappeared beneath the waters, leaving survivors adrift in the vast ocean. Humanity's only salvation was the «Isla Muvimi», floating islands that became centers of trade, war and survival. Your home is your fortress Your mobile island is a living machine that requires resources. Collect artifacts, earn money in dangerous forays and invest it in upgrading your buildings. Personal mission In this story, inspired by the atmosphere of "Waterworld" starring Kevin Costner, you embark on a search for your missing father. He vanished, leaving only clues leading to a mythical continent. And only your «Isla Movie» can help you reach him. Grow your mobile island to discover the most remote regions and change the fate of the sunken world. Heart of the Island: Build, manage, and upgrade your floating base. Each building is a step toward technological superiority and new capabilities. Steampunk arsenal: Man turrets to repel attacks, command fighter jets or don a heavy diving suit for dangerous dives to the ocean floor. A vibrant economy and progression: Complete missions and discover rare resources to transform your island into an impenetrable fortress. Mysteries of the deep sea: From surface battles to uncovering the secrets of sunken cities, your capabilities grow with experience and equipment. Are you ready to face the ocean?
