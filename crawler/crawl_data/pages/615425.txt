And where are his friends? Explore strange and beautiful islands to find all the little kittens hidden there. You are an adventurer, the last of the Seekers of Cats, and you are tasked with an important job. Scattered on those islands are little cats, some of them are cold and hungry and yet still hoping someone will find them. Others don't really care about you or your quest but meow insistently anyway so you should probably stop for a moment and pet them too.
