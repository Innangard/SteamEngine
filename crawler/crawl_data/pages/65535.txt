Află mai multe Ce au de spus producătorii: De ce acces timpuriu? „Dimraeth is the most ambitious project our team has ever taken on, an open-world action RPG with co-op, crafting, base-building, character classes, and dozens of overlapping systems. As a small studio, the best way to get those systems balanced, the world feeling alive, and the player journey landing right is to put it in your hands as early as possible and tune it together. We've already been collecting playtest data and Discord feedback for months, and it's directly shaped the game. Early Access is the next step of that loop.” Aproximativ cât timp se va afla acest joc în acces timpuriu? „We aren't setting a fixed date as our priority is quality and reaching every milestone on our roadmap before we leave Early Access. Roughly we expect a 18 to 24 month window, but the pace at which we land major content updates (new zones, races, end-game systems)  and act on player feedback will guide the timeline more than any calendar. We'll keep the community updated as milestones approach.” Cum se va diferenția versiunea completă de cea disponibilă în acces timpuriu? „We plan to keep growing the world, the character options, and the depth of every system. That includes new biomes and zones beyond the current Earlwood and Wildwood regions, additional playable races and classes, an expanded late-game skill and equipment ceiling, more boss encounters, deeper crafting and building options, and a longer overall story arc. Throughout Early Access we'll also be tuning combat, co-op pacing, and progression based on what we're learning from the community.” Care este starea actuală a versiunii aflate în acces timpuriu? „We have currently 3 playable races (minotaur, elves, humans) and classes (brawlers, mages, shadows). Dimraeth is fully playable today as a co-op action RPG. The current build includes multiple playable races and classes, a complete starter region (TheLostCaverns through Earlwood Village and into the Wildwood Forest), the full crafting and base-building loop, a working skill tree and attribute system, equipment with rarity tiers and upgrades, online co-op for friend groups, and multiple boss encounters. The core gameplay loop, explore, fight, gather, build, level up, group up, is in and stable. Early Access will expand the world around it and deepen every system.” Jocul va avea prețuri diferite pe durata accesului timpuriu și după părăsirea acestuia? „Yes, we plan to gradually raise the price as we add more content and polish during Early Access, with a higher price at full release. Early Access supporters get the best deal and shape the game with us; the price reflects the growing scope as we go.” Cum intenționezi să implici comunitatea în procesul tău de dezvoltare? „Our community is central to how Dimraeth is being built. Our Discord server is where everything happens day-to-day patch notes, bug reports, balance discussion, build sharing, and direct conversation with the dev team. We also read every Steam review and discussion thread, run regular playtest waves, and instrument the game with detailed anonymous analytics so we can see where players struggle and what they love, then act on it. Explore a handcrafted pixel-art fantasy world alone or with up to seven other players in seamless drop-in/drop-out co-op. The Shadow Over Mirkea Corruption spreads across Mirkea. Sylbos, Last of the Ogre Magi, seeks to claim Dimraeth and the power upon which it stands. By a twist of fate, you are granted an ancient power you do not understand. The venerable wizard Faustas recruits you to stand with Mirkea’s free peoples against Sylbos and the dark gods. Create Your Archetype Combine any of eight races with any of six classes to create 48 distinct Archetypes. Each pairing has its own skill tree and playstyle. Every Attack Is a Commitment, Manage your stamina, read enemy telegraphs, and position yourself before choosing to strike, dodge, block, or parry. Equip five spells at a time and build tactical rotations and combinations around your loadout. Your race and class offer their own spells, while crafted spellbooks grant access to universal magic, provided you meet each spell’s attribute requirements. More than 300 spells are available in total. Test your build against demanding enemies and bosses. Adventure, Then Return Home Explore Mirkea, completing quests and recruiting allies as you fight, claim loot, and gather resources. Return home to craft equipment, brew potions, and cook hearty meals that increase your vitality. Build farms, quarries, and other structures for a steady supply of crops and raw materials. Deeper relationships unlock new conversations, personal quests, and potential romances. Invest materials in your allies’ facilities to expand shop inventories, add new crafting services, and open more farming plots. Every journey leaves your hero stronger and your home more complete. Together or Alone Play alone or in seamless drop-in/drop-out co-op for up to eight players. Your hero’s level, equipment, inventory, spells, and personal progression travel between worlds, while story progress, recruited allies, and buildings remain tied to each world. Dimraeth is in active development toward Early Access Release on September 15th 2026.
