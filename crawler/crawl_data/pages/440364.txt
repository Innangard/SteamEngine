View Community Hub About This Game Welcome to MY HOTEL – a place where every corridor hides a secret, and every step could be your last. The 1950s, Chicago. You take control of The Griffin Royal Hotel – a legendary place that once thrived with life, but now stands as a silent witness to a dark history. Add "My Hotel" to your wishlist on Steam if you dare to uncover the secrets that have endured within its walls. Decades ago, the hotel belonged to your uncle, William Griffin, a man whose story did not end on his own terms. He was brutally murdered, and the culprit was never found. Now, twenty years later, you return to Chicago as Thomas Griffin. You face a difficult mission – unraveling the mystery of his death and restoring the hotel to its former glory. But remember, every answer might lead to more questions, and the past rarely reveals its secrets without a fight. Forge alliances to survive. Four factions rule Chicago – each one powerful, ruthless, and willing to do whatever it takes to achieve their goals. The Local Police, the Italian Mafia, the Russian Gang, and the enigmatic Damocles. Take on their tasks, earn their trust, but be careful – one wrong move could destroy everything you've built. Every mission carries risk, and if one of the factions marks you as an enemy... well, in Chicago, there’s no way out of that. Run the hotel as if your life depends on it. Your guests are as diverse as they are mysterious, and your choices have consequences. Manage the rooms, run the bar, fight off rats, and pay attention to every detail because every moment of negligence could cost you more than you think. Collect your uncle Griffin’s antiques – pieces of history that may prove crucial. Choose your guests wisely, for not everyone comes with good intentions. Solving the mystery of William Griffin’s death, balancing alliances, and managing the hotel are challenges that won’t forgive mistakes. You might not succeed on your first attempt – you might never discover everything hidden within the Griffin Royal. But if you have the courage, this place is waiting for you. The only question is, are you ready to face the dark past of The Griffin Royal Hotel?
