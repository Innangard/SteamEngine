But the plan unravels as they discover they are not alone. Many objects in the hotel are interactable and can be picked up, inspected, or combined in creative ways. The game relies on stealth and tension rather than combat or gore. It can be played in two-player co-op or single-player. What begins as a simple heist quickly turns into a nightmare as you discover the hotel is haunted by a Percht and other creatures from Alpine folklore. Uncover the dark secret of this forsaken place by exploring its haunted halls, collecting clues, and piecing together the truth about what was really going on in this so called ski hotel. The Percht reacts dynamically to light, sound, and movement, can open doors and actively searches rooms, and will pursue you if detected. You cannot fight back and must rely on hiding, observation and teamwork. A CCTV surveillance system helps you track his movements throughout the hotel. Survival depends on cooperation and wit as you solve intricate puzzles that guard the hotel's darkest secrets. Coordinate with your partner to unlock sealed areas, complete arcane rituals, and gather the items needed for your escape. Only by working together can you unleash the Alpine magic and make it out of the Alps alive.
