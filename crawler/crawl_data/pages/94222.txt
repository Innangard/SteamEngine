We celebrate you, our royal hero, as we embark on great journeys in expressive new ways together! In the Kingdom Two Crowns: Regent's Royal Wardrobe you’ll get access to three new looks for your Monarch: the Eloquent Queen, the Dark Templar King and the Mycelium Monarch. These looks will unlock on all campaign settings except Dead Lands. They are only visible when you play on Steam. Transferring your save to a different platform will disable them while you play on that platform.
