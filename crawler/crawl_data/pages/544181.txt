Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Voice of Belldona already offers a complete and highly replayable roguelike deckbuilding foundation, including deckbuilding combat, blessings, allies, progression systems, events, and multiple build paths. However, we still want to continue improving the game through real player feedback, especially in areas such as balance, content variety, UI/UX, localization quality, and overall polish. We chose Early Access because we want to develop Voice of Belldona together with the community through active player feedback, rather than staying in closed development for a much longer period of time before release. We believe this approach will help us create a more complete, balanced, and satisfying Full Release experience.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We currently expect Voice of Belldona to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 months. During this period, we plan to continue expanding the game with new systems, cards, blessings, events, allies, difficulty options, story content, balance updates, and localization improvements. If development progresses smoothly, the Full Release may arrive earlier. However, we currently want to leave enough time for proper polishing and iteration to ensure the final version reaches the level of quality we aim for.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The Full Release version is planned to significantly expand and polish the current Early Access version with additional content and improvements, including: More cards, blessings, events, and allies Additional difficulty options and challenges A more complete Story Mode experience Additional systems and gameplay mechanics Further balance improvements Improved UI/UX and quality-of-life features More polished English localization and text improvements Additional optimizations and adjustments based on community feedback Our goal is for the Full Release version to provide a more complete, polished, and content-rich experience while preserving the core roguelike deckbuilding gameplay foundation.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Early Access version already includes the main playable foundation of Voice of Belldona and offers a complete, highly replayable roguelike deckbuilding experience. The current version already includes: A complete core deckbuilding combat system 120+ cards 50+ blessings Multiple progression and build paths Multiple explorable regions and enemies Recruitable allies 40+ narrative events Difficulty progression and replay-focused content Complete run-based gameplay loops While we still plan to continue expanding and polishing the game during Early Access, the current version already provides a substantial amount of content and a fully playable gameplay experience.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “We currently plan to slightly increase the price of Voice of Belldona when the game transitions from Early Access to Full Release. During the Early Access period, we plan to continue expanding the game with additional content, gameplay systems, balance improvements, and overall polish. Because the Full Release version is expected to offer a more complete and content-rich experience, the final price may increase accordingly.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We primarily plan to use Discord and QQ as our main platforms for communicating with the community, allowing us to maintain fast and efficient feedback and interaction channels with players. As long as players share discussion-worthy feedback, suggestions, questions, or ideas related to Voice of Belldona on these platforms, we will do our best to respond and communicate as actively and quickly as possible whenever we see them. She does not remember who she is, or what she once carried. Everything around her fills her with curiosity, though she is a little shy with strangers—yet whenever someone needs help, she steps forward without hesitation. Guided by two trusted mentors, she sets out on a journey to reclaim her lost past, and to understand who she truly is and what her power was meant for. Battle | Assemble, Arrange, Prevail Recruit allies to your cause and arrange your front and back rows—every position can turn the tide. Turn-based battles where every card demands careful thought. Journey | Every Departure, a New Fate The land reshapes itself with every journey, reordering its events, battles, shops and fearsome foes.
