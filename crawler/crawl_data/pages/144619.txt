View Community Hub About This Game Build. Organize. Discover. Complete the keyboard. Welcome to a satisfying world of keyboards, keycaps, and absolute desk chaos. With 2,692 unique keycaps scattered across your workspace, your goal is simple: find the right keycaps, place them in the correct positions, and bring each keyboard back to life. But every keyboard has its own layout, shape, and style. Square or round, patterned or colorful, every board asks for its own keys. Pay attention, search through the mess, and carefully match each keycap to its rightful place. FEATURES • 2,692 keycaps to search through • 32 different keyboards to complete • Classic square boards and round ones, each with its own layout • Unique designs, from bold colors to full-board artwork • Every keycap has its legend printed on it. Read closely to find the right one • Your keyboard collection lines the walls. Pick any board and bring it to your desk • Watch boards light up as you fill them in • Relaxing gameplay with no complicated mechanics • Unwind with a desk radio playing a laid-back soundtrack • Steam Achievements CAN YOU FIND THEM ALL? And when you finally place that last keycap... Perfection.
