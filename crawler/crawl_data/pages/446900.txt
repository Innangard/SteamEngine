You have to be brave and determined to learn all the secrets hidden in dark corners. Good luck, you will need it! Do you dare to face the revenge-seeking, bloodthirsty Krasue or the haunting demon Charles? This is just the beginning, more monsters are waiting for you. Collect as many bags of money as possible and escape from creatures. If by some miracle, you manage to find out where the threat came from and what is lurking around the corner, you will have a chance to solve this puzzle! Discover the secrets of an abandoned homestead, try to survive in a haunted hospital and walk the corridors of the possessed school. There are traps at every side, you can't feel safe anywhere. Collect runes - eyes - painted on the walls. Thanks to them, you gain the ability to see through the eyes of your opponent and you can try to predict his movements.
