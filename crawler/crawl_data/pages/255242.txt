A retro puzzle game with short but complex puzzles. The goal is to clear the board until only one piece remains! Only one piece must remain Pieces can only move on other pieces of different color. Consume other pieces until only one remains. Different piece, different rules Only some pieces can move, others are stationary, some change color and more! What awaits you 90 challenging levels Sound Blaster 16 style soundtrack Customize your experience. Not a fan of the CRT-look? Turn it off! Colorblind support Play with mouse only or use keyboard hotkeys! In-game jukebox.
