Complete your gallery Choose from various illustrations ranging in complexity. Simple and intuitive "drag and drop" controls. Calming soundtrack and satisfying sound effects. The perfect game to disconnect and clear your mind. 🚫 What Shapes of Memory does NOT offer: Complex logic puzzles or punitive difficulty. Multiplayer or online features. New stories or deep narratives (the focus is on the assembly gameplay). Stressful timers or leaderboards.
