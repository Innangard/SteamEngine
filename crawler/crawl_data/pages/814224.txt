View Community Hub Discord About This Game Congratulations, recruit. Team Ballistic has selected you for an extremely important assignment: collect cards. Lots of them. From your desk, you’ll rip packs, sort through your hits, and build the finest collection of Critkin cards. Drew a duplicate? Sell it! New card? Flip through the binder pages to find the right spot and lock it in! Celebrate god-tier pulls, enjoy the thrill, and make your collection grow. 1,809 Cards. One Binder. Start simple: open packs by hand, inspect your best pulls up close, and find a home for every new addition. Discover variants, hunt down exclusive promo cards from our community art competition, and buy higher value packs like the First Edition Boosters, and fill each binder page. Every Critkin you find is logged in your CritDex, and the stats screen keeps score of just how deep the obsession goes. Can’t get your hands on special cards? Take your chances at the Black Market, see what’s on sale, and unlock Black Market upgrades to tip the odds in your favor. For extra income, shake your piggy bank and make it rain! Unlock a Better Way to Rip Your humble desk won’t stay humble for long. Grow your arsenal of strange gadgets by spending Gadget Points. Load packs, boxes or even entire cases into the Rip Rig, speed it up, then add an auto slot so it refills itself. Put the Binder Bot to work moving cards from your mat straight into the binder, and slash its cooldown until it barely stops. Every new card you place in your binder earns XP, with rare cards getting you much closer to the next level and a fresh GP! Your Rhythm. Your Playstyle. There are no timers and no pressure to get the job done quickly. Take your time as you inspect card art, vibe to the music, and find your groove. Need something for idle hands between packs? Fidget the seal, mash some buttons, or give the skateboard a spin. Play in your own style: randomly draw cards from a huge pile to move through your latest haul, arrange them neatly on your desk before you slot them, or upgrade those gadgets to automate the process. Let’s scratch that collector’s itch!
