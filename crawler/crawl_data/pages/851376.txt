At their peak over 600 examples were in service, and they have been running on the Deutsche Bahn network since the late 1990s. In Train Simulator, virtualRailroads’ Hbis-tt Wagon Pack 01 includes an authentic recreation of the wagon and features the RandomSkin system to deliver a realistic unsorted appearance of different logos on consists of wagons.
