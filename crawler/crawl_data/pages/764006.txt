To obtain new goods (outfits and weapons), you will have to hunt legendary animals coming with the Wild Game update and then craft new items. When playing co-op, everyone in the session will gain access to the DLC content, provided that the host has the pack installed. Please note that if the host does not own the DLC, the content will not be available to anyone in the co-op session.
