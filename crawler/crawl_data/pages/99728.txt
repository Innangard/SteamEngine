View Community Hub About This Game Glory on Pluto is a space rocket management roguelite where you build and manage fuel cells and synergise unique items to power the engine. Find the right combination of weird and wacky fuels to generate enough power to break orbit. Optimise your build to reach increasingly further destinations. Discover waypoints and grab bonuses to aid your trip. Look out for the pirates! Recruit and promote dynamic crew members to help your ship. BOOST to each planet, till we reach the Glorious Pluto (and beyond!). Earth is a perfect utopia. V.I.L.L.U, the AI that runs the planet, has ensured so, scrubbing all traces of human history so people may live in ignorant bliss. But one group of people grows restless, striving to uncover humanity’s greatest achievements, proving that there is merit in creating and regaining the essence of what makes them human. Thus was born the rebellious unofficial nation, Wuldoria. In their attempts to unearth humankind’s history, they discovered space travel and a denounced planet known as Pluto. They built a space craft, but are still unclear on how fueling works. This is where you come in - Wuldoria’s finest new recruit. With a space engine that can ingest any material, it’s up to you to find the perfect combination of fuels to power an epic journey to a long forgotten planet. Fuel Your Engine Your engine can hold up to 18 fuel items , split across 3 rings and 6 segments . ANYTHING can be fuel. Load it up, arrange your materials to create powerful synergies within rings and segments then hit BOOST . Each fuel item will provide power, build up your RPM and speed. Reaching each successive waypoint and planet will require a progressively greater amount of power. Replace your fuels, scale them up and optimise your builds. Watch out for potential chaotic events! The core engine can transform unpredictably. Be ready to adapt and triumph over these challenges. Explore the 3 Fuel Types ECOLOGICAL: Cultivate strong synergies as a group of fuels, working together to grow stronger INDUSTRIAL: Harvest resources from other materials to build the strength of one master fuel cell SCIENTIFIC: Utilise the latest Wuldorian Tech and the surrounding environment of your ship Shape Your Run Your journey to each planet is broken up into phases, with waypoints offering random events and encounters. Whether it’s traders, Wuldorian outposts or pirates (or worse), you get to chart your own course. Add persistent upgrades and new fuels, discard the ones dragging you down and recruit new crew members to help you run the ship. Acquire segment modifiers (modulators) to place in your engine, affecting the way items in a segment behave. A modulator may change the efficiency of your materials or even apply mutators to your segments. Find the right ones to optimize your engine. Key Features Engine-builder roguelite focused on synergising unique fuel cells Over 250 weird and wonderful fuel items over multiple categories, each with their own distinctive archetypes and playstyles Over 50 crew members to hire and promote Segment modifiers to further shape your build Waypoints with events, traders and encounters in between planets A sense of glory every time you navigate to a new planet! Find the perfect synergies, or watch your builds get out of control and break your engines.
