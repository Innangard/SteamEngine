Vis fællesskabshub Om dette spil CRACK is a 1-4 player co-op adventure where you and your friends work together to uncover what's inside a mysterious giant egg. There is an egg. It is very big. Your job is simple: crack it open. Don’t ask why. Who built this laboratory? Why are you here? Unlock hidden rooms and upgrade your tools throughout the facility. The Egg reacts to you. Listen closely. Find out what's inside. Whatever happens after that is above your pay grade. I’m building CRACK in public from Day 1!
