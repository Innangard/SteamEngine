This musical collection accompanies Prince Jerian’s journey — from his first steps in the palace to fateful decisions that can change the course of history. Inspired by the classical musical tradition of the XVIII–XIX centuries, the soundtrack brings together orchestral sound, expressive piano parts, and a rich palette of string instruments, creating music that does more than accompany events: it becomes part of the narrative. The soundtrack includes 35 musical compositions reflecting different sides of the game world: the grandeur of the Empire, aristocratic intrigue, religious conflict, personal tragedy, and glimmers of hope. Many tracks are enhanced with recordings of live instruments — violin, morin khuur, jaw harps, and others — giving each composition its own sound and character.
