This is the last job you'll ever have, and you’ll find the work so rewarding that you’ll never want to leave! Kharnym Isle Post is the most efficient mail service for 4,000 miles, which means you have a big job ahead of you. Stamp, sort, deliver, and keep the customers happy throughout your shift.  Feel your shoulders tense as you work?  Feel eyes upon your back as you walk through each door?  These are just the normal first week jitters that anybody would expect as they take on such an important role! There’s a world beyond the doors of the post office, but stepping outside isn’t part of the schedule. At no point should you ever try to leave. And why would you? The schedule doesn’t say anything about leaving, everything you need is right here. Instead, stay safe and secure within the confines of your mandatory room and board. And if you're feeling overwhelmed, don't worry!  Your benevolent boss, Liv, is there to help you through even the hardest days at the post office.  Rely on her to keep your head on straight as you navigate this fast-paced job!
