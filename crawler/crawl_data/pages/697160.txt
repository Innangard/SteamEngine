The Rebuild Expansion includes the following content: • The Timberworks Pack, which includes 3 new forestry-themed vehicles and expands gameplay for industrial, resource, and off-road missions. • The Invictus Type A Scout, a rugged search and rescue vehicle engineered for extreme conditions. With its military-inspired design, powerful engine, and wide, stable base, it offers exceptional balance and control on any terrain. • Two new 4km² maps, each with unique terrain and mission types. Contamination : A dense evergreen forest region in summer, scattered with lakes and rivers. After a train carrying liquid chemicals derailed due to prior seismic damage, the area is threatened by widespread contamination. Players must scout, plan routes, and deploy mobile filtering stations to clean up spills and support a large water treatment facility under construction. Washout : Set in the autumnal swamps near the collapsed dam from the "Deluge" map of the base game, floodwaters have overtaken partially built drilling sites, leaving behind sensitive equipment and hazardous material. Navigate the bogs using treaded vehicles, contain the flooding, and restore critical paths between facilities using player-constructed infrastructure. • Five new vehicles, including the Vostok MU-97 “Ant” and the Tayga 6455B, two Bridge Layers capable of deploying their own crossing structures in the field—perfect for navigating broken terrain and flooded routes. • New mission themes: Face the consequences of negligence and environmental collapse. Clean chemical spills, recover vital equipment, construct bridges, and manage logistics across dynamic, hazardous landscapes.
