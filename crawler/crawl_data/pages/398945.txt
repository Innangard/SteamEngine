View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game CAN YOU SURVIVE 49 DAYS? Survive for 49 days to escape the zone where you've been imprisoned by Z.O.L. Each shift, equip your backpack, pull the main power breaker, and explore the zone. Scavenge valuable items, convert them into scrap, and ship them via the terminal at the starting point. Complete quota deliveries and make it back to your cell before time runs out. But it's never that simple. Evade distinct entities, hide, and stay quiet; they can hear you. Every action impacts your team's financial balance. Your account can drop into the red, but during the audit every 3 days, your balance must remain above 0. Z.O.L. never tolerates losing money. The layout is procedurally generated on every shift. Walls, items, room types, and overall structure shift every time. You aren't confined to classic yellow walls; as time progresses, new zone themes and broader variety will be added to the game. Scavenge, recycle into scrap, ship cargo, deliver, and survive 49 days. (Regularly updated based on community feedback.) It Started as a Delivery. It Became a Contract. You were an ordinary courier crew working for Z.O.L. A late-night delivery popped up on the terminal: an abandoned distribution warehouse, high payout, short route. Hazardous, but seemed doable. You accepted the gig. The moment you stepped inside, the exit vanished behind you. Now you are trapped inside the Backrooms—an endless labyrinth of yellow hallways, buzzing fluorescent lights, and service doors leading nowhere. Z.O.L. gives you only one option: complete the 49-day contract, clear your debt, and earn your freedom. Everything comes at a cost. Ammunition, tools, failed deliveries, damaged cargo, and casualties are deducted from the team's account. A single mistake won't just cost your life—it could void your entire contract. The 49-Day Exit Contract Every three days, Z.O.L. audits the team account. If your balance falls below 0, your exit rights are permanently revoked. Your contract is terminated and you remain in the Backrooms forever. Survival isn't just about breathing. What you collect, what you sell, which deliveries you prioritize, and how much you spend will decide the fate of your entire crew. Gameplay Explore the Backrooms: Search procedurally generated corridors, rooms, storage facilities, and maintenance areas each shift for valuable loot. Scavenge & Scrap: Collect items, convert them into scrap, and increase your team balance. Ship & Deliver: Send collected cargo through the starting terminal and meet delivery quotas before time runs out. Survive the Entities: Evade terrifying creatures, hide, and keep quiet. Each threat exhibits unique behavioral patterns and danger levels. Protect the Budget: Expenses, failed objectives, broken cargo, and teammate deaths drain the shared pool. Keep your balance positive every 3 days. Upgrade Your Gear: Unlock new survival tools, perks, and equipment to prepare for increasingly perilous shifts. Coordinate with Your Crew: Split tasks with up to 6 players. Sticking together is safer, but splitting up is often necessary to gather enough supplies. Features 1–6 Player Online Co-op — Explore, manage logistics, and fight for survival alongside your crew. Procedurally Generated Maps — Walls, chambers, hazards, and floorplans mutate with every single shift. Evolving Environments — New liminal sectors and environmental designs are introduced over time. Shared Team Economy — Operational expenses, cargo profits, and financial penalties directly impact your unified balance. Atmospheric Survival Horror — Evade sound-sensitive entities in ever-shifting, claustrophobic liminal environments. Progression & Loadout System — Purchase high-tier gear to boost your survival odds on subsequent shifts. You Don't Respawn. Your Debt Does. Backrooms Manager - Together is a tactical co-op survival and logistics game for up to 6 players. Explore, scavenge, recycle, dispatch cargo, balance your budget, and survive long enough to fulfill Z.O.L.'s grueling contract. You have 49 days. Keep the account in the green. Keep your teammates alive. Earn your ticket out. Help & Support F1 — Open the in-game Guide. Ctrl + R — Submit feedback. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game contains mature content including frequent depictions of violence, gunfire, and combat against hostile creatures in a horror setting. Players will encounter dead bodies wearing yellow hazmat suits throughout the environment, and may be attacked by grotesque, elongated humanoid entities inspired by the Backrooms mythos. The game relies on psychological tension, jump scares, and creature encounters rather than graphic gore. There is no blood, no realistic weapon models, no sexual content, no drug or alcohol use, and no depictions of sexual assault, self-harm, or hate speech.
