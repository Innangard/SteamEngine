You set out to find her. A mysterious mountain settlement, a gloomy old castle, an underground laboratory, and the ruins of an ancient city await you. FEATURES: Start the game with nothing—no map, weapons, or aiming sights. Explore dark, intricate locations. Face dangerous bosses. Encounter a variety of monsters. Explore the game world. DEFEAT THE BOSSES! Each boss has unique attacks and weaknesses; sometimes, you must interact with the arena environment to secure a victory. EXPLORE INTRICATE LABYRINTHS! Navigate the labyrinths to find hidden items that will aid your progress. COLLECT WEAPONS AND DEFEAT DEMONS! Weapons aren't available immediately, but unlocking them gives you an advantage; blueprints hidden throughout the levels allow you to upgrade your arsenal. FIND CHESTS, KEYS, AND USEFUL ITEMS! Locate items such as quick weapon switching, rapid medkit use, aiming sights, level maps, health upgrades, night vision, and much more. SOLVE PUZZLES! Sometimes opening a door requires a simple key, but other times you'll need to solve a puzzle—whether simple or complex.
