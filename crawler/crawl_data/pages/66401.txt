These properties need a little love, and with the right tools and a good crew, you can bring them back to life one yard at a time. Grab a Tool and Get to Work Mow grass, trim edges, water plants, burn debris, and haul away trash as you transform messy spaces into something you can be proud of. Every tool has its purpose, and using them well makes the job smoother, faster, and more satisfying. There’s no wrong way to help out, but teamwork always pays off. Better Together Grass is built around co-op play. Split up tasks, cover more ground, and coordinate your tools to get the job done efficiently. One person mowing while another trims and cleans turns hard work into a smooth, shared rhythm. Solo play works too, but everything’s better with friends. Work Smart, Relax More You can tackle jobs in any order, but planning ahead makes a difference. Communicate, avoid overlapping work, and watch the yard come together piece by piece. The cleaner and faster the job, the better the outcome. It’s all about finding a flow that feels good. Grow Your Reputation Finish jobs to earn rewards and build your reputation through the mower app. Strong performance unlocks new opportunities, and progress carries across sessions. Whether you’re playing solo or as a group, every job helps you grow. Take Pride In the Result Grass is about visible progress, shared effort, and the quiet satisfaction of standing back and admiring a job well done. Grab your crew, fire up the equipment, and leave each place a little better than you found it.
