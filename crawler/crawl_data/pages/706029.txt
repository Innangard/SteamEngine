It can be disabled in the settings. A giant toy store. Thousands of toys out of place. One mission: put everything back where it belongs. Choose your challenge, from 2,694 toys to ∞, and explore colorful multi-story stores filled with dinosaurs, robots, dolls, vehicles, blocks, plushies, and more. Pick up toys, sort them into their correct sections, and transform complete chaos into a perfectly organized store. Earn coins as you clean and spend them on the Ball Machine , where every ball grants a new upgrade to help you organize faster and more efficiently. A Mountain of Toys to Master Thousands of tiny items, one very determined manager. Build order from toy-store chaos and keep every shelf under control. Unlock Powerful Perks Choose upgrades like helper bots to automate the busywork and keep the store moving while you focus on bigger goals. Organize Everything Sort, place, and tidy every corner until the whole shop runs like a perfect little machine. Take Care of Your Baby Your smallest helper needs attention too. Keep the baby happy while the store gets busier around you. Three Floors of Tiny Chaos Move fast across multiple floors, discover new areas, and turn every level into a perfectly managed toy empire. Unlock Powerful Upgrades Build your own playstyle with dozens of perks and abilities: Sprint faster Throw objects Highlight nearby toys Attract distant objects Coin magnet Object magnet Double, Triple, Quadruple, Quintuple, and Sextuple Jump Jetpack Wings Direction beacon showing where items belong Storage boxes for transporting multiple toys Place multiple nearby objects at once Helper robots that organize toys for you Movement speed multipliers And many more surprises A Playful Companion You're not alone. A curious baby accompanies you throughout the store, playing with toys, wandering around, and occasionally creating a little extra mess for you to deal with. Sometimes helpful, sometimes distracting, always adorable. Relaxing Chaos Take your time, optimize your routes, unlock upgrades, and watch the store slowly transform from complete disorder into a perfectly organized toy paradise. Can you place all 2, 694 toys back where they belong?
