Fight your way across high mountains, forest paths, deep caves, graveyards, spooky towns, and even Santa's toy factory. Experience 16 levels in total, all set within Christmas and Halloween themes. Massive Variety of Weapons - Find Your Favorites! Choose from a wide selection of firearms and melee weapons found in gift boxes. From lighter options like assault rifles and shotguns to heavy hitters like miniguns, rocket launchers, and flamethrowers, there's something for everyone. Laid-Back SOLO or CO-OP Action It’s always more fun to play together! Relax on the couch, grab a controller, and team up with a friend to battle Krampus's minions. While the game is fully playable in single-player mode, having a companion makes things easier and even more enjoyable. This game supports local couch co-op and Steam Remote Play Together but does not feature an online multiplayer mode. Tons of Enemies and Bosses Each level introduces new enemy types and culminates in a unique boss fight. Encounter them, discover their weaknesses, and take them down!
