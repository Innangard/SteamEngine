View Community Hub About This Game A survival challenge game set in a post-apocalyptic zombie world. In the game, day and night alternate. Zombies are inactive during the day, allowing you to safely build your home. At night, you must defend your camp against hordes of zombies! It's a light blend of "Simulation Management (Daytime)" + "Tactical Combat (Nighttime)," emphasizing strategy over complex operations, with some roguelite elements. Each playthrough offers a unique experience! You can experiment with different strategies and playstyles to challenge your survival limits! The game features over 30 types of zombies, each with unique special abilities. You must adapt your tactical approach and playstyle based on the types of zombies you encounter, or you'll easily find yourself in a passive and difficult situation. The game contains over 30 types of items and special supplies. You need to plan your strategy around the items you hold, as a single small item can decide the outcome of a battle at a critical moment. Meanwhile, over ten different professions each have distinctive talents, which play important roles in both camp development and combat. You can modify weapons or survivors to give weapons unique traits and grant survivors bio-enhanced abilities, unlocking new effects or powers. This system is particularly crucial in the later stages of the game's combat. The game centers around the "Survival Challenge," where you can push your limits to survive as long as possible. It also offers two completion modes: "Standard Clear" and "Perfect Clear." To achieve the latter and potentially save all survivors, you must accomplish more than just basic survival.
