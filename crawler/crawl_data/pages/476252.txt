One day, he suddenly received an invitation personally signed by Nikola Tesla, inviting him to attend the once-in-a-decade scientific gathering at the Eiffel Tower in Paris. Hayao set off for Paris full of anticipation, but during Tesla's opening speech, the venue suddenly plunged into darkness. When the lights came back on—Tesla mysteriously vanished in plain sight! While the police launched a comprehensive investigation, Hayao and his clever dog Alpina resolutely set out on their own quest for the truth. Determined to uncover the truth behind this bizarre disappearance case. At its core, this game uses puzzles to drive the story forward. Players will explore a meticulously crafted Paris, engaging in conversations with characters across various scenes or interacting with objects within the environment. Each character or interactive object may hold a hidden puzzle. Solving these puzzles will advance the story or yield new clues. One by one, the puzzles eventually weave together into a captivating story. The game features a wide variety of puzzles, each one thoroughly engaging, challenging your powers of observation, reasoning, and logical thinking from every angle. If you hit a snag while solving a problem, you can always check the hint scroll in the bottom-left corner—no need to worry about getting stuck. Not everyone in the game will be honest with you. Some people weave lies endlessly to conceal the secrets buried deep within their hearts. You must meticulously analyze their testimonies, pinpoint logical inconsistencies in their dialogue, and turn "evidence" into ammunition to shatter their psychological defenses—extracting crucial clues that advance the plot. Maelstrom LEGACY: The Tesla Mystery employs exquisite cartoon artistry, blending steampunk mechanical elements with Art Nouveau's elegant curves to create a dreamlike and uniquely imagined Paris. Every scene in the game is meticulously hand-drawn, brimming with vivid details—from the grandeur of the Eiffel Tower to the tranquility of rural lanes. The character designs are charming and full of personality, brought to life with smooth animation in a whimsical cartoon world.
