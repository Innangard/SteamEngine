View Community Hub About This Game Warehouseman is a warm and detailed simulation game set in a rural region during the 1990s, where you begin your journey with a small warehouse of your own. Make deals with different brands, store their products, load customer vehicles using forklifts and hand trucks, then hop into your own vehicle to deliver orders across country roads, farms, and remote delivery points. Start small, grow your warehouse step by step, upgrade your equipment, and become a trusted warehouse business known throughout the region. Drive and Deliver Load up your vehicle and drive through country roads, mountain passes, and remote delivery points to reach customers across the region. Serve Your Customers Take orders from customers visiting your warehouse, prepare their goods, and carefully load their vehicles using forklifts or hand trucks. Keep the Warehouse Full Order new products from partnered brands, receive incoming deliveries, and carry pallets into your warehouse to keep the business running. Everything in Its Place Place incoming products on the shelves, keep your stock in order, and maintain a clean, practical warehouse to prepare every order more efficiently. Manage your Business Use your office computer to track orders, sign contracts with brands, check the delivery map, read your emails, and expand your warehouse as your business grows.
