View Community Hub About This Game Some secrets are older than humankind. Others are so obscure that they would turn even the most sane man crazy. Are you ready to face what lies ahead? Dorian Cannon is a lighthouse engineer responsible for repairing historic lighthouses and installing new autonomous systems. During one of his assignments, he is also tasked with rescuing the team of lighthouse keepers stationed on Darenne Island and bringing them safely back to the mainland. But upon arriving, it quickly becomes clear that something is terribly wrong. You find yourself on a remote island near Innsmouth, home to a towering lighthouse and an abandoned mine. Explore the island, solve intricate puzzles, and struggle to keep your sanity as you uncover the truth behind the island's dark past. Inspired by the Lighthouse of Madness is a first-person puzzle adventure inspired by the works of H.P. Lovecraft and the immersive challenge of escape rooms. Suspense and horror await around every corner. How do you reach the lighthouse? What secrets lie beneath the abandoned mine? And what happened to the missing lighthouse keepers? The island holds mysteries far beyond your imagination. Armed only with a lantern, explore abandoned buildings, investigate strange locations, solve the puzzles that stand in your way, and try not to lose your mind during this haunting journey. Key Features Sanity mechanic: inspired by escape rooms, your sanity gradually deteriorates over time, affecting gameplay. Challenging environmental and logic-based puzzles. An atmosphere inspired by the works of H.P. Lovecraft. Uncover the mystery that haunts the island.
