After years apart, sisters Samara and Amani journey with their ancient spirit-mech to ruin their corporate sister’s fast food empire, reconnect communities with their long-lost traditions, and reconcile with their loved ones. FEATURES: A story with ingredients that go down smoothly, like reconciling with family, uncovering trauma, and restoring culture. Flavorful turn-based combat that’ll get your head spinning from the action and aromas. It comes with tasty side dishes like rhythm action, mini-games, and fishing. Traverse unique villages in a customizable and upgradable ancient spirit-mech named Goddess. With it, you’ll discover hidden paths, suppressed memories, local ingredients to cook with, and new friends. A detailed progression system simmering under the surface that keeps gameplay dynamic and delicious over the game’s 10-hour runtime.
