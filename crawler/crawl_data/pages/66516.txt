The Next Room is a fast-paced 3D action roguelite where every room is a new challenge and every decision can mean the difference between survival and death. Fight your way through relentless monsters, avoid deadly traps, uncover hidden secrets, and defeat powerful bosses guarding the path ahead. No two runs are the same. Experiment with different builds, discover rare equipment, craft stronger gear, and adapt your strategy as you venture deeper into the unknown. Will you spend your hard-earned gold in the shop, search for hidden rooms, or save your resources for the battles yet to come? Only those who keep moving will discover what lies behind... the next room. 🎯 Gameplay Features 🤺 Battle through rooms filled with dangerous enemies. 👹 Face challenging bosses with unique abilities and attack patterns. 🛠️ Craft and upgrade your equipment to create powerful builds. 🎭 Customize your character to match your preferred playstyle. 🪤 Survive deadly traps scattered throughout the dungeon. 🛒 Discover and visit the shop to buy valuable items and upgrades. 🔐 Discover secret rooms containing rare rewards and hidden surprises. 🎒 Item system: Single-Use Items – powerful effects for critical moments. Run Items – temporary buffs lasting until death. Permanent Upgrades – long-term progression across all runs. 🔄️ Highly replayable runs with randomized layouts, enemies, and builds.
