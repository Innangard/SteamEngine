View Community Hub About This Game A DEADLY JOB INTERVIEW FOR AN EVIL CORPORATION! HOSTYLE follows agent CRYPTIC through her job interview to become an assassin for one of the most corrupt companies on earth. Within the world of the simulated job interview, CRYPTIC is tasked with traversing various simulated worlds to eliminate her target as quickly and as stylishly as possible. PURE STYLE! This fast-paced 3D platformer combines stylish movement options alongside a futuristic but nostalgic 6th-generation aesthetic inspired by the Y2K era . Get harassed by your cutesy but demonic supervisor as you challenge yourself to ascend the ranks of the HOSTYLE corporation through pure style. CHOOSE YOUR PATH!          TWO GAMEPLAY STYLES! Different characters will guide you through the simulated world of HOSTYLE,  encouraging you to progress through the game in different fashions. Focus on either the TIME-TRIALS speedrunning and earning better ranks OR relax and explore the vast HUB WORLDS hunting for secrets and various platforming challenges! Who knows how things will end if you only focus on one or the other?
