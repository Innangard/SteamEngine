The invasion is led not only by standard combat drones but also by deadly Titans: colossal war platforms capable of leveling entire cities. Your mission: fight, level up, strengthen your squad, uncover the truth behind the invasion, and liberate the planet from its mechanical oppressors 4-Player Co-op Assemble a squad of distinct combat roles and push through relentless enemy waves together. Combine offensive, defensive, and support abilities to create powerful team synergies and adapt to any battlefield challenge Titan Battles Each Titan is a lethal war machine with unique weaponry and combat patterns. Turrets, missile barrages, pulse attacks, and devastating heavy artillery will demand perfectly tuned builds, seamless coordination, and smart tactics from your team Roguelite Progression & Builds Collect weapons, upgrade your characters, and mix elemental damage types, status effects, and combat modifiers to craft devastating builds tailored to your playstyle. Every run is a chance to grow stronger and push further than before Destructible Environment Turn the battlefield into chaos. Level buildings, collapse bridges, blast through cover, and use environmental destruction to your advantage - or trigger disasters right beneath your allies' feet Long-Eared Allies Rescue the planet's inhabitants and bring them along on dangerous raids.
