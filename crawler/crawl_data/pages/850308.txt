View Community Hub About This Game Sort Them Fish is a cozy organising simulator about sorting your fish collection after they all mysteriously ended up on the floor. Sort 2538 fish across 12 store sections. Watch fish swim around in their tanks as you restore order at your own pace. Could they have all flopped out at the same time in a miraculous feat of coincidence? Nobody knows, but what you do know is they need to get back to their aquariums. Don't worry, the fish are not in danger, but you probably shouldn't leave until they're all safe and back in their tanks. Unlock abilities as you play which increases your sorting efficiency. Carry more fish, run faster, make matching fish flop into your arms, and others that make organising the fish faster. Thousands of fish of  different species are flopping around on the floor. Reef fish, ocean fish, lake fish, from tiny clownfish to the giant goliath grouper. It's your job to pick up every fish and place it back in the proper tank. Fish are sorted by size and fun categories, as well as freshwater and saltwater store sections. Key Features Sort  2538 fish into their correct tanks in over 12+ sections. Discover hundreds of fish species and complete every category. Organize fish into fun collections such as puffers, reef fish and ocean predators. Unlock abilities that improve carrying capacity, movement speed, and fish discovery. The fish are not in danger. The gameplay is relaxing with no time limits of penalties. Enjoy AI-free gameplay, every asset was made by a human.
