But reincarnation is broken and your boss Zhong Kui is missing. Dive into the chaos and join a hilarious, action-packed brawl across the realms! Face endless waves of Yaoguai on a treacherous battlefield. Carve a whirlwind of destruction through the crowd and savor the simple, visceral joy of underworld envoys doing their jobs. Choose your champion from unique underworld envoy generals and dominate the battlefield. Unleash unpredictable power by freely combining fate stones and yin spells . Use underworld currency to stock up on powerful paper crafts, forging countless synergies and combat styles. Master your unique build and discover that no two runs are ever the same. Your duties go beyond battle. Solve the underworld's daily chaos such as feed a hungry Yaoguai, help a misplaced god or untangle a fox spirit in his cultivation... A top-tier envoy masters both the pen and the blade. Ready to negotiate peace or clear the scene! Game designers are committed to providing players with fresh, timely and interesting gameplay.
