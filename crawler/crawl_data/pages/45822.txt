View Community Hub Wishlist & Play the demo first! ✨ About This Game 🍸 Welcome to Mixed Spirits , A Cozy Bartending Experience 🍸 Step into your evening shift, where the lights are low, the music is mellow, and the drinks are anything but ordinary. Mixed Spirits is a cozy, creative bartending game where you mix custom drinks, charm your customers, and grow your dream bar, one glass at a time. Serve, Mix, Repeat Run your own bar and take orders from colorful customers looking for the perfect drink. Whether they ask for something straight from the menu or give you vague hints (I want something sweet and a little bitter), it’s your job to get it right! Bar Scene: Greet customers, chat a little, and take their drink orders in a cozy, warmly lit setting. Mixing Scene: Head to the mixing station to craft each drink from scratch with a variety of ingredients, ice types, and decorative touches. Ingredient Preparing Scene: Head back to your home to grow your mint, cut lemons and more minigames to prepare for the next day at the bar. Freeform Drink Mixing Create original drinks by combining different ingredients with attributes like Sweet, Bitter, Spicy, or Relaxing. The better you match the customer’s desires, the bigger your tip! Secret Recipes Experiment enough and you’ll unlock hidden drink recipes! These special discoveries can be added to your menu for future orders. Full Drink Customization Customize every aspect of your drink, glass size, type of ice, decorations, and garnish. Want to impress a picky guest who wants a “big, refreshing drink with mint and umbrella”? You can! Creative, Chill Experience No timers. No stress. Just you, your bar, and your creativity. The perfect game to relax with after a long day. End-of-Shift Wind Down After the bar closes, rest at home and prep for the next day. Visit shops to restock your ingredients or upgrade your setup: Liquor Store Glassware Shop Fruit Market Decoration & Garnish Store Recipe Book Merchant Meaningful Interactions Chat with guests before and after their drinks. Build your own little world behind the bar, one story at a time. 🖼️ Aesthetic & Vibes Mixed Spirits captures the magic of cozy night work, golden light, jazzy piano, softly clinking glasses, and beautifully animated drinks that glow with personality. 🎵 Chill. Sip. Stay a While. Mixed Spirits is perfect for players who want a relaxed, sandbox-y, creative gameplay loop. Whether you're here to master the art of mixology or just vibe with your regulars, your bar is always open. 🥃 Discover your signature drink. 🕯️ Decorate your station. 🧊 Serve the perfect nightcap.
