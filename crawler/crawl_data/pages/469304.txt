View Community Hub About This Game Hatch eggs, discover different bird species, and care for your growing flock. Feed them, pet them, and watch them wander through your screen as they slowly bring more life and movement to your space. From common birds to rare and extinct species, each companion adds its own charm. Feather Land includes simple tools to help you manage your time without pulling you out of the mood. Use the built-in Pomodoro timer, organize your tasks with a to-do list, and write down quick thoughts in the notebook. Soft birdsong, ambient nature sounds, and calming music create a soothing space for concentration, while your focus time helps you earn in-game coins, turning productivity into a relaxing way to grow your world. Expand your Feather Land by unlocking new lands, each with its own atmosphere, scenery, and bird species. Build cozy habitats across forests, mountains, deserts, beaches, and more. Every biome gives you a new cozy space to decorate, customize, and fill with birds. Make the space feel like yours. Add decorations, choose your favorite birds, adjust the weather, and change the time of day – from a sunny morning to a rainy afternoon or a quiet night. Whether you want a soft study corner or a tiny bird paradise, you can shape the atmosphere around your own rhythm. With Wallpaper Mode, Feather Land can stay on your screen as a gentle background companion while you study, work, or relax. Your birds continue to live quietly beside you, giving your desk a little more warmth without demanding your attention.
