FEATURES: During your stay in San Hugo you will be able to enjoy a city with areas of both high society and the hood, buy clothes and accessories or get a haircut in the center or on the San Hugo pedestrian street. You can tune up your vehicles at the Mecánico Tejada in front of the plaza, buy weapons at the neighborhood tranza, you can wear the shirts of the popular Argentine teams. And if you prefer something quieter, tour the city in a "borrowed" vehicle with Alexis, the main character of this story. Of course, be careful with the San Hugo police and with the gangs that lead the city, have fun and luck... you will need it.
