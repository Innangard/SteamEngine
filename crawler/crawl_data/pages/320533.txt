View Community Hub About This Game A simulation game where you sell your special recipe juices in medieval villages. Work alone or with your friend to expand your business. Co-Op Mode and Single Player Share the duties with your friend in co-op mode (for up to 2 players). You can also go solo and manage everything alone. You can invite your friend anytime if you change your mind. Features Buy fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices, herbs, sugar, milk, and yogurt to craft your special recipe juices. Each ingredient has different effects (immunity booster, skin refresher, weight loss helper, hydration booster etc). Interact with everyone in the villages to learn customer preferences, craft juices to achieve the desired effects, and make them your customers. Send letters to your grandpa about your business status and receive his suggestions and machine designs. Hire new teammates like a seller, citrus worker, dishwashing worker, juicer worker, mixing worker, and shopper. Get permission to enter new villages, rent new workplaces, and expand your business throughout the new villages.
