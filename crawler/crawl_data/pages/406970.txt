In this story expansion, you will lead the rising power of French buccaneers and challenge the most powerful empire in the New World. A Bloody Trail of History A large-scale story campaign based on real historical events awaits you. Walk side by side with legendary and ruthless French captains — Michel le Basque, François l’Olonnais , and Daniel Montbars . Execute daring raids, participate in massive assaults on Spanish cities, and carve your name into the chronicles of buccaneer glory. Confrontation with the "Inquisition of the Seas" The Spanish Crown is tired of suffering losses. Deployed against the Brethren of the Coast is the Armada de Barlovento (Windward Fleet) — Spain's special punitive fleet created with a single purpose: to wipe the French buccaneers off the face of the earth and send the survivors to the gallows. Can you outsmart their spies and assassins and crush their fleet led by the invincible galleon "Margarita"? KEY FEATURES: New Minor Faction — Buccaneers: Establish profitable trade with the hunters of Tortuga and earn their trust to gain special advantages. Historical Campaign: Take part in famous 17th-century raids and expeditions. Experience all the cruelty and romance of the Golden Age of Piracy. New Arsenal: Replenish your supplies with a powerful new musket and a terrifying cutlass featuring a handle crafted from human bone. The Fate of Nicolas Rimalaye: The familiar musket officer finally gets a face! Complete the quest of this veteran buccaneer, uncover the secrets of his past, and prove worthy of his loyalty. Evolution of the Buccaneer Outpost: Start with a small buccaneer camp and turn it into a well-developed settlement. As you progress, the camp will fill with new characters and expand its functionality. Base expansion is especially effective for pirate faction players. Unique Ship: Capture the legendary galleon "Margarita" — the flagship of the Barlovento fleet with unique characteristics. Promotional material may differ from the final result. Walkthrough Tips: The quest can be obtained on the streets of Tortuga once the main character reaches level 5. The DLC's difficulty scales gradually, progressing from early to late game.
