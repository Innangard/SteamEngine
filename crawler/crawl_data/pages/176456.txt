Content For This Game Browse all (1) AutoLand Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game In AutoLand , you play as a little robot navigating through 24 levels, searching for the teleport while overcoming tricky obstacles. This unique one-button platformer features simple but engaging mechanics like jumping, wall jumping , and using power-ups to double jump or shoot bullets . As you progress, you’ll face increasingly difficult challenges, including collecting keys to unlock lock blocks, riding on moving platforms, and avoiding deadly spikes. Timing and persistence are key as you master each level’s unique challenges. With its minimalist pixel art and an original electronic soundtrack , AutoLand offers an enjoyable mix of precision and fun. Compete for the fastest times with your friends and the world, as your completion times are added to the online leaderboards after every level! Key Features : One-button gameplay : Jump, wall jump, and use power-ups like double jump and bullet shooting with just one button. 24 levels of increasing difficulty : Face new obstacles like moving platforms, spikes, and lock blocks that require keys. Power-ups : Collect items to unlock double jump and shooting abilities. Simple yet charming pixel art : Retro-style visuals combined with an immersive original electronic soundtrack . Online leaderboards : Compete globally by posting your best times after each completed level. Achievements & cloud saves : Track your progress and save your game with full support for Steam features. Gamepad support : Enjoy smooth gameplay with full gamepad compatibility. Perfect for casual gamers looking for a quick, rewarding challenge, AutoLand blends classic platformer elements with modern design for a satisfying gameplay experience.
