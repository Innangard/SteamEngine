Stranded in a barren landscape, you are the only one alive able to master the ancient art of growing vegetation. Your world is fragile, and you must find a way to end the recurring cataclysms that threaten it. Grow a verdant forest from nothing and create different tree species to adapt to the various biomes and weather. Transform the barren land into lush valleys, one tree at a time. Explore an intriguing ever-changing world on your stilts, a locomotion system designed for walking across the landscapes and climbing up in trees. Stumble on ancient structures and awaken them to discover their secrets. Enjoy a procedural living soundtrack that reacts to the world around you from award-winning composers Vibe Avenue and see the landscape transform into a forest one tree at a time.
