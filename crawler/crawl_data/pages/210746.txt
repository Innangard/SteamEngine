Journey through ever-changing worlds, from scorching lava fields and blizzard mountains to starry cosmos. Dodge waves of enemies, land precise skill shots, and overcome each battle with quick decisions. Play solo for a tense, skill-based challenge, or team up in up to 4-player co-op, where different combat styles come together to create powerful synergies. Combine ever-changing skills and upgrades to carve your own path of progression, and challenge the dream world in a new way with every run. No two challenges are the same, and no fixed build or strategy will always carry you to victory. From swift close-range assaults and precise skill shots to wide-area attacks and supportive playstyles, each Traveler approaches combat in a different way. Build around your chosen Traveler’s combat style and face enemies in a new way with every run. When playing solo, dodge incoming attacks, land your skills with precision, and shape your build through your own decisions. In up to 4-player co-op, combat becomes even more dynamic. When different Travelers’ abilities and builds come together, powerful combinations and explosive synergies emerge—ones that could never be created alone. Use ever-changing choices to alter your attacks, enhance your abilities, and discover unexpected combinations. Even with the same Traveler, the build you create can lead to a completely different combat style. There is no single answer. Build your strategy from the choices presented in each run, and take on challenges that may seem impossible. The dream world is full of changing variables. Four difficulty levels, distinctive boss encounters, and Lucid Dream modifiers demand different tactics and build choices with every run. Rather than following the same familiar path, try new combinations that adapt to each changing situation. The more you play, the more powerful strategies you will discover as you break through the dream world in your own way. The dream awaits.
