View Community Hub Follow us About This Game Re:Night is a strategy game that combines turn-based tactical combat with roguelike deckbuilding. Build your deck over the course of each run while navigating battlefields filled with tactical possibilities. Any combination of three characters can create more than 20 unique builds, and the interaction between multiple strategic systems delivers deep, rewarding, and highly replayable combat. Travel to a mysterious town, break the endless cycle of night with your own hands, and restore dawn to the world. Tactical combat introduces strategic mechanics such as frontline and backline positioning, flanking, blocking, and protecting allies. Combined with elemental counters, equipment, and item effects, these systems create countless opportunities for powerful combinations. Command three characters simultaneously in every battle, each with a distinct role. Whether focusing on burst damage, battlefield control, healing, or environmental interactions, careful coordination and party synergy are the keys to victory. Fire, Water, and Grass form a cyclical elemental counter system. Exploit elemental advantages to unleash devastating critical hits and deal massive damage. Coordinate your three characters to trigger elemental critical hits while positioning them to deny your enemies the same advantage. Every move requires careful planning, but victory also depends on adapting to an ever-changing battlefield. Every character features unique talents and an exclusive pool of character-specific cards. Combine more than 300 cards with over 200 pieces of equipment and items to create more than 20 distinct builds for any three-character party. Become an unstoppable brawler, a demolition expert, or a relentless lancer. Even the stone walls of the battlefield can become powerful weapons. In Re:Night, your imagination is your greatest strength. Undo your last action, rewind to the start of the current turn, or even restart the battle from the very beginning at any time. Never let a single mistake ruin your adventure, and face unfamiliar enemies with the freedom to explore different strategies. Study enemy behavior, experiment with different positioning, card sequences, and elemental combinations, then use each rewind to test new ideas, refine your strategy, and discover your own path to victory. Every run offers a fresh adventure, with diverse maps, random events, challenging battles, and unexpected encounters waiting to be discovered. Begin your journey beside the stream outside the town, venture through the dangers hidden within the village, and ultimately ascend to a castle shrouded in mystery. With every adventure, you'll unlock new content, playable characters, and even greater challenges to overcome. Each character has their own memory fragments, known as Chrono Shards. Collect them to unlock hidden cards and equipment, expanding your tactical options with every discovery. Gather every Chrono Shard to uncover the truth behind the endless cycle of night.
