Simulator 3 – Chicago O’Hare International Airport (KORD) Step into the supervising role at one of the world’s most iconic and busiest aviation hubs: Chicago O’Hare International Airport (KORD). Situated on the northwest side of Chicago, this super-hub blends immense scale, varied traffic flows, and full-sized international operations to deliver an ATC simulator experience you’ll never forget. GROUND CHART As the air traffic controller, you’ll manage massive volumes of flights — short-haul, long-haul, cargo and regional — across multiple parallel runways, complex taxiway systems, and heavy ground movements. With operations numbering in the hundreds of thousands annually and nonstop service to destinations all around the globe, O’Hare challenges you to keep arrivals, departures and ground operations moving with precision and efficiency. Built for Tower! Simulator 3, this detailed add-on brings the full scale of a global aviation hub to your desktop: authentic runway layouts, high-traffic sequencing, bustling apron and ground operations and the freedom to fine-tune traffic loads, aircraft mix, weather and time of day for your ideal challenge.
