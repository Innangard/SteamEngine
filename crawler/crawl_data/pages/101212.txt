Release Date: Oct 15, 2026 View discussions Find Community Groups Embed Links & info Free Demo Play Refuel Together Demo Install Demo Check out the full game View Community Hub About This Demo Welcome to Refuel Together (Demo), where you can run a gas station as a co-op with 1-4 player. In this demo version, refuel and clean cars arriving at the gas station. Serve snacks to your customers at your cafe. Play Refuel Together Demo solo or in Co-Op for up to 4 people. Join other players games and run a gas station together. The demo offers a taste of early progression, unique qualifications, financial challenges, and creative gas station upgrades that await in the full release. Expand your station by adding new gas pumps. You can start with a single gas pump and expand your gas station to the four pumps this demo offers. This will give you an idea of ​​how to operate the massive gas station in the full version. Run your Cafe Cook Hot Dogs for your customers and serve them to their tables. You can also offer your customers water and soda options. These will give you an idea of ​​a full-fledged Cafe that you can run as a co-op in the full release. Upgrade & Progression The upgrades offered in the demo will bring more cars to your gas station. The more cars come, the more you earn. Track your stock You'll need to constantly order sausages, hot dogs, and drinks for your cafe. You'll also need to order more gas as more cars arrive at your gas station. You must place new orders and keep your stocks up to date. Main Features: Play Co-Op with up to 4 players. Demo-focused slice of the core loop: Fill the cars with gas. Make hot dogs and serve them to your customers. Expand your station up to 4 pumps with demo upgrades. Cook Hot Dogs for your customers and serve them to their tables.
