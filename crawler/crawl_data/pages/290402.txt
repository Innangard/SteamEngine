View Community Hub About This Game Dark Return part one is a horror and mystery game that puts you in the role of John, a man who traveled far away in search of a cure. But his recovery journey is turned upside down when he receives a mysterious message from a family member, asking him to return home. Upon his return, he finds the house plunged into darkness, the family disappeared, and an atmosphere saturated with secrets and strange events. In the game, you will explore room after room in a house filled with family secrets. With each step, John discovers one horrific truth after another, and engages in uncovering mysterious details about what happened to the family, while a terrifying truth about his grandfather begins to emerge. Game Features: A unique horror experience featuring an exciting and complex story. Fine graphics and details enhance the dark atmosphere of the game. Intertwining puzzles that require exploration skills and concentration. A mysterious ending that paves the way for an exciting sequel. Will you be able to discover the hidden truth? And can you confront the dark secrets that bind the family members?
