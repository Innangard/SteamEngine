Learn more Leaving Early Access: Q2 2027 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We are a very small team making an RTS which is usually made with a much larger team. The community can provide us with valuable feedback and test on a larger scale than we can on our own. The RTS community is very passionate and we want to hear from you about how we can improve the game before a full release.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “We estimate that it will take around 3 years to finish the game from the release of Early Access. (The estimate was updated from 1 year originally, due to an increase of planned content and amount of features and polish.)” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We hope to more than double the amount of content by 1.0 release, compared to the start of Early Access. We plan to make a total of 16 campaign missions. We also plan to add a lot of QoL features, improve performance and improve the AI. For 1.0 release we hope to add more languages. For more planned features, check out the roadmap which you can find in 'Development Update' news posts.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “First 10 missions of the story campaign (Act 1 and 2). 20 Skirmish maps, solo or co-op against AI, or PvP, in any team configuration. 7 challenge maps for solo or co-op play. 5 Baltic faction heroes - Saule, Meness, Ausrine, Vakarine and Indraja. 2 Order faction heroes - Archangel Michael, Bishop. A third hero, George, is only available to play in campaign right now.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The price will most likely stay the same. Our goal is for the content-to-price ratio to be be most fair at full release.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “You can join our official Discord channel where you can reach us and fellow players. Divine Heroes and Abilities - Master the various divine heroes, each with their own set of abilities. Heroes gain unique units, buildings and upgrades in addition to the ones from their faction, giving each of them a unique playstyle. Protect your followers and burn your enemies with the light of the sun as the goddess Saule, or strike under the cover of darkness as the moon god Meness. Or join the Order and smite the heathens as the archangel Michael. Build and Be Worshipped - Devoted followers will flock to your shrine. Have them gather resources and build cities so you can raise great armies for battles to come. Protect them while they pray to grant you faith, a powerful resource used for mythical creatures and god powers. Godsworn features a unique and easy to pick up economy system where workers automatically respond to globally issued tasks and return to generating faith when idle. Story Campaign with optional co-op - Play a fully voiced epic campaign in single-player or co-op. The campaign has been designed with co-op in mind, with the character choice leading to unique interactions or at times a completely different gameplay role. Explore the maps to find secrets and gather glyphs that will help you turn the tide of battle. Use the glyphs that you have found to add variation to your hero and faction in both skirmish and challenge maps. The wild pagan lands have been under the watchful eye of the gods for as long as time. The arrival of a knight order bearing the banner of Heaven threatens to upset the uneasy alliance. Will the realm burn, or will blood of both divine and mortal be spilled to protect it?
