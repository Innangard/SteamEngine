The dungeon builder is fully functional and it's possible to save, upload and export dungeons locally. New rooms can be added with a click and freely expanded or shrunk making dungeon building an easy and fun experience. We have a linking system in place so events such as making items appear or opening doors when all of the enemies are defeated etc. can be triggered. This system gives the player a ton of flexibility to create unique dungeons with interesting puzzles and events so no two dungeons are the same! We have dark rooms that light up when torches are lit. We have co-op multiplayer for up to four players fully functioning locally and over Steam Remote Play Together! Dungeons can be designed to be only playable by a specific number of players or for any number of players, whatever the designer decides. Create multiple rooms in various sizes as you create your dungeon just the way you want it. Our user friendly drag and drop UI makes creating dungeons fun and easy! Upload your levels and play levels from other designers! Inspired by games like The Legend of Zelda and Super Mario Maker. Features Creating dungeons is easy! Players can add new rooms with a single click. To resize a room simply drag from the corners. Add doors to create room transitions with just a few clicks. Players can decorate their dungeons and add objects to make a unique layout and create intricate puzzles. Use the linking system to create puzzles and challenges! Link objects and enemies together to create logic that defines how puzzles are solved. For example: link an enemy to a closed door, now that door will open when that enemy is defeated! Link one object to many others to create unique events! Group objects together and they will only trigger what they are linked to when every object in the group is activated! For example: Group a Gooey, Orb Switch and Torch together, then create a link to a Closed Door. Now that Door will only open once everything in group is taken care of! Collectables can be scattered around your dungeon such as keys, gold, arrows, bombs, hearts and more. Place them on top of Chests or Enemies with a single click and those items will drop from them! The Frog Glove lets you eat small enemies, stun larger ones, and latch onto some objects so you can fly over pits! Dash Boots will help you clear 1 tile gaps and knock enemies to the side! Create Single or Multiplayer dungeons and play together with friends in local co-op and online via Steam Remote Play together! Enemies with unique characteristics are at your disposal. Blobs will wander aimlessly while Bats will chase the player if they get too close. Lizards will take aim and fire their bow at the player. Flames will give chase and light torches, and Warthogs will charge at the player when they are in sight. Create Custom Boss Fights using links! You can link from a Boss during different phases to create unique and challenging situations. Upload and download levels online from creators all over the world!
