View Community Hub About This Game About JUJUTSU KAISEN RUMBLE: SURVIVATON From the creator of Vampire Survivors Luca Galante and Shueisha Games, a new take on the genre. A poncle production set in the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, creating a new genre. Survive, Exorcise, Change the game JUJUTSU KAISEN RUMBLE: SURVIVATON is a survivors royale where you battle cursed spirits, rivals, and grow stronger throughout the match. Outscore other contestants on your way to the final showdown. Defeat enemies, build your loadout, unlock rule-changing effects, and fight to become the last player standing victorious. Online play supports up to 8 players. Solo play mode available. ◆ Earn Points and Overwhelm Your Rivals! Cut through waves of cursed spirits, rack up points, and keep pace with your rivals' as the match grows more intense. Your attacks trigger automatically, so the focus is on movement, positioning, and choosing the right moment to strike back with rule-changing effects. At 100 points, activate a "Rule Addition" to disrupt your opponents, steal momentum, and climb the rankings. In the end, the top 2 players face off in a 1v1 duel, where only one will emerge victorious. ◆ A roster of 20＋ characters from Jujutsu Kaisen! Play as 20+ characters from the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, each with their own skills and playstyle. Unlock new fighters by meeting specific conditions, experiment with different strengths, and find the character that fits your approach. Powerful BOSSES can also appear mid-battle, turning the match into an even more chaotic clash of Jujutsu Sorcerers, Curse Users, and Cursed Spirits. ◆ Overwhelm Your Rivals with Unique Special Moves Build up your gauge in battle, then unleash devastating character-specific techniques at the perfect moment. Activate techniques iconic to JUJUTSU KAISEN from Domain Expansion, Black Flash, and beyond. These powerful moves can turn the tide of a match in an instant. Special Moves can expose nearby rivals and can be directly targeted to a variety of game-altering attacks from the caster for the duration. ◆ Every Choice is Yours. Skills That Change with Every Run. Collect EXP from defeated cursed spirits and level up throughout the match. As players level up, they can not only strengthen the skills of their chosen character, but also incorporate the techniques of other characters into their own build through " Unite Build" . Each level gives you new skill options to shape your build on the fly: strengthen what you already have, add new abilities, and discover powerful synergies that can carry you to victory. ◆ About JUJUTSU KAISEN Created by Gege Akutami, JUJUTSU KAISEN was serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from Issue 14 of 2018 (March 5, 2018) through Issue 44 of 2024 (September 30, 2024), spanning 30 volumes. Curses. Sinister forces born from negative human emotions, such as hardship, regret, and shame, are driving people toward death. When the seal on a powerful “Cursed Object” is broken, high school student Yuji Itadori steps into a world of battles surrounding curses...! A groundbreaking new frontier in dark fantasy, opened by a singular talent! The manga spans 30 volumes, with Volume 0 also available as JUJUTSU KAISEN 0: Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical School. The series has surpassed 150 million copies incirculation (including digital editions)Worldwide.  Season 1 of the TV anime aired from October 2020 to March 2021. Released in December 2021, JUJUTSU KAISEN 0: The Movie became a major hit, grossing 26.5 billion yen worldwide and becoming a cultural phenomenon. Season 2 of the TV anime, which aired from July to December 2023, was also widely acclaimed for its exceptional quality. In May 2025, JUJUTSU KAISEN: Hidden Inventory / Premature Death was released theatrically as a compilation film. That October saw a theatrical re-release of JUJUTSU KAISEN 0, followed in November by the theatrical release of JUJUTSU KAISEN: Execution. Season 3 of the TV anime, JUJUTSU KAISEN: Culling Game Part 1, aired from January to late March 2026.
