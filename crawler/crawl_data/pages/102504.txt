Escape the research facility through many dangers and flee COLD CALL. You can't stay here forever. Explore The Large Facility of Northbridge Northbridge is a sprawling, multi‑level research labyrinth filled with secrets, forbidden experiments, and anomalies pulled from a broken Earth. Experience the many anomalies captured from the broken Earth and understanding the behavior of creatures out of this world. Manage your inventory and take what is necessary with you to carry on your escape.
