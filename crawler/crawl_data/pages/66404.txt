Learn more What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “Office Manager is a complex management building game with many interconnected systems, such as economics, tenant behavior, and infrastructure management. Since I am developing the game primarily by myself, Early Access is the ideal way for me to balance the gameplay, test stability, and add new content based exactly on what players enjoy the most” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “I expect the game to remain in Early Access for approximately 6 to 12 months. However, the exact duration will depend on community feedback and the amount of new content we ultimately decide to implement into the full version.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The full version will offer much richer content and deeper mechanics. I plan to add more types of unique companies (tenants) with different requirements, a wider range of unpredictable events and disasters, more complex office customization options, and a fully balanced economy. My goal is to create a sandbox building management simulator where every play session brings new challenges.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The game is currently fully playable and already offers many hours of content within a functional core gameplay loop. Players can build offices, route utility networks, sign contracts with their first tenants, and deal with various events. The reason for Early Access is that the game is not yet fully tested and lacks final economic balancing and deeper 'late-game' content. Together with the players, I want to make sure that the progression remains fun and rewarding not only at the beginning, but also when you are managing a massive and complex office colossus.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “"It is possible that the price of the game will increase slightly after the full release, but I haven't made a final decision yet. It will mostly depend on how massively the game grows during Early Access thanks to community support. If we add a truly large amount of new content and mechanics, the price could appropriately increase for full version In any case, players who support the game in Early Access will get it at the best possible price.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “Player feedback is absolutely crucial to me. I don't want to develop the game just for myself. I plan to actively communicate with the player community. The main feature, however, will be regular community voting. I want to let the players themselves vote and decide which new features, tenant types, disasters, or gameplay mechanics will get development priority. Your rules. Your nightmares. Office Manager is a strategic office building management simulator where every decision affects your bottom line. Turn an empty lot into a thriving corporate complex, rent spaces to demanding companies, and handle daily crises in real time. Will you survive fires, thieves, and disgruntled managers, or will your empire go bankrupt? Build Everything from Scratch Walls and floors are just the beginning. A true manager must think about logistics and utilities. Complete control: Design your building using over 14 different room types—from open-space offices to research labs and server rooms. Infrastructure: Build electricity, water, and internet grids. Every desk and coffee machine consumes energy that you have to pay for. Space optimization: Monitor noise heatmaps and lighting. A noisy generator next to a break room will cost you tenants. Satisfy Needs, Maximize Profit The companies in your building employ real people who have their own daily routines and limits. Needs simulation: People need to use the toilet, eat, and relax. Neglecting their needs leads to a drop in productivity. Company ratings: Companies rate your building across four dimensions: Services, Reliability, Quality, and Decoration. Try to keep everything highly rated. Commitments: Receive emails from CEOs with expansion requests. Missing a deadline means your reputation will take a hit. Hard Business and Numbers This isn't just about putting up walls; it's about building a functional economy. Become a ruthless landlord! Your own employees: Hire your own elite staff—security guards, receptionists, or analysts—and assign them to specialized tasks. Financial analytics: Track your daily cash flow down to the last cent, optimize energy costs, sign lucrative service contracts, and apply for bank loans in a crisis. Tenant market: Choose the best offers on the market, negotiate rent, and kick out companies that are no longer profitable. Survive the Unexpected Reality will eventually throw a wrench in the works. Crisis management will test how well you've prepared your building. Thieves & Security: Criminals will target your property. Install camera systems and hire guards for early detection. Fires: Make sure to protect your building against potential fires; buy smoke detectors and catch problems early! Hackers & Vandalism: Fight off cyber attacks on your servers and send maintenance workers to scrub graffiti off the walls so you don't scare away luxury clients. From Empty Lot to Tech Giant Your ambitions must have no limits. Expand, research, and dominate the market. Research tree: Lock your team of analysts in the lab and earn XP. Unlock advanced technologies across 6 different categories (Building, IT, HR, Services, Management, Security). Scaling: Expand your building and attract the world's biggest corporations that will pay astronomical amounts for rent.
