Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game Musgro Farm is a 3D incremental farming factory game. Start off with a modest farm on a small plot of land and grow it into an automated farming empire. You start with only a couple basic crops to sell, but you'll soon unlock more crops, recipes, and increasingly complex systems. Fields grow crops over time. Buildings assemble recipes. Vehicles pick up, deliver, and sell products. You can invest in improving the efficiency of each one, but you'll need to balance your choices to avoid overwhelming any individual part of the chain. If you wish to pursue peak efficiency then you'll need to optimize your layouts, invest in the right upgrades, and plan out your technology choices. Or, you can just relax and watch as the farm runs and the numbers go up. Musgro Farm incorporates elements from both incremental games (near-infinite upgradability, exponential level growth, huge numbers) and factory games (logistics, supply chains, recipes, layout planning) . While the game has a relatively chill atmosphere, there are a plethora of optimizations available to accelerate your progress. Build in whichever style you want: a simple farm following market trends, or a massive farming empire spanning for miles and churning out a vast array of complex recipes.
