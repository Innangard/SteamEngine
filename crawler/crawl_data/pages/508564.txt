Use special abilities to manipulate those particles, solve puzzles, avoid detection, and navigate this sinister and beautiful land. Along the way, meet various creatures, some of which will help you understand your purpose and others who are tasked with hiding it from you. Collect memories, find hidden paths, and piece together your connection to this land that isn’t always what it seems. ■  Gain multiple abilities that allow you to manipulate the particles around you. ■  Use your abilities to solve puzzles, evade enemies, and gain access to hidden areas. ■  Freely explore open environments any way you’d like. ■  Experience a story where the line between friend and foe is often blurred.  Meet various creatures that help you unravel your purpose in this land.
