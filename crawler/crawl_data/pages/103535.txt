A Figure Skater Training Simulation Step into the role of a figure skating coach and nurture your athletes to claim victory at the world’s most prestigious competition, “On the Edge.” This is a training simulation game where you guide vibrant characters and watch them shine through stunning 3D graphics. Build Your Skater’s Program You, as the coach, will determine the music , costumes , and techniques your skater performs in competition. Will you choose a well-balanced routine that fully utilizes your skater’s abilities? Or will you risk it all with high-difficulty elements for a potential dramatic comeback? The choice is yours. Gauge your skater’s talents and potential to craft the perfect program. Compete in Tournaments Take on each competition with the skater you’ve carefully trained! Your skater will execute the exact program you designed, vying for top placement. Trust in their abilities and hope for victory! Multi-Story & Multi-Endings Rivalries, the outcome of each competition, and your skater’s growth will all influence the direction of the story, leading to multiple possible endings. What kind of drama awaits, shaped by your guidance and decisions? Strive for the best ending and create your own unique figure skating tale. Experience the fusion of strategic training and character-driven drama in this game. Feel the excitement of the journey to the top, all in your own hands.
