Add to your wishlist and get notified when it becomes available. View Community Hub About This Game They say getting rich is all about the journey. Your journey starts with a job, where you spend your time working hard, getting paid less than you're worth, just to make someone else rich. Net worth determines your step on the ladder. Reach one billion dollars to win. Job Market: It ain’t much, but it’s honest work. Start by trading your time for cash. Then invest your earnings to escape the wage cage. New jobs are posted daily at 7am. Real Estate: Flip and rent your way to riches. Time the market for quick gains, or buy and hold to collect rent. Property values follow market sentiment while rent provides a steady passive income. Prices adjust and rents pay out daily at 11am. Entrepreneurship (unlocks at $50K net worth): Build your business empire from scratch. Hire employees, choose upgrades then scale each venture to maximize a real time passive income drip. Acquisitions (unlocks at $500K net worth): Buy already profitable and partially upgraded businesses. Negotiate good deals into great ones. Keep your eyes open and your wallet ready for exclusive business ventures only obtainable through acquisitions. New offers appear every five days. Stock Market (unlocks at $1M net worth): Diversify into stocks to capture equity swings of up to 30% and daily dividend returns of up to 3%. Stock prices move and dividends are paid daily at 9am. Crypto Launchpad (unlocks at $10M net worth): Ape into a purely degenerate, high risk investment vehicle. Risk losing it all in a rugpull for the chance of catching a whale run with returns exceeding +5,000%. A new crypto launches every ten days. Mega Projects : With the right connections and assets take on monumental projects that provide perm bonuses to the other money making systems. Your journey to a billion is documented. Visualize your wealth growing, track where your money went, and see which parts of your empire are actually pulling their weight. The Vault : Watch the vault fill as your net worth climbs its way to a billion dollars. Each stack represents $10K, each layer is $1M, and each pillar is $50M. Statistics: A breakdown of your cash, net worth, and the performance of each money making opportunity detailing your investment into it, and the return out of it. Cash History : Review your most recent cash transactions and see exactly where your money came and went. Achievements: Earn 73 satire heavy and meme enriched achievements throughout your rise to billionaire status. Time, economy, and game modes. One game day lasts 60 seconds in real life, creating a constant flow of opportunities, market changes, income and decisions. Your best completion time (in game days) is recorded for each difficulty, for both self-made and trust fund game modes. Optional offline progression can also keep parts of your empire earning while the game is closed. Every playthrough uses a seeded economy. You can enter your own seed when starting a new game or share one with other players so they can experience the same economic conditions. Difficulty determines how strongly those conditions affect the economy. In Chad Billionaire , everyone starts from $0 in the default Self-Made experience. After beating the game in Self-Made, you unlock two additional modes designed to make future playthroughs faster and more customizable. Self-made is the default “started from the bottom” experience. Trust-fund runs begin with a small amount of starting cash and receive boosts that accelerate the early Job Market phase. Trust Fund bonuses scale based on the highest difficulty completed. Bespoke is a lightweight sandbox mode that lets you choose which systems are unlocked from the start and you can begin with up to $100M in cash. Bespoke games do not record completion times or award achievements. Chad Millionaire: The Chad Billionaire Demo Want to see what the game is about before getting heavily invested? Play Chad Millionaire and experience the journey to your first million bucks. If you decide to continue chasing a billion dollars, the full version will pick up right where you left off.
