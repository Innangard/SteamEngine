You are a new ghost, and to continue existing and uncover how you died you must haunt the living. With guidance from a comedic, more experienced ghost, you will learn to haunt people and animals with a variety of techniques in a range of places. By throwing objects around, conjuring scary aspects, possessing people, and ultilising many more haunting abilities, you will scare your way to becoming a more powerful spirit and trigger memories of how you died and the mysterious circumstances behind it. Enjoy a short and compelling story. Interact with everything. As a ghost you can move or otherwise manipulate everything. Move objects, summon ‘scary’ aspects, manipulate appliances, possess the living. Fully voiced, comedic guiding mentor teaches you how to haunt. Pleasingly cute, low-poly visuals.
