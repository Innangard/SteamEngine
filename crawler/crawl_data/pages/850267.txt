Content For This Game Browse all (1) VOXOTRON Soundtrack Free View Community Hub About This Game A psychedelic hyper-speed racer with no speed limit. You pilot an insanely fast ship around a cosmic cylinder. There's no speed limit - only your ability to stay focused. No brakes. No speed limit. This is not a game about slowing down. It's about going faster. There are no brakes. Brakes are boring. But there is a boost button. It's free to use, and it's always on the table. Going faster isn't a punishment. It's the entire point of the game. Short runs. Instant Death. Most runs last under 30 seconds. If you hit something - you die. Three speed classes. Prove your skill across three speed classes: FLOW - Plenty of time to react and learn the tracks. You can keep the boost pressed the whole way. HYPER - The boost gets serious. You can still probably hold it the entire run. OVERDRIVE - Speedrunner territory. Insanely fast. If you can't hyperfocus and stay fully locked in, it's not for you. Insane skill ceiling. So you can hold the boost the entire run. Good. You can still go faster: Grazing - near misses give you extra speed. The more you risk, the bigger the return. Smoothness - turning scrubs speed. Carve clean lines and keep the speed you've earned. The Line - use the cylinder's curvature to find the shortest path and shave off milliseconds. Global leaderboards. Ghost replays. You just got Gold after 30 runs on the same track. Then you check the leaderboard - the world record is five seconds faster than your time. You load its Ghost Replay. You know you can do better. One more run. Daily, weekly and monthly challenges. Fresh challenges every day, week and month, each with its own global ranking. Yesterday's champion starts from zero. Become the best of the best. Multiplayer. Nobody knows the track. Randomized runs mean your 200 hours don't guarantee a win against someone with 2 - whoever survives furthest takes the race. Up to 8 players. Track editor. Build the craziest track you can imagine and share it on Steam Workshop. Then play what everyone else built - if you can survive it. Features No speed limit. Most racing games cap your top speed. Not this one. A track that wraps 360°. Slits come from every angle. Your "ground" is the entire tube. Three speed classes. From "I can see what's coming" to "I'm one with the track now." Multiplayer. Race up to 8 players on randomized tracks. Track editor. Build your own tracks and share them on Steam Workshop. Medal tiers and global leaderboards. The world record is waiting for you. Daily, weekly and monthly challenges. Fresh challenges, each with its own global ranking. Short runs, deep ceiling. Easy to start. Designed to keep getting better the more you play. Psychedelic voxel aesthetic. Designed to look as fast as it feels. Hyperfocus by design. The speed leaves you no choice - lock into flow, or die trying. Psytrance soundtrack. Powerful pumpy music that matches the speed of the race.
