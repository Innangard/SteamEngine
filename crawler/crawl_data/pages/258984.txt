Earth has been transformed into a desolate planet, having lost its former prosperity due to the spread of a deadly virus, severe food shortages, extreme weather, and endless wars that have decimated the population and destroyed settlements. Amidst this catastrophic situation, a mysterious signal suddenly begins broadcasting from Antarctica to countries around the world. It was a faint glimmer of hope, hinting at the existence of abundant resources. Humanity entrusts its last hope to a single child, sending them to the South Pole, where extreme environmental pollution has advanced. Survival Crafting Against the Harsh Forces of Nature Antarctica’s environment changes by the minute, with raging blizzards and extreme cold relentlessly draining your strength. Rely on weather forecasts, gather resources along the way, and craft the items you need while optimizing your gear within a limited inventory. Do you press on, or turn back? Constantly facing difficult choices and managing scarce resources with care will be the key to survival. Bonds with Animals That Support You on Your Solitary Journey In a world of ice stretching as far as the eye can see, you are never truly alone. The Antarctic animals you encounter along the way are irreplaceable companions, sharing in the struggle to survive this unforgiving environment. With their help—and by supporting one another—new paths may open before you. Warm moments spent connecting with these animals in the bitter cold become a source of hope throughout your frozen journey. Memories of the Past, Buried in a Ruined World This is more than just a frozen wasteland. As you venture deeper, you will uncover advanced technology—lost technology—and records left behind by a civilization that once existed here. Abandoned supplies. Subtle anomalies hidden within the landscape. By piecing together these fragmented clues like a puzzle, the truth of what happened to this world will gradually come to light. Follow the traces of the past and journey deeper into the heart of Antarctica. Countless Choices Lead to Your Own Unique Ending Which route to take, how to use your items, and how you choose to interact with those you meet. Every decision you make along the journey can have a major impact on what unfolds next. Depending on your playstyle and the choices you make along the way, the story can lead to a variety of different endings. At the end of this epic journey, what answer will you find? What will you prepare? Who will you walk beside? What will you discover—and what answer will you choose? Follow the memories left behind across the white expanse, and embark on an Antarctic journey where wonder and hardship intertwine.
