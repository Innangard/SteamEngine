Start with the 12-step guided tutorial, or skip it and go straight to the table. Pick the balls. Keep the marks. Call bingo. A single-player bingo roguelite where your card keeps every mark. Set up lines over several hands, close three at once and watch your jokers multiply the score. The card remembers The tray is random. Every choice after it is yours. Each hand, pick balls from the tray and mark your card. The marks stay: set up rows, columns, diagonals and corners over several hands and close them together. DOUBLE LINE. TRIPLE LINE. BINGO! Before you play, you see the exact score: CHIPS × MULT. What you see is what you score, counted hit by hit. Each table has a target. No timer. Build your engine Jokers change what every ball is worth, and order matters: one repeats the joker to its left, another grows with every stop you clear. Finishes power them up. Close patterns hand after hand to raise the COMBO. Between tables, shop for jokers, items and upgrades, or open a LOT: pick 1 of 3 jokers. Keepers: bosses with their own rule Cross 12 stops in 4 rooms. At the end of each room, a keeper shows its rule before your first ball. Only two balls per hand. Only five balls in the tray. Your first joker scores nothing. Short on resources? Skip a table without a keeper for one of 7 stubs. The arcade closes at dawn. Mara keeps a message she could never send, and four dark relays wait upstairs.
