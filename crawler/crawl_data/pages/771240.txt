Thanks to the stones, all the kingdoms of the southern continent used to live in peace and harmony, but that is about to change. Lox, leader of the kingdom of Belvedor, has always wanted to possess this power to carry out his evil plan. He recently kidnapped Princess Amanda because she is one of the few people who knows where the stones are hidden. Now Diego will enter a very dangerous adventure, but he will have the help of his wizard Bartolo to find the 5 stones of Praxx and rescue the princess before it's too late.
