The protagonist Olena tries to prevent a nuclear catastrophe. Olena travels through numerous realities in order to find a way to rescue her own world.   You play as a doctor Olena Ponomarenko, a scientist working on space and time travel, but not everyone likes this. The top secret and dangerous mission can't wait. Is there any way out of this situation? She has to find out! Change the history and face the challenges you will meet in Chernobyl and Pripyat... You can't fail because the price is too high. Rescue Your Reality It isn't easy to live without parents. Our main character - Olena Ponomarenko, knows it best. She has always wanted to see her parents again to tell them how much she loves them and that is why she is in a place like this. As a scientist, she tries to find a way to travel in time. Is it possible? She doesn't know it yet, but maybe she will find it out in the upcoming future. Could you help her? It would be great to go on this adventure together! Describe Your Enemy As you may know - not every person is trustworthy. Some people might not have as good intentions as you think. It will be important to carefully observe the situation. You never know where you can find help and who can be trusted. Maybe you are not just a tool in somebody else’s hands. You have to look out and think reasonably. CORE FEATURES A grim story about conspiracy, crime, and an attempt to rescue people's lives. Delve into a complex and terrifying story about the place and the people suffering from the consequences of the catastrophe. Of course, you can't believe everyone, but it is up to you who you trust. Story based on Chernobyl localization The game has a unique photorealistic feeling of the environment thanks to photogrammetry scanning. Our experience as developers who visited the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone several times let us create an immersive world full of emotions and secret meanings. Visit the most well-known places in Chernobyl and find a way to rescue it. Interact with objects and the environment You will have the opportunity to use many futuristic devices to travel in time and space! Your interactive watch will help you navigate and discover the dark secrets of the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone, and your special suitcase will help you explore various realities! Immersive gameplay Virtual exploration of Chernobyl has never been so exciting! The correlation between the story and Virtual Reality technology draws players deep into the game's world. Every single move has an important influence on the evolution of the story. Interactive puzzle mechanics VR controllers will allow you to interact with the environment and solve the problems emerging on your way! Not everything is as simple as it looks. You will discover a lot of riddles that will help you find the path to a better future! But, are you ready to uncover the truth? Unique assets necessary to time travel Your suitcase is a unique object. You need to find a way to figure out how it works. Once it’s done, you will be ready to explore space and time! Will it be enough to solve this conspiracy? You need to find out!
