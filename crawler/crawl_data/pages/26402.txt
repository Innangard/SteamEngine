About This Content This Pack includes: F/A-18С Early (Rank 8 USA); Premium account for 30 days; 3000 Golden Eagles. The F/A-18C Early is an earlier modification of this legendary multirole fighter-bomber developed by McDonnell Douglas in the late 1970s. It features an AN/APG-65 radar with advanced tracking and engagement capabilities, along with a large number of AIM-9L and AIM-7M air-to-air missiles and a 20 mm Vulcan cannon. For ground combat, you can equip a wide array of guided weaponry, including the GBU-12, GBU-16, GBU-10, and GBU-24 laser guided bombs, and the AGM-65E and AGM-65F Maverick guided missiles along with a thermal targeting pod. On top of the impressive arsenal is similarly impressive flight performance, with high speed, climb rate, and very high agility. One of the greats, the F/A-18C Early is a highly capable aircraft, excelling in both air combat and close air support roles. Note!
