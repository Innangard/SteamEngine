Expand your fleet with 10 new buses and plan efficient routes to fulfill the extensive contracts from various attraction providers, ensuring tourists reach their destinations on time! 10 New Buses With this DLC, you can add a total of 10 new buses to your fleet, varying in size and transport capacity. These include minibuses, coaches and double-deckers. You can also modify your buses with audio guides to increase passenger satisfaction on sightseeing routes. Each model offers different advantages to help you tackle upcoming challenges. Decide how to design your bus fleet based on passenger demand. New Type of Lines Take advantage of the brand-new Hotel Shuttle route type, where you receive contracts from attraction providers. Whether it's a water park, beach, or rafting excursion, every evening you’ll find out how many guests have checked in at various hotels. Plan the necessary routes to pick up tourists from their hotels in the morning and bring them back in the evening. Be sure to allocate sufficient bus capacity to keep all guests satisfied. Besides the hotel shuttle route type, a new sightseeing route has been introduced that allows you to transport the paying tourists from hotels to fun points of interest and iconic sights. New Scenarios and Events Try out three brand-new scenarios that will test your skills. Alongside these, you’ll encounter a variety of new scenario goals and events, adding even more diversity to your gameplay. With new decorations and a tropical depot background, you’ll bring a true vacation vibe right into your bus depot. New Ticket Type Besides the new hotel shuttle line, a sightseeing route has been added along with its very own sightseeing day ticket! Make sure that your sightseeing routes include as many hotels, fun points of interest and sights as possible and adjust the prices accordingly for maximum profit.
