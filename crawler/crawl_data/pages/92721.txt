Note on Compatibility The Ratline is not optimised for Steamdeck. Please use Mouse and Keyboard for optimal experience. Summary 1971. A dead priest. A list of Nazi fugitives. The hunt begins. When a Catholic priest is found murdered in Budapest, a secret list is discovered —names of Nazi war criminals who escaped justice through a covert network known as The Ratline . Now, the case is yours. In this gritty detective thriller set in 1971, you play an investigator chasing shadows across continents. Use your wits, intuition, and network of contacts to follow leads, dig through documents, and piece together the truth. You will: Examine documents, photographs, and recovered artifacts Make independent deductions with no hand-holding Act like a proper sleuth , working contacts and shaking down sources from Rome to Buenos Aires Hunt down your marks - put together their identities, check your work and bring them to justice. Made by Owlskip , the studio behind acclaimed narrative detective games Family , Rivals , Conspiracy , Echo Beach , Life & Crimes , and Riley and Rochelle . The war may be over—but the reckoning has just begun. The Ratline Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: This game is concerned with the historical events of the Ratline - the fleeing of Nazis from Europe shortly after the war. It takes this subject very seriously and deal with it sensitively.
