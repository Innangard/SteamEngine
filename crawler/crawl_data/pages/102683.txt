View Community Hub About This Game A solitaire game is a great way to escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Go play it in relaxing places with the company of your pet friends, the urban dweller Buddy and the nature lover Lucky. It just depends on your mood. Choose the city locations and play with the Buddy card deck, or the countryside locations, with the Lucky card deck. Enjoy the beautifully illustrated card decks while playing a match, and take a break from time to time to enjoy the landscape around you - within reach of a mouse move! The game offers Classic Solitaire (Klondike) in Draw 1 and Draw 3 modes. Buddy and Lucky animating the cards, in lovely illustrations by Katia Numakura. 6 different scenarios to enjoy - in nature and in the city. A perfect relaxing soundtrack. Collect in-game bonus cards finding special items in the landscape.
