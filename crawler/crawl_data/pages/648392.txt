What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “The game will release episodically but I don't want to charge people per episode. So it will be a one time price and then you will get access to the new episodes as they release. The initial early access release will contain the first full episode. After which I plan to release 2 more episodes with the possibility of bonus episodes after 1.0. As well as this it means I can take in feedback from players and improve the game as I continue to develop it based on said feedback. Involving the community in the shaping of the final product.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The planned release window for all episodes to be complete is late 2027.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “The game will be considered the full version when all episodes I have planned are released. As well as quality of life improvements and bug fixes. When the game leaves early access it is planned to consist of 3 episodes.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current build contains all of the levels of the first episode. This concludes the first part of the story. Around 6 - 7 hours for the first playthrough - after which you can replay the game with gameplay modifiers, new difficulties etc via the custom game menu.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “The game will be cheaper during early access of course. The price won't go up too much though after the 1.0 release. A small price raise will occur for each new episode, so when episode 2 releases I'll put the price up by a couple of dollars/euros. 1.0 should cost around $18/16 euro.” How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process? “We will be asking for feedback during early access and will tune the game based on this feedback.” Read more Download COVEN Demo Download Buy Coven $14.99 Add to Cart Buy Warlocks and Witches BUNDLE (?)
