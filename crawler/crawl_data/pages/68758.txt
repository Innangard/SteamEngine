View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game SCP: Equestrian is a fast-paced first-person shooter set in the SCP universe. You will play as a member of Mobile Task Force Unit Rho-47, also known as "The Equestrians," a highly specialized unit usually tasked with raiding hostile Groups of Interests and combating major threats to the SCP Foundation using cutting-edge mobility gear. Designed for speed and style, this game promises silky smooth movement, quick gunplay, and rapid on-the-feet decision-making, all wrapped up in futuristic and aesthetic low-poly graphics with smooth and satisfying weapon animations. Movement Play as Chimera, a new member of Rho-47 that transferred from MTF Unit ██████. Equipped with an experimental anomalous mobility gear, utilize double-jumping, dashing, sliding and many more movement mechanics to speed past your enemies with ease. Weapons Be equipped with a variety of weapons from a expansive arsenal of guns, blades and anomalous gear. Combo different weapons together and maximize damage by animation-canceling and utilizing weapon abilities. Levels Play short and sweet levels that could take less than a minute to beat for skilled players. Enjoy replaying levels to beat your previous records, and/or discover new secrets and Easter eggs. Enemies Face off against a diverse roster of enemies, Chaos Insurgents, Broken God Cultists, and SCP Anomalies. Be challenged with unique combat situations forcing you to think and shoot while evading the relentless attacks from the enemies. Style Enjoy stylized low-poly graphics with fluid and stylish animations, ensuring performance and clarity, even amidst the chaotic dance of combat. Challenge your FPS skills with SCP: Equestrian, master the speed, precision and skill required to uncover and neutralize the biggest threats to the SCP Foundation. Your duty awaits. Mature Content Description The developers describe the content like this: There is a large amount of blood with minimal dismemberment. Display and firing of fictional firearm-like weapons, as well as cold weapons such as swords and axes.
