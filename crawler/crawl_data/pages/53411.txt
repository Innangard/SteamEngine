View Community Hub About This Game MakeRoom is an adorable sandbox building game. Start with a few gentle design requests to learn the ropes, or jump straight into freeform building with more than 1,100 objects and the ability to create your own custom furniture. What will you make? Whether it's a cozy room for cats, a moody hideaway for a vampire, or a compact camper van, the choice is yours! Start with a few simple, guided requests Dive right in with gentle design requests to discover MakeRoom 's tools and extensive furniture catalog. Get inspired by the cozy, cute, and weird variety of gentle design requests on MakeRoom Island. Customize everything with powerful design tools Start with four different settings for your diorama: Room, Camper, Boat, or Garden. Use powerful design tools to create your own custom furniture from scratch. Move, rotate, resize, and recolor objects to your heart's content. Add the finishing touches with dozens of backgrounds, environments, and even ambient sound options. Dazzle the MakeRoom community with your creations Snap beautiful photos with filters and visual effects like tilt shift, share your creations with other players via Steam Workshop, and discover secret codes to unlock even more objects. There are 6,000+ creations to explore!
