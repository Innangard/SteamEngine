In Gold Digger , every coin helps your little mine grow. Start digging, hire some animals to help, and watch a quiet patch of forest fill with busy workers and bouncing shovels. Point at a shovel to dig for gold and earn coins. Spend them on more shovels and extra space for your growing crew. Watch a quiet patch of grass turn into a busy little mine. Hire moles to handle the digging, magpies to search for better digging spots, and foxes to keep everyone motivated. Join in when you feel like it, or sit back and watch your crew get to work. As you earn coins, you level up and gain points to spend in the skill tree. Make your workers faster, find rare treasure more often, or unlock silver, gold and diamond shovels. Pick what you want to improve next. You can also dig up treasure chests full of equipment. Give your workers better hats and boots, or fit your shovels with better parts. Try different combinations to help your crew work faster and bring in more gold.
