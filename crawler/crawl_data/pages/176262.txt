View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions About This Game Welcome to Horror Nightmare Collection, a series of short, retro-style horror games that aim to spook you with unique experiences. Horror is simple at its core; you don't need fancy budgets or high-tech stuff. All it takes is a raw idea and the guts to make it happen. Some games here focus on horror and tension. You'll face different stories, each pulling you into dark twists and forcing you to confront creepy creatures. This collection keeps growing, bringing more horror games with various gameplay styles and different stories. From messing with your mind in psychological horror to cringe-worthy grotesque body horror, we've got it all. Already in the collection are eight unnerving games: Forsaken Prison: Awaken in a forgotten prison with a cryptic note guiding your escape. Unravel grim discoveries, help a desperate fellow prisoner, and explore dark corridors with secrets waiting to be uncovered. A flashlight leads you to a numbered cell, revealing a nightmarish transformation. Each revelation raises questions about the enigmatic nature of the forsaken prison. Paul’s Lost Remains: Wake up to a horrifying scream in the middle of the night. Your friend Paul is missing, and the forest holds a gruesome secret. Can you collect and assemble his remains on a cross in the center of the forest? Test your nerves and challenge your sanity in this short horror game. Evil Crow: You are stranded in a rural area with a broken car and no help in sight. In this first-person horror game, you have to explore a barn and a tunnel system, and collect tools. You will also witness a dark ritual involving a pentagram that unleashes a terrifying enemy that will hunt you down. Haunted Mine: Haunted Mine is a horror game that puts you in the shoes of a demolition expert who is hired to destroy an old mine. You have no clue who your employer is or what their motive is, but you don't bother to ask. You just want to finish your task and collect your reward. But as you venture deeper into the mine, you discover that something is terribly wrong. The mine is not abandoned. It is alive. And it is watching you. Terror Gnome: You, a young child, is sent on a simple task by your mother: gather flowers from the nearby woods. But a terrorizing gnome is causing trouble along the way. Will the child succeed, or will things take a different turn? Port To Hell: Port to Hell is an indie first-person shooter-horror game where you play as a scientist who has just completed a teleportation portal. Attempting to teleport to Paris, you find yourself instead in a dark dungeon. The portal you used to enter this realm is now non-functional, leaving you stranded. Face enemies like zombies, hellish dogs, and skeleton spiders as you seek a way back home. Uncover the force behind your misdirected teleportation and fight to survive against overwhelming odds. Terminal Flesh: Terminal Flesh is a first-person horror game that blends exploration, combat, and surreal body horror. As a terminal patient undergoing a procedure, you find yourself trapped between reality and a living nightmare. While doctors fight to save your life in a hospital room, your mind descends into a grotesque world of flesh, veins, organs, and tumors. Deep SOUP: Deep Soup is a surreal, soup-like exploration game. Each room contains one unique object. When you get close to it, you can interact to move on to the next room. Stay tuned for more nightmares are on the way as our collection continues to grow!
