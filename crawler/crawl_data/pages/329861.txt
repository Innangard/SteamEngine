Learn more Leaving Early Access: Nov 6, 2026 What the developers have to say: Why Early Access? “We have had a long-standing passion for narrative and simulation games, and to create the best possible experience, we recognize the need for community involvement. Early Access provides a unique opportunity to work with players and shape the direction of the game, ensuring that it meets the needs and desires of the community. As the game is still in development, players can expect exciting changes and updates to the gameplay, making it an ever-evolving experience.” Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access? “The duration of Early Access is subject to player engagement and interest in the project. We're committed to delivering a polished and complete game, and will exit Early Access once the story arc has been fully realized. If the game proves to be successful and well-received, our goal is to transition out of Early Access within up to 2 years.” How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version? “We plan to expand the game world with additional gameplay tiers, new locations, and richer storyline branches. We plan to add more customization options, advanced NPC routines, additional criminal scenarios, and new features like dynamic town events. In the final release we plan to include fully developed character arcs, more mini-games, expanded relationship mechanics, and polishing across the board – including UI/UX, performance, and audio-visual improvements. We’re also planning to add voice acting and achievement support.” What is the current state of the Early Access version? “The current Early Access version includes the full core gameplay loop: managing and customizing your saloon, preparing and serving drinks and dishes, and influencing customer behavior. Criminal underworld interactions and multiple mini-games are also already available. All major systems are functional and being continuously improved based on community feedback.” Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? “Price varies based on content. View Community Hub Report bugs and leave feedback for this game on the discussion boards See all discussions Roadmap About This Game Start by running a saloon and carve your own place in the Wild West. Shape your bar, hire help, and get drawn into the lives and troubles of the people who gather around your counter. Out here, honest business is rarely enough. See what it takes to survive the frontier. Run a working saloon from behind the counter. Mix drinks, cook meals, and keep supplies moving as the town slowly comes to life. It all starts as honest work, but never stays simple for long. Shape the look of your saloon, clean up after long nights, and decide how much work you take on yourself. Hire staff to keep things running and turn the place into something the town recognizes as yours. The Wild West rarely plays fair. Fights break out, bets are placed, and tempers flare. Survival often depends on how well you handle pressure and chaos. And you may bet, those two things are more than certain. Cross the Line even further… Sometimes, honest work is not enough. In Blueberry, opportunities appear in the gray areas of the frontier. What you choose to do when they surface is a different kind of business altogether. Every face at the bar has a story. Get involved in the lives of Blueberry’s residents and watch personal tales unfold. Funny, tense, and sometimes dark. Your saloon is the hub for all good stories. Core mechanics Saloon customization - personalize your saloon with furniture sets, bar equipment, decorations, color tints, and functional upgrades. Serve Food & Drinks - Prepare meals, mix drinks, and influence customer moods through hands-on bar mechanics. Quest System - Meet unique characters with their own stories, ambitions, and problems that become part of your journey. Criminal Opportunities - Explore the darker side of the Wild West and choose how far you are willing to go. Saloon & Business Management - Expand with gambling tables, rooms for rent, staff hiring, and side ventures. Reputation System - Earn trust, unlock rewards, and watch the town react to your growing influence. Frontier Activities - Encounter side mechanics and unexpected moments that capture the spirit of the Old West.
