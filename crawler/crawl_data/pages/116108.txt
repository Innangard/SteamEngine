View Community Hub About This Game Deep Space Exploitation is a space mining game featuring 2D physics and a destructible environment. Set in the distant future where mining ship pilots work for exploitative corporations; you will have to upgrade your ship, master the asteroids, and make difficult decisions if you want to survive and secure your future. 2D Physics + Pixel Art: Chunky pixel art and physics provide satisfying crashes, shots, and explosions. Choose Your Equipment: Upgrade your ship and choose the best tools for the job, but remember; everything has a price. Master The Asteroids: The resources of the asteroid fields aren't easy to take; you will need both courage and caution if you want to succeed. Survive Bureaucracy: Choose how your story unfolds; stick to corporate law to survive, or take your future into your own hands. No generative AI was used at any point in the creation of this game.
