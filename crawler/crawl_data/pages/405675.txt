Timekiller game "for the rage of the day", in which the player acts as a miner and tries to obtain the maximum amount of currency. There is a multitude of currencies in the game, from well known Bitcoins, to our own made up Criptomoneya coin (CMCN). The best simulator of bitcoin-mining, you can now obtain cryptocurrency even on a weak computer! Get a virtual workstation and earn coins by solving simple puzzles. The main goal of the game is to find matching currencies in a set time, receiving in-game coins with which you can buy boosters and upgrades. Collect the maximum amount of all the currencies, prove that you are the top miner! Available: - Game mining of cryptocurrencies in two modes. Simple and difficult. Difficult mode nets more coins. - Game boosters. - Achievement. - Trading cards.
