Hartsfield-Jackson has the tallest air traffic control tower in North America (398 feet or 121 meters) and is the fourth tallest in the world. The airport is the primary hub of Delta Air Lines. With just over 1,000 flights a day to 225 domestic and international destinations, the Delta hub is the world's largest airline hub.  The complex includes the Domestic and International terminals and Concourses T, A, B, C, D, E and F. Within these concourses, there are a total of 192 gates comprised of 152 domestic and 40 international gates. All international arrivals are processed in Concourses E and F; Concourse F is the only concourse in the airport that has a gate that can support an Airbus A380, the largest passenger aircraft in the world. On average, there are 2,700 arrivals and departures daily.
