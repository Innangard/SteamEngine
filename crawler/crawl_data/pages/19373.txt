Bird Game 3 is a multiplayer game where you and your friends battle in an asymmetric game mode for eggs. The Eagle The mighty eagle has to protect its nest at all costs! Use the powerful Eagle Eye Ability to keep an eye on your eggs, as well as possible intruders. The Ravens The other players get to play together as ravens. Teamwork is key. Spot the egg, fly in – and poop back out. The ravens get a unique speed boost ability – powered by poop. Now they have to escape with the egg and throw it in a giant pool of lava! Customize & Hang out Customize your bird in the lobby, where you can hang out pre game, play around or try to complete the race challenge with a new record time! 2 – 8 Players Bird Game 3 is best enjoyed with a minimum of 4 players, but lobbies can support groups of up to 8 of you! Dive in and happy plucking!
