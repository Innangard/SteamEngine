View Community Hub About This Game STAND AGAINST THE DREADMARCH Hell’s armies sweep across the world, and only the Champions of the Celestial Bastion can resist them. Travel across sprawling Campaign Maps, enter battlefields overrun by infernal hordes, and grow stronger with every victory. Unlock devastating weapons, recover powerful relics, and reclaim humanity’s fallen kingdoms. Push back the corruption and shatter the ranks of the Dreadmarch. As your kill count rises, infernal Commanders begin hunting you, unleashing unpredictable events throughout each campaign. Earn gold, discover rare items, and strengthen your Champion between battles. Explore abandoned locations, liberate strongholds, and survive increasingly deadly invasions as the war spreads across the world. Slay endless enemies, confront powerful Commanders, and challenge the ultimate force behind the march of darkness. Key Features: Survivor-Style Combat: Battle endless waves of enemies in short, intense runs. Celestial Bastion Progression: Upgrade heroes, weapons, and global buffs between runs. World Map Strategy: Decide which locations to defend, explore, or reclaim. Hero Variety: Unique heroes, each with special weapons, stats, and powers. Weapon Evolutions: Unlock upgrades across multiple weapons and powers. Dynamic Events: Infernal Commanders appear when the invasion reaches critical thresholds, shaking up every map.
