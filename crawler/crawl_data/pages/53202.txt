From Early Access to the full 1.0 release, your reviews, suggestions, and shared experiences have helped this town—first imagined on paper—find a community of residents to call its own. Many of the characters, buildings, and devices that once existed only as rough sketches gradually became part of the game world we have all experienced together. This digital artbook contains more than 330 pieces of artwork, covering the streets of Doloc, wasteland architecture, farming equipment, character designs, and mechanical beings from the Old City. Many of the designs in Doloc Town went through a complete creative process, from initial sketches and structural studies to added details and their final implementation in the game. Through this collection, we hope to share the creative journey behind Doloc Town and preserve the story of how this town blooming amid the wasteland grew from hand-drawn line art into a complete game world. Volume I: The Town and Architecture Explore the design process behind Doloc Town and its major buildings. Volume II: The Farm, Wilderness, and Dungeons Features original designs for farming facilities, wilderness environments, the Old City Ruins, monsters, and mechanical beings. Volume III: Characters, Items, and the Main Story Features designs for the protagonist, townspeople, characters from different factions, and important devices and items connected to the main storyline. Most of the content in this artbook consists of original artwork drawn by hand during the game’s development and preserved as high-resolution scans. Wherever possible, we have retained the handwritten notes, original linework, and early design ideas to offer a more authentic look at Doloc Town before it took its final form. Due to the volume of material involved, many pages of original artwork—particularly their handwritten notes and annotations—have not been fully localized into English. We appreciate your understanding. How to Access the DLC This DLC is a digital artbook and does not include additional gameplay content. You must own the Steam version of Doloc Town to access it. After purchasing and installing the DLC, follow these steps to access the three PDF volumes: Find Doloc Town in your Steam Library. Right-click the game and select Properties, then open the DLC tab. Make sure Doloc Town - Digital Artbook is checked and installed. Return to your Steam Library and right-click Doloc Town. Select Manage → Browse Local Files. Open the game’s root directory to find the three artbook PDF files.
