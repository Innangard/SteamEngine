Literally. Compacted is a slot machine roguelite where every spin costs precious space. Earn gold, gain experience, reduce your debt and force back the walls before they close around you. Each run revolves around five symbols from a roster of 17 unlockable symbols. Level up to upgrade them, choose items that reshape your strategy and combine their effects into increasingly powerful builds. Landing three matching symbols triggers a jackpot, producing effects that can multiply rewards, reduce debt or buy you enough room to survive another round. Randomness determines what appears, but your decisions determine whether it works. Balance immediate survival against longterm growth, decide which symbols deserve upgrades and adapt your strategy before the lender’s demands become impossible to meet. Every stage introduces a new lender and a larger debt. Fail to repay them before the walls close, and the rules of the Lenders Association are simple: You get compacted. Features of Compacted: 24 increasingly demanding stages, each featuring a new lender. Choose five active symbols from a roster of 17 unlockable symbols. Run-changing items that create new combinations and strategies. Jackpots triggered by landing three matching symbols. Permanent upgrades unlocked between runs using earned chips. 18 Steam achievements. No real gambling. Success comes from strategy, probability and resource management.
