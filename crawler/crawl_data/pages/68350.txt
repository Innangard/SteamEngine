There are two modes: Relaxed and Competitive. Relaxed Mode: You can choose what background art, type of bubble and fall speed you want and let it go. In relaxed mode the only goal is to chill. You don't even need to pop anything, the bubbles will pop themselves at the bottom of the screen. Competitive Mode: Race against the clock to pop as many bubbles possible before three bubbles hit the ground. The backdrops are chosen at random, so you won't always play the same order. Compete against others to take your spot on the Top 10 leaderboard for both the Weekly and Daily categories.
