Everyone is both a target and a hunter, blend in with NPCs to avoid suspicion, while collecting clues to unmask and eliminate your target before your hunter gets you. Your goal: collect as many coins as possible before the round ends. Earn coins by eliminating your target or your hunter. Blend in with NPCs to avoid suspicion. Explore the map and arm yourself with deadly weapons; crossbows, dynamite, daggers, the choice is yours! Identify your target among the crowd, sneak close, and strike before your hunter strikes you.
