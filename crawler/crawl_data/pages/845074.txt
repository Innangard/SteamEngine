Simulator 3 – Vancouver International Airport (CYVR) Take charge of air traffic at Vancouver International Airport (CYVR / YVR) in Tower! Simulator 3 and experience the challenge of Canada’s key west-coast gateway. Situated on Sea Island near Richmond, British Columbia, YVR combines large-scale international operations with busy regional and domestic traffic, giving you a rich, layered ATC experience. Airport ground chart: The add-on brings a detailed, realistic YVR environment: authentic runway and taxiway layout, realistic ATC frequencies, and full customization. Whether you're controlling during a summer surge of trans-Pacific flights or a calm evening of regional operations, your decisions will shape traffic flow and ground efficiency. With three runways — two long parallel main runways (08R/26L at ~3,505 m and 08L/26R at ~3,030 m) plus a crosswind runway (13/31) — the airport offers complex arrival and departure patterns. Features – Realistic modeling with over 80 custom modelled buildings. – Detailed ground polygon model. – Updated Radar screens. – Modelled Taxi signs. – Updated taxi light models that follow the curve of the lines and wigwag lights placed at runway entrances. – Dynamic runway approach lighting. – Beautiful background cityscape from OSM (open street maps) Key Specs ICAO Code: CYVR Name: Vancouver International Airport (YVR) Location: Richmond, British Columbia, Canada — on Sea Island Wikipedia Terminals: 2 (“Main” + “South / float plane”) Airport Spotting System Requirements Minimum: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: 64 bit Windows 8.1, Windows 10 or Windows 11 Processor: 3.5 Ghz Memory: 16 GB RAM Graphics: 4GB VRAM Network: Broadband Internet connection Storage: 20 GB available space Additional Notes: Requires keyboard and microphone / headset Recommended: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system OS *: 64 bit Windows 8.1, Windows 10 or Windows 11 Processor: 3.7 Ghz Memory: 32 GB RAM Graphics: 8GB VRAM Network: Broadband Internet connection Storage: 20 GB available space Additional Notes: Requires keyboard and microphone / headset * Starting January 1st, 2024, the Steam Client will only support Windows 10 and later versions.
