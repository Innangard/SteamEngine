Download Railroad Corporation - Competitive Spirit DLC Free To Play Download View Community Hub About This Content Competitive Spirit DLC is a set of different sandbox missions featured with AI competitors. A special interactive mode is available for a relaxed long-time play simultaneously against one, two or three AI players on the map. In this DLC there is a unique interaction mode where you are asked to complete different quests and tasks to be able to outrun the scenario competitors. Interaction mode is still a variety of a sandbox mode, thus it's also a relaxing, penalty-free, endless mode. Once starting the scenario there is a choice either to launch a pure sandbox mode or an interactive one. So it is up to the player the way this or that map will be played. Missions in this DLC: The Swamp A sandbox map with a unique terrain covered with a lot of water will require all your rails laying skills. The terrain provides high railroads building challenge. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. The Wild Plain Mountains and lakes are across the river. Here there is a huge plain. But you are not alone on these territories anymore. Competitors have also decided to run some business here. Mission can be played in an interactive or pure sandbox mode. Three Rivers Valley Huge territory split by rivers split competitors far from each other. But for how long? This a sandbox map with a relatively flat terrain, which doesn't causes much railroad building challenges, however, it could be suitable for those who like to build big junctions with turnabouts, which require a lot of space. Mission can be played in an interactive mode or as a pure sandbox.
