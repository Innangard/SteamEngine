In an arena shrouded in shadow, pay close attention to your surroundings—vision is restricted and monsters can be anywhere. Swap between Circle and Cone Vision modes: one lets you see nearby, while the other lets you see farther in one direction. Fight escalating hordes, bosses, and survive! Build Crafting Collect EXP from enemies to level up and discover hundreds of upgrades. From singularities to radiation, the possibilities are limitless. You can wield lightning and fire, shoot thousands of bullets, create a whirlwind of swords... the choice is yours. Every upgrade tree changes the way you play. Even Vision itself can be shaped to your advantage: will you sacrifice it to increase power, or bolster it as your beacon in the depths? Unlocks Every run rewards you with Credits to unlock new weapons, gear, and permanent upgrades. Each weapon has its own unique projectiles and perks, offering plenty of options to suit your playstyle. (There's a flamethrower!) Invest your earnings into powering up your character or adding run modifiers that reshape future runs.
