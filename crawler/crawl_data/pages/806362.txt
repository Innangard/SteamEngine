This album features powerful full-orchestra arrangements of tracks from A REALM REBORN, Heavensward, and STORMBLOOD, performed at "FINAL FANTASY XIV ORCHESTRA CONCERT 2019". The CD album FINAL FANTASY XIV Orchestral Arrangement Album Vol. 2 was released on September 18, 2019, and the second FFXIV orchestra concert took place on September 21 and 22, 2019, in Yokohama. Fans can relive the journey of the Warriors of Light through luxurious orchestral arrangements, experiencing the excitement of Eorzean music over and over again—both with the ears and the imagination.
