In his altered and fading memories, he imagines himself as a little boy named Griffin who finds solace in the company of a teddy bear that embodies the spirit of his deceased son. Trying to evade the ever-present grasp of Death itself, you will meet the most important characters of Griffin’s life in this mysterious world: a cruel and harsh father who turned into a giant knight, and a fragile but protective mother embodied in the form of a flower fountain. Travel through a mysterious world of craggy caves and floating castles, fairytale forests and rolling plains. Prepare to be enthralled by the captivating world of Daydream, discover the transformative power of resilience, and accept the bittersweet beauty of letting go. Key features — Dive deep into a sprawling story, revealing secrets and unravelling the mysteries of an enchanting narrative; — Step into an unforgettable world accompanied by dreamy melodies and mesmerizing music; — Encounter memorable characters, such as the endearing young Griffin and his steadfast teddy bear companion Birly; — Command an adorable teddy bear friend who assists you along the journey; — Collect all memory moths to help Griffin find peace.
