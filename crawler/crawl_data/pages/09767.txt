The game focuses on challenging fights, skill mastery, and atmosphere rather than gear management, leveling, or grinding. As a lifelong fan of Souls-like and adventure games, I tried to create my own take, looking more into the genre's old-school roots. Everything, from the art and code to the music, has been handcrafted by me with care and attention to detail. No AI was used in the development process. Over 6 years of development, I've poured my heart and soul into this game, and I truly hope you'll enjoy it! About This Game In a realm where the gods of life reign, Death starts to spread. It is foretold that you, a husk of a once-diligent scholar, will find a legendary mask and slay this eldritch evil. In the mountaintop kingdom of Wisdom, a prophecy begins to unfold, but it is your decision to fight for the gods and fulfill the prophecy or to become an apostle of Death. Fast-paced combat Evade enemy attacks, chain together your learned combat arts into unique combos, and channel the power of gods to crush your enemies! Copy your enemies' attacks Your mask grants you the ability to steal your enemies' combat skills. Learn unique attacks from each enemy you kill, and grow your arsenal of dozens of deadly combat arts. Challenging boss fights The boss fights will truly put your fighting skills to the test. From giant arachnoids and mechanical beasts to dragons and terrifying angelic beings, every single boss is designed with their unique move set to be challenging but fair. Unique World You will travel through lush swamps, chilling ruins, and ancient cities. Each world features unique enemies and secrets. Explore the twisted lands of the gods and uncover the truth behind the spreading of Death in this once thriving world. Steal your enemies' attacks and weave their moves into your own combos Face off against unique and challenging monsters and terrifying bosses Explore large, interconnected surreal worlds and uncover their secrets Acquire the god's powers to solve puzzles and gain an edge in combat Decide the fate of this world: rescue the gods of Life or become an apostle of Death All lands have fallen to Death and been corrupted beyond repair. Let them live on or destroy them once and for all!
