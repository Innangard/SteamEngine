Content For This Game Browse all (1) Recommended Corpus et Spiritus - Supporter Pack $4.99 $4.99 Add all DLC to Cart View Community Hub About This Game Corpus et Spiritus is a roguelite deckbuilder where your alchemical skill will decide your fate. You signed a pact with a Homunculus: a demon that will guide you on the path of alchemy, but at the cost of your life force. Use your workshop to craft new cards, transmute metals and discover magical talismans. Become smarter than your dangerous partner and break out of the pact before it kills you! Unravel the secrets of alchemy Satisfy the Homunculus — Trade your life for great boons. Or don't, but beware the wrath of the Homunculus. Controlled randomness — Use your workshop to create cards and magical talismans. The metals you insert impact its output and what choices you have. Experiment — Seek new recipes, cards and talismans. Unravel the deepest secrets of the art of alchemy or die trying. The pact — At the start of a run, customize your pact by selecting boons and challenges that will help or hinder you. Get closer to breaking the pact and gain tokens that will unlock new alchemy stations and secret cards. Master your deck-building skills Build the perfect deck — Choose cards from 7 different families of body, mind and soul, each with their own gameplay style. Assemble powerful combos — Cards and talismans combine to create increasingly stronger effects. Manage your resources — Metals aren't your only resource; keep your health, wits and stamina in balance against the demons attempt to consume them. Wise management leads to even greater knowledge.
