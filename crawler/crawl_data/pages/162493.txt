Just Click The Button is a game about clicking on a button. The more you click the button, the more the game evolves. Level up, acquire new abilities, choose your playstyle, and build a powerful button! The game can be played indefinitely, allowing you to acquire (almost) every single ability, which will then cause there to be way too much stuff going at once. Or attempt to beat the game. The game can be beaten via 1 of 4 endings. Some builds will make certain endings easier than others, which encourages you to choose specific abilities for whatever ending you will attempt.
